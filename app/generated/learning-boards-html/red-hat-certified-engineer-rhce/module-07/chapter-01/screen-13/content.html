Module 7›Chapter 7.1›Key Concepts
Ansible Role
A self-contained unit grouping tasks, handlers, templates, files, and variables for one concern.
ansible-galaxy
Command-line tool to initialize and manage role skeletons for consistent layouts.
Role Directory Structure
Use tasks, handlers, templates, files, defaults, vars, and meta to keep content discoverable.
Takeaway

Design roles for reuse and idempotency, scaffold with ansible-galaxy, and manage variables by precedence to avoid surprises.
