Deploy safely and avoid common traps
A simple process reduces the risk of lockout and misconfiguration.
Recommended rollout process
1. Ensure package and service are present and enabled.
2. Add SSH allowance for your management path to avoid lockout.
3. Apply changes to a test host with immediate yes and validate connectivity.
4. Canary to a small group, then roll to production after verification.
Common mistakes
  • Locking yourself out by removing management rules.
  • Forgetting permanent or immediate flags and misreading behavior.
  • Applying rules to the wrong zone because of misassigned interfaces.
Scenario tip
Before a widespread change, temporarily open a maintenance window, allow your management subnet explicitly, then make your restrictive rules. Remove the temporary allowance after you confirm success.
Check your understanding
If a rule doesn't take effect, what are two places to check? (Answer: zone assignment of interface/source, and whether the change is runtime vs permanent.)
