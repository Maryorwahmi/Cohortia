Runtime and permanent settings
Understand persistence and immediate application to avoid surprises.
Runtime
Changes apply immediately but are temporary. They are useful for quick tests or when you want to trial a rule before persisting it.
Misconception
Assuming runtime changes survive reboots. They do not unless also saved to permanent configuration.
Permanent
Written to the persistent configuration so rules survive reloads and reboots. They do not automatically affect the current runtime unless applied immediately or the daemon is reloaded.
Recommended practice
Use permanent: yes together with immediate: yes in Ansible when you want both persistence and instant effect, and plan for controlled rollouts to avoid connection disruptions.
