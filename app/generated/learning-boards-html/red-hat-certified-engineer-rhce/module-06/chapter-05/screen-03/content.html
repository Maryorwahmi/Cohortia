Safety checks

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Verification checklist
  • Confirm firewalld service is installed and enabled on target hosts.
  • Confirm permanent and runtime entries exist for added services or ports.
  • Test access from allowed and denied hosts to verify scope.
  • Ensure SSH remains permitted from your management channel before committing large changes.
Tip: Use a maintenance window or console access if you're making broad changes to avoid accidental lockout during testing.
