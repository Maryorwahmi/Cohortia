Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
What to remember
Three compact concept cards and a final takeaway.
Zones
Assign trust to interfaces and sources so policies apply to the right traffic.
Services & Ports
Use services for readability and ports for precision; inspect service definitions first.
Rich Rules
Compose source, port, protocol, and action for exact access controls; validate strings before automating.
Final takeaway

Automate thoughtfully: make changes persistent and immediate when needed, test and verify, protect management access, and prefer clear service names unless precision requires ports or rich rules.
