Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › key_options — restricting key capabilities
Key options example
Add key_options to limit where and how a key may be used.
ansible.posix.authorized_key:
  user: "{{ automation_user }}"
  state: present
  key: "{{ lookup('file', automation_public_key_path) }}"
  key_options: 'from="192.168.1.0/24",no-port-forwarding'
Why it matters
Options shrink the attack surface and can prevent misuse even if the private key is stolen.
Misconception
key_options are not a substitute for network segmentation or proper bastion workflows, but they add defense in depth.
