Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Permissions and ownership — why SSH refuses keys
Required modes
  • ~/.ssh directory: 0700
  • ~/.ssh/authorized_keys: 0600
  • Files must be owned by the target user, not root or another account
Fix commands
chown -R devops_user:devops_user /home/devops_user/.ssh
chmod 0700 /home/devops_user/.ssh
chmod 0600 /home/devops_user/.ssh/authorized_keys
Debug checklist
  1. Confirm the user account exists.
  2. Check ownership and modes of ~/.ssh and authorized_keys.
  3. Inspect SSH server logs for permission-related errors.
Common mistake

Leaving ~/.ssh group- or world-writable will cause SSH to ignore authorized_keys; always enforce strict modes in automation.
