Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Key management and revocation policies
Operational recommendations
  • Generate dedicated automation keys and store private keys securely on the control node.
  • Keep descriptive comments in public keys for easier identification in authorized_keys.
  • Automate revocation using state: absent and maintain a record of distributed keys.
  • Rotate keys periodically and combine key_options with network segmentation and bastions.
  • Test revocation and deployments in a staging group before rolling to production.
Key Takeaway

Strong operational policies and automated revocation reduce exposure and keep access auditable and reversible.
