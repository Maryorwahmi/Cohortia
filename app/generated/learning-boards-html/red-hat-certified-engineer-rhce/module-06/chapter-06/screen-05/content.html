Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Chrony vs ntpd
Contrast: operational strengths
Faster synchronization
Chrony converges more quickly after boot or network reconnection, reducing windows of drift.
Intermittent networks
Designed to work well on systems with sporadic connectivity, such as edge hosts.
Better hardware support
Supports kernel PPS and records drift for long-term correction.
Practical note for automation

When writing Ansible automation target the chrony package and chronyd service. This keeps your inventory consistent and leverages chrony's operational benefits without per-host tuning in most cases.

Key Takeaway

Prefer chrony on RHEL for automated fleets because of faster convergence and better behavior on intermittent networks.
