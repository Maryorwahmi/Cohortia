Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › NOPASSWD: convenience vs. risk
With NOPASSWD
Example entry: %devops ALL=(ALL) NOPASSWD: /usr/bin/systemctl restart httpd
Benefits
  • Enables non-interactive automation.
  • Reduces friction for scripted operations.
Without NOPASSWD
Commands require the user's password when using sudo. This adds an authentication step and reduces risk of automated misuse.
Risk mitigation
  • Specify full binary paths to avoid PATH-based attacks.
  • Limit commands to exact operations, not wildcards when possible.
  • Enable sudo logging to audit NOPASSWD actions.
