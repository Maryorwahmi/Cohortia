Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Integrating SELinux steps into Ansible workflows
Integration pattern
Role structure
  • tasks/main.yml: ensure dirs, sefcontext, restorecon, seboolean
  • defaults/main.yml: expected types and booleans
  • tests/verify.yml: ls -Z and getsebool checks
CI checks
Include post-deploy verification to assert expected contexts and booleans, and scan audit logs for new denials.
Key Takeaway
Embedding SELinux automation into roles and CI makes fixes repeatable, auditable, and less likely to regress over time.
