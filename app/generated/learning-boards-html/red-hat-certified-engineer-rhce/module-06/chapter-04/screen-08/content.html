Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › restorecon behavior and changed_when nuance
restorecon command
restorecon -Rv /srv/mywebapp

Runs recursively and applies types from the policy to files on disk.

Ansible nuance

restorecon exits zero whether it changed files or not. Use changed_when: true to record a change and trigger handlers or audits.

Mini example
changed_when: true
Common mistake
Skipping changed_when hides relabel actions from reports and may prevent follow-up tasks.
Key Takeaway
Declare change intent for commands whose exit codes aren't sufficient for idempotence tracking.
