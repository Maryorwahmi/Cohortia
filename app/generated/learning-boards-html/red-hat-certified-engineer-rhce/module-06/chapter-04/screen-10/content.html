Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Troubleshooting SELinux denials
Diagnostic steps
  1. Collect denial entries from /var/log/audit/audit.log or journalctl -xe.
  2. Run sealert -a /var/log/audit/audit.log to get interpreted advice.
  3. Decide: boolean, context, or custom module. Prefer booleans and contexts.
  4. Translate the suggested fix into ansible.posix.sefcontext or ansible.posix.seboolean tasks.
  5. Apply restorecon if you changed contexts and verify with ls -Z and test the service.
Caution
audit2allow can produce policy that resolves a denial but may be broader than necessary. Review generated rules carefully.
Key Takeaway
Convert log findings into Ansible tasks so fixes are repeatable and documented.
