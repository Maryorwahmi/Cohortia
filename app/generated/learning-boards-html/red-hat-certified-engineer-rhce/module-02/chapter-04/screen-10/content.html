Process Flow
Handlers: scheduling and flushing
Normal behaviour
Handlers are queued and executed at play end. Multiple notifications collapse to one handler run.
Use flush_handlers only when later tasks depend on the handler having run, but weigh the risk of restarting services mid-play.
Example sequence
- name: Update config A
  ansible.builtin.template:
    src: A.j2
    dest: /etc/service/A.conf
  notify: Restart Service

- name: Update config B
  ansible.builtin.template:
    src: B.j2
    dest: /etc/service/B.conf
  notify: Restart Service

- name: Flush handlers early (if needed)
  meta: flush_handlers
Observe that without the flush, the Restart Service handler runs once at the end. With the flush, it runs immediately at the flush point.
