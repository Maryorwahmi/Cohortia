Organizing Playbooks with Roles

Learn the role directory standard, scaffolding with ansible-galaxy, and how to apply roles from a playbook to create reusable automation components.

In this lesson
Why roles matter and the standard layout you will use every day.
Coming up
Scaffold a role, write tasks and handlers, and call it from a playbook.
Why it matters
Modularity reduces duplication and prevents drift in multi-host deployments.
Role Concept Map
Illustrative
Role: my_nginx_role
tasks/main.yml
entry point: installs, config, service control
handlers/main.yml
restart or reload notifications
defaults/main.yml
sensible, overridable defaults
templates/nginx.conf.j2
parameterized configs
files/ for static assets
meta/main.yml for metadata and dependencies
Tip: Use roles to share and version testable automation.
