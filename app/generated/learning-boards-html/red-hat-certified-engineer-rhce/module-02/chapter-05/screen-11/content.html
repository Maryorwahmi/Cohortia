Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › ansible-galaxy init

Terminal command

ansible-galaxy init my_nginx_role
This creates a role skeleton. Open the generated folders and implement your tasks, handlers, defaults, and templates.

Scaffold review

my_nginx_role/
├── defaults/
├── handlers/
├── tasks/
├── templates/
├── files/
├── vars/
├── meta/
└── README.md
Remember: init prepares placeholders. Implementation is still required to make the role functional and idempotent.
