Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › roles: in playbooks

Playbook example

---
- name: Deploy web infrastructure
  hosts: webservers
  become: yes
  roles:
    - role: my_nginx_role
      nginx_port: 80

Notes & verification

  • Pass role parameters under the role entry to override defaults for that invocation.
  • Ensure become: yes if role performs package or system changes.
  • Verify by checking the service and listening ports after running ansible-playbook.
Misconception: roles hide behavior. They do not; inspect the role folders to see the exact tasks and handlers being executed.
