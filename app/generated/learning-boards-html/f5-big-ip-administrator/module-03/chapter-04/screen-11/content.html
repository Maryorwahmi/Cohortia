Managing SSL Certificates and Keys

Ensuring Continuous Secure Communication

Importing Certificates

Bring certificates and keys (e.g., PEM, PKCS#12) into the BIG-IP, including the CA chain for trust.

Renewing Certificates

Monitor expiration dates and proactively renew certificates to prevent service outages and trust errors.

Assigning to Profiles

Once imported, certificates and keys are assigned to the relevant Client SSL Profiles for active use.

Pro Tip

Configure BIG-IP alerts for upcoming certificate expirations to ensure timely renewals and avoid service disruptions.

Key Takeaway

Proactive certificate management is vital for continuous secure communication, preventing outages, and maintaining user trust.
