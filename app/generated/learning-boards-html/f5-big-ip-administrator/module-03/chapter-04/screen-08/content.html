Key Parameters

Client SSL Profile Settings

  • Certificate:

    The public certificate identifying your application, issued by a CA.

  • Key:

    The corresponding private key, essential for decryption and kept secure.

  • Chain:

    The certificate chain (intermediate and root CAs) to establish trust.

  • Cipher String:

    Defines allowed cryptographic algorithms for encryption and key exchange.

  • Protocols:

    Specifies allowed SSL/TLS protocols (e.g., TLSv1.2, TLSv1.3).

Secure Cipher String Example
DEFAULT:!SSLv3:!TLSv1:!TLSv1_1
Common Mistake

Using weak cipher suites or outdated protocols can expose your application to known vulnerabilities.

Pro Tip

Regularly review and update your cipher strings and protocol settings to align with current security best practices.

Key Takeaway

Careful selection of certificates, keys, cipher strings, and protocols in a Client SSL Profile is crucial for strong security and preventing vulnerabilities.
