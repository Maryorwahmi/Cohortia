Monitor Parameters
Probes (Retries) and Best Practices
Fine-tuning failure detection and recovery behavior.
Probes (Retries)

Number of consecutive successful/failed probes required to change a member's status (e.g., 3 retries).

Common Mistake

Overly aggressive monitors (short intervals) can overwhelm backend servers, especially during recovery.

Key Takeaway

Balance Interval, Timeout, and Retries to ensure quick, accurate failure detection without causing undue stress on your application infrastructure.

Example: Failure Detection Timeline
Interval: 5sProbe sent every 5s.
Timeout: 16sMax wait for response.
Retries: 33 consecutive failures to mark DOWN.
If unresponsive, member marked DOWN after 3 * 16s = 48 seconds.
