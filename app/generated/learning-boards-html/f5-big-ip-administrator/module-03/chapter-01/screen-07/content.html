Dynamic Load Balancing Methods

  • •**Observed:** Monitors active connections and response times, favoring historically better-performing members.
  • •**Predictive:** Uses advanced algorithms to predict the most efficient member for new connections based on current and historical data.
  • •**Benefits:** Excellent for highly dynamic environments with unpredictable server load fluctuations.
  • •**Consideration:** Requires a learning period to gather sufficient performance data.
Concrete Example

For a live sports streaming service, Observed or Predictive methods can dynamically shift traffic to servers that are currently handling fewer streams or exhibiting lower latency, ensuring a smooth viewing experience for all users.

Key Takeaway

Observed and Predictive methods offer intelligent, adaptive traffic distribution by learning from and predicting server performance, ideal for fluctuating workloads.

Observed vs. Predictive

Observed Method

**Focus:** Historical performance (connections, response time).

**Mechanism:** Favors members that have performed better in the past.

Predictive Method

**Focus:** Future efficiency (sophisticated algorithm).

**Mechanism:** Predicts which member will handle a new connection most efficiently.

Initial Behavior Note

Both methods require a learning period; initial behavior might be less predictable until sufficient data is gathered.
