Why Advanced Load Balancing?

  • •**Performance Optimization:** Distribute traffic based on real-time server load, not just sequentially.
  • •**Resource Utilization:** Ensure all backend servers are equally utilized, preventing bottlenecks.
  • •**User Experience:** Maintain consistent response times and prevent service degradation.
  • •**Application Resilience:** Adapt to varying server capabilities and failures gracefully.
Concrete Example

Imagine an e-commerce site during a flash sale. Round Robin could send new customers to an already overloaded server, leading to slow page loads or failed transactions. Advanced methods would intelligently direct them to available servers.

Key Takeaway

Advanced load balancing methods are essential for dynamic, high-performance applications, moving beyond simple distribution to intelligent traffic management.

Traffic Distribution Evolution

Round Robin

Simple, sequential distribution. Ignores server load.

Least Connections

Dynamic, based on active connections. Adapts to load.

Ratio & Dynamic Methods

Weighted or predictive distribution. Highly adaptive.
