Key Concepts

Reinforce your understanding of these essential terms.

Ratio (Member/Node)

Distributes connections proportionally based on assigned weights to Pool Members or Nodes.

Observed

Dynamically ranks Pool Members based on performance metrics (response times, connections) and directs traffic to the best-performing one.

Predictive

Uses historical and current performance data to anticipate the most available Pool Member, proactively avoiding overload.

Chapter Summary

Advanced methods like Ratio, Observed, and Predictive offer sophisticated traffic management. The choice of method is heavily influenced by application characteristics, ensuring tailored and efficient traffic distribution.
