Application State

Stateful vs. Stateless

Consider if your application maintains session information. Stateful apps often require persistence alongside load balancing.

Server Homogeneity

Identical or Varied Capacity?

If servers are identical, Round Robin might work. If capacities vary, Ratio is a better choice to maximize powerful machines.

Connection Dynamics

Duration and Intensity

For variable connection durations or resource intensity, dynamic methods like Least Connections, Observed, or Predictive are more effective.

Key Takeaway

Align your load balancing method with your application's specific needs and server characteristics for optimal performance and efficiency.

Decision Flow for Load Balancing
