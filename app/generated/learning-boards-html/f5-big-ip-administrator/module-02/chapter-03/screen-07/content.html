Traffic is distributed based on the assigned ratio, e.g., 3:2:1.
Operational Principle

Weighted Proportionality

Traffic is distributed to Pool Members or Nodes based on a numerical weight (ratio) assigned to each, ensuring proportional distribution.

Ideal Use Case

Heterogeneous Server Capacities

Perfect for environments with servers of varying processing power, allowing more capable machines to handle a larger share of the load.

Configuration Flexibility

Member or Node Level

Ratios can be applied at the individual Pool Member level or to the underlying Node, offering granular control over traffic flow.

Key Takeaway

Ratio load balancing is essential for optimizing performance when your backend servers have different processing capabilities.
