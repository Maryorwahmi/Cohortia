Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember.

Node

A network object representing a physical or virtual server with an IP address, known to the BIG-IP.

Pool Member

A specific service instance on a Node, defined by its IP address and service port, belonging to a Pool.

Health Monitor

A specialized probe used by the BIG-IP to continuously check the operational status of Nodes or Pool Members.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding Nodes, Pool Members, and Health Monitors is fundamental for configuring F5 BIG-IP to ensure high availability and intelligent traffic management for your applications.
