Understanding Node and Pool Member Status

Visual cues for availability and administrative states.

✓

Green (Available)

The Node/Pool Member is up and responding to all assigned health monitors. Traffic is being sent to it.

  • •Operational: Fully functional and healthy.
  • •Traffic: Actively receiving new connections.
✗

Red (Offline)

The Node/Pool Member is down and failing its health monitors. No new traffic is sent to it.

  • •Operational: Unhealthy or unresponsive.
  • •Traffic: No new connections.

Visual Status Indicators

✓Available (Green)
✗Offline (Red)
⏸Disabled (Blue)
?Unknown/Pending (Yellow)
Key Takeaway

Color-coded status indicators in F5 BIG-IP provide immediate visual feedback on the operational and administrative state of Nodes and Pool Members, crucial for quick assessment.
