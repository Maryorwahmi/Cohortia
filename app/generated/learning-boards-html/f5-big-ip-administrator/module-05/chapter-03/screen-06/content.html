Security Models

Negative Security Model & Attack Signatures

Blocking known malicious patterns with blacklists.

Blacklist Approach

Identifies and blocks traffic that matches known malicious patterns or signatures.

Attack Signatures

Predefined rules or patterns that detect specific exploits and vulnerabilities, regularly updated by F5.

Limitations

Less effective against zero-day attacks or highly obfuscated exploits that lack known signatures.

Key Takeaway

The negative security model uses attack signatures to block known malicious patterns, effective against common threats but vulnerable to zero-days.
