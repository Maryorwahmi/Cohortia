Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Active-Active HA

Both BIG-IPs process traffic simultaneously, typically for different applications or traffic groups.

Device Group

A collection of BIG-IP devices that share and synchronize their configuration objects.

Traffic Group

A logical collection of network resources that fail over together as a unit.

Summary Takeaway

Active-Active HA maximizes resource use, while Device Groups ensure configuration consistency and Traffic Groups manage failover resources. Network Failover and Connection Mirroring are critical for seamless transitions.
