Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

High Availability (HA)

A system design ensuring high operational continuity and uptime for critical systems.

Failover

Automatic transfer of control from a failed primary device to a healthy secondary device.

Active-Standby HA

One BIG-IP processes traffic, the other remains idle, ready to take over upon failure.

Summary Takeaway

HA ensures continuous service through failover, with Active-Standby offering a simple, reliable redundancy model.
