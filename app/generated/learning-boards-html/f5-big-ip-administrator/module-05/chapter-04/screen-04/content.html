What is High Availability (HA)?

Ensuring Continuous Service Uptime for Critical Applications

Definition: High Availability

High Availability (HA) is a system design approach that ensures a high level of operational continuity and uptime for critical systems and applications. It involves deploying redundant components to eliminate single points of failure.

  • Prevents service interruptions due to hardware or software failures.
  • Achieved through redundant components and automatic failover mechanisms.
  • Critical for applications where downtime is unacceptable.

The Role of Failover

Failover is the automatic process of transferring operational control and traffic processing from a failed primary device to a healthy secondary device. This ensures minimal disruption to users.
