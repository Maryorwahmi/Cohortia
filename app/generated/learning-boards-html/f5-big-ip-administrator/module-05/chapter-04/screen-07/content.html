Decision Point

Comparing Active-Standby and Active-Active

Understanding the Trade-offs for Your Deployment

Active-Standby

  • Simplicity: Easiest to configure and manage.
  • Reliability: Clear roles, predictable failover.
  • Resource Utilization: One device is idle (standby).
  • Troubleshooting: More straightforward due to dedicated roles.

Active-Active

  • Complexity: More intricate design and management.
  • Resource Utilization: Both devices actively process traffic.
  • Performance: Requires careful traffic group assignment to avoid bottlenecks.
  • Troubleshooting: Can be more challenging due to shared responsibilities.
Key Takeaway

For most, Active-Standby offers the best balance of simplicity and reliability, while Active-Active suits advanced needs for resource optimization.
