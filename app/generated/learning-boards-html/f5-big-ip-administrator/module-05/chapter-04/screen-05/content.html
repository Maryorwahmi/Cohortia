HA Configuration Modes

Active-Standby: Simplicity and Reliability

One Device Active, One Ready to Take Over

Active-Standby Failover Process

1. Active Unit

Processes all traffic.

2. Standby Unit

Monitors, synchronizes.

3. Failover

Standby becomes Active.

Failure Detected
Key Characteristics
  • Clear role separation: one active, one standby.
  • Standby unit continuously synchronized.
  • Simplest HA deployment for F5 BIG-IP.
Key Takeaway

Active-Standby provides reliable failover with minimal complexity, making it ideal for many BIG-IP HA deployments.
