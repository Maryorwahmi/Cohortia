Traffic Groups: Logical Collections for Failover

Grouping Network Resources for Coordinated Failover

What is a Traffic Group?

A Traffic Group is a logical collection of network resources, including floating self-IP addresses, virtual servers, and SNATs, that fail over together as a unit.

  • Defines which resources move together during a failover.
  • Allows for granular control over failover behavior.
  • Essential for both Active-Standby and Active-Active modes.

Traffic Group Example (Active-Standby)

BIG-IP A (Active)Traffic Group 1
BIG-IP B (Standby)Traffic Group 1
Traffic Group 1 fails over as a single unit if BIG-IP A fails.
Key Takeaway

Traffic Groups define logical collections of network resources that fail over together, enabling flexible and efficient HA designs.
