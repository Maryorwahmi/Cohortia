Security Strategy

The Importance of Layered Security

Building a comprehensive defense from edge to application logic

Why Layered Security?

No Single Point of Failure

  • Comprehensive Protection: Addresses threats at multiple layers (network, transport, application).
  • Increased Resilience: If one layer is breached, others provide continued defense.
  • Diverse Threat Mitigation: Catches a wider array of attacks that single controls might miss.
  • Enhanced Availability & Integrity: Ensures applications remain accessible and data remains secure.
Key Takeaway

A layered security approach with F5 BIG-IP (LTM, AFM, ASM) is essential for comprehensive protection, ensuring no single control is a point of failure against diverse threats.

Analogy: The Fortress

Protecting Your Application

Imagine your application as the treasure within a fortress. LTM is the outer gate and guards. AFM is the strong walls and watchtowers, blocking sieges. ASM is the inner sanctum's security, inspecting everyone and everything that makes it past the outer defenses. Each layer adds to the overall strength.

Consequence of Omission

Single Point of Failure

Relying on only one security module or LTM's inherent features creates a single point of failure. A sophisticated attacker can bypass that single defense, leaving your application completely exposed to threats it wasn't designed to handle.
