Choosing the Right Tool: LTM Policy Scenarios

When to leverage the simplicity and performance of LTM Policies.

Key Characteristics

  • Straightforward, declarative tasks.
  • Clear conditions and actions.
  • No need for complex scripting or external interaction.
  • Focus on common traffic management patterns.
Common Mistake

Using an iRule for simple tasks like HTTP redirects, leading to unnecessary complexity and potential performance issues.

Typical LTM Policy Use Cases

HTTP to HTTPS Redirection

Simple URL Rewrites

Blocking Specific IPs

Pool Selection by Host/URI

Key Takeaway

For common, well-defined traffic tasks, LTM Policies offer a simpler, more performant, and less error-prone solution than iRules.
