Choosing the Right Tool: iRule Scenarios

When ultimate flexibility and complex logic are required.

Key Characteristics

  • Requires Tcl scripting for programmatic control.
  • Needs access to raw connection/application data.
  • Involves interaction with external systems or session tables.
  • Highly custom or dynamic behaviors.
Pro Tip

Always consider if an LTM Policy can achieve the desired outcome before resorting to an iRule to avoid unnecessary complexity.

Typical iRule Use Cases

Advanced String Manipulation

Complex Conditional Logic

Interaction with Session Tables

Custom Logging & External Systems

Key Takeaway

iRules are indispensable for highly custom, complex traffic management scenarios that require deep programmatic control and external integration.
