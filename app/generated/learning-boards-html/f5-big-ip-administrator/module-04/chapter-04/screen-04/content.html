Introducing Data Groups

Centralizing data for cleaner and more manageable iRules.

What are Data Groups?

Data groups are lookup tables that store lists of items like IP addresses, URLs, or strings, separate from iRule code. They act as dynamic dictionaries for iRules.

Why Use Them?

  • Cleaner, more readable iRules.
  • Easier and safer updates to lists.
  • Reduced risk of iRule syntax errors.
  • Improved operational agility.
