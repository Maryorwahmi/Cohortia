Creating and Applying LTM Policies

Implementing declarative rules for traffic management.

Policy Creation Steps

  1. Navigate to Local Traffic > Policies in the GUI.
  2. Define a new policy and add rules.
  3. Specify conditions (criteria) and actions (operations) for each rule.
  4. Apply the policy to a Virtual Server.
Safety Note

Always test policy changes thoroughly in a controlled environment to prevent unintended traffic disruptions.

Policy Evaluation Order

Incoming Traffic

LTM Policy Evaluation

First match executes actions.

iRule Evaluation (if not preempted)

Key Takeaway

LTM Policies are evaluated before iRules, a critical order of precedence to remember when designing and troubleshooting traffic flow.
