Phased Upgrade Strategy

A phased approach involves testing the upgrade in a lab or non-critical environment first, then gradually rolling it out to production systems. This minimizes risk by catching issues early.

Key Takeaway

Strategic upgrade methods like phased and rolling deployments are essential for maintaining high availability and minimizing service disruption in production environments.
