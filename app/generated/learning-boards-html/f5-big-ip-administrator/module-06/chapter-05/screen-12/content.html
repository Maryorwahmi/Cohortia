Upgrade Planning

Developing Your BIG-IP Upgrade Strategy: Phase One

Laying the groundwork for a successful upgrade with clear objectives, research, and thorough pre-checks.

01

Define Objectives

Clearly articulate the target version and the 'why' behind the upgrade (features, bug fixes, security). This guides the entire plan.

Why it Matters

Clear objectives ensure the upgrade aligns with business needs and provides measurable success criteria.

02

Research & Prerequisites

Consult F5 documentation for supported upgrade paths and prerequisites (disk space, memory). Identify any intermediate upgrades needed.

Common Mistake

Ignoring the F5 upgrade matrix can lead to configuration corruption or an unbootable system.

03

Perform Pre-Checks

Generate a qkview for iHealth analysis, create a UCS backup, and verify sufficient disk space using df -h.

Key Takeaway

Thorough pre-checks are non-negotiable for identifying potential issues and ensuring a smooth upgrade process.
