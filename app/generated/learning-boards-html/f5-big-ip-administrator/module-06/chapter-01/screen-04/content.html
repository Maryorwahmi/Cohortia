Initial Setup and Licensing

Activating your F5 BIG-IP system for first use.

Key Steps
  • Access GUI via temporary IP or serial console.
  • Assign management IP, hostname, and network settings.
  • Enter base registration key for licensing.
  • Activate license online or offline via F5 portal.
Common Mistake

Not verifying license status post-activation can lead to unexpected feature limitations. Always confirm all modules are provisioned.

Key Takeaway

Proper initial setup and licensing are critical first steps to ensure your F5 BIG-IP system is fully functional and all purchased modules are enabled.
