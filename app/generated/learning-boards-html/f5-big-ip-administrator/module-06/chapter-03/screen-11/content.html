Interpreting Performance Graphs: Spotting Anomalies

Translate visual data into actionable insights for troubleshooting.

What to Look For
  • Sudden Changes: Unexpected spikes or drops in metrics.
  • Sustained High Values: Metrics consistently above normal thresholds.
  • Unusual Patterns: Irregular fluctuations or flatlines where activity is expected.
  • Deviations from Baseline: Any metric behaving differently from its established normal range.
Concrete Example

Consistently high CPU usage (e.g., >80%) suggests a BIG-IP bottleneck, potentially requiring optimization or scaling. Frequent 'up/down' status for pool members indicates backend instability or an overly sensitive monitor.

Key Takeaway

Effective graph interpretation involves recognizing anomalies and understanding their potential causes, enabling proactive problem diagnosis and resolution.

Common Performance Graph Scenarios
Sustained High CPU

Indicates heavy load, potential bottleneck. Consider optimization or scaling.

Fluctuating Pool Members

Suggests backend instability or an overly aggressive health monitor.

Sudden Connection Rate Spike

Could indicate a DDoS attack or a legitimate surge in traffic.

Memory Leak Pattern

Gradual, continuous increase in memory usage over time, not returning to baseline.
