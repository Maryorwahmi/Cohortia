Dedicated Performance: Physical Appliances

High-throughput solutions for demanding on-premise environments.

  • •iSeries Platforms: Purpose-built hardware for maximum performance and throughput.
  • •Optimized for Data Centers: Designed for high-volume, low-latency application traffic.
  • •Dedicated Resources: Ensures consistent performance without resource contention.
Key Takeaway

Physical BIG-IP appliances offer unmatched performance and reliability for critical, high-demand applications, especially when deployed in traditional data center infrastructures.

iSeries Platforms

Dedicated hardware devices offering high performance and throughput for demanding environments.

VIPRION Chassis & Blades

Modular system allowing dynamic scalability and redundancy by adding or removing processing blades.

Common Misconception

Physical appliances are not obsolete; they are crucial for specific high-performance, low-latency, and large-scale deployments where virtualized environments might introduce overhead.
