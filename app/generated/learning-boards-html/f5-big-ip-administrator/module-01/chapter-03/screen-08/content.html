ARP Resolution Process

IP to MAC Mapping

1

BIG-IP Needs MAC: Wants to send packet to 10.1.1.10.

2

ARP Request: Broadcasts "Who has 10.1.1.10?"

3

Target Responds: 10.1.1.10 replies with its MAC address.

4

BIG-IP Forwards: Uses MAC to send packet directly.

Troubleshooting Tip

Incorrect switch handling of gratuitous ARP can lead to traffic blackholing after an HA failover.

Key ARP Concepts
  • •
    ARP Request: Broadcast to discover MAC for an IP.
  • •
    ARP Reply: Target device responds with its MAC.
  • •
    Gratuitous ARP: Sent by BIG-IP during HA failover to update switch MAC tables.
Example

During an HA failover, the newly active BIG-IP sends gratuitous ARP to ensure switches update their MAC tables and redirect traffic.

Key Takeaway

ARP is essential for local network communication, and gratuitous ARP is critical for seamless High Availability failovers in BIG-IP.
