Azure Compliance: Meeting Regulatory Requirements

Azure's Commitment to Industry-Specific and Global Standards

Azure offers a comprehensive set of compliance certifications and attestations, helping organizations meet stringent regulatory requirements.

Azure's Role

Underlying infrastructure and services meet stringent standards.

Your Role

Ensure your applications and data within Azure also comply.

Key Takeaway

Azure offers comprehensive compliance certifications (GDPR, HIPAA, ISO 27001) for its infrastructure, while users are responsible for compliance within their applications.

Key Compliance Standards

GDPR: General Data Protection Regulation.

HIPAA: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.

ISO 27001: Information Security Management.

PCI DSS: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard.
