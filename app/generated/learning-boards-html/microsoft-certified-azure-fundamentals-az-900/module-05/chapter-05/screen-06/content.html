Blueprint Advantage

Blueprints vs. ARM Templates

Beyond Deployment: Maintaining a Relationship with Deployed Resources

While both automate deployments, Azure Blueprints maintain a relationship with deployed resources, allowing for versioning and updates, unlike ARM templates.

ARM Templates

Declarative deployment, one-time execution.

Azure Blueprints

Deployment + ongoing relationship, versioning, updates.

Key Takeaway

Unlike ARM templates, Azure Blueprints maintain a relationship with deployed resources, allowing for versioning and updates to ensure continuous compliance.

Key Differences

Deployment Focus: ARM templates are for deployment, Blueprints for governance.

Relationship: Blueprints maintain a link, ARM templates do not.

Versioning: Blueprints support versioning and updates.
