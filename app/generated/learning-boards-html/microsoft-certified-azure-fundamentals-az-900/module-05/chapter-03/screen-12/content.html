The Principle of Least Privilege: A Core Security Tenet

Granting only the necessary permissions for job functions.

What is Least Privilege?

  • Users, programs, or processes are granted only the minimum permissions required.
  • Crucial for minimizing the attack surface in cloud environments.

Benefits of PoLP

  • Reduced Attack Surface: Fewer opportunities for exploitation.
  • Limited Blast Radius: Damage from a breach is contained.
  • Improved Auditability: Clearer understanding of actions.
Key Takeaway

Always apply the Principle of Least Privilege by granting only the essential permissions, enhancing overall security and reducing risk.
