Policy Design

Crafting Robust Conditional Access Policies

Best practices and common pitfalls in policy creation.

Best Practices for Policy Design

  • Start with clear, simple objectives for each policy.
  • Test policies thoroughly in a controlled environment before broad deployment.
  • Regularly review and update policies to ensure continued relevance and effectiveness.
Pro-Tip

Use the 'Report-only' mode to evaluate the impact of new policies before enforcing them.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Creating overly complex or conflicting policies that can block legitimate users.
  • Not testing policies thoroughly, leading to unexpected access issues.
  • Failing to consider the user experience, which can lead to workarounds or frustration.
Key Takeaway

Effective Conditional Access policies are simple, well-tested, and regularly reviewed to maintain a balance between security and usability.
