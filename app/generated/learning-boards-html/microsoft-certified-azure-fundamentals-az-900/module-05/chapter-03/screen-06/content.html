Strengthening Security with Multi-Factor Authentication

Adding layers of verification beyond just a password.

What is MFA?

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) requires users to provide two or more verification factors to gain access to a resource.

  • Combines 'something you know' (password) with 'something you have' (phone) or 'something you are' (biometrics).
  • Significantly reduces the risk of identity compromise.
Misconception

MFA is not just for high-privilege accounts; it should be enabled for all users for baseline security.

MFA Methods in Azure AD

Azure AD supports various methods to provide the second factor of authentication.

  • Microsoft Authenticator app notifications.
  • SMS codes to a registered phone number.
  • Automated phone calls for verification.
  • Hardware tokens for specific use cases.
Key Takeaway

MFA is a powerful, easily implementable security layer that significantly reduces the risk of identity compromise.
