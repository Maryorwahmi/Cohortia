Access Management

Understanding Azure AD Roles

Defining administrative permissions within your tenant.

Global Administrator

Has ultimate control over the entire Azure AD tenant. Can manage all administrative aspects.

Highest privilege role.

User Administrator

Manages users and groups, including creating new users, resetting passwords, and managing group memberships.

Focused on identity management.

Billing Administrator

Manages subscriptions, purchases, and support tickets. Does not manage users or resources directly.

Focus on financial and support aspects.
Key Takeaway

Azure AD roles define administrative permissions within the tenant; always assign the least privileged role necessary.
