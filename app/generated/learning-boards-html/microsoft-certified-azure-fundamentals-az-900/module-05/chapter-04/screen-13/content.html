Azure Policy: Best Practices for Implementation

Ensuring Consistency, Security, and Cost Management

Recommended Policy Implementation Flow
1

Define Policy: Create a clear policy definition that specifies the rule and its desired effect.

2

Assign Scope: Apply the policy to the appropriate management group, subscription, or resource group.

3

Test in Audit Mode: Initially assign policies with an 'Audit' effect to identify non-compliance without blocking operations.

4

Enforce Policy: Once validated, change the effect to 'Deny' or 'Modify' for full enforcement.

Governance Benefits

Ensures consistency, security, and cost management by preventing non-compliant resource deployments.

Common Mistake

Creating overly restrictive policies without proper testing can block legitimate deployments and hinder productivity.

Key Takeaway

Test Azure Policies thoroughly in audit mode before enforcing them to ensure they meet organizational goals without unintended side effects.
