Azure Policy: Enforcing Organizational Standards

Creating and Managing Rules for Resource Compliance

Purpose

Enforces rules and effects over Azure resources to ensure compliance with corporate standards and SLAs.

Policy Effects
  • Audit: Reports non-compliance.
  • Deny: Prevents non-compliant resource creation.
  • Modify: Changes resources to comply.
Key Takeaway

Azure Policy is fundamental for automated governance, ensuring consistency, security, and cost management across your cloud estate.
