IaaS, PaaS, and Serverless

Understanding the Spectrum of Cloud Control

IaaS (Virtual Machines)

Highest control over OS, middleware, and applications. You manage more, but gain maximum flexibility.

YOU MANAGE MORE

PaaS (App Service)

Azure manages OS and runtime; you focus on application code. Balanced control and reduced operational burden.

SHARED MANAGEMENT

Serverless (Functions)

Azure manages all servers; you only provide code. Least control, but lowest management overhead and pay-per-execution.

AZURE MANAGES MORE
Key Takeaway

The choice between IaaS, PaaS, and Serverless depends on your desired level of control, management responsibility, and application requirements.
