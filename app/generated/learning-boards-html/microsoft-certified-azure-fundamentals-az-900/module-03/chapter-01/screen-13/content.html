Avoiding Common Compute Mistakes

Optimizing Your Azure Compute Strategy

Mistake 1: App Service for Stateful Apps

Pitfall: Using Azure App Service for applications requiring persistent state or long-running background processes. Correction: App Service is best for stateless web-facing apps; consider AKS or VMs for stateful or complex background tasks.

Mistake 2: Functions for Long-Running Tasks

Pitfall: Deploying CPU-intensive or long-duration tasks to Azure Functions. Correction: Functions are for short, event-driven executions; VMs or App Service might be more cost-effective for heavy processing.

Best Practice 1: Match Control to Need

Guidance: Don't default to VMs if a PaaS or serverless option can meet your needs. Choose the service that offers the right balance of control and managed services.

Best Practice 2: Consider Cost & Scalability

Guidance: Evaluate the cost model and scaling capabilities of each service against your workload's predictability and budget. Serverless is great for intermittent, PaaS for predictable web traffic.

Key Takeaway

Optimizing Azure compute involves understanding service limitations and aligning choices with workload characteristics to avoid common pitfalls and maximize efficiency.
