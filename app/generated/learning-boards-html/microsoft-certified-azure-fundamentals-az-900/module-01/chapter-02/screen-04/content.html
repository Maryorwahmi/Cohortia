Core Concept

Cloud Service Models: An Overview

Different levels of management and flexibility in the cloud.

Pizza as a Service Analogy
On-Premises

Make pizza from scratch at home.

IaaS

Buy frozen pizza, cook at home.

PaaS

Order delivered pizza, eat at home.

SaaS

Dine out at a pizza restaurant.

Key Idea

Cloud service models define how much of the IT stack you manage versus what the cloud provider handles.

Analogy Explained

The 'pizza as a service' analogy helps visualize the shifting responsibilities from on-premises to SaaS.

Key Takeaway

Each model offers a different balance of control, flexibility, and operational overhead, impacting your cloud strategy.
