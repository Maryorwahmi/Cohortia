Key Benefit

Cost-Effectiveness

Shifting from Capital to Operational Expenses

Traditional IT requires significant Capital Expenditure (CapEx) for hardware and infrastructure.

Cloud computing shifts costs to Operational Expenditure (OpEx) – pay-as-you-go services.

Reduces upfront investment, avoids over-provisioning, and lowers maintenance burdens.

Financial Model Comparison

On-Premises

High CapEx, Depreciation

Cloud

Flexible OpEx, Pay-as-you-go

Key Takeaway

Cloud computing offers cost-effectiveness by shifting from large upfront CapEx to flexible OpEx, aligning costs with actual usage.
