Scalability and Elasticity

Handling growing and fluctuating workloads

Scalability: The ability to easily increase or decrease computing power, storage, or network capacity.

Elasticity: The ability to automatically and dynamically scale resources up or down to match demand in real-time.

Contrast with on-premises: avoids costly over-provisioning and slow manual scaling.
