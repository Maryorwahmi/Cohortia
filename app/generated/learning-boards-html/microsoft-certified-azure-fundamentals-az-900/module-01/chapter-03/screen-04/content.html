Core Concept

Understanding Cloud Deployment Models

Where your cloud resources physically reside and who manages them.

What They Are

Cloud deployment models dictate the physical location and management responsibility of cloud resources.

Why They Matter

They offer a unique balance of control, cost, and security, making them suitable for different organizational needs.

Key Differentiators

Ownership, management responsibilities, and how resources are shared among tenants.

Key Takeaway

Choosing the right deployment model is a strategic decision that impacts your entire cloud architecture and operational efficiency.

MODEL OVERVIEW

Public Cloud

Shared, internet-delivered services.

Private Cloud

Dedicated to a single organization.

Hybrid Cloud

Combines public and private resources.
