Deployment Model

Public Cloud: Scalability and Cost-Effectiveness

Services delivered over the internet by a third-party provider.

Provider Managed

Hardware, software, and infrastructure are owned and managed by the cloud provider (e.g., Microsoft Azure).

Shared Resources

Resources are shared among multiple tenants, but logically isolated for security and privacy.

Pay-as-you-go

Operates on an OpEx model, with no upfront capital expenditure required.

Key Takeaway

Public cloud offers unparalleled scalability, elasticity, and cost efficiency, making it ideal for dynamic workloads and rapid innovation.

ADVANTAGES

High Scalability & Elasticity

Easily scale resources up or down based on demand, handling traffic spikes efficiently.

Cost-Effective (OpEx)

No upfront capital expenditure; pay only for the resources you consume.

Minimal Maintenance

Cloud provider handles infrastructure maintenance, freeing up your IT staff.
