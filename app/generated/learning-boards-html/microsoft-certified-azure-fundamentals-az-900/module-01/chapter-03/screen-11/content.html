Decision Framework

Choosing the Right Cloud Deployment Model

Factors influencing your strategic cloud decisions.

Budget & Cost Structure

Evaluate OpEx (public) vs. CapEx (private) and overall cost-effectiveness.

Security & Compliance

Assess data sensitivity, regulatory mandates, and required levels of isolation.

Existing IT Investments

Consider leveraging current on-premises infrastructure with a hybrid approach.

Key Takeaway

A thoughtful assessment of budget, security, existing IT, and application needs is crucial for selecting the optimal cloud deployment model.

DECISION FACTORS

Budget & Financial Model

Consider operational vs. capital expenditures and long-term cost implications.

Security & Compliance Needs

Data sensitivity, industry regulations, and legal requirements for data residency.

Application Requirements

Scalability, performance, latency, and specific customization needs of your workloads.
