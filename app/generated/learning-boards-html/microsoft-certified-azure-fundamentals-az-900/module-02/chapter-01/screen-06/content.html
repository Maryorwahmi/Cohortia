Azure Region Pairs

Ensuring Business Continuity

Most Azure regions are paired with another region in the same geography, hundreds of miles apart.

Data is asynchronously replicated between paired regions, balancing RPO with performance.

Azure prioritizes recovery of one region in a pair during a major disaster.

Planned updates are rolled out to only one region of a pair at a time.

North Europe & West Europe

These regions form a pair, providing disaster recovery for applications deployed across them.

Key Takeaway

Region pairs are essential for protecting against region-wide failures.
