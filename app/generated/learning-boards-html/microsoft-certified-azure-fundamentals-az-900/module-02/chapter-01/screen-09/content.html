Region Pairs vs. Availability Zones

Understanding the Difference for Resilience

Region Pairs: For disaster recovery against entire region failures. Two regions, hundreds of miles apart, within the same geography. Asynchronous data replication.

Availability Zones: For high availability within a single region. Physically separate data centers with independent infrastructure. Protects against localized failures.

Combined Strategy: Use Availability Zones for intra-region HA and Region Pairs for inter-region DR.

East US & West US

These form a Region Pair for disaster recovery. Deploying across Availability Zones within East US provides high availability.

Key Takeaway

Choose the right resilience strategy based on the scope of failure you need to protect against.
