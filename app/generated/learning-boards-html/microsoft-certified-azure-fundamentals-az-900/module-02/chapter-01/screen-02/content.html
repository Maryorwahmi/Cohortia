Learning Objectives

What you will learn in this chapter

01

Differentiate Geographies, Regions, and Availability Zones

Understand the distinct roles and physical locations of these infrastructure components.

02

Explain Azure Region Pairs

Grasp the concept and benefits of region pairs for disaster recovery and business continuity.

03

Describe Availability Zone Benefits

Learn how availability zones enhance high availability within a single Azure region.

04

Identify Importance of Data Residency and Compliance

Recognize why choosing the right Azure locations is critical for meeting legal and regulatory obligations.
