Choosing the Right Location

Balancing Performance, Compliance, and Resilience

User Proximity: Deploy in regions closest to your users to minimize latency.

Compliance & Data Residency: Select Azure Geographies and Regions that meet legal and regulatory requirements.

Resilience: Use Availability Zones for intra-region HA and Region Pairs for inter-region DR.

Example Strategy: Primary deployment in Region A, DR in Region A's pair, with AZs used within Region A.

West US 2 & West US 3

Deploying critical services in West US 2 and its paired region, West US 3, ensures disaster recovery. Utilizing Availability Zones within West US 2 enhances immediate uptime.

Key Takeaway

A well-chosen location strategy is fundamental to building robust, compliant, and performant cloud applications.
