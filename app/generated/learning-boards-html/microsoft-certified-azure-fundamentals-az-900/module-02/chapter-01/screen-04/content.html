Core Concept

Azure Geographies

Defining Data Residency and Compliance Boundaries

Azure Geographies are discrete market areas designed to preserve data residency and compliance boundaries.

They are crucial for organizations needing to adhere to specific data sovereignty laws, ensuring data remains within a particular country or continent.

Examples include Europe, North America, and Asia Pacific.

Europe

Ensures data stays within the European Union, meeting GDPR requirements.

Key Takeaway

Geographies are the highest level of data governance, ensuring legal compliance.
