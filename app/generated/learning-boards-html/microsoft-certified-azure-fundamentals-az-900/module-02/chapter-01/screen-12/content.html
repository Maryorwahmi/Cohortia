Common Misconception

Misconceptions About Azure Locations

Clarifying Key Differences

Misconception 1: Regions provide HA within a region. Reality: Regions offer fault tolerance, but Availability Zones provide HA against data center failures within a region.

Misconception 2: Availability Zones protect against region-wide failures. Reality: AZs protect against localized failures within a region; Region Pairs protect against region-wide failures.

Misconception 3: Region pair replication is synchronous. Reality: Replication is asynchronous; failover is a planned DR process.

Understanding Scope

Correctly understanding the scope of protection offered by Availability Zones versus Region Pairs is vital for effective resilience planning.

Key Takeaway

Accurate knowledge of Azure's infrastructure components prevents costly design errors and ensures reliable applications.
