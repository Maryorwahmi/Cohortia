Data Residency and Compliance

Meeting Legal and Regulatory Obligations

Data residency refers to the physical location where data is stored, often dictated by laws and regulations.

Choosing the correct Azure Geography ensures data resides within mandated legal jurisdictions.

Compliance is critical to avoid fines, reputational damage, and loss of trust.

Azure's global infrastructure provides the framework for compliant-by-design solutions.

GDPR Compliance

By deploying within the 'Europe' geography, organizations ensure personal data of EU citizens remains within the EU, adhering to GDPR.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize data residency and compliance when selecting Azure locations.
