Authentication & Authorization with ARM

Securing Access Through Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Authentication

Verifying user or service identity.

Authorization (RBAC)

Defining what actions are permitted on resources.

Principle of Least Privilege

Granting only necessary permissions to minimize risk.

Granular Control

Specific permissions for specific actions on specific resources.

Key Takeaway

ARM's integrated authentication and RBAC are crucial for securing your Azure environment and enforcing access policies.
