Choosing the Right Container
Identifying the correct Azure component for specific organizational needs.

Scenario: Company needs separate billing, policy, and access control per department.

Question: What Azure component is most appropriate?

Options: A) Resource Groups, B) Subscriptions, C) Availability Zones, D) Regions.

Correct Answer: B) Azure Subscriptions.

Reasoning: Subscriptions provide the primary boundary for billing, access, and policy enforcement across organizational units.
