Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Managing who can do what with your Azure resources.

RBAC grants specific permissions to users, groups, or service principals.

Enforces the principle of least privilege: only necessary permissions are granted.

Roles can be assigned at various scopes: Management Group, Subscription, Resource Group, or Resource.

Common roles include Reader, Contributor, and Owner.

Leverages Azure Active Directory (AAD) for identity management.
