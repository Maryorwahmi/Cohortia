Azure Cognitive Services

Pre-built AI Capabilities

Integrating vision, speech, and language AI into your applications.

Vision AI

Computer Vision API

Analyzes images to identify objects, detect faces, read text, and determine content appropriateness. Useful for auto-tagging images or scanning documents.

Speech & Language AI

Speech & Language Services

Includes Speech-to-Text, Text-to-Speech, sentiment analysis, key phrase extraction, and language detection for voice and text processing.

Ease of Integration

Focus on Business Logic

Cognitive Services APIs are designed for straightforward integration, allowing developers to focus on their application's core logic rather than the complexities of building AI models from scratch.

Key Takeaway

Azure Cognitive Services provides accessible, pre-built AI models that simplify the integration of advanced capabilities like vision, speech, and language processing into applications.
