Navigating the Azure Portal

Customization, Monitoring, and Billing at Your Fingertips

Enhancing Productivity

  • Customizable Dashboards: Pin frequently used services and resources for quick access.
  • Global Search Bar: Rapidly locate any resource, service, or documentation across your subscription.
  • Resource Health Monitoring: Gain insights into the operational status and performance of your Azure services.
  • Billing Information Review: Transparently track and analyze your cloud consumption and costs.
Practical Example

Pin your most critical Virtual Machines and Storage Accounts to your dashboard for immediate oversight and management.
