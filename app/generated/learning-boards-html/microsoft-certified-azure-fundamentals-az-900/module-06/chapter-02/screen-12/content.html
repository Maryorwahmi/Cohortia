B2B Management

Managing Guest Access and Security Posture

Best practices for governing external identities and maintaining strong security.

Key Management Practices

  • •
    Assign Permissions: Grant access based on the principle of least privilege.
  • •
    Regular Reviews: Periodically audit guest user access for continued necessity.
  • •
    Timely Revocation: Remove access promptly when projects conclude or roles change.

Example: Project Collaboration

A consulting firm invites a client's project manager to a shared SharePoint site.

  • •
    Client uses own login: Project manager authenticates with their company's Azure AD.
  • •
    Access assigned in your tenant: Permissions are granted to the guest user's representation.
  • •
    Post-project action: Access must be revoked once the project is completed to prevent unauthorized lingering access.
Key Takeaway

Effective management of B2B guest access, including regular reviews and timely revocation, is crucial for maintaining a strong security posture and preventing potential vulnerabilities.
