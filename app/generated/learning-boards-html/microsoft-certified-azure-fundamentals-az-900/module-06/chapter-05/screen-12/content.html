Proactive Cost Management: Setting Budgets

Using Azure Cost Management to prevent unexpected expenses and maintain financial control.

Step 1

Log in & Navigate

Sign in to Azure Portal, search for "Cost Management + Billing".

Step 2

Create Budget

Click 'Budgets', then '+ Add'. Define scope, name, period, and amount.

Step 3

Configure Alerts

Set alert conditions (e.g., 80% of budget) and recipient email.

Verification

You will receive an email notification if your subscription's spend reaches 80% of your 'MonthlyDevBudget', helping you stay within limits.

Key Takeaway

Setting budgets in Azure Cost Management is a fundamental step for proactive financial control and avoiding unexpected cloud expenses.
