Cost Drivers

Understanding What Drives Your Azure Bill

Resource type, consumption, and region are the primary influences on Azure costs.

Resource Type

Different services (VMs, storage, databases) have unique pricing models.

Consumption

Amount of data processed, stored, or transferred (e.g., egress data).

Region

Pricing can vary significantly based on the chosen Azure geographical region.

Example Scenario

A D2v3 Virtual Machine in East US will have a different hourly rate than a D2v3 VM in West Europe, and its total cost will depend on how many hours it runs and its associated storage and network egress.

Key Takeaway

Proactively understanding these cost drivers is essential for accurate budgeting and preventing unexpected cloud expenses.
