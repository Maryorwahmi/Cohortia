Achieving a Unified Identity Experience

Before Unified Identity

Fragmented Access

  • Multiple usernames and passwords for different systems.
  • Increased risk of weak or reused passwords.
  • Complex administration and auditing.
  • User confusion and frustration.
Problem: Inconsistent security policies and higher operational costs.
After Unified Identity

Single Source of Truth

  • One set of credentials for all on-premises and cloud resources.
  • Enhanced security through consistent policy enforcement.
  • Simplified user experience and reduced help desk burden.
  • Streamlined compliance and auditing.
Solution: Improved security posture and operational efficiency.
Key Takeaway

A single source of truth for identity is critical for security, consistency, and efficiency in hybrid cloud environments.
