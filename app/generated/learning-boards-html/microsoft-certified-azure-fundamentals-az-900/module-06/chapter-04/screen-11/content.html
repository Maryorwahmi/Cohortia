Integrated Governance

Policy and RBAC in Action

A unified approach to Azure governance.

Azure Policy

Defines what resources can be deployed and how they are configured.

RBAC

Controls who can access what resources and what they can do.

Practical Scenario: Dev Team Access

  • Goal: Dev Team manages resources in DevEnvironment RG, but no deletion, and no ProdEnvironment access.
  • RBAC: Assign 'Contributor' role to 'Development Team' group, scoped to DevEnvironment RG.
  • Policy: Apply a 'Deny' policy at DevEnvironment RG to prevent deletion of 'critical' tagged resources.
  • Least Privilege: Ensure no RBAC roles for Dev Team in ProdEnvironment subscription.

Unified Governance Benefits

  • Enhanced Security: Prevents unauthorized actions and ensures secure configurations.
  • Compliance Assurance: Automatically enforces regulatory and organizational standards.
  • Operational Efficiency: Streamlines management of large-scale cloud environments.
Key Takeaway

By integrating Azure Policy and RBAC, you establish a comprehensive governance framework that enforces rules and controls access across your entire Azure estate.
