Grouping Policies with Initiatives

Streamlining compliance management for complex requirements.

Azure Policy Initiative

A collection of policy definitions grouped for a larger goal.

Policy A
Policy B
Policy C

Benefit: Efficiency

Assigning one initiative is more efficient than assigning multiple individual policies, especially for complex compliance needs.

Benefit: Organization

Keeps related governance controls logically grouped, improving clarity and management.

Example: Regulatory Compliance

An initiative called 'Regulatory Compliance' could include policies for data encryption, network isolation, and logging, all assigned together.

Common Mistake

Creating too many individual policies when an initiative would be more appropriate for grouping related controls.

Pro Tip: Built-in Initiatives

Always check the extensive library of built-in Azure Policy definitions and initiatives first, as they cover many common scenarios.

Key Takeaway

Initiatives streamline policy management by grouping related definitions, making it easier to enforce complex compliance requirements.
