The Principle of Least Privilege

Granting only the minimum necessary permissions for a task.

Definition

Users, groups, or services should only have the permissions absolutely required to perform their job functions.

Why it Matters

Minimizes the potential impact of a security breach by limiting what an attacker can do if an account is compromised.

Implementation

Assign the narrowest possible role at the smallest possible scope (e.g., Resource Group instead of Subscription).

Key Takeaway

Always apply the principle of least privilege to reduce security risks and maintain a robust access control posture.

Least Privilege vs. Over-Privilege
Least Privilege: Focused Access

Developer has 'Virtual Machine Contributor' role on 'Dev RG' only.

Over-Privilege: Broad Access

Developer has 'Owner' role on entire 'Subscription'.

Pro Tip: Regular Review

Periodically review RBAC assignments to ensure they are still appropriate and remove access when no longer needed.
