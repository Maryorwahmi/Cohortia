Core Concept

What is Azure Policy?

Enforcing organizational standards and compliance at scale.

Definition

Azure Policy is a service that helps you enforce organizational standards and assess compliance across your Azure resources.

Purpose

It defines rules that resources must adhere to, ranging from simple requirements like region restrictions to complex security configurations.

Key Benefit

Ensures consistency, security, and compliance by preventing non-compliant deployments or auditing existing ones.

Key Takeaway

Azure Policy acts as a guardrail, ensuring your cloud resources meet defined standards and regulatory requirements.
