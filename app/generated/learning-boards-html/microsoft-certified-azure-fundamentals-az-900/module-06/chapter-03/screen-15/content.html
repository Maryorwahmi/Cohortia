Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Reinforcing your understanding

Resource Group

A logical container for related Azure resources that share a common lifecycle, simplifying deployment, management, and deletion.

Hierarchy

The structured layering of Azure governance constructs (Tenant -> Management Groups -> Subscriptions -> Resource Groups).

Integrated Governance

The combined use of all governance constructs to organize, manage, and secure Azure resources effectively.

Key Takeaway

The structured approach from Tenant to Management Groups, Subscriptions, and Resource Groups provides a clear and powerful way to organize, manage, and govern your Azure resources effectively.
