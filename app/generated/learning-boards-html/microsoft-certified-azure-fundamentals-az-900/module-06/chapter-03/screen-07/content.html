Practical Application

Managing with Multiple Subscriptions

Segmenting workloads and isolating environments for better control

Single Subscription

  • Simpler for small-scale deployments.
  • All costs consolidated into one bill.
  • Less granular control over resource allocation and access.
  • Risk of 'noisy neighbor' if resources are not isolated.
Consideration

Can lead to management challenges as your cloud footprint grows.

Multiple Subscriptions

  • Enables segmentation by department, project, or environment (Dev/Test/Prod).
  • Provides granular cost allocation and budget management.
  • Enhances security through isolation of workloads.
  • Facilitates tailored access control and policy application.
Benefit

Ideal for large enterprises needing robust governance and clear accountability.

Key Takeaway

Using multiple subscriptions strategically allows organizations to segment workloads, manage costs, and isolate environments, leading to better control and security.
