Management Groups: Organizing Subscriptions at Scale

Centralizing governance and policy application above subscriptions

Management Group

A logical container that sits above subscriptions, allowing for hierarchical organization and centralized governance.

Subscription A (Dev)
Subscription B (Test)
Subscription C (Prod)
Centralized Policy

Apply governance policies (e.g., security, compliance) to multiple subscriptions at once.

Consistent Access

Manage Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across groups of subscriptions.

Simplified Management

Reduces administrative overhead by avoiding individual policy application.

Avoid Common Mistake

Not using Management Groups leads to a flat, difficult-to-govern subscription structure as it scales.

Key Takeaway

Management Groups enable centralized governance by organizing subscriptions hierarchically, allowing policies and access controls to be applied at scale.
