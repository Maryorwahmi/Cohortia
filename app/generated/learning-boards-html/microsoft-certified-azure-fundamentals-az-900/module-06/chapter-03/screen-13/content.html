Best Practices for Azure Governance

Ensuring efficient and compliant cloud operations from the outset

Start with Management Groups Early

Establish a logical hierarchy mirroring your organization from the beginning to centralize policy and access control.

Align Subscriptions with Business Units

Map subscriptions to departments or projects for clear billing, cost tracking, and accountability.

Group Related Resources in Resource Groups

Place resources that share a common lifecycle (deploy, update, delete together) into the same Resource Group.

Leverage Policy Inheritance

Use Management Groups to apply consistent Azure Policies and RBAC across multiple subscriptions, ensuring compliance.

Key Takeaway

Proactive and strategic use of Azure's governance hierarchy is essential for building a scalable, compliant, and efficiently managed cloud environment.
