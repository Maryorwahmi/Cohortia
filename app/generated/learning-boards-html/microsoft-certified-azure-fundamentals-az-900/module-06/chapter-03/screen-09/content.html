Policy Inheritance with Management Groups

Cascading governance across your Azure estate

Automatic Enforcement

Policies applied at a Management Group automatically extend to all child subscriptions and their resources.

Hierarchical Depth

The hierarchy can be up to six levels deep, allowing for very granular organizational structures.

Common Mistake

Failing to use Management Groups leads to a flat subscription structure that is difficult to govern and scale.

Example: Encryption Policy

Apply a policy to a Management Group to enforce encryption on all storage accounts within its scope.

Parent Management Group

Policy: Enforce Storage Encryption

POLICY APPLIED
Child Management Group

Inherits encryption policy

Subscription A

Inherits encryption policy

Key Takeaway

Management Groups enable consistent governance through policy inheritance, ensuring rules cascade down to all child subscriptions and resources, simplifying large-scale compliance.
