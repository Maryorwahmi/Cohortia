AWS CodeDeploy for Application Deployment
Automate application updates and traffic shifts across EC2, Lambda, and ECS.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson

Understand CodeDeploy's role, learn the appspec.yml structure and lifecycle hooks, compare in-place and blue/green strategies, and configure deployment groups and rollbacks for EC2, Lambda, and ECS.

Core focus
Consistent deployments with lifecycle scripts and traffic control.
Why it matters
Minimize downtime and enable fast rollback to a known-good version.
