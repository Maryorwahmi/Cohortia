Definition Deployment configs & traffic policies
Control update speed and validation windows
Deployment configurations (EC2)
  • AllAtOnce — fastest, higher risk.
  • OneAtATime — slow, low risk.
  • HalfAtATime — batch updates for balance.
Choose based on your capacity tolerance and rollback speed requirements.
Traffic shifting (Blue/Green)
AllAtOnce moves traffic immediately; Linear moves equal increments over time; Canary moves a small percentage first then finishes. Each gives windows for validation and alarms to trigger automatic rollback.
Practical tip
Align alarm evaluation periods with your shifting policy so failures are detected during the window where they matter.
Key Takeaway

Pace controls risk: slower, incremental shifts give more time to validate and rollback when needed.
