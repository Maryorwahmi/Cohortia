Comparison In-place vs Blue/Green
Balance downtime, cost, and rollback speed
In-place deployment
Stops the app on each instance, installs the new revision, restarts, and validates. Simple and resource-efficient but introduces brief downtime per instance.
When to use
Non-critical services or environments where brief reduced capacity is acceptable.
Blue/Green deployment
Provision a new environment with the updated version, validate it, and shift traffic from blue to green. Minimizes downtime and enables quick rollback by switching traffic back.
When to use
Production systems requiring high availability and an immediate rollback path.
Benefit
Blue/Green reduces customer impact during rollout and simplifies rollback.
Cost tradeoff
Blue/Green temporarily requires additional resources but buys safer deployments.
Misconception
Automation alone is not enough; combine strategy with validation hooks and monitoring for safety.
