Summary Key Concepts
What to remember
AWS CodeDeploy
Automates deployments to EC2, Lambda, and ECS to ensure repeatability and reduce manual errors.
appspec.yml
The deployment playbook: files to copy and lifecycle hooks to run at defined stages.
Deployment Strategies
In-place for simplicity; blue/green for zero-downtime and quick rollback. Match the strategy to availability needs.
Summary Takeaway

Combining a correct appspec.yml, lifecycle validation, appropriate strategy, and monitoring enables safe, automated deployments.
