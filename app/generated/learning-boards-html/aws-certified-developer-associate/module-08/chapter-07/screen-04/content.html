Core Concept
Why staged deployments lower risk
Staged rollouts reduce blast radius and let you validate assumptions against real traffic and data.
Definition
Expose a new version to a small set of users, monitor, then expand or revert.
Common misconception
Pre-prod testing replaces staged rollouts. It doesn’t—real traffic can reveal new issues.
Concrete example
Roll out a payment change to 3% of users and watch 5xx errors and checkout completion rates before expanding.
Key Takeaway

Staged rollouts make production changes observable and reversible, reducing risk and MTTR.

Analogy & quick checklist
Think of a canary as a single miner testing a new shaft. If the canary shows trouble, you stop before everyone enters. Use this checklist when planning a staged release:
  • Define target traffic percentage (1–5%).
  • Choose critical metrics (errors, latency, business KPIs).
  • Set monitoring windows and thresholds.
  • Prepare automated rollback or manual cutover steps.
Check your understanding
Which metric would you trust most for a canary on a front-end feature: latency, 5xx rate, or conversion? Answer depends on feature goals; define that before rollout.
