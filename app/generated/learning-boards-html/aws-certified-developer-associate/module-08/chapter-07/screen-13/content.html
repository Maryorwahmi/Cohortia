Best Practices
Secure, repeatable deployments
Parameterize templates
Use Parameters for environment-specific values to avoid duplicated templates and drift.
Require change-set reviews
Preview resource-level changes and enforce approvals for production execution.
Define multiple alarms
Combine error, latency, and resource metrics to avoid false positives and detect real issues.
Operational additions
Test rollback automation in staging, maintain runbooks for manual steps if automation fails, and capture logs and traces during early rollouts to speed diagnosis after a rollback.
Common mistake

Relying solely on automated rollback without runbooks or post-rollback diagnostics can delay recovery and obscure root cause analysis.
