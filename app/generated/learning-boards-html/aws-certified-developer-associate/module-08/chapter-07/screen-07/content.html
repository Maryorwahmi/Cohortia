Architecture
CloudWatch → CodeDeploy rollback flow
Design alarms for critical signals and attach them to the deployment group so CodeDeploy can act automatically when thresholds are breached.
Example alarm
Metric: 5xxErrorRate
Threshold: > 1% for 5 minutes
Action: Trigger CodeDeploy rollback
Key Takeaway

Automate rollback with multiple alarms for complementary signals to reduce false positives and speed recovery.

Operational checklist
  • Pick 2–3 critical metrics, not just one.
  • Set thresholds and windows that reflect typical noise.
  • Test rollback in staging to confirm behavior and notifications.
  • Document rollback conditions and post-rollback diagnostics.
Misconception
Automation solves all problems. It helps reduce MTTR, but you must tune alarms and verify the rollback path to avoid unnecessary flips.
