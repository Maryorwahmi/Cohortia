Module 08 › Chapter 8.7 › Key Concepts
Canary Deployment
Roll out a new version to a small percentage, monitor metrics, and expand or rollback based on health.
A/B Testing
Split traffic to compare two variants and measure which performs better against a business metric.
Automated Rollback
CloudWatch alarms can trigger CodeDeploy to revert a deployment when thresholds are breached.
Summary
Combine staged rollouts, monitoring, automated rollback, and IaC with change sets to make deployments safer and auditable.
