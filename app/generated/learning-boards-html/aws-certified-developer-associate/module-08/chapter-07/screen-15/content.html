Module 08 › Chapter 8.7 › What to Remember
Start small
A canary or weighted split limits exposure and reveals issues early.
Automate safety
Link alarms to rollback to reduce MTTR when deployments fail.
Treat infra as code
Use CloudFormation, parameters, and change sets to apply repeatable, reviewable updates.
Final question
Which metric will you place at the center of your next canary, and what rollback window will you use?
