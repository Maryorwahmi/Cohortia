Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Key Concepts
Troubleshooting
A systematic process: locate the failing stage, collect relevant logs, test hypothesized fixes, and iterate until resolved.
CloudWatch Logs
Centralized logs for CodeBuild and Lambda; use filters and metrics to detect and alert on failures.
CodeDeploy Agent Logs
On EC2 targets, examine /var/log/aws/codedeploy-agent/codedeploy-agent.log for lifecycle hook output and script errors.
Summary takeaway
Treat pipeline failures as a predictable sequence: identify stage, gather service logs, confirm permissions, reproduce locally, then iterate and alert.
