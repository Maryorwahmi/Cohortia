Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Approvals & SNS
Approval pattern
Insert a Manual Approval action where human checks are required, typically before production deploys. When the pipeline pauses, an SNS notification can be sent to approvers to prompt review.
Use case
A release affecting billing or compliance requires business sign-off before deployment.
Notification
CodePipeline integrates with SNS to alert teams when approvals are pending, including links to execution details.
Contrast & best practice
Contrast: rely on automated tests to catch regressions early; reserve approvals for policy or risk decisions. Best practice: keep approvals focused, short, and documented so they don't impede fast feedback loops.
Pro tip
Combine automated gates with a single approval step before production. Use SNS filters to notify only relevant approvers.
Key Takeaway

Approvals add human judgment where automation cannot cover business risk, and SNS delivers timely alerts to reviewers.
