Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Artifacts & S3
Artifact lifecycle
  1. Source action creates a source artifact and uploads it to the pipeline's S3 artifact store.
  2. Build action downloads the source artifact, produces a build artifact, and uploads it to S3.
  3. Deploy action downloads the build artifact and applies it to the target environment.
Why artifacts matter
They are a stable record of what was deployed and allow replays, rollbacks, and auditing.
Common pitfall
Incorrect artifact structure or mismatched artifact names cause downstream actions to fail even when the build succeeds.
Concrete checks
Check artifact contents
Open the S3 artifact and confirm expected files such as appspec.yml or index.html are at the correct paths.
Match names
Ensure the name of the artifact emitted by the build action matches the input artifact name configured for deploy.
Key Takeaway

Artifacts are the contracts your actions rely on. Validate their structure and naming before assuming the pipeline will succeed.
