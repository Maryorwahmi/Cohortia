Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Final Reminders
Artifacts
Validate artifact contents and layout in S3 before assuming downstream success.
Permissions
Audit the pipeline service role for provider-specific actions and S3 rights.
Gating
Put manual approvals where business risk is highest and keep automated tests earlier in the pipeline.
Final Reflection

Before building your next pipeline, list the artifacts and permissions required by each stage. That checklist prevents simple configuration errors from breaking your automation.
