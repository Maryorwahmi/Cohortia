Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Service Role Permissions
Why permissions matter
The pipeline service role lets CodePipeline call other AWS services on your behalf. If permissions are missing, the corresponding action cannot execute and the pipeline stalls or fails.
Example permissions
CodeCommit: codecommit:GetCommit, codecommit:UploadArchive. CodeBuild: codebuild:StartBuild. CodeDeploy: codedeploy:CreateDeployment. S3: PutObject for artifacts.
Troubleshooting tip
When an action fails with access errors, inspect the pipeline's execution history and the IAM policy attached to the service role for missing actions.
Common mistakes
  • Using a role without S3 PutObject permissions so artifacts cannot be stored.
  • Granting generic Deny policies elsewhere that block provider actions.
  • Forgetting service-specific permissions like codebuild:BatchGetBuilds needed to inspect build status.
Key Takeaway
Audit the pipeline service role early. Missing permissions are fast to fix once identified in execution logs.
