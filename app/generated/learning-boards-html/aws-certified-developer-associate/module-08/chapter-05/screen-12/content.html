Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Key Concepts
Pipeline Stages
Logical divisions such as Source, Build, Test, and Deploy that sequence actions in a pipeline.
Artifacts
Files stored in S3 that are produced and consumed by pipeline actions, ensuring traceability.
Approvals & Notifications
Manual approval actions pause execution; SNS delivers alerts to reviewers so they can approve or reject a change.
Summary

Design your pipeline with clear artifact contracts, appropriate permissions, and a balance of automated checks and human approvals to keep releases safe and fast.
