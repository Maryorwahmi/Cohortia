Interpretation cards
Fallbacks
If Rpl_semi_sync_source_no_times_no_slaves increases, the source is falling back to async. Consider increasing rpl_semi_sync_source_timeout or investigating replica responsiveness.
Connected clients
Rpl_semi_sync_source_clients shows how many replicas acknowledged. Expect at least one for the semi‑sync guarantee to hold.
