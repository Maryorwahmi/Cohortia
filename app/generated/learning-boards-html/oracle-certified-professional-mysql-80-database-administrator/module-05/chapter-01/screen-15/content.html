Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Cheat Sheet
Enable binlog
Confirm log_bin is ON and set binlog_format intentionally for your workload.
Monitor threads
Track dump, I/O, and SQL thread health and replication lag to identify bottlenecks.
Keep backups
Use replication for availability and scaling; keep regular backups for recovery from corruption or accidental deletes.
Final takeaway
Replication is a powerful operational tool when configured correctly; combine it with tests, monitoring, and backups for safe production systems.
