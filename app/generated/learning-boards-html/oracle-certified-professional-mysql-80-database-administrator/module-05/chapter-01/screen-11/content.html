Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Pitfalls & Safety
Checklist of mistakes
  • Binary logging not enabled on the source.
  • server_id left at 0 or duplicated across servers.
  • Replica initialized from inconsistent backup snapshot.
  • Network/firewall blocking replication connections.
Safety practices
Configure replicas as read-only, maintain regular backups, keep binlog retention long enough for slow replicas, and document promotion steps for failover to avoid split-brain.
Practical note
Practice initializing a replica from a snapshot and replaying events in a test environment to make the real procedure routine and safe.
