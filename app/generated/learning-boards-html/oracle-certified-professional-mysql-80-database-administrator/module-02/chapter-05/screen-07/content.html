Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › LRU & Scan Resistance
How midpoint insertion works
New pages are placed near the middle of the LRU to avoid immediate eviction of hot pages during scans.
Bullets to remember
  • Midpoint insertion protects hot pages from being displaced by one-off reads.
  • innodb_old_blocks_time controls how long pages stay in the old list before promotion.
  • Raising old_blocks_time increases scan resistance but may slow promotion of legitimately hot pages.
Example
A long reporting query that scans a table will not immediately evict hot OLTP pages if midpoint insertion and an appropriate old_blocks_time are in place.
Tuning notes
Default innodb_old_blocks_time is 1000 ms. Increasing it to several seconds can help when scans flood the pool, but test effects on promotion latency.
Common mistake
Setting old_blocks_time extremely high and blocking legitimate pages from becoming hot quickly enough for OLTP peaks.
Key takeaway

Midpoint insertion plus an appropriate old_blocks_time helps preserve hot pages during heavy scans; tune both by measuring hit ratio before and after.
