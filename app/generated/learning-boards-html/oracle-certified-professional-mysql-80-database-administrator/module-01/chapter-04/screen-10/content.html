Core Concept
Principle of Least Privilege
Grant minimal permissions and scope them by database, table, or host. Avoid global rights for application accounts and restrict admin users by host where possible.
Example: Scoped app privileges
GRANT SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE ON my_app_db.* TO 'web_app_user'@'localhost';
Checks and reviews
  • Run SHOW GRANTS to audit permissions.
  • Schedule periodic privilege reviews to detect privilege creep.
  • Use separate accounts for automated processes and rotate secrets.
