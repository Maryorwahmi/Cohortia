OS choices
Linux distributions like CentOS/RHEL and Ubuntu/Debian are preferred in production due to stability, tuning options, and ecosystem tools. Windows is viable for dev or specific shops but less common at scale.
ext4: mature, stable, a solid default.
XFS: scales well on large volumes and concurrent I/O.
ZFS/Btrfs: advanced features like snapshots, but operational complexity.
Operational notes
Document expected snapshot and backup procedures, kernel tuning, and I/O scheduler settings. Ensure the team understands recovery steps for the chosen filesystem.
Concrete example: Choose XFS for larger database volumes to gain scalability, but include snapshot and restore playbooks for recovery.
Common Misconception

Assuming a feature-rich file system automatically improves performance. In reality, operational readiness and tuning determine outcomes.
