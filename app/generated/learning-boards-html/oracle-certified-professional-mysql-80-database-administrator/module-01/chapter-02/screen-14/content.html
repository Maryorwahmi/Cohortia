Hardware Provisioning
Allocating CPU, RAM and storage to meet requirements and growth.
RAID
Combines disks for redundancy and performance; RAID 10 is preferred for databases.
File System
ext4, XFS, ZFS/Btrfs influence I/O and operational practices such as snapshots.
Takeaway
Use these short definitions as anchors when you make choices and write your installation checklist.
