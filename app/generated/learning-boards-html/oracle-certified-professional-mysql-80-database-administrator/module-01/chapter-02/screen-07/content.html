Storage options
SSDs outperform spinning disks for random reads/writes. NVMe provides even lower latency and higher throughput than SATA SSDs.
SSDs: strong random I/O, lower latency than HDDs.
NVMe: higher throughput for demanding workloads.
HDDs: cost-effective for large cold storage, not ideal for high I/O databases.
RAID choices
RAID 10 usually offers the best mix of performance and redundancy for databases. RAID 5 can be risky for write-heavy systems due to rebuild time and write penalty.
Capacity planning: include space for binary logs, slow query logs, temporary files and growth buffer.
Concrete example: an analytics workload on 1 TB data benefits from NVMe RAID 10 to sustain high query throughput and reduce recovery windows.
Common Mistake

Assuming RAID replaces backups. RAID protects disk failure, not accidental deletes or logical corruption.
