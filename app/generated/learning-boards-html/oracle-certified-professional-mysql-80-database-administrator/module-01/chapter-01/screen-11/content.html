Notes
Transactional consistency
Mixing transactional and non-transactional engines affects atomicity. Keep critical data in transactional engines.
Operational overhead
Different engines may require different backup and recovery approaches.
Key Takeaway

Select engines by workload needs and recovery requirements; prefer InnoDB for critical, transactional data.
