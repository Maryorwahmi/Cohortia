Common Mistakes
Pitfalls to avoid
Enable too much
Turning on all instruments and consumers at once creates overhead and large state.
TIMED omitted
Without TIMED='YES', you lose duration metrics that are essential for performance analysis.
Same-session testing
Generate events from a separate session to see them in history tables.
Quick Checklist

Map symptom → enable specific instruments and consumers → reproduce in another session → query history tables → disable when done.
