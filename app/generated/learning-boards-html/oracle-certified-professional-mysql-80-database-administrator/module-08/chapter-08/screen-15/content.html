Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Operational Concepts
Baseline Performance
A measured set of normal metrics that lets you detect deviations and investigate quickly.
Incident Response
Contain, diagnose, resolve, and restore service while preserving evidence for the post-mortem.
Continuous Improvement
Use post-mortem insights to refine monitoring, capacity plans, and runbooks, reducing repeat incidents.
Summary

Operational rigor—clear baselines, structured response, and documented learning—keeps MySQL services resilient and predictable.
