Module 08 › Chapter 8.3 › Final reminders
Use sys views for quick observable metrics and short queries that surface the most actionable items.
Focus on processlist, statements_with_runtimes_in_host, io_global_by_file_by_latency, and memory_global_by_current_bytes for most investigations.
Validate Performance Schema instruments and use raw tables when sys aggregation hides necessary detail.
Final takeaway
Use sys for speed and Performance Schema for depth. That pairing lets you diagnose efficiently without losing the ability to investigate deeply when needed.
