What to Remember
Database Monitoring
Continuous observation to detect trends and prevent outages.
Status Variables
Dynamic counters that reflect server and session behavior.
SHOW ENGINE INNODB STATUS
A textual snapshot of InnoDB internals for detailed diagnostics.
Takeaway

Use server-native outputs as your first and most reliable source, and escalate to advanced schemas only when counters indicate deeper problems.
