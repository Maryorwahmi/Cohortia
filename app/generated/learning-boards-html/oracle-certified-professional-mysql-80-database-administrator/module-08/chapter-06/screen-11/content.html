Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Security Audits and Privilege Reviews
Audit steps
  • • List user accounts and inspect authentication methods.
  • • Use SHOW GRANTS FOR 'user'@'host'; to view privileges.
  • • Remove or disable unused accounts and revoke unnecessary rights.
  • • Verify strong passwords and current server patches.
SELECT user, host, authentication_string FROM mysql.user;
SHOW GRANTS FOR 'app_user'@'10.0.1.5';
Best practices
• Apply least privilege and role-based grants where possible.
• Schedule audits on a regular cadence and after team changes.
• Log privilege changes and test critical applications after revocation.
Common mistake
Leaving broad grants like ALL PRIVILEGES for convenience; this increases risk when accounts are compromised.
