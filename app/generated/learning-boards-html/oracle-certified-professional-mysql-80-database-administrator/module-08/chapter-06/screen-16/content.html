Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › What to Remember
Verify backups & slaves
Always confirm recovery and replication prerequisites before destructive maintenance.
Prefer analyze for stats
ANALYZE TABLE is fast and less disruptive to correct optimizer statistics after data changes.
Automate with checks
Use cron or Event Scheduler but add verification and alerts to catch failures early.
Summary

These three habits—verify, prefer low-impact fixes, automate with checks—are the foundation of safe and effective MySQL maintenance.
