Comparison Common mistakes vs safety
Common mistakes
  • Forgetting to take full backups.
  • Not testing upgrades in staging first.
  • Ignoring warnings from pre-checks or upgrade tools.
  • Trying unsupported direct upgrades from very old versions.
  • Using incompatible my.cnf settings on startup.
Safety measures
  • Use both logical and physical backups.
  • Rehearse the upgrade on a mirrored staging environment.
  • Run mysqlcheck and mysql_upgrade and act on warnings.
  • Upgrade replication slaves first; document rollback steps.
Key Takeaway: Safety practices reduce surprises and make upgrades reversible when possible.
