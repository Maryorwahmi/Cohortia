Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Deciding: skip events or full resync?
Quick decision flow
  1. Capture SHOW SLAVE STATUS and error text.
  2. Assess impact: single statement vs many dependent changes.
  3. If low impact and isolated, consider skipping with documentation.
  4. If high impact or uncertain, perform full resync from master backup.
Stakeholder step
Notify application owners when a skip or resync could change observed data to avoid surprise behavior in production.
Architecture view
Skipping keeps the slave running quickly but erodes a single source of truth. Resync reestablishes authoritative state but requires time and a maintenance window.
Key Takeaway

Balance speed versus correctness. When in doubt, prefer a full resync to protect data integrity.
