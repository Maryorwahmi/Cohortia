Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Key Concepts
Replication Failure
Slave stops applying master events; leads to data inconsistency unless resolved.
SHOW SLAVE STATUS\G
Primary diagnostic command. Capture thread states, errors, and positions before acting.
Safe Recovery
Prefer authoritative resync for critical data. Use skip only for isolated, acceptable events.
Summary Takeaway

Capture errors, choose the least risky fix, document actions, and always verify Slave_SQL_Running becomes Yes after recovery.
