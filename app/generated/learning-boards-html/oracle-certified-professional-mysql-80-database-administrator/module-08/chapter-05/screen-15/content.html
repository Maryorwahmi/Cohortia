Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › What to Remember
Stop & Record
Always stop the slave and record status fields before changes.
Skip with caution
Use sql_slave_skip_counter only when you can accept the resulting divergence.
Resync for integrity
If correctness matters, resync from a master backup and reconfigure with CHANGE MASTER TO.
Final thought

Practical DBAs focus on safe, auditable steps: diagnose, record, decide, act, and verify.
