Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › CHANGE MASTER TO
When to use
  • After restoring the slave from a backup to a known binlog position.
  • When switching replication to a new master host or promoted node.
Procedure
STOP SLAVE;
CHANGE MASTER TO
  MASTER_HOST='new_master_ip',
  MASTER_USER='repl_user',
  MASTER_PASSWORD='password',
  MASTER_PORT=3306,
  MASTER_LOG_FILE='mysql-bin.000123',
  MASTER_LOG_POS=12345;
START SLAVE;
SHOW SLAVE STATUS\G
Practical advice
Ensure the MASTER_LOG_FILE and MASTER_LOG_POS correspond exactly to the state of the restored dataset. Otherwise the SQL thread may immediately fail with errors stemming from mismatch.
Key Takeaway

CHANGE MASTER TO repositions the slave. Use it after a controlled restore or during a planned master change, and verify the slave applies events correctly.
