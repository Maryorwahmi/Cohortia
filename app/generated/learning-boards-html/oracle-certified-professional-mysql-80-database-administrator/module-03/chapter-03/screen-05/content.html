Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › validate_password plugin and policy levels
Policy level comparison
LOW
Minimum length checks only. Useful for non-production or development but risky in public environments.
MEDIUM
Enforces numbers, mixed case, and special characters. Good baseline for many teams.
STRONG
Includes dictionary checks to stop common words. Recommended for production systems.
Key Takeaway

Choose STRONG for production and tune length and counts to match your threat model.

Tuneable variables
validate_password.policy  -- 0=LOW,1=MEDIUM,2=STRONG
validate_password.length
validate_password.number_count
validate_password.special_char_count
validate_password.mixed_case_count
Example
Set policy to STRONG and minimum length to 12 or more for higher assurance.
