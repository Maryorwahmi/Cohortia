Key Concepts — TDE and Key Management
Master key
Top-level key that encrypts tablespace keys and must be backed up separately.
Keyring component
Plugin that stores master keys locally or integrates with external KMS.
Operational discipline
Backups, rotations, and tested restores are required for effective TDE.
Final takeaway

TDE adds strong protection for storage, but you must own the key management lifecycle to avoid data loss.
