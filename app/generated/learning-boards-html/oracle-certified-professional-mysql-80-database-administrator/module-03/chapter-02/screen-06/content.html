Concept

Role

A named container of privileges that can be granted to one or many users. Roles are designed to simplify privilege distribution and auditing.

Consistency
Update the role once to change privileges for all members.
Auditability
Inspect role privileges to know what members can do.
Design guidance
Create role names that reflect job function. Prefer composing small roles over monolithic roles to reduce accidental over-privileging.
Example roles
  • read_only_role — SELECT on reporting tables
  • app_writer_role — INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE on application tables
  • dba_role — administrative duties, tightly controlled
Common misconception

Roles replace careful privilege design. They do not — roles should still follow least privilege.
