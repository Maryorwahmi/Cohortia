Revocation steps
  1. Revoke the role from affected users.
  2. Check for direct grants that might still provide privileges.
  3. If no user needs the role, DROP ROLE to remove it entirely.
  4. Verify effective privileges after changes and require users to reconnect if needed.
Caution
Session activation and caching may delay effective changes; advise users to reconnect or deactivate/activate roles to refresh privileges.
Common scenarios
Orphaned privileges
Privileges granted directly to users remain after role revocation unless removed.
Dropped role behavior
Dropping a role revokes it from all users; document changes for audits.
Key action

Always verify effective privileges after revocation and coordinate with impacted users to avoid surprises.
