Key Concepts

WITH GRANT OPTION
Allows delegation of a specific privilege. Use rarely and audit holders regularly.
Privilege Levels
Global, database, table, column, and administrative privileges have different impacts. Choose the narrowest scope.
Roles
Named collections of privileges: grant to role, then grant role to users for scalable, consistent access control.
Summary
Use roles and narrow grants, avoid broad delegation and administrative privileges for routine accounts, and audit regularly.
