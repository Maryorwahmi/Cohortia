Best practices
  • Principle of least privilege for every account.
  • Use roles for common job functions and apps.
  • Keep roles small and composable rather than monolithic.
  • Avoid WITH GRANT OPTION unless absolutely necessary.
  • Audit privileges and role assignments regularly.
Common mistakes
Overuse of administrative privileges
Granting SUPER or FILE to non-admins increases risk dramatically.
Grant option misuse
Allowing application users to grant privileges leads to uncontrolled propagation.
Duplicate grants
Granting the same privileges directly and via a role complicates revocation and audits.
Pro tip

Treat roles as code: version their definitions, document changes, and include them in change control so privileges evolve predictably.
