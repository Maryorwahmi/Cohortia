Automated Parsing
From logs to alerts
Parse logs to detect repeated failures, privilege or schema changes, and high-volume data-modifying activity. Send those findings to an alerting system for rapid response.
High-value alerts
Repeated failed logins from one IP, successful logins at odd hours, GRANT/REVOKE statements, and bursts of DELETE/UPDATE operations.
Avoid alert fatigue
Start small, tune rules, and correlate events across logs to reduce false positives.
Tools & integration
Send logs to a centralized platform that supports searching and correlation. This makes incident triage faster and preserves logs off-host for integrity.
Key takeaway
Monitoring converts raw logs into actionable alerts; tune and correlate to maintain signal quality and reliable incident detection.
