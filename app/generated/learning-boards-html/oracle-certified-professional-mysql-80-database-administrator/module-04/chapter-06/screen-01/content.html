Disaster Recovery Planning and Best Practices

Design a DR plan that maps your recovery objectives to backup frequency, retention, monitoring, offsite storage, encryption, testing, and automation.

Orientation

This chapter teaches how to turn backups and logs into a repeatable recovery process. We emphasize measurable targets, documented steps, and tests that prove recovery works.

In this lesson

RTO/RPO mapping, retention, monitoring, offsite encryption, testing, and automation.

Why it matters

Without a documented, tested plan, backups alone won’t restore service in a crisis. Tests reveal gaps early.
