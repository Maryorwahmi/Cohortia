1. Align to RTO/RPO

Pick backup types and frequency that actually meet your recovery objectives when tested.

Encrypt and transfer backups offsite and manage keys separately from backup storage.

3. Test restores

Schedule restore drills, measure RTO, and update the DR plan based on findings.
