Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Binary Logs & PITR
Why it matters

Binary logs let you replay changes after restoring a full backup, reducing data loss to the time between log events.

Sizing note

Estimate gigabytes per hour of binary logs and ensure retention and archive capacity match your RPO window.

Operational checklist
  • Enable binary logging.
  • Archive logs offsite regularly.
  • Set expire_logs_days or implement safe purge scripts.
  • Test applying logs to a restored backup during drills.
