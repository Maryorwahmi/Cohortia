Learning Objectives

These objectives map practiced tasks to verified outcomes you can measure and repeat.

01
Design a DR plan

Document roles, steps, prerequisites and recovery procedures tied to RTO and RPO requirements. This makes recovery repeatable and auditable.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Establish retention policies

Choose GFS, compliance-driven retention, and binary log windows. Balance storage cost against regulatory and restore needs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Monitor backups and health

Alert on job failures, file corruption, log growth, and disk space so issues are visible before a disaster.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Secure offsite storage & testing

Encrypt backups in transit and at rest, store them offsite, and run regular restore drills to verify RTO/RPO compliance.
