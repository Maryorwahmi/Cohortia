Retention & Purging

Plan expire_logs_days and PURGE BINARY LOGS to match your recovery window and available disk space. Test purge commands safely in a staging environment before applying in production.

Auto purge
expire_logs_days removes old files automatically; choose a value that matches your RPO.
Manual purge
PURGE BINARY LOGS TO or BEFORE gives precise control for situations where you must free space immediately.

Troubleshooting & Verification

Confirm log_bin and server_id, check SHOW BINARY LOGS to list files, and capture MASTER_LOG_FILE and MASTER_LOG_POS during backups for PITR.

Common checks
SHOW VARIABLES LIKE 'log_bin'; SHOW BINARY LOGS; and examine backup metadata for starting position.
Verification step
After PITR, verify expected rows and timestamps to ensure you stopped before the incident.
