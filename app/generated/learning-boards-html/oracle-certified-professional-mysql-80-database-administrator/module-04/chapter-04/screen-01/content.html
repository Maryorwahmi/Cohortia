Why this matters

A full backup is a snapshot. Binlogs are the timeline of changes after that snapshot. Together they let you restore to any precise moment and keep replicas synchronized.

Quick check

Verify log_bin and server_id before you rely on PITR.

Key Takeaway

Treat binary logs as part of your backup footprint: configure, retain, and archive them so PITR stays possible when you need it.
