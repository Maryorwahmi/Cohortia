Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Common Mistakes

Frequently seen issues

  • Binary logging not enabled — PITR impossible.
  • No or non-unique server_id in replication topologies.
  • Choosing STATEMENT when queries are non-deterministic.
  • expire_logs_days set too low and required logs are purged.
  • Failing to archive binlogs offsite before purge.
  • Applying the wrong start/stop range when replaying events.
Danger

Mistakes in configuration or range selection can make recovery incomplete or worse. Always test and verify before relying on the process.

Quick fixes
Enable log_bin and set a unique server_id, prefer ROW format, capture MASTER_LOG_FILE and MASTER_LOG_POS at backup time, and implement offsite archiving of binlogs if your RPO requires long retention.
