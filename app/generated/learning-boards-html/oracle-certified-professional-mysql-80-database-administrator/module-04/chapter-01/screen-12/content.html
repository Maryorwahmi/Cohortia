Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Designing strategies

E-commerce scenario

Decide backup cadence and mechanisms for peak, normal, and historical data classes.

Peak sales
RTO: 30 minutes, RPO: 5 minutes. Use replication, continuous binary log archiving, and frequent physical backups.
Normal operations
RTO: 2 hours, RPO: 30 minutes. Use daily physical backups plus hourly incrementals or log shipping.
Historical data
RTO: 24 hours, RPO: 4 hours. Use weekly fulls with differential or infrequent incrementals and longer retention.
Key Takeaway

Design different backup cadences for each data class and verify them with timed restore drills to ensure targets are met.

Checklist for each plan
  • List backups required for restore and their order.
  • Include binary logs and retention policies.
  • Estimate prepare and restore durations for RTO.
  • Run periodic timed restore tests and adjust cadence as needed.
