Physical Backups with Percona XtraBackup
Understand hot backups, prepare and restore workflows that minimize downtime for large MySQL deployments.
This lesson focuses on why physical backups are faster for very large datasets, how a hot backup captures the LSN and redo logs for consistency, and the practical steps to back up and restore using XtraBackup. You will also learn everyday safety checks and common pitfalls to avoid when managing physical backup files.
In this lesson
  • Why physical backups beat logical exports at scale.
  • How InnoDB hot backups capture data plus redo logs.
  • Step-by-step XtraBackup commands for backup, prepare, and restore.
Explore Key Topics
Hot Backup
Copy files while server runs; captures LSN and redo.
Prepare Step
Apply redo logs to make files consistent before restore.
Restore
Copy prepared files back, fix permissions, and start MySQL.

LSN & Redo
LSN snapshot and redo logs are recorded for later prepare.
Prepare
xtrabackup --prepare applies redo, rolling forward committed changes.
Restore
xtrabackup --copy-back then fix ownership and start MySQL.
Key Takeaway

For very large MySQL deployments, hot physical backups with prepare provide fast backups and fast recovery, but they require careful preparation and validation.
