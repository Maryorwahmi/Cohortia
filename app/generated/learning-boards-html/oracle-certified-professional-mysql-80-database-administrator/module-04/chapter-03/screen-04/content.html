Module 04 › Chapter 4.3 › Physical vs Logical
Logical Backup (mysqldump)
  • Exports schema and rows as SQL; highly portable across versions.
  • Good for small datasets, schema migrations, or when portability is required.
  • Becomes slow and resource-heavy for very large databases.
Common misconception

People assume logical exports are always safer; in large systems they can cause unacceptable downtime due to long restore times.

Physical Backup (XtraBackup / MEB)
  • Copies raw files and logs, enabling much faster backup and restore at filesystem speed.
  • Supports hot backups for InnoDB by capturing LSN and redo logs for later prepare.
  • Requires environment compatibility and correct prepare/restore steps to succeed.
Key Takeaway

For large data sets choose physical backups to meet tight RTOs, but plan config compatibility and verification steps.
