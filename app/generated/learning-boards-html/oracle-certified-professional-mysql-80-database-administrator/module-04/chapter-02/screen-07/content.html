Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › --master-data & --flush-logs
What they do

--master-data inserts the binary log filename and position into the dump so you know where to resume binary log application. --flush-logs rotates binary logs before the dump, aligning the recorded position with a fresh log file.

Use --master-data=2 to record the position as a commented CHANGE MASTER TO line.
Pair with --flush-logs to simplify locating the recorded log file during recovery.
Misconception: skipping log rotation will still be fine. In reality, without rotation the recorded position may fall in the middle of a busy log file, making recovery ordering more complex.
Concrete example
mysqldump -u root -p --single-transaction --master-data=2 --flush-logs mydatabase > with_binlog_info.sql
Key Takeaway

Record binary log coordinates when preparing replication or point-in-time recovery, and rotate logs to simplify later log selection.
