Benefits vs Limitations
Benefits
  • Speed: almost instantaneous snapshot creation.
  • Minimal downtime: short lock window for consistency.
  • Fast restores: merge or rollback to snapshot state quickly.
Limitations
  • Dependent on storage technology and single-server focus.
  • Snapshots consume space and need lifecycle management.
  • Combine with binary logs for fine-grained point-in-time recovery.
Operational reminder

Plan snapshot sizing, monitoring, and offsite copying as part of any snapshot-based backup strategy to avoid surprises.

Key Takeaway

Use snapshots for speed, but pair with binary logs and an offsite copy to enable full disaster recovery and fine-grained restores.
