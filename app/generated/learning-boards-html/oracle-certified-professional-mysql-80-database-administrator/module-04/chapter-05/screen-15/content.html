Module 04 › Chapter 4.5 › What to Remember
Acquire consistency
Use FLUSH TABLES WITH READ LOCK and hold the session open until snapshot creation completes.
Manage snapshot space
Allocate copy-on-write space and purge snapshots promptly to avoid performance impact.
Combine with logs & offsite
Copy snapshots offsite and retain binary logs to recover to points between snapshots.
Summary

Snapshots are a fast, practical tool in your backup toolbox when used with consistent locking, careful lifecycle management, and offsite/log retention strategies.
