Common Mistakes
Over-instrumentation & Hint Misuse
Both mistakes are reversible if detected early, but they can create noise and regressions. Use a controlled, measured approach.
Mistake: enabling all Performance Schema instruments; consequence: measurable overhead and larger tables to query.
Mistake: adding hints without tests; consequence: future regressions when data changes.
Safety checklist
  • Enable only necessary instruments
  • Measure overhead on staging
  • Prefer rewrites over hints
  • Document hints with removal criteria
Pro tip
Automate periodic reviews of production hints and instrumentation settings as part of your change control process.
Key Takeaway

Advanced tools give power and risk; apply them with measurement, documentation, and rollback plans to stay safe.
