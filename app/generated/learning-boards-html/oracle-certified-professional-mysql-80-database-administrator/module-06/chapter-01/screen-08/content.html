SHOW GLOBAL STATUS and variables — what to capture

Capture runtime counters and config values, and compare snapshots to compute rates and detect trends.

Key counters to log
  • Threads_connected and Threads_running
  • Questions, Queries, and Slow_queries
  • Innodb_buffer_pool_read_requests and Innodb_buffer_pool_reads
  • Lock_waits and related InnoDB lock counters
Delta example

Take snapshot A and snapshot B an hour later. Subtract counters to get rates per hour. For example, delta of buffer pool reads divided by delta of read requests shows physical read proportion during the interval.

Common mistake

Reading single cumulative counters without computing deltas leads to misinterpreting the workload.

Key Takeaway

Collect status snapshots over time, compute deltas for rates, and use those rates to test your hypothesis about cache or query problems.
