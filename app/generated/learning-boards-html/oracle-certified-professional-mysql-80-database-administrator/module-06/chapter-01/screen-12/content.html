A compact checklist for your first 15 minutes

Follow these steps to collect evidence and leave the system in a known state.

Minute 0–2: quick snapshot
Run STATUS and SHOW FULL PROCESSLIST to identify any obvious blockers.
Minute 2–8: capture counters
Collect SHOW GLOBAL STATUS/VARIABLES and mysqladmin extended-status for delta analysis.
Minute 8–12: collect queries
Copy long-running query text and run EXPLAIN in a safe environment for plan inspection.
Minute 12–15: decide next step
Document baseline and plan one controlled change in staging or request more data for deeper analysis.
Why this matters

A short, focused investigation prevents ad-hoc changes that introduce risk and gives you the evidence necessary to choose the right remediation path.

Key Takeaway

A calm 15-minute routine yields the observations needed for confident, safe tuning decisions.
