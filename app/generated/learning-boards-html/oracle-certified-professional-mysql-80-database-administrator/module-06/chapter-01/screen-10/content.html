Common initial mistakes when diagnosing performance

Compare quick fixes versus measured, staged changes to understand risk and effectiveness.

Symptom chasing
Reacting to a single metric spike without correlating to other counters or process lists.
Evidence-driven change
Form a hypothesis from correlated metrics, test in staging, and then deploy with monitoring for verification.
Concrete scenario

If CPU is high and disk reads are high, investigate queries and buffer pool usage before recommending CPU upgrades. A hardware change alone can mask a deeper problem.

Pro tip

Capture status snapshots and process lists before and after any action so you can attribute effects to your change.
