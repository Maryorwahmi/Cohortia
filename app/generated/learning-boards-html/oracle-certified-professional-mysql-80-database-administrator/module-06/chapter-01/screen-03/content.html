Bloom: Apply → Master

Apply these skills immediately

These four applied objectives form your initial incident checklist and routine review tasks.

01 — Recognize bottleneck signs

Spot rising latency with steady or higher QPS, high physical reads compared to logical reads, and recurring lock waits as clear warnings to investigate.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Form safe hypotheses

Decide whether to investigate queries, cache sizing, or locks by listing which metrics will confirm each hypothesis.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Collect baseline data

Use mysqladmin status and SHOW GLOBAL STATUS to capture an initial snapshot you can compare to later outputs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Use staging for changes

Replicate production load in staging before applying configuration changes. This minimizes risk and reveals unintended side effects.
