Learning Objectives

Each objective explains what you will practice and why it matters to production reliability.

01
Explain the importance of performance

You will articulate how latency and throughput affect user experience and costs. This helps prioritize fixes that matter to the business.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe the iterative tuning cycle

You will map monitoring to analysis to tuning and verification, making tuning reproducible and measurable.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify common performance metrics

You will name and interpret QPS, TPS, latency, buffer pool hit ratio, and lock waits to assess health and spot regressions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Use basic MySQL command-line tools

You will gather status output and process lists to form an evidence-based diagnosis before changing configuration.
