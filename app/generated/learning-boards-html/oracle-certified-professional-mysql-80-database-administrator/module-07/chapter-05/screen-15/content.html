Final quick recall
Three short reminders to guide immediate decisions.
1. Align with queries
Partition only when queries filter on the partition expression frequently.
2. Shard only when needed
Sharding is for when single-server I/O or storage is the bottleneck and you accept added complexity.
3. Test and validate
Prototype partitioning or sharding decisions with real queries to measure pruning and distribution before production rollout.
Final takeaway

The right scaling choice balances query patterns, resource limits, and the operational cost you can support.
