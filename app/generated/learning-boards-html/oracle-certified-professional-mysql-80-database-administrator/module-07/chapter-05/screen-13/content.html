Assessing trade-offs with scenarios
Two guided scenarios to practice decision-making.
Scenario A: Partitioned transactions still slow
Problem: high disk I/O and slow cross-year queries despite partitioning. Correct escalation: shard to distribute I/O and storage across machines.
Scenario B: Hash-based customers
Advantage: even distribution and reduced hot spots. Disadvantage: inefficient range queries because hashed keys scatter across shards.
Reflective question

Which workload patterns in your environment would push you toward partitioning versus sharding, and how would you validate the choice?
