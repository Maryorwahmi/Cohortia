Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Sharding Mistakes
Common mistakes and how to avoid them
Practical mitigations for frequent pitfalls.
Poor sharding key
Avoid sequential or heavily skewed keys for range sharding. Consider hashing or composite keys to spread load.
Cross-shard joins
Design to keep joined data together or denormalize to reduce expensive distributed joins.
Underestimated operations
Backup, restore, schema migrations, and monitoring multiply across shards. Invest in automation early.
Rebalancing cost
Plan shard rebalancing and routing updates as part of capacity planning; moving data is complex and error-prone without tooling.
Pro tip

Prototype sharding on a test workload and validate distribution and cross-shard query costs before migrating production traffic.
