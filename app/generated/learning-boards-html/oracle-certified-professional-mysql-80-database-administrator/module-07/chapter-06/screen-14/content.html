Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Key Concepts
Managed Database Service
Handles provisioning, patching, backups; frees teams to focus on data and apps.
High Availability Models
Multi-AZ, regional standby, or distributed storage determine failover behavior and recovery time.
Read Replicas & Scaling
Replicas scale reads; monitor lag and plan promotion steps for write takeover or migrations.
Summary takeaway
Choose services by matching HA and read-scaling behavior to application RTO and throughput requirements, and document which tasks remain your responsibility under the shared model.
