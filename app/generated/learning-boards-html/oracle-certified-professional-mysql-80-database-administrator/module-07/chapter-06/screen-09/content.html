Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › High Availability across providers
Multi-AZ / Standby
A standby is provisioned in another zone for automated failover; designed for availability rather than read scaling.
Read Replicas
Primarily for scaling reads. Often asynchronous and require promotion for writes; monitor replication lag.
Distributed Storage (Aurora)
Replicates storage across zones, enabling faster failover and efficient, low-latency read replicas that share storage.
Example decision
If your recovery target is very strict and read scale is critical, prioritize architectures with fast failover and efficient replica designs.
