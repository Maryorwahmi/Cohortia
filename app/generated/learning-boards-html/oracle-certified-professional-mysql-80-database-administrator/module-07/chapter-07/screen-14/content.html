What to Remember
Disaster Recovery (DR)
Preparing for and recovering from catastrophic events to restore operations and data.
RTO & RPO
RTO is allowed downtime; RPO is allowed data age. Both decide architecture and cost.
Cross-Region & Multi-Cloud DR
Use async replication between independent clusters or provider replicas to protect against region outages.
Takeaway

Design DR around measurable objectives, automate where possible, and prove readiness through regular, segregated tests.
