Comparison
Self-Managed Group Replication
  • Full control over replication topology and promotion steps.
  • Requires detailed runbooks and infrastructure provisioning for DR sites.
  • Operational burden for failover automation and testing.
Managed Cloud Services
  • Built-in cross-region replicas, geo-redundant backups, and provider automation.
  • Lower operational overhead, but you must understand provider failover characteristics.
  • Still requires documentation and regular DR tests.
Concrete example
Aurora Global Database can deliver faster cross-region replication than manual async replication in many cases, enabling lower RPO. Azure and Google Cloud provide cross-region replicas and geo-backups that simplify architecture.
Decision guide
If you need strict control over promotion behavior and custom scripts, choose self-managed. If you prefer provider automation and fewer maintenance tasks, consider managed services but still test failovers.
Key Takeaway

Managed services lower operational overhead but do not remove the need for documentation and testing; self-managed gives control at the cost of more procedures to maintain.
