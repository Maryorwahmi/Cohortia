Comparison
Warm Standby
Minimal resources running; scales up on demand.
Pros: Lower ongoing cost. Cons: Longer RTO due to provisioning time.
Hot Standby
Fully provisioned and ready to accept traffic with minimal steps.
Pros: Short RTO. Cons: Higher cost to maintain.
Scenario card
For RTO ≤ 1 hour, hot standby or fully automated warm provisioning must be used. For RTO ≤ 4 hours, warm standby with tested scaling may be acceptable.
Practical checklist
  • Measure provisioning time for warm standby automation.
  • Confirm data freshness for warm standby after scale-up.
  • Validate hot standby promotion steps and connectivity under load.
Key Takeaway

Estimate provisioning time and cost, and test the chosen standby model so it reliably meets your RTO targets.
