lsnrctl status

tnsnames.ora checks
Verify alias spelling, host, port 1521, and SERVICE_NAME or SID as appropriate.
SQL*Plus checks
SHOW USER, SELECT SYSDATE FROM DUAL, and queries against V$ views are fast verification tools.
Final takeaway

A small set of commands and a correct client mapping let you resolve most initial connectivity problems quickly.
