Multiplexing and Disk Separation
Practical layout rules to reduce risk and improve performance.
Redo logs
Place members on multiple disks and prefer dedicated low-latency devices for sequential writes.
Control files
Multiplex control files on separate physical devices to avoid an unrecoverable restart.
Data files
Distribute large or hot tablespaces across arrays to reduce I/O hotspots and allow targeted backups.
Concrete example
Use three control file copies on three mount points, redo members on two arrays, and place heavy OLTP tablespaces on dedicated spindles or LUNs.
Check
Run V$LOGFILE and V$CONTROLFILE to confirm distribution, then validate by simulated failover tests.
