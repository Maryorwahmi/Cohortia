Process Flow

Decision checklist

  1. List non-functional requirements: throughput, availability, recovery objectives.
  2. List functional needs: partitioning, clustering, advanced security.
  3. Estimate growth and capacity needs against edition resource caps.
  4. Balance features against licensing cost and operational overhead.
Example mapping
Requirement: Partitioning
EE required; SE2 and XE do not include partitioning.
Requirement: Moderate concurrency, limited budget
SE2 may be appropriate if CPU/thread limits satisfy projected load.
Requirement: Local development, zero cost
XE is the right starting point, with an explicit migration plan to production edition.
Key Takeaway

A clear checklist prevents surprises: match requirements to edition features and validate the migration path early.
