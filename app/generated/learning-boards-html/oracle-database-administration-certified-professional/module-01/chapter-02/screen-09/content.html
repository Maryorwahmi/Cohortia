Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › PMON & SMON
PMON — Process Monitor

PMON cleans up after failed user processes by freeing locks and resources, and by rolling back or releasing session state where appropriate.

SMON — System Monitor

SMON performs instance recovery at startup, coalesces free space, and cleans temporary segments to maintain database health.

Example

After an unclean shutdown, on restart SMON applies any necessary recovery actions so data files reach a consistent point. PMON will tidy orphaned resources from abruptly terminated sessions.

Misconception

Assuming PMON and SMON remove the need for monitoring. They automate recovery and cleanup but cannot fix missing archives or corruption without DBA intervention.

Key Takeaway

PMON and SMON automate cleanup and recovery, but always check logs after crashes to confirm successful recovery.
