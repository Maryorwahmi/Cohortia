Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Learning Objectives
01
Explain legacy issues
Why java.util.Date and Calendar led to bugs and how java.time fixes them.
Describe mutability and thread-safety concerns, and how the newer API separates date, time, and zone responsibilities.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use LocalDate/LocalTime
Model dates or times without zones, such as birthdays and opening hours.
Practice creation, plus/minus operations, and remember they don’t carry zone information—interpretation is contextual.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Work with Instants & Intervals
Record timestamps with Instant; measure intervals with Duration and Period.
Learn when to use Duration (time-based) versus Period (date-based) and why Instant is ideal for UTC storage.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Handle time zones
Use ZonedDateTime and ZoneId to represent real-world times across regions.
Practice converting between zones and avoid implicitly depending on the system default zone for persistent data.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
