Definition

Inheritance explained

Inheritance allows one class to acquire properties and behaviors from another class to promote reuse and express subtype relationships.

• Reuse common fields and methods defined in a superclass.
• Model a natural "is-a" relationship, enabling clearer design intent.
• Avoid duplicating logic across multiple related classes.
Key Takeaway

Choose inheritance when subclasses truly are specialized forms of a superclass, not merely to copy code.

Example

Vehicle has brand, speed, and start(); Car and Motorcycle automatically inherit these members and share behavior unless overridden.

Analogy and structure
Analogy

Think of a blueprint for Vehicle and individual house models that inherit the blueprint's rooms and plumbing. Subclasses add or change specifics.

Misconception

Inheritance is not a free-for-all for reuse; it should model a genuine subtype relationship rather than just share code.
