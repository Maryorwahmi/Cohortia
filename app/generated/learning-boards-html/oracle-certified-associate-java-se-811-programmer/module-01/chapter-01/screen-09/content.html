Selecting and Downloading Your JDK

Choose the appropriate JDK version and download the installer for your operating system from a trusted source.

1

Visit Official Sites

Go to Oracle's official JDK download page or OpenJDK distributors like Adoptium (Eclipse Temurin).

2

Choose LTS Version

Select a Long-Term Support (LTS) release like JDK 11 or newer for stability and compatibility with course content.

3

Download OS-Specific Installer

Download the installer package (e.g., .exe for Windows, .dmg for macOS) that matches your operating system.
