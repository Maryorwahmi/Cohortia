Dark patterns vs ethical alternatives
Choose transparency and consent over manipulation and hidden mechanics.
Dark pattern example
A purchase prompt that hides exact odds or pre-checks recurring billing by default.
Ethical alternative
Display exact odds for purchases, require explicit opt-in for recurring charges, and provide clear cancellation steps.
Addiction mechanics — design check
If a mechanic increases engagement by exploiting variable rewards or persistent nudges, consider adding pacing options, reminders, or limit settings to protect players.
Key Takeaway

Ethical choices reduce harm and build long-term trust. Prefer transparency, player control, and respectful representation over short-term manipulation.
