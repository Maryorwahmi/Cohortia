Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
Intellectual Property
Protect creative works and document ownership with asset records and registration when needed.
Legal Documents
EULAs, licenses, and contributor agreements set rights and responsibilities for players and creators.
Privacy & Ethics
Minimize data, obtain consent, implement accessibility, and avoid manipulative monetization.
Summary
Use IP records and contracts to reduce legal risk, and embed privacy, accessibility, and ethical checks into design decisions to build trust and reach more players.
