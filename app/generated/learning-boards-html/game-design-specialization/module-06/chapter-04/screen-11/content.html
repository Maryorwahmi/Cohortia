Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Data Privacy
What to track and why
List data types and design consent, retention, and deletion policies before shipping.
Common data types
  • Player IDs and account details
  • Purchase and transaction history
  • Gameplay telemetry and metrics
  • Device identifiers and IP addresses
Regulatory focus
Rules like GDPR and CCPA require clear notices, consent mechanisms, and rights for data access or deletion.
Concrete example
If you store purchase history for receipts, inform players why it's stored, how long it will be kept, and provide a way to request deletion if law requires it.
Key Takeaway

Design data handling with a minimize-and-document approach: only collect what you need and make retention and consent explicit.
