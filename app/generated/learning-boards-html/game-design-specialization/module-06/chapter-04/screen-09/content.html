Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › EULA Clauses
Key clause types
  • License grant: what use is permitted.
  • Restrictions: no reverse engineering or modification unless allowed.
  • Data and privacy: how player data is collected and used.
  • Dispute resolution: arbitration or court choices.
Common misconception
Because most players do not read EULAs, developers may think terms are irrelevant. In truth, clear, fair terms reduce disputes and improve trust.
Stylized clause example
License Grant: Publisher grants you a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to install and use the Software for personal entertainment purposes only.
Restrictions: You may not reverse engineer, decompile, or create derivative works unless expressly permitted. You may not distribute unauthorized copies.
Data: The Software may collect device identifiers and gameplay metrics to provide services and improve quality. By using the Software you consent to collection as described in the Privacy Policy.
Dispute Resolution: Claims will be resolved by binding arbitration under the governing law specified below.
Quick guidance
  • If you want modding, add an explicit modding permission.
  • Be explicit about data types collected and consent points.
  • Consider how restrictive dispute clauses affect player relations.
