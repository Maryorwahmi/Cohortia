Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Licensing Assets
Asset licensing process
Track each asset, its license, and permitted uses to avoid surprises at release.
Step 1
Inventory all assets in the build.
Step 2
Record source, license text, and attribution needs.
Step 3
Upgrade to commercial licenses when needed.
Concrete example
A free asset marked for non-commercial use cannot be included in a paid release. Either replace it or acquire a commercial license before publishing.
Key Takeaway

Licenses vary; a central manifest reduces licensing errors and supports a clean release process.
