Trademarks and Branding
Protect names, logos, and identity used in commerce to prevent confusion.
Clearance & registration
Practical steps
  • Search marketplaces for similar names or logos.
  • Consider regional scope — where will you sell and market?
  • File for registration when you commit to commercial use.
Concrete example
Before announcing a title, search storefronts and do a basic trademark clearance to reduce the chance of rebranding later.
Common misconception
Trademarks only matter for big companies. In truth, any commercial use benefits from clearance and possibly registration.
Key Takeaway
Make branding checks part of your milestone checklist so identity issues don't force expensive changes later.
