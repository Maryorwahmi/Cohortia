Why it matters
Legal missteps can halt releases; ethical missteps harm players and reputation.
Quick orientation
This chapter treats legal protections and ethical design as tools you use during design and planning. Think of IP as what you own, contracts as the rules you and others agree to, privacy as promises to players, and accessibility as design choices that expand your audience.
Use this lesson to prepare a checklist for your team
Concept map
IP → Contracts → Privacy → Accessibility → Ethics
Use intellectual property to protect original art and code. Contracts allocate ownership and risk. Privacy controls how you collect and store player data. Accessibility and ethics guide design decisions that affect players' wellbeing.
