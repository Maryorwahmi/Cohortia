Quick operational checklist
Add these checks to your next sprint or milestone to operationalize legal and ethical practices.
Asset manifest
Map each file to a license and author.
Contributor agreements
Ensure sign-off on IP assignment or work-for-hire.
Privacy notice
Draft a short clear notice describing data collection and consent.
Accessibility
Implement at least two high-impact features early.
Final Thought
Small, early steps like a manifest and privacy sketch prevent large legal and ethical costs later.
