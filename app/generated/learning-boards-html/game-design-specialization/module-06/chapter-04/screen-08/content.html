Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Legal Documents
Document workflow for game projects
Collect templates early and attach license records to all third-party assets.
1
EULA
Contract with players covering permitted uses and disclaimers.
2
Licensing agreements
Defines use of third-party assets and engine components.
3
Employment/Freelancer
Sets ownership of deliverables, compensation, and confidentiality.
4
Asset manifest
A record tying each asset to a license and author.
Example process
  1. Inventory assets and mark original vs. third-party.
  2. Attach license or assignment document to each third-party item.
  3. Require contributors to sign IP assignment or work-for-hire.
  4. Publish a clear EULA at distribution and retain records for enforcement.
