Copyright basics
Protects original expression in code, art, music, and written text. Registration optional but helpful.
Practical rules
  • Always record authorship and dates in source control and asset manifests.
  • Obtain licenses for third-party music, images, or engine components when required.
  • Keep copies of licenses and permission emails with the project files.
Concrete example
Licensing a music track should state whether you may modify, loop, or use it in trailers and whether the composer requires credit.
Common mistake
Assuming that a short clip, or changing color or scale, makes a borrowed asset safe to use. Protection focuses on substantial similarity and expression, not small edits.
Quick action checklist
  • Identify all third-party assets in your build.
  • Match each asset to a license or original author record.
  • Remove or replace assets without clear permission before publishing.
Key Takeaway

Documentation is your strongest immediate defense: date-stamped assets and licenses reduce legal risk.
