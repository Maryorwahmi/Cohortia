Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Patents vs Secrets
Patents
Protect inventions through filing and public disclosure. Useful for novel technical systems but costly.
  • Requires novelty and non-obviousness
  • Filing and maintenance costs
  • Public disclosure of the invention
Trade secrets
Keep valuable processes confidential without disclosure. Protection depends on maintaining secrecy.
  • No filing, protection lasts while secret
  • Requires internal confidentiality measures
  • Risky if details are leaked or independently discovered
Concrete scenario
A unique server optimization for multiplayer matchmaking might be kept as a trade secret with strict access, while a genuinely novel game engine component could be considered for patent only if it is core to long-term business strategy.
