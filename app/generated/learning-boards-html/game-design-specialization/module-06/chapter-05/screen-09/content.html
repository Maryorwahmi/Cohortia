Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Building Genuine Connections
Process
A simple 4-step networking rhythm
  1. Prepare: 1–2 talking points about a recent game or talk.
  2. Engage: Ask a question and listen—show curiosity.
  3. Follow-up: Send a 2–3 line message with a resource or thanks.
  4. Nurture: Offer help, ask a short follow-up later.
Quick Tips
  • Keep a contact log with one action per person.
  • Share short lessons from your projects—value attracts replies.
  • Respect time: ask for 15 minutes, not an hour.
Common Mistake

Treating networking as transactional. This leads to shallow connections and fewer long-term opportunities.

Key Takeaway

Small, genuine exchanges build stronger career opportunities than mass outreach.

Scenario
Post-Game Jam Follow-Up
Practical example

Write a 2-line message: thank your teammate, mention one concrete thing you learned, and offer to share the build or a short clip. Attach a single-link to the prototype and one screenshot.

This shows you can communicate concisely and demonstrates your process—more powerful than a long, generic message.

Why it matters
Referrals and mentorship often come from trusted, repeated interactions. Small follow-ups convert casual acquaintances into advocates.
