Continuous Learning & Personal Brand
Small practices that make your portfolio believable and your career sustainable.
Continuous Learning
Participate in game jams, iterate small prototypes, and invite critique regularly. Each cycle should yield a small artifact to add to your portfolio.
Mnemonic: Jam → Iterate → Document
Design Philosophy
Write a 2–3 sentence statement that summarizes your approach and what you value in games.
Example prompt: "I design for emergent player choice through systems that reward experimentation."
Document Lessons
For each project include a short 'lessons learned' note explaining trade-offs and what you'd change next time.
This shows reflection and a growth mindset.
Quarterly Review
Every few months prune weak projects, refresh visuals, and update metrics you can quantify.
Habit: one small update per quarter keeps your portfolio current.
