Core Concept
Portfolio Structure
Each project entry should make your decisions visible and your impact clear.
  • Overview: What is the project and your role? One short paragraph.
  • Process artifacts: GDD excerpt, flowchart, system diagrams.
  • Visuals: screenshots, short gameplay clips, prototypes.
  • Contributions & lessons: What you did and what you learned.
Key Takeaway

Treat each project as a short case study: goal, process, outcome, lesson.

Concrete Example
Combat System: goal = high-risk, high-reward; include combat loop diagram, ability breakdown, and a 30s gameplay clip to illustrate feel.
Common Mistake
Posting many small unfinished projects without process notes makes it impossible to judge your decision-making.
