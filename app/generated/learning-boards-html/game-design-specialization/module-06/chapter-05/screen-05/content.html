Definition
Portfolio Entry Process
Follow this five-step checklist to create consistent case studies.
01
Overview
One paragraph: game, role, and design goal. This sets reviewer expectations immediately.
02
Process Artifacts
Include GDD excerpts, diagrams, or wireframes that reveal your reasoning and iterations.
03
Visuals
Screenshots or a short gameplay video communicate feel faster than text alone.
04
Contributions
List what you specifically designed, implemented, or balanced so the reviewer can judge your work.
05
Lessons Learned
A candid reflection on trade-offs and how you would improve the design shows maturity.
Example: Combat System
Overview: high-risk, high-reward combat system to reward aggressive play. Artifacts: combat loop diagram and ability spreadsheets. Visual: 30s clip of core encounter. Contributions: designed ability cooldowns and balancing. Lesson: reduce burst damage to lower frustration.
Pro Tip
Keep a single slide or page per project for quick walkthroughs. Use timestamps in videos to point to the moment that shows your key decision.
