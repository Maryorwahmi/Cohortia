Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Informational Interview
Template: 20-Minute Chat
Agenda
  • Intro (2 min): Quick background & goal.
  • Questions (12 min): Role, daily tasks, skills, studio culture.
  • Wrap (3 min): Ask for one resource or next person to speak to.
Note-taking
Capture one insight and one action. Example: "They use scripted encounters—learn to prototype enemy timing." Action: add a prototype clip to portfolio and note the tools used.
Common Mistake

Asking unfocused or generic questions such as "How do I get a job?" leads to vague answers. Ask targeted questions linked to their role.

Example
Level Designer: First 90 Days

Ask: "What will I be expected to deliver in the first three months?" If they say "blockouts and small puzzles," you can prepare by adding one or two level blockout videos to your portfolio and documenting iteration notes.

Key Takeaway

Use each conversation to create a single, measurable improvement to your portfolio or preparation plan.
