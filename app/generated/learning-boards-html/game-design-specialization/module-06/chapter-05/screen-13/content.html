Project Outline Template

What is the game, your role, and the core player experience?

List features you owned with process notes.

Design Philosophy

Write 2–3 sentences that state your design priority and how you achieve it. Example provided: "I design systems that reward player curiosity through emergent interactions and iterative testing." Make yours specific and actionable.
