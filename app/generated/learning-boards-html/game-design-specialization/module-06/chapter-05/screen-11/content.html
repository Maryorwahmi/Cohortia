Comparison
Live Challenge vs. Portfolio Review
Live Challenge
Clarify constraints, brainstorm, choose trade-offs.
Portfolio Review
Tell process, highlight your decisions, reflect on lessons.
Common Mistake

Giving a single pitch without exploring trade-offs or failing to show what you learned from iteration.

Walkthrough
3-minute project story
  1. Goal: What player feeling or behavior were you designing for?
  2. Your decision: Which mechanic or change did you choose and why?
  3. Result & lesson: Player reaction, metrics if available, and what you would change next.
Practice Prompt

Design a scarcity mechanic for a horror game. Ask 2 clarifying questions, suggest three options, and pick one with trade-offs. Time yourself and record for review.
