Resume Structure
Suggested order: Contact → Summary → Selected Projects → Skills → Education/Experience → Links. Keep it to one page for entry-level roles and ensure portfolio links are prominent.
  • Summary: 1–2 sentences of design philosophy
  • Selected Projects: name, your role, tools, and a short measurable outcome
  • Skills: engines, design tools, scripting languages
Key Takeaway

A tailored, proofread resume that points directly to portfolio evidence increases interview chances.

Example Project Line (for resume)
Echoes of the Void — Level & Systems Designer
Tools: Unity, Figma, Spreadsheet balancing
Role: Designed three levels and a procedural map system
Outcome: Improved retention in prototype levels by 15% (playtest)
Use concise bullets and always link the project line to the full case study on your portfolio.
