Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Game Design Portfolio

A curated set of projects showing process, decisions, and measurable outcomes that evidence your design thinking.

Game Design Document (GDD)

A living document that communicates vision, mechanics, and systems—use excerpts to show your planning and systems thinking.

Resume

A concise, role-focused summary highlighting relevant projects, tools, and quantifiable outcomes that signal readiness for the position.

Summary

Keep three polished artifacts ready: one strong portfolio entry, a GDD excerpt that shows systems thinking, and a resume tailored to the role you want.
