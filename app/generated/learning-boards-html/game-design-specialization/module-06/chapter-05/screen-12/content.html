Rotate these weekly to maintain variety and ensure continual skill improvement and visible output that your portfolio can reference.

Personal Brand Checklist
  • One-sentence design philosophy on your site.
  • Consistent project formatting and clear contribution notes.
  • Two channels with a weekly or biweekly posting cadence.
Micro-commitment
30-day practice plan
  1. Week 1: Rebuild a small mechanic and record a 20-second clip.
  2. Week 2: Write a short post explaining your design choices.
  3. Week 3: Share the clip and post; collect feedback.
  4. Week 4: Iterate the prototype and add a note to your portfolio entry.
Key Takeaway

Small, repeatable outputs build your skills and public record—consistency beats sporadic perfection.
