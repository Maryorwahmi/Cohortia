Process Flow
Hosting & Presentation
Preferred hosting: personal site or established game platform. Keep a single landing page and full case studies behind clear links. Always label your role, include a one-paragraph context, and add timestamps for video highlights.
  • High-quality screenshots or short edited clips
  • One-paragraph context + contributions list
  • Contact info and downloadable PDF or slide for interviews
Common Mistake
Low-resolution images or vague role descriptions make it hard to judge you.
Quick Fix
Add a single slide per project and a timestamped video clip for fast walkthroughs.
Presentation Checklist
Landing page with 3 projects and design philosophy
Case study per project: overview, artifacts, visuals, lessons
Contact info and resume download
PDF/slide for interview walkthrough
