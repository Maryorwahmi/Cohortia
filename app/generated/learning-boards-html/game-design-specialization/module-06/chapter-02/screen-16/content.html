Write reproducible steps
Numbered, precise, and include environment and attachments.
Use severity + priority
Severity = impact; priority = urgency for scheduling fixes.
Structure player feedback
Categorize, prioritize by frequency and impact, and convert to test cases when reproducible.
Closing Thought
Quality assurance is a team habit. Clear tests and reports make that habit productive and reliable.
