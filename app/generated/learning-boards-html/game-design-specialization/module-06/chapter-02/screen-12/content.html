Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Player Feedback
Feedback Channels
  • Internal playtests and QA reports.
  • Closed and open betas.
  • Community forums and support tickets.
Categorization
Group items into bugs, usability issues, difficulty balance, and feature requests for prioritization.
Prioritization & Action
Use frequency and player impact to assign priority. Convert reproducible feedback into test cases or bug reports, and communicate decisions back to players to build trust.
Key Takeaway

Structured feedback collection and prioritization help you make player-informed decisions aligned with design goals.
