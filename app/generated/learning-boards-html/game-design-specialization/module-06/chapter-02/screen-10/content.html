Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Good vs Poor
Good Bug Report
Includes: title, severity, priority, numbered steps, expected/actual results, environment, and attachments.
Example
Title: Character falls through floor in Level 3 after jumping. Steps list coordinates and input. Includes video and logs.
Poor Bug Report
Lacks steps, environment, expected result, and attachments. Forces developer to ask follow-ups.
Example
"Game crashes sometimes when fighting enemies." No build, no steps, no logs.
Key Takeaway

The single best habit: always include clear steps to reproduce and environment details.
