Bug Report Fields
  • Bug Title: concise summary of the issue.
  • Severity: impact on testing or play.
  • Priority: fix urgency relative to other bugs.
  • Steps to Reproduce: numbered, precise, and repeatable.
  • Expected vs Actual Result, Environment, Attachments.
Tools
Jira, Bugzilla, Trello are commonly used to track reports and status.
Example Bug Report
Bug Title: Character falls through floor in Level 3 after jumping
Severity: Major
Priority: High
Steps to Reproduce:
  1. Load 'Level_Forest_03'.
  2. Move to coordinates (X:120, Y:50, Z:15) near the large oak tree.
  3. Press 'Spacebar' to jump.
Expected Result: Character lands on terrain normally.
Actual Result: Character falls through the terrain mesh into the void and cannot recover.
Environment: Build 0.5.1, PC, Keyboard input
Attachments: Video of reproduction, console log excerpt
Key Takeaway

Precise reproduction steps and attachments remove ambiguity and let developers reproduce the bug quickly.
