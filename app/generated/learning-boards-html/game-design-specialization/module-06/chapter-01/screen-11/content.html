What is scope creep?

Uncontrolled addition of features or requirements that exceed original time and resource estimates, leading to delays and budget overruns.

Signs: frequent late requests, backlog filled with low-impact tasks, core features postponed repeatedly.
Mitigation checklist
  • Prioritize backlog by impact and feasibility.
  • Time-box experiments and prototypes.
  • Require acceptance criteria and estimates for new items.
  • Hold retrospectives to adjust planning and expectations.
Decision rule example: any late request estimated as large must be deferred unless it resolves a critical blocker or has high player impact.
