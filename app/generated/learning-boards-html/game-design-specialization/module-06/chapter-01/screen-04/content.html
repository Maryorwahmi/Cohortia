Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Waterfall vs Agile
Waterfall
Linear, phase-based
  • Plan once up front and follow phases sequentially.
  • Works when requirements are well-defined and unlikely to change.
  • Risk: late discovery of design problems leads to costly rework.
Misconception: Waterfall is always more predictable. It can be predictable only when scope is fixed and small.
Agile / Scrum
Iterative, feedback-driven
  • Work in short sprints, deliver playable increments, adapt based on feedback.
  • Supports changing design and rapid learning from playtests.
  • Requires continuous prioritization and strong collaboration.
Pro tip: Time-box experiments in early sprints to reduce risk and validate design quickly.
