Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Community Building
Core Concept
Channels, Roles, Rhythm
Design the community and the team that supports it
Channels
Discord for real-time, forums for deep discussion, social for discovery and highlights.
Roles
Community manager, volunteer moderators, and dev liaisons to triage feedback.
Rhythm
Weekly updates, monthly events, and transparent roadmaps to keep players engaged.
Example Structure
A practical staffing and process sketch
Community manager publishes weekly dev notes and triages player reports.
Volunteer moderators enforce rules and curate positive highlights.
Feedback triage converts community suggestions into tickets for the roadmap.
Key Takeaway

A healthy community needs channels, clear roles, and a consistent engagement rhythm to thrive.
