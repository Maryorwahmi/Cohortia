Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Monetization Models
Comparison
Model trade-offs
How each model shapes reach, revenue, and design
Premium
One-time buy. Clear value proposition; suits complete single-player experiences.
F2P + Ads
Free entry, ad revenue. Good for casual mobile but may disrupt immersion.
F2P + IAP
Purchases for cosmetics or convenience. High upside but ethical risks if manipulative.
Subscriptions / Battle Pass
Recurring revenue or seasonal engagement. Requires sustained content and value.
Concrete Example
Choosing for an indie puzzle game
If your puzzle game is casual and discovery-driven, F2P with cosmetic IAP can maximize reach while keeping gameplay fair. If the game is a focused, narrative single-player experience, a premium model may attract players who dislike microtransactions.
Key Takeaway

Monetization should support your design goals and respect players; choose with empathy and foresight.
