Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Post-launch Growth
Timeline
90-Day Post-launch Plan
Content drops, events, and listening milestones
Week 1–2: Patch critical bugs and publish a public roadmap.
Week 3–6: Plan the first content update and a small event to re-engage players.
Month 2–3: Seasonal event and cross-promotion; analyze retention metrics.
Practical Tactics
What to do right after launch
Publish clear changelogs and prioritize fixes that affect retention and playability.
Run short events with small, meaningful rewards to boost daily active users.
Listen to community feedback and communicate timelines for fixes and content.
Key Takeaway

Sustained growth requires new value, community care, and transparent communication — plan a steady cadence.
