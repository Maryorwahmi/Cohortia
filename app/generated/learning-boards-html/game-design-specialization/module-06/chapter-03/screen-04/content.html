Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Game Lifecycle
Process Flow
Lifecycle Timeline
Pre-launch, Launch, Post-launch — tactics and goals
1
Pre-launch
Dev logs, teasers, wishlists, press kit to build awareness and grow a list of interested players.
2
Launch
Coordinate press, influencers, and store pages to convert interest into downloads or purchases.
3
Post-launch
Updates, events, and community engagement focused on retention and advocacy.
Practical Example
A three-month pre-launch campaign, launch influencer previews, and monthly post-launch content
Pre-launch plan
  • Weekly dev logs and teaser trailer
  • Press kit and targeted outreach to niche outlets
  • Encourage wishlist adds and Discord sign-ups
Launch plan
  • Coordinate reviews and influencer streams
  • Run targeted ads to convert wishlists
  • Optimize store page assets and metadata
Key Takeaway

Plan marketing as a lifecycle with clear, time-bound activities for awareness, conversion, and retention.
