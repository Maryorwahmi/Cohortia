Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Influencers & Press
Core Concept
Working with creators
Pitch, provide assets, and define coverage expectations
Pitch
Short, audience-focused message with link to gameplay and press kit.
Access
Offer keys or early builds and specify coverage windows and disclosure expectations.
Diversify
Combine niche and mid-tier creators for reliable conversion rather than banking on a single star.
Practical Checklist
A simple outreach tracker
List 8–12 creators who play similar games and note audience size and engagement.
Prepare a one-paragraph pitch and include press kit links and a suggested coverage date.
Track replies, promised coverage, and follow up with campaign assets.
Key Takeaway

Creators amplify reach, but success depends on fit, clarity, and respectful partnerships.
