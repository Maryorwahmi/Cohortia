Process Flow
Launch Checklist
Critical activities and timing for launch week
Prepare store page: trailer, screenshots, and clear description. Test metadata and localization.
Coordinate press and embargoes: provide reviewers with keys and a clear release window.
Influencer schedule: agree coverage windows and disclosure terms.
Promotions: limited-time discounts or events to convert uncertain players.
Concrete Example
A coordinated day-one campaign
Day -7: Finalize store assets and schedule press releases.
Day 0: Publish trailer, run streamer sessions, and announce launch with a discount.
Day +7: Share player highlights and early patch notes to maintain momentum.
Key Takeaway

A launch succeeds with planning, ownership, and a mix of earned and paid attention to convert your early audience.
