Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Ethical Considerations
Definition
Dark Patterns & Fairness
Design choices that manipulate players vs choices that respect them
Examples to avoid
Hidden renewals, obscure odds on randomized rewards, intentionally frustrating progression that pushes purchases.
Better practices
Transparent pricing, visible odds, meaningful free progression, and cosmetic-first offerings over pay-to-win.
Policy Example
Simple rules to vet monetization
Publish odds for any randomized loot and provide a free, skill-based path to earn valuable items.
Avoid mechanics that give paying players unfair competitive advantages in multiplayer.
Use clear affordances in the UI so players understand purchases and subscriptions.
Key Takeaway

Ethical monetization protects revenue and reputation; design with player well-being in mind.
