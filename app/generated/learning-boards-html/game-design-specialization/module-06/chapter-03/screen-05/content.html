Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Pre-launch Marketing
Definition
Pre-launch Marketing
Build awareness and a ready audience before release
Dev logs
Regular behind-the-scenes updates that build a human connection and show progress.
Teaser trailers
Short videos that highlight the core loop and tone without spoiling content.
Press kit
Screenshots, key art, short description, and contact information for journalists.
Example Plan
Weekly schedule for three months before launch
Week 1–4: Weekly dev log posts and art reveals. Encourage wishlist adds.
Week 5–8: Teaser trailer and targeted outreach to niche press outlets.
Week 9–12: Influencer previews and final press kit distribution.
Key Takeaway

Start building an audience early with consistent updates and a press-ready asset set.
