Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Common Mistakes
Comparison
Quiet Launch vs Coordinated Launch
Contrast the outcomes and root causes
Quiet Launch
Late marketing, weak store page, no influencer alignment — low initial traction.
Coordinated Launch
Pre-launch wishlists, scheduled coverage, optimized assets — higher conversion and momentum.
Monetization Mistake
Dark patterns may spike revenue but reduce retention and trust long-term.
Mitigation Checklist
Actions to prevent the most common failures
Create a marketing calendar at least 3 months before launch; produce assets early.
Run a store-page review and A/B test trailers and descriptions if possible.
Establish moderation and feedback triage for your community before onboarding many players.
Key Takeaway

Identify top risks early and add clear mitigation tasks to your project plan to avoid a quiet launch or lost trust.
