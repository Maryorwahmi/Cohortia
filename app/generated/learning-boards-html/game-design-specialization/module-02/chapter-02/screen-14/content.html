Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Input Device
Hardware used by players to send commands: keyboard, mouse, gamepad, touch, or motion.
Control Scheme
The mapping from physical inputs to in-game actions; should be intuitive and prioritized by frequency.
Intuitive Controls
Bindings that players discover quickly by following established conventions and ergonomics.
Responsiveness
Speed and clarity of reaction to input, including animation and audio feedback.
Key Remapping
Allowing players to change bindings increases accessibility and comfort.
Accessibility
Toggle-hold, single-button modes, and hitbox tuning help players with diverse needs.
Summary

Always match device to game needs, map intuitively, make controls responsive, and add simple accessibility options early.
