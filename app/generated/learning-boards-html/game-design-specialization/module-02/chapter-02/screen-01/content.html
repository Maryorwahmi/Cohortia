Player Input & Control Schemes
How device choice, mapping, responsiveness, and accessibility shape player experience.
Orientation

This chapter shows how to select input devices, design intuitive mappings, and add checks like grounding to preserve intended mechanics. You'll see pseudo-code, a platformer mapping example, and a hands-on activity for a space-mining game. Expect to practice justification and propose at least one accessibility option.

Visual Concept
Inputs → Mapping → Actions
Keyboard: many discrete inputs, good for simulations and RPGs.
Gamepad: ergonomic, analog control and vibration for tactile feedback.
Touch: direct manipulation, gestures, larger hitboxes for fingers.
Key Takeaway

Controls are both technical and experiential: choose device and mapping to match precision, pace, and who you want to include as players.
