Learning Objectives
Bloom progression: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Analyze Devices

Compare keyboard, mouse, gamepad, and touch for genre fit and precision. Why it matters: matching device to genre reduces friction.

Verified Competency Milestone
02
Design Intuitive Schemes

Map common actions to familiar inputs and minimize learning. Why it matters: intuitive mappings reduce tutorial time and player frustration.

Verified Competency Milestone
03
Implement Mapping

Express control logic in conceptual code including checks such as grounding. Why it matters: it prevents unintended actions and preserves challenge.

Verified Competency Milestone
04
Plan Accessibility

Include remapping, toggle-hold and hitbox adjustments. Why it matters: accessibility expands your player base and prevents needless exclusion.

Verified Competency Milestone

Use these objectives as a checklist during design and playtests. Each milestone ties to a concrete deliverable: a justified device choice, a mapping document, runnable pseudo-code, and an accessibility proposal.
Key Takeaway

These objectives move you from comparison to practical implementation and inclusivity in control design.
