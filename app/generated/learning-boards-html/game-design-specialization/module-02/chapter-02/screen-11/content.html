Proposed Control Scheme (PC: Keyboard & Mouse)
Movement
  • Move Forward: W
  • Move Backward: S
  • Strafe Left: A
  • Strafe Right: D
  • Move Up (Ascend): Spacebar
  • Move Down (Descend): Left Shift
Rotation / Pitch
Rotate Left/Right: Mouse X-axis; Pitch Up/Down: Mouse Y-axis
Actions
  • Fire Mining Laser: Mouse Left Click
  • Deploy Collection Drone: E
Justification
WASD is familiar for translational control, mouse gives aiming precision for laser and rotation, and common keys like space and shift provide easy vertical control without overloading fingers.
Accessibility Option
Allow remapping of all controls and a toggle option for laser fire so players don't have to hold the mouse button.
Key Takeaway

Justify mappings using convention and ergonomics, and provide simple accessibility toggles to broaden playability.
