Process Flow
Primary → Secondary → Tertiary

Decide the main channel that must be noticed, add reinforcement, then add weight. Align timing and avoid contradiction.

Example: Health pickup
Primary visual glow, secondary chime, tertiary soft rumble. Together they register clearly even in noisy scenes.
Timing
Align cues so the player perceives them as one event. Lag breaks the illusion of responsiveness.
Checklist
  • Does the player immediately know an action occurred?
  • Are cues consistent with past events?
  • Do the signals conflict or compete for attention?
Prototype tip
Test in gameplay loop not isolated. Timing and mask effects show up only in context.
