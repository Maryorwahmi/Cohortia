Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Auditory Feedback Deep Dive
Definition
SFX and music shape attention and mood

Short sounds confirm actions; musical stings change emotional context. Voice lines add narrative information and urgency.

Design rules
  • Keep key sounds distinct and recognizable.
  • Match intensity to event importance.
  • Synchronize audio with visuals for perceived responsiveness.
Missteps
Too loud or frequent sounds cause fatigue; similar sounds blur meaning between events.
Example uses
Pickup chime
Short pleasant chime for items; distinct pitch for rare items to induce excitement.
Alert tone
High-pitched, urgent tone for detection or incoming danger, paired with visual tint for clarity.
