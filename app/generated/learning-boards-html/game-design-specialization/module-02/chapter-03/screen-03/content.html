Practice templates
Combat: Critical Hit
Visual flash + distinct sound + double-pulse rumble. Unique animation on enemy to confirm high impact and reward player timing.
Stealth: Detection
Exclamation icon, UI meter spike, urgent music shift, and quick vibration pattern to create immediate urgency and guide decisions.
Assessment: Critique
Look for overload, misleading signals, or silence. Propose a minimal, layered fix that keeps the player informed and comfortable.
Why test these skills

Testing with players reveals whether signals are heard, seen, and felt. Small changes in timing or intensity drastically affect clarity and comfort.

Work through the hands-on activity to internalize these templates.
