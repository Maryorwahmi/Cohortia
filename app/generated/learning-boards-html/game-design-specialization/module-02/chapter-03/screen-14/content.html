Analysis
Feedback as behavioral design

Use signals to encourage exploration, create tension, or reward skill. Design with player mental models and emotional arcs in mind.

Nudge
Subtle glow or audio cue to hint at a secret route without removing discovery satisfaction.
Tension
Rising music and dim lighting to build dread before a boss encounter.
Reward
Rare-item sound plus visual flourish to evoke excitement and value.
Key Takeaway

Feedback shapes emotion and decisions; design cues to support the intended player journey through timing and context.
