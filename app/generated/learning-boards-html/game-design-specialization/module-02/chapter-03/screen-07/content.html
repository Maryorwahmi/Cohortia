Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Haptic Feedback & Physical Confirmation
Definition
Add physical weight to events

Use vibration patterns to signify hits, impacts, or tension. Vary intensity and duration to create distinct meanings.

Pattern vocabulary
Single short pulse = light hit. Long low rumble = heavy impact. Double pulse = critical moment.
Accessibility
Offer settings to reduce or disable haptics and provide alternate audio/visual cues.
Testing checklist
  • Test across controller models and platforms.
  • Listen and feel: do haptics match perceived weight?
  • Provide intensity settings and fallbacks.
