Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
Feedback
Information the game gives players in response to actions and state changes.
Feedback Types
Visual, auditory, and haptic channels should be chosen and layered for clarity and accessibility.
Feedback Loops
Positive loops amplify success; negative loops help catch-up. Balance depends on design goals.
Final reminder: document feedback sets, prototype in context, test with diverse players, and tune with telemetry to ensure clarity, fairness, and emotional impact.
