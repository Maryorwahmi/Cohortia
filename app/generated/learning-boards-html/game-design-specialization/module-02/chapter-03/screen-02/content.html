Bloom
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01 Explain
Describe why clear, immediate feedback prevents confusion and increases immersion. Practice by auditing a weak signal in a level and listing improvements.
Verified Competency Milestone
02 Differentiate
Recognize positive and negative feedback loops and predict their effects on player motivation and balance across sessions.
Verified Competency Milestone
03 Design
Create layered feedback sets for actions, like hit confirmation or ability readiness, that feel satisfying and clear.
Verified Competency Milestone
04 Analyze
Use feedback to guide player choices and emotional arcs; test and iterate to reduce ambiguity and discomfort.
Verified Competency Milestone
How to use these objectives
  • Work through definitions and examples for each feedback type.
  • Apply templates to a combat and a stealth scenario.
  • Test with players and iterate on clarity, volume, and timing.
Each objective pairs a concept card with a short practice so you can verify learning by building or critiquing feedback in a short prototype.
