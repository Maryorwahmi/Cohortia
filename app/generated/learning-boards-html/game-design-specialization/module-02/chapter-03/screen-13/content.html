Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Stealth Design Activity
Scenario
Player is a rogue sneaking past a patrolling guard. Critical Event: player is spotted.
Visual
Exclamation icon above guard, rapid detection meter fill, red edge tint.
Auditory
Sharp alert tone, guard shouts, music shifts to tense tempo.
Haptic
Series of quick, urgent pulses to create bodily urgency.
How feedback guides behavior
The combination creates immediate recognition and urgency, prompting hide, flee, or engage decisions depending on player skill and options.
Use the chapter template to document choices and test with two players to refine intensity and timing.
