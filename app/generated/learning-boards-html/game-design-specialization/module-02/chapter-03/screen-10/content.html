Process Flow
Steps to tune loops
  1. 1. Define goals: Decide whether the experience favors power fantasy or competitive balance.
  2. 2. Map accumulation: Where do players gain advantages that compound?
  3. 3. Prototype: Small tests reveal runaway behaviors quickly.
  4. 4. Instrument: Use telemetry to observe distributions and divergence.
  5. 5. Iterate: Apply soft caps, diminishing returns, or catch-up tweaks and retest.
Example adjustment
Reduce kill-streak duration or add cooldowns instead of removing rewards entirely to preserve player satisfaction while limiting snowballing.
Misconception
There is no one-size-fits-all fix; balancing often requires several coordinated adjustments.
