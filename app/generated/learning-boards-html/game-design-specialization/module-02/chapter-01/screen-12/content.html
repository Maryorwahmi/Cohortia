Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Prototype and Test the Core Loop
Testing checklist
  • Can the player perform the loop multiple times in one session?
  • Do players repeat the action voluntarily?
  • Does feedback clearly communicate success and next choices?
Simple metrics
Action frequency, time-between-actions, success rate, player intent notes.
Interview prompts
  • What were you trying to do next?
  • Which part felt rewarding or boring?
  • Was any feedback confusing or missing?
Key Takeaway

Testing reveals whether the loop invites repetition. Iterate feedback and transitions until it does.
