Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › The Iterative Loop
Prototype
Build a minimal playable slice that isolates the assumption you want to test. Keep time and assets low.
Playtest
Observe players, take notes, and log how often issues happen. Prefer in-person observation or recorded sessions when possible.
Analyze
Group feedback by frequency and severity. Turn observations into specific hypotheses: "Players miss the jump because jump arc is too high."
Iterate
Implement one or two focused changes, then re-test to measure impact. Document the outcome before the next round.
Example workflow
Observation: 6 of 8 players fail the same platform jump. Hypothesis: lower jump arc or add midpoint platform. Change: reduce jumpForce by 10% and add visual cue. Re-test with 6 players and compare success rate.
Misconception
Avoid making many simultaneous changes; you won’t know which change affected player behavior.
Pro tip
Label each build and keep a short changelog so results are traceable across iterations.
Key takeaway: each loop must yield an observable result you can compare to the previous build.
