Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Prioritization Strategies
Severity
Does this issue prevent players from progressing or substantially reduce enjoyment?
Frequency
How many players encountered the problem? Use counts or percentages from your sessions.
Vision alignment
Will the suggested change move the game toward the experience you intend for your target players?
Practical tool
Use a simple table with columns: Issue, Frequency, Severity, VisionMatch (Yes/No), Priority, Assigned. Add a hypothesis and a target metric for each prioritized item.
Example
Problem: Jump is floaty. Frequency: 75% of testers. Severity: Blocks level completion. Vision match: Yes. Priority: High.
Pitfall
Over-optimizing for a single tester who is not representative of your target audience.
Takeaway: combine the three lenses to create defensible, testable priorities for iteration.
