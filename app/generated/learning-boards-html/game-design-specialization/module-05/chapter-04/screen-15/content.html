Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Iterative Design

A repeating cycle of prototype, playtest, analyze, and iterate to converge on better gameplay.

Feedback Analysis

Find patterns, weigh severity and frequency, and convert observations into testable hypotheses.

Prioritization

Rank issues by impact and frequency and prefer fixes that align with your vision and target audience.

Takeaway

Treat iteration as disciplined experiments: one change, one hypothesis, one measurable outcome. That pattern is how games improve consistently.
