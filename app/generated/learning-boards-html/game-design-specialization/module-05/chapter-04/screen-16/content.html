Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts — Continued
Version Control

Record changes, use branches for experiments, and revert or merge based on results.

Incremental Changes

Make small edits so their effects are measurable and reversible.

Pivot

If structured iterations repeatedly fail, consider a change in direction based on evidence and cost analysis.

Final takeaway

Iteration is a disciplined practice: hypotheses, measured changes, versioned experiments, and evidence-based decisions lead to better game design.
