Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Analyzing Playtest Feedback
Collect
  • Record sessions and take time-stamped notes.
  • Log simple metrics: success rates, time-to-complete, error counts.
  • Include direct player quotes for clarity.
Categorize
Group feedback by frequency and severity. Tag items that block progression as high priority.
Prioritization matrix
High frequency / High severity: Fix first
High frequency / Low severity: Triage
Low frequency / High severity: Investigate context
Low frequency / Low severity: Consider later
Concrete example
If many players miss a platform jump, mark it high frequency and high severity and form a hypothesis to test a specific change like reducing jump arc.
Pro tip: track items in a simple sheet or Trello board and include a column for hypothesis and test results.
