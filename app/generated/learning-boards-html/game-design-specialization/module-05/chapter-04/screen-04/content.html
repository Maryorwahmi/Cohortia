Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › What is Iterative Design?
Definition
Iterative Design

A repeating cycle of prototype, playtest, analyze, and iterate used to discover what actually works in a game.

Analogy
Think of iteration as sculpting with feedback. Each pass removes rough edges revealed by players.
Common mistake
Treating every suggestion as mandatory instead of spotting patterns and aligning fixes with your vision.
Concrete example
Prototype observation: Players report the jump feels "floaty" and timing is inconsistent. Hypothesis: increase gravity and add variable jump height to give tighter control. Iteration: tweak gravity multipliers and add low-jump behavior. Re-test to confirm improved responsiveness.
Key takeaway
Small experiments give measurable signals; they guide the next iteration.
Check your understanding
Can you state one measurable metric you would use to validate a jump change?
