Iteration: Refining Your Game Design
A practical path from quick prototypes to polished gameplay.
In this lesson

You will learn the iterative loop, how to analyze playtest feedback, make incremental changes, and use version control to manage experiments safely.

Why it matters

Iterative design reduces risk: small experiments reveal real player problems early so resources are focused on what actually improves the experience.

Prototype & Playtest

Analyze & Prioritize
Find patterns in feedback, weigh severity and frequency, and align fixes with your core vision.
Version Control
Use branching to try changes safely and revert if an iteration introduces regressions.
