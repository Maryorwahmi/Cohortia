Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Paper Prototype Activity
Step-by-step
  1. Materials: paper, pens, scissors, tokens.
  2. Draw a simple maze with start, end, and branches.
  3. Create player, crystal, and monster tokens.
  4. Define rules: move one square, collect crystals, monsters cause penalty.
  5. Playtest and observe behavior and confusion points.
  6. Iterate: change one element, replay, and record learnings.
Observation Prompts
Is movement intuitive? Do players understand goals? Are there emergent strategies?
Concrete Example
Move 1 square; collect crystals by landing on them.
If players consistently ignore crystals, move them closer or change rewards and retest.
Misconception
Paper is only for analog games
Paper is also excellent for testing digital UI and flow before coding.
Key Takeaway

A short paper prototype gives evidence you can act on immediately. Reflect and repeat until the core interaction is clear.
