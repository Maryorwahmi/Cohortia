Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Benefits of Prototyping
Reduce Risk
Test critical assumptions early to avoid costly rework later.
Early Feedback
Let players reveal unexpected behavior so you can refine rules and clarity.
Foster Innovation
Rapid experiments let teams try more risky ideas and discover better designs.
Process Checklist
  1. Define the one question the prototype will answer.
  2. Choose lowest-fidelity approach that answers it.
  3. Test with players quickly and observe behavior.
  4. Iterate or discard based on the result.
Example
Paper rule test
You notice rule ambiguity in a single play session and fix it in minutes, avoiding developer time later.
Key Takeaway

Prototyping transforms unknowns into answers early, letting teams make confident choices.
