Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Prototype
An early, focused experiment that answers a design or technical question.
Fail Fast
Build the smallest test that yields useful evidence and pivot quickly when needed.
Paper Prototype
Low-cost way to test rules, flow, and UI with immediate iteration.
Summary

Choose the simplest prototype that answers your question, collect evidence from play, and use that evidence to guide the next experiment or development step.
