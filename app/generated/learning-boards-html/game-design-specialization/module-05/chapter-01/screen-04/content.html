Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › What is a Prototype?
Claim
A prototype is a focused experiment to test one design or technical assumption.
  • It prioritizes speed and learning over polish.
  • It isolates riskiest assumptions for early feedback.
  • It may be paper, digital, or strictly functional.
Concrete Example
Movement test level
A single room with platforms where you test jump and dash. No art, just play to see if the mechanic feels right.
Why this matters
A clear prototype shortens the feedback loop and prevents expensive rework. You learn the truth about an idea before you commit art, networking, or narrative resources.
Misconception
Polish equals success
Polish can hide bugs and make playtesters focus on graphics instead of core mechanics.
Pro Tip
Define a single question
Write one sentence: "Does X feel fun?" and build only what answers it.
Key Takeaway

A prototype is judged by how quickly and clearly it teaches you something, not by how finished it looks.
