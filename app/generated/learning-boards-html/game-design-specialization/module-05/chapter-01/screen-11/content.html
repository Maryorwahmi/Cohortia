Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Choosing What to Prototype
Quick prioritization
Map assumptions by importance and uncertainty; prototype those high on both axes first.
Example
Platformer movement
Movement is critical and often uncertain. Prototype jump and controls before level art or story.
Rule
Test the smallest thing that could falsify your assumption
Common Mistake
Prototyping everything at once
Focus yields clearer evidence; scattershot tests produce confusion.
Key Takeaway

Prototype the riskiest and most important elements first using the simplest valid approach.
