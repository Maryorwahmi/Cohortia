When to go digital
Choose digital when your question depends on physics, input timing, or animation feel.
Example
Block-out movement
A single test level with cubes to test jump arcs and collision response.
Mini Pseudocode
// Movement test loop
spawn player at start
while playtest running:
  apply player input
  simulate physics
  log collisions and fall times
end
// Observe feel, tweak gravity and jump impulse
Fidelity Guide
Use lowest fidelity that answers your question. Placeholder art is fine.
Misconception
More polish equals more insight
Polish shifts focus to visuals and slows iteration. Keep tests narrow and repeatable.
Key Takeaway

Digital prototypes are for feel and timing. Keep them minimal and focused on the behavior you must validate.
