Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Paper vs Digital vs Functional
Paper Prototype
Fast, low-cost, ideal for rules, layout, and flow testing. Change instantly on the spot.
Digital Prototype
Engine-based tests for movement, collision, and pacing. Fidelity can vary widely.
Functional Prototype
Focused digital tests that prove a mechanic or technical approach without building a whole game.
Decision Guide
  • Testing rules or flow? Use paper for speed.
  • Testing feel or physics? Use a digital engine block-out.
  • Testing technical feasibility? Build a functional prototype focused on the mechanism.
Example
Bridge-building RTS mechanic
Paper could test strategic placement, but a functional prototype is required to validate dynamic pathfinding and bridge creation logic.
