Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Learning Objectives
01
Explain prototyping
Define prototypes and why they speed design decisions.

You will describe prototypes as focused experiments and explain how early learning prevents costly rework later.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate types
Match paper, digital, and functional prototypes to use cases.

You will list when to use a paper mock, a quick engine block-out, or a focused functional test and why each is efficient.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Adopt fail-fast
Use rapid iteration to uncover problems early.

You will practice building small experiments to test assumptions and learn when to discard an idea quickly.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Prioritize tests
Identify the riskiest assumptions to prototype first.

You will learn a simple decision approach: prototype what threatens the game's core success or is hardest technically.
