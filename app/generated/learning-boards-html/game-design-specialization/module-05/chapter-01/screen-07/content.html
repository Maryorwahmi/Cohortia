Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Paper Prototyping
How it works
Simulate gameplay with paper, tokens, and a clear rule set. Iterate instantly based on observations.
Materials
Paper, pens, scissors, tokens
Use Cases
Rule clarity, UI layout, early player flow
Mini Scenario
Maze: crystals and shadow monsters
Draw a maze, cut tokens for the player, crystals, and monsters. Define move rules and play. Note ambiguities and iterate immediately.
Common Mistake
Polish distracts testers
Avoid making paper prototypes look like final art. Keep focus on rules and interactions.
Practical Tip
Iterate in minutes
Change a rule or layout and replay immediately. Fast loops produce better insights.
Key Takeaway

Paper prototypes reveal rules, flow, and clarity problems faster than discussion or planning alone.
