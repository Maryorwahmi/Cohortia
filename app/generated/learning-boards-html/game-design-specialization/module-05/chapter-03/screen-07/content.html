Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Watching Players
Observation

What to record

  • Actions attempted and sequence (with timestamps).
  • Pauses, hesitations, and emotional reactions.
  • Unexpected strategies or creative solutions by players.
Use neutral language in notes. Replace judgments with descriptive sequences so analysis focuses on the event, not the observer’s opinion.
Common mistake

Interrupting the player to clarify often biases the session and prevents natural discovery; avoid unless safety or severe confusion requires it.

Example note
"00:45 — Player enters area, attempts to dash twice using button X, no effect. Pauses 8s, looks at controller, says 'I thought dash was Y'."
Post-play prompts
  • "What was your favorite part and why?"
  • "Describe the most confusing moment."
  • "How did you expect the mechanic to work?"
