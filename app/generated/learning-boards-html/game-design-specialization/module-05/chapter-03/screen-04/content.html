Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › What is Playtesting?
Core Concept

Playtesting defined

Playtesting is an observational method that validates whether players can understand and enjoy your game. It is evidence-driven and iterative.

Purpose
Identify usability, clarity, engagement, and emotional responses that guide changes.
Misconception
Not just to confirm fun — it uncovers why players behave a certain way and how often problems occur.
Key Takeaway

Treat playtesting as a diagnostic step: collect data, find root causes, and plan fixes that align with your vision.

Concrete example
Imagine a 2D platformer where players can jump, wall-jump, and dash. You believe the wall-jump is discoverable. In an external session, three out of five players repeatedly miss the wall-jump until told about it. This reveals a discoverability problem, not a skill problem, and suggests design changes to signpost or simplify the input.
Check your understanding
  • What hypothesis does playtesting confirm or refute?
  • Is a repeated issue likely a design problem or an isolated user error?
  • How will you measure frequency and impact?
