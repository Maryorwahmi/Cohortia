Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Analyzing Results
Process

Turn observations into prioritized work

  1. Aggregate notes and survey scores across sessions.
  2. Identify recurring issues and categorize them (Bug, Usability, Balance, Fun).
  3. Prioritize by frequency and impact; write concise problem statements with evidence.
  4. Propose minimal fixes and design a verification test.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize fixes that unblock the core experience, and verify with a follow-up test rather than implementing every suggestion.

Example problem
"60% of players failed to perform wall-jump on first attempt. Representative quote: 'I didn't know you could jump off the wall.'"
Proposed fix
Add a subtle level ledge and short hint before the first wall-jump, then re-test with 5 similar players to measure change in success rate.
