Playtesting & Gathering Feedback

Learn how to run internal, external, and remote playtests; ask unbiased questions; collect qualitative and quantitative data; and prioritize fixes based on recurring patterns.

In this lesson
Estimated run: 8 minutes of mixed slides and clips
Why it matters

Playtesting converts assumptions into evidence so you fix the right problems and avoid costly rework.

Who benefits

Designers, producers, QA, and anyone who needs to validate that players can learn and enjoy the core interactions.

Outcome

Actionable, prioritized fixes and clearer design hypotheses for the next iteration.

Key Takeaway

Playtesting reveals what players actually do and think. Use structured observation plus open-ended interviews to gather both qualitative insight and quantitative signals.
