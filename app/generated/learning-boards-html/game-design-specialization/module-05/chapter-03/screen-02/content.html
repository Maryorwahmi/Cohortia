These stages guide how you move from understanding playtesting to running and analyzing your own sessions.

01
Role of Playtesting

Explain why testing validates design hypotheses and prevents developer assumptions from guiding the final build.

Verified competency milestone
02
Types of Playtests

Differentiate internal, external, and remote tests and select the one that matches your goals and constraints.

Verified competency milestone
03
Recruitment & Prep

Find representative testers, prepare stable prototypes, and avoid over-explaining mechanics that bias results.

Verified competency milestone
04
Gather & Analyze

Use observation, open interviews, and simple Likert surveys to collect qualitative and quantitative data you can prioritize and act on.

Verified competency milestone
Mnemonic
Think P.R.O.B.E: Plan, Recruit, Observe, Build questions, Examine data. This helps you run focused, bias-aware playtests.
