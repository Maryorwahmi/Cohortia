Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Qualitative vs Quantitative
Qualitative

Words and behaviors

Observations, quotes, and descriptions that explain why a player made a choice.

Example: "Player tried to combine blue and red gems, thought action was required, and said 'nothing happened'."
Quantitative

Counts and scales

Likert items, failure counts, and metrics that indicate frequency and impact.

Example: 3 of 5 players failed to complete the jump sequence; average tutorial rating 2.4/5.
Analysis tip
Map counts to categories (Bug, Usability, Balance, Fun). Prioritize by frequency and severity. Use quotes and timestamps to design targeted fixes.
