Consent and privacy basics

  • Explain recording, storage, and access before the session.
  • Obtain written or verbal consent for recordings and data use.
  • Use anonymized notes and avoid collecting unnecessary personal info.
If testing minors, require parental consent and adapt materials. Respect all opt-outs immediately.
Quick checklist
  • Consent obtained and recorded.
  • Recording and storage plan explained.
  • Opt-out and stop procedures clear to participant.
