Definition

Open vs. leading questions

Open questions invite descriptions and reasoning. Leading questions suggest the desired answer and bias responses.

Good: Open-ended
"How did you feel about the combat system?"
Bad: Leading
"Don't you agree the combat felt exciting?"
Suggested script
  1. Open prompt: "What was your favorite part?"
  2. Clarify: "What did you do next?"
  3. Scale: "Rate the tutorial 1–5 — why did you pick that score?"
Pro-tip
Record interview audio when possible. Transcripts make it much easier to extract recurring phrases and mental models during analysis.
