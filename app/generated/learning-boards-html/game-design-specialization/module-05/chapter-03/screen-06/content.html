Process

Recruitment checklist

  • Define target demographics and play habits.
  • Create 3–5 screening questions to confirm fit.
  • Plan session length, incentives, and consent for recording.
Stabilize the prototype for core flows. If crashes are frequent, postpone the session or limit tasks to stable features.
Common mistake

Recruiting convenience testers who don’t match your audience yields misleading feedback and poor prioritization.

Environment checklist
  1. Quiet space and comfortable seating.
  2. Working recording setup (if allowed) and spare devices.
  3. Printed consent and note templates for observers.
Remote test tips
  • Confirm hardware, OS, and network ahead of time.
  • Provide clear joining instructions and expected durations.
  • Use simple feedback forms and record sessions when consented.
