Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › MVP Checklist
Process

Step-by-step

1. State the core loop

Write it in one sentence so scope stays focused.

2. Pick essential mechanics

Only include systems required to exercise the loop.

3. Use placeholders

Replace non-essential art and audio to save time.

4. Minimal content & stability

Enough levels to test the loop and robust builds for reliable data.

Instrumentation

Choose two or three metrics that map directly to your hypothesis. Examples: retention to level two, average time to complete the loop, frequency of a core ability usage. Use short surveys to capture qualitative feeling.

Key Takeaway

MVPs are about clarity: clear hypothesis, minimal scope, and measurable outcomes.
