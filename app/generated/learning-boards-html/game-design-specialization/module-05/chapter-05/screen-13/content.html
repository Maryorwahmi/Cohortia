Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Roguelike — Vertical Slice
Activity

Vertical Slice Plan — 5 minutes

Player

One fully animated character with 3–4 abilities and VFX/SFX.

Enemies

2–3 distinct enemy types with varied behaviors and telegraphed attacks.

Environment

Hand-crafted dungeon section with dynamic lighting and hazards.

Loot & UI

Several unique items with equip animations and a polished UI showing impact.

Audio & Narrative

Professional SFX/music and brief environmental storytelling to set tone.

Polish

Smooth animations, clear feedback, and stable performance for a flawless demo.

Key Takeaway

Concentrate polish on a single memorable moment in the slice that demonstrates the game’s unique selling points and execution ability.

Resource Focus

Allocate senior art and audio time to the key moment and leave less-critical systems at lower fidelity.
