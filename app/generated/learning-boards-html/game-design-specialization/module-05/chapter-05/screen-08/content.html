Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › What is a Vertical Slice?
Definition

Vertical Slice

A polished, representative segment that shows the final look, sound, and integrated systems at target quality.

Purpose

Convince stakeholders and align teams by showing finished-quality execution in a small scope.

Misconception

It is not the whole game; it’s a deliberate, polished sample that communicates vision and feasibility.

Key Takeaway

A vertical slice proves execution quality and helps secure funding or team buy-in when polished evidence is required.

Example
MOBA Vertical Slice

Four detailed characters, polished map segment, professional audio, smooth UI and match flow to show the final experience.

Roguelike Vertical Slice

A hand-crafted 5-minute dungeon with final art, effects, and music that communicates tone and mechanical depth.

Integration

All major systems should interact as they would in the final game within the slice.

Polish

Animations, audio, and UI must be near-final quality in the slice’s scope.
