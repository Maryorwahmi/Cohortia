Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Building a Vertical Slice
Checklist
Pick a bounded segment

Single mission, short match, or hand-crafted 5-minute run that can be polished end-to-end.

Deliver near-final assets

Art, audio, animation, and VFX that show the intended style and quality.

Integrate systems & polish

AI, UI, and gameplay systems must interact smoothly with clear feedback.

Key Takeaway

A vertical slice shows execution capability; scope it narrowly and polish deeply within that slice.

Example Elements
  • Final-feel character animations and VFX.
  • Polished UI and audio feedback for actions.
  • Integrated systems showing intended gameplay loops.
  • Brief narrative hooks or environmental storytelling.
Presentation

Prepare a short guided demo or playthrough that highlights the slice’s best moments and explains how they scale to the full game.
