Skills & Outcomes

Practical outcomes you can demonstrate to teammates and publishers.

MVP Checklist
  • Core gameplay loop implemented and testable.
  • Only essential mechanics included.
  • Placeholder assets to save time.
  • Minimal content but playable goals.
  • Stable enough to collect reliable feedback.
Vertical Slice Checklist
  • Near-final art and audio for the slice.
  • Integrated systems working together.
  • Polished UI, feedback, and performance.
  • Short, complete experience that communicates tone.
