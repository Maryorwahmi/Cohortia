Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › MVP vs Vertical Slice
MVP

Learn fast

  • Goal: validate core mechanic
  • Cost: low
  • Team size: small
  • Deliverable: playable prototype with placeholder assets
  • Outcome: data-driven decision to continue or pivot
Vertical Slice

Show quality

  • Goal: demonstrate final-feel execution
  • Cost: higher
  • Team size: cross-discipline
  • Deliverable: polished playable segment
  • Outcome: publisher interest or team alignment on quality
Key Takeaway

Pick the milestone that best addresses your project’s primary risk: validation or execution proof.
