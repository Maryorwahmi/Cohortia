Decision

Is a Vertical Slice worth the cost?

When to choose it
  • You need publisher funding or a high-value pitch.
  • You need a team-wide quality benchmark.
  • You must demonstrate final-feel systems to stakeholders.
When not to
  • You have limited funds and the core mechanic is unvalidated.
  • You need rapid iterative learning rather than a polished demo.
  • The team cannot produce near-final assets without large risk.
Example Decision

An indie with a novel mechanic and minimal funds should pick an MVP. A studio seeking publisher backing with resources should favor a vertical slice to show final quality.

Key Takeaway

Match the milestone to the goal: validate cheaply or demonstrate quality with investment.
