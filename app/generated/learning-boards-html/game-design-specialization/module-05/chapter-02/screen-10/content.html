Core Concept
Time.deltaTime
Scale per-frame operations into per-second operations for consistent gameplay across machines.
Why it matters
Without it, movement and timers vary with frame rate, causing unfair or inconsistent behavior.
Concrete contrast
No scaling vs scaled
No scaling: position += speed; Scaled: position += speed * Time.deltaTime. The latter yields predictable units per second.
Misconception
Believing smooth frame rates alone fix movement speed differences. Smoothness and correct speed are separate concerns; use both time scaling and interpolation for polish.
Key Takeaway

Always multiply per-frame movement by the time delta to ensure consistent per-second motion across hardware.
