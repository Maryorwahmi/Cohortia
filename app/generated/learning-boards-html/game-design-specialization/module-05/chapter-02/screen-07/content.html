Comparison
Transform vs Rigidbody
Which approach answers your question fastest?
Transform Movement
Simple, deterministic position changes. Best for prototypes without physics interactions or when you control all collisions manually.
Rigidbody Movement
Uses physics solver for forces, gravity, and natural collisions. Choose this when interactions depend on physics behavior.
Concrete scenario
Example: obstacle knockback
If players should be pushed by moving objects, Rigidbody physics is the right choice. If you simply need to navigate a maze, Transform-based movement is faster to implement.
Pitfall
Applying transform changes to a Rigidbody-controlled object can conflict with physics and cause jitter. If you move a Rigidbody, prefer changing velocity or using forces through the physics API.
Key Takeaway

Choose the method that best validates your mechanic; refactor when physics become necessary.
