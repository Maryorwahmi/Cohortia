Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Tetris Core Loop
Breakdown
Player Action: move or rotate a piece. System Response: piece updates position and continues falling. Feedback: visible change in grid, fall speed, and line-clear effects. New Action: decide next move for the piece or react to upcoming pieces.
Why it works
Urgency from continuous fall and immediate line clear rewards produce constant decision pressure and clear success signals.
Assessment tip
Use Tetris to illustrate timing, feedback, and how small decisions accumulate into strategy.
Key Takeaway

Simple, observable loops with crisp feedback scale into deep systems through pace and player skill growth.
