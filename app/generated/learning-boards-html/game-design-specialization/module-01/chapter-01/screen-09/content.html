Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Designing for Flow
Three-stage curve
Introduction: low challenge, introduce mechanics.
Ramp: increase difficulty gradually, add decisions.
Mastery: present satisfying rewards for skillful play.
Measurement
Observe success rates, hesitation, and repeat attempts during a session to judge flow.
Misconception
Difficulty spikes or unclear goals break flow more reliably than they create excitement.
Key Takeaway

Tune progression in small steps and focus on feedback so players sense improvement and remain absorbed.
