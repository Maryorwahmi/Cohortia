Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What 'Fun' Means
Competition
Fun through rivalry, leaderboards, and balanced mechanics.
Mastery
Fun through skill improvement, predictable systems, and satisfying control.
Discovery
Fun through exploration, secrets, and emergent surprises.
Align loop to emotion
Choose rewards and feedback that amplify the targeted emotional response. Mastery needs clear metrics, discovery needs layered content.
Checklist
Name your intended fun type, test with players who seek that experience, and iterate based on their reactions.
