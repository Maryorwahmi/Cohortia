Practice loop for MDA
Concrete steps to train design judgement using playtests.
Step 1
List mechanics
Record explicit rules and inputs during a short session.
Step 2
Observe dynamics
Note recurring strategies, pacing, and emergent player patterns.
Step 3
Name aesthetics
Describe the emotional response the play session produced.
Quick exercise
Play Pong for five minutes. List 3 mechanics, 2 dynamics, and 1 primary aesthetic. Propose one mechanic tweak to shift the aesthetic and justify it.
