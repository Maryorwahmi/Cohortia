MDA at a Glance
M
Mechanics
Rules and components you build: inputs, actions, constraints.
D
Dynamics
Emergent behavior, strategies, and pacing that arise in play.
A
Aesthetics
Player feelings: challenge, discovery, immersion, and social joy.
Use MDA as a translator between code and emotion when planning features.
