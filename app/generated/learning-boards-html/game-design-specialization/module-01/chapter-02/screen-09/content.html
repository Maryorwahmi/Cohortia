Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Design Chain
Aesthetic → Dynamics → Mechanics
A practical chain for intentional design and testable hypotheses.
Aesthetic
Write the player feeling in one sentence and the measurement you will use.
Dynamics
Describe the emergent behaviors that would produce that feeling.
Mechanics
Choose minimal rules and feedback that nudge players toward the dynamics.
Template hypothesis
If we add [mechanic], then players will [dynamic], and will report [aesthetic measure].
Pro tip

Keep mechanics minimal. Each rule should be justified by the dynamics it creates and the feeling it produces.
