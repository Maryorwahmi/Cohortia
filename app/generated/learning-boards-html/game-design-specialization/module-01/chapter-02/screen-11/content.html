MDA Analysis Exercise
Follow these steps to practice linking rules to feeling.
Mechanics
List 3–5 explicit rules in the chosen game.
Dynamics
Describe 2–3 emergent behaviors or strategies.
Aesthetics
Name 1–2 feelings the game evokes and explain why.
Extension
Propose one mechanic tweak, predict its effect on dynamics and aesthetics, and then test in a quick play session.
