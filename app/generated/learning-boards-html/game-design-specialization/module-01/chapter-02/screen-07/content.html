Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Designer vs Player
Designer path
Mechanics → Dynamics → Aesthetics
Plan mechanics
Define rules with intended player choices.
Predict dynamics
Sketch expected behaviors across sessions.
Target aesthetics
Choose emotional goals and measurement criteria.
Player path
Aesthetics → Dynamics → Mechanics
Feel the aesthetic
Players sense mood before rules.
Discover patterns
Players find strategies and habits in play.
Infer rules
Mechanics become clear through repetition and feedback.
Designer hint

Provide clear feedback and scaffolding so players discover intended dynamics and the desired aesthetic follows naturally.
