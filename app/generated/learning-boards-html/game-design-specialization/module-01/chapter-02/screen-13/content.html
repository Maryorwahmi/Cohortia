Iteration and Playtests
Small experiments validate whether mechanics produce intended dynamics and aesthetics.
Hypothesis
State the expected dynamic and aesthetic change from a single mechanic tweak.
Isolation
Test one variable at a time in short sessions.
Prompts
Ask players what they did, why, and how they felt.
Metrics to record
Round length, frequency of a strategy, success rate, and subjective challenge rating.
