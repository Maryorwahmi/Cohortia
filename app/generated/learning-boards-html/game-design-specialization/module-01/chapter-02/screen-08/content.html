Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Flappy Bird Case Study
Mechanics
Tap to flap, gravity, moving pipes, collisions, score increment.
Predicted dynamics
Rhythmic tapping, anticipatory timing, short-session mastery attempts, frequent retries.
Aesthetics
Primary: Challenge. Secondary: Sensation and Submission as quick pastime.
Design exercise
If you reduce gravity slightly, predict the changes in dynamics and aesthetics. Consider testing by comparing average run length and player reported frustration.
Common misconception
More features equal better engagement. Simplicity with tuned feedback often creates stronger emotional response.
Check your understanding
List three metrics you would record to evaluate whether the tweak improved the aesthetic of challenge.
