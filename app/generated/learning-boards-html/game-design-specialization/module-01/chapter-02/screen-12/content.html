Design Diagnosis Exercise
Use MDA to find the weakest link in a design problem and propose testable fixes.
Step 1: List mechanics
Document every rule, input, and resource in the system.
Step 2: Predict dynamics
Describe how players will interact and whether strategies are discoverable.
Step 3: Evaluate aesthetics
Compare intended feelings with playtester reports.
Recommended fixes
Simplify mechanics, add clearer feedback, and phase mechanics with onboarding.
Key Takeaway

Use short, focused playtests to confirm whether changes produce the predicted dynamics and desired aesthetics.
