Aesthetics
The emotional experiences players have while interacting with the game.
Sensation
Sense-pleasure like sound and tactile feedback.
Fantasy
Role-playing and immersion in make-believe worlds.
Narrative
Drama, story, and character arcs.
Challenge
Skill-based obstacles and mastery.
Fellowship
Social interaction and shared play.
Discovery
Exploration and uncovering secrets.
Expression
Creativity and player identity through tools.
Submission
Pastime and repetitive comfort activities.
Design prompt
Choose a primary aesthetic for your project and write two sentences describing how you will know players experienced it after a playtest.
