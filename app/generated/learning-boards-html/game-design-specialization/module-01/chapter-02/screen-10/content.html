Common Pitfalls
Two frequent mistakes undermine meaningful play.
Mechanics without dynamics
Many features that do not interact coherently often create confusion and shallow play.
  • Symptoms: low engagement despite many options.
  • Fix: reduce mechanics and focus feedback on core choices.
Aesthetics without substance
Polish without underlying dynamics produces a hollow feeling.
  • Symptoms: players praise visuals but say play felt pointless.
  • Fix: connect visual cues to mechanics that create meaningful behavior.
Warning

If players can't form predictable strategies, your dynamics are failing even if mechanics are numerous.
