What you'll be able to do
Apply the theory with short, practical checks.
Classify player motivations
Given a game scenario, pick primary motivations and explain design evidence.
Design feedback signals
Plan visual, audio, and UI cues that confirm player actions immediately.
Outline progression
Create short-term and long-term goals that show growth and unlock choices.
Design a targeted feature
Choose a Bartle type and tune goals, challenge, and rewards to encourage flow.
Quick checklist
  • Are goals clear and measurable?
  • Is feedback immediate and understandable?
  • Does progression reward effort and skill?
  • Do player choices feel meaningful?
Use the checklist to verify your mini-design before submission.
