Bartle's Player Types
A practical lens to align mechanics with motivations.
Achievers
Drive: goals, points, mastery. Mechanics: leaderboards, titles, measurable rewards.
Explorers
Drive: discovery and curiosity. Mechanics: secrets, lore, complex systems.
Socializers
Drive: interaction and relationships. Mechanics: guilds, communication, cooperative objectives.
Killers
Drive: dominance and competition. Mechanics: PvP arenas, direct conflict systems.
Practical note: use Bartle to shape a primary audience and include secondary hooks. Always validate choices by watching player behavior rather than assuming labels stick.
Scenario
A new mode adds a global leaderboard and rare cosmetic prizes. Expect Achievers to engage first; add hidden objectives or lore fragments to reward Explorers and social features to encourage groups to form.
Common Mistake

Treating Bartle as a strict taxonomy. Players can move between categories; design for flexibility.

Try this: pick a feature, name the primary Bartle type it should attract, and list two mechanics that would support it.
