Choice and Agency
Meaningful decisions create ownership and identity.
Types of meaningful choices
  • Strategic: skill trees, builds
  • Narrative: branching dialogue or consequences
  • Cosmetic: personalization and social signaling
Reward balance
Pair intrinsic satisfaction with well-timed extrinsic rewards so progress feels earned.
Example: Skill tree
Choosing a branch in a skill tree grants distinct abilities, shaping playstyle. Small cosmetic unlocks reward exploration, while major skills provide new mechanics that change decisions.
Intrinsic
Joy of new tactics and mastery.
Extrinsic
Badges, titles, or rare items reinforcing choices.
Misconception: Giving too many trivial choices increases agency. Instead, ensure choices meaningfully alter experience or reward visible outcomes.
