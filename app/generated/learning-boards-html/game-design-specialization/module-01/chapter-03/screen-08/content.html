Progression hierarchy
Plan small loops that feed into larger arcs.
Short-term goals
Daily tasks, level-ups, or single-session achievements that provide immediate satisfaction.
Mid-term goals
Skill mastery, reputation progress, or unlocked mechanics that take multiple sessions.
Long-term goals
Major unlocks, narrative milestones, or top-tier cosmetics that define endgame objectives.
Key Takeaway

Make every short-term win visible and explicitly connected to longer goals so players feel meaningful progress.

Example: Leveling + cosmetics
Players earn experience in short loops to reach level milestones that unlock new abilities, while cosmetic drops act as intermittent rewards showing visible status.
Momentum
Short wins keep sessions satisfying.
Meaning
Long-term goals give direction and identity.
Misconception: Long-term progression must be grindy. Instead, design stepping stones that feel earned and tied to player choice.
