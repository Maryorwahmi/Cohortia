Design for Flow
A repeatable checklist to tune features toward immersion.
1. Define a clear goal
State exactly what success looks like for a session or task.
2. Design immediate feedback
Make outcomes obvious and informative for learning.
3. Calibrate challenge
Offer entry levels, adaptive suggestions, and harder optional variants.
4. Layer progression & rewards
Connect short loops to bigger milestones and meaningful rewards.
Concrete example
Daily Challenge Dungeon for Achievers: clear objective and timer, immediate score feedback, difficulty tiers with suggested settings, tokens for progression and leaderboard rewards. New players have optional lower-timer runs to learn mechanics.
Check Your Design
  • Can players state the goal after one session?
  • Is feedback immediate and informative?
  • Are difficulty and rewards clearly connected?
Misconception: Flow is innate. It is designed through clarity, feedback, scaffolding, and progression.
