Designing for a Player Type
Follow this structure to create a testable feature.
Step 1: Choose primary type
Pick Achiever, Explorer, Socializer, or Killer as your main audience.
Step 2: Define a single clear goal
State what success looks like in one sentence.
Step 3: Immediate feedback
List the cues players will get to learn and improve.
Step 4: Progression & rewards
Design short loops and long milestones; choose intrinsic and extrinsic rewards.
Example
Daily Challenge for Achievers: clear objective, live score feedback, challenge tokens for progression, leaderboard titles as rewards. Test by tracking repeat-play rate and asking if goals were clear.
Avoid

Don’t assume a single metric proves engagement. Use multiple signals: retention, subjective clarity, and observed behavior.

When you test, ask: did players experience flow? If not, which checklist item failed — goal clarity, feedback, challenge, or progression?
