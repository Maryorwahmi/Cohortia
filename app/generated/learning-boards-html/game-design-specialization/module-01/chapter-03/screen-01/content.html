Player Psychology & Engagement
Why players play and how to tune systems for lasting engagement and flow.

This lesson explores motivations like challenge, social play, fantasy, and discovery. You will learn a practical model of player types, how challenge and feedback form loops, what progression and agency do for retention, and how reward systems support motivation. We will finish with a short design activity that helps you target a feature to a player type.
