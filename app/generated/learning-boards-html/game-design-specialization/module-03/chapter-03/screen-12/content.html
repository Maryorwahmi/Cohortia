Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Memorable Characters
Checklist
Flaw
An internal weakness that creates stakes and growth opportunities.
Show-not-tell
Reveal the past through action and choices, not walls of text.
Unique voice
Consistent vocabulary or rhythm that makes lines recognizable.
Relationships
Ties to other characters that reveal priorities and tension.
Iconic moment
A scene designed to reveal core identity or transform the character.
Background NPCs
Give one distinct detail to make them feel alive without over-designing.
Exercise card
Pick one character and write: flaw, revealing action, voice trait, key relationship, and a single scene idea that reveals them. Use these five items to guide design decisions and dialogue lines.
Common mistake
Overloading characters with lists of traits; prioritize one strong flaw and one memorable detail.
