Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › PCs vs NPCs
Comparison

Player Characters

  • Designed for agency: let players feel ownership.
  • Can be silent, customizable, or pre-defined depending on desired experience.
  • Balance identity and player choice so choices feel authentic to the character.
Example types
Silent protagonists for projection, customizable avatars for player expression, pre-defined characters for strong narrative voice.

Non-Player Characters

  • Serve purpose: quest giver, world color, antagonist.
  • Important NPCs need distinctiveness and reactivity.
  • Minor NPCs still benefit from a single memorable trait.
Common mistake
Making important NPCs bland or making PCs too rigid so players feel locked out of meaningful choices.
Key Takeaway

Design choices should protect player agency for PCs and ensure clear purpose and personality for NPCs.
