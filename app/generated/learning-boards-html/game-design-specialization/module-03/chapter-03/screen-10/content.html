Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Arc Design
Step-by-step
  1. 1. Starting state: define flaw and baseline.
  2. 2. Inciting incident: event that challenges belief.
  3. 3. Trials: escalating tests connected to mechanics.
  4. 4. Transformation: final change and player-facing choice.
  5. 5. Tie to gameplay: ensure each beat has a playable moment.
Worked example
Character: reluctant hero. Start—avoidant and doubtful. Inciting incident—village attacked. Trials—rescue mission, moral test when offered vengeance, leadership choice in defense. Transformation—accepts responsibility and sacrifices to save others. Gameplay tie: leadership mechanic unlocks once the hero chooses to take command during a mid-arc battle.
Common mistake
Designing trials that are disconnected from mechanics; make each trial require a player decision that reflects the character's inner conflict.
Key Takeaway

An arc that maps to play gives emotional weight to player choices and creates memorable progression.
