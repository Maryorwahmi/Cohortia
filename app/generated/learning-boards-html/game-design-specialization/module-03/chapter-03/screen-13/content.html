Starter profile template
Character Type: Reluctant Hero / Sympathetic Villain
Character Name: [Your Name]

1. Archetype and twist:
   - e.g., "Chosen One" who refuses destiny.
2. Core motivation:
   - One-sentence motive and why it matters.
3. Backstory snippet (3-4 sentences):
   - Key formative event that explains motive.
4. Personality traits (3-4 words):
   - e.g., Cynical, Loyal, Impulsive
5. Flaw/Weakness:
   - The internal struggle that drives the arc.
6. Character arc idea:
   - Starting point → Inciting incident → Potential transformation
7. Visual hint:
   - One element that suggests history or motive.
8. Gameplay impact:
   - How motive or flaw creates unique dialogue options or quest behaviors.

Then write one in-character line that shows the motive or flaw without naming it.
Quick example
Type: Reluctant Hero. Motivation: protect younger sibling. Backstory: grew up as guardian after parents disappeared in a raid. Flaw: avoids leadership. Arc: forced to lead during attack → accepts duty. Visual hint: worn locket. Gameplay: dialogue offers protective choices and unlocks escort quests.
Check your profile
  • Can someone implement the character from this profile in under a minute?
  • Does one detail hint at a playable scene or mechanic?
  • Is the arc tied to at least one player decision?
