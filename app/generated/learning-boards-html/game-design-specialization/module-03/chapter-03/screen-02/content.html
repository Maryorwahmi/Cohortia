Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Learning Objectives
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify character components
Name archetype, motivation, and backstory and explain their design role. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic: Venn of archetype, motive, history. Knowing this helps you choose visuals and dialogue that feel consistent.
02
Differentiate PCs and NPCs
Explain design constraints for player-led vs world-led characters. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic: two silhouettes—one open for player agency, one closed for purpose-driven NPCs.
03
Develop character arcs
Outline start, inciting incident, trials, and transformation. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic: a small timeline showing change from flaw to growth or decline.
04
Align visuals with personality
Create silhouettes, attire, and animation that reflect role and motive. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic: silhouette + prop = instant storytelling about role.
