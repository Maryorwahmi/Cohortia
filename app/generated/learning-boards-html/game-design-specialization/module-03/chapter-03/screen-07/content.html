Hierarchy

Backstory levels

Public facts: What the player knows early. Keep concise.
Hints: Small revelations through quests or dialogue that deepen intrigue.
Designer knowledge: Private details that guide consistent behavior and dialogue choices.
Design tip: ensure hinted details connect to later gameplay or emotional payoff so players feel discovery matters.
Concrete example
Public: a guard mentions a childhood loss. Hint: the guard flinches at thunder. Designer: the guard lost a sibling in a raid. A quiet side mission reveals the raid and the guard's choice to forgive, creating emotional closure.
Common mistake
Dumping long biographies in text walls. Instead, reveal through action and small moments that reward exploration.
Key Takeaway

Keep a full designer backstory, but reveal only what serves gameplay and emotional payoff.
