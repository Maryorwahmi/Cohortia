Architecture

Visual elements

  • Silhouette: single-frame recognition test.
  • Costume: suggests role, culture, and status.
  • Facial features: scars, expressions, and telltale marks.
  • Animation: posture and movement that match temperament.
Design checklist: run a silhouette test, pick one costume detail that tells a story, and design one signature idle or walk cycle that communicates mood.
Example
Veteran soldier: narrow silhouette with pack, dented helmet, slow careful steps, wary head tilts. These visual signals imply history and make the character legible in the world.
Common mistake
Giving multiple characters similar silhouettes or relying only on color palettes. Silhouette trumps color for quick recognition.
Key Takeaway

Design visuals that signal personality and role immediately; match motion and pose to inner life.
