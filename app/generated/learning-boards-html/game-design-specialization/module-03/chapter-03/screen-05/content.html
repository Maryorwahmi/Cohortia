Foundation → Twist

Common archetypes

Hero
Action-focused, goal-driven, often growth-oriented.
Mentor
Guide and teacher; can hide flaws.
Villain
Source of conflict, often driven by motive.
Trickster
Unpredictable, challenges norms and expectations.
Design move: choose an archetype, then write one sentence that twists it. Ensure that twist shows up in at least two actions or lines of dialogue during play.
Example: Mentor with a twist
Mentor archetype: wise guide. Twist: secretly orchestrated the crisis to test the hero and regain lost power. This adds conflict and later revelation scenes without losing the mentor's authority early on.
Why small twists work
  • They keep recognition while adding surprise.
  • They can be revealed gradually to create payoff.
  • They avoid breaking player expectations abruptly.
Key Takeaway

Use archetype for clarity, twist for surprise; make sure the twist is believable and shown in behavior.
