Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Character Arcs
Timeline
Positive Arc
Start flawed, face trials, learn, and improve. Use to justify growth of player abilities or trust in others.
Negative Arc
Start sympathetic, then succumb to flaws or corruption. Use for tragedy or moral cautionary tales.
Flat Arc
Character remains steady and helps others change—useful as a moral anchor or guide.
Arc blueprint
  1. Starting state: who they are and their flaw.
  2. Inciting incident: forces them into change.
  3. Trials: challenges that test beliefs and skills.
  4. Transformation or reaffirmation: final state that resolves the arc.
Concrete scenario
A self-doubting captain must choose to lead a risky rescue. Success builds confidence and opens leadership dialogue and mechanics.
Common mistake
Designing arc beats that don't map to gameplay moments, leaving emotional beats unsupported by player action.
