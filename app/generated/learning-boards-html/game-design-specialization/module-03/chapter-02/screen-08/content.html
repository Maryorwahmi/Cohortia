Design cultures that create choices
Write a short culture card with: primary value, social structure, and a unique ritual.
Primary Value: Survival — produces communal resource sharing and strict laws.
Social Structure: Council of Elders — creates gatekeepers for knowledge and quests to win audience.
Ritual: Night of Repair — citizens repair public works; failure triggers social shame mechanics.
Comparison Exercise
Create two culture cards side-by-side listing: value, hierarchy, and fear. Use their differences to seed a three-step faction quest chain.
Culture A: Value — Honor; Hierarchy — Militarized clan; Fear — Loss of reputation.
Culture B: Value — Knowledge; Hierarchy — Guilds; Fear — Lost archives.
Quest Hook: A lost archive (Culture B) is guarded by a clan ritual (Culture A); players must negotiate or infiltrate.
Key Takeaway

Distinct values and fears create believable friction and quest opportunities.
