Hard vs. Soft Magic
Hard magic: explicit mechanics and costs. Soft magic: mysterious and narrative-focused.
Hard: Define inputs, outputs, and measurable cost.
Soft: Limit use and keep explanations sparse to preserve wonder.
Societal tie: Who learns magic and what stigma or privilege attaches?
Concrete Example
Hard rule: Casting a healing rune consumes one rare crystal and induces fatigue. Gameplay: Players must decide when to spend crystals during dungeon runs. Soft: A mythic healer appears in crises but can't be summoned at will.
Misconception Check
More power without cost reduces player choice. Add clear costs to preserve interesting decisions.
Key Takeaway

Constraints make magic meaningful; choose a style and design its cost and cultural impact.
