Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Technological Frameworks
Define tech as systemic
Pick three breakthroughs and describe inputs, outputs, and side effects for each.
Breakthrough: Automated harvesters — Input: power cells; Output: food surplus; Side effects: labor displacement.
Breakthrough: Ubiquitous AI — Input: data; Output: decision systems; Side effects: surveillance and inequality.
Breakthrough: Warp transit — Input: rare crystals; Output: fast travel; Side effects: geopolitical disruption.
Gameplay Integration
Make maintenance and resources part of systems: broken drones need parts, hacked infrastructure changes NPC behavior, and scarcity creates faction disputes.
Misconception Check
High tech without limits removes meaningful trade-offs. Always attach costs and access constraints.
Key Takeaway

Technology should alter society and produce gameplay challenges tied to resources and maintenance.
