Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › History & Lore
Anchor your world with a concise timeline
Select three major historical events and link each to a playable consequence.
Event 1: Founding — explains institutions and rituals.
Event 2: Cataclysm — explains ruins, hazards, and migrations.
Event 3: Recent Conflict — explains present factions and quests.
Delivery: sprinkle clues in optional texts, environmental props, and NPC dialogue so discovery feels earned and optional.
Example
A collapsed dam (cataclysm) explains why coastal villages built raised platforms and why water rights are contested quests. Evidence: rusted chains, a village anniversary, and law records in a side quest.
Avoid the Lore Dump
Break long histories into short fragments; use optional books, murals, and audio logs rather than single long expository scenes.
Key Takeaway

History should explain current mechanics and provide optional discovery moments.
