Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Quest Design & Level Layout
Three-act quest structure
Discovery → Complication → Resolution, each tied to a world element.
Discovery: An abandoned ledger hints at a smuggling ring.
Complication: A festival restricts travel, forcing stealth or bribery.
Resolution: Expose ring, shift trade power, and change NPC behavior.
Level Design Ties
Match terrain to objectives: narrow passes for ambushes, vertical ruins for traversal puzzles, markets for social checks.
Environmental Storytelling
Use props and optional notes to reveal context so players can piece together motives and history.
Key Takeaway

Quests and levels should feel like natural consequences of your world's rules and past events.
