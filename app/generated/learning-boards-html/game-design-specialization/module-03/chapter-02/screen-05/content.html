Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Consistency
Maintain internal logic
Follow a repeatable process to keep rules coherent as your world grows.
1. Define the rule clearly in one sentence.
2. Specify the cost or limitation that balances the rule.
3. List social and mechanical consequences.
4. Record exceptions with origins and evidence.
Use the world bible to make these steps searchable for your team.
Concrete Example
Rule: Magic drains a year of life per major spell. Cost: Mortality increases stigma. Consequence: Rituals exist to extend life at a price. Exception: A lost artifact temporarily negates cost but leaves traceable corruption.
Misconceptions
Changing rules to fit plot makes players distrust outcomes. Prefer documented exceptions with clear origins instead of ad hoc reversals.
Key Takeaway

A small, recorded process prevents contradictions and preserves player trust.
