Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Practical Checklist
Document rules
Log rules, costs, and exceptions in a world bible.
Seed discovery
Break lore into optional fragments and environmental clues.
Connect to play
Ensure each detail influences a mechanic, quest, or level layout.
Final Thought
Design with a curious-player test: if a player probes, will the world answer convincingly? If yes, you are on the right track.
