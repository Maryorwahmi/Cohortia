Design trade-offs
Every major choice should have an immediate mechanical cost and a longer narrative effect.
Immediate: Lose resources, gain allies, unlock a skill.
Long-term: Shift faction power, change access, alter NPC treatment.
Discovery: Provide clues so choices are informed, not blind.
Example
Helping a rebel faction grants safe houses (mechanical) but makes merchant caravans avoid you (narrative), changing trade quests and prices.
Misconception Check
Choices that only change cosmetic outcomes are poor substitutes for true branching; prioritize mechanical and narrative impact.
Key Takeaway

Meaningful trade-offs and discoverable consequences make choice satisfying and coherent.
