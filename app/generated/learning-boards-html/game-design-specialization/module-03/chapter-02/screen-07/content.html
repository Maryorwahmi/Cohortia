Design map-driven mechanics
Pick three biomes, assign a unique resource to each, and derive gameplay consequences.
Biome: Mountain — Resource: Ore veins — Consequence: Mining quests and territorial chokepoints.
Biome: Desert — Resource: Water wells — Consequence: Trade caravans and survival mechanics.
Biome: Swamp — Resource: Bioluminescent herbs — Consequence: Night exploration hazards and crafting materials.
Tip: Make resources socially meaningful — who controls them and why it matters.
Example
In a frozen tundra, geothermal vents become settlements' lifelines. Vents create trade routes, guard posts, and hazards to guard against scavengers. That single geography feature yields architecture, rituals, and quests.
Design Prompt
Sketch a map with three landmarks and list one gameplay problem each landmark creates for players to solve.
Key Takeaway

Let terrain and resources produce social structures and gameplay, not just scenery.
