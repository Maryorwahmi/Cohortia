Integration Process
A four-step loop connects setting to play.
1. Pick an element (history, resource, ritual).
2. Derive a mechanical effect (puzzle, ability, scarcity).
3. Design a level or encounter that highlights it.
4. Seed narrative hooks and optional lore to explain it.
Concrete Example
Element: A collapsed aquifer. Mechanic: periodic water levels that change paths. Level: a timed exploration with rising water and new routes. Narrative: villagers guard a map of safe paths that players must earn.
Check Your Design
For each new mechanic ask: does the world explain it, and does it create interesting player choices?
Key Takeaway

Design loops that make setting and mechanics support each other for richer play.
