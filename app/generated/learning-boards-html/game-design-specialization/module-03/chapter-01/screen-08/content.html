What Emergence Is
Stories from interacting systems, not scripted beats
Emergent moments happen when independent systems combine to create unexpected but explainable outcomes. They produce personal narratives that players claim as their own.
Designer Role
Build predictable rules, interesting failure modes, and meaningful feedback so emergence is noticeable and tied to theme.
Prototype
Test one system interaction—like resource scarcity and NPC morale—to see what stories surface during play.
Example Card
Colony Raid + Solar Flare
A raid plus a system event like a solar flare leads to a survival story: injured settlers, ruined stores, and improvised strategies—unscripted but coherent.
Why It Matters
Emergent stories increase player ownership and replayability when systems are reliable and communicative.
Misconception Check
Emergent does not mean meaningless randomness; design clear rules so outcomes are explainable and memorable.
