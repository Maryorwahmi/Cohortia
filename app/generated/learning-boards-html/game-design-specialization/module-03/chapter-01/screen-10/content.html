Hierarchy
Layer conflict and schedule beats on a timeline
Conflict can be internal, external or environmental. Combine them so tension feels natural and stakes compound.
Layer 1: Internal
Character doubts and moral choices.
Layer 2: External
Opponents, obstacles, or rival factions.
Layer 3: Environmental
Weather, resource scarcity, or hazardous terrain affecting play.
Planning Tip
Draw a five-point timeline and assign intensity to each beat, then insert calm beats to help peaks land emotionally.
Example
Haunted Mansion — Pacing Sketch
1) Calm arrival, 2) Curious exploration, 3) Sudden scare, 4) Quiet recovery and clue discovery, 5) Confrontation. Calm beats let clues register and make the final confrontation meaningful.
Misconception Check
Not every scene must escalate. Strategic calm scenes increase the impact of later conflict.
