Practice Habits
Prototyping, environmental storytelling, and playtest checks
Prototype Early
Sketch flowcharts and run quick plays to spot pacing and agency issues.
Show, Don't Tell
Use environment, objects and NPC behavior to communicate backstory and stakes.
Playtest Checks
Ask players what choices they remember; unclear choices are a red flag.
Quick Milestone
Run one prototype of a three-beat scene and measure whether at least 3 players can state the stakes afterward.
