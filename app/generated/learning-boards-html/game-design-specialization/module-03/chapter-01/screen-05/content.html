Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Narrative Structures
Process
Decide a structure that fits your goals
1. Linear
Predetermined sequence that delivers a single, polished arc. Great for cinematic pacing and consistent experience.
2. Branching
Decision trees that diverge. Offer player agency and replayability at the cost of more content and complexity to maintain consistency.
3. Emergent
Stories that arise from system interactions. Require robust rules and produce highly personalized, unscripted moments.
Decision Heuristic
Choose linear for controlled emotional beats, branching for meaningful player moral weight, and emergent when you want player-created stories.
Examples & Misconceptions
Linear Example
A campaign designed to hit precise emotional beats for every player; choices may vary but return the plot to the same arc.
Branching Example
A quest choice that alters later NPC relationships and possible endings, increasing replayability and moral consequence.
Emergent Example
A supply shortage plus a weather event creates a unique survival crisis that becomes the player's personal story.
Key Takeaway
Match your structure to the experience you want to create and plan scope so choices remain meaningful.
