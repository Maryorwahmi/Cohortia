Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Player Agency
Core Claim
Agency is about perceptible consequences
Track what matters to players and tie choices to those tracked states so consequences are visible and memorable.
Avoid Illusory Choice
If different options lead to the same outcome, players will feel cheated. Ensure choices alter state or flavor.
Prototype Check
Log state changes and ask testers to explain what their earlier choices changed in the world.
Example
Dialogue Choice Impact
Instead of identical results, tie dialogue options to NPC disposition, unlocking different side quests or altered NPC help later on.
Practical Tip
If you cannot branch, change resource availability, NPC lines, or quest rewards to preserve perceived agency.
Misconception Check
Agency is not only about endings; short-term meaningful consequences create strong agency too.
