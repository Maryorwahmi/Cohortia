Common design failures and quick fixes
These issues cross writing and systems. The fastest cure is a focused prototype and a short playtest script that checks for them.
Exposition Overload
Fix: move information into objects, NPC lines, and interactive discovery.
Ludo-Narrative Dissonance
Fix: align mechanics and story; change mechanics or story so they support the same theme.
Weak Agency
Fix: ensure choices change tracked states or visible outcomes and test if players notice.
Practical Steps
Quick Prototype
Sketch decision trees and run a short playtest to verify whether choices produce perceivable consequences.
Playtest Script
Ask: "What choices did you make? What changed?" If players struggle to answer, strengthen agency cues.
Align Systems
Ensure UI, mechanics, and narrative tone communicate the same expectations about play and consequence.
Key Takeaway
Small prototypes and targeted playtests are the most effective tools to find and fix narrative problems early.
