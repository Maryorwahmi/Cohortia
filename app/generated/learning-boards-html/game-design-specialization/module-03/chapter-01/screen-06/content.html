Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Linear Narrative
Definition & Use
Fixed arc for polished experience
Best when consistent timing and precise emotional beats are required. Designers can polish cinematics and ensure all players see the same story moments.
Strengths
  • Precise pacing and intended emotional impact.
  • Consistent story experience across players.
Limits
  • Less long-term player agency and replayability.
  • Risks of illusory choice if options do not change outcomes.
Key Takeaway
Add micro-agency or environmental variation to maintain player involvement while preserving a single crafted arc.
Scenario Card
Haunted Mansion — Linear
Player finds key, enters main hall, meets a cryptic ghost, solves a timed puzzle, and experiences a final reveal. The sequence and pacing are identical for all players.
Design Pro-Tip
Allow player approach choices that change tension and pacing even if they end at the same narrative checkpoint.
Misconception Check
Linear does not equal passive. Intentional micro-choices can preserve agency and still support a single arc.
