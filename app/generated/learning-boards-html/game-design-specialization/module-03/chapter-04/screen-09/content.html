Process Flow

Structure choices for voice and consequence

Design each node with: character line, player choices labeled by intent, immediate reaction text, and a flag or score change that influences later scenes.
Compact dialogue sketch
NPC: "You should not have come here."  
Choice A (Soothing): "I only want to help."  → NPC reacts: softens; Relationship +1; unlocks helper quest.  
Choice B (Accuse): "You caused this." → NPC reacts: defensive; Relationship -1; blocks companion scene.  

Note: use a single small flag for long-term branching and immediate reaction lines for feedback.
Guidelines
  • Keep choices intelligible by intent, not by exact outcome text.
  • Provide immediate, visible feedback so the player senses consequence.
  • Use a simple relationship metric to gate content and unlock paths.
Misconception
Choices must immediately alter endings. Small, believable consequences often feel more real and let players form relationships over time.
