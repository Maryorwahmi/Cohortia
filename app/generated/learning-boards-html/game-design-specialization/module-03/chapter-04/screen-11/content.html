Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Motivation & Exploration
Core Concept

Layer rewards to encourage curiosity

Combine sensory hints, mechanical benefits, and narrative value so exploration is both fun and meaningful.

Sensory
Visual or audio hooks that spark curiosity.
Mechanical
Upgrades, items, or shortcuts as rewards.
Narrative
Lore, character beats, or world revelations.
Practical tactic
Place three hints in a level and ensure one leads to a small, satisfying payoff. Track whether players notice and pursue these hints in playtests.
Key Takeaway
Curiosity is stoked by promise. Deliver at least one meaningful payoff to strengthen trust and encourage further exploration.
