Architecture

Shared artifacts and review cadence

Use a one-line theme, a level brief, and prototype loops as shared artifacts. Review at concept, prototype, and polish to stop misalignment early.

Artifact: one-line theme
A single sentence everyone refers to when making decisions.
Artifact: level brief
Beat, three clues, mechanic, and desired emotional response.
Practical tip
Annotate maps with narrative callouts and keep a short checklist to confirm each area supports the theme before art is finalized.
Key Takeaway
Early shared artifacts and frequent reviews reduce rework and keep narrative and mechanics aligned.
