Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Haunted Lighthouse
Process Flow

From single sentence to playable space

Beat
A keeper's grief turned the lighthouse into a place between worlds.
Three environmental clues
  • Diary pages describing isolation
  • Broken telescope aimed at a sunken ship
  • Rusting lantern gear with carved symbols
Core mechanics
  • Light control that reveals ghosts
  • Sound-detection to locate whispers
Choice & consequence
Decision example: appease the ghost or attempt banishment. Immediate result: ghost reacts differently during the encounter. Long-term result: certain rooms unlock or remain sealed, affecting exploration and ending beats.
Common mistake
Trying to include every mechanic or clue. Better: pick a single beat and a small set of supporting features.
Key Takeaway

One beat + three clues + mechanics that act on that beat = cohesive space.

Try this
Write your beat in one sentence, then list three props that would communicate it silently.
