Comparison

Accidental vs deliberate dissonance

Accidental
A contradiction that arises from design mismatch. Fix by aligning mechanics, feedback, or narrative claims.
Deliberate
A purposeful mismatch used to provoke reflection. Use sparingly and with clear supporting systems.
Practical test
Run a short loop and answer: which player actions does the game reward most? Do those actions support or contradict your story sentence? If contradict, change one mechanic or adjust narration to resolve the gap.
Example
When gameplay encourages indiscriminate killing but narrative frames the player as a savior, players experience dissonance that weakens emotional beats.
Key Takeaway
Use dissonance deliberately and sparingly; otherwise, align rewards with narrative claims.
