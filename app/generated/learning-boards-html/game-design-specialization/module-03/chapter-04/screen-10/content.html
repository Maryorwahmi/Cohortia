Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Player Choice
Core Concept

Design immediate and lasting consequences

Structure choices into trivial, local, and systemic impacts to balance player agency and production scope.

Trivial
Flavor text or small cosmetic changes.
Local
Alters a scene or unlocks a short quest.
Systemic
Changes arcs, factions, or endings.
Example pattern
Design 2–3 systemic nodes across your game and many local nodes. Show systemic traces: notes, NPC references, or locked content that later reveals the long-term consequence.
Key Takeaway
Prioritize clarity of consequence: players must sense both the immediate reaction and the later significance of choices.
