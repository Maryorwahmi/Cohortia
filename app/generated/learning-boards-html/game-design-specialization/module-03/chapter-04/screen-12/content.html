Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Pacing & Scope
Process

Map junctions and limit systemic branches

Identify where narrative must change because of player choice and mark only a few of these as systemic. Use templates and procedural techniques for optional content to avoid hand-crafting every branch.
Pacing tactics
Alternate play segments with short interactive beats, keep exposition concise, and use in-world discovery instead of long cutscenes.
Scope controls
Limit systemic branches, use quest templates, and schedule cross-discipline reviews.
Practical checklist
  • Mark systemic nodes (2–3 max).
  • Template repetitive content like side-quests or environmental props.
  • Use urgency or gating to keep main plot moving in open worlds.
Common mistake
Assuming every player will see every branch. Design for the most likely behavior and provide satisfying payoffs for others.
