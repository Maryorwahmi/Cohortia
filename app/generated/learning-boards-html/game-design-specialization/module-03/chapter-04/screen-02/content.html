Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Learning Objectives
01
Weave narrative into mechanics

Describe how actions, rewards, and constraints can reinforce a theme so the player's play itself becomes storytelling. This matters because congruence increases immersion and emotional impact.

Mnemonic: mechanics = theme mirror
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Environmental storytelling

Apply visual details, props, and architecture to imply history and character motives without explicit exposition, increasing player curiosity and discovery.

Mnemonic: leave clues, not answers
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Dialogue & meaningful choice

Design dialogue that reveals voice and advances relationships, and create decisions with discernible immediate or long-term consequences to protect player agency.

Mnemonic: voice + consequence
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Balance scope and pacing

Identify pacing points and scope risks, and propose trade-offs so narrative depth does not overwhelm development or player engagement.

Mnemonic: small scope, strong beat
Verified Competency Milestone
