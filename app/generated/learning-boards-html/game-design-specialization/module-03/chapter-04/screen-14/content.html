Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Integration
Mechanics, world, and characters must consistently express the game's theme so play becomes the story.
Environmental Storytelling
Use curated props and architecture to communicate history and mood without explicit exposition.
Mechanics & Theme
Design a core mechanic that repeatedly places players in situations that express the central theme.
Summary Takeaway
Pick one theme sentence for your level, place three curated clues, and choose a mechanic that makes discovering those clues feel purposeful.
