Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Integration
Core Concept

Design for congruence

Treat narrative as a set of constraints and invitations that mechanics must satisfy or deliberately challenge.

Definition
Integration = gameplay + environment + characters aligned around a theme.
Common misconception
Adding story on top of mechanics is enough. Instead design choices and systems to express the story.
Key Takeaway
Ask of every feature: does it confirm, remain neutral, or contradict the theme?
Practical alignment checklist
  • Write theme in one sentence.
  • List mechanics, level beats, and NPCs, then tag each as confirm/neutral/contradict.
  • For contradicts, choose: change mechanic, justify narratively, or accept deliberate dissonance for theme.
Concrete example
Theme: survival against a harsh planet. Mechanic: scarce resources and crafting. Result: players' daily choices and stakes reflect the narrative of desperation, reinforcing story through play.
Check your understanding
Name a contradiction in a game you've played. Would you change the mechanic or the story to resolve it?
Pro tip
Small changes to feedback, item descriptions, or NPC lines often fix perceived contradictions without major rework.
