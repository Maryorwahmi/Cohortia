HUD Design and Prioritization
What information must always be visible?
Prioritization steps
  1. Identify immediate player goals per gameplay mode.
  2. Mark data required within two seconds for decisions.
  3. Design hierarchy: primary, secondary, hidden.
  4. Integrate feedback into primary elements.
Example: In combat, health and ammo are primary. Quest text and social feed become secondary and can be hidden or toggled.
HUD layout cards
Primary
Health, ammo, crosshair, immediate objective
Secondary
Mini-map, buff timers, party status
Hidden/Expanded
Detailed stats, quest logs, inventory
Key Takeaway

Design HUDs to support split-second decisions. Prioritize and layer information, not everything at once.

Real-world check
Observe a new player in combat. If they look away to read info, simplify the HUD immediately.
