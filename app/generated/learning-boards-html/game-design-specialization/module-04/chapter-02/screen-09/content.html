Menus, Inventory and Onboarding
Logical grouping, quick actions, and in-context tutorials
Menu design rules
  • Group related controls and keep action locations consistent.
  • Provide search, filter and sorting for large lists.
  • Offer clear feedback on selection and confirmation.
Example: an equip action that always appears in the same spot with a count of inventory items beside it.
Onboarding best practices
  • Use progressive disclosure to teach one concept at a time.
  • Prefer in-world demonstrations to long text blocks.
  • Display contextual hints only when players struggle.
Misconception: Complete tutorials upfront are helpful. They often overwhelm and are skipped; embed learning in play instead.
Key Takeaway

Design menus and inventory for quick decisions. Teach mechanics progressively so players learn by doing.
