Process
Pacing plan
Pacing sequence
  1. Intro: teach controls with low risk.
  2. Build: introduce a new mechanic in a safe context.
  3. Peak: combine mechanics into a challenge.
  4. Rest: provide a safe zone with narrative or reward.
Example
A shooter opens with target practice, then a squad skirmish, then a quiet supply room before a miniboss.
Design checks
  • Does each challenge follow a learning moment?
  • Does each rest point offer a meaningful reward or narrative beat?
  • Do intensity rises feel earned instead of sudden?
Common mistake
Flat pacing: repeated identical encounters with no rising stakes or rest, which causes disengagement.
Improvement tip
Introduce a small puzzle or visual reward between similar combat rooms to break monotony.
