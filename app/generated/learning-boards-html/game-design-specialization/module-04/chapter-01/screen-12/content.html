Hands-on Activity
Deconstruction
Activity steps
  1. Play the level twice: first for feel, second for observation.
  2. Sketch a top-down map with flow arrows and annotations.
  3. Mark one chokepoint, one safe zone, and three breadcrumb cues.
  4. Propose one minimal improvement and justify it.
Starter template
Use colored arrows and simple symbols: green arrows for flow, red X for chokepoints, blue circles for safe zones.
Reflection prompts
  • Where did the level teach you something without text?
  • Where did you feel lost, and why?
  • Which object told the strongest story and what did it say?
Key Takeaway
Regularly deconstructing existing levels trains your eye to spot design patterns you can reuse or improve.
