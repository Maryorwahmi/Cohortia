Process Flow
Design steps
Step 1 — Define the route
Sketch the intended path and primary objectives. Keep it clear and testable.
Step 2 — Add strong landmarks
Use lighting, distinctive geometry, or color contrast to create anchors that draw attention.
Step 3 — Breadcrumbs
Place repeated small cues—lights, sounds, or props—that confirm direction without forcing it.
Teaching visual: flow checklist
  • Is the goal silhouetted or highlighted?
  • Do landmarks form a readable sequence?
  • Are there repeated cues that confirm choices?
  • Have you removed competing visual noise?
Example
A path lit by torches dims behind the player and brightens ahead, guiding movement without arrows.
Misconception
Avoid replacing level cues with constant UI markers; players should discover direction through design.
