Definition
Environmental Storytelling
Principles
  • Choose one micro-story per area.
  • Place 2–3 high-significance props that support the story.
  • Use lighting and sound to direct attention and mood.
Concrete example
A ruined library with toppled shelves and a single intact child's book implies a sudden evacuation and personal loss.
Guided exercise

Pick a room and decide its story in one sentence. Then select two props and choose a lighting treatment that emphasizes one prop as the narrative focus.

Why it matters
Players discover story through exploration, creating emotional resonance and deeper immersion.
Misconception
More detail is not always better; irrelevant clutter dilutes narrative clarity.
