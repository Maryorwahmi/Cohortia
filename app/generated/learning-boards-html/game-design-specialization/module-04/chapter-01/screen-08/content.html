Core Concept
Challenge balance
Difficulty hierarchy
Design a progression that teaches, reinforces, and then tests. Use one mechanic per learning step before combining them.
Example
Introduce a new enemy alone, then with easy allies, then in a hazardous arena.
Misconception
Assuming players will instantly adapt to combined mechanics without a teaching phase leads to unfair spikes.
Tuning checklist
  • Does each new mechanic have an isolated practice area?
  • Are resources placed to allow recovery but not trivialize challenge?
  • Do failure states teach how to improve rather than punish randomly?
Key Takeaway
Scaffold difficulty so the player grows competence and experiences meaningful success.
