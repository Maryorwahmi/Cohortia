Architecture
Structural elements
Chokepoints
Narrow passages that create controlled encounters; useful for dramatic moments but should have alternatives.
Safe Zones
Rest areas that reduce player stress and allow resource management or storytelling beats.
Rewards
Place rewards to reinforce exploration; balance visibility so they are found but feel earned.
Verticality
Multiple heights open tactical options and hidden areas that invite curiosity.
Combined example
A courtyard with a rooftop path (verticality) leads to a hidden chest (reward). An adjacent alley funnels enemies into a chokepoint that tests combat learned earlier, and a bench near the chest serves as a safe zone for narrative exposition.
