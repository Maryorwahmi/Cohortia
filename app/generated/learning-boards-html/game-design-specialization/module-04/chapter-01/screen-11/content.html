Common Mistakes
Before → After
Before: Ambiguous Navigation
Several similar doors and no landmarks cause player confusion and backtracking.
After: Clear Landmark
Remove one door, add a distinctive arch and a line of lanterns to confirm the intended path.
Before: Flat Pacing
Repeated identical combat rooms create boredom and repetitive play.
After: Varied Rhythm
Insert a small puzzle and a scenic rest area between combat rooms to restore rhythm and discovery.
Iterative fixing — one change at a time, followed by playtesting — is the fastest route to meaningful improvement without destabilizing the whole level.
