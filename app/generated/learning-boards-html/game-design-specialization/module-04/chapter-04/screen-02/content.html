Learning Objectives
Skills to define, analyze, and design environmental narrative.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define environmental storytelling
Explain how objects, architecture, light, and sound communicate narrative without dialogue.
Mnemonic: Venn of Visual, Audio, and Spatial cues.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify environmental techniques
Recognize object placement, architecture, lighting, sound, flora and fauna as narrative tools.
Mnemonic: Timeline of moment → artifact → mood.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Analyze examples
Break down how specific scenes communicate events and character through detail selection.
Mnemonic: Compare two scenes and list shared cues.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design a narrative scene
Create a small room or area that communicates a story with only environmental elements.
Mnemonic: 5 objects, 1 central theme.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
