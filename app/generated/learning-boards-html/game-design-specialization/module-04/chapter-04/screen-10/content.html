Comparison
Implicit vs. Explicit Narrative
Both methods have strengths; blend them to maximize discovery and clarity.
• Implicit: discovery, emotional ownership, rewards exploration.
• Explicit: clarity, ensures critical facts are understood.
• Blend: implicit clues plus minimal explicit confirmation for key stakes.
Key Takeaway

Decide which facts are necessary for gameplay and confirm them explicitly; leave texture and mood to implicit cues.

Scenario
Contaminated Planet Example
Environmental cues show ruin and mutation. Add a single explicit log explaining the source of contamination so players understand stakes and goals.
