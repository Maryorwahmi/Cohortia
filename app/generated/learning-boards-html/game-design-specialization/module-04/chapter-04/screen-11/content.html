Common Mistakes & Avoidance
Four frequent errors with practical fixes.
1. Clutter: prune unrelated props. Ask "Why is this here?"
2. Inconsistency: align environmental and explicit lore.
3. Over-subtlety: add minimal explicit confirmation for critical beats.
4. Hidden clues: place important items where players will naturally search.
Key Takeaway

Use the checklist and playtesting to ensure your environment communicates the intended story clearly and consistently.

Playtest Steps
  1. Observe what a fresh player notices first.
  2. Ask them to describe the scene in one sentence.
  3. Note missing or contradictory inferences.
  4. Iterate by pruning or emphasizing details, then re-test.
Designer Tip
If two players infer two different, plausible stories, ask which one is correct for gameplay and steer cues toward that reading.
