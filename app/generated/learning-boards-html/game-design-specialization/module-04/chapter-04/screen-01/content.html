Environmental Storytelling
Design choices that let players discover narrative through place.
This chapter shows techniques and trade-offs for telling stories with objects, architecture, light, sound, and living systems. The objective is to make a scene feel lived-in, hint at past events, and reward player curiosity without breaking game flow.

Narrative Tools
Architecture: scarred throne room signals fall from power.
Objects: overturned chair + broken sword = last stand.
Lighting: single candle draws eye to a ruined regalia.
Key Takeaway

Design every object and cue to serve the scene’s single story. If a detail doesn’t support that story, remove it.
