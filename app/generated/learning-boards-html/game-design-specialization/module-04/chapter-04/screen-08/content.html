Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Sound Design
Definition
Sound & Music
Sound fills emotional and temporal gaps that visuals alone may not communicate.
Audio Layers
  • Ambient: place identity and constant context.
  • Contextual: event-specific noises that hint at activity.
  • Musical: motifs that signal emotional beats.
Key Takeaway

Design an audio hierarchy so each sound serves narrative purpose and avoids conflicting cues.

Concrete Examples
Dripping Water in a Cave
Signals damp, abandoned place and guides attention to echoing spaces where clues might be found.
Distant Laughter in a Ruin
Hints at unseen inhabitants or memory, adding tension or melancholy depending on musical context.
Motif: A Lullaby
A recurring theme tied to a character can convert visual clues into emotional understanding.
Misstep
Layering too many competing sounds makes the narrative fuzzy. Always ask what each sound reveals about the scene.
