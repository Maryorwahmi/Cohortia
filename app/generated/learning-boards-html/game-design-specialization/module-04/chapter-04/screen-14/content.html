Design for Clarity — Assessment
Use the prompts below to test your ability to craft legible environmental narratives.
Question 1
Design three environmental details to show a patient obsessed with a shadow figure, without dialogue. Explain how each detail communicates obsession.
Model Answer
Repeated drawings of the figure; a worn open book on sleep disorders; defensive furniture placement. These indicate fixation, research, and paranoia.
Question 2
Explain the potential drawbacks of relying only on environmental storytelling and give an example where explicit text is necessary.
Key Takeaway

Assess both creation and critique: can learners design scenes that communicate reliably and decide when to add minimal explicit cues?
