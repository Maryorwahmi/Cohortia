Definition
Flora, Fauna & Ecosystem Clues
Living elements indicate time, hazard, or supernatural alteration and help players infer broader events.
• Overgrowth = long abandonment.
• Mutated species = environmental or magical corruption.
• Fresh nests/tracks = recent presence or activity.
Key Takeaway

Choose living details that scale from silhouette to close inspection and support—never contradict—the scene’s story.

Concrete Use Cases
Glowing Flora
Suggests strange energy or magic localized to an area; pair with warnings to imply danger.
Mutated Fauna
Indicates spreading contamination or experimental side effects, raising stakes for exploration.
Overgrown Furniture
Makes abandonment legible and rewards close inspection for hidden details.
Misstep
Introducing too many different biological cues can suggest multiple, conflicting causes. Keep the ecosystem’s story coherent.
