Comparison
Fortified Castle vs. Ornate Hall
Two building types communicate very different histories. Fortifications tell of conflict and defense; ornamentation tells of wealth and status.
Fortified Castle
Thick walls, narrow windows, scorch marks, and reinforced gates suggest long-term threats and defensive priorities.
Ornate Hall
High ceilings, decorative moulding, and ceremonial spaces indicate wealth, ritual, or power concentrated among elites.
Key Takeaway

Design structures to state who occupied them and what threats or priorities shaped their form; align props and wear to reinforce that history.

Design Checklist
  • Silhouette speaks fast: unique rooflines or towers convey culture.
  • Scale implies resources and priorities: bigger means more investment.
  • Damage patterns should be consistent, not contradictory.
  • Combine architectural hints with props for deeper reading.
Scenario
A palace with scorch marks and missing banners tells a different story than a palace with intact banners—add small props to indicate whether the departure was sudden or ceremonial.
