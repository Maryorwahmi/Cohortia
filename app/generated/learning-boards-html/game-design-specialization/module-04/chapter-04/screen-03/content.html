Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Practice & Assessment
Practice steps
Step 1
Choose a single clear story
Limit scope: one event or emotional beat.
Step 2
Pick 5–7 distinct elements
Each object must communicate a specific detail.
Step 3
Sketch layout and sightlines
Place key clues where players naturally look.
Assessment tasks
  • Describe three details that show obsession in a patient’s room and explain their narrative role.
  • Explain when explicit text or logs are required to prevent confusion.
Key Takeaway

Practice focused scenes and explicit checks reveal whether your cues communicate the intended story to average players.
