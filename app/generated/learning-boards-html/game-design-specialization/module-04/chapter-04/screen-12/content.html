Design a Narrative Scene
Use the steps below to craft and test a single-room story with no dialogue.
Step 1
Choose a story
Example: scientist flees failed experiment.
Step 2
List 5–7 elements
E.g., scorched console, overturned stool, bloodied glove, half-packed bag, warning sign.
Step 3
Justify each choice
Explain what each item reveals about the event.
Step 4
Place a focal object and align lighting to attract attention there.
Step 5
Decide minimal explicit confirmation if gameplay requires clarity.
Step 6
Playtest: ask a peer to summarize the scene in one sentence and iterate.
Key Takeaway

A quick design-justify-playtest loop reveals whether your chosen elements convey the intended narrative to most players.
