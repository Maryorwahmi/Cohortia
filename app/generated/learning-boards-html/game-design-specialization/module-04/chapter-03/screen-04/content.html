Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › What is Prototyping?
Definition

Prototyping is creating early, simplified versions of level spaces and mechanics to test specific gameplay questions quickly and cheaply.

Why it matters
  • Validates core mechanics before art investment.
  • Reveals scale and flow issues through direct play.
  • Enables faster team communication and alignment.
Key Takeaway

Treat prototypes as experiments with one clear question. If it fails, you learn. If it succeeds, you refine and add detail.

Concrete example
Example: Use cubes to lay out an arena, mark cover in red, elevated platforms in blue, then run a quick player run to test flank routes.
Analogy: A paper sketch for architecture — it shows circulation and proportions without finishes.
Common misconception: ‘‘I will wait for art to judge flow.’' Waiting increases cost of changes; test now with simple shapes.
Consider: what is the single player action you want to test in your next prototype?
