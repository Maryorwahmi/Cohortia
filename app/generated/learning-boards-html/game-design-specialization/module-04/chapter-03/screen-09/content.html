Common operations
  • Additive box: create rooms and platforms quickly.
  • Subtractive brush: carve doorways and holes without external modeling.
  • Extrude: extend faces into platforms or ramps.
  • Transform tools: move, rotate, and scale primitives to refine layout.
Check collision and navmesh early when using subtraction; they affect AI and traversal.
Practical example
Create a room with an additive box, then place a smaller subtractive box to carve a doorway. Add two low cover cubes and color them red.
Result: functional room ready for immediate movement tests and sightline checks.
Practice goal: be able to block out a small arena in under 20 minutes.
