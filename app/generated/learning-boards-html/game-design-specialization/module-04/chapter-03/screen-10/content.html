Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Common Pitfalls
Top mistakes
  • Over-detailing before layout is stable.
  • Relying on verbal feedback without observation.
  • Insufficient or biased playtesting.
  • Designing in isolation from other disciplines.
  • Feature creep during iteration.
Prevention checklist
Keep prototypes untextured until layout is approved.
Pair verbal notes with recorded observations.
Limit each iteration to one measurable hypothesis.
Invite at least one cross-discipline reviewer per test.
Key Takeaway

Keep iteration fast by preventing scope expansion and by basing changes on observed play, not assumptions.
