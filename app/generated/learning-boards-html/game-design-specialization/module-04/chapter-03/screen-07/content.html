Playtest recipe
  1. Define a single question to test.
  2. Choose blind or guided mode depending on clarity vs. objective testing.
  3. Observe behaviour: hesitation, repeated attempts, preferred routes.
  4. Ask open-ended follow-ups after observation.
  5. Prioritize one change and one quick tweak for the next iteration.
Observation cards
Hesitation point: where players stop and look around for cues.
Failure cluster: repeated attempts that indicate a mechanical or scale problem.
Ignored element: objects players never interact with, revealing poor affordance.
Common mistake
Ignoring observed behavior and leaning on what testers say. Always reconcile words with actions.
