Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Learning Objectives
01
Explain prototyping importance

Describe why fast, low-detail builds reveal core gameplay issues early and save art work. You will learn to prioritize mechanics over visuals during early stages.

Mnemonic: BUILD (Baseline, Iterate, Learn, Deploy)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe blockout stages

Identify blockout, greybox, and whitebox phases and their roles in testing scale, sightlines, and movement before art is applied. You will learn terminology and purpose.

Mnemonic: SCALE (Space, Circulation, Landmarks, Encounters)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Use editor blockout tools

Gain hands-on familiarity with ProBuilder or BSP brushes to place primitives, extrude faces, and carve geometry rapidly so you can test designs in-editor.

Mnemonic: TOOLS (Templates, Operations, Orientation, Light testing, Scale)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Run and analyze playtests

Design short playtests, ask open questions, observe behavior, and convert observations into focused iteration tasks rather than immediate fixes.

Mnemonic: TEST (Tell, Observe, Survey, Tweak)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
