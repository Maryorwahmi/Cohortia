Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Blockout Activity
Activity brief
  • Central open arena for combat.
  • At least three distinct cover points.
  • At least two elevated positions reachable by ramp or jump.
  • Clear entrance and exit.
  • Use only cubes, planes, and cylinders with simple colors.
Deliverable: a screenshot and a short note describing one prioritized iteration after a self-playtest.
Starter template (conceptual)
// Starter conceptual steps
// 1. Create floor: Plane or cube scaled to 30x1x30 units.
// 2. Place three low cubes as cover (e.g., 3x1x2 units) around the center.
// 3. Add two stacked cubes or a platform (e.g., 5x2x5 units) for elevated positions.
// 4. Make a ramp or small jump platform to reach elevation.
// 5. Define entrance/exit with openings in the walls or ramps.
// 6. Color-code: Floor=Light Grey, Cover=Red, Elevated=Blue.
After building, playtest focusing on movement and cover usage. Record one issue and one iteration.
