Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Blockout Stages
Stage steps
  1. Blockout: rough primitives to test scale and paths; ask ‘‘Can the player move as intended?''
  2. Greybox: tighter geometry for cover, ledges, and sightlines; verify movement and encounters.
  3. Whitebox: gameplay-ready geometry and collision behavior without final art; ensure AI and traversal work.
Tip: One question per build keeps iterations short and measurable.
Visual checklist
Scale checked with player capsule
Sightlines marked for combat encounters
Cover positions placed and tested
Elevations tested for access and advantage
Common mistake
Over-detailing too early slows iteration. If you need to move walls often, leave them untextured until the layout is stable.
Key Takeaway

Use staged fidelity to focus on the right questions: rough shapes first, precision later.
