Assessment alignment

Each objective maps to a clearly observable artifact. When you hand in a blockout, include a short note describing the single question you tested and what changed after the first iteration.

Milestone: Playable blockout with three cover points and two elevated positions.
Milestone: Playtest summary with at least two observed behavior notes and one prioritized adjustment.
