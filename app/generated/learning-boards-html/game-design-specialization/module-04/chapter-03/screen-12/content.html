Q1: Players stuck at a point

Likely issue: affordance or guidance failure in the blockout. First iteration: adjust the geometry or contrast of the intended interaction so it stands out without adding explicit hints. Verify by re-testing without instruction.

Do: change form or spacing. Don’t: immediately add UI hints or unrelated mechanics.
Q2: Why greybox early?

Greyboxing helps identify fundamental spatial or mechanical issues—impossible jumps, poor sightlines, or bad pacing—quickly and cheaply. Fixing these in a greybox avoids wasted artist time and costly rework later.

Example: a problematic jump can be resized in minutes with primitives instead of reworking modeled assets.
