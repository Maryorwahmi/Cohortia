Prioritize the top-left quadrant. Batch or defer low-impact items. Discuss high-effort items with stakeholders before committing.

Example iteration
Observation: Players miss a side route. Iteration: nudge a doorway by 1–2 units and re-test. Hypothesis: improved visibility increases route choice.
Observation: Jump failure rate is 60%. Iteration: reduce gap by 20% or add a small platform. Re-test jump success rate.
Document each change as a testable hypothesis and the metric you'll observe next.
