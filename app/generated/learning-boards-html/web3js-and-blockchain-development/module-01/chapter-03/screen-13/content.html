Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Challenges
Scalability
Blockchains can be limited in throughput and cost; Layer 2 and off-chain patterns help but introduce complexity.
User experience
Users must manage keys and approve transactions; onboarding and recovery flows are critical design work.
Regulatory landscape
Legal rules around tokens, custody, and data can affect architecture and operations of dApps.
Misconception
Decentralization does not mean absence of governance or legal responsibility; it changes who enforces rules and how.
Actionable guidance
Prototype with hybrid approaches, prioritize clear UX for signing and recovery, and keep regulatory constraints in mind when choosing custody and token models.
