Web3 technology stack
Blockchain
Shared ledger and consensus for verifiable state.
Smart Contracts
On-chain logic enforcing rules automatically.
Wallets
Cryptographic identity and signing capabilities.
dApps
Frontends that interact with contracts and wallets.
Decentralized Storage
Stores large content referenced by hashes on-chain.
Governance
Protocol rules, upgrades, and community decision-making.
Concrete guidance
Map features to layers: verifiable settlement on-chain, media in decentralized storage, UI polish in the frontend, and identity via wallets. Use hybrid designs to balance cost and performance.
Design question
Which parts of your application require verifiability and immutability, and which can remain off-chain for performance?
