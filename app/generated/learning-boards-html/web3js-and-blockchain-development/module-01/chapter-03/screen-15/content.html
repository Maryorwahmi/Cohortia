Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Smart Contracts
Self-executing rules on-chain; use for verifiable, high-value logic and plan for audits.
Decentralized Storage
Store files off-chain, keep hashes on-chain for integrity and reference.
Wallets
Hold keys and enable portable ownership; design recovery flows carefully.
Summary takeaway
Aim for pragmatic designs that use decentralization where it adds clear value, while keeping the user experience clear and reliable.
