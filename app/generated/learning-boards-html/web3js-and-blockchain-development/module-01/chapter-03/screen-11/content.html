This sequence applies to actions like posting content, transferring tokens, or interacting with a marketplace. Each step should surface status and any fees to the user.
Concrete example
Upload media to decentralized storage, get a content hash, call a smart contract method to publish the hash, sign the transaction in the wallet, then wait for confirmation before showing the post as published.
UX tip
Show pending states and confirmations clearly to avoid users resubmitting or thinking actions failed when they are simply awaiting network confirmation.
