IPFS and content hashing
IPFS stores content by hash. The blockchain stores hashes as proofs or references, not the heavy files themselves.
Concrete flow
Upload media to IPFS, receive a content hash, write that hash into a smart contract or metadata on-chain so that other dApps can verify the content's integrity.
Design checklist
  • Keep media off-chain; use IPFS or similar systems.
  • Store content hashes on-chain for verifiability.
  • Plan for pinning or hosting to ensure content availability.
Key takeaway
Use decentralized storage for files and the blockchain for proofs. This combination balances cost, performance and verifiability.
