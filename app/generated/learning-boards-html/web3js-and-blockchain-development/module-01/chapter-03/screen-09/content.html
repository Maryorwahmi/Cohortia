Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Web2 vs Web3
Login method
Web2 uses usernames and passwords. Web3 uses wallet authorization and signatures that prove control of an address.
Data ownership
Web2 platforms own user data; Web3 gives users portable ownership via keys and on-chain references.
Control & censorship
Web2 platforms can censor or de-platform. Web3 resists unilateral takedowns but governance and network constraints still exist.
Portability
Web2 data is often locked to a platform. Web3 profiles and assets are designed to move between frontends when tied to a wallet.
Practical question
Which trade-offs are acceptable for your users: the ease and polish of Web2, or the ownership and portability of Web3?
