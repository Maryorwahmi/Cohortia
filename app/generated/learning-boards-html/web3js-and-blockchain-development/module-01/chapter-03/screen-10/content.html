Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Social Media Example
Web2 social platform
  • Profile and posts stored on company servers.
  • Platform controls accounts, monetization, and moderation.
  • Switching platforms often loses your history and connections.
Web3 decentralized social graph
  • Profiles tied to wallet addresses; data referenced on-chain or in decentralized storage.
  • Users control identity and can move it between frontends.
  • Requires new models for moderation, discovery, and onboarding.
Concrete takeaway
Portability is powerful, but building a healthy social experience still requires thought about content moderation and user discovery that respects ownership.
Check your understanding
How would you index profiles in a decentralized social network while preserving portability? Consider trade-offs between central indexes and distributed discovery.
