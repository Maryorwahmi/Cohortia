Core Concept

Why consensus matters

Consensus is how a distributed network chooses which proposed updates become part of the single, accepted ledger history.

Agreement
Nodes follow rules to accept the same history and avoid forks that persist.
Fault tolerance
Consensus tolerates some dishonest or offline participants while ensuring a correct ledger.
Example families
Proof of Work and Proof of Stake are two common families of consensus mechanisms that define different selection and validation strategies.
Misconception
Consensus doesn't imply instant or identical finality across all networks; confirmation speed and finality guarantees vary by protocol.
Key Takeaway

Know the consensus guarantees of the network you target: they determine confirmation speed, resistance to attacks, and design choices for dApps.
