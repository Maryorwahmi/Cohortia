Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What to Remember
Check
Node distribution & consensus
These determine security, finality, and trust assumptions.
Remember
Hash chaining enforces immutability
Changing past data alters hashes and breaks later blocks.
Decide
On-chain only when shared verification matters
Otherwise prefer centralized or hybrid approaches for efficiency and privacy.
Reflection

As you build with Web3.js, ask whether immutability and shared verification solve a real trust problem for your application before committing data on-chain.
