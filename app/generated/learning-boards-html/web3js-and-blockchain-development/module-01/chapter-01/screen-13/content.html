Applications

Use cases beyond finance

Supply chain
Record provenance and verifiable steps from origin to retail, improving traceability.
Digital identity
Store attestations or public keys that users control, enabling portable identity without a central provider.
Records & contracts
Time-stamped, tamper-evident records can support audits and contract enforcement when parties share trust needs.
Concrete example
In healthcare, store a hash of consent or provenance on-chain and keep sensitive medical data off-chain, so verifiability is preserved without exposing private records.
Trade-off
Blockchain adds verifiability and shared trust but introduces coordination overhead, storage duplication, and potential latency compared with centralized databases.
Key Takeaway

Use blockchain when shared verification and tamper evidence add significant value to multi-party workflows; otherwise, centralized systems may be preferable.
