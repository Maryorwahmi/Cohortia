Myth vs Reality

Clarifying common confusions

Myth: Blockchain equals Bitcoin
Reality: Bitcoin is an application; blockchain is the ledger technology that supports many applications beyond currency.
Myth: Public = fully exposed identities
Reality: Transactions are visible but typically tied to pseudonymous addresses; personal data handling is a separate design choice.
Myth: Immutability makes change impossible
Reality: Immutability depends on cryptographic linking and network consensus; changing history requires overcoming those mechanisms.
Concrete guidance
Match the tech to the requirement. Use blockchain for shared, verifiable records; prefer private databases for fast, confidential internal workflows.
Misconception to watch
Avoid assuming decentralization removes all governance needs. Governance is still required to manage upgrades, access, and dispute resolution.
Key Takeaway

Separate the hype from the mechanics: blockchain brings verifiability and shared control, but also costs and governance responsibilities.
