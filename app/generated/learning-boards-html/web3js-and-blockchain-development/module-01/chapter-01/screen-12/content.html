Architecture

Nodes keep copies

Full nodes store the ledger, validate blocks, and help the network reach consensus. Redundancy means the system remains operational when individual nodes fail.

Full node
Stores a complete copy and validates rules independently.
Light client
Relies on proofs and headers rather than a full copy for efficiency.
Concrete scenario
Three independent nodes each hold the ledger. If one falls offline, the other nodes continue to validate and append blocks according to consensus rules.
Misconception
More nodes help, but node diversity matters more than raw count. Independent operation, geographic distribution, and honest participation influence real resilience.
Key Takeaway

Assess a network's resilience by looking at node distribution, operator independence, and the consensus rules that determine how many nodes must agree.
