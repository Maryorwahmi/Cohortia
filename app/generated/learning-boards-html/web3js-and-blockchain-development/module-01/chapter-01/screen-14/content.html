Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Blockchain
A decentralized, distributed, immutable ledger of cryptographically linked blocks.
Decentralization
Distribution of control across nodes, reducing single points of failure and unilateral control.
Immutability
Cryptographic linking makes altering past records detectable by the network.
Final takeaway

Use blockchain when shared verification and tamper evidence across multiple parties are essential; match the DLT architecture and consensus model to your application's needs.
