Account Type

Externally Owned Account (EOA)

Controlled by a private key; can sign transactions and initiate actions on-chain.

Control
Private key held by a user or wallet like MetaMask.
Capabilities
Sign and send transactions; pay gas; call contracts.
Account Type

Contract Account

Code stored at an address; executes when invoked by transactions or other contracts. No private key.

Control
Governed by its code; cannot sign transactions.
Capabilities
Hold ETH, update internal storage, and respond to calls with logic.
Key takeaway

Secure EOAs with good key management and secure contract code to protect contract accounts from logic bugs and exploits.
