Learning Objectives

These objectives map to practical skills you'll verify before writing Web3.js code.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain Ethereum vs Bitcoin
Describe Ethereum's programmability and why it's called a 'world computer'. This skill matters when choosing platforms for dApp ideas.
Mnemonic: ledger vs computer
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand the EVM
Explain deterministic execution and the role of bytecode. This helps you reason about contract behavior across nodes.
Mnemonic: CPU for the blockchain
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Define Gas and transaction cost
Explain gas units, gas limit, and gas price and why they determine your transaction cost and speed.
Mnemonic: fuel meter
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Differentiate account types
Know EOAs versus contract accounts and why private keys control one and not the other.
Mnemonic: person vs program
