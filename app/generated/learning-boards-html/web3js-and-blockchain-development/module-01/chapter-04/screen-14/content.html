Key Concepts
Ethereum
A programmable blockchain for deploying smart contracts.
Gas
Unit of computation; paid in ETH to process transactions.
EVM
Deterministic runtime executing contract bytecode across nodes.
Smart Contract
Immutable code on-chain enforcing rules and state changes.
EOA
Externally Owned Account controlled by a private key; used to sign transactions.
Contract Account
Account controlled by deployed code; no private key.
Takeaway

These concepts determine how you design, deploy, and pay for dApp functionality with Web3.js.
