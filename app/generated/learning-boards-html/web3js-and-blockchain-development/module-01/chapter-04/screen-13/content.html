Designing for Gas Efficiency

Small design choices can have large effects on gas cost; prioritize efficient storage and minimal on-chain work.

Storage patterns
Use mappings and pack variables to reduce storage expansion.
Off-chain computation
Compute heavy tasks off-chain and submit only verified results to contracts.
Events vs storage
Emit events for indexing and analytics rather than storing large logs on-chain.
Key takeaway

Evaluate what truly needs consensus; keep that small to reduce gas and make your application economically viable.
