Definition

Gas, Gas Limit, Gas Price

Gas quantifies computation; gas limit prevents runaway execution and gas price signals how quickly you want miners/validators to include the transaction.

Gas unit
Cost per opcode executed.
Gas limit
Maximum gas allowed for the transaction.
Gas price
ETH paid per gas unit; determines priority.
Concrete example
If a deployment consumes 3,000,000 gas and you set a price, the cost equals gas used times gas price; set a safe gas limit above expected use to avoid revert.
Practical notes
Use Web3.js gas estimation helpers, check network congestion, and test on a testnet to get realistic numbers before spending real ETH.
Common mistake
Underestimating gas limit causes reverts; failing to adjust gas price during congestion causes long delays.
Key takeaway

Treat gas as a deliberate budget: estimate, test, and set limits and prices to balance cost and speed.
