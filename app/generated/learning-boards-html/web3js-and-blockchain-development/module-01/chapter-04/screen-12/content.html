Common Mistake

Gas, Immutability, and Testing

Three recurring pitfalls that lead to failed transactions, lost funds, or permanent bugs on-chain.

Gas errors
Underestimates cause reverts; wrong price causes delays. Estimate and set safe margins.
Immutability
Deployed code is permanent. Test, audit, and design upgrade patterns before deploying.
Skip testnet
Testing only on mainnet is risky and expensive. Use testnets and faucets to iterate safely.
Action plan

Estimate gas, design for upgrades, and validate flows on testnets to reduce risk and cost when moving to mainnet.
