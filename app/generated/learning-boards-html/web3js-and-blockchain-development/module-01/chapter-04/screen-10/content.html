Hands-on Activity (Conceptual)

MetaMask & Testnet Setup

Follow the conceptual steps to prepare a wallet and testnet environment for contract deployment and interaction.

Step 1
Install wallet and create EOA. Secure seed phrase offline.
Step 2
Switch to a testnet like Sepolia or Goerli for development.
Step 3
Request test ETH from a faucet for your testnet address.
Step 4
Observe balance and practice signing transactions on testnet before mainnet.
Common mistake
Using mainnet for early tests or exposing seed phrases; this risks loss of funds and keys.
Why it matters

Testnets let you simulate real transactions and gas usage without economic risk, building confidence before mainnet deployment.
