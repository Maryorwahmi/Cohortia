Comparison

Bitcoin: Ledger-first

Designed primarily for secure peer-to-peer transfers and store of value. Scripting is limited and focused on transaction validation.

Primary use
Value transfer and censorship-resistant money.
Execution model
Limited script for validation, not a general runtime.
When to choose
Simple payments and store-of-value applications.
Why it matters

Choosing a platform affects what logic can run on-chain and how much it costs. Programmability comes with complexity and gas costs.

Comparison

Ethereum: Programmable platform

Runs deterministic smart contract bytecode on the EVM so developers can build dApps with on-chain logic and state.

Primary use
Deployable programs and decentralized applications.
Execution model
EVM executes bytecode deterministically across nodes.
When to choose
Applications requiring enforced on-chain rules, transparency, and composability.
Key takeaway

Use Ethereum when your application needs on-chain enforcement or interoperable on-chain state; otherwise prefer simpler ledgers.
