Practical Note
Private key safety and wallet management
Why you must never share a private key and how wallets help
Risks of exposure
  • If leaked, an attacker can sign transactions and steal funds.
  • Copying keys into scripts or web forms is a common leak vector.
  • Single backups without redundancy risk permanent loss.
Best practices
Use hardware wallets for high-value accounts, encrypted offline backups, and wallet software that handles signing so your application never stores raw private keys.
Warning
Never provide your private key to a service. Legitimate wallets ask you to sign data, not hand over your key.
Key Takeaway
Protect private keys with secure storage and use wallets to minimize exposure during signing operations.
