Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › What to Remember
Protect private keys
Use secure wallets, hardware keys, and encrypted backups. Never paste keys into untrusted forms.
Verify integrity with hashes
Recompute hashes after any edit to detect tampering; small input changes yield large digest changes.
Treat signatures as evidence
Signatures prove origin and integrity, but their value depends on key custody and secure procedures.
Final Takeaway

Combine hashing and public-key signatures to ensure both the integrity and authority of blockchain transactions, and protect your private keys to preserve that trust.
