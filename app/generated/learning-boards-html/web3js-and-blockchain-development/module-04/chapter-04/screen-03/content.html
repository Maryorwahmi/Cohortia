Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Learning Objectives
Practical Steps
Distinguish test flows from production patterns and perform safe transaction operations.
Test: Local signing
Use Ganache private keys to sign and inspect receipts while learning.
Production: Wallet signing
Delegate signing to MetaMask or other wallets; avoid embedding private keys.
Gas strategy
Estimate gas, add a 10–20% buffer, and choose gasPrice sensibly for confirmation speed.
Key Takeaway

Use local signing for development and wallet-based signing for live dApps. Always fetch pending nonces and use estimateGas with a small buffer.
