Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Troubleshooting
Error: Insufficient funds
Compare balance to value + gasLimit * gasPrice. If short, fund the account or lower value/gas settings.
Error: Nonce issues
Fetch web3.eth.getTransactionCount(sender, 'pending') to include unmined transactions and avoid reuse.
Error: Gas limit too low
Use estimateGas, add buffer, and re-send. Out-of-gas consumes fees and reverts state changes.
Error: Transaction reverts
Check contract require statements, simulate with call(), and inspect logs or revert reasons in your node provider.
Practical troubleshooting pattern
  1. Reproduce locally on Ganache to iterate fast.
  2. Log nonce, estimated gas, gasPrice, and balances before signing.
  3. Read the receipt and error message, then adjust values and re-send.
