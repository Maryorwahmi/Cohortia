Gas, Gas Price, Gas Limit
How these values determine cost and behavior for transactions.
Gas
A unit per EVM operation. More complex calls use more gas.
Gas Price
Wei paid per gas unit. Higher price speeds inclusion on congested networks.
Gas Limit
Maximum gas to spend; prevents runaway execution and caps your cost.
Example calculation
If estimated gas is 21000 and gasPrice is 20 Gwei: fee = 21000 * 20 Gwei. You pay gasUsed, which the receipt reports.
Common mistakes & tips
  • Setting gasLimit too low leads to out-of-gas and reverts while still consuming gas.
  • Setting gasPrice too low can delay mining on busy networks.
  • Adding a 10–20% buffer to estimated gas prevents most edge-case failures.
Key Takeaway

Use estimateGas to find needed gas, add a small buffer, and set gasPrice based on desired confirmation speed.
