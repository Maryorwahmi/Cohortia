Learning Objectives
Skill-focused milestones for sending transactions and managing gas.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Construct & sign transactions
Assemble the raw transaction fields and sign with a private key or wallet to authenticate the sender.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain gas concepts
Describe gas, gas price, and gas limit, and how they determine execution cost and speed.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Estimate gas accurately
Use web3.eth.estimateGas and add a safety buffer to avoid out-of-gas failures.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Broadcast & troubleshoot
Send signed transactions, inspect receipts, and resolve nonce or gas related errors.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
