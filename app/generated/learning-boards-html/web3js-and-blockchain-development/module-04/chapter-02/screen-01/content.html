Accounts, Keys, and Wallets
A concise orientation for developers: what to generate, what to trust, and what to delegate.
Why this chapter matters

Understanding key ownership and proper signing flows prevents catastrophic losses and keeps users' private keys safe. This chapter pairs practical Web3.js examples for local testing with production-safe integration patterns using browser wallets.
