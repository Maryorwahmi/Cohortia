Write Operation
A state-changing call that consumes gas and requires a signed tx.
encodeABI
Encodes function selector and params into tx data used by the EVM.
estimateGas
Call-specific gas estimate; add a small buffer to compute gasLimit.
Summary

Always encode function calls, estimate gas per method, sign and send safely, and check receipt.status before updating state.
