Best Practices Error handling & UX

Error handling, retries, and UX for reverts

Patterns to diagnose failures and keep users informed without introducing risk.

Developer checklist
  • • Wrap sendSignedTransaction in try/catch and log error.message.
  • • Check for 'reverted', 'insufficient funds', 'nonce too low' in messages.
  • • On revert, re-query contract state instead of assuming change.
UX guidance
  • • Show transaction hash immediately so users can track progress.
  • • Distinguish signature rejection from network errors and contract reverts.
  • • Never optimistically show success; wait for receipt.status true.
Common mistake

Automatically retrying a transaction without checking its on-chain status can cause duplicate effects or nonce clashes. Design retries with care and always confirm the txHash first.
