Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Block
A unit containing transactions and metadata; request full transactions only when needed.
Transaction
Use getTransaction to read fields; null blockNumber means pending or unknown.
Events & Subscriptions
Subscribe via WebSocket for headers, pending txs, and contract logs; filter by indexed params.
One-line takeaway
Choose efficient read patterns and the right provider to make your dApp live and responsive.
