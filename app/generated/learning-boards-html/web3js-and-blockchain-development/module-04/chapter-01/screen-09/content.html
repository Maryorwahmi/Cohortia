How to initialize Web3 with Infura
// Example using Infura
const infuraProvider = 'https://sepolia.infura.io/v3/YOUR_INFURA_PROJECT_ID';
const web3 = new Web3(infuraProvider);

// Then verify with isListening and getId
Keep your project ID in environment configuration; avoid shipping it inside client-side bundles that expose credentials.
Troubleshooting hosted connections
  • Authentication errors often mean a wrong or revoked project ID.
  • Rate limiting or blocked referrers can prevent responses; check the provider dashboard.
  • Hosted providers may require HTTPS; ensure protocol is correct.
Why it matters

Hosted providers enable production-grade access without node maintenance, but they shift trust and may require careful credential management.
