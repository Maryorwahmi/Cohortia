What to Remember › Practical Tips
Convert Units
Use fromWei or the token's decimals to display human-readable amounts.
Limit Event Queries
Use fromBlock/toBlock and filters to avoid provider rate limits and long waits.
Verify ABI & Address
Mismatched ABI or incorrect address is a leading cause of read failures; check them after deployment.
Final takeaway

Keep reads free and fast by using correct ABI/address, converting units, and designing efficient event queries.
