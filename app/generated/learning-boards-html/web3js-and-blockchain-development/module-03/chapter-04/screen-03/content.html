Lab-Focused Objectives

Tasks to complete in Remix and checkpoints to confirm behavior

Practical checkpoints
Build whitelist

Implement add, remove and isAddressWhitelisted methods and a constructor limit.

Trigger errors

Add beyond the max, add duplicate, and call as a non-owner to observe reverts or custom errors.

Measure gas effects

Notice that require and revert refund remaining gas, while assert consumes it on failure.
