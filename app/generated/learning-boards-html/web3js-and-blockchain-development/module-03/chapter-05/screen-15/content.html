Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Key Concepts
Compilation
Produces bytecode (deployed) and ABI (interface used by clients).
Deployment
A transaction that runs constructor once and stores the runtime bytecode at a new address.
Interaction
View/pure functions are free off-chain reads; state-changing calls require signed transactions and gas.
Summary takeaway

Keep ABI and deployed address together for Web3.js integration, test on local networks first, and protect keys. Update ABI after changes and always verify transactions via receipts and events.
