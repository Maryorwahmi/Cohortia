Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Common Mistakes
Mistakes to watch for
  • Compiler mismatch: Use the correct solc or Remix compiler version for your pragma.
  • Forgetting payable: Functions that should receive Ether must be marked payable or transactions will revert.
  • Gas limits: Test heavy operations locally; long loops or large writes are costly.
  • ABI mismatch: Update client ABI after redeploying changed contracts.
  • Private keys: Never expose them; use secure wallet providers or environment variables.
Developer guardrails
Automate ABI export, version your deployment addresses, and run gas profiling in a local network before public deployment.
