Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Quick Reference
Bytecode & ABI
Bytecode executes on-chain; ABI describes the interface used by Web3.js.
Local networks
JavaScript VM for ephemeral tests; Ganache/Hardhat for persistent and debuggable local chains.
Call types
View calls are free reads; state changes require signed transactions and gas.
Final takeaway

Keep ABI, bytecode, and address synchronized in your toolchain, test locally, and design your frontend to handle transactions and confirmations safely.
