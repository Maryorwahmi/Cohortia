What to Remember

Three concise, practical reminders to carry into your coding practice.

Pin the Compiler
Match the pragma and your build tools to avoid surprises in emitted bytecode and gas behavior.
Profile Gas
Measure gas for storage writes and optimize hot paths to reduce repeated costs.
Design for Immutability
Assume deployed code is permanent; add access controls and plan upgrades intentionally.
Closing question

Which of these three practical reminders will you implement in your next contract draft?
