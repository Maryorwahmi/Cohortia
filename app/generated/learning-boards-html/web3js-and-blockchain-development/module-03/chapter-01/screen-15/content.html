Key Concepts

Three concise concept cards to carry forward.

Solidity
High-level, contract-oriented language that compiles to EVM bytecode.
EVM
Deterministic runtime that executes bytecode identically across nodes.
Gas
Unit of computational effort; paid in Ether and enforces execution limits.
Final takeaway

Combine careful design, matching compiler versions, and gas-aware testing to build secure, efficient contracts.
