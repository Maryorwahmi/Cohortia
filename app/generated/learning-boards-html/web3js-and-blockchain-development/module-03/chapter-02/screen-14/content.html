Key Concepts

Value vs Reference
Copies vs pointers change semantics and gas. Choose with intent.
Type selection
Pick appropriate integer sizes, addresses and bytes for clarity and gas efficiency.
Mappings & patterns
Mappings are fast but non-iterable; maintain auxiliary structures only if iteration is required.
Summary takeaway

Design types and visibility with least privilege, test edge cases, and move heavy data off-chain to control gas and security.
