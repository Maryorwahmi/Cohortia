Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Learning Objectives
01
Emit and design events
Declare event signatures and use indexed parameters for efficient off-chain filtering.
Mnemonic: events are a guest book — they record who did what and when without inflating contract storage.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate calls and transactions
Practice read-only calls that cost no gas versus state-changing transactions that do.
Mnemonic: asking versus telling — queries neither cost gas nor change state; transactions do both.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Lab fluency
Deploy and test modifiers and events in Remix; observe logs and role-based behavior across accounts.
Mnemonic: practice deploy, test, observe logs — repeat until behavior is predictable.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design clear require messages
Write informative revert messages so callers and frontends can surface helpful errors.
Mnemonic: a good error message saves hours in debugging and improves UX.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
