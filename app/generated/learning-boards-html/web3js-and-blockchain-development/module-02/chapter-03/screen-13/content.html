Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Hands-on Estimation Activity
Steps
  1. Insert your Infura project ID into estimateGas.js and run node estimateGas.js.
  2. Record baseFeePerGas and the total estimated ETH for low and high priority tips.
  3. Repeat the script across several minutes and note variability.
  4. Increase gasLimit to model a complex contract call and compare totals.
Observation

Base fee will rise when blocks are fuller and fall when they're emptier. Priority tip changes estimate linearly by tip amount times gasUsed.

Reflection

Decide how you'll present changing estimates in your dApp: show ranges, display both base and tip breakdowns, and explain burning vs tips.
