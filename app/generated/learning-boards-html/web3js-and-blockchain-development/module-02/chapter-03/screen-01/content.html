Visual summary
Fuel, Price, and Burn
Gas quantifies work. gasLimit sets the cap, EIP-1559's base fee is burned, and priority fee tips validators. Learn to estimate costs and avoid common mistakes like underestimating gasLimit or mis-converting units.
Example
Simple ETH transfer
A standard transfer uses 21,000 gas. If base fee plus tip equals 100 Gwei, multiply gasUsed by that sum and convert from Wei to Ether to find ETH cost.
Why it matters
User experience and cost predictability
Designing gas-efficient contracts reduces user cost and failed transactions, which directly affects your dApp's adoption.
Quick guide
Start by reading the latest base fee on the network, set a sensible maxPriorityFee for speed, and always convert units with web3.utils.toWei/fromWei before computing costs.
