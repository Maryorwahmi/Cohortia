Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Gas-Efficient Design
Patterns to reduce gas
  • Minimize storage writes: Storage is expensive; cache or batch writes.
  • Choose efficient structures: Mappings for lookup, avoid unbounded loops.
  • Move heavy work off-chain: Compute off-chain and verify on-chain where possible.
Concrete scenario
Batch token processing

Process multiple items in a single transaction to amortize fixed gas overhead, but watch block gas limits to avoid failures.

UX guidance

Show estimated gas and cost ranges, offer a safety toggle for higher gas limits, and explain why a transaction might fail with out-of-gas rather than blaming the network.

Pro tip

Instrument contract functions with view-only estimation calls so the UI can present realistic gas and refund expectations before users sign.
