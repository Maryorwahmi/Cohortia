Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › What is Gas?
Definition

Gas is the unit that measures computation cost on the EVM. Each opcode and operation consumes gas, which users pay for to prevent abuse and reward validators.

Concrete example
Simple transfer

A standard ETH transfer uses 21,000 gas. More complex contract functions require more gas because they execute many operations.

Common mistake
Underestimating gasLimit

If gasLimit is too low, the transaction reverts but consumed gas is still paid. Always estimate with a buffer for complex calls.

Key Takeaway

Gas is a precise measure of computation. Design and estimate to avoid out-of-gas failures and unexpected user charges.

Analogy

Think of gas like car fuel: each operation consumes a fixed amount of fuel. Running heavy operations is like driving uphill with the air conditioning on — costs more fuel.

Operation cost
Storage writes

Writing to storage is significantly more expensive than arithmetic, so minimize unnecessary writes.

Operation cost
Read and compute

Reads and math are cheaper but still add up inside complex loops.
