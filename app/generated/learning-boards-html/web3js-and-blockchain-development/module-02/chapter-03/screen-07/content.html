Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Pre-EIP-1559 Auction
How it worked

Users bid a gasPrice. Miners prioritized highest bids. Fees were paid entirely to miners, and congestion led to bidding wars.

Example
Spike during congestion

When blocks filled, wallets recommended higher gasPrice, causing many users to overpay to get inclusion fast.

Downside
Unpredictability

Users couldn't reliably predict what price would be necessary for timely inclusion, harming UX.

Developer impact
  • Need for gas oracles and complex wallet logic.
  • Refunds or retries when transactions underpriced or pending indefinitely.
  • Difficulty explaining fees to users in a predictable way.
Reflective question: If your dApp relied on fast inclusion for UX, how did you protect users from paying excessive gasPrice in the auction model?
