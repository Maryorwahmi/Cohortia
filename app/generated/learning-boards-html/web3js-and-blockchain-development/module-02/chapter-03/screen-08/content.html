Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › EIP-1559 Components
Base fee

Network-set fee that adjusts with congestion and is burned. It stabilizes pricing by responding to block fullness.

Priority fee (tip)

Optional tip to validators that speeds inclusion. Unlike the base fee, this amount goes directly to the validator.

Misconception
Base fee is not a tip

People sometimes assume the base fee rewards validators; in reality it is burned, changing economic incentives.

Payment flow
  • maxFeePerGas: The most you're willing to pay per gas unit.
  • maxPriorityFeePerGas: Your tip ceiling to the validator.
  • actualPriorityFee: The lesser of maxPriorityFeePerGas and maxFeePerGas minus baseFee.
Key Takeaway

EIP-1559 makes fees more predictable and burns base fee; use maxFee and maxPriorityFee to protect users from sudden spikes.
