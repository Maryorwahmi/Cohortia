Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Key Concepts
Solidity
A contract-oriented language that compiles to EVM bytecode.
State Variable
A variable stored permanently on-chain as part of a contract's state.
Event
A log emitted by a contract for off‑chain listeners to detect actions or changes.
Final takeaway
Keep terminology precise: it shapes how you design contracts, integrate with front‑ends, and reason about immutability and security.
