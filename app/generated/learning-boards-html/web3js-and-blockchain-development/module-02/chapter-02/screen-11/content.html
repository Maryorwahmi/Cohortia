Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Inclusion & Confirmations
Timeline to finality
Inclusion in a block is the first milestone; confirmations increase confidence by reducing fork risk.
Signed
Transaction serialized and broadcast to mempool.
Included
A miner/validator includes it in a block that gets propagated.
Confirmed
Additional blocks build on top; each block is another confirmation.
Example
A transfer included in block A then followed by 12 more blocks is far less likely to be reversed than one with zero confirmations.
Misconception
Treating a single confirmation as irreversible; high-value operations often require many confirmations.
Practical guidance
Choose confirmation thresholds based on value and accept that waiting increases safety at the cost of latency.
Key Takeaway

Confirmations improve finality. Align confirmation counts with operational risk for your application.
