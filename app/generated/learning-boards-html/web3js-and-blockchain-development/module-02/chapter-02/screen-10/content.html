Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Mempool & Priority
Mempool lifecycle
Signed transactions live in node mempools until validators include them in a block or they are dropped or replaced.
Node mempool
A transient collection of pending transactions propagated across nodes.
Priority
Higher gasPrice transactions are more likely to be chosen by miners or validators.
Example
A low-fee transfer may sit in mempool for minutes or hours while higher-fee transactions are mined first.
Misconception
Assuming mempools are identical across nodes. Differences and network topology can change propagation and inclusion timing.
Practical tip
If a transaction is stuck, consider using replacement with a higher gas price while keeping the same nonce.
Key Takeaway

Fees determine priority. Monitor mempool behavior and use replacements to accelerate stuck transactions.
