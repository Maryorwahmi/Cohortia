Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Seed Phrases
Mnemonic seed phrases
A 12- or 24-word phrase encodes a master seed that deterministically derives private keys for multiple accounts.
Recovery
Entering the seed phrase into a compatible wallet recreates the same deterministic accounts and keys.
Example
A 12-word phrase written on paper and kept in two secure places allows recovery if a device is lost.
Misconception
Assuming that partial phrase exposure is harmless; any full phrase grants full control, partial phrases are still sensitive.
Practical advice
Write seed phrases on paper or metal, store them offline in multiple secure locations, and never enter them into unknown websites or apps.
Key Takeaway

Seed phrases are master keys. Treat them as the most sensitive credential and protect them offline.
