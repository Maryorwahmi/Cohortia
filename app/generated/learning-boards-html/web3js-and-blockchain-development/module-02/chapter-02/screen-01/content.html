Ethereum Accounts, Wallets, and Transaction Lifecycle
Understand how keys, wallets, and transactions interact to move value and execute logic on Ethereum.
In this lesson

You will trace a private key to an address, see how a wallet signs a transaction, learn each transaction field, and follow a signed transaction until it gains confirmations.

Why it matters

These mechanics are the foundation for secure dApp interactions, safe key management, and reliable transaction handling in development and production.
