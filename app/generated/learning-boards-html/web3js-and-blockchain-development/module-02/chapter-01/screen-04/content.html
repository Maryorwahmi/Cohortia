Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Account Model Comparison
Ethereum (Account-based)
State is a global mapping of addresses to account objects
  • Account object contains nonce and balance; contracts also have storageRoot and codeHash.
  • Transfers update balances directly and change the world state.
  • Ideal for persistent contract storage and stateful dApps.
Concrete example
Alice sends 1 Ether to Bob: Alice.balance decreases, Bob.balance increases, Alice.nonce increments, and the stateRoot changes to reflect the new world state.
Bitcoin (UTXO) — quick contrast
Transactions consume outputs and create new outputs
  • State is implicit through unspent outputs rather than a single global mapping.
  • Tracing funds follows outputs; updates are handled by spending and creating outputs.
  • Good for simple currency transfer semantics but more awkward for persistent contracts.
Misconception
People assume account models always simplify privacy or scaling. In practice, they change how you reason about ownership and state, but privacy and scaling are separate trade-offs.
Key takeaway
Account model is the foundation for contract state and the EVM’s in-place updates.
Try this
When designing data for a dApp, map entities to accounts or contract storage rather than UTXO outputs.
Check
Can a state change be represented as a direct update to an account object? If yes, account model fits well.
