Check contract payability
Confirm a contract has a payable fallback or function before sending native Ether to avoid locked funds.
Inspect block roots
Use transactionsRoot, stateRoot, and receiptsRoot to validate inclusion and observe final state after execution.
Merkle roots = commitments
A root commits to data but does not reveal it; use proofs to verify specific items against a known root.
