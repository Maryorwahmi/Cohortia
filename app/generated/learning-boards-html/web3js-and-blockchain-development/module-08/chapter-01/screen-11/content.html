Diagnose insufficient funds
  1. Check the public address derived from your PRIVATE_KEY.
  2. Confirm the balance with a quick RPC call or explorer lookup.
  3. If on Sepolia, use a faucet to request test ETH for that address.
  4. Re-run the deployment once funded.
Pro Tip

Log the deployer balance at the start of your script and optionally halt with a clear error if funds are insufficient.

Gas estimation checklist
  • Use Hardhat's estimate to preview gas usage.
  • Account for constructor complexity — large initial storage costs more gas.
  • Add a margin above estimates to avoid out-of-gas failures during network congestion.
