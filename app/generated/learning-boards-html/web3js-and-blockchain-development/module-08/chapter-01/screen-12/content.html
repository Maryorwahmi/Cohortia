Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Testnet vs Mainnet
Testnet benefits
  • Free test ETH from faucets for realistic trials.
  • Safe environment to catch constructor and logic errors.
  • Opportunity to test provider and script behavior under real network conditions.
Mainnet requirements
  • Rigorous audits and automated tests first.
  • Secure key management and possibly hardware or multi-sig signing.
  • Careful gas budgeting and review of immutable behaviors.
Key Takeaway

Always rehearse on testnets and only promote to mainnet after audits, secure signing, and a deployment checklist.
