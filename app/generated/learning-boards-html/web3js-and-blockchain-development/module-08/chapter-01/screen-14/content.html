Quick pre-deploy checklist
  • Compile succeeds and ABI/bytecode are available.
  • .env contains RPC URL and PRIVATE_KEY and is in .gitignore.
  • Deployer address matches the intended account and has enough ETH.
  • Constructor arguments are correct in type and order.
  • Script awaits myContract.deployed() and logs the resulting address.
  • Test the full flow on Sepolia and verify state before considering mainnet.
Key Takeaway

A short checklist and a testnet rehearsal catch most deployment errors and protect your mainnet assets.
