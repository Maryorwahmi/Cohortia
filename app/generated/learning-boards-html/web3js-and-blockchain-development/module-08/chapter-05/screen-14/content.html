Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Key Concepts
Performance Bottlenecks
Slowdowns mostly come from repeated RPCs or wide-range past-event scans.
Event Indexing
Listen to events and store them off-chain for fast historical queries.
Batching
Use BatchRequest or Multicall to combine reads and reduce HTTP requests.
Summary
Measure, pick one optimization, implement, then re-measure to ensure meaningful gains.
