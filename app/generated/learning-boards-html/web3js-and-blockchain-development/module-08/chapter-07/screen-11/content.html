Process Flow
Deposit → Mint → Withdraw
Deposit (L1 → L2)
1. Lock tokens in L1 deposit contract. 2. Bridge creates wrapped tokens on L2. 3. User receives wrapped balance.
Withdrawal (L2 → L1)
1. Burn wrapped token or call withdrawal on L2. 2. Bridge signals L1. 3. After challenge/proof, original tokens unlock on L1.
UX considerations
Surface status, expected wait times, and whether withdrawals are instant or delayed by a challenge window.
Watch events on both chains to provide users with progress updates and to detect completion or disputes.
Key Takeaway

Bridge flows require cross-chain monitoring and clear UX to handle delays and security checks during deposit and withdrawal.
