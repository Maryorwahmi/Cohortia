GameFi choice: ZK or Optimistic?
Consider user expectations: many microtransactions and the need for fast withdrawals point toward ZK-Rollups for better UX despite higher implementation cost.
If you need instant withdrawals and can manage proof tooling, choose ZK-Rollup.
If you need fast time-to-market and EVM parity, choose Optimistic but prepare users for withdrawal wait times.
Action: map your app flows to required withdrawal times and developer resources. That will determine the right L2 approach for your product.
Quick checklist
• Do users need instant asset movement? If yes, favor ZK.
• Can your team adapt to new tooling? If no, consider Optimistic.
• Plan bridge monitoring and liquidity strategies either way.
Key Takeaway

Align rollup choice to user-facing latency needs and your team's capacity to handle proof or migration work.
