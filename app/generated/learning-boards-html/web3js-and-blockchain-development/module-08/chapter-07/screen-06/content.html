Comparison
Optimistic vs ZK
Withdrawal latency: Optimistic has long challenge windows; ZK offers near-instant withdrawals with proofs.
EVM compatibility: Optimistic tends to be more EVM-compatible; ZK often requires special compilers or runtimes.
Implementation cost: ZK proof generation is heavier computationally and may need new tooling.
Concrete scenario
A GameFi app that needs instant in-game asset deposits and withdrawals would benefit from a ZK-Rollup for immediate finality. A DeFi contract that relies on many existing Solidity libraries may favor Optimistic Rollups to avoid migration headaches.
Misconception check
Instant finality does not always trump developer velocity; choose the rollup that aligns with your team's ability to modify contracts and manage proof pipelines.
Key Takeaway

Match rollup choice to user needs: immediate finality versus developer compatibility and operational complexity.
