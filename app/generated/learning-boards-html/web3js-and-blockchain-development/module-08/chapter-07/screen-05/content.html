Process Flow
Batch → Proof → Settle
1. Execution: L2 executes transactions with lower per-action cost by grouping operations.
2. Aggregation: batches of transactions are formed and either left 'optimistically' valid or turned into a cryptographic proof.
3. Settlement: a single on-chain submission anchors many off-chain transactions to L1 security.
Concrete Example
Ten thousand micro-payments processed on L2, submitted as a single batch to L1, greatly lowering total gas consumed.
Optimistic vs ZK summary
Optimistic Rollup
Assumes validity; includes a challenge period. Simpler and often EVM-compatible, but long withdrawals.
ZK-Rollup
Uses cryptographic proofs to show validity and enables fast withdrawals, with higher proof complexity.
Common mistake
Believing all rollups give instant withdrawals; only ZK proofs eliminate the challenge period for immediate finality.
