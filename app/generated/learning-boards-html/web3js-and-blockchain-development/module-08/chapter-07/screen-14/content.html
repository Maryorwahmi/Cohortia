Module 08 › Chapter 8.7 › Key Concepts
Layer 1 (L1)
The base chain where transactions are ultimately settled and security is enforced.
Layer 2 (L2)
Processes transactions off-chain and posts summaries or proofs to L1 to increase throughput and lower fees.
Cross-chain Bridges
Protocols that enable asset and data transfer across networks using lock-and-mint patterns, with security trade-offs.
Takeaway

Choose your L2 and bridge strategy by matching user expectations for cost and withdrawal speed with your team’s operational and security capacity.
