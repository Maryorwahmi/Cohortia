Definition
Benefits
• Dramatically lower per-transaction costs.
• Higher throughput enabling complex UX.
• Better user experience during peak usage.
Concrete gain
Enabling microtransactions in a game or frequent small updates in a social dApp without prohibitive gas costs.
Trade-offs
• Developer overhead: multiple chains, RPCs, and bridge integrations.
• Fragmented liquidity and UX complexity.
• Additional security surface from bridges and L2 contracts.
• Potential tooling and compiler differences for ZK solutions.
Misconception

Treating L2 deployment as trivial ignores integration, monitoring, and liquidity challenges that materially affect product design.
