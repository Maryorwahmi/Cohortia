Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Key Concepts
Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO)
An organization defined by on-chain rules where decisions are made by token-holder voting.
Governance Tokens
Tokens that grant voting rights and influence over DAO proposals and treasury decisions.
Non-Fungible Token (NFT)
A unique token representing ownership and provenance of a digital or real-world item on-chain.
Takeaway
Use these core concepts to reason about ownership, governance, and the role of tokens in user-facing applications.
