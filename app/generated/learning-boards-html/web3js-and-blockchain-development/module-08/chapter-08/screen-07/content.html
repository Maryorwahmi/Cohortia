Comparison

ERC-721 vs ERC-1155

ERC-721
Unique token IDs, single ownership per token, widely used by NFT marketplaces and collectors.
ERC-1155
Multi-token standard supporting batch operations and both fungible and non-fungible items within the same contract.
Developer guidance
If you need simple unique items for a marketplace or art collection, ERC-721 remains highly compatible with tooling. If you have numerous similar items that benefit from batch transfers or quantities, ERC-1155 reduces gas and complexity of managing many token contracts.
Concrete Example
A game with swords and potions: swords as unique ERC-721 artifacts, potions as stackable ERC-1155 items, or use ERC-1155 for both to unify the contract and benefit from batch transfers.
Key Takeaway

Match the token standard to your asset semantics and UX; market support and metadata patterns influence integration work.
