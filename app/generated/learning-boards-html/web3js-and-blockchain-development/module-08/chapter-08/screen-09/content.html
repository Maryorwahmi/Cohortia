Sector snapshot

DeFi: financial services on-chain. GameFi: play-to-earn with NFTs. Metaverse: persistent virtual worlds with portable assets.
DeFi
Lending, AMMs, and composable finance. Accuracy and security are top priorities.
GameFi
Play-to-earn and NFT-based economies. Smooth inventory and trade UX matters most.
Metaverse
Persistent virtual environments where identity and asset portability are key.
Design implications
Think about latency, transaction visibility, and how to present on-chain proofs. Integrate clear signals for risk and provenance depending on sector expectations.
