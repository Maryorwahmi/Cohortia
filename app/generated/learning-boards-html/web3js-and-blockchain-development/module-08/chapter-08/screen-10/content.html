Architecture

Consensus & Layering

Consensus improvements and Layer 2 rollups reduce cost and increase throughput, but introduce new UX and bridging considerations for developers and users.
Layer 1
Settlement and security; higher fees and slower throughput historically.
Layer 2 / Rollups
Execution or data aggregation that improves performance and lowers costs, but requires bridging and network-awareness.
Developer checklist
  • Detect network and present clear prompts when user is on a different chain.
  • Handle confirmations and cross-layer bridge flows transparently for users.
  • Design retry strategies and show finality expectations based on target layer.
Key Takeaway

Architectural choices affect UX and complexity; build frontends that clearly explain network trade-offs and transaction finality to users.
