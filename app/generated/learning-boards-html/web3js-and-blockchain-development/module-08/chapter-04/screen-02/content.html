Learning Objectives

Clear, verifiable skills you will demonstrate after implementing the lesson steps.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify common risks

Recognize network mismatches, phishing prompts, exposed keys, and dependency vulnerabilities so you know what to protect against.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Secure wallet connections

Detect chainId, respond to chainChanged and accountsChanged events, and prompt safe network switching to avoid accidental Mainnet interactions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Validate and sanitize inputs

Use utilities like web3.utils.isAddress and numeric checks to prevent malformed transactions and precision errors.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Protect secrets and rate-limit

Keep API keys on a backend proxy, apply rate limiting, and audit dependencies to reduce supply chain exposure and quota abuse.
