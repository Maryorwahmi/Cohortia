Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Pinning Services vs Running Your Own Node
Pinning Service
Convenience & reliability
  • Managed nodes and guaranteed pinning while under service.
  • SDKs and dashboards for quick deployment.
  • Requires API key management and trust in provider.
Own Node
Control & maintenance
  • Full control and no third-party dependency.
  • Requires continuous operation and storage management.
  • Better for projects that demand trust minimization.
Concrete example
Use Pinata to upload build/ with pinFromFS and receive a CID, saving the overhead of running your own node during development and early production.
Common mistake
Assuming pinning services equal immutability forever. They maintain availability while pinning, but permanence requires a different model or Arweave's economic guarantee.
Key Takeaway

Pick pinning for ease of deployment and Arweave for permanent archival; running your own node is for projects that require full control.
