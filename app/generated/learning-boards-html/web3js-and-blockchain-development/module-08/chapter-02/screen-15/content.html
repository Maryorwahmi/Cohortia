Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › What to Remember
Verify builds
Always confirm build/ contents and test locally before pinning.
Record CIDs
Log each CID alongside a release note so you can trace which snapshot was deployed.
Choose permanence
Use IPFS for updates and Arweave for one-time permanent archival when required.
Final takeaway
A reliable dApp equals reliable frontend hosting. Use content-addressing, pin carefully, and archive when permanence matters.
