Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Key Concepts
Decentralized Storage
Distributes files across nodes to reduce single points of failure and resist censorship.
IPFS & CIDs
Content-addressed identifiers verify file integrity; different content produces different CIDs.
Pinning Services
Services like Pinata run nodes that keep your CID pinned and accessible via public gateways.
Summary
Follow the workflow: build your app, pin the static build, verify the CID using a gateway, and choose Arweave when you need true permanence.
