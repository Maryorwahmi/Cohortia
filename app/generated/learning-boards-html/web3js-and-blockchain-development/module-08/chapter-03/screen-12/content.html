Best Practices

Secure Coding Checklist & Failsafes

Concrete items to include in your deployment checklist.

Checklist items
  • Input validation with require and clear revert messages.
  • Apply checks-effects-interactions where external calls occur.
  • Use OpenZeppelin guards like nonReentrant and AccessControl.
  • Prefer Solidity 0.8+ for safe arithmetic and audit legacy code.
Failsafes & monitoring
Implement pausable controls, emergencyStop functions, and multi-signature wallets for high-risk operations. Add on-chain monitoring and alerts for unusual activity patterns.
Misconception: pausable contracts are inherently risky. In reality, properly controlled pause functionality limits damage during incidents.
Key Takeaway

A checklist and a small set of failsafes reduce exposure and make auditing and incident response far simpler.
