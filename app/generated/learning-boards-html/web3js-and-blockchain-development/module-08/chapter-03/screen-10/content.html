Comparison

Ownable vs AccessControl

Weigh simplicity against flexibility and the risk of over-privileging accounts.

Ownable — Pros & Cons
  • Pros: simple, easy to audit, clear authority.
  • Cons: single point of failure unless combined with multisig.
Best for smaller teams or clear single-admin flows.
AccessControl — Pros & Cons
  • Pros: granular roles, separation of duties, flexible governance.
  • Cons: more complexity, must carefully assign and manage roles.
Best for protocols with multiple operators or staged responsibilities.
Key Takeaway

Choose the simplest model that enforces least privilege and integrates with your governance or multisig strategy.
