OpenZeppelin & Access Control

Leverage audited contracts for ownership, role management, and security primitives.

Ownable
Provides a single owner address with onlyOwner modifier to protect functions. Ideal for simple projects where a single authority must act.
Example usage: protect emergency stop or treasury withdrawal with onlyOwner so only the deployer or a delegated admin can act.
AccessControl
Role-based permissions allow multiple, scoped administrators. Define roles for pausing, upgrading, or other sensitive operations and grant them to specific addresses.
Architectural choice: prefer AccessControl when multiple teams or signers must share duties safely.
Integration checklist
  • Import specific OpenZeppelin versions and review changelogs.
  • Grant roles intentionally; avoid default admin permissions for many accounts.
  • Combine role checks with pausable or multi-sig where needed for high-risk actions.
