What to Remember

Key Concepts — Part 1

Compact definitions to keep at hand while coding and reviewing.

Reentrancy
An external call triggers a callback into the original contract before it finishes, enabling repeated actions like withdrawals.
Checks-Effects-Interactions
Validate inputs, update state, then perform external calls to avoid stale-state exploits.
Integer Overflow/Underflow
Arithmetic wrap-around that historically caused incorrect values; modern Solidity reverts on these errors.
Summary

When reviewing code, first look for external calls and numeric operations: confirm ordering and arithmetic safety before deployment.
