Troubleshooting Checklist
A short diagnostic sequence to isolate common failures.
1. Provider
Is Web3 initialized? If Web3.givenProvider is null, use a configured HttpProvider as fallback.
2. Network ID
Call web3.eth.net.getId() to confirm the chain matches your contract deployment.
3. ABI
Ensure the ABI file matches the currently deployed contract; mismatches cause method errors.
4. Address
Confirm the 0x address corresponds to the deployment on this network.
Pro Tip

Log storageContract.options.address and storageContract.options.jsonInterface.slice(0,2) immediately after instantiation to surface basic diagnostics.
