Concept

Gas Limit and Gas Price

Gas limit caps computation. Gas price sets payment per unit. Together they determine cost and whether the transaction completes.

Gas Limit
Maximum gas units you allow. Too low → out-of-gas and revert.
Gas Price
Payment per gas unit. Too low → delayed or ignored by miners.
Practical example
const gasEstimate = await contract.methods.store(42).estimateGas({ from: account });
// Use: gas: gasEstimate + 10000
Add a small buffer to cover minor variance in gas usage. Always check sender's balance can cover gasLimit × gasPrice.
