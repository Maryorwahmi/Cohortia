Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
send()
Executes state-changing contract calls by creating, signing, and broadcasting a transaction.
Gas & estimateGas
Estimate gas with web3.eth.estimateGas and add a buffer to avoid out-of-gas failures.
Transaction receipt
A mined receipt confirms the transactionHash, blockNumber, gasUsed, and any emitted events.
Takeaway

Treat send(), gas estimation, and receipt handling as a single sequence when building reliable transaction flows.
