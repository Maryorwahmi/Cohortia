Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Errors & Diagnosis
Revert
Contract logic prevented execution. State rolled back but gas consumed. Check require conditions and revert reasons in error messages.
Out of gas
Gas limit too low. Use estimateGas and add a buffer to avoid premature termination that still consumes gas.
Insufficient funds
Sender lacks enough Ether to cover gasLimit × gasPrice. Verify balances before sending and present clear error messages.
Mitigation checklist
  • Use estimateGas and add a safety buffer (10-20% or 10,000 units).
  • Pre-check contract state to avoid predictable reverts.
  • Confirm sender balance covers estimated gas cost.
  • Catch errors and show user-friendly guidance: 'increase gas', 'check funds', or 'review inputs'.
