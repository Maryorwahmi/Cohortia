Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Replacement
Speed up
Resend the same action with identical data and nonce but a higher gas price. Miners will usually accept the replacement transaction.
Cancel
Send a no-op send to your own address with the same nonce and higher gas price. This replaces the original and performs minimal work.
Practical rules
  • Always use the exact nonce of the stuck transaction.
  • Set gas price at least 10–20% higher than original and above current network price.
  • Re-fetch the network gas price to avoid underbidding replacement transactions.
Common mistake

Attempting to cancel with a lower or equal gas price will not replace the pending transaction and will likely fail.
