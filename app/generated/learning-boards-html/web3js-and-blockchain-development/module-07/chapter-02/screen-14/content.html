Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
Gas
Unit of computational work required to execute transactions on Ethereum.
Gas Price
Amount paid per gas unit; determines priority and cost (commonly shown in Gwei).
Gas Limit
Maximum gas a transaction can consume; unused portion is refunded.
Summary

Estimate gas precisely and choose a gas price with fallbacks. Control nonces explicitly and provide replacement options to unstick transactions.
