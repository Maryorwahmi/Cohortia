Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
Nonce
A sequential number that enforces order and prevents replay of transactions.
Transaction Speed-up
Replace a pending tx with the same nonce and higher gas price to get it mined sooner.
Transaction Cancellation
Send a no-op to your own address with the same nonce and higher gas price to replace a stuck tx with a harmless one.
Final takeaway

Combine explicit nonce handling, reliable gas estimation, and clear replacement flows to keep your dApp responsive and predictable.
