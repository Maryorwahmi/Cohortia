Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Verification
01 • Gas estimation
Show an estimate plus buffer

Fetch estimateGas for a sample call and apply a 10–20% buffer. Run the transaction on a testnet to confirm it doesn't fail with out-of-gas.

02 • Nonce sequencing
Send multiple txs with explicit nonces

Send three transactions with incremented nonces and confirm they mine in order. Then create a stuck low-fee tx and replace it to show recovery.

03 • Speed up / cancel
Replace pending transactions

Speed up by resending with same nonce and a higher gas price. Cancel by sending a no-op to your address with the same nonce and higher gas.

04 • Gas optimization
Measure and reduce gasUsed

Refactor a small contract to reduce storage writes or use calldata and compare gasUsed between versions to demonstrate savings.
