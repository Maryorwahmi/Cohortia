Core Concept
Total fee calculation

The total transaction fee equals gasUsed multiplied by gasPrice. Gas measures work; gasPrice is the unit cost.

Explanation
  • Gas units are consumed by EVM opcodes; storage writes cost the most.
  • Gas price fluctuates with network demand; higher price tends to reduce waiting time.
  • Unused gas is refunded, but low gas limit causes failure and wasted fees.
  • Estimate gas for the exact call and add a buffer rather than hardcoding an arbitrary limit.
Example
// Fee calculation // gasUsed: measured after execution, e.g. 45,000 // gasPrice: current price in wei, e.g. 100 gwei // totalFee = gasUsed * gasPrice
Key Takeaway

Separate gas estimation from gas price. Estimate gas accurately, then choose price to balance speed and cost.

Common misconception
Some developers think increasing the gas limit will make a transaction faster. It does not change miner prioritization; gas price does. A high gas limit only raises the maximum gas the transaction can use.
Why it matters
Accurate fee calculation prevents failed transactions and surprising costs for users. It also informs UI choices when offering fast, standard, and slow options.
