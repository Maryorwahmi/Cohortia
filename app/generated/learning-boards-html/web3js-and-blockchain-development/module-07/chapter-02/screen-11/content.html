Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › dApp Patterns
Patterns to apply
  • Batch multiple actions into one contract call when safe to reduce base fees.
  • Move expensive computation off-chain and validate results on-chain when needed.
  • Use event subscriptions for live updates instead of repeated polling.
  • Provide clear gas presets and an advanced custom option for power users.
UX example
Show three buttons: Fast, Standard, Slow. For most users, choose Standard by default and explain the expected wait versus cost in plain language.
