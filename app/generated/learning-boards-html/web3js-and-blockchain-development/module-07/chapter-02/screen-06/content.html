Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Gas Price Sources
RPC (web3.eth.getGasPrice)
Pros: always available from your node, fast to query. Cons: single median suggestion, may not reflect tiered priorities or predictive spikes.
External trackers
Pros: provide fast/standard/slow tiers and historical trends so UIs can present choices. Cons: requires integration and fallback logic.
Practical pattern
  1. Fetch web3.eth.getGasPrice() as a quick default.
  2. Optionally query a gas tracker for tiered options.
  3. Offer fast/standard/slow choices and explain trade-offs to users.
UX note
Show estimated fee in Gwei and an approximate wait time. Let advanced users input a custom gas price while providing safe defaults for most users.
