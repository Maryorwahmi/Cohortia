Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Connection resilience
Lifecycle pattern
  1. Init provider and subscription inside useEffect.
  2. Store subscription ref so you can unsubscribe later.
  3. On unmount, call subscription.unsubscribe and disconnect provider if supported.
Reconnection
Implement retry with exponential backoff and on reconnect fetch past events for any missed block range.
Practical checklist
  • Limit subscription scope with address/topics.
  • Debounce UI updates to avoid rendering storms.
  • Persist last processed block to resume safely after reconnect.
Key Takeaway

Graceful cleanup and reconnection with backfill are essential to avoid lost or duplicated events.
