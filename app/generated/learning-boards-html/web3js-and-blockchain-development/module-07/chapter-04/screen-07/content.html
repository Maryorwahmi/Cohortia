Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Front-running
How front-running works
Mempool visibility lets attackers observe and reorder transactions by paying for priority inclusion.
Observe pending transaction
Submit higher-fee transaction to be mined earlier
Profit from re-ordered execution
Mitigations
Commit‑reveal scheme: hide intent until reveal phase
Batched processing or time-locks to prevent single-transaction advantage
Design contracts to reduce sensitivity to ordering when possible
Even where mitigation is not possible, clearly communicate front-running risk to users and consider optional transaction delays or slippage tolerances.
