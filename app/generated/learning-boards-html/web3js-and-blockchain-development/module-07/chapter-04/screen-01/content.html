Why this matters

Once deployed, smart contracts cannot be easily changed. That permanence makes development errors costly. The chapter gives you patterns and tools to limit risk, detect problems early, and educate users to avoid social-engineering losses.

Who should care

Developers deploying contracts, frontend engineers building dApp UX, auditors running code analysis, and product teams responsible for user safety and trust.
