Integer Overflow / Underflow
Arithmetic errors appear when numbers go outside their type limits.
Why it matters
Incorrect arithmetic can alter balances or token supplies, enabling theft or logic bypass.
Practical fix
Use Solidity 0.8.0+ or SafeMath for older code. Add unit tests targeting boundaries.
Check your tests

Include edge-case arithmetic tests and simulate large transfers or zero-value subtracts to reveal wrap behavior.
