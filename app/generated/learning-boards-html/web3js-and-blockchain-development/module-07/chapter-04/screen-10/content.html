Denial of Service (DoS)
Design contracts so core functions cannot be blocked by scale or single accounts.
Problem examples
  • Unbounded loops over user lists that grow unboundedly.
  • Push-based payment to many recipients in one transaction.
Mitigations
  • Use pull payments: users withdraw their share.
  • Batch work and rate-limit on-chain loops.
  • Move heavy computation off-chain when possible.
Simulate growth and worst-case sizes during testing to verify functions remain executable under load.
