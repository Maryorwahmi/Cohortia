Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Access Control
Role patterns
Owner, multi-role RBAC, and emergency pausers are common building blocks.
onlyOwner modifier: single admin control
Role-based access control: assign multiple granular permissions
Pause/Unpause: emergency mitigation without full shutdown
Design checks
  • Audit initialization paths to prevent accidental admin assignment.
  • Log and limit ownership transfer operations.
  • Keep governance actions distinct from funds transfers.
Combining clear access control with pause mechanisms and upgrade-safe patterns reduces blast radius when a bug appears.
