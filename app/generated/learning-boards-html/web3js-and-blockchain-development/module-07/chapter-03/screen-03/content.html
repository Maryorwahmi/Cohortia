Operational and design considerations
Pinning, privacy, mutability, and multi-node availability.
Pinning & Availability
Pin content to prevent garbage collection; use services or multiple nodes for critical assets.

Privacy & Encryption
Encrypt sensitive data before adding to IPFS and manage keys off-chain; IPFS content is public by default.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mutable patterns
Publish new CIDs for updates and store the latest CID on-chain; consider IPNS for mutable pointers when needed.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Failure modes
Avoid single-node reliance; plan for multi-node pinning and monitor gateway availability for critical user experiences.
