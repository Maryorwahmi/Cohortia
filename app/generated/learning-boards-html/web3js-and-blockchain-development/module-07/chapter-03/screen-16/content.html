Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Quick Reference
1. CIDs are content fingerprints
A single byte change produces a new CID; this is how you verify integrity.
2. Pin for persistence
Pin important assets on trusted nodes or via services to keep them available.
3. Store CIDs on-chain
Avoid gateway URLs in contracts to prevent third-party coupling.
Final takeaway

Design with content addressing, pinning, and minimal on-chain pointers to build verifiable and scalable dApps.
