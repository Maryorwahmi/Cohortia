Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Troubleshooting
Common mistakes
  • Not pinning content and expecting permanence.
  • Storing gateway URLs on-chain and creating coupling to a provider.
  • Not handling IPFS or transaction errors in the UI.
Quick checks
Confirm the CID returned by ipfs.add, query your contract to verify storage, and test gateway fetches for the CID.
Remediation steps
  1. Pin the CID on at least one reliable node or via a pinning service.
  2. Implement clear UI status messages and retries for uploads and fetches.
  3. Prefer storing the bare CID on-chain rather than a gateway URL.
These practices reduce user frustration and harden the integration between your dApp, IPFS, and the blockchain.
Key Takeaway

Treat pinning and error handling as first-class parts of your IPFS integration to maintain availability and trust.
