Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Real-time updates: WebSockets vs polling
Why subscriptions win
Subscriptions push events as they occur, reducing redundant requests and improving real-time responsiveness.
Use web3.eth.subscribe('logs') for Transfer events and similar logs.
On reconnect, fetch a short window of past events to reconcile missed messages.
Fallback to HTTP polling only when WebSockets are unavailable, and choose a longer interval to limit load.
Example
A transfer feed should subscribe to Transfer logs. When a log arrives, update the cache for only the affected accounts rather than reloading the entire transfer history.
Check your understanding
How would you reconcile missed events after a temporary network drop? Answer: re-sync a short historic range and apply updates incrementally.
