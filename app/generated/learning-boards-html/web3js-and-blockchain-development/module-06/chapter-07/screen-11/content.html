Architecture
Where to create subscriptions
Choose the subscription owner based on reuse and scale requirements.
Component-level
Good for one-off listeners, but risks duplicate listeners if multiple components mount.
Hook-level
Centralizes logic; a single hook can expose state to many components and manage a single subscription and cleanup.
Backend indexer
Best for large histories, pagination, and advanced queries. Relays events to clients over websockets or APIs.
Key Takeaway

For most dApps, a hook-level subscription balances simplicity and reuse. Move to indexed backends only when history or scale demands it.
