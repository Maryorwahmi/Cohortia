Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Accessibility and Responsiveness
Accessibility checklist
  • Use semantic <label> elements and associate them with inputs.
  • Expose status messages via aria-live regions for screen readers.
  • Ensure high color contrast for status banners and buttons.
  • Make interactive elements reachable by keyboard and use visible focus styles.
Responsive form tips
Stack fields vertically on narrow screens, provide larger touch targets, and use unit selectors that collapse appropriately on mobile.
Pro tip
Test with keyboard-only and mobile breakpoints. Small layout changes often reveal big UX problems.
Key Takeaway

Treat accessibility and responsiveness as part of your QA checklist — they protect users and prevent errors.
