Component Catalog
WalletConnectButton
Detects wallet presence and initiates connection. Keeps logic isolated and retryable.
AccountInfo
Shows truncated address, balance, and network. Reads from context or hook.
ContractInteractionForm
Encapsulates inputs, validation, and submission for a single contract function.
Pattern advice
  • Keep UI components declarative; move side-effects into hooks.
  • Compose small components; reuse TransactionFeedback across flows.
  • Place web3 provider at app root so components remain simple.
Common pitfall
Mixing wallet connect logic with form rendering makes components hard to maintain and test.
