Validation checklist
Concrete items to test in your app before shipping write functionality.
From address present
Ensure your useWeb3 hook exposes currentAccount and block send calls when it's missing.
Unit conversion
Always use web3.utils.toWei for Ether amounts to avoid floating-point issues.
Event listeners
Implement handlers for transactionHash, receipt, and error and reflect them in UI states.
Simulate failures
Test out-of-gas, network mismatch, and user rejection to ensure graceful recovery.
