What to include
<!-- .env (visual only) -->
VITE_INFURA_PROJECT_ID=YOUR_INFURA_PROJECT_ID_HERE
VITE_CONTRACT_ADDRESS=0xYourDeployedContractAddressHere
Template hint

Also commit a .env.example that lists the keys without values so collaborators know what to provide.

Update .gitignore

Add the line .env to your .gitignore to avoid committing secrets.

Key Takeaway

Never commit .env. Use .env.example to document required variables without leaking secrets.
