Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts — Tools & Strategies
web3.eth.getBalance
Retrieves an account's Wei balance as a string; use it in async effects and handle errors.
web3.utils.fromWei
Converts a Wei string to an Ether string; use before formatting or display.
Polling
Repeatedly re-fetch balance at intervals; clear timers on cleanup and balance network usage against responsiveness.
One-line takeaway
Use getBalance and fromWei to present accurate balances, handle loading/errors cleanly, and update intelligently to maintain trust.
