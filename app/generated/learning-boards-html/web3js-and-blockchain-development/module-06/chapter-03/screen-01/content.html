Displaying Wallet Information and Balances
Present a clear wallet address, convert Wei to Ether for readability, and keep balances synchronized with the blockchain state.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson you'll implement display and update patterns inside a React Web3Provider.
Address
Truncate long addresses and offer copy affordances to improve readability.
Balance
Fetch Wei, convert with fromWei, format decimals and attach currency symbols.
Updates
Use polling or re-fetch after transactions to keep the UI current.
Why this matters
Users rely on your dApp to identify their connected wallet and see accurate holdings. Simple formatting and timely updates increase trust and reduce errors.
