Rendering tips
  • Guard against missing provider or account to avoid runtime errors.
  • Keep format logic small in components; prefer provider or hooks for complex transformations.
  • Use monospace for addresses to aid visual scanning.
Key Takeaway

Components should render formatted values and handle null/loading states; heavy logic belongs to provider or hooks.
