01 — Render the account
Expose currentAccount from Web3Provider and display a truncated address along with a copy affordance. This reassures users and prevents accidental actions with the wrong wallet.
Mnemonic: first six, last four — 0x1234...abcd
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Fetch and convert balance
Call web3.eth.getBalance(account) inside a useEffect, then convert with web3.utils.fromWei(wei, 'ether') before storing state for display.
Mnemonic: Wei is big, convert to Ether for humans.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Keep data current
Use setInterval polling or re-fetch after a confirmed transaction. Always clear intervals on unmount to avoid memory leaks.
Mnemonic: poll or confirm-and-fetch.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Format for display
Round decimals, append 'ETH', and show 'Fetching balance...' while loading to avoid undefined displays or flicker.
Mnemonic: show the label, round the digits.
