Practical outcome
Build a Web3Provider that initializes web3 and exposes connection state.
Wrap App with Web3Provider to avoid prop drilling.
Use useWeb3 to read currentAccount and chainId from any component.
Trigger eth_requestAccounts on explicit user intent, not automatically.

Testing checklist
How to validate your implementation.
Open console and check window.ethereum exists when MetaMask is installed.
Click Connect Wallet and confirm eth_requestAccounts resolves to an account array.
Switch accounts and networks in MetaMask and observe accountsChanged and chainChanged events update state.

Common pitfalls
Watch for these implementation traps.
  • Assuming detection equals permission.
  • Not re-initializing Web3 on chain change.
  • Attaching listeners without cleanup in useEffect.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Reflection
Why centralize connection logic?
Centralizing reduces duplicated listener setup, simplifies UI updates on events, and makes testing and maintenance easier. It also clarifies where network re-initialization should occur when chainId changes.
