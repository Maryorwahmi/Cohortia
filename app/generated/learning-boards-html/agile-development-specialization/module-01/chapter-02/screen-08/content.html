Core benefits

Risk reduction
Small increments make problems visible early, when fixes cost less.
Early feedback
Real users validate assumptions and shape future increments.
Faster time to market
Core value can be released as an MVP and improved incrementally.
Adaptability
Teams can pivot without discarding months of work.
Warning
Skipping quality gates for speed introduces technical debt. That debt slows future increments and can erase early benefits.
Key Takeaway

Benefits come from short cycles and shippable increments only when quality is preserved across deliveries.
