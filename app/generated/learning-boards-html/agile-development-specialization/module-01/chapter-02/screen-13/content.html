Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Iterative Development
Short cycles of build, review, and refine that embed learning.
Incremental Development
Deliver small, usable, and tested pieces that each add value.
Waterfall Model
A sequential, single-pass process where late feedback increases rework risk.
Summary Takeaway

Use vertical, shippable increments and short iterations to surface risk early, deliver value fast, and adapt based on real feedback.
