Learning Objectives

Each objective is measurable and tied to practical skills you can demonstrate.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define
Define iterative and incremental development and explain why they are different yet complementary.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain
Explain how iterations and increments combine in Agile sprints to deliver and improve shippable functionality.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Compare
Compare iterative/incremental approaches with the Waterfall model and identify when Waterfall increases project risk.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply
Break a product into at least three shippable increments and explain why each provides standalone value.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic
Iterative = refine. Think repeated sketches.
Incremental = deliver. Think one finished room at a time.
Practice: create three shippable slices from a product idea to confirm understanding.
