Skills to demonstrate

These are measurable actions: define, compare, and create shippable increments.

Explain
Describe how repeated cycles and small deliveries interact in Agile.
Compare
Contrast single-pass Waterfall with multi-pass Agile and identify risks.
Apply
Break a product into at least three shippable increments and justify them.
Evaluate
Judge whether an increment is truly shippable or merely a technical layer.
Quick rubric
  • Shippable = usable by users and tested.
  • Iteration = frequent learning checkpoint.
  • Avoid delivering isolated layers that cannot function independently.
