Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Assessment & Reflection

Sample assessment items

  1. Identify whether a sequential, layer-based plan represents Waterfall or incremental delivery.
  2. Given a two-week cycle scenario, determine whether it demonstrates iteration or one-off development.
  3. Break a product into three shippable increments and justify each as vertically integrated.
Scoring rubric
Give credit for vertical slices that include frontend, backend, and tests. Penalize horizontal slices that produce no user value alone.
Reflection prompt
How would you re-slice a past project to surface risks earlier? Describe the first increment and the iteration plan.
