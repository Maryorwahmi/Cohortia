Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Iteration Walkthrough

Scenario

Initial increment: create and complete tasks. Post-release feedback reveals missing due dates and sorting.

Iteration 1
Add due date field, implement sort by date, add validation and tests.
Iteration 2
Add notification scheduler based on due dates and collect usage metrics.
Quality step
Each iteration includes tests and acceptance checks to keep the increment releasable.
Misconception
Iterations are not just for new features. Use them to improve quality, fix issues, and adapt based on data.
Key Takeaway

Plan iterations around learning outcomes and quality goals, not only feature lists.
