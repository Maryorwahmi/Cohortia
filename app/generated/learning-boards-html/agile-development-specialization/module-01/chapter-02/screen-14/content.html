Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Terms
Iteration
A short timebox used to build and refine a working increment.
Increment
A small, functional, and ideally shippable piece of the product.
Technical Debt
Deferred work that increases future cost unless intentionally managed.
Final Tip

When planning, prefer vertical slices that are shippable and plan iterations to learn. This keeps risk low and feedback meaningful.
