Iterative & Incremental Development

Two complementary strategies that change how teams learn, build, and reduce risk. Learn how short cycles and shippable pieces work together.

In this lesson
Define iterative vs incremental, compare with Waterfall, and apply a feature-breakdown activity.
Coming up
Examples, common pitfalls like non-shippable increments, and how to avoid technical debt.
Why it matters
You will plan deliverable slices and iterate on them to reduce risk and get feedback early.
Visual Concept
Castle building analogy: small towers, repeated refinement
Iterative
Repeat cycles that refine a working piece. Each pass improves quality and responds to feedback.
Incremental
Deliver small, usable features that stand on their own and provide value from day one.
How they combine
Build a small, shippable feature, then iterate on it across short cycles to improve functionality and usability based on feedback.
