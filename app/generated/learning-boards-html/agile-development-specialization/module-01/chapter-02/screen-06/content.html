Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Iterative + Incremental
Process Flow

Plan an increment → deliver across iterations

1
Define a small, shippable increment and acceptance criteria.
2
Deliver initial functionality in the first iteration and gather feedback.
3
Refine and harden the increment in subsequent iterations until it meets quality goals.
4
Release or integrate the increment and use real feedback to plan the next increment.
Scenario card
Team A builds search as Increment 1. Iteration 1: basic search. Iteration 2: performance and filters. Iteration 3: relevance tuning after user testing.
Misconception
Increments replace the need to iterate. In reality, increments often require multiple iterations to reach production quality.
Key Takeaway

Combine planning for shippable pieces with short cycles of refinement to stay responsive and reduce risk.
