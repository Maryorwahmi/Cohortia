Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Embedding Feedback
Practical practices
Small routines that generate continuous signals.
Automated tests
Fast regression checks that let developers fix issues before a demo.
Pair programming & reviews
Early design and readability feedback from colleagues.
Sprint reviews & usability testing
Customer-facing validation of fit and value.
Retrospectives
Process improvements and ownership of team behavior changes.
Connecting outputs
Ensure outputs from tests, reviews, and demos are captured as backlog or retrospective items and assigned owners and measures.
Key Takeaway

Create a routine where signals at every cadence feed the backlog and retrospectives so learning becomes continuous and visible.
