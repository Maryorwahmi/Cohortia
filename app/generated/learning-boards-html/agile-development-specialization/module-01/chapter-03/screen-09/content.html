Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Learning Loop
Plan → Build → Measure → Learn → Adapt
A repeatable iteration cycle that turns work into validated learning.
Plan
Choose a clear assumption and a success metric.
Build
Deliver the smallest increment that tests the assumption.
Measure
Collect automated and human feedback that maps to your metric.
Learn
Discuss results and surface root causes and surprises.
Adapt
Update the backlog and process for the next iteration.
Example metric
Goal: increase 'reorder' usage by 10%. Measure: click rate on the reorder button and customer feedback during the sprint review.
