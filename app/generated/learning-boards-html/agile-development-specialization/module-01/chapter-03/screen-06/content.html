Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Internal vs External Feedback
Internal feedback
Fast technical signals that protect quality.
Examples
Code review, pair programming, unit tests, CI alerts, and daily stand-ups.
What it protects
Code quality, design consistency, and fast defect detection.
External feedback
Signals about customer needs and business value.
Examples
Sprint reviews, usability tests, UAT, and product-owner conversations.
What it protects
User satisfaction, product-market fit, and correct prioritization.
Synthesis
Use both internal and external signals and have the product owner synthesize customer feedback into prioritized backlog items. Balance technical health with user needs.
