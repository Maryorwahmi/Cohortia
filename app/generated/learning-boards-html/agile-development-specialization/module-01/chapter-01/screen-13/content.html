Agile-in-name-only and tool-first traps
When rituals exist but the mindset does not, benefits disappear.
Definition
Adopting ceremonies or tools without changing decision-making, incentives, and collaboration norms.
Concrete example
A team reports into a tool but never acts on issues raised in stand-ups, so impediments persist.
Misconception
Buying an Agile tool will not create trust, nor will a calendar invite replace a meaningful retrospective.
Corrective steps
  • Pair each ritual with one measurable outcome.
  • Grant clear decision authority to a product representative.
  • Reserve time to practice principles and measure improvements.
Key Takeaway
Focus on decision-making, outcomes, and measurable improvements, not just rituals or tools.
