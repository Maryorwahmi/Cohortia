Learning Objectives
Progress from understanding context to applying Agile principles in practice.
01
Explain the origin and motivation
Describe why the Manifesto emerged and what problems it was addressing in traditional development. This helps you judge when Agile thinking will help a team.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify and explain the four values
State each value and show a short example of how it changes a team decision, such as preferring a demo over a long report.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe the twelve principles
Summarize how selected principles like frequent delivery and welcoming change support the values and lead to concrete practices.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Analyze Agile vs traditional approaches
Compare decision points, such as handling changing requirements, to determine when Agile principles yield better outcomes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
