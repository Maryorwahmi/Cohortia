Frequent delivery, welcoming change, and close collaboration
A short process that turns feedback into value.
1. Deliver frequently
Ship small, working increments on a short cadence to surface feedback early.
2. Demo & gather feedback
Show working software to stakeholders and collect prioritized feedback.
3. Welcome change
Treat new requirements as opportunities; re-prioritize the backlog where needed.
4. Plan the next increment
Translate feedback into a clear, prioritized scope for the next cycle.
Example
A two-week delivery cycle produced a UI fix after one demo rather than a four-month delayed patch.
Misconception
Frequent releases without a feedback loop just increase churn; the loop must include interpretation and prioritization.
Key Takeaway
Combine cadence, collaboration, and a clear backlog to convert feedback into progress.
