The twelve principles that support the values
Principles translate values into repeatable team practices.
Customer first
Satisfy the customer through early and continuous delivery of valuable software.
Welcome change
Even late requirements are opportunities to improve competitive advantage.
Frequent delivery
Deliver working software in short timescales — weeks rather than months.
Collaboration
Business people and developers must work together daily.
Motivated individuals
Build projects around motivated people and trust them to get the job done.
Face-to-face communication
The most efficient and effective method of conveying information is conversation.
Sustainable pace
Maintain a constant pace indefinitely to preserve long-term productivity.
Technical excellence
Continuous attention to technical excellence and good design enhances agility.
Simplicity
Maximize the amount of work not done; prefer simple solutions.
Self-organizing teams
Teams that organize themselves generate the best architectures and designs.
Regular reflection
At intervals, teams reflect and adjust behavior to become more effective.
Note
These principles are best learned by mapping them to practices on your team.
Key Takeaway
The principles are concrete guidance that make values actionable; use them as lenses to evaluate practices.
