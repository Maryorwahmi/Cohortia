Sustainable pace, technical excellence, and simplicity
Quality investments preserve long-term velocity and reduce risk.
Sustainable pace
Maintain a steady workload so the team can consistently deliver without burnout.
Technical excellence
Invest in tests, refactoring, and design to keep the codebase adaptable.
Simplicity
Focus on the simplest solution that meets needs to avoid unnecessary complexity.
Example
Budget small refactors and tests into each iteration to make future changes cheaper and safer.
Misconception
Quality work is not optional luxury; it's an investment that reduces long-term cost and risk.
Key Takeaway
Protect time for improvement and choose simple solutions to keep agility sustainable.
