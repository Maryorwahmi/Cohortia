Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
Partner with stakeholders to steer the product toward real value.
Definition
Continuous engagement with customers to shape priorities and validate outcomes rather than rigidly following a pre-set contract.
Concrete example
Weekly demos with prioritized feedback from the owner that lead to small adjustments and clearer priorities.
Misconception
Collaboration is not chaotic input; it is structured engagement with a clear decision process and prioritized outcomes.
Practice card
Demo & prioritize
A short, regular demo plus a triage session with the customer converts feedback into ranked backlog items and reduces wasted work.
Key Takeaway
Make customers partners: invite feedback early, then translate it into prioritized, testable changes.
