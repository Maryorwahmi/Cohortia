How principles map to Scrum practices
Concrete mappings that show why rituals matter.
Daily stand-up
Supports face-to-face collaboration and quick impediment removal, aligning work to the sprint goal.
Sprint review
Delivers working software and invites customer collaboration and feedback.
Retrospective
Embodies regular reflection and leads to measurable improvements.
Concrete walkthrough
Two-week sprint example
  1. Plan a small, high-value increment.
  2. Meet daily to align and remove blockers.
  3. Demo the increment to gather feedback and re-prioritize.
  4. Hold a retrospective and pick one measurable improvement.
Key Takeaway
Ceremonies only embody principles when paired with decisions and follow-through that change behavior.
