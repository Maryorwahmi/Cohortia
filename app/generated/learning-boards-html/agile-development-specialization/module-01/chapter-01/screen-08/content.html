Responding to change over following a plan
Treat change as a source of insight and competitive advantage.
Definition
Plans are created and used, but teams must have mechanisms to adjust them when new information improves the outcome.
Concrete example
User feedback shows a core flow is unusable; the team re-prioritizes and delivers a fix in the next iteration instead of waiting for the next major release.
Misconception
Adapting to change is not license for chaos; it requires agreed processes for prioritization and scope control.
Mechanisms
Short iterations, product-owner decisions, and backlog refinement
These allow teams to re-evaluate priorities frequently and incorporate new information without losing control.
Key Takeaway
Design plans as living artifacts—use feedback and short decision cycles to keep them aligned with real needs.
