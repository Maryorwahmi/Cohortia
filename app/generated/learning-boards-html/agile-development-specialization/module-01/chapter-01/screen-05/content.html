Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
A preference for human communication and empowered teams.
Definition
Successful projects rely more on people collaborating effectively than on rigidly following rules or depending on tooling alone.
Concrete example
A quick design huddle prevents a week of rework that would otherwise appear only when a ticket is pulled into development.
Misconception
This doesn't reject tools or processes; it warns against replacing human judgment with rote procedures.
Comparison
Human conversations
Fast alignment, shared context, immediate clarifications.
Vs.
Rigid process or tool
May enforce steps but hide misunderstandings until later stages.
Key Takeaway
Invest in communication rituals and norms first; let processes and tools serve those interactions.
