Sprint Backlog — team-owned plan

Contains selected Product Backlog items plus the plan for delivering them and achieving the Sprint Goal.

Contents
Selected items, tasks, design notes, and a forecast of work needed to reach the Sprint Goal.
Ownership
Owned solely by the Development Team — they set the plan and updates as they learn.
Adaptation
Updated continuously to reflect current understanding while keeping the Sprint Goal stable.
Concrete scenario
During a Sprint to build add-to-cart, the team adds tasks for UI, API, and unit tests to the Sprint Backlog and rearranges them when an API dependency appears.
Misconception
Viewing the Sprint Backlog as an inflexible contract removes the team’s ability to adapt to real discoveries.
Key Takeaway

The Sprint Backlog is a living plan the Development Team uses to inspect progress and adapt toward the Sprint Goal.
