Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
Product Backlog
An ordered, emergent list owned by the Product Owner; keep it DEEP.
Sprint Backlog
Development Team–owned plan of selected items and tasks, adapted during the Sprint.
Increment & DoD
Increments must meet a strict Definition of Done to be considered releasable and transparent.
Summary

Keep backlogs current, let the team own and adapt Sprint plans, and enforce DoD so progress remains visible and trustworthy.
