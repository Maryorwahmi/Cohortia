Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Common mistakes

Avoid these mistakes

Simple, repeatable fixes keep artifacts useful and accurate.

Stale Product Backlog
Fix: schedule short, regular refinement sessions and involve the Development Team.
Weak Definition of Done
Fix: agree specific, testable DoD items and apply them strictly at review and release.
Rigid Sprint Backlog
Fix: treat the Sprint Backlog as a forecast and adapt tasks as learning occurs while keeping the Sprint Goal intact.
Ownership confusion
Fix: Product Owner manages backlog content; Development Team manages Sprint Backlog and execution.
Practical tip
After Sprint Review, immediately capture feedback as backlog items and schedule a short refinement to prepare the top items for the next Sprint.
Key Takeaway

Discipline in refinement, DoD, and ownership prevents hidden rework and keeps delivery predictable.
