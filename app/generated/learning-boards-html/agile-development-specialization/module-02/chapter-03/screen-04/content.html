The three artifacts

Artifacts provide a shared, inspectable representation of work and progress, enabling adaptation.

Product Backlog
Single, ordered source of everything needed for the product. Emergent and owned by the Product Owner.
Sprint Backlog
Team-owned plan plus selected Product Backlog items for the Sprint. Adapted as the team learns.
Increment
Sum of Done Product Backlog items that meet the Definition of Done — usable and potentially releasable.
Example: Online Food Ordering
Product Backlog: view menus, secure payment, order history. Sprint Backlog: build menu display and add-to-cart this Sprint. Increment: tested menu display ready for staging that meets DoD.
Common Mistake
Treating artifacts as static documents leads to outdated plans and poor decisions.
Key Takeaway

Artifacts are the team's shared information radiators: keep them current to enable inspection and adaptation.
