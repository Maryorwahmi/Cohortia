Product Backlog vs Sprint Backlog

Compare ownership, lifespan, granularity, and how each evolves to make practical distinctions clear.

Ownership
Product Backlog: Product Owner. Sprint Backlog: Development Team.
Lifespan
Product Backlog: ongoing across product. Sprint Backlog: lives for one Sprint as a plan.
Granularity
Product Backlog: mixed detail. Sprint Backlog: task-level detail for execution.
Evolution
Product Backlog: emergent and reordered by value. Sprint Backlog: adapted by team as learning occurs within Sprint.
Practical example
For a two-week Sprint to enable basic smart device control, the Product Backlog lists many features; the Development Team pulls specific stories like "register light bulb" and breaks them into tasks in the Sprint Backlog to meet the Sprint Goal.
Common Mistake
Expecting the Product Owner to manage daily task updates or letting the Sprint Backlog become a fixed contract removes team agility.
Key Takeaway

Respect different owners and cadences: one artifact guides product direction, the other guides short-term execution.
