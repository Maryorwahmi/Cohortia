Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Quick Reference
Product Backlog
Ordered list of potential product work; DEEP ensures readiness and value focus.
Sprint Backlog
Team-owned plan for the Sprint; contains selected items and tasks and evolves with learning.
Increment
All Done backlog items that meet the Definition of Done; always potentially releasable.
Final takeaway

Use these pocket definitions as decision filters during refinement, planning, review, and release conversations.
