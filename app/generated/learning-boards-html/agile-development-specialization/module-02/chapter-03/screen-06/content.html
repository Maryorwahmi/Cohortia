Backlog Refinement: Process

A lightweight, regular habit to keep top backlog items ready without blocking delivery.

Step 1 — Clarify
Agree on acceptance criteria and the expected user outcome for the story.
Step 2 — Split
Break large epics into smaller, independently testable stories.
Step 3 — Estimate
Assign relative effort to enable forecasting during Sprint Planning.
Step 4 — Prioritize
Order by value, risk, and dependencies so the team builds the most important things first.
Practical guidance
Keep sessions short and focused. Reserve a small portion of capacity for refinement activities so they don't consume delivery time.
Misconception
Refinement is not a one-person job nor a single meeting; it is continuous and collaborative.
Key Takeaway

Timebox and share refinement so the backlog remains actionable and Sprint Planning stays efficient.
