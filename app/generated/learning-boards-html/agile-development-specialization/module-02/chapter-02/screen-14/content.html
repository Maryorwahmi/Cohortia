Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Sprint
A time-box of one month or less for creating a Done Increment.
Sprint Planning
Event to set a Sprint Goal and select Product Backlog items for the Sprint.
Daily Scrum
Short 15-minute planning meeting for the Development Team to adapt daily.
Summary
These concepts create a disciplined cadence that enables regular inspection and adaptation of both product and process.
