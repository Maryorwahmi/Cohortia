Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Sprint Retrospective
Purpose & Questions
  • Inspect team’s process and interactions.
  • Discuss what went well and what could improve.
  • Commit to one or more actionable improvements for the next Sprint.
Example action
Create a CI checklist and assign an owner to reduce broken builds, with success measured by reduced integration failures over the next Sprint.
Avoiding the blame game
Facilitate psychologically safe conversation. Convert concerns into experiments and actions rather than assigning fault. Ensure each action has an owner and measurable criteria.
Key Takeaway

Retrospectives drive continuous improvement when the team commits to concrete actions with owners and success criteria.
