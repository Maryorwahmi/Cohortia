Two-week Sprint timeline (illustrative)
Day 1 — Sprint Planning
Set Sprint Goal and Sprint Backlog. Time-box ~4 hours.
Days 2–9 — Daily Scrum
15 minutes each day to inspect progress and adapt plan.
Day 10 — Sprint Review
Demonstrate Increment and gather stakeholder feedback. Time-box ~2 hours.
Day 10 — Retrospective
Inspect process and commit to improvements. Time-box ~1.5 hours.
This rhythm forms a feedback loop: Planning sets the work, Daily Scrums keep it aligned, Reviews inform product direction, and Retrospectives improve team effectiveness for the next Sprint.
