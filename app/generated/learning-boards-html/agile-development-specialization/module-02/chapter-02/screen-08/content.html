Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Daily Scrum Coaching
Common anti-patterns
Status report to Scrum Master or Product Owner.
Long technical problem-solving during the meeting.
Irregular schedule that breaks team rhythm.
Coaching moves for Scrum Masters
  • Remind the team this is their planning meeting and prompt them to adapt the Sprint Backlog.
  • When deep discussion starts, request an offline follow-up and note the participants.
  • Keep the meeting at the same time and place to create a predictable cadence.
Quick script
"What’s our plan for the next 24 hours to reach the Sprint Goal? Who is blocked and who can help? If we need a longer dive, let’s meet right after with the necessary people."
