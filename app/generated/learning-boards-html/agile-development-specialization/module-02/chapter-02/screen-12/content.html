Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Retrospective → Action
Common anti-patterns
Many observations, no actions or owners.
Blame-focused conversation that discourages openness.
Action planning recipe
  1. List potential improvements and vote to prioritize.
  2. Select one or two items the team can try next Sprint.
  3. Assign an owner and define measurable success criteria.
Key Takeaway

Limit commitments to achievable experiments, assign owners, and measure success at the end of the next Sprint.
