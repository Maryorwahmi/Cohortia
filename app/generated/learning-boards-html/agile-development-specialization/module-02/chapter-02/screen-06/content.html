Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Planning Pitfalls
Anti-patterns
Over-commitment: choosing too much scope for the Sprint.
Being told what to do: Development Team lacks ownership of the plan.
Unrefined backlog: planning stalls while items are clarified.
Practical corrections
  • Use historical velocity and explicit capacity when forecasting.
  • Facilitate collaborative planning; Product Owner explains priorities and team chooses the plan.
  • Refine backlog items beforehand so planning focuses on choices rather than clarification.
Two-week Sprint checklist
Refined items, capacity calculation, draft Sprint Goal, task breakdown, Done criteria, acceptance by Development Team.
