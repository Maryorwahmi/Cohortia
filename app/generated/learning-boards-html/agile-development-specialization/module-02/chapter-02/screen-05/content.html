Sprint Planning steps
1. Product Owner presents priority items and the desired outcome.
2. Development Team forecasts capacity and selects items they can complete.
3. Team defines the Sprint Goal and creates the initial Sprint Backlog with tasks and Done criteria.
Example — 2-week Sprint
Time-box: ~4 hours. Activities: clarify priorities, estimate tasks, commit to a Sprint Goal that guides daily work.
Common mistakes & fixes
  • Anti-pattern: Team told what to do — fix by involving Development Team in commitment decisions.
  • Anti-pattern: Over-committing — fix by using realistic capacity and historical velocity.
  • Anti-pattern: Unrefined backlog — fix with regular backlog refinement prior to planning.
Key Takeaway

Sprint Planning creates a shared Sprint Goal and a realistic Sprint Backlog. Preparation and team ownership prevent over-commitment.
