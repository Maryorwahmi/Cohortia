Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Daily Scrum
Purpose & Rules
15-minute time-box for the Development Team to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the Sprint Backlog.
Held at the same time and place each day to create rhythm.
Focus on planning the next 24 hours; not a status report to managers.
Scrum Master ensures it occurs; Product Owner may attend as observer.
Example & Tip
Example: "I’m finishing the login feature; blocked on API key. I can pair with Alex after this meeting to remove the blocker."
Common mistake
Turning the Daily Scrum into lengthy technical problem-solving. Fix: schedule a short follow-up with just the needed team members.
Key Takeaway

Use the Daily Scrum to adapt the plan for the next day. Keep it short, focused, and action-oriented.
