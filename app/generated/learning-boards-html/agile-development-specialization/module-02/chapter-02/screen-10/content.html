Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Review → Action
Make feedback actionable
  1. Invite decision-makers who can change priorities.
  2. Demonstrate real functionality, not slides.
  3. Capture suggested changes as backlog items with acceptance details.
Avoid these mistakes
Logging feedback without owners or next steps leaves learning unused.
Treating Reviews as formal gates rather than collaborative adaptation points.
Key Takeaway

Finish Reviews with prioritized backlog changes and clear owners so feedback results in concrete next steps.
