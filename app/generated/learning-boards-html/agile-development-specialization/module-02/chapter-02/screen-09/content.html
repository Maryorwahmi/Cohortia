Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Sprint Review
Purpose & Activities
  • Inspect the Increment and gather stakeholder feedback.
  • Product Owner explains what is Done and what is not.
  • Collaborate on what to do next, updating the Product Backlog.
Example — 2-week Sprint
Time-box: ~2 hours. Demonstrate features, collect feedback, and list Product Backlog adjustments for prioritization.
Anti-pattern & remedy
Anti-pattern: a one-way demo without stakeholder collaboration. Remedy: invite active stakeholders, ask for specific feedback, and agree on backlog updates before closing the Review.
Key Takeaway

Treat the Review as a joint inspection that leads directly to Product Backlog adaptations and informed next steps.
