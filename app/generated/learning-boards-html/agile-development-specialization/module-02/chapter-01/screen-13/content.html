Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
Product Owner
Accountable for maximizing product value through backlog management and prioritization.
Scrum Master
Servant-leader who coaches, facilitates, and removes impediments so Scrum can work effectively.
Development Team
Self-organizing, cross-functional professionals who deliver a Done increment each Sprint.
Summary takeaway
Respect these accountabilities, collaborate continuously, and protect the team’s ability to deliver predictable value.
