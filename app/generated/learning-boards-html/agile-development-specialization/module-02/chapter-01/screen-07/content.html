Characteristics

Self-organizing & Cross-functional

  • The team chooses how to accomplish work and who will do it.
  • Members collectively possess design, development, testing, and other needed skills.
  • No fixed titles inside the team; everyone is a Developer with shared accountability.
Concrete example

A team delivering a secure login feature splits tasks so frontend, backend, and security testing happen as a coordinated effort, not as isolated handoffs.

Misconceptions
  • Cross-functional does not require every person to be expert in every skill.
  • Self-organization does not mean lack of coordination; it means the team coordinates internally without external assignment.
Key Takeaway
A team that organizes itself and covers needed skills completes work faster and reduces dependencies.
Pro tip
Encourage pair work and knowledge sharing so the team becomes more robust and flexible.
