Assessment Idea

Who acts when interruptions occur?

  1. Surface the interruption and its impact on the Sprint Goal.
  2. Product Owner evaluates priority and possible backlog reorder.
  3. Scrum Master negotiates with stakeholders and removes process blockers.
  4. Development Team adapts the plan if the work is accepted into the Sprint.
Concrete example

A security hotfix appears during a Sprint. The Product Owner assesses urgency; the Scrum Master expedites approvals and shields the team from unrelated requests; developers integrate the fix and update tests to keep the increment releasable.

Key Takeaway

Protection is a disciplined practice. The Scrum Master mediates interruptions; the Product Owner decides priorities and the Development Team implements the change.
