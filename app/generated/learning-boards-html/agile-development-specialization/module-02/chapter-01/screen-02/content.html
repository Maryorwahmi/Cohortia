How to use these objectives
  • Use objective 01 to map stakeholder requests to accountable roles.
  • Use objective 02 to design meetings and interactions that support the Sprint Goal.
  • Use objective 03 to spot and correct role drift early.
  • Use objective 04 to recruit or train a team with the right mix of skills.
Why this matters
Clear objectives help teams adopt Scrum intentionally and avoid common pitfalls that slow delivery.
