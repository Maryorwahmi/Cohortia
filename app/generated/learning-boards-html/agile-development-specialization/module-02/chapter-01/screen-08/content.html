Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Anti-Patterns
Proxy Product Owner

Problem: PO just relays stakeholder requests.

Fix: Empower the PO to make trade-offs and add strategic context; require backlog decisions to be explicit.

Task-master Scrum Master

Problem: SM assigns tasks and controls execution.

Fix: Coach with questions, enable team decisions, and remove blocking organizational constraints instead of assigning work.

Siloed Developers

Problem: Specialists hoard work and create handoffs.

Fix: Encourage pairing, shared ownership, and expand the Definition of Done to include integration and testing by the team.
