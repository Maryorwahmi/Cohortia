Process

Backlog management workflow

1
Gather and clarify needs
Collect stakeholder input and translate into clear backlog items with acceptance criteria.
2
Order by value and risk
Prioritize items to maximize impact, balancing short-term wins and long-term goals.
3
Refine with Developers
Collaborate to split stories, estimate, and remove ambiguity before Sprint planning.
Concrete example

A Product Owner reviews customer support data, decides the login flow causes most friction, prioritizes a secure login enhancement, and provides acceptance criteria that define success.

Common anti-pattern

When the PO acts as a message relay, backlog items pile up unprioritized. Correct by empowering the PO to make trade-off decisions and make priorities transparent.

Key Takeaway

The Product Owner ensures the backlog is the single source of prioritized work and adds strategic insight, not just stakeholder wishes.

Pro tip
Use small, frequent refinement sessions to keep backlog items ready for the next Sprint.
