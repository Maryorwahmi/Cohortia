Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Three Accountabilities
Definition

Product Owner

Accountable for maximizing product value by managing and ordering the Product Backlog. Ensures backlog transparency and clear Product Backlog items.

Definition

Scrum Master

Servant-leader who ensures Scrum is understood and enacted, coaches self-organization, removes impediments, and supports Product Backlog practices.

Definition

Development Team

Self-organizing, cross-functional professionals who deliver a potentially releasable Done increment at each Sprint's end.

Practical examples
  • Product Owner: Decides the priority of a new payment feature after reviewing customer data.
  • Scrum Master: Coaches the team to run an effective Sprint Retrospective and removes blocking approvals from outside the team.
  • Development Team: Determines how to split a story into tasks, including design, coding, and tests, to meet the Definition of Done.
Key Takeaway
Treat these as accountabilities to assign decisions, not as rigid job titles.
Common Mistake
Assuming Developers should wait for assignments rather than self-organize who does the work.
