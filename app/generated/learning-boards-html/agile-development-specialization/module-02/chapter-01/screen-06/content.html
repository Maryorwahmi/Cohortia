Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Scrum Master
Role focus

Coach, Remove Impediments, Protect Focus

  • Coach team in self-organization and cross-functionality.
  • Facilitate Scrum events to ensure they are effective and timeboxed.
  • Remove impediments, including organizational obstacles outside the team.
Common Misunderstanding
The Scrum Master is not a project manager who assigns work. That undermines self-organization and reduces team ownership.
Concrete scenario

Stakeholders interrupt the team with urgent tasks. The Scrum Master intervenes by coaching stakeholders, defining a clear request path to the Product Owner, and protecting the Sprint Goal so the team can finish their committed work.

Key Takeaway
Scrum Masters create conditions for teams to thrive rather than directing daily tasks.
Pro tip
Use coaching questions in retrospectives to guide teams toward self-solved impediments instead of providing solutions immediately.
