Architecture

Role interactions across Scrum events

Backlog Refinement
PO clarifies; Developers split and estimate; SM coaches the session.
Sprint Planning
PO explains goals; Developers commit to a plan; SM ensures understanding and timeboxing.
Daily Scrum
Developers inspect and adapt; SM coaches cadence; PO participates only for clarification.
Sprint Review
PO inspects increment with stakeholders; feedback informs backlog order.
Sprint Retrospective
Team inspects process; SM facilitates improvements and removes systemic impediments.
Concrete walkthrough

When a payment gateway is blocked, the SM negotiates access with infrastructure teams, the PO reprioritizes backlog if needed, and Developers adjust their plan to avoid idle time.

Key Takeaway

Roles collaborate frequently, but responsibilities remain distinct: ordering, enabling, and delivering.
