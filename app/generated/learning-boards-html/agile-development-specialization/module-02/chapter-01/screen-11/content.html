Activity list
  1. Prioritizing new features based on market research — Product Owner
  2. Facilitating Daily Scrum to identify blockers — Scrum Master
  3. Writing code for a secure login module — Development Team
  4. Ensuring understanding of the Definition of Done — Scrum Master & Development Team
  5. Gathering stakeholder requirements for a release — Product Owner
  6. Helping the team improve estimation in a retrospective — Scrum Master
  7. Testing newly developed features — Development Team
  8. Deciding which tasks to pull from the Sprint Backlog today — Development Team
Reflection
When you explain your mapping, justify which accountability makes the decision and which role supports or enables it.
Concrete example

If stakeholders ask for an urgent promo feature, the Product Owner decides how it fits with priorities. The Scrum Master protects the team from interruptions while the Development Team adapts implementation choices to meet the Sprint Goal if the item is accepted.

Key Takeaway
Practice mapping activities to roles to reduce ambiguity and speed decisions during a Sprint.
