Weighted Shortest Job First

Prioritize items by dividing Cost of Delay by Job Size to surface the highest economic value per effort.

Components
  • Cost of Delay: business value, time criticality, risk reduction
  • Job Size: estimated effort or duration
Example

A small, time-critical compliance fix with high business value will rate highly under WSJF, even if larger features also have value.

Practical notes

Use WSJF to inform ordering in contexts with many competing high-value items. Update inputs as understanding evolves.

Key Takeaway

WSJF helps prioritize to maximize economic throughput, but inputs are relative and should be revisited.
