MoSCoW Prioritization

Classify items as Must, Should, Could, or Won't have to make release trade-offs explicit.

Must have

Essential items without which the product is unusable (e.g., view balance).

Should have

Important items that add significant value but are not critical.

Could have

Nice-to-haves that can be deferred if time is limited, like theme customization.

Won't have (this time)

Explicitly deferred items to reduce scope and focus the release.

Example activity

Use the MoSCoW categories during refinement to quickly align the team and stakeholders on what must be delivered this release.

Key Takeaway

MoSCoW keeps focus on essential outcomes and makes scope trade-offs explicit.
