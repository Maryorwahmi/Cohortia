Ordering the Backlog

The Product Owner orders items considering value, dependencies, and team input.

Example

An infrastructure task may be prioritized ahead of a visible feature to avoid blocking future deliveries.

Collaboration

The Product Owner consults engineers on dependencies and effort so ordering is realistic.

Pitfall

Letting urgent stakeholder requests constantly interrupt the ordered backlog erodes predictability.

Key Takeaway

Ordering requires balancing short-term value and long-term strategy while avoiding blocked work.

Reflect

Which item in your backlog is high-value but blocked by a technical dependency? How would you reorder to reduce delay?
