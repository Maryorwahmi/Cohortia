Common Anti-Patterns

Recognize behaviors that lead to unclear work and unpredictable sprints.

Big-bang grooming

Waiting until planning to do all refinement causes rushed decisions and poor estimates.

Delegated prioritization

Leaving prioritization only to the team or stakeholders without a strategic owner reduces clarity.

Backlog as wish list

A backlog of unmanaged requests becomes noise and erodes focus.

Contrast

Replace large, infrequent sessions with short, regular refinement conversations. Ensure the Product Owner applies a strategic lens when ordering items.

Key Takeaway

Small, frequent refinement habits and clear owner accountability prevent the common anti-patterns from emerging.
