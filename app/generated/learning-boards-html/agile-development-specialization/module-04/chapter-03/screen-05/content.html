Process Flow

Ongoing refinement steps

Refinement repeats as items evolve from idea to ready-to-work stories.

Step 1 — Add items

Capture ideas, feedback, and bugs in the backlog as they arise.

Step 2 — Split epics

Break large epics into small, independently valuable stories.

Step 3 — Clarify

Add acceptance criteria and discuss potential technical approaches.

Step 4 — Estimate

Update story sizes as understanding improves and resurface uncertainties.

Step 5 — Order

Product Owner orders items to reflect current business goals and dependencies.

Example

An epic for payments is split into concrete stories, each with acceptance criteria and estimates so some can be pulled in the next sprint.

Key Takeaway

Make refinement a habit so refinement work spreads out and planning becomes predictable.
