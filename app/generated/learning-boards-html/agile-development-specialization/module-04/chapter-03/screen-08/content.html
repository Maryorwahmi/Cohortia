Estimating and Updating

Estimates evolve as teams uncover technical details and acceptance criteria are clarified.

Concrete example

A transfer story grows from 5 to 8 points after the team identifies authentication work and external APIs.

Misconception

Estimates are not immutable commitments. They are forecasts to guide prioritization and sprint planning.

Estimation habits
  • Estimate relative to completed work.
  • Revisit estimates when acceptance criteria change.
  • Keep conversations short and focused.
Key Takeaway

Use updated estimates to make better prioritization decisions and create realistic sprint plans.
