Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Product Backlog
A dynamic, ordered list serving as the single source of requirements.
Backlog Refinement
Ongoing collaboration to add detail, estimates, and order to items.
Prioritization
Ordering items by relative importance, value, and dependencies.
Takeaway

Maintain a visible, ordered backlog through continuous refinement and choose prioritization techniques that fit your context.
