Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Acceptance Criteria

Write clear acceptance criteria

Criteria should be testable, specific, and reflect the user outcome.

Concrete example

For 'view account balance': balance must refresh within 10 seconds; display currency and mask account numbers.

Team conversation

During refinement, the team asks how data will be fetched and whether offline states exist, surfacing hidden complexity early.

Common mistake

Leaving acceptance criteria incomplete causes clarifying questions mid-sprint and unpredictable scope changes.

Key Takeaway

Define acceptance criteria during refinement so the team can estimate and build with confidence.
