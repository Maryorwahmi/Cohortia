Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Product Backlog
Definition

Product Backlog as Single Source of Truth

A backlog holds every known requirement, ordered by value and readiness, and must be actively maintained to guide delivery.

Concrete example

A banking backlog lists 'view balance' above 'customize theme' because balance is core to usability and higher value.

Common misconception

Treating the backlog as static creates a stale plan; continuous refinement keeps it aligned to changing needs.

Key Takeaway

A living backlog enables predictable delivery by keeping high-value, ready items at the top.
