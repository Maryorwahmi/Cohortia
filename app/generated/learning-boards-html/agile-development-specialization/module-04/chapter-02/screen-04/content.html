Core Concept › What are Acceptance Criteria?
Definition & purpose
Acceptance criteria set observable conditions used to declare a user story done and accepted by the Product Owner.
Why it matters
They prevent misunderstandings, guide testing, and reduce rework.
Common misconception
Not a test plan or technical spec. Keep them focused on behavior and outcomes, not implementation.
Key takeaway
Write criteria as observable statements that QA can verify and the PO can accept.
Concrete example (login story)
Scenario: Successful Login
Given I am on the login page
And I have a registered account with username "testuser" and password "Password123"
When I enter "testuser" into the username field
And I enter "Password123" into the password field
And I click the "Login" button
Then I should be redirected to my account dashboard
And I should see a welcome message with my username
This scenario is observable: redirect and visible welcome message are measurable checks that determine success.
Misstep
Saying "The UI should be user friendly" is too vague to accept or reject.
Pro tip
Make results observable: use specific messages, redirects, or element states.
