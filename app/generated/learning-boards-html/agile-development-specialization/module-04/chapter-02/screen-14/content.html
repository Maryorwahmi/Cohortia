Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
Acceptance Criteria
Conditions a story must meet to be considered complete and accepted.
Gherkin Syntax
Given‑When‑Then structure used to write clear, testable scenarios.
Definition of Done (DoD)
Team agreement on quality; acceptance criteria supply story‑level evidence to meet it.
Summary

Write focused, observable acceptance criteria using Gherkin and refine them with PO, dev, and QA to reduce ambiguity and speed delivery.
