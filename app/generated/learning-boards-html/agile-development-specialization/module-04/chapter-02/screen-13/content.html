Assessment › Evaluating Acceptance Criteria
Quick evaluation steps
  1. Is the outcome observable by the user or tester?
  2. Does it avoid implementation details?
  3. Is it necessary for acceptance or better left to QA?
Example question
Which criterion best accepts an enrollment story? The Gherkin scenario that names a precondition, action, and observable result is best because it is testable and user centered.
Common fixes for weak criteria
  • Replace subjective words with measurable thresholds or exact messages.
  • Split compound statements into separate scenarios.
  • Prioritize checks that block delivery and move low‑priority tests to QA artifacts.
Key takeaway
Judge acceptance criteria by whether they allow a tester to declare pass or fail without assumptions, and whether they protect product value.
