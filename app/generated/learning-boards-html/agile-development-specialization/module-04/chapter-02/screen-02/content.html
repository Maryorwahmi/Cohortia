Learning Objectives
Build testable acceptance criteria and collaborate effectively to define done.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain the role of acceptance criteria
Describe how criteria form the Definition of Done and help the team agree when work is complete. This matters because it prevents disputes at demo and release.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Formulate Gherkin acceptance criteria
Write clear Given‑When‑Then scenarios that are human readable and directly testable by QA. This saves time in test creation and automation.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Distinguish clear vs ambiguous criteria
Recognize vague statements and convert them into measurable expectations to reduce rework and misalignment.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Collaborate to elicit and refine criteria
Engage Product Owner, developers, and QA to uncover edge cases and make acceptance steps testable and realistic.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
