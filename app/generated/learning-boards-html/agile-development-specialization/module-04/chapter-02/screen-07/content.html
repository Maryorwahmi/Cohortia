Comparison › Good vs Vague Acceptance Criteria
Good
Measurable and observable
Example: "Given I am on a product page, When I click 'Add to Cart', Then the item appears in My Cart and a confirmation message reads 'Added to cart'."
Why this works
It names the state change and the exact message to check, enabling QA to reproduce and verify acceptance quickly.
Vague
Subjective or broad language
Example: "The UI should be user friendly and fast."
Why this fails
No one can verify "user friendly" without acceptance criteria that specify measurable cues, and "fast" lacks thresholds and measurement conditions.
Rewrite practice
Pick a vague criterion and rewrite it as a Given‑When‑Then scenario with observable outcomes. Doing so reveals assumptions and makes tests executable.
