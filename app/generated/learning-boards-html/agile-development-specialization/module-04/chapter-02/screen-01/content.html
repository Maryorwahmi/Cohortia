Crafting Effective Acceptance Criteria
Translate user intent into specific, testable conditions that form your Definition of Done.
In this lesson you'll learn the Gherkin Given‑When‑Then pattern, see concrete scenarios for login and event creation, and practice avoiding vague or exhaustive lists. Acceptance criteria are a short, shared contract that teams use to confirm completion and reduce rework.
