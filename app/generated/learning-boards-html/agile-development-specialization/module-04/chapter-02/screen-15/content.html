Chapter 4.2 › What to Remember
Make criteria observable
Describe outcomes testers can verify, like redirects, messages, or data changes.
Use Given‑When‑Then
Structure scenarios to set context, action, and expected result for clarity.
Collaborate early
PO, developers, and QA together refine criteria and reveal assumptions before coding.
Final exercise

Pick an ambiguous backlog item and rewrite its acceptance criteria as a focused Gherkin scenario. Notice how the rewrite clarifies acceptance and reduces assumptions.
