Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Planning Poker
Step-by-step
  1. Product Owner reads story and answers questions.
  2. Team privately selects a Fibonacci card.
  3. Simultaneous reveal and discussion of extremes.
  4. Re-vote until consensus or compromise.
Facilitation tips
Use anonymous reveal, limit influence, and keep discussions focused and time-boxed to avoid drawn-out debates.
Concrete example
For 'add to cart', some pick 3, others 8. The 8-point view mentions integration with inventory and promo rules; that reveals hidden complexity and shifts the final estimate.
Common mistake
Allowing early reveals or a dominant voice undermines independent thinking and reduces the method's effectiveness.
Key Takeaway

Simultaneous reveal and discussion of extremes uncover assumptions and yield stronger shared estimates.
