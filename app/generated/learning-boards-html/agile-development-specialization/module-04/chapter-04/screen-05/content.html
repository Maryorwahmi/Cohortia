Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Story Points
What a Story Point captures
  • Effort: work required to complete the story.
  • Complexity: algorithmic or integration difficulty.
  • Uncertainty: unknowns and risk that could increase effort.
Modified Fibonacci
Sequences like 1,2,3,5,8,13 reflect rising uncertainty; choose larger jumps as size increases to represent greater unknowns.
Key Takeaway

A point is comparative. Always anchor estimates to a clear baseline example to keep them meaningful.

Concrete example
Baseline: 'Logging in' = 3 points. 'Password reset' similar → 3 points. 'New payment gateway integration' higher complexity and unknowns → 13 points.
Misconception check
Don’t map points to hours. Two teams may map 5 points differently; points are team-relative and intended for planning and forecasting.
