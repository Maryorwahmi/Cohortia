Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Velocity
Calculation
Sum the story points of all fully completed stories in a sprint. Average across recent sprints for forecasting.
Proper use
Use velocity to plan how many points to pull into a sprint and to forecast releases; accept variation and use averages.
Key Takeaway

Velocity informs planning; never use it to rank or pressure teams or individuals.

Concrete example
Team completes 20 and then 22 points; average is 21. For a 100-point backlog, expect about 5 sprints, allowing for variability and scope changes.
Misconception: higher velocity equals better team. Reality: velocity is a planning tool and varies by team context and point scale.
