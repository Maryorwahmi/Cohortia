Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Planning Poker Pitfalls
Anchoring
Early visible numbers cause others to conform. Fix: simultaneous reveal and private card selection.
Dominance
Senior voices can sway estimates. Fix: ask for brief rationale from extremes and encourage all voices.
False precision
Mapping points to hours invites pressure. Fix: use velocity for forecasting, not points-to-hours conversions.
Practical checklist
  • Private selection and simultaneous reveal.
  • Time-box discussion; focus on clarifying assumptions.
  • Record causes of discrepancy and address them in refinement.
Common mistake

Using estimates to compare team performance will damage trust and destroy the value of estimation.
