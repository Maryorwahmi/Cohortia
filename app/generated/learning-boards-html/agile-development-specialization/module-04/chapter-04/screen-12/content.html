Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Planning Poker Simulation
Activity instructions
  1. Read each user story and imagine you're a developer.
  2. Choose a Story Point from the Fibonacci sequence and note one reason.
  3. Compare hypothetical estimates and discuss differences to reach consensus.
User Story 1
Add items to shopping cart — consider UI state, persistence, and inventory checks.
User Story 2
View detailed product information — images, descriptions, pricing, and layout concerns.
User Story 3
Apply discount code at checkout — business rules, validation, and pricing impact.
Reflection prompt

If others estimate differently, ask what assumptions they made, and what unknowns you can clarify to reduce variance going forward.

Example log
Story 1: 3 — simple UI and existing cart service
Story 2: 5 — images and formatting, moderate work
Story 3: 8 — discount rules plus edge-case validations
Takeaway
Write down reasons with each estimate; the reasons are the source of improved future estimates.
