Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › T-shirt & Affinity
T-shirt sizing
Label items S, M, L, XL for quick prioritization. Later map sizes to point ranges for sprint planning.
Affinity Estimation
Group similar items together quickly and then assign point values to the groups. Good for large backlogs.
Concrete scenario
During early discovery for an e-commerce site, quickly mark UI copy tweaks as S, product import tasks as L, and a cross-border payment compliance feature as XL to focus refinement effort.
Reminder: refine coarse groupings with Story Points in later sessions to build a velocity-backed plan.
Key Takeaway

Use coarse sizing for early ordering; follow up with relative points and consensus methods for sprint-ready estimates.
