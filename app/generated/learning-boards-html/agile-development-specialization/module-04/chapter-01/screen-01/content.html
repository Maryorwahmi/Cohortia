Understanding User Stories
A practical guide to writing short, testable, and valuable descriptions of desired functionality that focus on user needs and outcomes.
In this lesson
Write stories that start conversations
Focus on user, goal, and benefit. Judge stories with INVEST.
Explore Key Topics
Template
As a [role], I want [goal], so that [benefit].
INVEST
Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable.
Pitfalls
Avoid epics, avoid technical 'how' in the story.
This board prepares you to turn vague requests into focused user stories, making backlog planning and sprint delivery more predictable and user-focused.
