Common Pitfalls
Technical 'how'
Specifying implementation in the story reduces negotiation and blocks better design choices.
Oversized epics
Large stories that can't be finished in a sprint should be split into smaller, testable slices.
Missing benefit
Without the 'so that' clause, the value and priority are unclear and testing becomes difficult.
Quick recipe to fix problems
Rewrite to name the user and benefit, split large stories into vertical slices, and add acceptance criteria for testability.
