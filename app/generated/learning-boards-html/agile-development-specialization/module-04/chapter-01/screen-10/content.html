Core Concept
Estimable explained
Teams should be able to estimate a story's size. If they cannot, add clarification or split the work into smaller stories.
Use discovery spikes for unknowns or define acceptance details that make estimates possible.
Scenario
A story referencing 'popular' articles must define the metric before it becomes estimable. Clarify the metric, scope, and display format first.
This definition enables the team to estimate data queries, UI work, and testing required.
Common mistake
Assuming the team can estimate without clarifying ambiguous terms or scope.
Check
If estimates vary wildly, refine the story until estimates converge.
