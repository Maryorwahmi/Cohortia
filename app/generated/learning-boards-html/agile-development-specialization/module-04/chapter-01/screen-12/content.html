Core Concept
Testable explained
Ensure acceptance criteria exist so the team can verify the story meets its intended purpose.
  • • Acceptance criteria make success measurable.
  • • Tests can be manual or automated but must be explicit.
  • • Testability reduces ambiguity and supports demos.
Concrete acceptance example
Acceptance: User can pay with saved card; payment completes successfully and confirmation appears within 5 seconds in 95% of test trials.
This level of specificity lets developers and QA confirm whether the story is done.
Check
If you cannot write a simple acceptance test, refine the story until you can.
