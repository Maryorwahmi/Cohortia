Key Concepts
User Story
A short, user-centered description that invites conversation and focuses on value.
Template
Use 'As a [role], I want [goal], so that [benefit]' to structure stories for empathy and value.
INVEST
Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable — use as a readiness checklist.
Keep these three ideas handy when you review backlog items: user focus, clear template, and INVEST checks.
