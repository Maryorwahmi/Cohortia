Key Concepts
Epic
A large story that must be split into smaller, sprint-ready stories.
Value Proposition
The 'so that' clause that explains why the feature matters to the user or business.
Practice
Regular hands-on rewriting and INVEST checks are the fastest route to a healthy backlog.
Final tip: before a story enters a sprint, verify user, goal, and benefit, and confirm acceptance tests so the team can deliver with confidence.
