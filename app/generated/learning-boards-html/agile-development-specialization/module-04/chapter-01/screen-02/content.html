Articulate purpose & benefits
Explain why user stories replace heavy specs and how they keep teams user-focused and adaptable.

Construct the template
Use "As a [role], I want [goal], so that [benefit]" to capture user perspective, clear action, and value.
Mnemonic: three stacked labels (role, goal, benefit).

03
Evaluate with INVEST
Apply Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable as a readiness checklist.
Mnemonic: six-letter strip.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Differentiate from requirements docs
Know when to use a short story and when a longer spec or acceptance criteria are needed.
Mnemonic: comparison timeline card.
