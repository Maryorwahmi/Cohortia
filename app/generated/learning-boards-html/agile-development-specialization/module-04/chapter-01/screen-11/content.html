Core Concept
Small explained
Aim for stories that fit in a single sprint. Smaller stories mean faster feedback and clearer progress.
  • • Prefer vertical slices that deliver usable outcomes.
  • • Avoid splitting by technical layers only.
  • • Break epics into prioritized, testable stories.
Example slicing
Epic: Support payments. Split into: save card for quick checkout; charge a saved card at ride completion; show payment receipts to users.
Each slice delivers real value and can be validated independently.
Check
Can this be demonstrated and tested in a single sprint? If not, split further.
