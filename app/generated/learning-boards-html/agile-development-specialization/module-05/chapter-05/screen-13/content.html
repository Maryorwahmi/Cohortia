Role & Skill
Scrum Master: Facilitation & Safety
Guide the team through structured reflection while protecting psychological safety and ensuring follow-through.
Key behaviors
  • Remind the Prime Directive and meeting goals.
  • Use silent brainstorming and affinity mapping.
  • Timebox and convert decisions into backlog tasks.
Intervention model
If conversation becomes personal, pause, restate evidence, and reframe to a process question.
Checklists
  • Did we state the Prime Directive?
  • Are action items concrete with owners?
  • Will we review these items during the sprint?
Key Takeaway

The Scrum Master enables safe, evidence‑based reflection and converts insights into tracked experiments the team owns.
