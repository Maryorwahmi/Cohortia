Concept
Kaizen: Small Continuous Improvements
Small experiments done constantly produce sustainable improvements in workflow and quality.
Why Kaizen works
Lower risk, faster feedback, and a culture that values learning over blame.
Example experiment
Require test cases before stories enter the sprint and measure blocked story count at sprint end.
Cultural practices
  • Encourage everyone to propose experiments, not just leaders.
  • Accept failed experiments as learning, not personal failure.
  • Review the effect of experiments regularly and iterate.
Key Takeaway

Make improvement a habit: small, owned experiments tracked and inspected drive real progress.
