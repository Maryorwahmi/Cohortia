Common Pitfall
Too Many Action Items
Overloading the next sprint dilutes focus and prevents meaningful adoption of improvements.
Why it fails
Teams spread attention thinly and none of the experiments receives consistent effort or measurement.
Alternative
Rank ideas by impact and effort, then pick the top one or two achievable experiments.
Practical checklist
  • Limit to 1–2 items.
  • Assign a clear owner for each item.
  • Define how success will be measured.
  • Add items as backlog tasks and review regularly.
Key Takeaway

Focus increases completion. A small number of measured experiments yields more learning than many unfinished tasks.
