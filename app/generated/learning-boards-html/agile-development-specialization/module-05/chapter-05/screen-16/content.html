Prime Directive
Frame the meeting to focus on process and shared learning, not individual blame.
Gather Data
Use timelines and metrics to ground insights so experiments target root causes.
Action Items
Pick 1–2 measurable items, assign owners, add to backlog, and inspect results next retro.
One-sentence takeaway
Retrospectives become improvement engines when they are safe, evidence‑based, and result in a small number of tracked experiments with owners.
