Definition
Setting the Stage & the Prime Directive
A short opening that creates safety and prepares the team to discuss process issues without blaming individuals.
Check-in techniques
  • One‑word mood check.
  • Thumbs up / sideways / down summary.
  • Silent brainstorming to reduce anchoring.
Facilitator prompts
Read the Prime Directive aloud, remind people to focus on process, and set a goal for the meeting’s outcomes.
Misconception
Psychological safety does not mean avoiding critical discussion; it’s about framing feedback so failures become learning signals.
Pro tip
Use silent note writing for the first 5 minutes to gather more viewpoints and reduce early groupthink.
Prime Directive
"Regardless of what we discover, we understand and truly believe that everyone did the best job they could, given what they knew at the time, their skills and abilities, the resources available, and the situation at hand."
Check your facilitation
  • Did we state the Prime Directive?
  • Did everyone contribute at least one item?
  • Are action items focused on process not people?
