Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Concepts
Sprint Retrospective
A time‑boxed event to inspect team process and plan improvements.
Kaizen
Continuous small improvements made consistently by the team.
Psychological Safety
A safe environment where people can raise issues without fear of blame.
Summary Takeaway
Retrospectives succeed when they are evidence‑based, produce a small number of measurable experiments, assign owners, and close the loop by inspecting outcomes in the next sprint.
