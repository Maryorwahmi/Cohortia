Core Concept
What is the Sprint Retrospective?
A time‑boxed event where the team inspects past sprint work, identifies root causes, and agrees on concrete improvements.
Common misconception
Retrospectives are not vent sessions. They must end with actionable, measurable steps owned by the team.
Example
If stakeholder interruptions broke focus, propose routing updates through the Product Owner and measure interruption count next sprint.
Key Takeaway

Retrospectives should produce one or two measurable experiments with owners so the team can inspect the results next sprint.

Definition cards
Inspect and Adapt
Inspect the sprint and adapt the process through small experiments.
Prime Directive
Assume everyone did their best; focus on process, not people.
Outcome
A small set of concrete action items with owners and measurable targets.
