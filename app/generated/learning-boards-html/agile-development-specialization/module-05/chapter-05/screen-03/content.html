Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Facilitation & Follow-through
Facilitation essentials
  • Remind the team of the Prime Directive to reduce blame and support honest reflection.
  • Use prompts and silent brainstorming to include quieter voices.
  • Rotate formats to prevent predictability and disengagement.
  • Keep the session structured so action items are explicit, measurable, and owned.
Follow-through checklist
Add agreed action items to the sprint backlog as tasks.
Assign a single owner for each action and set a measurable target.
Review the status of these items during the sprint and start the next retrospective by inspecting their outcome.
