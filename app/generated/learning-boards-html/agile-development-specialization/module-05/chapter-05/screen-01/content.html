Continuous Improvement: Retrospectives and Kaizen
How regular reflection and small, owned experiments keep teams learning and improving.
Why retrospectives matter
They are the formalized time to inspect process, surface root causes, and create concrete experiments to improve the next sprint.
Kaizen connection
Kaizen encourages small incremental changes. Applied to sprints, it means pick one or two doable experiments, track them, and learn.
Explore Key Topics
Retrospective Flow

Formats
Start/Stop/Continue, Mad/Sad/Glad, Four L's — rotate formats to keep energy high.
Action Items
Pick one or two measurable items with owners and add them to the sprint backlog.
