Comparison
Popular Formats
Start / Stop / Continue
Structured, outcome oriented; great for process changes and easy to convert into action items.
Mad / Sad / Glad
Surfaces emotions and morale; useful when team climate affects performance.
Four L's
Encourages reflection on what was liked, learned, lacked, and longed for during the sprint.
How to choose
  • Pick Start/Stop/Continue when you need concrete process changes.
  • Use Mad/Sad/Glad to explore team morale and emotional signals.
  • Try Four L's for deeper reflection on learning and unmet needs.
Facilitator tip
Keep a format backlog and rotate deliberately. After each retrospective ask: did this format help us arrive at an owned action item?
