Definition & purpose

One guiding objective

The Sprint Goal explains why the increment matters. It keeps the team aligned and enables scope adjustments in service of value.

Good example
Enable customers to securely access and review their purchase history.
Bad example
Complete stories A, B, and C.
Reflection

Convert your planned stories into one sentence that states customer benefit and measurable intent.

Why it helps
  • Provides focus during mid-sprint trade-offs.
  • Communicates value to stakeholders succinctly.
  • Enables flexibility when implementation details change.
Check
Misconception
A Sprint Goal is not a wish list; it must be achievable and linked to the selected backlog items.
