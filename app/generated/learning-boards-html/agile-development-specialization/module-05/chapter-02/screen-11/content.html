Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Pitfalls & Scrum Master Role
Common mistakes
  • Overcommitment by ignoring current capacity adjustments.
  • Product Owner dictating scope; no negotiation.
  • Insufficient task breakdown that hides dependencies.
  • Zero buffer for unplanned work or bug fixes.
Remedy

Facilitate negotiation, adjust velocity for availability, require task breakdown, and reserve a small buffer.

Scrum Master actions
Facilitate a prioritization conversation when the Product Owner’s requests exceed capacity.
Encourage transparent estimates and highlight capacity adjustments.
Coach the team to break down stories and identify risky tasks early.
Assessment prompt
Multiple choice example
If the Product Owner insists on items beyond capacity, the Scrum Master should facilitate a negotiation to prioritize the most valuable subset and defer others.
