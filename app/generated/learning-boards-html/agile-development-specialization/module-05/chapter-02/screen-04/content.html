Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › What is Sprint Planning?
Definition

A collaborative commitment ceremony

Sprint Planning produces the Sprint Backlog and a Sprint Goal. It answers the two questions: what will be done, and how will it get done?

Example scenario
Team selects two customer stories and one small tech task to match capacity.
Misconception
Not a place for the Product Owner to unilaterally set scope; selection is collaborative.
Key Takeaway

Sprint Planning turns priority into a realistic plan and an aligned Sprint Goal that the team owns.

Quick checklist
  • Product Owner presents highest-priority items and value.
  • Team asks clarifying questions and inspects acceptance criteria.
  • Team selects items by capacity and velocity.
  • Team breaks stories into tasks and records estimates.
  • Team agrees on a single Sprint Goal.
