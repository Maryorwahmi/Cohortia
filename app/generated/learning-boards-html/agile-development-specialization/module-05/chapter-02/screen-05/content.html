Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Selecting Product Backlog Items
Process

Negotiate scope with value and capacity

The Product Owner explains value. The team inspects, asks questions, and selects items that match capacity. Agreement is a commitment.

Concrete check
For each chosen item, state how it contributes to the Sprint Goal and how many story points it consumes.
Avoid this mistake
Accepting dictated scope or failing to factor in vacations and training.
Pro tip

Include a small unallocated buffer to handle urgent fixes or integration surprises.
