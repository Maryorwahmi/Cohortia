Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts
Sprint Planning
A time-boxed event where the team plans the upcoming sprint and creates the Sprint Backlog.
Sprint Goal
A short objective that guides decisions and preserves value when scope shifts.
Capacity Planning
Adjust velocity for team availability and reserve buffer for unplanned work.
Takeaway

Plan collaboratively: select valuable items by adjusted capacity, break them into tasks, and create a Sprint Goal that keeps the team aligned.
