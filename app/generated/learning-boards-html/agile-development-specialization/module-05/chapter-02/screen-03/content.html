Practice goals
  • Simulate selection using a team velocity (e.g., 25 story points).
  • Draft a focused Sprint Goal that communicates value to stakeholders.
  • Break one user story into 5–7 technical tasks covering all layers.
  • Reflect on risks and mitigations to avoid common planning errors.

What is one risk and how would you detect it early in the sprint?
