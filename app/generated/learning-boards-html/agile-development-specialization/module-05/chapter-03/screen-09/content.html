Affinity Estimation steps
A silent, visual grouping technique for bulk backlog sizing.
  1. Prepare story cards and an open board with empty buckets (Small, Medium, Large).
  2. Team silently places cards into buckets based on perceived similarity.
  3. Review placements and move obvious outliers.
  4. Assign story points to each bucket and refine ambiguous cards.
Why it works
Silence reduces anchoring. Visual grouping reveals natural clusters and speeds bulk sizing.
Key Takeaway

Affinity Estimation makes large backlog sizing fast and identifies where refinement effort should be focused.

Practical tip
Keep the first pass under ten minutes and only discuss cards that multiple people placed into different buckets.
Common mistake
Debating every card defeats the purpose. Use silence and quick passes to let natural similarity emerge before refining.
Try this
Run affinity on a 50-item backlog subset to find high-level clusters and then assign point ranges per bucket.
