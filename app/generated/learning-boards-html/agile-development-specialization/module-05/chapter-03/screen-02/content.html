Learning Objectives
What you will be able to do and why it helps your team plan better
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain Relative Sizing
Describe why teams compare items rather than predict exact hours.
This skill helps you avoid false precision. When you compare two stories, you surface complexity without promising a clock value.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use Story Points Consistently
Set and use a baseline so points map to team experience.
This develops consistent forecasts. A baseline example makes future sizing more stable across sprints.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Facilitate Planning Poker
Run reveal-and-discuss sessions to reach shared estimates.
This encourages full-team input and uncovers divergent assumptions that affect implementation risk.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply Alternatives
Choose T-shirt or Affinity methods for early or bulk sizing.
Picking the right technique speeds backlog grooming and helps teams make coarse forecasts without overcommitting to detail.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
