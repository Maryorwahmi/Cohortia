Relative Sizing
Compare items against a baseline to reduce false precision and reveal uncertainty.
Story Points
An abstract measure of effort, complexity and uncertainty used for team forecasting.
Planning Poker
A consensus-driven reveal-and-discuss technique that prevents anchoring and surfaces assumptions.
Summary
Use comparison, collaborative discussion, and regular calibration to make estimation a useful planning tool rather than a promise.
