What is T-shirt Sizing?
A fast, high-level scale using XS through XL for large or uncertain work.

• Fast alignment on scope for epics or early backlog rounds.

• Useful to triage which items need deeper analysis and which can be deferred.

• Avoid mapping sizes to precise hours until the team refines the stories.

Example mapping
XS: trivial UI tweak. S: small feature. M: multi-step feature. L/XL: cross-cutting or multi-component work that likely needs breaking down.
Key Takeaway

T-shirt Sizing speeds high-level planning and signals which items need follow-up refinement and point estimation.

When to use it
During early roadmap conversations, backlog triage, or when epics lack sufficient detail for points.
Pitfall
Do not convert sizes directly into hours or compare sizes across teams without calibration; they are context-specific.
Try this
Run a quick T-shirt Sizing round over ten backlog items to identify which need deeper refinement.
