Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Choosing the Right Technique
Compare techniques
A quick guide to when each method is most effective.
Story Points + Planning Poker
Use for sprint-level planning and consistent forecasting when stories are well understood.
T-shirt Sizing
Use during early discovery or roadmap planning to get a high-level sense of scope quickly.
Affinity Estimation
Use for large backlogs and bulk sizing; it clusters similar items fast and reduces meeting overhead.
Decision rule
If you need detail for the next sprint choose points. If you need speed for roadmap planning choose T-shirt. For many items choose Affinity.
Common error
Avoid switching methods mid-stream without recalibrating your baseline; different approaches create different reference frames.
