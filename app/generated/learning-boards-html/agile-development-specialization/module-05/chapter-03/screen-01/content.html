Agile Estimation Techniques
Learn how teams estimate by comparison, not by exact hours, and use collaborative rituals to surface assumptions.

Estimation in Agile focuses on relative sizing to cope with uncertainty. Story Points encode effort, complexity, and risk rather than calendar time. Planning Poker is a team ritual that turns estimation into a consensus-building conversation.

Why it matters: relative estimates reduce false precision and build shared understanding, making planning adaptive and more accurate over time.
