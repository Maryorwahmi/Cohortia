Planning Poker Process
A five-step reveal-and-discuss technique to reach a shared estimate.
1
Discuss the story
Product Owner reads the story and answers clarifying questions so everyone understands scope.
2
Private estimate
Each team member picks a Fibonacci card privately to avoid anchoring.
3
Reveal simultaneously
All cards are shown at once so the group sees the full spread of opinions.
4
Discuss divergences
Highest and lowest estimators explain assumptions and risks to reveal hidden context.
5
Re-estimate to consensus
Repeat until the team agrees or selects a pragmatic compromise after clarifying actions.
Key Takeaway

Planning Poker avoids anchoring and makes assumptions visible, producing better shared estimates.

Example reveal
Cards shown: 3, 5, 5, 13, 21. Ask the 21 and 3 to explain — maybe 21 expects complex processing while 3 assumes a library handles most work.
Misconception
Planning Poker is not a popularity contest. It is a structured conversation: the goal is shared understanding, not forcing identical reasoning.
Check your understanding
