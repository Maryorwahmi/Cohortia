Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Planning Poker Simulation — Stories A, B, C
Stories for practice
Baseline: one-point story = add a new field to a form without validation.
Story A
Login with email and password.
Consider authentication logic, hashing, and session management when sizing.
Story B
Export registered users to CSV.
Consider data volume, query performance, and download streaming.
Story C
Email notification on order status changes.
Consider integration with email service, templating, and triggers.
Simulated team reveal
Example reveal for Story A: 5, 5, 8, 3, 5. Ask the highest and lowest to explain their reasoning to find hidden assumptions.
Reflection prompts
  • • What assumption would raise Story A from 5 to 13?
  • • Does Story B require pagination or streaming for large exports?
  • • For Story C, do you have an existing email service or will you integrate a new one?
Key Takeaway

A quick poker simulation makes assumptions visible and produces an agreed estimate plus any follow-up items like spikes.
