Anti-Patterns
How metrics are misused
List of common misuses: using velocity to measure individuals, cross-team velocity comparisons, inflating estimates, and treating forecasts as commitments without updating them.
Weaponization risk
When metrics are used for blame, teams hide problems and manipulate numbers rather than improve flow.
Healthy practice
Make data visible, ask diagnostic questions, and run experiments to address the root cause.
Mini-waterfall example
A sprint with a flat burn-down then a late spike indicates work is not reaching 'Done' incrementally—likely integration or testing was delayed. Remediate by shifting testing earlier and breaking work into smaller, completable slices.
Pro-tip

Use short experiments such as enforcing WIP limits or pairing across handoffs, then measure effects with the same metrics.
