Assessment
Flat then drop burn-down
This pattern most often points to delayed integration/testing and large story batches. It reduces predictability and increases risk.
Immediate checks
  • Were stories passing acceptance criteria early?
  • Were tests or environments blocking delivery?
  • Were stories broken into small, testable increments?
Suggested experiments
  1. Enforce incremental 'Done'—at least one story completed by mid-sprint.
  2. Reduce batch size; slice stories into smaller deliverables.
  3. Shift testing earlier or pair dev and QA to remove handoffs.
Key Takeaway

Use the burn-down shape to design targeted experiments that promote continuous delivery, not end-of-sprint heroics.
