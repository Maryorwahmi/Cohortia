Definition
Lead time and cycle time
Lead time is full request-to-delivery latency. Cycle time is active work start-to-delivery. Use both to pinpoint waiting versus work duration.
Example
Backlog wait 5 days + active work 3 days = lead time 8 days, cycle time 3 days.
Where to inspect
If lead time is long but cycle time short, focus on prioritization, readiness, or handoffs rather than coding speed.
Practical steps
  1. Measure lead and cycle times for representative items over a period.
  2. Plot distributions to find typical and tail latencies.
  3. Design experiments to reduce the largest contributors, such as clearer acceptance criteria or parallel testing.
Key Takeaway

Lead time shows customer-facing latency; cycle time shows how long developers spend actively working. Use both to choose improvement experiments.
