Process
Reading a CFD
Bands represent states. Vertical widening shows accumulation. Horizontal distance approximates lead/cycle time for an item moving through states.
Detection
A widening Testing band suggests testing is the bottleneck and should be the focus of improvement experiments.
Action
Set WIP limits or shift capacity to the bottleneck to reduce queues and shorten lead time.
Concrete scenario
If your CFD shows a steady increase in 'Testing' items, try a short experiment: add test automation time or pair developers with testers for quicker feedback. Measure whether the Testing band narrows in subsequent weeks.
Key Takeaway

CFDs make queues visible. Use them to identify systemic bottlenecks and design experiments that reduce WIP and improve flow.
