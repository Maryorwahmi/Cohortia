Architecture
Compose a metrics dashboard
Include complementary views: sprint burn-down, release burn-up, velocity trend, lead/cycle time, and a small CFD. Use annotations and experiment history, not just numbers.
Decision flow
Signal → Question → Hypothesis → Experiment. Let metrics drive short experiments, then observe changes in the dashboard.
Context matters
Always annotate metric shifts with recent changes so the team can connect cause and effect.
Practical checklist
  • Limit dashboard views to signals the team will act on.
  • Use short narratives and recommended experiments alongside graphs.
  • Revisit dashboard contents quarterly to avoid stale metrics.
Key Takeaway

Design dashboards for conversation and experiments, not for producing perfect forecasts or finger-pointing reports.
