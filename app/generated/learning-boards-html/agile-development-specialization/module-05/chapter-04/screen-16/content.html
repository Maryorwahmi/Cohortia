Velocity
Team-level rate of completed story points per sprint; use to forecast capacity.
Lead & Cycle Time
Lead = request-to-delivery; Cycle = start-to-delivery. Compare them to find waiting vs active delays.
Cumulative Flow Diagram
Visualizes counts in each workflow state; widening bands indicate bottlenecks.
Final Takeaway

Combine a small set of complementary metrics, protect them from misuse, and convert signals into short experiments that improve flow and value delivery.
