Key Concepts — Part 1

Three compact concept cards to anchor your memory.

Release Planning

A strategic activity that defines a high-level goal and scope across a set of iterations to deliver an increment of customer value.

Release Goal

A concise, measurable objective that states the primary purpose and expected outcome of the release.

Release Roadmap

A high-level visual plan that sequences features or epics to show how value will be delivered over multiple sprints.

Summary

Keep goals outcome-focused, use roadmaps to reveal dependencies, and maintain a living release backlog to adapt to learning.
