Estimating Release Scope with Velocity

A simple forecast method that requires clear assumptions and regular updates.

Step-by-step
  1. Calculate average velocity from recent sprints.
  2. Multiply by planned number of sprints to forecast capacity.
  3. Record assumptions and risks behind the forecast.
  4. Review and update after each sprint.
Concrete example
/* Example calculation */ AverageVelocity = 30 // story points per sprint PlannedSprints = 5 ForecastCapacity = AverageVelocity * PlannedSprints // 30 * 5 = 150 story points
Note: treat 150 story points as a forecast to guide prioritization and decisions, not a fixed scope commitment.
Assumptions to record
  • Team composition stays stable.
  • No major external dependencies cause delay.
  • Time allocated for refactoring and integration.
Misconception

Treating the initial forecast as a deadline causes scope pressure and reduces quality.

Key Takeaway

Use velocity to forecast capacity, record assumptions, and update forecasts after each sprint as you learn.
