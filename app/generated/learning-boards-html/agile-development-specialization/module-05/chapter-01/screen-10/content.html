Release Backlog Grooming & Evolution

Make grooming a collaborative rhythm so high-priority items are always ready.

Grooming activities
  • Clarify acceptance criteria and success metrics.
  • Break epics into testable vertical slices.
  • Estimate or re-estimate items as scope becomes clearer.
  • Reprioritize based on stakeholder feedback and new risks.
Signals grooming is needed
  • Sprint planning regularly runs long.
  • Stories enter sprints without clear acceptance criteria.
  • Frequent incomplete stories or scope creep.
Concrete example

After an early prototype shows users struggle with navigation, reprioritize usability-related epics and refine them so they can be planned in the next sprint.

Key Takeaway

Groom frequently and collaboratively. A living release backlog keeps sprint planning predictable and aligned to value.
