Defining a Good Release Goal

Outcome-focused, measurable, and concise goals guide prioritization and MVP selection.

Checklist for a strong goal
  • Names the target users or segment.
  • Describes the key action or experience to enable.
  • Includes a measurable outcome or hypothesis to test.
Example
"Enable customers to self-service password resets, reducing support calls by 20%."
MVP
Pick the smallest feature set that delivers the outcome and yields learning.
Concrete framing exercise

Use this template: "To enable [target users] to [key action] by [timeframe], resulting in [measurable outcome]." Fill it in, then use it to decide which epics are essential for the release.

Key Takeaway

A measurable outcome keeps conversations about trade-offs grounded in customer impact, not feature lists.
