Activity: Draft a Release Goal & High-Level Roadmap

A worked example for a mobile banking initial release to guide your practice.

Step 1 — Write the goal

Use this template: "To enable [target users] to [key action] by [timeframe], resulting in [measurable outcome]."

Example Goal
To enable customers to view account balances and recent transactions within three sprints, reducing routine balance inquiries to support.
Step 2 — Identify core epics
  • Secure user authentication
  • Account balance overview
  • Transaction history & filters
  • Internal fund transfers
Step 3 — Sequence into sprints
Sprint 1: Authentication + Account overview. Sprint 2: Transaction history + Internal transfers. Sprint 3: External transfers + polish.
Step 4 — Pick an MVP
MVP: authentication, account overview, and recent transactions — enough to validate value and reduce support load.
Key Takeaway

A clear goal plus a simple roadmap helps you prioritize an MVP that produces early learning and value.
