Definition

The Release Backlog

A prioritized subset of the product backlog focused on items for a specific release.

Core responsibilities
  • Prioritize by value, risk, and dependencies.
  • Refine items so they are ready for sprint planning.
  • Reprioritize as the team learns from completed sprints.
Practical checklist
  • Does each high-priority story have clear acceptance criteria?
  • Are dependencies identified and scheduled early?
  • Are items small enough to be completed within a sprint?
Concrete example

Before Sprint 1, refine authentication stories with acceptance criteria and backend integration tasks so they can be estimated and planned reliably.

Common Mistake

Allowing too many unrefined 'must-have' stories leads to long sprint planning and poor predictability.

Key Takeaway

Keep the release backlog focused, prioritized, and groomed so sprint planning can pull ready items with confidence.
