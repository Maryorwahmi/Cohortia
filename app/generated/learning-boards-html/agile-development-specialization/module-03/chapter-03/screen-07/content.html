Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Flow Metrics

Definitions you will use

Lead Time

End-to-end elapsed time from request to delivery—what customers experience.

Cycle Time

Elapsed time from when active work begins until the item is completed—internal efficiency.

Throughput

Number of items completed per chosen time interval; a measure of delivery rate.

Concrete example

Reduce dev WIP by one. Observe median cycle time drop and throughput increase over several weeks to validate improvement.

Misconception

Throughput may not rise immediately; reducing variability and lead time are often first signs of better flow.

Key Takeaway

Measure median values and compare over several weeks. Use metrics plus visual cues to judge real improvement.
