Metrics to measure flow

Learn the definitions and how to use them in experiments.

Lead Time

Total elapsed time from request to delivery, including waiting and processing.

Cycle Time

Time from when active work begins until the item completes.

Throughput

Number of items completed per unit of time, e.g., per week.

Practical use
  • Measure cycle time before and after reducing WIP to confirm improvement.
  • Use throughput to check if delivery rate rises as WIP falls.
  • Watch lead time to ensure customer experience improves end-to-end.
Bottleneck signal

A column consistently exceeding its WIP limit while downstream columns are emptier is the clearest visual sign of a bottleneck. That observation triggers interventions like swarming or reducing batch size.

Key takeaway

Metrics let you verify whether changes to WIP limits actually speed delivery and reduce waiting.
