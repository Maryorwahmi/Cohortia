Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Why limit WIP?

Multitasking costs

Context switching increases error rate and delays completion. Limiting WIP is a practical antidote.

Concrete example

A developer with five open tasks spends time re-opening branches, re-reading specs, and restoring context, lengthening cycle time for each item.

Misconception

High individual utilization is not the same as system throughput. Busy people can hide slow flow.

Analogy

A cook focusing on a few dishes completes each faster and with higher quality than a cook juggling many. The principle transfers to knowledge work.

Key Takeaway

Reducing WIP reduces context switches and shortens cycle time for individual items.
