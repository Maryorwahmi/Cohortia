Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Enforcing WIP

Discipline and exceptions

Treat WIP limits as signals that trigger investigation and coordinated action. Allow exceptions only via a formal policy like an expedite lane.

Bad habit

Pulling work whenever someone is busy without discussion undermines flow.

Good practice

Treat full columns as prompts for team conversation and targeted action.

Policy example

If a true emergency arises, use an expedite lane with documented rules and a post-mortem to prevent misuse.

Key Takeaway

Consistent enforcement and clear exception policies keep WIP limits effective and trustworthy.
