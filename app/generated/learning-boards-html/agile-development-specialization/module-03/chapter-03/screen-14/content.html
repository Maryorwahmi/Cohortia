Scenario question

A WIP limit of 3 is reached. A high-priority bug appears. What should the team do?

A) Pull the bug immediately, exceeding the WIP limit.
B) Wait until one existing item moves out before pulling the bug.
C) Move an existing card to Done prematurely to make space.
D) Create an expedite lane that always ignores WIP limits.
Correct response is B: respect the limit and finish something first. An expedite lane can exist but must be rare and governed.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize finishing work to free capacity instead of routinely breaking WIP limits.

Reflect

Document any expedite uses and review them to prevent abuse and to refine your policies.
