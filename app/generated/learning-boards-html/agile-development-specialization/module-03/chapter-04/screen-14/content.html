Core Concept
Kaizen and Continuous Improvement
Turn metric insights into small, testable changes and inspect results in your cadences.
Hypothesis
Form a causal claim based on a metric, e.g., "Reducing batch size will reduce lead time variance."
Experiment
Make a small change for a fixed period and track relevant metrics like lead time distribution or WIP.
Inspect & adapt
Use cadences to review results and either adopt, tweak, or abandon the change based on evidence.
