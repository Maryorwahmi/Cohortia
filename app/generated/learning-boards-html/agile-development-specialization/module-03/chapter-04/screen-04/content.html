Core Concept
Definition + analogy
Cadence definition

A cadence is a regular feedback loop used to inspect and adapt the Kanban system. It creates a predictable opportunity to look at facts, decide actions, and run experiments.

  • Not a status meeting: focused on flow and decisions.
  • Different cadences solve different horizons: daily to strategic.
  • Value depends on pairing with objective metrics.
Analogy & example
Think of cadences as maintenance checks for a production line: daily checks catch jams, and monthly reviews check throughput and customer outcomes.
Concrete example
A team uses a daily flow check to discover three cards stalled in testing and decides to pair-test to clear the queue that day.
Key Takeaway

Cadences create the repeatable opportunities needed to learn and adapt the Kanban process.
