Comparison
Operations Review
Short-to-medium horizon. Focus on cross-team flow, identify systemic blockers, agree on operational fixes or policy changes.
  • Participants: multiple teams or managers.
  • Evidence: CFD snapshots, blocked counts, throughput dips.
  • Outcome: operational Kaizen or policy change.
Strategy Review
Long horizon. Align Kanban policies to organizational goals, shift priorities based on market or strategic changes.
  • Participants: leadership and product owners.
  • Evidence: trend charts, customer feedback, capacity forecasts.
  • Outcome: strategic prioritization or investment decisions.
