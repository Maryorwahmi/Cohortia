Definition
Lead Time Distribution Chart
Assess predictability and identify outliers in delivery times.
What it shows
A histogram of completed items by lead time. Use percentiles (e.g., 85th) to form forecasts.
  • Narrow spread → predictable delivery.
  • Long tail → inconsistent outcomes and risky forecasts.
Concrete example
If 85% of items finish within 10 days, use ten days as a conservative commitment window for similar items and run a Kaizen to shrink the tail.
