Process Flow
Daily Kanban
Quick daily check for obstacles and immediate actions to restore flow.
Guiding questions
  • What is impeding flow right now?
  • Which cards are blocked or aging in a column?
  • Who can take an action in the next few hours to move them?
Example
Team notices three cards in testing past WIP limit and assigns a developer to pair with QA to unblock two items the same day.
Pro tip
Use a short metric like current WIP vs WIP limit to decide whether to pull new work during the meeting.
