Process Flow
Replenishment Meeting
Select and confirm ready items based on capacity and metrics.
1. Review capacity and WIP
Look at current WIP, recent throughput, and any blocked work before choosing new items.
2. Check readiness criteria
Confirm acceptance criteria, dependencies, and required test environments are known.
3. Prioritize and pull
Choose items that match capacity and bring the most value and remove items that cause excess batch size.
Concrete scenario
With an average throughput of two items per day and WIP near limit, the team decides to pull only two new items and break larger stories into smaller pieces for predictability.
Common mistake

Pulling too much work because everyone looks idle increases WIP and lengthens lead time; use capacity and throughput to guide decisions.
