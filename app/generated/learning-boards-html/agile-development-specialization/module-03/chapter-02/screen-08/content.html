Swimlanes example

Horizontally divide the board to show different priorities or teams in parallel while keeping all work on one board.

Expedite (top lane)
High-priority, urgent items such as production outages. Rule: only customer outages allowed.
Standard Features (middle lane)
Routine development work delivered in normal cadence.
Maintenance (bottom lane)
Technical debt and small maintenance tasks that are lower priority but still tracked.
Example
A payment outage card in Expedite jumps ahead of a feature card in Standard.
Misconception
Swimlanes don't prioritize automatically; you must define how items are treated across lanes.
