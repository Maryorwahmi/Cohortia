Why boards matter

Boards create a single source of truth for status, reduce confusion about ownership, and reveal where the system is constrained.

From simple to rich

Start with To Do, Doing, Done and expand columns, add policies and swimlanes as your workflow needs more fidelity.

Choose your medium

Physical boards are tactile for co-located teams; digital boards help distributed teams and provide analytics.
