Learning Objectives — Practical Focus

Short, measurable exercises help you reach each objective and show competency to your team.

01
Explain the purpose

Exercise: Tell a colleague why making work visible prevented an unnecessary handoff in a recent example.

Mnemonic
Visibility → Shared understanding → Faster decisions.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Identify components

Exercise: Build a To Do/Doing/Done board and add three sample cards with a title, type, and owner.

Mnemonic
Card fields = title, type, owner.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Design expanded boards

Exercise: Draft a pipeline for development that includes Ready, In Review, and In Testing and define one explicit rule for a handoff.

Mnemonic
Column = stage, Policy = rule for moving cards.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Choose a medium

Exercise: Create a physical or digital board and note the trade-offs you experienced in one paragraph.

Mnemonic
Co-located vs distributed, analytics vs tactile.
Verified Competency Milestone
