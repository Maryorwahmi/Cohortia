Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Board hygiene
Common mistakes
  • Letting cards go stale and not updating status or owners.
  • Over-complicating columns before they solve a problem.
  • Making cards too large and hard to finish quickly.
Prevent
Daily lightweight updates and an assigned owner per card keep the board accurate.
Simplify
Start with simple columns and add new stages only when you need clarity on a handoff.
Practical checklist
  1. Assign an owner for every active card.
  2. Add a short blocking note if waiting externally.
  3. Review and archive old cards monthly in digital tools.
Key Takeaway

Treat the board as a living artifact: simple, current, and trustworthy.
