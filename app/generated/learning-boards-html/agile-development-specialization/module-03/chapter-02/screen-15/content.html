What to Remember

A compact set of reminders for daily use of Kanban boards.

Swimlanes
Use lanes for Expedite, Standard, Maintenance to keep streams visible.
Blocked
Make blocked items visible with a note explaining the dependency.
Card conventions
Color-code types, keep fields minimal, and assign an owner for each card.
Final takeaway
Design boards to reveal problems and support action; keep them simple, current, and focused on flow.
