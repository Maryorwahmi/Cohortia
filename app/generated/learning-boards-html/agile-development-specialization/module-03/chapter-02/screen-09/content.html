Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Physical vs Digital
Physical board
  • Tactile and highly visible for co-located teams.
  • Encourages face-to-face discussion and quick updates.
  • Limited history and poor for distributed teams.
Example
A team with a co-located room may use sticky notes and a whiteboard for daily standups.
Digital board
  • Works for distributed teams and provides history and analytics.
  • Supports automation, integrations, and reporting on cycle time.
  • Requires discipline to keep current or it becomes stale.
Key Takeaway

Choose the medium your team will maintain and that supports your need for data or face-to-face interaction.
