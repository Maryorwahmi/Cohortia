Interpreting pile-ups
A build-up of cards in one column usually means that stage can't process items quickly enough. Investigate capacity, clarity of entry criteria, and external dependencies before changing upstream behavior.
Investigate
Talk with people doing the work, review policies, and check for blockers or unclear criteria.
Intervene
Try temporary capacity shifts, clearer rules, or smaller work items to reduce queue time.
Avoid
Don't push more work upstream; that hides the problem and increases context switching.
Measure
Use cycle time and lead time if available to confirm the bottleneck and to validate improvements.
Assessment prompt
If Review has many cards and In Progress is empty, what does that indicate? It usually means Review is the bottleneck; investigate capacity or rules for review.
Key Takeaway

Use the board to reveal where to experiment and measure changes, not to assign blame.
