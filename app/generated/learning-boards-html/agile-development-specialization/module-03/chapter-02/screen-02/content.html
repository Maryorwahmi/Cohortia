Learning Objectives

Follow these targets to design a meaningful Kanban board and use it confidently.

01
Explain purpose
Explain the purpose and benefits of a Kanban board

You will describe how visibility, shared understanding, and flow improvement come from a visible board; this helps teams adopt pull-based work.

Mnemonic: a lightbulb icon = visibility
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Identify parts
Identify and describe columns and cards

You will name the typical To Do, Doing, Done columns and explain how a card represents a single unit of work.

Mnemonic: a stack icon = columns & cards
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Design boards
Design expanded boards with additional columns and swimlanes

You will practice splitting in-progress work into Development, Code Review, Testing and adding swimlanes for priority classes.

Mnemonic: a lane icon = swimlanes
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Choose medium
Differentiate physical and digital boards and pick the right one

You will weigh tactile benefits of physical boards against analytics and distribution advantages of digital tools like Trello or Azure DevOps.

Mnemonic: two squares = physical vs digital
Verified Competency Milestone
