Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Limit WIP
What a WIP limit does
It caps active items to match team capacity, making finishing work the priority and reducing context switching.
Example: personal week
Set Doing WIP to 2. Focus on studying and the Cohortia project. You’ll likely finish more than when juggling five tasks.
How to choose a limit
  • Start small and observe cycle time.
  • Adjust based on measured throughput and team feedback.
  • Treat limits as experiments, not edicts.
Signals and behavior
When a WIP limit is reached, the team must either finish work, remove blockers, or negotiate. That visible pressure drives improvement.
Common mistake
Setting limits too low without measuring capacity, which creates constant blockers instead of learning signals.
Key takeaway
Treat WIP limits as testable constraints that reveal capacity and force finishing behavior.
