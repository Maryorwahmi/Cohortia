Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Kanban
A method for managing work by visualizing flow and limiting concurrent items.
Work In Progress (WIP)
The number of items actively worked on; limits reduce multitasking and improve throughput.
Flow
The smooth movement of items through stages; measured by lead time and throughput.
Summary takeaway
Use visualization, WIP limits, and small experiments to convert overload into measurable, improvable workflow.
