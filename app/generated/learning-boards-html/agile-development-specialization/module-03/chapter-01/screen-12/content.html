Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Feedback Loops
Types of loops
  • Daily flow stand-ups focused on blockages and WIP.
  • Service delivery reviews with stakeholders for outcomes.
  • Operations reviews to analyze system performance and metrics.
Example agenda
Daily: What’s blocking flow? Who needs help? What will we finish today? Output: one unblock action.
Practical guidance
  1. Keep loops short and purposeful.
  2. Always close with a decision or a small experiment.
  3. Record metrics so discussions are evidence-based.
Key takeaway
Design feedback loops that produce decisions and experiments, not just status updates.
