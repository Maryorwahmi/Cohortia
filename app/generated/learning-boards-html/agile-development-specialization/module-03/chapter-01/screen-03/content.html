Skills to practice

Each objective is paired with a short, observable task you can complete in minutes to a few hours.

Practice

Write a short note linking just-in-time manufacturing to visual Kanban in your context.
Practice
Describe incremental change
Outline one small experiment your team could run this sprint without changing roles.
Practice
Build a personal board
Create columns, add five tasks, set Doing WIP limit to two and simulate progress.
Assessment idea

Measure one metric—lead time for a task—before and after your experiment. Small measurable change is the goal.

Common mistake

Trying to change everything at once. Kanban favors incremental experiments, not wholesale reorganization.
