What to measure
  • Lead time: start to finish for an item.
  • Cycle time: time spent in a particular stage.
  • Throughput: items completed over a period.
Example experiment
Rotate a reviewer for one sprint and set Review WIP to one. Measure average review cycle time before and after.
Interpreting signals
Many cards piling in one column is a clear signal of a bottleneck. Fewer started but more finished items show improved flow.
Misconception
Confusing busyness with progress. Busy teams may have low throughput; measure finished pieces instead of activity alone.
Why it matters
Flow measurement makes improvement decisions measurable and lowers the risk of changes that harm delivery.
