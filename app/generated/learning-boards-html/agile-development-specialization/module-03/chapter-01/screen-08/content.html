Board template
Columns
  • To Do
  • Doing (consider sub-columns like In Progress / Review)
  • Done
  • Optional: Blocked, Waiting for Feedback
Card example
Study for agile exam — Owner: You — Est. 3 hours — Blocker: none
Key takeaway
Keep the board simple and live. Use it to reveal queues and trigger experiments.
Simulation activity
Steps
  1. List 5 tasks on cards and place them in To Do.
  2. Set Doing WIP limit to 2 and move two cards to Doing.
  3. If a dependency appears, move a card to Blocked and note the reason.
  4. Complete a card and move it to Done; observe lead time.
Common mistake
Adding too many columns before understanding where queues actually form.
