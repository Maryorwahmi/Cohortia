Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › ART Roles
Role Cards
R
Release Train Engineer (RTE)
Servant leader who facilitates ART events, coaches, and helps remove impediments.
PM
Product Management
Owns Program Backlog, defines features, and aligns priorities with business owners.
SA
System Architect
Guides technical vision and ensures solution architecture across the ART.
SM
Scrum Master & Product Owner
Team-level roles that maintain Agile practices and translate features into stories.
Misconception
The RTE does not assign team work; the role enables teams to negotiate commitments and remove impediments.
Example scenario
During PI Planning the RTE runs facilitation, Product Management communicates feature priorities, and System Architects highlight a constraint that changes sequencing. Teams then adjust commitments and document dependencies on the Program Board.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Clear role boundaries let leaders support teams without undermining team autonomy.
