Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › PI Planning
Typical Agenda
  1. Business context and vision presentation.
  2. Architecture and feature briefings.
  3. Team breakout planning and draft objectives.
  4. Program draft review and Program Board updates.
  5. Confidence vote and final commitments.
Good practices
Prepare the Program Backlog, present clear business context, ensure architecture guardrails are known, and empower teams to make realistic commitments.
Key Outputs
PI Objectives
Team and ART objectives with business value and committed scope.
Program Board
Visual map of dependencies and milestones; used to coordinate delivery and flag risks.
Risks & Mitigations
Identified risks are categorized and assigned owners for mitigation during the PI.
Common mistake
Treating PI Planning as a command-and-control session. This reduces ownership and leads to missed commitments. Facilitation and team negotiation are essential.
