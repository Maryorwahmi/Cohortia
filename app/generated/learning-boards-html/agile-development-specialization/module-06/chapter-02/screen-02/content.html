Explain SAFe values & principles
You will articulate Lean-Agile principles and why they guide economic and systems thinking in SAFe. This helps teams prioritize outcomes over activities.

Describe SAFe configurations
You will distinguish Essential, Large Solution, Portfolio, and Full SAFe and when to adopt each configuration. This reduces mismatch between framework scope and organizational needs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify SAFe roles
You will explain RTE, Product Management, System Architects, Scrum Masters and Product Owners and how they collaborate. Solid role clarity prevents handoff confusion.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Run effective PI Planning
You will understand PI Planning inputs, outputs, and common pitfalls so you can ensure alignment and realistic commitments during a PI.
