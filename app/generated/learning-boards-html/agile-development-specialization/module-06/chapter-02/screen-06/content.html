Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Value Streams & ART
Definition
Value Stream
A value stream is the sequence of activities that deliver value to the customer, from idea to production and operation.
The ART
An Agile Release Train organizes multiple teams around a value stream to plan and deliver together on a synchronized cadence.
Example
A customer onboarding value stream combines UI, API, identity integration, and reporting teams into an ART that commits to shared PI Objectives aligned to reducing manual processing time.
Program Backlog & Cadence
Program Backlog
Features prioritized to flow through the ART and be planned into upcoming PIs.
PI Cadence
An 8–12 week timebox where teams plan, build, integrate, demo, and reflect together.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Align teams into ARTs around value streams to reduce handoffs and make integrated delivery predictable.
