Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Adoption Steps
Practical rollout
  1. Choose a value stream and form a pilot ART.
  2. Prepare Program Backlog and business context.
  3. Train and coach RTEs, Product Management, and teams.
  4. Run PI Planning and execute the PI.
  5. Inspect & Adapt; apply lessons before scaling further.
Metrics to watch
Integration frequency, PI Objective achievement, lead time, and reduction in cross-team defects show whether adoption is delivering value.
Inspect & Adapt workshop
A structured program-level retrospective that includes quantitative measurement, problem-solving workshops, and actionable improvement experiments for the next PI.
The workshop looks for root causes when outcomes miss expectations and defines experiments for the next PI. This is how SAFe stays adaptive and avoids turning into a rigid process.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Adopt SAFe iteratively: pilot, coach, measure, and inspect so the framework evolves to fit your organization.
