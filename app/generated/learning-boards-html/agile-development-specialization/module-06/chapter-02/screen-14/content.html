Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe)
A prescriptive framework that aligns teams, programs, and portfolios to deliver complex systems.
Lean-Agile Principles
Foundational decision rules like systems thinking and decentralization guide sustainable choices.
Agile Release Train (ART)
A long-lived group of teams that plan and deliver together on a PI cadence.
Summary takeaway

Use SAFe to align teams around value, use PI cadence to synchronize delivery, and always inspect and adapt rather than defaulting to paperwork.
