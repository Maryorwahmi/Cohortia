Learning Objectives
Grow from understanding the ideas to designing a simple CI/CD pipeline and identifying feedback needs.
01
Define DevOps
Explain DevOps as culture plus practices that extend Agile to deployment and operations. Knowing this helps align teams on shared responsibility.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify CALMS
List and describe Culture, Automation, Lean, Measurement, and Sharing. Use CALMS to prioritize improvement efforts.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain CI, CD, CD
Distinguish Continuous Integration, Continuous Delivery, and Continuous Deployment and their gating strategies for releases.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design pipelines & feedback
Sketch a basic CI/CD pipeline using version control, automated tests, IaC, and monitoring to enable continuous improvement.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
