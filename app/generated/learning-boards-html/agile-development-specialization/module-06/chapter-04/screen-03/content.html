Skills & Evidence
Understand what demonstrates competency and prepare a tangible pipeline sketch as evidence.
Skill: Pipeline design
Compose stages, choose tools like Git, CI runners, container builders, and explain the purpose of each stage for quality and speed.
Mnemonic: Think of a relay race where every handoff is automated and measured.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Skill: Feedback & monitoring
Identify the key metrics to track, such as deployment frequency and mean time to recovery, and set up alerts that enable fast response.
Mnemonic: Measure to improve — if you can’t measure it, you can’t improve it.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Skill: Automation with safety
Automate builds, tests, and deployments, but include gates, rollbacks, and security checks to avoid unsafe releases.
Mnemonic: Automate, but keep guardrails.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Skill: Shared responsibility
Communicate changes, share runbooks and postmortems, and ensure teams collaborate on incidents and improvements.
Mnemonic: Everyone owns uptime and quality.
