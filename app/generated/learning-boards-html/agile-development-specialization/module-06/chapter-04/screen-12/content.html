Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Monitoring & Feedback
Feedback loop example
Deploy → Monitor → Alert → Investigate logs → Fix → CI verification → Redeploy. That closed loop turns incidents into learning opportunities.
Alert strategy
Define alerts for user-impacting metrics and avoid high-noise signals to keep on-call focus where it matters.
Concrete practice
  • Integrate deployment metadata into logs to trace regressions to specific releases.
  • Automate health checks and smoke tests after deploys to validate behavior.
  • Route alerts to the appropriate on-call rotation and include runbooks.
Key Takeaway

Monitoring and clear alerts close the feedback loop, letting teams detect and fix regressions quickly and learn from incidents.
