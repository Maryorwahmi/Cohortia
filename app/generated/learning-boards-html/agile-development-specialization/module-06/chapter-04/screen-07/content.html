Lean: reduce waste
Identify delays and handoffs that slow delivery and remove or automate them to improve flow.
Examples of waste
  • Long-lived branches that require large merges
  • Manual environment provisioning that creates queues
  • Slow test suites that block pipelines
Measurement: actionable metrics
Measure deployment frequency, lead time for changes, and mean time to recovery to understand delivery health and guide improvements.
Practical tip
Track a small set of metrics and review them in retrospectives to drive experiments that remove bottlenecks.
Key Takeaway

Use Lean to target waste and Measurement to prove whether changes improved flow and quality.
