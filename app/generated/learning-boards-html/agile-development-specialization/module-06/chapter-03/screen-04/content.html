Core Concept

Scrum at scale, not scaled Scrum

Preserve Scrum. Minimize added roles and artifacts while coordinating many teams on one product.

What this means
  • One Product Owner for the whole product and a single Product Backlog.
  • Shared Sprint Review and Sprint Planning Part 1 to align teams.
  • Teams remain cross-functional and self-managing; management layers are reduced.
Example
Three teams build one app product with a single backlog and one Product Owner who prioritizes customer outcomes across teams.
Common mistake
Keeping many management layers and not empowering teams undermines the simplicity LeSS needs to work.
Practical picture
Shared events
Sprint Planning Part 1 brings all teams together with the Product Owner to select and clarify backlog items for the sprint.
Whole-product focus
Teams collaborate on one backlog to avoid local optimization and ensure a coherent customer experience.
Key takeaway
LeSS reduces added structure but requires stronger empowerment and alignment to the Product Owner and backlog.
