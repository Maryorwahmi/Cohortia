Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › LeSS Configurations
Definition

Basic LeSS

Designed for 2–8 teams. Minimal changes to Scrum: single Product Owner, shared events, and multi-team retrospectives.

Good when the product is coherent and teams can work closely on overlapping areas.
Requires teams to be empowered and collaborate frequently to avoid dependencies.
Hierarchy

LeSS Huge

Used when more than eight teams work on one product. Introduces Requirement Areas and Area Product Owners to help scale refinement while keeping one product backlog and Product Owner.

Example
A large marketplace splits work into areas like search, listings, checkout. Area Product Owners focus backlog details for their area while aligning to the overall backlog.
Misconception
Area Product Owners do not replace the single Product Owner; they supplement refinement for scale under the same product vision.
Key takeaway
Introduce area roles only to manage scale; preserve a single backlog to keep the product coherent.
