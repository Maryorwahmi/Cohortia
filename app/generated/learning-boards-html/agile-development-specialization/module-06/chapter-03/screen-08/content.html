Core Concept

Fractal coordination and leadership support

Scrum@Scale connects teams with Scrum of Scrums, and uses an Executive Action Team for systemic impediments and strategic alignment.

Scrum of Scrums
A representative meeting to surface dependencies and coordinate cross-team work.
Executive Action Team
A small leadership group empowered to remove organizational impediments and align strategy.
Practical example
Two groups of three teams each run their team-level Scrum events. Each group elects a Scrum Master or representative to attend a group-level Scrum of Scrums weekly to surface integration issues. If the issue requires policy change, the Executive Action Team prioritizes and removes the impediment.
Misconception
Treating SoS or EAT as reporting rituals defeats their purpose; they must be empowered to act.
Key takeaway
Scrum@Scale grows coordination by repeating Scrum patterns, but success depends on meaningful decision authority at higher layers.
