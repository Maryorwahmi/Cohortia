Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
Large-Scale Scrum (LeSS)
Apply Scrum with one Product Owner and shared events to keep product cohesion.
Scrum@Scale
Modular scaling: Scrum of Scrums plus an Executive Action Team for impediment removal.
Choosing fit
Match framework to product scope, culture, and appetite for decentralized decision-making.
Summary takeaway

Both frameworks favor Scrum principles over heavy process. Decide based on your need for single-backlog coherence, modular coordination, and whether leaders will empower teams and escalation mechanisms.
