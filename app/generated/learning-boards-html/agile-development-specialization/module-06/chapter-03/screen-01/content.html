LeSS and Scrum@Scale

Two frameworks that scale Scrum without adding heavy prescriptive layers. Learn the different ways they keep teams aligned to a product and to each other.

Duration: ~11 min video brief
Orientation

You will learn: how LeSS keeps one Product Owner and shared events for a single product, and how Scrum@Scale uses Scrum of Scrums plus an Executive Action Team to remove organizational impediments. We will compare each with SAFe and show when each choice fits an organization.

Quick start

If your product has many teams but a single product vision, LeSS preserves Scrum purity. If you need modular, incremental scaling with leadership impediment removal, consider Scrum@Scale.
