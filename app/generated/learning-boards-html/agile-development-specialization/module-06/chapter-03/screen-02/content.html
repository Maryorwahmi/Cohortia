Learning Objectives

Learn, understand, apply, and begin to master LeSS and Scrum@Scale concepts for team coordination and organizational change.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain core differences
Describe how LeSS and Scrum@Scale scale Scrum differently and why those differences matter in practice.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify LeSS roles
Distinguish Basic LeSS from LeSS Huge and the role of Area Product Owners when needed.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe Scrum@Scale mechanics
Explain the Scrum of Scrums pattern and the Executive Action Team responsibilities.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Compare with SAFe
Contrast the prescriptive nature of SAFe with the minimalist or modular styles of LeSS and Scrum@Scale.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
