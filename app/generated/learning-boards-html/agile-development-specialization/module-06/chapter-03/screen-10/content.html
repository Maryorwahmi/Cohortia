Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Executive Action Team
Definition

Leadership that removes impediments

The EAT is a small, empowered team that resolves organizational obstacles and aligns strategy with scaled Scrum implementation needs.

Authority: Must have the ability to change policy, allocate resources, and prioritize transformation work.
Focus: Remove impediments blocking many teams or that require executive action.
Practice: Keep a visible impediment list with owners and deadlines, and act quickly.
Concrete example
Teams report blocked integration testing due to procurement delays. The EAT authorizes a change to provisioning policy and directs an interim budget, resolving the blocker within days rather than weeks.
Common mistake
Staffing an EAT without decision authority turns it into a reporting body and leaves impediments unresolved.
Key takeaway
The EAT must be empowered to act, with a short, visible list of impediments and clear ownership for removal.
