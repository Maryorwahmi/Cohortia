Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › One Product Owner
Core Concept

One Product Owner for the product

A single PO keeps prioritization coherent across teams and reduces the risk of local optimizations that fragment the product experience.

Responsibility: Own the Product Backlog, set priorities, and represent the customer for the entire product.
Practice: Participate in shared backlog refinement and Sprint Planning Part 1 so teams align early.
Delegation: Use clear backlog items and area-oriented collaboration instead of micro-management.
Scenario
Three teams building payments need the PO to prioritize completing the end-to-end payment flow rather than separate, incomplete features. The PO must coordinate trade-offs so the integrated increment delivers customer value.
Misconception
One PO does not mean one-person control; the PO collaborates heavily with teams and may use Area Product Owners for scale while remaining accountable for the whole backlog.
Key takeaway
Single PO centralizes vision and priorities, enabling integrated increments and coordinated trade-offs across teams.
