Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Scrum of Scrums
Process Flow

Coordination for 6 teams, two groups

Use group SoS meetings to resolve cross-team dependencies and escalate systemic issues to the EAT when needed.

Team-level Scrum
Each team keeps standard events. Representatives bring integration issues to the group SoS.
Group-level SoS
Representatives discuss impediments, plan integration work, and maintain a short parking-lot for items that need escalation.
Escalation
When an impediment requires organizational action, the SoS escalates it to the Executive Action Team for resolution.
Example agenda
1) Quick sync on what each team did yesterday. 2) What each team plans next. 3) Cross-team impediments and required integrations. 4) Items for escalation to EAT.
Common mistake
Using SoS as a status report to leadership rather than a problem-solving forum among teams.
Pro tip
Keep SoS short, time-boxed, and focused on dependencies. Use a shared parking-lot for items that need EAT attention.
