Process Flow

Shared planning and reviews

LeSS keeps planning and review events that create shared understanding and one integrated increment.

Sprint Planning Part 1
All teams with Product Owner select and clarify backlog items together.
Sprint Planning Part 2
Each team plans how it will deliver the agreed items.
Shared Sprint Review
Stakeholders review one integrated product increment for consolidated feedback.
Example walkthrough
A checkout enhancement is chosen in Part 1. Team A owns UI, Team B owns payments, Team C owns integration. In Part 2 each team plans tasks and coordinates dependencies discovered in Part 1. At review, stakeholders see the complete flow end-to-end and provide consolidated feedback.
Common mistake
Running separate reviews or isolated planning silos that fragment backlog ownership.
Key takeaway
Shared events create collective ownership and reduce cross-team rework by aligning priorities early.
