Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Adoption pitfalls
Common Mistake

Change authority, not just structure

Adding meetings or titles without giving decision authority or changing culture leads to superficial adoption and frustration.

Document escalation paths with owners and SLAs so impediments do not get lost.
Empower the EAT with clear decision-making authority and visible actions.
Train managers to coach and enable teams rather than control tasks.
Example
A Scrum of Scrums lists 10 impediments, but no one resolves them because the EAT cannot change procurement policy. Fixing this requires delegating authority to the EAT and setting an SLA for response.
Mitigation
Define who can act, what they can change, and how quickly they must respond. Make actions visible.
Key takeaway
Structural changes must be accompanied by changes in decision authority and culture to succeed.
