Comparison

When to choose each approach

SAFe
Prescriptive, detailed roles and governance. Fits large, complex, or regulated organizations needing structure.
LeSS
Minimalist; single Product Owner and shared events. Best for organizations willing to decentralize and keep Scrum purity.
Scrum@Scale
Modular and fractal, with Scrum of Scrums and EAT. Good for incremental scaling and flexible coordination needs.
Example decision
If regulatory demands require strict governance, SAFe can provide the needed structure. If your priority is product coherence with empowered teams, prefer LeSS. If you want to grow coordination features gradually, pick Scrum@Scale.
Pitfall
Forcing a framework without assessing culture leads to surface compliance rather than real agility.
