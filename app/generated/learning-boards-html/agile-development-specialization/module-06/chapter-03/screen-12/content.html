Design a Scrum@Scale coordination plan

Use the template to specify teams, group-level coordination, product-level sync, and an Executive Action Team with clear escalation rules.

1. Organization overview
Total Teams: 6 — Group A: Storefront (3), Group B: Fulfillment & Inventory (3). Overall product: E-commerce platform.
2. Team-level Scrum
Each team keeps Product Owner, Scrum Master, Daily Scrum, Sprint Planning, Review, and Retrospective.
3. Group-level SoS
Purpose: surface dependencies and integration risks. Attendees: Scrum Masters or designated reps. Frequency: 2-3 times/week or as needed.

# Scrum@Scale Coordination Design

Organization Structure Overview:
Total Teams: 6
Group A: Storefront Team 1, Team 2, Team 3
Group B: Fulfillment Team 1, Team 2, Team 3

Group-Level SoS:
Purpose: coordinate integrations and dependencies
Event: Group SoS, 3x week, 30 minutes. Attendees: Scrum Masters.

Overall Coordination:
Event: Representatives meeting weekly. Escalate to EAT for organizational impediments.

EAT:
Members: senior leaders able to change policy and allocate resources.
Escalation SLA: 48-72 hours for critical impediments.
Challenge
Avoid vague escalation paths. Assign owners and SLAs so escalations are resolved quickly.
Key takeaway
A documented coordination model with empowered escalation makes Scrum@Scale operational and effective.
