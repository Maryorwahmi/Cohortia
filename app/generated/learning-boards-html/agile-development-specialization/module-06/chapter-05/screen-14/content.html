Key Concepts — Flow & Scaled Metrics

Flow Metrics
Lead Time, Cycle Time, Throughput, WIP measure how value moves through a system.
Value Delivery & ART Predictability
Measure customer impact and an ART's reliability to inform portfolio decisions.
Kaizen & Learning
Small, owned experiments driven by metrics create sustainable improvements and shared knowledge.
Summary

Use flow and outcome metrics together, design hypothesis-led experiments, and protect psychological safety so measurement becomes a tool for learning and improvement.
