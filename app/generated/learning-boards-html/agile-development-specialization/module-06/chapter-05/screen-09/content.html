ART Predictability & Value Delivery Metrics

Two complementary views: how reliably work is delivered, and whether it delivers business value.

Measurement: Strategic
ART Predictability Measure
A ratio expressing how well an ART delivered its committed PI objectives. Useful for forecasting and improving planning quality.
How it's calculated
Delivered Objectives / Committed Objectives × 100%
What it signals
Planning fidelity, dependency coordination, execution risk.
Value Delivery Metrics
Measures that show whether delivered features moved business KPIs or validated hypotheses.
Examples
Conversion lift, revenue impact, engagement change versus hypothesis.
Misuse risk
Counting delivered features without validating user impact.
Comparison Visual
Scenario A: High Predictability, Low Value
  • ART consistently meets commitments.
  • Features do not move customer KPIs.
  • Action: shift backlog priorities; run experiments to validate hypotheses.
Scenario B: Low Predictability, High Value
  • Teams deliver valuable changes but unpredictably.
  • Action: stabilize planning, improve dependency resolution, protect teams from interruptions.
Key Takeaway

Use ART predictability to guide planning discipline and value metrics to validate outcomes. Neither alone is sufficient.
