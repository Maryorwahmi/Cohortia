Interpreting metric combinations

Combinations of metrics form signals. Treat them as hypotheses about your system, not absolute diagnoses.

High Lead Time + Low Throughput
Likely queueing before work begins or slow handoffs. Action: map value stream and reduce pre-start delays.
High Cycle Time + High WIP
Too much multitasking. Action: lower WIP and encourage swarming to complete items faster.
Low Cycle Time + Low Throughput
Work completes quickly when started but few items are being started. Action: look for limiting policies or priorities upstream.
Check Your Understanding

If your lead time is long but cycle time is short, where would you focus first? The likely answer is the pre-work queue or prioritization process.

Scenario Card
Team Beta reports: throughput 4 items/week, avg cycle time 8 days, lead time 24 days, WIP average 14 items. Interpretation: items sit long before active work. Value stream mapping shows a 10-day wait in backlog review. Proposed small experiment: reduce backlog review queue by scheduling daily triage and setting WIP limit on "Ready for Dev." Measure lead time over the next two weeks.
Common Mistake

Treating metrics as targets. When metrics become targets, teams may game numbers instead of improving flow. Use them as observational signals.

Key Takeaway

Form a hypothesis from metric combos, run a small experiment, and measure the outcome in the next retrospective.
