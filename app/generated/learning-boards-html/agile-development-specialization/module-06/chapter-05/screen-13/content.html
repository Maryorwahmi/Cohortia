From Data to Kaizen: a practical loop

A repeatable sequence that turns signals into experiments and organizational learning.

1
Measure
Collect the right metric and confirm the signal with recent data.
2
Analyze
Look for patterns, correlations, and likely root causes.
3
Hypothesize & Experiment
Design a small, time-boxed change to test a single variable.
4
Run & Measure
Execute the experiment and capture the primary and guardrail metrics.
5
Follow-up
Review results, decide to adopt, adapt, or abandon, and document learning.
Experiment Card — Template
Hypothesis: Lowering WIP will reduce median cycle time.
Primary metric: Median cycle time (days).
Guardrail: Escaped defects per release.
Duration: 1 sprint.
Owner: Scrum Master & team lead.
Practical Tips
  • Time-box experiments so learning is fast.
  • Limit variables: change one thing at a time.
  • Document results and share in the next retrospective or I&A.
  • Assign a visible owner and a follow-up date.
Key Takeaway

Small, owned experiments driven by metrics produce the learning that scales. Always close the loop with a follow-up and clear decision.
