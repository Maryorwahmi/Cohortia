Learning Objectives
These objectives pair practical metrics with facilitation patterns so teams can learn and improve safely.
01
Explain Flow Metrics

You will define Lead Time, Cycle Time, Throughput and WIP and explain what each measures and why they matter to flow.

Mnemonic: LCTW (Lead, Cycle, Throughput, WIP)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use Value Stream Mapping

You will map end-to-end steps, measure wait and process time, and identify waste and handoffs that slow delivery.

Mnemonic: MAP (Measure, Analyze, Plan)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Facilitate Retrospectives

You will practice formats like Start/Stop/Continue and Mad/Sad/Glad to generate actionable improvement items with psychological safety.

Mnemonic: SSC / MSG
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Adopt Kaizen Mindset

You will describe Kaizen and design small, incremental experiments that accumulate into measurable improvements.

Mnemonic: KAI (Keep, Adjust, Improve)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
