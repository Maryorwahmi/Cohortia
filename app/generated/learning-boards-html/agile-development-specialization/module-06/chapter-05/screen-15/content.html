Key Concepts — Quality, Safety, Practice

Quality Metrics
Use defect density, escaped defects, and MTTR to guide engineering investments.
Psychological Safety
Measure engagement and act transparently so teams feel safe to surface issues.
Measurement Practice
Run hypothesis-driven experiments with guardrails and visible follow-up to create learning.
Final Takeaway

Make measurements humane and useful: focus on normalized quality metrics, protect safety, and convert signals into small experiments that drive organizational learning.
