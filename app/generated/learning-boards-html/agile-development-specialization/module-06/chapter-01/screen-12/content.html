Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Scaling Agile
Applying Agile across teams to produce integrated solutions while preserving team autonomy.
Agile Chaos
Productive teams that lack coordination, causing integration issues, conflicting priorities, and delays.
Frameworks: SAFe, LeSS, Scrum@Scale
Different philosophies: prescriptive, minimalist, or flexible networks — choose based on fit and culture.
Summary
Pick the smallest pilot that targets your highest-cost coordination problem, measure outcomes, and keep team autonomy as a guiding constraint.
