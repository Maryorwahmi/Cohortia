How you'll practice

Create a one-paragraph framework recommendation that ties the choice to the organization's drivers, such as predictability or cross-team integration.

List two expected organizational changes required: a coordination role and a planning cadence that aligns teams for integrated delivery.

Identify one anti-pattern to avoid, such as centralizing team decision-making under a new Agile PMO that dictates team backlogs.

Mnemonic
Remember FIT: Fit to context, Investment required, Team autonomy preserved, Time to value improved.
