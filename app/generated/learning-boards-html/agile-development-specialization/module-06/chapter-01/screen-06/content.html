Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Common Anti-Patterns and Pitfalls
Anti-patterns
Centralized 'Agile PMO'
Dictating team-level backlogs removes self-organization and reintroduces command-and-control.
Add roles, no structure
Hiring more coaches or Scrum Masters without changing coordination mechanisms increases ceremony without solving dependencies.
Framework copy-paste
Taking a framework wholesale without tailoring to culture and complexity causes resistance and superficial compliance.
Ignore culture
Failing to invest in leadership alignment, training, and psychological safety undermines any structural change.
Check your approach
Use these quick checks: Are priorities decided collaboratively? Are dependencies visible? Are measurable defects or delays reducing?
Mini Activity
List one structural blocker in your organization and one cultural blocker. Propose a single experiment that addresses the higher-cost blocker first.
