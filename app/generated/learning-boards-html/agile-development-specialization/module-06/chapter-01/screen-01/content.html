Introduction to Scaling Agile
How multiple Agile teams coordinate to deliver integrated solutions at enterprise scale.

Scaling is about enabling many small teams to work together toward a shared vision, not growing each team. It introduces coordination constructs, alignment roles, and shared artifacts so work integrates cleanly.

When done well, scaling preserves team autonomy while reducing integration risk and improving predictability. When done poorly, it can create more ceremony than value.
