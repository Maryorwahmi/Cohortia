Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › What to Remember
Focus on the problem
Scale to solve the highest-cost coordination pain, not to adopt a framework for its own sake.
Preserve autonomy
Alignment should enable teams to deliver end-to-end value without stripping decision rights.
Pilot and measure
Run a small experiment, measure integration defects and lead time, and iterate based on results.
One-line takeaway
Use fit, pain, culture, and change effort to choose an approach, and prefer the smallest change that produces measurable improvement.
