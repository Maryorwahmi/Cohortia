Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Large-Scale Scrum (LeSS)
Core idea
LeSS applies Scrum principles to multiple teams building the same product while keeping added structure minimal.
Single product backlog
One prioritized backlog keeps teams aligned on product goals.
Joint events
Shared sprint reviews and retrospectives maintain shared learning and integration focus.
Strengths and caveats
Strengths: simplicity, close adherence to Scrum principles. Caveats: needs strong Scrum discipline and leadership support to work without added orchestration.
Concrete example
Three teams share one backlog for a single product. Joint sprint reviews reveal integration gaps sooner and keep the product coherent.
