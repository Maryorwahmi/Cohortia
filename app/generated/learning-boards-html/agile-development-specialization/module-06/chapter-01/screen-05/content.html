Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Why Organizations Scale Agile
Drivers (Process Steps)
01
Speed to market
Reduce cross-team delays through shared cadences and integrated planning.
02
Quality
Systematic integration and common acceptance criteria catch defects earlier.
03
Engagement
Teams stay motivated when they see end-to-end impact of their work.
04
Predictability
Shared planning windows create reliable release commitments.
Scenario Example
An ERP program with modules for inventory, finance, and reporting has repeated integration regressions because each team deploys independently. After introducing synchronized program increments and a solution backlog, integration happens on cadence and quality issues are found sooner.
Misconception
Scaling eliminates the need for team-level improvement. It does not — team practices must remain strong.
Pro Tip
Start by identifying the highest-cost dependency and pilot a cadence that targets it rather than launching full framework adoption immediately.
Key Takeaway

Focus scaling around the business problem — speed, quality, engagement, or predictability — and measure whether practices reduce the pain.
