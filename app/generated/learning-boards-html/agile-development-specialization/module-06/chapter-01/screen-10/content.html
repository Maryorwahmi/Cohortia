Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Scrum@Scale
How it scales
Scaling is done by connecting teams via Scrum of Scrums and leadership cycles, preserving team Scrum while creating escalation paths.
Scrum of Scrums
A coordination meeting where team representatives resolve cross-team dependencies.
Executive Action Team
A leadership body that removes systemic impediments and aligns strategy with operations.
Benefits and fit
Flexible and modular, Scrum@Scale is ideal when you want to extend Scrum with clear escalation without adopting a full prescriptive framework.
Concrete example
Teams agree representatives meet weekly to clear integration tasks; strategic blockers are elevated to a leadership group that decides policy changes.
