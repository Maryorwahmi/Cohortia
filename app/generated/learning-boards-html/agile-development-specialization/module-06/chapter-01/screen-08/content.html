Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe)
What SAFe provides
Defined roles, program cadences, and artifacts to coordinate many teams across programs and portfolios.
Agile Release Train
A long-lived team of teams that plans and delivers together on cadence.
Program Increment
A fixed planning window where teams commit to integrated features.
Strengths and cautions
Strengths: clarity, governance, predictable integration. Cautions: risk of excessive ceremony if adopted without tailoring or culture work.
Concrete example
A 50-team organization adopted Program Increments to align releases and cut integration defects by creating predictable integration windows.
Misconception
SAFe replaces the need for team-level improvement. In reality, team practices must stay healthy for SAFe to work.
