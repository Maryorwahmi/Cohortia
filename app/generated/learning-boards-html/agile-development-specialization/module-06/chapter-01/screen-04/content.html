Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › What 'Scaling Agile' Means
Core definition
Scaling Agile is applying Agile principles across multiple teams to deliver integrated solutions while preserving autonomy and agility.
Why it matters
Prevents integration regressions, aligns priorities, and enables predictable end-to-end delivery when many teams contribute to one product.
Concrete example
Ten teams working on ERP modules adopt a shared solution backlog and synchronized program increments so features spanning modules release together.
Common misconception
Scaling equals centralized control. In truth, it should add alignment without removing team decision-making.
Visual cards
Shared Artifacts
Program backlogs, roadmaps, and integration plans.
Coordination Roles
Product Management, Release Train Engineers, solution leads.
Cadence
Synchronized planning and integration windows reduce surprises.
Preserve Teams
Keep team-level Scrum practices while aligning at higher levels.
Key Takeaway

Scaling targets coordination problems so teams deliver integrated customer value, not to add bureaucratic layers.
