Team vs Program roles
Team roles
Product Owner, Scrum Master, cross-functional team members who own iteration outcomes.
Program roles
Product Management, Release Train Engineer, Solution Architect to coordinate across teams and stakeholders.
Implementation tip
When adding a program role, define decision rights clearly and coach both the new role and the teams for at least two program cycles.
Key artifacts and cadence
Solution backlog
Holds larger features that span teams and guide program priorities.
Program increment
A shared planning cadence used to align teams on integrated deliverables.
Definition of Done
An integrated acceptance definition for cross-team features.
Inspect & Adapt
Program-level retrospectives to continuously improve coordination.
Common mistake

Creating roles without coaching and responsibility clarity turns coordination into handoffs; pair role introductions with explicit ownership and training.
