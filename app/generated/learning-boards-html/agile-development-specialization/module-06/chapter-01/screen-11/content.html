Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Choosing a Scaling Framework
Decision checklist
1. Primary pain: integration, predictability, or siloed priorities?
2. Size & complexity: how many teams and shared dependencies exist?
3. Culture & leadership style: prescriptive or minimal?
4. Change effort: training, coaching, and role changes needed?
Example recommendation
Scenario: 10 teams on ERP, hierarchical leadership preferring structure. Recommendation: pilot essential SAFe constructs — program increment cadence and product management — while measuring integration defects and delivery predictability.
Pitfalls & metrics
Guard against: centralizing decision-making, adding ceremony without value, and skipping cultural coaching. Track metrics like integration defects, lead time for end-to-end features, and predictability of program commitments.
Key Takeaway

Choose the smallest, measurable intervention that addresses the highest-cost coordination problem and iterate from there.
