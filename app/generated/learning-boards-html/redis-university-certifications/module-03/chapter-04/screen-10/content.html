Architecture › Sentinel Quorum
sentinel.conf example
# sentinel monitor    
sentinel monitor mymaster 127.0.0.1 6379 2

# common options
sentinel down-after-milliseconds mymaster 5000
sentinel failover-timeout mymaster 60000
sentinel parallel-syncs mymaster 1
Deployment guidance
  • Run three or more Sentinels across separate hosts or zones.
  • Set quorum to a majority so at least that many Sentinels must agree.
  • Avoid co-locating Sentinels with the master to reduce correlated failures.
Key Takeaway

Quorum prevents split-brain. Choose placement and quorum to favor reliable failover without blocking recovery.
