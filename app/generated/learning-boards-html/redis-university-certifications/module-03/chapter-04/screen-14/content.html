What to Remember › Key Concepts
Backup
Create copies of dump.rdb and appendonly.aof and keep them off-site.
Restore
Recover data by placing persistence files in the Redis dir and restarting.
High Availability
Systems remain accessible despite component failures; Sentinel helps automate failover.
Summary

Combine reliable backups, off-site storage, tested restores, and Sentinel for availability to build resilient Redis services.
