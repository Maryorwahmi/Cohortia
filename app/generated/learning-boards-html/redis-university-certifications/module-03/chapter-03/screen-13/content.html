Question 1
You run a critical, real-time system where even a few seconds of data loss is unacceptable. Which persistence mechanism or combination do you recommend, and what appendfsync setting would you choose?
Expected answer
Enable both RDB and AOF. Configure AOF with appendfsync always for highest durability, or appendfsync everysec if latency must be balanced and you accept up to one second of possible loss.
Question 2
You maintain a regenerable cache of thumbnails. Which persistence strategy is appropriate and why?
Expected answer
RDB-only is often sufficient because the cache can be rebuilt. Use reasonable snapshot intervals to balance disk I/O and the acceptable recency of cached items.
