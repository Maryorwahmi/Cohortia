Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Quick Reminders
Enable Persistence
Never run stateful Redis without a persistence plan that matches your data needs.
Prefer BGSAVE
Use background saves to avoid blocking the server; configure sensible save directives.
Tune AOF
Select appendfsync according to durability needs and schedule BGREWRITEAOF to compact logs.
Final takeaway
Persistence settings are a business decision: choose them based on acceptable data loss, performance implications, and operational monitoring.
