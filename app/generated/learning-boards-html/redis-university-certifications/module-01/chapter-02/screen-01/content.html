Working with Redis Strings

Binary-safe storage, expirations, atomic counters, multi-key operations, and partial updates — practical tools you will use every day.

What you'll learn
Practical command patterns for strings, expirations, atomic counters, bulk reads, and partial edits.
Why it matters
These commands keep your cache correct, your counters race-free, and your network usage efficient.
Explore Key Topics
Strings as bytes
Binary-safe values up to 512 MB for text, blobs, and serialized objects.
Expirations
Control lifetimes with EXPIRE/TTL and millisecond variants.
Atomic counters
INCR/DECR keep counters correct under concurrent updates.
