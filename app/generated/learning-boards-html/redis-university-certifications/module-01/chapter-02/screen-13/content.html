Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Workflow Timeline
Timeline
  1. Initialize — SET counters and session payloads; MSET related keys.
  2. Operate — INCR counters for events; GET / GETRANGE for reads.
  3. Manage TTL — EXPIRE or PEXPIRE sessions; check TTL to debug.
  4. Refresh or Persist — EXPIRE again on activity or call PERSIST to make permanent.
  5. Cleanup — rely on expiry for removal and monitor for missing expirations.
Practical check

Add logging on expiry-related events and when INCR fails due to non-numeric values. These are the points that most often need operational visibility.
