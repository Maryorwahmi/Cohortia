Learning Objectives

Build the knowledge to choose and operate Redis persistence safely.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate RDB and AOF

Explain how snapshot files differ from append-only logs, and why their restart and recovery behaviors differ.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe RDB snapshotting

Understand the fork/write/replace sequence and the tradeoff between compact backups and potential recent data loss.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain AOF and fsync options

Tell how appendfsync settings affect data durability and write latency, and why everysec is often a good compromise.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Operate persistence safely

Edit redis.conf for save, appendonly, and dir; use BGSAVE and BGREWRITEAOF for non-blocking operations; avoid disk full conditions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
