Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Persistence
Ensures data survives restarts and crashes by writing state or write history to disk.
RDB Snapshot
Point-in-time binary snapshot written by a child process for compact backups and fast restarts.
AOF
Append-only command log that can be replayed at restart; durability depends on appendfsync policy.
One-line summary
Choose RDB for compact backups and fast restarts, AOF for stronger durability, and use both for critical systems while maintaining disk safety and backups.
