Assessment Idea
Validate your knowledge
Short hands-on checks and a conceptual question you can answer in writing.
Hands-on checklist
  1. Start redis-server and open redis-cli.
  2. SET my_name, my_age, my_city and GET each one.
  3. GET a non-existent key and observe (nil).
Quiz question
Which statement best explains why Redis is chosen for caching? Answer in two sentences, contrasting in-memory speed with disk-based databases and noting the effect on primary DB load.
Key Takeaway

Use these checks to confirm both that your environment works and that you can explain the architectural reasons for choosing Redis in caching scenarios.
