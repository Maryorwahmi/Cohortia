Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Overview
Redis Sentinel: Automated Failover and Monitoring
A distributed supervisor that keeps your Redis master available and clients informed.
In this lesson
Understand Sentinel architecture, the failover lifecycle, how quorum works, and how clients discover the active master.
Why it matters
Automated failover reduces human response time and keeps your service available during master outages.
Explore Key Topics
Sentinel Architecture
How multiple Sentinels work together to avoid a single point of monitoring failure.
Failover Lifecycle
From subjective down to objective down, election, promotion and reconfiguration.
Client Discovery
Clients query Sentinels for the current master's address instead of hardcoding it.
Concept Visual
Multiple Sentinels form a supervisory layer. They monitor master and replicas, vote on failures, elect a leader, and reconfigure replicas when promoting a new master.
Master
Primary instance responsible for writes. If unreachable, Sentinels begin monitoring for a failover.
Replica
Candidates for promotion. Selection uses replication offset and priority.
Sentinel
Runs monitoring, voting, election, and client discovery for the monitored service name.
