Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Failover Steps
Step-by-step
  1. Sentinels detect SDOWNs and reach quorum, marking the master ODOWN.
  2. Sentinels elect a leader among themselves to coordinate failover.
  3. The leader chooses the best replica by replication offset and priority.
  4. The leader promotes the replica and instructs others to follow it.
  5. Sentinels update their configs and notify clients of the new master.
Example commands
Promotion uses commands equivalent to REPLICAOF NO ONE on the promoted replica and REPLICAOF <new_master_ip> <new_master_port> on others.
Selection considerations
Sentinel prefers replicas that are up-to-date and eligible by configuration; replicas with lag or higher priority numbers may be deprioritized.
Failover timeout
failover-timeout limits how long promotion and reconfiguration may take.
Parallel syncs
parallel-syncs controls how many replicas sync with the new master at once to avoid overload.
