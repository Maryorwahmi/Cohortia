Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Lab Walkthrough
Step timeline
  1. Confirm master on 6379 and replicas on 6380, 6381 are running.
  2. Create sentinel1.conf, sentinel2.conf, sentinel3.conf with ports 26379, 26380, 26381 and monitor mymaster 127.0.0.1 6379 2.
  3. Start each with redis-sentinel sentinelN.conf and ensure Sentinels connect to each other.
  4. Use redis-cli -p 26379 then INFO sentinel and SENTINEL masters to inspect state.
  5. Shut down the master with SHUTDOWN and observe SENTINEL masters for sdown → odown → new master.
Verification & checks
After a new master appears, connect to its port and run INFO replication to confirm role and follower reconfiguration.
Important
Ensure firewall rules allow Sentinel and Redis ports between all nodes to avoid false SDOWN reports and failed elections.
