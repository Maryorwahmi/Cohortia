Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Monitoring & Failover
Monitoring
Sentinels poll Redis instances and exchange state among themselves to determine reachability and responsiveness.
Parameter
down-after-milliseconds controls how long to wait before marking a node subjectively down.
Notification
Sentinels emit events so administrators or automation can be alerted to state changes.
Automatic failover
When quorum agrees a master is objectively down, Sentinels elect a leader to promote a replica and reconfigure the group.
Failover timing
failover-timeout sets an upper bound on how long failover should take and helps avoid stuck elections or long partitions.
Replica sync
parallel-syncs controls how many replicas sync with a promoted master concurrently during failover.
Example

If down-after-milliseconds is 5000 and two Sentinels cannot reach the master for that time, they can mark it subjectively down; if quorum is met the master becomes objectively down and failover begins.
