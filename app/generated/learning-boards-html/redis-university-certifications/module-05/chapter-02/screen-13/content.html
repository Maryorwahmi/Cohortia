Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Design & Pitfalls
Odd Sentinels
Prefer 3 or 5 Sentinels to simplify majority voting and avoid split-brain scenarios.
Network rules
Ensure Sentinels and Redis nodes can reach each other; blocked ports cause false SDOWNs.
Timing values
Tune down-after-milliseconds and failover-timeout for your latency characteristics to avoid premature or delayed failovers.
Operational tip

Test failover flows in a staging environment: observe sdown → odown transitions, confirm the promoted master’s replication info, and adjust configurations before rolling to production.
