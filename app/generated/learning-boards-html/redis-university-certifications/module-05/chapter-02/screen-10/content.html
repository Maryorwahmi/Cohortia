Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Leader Election
Election mechanics
Once ODOWN is declared, Sentinels elect a leader to coordinate failover. The leader takes exclusive responsibility for promotion actions.
Why elect a leader?
Leader centralizes actions to avoid conflicting promotions and reduces coordination complexity.
Replica selection
Leader chooses the most up-to-date, eligible replica based on replication offset and priority settings.
Post-election actions
Promotion, reconfiguration of followers, update of Sentinel configuration, and client notification.
Check after failover
Run INFO replication on the new master to confirm role and ensure followers point to the correct address.
