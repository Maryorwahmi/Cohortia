Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Practice Goals
Verify Sentinel Status
Connect to any Sentinel with redis-cli and run INFO sentinel and SENTINEL masters to inspect monitored services and current state.
Check
Look for sdown/odown flags and master address entries for the service name you configured.
Simulate and Observe
Shut down the master and watch Sentinels move from subjective down to objective down, elect a leader, and promote a replica.
Verification
After promotion, connect to the new master and run INFO replication to confirm roles.
Deployment & Quorum
Use at least three Sentinels on separate hosts and prefer an odd number to simplify majority decisions during elections.
Common Pitfalls
Avoid single Sentinel deployments and ensure firewall rules allow Sentinel and Redis communication to prevent false positives.
