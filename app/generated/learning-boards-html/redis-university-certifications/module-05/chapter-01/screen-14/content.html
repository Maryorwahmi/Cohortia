Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Master‑Replica Architecture
Master handles writes; replicas mirror and serve reads for scaling and redundancy.
Asynchronous Replication
Master streams commands and does not wait for replica acknowledgements, favoring performance over immediate global durability.
Full and Partial Sync
Initial full sync uses an RDB snapshot and buffered commands; partial resync can replay missing commands from a backlog when possible.
Takeaway
Use replicas for read scaling and redundancy, monitor INFO replication and offsets, and enable persistence on the master to protect against data loss.
