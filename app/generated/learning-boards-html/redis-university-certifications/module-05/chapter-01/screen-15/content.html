Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Quick Reminders
Configure
Use REPLICAOF or replicaof in redis.conf and verify role with INFO replication after startup.
Monitor
Check master_link_status and offsets regularly to detect lag or disconnects.
Persist
Enable RDB or AOF on the master so data is recoverable after outages.
Final takeaway
Replication gives you read scaling and redundancy, but its value depends on correct configuration, active monitoring, and durable persistence on the master.
