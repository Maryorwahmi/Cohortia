Learning Objectives
Learn the concepts, apply configuration, and verify replication health.

Master
01
Explain master-replica architecture

Describe roles: master accepts writes; replicas mirror data and serve reads. This clarifies where to place write-only traffic versus read scaling.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Configure a replica using REPLICAOF

Use REPLICAOF or redis.conf replicaof to join a master; know the default read-only behavior and why writable replicas are discouraged.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Monitor replication health

Interpret INFO replication output, read the replication offset and connected replicas, and use this to detect lag or disconnects.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Manage consistency tradeoffs

Understand asynchronous replication implications for potential short windows of data loss and the role of persistence and failover tools.
