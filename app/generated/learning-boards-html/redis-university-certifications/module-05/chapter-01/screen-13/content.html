Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Troubleshooting & Persistence
Checklist of common mistakes
  • Forgetting replicaof so instance starts as master.
  • Attempting writes on read-only replicas and not handling errors.
  • Firewall or routing blocking replication ports between master and replicas.
Persistence advice

Enable RDB or AOF on the master so that a restart does not lose the authoritative dataset. Replicas mirror in-memory state but rely on the master's persistence for full recovery after total outages.

When to act
If INFO replication shows master_link_status:down or rising offset diffs, investigate network and replica performance immediately.
Pro tip
Automate configuration deployment and health checks to avoid human errors like missing replicaof lines.
Key Takeaway
Operational hygiene—correct configs, enabled persistence, and network checks—prevents most replication failures.
