Comparison

Union, intersection, difference

Use SUNION, SINTER and SDIFF to compute OR, AND and NOT across sets. They are fast and can operate on multiple sets at once.

SUNION
Returns members in any of the sets. Useful for broad audiences.
SINTER
Returns members common to all sets. Ideal for intersection queries like "tag A AND tag B".
Performance note: results can be large. Use STORE variants to persist expensive computations for reuse rather than re-running repeatedly.
Examples
SUNION set1 set2         # union
SINTER set1 set2         # intersection
SDIFF set1 set2          # difference
SINTERSTORE out set1 set2  # store intersection result
Scenario
Find users who like "sports" AND "news" with SINTER sport:users news:users, or store the result for repeated lookups with SINTERSTORE.
