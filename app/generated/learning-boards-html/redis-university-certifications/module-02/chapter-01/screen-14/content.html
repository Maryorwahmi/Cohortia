Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What to Remember
Group multi-key reads

Use MGET and MSET to reduce round trips when you consistently access multiple small keys together.

Use atomic increments

INCR and HINCRBY keep counters and numeric fields consistent without locks.

Model for access patterns

Prefer Hashes for mutable objects and Strings for whole-document or large binary blobs.

Final takeaway

Design your Redis schema by asking: what am I reading and writing most often? Match the type to the access pattern and prefer field-level operations to avoid costly rewrites.
