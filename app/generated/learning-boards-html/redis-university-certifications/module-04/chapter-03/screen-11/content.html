Module 04 › Chapter 4.3 › Issues & Mitigations
Stale Data
Fix by invalidating cache after DB update, or use short TTLs and event-driven invalidation to maintain freshness.
Cache Stampede
Mitigate with SETNX locks, probabilistic early expiration, or a background refresh to avoid a thundering herd on rebuild.
Cache Penetration
Cache negative results or use a Bloom filter to avoid repeated DB hits for non-existent keys.
Design trade-offs

Locks and filters add complexity and resource cost. Start simple: short TTLs and explicit invalidation, then add locks or Bloom filters if traffic patterns require stronger protections.
