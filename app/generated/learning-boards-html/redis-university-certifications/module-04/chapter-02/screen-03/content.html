Identify Common Pitfalls

06
Apply Retry Patterns
Implement loops that reattempt transactions after a WatchError, with small randomized sleeps to avoid livelock.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07
Use UNWATCH / reset
Know to release watches when aborting a transaction, either explicitly with UNWATCH or by resetting your pipeline to avoid stale locks.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08
Escalate to Lua When Needed
Recognize cases where complex logic or iteration requires server-side atomicity and fewer round trips, and move that logic into Lua scripts.
