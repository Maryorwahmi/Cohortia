Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Performance & Contention
Strategies to reduce contention
  • Watch only essential keys.
  • Keep processing between WATCH and EXEC minimal.
  • Use randomized backoff when retrying.
  • Consider Lua scripts to avoid repeated round trips.
Profiling under realistic concurrency reveals whether WATCH retries are a performance problem. Track WatchError counts, retry rates, and latencies to decide whether to refactor logic or use server-side scripting.
Measure
Log retry events and measure request latency to identify contention hot spots.
Key Takeaway
Design transactions to minimize the chance of aborts; switch to Lua when retry cost dominates.
