Monitoring and Observability for Redis

Built-in commands give immediate operational insight; external tools add history, dashboards, and alerts. Learn where to look and how to interpret the signals.

In this lesson
Use INFO, SLOWLOG, CLIENT LIST and other tools to assess Redis health.
Why it matters
Catch fragmentation, long commands, and client issues early to avoid outages.
Coming up
Hands-on checks with redis-cli and guidance on RedisInsight and Prometheus stacks.
Quick checklist
Run INFO, evaluate memory and stats, check slowlog entries.
When to escalate
High fragmentation, persistent slow commands, or low cache hit ratios require action.
Goal
Move from reactive debugging to proactive alerting and capacity planning.
