Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Tuning Process
Practical tuning steps
A staged approach reduces risk and identifies real impact.
Low risk
Client pipelining, increase concurrency, collocate clients and server.
Medium risk
Adjust appendfsync, change maxmemory-policy, test eviction behavior.
Higher risk
Sharding, changing persistence dramatically, or altering data semantics.
Example
Start by enabling pipelining for bulk writes and re-run the benchmark. If RPS improves and tail latency drops, document that change and proceed to other optimizations.
Key Takeaway

Adopt a staged plan: reproduce, change one thing, measure, and document. That keeps tuning accountable and safe.
