Turtlesim services
reset, clear, spawn, teleport, set_pen, kill — practice with CLI and scripts.
Stateless vs stateful
Stateless services are safer to retry; stateful ones depend on or change system state.
Topic + Service pattern
Use topics for continuous data and services for triggered commands like LEDs or grippers.
Summary takeaway

Practice with turtlesim services to build reliable service interfaces for your robots and follow design rules for fast, idempotent, and informative services.
