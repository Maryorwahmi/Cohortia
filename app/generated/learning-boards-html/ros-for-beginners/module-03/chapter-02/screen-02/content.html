Why these objectives matter
  • Services provide a reliable way to issue discrete commands that change robot state immediately.
  • Combining topics and services is a common architecture: topics stream data; services perform actions based on that data.
  • Designing services to be fast and idempotent prevents bugs when clients retry or experience latency.
