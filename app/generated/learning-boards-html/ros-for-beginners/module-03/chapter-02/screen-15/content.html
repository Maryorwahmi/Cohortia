Design best practices
Keep services fast, idempotent, and include success/error information.
Practical examples
Gripper control, mode switching, PID updates, calibration, and map saving are natural service uses.
When not to use services
Avoid services for streaming or long-running tasks; topics and actions are better suited.
Next steps
Implement the hands-on lab and a gripper service. Review your service responses and make them informative so clients can recover from failures.
