Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Topics vs Services vs Actions
Quick comparison
PatternUse caseBlocking
TopicsStreaming sensor data, continuous updatesNo
ServicesQuick queries and immediate commandsYes
ActionsLong-running goals with feedback and cancellationNo (async)
Design advice
  • Avoid polling services at high frequency; switch to topics for telemetry.
  • Use services for confirmations and single-shot commands.
  • Choose actions when operations are long and you need progress or cancellation.
Key Takeaway
Match your communication pattern to frequency and blocking requirements: streaming data uses topics, immediate queries use services, long tasks use actions.
