Understanding ROS Services
Synchronous request-response communication for fast, discrete robot tasks.

Services are the pattern you choose when a node needs an immediate answer or confirmation. They behave like remote function calls: a client calls, waits, and receives a response. Use services for short tasks such as querying status or toggling hardware state. Avoid services for streaming or long-running operations where non-blocking behavior is required.

Key Takeaway

Use services for short, discrete interactions where a node must wait for an answer. For continuous or high-frequency data prefer topics; for long-running goals prefer actions.
