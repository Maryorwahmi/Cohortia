Compare
Actions vs Services vs Topics
Short requests use services. Continuous sensor streams use topics. Multi-second, cancellable tasks use actions. This helps pick the right pattern for safety and responsiveness.
Practice
MoveRobot action lab
Define a MoveRobot.action, simulate motion for 5 seconds, publish feedback every 0.5 seconds, and implement cancel handling.
Reflect
State observation
Observe PENDING → ACTIVE → PREEMPTED or SUCCEEDED transitions and design clients to handle race conditions.
