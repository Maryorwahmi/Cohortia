Why actions are needed for long-running tasks
Services block. Actions keep an interactive session with feedback and cancellation.
Definition and contrast
  • Services: synchronous request-response. Good for short tasks like reading sensors.
  • Actions: asynchronous, continuous interaction with feedback and cancel support.
  • Topics: continuous publish/subscribe streams, not goal-directed tasks.
Concrete example
A robot arm move taking five seconds. With an action the client can receive progress and cancel if an obstacle appears. With a service the client is blocked and cannot cancel.
Common mistake
Running blocking services in threads does not give a standard feedback or preemption protocol. Actions are the formal solution.
Visual: decision cards
Use a service
Quick commands: get battery, set LED.
Use a topic
Sensor streams and continuous telemetry.
Use an action
Long-running tasks with progress and cancelation.
Key Takeaway

If you need feedback and cancellation for a multi-second task, design it as an action.
