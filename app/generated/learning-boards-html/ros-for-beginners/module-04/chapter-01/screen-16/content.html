Build steps
add_action_files, generate_messages, and declare actionlib_msgs in CMake and package.xml.
Server checklist
Check preemption each loop, publish feedback, and set_succeeded or set_preempted.
Client checklist
wait_for_server, send_goal with callbacks, use cancel_goal when needed, inspect status codes.
Final reflection

What long-running task on your robot would benefit from being converted to an action? Try implementing it as a MoveRobot-style action to see the benefits firsthand.
