status_monitor & led_controller

Publish a periodic RobotStatus and drive LED indicators via a service for quick debugging and demo visibility.

RobotStatus contents
position: x,y,z
obstacle_detected: true/false
waypoint_index: integer
Test

Use rostopic echo /robot_status and replay rosbag inputs to confirm correct content and timing.

LED service

/set_led service toggles three LEDs: red for obstacle, green for motion, yellow for at-waypoint. Call this service from status_monitor logic or set it manually during demos.

Key Takeaway

Expose observable state via topics and services so both tools and people can confirm system health quickly.
