Key Concepts — Part 1

Three compact reminders to guide your implementation and demo.

Single-responsibility nodes

Clear responsibilities and documented interfaces simplify testing and integration.

Dual-layer safety

Immediate stop plus a persistent emergency parameter ensures predictable halting.

Reproducible launch

One launch file that starts the simulation, nodes, and parameters makes demos reliable.

Summary

Focus on clear interfaces, safe-stop semantics, and reproducible orchestration to deliver a solid capstone demo.
