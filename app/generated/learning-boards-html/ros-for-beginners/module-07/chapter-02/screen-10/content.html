remote_controller (action client)

Send waypoint goals and demonstrate cancellation and feedback during the demo.

Demo script
  1. Start patrol and observe normal cycle.
  2. Send a remote goal to waypoint 2 and watch feedback.
  3. Cancel the goal mid-drive and confirm the robot stops and reports preemption.
Observation checklist
  • Action server accepts the goal and publishes feedback.
  • Cancel triggers preemption and the robot stops.
  • /robot_status reflects updated waypoint or cancellation state.
Common mistake

Not stopping /cmd_vel on preemption leaves the robot moving after cancellation; always publish zeros on cancel.
