Floating
6 DOF free body
Example: wheel joint
Use a continuous joint for wheel links. Set axis to rotate about the wheel axle and ensure origin places the wheel at the correct position relative to base_link.
Misconception
Choosing continuous instead of revolute removes angle limits and can break controllers expecting bounded motion. Always match joint type to your control requirements.
Check your understanding

If you change a wheel joint to revolute with tight limits, what controller behavior would change and why? Consider continuous rotation and encoder wrapping.
