Process
Add LiDAR and Camera
Attach a gazebo_ros_ray plugin for LiDAR. Configure topic name as /scan and an appropriate update rate.
Attach a gazebo_ros_camera plugin for a forward-facing camera that publishes /camera/image_raw.
In the lab: publish commands, inspect /scan and /camera/image_raw, and view images in RViz if available.
Example check commands
Use rostopic echo /scan to observe range arrays. Use rostopic echo /camera/image_raw or RViz Image display to inspect camera output.
Common pitfall
Expecting perfect data. Simulated sensors lack real-world noise and may require noise models for realistic testing.
