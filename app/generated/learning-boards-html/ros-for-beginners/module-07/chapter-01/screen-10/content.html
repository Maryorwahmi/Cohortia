Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Controlling the Robot
Process
cmd_vel → plugin → wheels → odom
  1. Publish geometry_msgs/Twist to /cmd_vel with linear.x and angular.z.
  2. Plugin computes wheel velocities from robot geometry and commanded twist.
  3. Plugin applies torques to joints and publishes odometry on /odom.
  4. robot_state_publisher and TF broadcast the odom→base_link transform for consumers.
Test Publisher Example
rostopic pub /cmd_vel geometry_msgs/Twist "linear:
  x: 0.5
  y: 0.0
  z: 0.0
angular:
  x: 0.0
  y: 0.0
  z: 0.2"
Verify
  • rostopic echo /odom to watch position updates.
  • Use TF viewers to confirm odom→base_link transform updates.
Pitfall
Publishing at an irregular or too-low rate can lead to jerky motion; controllers expect steady update frequency.
