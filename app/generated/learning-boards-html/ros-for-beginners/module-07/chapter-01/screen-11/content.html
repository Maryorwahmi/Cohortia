Process
From simulation to real robot
Simulated physics and sensors are approximations. Plan for friction, motor limits, and sensor noise when you move to hardware.
Start with low speeds and conservative torque limits both in sim and on hardware. Add safety checks and emergency stops.
Test incrementally: spawn, command, verify odometry, add sensors, then integrate autonomy layers gradually.
Why it matters
Prevents damage and builds confidence before field tests.
Practical step
Add sensor noise models or tune inertial values to better mirror hardware behavior.
Common pitfall
Overfitting to simulator detail makes real-world transfer harder; keep tests broad and incremental.
