Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Key Concepts
Match URDF & plugins
Ensure joint and link names referenced by plugins match exactly those in the URDF.
Simplify collisions
Use simple boxes, cylinders, or spheres for collision geometry to keep simulation performant.
Start conservatively
Use low velocities and torques in early tests to avoid unstable simulation and hardware surprises.
Final takeaway

Simulation accelerates learning and reduces risk. Use URDF and Gazebo intentionally, validate frequently, and treat simulation as an approximation that helps you prepare for real hardware.
