Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Simulation vs Reality
Differences to expect
  • Motors: friction, latency, and torque limits differ from idealized simulation.
  • Sensors: real noise, dropouts, and calibration errors change perception results.
  • Environment: real surfaces, obstacles, and interactions are less predictable.
Make simulation realistic
Add noise models to sensors, tune friction and damping, and test with non-ideal collision shapes to better emulate hardware.
Deployment checklist
  • Run low-speed hardware tests first.
  • Retune controller gains and verify sensor calibration on the robot.
Reflection
If you added realistic noise to sensors, how would your obstacle avoidance thresholds change? Consider increased false positives and conservative margins.
