Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Key Concepts
URDF: links & joints
Describes kinematics, visuals, collisions, and inertia for simulation and TF.
Gazebo: physics sandbox
Simulates collisions, sensors, and dynamics so you can validate behavior safely.
Plugins: ROS bridge
Translate ROS topics to simulated forces and publish sensor outputs back into ROS.
Takeaway

Use URDF and Gazebo together to iterate quickly and safely, but validate with hardware before deploying autonomy.
