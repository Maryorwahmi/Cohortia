Callbacks are reactive
ROS calls your function when messages arrive; avoid blocking it.
Spin to stay alive
Use spin so callbacks are dispatched until shutdown.
Naming & buffering
Use anonymous names for multiple instances; tune queue_size for buffering needs.
Final thought
Practice the Python and C++ examples, observe behavior with rostopic tools, and reflect on where callbacks should block or defer work to meet your system requirements.
