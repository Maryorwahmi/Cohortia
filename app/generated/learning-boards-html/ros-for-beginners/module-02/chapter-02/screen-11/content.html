Assessment scenario

Slow callback and publisher rate

Publisher at 1 Hz, callback takes 5 seconds. Compare queue_size 1 and queue_size 10.

queue_size = 1
Node processes 2 messages in 10 seconds (at t=0 and t=5). Intermediate messages are dropped; only the most recent buffered message survives while the callback is busy.
queue_size = 10
Node still processes 2 messages in 10 seconds, but up to 8 messages accumulate in the buffer. Those buffered messages may become stale if processing doesn't catch up.
Practical guidance
Buffering helps when short bursts cause temporary overload, but it does not increase long-term throughput. To handle steady overload, reduce processing time or parallelize work.
Key Takeaway

queue_size affects buffering but cannot overcome a slow single-threaded consumer; address the bottleneck in processing or change the producer rate.
