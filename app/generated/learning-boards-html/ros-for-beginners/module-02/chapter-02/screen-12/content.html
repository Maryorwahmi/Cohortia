Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Subscriber & Callback
ROS pushes messages to your callback; callbacks should be efficient to avoid delays.
Spin / Event Loop
Call spin to keep the node alive and let ROS dispatch callbacks until shutdown.
Buffering & queue_size
queue_size sets buffering; it doesn't increase processing capacity of a slow callback.
Summary takeaway
Use the subscriber–callback pattern with careful attention to callback duration, buffering, and the appropriate spinner for your language to build reliable ROS pipelines.
