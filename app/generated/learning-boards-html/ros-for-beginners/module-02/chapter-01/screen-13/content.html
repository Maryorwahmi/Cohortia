Assessment question

A camera at 30 Hz and a processor at 5 Hz: what happens to unprocessed images? How does queue_size change behavior?
Correct explanation
Images accumulate until the buffer fills. When full, older frames are dropped; queue_size trades freshness for history.
Scalability note
Publish-subscribe avoids O(N×M) point-to-point connections by allowing sensors to publish once while many consumers subscribe independently.
Reflective prompt
Which data from your robot would you publish as a topic and which would you handle with services or actions where request-response or guaranteed delivery is required?
