Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Queue Size & Buffering
Concept

queue_size controls local buffering

It determines how many messages the publisher will keep if subscribers are slow. When full, older messages are dropped to make room for new ones.

Example
Camera at 30 Hz vs processor at 5 Hz: with queue_size=10 the subscriber will keep the last up to 10 frames and drop earlier ones.
When to use small queues
For time-sensitive data where freshness matters, use queue_size=1 to always process the latest sample.
Common pitfalls
Misconception
queue_size guarantees delivery. It does not; it simply limits buffered messages and older ones are dropped when full.
Design question
Decide whether freshness or completeness matters for each topic and set queue_size accordingly.
Key Takeaway

Match queue_size to your data semantics: small for live sensors, larger for logs or events that must be replayed.
