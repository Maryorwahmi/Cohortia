Module 7 › Chapter 6.2 › Verification Steps
rqt_graph check
Confirm publisher and subscriber edges exist. If a node is isolated, you found a missing connection or launch issue.
RViz check
Set the fixed frame to a valid frame and ensure RobotModel reads /robot_description to display meshes and links.
rqt_plot check
Plot setpoint and measurement together. Look for spikes, oscillations, steady offsets, and noise that inform fixes.
rqt_reconfigure
Adjust gains or thresholds in real time and observe behavior without restarting the node.
rosbag
Record a short bag, inspect it with rosbag info, and replay to reproduce a bug offline.
