Module 7 › Chapter 6.2 › Record & Replay
Capture and reproduce runs
Record only the topics you need, then replay to validate subscribers and algorithms offline.
Lab sequence
  1. Record a short bag of /turtle1/cmd_vel and /turtle1/pose.
  2. Stop turtlesim and play back the bag.
  3. Use rostopic echo and a subscriber script to confirm messages are identical.
Commands
# Record pose and cmd_vel
rosbag record -O turtle_run.bag /turtle1/cmd_vel /turtle1/pose

# Stop turtlesim, then play back
rosbag play turtle_run.bag

# Inspect contents
rosbag info turtle_run.bag

# Echo during playback to confirm
rostopic echo /turtle1/pose
Practical tip
Keep recordings short and focused so inspection and replay are fast and reproducible.
Key Takeaway

Replay recorded data to reproduce behavior, validate subscribers, and decouple debugging from hardware availability.
