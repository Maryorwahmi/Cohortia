Recording and playback essentials
Record only the topics you need and replay with control over rate and looping to reproduce runs.
Why use rosbag
  • Reproduce bugs offline
  • Test algorithms with identical inputs
  • Collect datasets for analysis and learning
Commands
# Record all topics
rosbag record -a

# Record specific topics
rosbag record /camera/image_raw /scan /cmd_vel

# Record with custom filename
rosbag record -O my_data.bag /camera/image_raw /scan

# Inspect a bag
rosbag info my_data.bag

# Play back a bag
rosbag play my_data.bag

# Play back at half speed
rosbag play my_data.bag -r 0.5

# Loop playback
rosbag play my_data.bag -l
Common mistake
Recording everything creates large bags. Selectively record the topics relevant to the bug or test to save space and speed inspection.
Key Takeaway

rosbag is your reproducible data source: record concise topic sets, inspect them, and replay to validate fixes and tests offline.
