Daily debugging reminders
Observe
List nodes and topics to build a mental map before changing things.
Verify
Confirm message types and registrations to avoid silent mismatches.
Collect
Gather ~/.ros/log/latest for post-mortem analysis after failures.
These compact rules will help you act with confidence during debugging and reduce the time between an observed failure and a fix.
