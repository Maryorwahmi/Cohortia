Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Common diagnostics for subscriber silence
Step-by-step checklist
Step 1 — Does the topic exist?
rostopic list
Step 2 — Who's publishing?
rostopic info /topic_name
Step 3 — Are messages present?
rostopic echo /topic_name
Step 4 — Type compatibility
rostopic type + rosmsg show
Step 5 — Check logs
~/.ros/log/latest for node errors and stack traces
Step 6 — Restart if needed
rosnode kill and relaunch after investigating logs
This ordered checklist moves you from discovery to verification to evidence collection. It reduces unnecessary edits during debugging and points you to logs when behavior is intermittent or leads to a crash.
