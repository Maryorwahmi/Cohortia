ROS Command-Line Tools and Logging

A practical, terminal-first approach to inspect runtime ROS systems and collect logs for debugging.

In this chapter you will use the core CLI commands to answer three common questions: what is running, what data is flowing, and why did something fail. Commands such as rosnode, rostopic, and rosservice reveal registrations and live connections. rosparam and rospack help you interrogate configuration and package locations. Logging shows runtime errors and traces in ~/.ros/log for post-mortem debugging.
