  • After apt install: check ubuntu's disk space and apt list for ros-noetic-desktop-full

Why verification matters

Seeing turtlesim move and observing rosnode and rostopic output confirms message types, topics, and the master are operating correctly. Build and source steps are not just formalities: they expose compiled artifacts and message bindings ROS uses at runtime.
