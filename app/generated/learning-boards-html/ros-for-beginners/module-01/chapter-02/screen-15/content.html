Catkin workspace layout
src holds packages; catkin_make creates build and devel directories containing compiled artifacts.
Sourcing is required
Add setup scripts to ~/.bashrc or manually source them in each new terminal session.
Verify with turtlesim
Running roscore, turtlesim_node, and teleop confirms the master, nodes, and topics are functional.
Final takeaway

A reproducible install, consistent sourcing, and systematic verification let you move from setup to building your own ROS packages with confidence.
