Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What to Remember
Agree on names
Consistent topic and message names prevent integration mismatches.
Use correct primitives
Topics for streams, services for queries, actions for long tasks with feedback.
Simulate then test
Use Gazebo and RViz to iterate quickly, then validate on real hardware.
Final takeaway

Design nodes and channels intentionally, reuse community packages where appropriate, and validate behavior in simulation before deploying to hardware.
