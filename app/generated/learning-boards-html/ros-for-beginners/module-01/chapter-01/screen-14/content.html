Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
ROS = Middleware
Standardizes communication on top of an OS like Ubuntu.
Nodes
Independent programs, each with a focused responsibility.
Topics, Services, Actions
Streaming, request-response, and long-running tasks with feedback.
Takeaway

Use ROS when modularity, reuse, and simulation accelerate your robot project; prefer simpler embedded systems when low overhead or hard real-time is required.
