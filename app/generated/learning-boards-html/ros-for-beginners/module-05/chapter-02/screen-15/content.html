Module 6 › Chapter 5.2 › What to Remember
Modular includes
Compose systems with include and pass args for flexibility.
Param vs Rosparam
Use param for single values and rosparam for YAML with multiple keys.
Verify
Use output="screen", rosnode, rosparam, and rostopic to confirm runtime state.
Final thought

Treat launch files as part of your codebase: test, version, and modularize them so your robot system is repeatable and maintainable.
