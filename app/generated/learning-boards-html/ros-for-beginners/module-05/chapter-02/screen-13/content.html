Lab: Build my_system.launch
Follow these explicit steps and verify behavior
Step 1
Create launch/my_system.launch and set <param name="robot_name" value="turtlebot_01"/> and load <rosparam file="config/robot_params.yaml" command="load"/>.
Step 2
Launch Chapter 2.1 publisher as number_publisher and Chapter 2.2 subscriber as number_counter.
Step 3
Launch a second anonymous publisher instance and remap /number to /number_copy for it.
Step 4
Include the turtlesim launch file with <include file="$(find turtlesim)/launch/multisim.launch"/>.
Step 5
Run roslaunch and verify rosnode list and rosparam list show the expected nodes and parameters.
Step 6
Kill one node with rosnode kill and observe behavior. Then set required="true" on a node and observe whole-launch shutdown when it dies.
Learning outcome

Completing this lab gives practical experience composing, verifying, and debugging multi-node launch files and shows the effects of remapping and required nodes at runtime.
