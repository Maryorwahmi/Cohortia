Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › What to Remember
kubectl trio
kubectl logs, describe, and get events give pod and cluster context for failures.
Service Accounts
Cloud Build SA must have push roles; GKE pods use Workload Identity to access other GCP services.
Incremental fixes
Apply one small change at a time and re-run the pipeline to find the true root cause.
Closing thought

Effective CI/CD troubleshooting is mostly about the right questions: which logs show the error, what step failed, and what single change will remove the failure?
