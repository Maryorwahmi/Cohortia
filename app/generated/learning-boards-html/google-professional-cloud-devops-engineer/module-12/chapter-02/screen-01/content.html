Why structured logs matter

Structured logs are JSON objects that let you embed fields like user, ip_address, and transaction_id. That makes filtering, creating metrics, and routing much simpler and more reliable than parsing free-form text.

{
  "message": "User failed to login",
  "severity": "ERROR",
  "user": "john.doe",
  "ip_address": "192.168.1.100",
  "service": "auth-service",
  "transaction_id": "abc-123"
}
