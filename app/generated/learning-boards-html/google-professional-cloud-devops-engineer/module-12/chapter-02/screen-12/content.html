Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Volume & Retention
Practical strategies
  • Exclude or sample verbose logs in production.
  • Route infrequent or archival logs to Cloud Storage.
  • Use BigQuery for analytics, not for short-term monitoring.
  • Set retention policies on sinks to expire old logs automatically.
  • Create metrics so live monitoring uses aggregates, not raw logs.
Measure ingestion costs before and after changes. Use exclusion filters to prevent storing logs that are never used, and keep a policy that balances compliance needs with operational efficiency.
Key Takeaway

Be intentional: log what you need, archive the rest, and measure the cost impact of your choices.
