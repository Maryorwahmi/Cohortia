Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › RCA & Prevention
RCA checklist
- Capture timeline of events and actions taken.
- Link evidence to each hypothesis and test outcome.
- Identify a single root cause and supporting contributing factors.
- Define prevention tasks with owners and deadlines.
Example prevention

Add CI checks to validate container image references to prevent accidental typos reaching production.

Runbook update
Create a short playbook: symptom, first-read checks (describe, pod events), one or two diagnostic commands, and rollback steps. This reduces decision latency in future incidents.
Key Takeaway
Treat RCA as a deliverable with concrete actions. Prevention is the final, essential step of the debugging lifecycle.
