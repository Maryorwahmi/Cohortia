Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Key Concepts
Troubleshooting Mindset
Evidence-first, hypothesis-driven investigation.
Debugging Lifecycle
Identify, investigate, hypothesize, test, fix, verify, prevent.
Problem Scope
Determine whether the issue is per-user, regional, or global before deep dives.
Keep a short checklist: scope, describe outputs, hypothesis, minimal test, verification, and documentation. That checklist turns incidents into durable improvements.
