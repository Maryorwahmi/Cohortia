Process

Canary, Observe, Decide, Rollback

Canary changes
Deploy to a small subset of traffic to validate a fix before global rollout.
Observation window
Define what you will monitor and for how long before deciding to roll forward or back.
Rollback plan
Ensure a quick, tested path to revert changes, such as redeploying a previous image or toggling a feature flag.
Scenario
Example
A configuration change increases timeouts. Roll it out to a canary pool and monitor error rates and latency. If errors increase, revert the canary and investigate in staging.
Best practice
Practice rollbacks in non-production so your team can act calmly during real incidents.
