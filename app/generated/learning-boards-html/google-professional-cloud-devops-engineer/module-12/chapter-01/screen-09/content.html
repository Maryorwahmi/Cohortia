Pitfalls to watch for

Shotgun debugging
Making many changes at once prevents knowing which change affected the system and risks introducing new failures.
Skipping verification
Assuming a fix works without measuring the original symptoms and side effects can lead to regressions.
No documentation
Failing to record actions and outcomes makes post-incident analysis and prevention impossible.
Practical guardrails
  • Make one change and observe for a defined period.
  • Always have a rollback plan and test it in staging.
  • Document experiments and results immediately in the incident log.
Check your understanding

If you feel pressured to try multiple fixes, stop and write a short hypothesis; it will save time and reduce risk.
