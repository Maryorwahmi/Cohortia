Each milestone pairs a conceptual check with exercises: run gcloud commands, inspect events, and practice one-change-at-a-time in a sandbox cluster.

How to practice

Work through short exercises: inspect a VM state, describe a cluster, and debug a misconfigured GKE deployment. Record actions and results to build your incident history and prevention checklist.

Mnemonic
D-G-H-T-F-V-P: Define, Gather, Hypothesize, Test, Fix, Verify, Prevent.
Quick tip
Always ask: what changed? It narrows the search space faster than blind checks.
