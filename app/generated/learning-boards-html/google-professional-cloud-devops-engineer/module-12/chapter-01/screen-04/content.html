Definition

Evidence-first investigation

Move from symptoms to hypothesis. Observe, gather evidence, and avoid immediate fixes until you can test a proposed cause.
Concrete example
If a VM restarts, use a describe command to find a recent 'lastEvent' that explains why it rebooted before touching application code.
Common misconception
Assuming logs alone give the cause. Logs are evidence; you must test hypotheses that explain the evidence.
Key Takeaway
One change at a time and clear documentation save time and help later RCA.
Visual steps
1
Describe the symptom
Who, what, when, and where. Avoid vague reports.
2
Gather evidence
Logs, status fields, recent events, and monitoring trends.
3
Form and test
Make a focused hypothesis and design a minimal test.
Key Takeaway

Make observations repeatable and changes reversible. That protects production and helps post-incident learning.
