Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Debugging Lifecycle
Process Flow

Identify → Investigate → Hypothesize → Test → Fix → Verify → Prevent

Identify
Capture who is affected, when it started, and the initial symptoms.
Investigate
Collect logs, metrics, and resource descriptions to form facts.
Hypothesize & Test
Make a small, reversible change or reproduce the condition in staging to validate your theory.
Example scenario
Symptom
A microservice returns 500s after a deployment.
Investigation
Inspect pod events and describe the cluster to find image pull failures or misconfigured ports.
Takeaway
Follow the lifecycle: verify fixes and add monitoring to catch similar regressions early.
