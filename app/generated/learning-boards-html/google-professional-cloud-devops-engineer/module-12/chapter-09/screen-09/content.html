Strategy comparison

Canary
Small traffic subset, monitor metrics, expand if stable. Good for minimizing blast radius and testing performance in production.
Blue/Green
Parallel environment validated before switch. Strong for zero-downtime and clean rollback but higher temporary cost.
Rolling
Incremental replacements. Cost-efficient but risks exposing a wider user base quickly if issues arise.
Decision card
When exam scenarios require zero downtime plus automatic rollback on critical errors and mandate minimizing operational overhead, a blue/green or canary with automated monitoring-triggered rollback is likely the best choice. Match the scenario’s explicit constraints—cost sensitivity, need for gradual testing, and rollback automation—before selecting an answer.
Key Takeaway

Identify the scenario’s priority—cost, safety, or automation—then pick the deployment pattern that aligns with that priority.
