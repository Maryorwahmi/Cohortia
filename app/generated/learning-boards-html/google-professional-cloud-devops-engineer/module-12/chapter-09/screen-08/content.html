Definition

SLIs, SLOs, and error budgets

SLIs are the measurable indicators of user experience. SLOs define acceptable targets. Error budgets quantify how much missed target is tolerable and inform operational decisions such as performing risky changes or initiating rollbacks.
Concrete example
If latency SLI shows 99.5% but SLO is 99.9%, the error budget is being consumed and pushes the team to prioritize reliability actions over new features.
Common misconception
Confusing SLIs with raw metrics. Choose user-centric indicators that reflect experience, not every internal metric.
Operational cards
Incident response
Runbooks, communication, and post-mortems translate SRE policy into actions during outages.
Toil reduction
Automate repetitive tasks so human attention focuses on higher-value reliability work.
Key Takeaway

Translate user impact into SLIs and SLOs; use the error budget to decide whether to pause changes or expedite fixes.
