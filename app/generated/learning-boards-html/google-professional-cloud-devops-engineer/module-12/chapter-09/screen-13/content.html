Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Performance & Cost
Definition

Balance performance and cost

Choose autoscaling, managed services, and right-sizing based on traffic patterns and SLO requirements. Always justify trade-offs using measurable targets.
Concrete example
For bursty traffic, use autoscaling with warm pools or horizontal pod autoscaling to meet latency targets while avoiding constant overprovisioning.
Common misconception
Picking the lowest-cost option without validating SLOs. Cost savings are only meaningful if reliability and latency requirements are still met.
Supporting cards
Autoscaling
Use autoscaling policies tuned to user-facing SLIs so resources scale to meet demand without excess cost.
Cost monitoring
Monitor resource usage trends and set alerts for unexpected cost growth as part of operational hygiene.
Key Takeaway

Recommend autoscaling and managed services when they meet SLOs and reduce operational overhead while controlling cost.
