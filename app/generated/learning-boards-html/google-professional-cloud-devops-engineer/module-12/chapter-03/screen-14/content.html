Custom spans and annotations

Use manual spans to capture key business operations and attach attributes that let you filter traces for those scenarios.

Good attributes
Stable IDs like user_id or item_id that help filter traces without excessive cardinality.
Avoid
High-cardinality values like raw emails or long text blobs on every span.

Sampling and pitfalls

Sampling reduces volume by tracing only a subset of requests. Choose a sensible default and increase sampling for incidents or critical endpoints.

Sampling example
1 in 100 steady-state, but always-sample payment transactions during a rollout.
Common mistake
Over-instrumentation creates noise and overhead; under-instrumentation leaves blind spots. Aim for balanced coverage around critical paths.
