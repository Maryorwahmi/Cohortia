Definition

Span hierarchy and attributes

Spans form a tree where each child span is a sub-operation of its parent. Attributes let you add business context used for filtering.

Span contents
Name, start/end, duration, attributes, status, and events.
Example tree
process_order → validate_item → call_payment_service → write_db.
Practical cards
Why hierarchy matters
It shows nested latencies and makes root cause analysis local to a span's subtree.
Misconception
Recording every micro-operation as a span can add overhead; focus on meaningful boundaries.
Key takeaway
Design spans to capture clear business or technical steps so traces are actionable.
