Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › VPC Flow Logs
What they are

Structured records of network flows

Flow logs include src/dest IP and port, protocol, whether the flow was allowed, and a reporter field that often shows FIREWALL when a rule denied the flow.

Sample Cloud Logging filter
resource.type="gce_subnetwork"
jsonPayload.connection.src_ip="10.128.0.5"
jsonPayload.connection.dest_ip="10.128.0.6"
jsonPayload.connection.is_allowed=false
  • Enable at the subnet level; consider exporting to BigQuery for complex queries.
  • Look for is_allowed=false and reporter="FIREWALL" to prove a firewall denial.
  • Use timestamps and tags to correlate with incident windows.
Concrete example
Denied internal connection

A flow log entry showing is_allowed=false between two internal IPs points directly to a firewall rule. The reporter field often indicates where the decision occurred.

Misconception
Flow logs are sampled and not packet-by-packet captures. They are for pattern and configuration analysis, not raw packet debugging.
Key Takeaway

Use flow logs to gather conclusive evidence of allowed or denied traffic before changing network rules.
