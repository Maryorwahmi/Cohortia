Definition › SLOs & Error Budgets
SLO concepts
Define SLIs carefully, pick a target SLO, and monitor error budget burn to prioritize action.
Example
SLI: request success rate where status code is 2xx. SLO: 99.9% success over rolling 30 days. Error budget: 0.1% of requests may fail during that period.
Burn-rate alert
Set a burn-rate threshold, for example 2x over a one-hour lookback, to warn when budget consumption is rapid.
Operational impact
Using SLOs shifts decision making: you may pause feature launches or reduce risk when error budget burn is high, and focus engineering time where it most improves customer experience.
Key Takeaway

SLOs convert business expectations into operational signals. Watch burn rate to act before an SLO violation occurs.
