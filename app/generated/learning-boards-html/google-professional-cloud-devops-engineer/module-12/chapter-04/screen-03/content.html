What success looks like
Concrete validation steps you can run in the lab to confirm competency.
Dashboard validation
  • Charts for p99 latency, throughput, and 5xx error rate populate from simulated logs.
  • Time ranges and aggregator choices reveal consistent trends rather than noisy spikes.
Alert validation
  • A single multi-condition alert fires only when both error rate and throughput thresholds are met.
  • Notification channels correspond to severity and on-call schedules.
SLO and custom metric validation
  • SLO displays error budget burn rate and the burn-rate alert triggers under simulated sustained errors.
  • Custom metrics appear as time series and accept labels for filtering and grouping.
Common misconception
More charts do not equal better observability. Focused, business-aligned metrics give faster, clearer answers during a real incident.
