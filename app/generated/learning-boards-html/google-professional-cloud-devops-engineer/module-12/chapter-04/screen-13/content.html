Architecture › Observability Strategy
Integrated pipeline
Instrument, visualize, alert, and run playbooks that connect metrics, logs, traces, dashboards, and SLOs.
Instrument
Emit metrics and traces, write structured logs, and consider custom metrics for business signals.
Visualize
Design dashboards aligned to business flows and the four golden signals for quick triage.
Alert & Operate
Create multi-condition alerts and SLO burn-rate policies, route notifications by severity, and run incident playbooks.
Practice and testing
Run tabletop exercises and simulate errors so you validate detection, diagnosis, and response channels before a real outage.
Key Takeaway

Observability works when telemetry is instrumented, dashboards tell a clear story, alerts are precise, and teams practice the response.
