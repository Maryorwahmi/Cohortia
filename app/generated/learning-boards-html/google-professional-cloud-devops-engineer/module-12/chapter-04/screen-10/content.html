Process Flow › Create SLO
Step-by-step
  1. Define SLI: success = JSON log status_code = 200 or metric 2xx count.
  2. Choose SLO target and period, e.g., 99.9% over rolling 30 days.
  3. Create Service and map SLI numerator and denominator to metrics.
  4. Configure burn-rate alert: threshold and lookback (e.g., 2x over 1 hour).
  5. Assign notification channels and test by simulating errors.
Simulation checklist
  • Run simulated error log writes to produce 5xx entries.
  • Wait for log-based metrics to populate and for SLO dashboard to update.
  • Confirm burn-rate alert triggers and notification is received by on-call.
Key Takeaway

SLOs and burn-rate alerts give you early operational signals to act before customer-visible violations occur.
