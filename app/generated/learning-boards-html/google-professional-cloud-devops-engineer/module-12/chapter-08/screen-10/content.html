Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Communication
Internal

Concise, actionable updates

Use an incident channel, assign roles, and post status updates at regular intervals. Include actions and next checks.

Example:
P1: Checkout 5xx spike. Rollback initiated for revision-42. Monitoring recovery. Next update in 10 minutes. IC: @alice
External

Transparent, non-technical messages

Provide users with clear impact statements and expected updates through a status page or customer-facing channel.

Example:
We are experiencing issues with checkout causing failed purchases for some users. We are investigating and working to restore service. Updates at xx:xx.
