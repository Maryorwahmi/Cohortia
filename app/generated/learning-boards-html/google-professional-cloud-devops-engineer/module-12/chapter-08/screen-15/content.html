Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › What to Remember
Alert context
Every alert should include service, metric, threshold, recent deploy, and a runbook link for fast diagnosis.
Communicate clearly
Internal updates are actionable; external updates are transparent and non-technical to manage expectations.
Track actions
Make post-mortem items specific, assign owners, set due dates, and verify completion to reduce future incidents.
Final reflection
Which single runbook or automation would reduce MTTR most in your environment? Commit to implementing it.
