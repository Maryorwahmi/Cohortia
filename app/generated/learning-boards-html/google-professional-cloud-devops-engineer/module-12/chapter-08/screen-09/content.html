Recovery

Stabilize and validate customer journeys

Use end-to-end checks and metrics to confirm restoration. Observe error rates, success metrics, and latency for a prescribed window before closure.

Perform synthetic checks for critical flows like checkout and login.
Scale resources if needed and watch for regressions for at least one monitoring window.
Handoff checklist
  • Confirm incident timeline is complete.
  • Record mitigation steps and verification evidence.
  • Assign owners for follow-up action items.
Key takeaway
Recovery is more than 'service up'. It is verified stability and a clear plan for follow-up work.
