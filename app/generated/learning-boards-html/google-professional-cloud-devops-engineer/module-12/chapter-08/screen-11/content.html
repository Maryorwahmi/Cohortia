Post-mortem

Focus on learning, not blame

Summary: Concise high-level overview of what happened.
Impact: What failed, who was affected, duration.
Detection: How it was discovered and MTTD.
Response: Actions taken with timestamps and actors.
Action items
  • Assign measurable tasks with owners and due dates.
  • Link runbook updates and required tests to each item.
  • Track completion as part of reliability improvements.
Example action
Add a checkout 5xx alert with deploy context and automate collection of top error logs. Owner: @devops. Due: 2 weeks.
