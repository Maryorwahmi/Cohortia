Mitigation

Restore service with reversible steps

  • Rollback recent deploys when a release is suspected.
  • Restart or scale pods to restore capacity.
  • Disable features or add rate limiting to reduce pressure.
Action logging
Record every mitigation step with time and actor. These entries feed the post-mortem and help verify effectiveness.
Example: rollback sequence
1) Confirm suspect revision. 2) Trigger rollout undo. 3) Monitor rollout status until healthy. 4) Update incident timeline.
Misconception
Mitigation must be perfect. In practice, quick, reversible actions that restore service are best; permanent fixes are done after diagnosis.
