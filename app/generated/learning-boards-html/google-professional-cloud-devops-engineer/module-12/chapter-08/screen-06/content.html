Severity matrix

Decide response by impact and urgency

P0
Complete outage of critical flow. Mobilize all hands and immediate rollback options.
P1
Significant degradation affecting many users. Assign incident commander and start mitigation.
P2+
Limited impact, lower priority, tracked for regular work.
Decision anchors
  • Percent of users affected or failure rate.
  • Business-critical flows affected (payments, signups).
  • Expected duration and ability to mitigate quickly.
Example triage flow
  1. Confirm alert with logs and metrics.
  2. Check recent deploys and feature flags.
  3. Declare severity and assign roles (IC, Tech Lead, Comm Lead).
  4. Open incident channel and record timeline entries.
Pro tip
Automate initial context collection: recent deploy ID, pod status, and top error logs to save diagnosis time.
