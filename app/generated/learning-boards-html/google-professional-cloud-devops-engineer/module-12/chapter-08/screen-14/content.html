Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Key Concepts
Incident Management
A structured process for responding to and learning from service disruptions.
Runbooks & Automation
Documented steps and small scripts that reduce manual work and human error.
Blameless Post-Mortem
A review that focuses on causes, lessons, and measurable action items—never individuals.
Summary takeaway
Measure MTTD and MTTR, automate common actions, and turn every incident into tracked improvements.
