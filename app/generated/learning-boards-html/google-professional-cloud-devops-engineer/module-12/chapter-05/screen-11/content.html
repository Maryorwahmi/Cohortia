Comparison › Snapshot vs Logpoint
Snapshot
Captures full state and call stack for a specific execution. Ideal for deep debugging of rare or complex failures.
  • Deep variables and stack.
  • Limited hits to control overhead.
  • Not for continuous monitoring.
Avoid placing snapshots in tight loops or very high-traffic paths.
Logpoint
Injects formatted log messages with evaluated expressions. Use for repeated observations, frequency analysis, and quick checks.
  • Lightweight, streams to Cloud Logging.
  • Good for trend detection and high-frequency lines.
  • Does not capture call stack or full locals.
Pro-tip: minimize logged fields to prevent excessive log volume and to avoid exposing sensitive information.
