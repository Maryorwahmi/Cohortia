What to Remember
Cloud Debugger
Inspect running apps without stopping traffic.
Snapshot vs Logpoint
Snapshot for full state; logpoint for lightweight repeated logs.
Safety & Mapping
Ensure IAM, accurate module/version, minimal captures, and cleanup.
Summary Takeaway

Use Cloud Debugger to inspect live systems safely: pick the right point type, prepare your agent and mapping, and always limit and remove temporary points to protect production stability and data privacy.
