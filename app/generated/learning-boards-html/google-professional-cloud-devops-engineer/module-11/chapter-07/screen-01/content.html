Advanced Release Strategies and Progressive Delivery

Learn progressive delivery patterns—canary, blue/green, dark launches, and feature flags—and the GCP services that implement them. Focus on safe rollouts, experimentation, and automated rollback.

Canary Deployments
Gradually shift traffic to a new version and monitor metrics before full rollout.
Blue/Green
Maintain two environments and switch instantly to rollback quickly.
Feature Flags
Decouple deploy from release to toggle features and run experiments.
Visual Concept
Controlled exposure, monitor, expand or revert.
