Architecture › Traffic Management
GCP traffic primitives
  • • Global HTTP(S) Load Balancer: backend services, URL maps, weight-based routing.
  • • Cloud Run: native revision traffic splitting by percent.
  • • Istio/Anthos & Traffic Director: fine-grained routing, header rules, and mirroring.
Combination pattern
Use load balancer for coarse weighting and a service mesh to refine routing inside clusters when you need header-based control.
Common mistake
Overcomplicating routing with too many layers makes troubleshooting and automation harder during incidents.
Key Takeaway

Choose the simplest traffic primitive that satisfies your control needs and automate weight changes through a pipeline for consistency.
