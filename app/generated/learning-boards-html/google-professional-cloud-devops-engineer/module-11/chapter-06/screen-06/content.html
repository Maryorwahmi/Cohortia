Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Demand-based Planning
Method
Correlate a business metric to resource units.
  • Choose a representative business metric (concurrent users, TPS).
  • Measure resource consumption per unit of that metric.
  • Forecast growth in the business metric and scale resources accordingly.
Example
If 100 concurrent users require 1 vCPU and 2GB memory, then 1,200 users map to 12 vCPUs and 24GB across instances; add a safety margin for bursts.
Validation & pitfalls
Re-validate the mapping after code changes, traffic pattern shifts, or the introduction of caching, because these factors alter the resource-per-user ratio.
Monitoring tip
Plot the business metric and CPU usage on the same dashboard to observe correlation and detect drift.
Key Takeaway

Demand-based planning maps business growth directly to technical capacity, making forecasts actionable.
