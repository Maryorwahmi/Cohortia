Comparison
Least Privilege vs Defense-in-Depth
Least Privilege: Grant minimal permissions needed by identities.
Defense-in-Depth: Use multiple independent controls to reduce single points of failure.
Together they reduce both the probability and impact of a compromise by limiting access and providing backups when a control fails.
Concrete Measures
  • Scope IAM roles to specific secrets and APIs.
  • Use Artifact Registry and Binary Authorization to block untrusted images.
  • Enable network and runtime protections as additional layers.
Common Mistake

Relying solely on one layer, like IAM, without runtime or artifact checks leaves gaps attackers can exploit.

Key Takeaway

Combine least privilege with layered defenses to minimize impact and catch failures early.
