Runbooks, Playbooks, and GCP Automation

Design operational procedures, automate safe remediation, and orchestrate actions across GCP services.

Estimated time: 12 minutes
In this lesson
Compare runbooks vs playbooks, build an automated diagnostic runbook, and explore self-healing patterns with safety controls.
Why it matters
Automation reduces manual steps, speeds resolution, and makes incident response repeatable and auditable.
Concept Map
