Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › On-call Rotation
Design for on-call
Provide clear runbooks, mentorship during initial rotations, and post-shift time to remediate technical debt discovered while on-call.
Benefits
  • Increased empathy and better design choices.
  • Distributed knowledge reducing single points of failure.
  • Faster diagnosis through ownership context.
Avoid these mistakes
  • Placing inexperienced people on-call without pairing.
  • No time allocated for fixing issues found during on-call.
  • Not updating runbooks after incidents.
Key Takeaway

Make on-call an educational, supported experience and ensure findings lead to tracked improvements.
