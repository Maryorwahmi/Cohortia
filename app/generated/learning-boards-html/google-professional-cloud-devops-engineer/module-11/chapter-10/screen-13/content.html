Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Hands-on Activity
Scenario
A 30-minute checkout outage due to a misconfigured network policy that blocked the external payment gateway. The service resumed after rollback.
Post-mortem outline
  1. Incident summary: what, impact, duration.
  2. Timeline: detection, diagnosis, mitigation, resolution.
  3. Root cause: technical, process, human factors.
  4. Lessons learned: what worked and what failed.
  5. Action items: concise tasks with owners and deadlines.
Suggested actions
  • Implement automated network policy validation in CI (Owner: DevOps, Due: 2 weeks).
  • Update payment gateway documentation with required egress IPs (Owner: SRE, Due: 1 week).
  • Run training on network policy best practices (Owner: SRE Lead, Due: 3 weeks).
  • Explore canary deployment for policy changes (Owner: DevOps, Due: 1 month).
Expected Outcome

A concise, blameless post-mortem with specific, assigned actions that reduce recurrence and improve future detection and validation.
