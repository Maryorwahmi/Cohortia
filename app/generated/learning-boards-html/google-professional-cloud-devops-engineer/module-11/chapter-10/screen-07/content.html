Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Feedback Loops
Core loops
SLO Reviews: monitor error budget trends and flag services needing reliability work.
Design Reviews: SRE input in planning to shift-left operational concerns.
On-call Rotation: developers take turns on-call to gain operational empathy.
Concrete example:
If checkout latency spikes repeatedly, an SLO review surfaces the trend. Product and engineering can then agree to re-prioritize database tuning over a new UI feature, reducing error budget consumption and improving user experience.
Checklist to make loops effective
  • Schedule reviews at regular cadences and publish minutes.
  • Assign owners for decisions and track follow-up in a visible board.
  • Measure success by the operational signal that triggered the loop.
Misconception: ad-hoc meetings without documented outcomes are not feedback loops; they are conversations. Make them measurable.
