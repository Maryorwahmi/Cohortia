Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Toil Reduction
Identify & quantify toil
List repetitive tasks, measure frequency and average time, and compute annual time cost. Use this to prioritize automation candidates.
Example prioritization
  • High frequency, high time: automate first.
  • Low frequency, high risk: consider automation plus safety checks.
  • Low benefit: document a runbook rather than automating immediately.
Automation best practices
  • Write tests for automation and monitor its health.
  • Keep automations small, observable, and reversible.
  • Document assumptions and failure modes in a runbook.
Key Takeaway

Focus on automating high-frequency, low-judgment tasks and monitor automation outcomes.
