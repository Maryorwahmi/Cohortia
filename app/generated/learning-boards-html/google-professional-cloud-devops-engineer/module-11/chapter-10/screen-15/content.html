Safe Reporting
Encourage admission of mistakes and near-misses to enable learning.
Action & Follow-up
Always end post-mortems with owned, timeboxed action items and verify their completion.
Institutionalize Feedback
Make SLO reviews and design reviews regular, documented, and measurable.
Final reflection
Pick one small experiment from these reminders and run it for one month. Measure the outcome and iterate.
