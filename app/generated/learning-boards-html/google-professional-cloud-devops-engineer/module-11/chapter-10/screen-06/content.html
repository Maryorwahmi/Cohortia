Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Blame vs Learning
Blame Cycle
Symptoms: secrecy, short-term fixes, and a focus on the individual responsible.
  • Post-mortems emphasize who erred.
  • Engineers hide near-misses to avoid consequences.
  • Repeated outages with superficial fixes.
Result: higher operational debt and slower long-term velocity.
Learning Cycle
Symptoms: transparent reporting, tracked action items, and shared responsibility for reliability.
  • Blameless post-mortems focus on system causes.
  • Action items are assigned, visible, and measured.
  • Design choices shift to reduce operational risk.
Result: fewer repeats, more automation of toil, and improved product quality.
Practical step
Start one small ritual this month—an explicit blameless post-mortem or a recurring SLO review—and track the first three action items to completion.
