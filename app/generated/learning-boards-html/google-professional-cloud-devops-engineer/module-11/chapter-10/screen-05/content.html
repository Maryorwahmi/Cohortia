Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Blameless Post-Mortem
Standard post-mortem steps
1. Incident summary
2. Timeline of events
3. Root cause & contributing factors
4. Action items with owners
Concrete example
30-minute checkout outage from blocked payment gateway due to a broad network policy. Actions: automated validation in CI, update docs for gateway IPs, and run training session.
Key Takeaway

A post-mortem must end with assigned, prioritized action items that are tracked to closure.

Checklist to avoid common mistakes
  • Make the post-mortem blameless and focus on systemic causes.
  • Limit the report to a concise timeline and 3–5 contributing factors.
  • Create no more than seven action items and assign an owner and due date for each.
Pro tip
Integrate action items into your tracking board and review them during SLO reviews. Visibility and deadlines are what turn learning into change.
Misconception check: A long narrative without owners rarely prevents recurrence—prioritize tracked fixes.
