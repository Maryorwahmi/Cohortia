Embed SRE in design reviews
Require an operability section in every design document that addresses monitoring, rollback strategy, performance limits, and failure modes.
Observability: instrumentation and meaningful metrics.
Resilience: retries, timeouts, and circuit breakers.
Operational playbooks: runbooks and rollback steps.
Concrete example: require design docs to list expected SLOs, monitoring dashboards, and a simple rollback plan. SREs validate these items during the review and suggest mitigations where needed.
Key Takeaway

Early SRE participation prevents reliability debt and reduces later firefighting.
