Rituals that anchor culture
SLO Reviews
Regularly review SLO compliance and error budget trends with product and engineering to prioritize reliability work.
Design Reviews
Invite SREs into early design conversations to shift-left reliability and avoid embedding known failure modes.
On-call Rotation
Rotate developers into on-call to increase empathy and reduce reruns of the same operational mistakes.
Measurables to track progress
  • Action item closure rate from post-mortems and the age of open items.
  • Error budget consumption trends and frequency of SLO breaches.
  • Number of runbook updates, tech talks held, and mentorship pairings created.
Start with one measurable and iterate. Measurement drives attention, and attention drives behavior change.
