Fail fast
Block progression on critical scenario failures to prevent regressions from advancing.
Publish reports
Upload HTML or XML artifacts every run so failures are documented and diagnosable.
Stabilize tests
Ensure scenarios are independent and environments are predictable to avoid flaky results.
Final takeaway

When BDD is integrated into CI/CD correctly, executable specifications become living documentation and trusted quality gates that protect delivery velocity and product behavior.
