BDD for Microservice Integration

Orchestrating scenarios to validate end-to-end business processes across multiple services.

Complex Orchestration

Integration tests require careful setup and coordination of multiple services and their dependencies.

Common Mistakes

Avoid overly complex or brittle scenarios; ensure clear API contracts for each service.

End-to-End Scenarios

BDD scenarios can define business processes that span multiple microservices, validating their collective behavior.

Production Mimicry

Run integration tests in environments that closely resemble production to ensure realistic validation.

Key Takeaway

BDD for microservice integration ensures that complex business flows work across services, but requires careful design to avoid brittleness.
