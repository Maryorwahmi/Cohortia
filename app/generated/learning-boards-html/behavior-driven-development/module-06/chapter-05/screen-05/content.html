Key Challenge

Maintaining Ubiquitous Language at Scale

Ensuring consistent terminology across diverse teams and domains.

The Problem: Inconsistency

Different teams interpret the same business terms differently, leading to miscommunication and incorrect Gherkin scenarios.

Example: 'Customer' Definition
  • Team A: Defines 'customer' as an individual user.
  • Team B: Defines 'customer' as a corporate client.
  • Result: Conflicting behaviors and automation.
Key Takeaway

A shared, consistent ubiquitous language is fundamental for preventing miscommunication and ensuring accurate system behavior across all teams in large-scale BDD.

Solutions for Alignment
Centralized Glossary

Establish a single, authoritative glossary for all business terms, regularly reviewed and updated by stakeholders and teams.

Cross-Team Three Amigos

Conduct regular sessions at program or portfolio levels to align understanding on features spanning multiple teams.
