Advanced Collaboration: Story Mapping & CDC

Leveraging Story Mapping and Contract Testing for complex dependencies.

Story Mapping for Dependencies

Visualizes user journeys across teams, identifying dependencies and clarifying responsibilities for BDD scenarios.

Concrete Example

A 'Checkout' story map reveals dependencies between 'Cart,' 'Payment,' and 'Shipping' teams, guiding their BDD efforts.

Contract Testing (CDC)

Ensures services meet consumer expectations by validating API contracts, preventing breaking changes.

Why it Matters

Reduces integration issues and fosters proactive communication between interdependent teams.

Key Takeaway

Story Mapping and Contract Testing are vital for managing dependencies and ensuring robust cross-team collaboration in scaled BDD.
