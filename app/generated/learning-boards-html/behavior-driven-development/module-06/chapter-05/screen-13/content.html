Fostering a BDD Community of Practice

Establishing shared knowledge and reusable test data for consistent BDD.

Community of Practice (CoP)

A forum for BDD practitioners to share knowledge, discuss challenges, and evolve best practices across teams.

Why it Matters

Fosters organic growth, consistency in ubiquitous language, and shared automation patterns.

Shared Data Setup

Reusable tools or services for setting up consistent test data in shared environments.

Benefit

Reduces individual team burden, ensures consistency, and makes scenarios more reliable and repeatable.

Key Takeaway

A BDD Community of Practice and shared data setup are vital for fostering organic growth, consistency, and efficiency in large-scale BDD adoption.
