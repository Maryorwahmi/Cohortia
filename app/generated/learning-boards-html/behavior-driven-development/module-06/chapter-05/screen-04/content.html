Core Concept

Scaling BDD: Beyond a Single Team

Understanding the complexities of enterprise-wide adoption.

Key Challenges
  • Managing distributed knowledge across diverse teams.
  • Ensuring consistent automation and shared step definitions.
  • Handling complex cross-team dependencies effectively.
  • Fostering a culture of collaboration across organizational silos.
Why it Matters

Scaling BDD successfully leads to higher quality software, reduced miscommunication, and a more aligned organization, delivering greater business value.

Key Takeaway

Enterprise BDD is a cultural transformation requiring deliberate strategies for managing distributed knowledge, shared automation, and cross-team dependencies.
