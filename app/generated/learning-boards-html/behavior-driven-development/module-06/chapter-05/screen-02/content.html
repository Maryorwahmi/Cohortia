Learning Objectives

What you'll achieve by the end of this chapter.

LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Identify Scaling Challenges

Recognize the unique complexities of implementing BDD across multiple teams or large organizations. This skill is vital for proactive problem-solving and strategic planning.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Formulate Shared Gherkin Strategies

Develop effective strategies for managing shared Gherkin feature files and step definitions in a multi-team environment. This ensures consistency and reduces duplication.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Apply BDD to Microservices

Understand how BDD principles can be effectively applied to complex architectural patterns like microservices. This helps in defining and validating service contracts.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Discuss Cultural Shifts

Examine the cultural and organizational shifts required for successful enterprise-wide BDD adoption. This includes leadership buy-in and cross-functional collaboration.
