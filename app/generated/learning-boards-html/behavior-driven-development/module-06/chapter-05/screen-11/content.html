Cultural Transformation for BDD Adoption

Essential cultural and organizational shifts for successful BDD at scale.

Leadership Buy-in

Senior leadership must champion BDD, supporting investments in training, tools, and process changes.

Cross-Functional Teams

BDD thrives when product, development, and QA collaborate closely, often requiring team restructuring.

Training & Coaching

Provide continuous training on BDD principles, Gherkin writing, and automation best practices.

Key Takeaway

Scaling BDD requires a fundamental cultural shift, driven by leadership, cross-functional teams, and continuous learning.
