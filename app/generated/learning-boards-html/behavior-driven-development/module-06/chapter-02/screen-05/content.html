Three Amigos
A short workshop with Product, Developer, and Tester to produce examples that define expected behavior.
  1. Start with a user story or need.
  2. List business rules that apply.
  3. Pick a few concrete examples to express rules as scenarios.
Pro tip
Keep examples small and representative. Two or three well-chosen scenarios often beat a long list of edge cases.
Example Mapping snapshot
A typical result is a compact map: rules in the center, examples to one side, open questions to the other. The map produces scenario candidates that can be refined into Given–When–Then.
Concrete scenarios
Scenario: Add a new product successfully
  Given I am an authenticated administrator
  When I submit a product with name "Laptop Pro", price 1200.00, and stock 50
  Then the response is 201 Created and the product includes an id

Scenario: Attempt to add product with missing name
  Given I am an authenticated administrator
  When I submit a product without a name
  Then the response is 400 Bad Request and the error "Product name is required."
Key Takeaway

Discovery produces testable scenarios that travel through design and development.

Common mistake

Prioritizing many edge cases over representative examples, which creates maintenance overhead.
