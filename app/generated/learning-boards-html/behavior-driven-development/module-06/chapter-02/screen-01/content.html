BDD Across the Software Development Lifecycle
Use concrete examples to create shared understanding from discovery through maintenance. Make scenarios your design, development, and safety net.
In this lesson
You will learn how Three Amigos sessions, Gherkin scenarios, and executable feature files influence requirements, API and UI design, implementation, and post-release maintenance.
Why it matters
BDD reduces ambiguity, surfaces technical risks early, and creates living documentation that speeds onboarding and reduces regressions.
