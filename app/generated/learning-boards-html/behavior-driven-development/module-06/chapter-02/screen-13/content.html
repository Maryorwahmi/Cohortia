Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Integrating BDD
Practical steps
  1. Run Three Amigos during refinement for priority stories.
  2. Require at least one executable scenario for definition of ready.
  3. Automate critical behaviors and add to CI pipelines.
Ownership
Assign scenario maintenance to the owning team and review scenario changes like code changes.
Measure impact
Collect simple signals: fewer acceptance rejections, faster onboarding from living docs, and reduced regression incidents after CI catches behavior breaks.
Key Takeaway

Small, repeatable practices and team ownership make BDD sustainable and valuable in the long run.
