Living documentation explained

When feature files are maintained and automated they act as a human-readable specification. This requires regular updates and team discipline so scenarios reflect current behavior.

Concrete example

A registration scenario without the new email verification step will misrepresent the system; update the scenario when the flow changes.

Common misconception

Writing features once is not enough; they must be reviewed whenever related code changes.

Why it matters

Living docs make behavior explicit and reduce ambiguity between stakeholders and implementers.

Risk

If scenarios are outdated they become noise and reduce confidence in the test suite.

Check your understanding
  • Does the scenario reflect current business rules?
  • Is the language understandable to non-technical stakeholders?
Key Takeaway

Treat feature files as living artifacts: update them with feature changes and verify behavior after refactor.
