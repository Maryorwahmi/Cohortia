Quantitative Measurement

Tracking BDD's Tangible Impact

Key metrics to evaluate BDD effectiveness.

Feature Completion Rate

Percentage of Gherkin features with all scenarios passing. Indicates implementation success.

Scenario Stability/Pass Rate

Consistency of automated BDD scenarios. High rate means reliable test suite.

Defect Escape Rate

Defects found in production not caught by BDD tests. Lower is better.

Lead Time / Cycle Time

Time from idea to deployment. BDD aims to shorten this.

Key Takeaway

Quantitative metrics provide objective data points to assess BDD's impact on development efficiency and product quality.
