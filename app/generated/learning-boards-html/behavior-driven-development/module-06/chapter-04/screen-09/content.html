Qualitative Impact

Fostering Collaboration and Shared Understanding

Assessing the human element of BDD success.

Improved Collaboration

Are the 'Three Amigos' regularly meeting and genuinely collaborating? Are product owners, developers, and testers speaking a shared language?

Assessment Methods

Team surveys, observation of meetings, and informal feedback can reveal reduced misunderstandings.

Enhanced Shared Understanding

Does the team have a clearer understanding of the business domain and user needs? Are Gherkin scenarios effective communication tools?

Assessment Methods

Retrospectives are ideal for discussing clarity, and observing scenario discovery sessions helps gauge understanding.

Key Takeaway

BDD's qualitative success is evident in improved team collaboration and a deeper, shared understanding of project requirements.
