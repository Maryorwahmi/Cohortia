Critical Practice

Prioritizing Flaky Test Resolution

Why stable tests are paramount for BDD trust.

Understanding Flaky Tests

Automated tests that sometimes pass and sometimes fail without any changes to the underlying code or environment, often due to non-determinism.

Impact

Flaky tests erode trust in the test suite, leading developers to ignore failing builds and potentially missing real regressions.

Immediate Action Required

Prioritize identifying and stabilizing or removing flaky tests as soon as they appear. Investigate non-deterministic elements (e.g., race conditions, timing issues).

Safety Note

A test suite with high flakiness is dangerous, providing false signals and compromising quality assurance.

Key Takeaway

Flaky tests are a critical threat to BDD's effectiveness; their immediate resolution is essential to maintain trust and reliable quality signals.
