Quality & Efficiency Metrics

Defect Escape Rate & Lead Time

Assessing production quality and delivery speed.

Defect Escape Rate

Measures defects found in production that were *not* caught by automated BDD tests. A decreasing rate indicates effective early issue detection.

Why it matters

Lower escape rates mean higher quality software and reduced costs from late-stage bug fixes.

Lead Time / Cycle Time

Tracks the total time from idea conception to deployment for a new feature. BDD aims to shorten this by clarifying requirements and reducing rework.

BDD's Influence

Clearer requirements and reduced rework from BDD directly contribute to faster delivery cycles.

Key Takeaway

BDD helps reduce defects in production and shortens delivery times by improving clarity and efficiency throughout the development process.
