Evolving BDD

Adapting BDD to Your Context

Strategies for continuous evolution and refinement.

Start Small

Don't try to apply BDD to every aspect overnight. Pick a critical feature, implement BDD there, learn, and then expand gradually.

Experiment with Tools

Explore various BDD tools like SpecFlow or Behave, or even custom solutions, to find what best fits your team's ecosystem and needs.

Refine Your Gherkin

Continuously work on improving your ubiquitous language and the clarity of your Gherkin scenarios. This is an ongoing process for better communication.

Address Flakiness Immediately

Flaky tests erode trust. Prioritize fixing them as soon as they appear to maintain confidence in your automated test suite.

Key Takeaway

BDD is flexible; adapt it by starting small, exploring tools, refining Gherkin, and promptly fixing flaky tests to ensure its continued value.
