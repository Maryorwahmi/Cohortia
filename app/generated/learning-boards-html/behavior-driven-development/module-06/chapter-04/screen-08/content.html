Performance & Beyond Numbers

Test Execution Time & Qualitative Insights

Balancing speed with the human aspects of BDD.

Test Execution Time

Monitor the time it takes for your automated BDD test suite to run. Long execution times hinder fast feedback and can lead to ignored tests.

Optimization Tip

Use test tagging and parallelization strategies to keep execution times manageable and maintain fast feedback loops.

Qualitative Benefits

BDD's greatest strengths often lie in benefits like improved collaboration, shared understanding, and increased confidence, which are harder to quantify.

Measurement Approach

Assess through team surveys, observation, informal feedback, and dedicated retrospective discussions.

Key Takeaway

While optimizing test speed is important, BDD's qualitative benefits like collaboration and confidence are equally vital and require different assessment methods.
