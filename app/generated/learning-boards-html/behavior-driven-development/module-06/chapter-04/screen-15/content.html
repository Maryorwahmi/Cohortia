Key Concepts

What to remember from this chapter

Retrospective

A regular meeting where a team reflects on its recent work, identifies what went well, what could be improved, and creates actionable plans.

Continuous Improvement

An ongoing effort to enhance products, services, or processes, crucial for evolving BDD practices.

Adaptation Strategy

A plan for continuously evolving BDD practices based on team needs and project context.

Chapter Summary

Retrospectives and continuous improvement are fundamental to adapting BDD, ensuring it remains effective and aligned with evolving team and project needs.
