Defining Scope

Feature and Rule: Setting the Stage

From overall goal to specific business constraints.

Blue Card: Feature/Story

This is the starting point, representing the user story or feature being discussed. It defines the overall goal from the user's perspective.

Example: "As a user, I want to place an online order."

Yellow Card: Rule

These cards represent the specific business rules that govern the feature. A feature often has multiple rules that dictate its behavior.

Example: "Minimum order value must be met."

Blue Card: Online Order Placement
Yellow Card: Minimum order value must be met
Yellow Card: Customer must be logged in
Key Takeaway

Blue Cards define the feature's overarching goal, while Yellow Cards specify the individual business rules that govern its behavior, forming the foundation for detailed exploration.
