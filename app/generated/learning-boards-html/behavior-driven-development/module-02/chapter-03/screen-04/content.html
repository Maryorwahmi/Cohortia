Core Concept

Why Example Mapping?

A visual, collaborative technique for complex features.

Purpose

To explore complex features or business rules in detail, breaking them down into concrete examples and identifying ambiguities.

Key Benefits

  • Ensures shared understanding among the team.
  • Uncovers edge cases and hidden assumptions early.
  • Clarifies ambiguities before development.
  • Provides a clear visual representation of feature scope.

Collaborative Discovery

The 'Three Amigos' (Product Owner, Developer, Tester) work together to map out feature behavior.
