Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Example Mapping Fundamentals

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Example Mapping

A collaborative BDD technique using visual cards to explore a feature's business rules through concrete examples and identify open questions.

Blue Card (Feature/Story)

Represents the main user story or feature being discussed, defining the overall goal.

Yellow Card (Rule)

Represents a specific business rule that governs the feature's behavior.

Summary Takeaway

Example Mapping provides a structured, visual approach to BDD, starting with the Feature (Blue Card) and defining its governing Rules (Yellow Cards) to ensure a shared understanding.
