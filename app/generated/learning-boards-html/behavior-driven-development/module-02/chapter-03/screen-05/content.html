The Four Pillars of Example Mapping

Each card type plays a distinct role in clarifying behavior.

Blue Card: Feature/Story

The user story or overall goal being discussed.

Yellow Card: Rule

A specific business rule governing the feature's behavior.

Green Card: Example

Concrete instances illustrating how a rule applies.

Red Card: Question

An ambiguity or uncertainty requiring clarification.

Key Takeaway

The four distinct card types—Feature, Rule, Example, and Question—form the structured framework for Example Mapping, guiding collaborative discovery and clarity.
