Maximizing Example Mapping's Impact

Uncovering complexity and avoiding pitfalls.

Key Benefits

  • Quickly exposes complexity and hidden assumptions.
  • Makes abstract rules tangible, preventing misunderstandings.
  • Provides clear visual representation of feature scope.
  • Reduces costly rework by clarifying early.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rushing Questions: Not explicitly capturing or resolving red cards.
  • Technical Focus: Getting bogged down in implementation details instead of behavior.
  • Lack of Facilitation: Allowing discussions to stray or dominate.
  • Ignoring Edge Cases: Not exploring unusual scenarios thoroughly.
Key Takeaway

To maximize Example Mapping's benefits, focus on thorough exploration of rules and examples, explicitly resolve all questions, and ensure discussions remain centered on business behavior, not technical implementation.
