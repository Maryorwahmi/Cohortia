Illustrating Behavior

Green Cards: Bringing Rules to Life

Concrete instances for both positive and negative scenarios.

Positive Example

Illustrates a scenario where the business rule is successfully met, leading to the expected positive outcome.

Order value $50, minimum $25 -> order proceeds

Negative/Edge Case Example

Demonstrates what happens when the rule is violated, or an unusual condition is met, leading to an error or alternative flow.

Order value $10, minimum $25 -> error message
Key Takeaway

Green Cards provide concrete examples, including both positive and negative scenarios, to clearly illustrate how business rules apply and form the foundation for Gherkin scenarios.
