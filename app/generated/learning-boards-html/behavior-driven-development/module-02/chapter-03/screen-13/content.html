Guiding Effective Example Mapping

Ensuring focus, participation, and question resolution.

Facilitator's Role

  • Keep Focused: Steer discussions to business behavior, not technical implementation.
  • Encourage Participation: Ensure all 'Three Amigos' contribute and feel safe to ask questions.
  • Time Management: Guide the session to cover all necessary rules and examples efficiently.
  • Neutral Stance: Remain impartial, focusing on clarifying understanding rather than dictating solutions.

Question Resolution

  • Address All Red Cards: Every question must be explicitly resolved, not ignored.
  • Immediate Answers: If possible, answer questions on the spot, leading to new rules or examples.
  • Explicit Deferral: If an answer isn't immediately available, assign an owner and a clear follow-up plan.
  • Embrace Questions: View red cards as valuable insights into complexity, not roadblocks.
Key Takeaway

Effective Example Mapping relies on skilled facilitation to maintain focus, ensure inclusive participation, and rigorously resolve all identified questions, leading to a truly robust understanding of the feature.
