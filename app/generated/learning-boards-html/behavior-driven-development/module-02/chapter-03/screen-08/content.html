Red Cards: The Power of Questions

Explicitly capturing uncertainties and areas needing clarification.

Red Card: What if...?

"What happens if a discount brings the order below the minimum value?"

Uncertainty
Ambiguity
Edge Case

Why They Matter

Red cards highlight potential bugs or missed requirements, forcing explicit discussion and resolution.

Resolution Path

Questions must be answered, leading to new rules/examples, or explicitly deferred with a plan.

Key Takeaway

Red Cards are crucial for capturing questions and ambiguities, ensuring thorough exploration of edge cases and preventing costly misunderstandings in development.
