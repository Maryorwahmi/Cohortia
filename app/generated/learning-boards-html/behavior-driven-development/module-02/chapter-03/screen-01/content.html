Example Mapping: Bridging Conversations to Clarity

Transforming abstract ideas into concrete, shared understanding.

Example Mapping is a powerful, visual technique that helps teams explore complex features by breaking them down into specific rules and concrete examples. It fosters deep collaboration among stakeholders, ensuring that every aspect of a feature is understood and any ambiguities are explicitly addressed before development begins. This proactive approach minimizes misunderstandings and reduces costly rework.

Key Idea

Example Mapping uses specific instances to clarify complex rules, ensuring everyone understands the desired behavior.
