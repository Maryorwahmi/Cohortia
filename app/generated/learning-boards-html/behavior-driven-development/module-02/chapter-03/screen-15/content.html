Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Deepening Your Understanding

Further terms for mastering Example Mapping.

Green Card (Example)

A concrete instance illustrating how a rule applies, forming the basis for Gherkin scenarios.

Red Card (Question)

Represents an ambiguity, uncertainty, or a point requiring further clarification or discussion.

Edge Cases & Ambiguity Resolution

Specific conditions revealing unexpected behavior, and the process of clarifying unclear statements, both key benefits of Example Mapping.

Summary Takeaway

Green Cards provide concrete examples for rules, Red Cards capture critical questions, and Example Mapping as a whole is vital for uncovering edge cases and resolving ambiguities, leading to robust specifications.
