Session Dynamics

The Collaborative Discussion Flow

From User Story to Concrete Examples

1

PO Presents User Story

Outlines the high-level 'what' and 'why' of the feature.

2

Open Discussion & Exploration

Amigos contribute perspectives, asking clarifying questions.

3

Focus on Concrete Examples

Abstract requirements become tangible through specific scenarios.

4

Uncover Ambiguities

Hidden assumptions and missing requirements are identified early.

Key Takeaway

The informal, example-driven nature of Three Amigos sessions is key to building a shared, explicit understanding of feature behavior.
