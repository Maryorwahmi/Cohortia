Key Concepts Revisited

Essential Roles and Benefits

Developer

Focuses on the 'how' of implementation, technical feasibility, and design.

QA Engineer/Tester

Concentrates on 'how we'll know it works,' identifying edge cases and ensuring testability.

Defect Prevention

The proactive identification and resolution of potential issues early in the development lifecycle, a key benefit.

Summary Takeaway

By integrating the Developer and QA perspectives early, Three Amigos sessions proactively prevent defects and build quality into the software from the start.
