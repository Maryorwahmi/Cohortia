Best Practices

Facilitating Effective Sessions

Maximizing Collaboration and Clarity

Ensure Active Engagement

Invite input from all participants, especially quieter ones, to ensure diverse perspectives are heard.

Encourage Open Questioning

Foster a safe environment where assumptions can be challenged and critical questions are welcomed.

Focus on Concrete Examples

Shift discussions from abstract requirements to specific, tangible scenarios to uncover ambiguities.

Capture Agreements

Document the agreed-upon behaviors and scenarios in a structured format for future reference.

Safety Note

Be mindful of power dynamics; ensure everyone feels comfortable contributing. The goal is shared understanding, not just agreement.
