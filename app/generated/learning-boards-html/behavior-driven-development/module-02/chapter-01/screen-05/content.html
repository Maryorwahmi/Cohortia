The Product Owner: Defining 'What' and 'Why'

Articulating Business Value and Desired Outcomes

Product Owner's Focus

Represents the business perspective, defining the 'what' (feature) and 'why' (business value).

Key Contributions
  • Articulates the business problem and desired outcome.
  • Explains the value the feature delivers to users.
  • Provides user stories, wireframes, or market research.
Example Question

"What specific user problem does this feature solve, and how will we measure its success?"
