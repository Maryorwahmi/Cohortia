Chapter Summary

Key Concepts Revisited

Reinforcing Core BDD Principles

Three Amigos Session

A collaborative meeting involving a Product Owner, Developer, and QA Engineer to discuss and clarify a feature or user story.

Shared Understanding

The primary goal of the session, ensuring all team members have a consistent and comprehensive understanding of requirements.

Product Owner

Represents the business perspective, defining the 'what' and 'why' of the feature.

Summary Takeaway

The Three Amigos session is a collaborative cornerstone in BDD, ensuring a unified understanding of features from business, technical, and quality perspectives.
