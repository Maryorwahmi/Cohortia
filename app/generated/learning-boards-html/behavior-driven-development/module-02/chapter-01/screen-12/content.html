Challenges

Avoiding Common Pitfalls

Ensuring Your Sessions Remain Effective

Pitfall 1: Status Update

Treating the session as passive information sharing, not active discussion.

Misses the opportunity for collaborative discovery.

Pitfall 2: One-Sided Conversation

Allowing one 'Amigo' to dominate, silencing other crucial perspectives.

Leads to incomplete understanding and missed issues.

Pitfall 3: Uncaptured Agreements

Failing to document agreed-upon behaviors and scenarios.

Shared understanding erodes, leading to future ambiguities.

Key Takeaway

Proactively addressing common pitfalls ensures Three Amigos sessions remain dynamic, inclusive, and effective in building shared understanding.
