Extending Steps: And and But Keywords

Adding clarity without repeating main Gherkin keywords.

Conciseness

Combines multiple conditions or actions under a single main keyword.

Improved Readability

Makes scenarios flow more naturally, enhancing human understanding.

Key Takeaway

'And' and 'But' keywords are essential for adding detail to Gherkin steps while maintaining readability and conciseness.

Full Scenario Example with And/But
Scenario: Successful login with valid credentials
  Given I am on the login page
  And I have a registered account with username "testuser" and password "password123"
  When I enter "testuser" into the username field
  And I enter "password123" into the password field
  And I click the "Login" button
  Then I should be redirected to the dashboard
  And I should see a welcome message "Welcome, testuser!"

Notice how 'And' extends each step, making the scenario detailed yet readable.
