Best Practices

Crafting Effective Scenarios: Do's and Don'ts

Ensuring clarity, conciseness, and business readability.

Focus on Business Readability

Use plain language, avoid technical jargon.

Avoid Multiple 'When' Steps

Each scenario should test a single, distinct action.

Describe Observable Outcomes

'Then' clauses should focus on user-verifiable results.

Don't Include Implementation Details

Focus on 'what' the system does, not 'how'.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize clarity, single actions, and observable outcomes in Gherkin to create effective and maintainable scenarios.

Scenario Refactoring Example
# Bad Scenario: Too many 'When' actions
Scenario: User adds product to cart and checks out
  Given I am logged in as "customer@example.com"
  And I am on the product page for "Laptop X"
  When I click "Add to Cart"
  And then I navigate to the checkout page
  And then I enter my shipping details
  And then I click "Place Order"
  Then the order should be placed successfully
  And I should receive an order confirmation email
# Good Scenario: Single action focus
Scenario: Add product to cart
  Given I am on the product page for "Laptop X"
  When I add "Laptop X" to my cart
  Then I should see "Laptop X" in my cart
