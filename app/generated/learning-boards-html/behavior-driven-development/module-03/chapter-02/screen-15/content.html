Key Concepts: Advanced Techniques

Structured data and data-driven testing approaches.

Data Table

A structured way to pass multiple values or complex data directly within a `Given`, `When`, or `Then` step.

Data-Driven Testing

A testing approach where a single test case is executed multiple times with different input data, often via `Scenario Outline`.

Anti-Patterns

Common misuses of Gherkin features, such as `When`/`Then` in `Background` or over-complex `Data Tables`, leading to confusion.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering `Data Tables` and understanding `Data-driven testing` principles, along with avoiding anti-patterns, leads to highly effective BDD specifications.
