Best Practices

`Background` Structure and Scope

`Given` steps only, executed before each scenario in a feature.

Key Rules for `Background`

  • `Given` Steps Only: A `Background` block should exclusively contain `Given` steps. Its purpose is to set up a state, not to perform actions (`When`) or verify outcomes (`Then`).
  • Placement: Defined once per feature file, after the `Feature` declaration and before any `Scenario` or `Scenario Outline`.
  • Scope: The steps in `Background` run before *every single scenario* within that specific feature file.
  • Conciseness: Keep `Background` short and focused on truly common, universal preconditions.
Why it Matters

Adhering to these rules ensures `Background` enhances clarity and maintainability without introducing confusion or unexpected test behavior.

Example: Calculator Feature with Background
Feature: Basic Calculator Operations
  As a user,
  I want to perform basic arithmetic calculations
  So that I can get quick results.

  Background:
    Given the calculator is turned on
    And the display shows 0

  Scenario: Add two numbers
    When I enter 5
    And I press "+"
    And I enter 3
    And I press "="
    Then the display should show 8

  Scenario: Subtract two numbers
    When I enter 10
    And I press "-"
    And I enter 4
    And I press "="
    Then the display should show 6
Key Takeaway

A `Background` should be concise, contain only `Given` steps, and apply universally to all scenarios in its feature.
