Learning Objectives

What you'll achieve in this chapter (Part 1)

LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Apply Feature Narrative Structure

Master the 'As a... I want... So that...' template to clearly define the user, their goal, and the business value of each feature. This ensures immediate context and alignment for all team members.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Formulate Clear Gherkin Steps

Craft concise, unambiguous Given, When, Then steps using ubiquitous language, focusing on 'what' the system does rather than 'how.' This ensures resilience to changes and broad understanding.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Identify & Avoid Anti-Patterns

Recognize and correct common Gherkin anti-patterns, such as overly technical steps, UI-specific dependencies, or excessively long scenarios. This improves maintainability and focus.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Structure Feature Files Logically

Organize feature files effectively using Backgrounds, Scenario Outlines, and Tags to enhance readability, promote maintainability, and facilitate team collaboration.
