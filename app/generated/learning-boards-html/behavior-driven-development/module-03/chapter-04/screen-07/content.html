Anti-Pattern

Avoiding Overly Long Scenarios

Focus on a Single Observable Outcome

Characteristics of Overly Long Scenarios

  • •

    Multiple 'When' Steps: Indicates testing too many actions.

  • •

    Convoluted 'Given' Setup: Complex preconditions suggest a lack of focus.

  • •

    Many 'Then' Assertions: Trying to verify too many outcomes from one action.

Impact

Difficult to read, understand, debug, and maintain. Provides unclear feedback on specific feature failures.

Key Takeaway

Keep scenarios focused on a single, distinct behavior to improve readability, maintainability, and the clarity of test results.

Refactoring Example

Poor: Multi-Action Scenario

Scenario: User logs in, adds item, and checks out
  Given I am logged in
  When I add "Product A" to cart
  And I proceed to checkout
  And I confirm payment
  Then my order should be placed

Tests too many distinct behaviors.

Better: Focused Scenarios

Scenario: Add product to cart
  Given I am logged in
  When I add "Product A" to cart
  Then my cart should contain "Product A"

Scenario: Place an order
  Given I have "Product A" in my cart
  When I proceed to checkout and confirm payment
  Then my order should be placed

Each scenario tests a single, clear outcome.
