Avoid Common Anti-Patterns

Learn to identify and avoid pitfalls like overly technical steps, UI-specific dependencies, and excessively long scenarios, ensuring your Gherkin remains robust and maintainable.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
06

Structure Feature Files Logically

Organize your feature files effectively using Backgrounds, Scenario Outlines, and Tags to enhance readability, promote maintainability, and facilitate team collaboration.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07

Focus on Behavior, Not Implementation

Understand the critical distinction between describing 'what' a system does (behavior) versus 'how' it does it (implementation details), ensuring Gherkin remains high-level and resilient.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08

Collaborate for Continuous Improvement

Recognize that Gherkin is a living document that benefits from regular team review and refinement, ensuring it remains accurate and reflects the current understanding of system behavior.
