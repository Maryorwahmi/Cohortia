Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential ideas from this chapter (Part 1)

"As a... I want... So that..." Narrative

A structured template for feature descriptions that clarifies the user's role, capability, and business benefit, ensuring shared understanding of the 'why'.

Ubiquitous Language

The shared, consistent language used by all team members (business and technical) in Gherkin, code, and discussions, avoiding technical jargon.

Declarative vs. Imperative

Gherkin steps should be declarative (describing 'what' happens) rather than imperative (describing 'how' it happens), focusing on observable behavior.

Summary Takeaway

By applying these foundational principles, you create Gherkin that is clear, collaborative, and truly reflects the business domain, serving as a robust living documentation.
