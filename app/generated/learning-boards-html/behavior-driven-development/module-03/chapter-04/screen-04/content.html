Core Concept

Crafting the Feature Narrative

The "As a... I want... So that..." Structure

The Narrative Template

As a [Role]

Identifies the user or actor. Provides perspective.

I want [Capability]

States the user's goal or desired action.

So that [Benefit]

Explains the business value or ultimate outcome.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined feature narrative ensures shared understanding of the 'why' behind a feature, aligning business goals with development efforts.

Concrete Example

Feature: Password Reset
  As a registered customer,
  I want to reset my password
  So that I can regain access to my account if I forget my credentials.
  • •

    Role: Clearly identifies the 'registered customer' as the user.

  • •

    Capability: States the user's goal: 'reset my password'.

  • •

    Benefit: Explains the value: 'regain access to my account'.

Pro-Tip

Regularly review feature narratives with business stakeholders to ensure they accurately reflect current understanding and business value.
