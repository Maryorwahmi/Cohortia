Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Gherkin

A plain-text, human-readable language for describing software behavior in BDD, bridging business and technical teams.

Feature

The highest-level Gherkin keyword, defining a single, distinct piece of functionality or a user story.

Scenario

A concrete example of behavior for a given feature, describing a specific sequence of actions and expected outcomes.

Chapter Summary

Gherkin provides a structured, human-readable way to define software behavior, with Features outlining capabilities and Scenarios detailing concrete examples.
