Key Concepts

The Building Blocks of Behavior

Given

A Gherkin keyword used to establish the initial state or context (preconditions) before an action occurs.

When

A Gherkin keyword describing the single action or event that triggers the behavior being tested.

Then

A Gherkin keyword describing the observable outcome or result that should occur after the When action.

Chapter Summary

The Given, When, and Then keywords form the core of Gherkin scenarios, defining the context, action, and expected outcomes of software behavior.
