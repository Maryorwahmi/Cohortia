Strategic Commenting

Providing clarity without clutter.

Good Use: Explain 'Why'

Add context for complex business rules or specific edge cases that are not immediately clear from the steps.

Good Use: Reference External Info

Link to design documents, bug reports, or other relevant artifacts for deeper understanding.

Key Takeaway

Comments should add essential, non-obvious context, clarifying the 'why' and linking to external information, without repeating the obvious or detailing implementation.

Comment Pitfalls to Avoid

Bad Use: Over-Commenting

Writing extensive comments for simple scenarios indicates the Gherkin itself might be unclear or too complex.

Bad Use: Explaining 'How'

Comments should not describe the technical implementation of a step; that belongs in the step definition code.

Misconception: Comments are a substitute for clear Gherkin. Instead, they are supplementary; strive for self-documenting scenarios first.
