Categorizing with Tags

Labels for Features and Scenarios.

What are Gherkin Tags?

Tags are labels starting with an @ symbol, applied to Features or Scenarios (including Scenario Outlines) to categorize them. They are placed on the line immediately above the Feature or Scenario keyword.

Key Takeaway

Tags provide a flexible way to group and organize your Gherkin specifications, making it easier to manage and understand your test suite.

Common Tag Examples

@smoke

Critical, fast-running tests.

@regression

Comprehensive test suite.

@wip

Work in progress scenarios.

@bug-123

Linked to a specific bug.

@api

Tests interacting with APIs.

@database

Tests involving database operations.

Example: A scenario tagged @smoke and @login indicates it's a critical login test.
