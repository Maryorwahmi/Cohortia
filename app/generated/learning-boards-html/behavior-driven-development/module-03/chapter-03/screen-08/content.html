Core Concept

Adding Context with Comments

Explanatory notes for human readers.

What are Gherkin Comments?

Comments are lines in Gherkin files that start with a # symbol. They are ignored by automation tools and serve to add human-readable explanations, context, or notes within your feature files.

Key Takeaway

Comments are essential for providing supplementary information and clarifying the intent behind Gherkin scenarios, improving overall understanding for all stakeholders.

Purpose of Comments

Explain Complex Rules

Clarify intricate business logic that might not be fully expressed in the steps.

Reference External Docs

Link to design documents, bug reports, or other relevant project artifacts.

Clarify Intent

Explain the 'why' behind a scenario or step, especially for edge cases.
