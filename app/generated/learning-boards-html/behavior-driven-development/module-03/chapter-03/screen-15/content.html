What to Remember

Key principles for applying advanced Gherkin.

Test Filtering

The ability to select and run specific subsets of scenarios based on their tags, crucial for efficient test execution.

Consistency

Maintain consistent tag naming, comment styles, and Doc String usage across your team to avoid confusion and improve maintainability.

Readability

Ensure that all advanced features enhance, rather than detract from, the clarity and ease of understanding of your Gherkin specifications.

Summary Takeaway

By focusing on test filtering, consistency, and readability, you can leverage advanced Gherkin features to create robust, clear, and collaborative BDD specifications.
