Advanced Gherkin: Tags, Comments, and Doc Strings

Enhancing BDD Specifications for Clarity and Control

As your BDD projects grow, managing and understanding your Gherkin feature files becomes crucial. This chapter introduces powerful features like Tags for organization, Comments for context, and Doc Strings for handling complex data, enabling more robust and maintainable specifications.

Key Takeaway

Mastering advanced Gherkin features is essential for scaling BDD practices, improving collaboration, and ensuring the clarity and maintainability of your executable specifications.
