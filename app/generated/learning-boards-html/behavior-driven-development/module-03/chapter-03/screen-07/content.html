Best Practices

Effective Tag Management

Guidelines for clear and maintainable tagging.

Do: Strive for Consistency

Agree on tag meanings and naming conventions across your team. Use a consistent set of tags.

Do: Keep Tags Business-Focused

Tags should reflect business domains, user roles, or test characteristics, not implementation details.

Key Takeaway

Effective tagging requires team agreement, consistency, and a focus on business-level categorization to avoid confusion and maintain clarity.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

Don't: Over-Tag

Too many tags or redundant tags lead to confusion and make maintenance harder. Aim for a concise set.

Don't: Use for Implementation Details

Tags like @java-specific or @selenium-webdriver belong in code, not Gherkin.

Misconception: Thinking more tags always means better organization. In reality, it often leads to 'tag bloat' and reduced clarity.
