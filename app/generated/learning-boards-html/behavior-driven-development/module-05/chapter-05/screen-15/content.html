Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › What to Remember
Wait for signals
Assert after an observable event, not immediately after an action.
Isolate at boundaries
Mock or stub external calls; virtualize when you need complete protocol simulation.
Write behavior-focused Gherkin
Describe what the user sees or experiences, not the internal calls that make it happen.
Final takeaway
Apply waits, pick the right test doubles, and keep scenarios focused on observable behavior to build trustworthy BDD suites.
