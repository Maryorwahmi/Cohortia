Handling Complex Scenarios: Asynchronous Operations and External Dependencies

Practical techniques to make your BDD scenarios stable when operations complete later or depend on external services. Learn to wait for outcomes and to isolate integrations.

In this lesson
Wait for events, replace risky services, and design behavior-focused Gherkin.
Coming up

Explicit Waits
Wait for conditions like element text or visibility instead of sleeping.
Test Isolation
Mock or stub external services to keep tests fast and deterministic.
Service Virtualization
Simulate complex external systems so you can test many response cases.
Visual Concept
Interactions: Trigger → Wait → Verify → Isolate
Trigger: user action or API call
Wait: explicit wait or poll until condition
Verify: assertion after condition
Isolate: mock external side effects
