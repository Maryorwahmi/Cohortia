Comparison
Background vs Hooks
A quick decision table to choose the right tool.
Background
Feature-local, business-readable steps that establish a known state before each scenario.
Hooks
Code-level lifecycle and technical setup/teardown, global or tag-scoped.
Key Takeaway

Prefer Background for stakeholder-visible setup; prefer hooks for infrastructure and cleanup. Keep responsibilities separate.

Illustrative scenarios
Business step in Background
"Given I am logged in" - visible to product owners and relevant to scenario meaning.
Technical step in Hooks
Initialize WebDriver or clear test caches - implementation detail best kept in code.
Scope difference
Background = per feature; Hooks = suite-wide or tag-scoped.
