Key Concepts: Understanding BDD's Foundation

Recap of the essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Behavior-Driven Development (BDD)

An agile process fostering collaboration to define software behavior through concrete examples.

Test-Driven Development (TDD)

A development process where tests are written before code, focusing on low-level unit functionality.

Three Amigos

The collaborative group (Product Owner, Developer, QA) defining desired system behaviors.

Chapter Summary

BDD is a powerful agile process that enhances collaboration and communication, ensuring software development is aligned with business needs and delivers true user value by focusing on observable behaviors rather than just technical implementation.
