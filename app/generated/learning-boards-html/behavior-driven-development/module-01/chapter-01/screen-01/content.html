Unlocking Value with Behavior-Driven Development

Aligning Business Needs with Technical Execution

Behavior-Driven Development (BDD) is an agile software development process that encourages collaboration among developers, quality assurance (QA) personnel, and business stakeholders. It's a communication framework designed to ensure everyone shares a common understanding of what needs to be built and why, ultimately delivering software that truly meets business needs.

Key Takeaway

BDD fosters collaboration and shared understanding to deliver software that truly meets business needs and provides value to its users.
