The 'Three Amigos' Collaboration

Bringing diverse perspectives together for shared understanding.

Product Owner

Defines 'what' is needed from a business perspective.

Developer

Responsible for building 'how' it's done technically.

QA / Tester

Focuses on 'how' to verify the system works correctly.

Why it matters:

  • •Explores requirements and clarifies ambiguities.
  • •Agrees on concrete examples of desired behavior.
  • •Significantly reduces misunderstandings and rework.

Common Mistake:

Skipping these discussions and relying solely on written requirements often leads to misinterpretations and features that don't meet expectations.

Key Takeaway

The 'three amigos' collaboration is vital for proactive problem-solving and ensuring a unified understanding of features before development begins.
