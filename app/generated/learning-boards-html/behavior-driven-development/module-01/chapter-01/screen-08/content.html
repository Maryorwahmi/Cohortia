Focusing on Behavior: The 'What' Not the 'How'

Defining features by observable actions and outcomes.

Behavioral Focus

  • •Describes system functionality by observable actions.
  • •Emphasizes user interactions and business outcomes.
  • •Answers 'What should the system do?'
**Example:** 'A customer can add items to their cart.'

Avoid Technical Implementation

  • •Does not describe internal mechanisms or code.
  • •Avoids technical jargon and specific APIs.
  • •Answers 'How should the system be built?' (This is TDD's domain).
**Example:** Not 'The `addToCart` function updates the database.'
Key Takeaway

By focusing on observable behavior, BDD ensures that features are defined by their value and impact, making them robust and aligned with business goals.
