Key Concepts: BDD's Communication Tools and Benefits

Recap of BDD's collaborative language, specifications, and advantages.

Ubiquitous Language

A common, business-centric vocabulary used by all stakeholders to describe system functionality.

Executable Specifications

Detailed, unambiguous descriptions of system behavior that can be automated as tests.

Living Documentation

The concept that executable specifications serve as always-up-to-date documentation for system behavior.

Chapter Summary

BDD's core principles, including ubiquitous language, executable specifications, and living documentation, collectively enhance communication, ensure alignment with business value, and foster a robust culture of quality and collaboration in software development.
