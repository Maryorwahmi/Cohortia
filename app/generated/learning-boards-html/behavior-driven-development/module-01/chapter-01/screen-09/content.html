Executable Specifications: Living Documentation

Clear, concise examples of behavior that serve multiple purposes.

What They Are

  • •Detailed, unambiguous descriptions of system behavior.
  • •Often written in structured formats like Gherkin.
  • •Example: 'Given a user is logged in, When they click 'Logout', Then they should be redirected to the login page.'

Their Purpose

  • •Serve as living documentation that is always up-to-date.
  • •Can be automated as tests to validate system behavior.
  • •Provide a shared understanding and source of truth for functionality.
Key Takeaway

Executable specifications are a cornerstone of BDD, acting as both precise documentation and automated tests, ensuring continuous alignment between business expectations and system behavior.
