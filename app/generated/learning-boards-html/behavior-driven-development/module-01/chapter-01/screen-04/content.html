What is Behavior-Driven Development (BDD)?

A collaborative approach to defining software through desired behaviors.

Definition

BDD is an agile software development process that encourages collaboration among developers, QA, and business stakeholders.

Core Purpose

To ensure a common understanding of what needs to be built and why, delivering software that meets business needs and provides value.

Key Focus

Shifts focus from technical implementation to observable system behaviors and user value.

Scenario: Online Shopping Cart

Instead of 'Implement cart functionality,' BDD focuses on 'As a customer, I can add items to my cart and see the total update instantly.'

Analogy: Building a House

BDD is like a shared blueprint where the architect, builder, and homeowner all agree on how the house will be used and what behaviors it should support, not just the technical construction details.

Key Takeaway

BDD ensures that software development is driven by clear, shared understanding of business value and user behavior, preventing misaligned efforts.
