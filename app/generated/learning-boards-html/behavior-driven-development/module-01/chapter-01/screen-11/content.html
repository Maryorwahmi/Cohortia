Alignment with Business Value and Living Documentation

Ensuring development efforts deliver strategic impact and maintain up-to-date records.

Focus on Business Value

  • •Ensures development aligns with strategic goals.
  • •Leads to higher quality software that meets user needs.
  • •Avoids building features that lack real business impact.

Living Documentation

  • •Executable specifications are always up-to-date.
  • •Provides a reliable source of truth for system functionality.
  • •Simplifies onboarding and future maintenance.
Key Takeaway

BDD ensures that software development is driven by business value and supported by continuously validated, up-to-date documentation, fostering a culture of quality.
