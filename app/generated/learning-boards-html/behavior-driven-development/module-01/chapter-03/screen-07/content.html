The Formulate Phase: Crafting Gherkin Scenarios
Translating examples into executable specifications
Core Goal

Translate discovered examples and rules into formal, executable specifications using Gherkin syntax.

Collaborative Review
  • Product Owner: Confirms alignment with business expectations.
  • Developer & QA: Ensure specificity for automation and testing.
Output

Produces `.feature` files containing Gherkin scenarios, which serve as living documentation and drive automation.

Key Takeaway

Gherkin scenarios bridge the gap between business requirements and technical implementation, making specifications both human-readable and executable.

Feature: User Login
Scenario: Successful login with valid credentials
  Given I am on the login page
  When I enter "john.doe@example.com" into the email field
  And I enter "password123" into the password field
  And I click the "Login" button
  Then I should be redirected to the dashboard
  And I should see a welcome message
Focus

Gherkin focuses on 'what' the system should do, not 'how' it achieves it, maintaining a business-centric view.
