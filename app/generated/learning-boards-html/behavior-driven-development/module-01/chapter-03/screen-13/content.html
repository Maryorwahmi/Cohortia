Key Concepts: BDD Workflow Foundations
Essential terms and ideas from the BDD workflow

BDD Workflow

The iterative cycle of Discover, Formulate, Automate, Implement, and Refine, guiding teams through feature development.

Discover Phase

The initial collaborative stage where the "three amigos" explore requirements and define concrete examples of desired behavior.

Formulate Phase

The stage where discovered examples and rules are translated into formal Gherkin scenarios and written into `.feature` files.

Example Mapping

A collaborative technique used in the Discover phase to identify rules and examples for a given user story or feature.

Summary Takeaway

The BDD workflow is a structured, collaborative journey that ensures software development is always aligned with clear, shared business behaviors.
