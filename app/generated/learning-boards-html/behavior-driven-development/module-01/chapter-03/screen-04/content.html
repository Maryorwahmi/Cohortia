The BDD Workflow: An Iterative Journey
Understanding the cyclical nature of Behavior-Driven Development
Core Principle

BDD is a dynamic, iterative workflow, not a linear process, emphasizing continuous collaboration and feedback.

Key Phases
  • Discover: Shared understanding of desired behavior.
  • Formulate: Translate to Gherkin scenarios.
  • Automate: Write executable tests.
  • Implement: Develop application code.
  • Refine: Continuous feedback and updates.
Why it Matters

Ensures every development step aligns with business value, reducing rework and improving software quality.

Key Takeaway

The BDD workflow is a continuous cycle of collaboration and feedback, crucial for building software that truly meets business needs.
