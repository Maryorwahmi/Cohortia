Avoiding Common BDD Pitfalls
Recognizing and preventing anti-patterns in your workflow
Pitfall 1: Skipping Discovery

Directly writing Gherkin without collaborative discussion leads to assumptions and missed requirements.

Pitfall 2: One-Time BDD Activity

Treating BDD as a front-loaded task rather than an ongoing, iterative practice, neglecting the Refine phase.

Pitfall 3: Technical Gherkin

Scenarios become too focused on implementation details ('how') instead of desired behavior ('what'), losing business readability.

Key Takeaway

Proactive awareness of BDD pitfalls and anti-patterns is essential for maintaining a healthy, effective, and sustainable BDD practice.

Consequences of Pitfalls

Building the wrong feature or missing critical edge cases.

Extensive rework, increased costs, and project delays.

Outdated documentation that no longer reflects system behavior.

Reduced collaboration and communication breakdown between teams.

Pro-Tip

Regularly review your BDD practices and adapt them to your team's specific context and challenges.
