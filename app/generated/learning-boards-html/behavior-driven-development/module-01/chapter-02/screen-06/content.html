Crafting Your First Gherkin Scenario

Walk through a practical example of an e-commerce guest checkout feature.

Feature Block

Provides high-level description and motivation for the feature (As a... I want... So that...).

Scenario Steps

Uses Given-When-Then, along with 'And' and 'But', to detail specific actions and expected outcomes.

Key Takeaway

A well-structured Gherkin scenario clearly articulates a single behavior, making it easy for both humans to understand and machines to automate.

Feature: Guest Checkout

  As a customer who doesn't want to create an account,
  I want to be able to purchase items quickly,
  So that I can complete my order without extra steps.

  Scenario: Guest successfully purchases a single item
    Given I am a guest customer
    And I have a "Laptop" in my shopping cart
    When I proceed to checkout
    And I provide valid shipping and payment details
    And I confirm the order
    Then I should see an order confirmation page
    And my order status should be "Pending"
