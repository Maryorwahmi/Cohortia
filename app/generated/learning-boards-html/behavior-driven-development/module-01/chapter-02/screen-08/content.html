Keep Scenarios Focused and Concise

Avoid combining too many actions or outcomes into one scenario for better readability and maintainability.

01

Single Behavior

Each scenario should describe one distinct interaction or outcome, like a user logging in or adding an item to a cart.

02

Avoid Overloading

Do not combine too many actions or outcomes; this makes scenarios harder to read, understand, and debug.

03

Improved Maintainability

Concise scenarios are easier to update when requirements change and simpler to troubleshoot when tests fail.

Key Takeaway

Focusing each scenario on a single, clear behavior enhances its value as both documentation and an automated test, making your BDD efforts more effective.
