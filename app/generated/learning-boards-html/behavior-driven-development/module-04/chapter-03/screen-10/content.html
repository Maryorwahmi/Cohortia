Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Designing Maintainable Step Definitions
Principles
  • Single responsibility per step: do one thing well.
  • Abstract implementation with Page Objects or service clients.
  • Parameterize to avoid duplication and encourage reuse.
  • Place assertions in Then steps to express outcomes.
  • Clear scenario state with @Before and @After hooks.
Example abstraction
Step calls UserService.createUser(map) rather than directly inserting into the database; the service encapsulates implementation and can be mocked in tests.
Check your code
  1. Is this step reusable across scenarios?
  2. Does it hide implementation details?
  3. Are assertions limited to Then steps?
