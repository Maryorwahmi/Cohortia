Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
@CucumberOptions
Annotation to configure features, glue, and reporting plugins for Cucumber.
glue
Specifies package(s) where Cucumber should search for step definitions.
Test Runner
A JUnit class that instructs the test framework to execute Cucumber scenarios and produce reports.
Final takeaway

Confirm your features, glue package, and runner settings to make scenarios discoverable and executable.
