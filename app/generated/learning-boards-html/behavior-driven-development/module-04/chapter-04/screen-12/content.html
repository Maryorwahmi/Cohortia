Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Flaky Tests & Mistakes
Common causes
  • Shared state between scenarios due to incomplete cleanup.
  • Heavy per-step hooks that slow and destabilize tests.
  • Dependency on external services without mocks in CI.
Don't do this
Open and close browsers in step hooks or rely on network services without a stable test fixture.
Practical fixes
  1. Use @Before/@After for resource lifecycle and ensure @After always resets state.
  2. Mock external dependencies or use stable test doubles in CI.
  3. Isolate flaky scenarios and reduce their scope until root cause is fixed.
Key Takeaway

Prevent flakiness by ensuring deterministic setup and teardown, minimizing per-step work, and isolating external dependencies in CI.
