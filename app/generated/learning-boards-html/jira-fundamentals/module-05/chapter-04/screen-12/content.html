Setup Guide

Getting Started with Integrations

Finding and installing your desired app.

Why Follow Steps?

A structured approach ensures successful integration, proper configuration, and avoids common setup errors.

Atlassian Marketplace: Your primary source for trusted Jira apps and integrations.

Built-in Integrations: Some Atlassian tools offer native, pre-configured connections.

Key Takeaway

The first steps to integrating Jira involve carefully selecting the right app from the Atlassian Marketplace and following the guided installation process.

01

Find the App/Add-on

Go to Jira's "Apps" section and search the Atlassian Marketplace for the desired integration. Look for trusted sources.

02

Install the App

Install the app from the Marketplace. Some integrations, like Bitbucket for Jira Cloud, may be built-in.

Pro Tip: Check Compatibility

Always verify that the app is compatible with your specific Jira version (Cloud, Server, Data Center) before installation to avoid issues.
