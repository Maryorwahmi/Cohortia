What to Remember

Reinforcing critical ideas.

Communication Tools

Platforms like Slack or Microsoft Teams used for team messaging and collaboration, enhancing real-time awareness.

Traceability

The ability to track the history and relationships of work items, from requirements to code changes and deployments.

Atlassian Marketplace

A platform where you can find and install apps and integrations for Jira, expanding its functionality.

Summary Takeaway

Effective Jira integrations foster real-time communication, enhance traceability across the entire project lifecycle, and leverage the Atlassian Marketplace for extended capabilities.
