Watchers: Keeping Stakeholders in the Loop

Receive Notifications on All Issue Updates

What is a Watcher?

An individual who receives automatic notifications about all updates to a specific Jira issue.

  • Status changes
  • New comments
  • Attachments
  • Field modifications

Assignee vs. Watcher

Assignee: Responsible for completing the work.

Watcher: Needs to stay informed about progress without direct responsibility.

  • Team leads
  • Product managers
  • Cross-functional teams
Key Takeaway

Watchers are crucial for keeping non-responsible stakeholders informed about issue progress, ensuring transparency without direct task ownership.
