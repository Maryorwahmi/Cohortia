External Communication

Sharing Jira Information with Stakeholders

Disseminate Project Status and Progress Beyond Active Users

Sharing Issues

  • Send direct URLs via email or chat.
  • Use Jira's built-in 'Share' function for email with a message.
  • Ideal for specific updates to non-Jira users.

Sharing Dashboards

  • Provide high-level project status overviews.
  • Useful for executive summaries or regular reports.
  • Ensures transparency and alignment for all stakeholders.

Pro-Tip: Consider Audience Permissions

When sharing, remember that recipients will only see what their Jira access allows. Verify visibility beforehand.

Key Takeaway

Jira's sharing features enable broad dissemination of project information, ensuring transparency and alignment with all stakeholders, regardless of their active Jira usage.
