Key Concepts: Collaborating in Jira

Essential Tools for Effective Teamwork

Comments

Textual updates and discussions logged directly within a Jira issue, forming its historical record.

Mentions

A feature to directly notify specific Jira users within a comment or description using the '@' symbol.

Attachments

Files (documents, images, code) uploaded and associated directly with a Jira issue for centralized context.

These tools form the foundation of direct, in-issue communication, ensuring your team stays connected and informed.
