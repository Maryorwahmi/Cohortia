Strategic Use of Watchers to Avoid Notification Fatigue

Ensure Relevant Stakeholders are Informed, Not Overwhelmed

Who Should Watch?

  • Team Leads monitoring critical tasks.
  • Cross-functional members with dependencies.
  • Stakeholders needing high-level awareness.

Who Shouldn't?

  • Individuals with no direct relevance.
  • Those who only need occasional summaries.
  • Anyone prone to notification overload.

Common Pitfall

Adding too many watchers leads to notification fatigue, causing important updates to be missed.

Key Takeaway

Manage watchers judiciously, ensuring only truly relevant individuals receive notifications to maintain their value and prevent information overload.
