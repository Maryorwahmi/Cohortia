The Cumulative Flow Diagram (CFD)

Visualizing Workflow Efficiency and Bottlenecks

Holistic View

Provides a comprehensive view of your workflow, showing cumulative issues in different statuses over time.

Colored Bands

Each band represents a workflow status (e.g., To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done), with its width indicating issue count.

Bottleneck Indicator

A widening band (e.g., 'In Review') signifies a bottleneck, meaning issues are entering faster than they are leaving that status.

Key Takeaway

The CFD is a powerful diagnostic tool for pinpointing workflow inefficiencies and understanding where work gets stuck.
