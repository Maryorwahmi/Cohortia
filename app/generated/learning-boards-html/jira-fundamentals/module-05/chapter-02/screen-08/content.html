Velocity Report: Measuring Team Throughput

Quantifying Work Completion for Future Planning

What it Measures

The amount of work a team completes in a sprint, typically in story points or issue count.

Forecasting Tool

Uses average velocity from past sprints to help teams forecast realistic commitments for future sprints.

Example

If a team's average velocity is 30 story points, they should aim to commit around 30 points in the next sprint for sustainable planning.

Key Takeaway

Velocity is a crucial internal metric for improving a team's rhythm, predictability, and making informed workload decisions.
