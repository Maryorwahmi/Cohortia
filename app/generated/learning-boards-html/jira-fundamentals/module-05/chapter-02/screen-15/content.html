Key Concepts: Agile Reports

Core Tools for Project Visibility

Burndown Chart

Tracks remaining work against time in a sprint or version, showing progress towards completion.

Burnup Chart

Tracks cumulative work completed and total scope over time, useful for visualizing scope changes.

Velocity Report

Measures the amount of work a team completes in a sprint, used for future sprint planning and forecasting.

Summary Takeaway

These Agile reports are fundamental for transparently tracking progress, managing scope, and predicting team capacity in Jira.
