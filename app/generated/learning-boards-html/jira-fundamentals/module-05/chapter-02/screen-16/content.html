Key Concepts: Flow & Context

Advanced Insights and Critical Interpretation

Cumulative Flow Diagram (CFD)

Visualizes cumulative issues in statuses over time, ideal for identifying bottlenecks and understanding flow efficiency.

Sprint Report

Summarizes the activity and outcomes of a specific sprint, crucial for retrospectives.

Scope Creep

The uncontrolled growth or expansion of project scope after the project has begun, often visualized by Burnup Charts.

Summary Takeaway

Effective project management relies on understanding workflow dynamics and critically interpreting reports within their full context.
