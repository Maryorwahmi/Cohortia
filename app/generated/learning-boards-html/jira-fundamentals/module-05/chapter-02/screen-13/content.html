Generating and Customizing Jira Reports

Tailoring Reports to Your Project's Needs

1

Navigate to Reports Section

In your Jira project, locate and click on 'Reports' in the left-hand sidebar to access the reporting dashboard.

2

Select Report Type

Choose the desired report from the list, such as 'Burndown Chart', 'Velocity Report', or 'Cumulative Flow Diagram'.

3

Customize Parameters

Adjust report-specific settings like sprint, version, or time period using the provided dropdowns and filters to refine your data view.

Key Takeaway

Mastering report generation and customization ensures you can extract precise and relevant insights for any project analysis.
