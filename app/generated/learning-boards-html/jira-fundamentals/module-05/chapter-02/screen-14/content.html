Critical Interpretation

Interpreting Reports: Beyond the Numbers

Considering Context and Avoiding Common Mistakes

Context Matters

Always consider factors like scope changes, team availability, external dependencies, and consistent 'Definition of Done'.

Common Pitfalls

Avoid comparing teams, blaming individuals, or making decisions based on a single report without other data points.

Best Practice

Use reports as conversation starters for continuous improvement, identifying patterns and areas for attention.

Key Takeaway

Reports are tools for insight, not judgment. Always combine data with qualitative feedback for a complete picture.

Factors Influencing Reports

Scope Changes: Directly impacts Burndown/Burnup charts.

Team Availability: Affects Velocity and overall progress.

External Dependencies: Can cause unexpected blockers and delays.

Definition of Done: Inconsistent application skews data accuracy.
