Understanding the Burndown Chart

Tracking Remaining Work Against Time

What it Shows

Tracks the remaining work in a sprint or version against the time left, typically in story points or issue count.

Key Lines

Features a 'perfect burndown' line (ideal progress) and an 'actual burndown' line (real-time work completion).

Interpretation

Actual line above perfect: behind schedule or over-committed. Actual line below perfect: ahead of schedule or under-committed.

Key Takeaway

The Burndown Chart is a visual thermometer for sprint health, quickly showing if your team is on track to meet its commitments.
