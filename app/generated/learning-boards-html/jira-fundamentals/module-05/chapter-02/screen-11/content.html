CFD: Understanding Flow Metrics

Visualizing Lead Time and Cycle Time

Lead Time

The total time from when a work item is requested until it is delivered. On CFD, it's the horizontal distance from 'To Do' to 'Done'.

Cycle Time

The time an item spends actively being worked on within a specific part of the workflow. Horizontal distance between two points within a band.

CFD Insight

By observing these horizontal distances, teams can understand how long work flows through their process and identify optimization areas.

Key Takeaway

Measuring Lead and Cycle Time with CFD helps teams improve delivery speed and overall workflow efficiency.
