Additional Reports

Beyond Agile: Other Essential Jira Reports

Diverse Reports for Comprehensive Project Overview

Sprint Report

Summarizes sprint activity, including completed, uncompleted, and added issues. Essential for retrospectives.

Version Report

Tracks progress of a specific release (version), showing resolved, unresolved, and overall completion status.

Created vs. Resolved

Shows trends of issues created versus resolved, indicating backlog growth if 'created' consistently exceeds 'resolved'.

Resolution Time

Identifies typical time taken to resolve issues, highlighting areas for process improvement and efficiency gains.

Key Takeaway

Leverage Jira's diverse reports to gain a holistic understanding of project health, from sprint performance to backlog management.
