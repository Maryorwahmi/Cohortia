Roadmaps, Sprint Health, and Burndown: Tracking Progress

Monitor release planning, sprint progress, and remaining work.

Jira Road Map Gadget
  • •Purpose: Displays upcoming versions and their progress.
  • •Benefit: Essential for release planning and managing stakeholder expectations.
Sprint Health Gadget
  • •Purpose: Quick overview of a sprint's progress.
  • •Metrics: Shows remaining work, completed work, and issues not started.
Velocity/Burndown Chart Gadgets
  • •Purpose: Embeds existing reports for real-time updates.
  • •Benefit: Tracks team velocity and work completion rate.
Key Takeaway

These specialized gadgets provide crucial real-time insights into both long-term release planning and immediate sprint execution, enabling proactive management and adjustments.

Scenario: Monitoring Sprint Progress

Problem

As a Scrum Master, you need to quickly identify if your team is on track to complete the current sprint's work.

Solution

Add a Sprint Health Gadget and a Burndown Chart Gadget to your 'Sprint Overview' dashboard.

Insight

The Sprint Health Gadget shows remaining vs. completed work, while the Burndown Chart visually tracks progress against the ideal line, immediately flagging if the team is falling behind.

Pro Tip

Regularly review these gadgets during daily stand-ups to foster transparency and enable early intervention for potential sprint risks.
