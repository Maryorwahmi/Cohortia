Key Concepts (Continued)

Advanced terms for dashboard design.

Two Dimensional Filter Statistics

A gadget that displays issue counts grouped by two different fields, similar to a pivot table.

Audience-Specific Dashboards

Creating different dashboards tailored to the information needs of various stakeholders (e.g., team members, managers, executives).

Final Thought

By combining powerful gadgets with thoughtful, audience-centric design, you can transform raw Jira data into highly actionable and insightful dashboards.
