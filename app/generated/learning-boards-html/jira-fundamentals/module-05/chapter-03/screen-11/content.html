Crafting Effective Dashboards: Focus, Filters, and Review

Key principles for designing informative and actionable Jira dashboards.

Keep it Focused
  • •Avoid clutter; include only essential information.
  • •Aim for clarity, not quantity, of gadgets.
Use Meaningful Filters
  • •Ensure filters are precise and retrieve intended data.
  • •Poor filters lead to irrelevant or misleading data.
Visualize Appropriately & Review Regularly
  • •Choose the right gadget type for your data (pie, bar, list).
  • •Regularly review and update dashboards for relevance.
Key Takeaway

Effective dashboards are characterized by focus, precise filtering, appropriate visualization, and continuous review to ensure they remain relevant and actionable.

Common Mistakes & Pitfalls

Too Generic / Cluttered

Trying to serve too many audiences or packing too many gadgets makes it unusable.

Ineffective Filters

Using broad or incorrect saved filters leads to irrelevant data display.

Misinterpretation

Relying solely on dashboard visuals without understanding underlying data context can lead to wrong conclusions.

Safety Note

Be mindful of sharing dashboards with sensitive information. Users only see issues from projects they have access to, regardless of what the dashboard attempts to display.
