Understanding Permission Schemes

Safeguarding Your Project with Granular Access

Control User Actions

Permission schemes define who can create, edit, transition, or view issues within a project, ensuring security and preventing unauthorized actions.

Principle of Least Privilege

Grant users only the minimum necessary permissions to perform their job functions, minimizing security risks and maintaining productivity.

Key Takeaway

Properly configured permission schemes are fundamental for securing your Jira projects and ensuring that users have appropriate, not excessive, access.

Permission Scheme Controls

  • Create Issues: Who can add new tasks, bugs, stories.
  • Edit Issues: Who can modify existing issue details.
  • Transition Issues: Who can change an issue's status.
  • Delete Issues: Who has the power to remove issues permanently.
  • View Sensitive Info: Who can see restricted fields or comments.
Common Mistake

Granting overly broad global permissions or making project permissions too restrictive, hindering productivity.

Safety Note

Always follow the principle of least privilege when setting up permissions to enhance security and prevent unintended access.
