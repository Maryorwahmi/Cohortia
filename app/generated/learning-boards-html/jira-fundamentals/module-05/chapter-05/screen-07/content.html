Process Discipline

Accurate Estimates and Workflow Adherence

Guiding Issues Through Their Lifecycle

Accurate Estimates

Strive for consistency in story points or time estimates. Regularly refine them to reflect actual effort, ensuring realistic planning.

Adherence to Workflows

Understand and follow your project's defined workflow. Deviating can break automation and reporting, causing confusion.

Key Takeaway

Reliable estimates and strict workflow adherence are cornerstones of predictable project management and accurate data in Jira.

Workflow Adherence Benefits

  • 1. Clear Purpose: Each status and transition has a role.
  • 2. Guided Lifecycle: Issues move predictably.
  • 3. Automation Integrity: Prevents breaking automated rules.
  • 4. Accurate Reporting: Ensures data reflects reality.
Consequence of Deviation

Deviating from workflows can lead to broken automation, misleading reports, and confusion about an issue's true state.

Example Scenario

A team member skips 'In Review' and moves an issue directly to 'Done.' This bypasses quality checks and makes the 'In Review' report inaccurate.
