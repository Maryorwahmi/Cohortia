Operational Health

Regular Jira Maintenance

Keeping Your Instance Lean and Efficient

Regular Cleanup

Periodically review and remove unused filters, dashboards, and obsolete issues to prevent clutter and maintain efficiency.

JQL and Report Review

Ensure shared filters and dashboard gadgets use optimized JQL. Regularly assess if dashboards and reports provide value and accurate insights.

Key Takeaway

Proactive maintenance keeps your Jira instance lean, fast, and reliable, ensuring it continues to provide accurate and valuable insights.

Maintenance Checklist

  • 1. Filters: Delete unused or temporary filters.
  • 2. Dashboards: Remove obsolete or underutilized dashboards.
  • 3. Issues: Archive or delete truly obsolete issues.
  • 4. JQL: Optimize queries for performance and accuracy.
  • 5. Reports: Verify relevance and utility of all generated reports.
Impact of Clutter

A cluttered Jira instance becomes less efficient, harder to navigate, and can lead to misleading reports or slow performance.

Example Scenario

A project manager notices a dashboard gadget is showing incorrect data. Upon review, they find the underlying JQL filter has a typo, causing the misleading report.
