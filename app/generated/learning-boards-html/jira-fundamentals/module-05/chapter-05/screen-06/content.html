Team Collaboration

Keeping Issues Current

Ensuring Visibility and Informed Decisions

Timely Status Updates

Move issues to 'In Progress' when starting, 'Done' or 'In Review' when finished. Stale statuses lead to inaccurate reports.

Meaningful Comments

Use comments for updates, questions, and decisions. Leverage @mentions to notify specific team members.

Key Takeaway

Consistent updates and rich comments ensure project transparency and facilitate informed decision-making for the entire team.

Issue Lifecycle Transparency

  • Start Work: Move to 'In Progress'.
  • Finish Work: Move to 'In Review' or 'Done'.
  • Questions/Updates: Add comments with @mentions.
  • Decisions: Document in comments for historical context.
Impact of Stale Statuses

Inaccurate reports, hindered team visibility, and poor resource allocation due to a false impression of progress.

Example Scenario

A developer finishes a task but forgets to update its status. The project manager sees it as 'To Do' and assigns it to someone else, causing duplicated effort.
