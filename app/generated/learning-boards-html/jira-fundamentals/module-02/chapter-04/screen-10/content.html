Sub-task Lifecycle and Best Practices

Ensuring Parent Completion and Avoiding Over-granularity

Parent-Child Dependency

A parent issue's progress is directly tied to its sub-tasks. Typically, a parent cannot be closed until all its sub-tasks are resolved.

Creation Process

Sub-tasks are usually created directly from the parent issue's view screen, often via a dedicated 'Create Sub-task' button or option.

Best Practice: Focused Work

Sub-tasks should be focused, contribute directly to the parent's completion, and ideally be completable within a short timeframe (hours to a day).

Common Mistake

Over-granularity or Too Large

Creating sub-tasks that are either too small (excessive administrative overhead) or too large (making them mini-stories) can hinder manageability and dilute the focus of the parent issue. Find the right balance.

Key Takeaway

Sub-tasks enforce completion of all granular work before a larger task is considered done, but require careful sizing to remain effective.

Pro Tip

Visual Progress

Many Jira boards and reports can display sub-task progress directly on the parent issue, offering a quick visual indicator of how close a larger task is to completion without needing to drill down.
