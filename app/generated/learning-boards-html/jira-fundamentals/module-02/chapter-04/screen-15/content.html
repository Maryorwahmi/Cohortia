Key Concepts: Hierarchy & Structure

Sub-tasks, Epics, and Jira's Overall Structure

Sub-task

A smaller, granular piece of work that belongs to and contributes to the completion of a single parent issue.

Parent Issue

The larger issue to which one or more sub-tasks are associated, often requiring sub-task completion for its own resolution.

Epic

A large body of work that serves as a container for multiple Stories, Tasks, and Bugs, representing a major initiative.

Summary Takeaway

Jira's hierarchy, comprising Epics, Stories/Tasks/Bugs, and Sub-tasks, provides a robust framework for organizing and tracking work at various levels of granularity.
