Beyond the Core: Other Standard Issue Types

Understanding additional pre-configured options and their specific uses

New Feature

Similar to a Story, but can be broader. Used for new functionalities or capabilities that might not be strictly user-centric or span multiple user interactions.

Improvement

An enhancement or refinement to existing functionality. Used when you're making something better rather than building something entirely new from scratch.

Sub-task

A smaller, granular piece of work that is part of a larger parent issue (e.g., a Story or Task). Allows for breaking down work into finer, assignable steps.

Key Takeaway

Leveraging these additional standard types, along with Sub-tasks, allows for more precise work breakdown and categorization, enhancing project clarity and management.
