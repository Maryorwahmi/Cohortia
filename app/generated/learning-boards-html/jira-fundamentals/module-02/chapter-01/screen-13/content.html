Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter (Part 1/2)

Issue

A single unit of work, a defect, or a request tracked within Jira.

Issue Type

A category that defines the nature of a specific piece of work (e.g., Story, Task, Bug, Epic).

Story

A user-centric requirement, typically small enough for a sprint, delivering direct user value.

Chapter Summary

Issue types are the backbone of structured work in Jira, categorizing tasks for clarity, consistency, and effective project management.
