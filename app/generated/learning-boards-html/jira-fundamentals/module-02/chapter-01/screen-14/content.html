Key Concepts

Reinforcing your understanding (Part 2/2)

Task

A general piece of work that needs to be completed, often supporting project goals.

Bug

A defect or error in the software or system that needs to be fixed.

Epic

A large body of work broken into smaller issues, spanning multiple sprints.

Custom Issue Type

A user-defined issue type created to meet specific project or organizational needs.

Chapter Summary

Mastering issue types, both standard and custom, is crucial for effective work categorization, streamlined workflows, and accurate reporting in Jira.
