Structured Categorization: Components

Organizing Work by Project Sub-Sections

What are Components?

Distinct sub-sections or modules of your project, typically pre-defined by administrators. Provide a formal way to categorize work by product area.

Key Characteristics
  • Structured and formal.
  • Admin-defined, consistent taxonomy.
  • An issue can belong to multiple components.
Key Takeaway

Components offer a stable, architectural way to categorize issues, facilitating formal reporting and long-term project organization.
