Core Assignment & Urgency Fields

Who, What, and How Important

Assignee

The individual responsible for working on and resolving the issue. Ensures accountability.

Reporter

The person who created the issue, useful for initial context and clarification.

Priority

Indicates the relative importance or urgency (e.g., Highest, High, Medium, Low, Lowest). Helps prioritize workload.

Common Mistake

Marking every issue as 'Highest' priority. This renders the field meaningless and hinders true prioritization. Establish clear team definitions for each level.

Priority Levels & Impact
  • Highest: Production down, immediate fix required.
  • High: Significant impact, needs to be addressed next sprint.
  • Medium: Standard work, can wait a few sprints.
  • Low: Minor impact, can be addressed when time permits.
Key Takeaway

Consistent use of Assignee, Reporter, and well-defined Priority levels ensures clear ownership and effective workload management.
