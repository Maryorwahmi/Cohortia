Labels, Components, and Due Dates

Further organizing and scheduling issues for better project management.

Labels

Flexible tags for categorization (e.g., `frontend`, `database`, `authentication`).

Components

Represents specific parts or modules of your project (e.g., `User Management`).

Due Date

A target date for completion, crucial for planning and tracking deadlines.

Key Takeaway

Labels, Components, and Due Dates provide flexible and structured ways to categorize, filter, and schedule issues for effective project oversight.
