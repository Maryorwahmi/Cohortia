Crafting an Effective Summary

The most important field: a concise, descriptive title that conveys the issue's essence.

Newspaper Headline

Should be concise and immediately convey the core problem or task at a glance.

Avoid Vague Titles

Don't use generic phrases like "Fix bug"; instead, be specific (e.g., "Login button unresponsive on mobile").

Key Takeaway

A strong summary is a standalone, descriptive title that enables quick understanding and efficient issue management.

Good Summary

"Incorrect product price displayed on checkout page for logged-in users."

Bad Summary

"Fix bug on checkout."
