Bulk Editing: Efficiency & Risks

Modifying multiple issues simultaneously, a powerful feature requiring careful precaution.

Simultaneous Modification

Change common fields (Assignee, Labels, Priority) for many selected issues at once.

Crucial Precaution

Always double-check your selection before confirming to prevent accidental, widespread changes.

Key Takeaway

Bulk editing is highly efficient but requires extreme caution and verification to avoid unintended and difficult-to-revert changes.
