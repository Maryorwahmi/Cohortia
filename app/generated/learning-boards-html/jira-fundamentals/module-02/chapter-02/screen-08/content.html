Assigning Responsibility & Urgency

Understanding Reporter, Assignee, and Priority for clear ownership and focus.

Reporter

The user who created the issue; can be changed if creating on behalf of someone else.

Assignee

The individual responsible for completing the work; can be left unassigned or assigned directly.

Priority

Indicates the urgency or importance (e.g., Highest, High, Medium, Low, Lowest) to guide focus.

Key Takeaway

Properly setting Reporter, Assignee, and Priority ensures clear ownership and helps the team prioritize work effectively.
