Module 4›Chapter 4.3›Creating Your First Filter
Step 1

Execute Your Search

Perform a Basic or Advanced JQL search to get the exact set of issues you need. Ensure the results are precisely what you want to save.

Step 2

Click "Save as"

Locate the "Save as" button, usually found above the search results. This action initiates the filter creation process.

Step 3

Give it a Meaningful Name

Enter a descriptive name that clearly indicates the filter's purpose. This helps you and your teammates quickly identify it later.

Example Names
  • "Open Bugs in Project X"
  • "Tasks Due This Week"
  • "Blocked Stories - Sprint 3"
  • "My Team's Active Work"

Search to Filter Flow

status = "Open" AND type = "Bug"

JQL Query

"All Open Bugs"

Saved Filter

A descriptive name ensures clarity and ease of use for all team members.

Key Takeaway

Always give your saved filters clear, descriptive names to maximize their reusability and ensure team members can easily understand their purpose.
