Module 4›Chapter 4.3›Setting Up Filter Subscriptions
Step 1

Access Subscription Management

Go to your saved filter, click "Details" or the "..." menu, and select "Manage subscriptions."

Step 2

Configure Frequency & Recipients

Set how often you want to receive updates (daily, weekly, hourly) and specify who should receive the emails (yourself, other users, or groups).

Step 3

Set Notification Time

Choose a specific time of day for the email notification to be sent, ensuring it arrives when most convenient.

Benefit

Keeps stakeholders informed without manual effort, fostering proactive communication and timely responses to critical issue changes.

Subscription Configuration

Frequency: Daily
Recipients: Team Group
Time: 9:00 AM
Filter: "Blocked Issues"

Customizing these settings ensures relevant updates are delivered efficiently.

Key Takeaway

Filter subscriptions offer flexible configuration options to tailor automated email notifications, ensuring critical updates reach the right people at the right time.
