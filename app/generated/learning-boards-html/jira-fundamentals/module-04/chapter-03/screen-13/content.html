Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Subscription

Automated email notifications for changes to issues matching a filter's criteria.

Starred Filters

Filters marked as favorites for quick access from the main "Filters" menu.

Manage Filters

The central location in Jira to view, edit, delete, and oversee all saved filters.

Chapter Summary

Subscriptions provide automated updates, starred filters offer quick access, and the 'Manage Filters' page serves as the central hub for all filter operations. These tools collectively enhance your ability to proactively manage and stay informed about critical issues in Jira.
