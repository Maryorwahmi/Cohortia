Accessing Filters

Navigate to "Issues" > "Manage filters" to view all your saved, shared, and starred filters in one central location.

Starring Filters

Click the star icon next to frequently used filters to add them to your favorites for quick access from the main "Filters" menu.

Editing Filters

Modify the JQL query of a saved filter. "Save" updates the existing filter, while "Save as" creates a new, distinct copy.

Pro Tip

Use "Save as" when you want to create a slightly different version of an existing filter without altering the original, especially if it's shared.

Key Takeaway

Starring filters provides quick access to your most important views, while understanding the difference between "Save" and "Save as" is crucial for effective filter modification.
