Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Saved Filter

A named, persistent JQL query that can be reused and shared across Jira.

Reusability

The ability to quickly access and apply complex queries without re-typing them, saving time and ensuring consistency.

Permissions

Settings that control who can view and use a saved filter (Private, Public, Project, Group).

Chapter Summary

Saved filters are essential for transforming JQL queries into reusable, shareable tools. Understanding how to create them, configure their permissions, and leverage their reusability is fundamental for efficient Jira project management and team collaboration.
