What is a Subscription?

An automated email notification service that sends updates when issues matching a filter's criteria change. It's a proactive way to stay informed.

Why is it Useful?

Helps you stay updated on critical issues without constantly checking Jira, ensuring timely awareness and response to important changes.

Example Scenario

Subscribe to a "High Priority Bugs - Unassigned" filter to receive a daily email summary of new critical bugs needing immediate attention.
