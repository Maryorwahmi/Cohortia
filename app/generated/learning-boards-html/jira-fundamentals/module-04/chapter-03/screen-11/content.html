Deleting Filters

Remove unneeded filters from the "Manage filters" page. Exercise caution, as deleting shared filters can impact dashboards or reports.

Best Practices

  • Avoid creating too many similar filters.
  • Always use descriptive, clear names.
  • Check for existing filters before creating new ones.
Common Mistake

Creating overly generic names like "My Filter" or "Test Filter" which provide no context and make filters hard to identify and use.

Key Takeaway

Proactive filter management, including cautious deletion and adherence to naming conventions, ensures your Jira environment remains organized and efficient for all users.
