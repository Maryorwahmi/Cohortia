What are Permissions?

Settings that control who can see and use your saved filter. Essential for managing access to specific issue data.

Default: Private

By default, a new filter is only visible to its creator. You must explicitly change permissions to share it.

Permission Types

Private

Only visible to you.

Public

Visible to everyone (use with caution).

Project

Visible to users in specific projects.

Group

Visible to members of specific user groups.

Key Takeaway

Carefully consider filter permissions to balance data visibility with security and prevent information clutter for your team.
