Module 4›Chapter 4.3›Configuring Filter Permissions
Step 1

Access Filter Details

After saving, find the "Details" or "Edit" option next to your filter name to access its settings.

Step 2

Configure Sharing

Look for a "Share" button or section. Here, you can add or remove permissions for users, groups, or projects.

Step 3

Save Changes

Click "Add" and then "Save" to apply your chosen permissions. Always verify who needs access.

Common Mistake

Forgetting to share a filter intended for team use, leading to teammates being unable to find the shared view or duplicating effort.

Best Practice

Always consider who needs access to the information and share accordingly. Over-sharing can lead to clutter, while under-sharing hinders collaboration.

Key Takeaway

Thoughtful configuration of filter permissions is essential for fostering effective team collaboration and ensuring information is accessible to the right stakeholders.
