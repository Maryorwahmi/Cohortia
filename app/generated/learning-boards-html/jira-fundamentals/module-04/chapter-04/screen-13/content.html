Arranging Your Dashboard: Optimizing Layouts

Organizing gadgets effectively for a clear and digestible overview.

Why Layout Matters

A well-organized dashboard reduces cognitive load, allowing users to quickly find and interpret critical information without feeling overwhelmed by clutter.

Key Takeaway

Design your dashboard layout with a logical flow, prioritizing critical information to ensure maximum readability and impact.

Layout Customization Steps
  • 1. Change Layout: Click the "Change layout" button to select column configurations (e.g., 1, 2, or 3 columns).
  • 2. Drag & Drop: Easily move gadgets within or between columns to optimize placement.
  • 3. Prioritize: Place the most important gadgets at the top or in the most prominent column.
  • 4. Avoid Clutter: If a dashboard becomes too dense, consider creating multiple, focused dashboards.
Best Practice Example

A project manager creates a two-column layout. The left column holds critical sprint metrics (burndown, open blockers), while the right column displays team workload and recent activity, ensuring immediate focus on key performance indicators.
