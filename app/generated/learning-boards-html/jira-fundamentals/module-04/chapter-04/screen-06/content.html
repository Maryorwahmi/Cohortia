Creating Your First Dashboard: The Initial Steps

Naming, describing, and defining the scope of your new dashboard.

Why it Matters

A clear name and description help users quickly understand the dashboard's purpose and content, improving navigation and usability across your Jira instance.

Key Takeaway

Start with a clear name and description to ensure your dashboard is easily identifiable and understood by its intended audience.

Step-by-Step Creation
  1. 1. Navigate: Click "Dashboards" in the top navigation bar.
  2. 2. Initiate: Select "Create dashboard" from the dropdown menu.
  3. 3. Name: Provide a descriptive name (e.g., "My Daily Work").
  4. 4. Describe: Add a brief description explaining its purpose.
  5. 5. Create: Click the "Create" button to finalize.
Example Naming

Instead of just "Dashboard 1", use "Team Alpha Sprint Progress" or "My Open Bugs & Tasks". This immediately conveys the dashboard's focus and helps others find relevant information.
