Gadgets: The Interactive Modules of Your Dashboard

Small, configurable units displaying specific types of project information.

What are Gadgets?

Gadgets are the modular components that you add to a Jira Dashboard to display various types of data, statistics, and information in a visually digestible format.

Key Takeaway

Gadgets are the essential building blocks that transform a blank dashboard into a dynamic, informative project overview.

Adding Gadgets
  1. 1. Empty Dashboard: Your new dashboard starts blank.
  2. 2. Click "Add Gadget": Locate this button on your dashboard.
  3. 3. Browse/Search: Explore Jira's library of available gadgets.
  4. 4. Select & Configure: Choose a gadget and set its specific display options.
Gadget Library

Jira offers a diverse range of gadgets, each tailored for specific data visualization needs, such as displaying issue lists, statistical charts, or recent activity feeds. This variety allows for highly customized dashboards.
