Sharing Your Dashboard: Controlling Access and Collaboration

Setting permissions to ensure the right people see the right information.

Why Permissions Matter

Proper sharing ensures that team members and stakeholders can access the critical project information they need, fostering transparency and collaboration. Incorrect settings can lead to information silos.

Key Takeaway

Always configure dashboard sharing permissions carefully to match your audience, preventing access issues and promoting team-wide visibility.

Sharing Options
  • Private: Only visible to you. Ideal for personal task tracking.
  • Public: Visible to everyone in your Jira instance. Use for general company-wide overviews.
  • Project: Shared with specific projects. Ensures relevant teams see their data.
  • Group: Shared with specific user groups. Useful for cross-project teams or departments.
Common Mistake

Creating a team dashboard but forgetting to share it with the relevant project or group. This makes the dashboard inaccessible to others, defeating its collaborative purpose.
