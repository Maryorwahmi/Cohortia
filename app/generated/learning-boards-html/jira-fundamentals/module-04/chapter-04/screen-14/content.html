Dashboard Efficiency: Best Practices and Performance

Ensuring your dashboards are both informative and performant.

Performance Impact

Dashboards with too many complex gadgets or inefficient JQL queries can lead to slow loading times, diminishing the user experience and the dashboard's utility.

Key Takeaway

Design dashboards for efficiency by prioritizing critical information and using targeted, optimized filters to ensure quick loading and responsiveness.

Best Practices for Efficiency
  • Optimize Filters: Use precise JQL queries to narrow down data for gadgets.
  • Prioritize Gadgets: Only include the most essential gadgets for the dashboard's purpose.
  • Consider Multiple Dashboards: Create specialized dashboards for different needs instead of one overloaded dashboard.
  • Regular Review: Periodically check gadgets and filters for continued relevance and efficiency.
Example of Optimization

Instead of a gadget using a filter for "all issues in the company," create a filter for "open issues assigned to my team in the current sprint." This significantly reduces the data load and speeds up the dashboard.
