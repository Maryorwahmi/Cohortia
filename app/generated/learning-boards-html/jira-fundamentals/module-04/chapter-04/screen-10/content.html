Two Dimensional Filter Statistics: Visualizing Data Distributions

Categorizing issues by two selected fields to reveal patterns and insights.

What it Does

This gadget displays a matrix of issues, allowing you to cross-reference two different issue fields (e.g., Status vs. Assignee) to understand their distribution and relationships.

Key Takeaway

Use the Two Dimensional Filter Statistics Gadget to gain deeper insights into issue distributions and quickly identify trends or imbalances in your project data.

Configuration & Insights
  • Requires Saved Filter: Just like Filter Results, it needs a filter to define the dataset.
  • X-axis Field: Choose the first field for categorization (e.g., Status).
  • Y-axis Field: Choose the second field for categorization (e.g., Assignee).
  • Visualizes Patterns: Helps identify workload distribution, issue type prevalence, or priority trends.
Example Visualization

A dashboard shows a matrix of 'Issue Type' vs. 'Priority'. This immediately highlights that most 'Bugs' are currently 'High' priority, indicating a need for immediate attention from the QA team.
