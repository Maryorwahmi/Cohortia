Introduction to Jira Dashboards

Your Personalized Command Center for Project Visibility

Jira Dashboards are highly customizable overview pages that display various project statistics, issue statuses, and team progress using interactive "gadgets." They are indispensable for monitoring project health, tracking individual and team progress, identifying bottlenecks, and keeping stakeholders informed.
