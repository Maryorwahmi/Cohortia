Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from This Chapter (Part 2)

Dashboard Permissions

Controls who can view and edit a dashboard, with options like Private, Public, Project, and Group sharing.

Dashboard Subscriptions

Automated emails sending snapshots of a dashboard at regular intervals for effortless stakeholder reporting.

Maintenance

The ongoing process of reviewing, updating, and optimizing dashboards for relevance and performance.

Summary Takeaway

Effective dashboard management involves not just creation, but also strategic sharing, automated reporting, and continuous optimization for sustained value.
