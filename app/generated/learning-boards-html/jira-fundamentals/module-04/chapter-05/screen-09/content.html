Dashboard Design: Naming & Prioritization

Crafting Effective and Actionable Dashboards (Part 2)

3. Use Meaningful Names

  • •Name dashboards and filters descriptively.
  • •'Team X Sprint Progress' is better than 'Dashboard 1'.
  • •Clear names reduce confusion and improve accessibility.
Example

A filter named 'Open Bugs in Project Alpha' clearly states its purpose.

4. Prioritize Key Metrics

  • •Place most important information in prominent positions (e.g., top-left).
  • •Critical open issues, blockers, or sprint progress should be highly visible.
  • •Guide users to the most vital insights quickly.
Common Mistake

Treating all gadgets equally, resulting in a flat, unprioritized display.

Key Takeaway

Strategic naming and visual prioritization ensure your dashboard effectively communicates the most critical information.
