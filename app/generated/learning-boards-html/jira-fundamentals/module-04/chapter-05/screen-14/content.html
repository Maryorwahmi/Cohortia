Sustaining Value: Maintaining & Optimizing Dashboards

Ensuring Relevance and Performance Over Time

1. Remove Outdated Elements

  • •Delete gadgets or filters associated with completed projects or sprints.
  • •Clutter reduces dashboard utility and relevance.
Example

Remove a 'Sprint 1 Health' gadget once Sprint 1 is finished.

2. Update Filters & Seek Feedback

  • •Ensure JQL queries are accurate and efficient.
  • •Actively solicit input from users on dashboard utility and clarity.
Common Mistake

Assuming filters remain relevant without periodic review, leading to stale data.

3. Monitor Performance

  • •Address slow-loading dashboards by simplifying filters or reducing gadgets.
  • •Poor performance discourages user adoption.
Pro Tip

Use simpler JQL queries where possible to improve load times.

Key Takeaway

Proactive maintenance and optimization are crucial for ensuring your dashboards remain valuable, accurate, and performant over time.
