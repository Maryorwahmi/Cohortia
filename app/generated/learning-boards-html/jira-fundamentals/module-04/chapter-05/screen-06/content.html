Measuring Efficiency: Resolution Time & Sprint Health

Key Metrics for Performance and Progress

Resolution Time Gadget

  • •Displays average time to resolve issues.
  • •Crucial for SLA tracking and identifying process bottlenecks.
  • •Helps pinpoint areas for efficiency improvement.
Example

Identify if critical bugs take significantly longer to resolve than minor tasks.

Sprint Health Gadget

  • •(Jira Software specific) Summarizes current sprint progress.
  • •Shows completed, remaining issues, and scope changes.
  • •Essential for Scrum teams to monitor sprint health.
Pro Tip

Combine with a 'Filter Results' gadget to see specific issues impacting sprint health.

Key Takeaway

These gadgets provide actionable data for improving team efficiency and ensuring sprint commitments are met.
