Visualizing Distribution: Workload Pie & Issue Statistics

Understanding Resource Allocation and Issue Breakdown

Workload Pie Chart Gadget

  • •Visualizes issue distribution by a specific field (e.g., assignee, priority).
  • •Helps identify workload imbalances or priority concentrations.
  • •Uses a saved filter to define the scope of issues.
Example

See at a glance which team members have the most open tasks or which priority level dominates.

Issue Statistics Gadget

  • •Provides deeper analysis of issue counts by various fields.
  • •More configuration options than basic filter statistics.
  • •Useful for breaking down issues by component, label, or custom fields.
Pro Tip

Use this to analyze the distribution of bugs across different product components.

Key Takeaway

These gadgets are crucial for understanding where your team's efforts are concentrated and for making informed resource allocation decisions.
