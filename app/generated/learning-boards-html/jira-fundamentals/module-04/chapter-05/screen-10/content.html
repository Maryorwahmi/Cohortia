Dashboard Design: Accuracy & Interaction

Crafting Effective and Actionable Dashboards (Part 3)

5. Ensure Data Accuracy

  • •Regularly review underlying filters for relevance and correctness.
  • •Outdated filters lead to misleading information and poor decisions.
  • •An inaccurate dashboard is worse than no dashboard.
Example

Verify that a 'Current Sprint Issues' filter still excludes completed sprints.

6. Encourage Interaction

  • •Dashboards are interactive; encourage users to click and drill down.
  • •Active engagement leads to deeper understanding of the data.
  • •Fosters a more data-driven and curious team culture.
Pro Tip

Highlight that clicking on chart segments reveals the underlying issues.

Key Takeaway

Maintaining data accuracy and encouraging interaction are crucial for building trust and maximizing the value of your dashboards.
