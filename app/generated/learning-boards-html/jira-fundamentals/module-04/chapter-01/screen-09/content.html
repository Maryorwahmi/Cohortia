Understanding Search Contexts

Global Search vs. Board-Specific Filters

Global Search

  • Accessed via "Issues" > "Search for issues".
  • Searches across ALL projects you have access to.
  • Provides a comprehensive view of issues across your entire Jira instance.
  • Example: Finding 'login bug' globally shows all matching issues from all projects.
Why it matters

Essential for cross-project reporting and broad issue discovery.

Board-Specific Filters

  • Located on the quick filter bar of a specific Scrum/Kanban board.
  • Filters issues ONLY within the scope of that particular board.
  • Does not affect issues outside the current board's context.
  • Example: Filtering 'Assignee: John Doe' on a board only shows his issues on that board.
Common Pitfall

Confusing board filters with global search can lead to missing issues.
