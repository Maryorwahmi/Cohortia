Status vs. Resolution: A Critical Distinction

Understanding Issue Lifecycle States for Accurate Reporting

Issue Status

  • What it is: The current stage of an issue in its workflow.
  • Examples: To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done.
  • Purpose: Reflects active work and workflow progression.
Example

A task is In Progress while a developer is actively coding it.

Issue Resolution

  • What it is: The final outcome or reason an issue was closed.
  • Examples: Fixed, Won't Do, Duplicate, Unresolved.
  • Purpose: Describes the conclusion of the work, not just its completion.
Common Pitfall

An issue can be Done (Status) but still Unresolved (Resolution) if not explicitly set.
