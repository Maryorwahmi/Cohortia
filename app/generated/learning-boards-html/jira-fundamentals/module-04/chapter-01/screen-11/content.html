Avoiding Common Search Pitfalls

Ensuring Accurate and Expected Results in Jira

Common Mistakes & Solutions

  • Forgotten Filters: Always double-check and clear previous criteria if results are unexpected.
  • Incorrect Project: Verify the 'Project' filter is set to the correct project scope.
  • Status vs. Resolution: Understand the distinct meanings; use 'Status' for workflow, 'Resolution' for outcome.
  • Over-filtering: Start broad and gradually add filters to avoid missing results.
Key Takeaway

Proactive checking of filters and understanding key field distinctions are vital for reliable Jira search results.

Troubleshooting Your Search

Pitfall: Old Filters Active

If your search is empty, look for a 'Clear all' button or manually reset filters.

Pitfall: Wrong Project Selected

Always confirm the 'Project' dropdown matches where your issue is located.
