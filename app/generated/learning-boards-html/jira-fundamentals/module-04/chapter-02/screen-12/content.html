Dynamic Filtering with JQL Functions (Part 2)

Leveraging Date Functions and `issueHistory()`

Date-Based Functions

  • •`startOfDay()`, `endOfWeek()`, `startOfMonth()`: Define dynamic time boundaries.
  • •`now()`: Refers to the current date and time.
  • •**Relative Dates:** `"-1w"` (last week), `"-4h"` (last 4 hours).
  • •**Example:** `duedate < endOfWeek()` (Issues due before end of current week).

Personal & History Functions

  • •`issueHistory()`: Finds issues you have recently viewed.
  • •`votedIssues()`: Finds issues you have voted for.
  • •`watchedIssues()`: Finds issues you are watching.
  • •**Example:** `assignee = currentUser() AND updated > "-1d"` (Your issues updated yesterday).
Key Takeaway

JQL's date and personal functions enable highly dynamic and context-aware queries, crucial for real-time reporting and efficient workload management.
