Advanced Searching with JQL

Unlock Granular Control Over Your Jira Data

Jira Query Language (JQL) is a powerful, flexible language that allows you to construct highly specific and complex queries to find exactly what you need. Think of JQL as SQL for Jira issues – a structured way to ask Jira for data with unparalleled precision.

Key Idea

JQL is a powerful, flexible language designed to construct highly specific and complex queries for Jira issues, enabling precise data retrieval.
