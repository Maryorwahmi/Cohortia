Logical Operators

Combining Clauses with AND, OR, NOT

Precision with `AND`, `OR`, and `NOT`

AND & OR Operators

  • •**`AND`:** Returns issues that match *all* conditions.
  • •**Example:** `project = "Marketing" AND issuetype = Story` (Must be in Marketing AND be a Story).
  • •**`OR`:** Returns issues that match *any* of the conditions.
  • •**Example:** `issuetype = Bug OR issuetype = Task` (Can be a Bug OR a Task).
Common Pitfall

Confusing `AND` and `OR` can lead to results that are either too broad or too narrow for your actual needs.

NOT Operator & Example

  • •**`NOT`:** Excludes issues that match the specified condition.
  • •**Example:** `status NOT IN ("Done", "Closed")` (Finds issues not in Done or Closed status).
  • •**Combined Example:** `project = Development AND (issuetype = Bug OR issuetype = Task) AND assignee IS NOT EMPTY`
Key Takeaway

`AND`, `OR`, and `NOT` are fundamental for building complex JQL queries, allowing you to precisely include or exclude issues based on multiple criteria.
