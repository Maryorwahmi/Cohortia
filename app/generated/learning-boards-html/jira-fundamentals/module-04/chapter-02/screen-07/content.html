JQL Operators: Defining Relationships

Using `=`, `IN`, `~`, and Comparison Operators

Key Operators & Examples

  • •`=` (equals): `project = "Website"`
  • •`!=` (not equals): `status != "Done"`
  • •`~` (contains text): `summary ~ "database error"`
  • •`IN` (is one of list): `status IN ("To Do", "In Progress")`
  • •`IS EMPTY` (has no value): `resolution IS EMPTY`

Comparison & Negation

  • •`!~` (does not contain text): `description !~ "legacy code"`
  • •`NOT IN` (is not one of list): `assignee NOT IN ("John Doe", "Jane Smith")`
  • •`IS NOT EMPTY` (has a value): `fixVersion IS NOT EMPTY`
  • •`<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=` (comparison): `duedate < "2024-12-31"`
Key Takeaway

Selecting the correct operator is crucial for precisely defining the relationship between a field and its value, ensuring accurate query results.
