Grouping Logic with Parentheses

Ensuring Correct Logical Evaluation

The Role of Parentheses

  • •**Override Precedence:** `AND` takes precedence over `OR` by default. Parentheses change this.
  • •**Group Clauses:** Combine multiple `OR` conditions to be evaluated as a single unit.
  • •**Ensure Accuracy:** Prevents misinterpretation of complex logical statements.
Common Error

Mismatched parentheses will cause syntax errors and prevent your query from running.

Example: Grouping `OR`

project = Development AND (issuetype = Bug OR issuetype = Task) AND assignee IS NOT EMPTY

Here, the parentheses ensure that issues are first filtered by being either a 'Bug' or a 'Task', before applying the 'Development' project and 'not empty assignee' conditions.

Key Takeaway

Always use parentheses to explicitly group `OR` clauses within `AND` conditions to ensure your query's logic is correctly interpreted.
