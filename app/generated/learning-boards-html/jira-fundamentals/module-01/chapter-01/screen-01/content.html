What is Jira?

Understanding its Core Purpose and Use Cases

Jira is a powerful work management tool that helps teams track tasks, manage projects, and streamline workflows. It provides a centralized system for transparency and collaboration, evolving from its origins as a bug tracker to a versatile platform for all departments.
