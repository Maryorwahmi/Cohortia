Jira Work Management: Streamlining Business Operations

Task management for marketing, HR, finance, and more

General Task Management

Provides simpler project templates and features focused on managing everyday tasks and projects.

Target Audience

Ideal for business teams such as Marketing, HR, Finance, and Legal, who need structured work management.

Example Use Cases

Marketing Campaign Management

A marketing team can use Jira Work Management to:

  • •Plan content creation for a new product launch.
  • •Track social media post schedules.
  • •Manage event planning tasks.
Key Takeaway

Jira Work Management extends Jira's core benefits to non-technical teams, enabling them to organize, track, and collaborate on their specific business operations effectively.
