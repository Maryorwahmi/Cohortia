Jira's Operational Pillars

Recap of how work flows and is visualized

Workflow

A set of statuses and transitions an issue follows from creation to completion, defining its lifecycle.

Board

A visual representation of issues within a project, used in agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban).

Jira Software

The Jira product specifically designed for agile software development teams.

Summary Takeaway

Workflows define the journey of tasks, Boards provide visual clarity, and specialized Jira products cater to the unique needs of different teams, enhancing overall operational efficiency.
