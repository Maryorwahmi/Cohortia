Boards: Your Visual Command Center

Scrum and Kanban for agile project management

Scrum Boards

Ideal for teams working in sprints, visualizing backlogs, and tracking progress within fixed iterations.

Kanban Boards

Focus on continuous flow, limiting work in progress to optimize efficiency and reduce bottlenecks in a steady stream of work.

Visualizing a Workflow

From To-Do to Done

Imagine a Kanban board with columns:

  • •To Do: All pending tasks.
  • •In Progress: Tasks currently being worked on (with a limit).
  • •In Review: Tasks awaiting feedback.
  • •Done: Completed tasks.

This visual layout makes bottlenecks immediately obvious.

Key Takeaway

Boards provide an essential visual interface for managing issues, supporting agile methodologies, and enhancing a team's ability to track and adapt to work progress.
