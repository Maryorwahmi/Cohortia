Jira Service Management: Empowering IT and Customer Support

Managing requests, incidents, and SLAs efficiently

Service Request Management

Provides a dedicated portal for users to submit requests and for support teams to manage them efficiently.

Incident & SLA Management

Helps IT and customer service teams track incidents, manage queues, and adhere to Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

Scenario: IT Help Desk

Resolving Employee Issues

An IT team uses Jira Service Management to:

  • •Receive requests for software installation.
  • •Track and resolve network outages (incidents).
  • •Ensure critical issues are resolved within defined SLA times.
Key Takeaway

Jira Service Management is indispensable for support teams, providing the structure and tools needed to deliver high-quality, timely service and manage complex service operations.
