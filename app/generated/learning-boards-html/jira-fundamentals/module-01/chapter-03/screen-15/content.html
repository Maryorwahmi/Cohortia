Key Concepts: Issue Types & Management

Important terms for classifying and managing work in Jira.

Issue Type Hierarchy

Classification of work (Epic, Story, Task, Bug, Sub-task) defining its nature and purpose.

Epic, Story, Task

From large initiatives (Epic) to user-centric features (Story) and general work items (Task).

Bug & Sub-task

Tracking defects (Bug) and breaking down work into granular steps (Sub-task).

Chapter Summary

Effectively utilizing Jira's issue types and understanding their hierarchical relationships is crucial for organizing, tracking, and collaborating on all work within your project.
