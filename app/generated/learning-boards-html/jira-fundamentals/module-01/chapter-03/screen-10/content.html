Definition

What is a Bug?

A Bug is an error or defect in the product that needs to be fixed. It represents a deviation from expected behavior or functionality.

Key Takeaway

Bugs are critical for maintaining product quality and ensuring a smooth user experience by systematically addressing defects.

Bug Characteristics

Defect Tracking

Specifically for errors that cause the product to not function as intended.

Prioritized Fixes

Addressed based on impact and urgency to minimize disruption.

Contextual Linking

Often linked to the Story or feature where the defect was found.

Impact on Users

Directly affects user experience and product functionality.

Common Misconception

Treating every minor UI adjustment or new feature request as a Bug. Bugs are for defects, not enhancements.
