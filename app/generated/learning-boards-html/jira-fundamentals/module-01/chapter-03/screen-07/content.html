Definition

What is an Epic?

An Epic is a large body of work or a significant feature that can be broken down into multiple smaller stories or tasks. It represents a major goal or initiative that might span multiple sprints.

Key Takeaway

Epics provide a high-level view of large initiatives, helping to connect daily work to broader strategic objectives.

Epic Characteristics

Large Scope

Represents a significant feature or initiative, often spanning multiple sprints.

Parent to Stories/Tasks

Acts as a container for smaller, actionable work items.

Strategic Goal

Aligns team efforts with broader business objectives.

Long-term Focus

Often spans multiple sprints or releases, providing a roadmap.

Common Pitfall

Using a 'Task' for a major feature like 'Build a new payment gateway' instead of an Epic. This obscures scope and makes tracking difficult.
