Core Concept

What is a Jira Project?

A Jira project is a dedicated container for all work items (issues) related to a specific initiative, product, or team. It provides a structured environment for planning, tracking, and managing work.

Key Decision

Choosing a Project Template

Templates pre-configure issue types, workflows, and board types. Selecting the right one is crucial for aligning Jira with your team's methodology.

Key Takeaway

The project template defines your team's initial workflow and issue structure. Choose wisely to match your operational style.

Common Templates Overview

Scrum Template

Ideal for fixed-length iterations (sprints) and product backlogs. Includes Scrum board, backlog, Epics, Stories, Tasks, Bugs.

Kanban Template

For continuous flow and limiting work in progress. Offers a Kanban board and similar issue types.

Basic / Work Management

Simpler setups for general task tracking, suitable for non-software teams or basic project management.

Common Pitfall

Choosing the wrong template can lead to misaligned workflows and require significant re-configuration later. Always consider your team's methodology first.
