Process Overview

Steps to Create an Issue

1

Click 'Create'

Initiate issue creation from the global navigation bar.

2

Select Project & Type

Choose the correct project and the appropriate issue type (Epic, Story, Task, Bug, Sub-task).

3

Fill Essential Fields

Populate Summary, Description, Assignee, Reporter, Priority, and Labels.

Key Takeaway

Clear and complete issue fields are vital for effective communication, accurate tracking, and efficient workflow management.

Essential Fields Explained

Summary

A concise, one-line title that quickly conveys the essence of the work.

Description

Detailed explanation, context, requirements, and background information.

Assignee

The team member responsible for completing the work.

Common Pitfall

Leaving descriptions vague or not assigning issues, leading to ambiguity and lack of ownership.
