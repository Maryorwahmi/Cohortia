Definition

What is a Story?

A Story is a user-centric requirement or feature, typically written from the perspective of the end-user. Stories are small enough to be completed within a single sprint.

Key Takeaway

Stories ensure that development efforts are focused on delivering tangible value to the end-user in manageable increments.

Story Characteristics

User-Centric

Focuses on value for the end-user, often phrased as 'As a user, I want...'.

Sprint-Sized

Designed to be completed within a single development sprint.

Breaks Down Epics

Smaller, actionable components that contribute to a larger Epic.

Deliverable Value

Each Story aims to provide a specific, testable piece of functionality.

Common Pitfall

Writing Stories that are too large or too technical, making them difficult to complete within a single sprint or to verify user value.
