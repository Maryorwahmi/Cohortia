Definition

What is a Sub-task?

A Sub-task is a smaller, granular piece of work that is part of a larger Story or Task. It helps break down complex work items into manageable steps.

Key Takeaway

Sub-tasks provide a detailed checklist for complex work, ensuring all steps are tracked and assigned for efficient completion.

Sub-task Characteristics

Granular Steps

Breaks down a parent Story or Task into very specific, actionable steps.

Child of Story/Task

Always associated with a larger parent issue, providing detailed context.

Enhanced Tracking

Allows for individual assignment and progress tracking of very small work units.

Supports Complex Work

Helps manage the intricacies of larger tasks by breaking them down.

Common Pitfall

Creating too many Sub-tasks for simple items, leading to unnecessary administrative overhead and clutter.
