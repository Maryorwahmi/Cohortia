The Project Sidebar

Contextual Navigation Within Your Project

Scrum Project Sidebar

  • •Backlog: Where new work is prioritized and refined.
  • •Active Sprints: Your Scrum board showing work in progress.
  • •Reports: For sprint reports, velocity charts, and other metrics.
  • •Releases & Pages: For version management and Confluence integration.

Kanban Project Sidebar

  • •Kanban Board: The primary view for managing continuous flow of work.
  • •Reports: For cumulative flow diagrams and other Kanban-specific metrics.
  • •Releases & Pages: Similar to Scrum, for versioning and documentation.
  • •Project Settings: Access to project-specific configurations and administration.
Key Takeaway

The Project Sidebar provides essential, context-specific navigation within a project, offering direct access to boards, backlogs, reports, and other tools tailored to the project's methodology.
