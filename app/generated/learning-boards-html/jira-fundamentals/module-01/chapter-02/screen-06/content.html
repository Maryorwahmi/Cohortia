The Global Navigation Bar

Your Central Compass in Jira

Definition: Persistent Top Bar

The Global Navigation Bar is a consistent element at the top of almost all Jira screens, serving as your primary tool for platform-wide navigation.

Purpose: Core Functionality Access

It provides quick access to Jira's core functionalities, such as projects, dashboards, filters, and the ability to create new issues, from anywhere in the system.

Key Takeaway

The Global Navigation Bar is your constant guide in Jira, ensuring you always have immediate access to essential tools and information, regardless of your current screen.
