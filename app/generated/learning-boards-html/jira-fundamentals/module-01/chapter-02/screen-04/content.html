Core Concept

The Jira Dashboard

Your Personalized Command Center

Definition: Personalized Homepage

The Jira Dashboard is your customizable homepage, providing an at-a-glance overview of your work, project statuses, and team activity. It's the first thing you see upon logging in.

Purpose: Consolidate Information

It consolidates dynamic information from multiple projects into a single, tailored view. This helps you stay informed and focused on what matters most to your role.

Key Takeaway

Your Jira Dashboard is a powerful tool for personal productivity, offering a customizable, real-time overview of your work and project health.

Dashboard Customization

1

Add, remove, and arrange various 'gadgets' to display dynamic information.

2

Tailor the dashboard to your specific role, whether you're a developer or project manager.

3

Consolidate information from multiple projects into a single, easy-to-read view.
