Understanding Dashboard Gadgets

Dynamic Insights Tailored to Your Role

Assigned to Me

Displays all issues currently assigned to you, providing a quick overview of your personal workload and priorities.

Filter Results

Shows issues based on a specific saved search query, allowing you to monitor custom sets of tasks or project statuses.

Activity Stream

Displays recent updates and changes across all your projects, keeping you informed about team activity and progress.

Two Dimensional Filter Statistics

Visualizes issue distribution based on two criteria (e.g., assignee and status), offering quick analytical insights into your project.

Key Takeaway

Dashboard gadgets are essential for creating a dynamic, personalized overview of your work, allowing you to monitor specific tasks, project progress, and team activity in real-time.
