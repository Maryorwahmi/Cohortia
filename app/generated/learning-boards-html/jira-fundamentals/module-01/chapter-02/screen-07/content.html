Global Navigation: Essential Tools

Home, Your Work, Projects, and Filters

Home / Jira Icon

Always takes you back to your default dashboard, serving as a quick reset or starting point for your Jira session.

Your Work

A personalized view showing recent projects, issues you've worked on, and issues assigned to you, ideal for quickly resuming tasks.

Projects

Lists all projects you have access to, allowing you to navigate to a project's specific overview, board, or backlog.

Filters

Provides access to saved searches and allows you to create new ones, enabling powerful filtering for specific sets of issues.

Key Takeaway

The 'Home', 'Your Work', 'Projects', and 'Filters' sections of the Global Navigation Bar are fundamental for quickly accessing your personal workspace, project overviews, and specific issue sets.
