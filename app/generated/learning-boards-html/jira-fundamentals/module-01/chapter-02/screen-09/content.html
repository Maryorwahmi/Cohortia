Global Navigation: Search & Support

Finding Information and Getting Help

Search Bar (Quick Search)

A universal search bar for quickly finding issues, projects, boards, or content within issues by typing keywords or issue keys.

Help (Question Mark Icon)

Provides access to Jira's documentation, support resources, and useful keyboard shortcuts to enhance your efficiency.

Notifications (Bell Icon)

Alerts you to updates on issues you're watching or mentioned in, ensuring you stay informed about relevant changes and discussions.

User Profile (Avatar)

Access your personal settings, profile details, and the option to log out, giving you control over your individual Jira experience.

Key Takeaway

The 'Search Bar', 'Help', 'Notifications', and 'User Profile' elements are vital for efficient information retrieval, support, staying updated, and managing your personal Jira settings.
