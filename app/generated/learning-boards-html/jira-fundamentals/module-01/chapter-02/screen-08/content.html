Global Navigation: Advanced Access

Dashboards, People, Apps, and Create

Dashboards Menu

Provides access to all dashboards you've created or have been shared with you, allowing for multiple personalized views.

People

Helps you find and connect with other users in your Jira instance, fostering collaboration and team communication.

Apps

Provides access to marketplace apps installed on your Jira instance, extending its functionality with powerful integrations.

Create Button

A prominent button that allows you to quickly create a new issue (task, bug, story) from anywhere within Jira, streamlining workflow initiation.

Key Takeaway

The 'Dashboards', 'People', 'Apps', and 'Create' elements in the Global Navigation Bar provide crucial access to personalized views, team connections, extended functionalities, and efficient issue creation.
