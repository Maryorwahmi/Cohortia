Optimization Strategy

Continuous Board Optimization

Evolving your board with your team's workflow

Key Takeaway

A Jira board is a living tool; continuous review and adaptation ensure it remains aligned with evolving team workflows, providing maximum clarity and utility.

Example Evolution

A simple 'To Do | In Progress | Done' board might evolve to include 'In Review' columns, Epic swimlanes, and 'Blocked' quick filters as the team matures.

The Optimization Cycle

1

Review: Regularly assess board relevance with your team.

2

Adapt: Adjust columns, swimlanes, and filters as workflow changes.

3

Refine: Update card layouts and general settings for efficiency.

4

Verify: Ensure changes provide clarity and utility with minimal overhead.
