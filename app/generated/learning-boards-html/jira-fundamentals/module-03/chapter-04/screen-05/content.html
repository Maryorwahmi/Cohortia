Mastering Column Management

Defining your workflow stages on the board

Key Takeaway

Columns are the backbone of your board's visual workflow. Keep them clear, concise, and reflective of your team's actual process to avoid clutter.

Common Mistake

Having too many columns makes the board cluttered and difficult to read, hindering rather than helping team efficiency.
