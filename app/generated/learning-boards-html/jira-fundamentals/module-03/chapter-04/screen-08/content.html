Creating and Managing Quick Filters

Rapidly narrowing down your board view with JQL

Key Takeaway

Quick filters are saved JQL queries that provide instant, personalized filtering of issues on your board, crucial for focused work and efficient stand-ups.

Common Mistake

Creating too many rarely used quick filters can clutter the board, making it harder to find the truly useful ones. Keep them relevant and frequently used.
