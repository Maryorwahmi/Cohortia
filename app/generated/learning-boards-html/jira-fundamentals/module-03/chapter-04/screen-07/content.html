Board Organization

Utilizing Swimlanes for Enhanced Visibility

Grouping related issues horizontally on your board

Key Takeaway

Swimlanes provide horizontal grouping of issues, offering immediate context and enhancing visibility for specific categories of work, such as tasks within a story or high-priority items.

Practical Use

Quickly identify personal workloads, track progress of larger initiatives, or see all sub-tasks related to a parent story at a glance.

Swimlane Types & Examples

Queries: Group by JQL (e.g., `assignee = currentUser()`).

Stories: Group sub-tasks under their parent story.

Assignees: Group issues by team member.

Epics: Group all issues belonging to a specific epic.
