Customizing Card Layouts

Displaying relevant information directly on issue cards

Key Takeaway

Tailoring card layouts to display key fields directly on issues reduces clicks, speeds up comprehension, and enhances decision-making for your team.

Example Use

Development teams can show 'Story Points', while support teams can display 'Time to SLA' for immediate context.
