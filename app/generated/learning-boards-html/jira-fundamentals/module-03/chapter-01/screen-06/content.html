Transitions: The Bridges Between Statuses

Actions That Drive Issue Progress

How Transitions Work
Action-Oriented

Transitions are the explicit actions users take to move an issue.

Connect Statuses

They act as bridges, linking one status to another.

Enforce Rules

Can be configured with conditions to ensure correct sequencing.

Example Transitions

  • Start Work: Moves issue from 'Open' to 'In Progress'.
  • Submit for Review: Moves issue from 'In Progress' to 'In Review'.
  • Resolve Issue: Moves issue from 'In Review' to 'Resolved'.
  • Reopen: Moves issue from 'Resolved' back to 'In Progress'.

Why They Are Crucial

Transitions prevent illogical jumps in an issue's lifecycle, ensuring that all necessary steps are completed and documented. This maintains the integrity of your process and data.

Key Takeaway

Transitions are the active controls that guide issues through their workflow, enforcing process and ensuring logical progression.
