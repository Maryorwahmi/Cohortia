Connecting the Dots: Defining New Transitions

Guiding Issues Through the New Review Phase

Steps to Create Transitions
1From In Progress to Ready for Review

Create a transition named 'Submit for Review' for developers.

2From Ready for Review to Done

Add a transition named 'Approve & Complete' for successful reviews.

3Optional: Back to In Progress

Create 'Revert for Rework' from 'Ready for Review' to 'In Progress'.

Example: Review Flow

A 'Task' moves from In Progress (developer finishes work) via Submit for Review to Ready for Review (awaiting peer feedback). After review, it moves via Approve & Complete to Done, or via Revert for Rework back to In Progress.

Why Both Directions?

Providing both forward and backward transitions ensures flexibility in the workflow, allowing teams to adapt to real-world scenarios where rework might be necessary after a review.

Key Takeaway

Transitions are vital for defining the precise paths issues can take, ensuring controlled and logical movement through the workflow.
