Enhancing a Workflow: Introducing a Review Step

Adding a 'Ready for Review' Status

Steps to Add a Status
1Navigate to Workflows

Go to Jira Settings > Workflows to access workflow management.

2Copy Existing Workflow

Always copy a workflow before editing to preserve the original and test changes safely.

3Add New Status

Click 'Add Status', name it 'Ready for Review', and assign it to the 'In Progress' category.

Scenario: Peer Review

Your team needs a mandatory 'Ready for Review' step for all 'Task' issues to ensure peer review before completion. This new status will facilitate that.

Why Copy First?

Modifying an active workflow directly can disrupt ongoing work. Copying allows you to test changes in isolation and ensures a smooth transition when publishing.

Key Takeaway

Always copy a workflow before making modifications, and ensure new statuses are correctly categorized to reflect their role in the issue lifecycle.
