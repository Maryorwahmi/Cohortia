Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Workflow

A set of statuses and transitions that an issue moves through during its lifecycle.

Status

A particular stage an issue is in, such as "Open," "In Progress," or "Done."

Transition

An action that moves an issue from one status to another, like "Start Work" or "Resolve."

Summary Takeaway

Workflows provide the structure for issue management, with statuses defining states and transitions controlling movement between them.
