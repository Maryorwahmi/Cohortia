What is a Jira Workflow?

A Jira workflow is a visual representation of a business process or software development lifecycle. It dictates the sequence of statuses and transitions an issue follows from creation to resolution.

Why it Matters

  • Ensures consistency in issue handling.
  • Provides clear visibility into progress.
  • Helps teams adhere to established processes.
  • Prevents issues from getting lost or steps being missed.
Key Takeaway

Workflows are the backbone of structured issue management, bringing order and predictability to project execution.
