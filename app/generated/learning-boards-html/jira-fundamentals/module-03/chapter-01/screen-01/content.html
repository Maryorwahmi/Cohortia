Understanding Jira Workflows

Guiding Issues from Creation to Completion

Every task, bug, or story in Jira follows a defined path. This chapter explores how workflows bring order to your projects, ensuring consistent progress and clear visibility from start to finish.

Key Idea

Jira workflows define the lifecycle of an issue, ensuring consistent and visible progress through structured statuses and transitions.
