Activating Your Enhanced Workflow

Publishing and Updating the Workflow Scheme

Steps to Activate
1Publish the Workflow

Click 'Publish Draft' in the workflow editor to make your changes live.

2Update Workflow Scheme

Navigate to Jira Settings > Workflow Schemes and edit the relevant scheme.

3Assign & Publish

Assign your new workflow to the target issue type (e.g., 'Task') and publish the scheme.

Verification is Key

After publishing, always create a new issue of the affected type (e.g., a 'Task') and observe its available transitions. This confirms your changes are correctly applied and functioning as intended.

Impact on Existing Issues

When you update a workflow scheme, existing issues using the old workflow will automatically transition to the new one, aligning with the updated process. Ensure this transition is logical.

Key Takeaway

Publishing a workflow and updating its scheme are the final steps to activate your changes, ensuring your project's issues follow the new, enhanced process.
