Key Concepts

Advanced Workflow Principles

Workflow Scheme

A configuration that maps workflows to specific issue types within a Jira project.

Simplicity

Design workflows with clear, meaningful statuses to avoid over-complication and user confusion.

Alignment

Ensure workflows accurately reflect actual team processes to prevent misuse and unreliable data.

Summary Takeaway

Effective workflow design involves leveraging workflow schemes for flexibility and adhering to principles of simplicity and alignment with real team processes.
