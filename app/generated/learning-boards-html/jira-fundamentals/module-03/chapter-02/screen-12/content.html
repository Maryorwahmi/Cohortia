Approval Workflow: Transition Details

Configuring specific transitions with advanced features.

01

Submit for Approval

From: Open To: Pending Approval

  • •Post-function: Assign issue to 'Manager' role.
  • •Post-function: Add comment: "Issue submitted for manager approval."
02

Approve Change

From: Pending Approval To: Approved

  • •Condition: Only 'Managers' group can perform.
  • •Post-function: Clear 'Approver' field.
  • •Post-function: Assign issue to 'Developer' role.
03

Reject Change

From: Pending Approval To: Rejected

  • •Condition: Only 'Managers' group can perform.
  • •Validator: 'Comment required'.
  • •Post-function: Assign issue to 'Reporter'.
  • •Post-function: Add comment: "Issue rejected. See comments for details."
Key Takeaway

Each transition is a carefully orchestrated sequence of conditions, validators, and post-functions to ensure controlled, compliant, and automated process flow.
