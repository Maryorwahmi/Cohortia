Beyond Basic Status Changes

Unlocking intelligent, conditional, and automated actions in Jira.

Advanced workflow configuration moves beyond simple status changes to intelligent, conditional, and automated actions. This allows you to truly automate and enforce your team's processes within Jira.

Key Takeaway

Conditions, validators, and post-functions are the core components that enable sophisticated workflow automation and process enforcement in Jira.

Conditions

Rules that determine if a transition is visible and available to a user. Controls 'who' can act.

Validators

Checks performed before a transition completes to ensure specific criteria are met. Controls 'what' must be true.

Post-Functions

Automated actions performed immediately after a transition successfully completes. Controls 'what happens next automatically.'
