Essential Workflow Elements

Revisiting the core components of advanced configuration.

Condition

A rule that determines whether a transition is visible and available to a user, enforcing roles and access.

Validator

A check performed before a transition completes to ensure specific criteria are met, guaranteeing data integrity.

Post-Function

An automated action performed immediately after a transition successfully completes, streamlining tasks.

Chapter Summary

Conditions, validators, and post-functions are the fundamental building blocks for creating intelligent, automated, and compliant workflows in Jira.
