Approval Workflow: Feature Summary

A consolidated view of conditions, validators, and post-functions.

Conditions

  • •'Approve' Transition: Only 'Managers' group.
  • •'Reject' Transition: Only 'Managers' group.

Purpose: Enforce roles and restrict access to critical actions.

Validators

  • •'Reject' Transition: 'Comment required'.

Purpose: Ensure data integrity and provide necessary context.

Post-Functions

  • •'Submit': Assign to Manager, Add comment.
  • •'Approve': Clear Approver, Assign to Developer.
  • •'Reject': Assign to Reporter, Add comment.

Purpose: Automate routine tasks and keep stakeholders informed.

Key Takeaway

This integrated approach ensures a robust, efficient, and compliant process, significantly reducing manual effort and improving data quality.
