Expanding Workflow Capabilities

Understanding global transitions and workflow properties.

Global Transition

A transition that allows an issue to move from any status to a specific target status, simplifying configuration.

Workflow Property

A key-value pair that defines specific behaviors for issues when they are in a particular status, influencing field display or editability.

Chapter Summary

Global transitions and workflow properties provide additional layers of flexibility and control, enabling you to fine-tune issue behavior and streamline complex processes in Jira.
