Key Concepts: Scrum and Kanban Specifics

Understanding the Core Elements of Agile Board Methodologies

Sprint

A fixed-length period (e.g., 1-4 weeks) in Scrum during which a team works to complete a set amount of work.

Backlog

A prioritized list of all work (stories, bugs, tasks) that needs to be done for a product or project.

WIP Limit

A constraint on a Kanban board that limits the maximum number of issues allowed in a specific column or stage at any given time.

Chapter Takeaway

Sprints, Backlogs, and WIP Limits are fundamental concepts that differentiate and define the operational mechanics of Scrum and Kanban boards.
