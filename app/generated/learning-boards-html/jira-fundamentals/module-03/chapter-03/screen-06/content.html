Key Component

The Backlog: Project's Master List

The Backlog is where all future work for the project resides, prioritized and estimated. It's a living document, constantly refined and groomed.

  • •Prioritized: Items ordered by value, risk, and dependencies.
  • •Dynamic: Continuously refined by the team and product owner.
  • •Comprehensive: Includes features, bugs, tasks, and technical debt.
Why it Matters

A well-maintained backlog ensures the team is always working on the most valuable items, aligning efforts with strategic goals.
