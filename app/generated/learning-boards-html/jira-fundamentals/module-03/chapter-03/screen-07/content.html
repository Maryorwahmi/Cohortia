Key Component

Active Sprint Board: Progress at a Glance

Once a sprint begins, issues move to the active sprint board, which typically has columns representing workflow statuses (e.g., "To Do," "In Progress," "Done").

  • •Visual Progress: Team members move issues across columns as they work.
  • •Daily Stand-ups: Used to discuss progress, impediments, and plans.
  • •Built-in Reports: Burndown charts (remaining work) and velocity charts (completed work over sprints).
Common Mistake

Not regularly updating the active sprint board leads to outdated information, hindering effective team communication and progress tracking.
