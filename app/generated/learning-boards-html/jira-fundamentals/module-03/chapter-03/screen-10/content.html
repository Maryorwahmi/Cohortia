Key Metrics

Lead Time: From Idea to Done

Lead Time measures the total time from issue creation to completion. It reflects the overall responsiveness of your system from a customer's perspective.

  • •Customer-Centric: Focuses on the total time a customer waits.
  • •System Responsiveness: Indicates how quickly value is delivered end-to-end.
Key Metrics

Cycle Time: Active Work Duration

Cycle Time measures the time from when work *starts* on an issue to its completion. It reflects the team's active working efficiency.

  • •Team-Centric: Focuses on the duration of active work.
  • •Process Efficiency: Helps identify bottlenecks within the active workflow stages.

Key Takeaway

Tracking Lead Time and Cycle Time helps teams identify inefficiencies and continuously improve their workflow for faster value delivery.
