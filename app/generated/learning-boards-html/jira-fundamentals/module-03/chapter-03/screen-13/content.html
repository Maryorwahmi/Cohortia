Key Concepts: Jira Boards Fundamentals

Essential Terms for Visualizing and Managing Work

Jira Board

A visual tool in Jira that displays issues as they progress through their workflow, facilitating tracking and collaboration.

Scrum Board

A type of Jira board designed for teams following the Scrum agile framework, featuring a backlog, sprints, and an active sprint board.

Kanban Board

A type of Jira board designed for teams focusing on continuous flow and limiting work in progress, without fixed-length iterations.

Chapter Takeaway

Jira Boards are essential for visualizing and managing work, with Scrum and Kanban offering distinct approaches to agile project management.
