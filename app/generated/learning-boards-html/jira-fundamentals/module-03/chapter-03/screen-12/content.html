Common Mistake

Misapplication of Board Types

Not understanding the fundamental differences between Scrum and Kanban can lead to misapplication, such as trying to run sprints on a Kanban board.

  • •Consequences: Confusion, undermined benefits, inefficient processes.
  • •Solution: Align board type with your team's actual working style and goals.
Common Mistake

Misalignment with Workflows

Not aligning the board's columns with the underlying workflow statuses can lead to issues appearing in the wrong columns or not moving correctly.

  • •Consequences: Inaccurate tracking, frustration, manual errors.
  • •Solution: Ensure every workflow status has a clear visual representation on your board.
