How to Create a New Jira Board

Creating a board in Jira is straightforward, allowing you to tailor it to your team's specific agile methodology.

  1. 1.Navigate to Project: Go to your Jira project and find 'Boards' in the sidebar.
  2. 2.Click 'Create board': Initiate the board creation process.
  3. 3.Choose Board Type: Select 'Scrum board' or 'Kanban board'.
  4. 4.Select Source: Create from an 'existing project' or 'existing filter'.
  5. 5.Name Your Board: Give it a clear, meaningful name.
  6. 6.Click 'Create board': Jira configures basic columns automatically.
Pro-Tip

When creating from an existing filter, ensure the filter is well-defined to include only the relevant issues for your board.
