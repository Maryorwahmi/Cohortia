Core Concept

Jira Boards: Your Project's Visual Hub

Jira boards are the primary interface for many teams, offering immediate insight into what's being worked on, what's next, and what might be blocked. They provide an intuitive, real-time view of issues as they progress through their workflow.

  • •Real-time Visibility: See issue status instantly.
  • •Enhanced Collaboration: Keep teams aligned and informed.
  • •Bottleneck Identification: Quickly spot and address impediments.
Key Distinction

Workflows define how an issue moves, while boards define how you visualize and interact with that movement.
