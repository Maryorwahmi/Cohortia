Decoding Flow Logs: A Practical Example

Understanding a Sample VPC Flow Log Entry

2 123456789010 eni-0123456789abcdef0 172.31.16.139 172.31.16.21 20641 80 6 20 1200 1608670731 1608670791 ACCEPT OK

This entry shows traffic from 172.31.16.139 to 172.31.16.21 on port 80 (HTTP), 20 packets, 1200 bytes, and was ACCEPTED.

Key Takeaway

Understanding the structure and content of a VPC Flow Log entry is crucial for accurately diagnosing network behavior and pinpointing specific traffic issues.

Flow Log Entry Breakdown

Source/Destination: IP addresses and ports involved in the traffic flow.
Protocol: The network protocol used (e.g., TCP, UDP, ICMP).
Packets/Bytes: The volume of data transferred during the flow.
Action: Indicates if the traffic was ACCEPTed or REJECTed by security rules.
Timestamps: Unix epoch time for the start and end of the traffic flow.
