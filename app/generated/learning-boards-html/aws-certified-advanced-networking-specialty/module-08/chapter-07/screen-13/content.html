Common Culprits: Routing, Load Balancers & Verification

Completing the Diagnostic Checklist and Confirming Resolution

Routing & Load Balancers

Incorrect routing or overloaded load balancers can severely impact network flow.

  • Are routing tables correctly configured? Watch for asymmetric routing.
  • Are all load balancer targets healthy? Is the load balancer under stress?
  • Is the hybrid connection (DX/VPN) saturated? Check `BpsIn/Out` metrics.

Test and Verify

Always confirm that implemented changes resolve the issue without introducing new problems.

  • Implement a change based on your diagnosis.
  • Re-monitor metrics and logs to confirm resolution.
  • Ensure no new issues have been inadvertently introduced.
  • Avoid jumping to conclusions without data.
Key Takeaway

Thoroughly checking routing, load balancer health, and hybrid connections, followed by rigorous testing, ensures comprehensive bottleneck resolution and prevents recurrence.
