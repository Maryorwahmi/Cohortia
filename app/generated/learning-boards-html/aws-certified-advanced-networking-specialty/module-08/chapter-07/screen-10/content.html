Systematic Troubleshooting: Defining the Problem

Starting with Clear Symptoms and Scope

Step 01

Define the Problem

Clearly articulate the specific symptoms observed, moving beyond vague statements to concrete details.

  • What specific symptoms are observed? (e.g., high latency, low throughput, application timeouts)
  • Are there error messages?
  • When did it start?
Step 02

Scope the Problem

Determine the extent of the issue to narrow down the potential areas of investigation.

  • Is it affecting all users or specific users?
  • Is it regional or global?
  • Is it impacting a specific application or all applications?
Key Takeaway

A systematic approach begins with precisely defining the observed symptoms and carefully scoping the impact, laying the groundwork for effective diagnosis.
