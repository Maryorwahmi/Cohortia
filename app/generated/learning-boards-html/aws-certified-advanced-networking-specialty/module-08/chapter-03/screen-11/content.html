Module 08 › Chapter 8.3 › Jumbo Frames & MTU
MTU concept
MTU is the largest payload a link transmits without fragmentation. Larger MTUs mean fewer headers per byte transferred for big payloads.
Benefit
Reduced header overhead and improved throughput for large transfers.
Risk and verification
Ensure every hop—VPC peering, Transit Gateway, Direct Connect, and on-prem equipment—supports 9001. Verify with diagnostics and test traffic.
Common mistake

Enabling jumbo frames only on some instances causes fragmentation that can reduce performance.
