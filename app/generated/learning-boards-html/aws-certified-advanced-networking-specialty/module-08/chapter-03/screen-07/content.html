Module 08 › Chapter 8.3 › ENA vs EFA
ENA
High bandwidth, high PPS, lower CPU overhead. Good for bulk transfer and many general network-heavy applications.
  • SR-IOV based adapter.
  • Requires appropriate driver in AMI.
  • Simpler for throughput-focused workloads.
EFA
OS bypass for user-level networking libraries, optimized for very low and consistent latency in HPC and distributed training.
  • Not primarily for raw bandwidth — for latency consistency.
  • Requires application-level support and specific instance types.
  • Test with representative message patterns.
Decision aid
If your workload exchanges frequent small messages and needs consistent low latency, consider EFA. If you transfer large volumes of data and need throughput and PPS, prioritize ENA-enabled instances.
