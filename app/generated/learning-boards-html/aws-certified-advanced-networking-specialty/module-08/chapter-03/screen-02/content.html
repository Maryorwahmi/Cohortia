Module 08 › Chapter 8.3 › Learning Objectives
01
Choose network-optimized instances
Identify families such as C5n, M5n and R5n and why they matter for throughput and PPS. Verified competency milestone: confirm documented network bandwidth for selected instance.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Enable and verify ENA and EFA
Understand SR-IOV, driver presence and when EFA’s OS bypass is appropriate. Milestone: verify ENA via ethtool and EFA via documented instance support.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Use placement groups effectively
Decide between cluster, spread, and partition based on throughput vs. availability. Milestone: launch instances into a cluster placement group successfully.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Configure and test jumbo frames
Apply MTU=9001 consistently and test end-to-end. Milestone: ip link shows mtu 9001 and iperf3 confirms expected throughput between instances.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
