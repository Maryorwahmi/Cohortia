Optimizing VPC Networking and EC2 Instance Performance
Practical guidance for selecting instances, adapters, placement, and MTU to achieve predictable, high-throughput networking inside a VPC.
In this lesson

You will learn how instance families, ENA and EFA adapters, placement groups, and jumbo frames affect throughput and latency, and how to configure and test them safely.

Why it matters

Network choices directly affect application throughput, cost, and fault behavior. Small misconfigurations can cause fragmentation or launch failures.
