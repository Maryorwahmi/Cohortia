Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Direct Connect vs VPN
Site-to-Site VPN
Lower setup complexity; uses public internet for transport and standard DTO rates apply.
Best when
Transfers are modest, unpredictable, or you need low upfront cost connectivity.
Pitfall
High-volume steady transfers over VPN incur large DTO charges.
AWS Direct Connect
Dedicated private connection with higher setup and recurring fees but lower DTO rates for hybrid traffic.
Best when
Large, predictable data transfers occur regularly between on‑premises and AWS.
Considerations
Include port charges and partner setup fees in your model when comparing to VPN.
Key Takeaway

Model data volumes and compare per‑byte DTO costs against Direct Connect port fees to choose the cost-optimal hybrid link.
