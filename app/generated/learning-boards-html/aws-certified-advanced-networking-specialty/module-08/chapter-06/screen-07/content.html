Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Transit Gateway Optimization
Architecture patterns
Practical ways to keep Transit Gateway costs in check while gaining central control.
Avoid hairpinning
Prevent local east‑west traffic from unnecessarily transiting the hub; keep heavy bilateral flows direct.
Aggregate at edges
Use edge or shared VPCs to collect external traffic before attaching to the Transit Gateway.
Use Network Manager
Visualize flows to find attachments and paths that incur the most data processing charges.
Example and checklist
If several VPCs share a heavy data producer, consider colocating consumers or peering rather than forcing all traffic through the Transit Gateway.
Checklist
  • Identify top TransitGateway-DataProcessing usage lines in Cost Explorer.
  • Pinpoint high-volume attachments and their owners.
  • Consider peering or consolidation for heavy bilateral flows and keep the hub for broad connectivity.
Key Takeaway

Centralization simplifies routing but can be costly for heavy central flows — measure and choose hybrid patterns accordingly.
