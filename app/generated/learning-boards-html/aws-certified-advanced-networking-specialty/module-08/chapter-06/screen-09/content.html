Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › NAT & EIP Costs
NAT Gateway
Charges for hours and data processed; heavy traffic increases NATGateway-Bytes lines on the bill.
Optimization tips
Provision one NAT Gateway per AZ for redundancy rather than one per subnet; avoid idle NATs and consolidate traffic where possible.
Misconception
Moving to a single NAT Gateway across AZs to save costs can create cross‑AZ transfer charges and single points of failure.
Elastic IPs
EIPs are free when associated with running instances. Unassociated EIPs incur charges.
Practical action
Audit and release any unassociated EIPs to remove unnecessary charges.
Concrete example
A test environment that leaves EIPs unattached will see recurring small charges that add up; regular cleanup reduces this waste.
Key Takeaway

Small housekeeping on NAT Gateways and EIPs prevents repeated small charges from ballooning across accounts.
