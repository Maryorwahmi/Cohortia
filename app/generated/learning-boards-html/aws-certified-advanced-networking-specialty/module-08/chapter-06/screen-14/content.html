Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Key Concepts
Data Transfer Out (DTO)
Bytes leaving AWS to the internet or across regions/AZs; the primary cost driver.
AWS Direct Connect
Dedicated private connection with lower DTO rates for sustained hybrid traffic.
Site-to-Site VPN
Secure tunnel over the internet; lower setup cost but standard internet DTO rates apply.
Summary

When in doubt, measure DTO and model total cost over time to choose the right connectivity or caching strategy.
