Cross-Zone Load Balancing
Weigh even distribution against potential data-transfer costs.
ALB
Cross-zone enabled by default and free. Distributes requests evenly across registered targets regardless of AZ.
NLB
Cross-zone enabled by default but cross-AZ traffic may incur charges. Disabling keeps traffic within the connection AZ.
Concrete example and checks
If cost is a major concern and you have AZ-local high availability patterns, disabling NLB cross-zone can reduce inter-AZ transfer costs. But monitor per-AZ request distribution and target utilization. If one AZ’s targets are overloaded while others are idle, re-enable cross-zone or rebalance capacity.
Misconception
Turning off cross-zone automatically saves money with no trade-offs. In reality, you must confirm even AZ capacity to avoid performance regressions.
Key Takeaway

Decide cross-zone settings based on both cost and how well you can keep AZ capacity balanced under load.
