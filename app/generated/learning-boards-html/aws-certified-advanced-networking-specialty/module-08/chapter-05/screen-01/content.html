Advanced Load Balancing and Auto Scaling for Performance
Choose, tune, and scale ALBs and NLBs to meet throughput, latency, and cost goals.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Duration: ~12 minutes module preview
In this lesson

You will compare ALB and NLB behavior, learn advanced health check and target group settings, and design ASGs that scale on network metrics like NetworkOut. Expect concrete configuration examples and a hands-on scaling scenario.

ALB (Layer 7)
Advanced routing for HTTP/HTTPS, path and host rules, integrates with WAF and sticky sessions. Best for microservices and web apps.
NLB (Layer 4)
High throughput, ultra-low latency, preserves client IP, suitable for TCP/UDP workloads and performance-critical services.
Auto Scaling
Scale using network metrics like NetworkOut or RequestCountPerTarget to avoid network bottlenecks.
Cross-Zone Trade-offs
ALB cross-zone is free; NLB cross-zone may incur charges. Balance cost versus even distribution.
