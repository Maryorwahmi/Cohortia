Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Quick Reference
Measure first
Establish baselines for meaningful metrics before changing architecture or capacity.
Match feature to cause
CloudFront for caching, Global Accelerator for routing, placement for intra-cluster latency.
Validate safely
Test in staging, change one thing at a time, and compare identical tests before and after.
Final takeaway
Optimization is iterative: choose the right metric, pick targeted fixes, and prove success with repeatable measurements.
