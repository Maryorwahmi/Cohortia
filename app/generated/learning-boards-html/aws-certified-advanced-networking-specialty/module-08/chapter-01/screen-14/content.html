Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Production Checklist
Pre-change
  • Capture baseline CloudWatch and iperf3 results under representative load.
  • Confirm staging mirrors production networking and security settings.
  • Plan a single-variable change and document expected impact.
During change
  • Monitor dashboards and alarms continuously.
  • Keep rollback steps ready and enforce a staged rollout.
  • Verify end-to-end MTU and compatibility if altering frame sizes.
Key Takeaway

A disciplined, measured approach prevents accidental regressions and proves the value of optimizations before wider deployment.
