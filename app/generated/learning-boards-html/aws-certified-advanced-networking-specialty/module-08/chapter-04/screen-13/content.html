Key Concepts

Essential terms for high-performance data transfer

S3 Transfer Acceleration

An S3 feature that speeds up data transfers to and from S3 buckets by routing traffic through CloudFront's Edge Locations over the AWS global network.

CloudFront Distribution

The configuration that defines how CloudFront delivers your content, including origins, caching behaviors, and security settings.

Origin Server

The source of your content (e.g., S3 bucket, EC2 instance, ALB) that CloudFront retrieves content from when it's not in the cache.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering S3 Transfer Acceleration and CloudFront Distributions, along with understanding Origin Servers, forms the bedrock of high-performance data transfer and content delivery architectures.
