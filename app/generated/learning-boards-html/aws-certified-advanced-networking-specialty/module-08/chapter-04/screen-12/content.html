Pitfalls and Cost Optimization

Best practices for CloudFront and S3 Transfer Acceleration

Common CloudFront Mistake

Applying a single, generic caching policy to all content, leading to stale dynamic content or inefficient static content delivery.

Common S3 TA Mistake

Enabling Transfer Acceleration without testing its actual performance benefits for your specific workload, potentially incurring unnecessary costs.

Key Takeaway

Fine-tuning caching behaviors and validating performance gains are crucial for cost-effective and high-performing content delivery and data transfer.

CloudFront Cost Drivers
  • Data transfer out from Edge Locations.
  • Number of HTTP/HTTPS requests.
  • Invalidation requests.
  • Field-level encryption requests.
S3 Transfer Acceleration Cost Drivers
  • Additional charge per gigabyte transferred via acceleration.
  • Standard S3 storage and request costs still apply.
