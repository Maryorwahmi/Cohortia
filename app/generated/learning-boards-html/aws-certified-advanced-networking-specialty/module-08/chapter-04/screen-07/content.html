Content Delivery Network

CloudFront for Static Content

Caching at the Edge for low-latency delivery

What is Static Content?

Files that do not change frequently, such as images, videos, CSS, and JavaScript files. These are ideal for caching.

How Edge Caching Works

CloudFront stores copies of static content at its global Edge Locations. When a user requests content, it's served from the nearest cache.

Benefits

Drastically reduced latency, faster load times for end-users, improved user experience, and reduced load on your origin servers.

Key Takeaway

CloudFront's strength in static content delivery comes from its global network of Edge Locations, ensuring content is always close to your users.
