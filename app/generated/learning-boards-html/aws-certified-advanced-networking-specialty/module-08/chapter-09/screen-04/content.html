Understanding Primary AWS Networking Cost Drivers

Identifying where your network spend accumulates is the first step towards effective optimization.

Key Insight

AWS networking costs are primarily driven by data transfer, dedicated connectivity services like Direct Connect, and centralized routing hubs such as Transit Gateway.

Data Transfer (Egress)

Data leaving AWS, between regions, or sometimes between AZs, incurs significant charges. This is often the largest and most unexpected cost.

AWS Direct Connect

Dedicated network connections incur hourly port charges, data transfer out fees, and Direct Connect Gateway charges for complex setups.

AWS Transit Gateway

Centralized network hub incurs hourly charges per attachment and data processing fees for traffic passing through it.
