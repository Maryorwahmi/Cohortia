Strategic Cost Management for AWS Direct Connect

Right-sizing ports and leveraging Direct Connect Gateway for efficiency.

Cost Components

Direct Connect costs include hourly port charges, data transfer out, and Direct Connect Gateway charges for multi-account/region setups.

  • •Right-Size Ports: Provision port speeds (e.g., 1 Gbps vs. 10 Gbps) based on actual bandwidth utilization to avoid unnecessary hourly charges.
  • •Direct Connect Gateway: Consolidate connections for multiple VPCs/accounts/regions to share a single DX connection, reducing physical port needs.
  • •TCO Comparison: Always compare the total cost of ownership for Direct Connect versus Site-to-Site VPN for your specific traffic volumes.
Key Takeaway

Regularly review Direct Connect utilization and leverage Direct Connect Gateway to ensure your dedicated connectivity remains cost-optimized.

Direct Connect vs. Site-to-Site VPN

Direct Connect

  • •Dedicated, consistent performance.
  • •Higher fixed costs (port charges).
  • •Cost-effective for high data volumes.

Site-to-Site VPN

  • •Internet-based, variable performance.
  • •Lower fixed costs (VPN connection fees).
  • •Cost-effective for lower data volumes.
