Identify Primary Cost Drivers

Learn to pinpoint the main sources of expenditure in AWS networking, including data transfer, Direct Connect, and Transit Gateway. This foundational understanding is key to effective cost control.

Minimize Data Transfer Costs

Implement strategies to significantly reduce egress data transfer charges by utilizing private connectivity options and content delivery networks. This directly impacts your monthly AWS bill.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Optimize Direct Connect & TGW

Evaluate and apply cost-effective configurations for AWS Direct Connect and Transit Gateway deployments. This includes right-sizing and consolidating connections.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Utilize AWS Cost Management Tools

Learn to effectively use AWS Cost Explorer and other tools to monitor, analyze, and continuously optimize your networking expenses. Data-driven decisions are key.
