Learning Objectives
Master the diagnostic signals, network checks, and hands-on recovery steps for ALBs and NLBs.
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify symptoms
Recognize 4xx, 5xx, timeouts, and slowness patterns and what they imply for LB vs backend faults.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use CloudWatch & logs
Interpret HTTPCode_ELB_5XX, TargetConnectionErrorCount, HealthyHostCount and query access logs for per-request detail.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Resolve health checks
Verify instance service, SGs, NACLs, and health path/port to restore healthy targets and routing.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Network path checks
Inspect load balancer SGs, NACLs, routes, DNS, and enable VPC Flow Logs to find blocked paths.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
