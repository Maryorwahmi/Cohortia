Module 10 › Chapter 10.9 › Quick Reminders
Correlate metrics
Don’t act on a single metric spike; look for matching signals across related metrics.
Confirm with logs
Use access logs and health status details to identify which component returned errors.
Validate network path
Check SGs, NACLs, routes, DNS, and VPC Flow Logs before blaming the application.
One-line takeaway

Evidence-first troubleshooting — correlate, confirm, validate — reduces downtime and leads to accurate remediation.
