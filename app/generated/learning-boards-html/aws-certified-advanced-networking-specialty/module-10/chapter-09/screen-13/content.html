Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them
Recognize recurring pitfalls and apply corrective practices that leave systems healthier after the fix.
Security group mismatch: targets must allow LB source; avoid opening wide CIDR ranges unnecessarily.
Wrong health check path/port: ensure the health endpoint returns expected codes and is reachable.
Idle timeout too low: long-running requests may need higher idle timeout to avoid 504s.
DNS caching: stale DNS can point to retired load balancers; validate resolution.
Avoid quick parameter changes as the first response. Gather logs, metrics, and connectivity evidence so your remediation addresses the true root cause, not just the symptom.
Mini checklist
  • Confirm SG and NACL rules before changing health checks.
  • Capture CloudWatch and access logs prior to remediation.
  • Check application resource metrics to rule out overload.
