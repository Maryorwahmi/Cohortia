Cost Efficiency

Practical Strategies for ELB Cost Optimization

Reducing operational expenses without compromising performance.

Why Optimize?

ELBs are foundational but can contribute significantly to cloud spend. Proactive optimization ensures cost-effectiveness.

Impact on Budget

Even small optimizations across multiple load balancers can lead to substantial savings over time.

Continuous Process

Cost optimization is not a one-time task but an ongoing effort requiring regular review and adjustment.

Optimization Tips

Right-size your ELB

Choose ALB or NLB based on actual application needs; avoid over-provisioning.

Consolidate ALBs

Use host-based/path-based routing to serve multiple apps from a single ALB.

Optimize application performance

Reduce latency and connection duration to lower LCU consumption.

Clean up unused resources

Regularly delete unused load balancers and target groups.

Key Takeaway

Effective ELB cost optimization involves selecting the right load balancer type, consolidating resources, tuning application performance, and diligent cleanup of unused components.
