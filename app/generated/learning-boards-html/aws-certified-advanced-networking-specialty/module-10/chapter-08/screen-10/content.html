Cost Management

Decoding Load Balancer Capacity Units (LCUs)

The primary cost driver for ALBs and NLBs.

What are LCUs?

Load Balancer Capacity Units are a standardized measure of the resources consumed by your ALB or NLB, directly impacting your AWS bill.

Why they matter

Understanding LCU consumption is key to predicting and optimizing ELB costs, ensuring your architecture remains budget-friendly.

Monitoring LCUs

The ConsumedLCUs metric in CloudWatch provides real-time insight into your load balancer's resource usage and cost drivers.

LCU Components by ELB Type

ALB LCU Factors

  • New connections per second
  • Active connections
  • Processed bytes
  • Rule evaluations

NLB LCU Factors

  • New connections
  • Active connections
  • Processed bytes
Key Takeaway

LCUs are the core billing metric for ALBs and NLBs, calculated based on connection activity, processed data, and (for ALBs) rule evaluations, making LCU optimization key to cost control.
