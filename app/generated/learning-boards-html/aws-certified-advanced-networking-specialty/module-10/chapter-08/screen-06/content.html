Preparing for Bursts: Load Balancer Pre-warming

Proactively scaling ALBs for anticipated high traffic events.

What it is

A proactive measure where AWS Support pre-scales your Application Load Balancer (ALB) to a desired capacity before an expected traffic spike.

Why it's necessary (for ALBs)

ALBs scale automatically, but this takes time. Pre-warming prevents initial performance degradation, connection errors, or latency during sudden, massive traffic surges.

NLB vs. ALB

NLBs, operating at Layer 4, are inherently more efficient at handling traffic bursts and typically do not require pre-warming due to their architecture.

Pre-warming Process
1

Anticipate Traffic Spike

Identify events like product launches or marketing campaigns.

2

Contact AWS Support

Provide details: ALB ARN, expected traffic volume, start/end times.

3

AWS Pre-scales ALB

Ensures sufficient capacity is available from the outset.

4

Handle Traffic Seamlessly

ALB is ready for the burst, preventing performance issues.

Key Takeaway

Pre-warming is a critical proactive step for ALBs to ensure readiness for anticipated high-traffic events, maintaining performance and user satisfaction.
