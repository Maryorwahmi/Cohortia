Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter.

CloudWatch Metrics

Performance and operational data points published by ELBs to Amazon CloudWatch, used for monitoring and alarming.

Access Logs

Detailed logs of requests processed by ALBs and NLBs, stored in Amazon S3, useful for auditing, troubleshooting, and analytics.

Load Balancer Capacity Units (LCUs)

A billing metric for ALBs and NLBs, based on new connections, active connections, processed bytes, and (for ALB) rule evaluations.

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging CloudWatch metrics and access logs for monitoring, alongside understanding and optimizing LCU consumption, are vital for cost-effective and high-performing ELB deployments.
