Weighted Target Groups: Blue/Green & A/B Testing

Distributing traffic proportionally across multiple target groups for controlled deployments.

What are Weighted Target Groups?

A feature allowing traffic to be distributed among multiple target groups based on specified weights within a single listener rule.

Blue/Green Deployments

  • Gradually shift traffic from 'blue' (old version) to 'green' (new version).
  • Minimizes risk and enables instant rollback by adjusting weights.

A/B Testing

Direct a small percentage of users to a new feature or design to compare performance against the existing version.

Key Takeaway

Weighted target groups are crucial for controlled, low-risk deployments and experimentation, enabling gradual rollouts and A/B testing.
