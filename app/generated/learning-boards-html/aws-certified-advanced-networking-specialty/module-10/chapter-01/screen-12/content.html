ELB Feature Comparison

ALB vs. NLB vs. GWLB

Key Differentiators
  • ALB: Layer 7, HTTP/HTTPS, content-based routing, microservices.
  • NLB: Layer 4, TCP/UDP/TLS, extreme performance, static IPs, high throughput.
  • GWLB: Layer 3/4, network appliances, GENEVE protocol, service chaining.
Key Takeaway

Choosing the right ELB depends on your traffic type, performance needs, routing requirements, and whether you're managing network appliances.
