Key Concepts: NLB Advanced Features

Expanding your NLB knowledge

TCP, UDP, TLS Listeners

NLB supports various protocols, including Layer 4 TLS termination and mutual TLS for client authentication.

Transit Gateway Integration

Register TGW ENIs as NLB targets for centralized Layer 4 load balancing across multiple VPCs.

Flow-Based Routing

NLB establishes direct client-to-target connections, minimizing latency and maximizing throughput.

Summary Takeaway

NLB's flexible listener types, Transit Gateway integration, and flow-based routing provide robust solutions for high-performance and complex networking challenges.
