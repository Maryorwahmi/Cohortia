NLB Listener Types

TCP, UDP, and TLS Protocols

  • TCP Listeners: Forwards general-purpose TCP connections directly to targets. Ideal for most web services and databases.
  • UDP Listeners: Forwards UDP datagrams to targets. Essential for DNS, streaming, and gaming protocols where low latency is key.
  • TLS Listeners: Terminates TLS connections at Layer 4, offloading encryption. Supports mutual TLS (mTLS) for client authentication.
Key Takeaway

NLB's support for TCP, UDP, and TLS listeners provides flexibility to handle diverse application protocols, including Layer 4 TLS termination and mTLS.
