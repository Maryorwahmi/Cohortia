Common Mistakes

Pitfalls to Avoid for Optimal Performance

GA vs. Route 53 Confusion

Global Accelerator optimizes traffic path *after* DNS resolution, while Route 53 influences *where* DNS resolves. They are complementary, not interchangeable.

Missing Health Checks

Failing to configure robust health checks can lead to Global Accelerator directing traffic to unhealthy application instances, degrading user experience.

Ignoring Cost Implications

Global Accelerator incurs charges for data transfer and fixed hourly rates. Monitor usage to avoid unexpected costs.

Custom Routing Misconfiguration

For custom routing, ensure EC2 instances are configured to listen on the specific private IP addresses and ports mapped by the accelerator.

Key Takeaway

Properly understanding Global Accelerator's role, configuring robust health checks, and managing costs are essential for a successful and efficient deployment.
