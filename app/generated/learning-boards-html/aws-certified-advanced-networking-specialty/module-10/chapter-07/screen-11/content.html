Shield Advanced Benefits

Financial Safeguards and Integrated Security

Shield Advanced provides cost protection against DDoS-related scaling charges and integrates seamlessly with AWS WAF for a unified defense.

Visibility & Diagnostics

Detailed attack diagnostics and metrics are available through CloudWatch, offering insights into attack patterns and mitigation effectiveness.

Key Takeaway

The financial protection and deep integration with WAF make Shield Advanced a comprehensive and economically sound choice for critical application security.

Cost Protection

Protects against scaling charges incurred on protected resources due to a DDoS attack (e.g., data transfer out).

Integration with WAF

Works in conjunction with WAF; Shield Advanced can automatically create WAF rules in response to detected DDoS events.

Important Note

While Shield Advanced protects against scaling costs, it does have its own monthly fee. Understand the cost model before enrollment.
