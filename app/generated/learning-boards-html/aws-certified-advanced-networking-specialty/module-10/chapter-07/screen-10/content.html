Shield Advanced Features

Proactive Defense and Expert Support

Shield Advanced offers advanced detection, automatic mitigations, and direct access to the AWS DDoS Response Team for comprehensive protection.

DDoS Response Team (DRT)

Direct, 24/7 access to AWS DDoS experts who can assist with attack analysis and custom mitigation strategies during sophisticated attacks.

Key Takeaway

The combination of automated, multi-layer protection and human expert intervention makes Shield Advanced an unparalleled solution for critical infrastructure.

Higher-Level Protection

Advanced detection and mitigation for larger, more sophisticated DDoS attacks across Layer 3, 4, and 7.

Always-On Detection

Automatic detection and inline mitigations are applied without manual intervention, ensuring rapid response.

Real-world Impact

During a major DDoS event, the DRT can provide crucial insights and help fine-tune mitigations, significantly reducing downtime and impact on your business operations.
