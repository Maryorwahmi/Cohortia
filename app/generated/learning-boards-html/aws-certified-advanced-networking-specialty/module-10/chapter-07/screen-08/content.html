Strategic WAF Deployment Approaches

Effective WAF deployment involves a combination of managed rules, custom logic, and rate limiting to create a comprehensive defense.

Testing is Crucial

Always test new WAF rules in COUNT mode first to monitor their impact and avoid blocking legitimate traffic (false positives) before setting them to BLOCK.

Key Takeaway

A multi-faceted WAF strategy, combining different rule types and careful testing, provides the most robust and adaptive protection for your web applications.

1. Baseline Protection

Start with AWS Managed Rule Groups for immediate defense against common exploits.

2. IP Whitelisting/Blacklisting

Use IP sets to control access, blocking known bad actors or allowing trusted sources.

3. Custom Rules

Add rules specific to your application's unique vulnerabilities or business logic.

4. Rate Limiting

Implement rate-based rules for effective Layer 7 DDoS mitigation.
