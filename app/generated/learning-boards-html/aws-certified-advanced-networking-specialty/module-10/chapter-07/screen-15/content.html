Reinforcing Your Knowledge

Key services and features to remember.

Rate-Based Rule

A WAF rule that blocks or counts requests from an IP if the rate exceeds a threshold within 5 minutes, for DDoS mitigation.

AWS Shield Standard

Automatic, always-on DDoS protection for all AWS customers, defending against common network and transport layer attacks.

AWS Shield Advanced

A paid service offering enhanced DDoS protection for specific AWS resources, including higher-level attack mitigation and DRT access.

Summary Takeaway

AWS WAF and Shield Advanced provide a powerful, multi-layered defense against web exploits and DDoS attacks, ensuring the availability and security of your critical applications.
