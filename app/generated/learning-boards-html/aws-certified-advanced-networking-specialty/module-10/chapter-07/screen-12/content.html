Multi-Layered Defense

Building a Unified Security Posture

Integrating WAF and Shield Advanced with your Load Balancers creates a powerful, multi-layered defense against diverse threats.

Safety Note

Misconfigured WAF rules can block legitimate traffic. Always test thoroughly in a staging environment and use COUNT mode initially in production.

Key Takeaway

A unified strategy combining WAF's application-layer filtering with Shield Advanced's comprehensive DDoS protection is essential for advanced networking security.

Step 1

Protect your ALB with WAF

Create a Web ACL, associate it with your ALB, and add AWS Managed Rule Groups, custom rules, and rate-based rules. Test rules in COUNT mode first.

Step 2

Protect your ALB with Shield Advanced

Enroll your account in Shield Advanced and explicitly protect your ALB (and other critical resources). This provides the highest level of DDoS protection.
