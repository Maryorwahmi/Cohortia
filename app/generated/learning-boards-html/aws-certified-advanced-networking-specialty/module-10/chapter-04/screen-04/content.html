Core Concept

GWLB: The Transparent Appliance Gateway

Solving the challenge of service chaining for virtual network appliances.

Service Chaining: Directs traffic through a sequence of network services (e.g., firewalls, IDPS) for inspection.

Transparent Gateway: GWLB acts as a 'bump in the wire,' forwarding traffic to appliances without altering original packet headers.

Scalability & Management: Simplifies scaling and managing fleets of third-party security appliances.

Key Takeaway

GWLB is purpose-built for transparent service chaining, enabling scalable and manageable integration of virtual network appliances into your traffic flow.

Visual Concept

Traditional vs. GWLB Appliance Integration

Traditional Method: Complex Routing

  • Manual route table adjustments
  • Source/Destination NAT often required
  • Difficult to scale and ensure high availability
  • Can alter original packet headers

GWLB Method: Transparent Service Chaining

  • Traffic routed to GWLB Endpoint
  • GWLB forwards to appliance fleet
  • GENEVE protocol preserves original packet headers
  • Automatic scaling and high availability
Example Scenario

Centralized Firewall Inspection

Imagine you need all outbound internet traffic from your application VPC to pass through a fleet of FortiGate firewalls for deep packet inspection. Instead of complex routing rules in each subnet, GWLB allows you to simply route traffic to a GWLB Endpoint, which then transparently steers it through your firewall fleet in a dedicated security VPC. The firewalls inspect the traffic and send it back, and GWLB forwards it to the internet, all without the application knowing the firewalls were in the path.
