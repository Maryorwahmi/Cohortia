Outbound Traffic Inspection with GWLB

Controlling and securing traffic from your VPC to the internet.

Traffic Source: Traffic originating from application subnets, destined for the internet.

Route Table Modification: Application subnet route tables send `0.0.0.0/0` to the GWLB Endpoint.

Inspection Flow: Traffic goes App Subnet → GWLBE → GWLB Service → Appliances → GWLB Service → NAT/IGW.

Key Takeaway

Outbound traffic inspection with GWLB provides granular control and security for all traffic leaving your application VPCs, preventing data exfiltration.

Outbound Traffic Flow

Application VPC to Internet

NAT Gateway / Internet Gateway
Scenario

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

A company needs to ensure no sensitive data leaves their VPC. By routing all outbound traffic through a GWLB to a fleet of DLP appliances, they can inspect and block unauthorized data transfers, protecting intellectual property and complying with regulatory requirements.
