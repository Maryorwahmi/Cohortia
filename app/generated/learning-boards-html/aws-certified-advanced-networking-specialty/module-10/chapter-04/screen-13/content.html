Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Gateway Load Balancer (GWLB)

A specialized load balancer for transparently deploying, scaling, and managing virtual network appliances.

Service Chaining

The process of directing network traffic through a sequence of network services or appliances for inspection.

GENEVE Protocol

A tunneling protocol used by GWLB to encapsulate original IP packets, preserving headers for transparent inspection.

Summary Takeaway

GWLB revolutionizes network security by enabling transparent, scalable, and highly available integration of virtual appliances through the GENEVE protocol, simplifying complex traffic inspection challenges.
