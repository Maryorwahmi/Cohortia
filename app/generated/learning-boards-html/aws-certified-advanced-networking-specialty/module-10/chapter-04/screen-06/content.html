Component Breakdown

GWLB Service & Virtual Appliances

Understanding the core components that perform traffic inspection.

GWLB Service: The central point that receives GENEVE-encapsulated traffic and forwards it to virtual appliances.

Target Group: Contains your fleet of virtual network appliances (e.g., EC2 instances running firewalls, IDPS).

Health Checks: GWLB monitors appliance health, routing traffic only to healthy instances for high availability.

Key Takeaway

The GWLB service, with its target groups of virtual appliances, provides a scalable and highly available platform for centralized network inspection.

Pro-Tip

Appliance Security Groups

Ensure your virtual appliance EC2 instances have security groups configured to allow inbound and outbound UDP traffic on port 6081 (for GENEVE) from the GWLB's subnets. Without this, traffic will be dropped, and your inspection will fail.
