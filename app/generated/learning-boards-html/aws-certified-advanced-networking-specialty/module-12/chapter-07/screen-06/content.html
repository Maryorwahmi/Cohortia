Analyzing Flow Logs with CloudWatch Logs Insights

Use CloudWatch Logs Insights to pinpoint traffic rejections.

Why Logs Insights?

Provides a powerful, SQL-like query language to efficiently search and analyze large volumes of VPC Flow Log data, making root cause analysis faster.

Key Filters

Filter by `action = 'REJECT'`, source/destination IP (`srcaddr`, `dstaddr`), and destination port (`dstport`) to quickly identify blocked traffic flows.

Key Takeaway

Interpreting `REJECT` actions alongside `log_status` (e.g., `NODATA` vs. `OK`) helps differentiate between NACL and Security Group blocks, guiding precise remediation.

CloudWatch Logs Insights Query Example
filter (srcaddr = 'WEB_SERVER_PRIVATE_IP' and dstaddr = 'DB_PRIVATE_IP' and dstport = 3306) or (srcaddr = 'DB_PRIVATE_IP' and dstaddr = 'WEB_SERVER_PRIVATE_IP' and srcport = 3306) | parse @message "* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * *" as version, account_id, interface_id, srcaddr, dstaddr, srcport, dstport, protocol, packets, bytes, start, end, action, log_status, vpc_id, subnet_id, instance_id, tcp_flags, type, pkt_srcaddr, pkt_dstaddr, flow_direction, traffic_path | sort @timestamp desc | limit 100
Common Mistake

Misinterpreting `log_status`. `NODATA` often means traffic was blocked before reaching the ENI (e.g., NACL), while `REJECT` with `OK` or `SKIP` points to the ENI's Security Group.
