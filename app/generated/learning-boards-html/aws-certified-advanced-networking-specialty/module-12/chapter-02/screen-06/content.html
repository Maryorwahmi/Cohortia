Designing Resilient Direct Connect Solutions

Ensuring Redundancy and Optimal Throughput

Multiple Connections

Provision more than one Direct Connect connection to create redundant paths for your traffic.

Diverse Locations

Connect to different AWS Direct Connect locations to protect against regional facility failures.

Common Mistake

Relying on a single Direct Connect connection creates a single point of failure, leading to outages if the link or associated equipment fails.

Different Customer Routers

Terminate connections on separate customer routers for device-level redundancy within your data center.

Virtual Interfaces (VIFs)

Establish VIFs to connect to public AWS services, private VPCs, or a Transit Gateway for flexible routing.

Key Takeaway

For robust Direct Connect, implement multiple connections across diverse locations and customer routers, utilizing VIFs for flexible and resilient routing.
