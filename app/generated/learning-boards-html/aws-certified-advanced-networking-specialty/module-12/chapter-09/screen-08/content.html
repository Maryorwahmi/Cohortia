Eliminating Distractors and Comparing Options

Refining your choice to the 'best' solution

Elimination Criteria

  • •Technically Impossible: Solutions that simply cannot work in AWS.
  • •Fails Constraint: Options that don't meet a key requirement (e.g., cost, availability).
  • •Irrelevant/Distractor: Technically correct but doesn't solve the specific problem.

Comparison Factors

  • •Scalability: Can the solution grow with demand?
  • •Cost: Is it the most economical choice given constraints?
  • •Operational Overhead: How complex is it to manage and maintain?
  • •Security: Does it meet the required security posture?

Example: A solution might be technically sound but fail if it doesn't meet the 'lowest latency' requirement, making it an incorrect choice for that specific question.

Key Takeaway

Always evaluate remaining options against ALL question requirements and beware of distractors that are technically correct but not the 'best' solution.
