Scenario Walkthrough: Question Analysis

Applying the systematic strategy to a complex problem

Question:

A large enterprise needs to connect its on-premises data center to over 100 AWS VPCs across multiple AWS accounts and Regions. They require a highly scalable, secure, and manageable solution that minimizes operational overhead. The on-premises network has a dedicated 10 Gbps Direct Connect connection to us-east-1. What is the MOST appropriate networking architecture for this requirement?

Full Scenario

Last Sentence First

'MOST appropriate networking architecture' - seeking optimal solution.

Keyword: Scale

'Large enterprise', 'over 100 AWS VPCs', 'multiple AWS accounts and Regions'.

Keyword: Requirements

'Highly scalable, secure, and manageable', 'minimizes operational overhead'.

Constraint: Existing Infra

'Dedicated 10 Gbps Direct Connect connection to us-east-1'.

Example: The phrase 'MOST appropriate' signals that multiple options might be plausible, but only one is optimal, requiring careful evaluation.

Key Takeaway

Thoroughly deconstructing the question by identifying its core objective, keywords, and constraints is fundamental to selecting the correct and optimal solution.
