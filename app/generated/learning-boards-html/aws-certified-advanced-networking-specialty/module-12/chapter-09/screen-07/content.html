Identifying Keywords and Constraints

Unlocking the true intent of the question

Keywords and constraints are critical elements in exam questions that define the problem's scope and requirements. They guide you to the most appropriate solution by specifying criteria like cost, performance, or security.

  • •Look for comparative terms: 'most cost-effective', 'highest availability', 'lowest latency'.
  • •Identify architectural requirements: 'hybrid cloud', 'multi-Region', 'centralized'.
  • •Note specific service mentions or existing infrastructure.
Key Takeaway

Meticulously identifying keywords and constraints is paramount; they are the explicit rules that determine the 'best' answer for any given scenario.

Example: If a question asks for the 'most cost-effective' solution, a technically superior but expensive option might be incorrect, even if it works.
