Navigating Common Exam Traps

Recognizing and sidestepping typical mistakes

Lift and Shift Mentality

Don't assume on-premises solutions directly translate to AWS. Embrace cloud-native services.

Over-engineering

Sometimes a simpler solution is the correct one, especially if extreme requirements aren't specified.

Ignoring Cost Constraints

If 'most cost-effective' is a keyword, an expensive but technically superior option might be wrong.

Single Points of Failure

Always look for solutions that address high availability and fault tolerance in critical scenarios.

Reading Too Quickly

Missing a single keyword like 'stateless' vs. 'stateful' can lead to choosing the wrong firewall type.

Example: Assuming an on-premises firewall configuration directly translates to AWS without considering Network Firewall or WAF is a classic 'lift and shift' trap.

Key Takeaway

Cultivate a cloud-native mindset and pay close attention to all details in the question to avoid common pitfalls and select the optimal AWS solution.
