Understanding the ANS-C01 Exam

Format, Duration, and Core Challenge

The AWS Certified Advanced Networking – Specialty exam (ANS-C01) is a challenging test validating expertise in designing and implementing complex AWS and hybrid network architectures. It requires understanding service interactions, trade-offs, and application to real-world scenarios.

  • •Typically 65 multiple-choice or multiple-response questions.
  • •170 minutes to complete, averaging 2.6 minutes per question.
  • •Focuses on applying knowledge to ambiguous, scenario-based problems.
Key Takeaway

Efficient time management and a deep understanding of service application are paramount for success in this challenging exam.

Example: Real-world scenarios often require quick, informed decisions, mirroring the exam's time pressure. Practicing under timed conditions helps build this crucial skill.
