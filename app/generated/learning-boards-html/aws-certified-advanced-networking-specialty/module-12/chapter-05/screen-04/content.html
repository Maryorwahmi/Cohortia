Scalability vs. Elasticity

Growing and Adapting Your AWS Network

Scalability: Handling More Work

Scalability refers to the ability of a system to handle a growing amount of work by adding resources. This can involve increasing the capacity of existing resources (vertical scaling) or adding more instances of resources (horizontal scaling).

  • Accommodates Growth: Designed to support increased load over time.
  • Resource Addition: Involves adding more compute, storage, or network capacity.
  • Planned or Reactive: Can be a planned upgrade or a response to sustained growth.

Elasticity: Adapting to Change

Elasticity is the ability of a system to automatically scale resources up or down in response to changes in demand. It's about dynamic adjustment, not just growth.

  • Automatic Adjustment: Resources are provisioned and de-provisioned automatically.
  • Cost Optimization: Pay only for the resources you actively use.
  • Demand-Driven: Responds to real-time fluctuations in traffic or workload.

Scalability: Growth by Adding Resources

Focuses on increasing capacity to handle more work, either vertically (larger instance) or horizontally (more instances).

Elasticity: Automatic Resource Adjustment

Involves automatically scaling resources up or down in real-time based on demand, optimizing performance and cost.

Key Takeaway

Scalability ensures your system can handle more work, while elasticity ensures it can adapt dynamically and cost-effectively to fluctuating demand.
