Essential Concepts for Scalable Networks

Reinforcing Your Understanding

Scalability

The ability of a system to handle a growing amount of work by adding resources, either vertically or horizontally.

Elasticity

The ability of a system to automatically scale resources up or down in response to changes in demand, optimizing performance and cost.

CIDR Block Planning

Strategic allocation of IP address ranges for VPCs and subnets to accommodate future growth and prevent IP exhaustion.

Summary Takeaway

Scalability, elasticity, and careful CIDR planning are fundamental for building robust, adaptable, and future-proof AWS network architectures.
