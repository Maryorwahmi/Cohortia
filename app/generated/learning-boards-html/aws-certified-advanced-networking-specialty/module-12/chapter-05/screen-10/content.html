What is an AZ?

An Availability Zone is a physically isolated location within an AWS Region, designed to be independent of other AZs, providing fault isolation.

Why Multi-AZ?

Deploying across multiple AZs protects against single points of failure, enhances fault tolerance, and provides a robust foundation for disaster recovery strategies.

Scaling Benefit

Multi-AZ deployment provides a larger pool of resources for Auto Scaling Groups to launch instances, ensuring balanced capacity and continued resilience during scaling events.

Key Takeaway

Multi-AZ architectures are fundamental for building highly available, fault-tolerant, and efficiently scalable applications in AWS, protecting against regional failures.
