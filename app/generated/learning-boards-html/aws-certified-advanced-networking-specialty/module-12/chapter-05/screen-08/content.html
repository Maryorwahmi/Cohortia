What is an ASG?

An Auto Scaling Group automatically adjusts the number of EC2 instances in response to defined metrics, ensuring your application has the right capacity.

Scaling Policies

ASGs use dynamic scaling policies (e.g., target tracking, step scaling) or scheduled scaling to automatically launch or terminate instances based on demand.

Desired Capacity

You define a minimum, maximum, and desired number of instances. The ASG works to maintain the desired capacity, scaling within the min/max range.

Key Takeaway

ASGs provide automated capacity management, ensuring your application can handle fluctuating demand while optimizing resource utilization and cost.
