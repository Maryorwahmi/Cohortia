Strategic CIDR Block Planning

Ensuring Room for Future Growth in Your VPC

VPC Network Layout
VPC (e.g., 10.0.0.0/16)
Public Subnet 1/24 (251 IPs)
Private Subnet 1/22 (1019 IPs)
Private Subnet 2/20 (4091 IPs)
Why /16 for VPC?

A /16 CIDR block provides 65,536 IP addresses, offering extensive room for growth. The primary VPC CIDR cannot be reduced after creation, making generous initial allocation crucial.

Why /20 or /22 for Subnets?

These larger subnet CIDRs (e.g., /22 for 1024 IPs, /20 for 4096 IPs) ensure individual subnets have enough IP addresses for many instances and services, even with 5 reserved IPs per subnet.

Common Mistake: Small CIDRs

Allocating overly small CIDR blocks (e.g., /24 or /25) for private subnets without considering future expansion leads to rapid IP exhaustion and complex re-architecting.

Key Takeaway

Plan your VPC and subnet CIDR blocks generously from the start to avoid IP exhaustion and ensure long-term network scalability.
