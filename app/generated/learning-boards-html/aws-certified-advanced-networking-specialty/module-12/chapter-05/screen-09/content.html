Synergistic Design

ELB handles intelligent traffic distribution, while ASG dynamically manages the underlying EC2 instance capacity, creating a complete elastic solution.

Multi-AZ Resilience

Both ELB and ASG are designed to operate across multiple Availability Zones, distributing load and instances to prevent single points of failure and enhance fault tolerance.

Example Scenario

An ALB distributes traffic to an ASG of EC2 instances across three private subnets, ensuring that as demand increases, new instances are launched into available capacity across different failure domains.

Key Takeaway

The combination of ELB and ASG is the cornerstone for building highly available, fault-tolerant, and automatically scaling applications in AWS.
