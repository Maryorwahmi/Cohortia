Advanced Routing and Transit Gateway

Centralizing and simplifying complex network topologies

Transit Gateway (TGW) Benefits

  • •Centralized Routing: Connects thousands of VPCs, VPNs, and DX.
  • •Simplified Management: Reduces operational overhead compared to peering meshes.
  • •Transitive Routing: Enables traffic flow between any attached network.

VPC Peering

  • •Direct VPC-to-VPC: For simpler, non-transitive connections.
  • •Limited Scale: Becomes complex with many VPCs.

Example: Hub-and-Spoke with TGW

Connect all departmental VPCs (spokes) to a central Transit Gateway (hub), then route all internet-bound traffic through a dedicated inspection VPC attached to the TGW.

TGW: The Central Hub

Key Takeaway

Transit Gateway is essential for scalable, centralized routing in complex multi-VPC and hybrid cloud environments.
