Key Concepts: Operations & Growth

Tools for efficiency and your future in AWS networking

Monitoring & Troubleshooting

Utilize VPC Flow Logs, CloudWatch, and Reachability Analyzer for network visibility and issue resolution.

Automation & IaC

Embrace CloudFormation/CDK and event-driven automation (Lambda, EventBridge) for consistent deployments.

Continuous Learning

Stay updated with new services, whitepapers, and hands-on experimentation to maintain expertise.

Certification Path

Leverage this certification as a milestone for advanced roles and explore further AWS specialty certifications.

Summary Takeaway

Operational tools and a commitment to continuous learning are vital for long-term success and career advancement in AWS networking.
