Hybrid Connectivity Solutions

Bridging your on-premises network with AWS

AWS Site-to-Site VPN

  • •Secure Internet-based: Encrypted tunnels over the public internet.
  • •Cost-effective: Good for initial connections or lower bandwidth needs.
  • •Redundancy: Multiple VPN tunnels for failover.

AWS Direct Connect (DX)

  • •Dedicated Private Link: Consistent, high-bandwidth, low-latency.
  • •Mission-critical: Ideal for large data transfers and sensitive applications.
  • •Redundancy: Multiple DX connections across different locations.

Supporting Services

  • •Direct Connect Gateway (DXGW): Aggregates DX connections, connects to TGWs.
  • •Transit Gateway (TGW): Centralizes routing for complex hybrid architectures.

Comparison: VPN vs. Direct Connect

VPN
  • •Internet-based
  • •Lower cost
  • •Variable performance
Direct Connect
  • •Private network
  • •Higher cost
  • •Consistent performance
Key Takeaway

Choose VPN for flexibility and cost-efficiency, Direct Connect for performance and dedicated links, always prioritizing redundancy.

Common Pitfall

Underestimating the need for redundant connections. A single point of failure can disrupt critical hybrid operations.
