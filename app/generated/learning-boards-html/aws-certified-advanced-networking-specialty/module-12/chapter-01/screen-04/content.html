Core Concept

Understanding High Availability and Fault Tolerance

Ensuring continuous operation and resilience against failures.

High Availability (HA)

The ability of a system to operate continuously without failure for a long time, minimizing downtime.

Fault Tolerance (FT)

The ability of a system to continue operating without interruption when one or more of its components fail.

Redundancy

The core principle of duplicating critical components or functions to eliminate single points of failure.
