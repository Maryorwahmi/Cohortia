Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter.

High Availability (HA)

System's ability to operate continuously without failure for a long time, minimizing downtime.

Fault Tolerance

System's ability to continue operating without interruption when one or more components fail.

Multi-AZ Deployment

Distributing resources across multiple Availability Zones within a single AWS Region to protect against AZ-wide outages.

Summary Takeaway

Building resilient AWS networks starts with understanding HA and FT, and implementing Multi-AZ deployments as a foundational layer of defense against failures.
