Multi-Region Architecture for Disaster Recovery

Achieving true disaster recovery and business continuity.

  • •Deploys application and infrastructure in geographically separate AWS Regions.
  • •Protects against catastrophic events impacting an entire AWS Region.
  • •Essential for robust disaster recovery and business continuity.
  • •Requires careful planning for data synchronization and traffic routing.

Common Mistake

Assuming multi-Region deployment alone makes an application highly available. Without proper data synchronization, DNS failover, and application-level awareness, it might just be a cold standby with high RTO.

Key Takeaway

Multi-Region architectures provide resilience against catastrophic regional failures, ensuring business continuity through geographical separation.
