Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter.

Multi-Region Architecture

Deploying applications and infrastructure in geographically separate AWS Regions for disaster recovery and business continuity.

Cross-Account Networking

Connecting network resources across different AWS accounts, typically using AWS Transit Gateway or Shared VPCs.

Direct Connect Redundancy

Provisioning multiple Direct Connect connections to different AWS locations and customer routers to ensure continuous connectivity.

Summary Takeaway

Advanced resilience strategies like Multi-Region architectures, Cross-Account Networking, and Direct Connect redundancy are crucial for enterprise-grade, highly available, and fault-tolerant AWS solutions.
