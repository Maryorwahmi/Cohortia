Key Concepts to Remember

Advanced strategies for optimizing your AWS network spend.

AWS PrivateLink

Enables private connectivity to services, potentially reducing data transfer costs for inter-VPC communication.

Direct Connect Cost Optimization

Balancing port fees and lower data transfer rates against VPN costs for high-volume hybrid traffic.

AWS Cost Explorer

A powerful tool for visualizing, managing, and analyzing AWS costs to identify optimization opportunities.

Summary Takeaway

Strategic service choices, such as PrivateLink and Direct Connect, combined with continuous monitoring via Cost Explorer, are key to advanced networking cost optimization.
