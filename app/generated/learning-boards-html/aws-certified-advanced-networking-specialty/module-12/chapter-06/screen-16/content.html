Key Concepts to Remember

Essential terms for AWS networking cost optimization.

Egress Data Transfer

Data leaving AWS, which is typically charged and often the largest networking cost component.

Ingress Data Transfer

Data entering AWS, which is typically free and does not incur charges.

Amazon CloudFront

A CDN that reduces egress costs by caching content at edge locations closer to users.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering the distinction between egress and ingress, and leveraging services like CloudFront, is fundamental to controlling AWS networking costs.
