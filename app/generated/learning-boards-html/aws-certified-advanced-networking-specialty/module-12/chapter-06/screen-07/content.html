Cost-Effective Hybrid Cloud: DX vs. VPN

Choosing the right connectivity for your on-premises to AWS traffic based on cost and performance.

AWS Site-to-Site VPN

  • •Lower initial setup costs.
  • •Charges per VPN connection hour.
  • •Standard internet data transfer rates apply.
  • •Suitable for lower, inconsistent data volumes.

Example: Small branch office connectivity or disaster recovery for non-critical data.

AWS Direct Connect

  • •Higher upfront (port, cross-connect) and recurring fees.
  • •Significantly reduced data transfer rates.
  • •Dedicated network connection, improved performance.
  • •Cost-effective for high, consistent data volumes.

Example: Large-scale data migration or real-time hybrid applications.

Key Takeaway

Direct Connect offers lower data transfer rates for high, consistent volumes, outweighing VPN's lower setup costs over time.
