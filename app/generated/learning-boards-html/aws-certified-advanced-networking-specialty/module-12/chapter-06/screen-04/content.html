Core Concept

Identifying Primary Networking Cost Drivers

Understanding where your AWS networking spend originates is the first step to effective optimization.

Egress Data Transfer

Data leaving AWS is charged, with rates varying by destination (internet, other Regions, Direct Connect).

NAT Gateway Usage

Incurs hourly charges plus data processing fees for traffic from private subnets to the internet.

Transit Gateway & Direct Connect

Incur attachment/port fees and data processing charges, depending on configuration.

Key Takeaway

Egress data transfer is the most significant and variable networking cost, varying by destination and volume.
