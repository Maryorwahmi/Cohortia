Ensuring VPN High Availability

Configuring redundant tunnels and avoiding common single points of failure.

Built-in Redundancy

AWS automatically provisions two VPN tunnels to two distinct AWS VPN endpoints. This provides inherent redundancy on the AWS side.

Common Mistakes

  • Configuring only one VPN tunnel on the customer gateway.
  • Not properly advertising routes over both tunnels.
  • Leads to a single point of failure and potential outages.
Key Takeaway

Always configure and verify both VPN tunnels on your customer gateway to leverage AWS's built-in redundancy and ensure continuous hybrid connectivity.

Pro Tip

Regularly monitor the status of both VPN tunnels using AWS CloudWatch metrics. Implement alerts to notify you immediately if a tunnel goes down, allowing for proactive troubleshooting.
