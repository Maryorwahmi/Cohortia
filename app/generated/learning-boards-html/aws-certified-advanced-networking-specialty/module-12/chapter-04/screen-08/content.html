Building Resilient Direct Connect Architectures

Leveraging multiple connections and Direct Connect Gateway for enhanced reliability.

Achieving Maximum Resilience

  • Implement multiple Direct Connect connections.
  • Use different AWS Direct Connect locations.
  • Utilize diverse customer routers on-premises.
  • Ensures failover and continuous operation.

AWS Direct Connect Gateway

A global resource that allows your Direct Connect connections to connect to multiple VPCs in the same or different AWS Regions, or to a Transit Gateway.

Key Takeaway

For mission-critical workloads, combine multiple Direct Connect connections with Direct Connect Gateway to build highly resilient and scalable hybrid architectures.

Consideration

While Direct Connect offers superior performance, it requires careful planning and coordination with a Direct Connect partner, leading to a longer provisioning time compared to VPN.
