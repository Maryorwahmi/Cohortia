AWS Transit Gateway: The Central Network Hub

Simplifying complex network topologies with a hub-and-spoke model.

Centralized Network Hub

Transit Gateway acts as a central hub for all your network connections, including VPCs, VPNs, and Direct Connect Gateways.

Benefits of Transit Gateway

  • Simplifies routing and eliminates point-to-point connections.
  • Enables transitive routing between all connected networks.
  • Reduces operational overhead and improves network scalability.
Key Takeaway

Transit Gateway is essential for managing complex hybrid cloud environments, providing a scalable and simplified network architecture.

Analogy

Think of Transit Gateway as a central airport for all your network traffic. Instead of building direct runways between every city (VPC) and every other city, all planes (data) go through the central airport (TGW) to reach their destination.
