Operational Considerations for Edge Deployments

Understanding service availability and fault tolerance.

Key Takeaway

Always verify service availability and design for high availability across multiple edge zones or with the parent Region, as edge zones extend a single AZ and offer a subset of services.

Pro-Tip: Multi-Zone Strategy

For critical applications, distribute components across multiple Local Zones or between a Local Zone and its parent Region to enhance fault tolerance beyond a single edge location.

Subset of Services

Edge zones do not offer the full breadth of AWS services available in a parent Region. Always verify specific service availability.

Single AZ Extension

Each Local Zone or Wavelength Zone extends a single Availability Zone from its parent Region, impacting inherent fault tolerance.

High Availability Planning

For high availability, applications may need to span multiple edge zones or combine edge zones with the parent Region.
