AWS Local Zones: Extending Your Region

Bringing select AWS services to metropolitan areas.

Physical Proximity

Physically located closer to end-users in major cities.

Subset of Services

Primarily offers EC2 instances, EBS volumes, and VPC networking.

Logical Extension

Logically part of the parent AWS Region's VPC.

Key Takeaway

Local Zones extend your VPC to metropolitan areas, enabling ultra-low latency for applications serving local users by physically placing select AWS services closer to them.
