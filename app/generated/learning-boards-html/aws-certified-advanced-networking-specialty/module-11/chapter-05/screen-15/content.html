Chapter 11.5 Review

Key Concepts (Continued)

Architectural patterns and strategic advantages

Active/Passive Architecture

A DR strategy with a primary (active) region and a standby (passive) region for failover.

Active/Active Architecture

A high-availability strategy where multiple regions simultaneously serve traffic.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Strategies to recover operations after disruption, greatly enhanced by Global Accelerator's fast failover.

Summary Takeaway

Global Accelerator empowers robust multi-region architectures and significantly improves disaster recovery capabilities through its advanced traffic management.
