Strategic Advantage

Global Accelerator for Disaster Recovery

Faster, more reliable failover than traditional DNS

Traditional DNS Failover Challenges

  • Propagation Delays: DNS changes can take minutes to hours due to TTLs and caching.
  • Inconsistent Experience: Users may experience varied downtime depending on their DNS resolver.
  • Manual Intervention: Often requires manual updates during a crisis.
  • Higher RTO: Longer recovery times due to inherent DNS limitations.

Example: Regional Outage Scenario

A critical application's primary region (e.g., us-east-1) becomes unavailable. With Global Accelerator, traffic is instantly rerouted to the healthy secondary region (e.g., eu-west-1) via its static Anycast IPs, bypassing DNS delays and minimizing downtime.

Global Accelerator's DR Advantages

  • Static Anycast IPs: Consistent entry point, no IP changes during failover.
  • Network-Level Rerouting: Bypasses DNS caching, enabling near-instant failover.
  • Automated Health Checks: Proactive detection and redirection of traffic.
  • Reduced Downtime: Significantly lowers Recovery Time Objective (RTO).
Key Takeaway

Global Accelerator provides a superior disaster recovery solution by offering static IPs and network-level failover, ensuring faster and more reliable recovery than traditional DNS methods.
