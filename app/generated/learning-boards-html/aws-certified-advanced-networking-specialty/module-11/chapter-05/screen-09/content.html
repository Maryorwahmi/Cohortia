Traffic Management

Managing Traffic with Traffic Dials

Granular control over regional traffic distribution

What are Traffic Dials?

  • Regional Control: Each endpoint group has a dial to set traffic percentage.
  • Default 100%: Region is fully active and receives traffic.
  • Set to 0%: Effectively takes a region out of service for new traffic.
  • Granular Adjustment: Allows for precise control over traffic distribution.

Example: Controlled Rollout

A new application version is deployed in eu-central-1. Initially, its traffic dial is set to 10%. After successful monitoring, it's gradually increased to 50%, then 100%, ensuring a safe and controlled rollout process.

Use Cases for Traffic Dials

  • Maintenance: Temporarily divert traffic from a region for updates.
  • Testing: Route a small percentage of users to a new environment.
  • Disaster Recovery: Manually shift traffic to a standby region.
  • Capacity Management: Adjust traffic based on regional load or capacity.
Key Takeaway

Traffic dials provide powerful, granular control over how Global Accelerator distributes traffic across your regional endpoint groups, enabling flexible operational strategies.
