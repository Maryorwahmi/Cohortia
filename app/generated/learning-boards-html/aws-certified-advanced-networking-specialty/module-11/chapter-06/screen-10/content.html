Deployment Considerations

Planning Your Outposts Deployment

Understanding the physical and environmental responsibilities for successful integration.

01

Physical Space

Ensure adequate physical space in your data center or edge location to accommodate the Outposts rack or server unit.

Consideration

Verify rack unit availability and floor space for larger deployments.

02

Power & Cooling

Provide robust power delivery and sufficient cooling infrastructure for efficient and reliable Outposts operation.

Consideration

Ensure compatible power outlets and adequate BTU/hr cooling capacity.

03

Network Connectivity

Establish local network connectivity within your on-premises environment to integrate the Outposts hardware.

Consideration

Plan for IP addressing, routing, and firewall rules for local integration.

04

AWS Management

AWS manages the Outposts hardware and software patching, ensuring its operational health.

Consideration

Focus on your applications and data, while AWS handles the underlying infrastructure.

Key Takeaway

Successful Outposts deployment requires customer responsibility for physical environment, power, cooling, and local networking, complemented by AWS's management of the hardware and software.
