Health Checks and Failover: A Powerful Duo

Route 53 health checks monitor endpoints, and if a primary resource becomes unhealthy, failover routing automatically directs traffic to a secondary resource.

  • •Achieves high availability and rapid disaster recovery.
  • •Ensures continuous service even during regional outages.
  • •Users experience minimal disruption during failover events.
Example Scenario

A primary Global Accelerator in `us-east-1` fails its health checks. Route 53 automatically updates DNS to point to a secondary Global Accelerator in `eu-west-1`, redirecting users to the healthy region.

Key Takeaway

The combination of Route 53 health checks and failover routing provides automated, resilient traffic steering, critical for maintaining application availability.
