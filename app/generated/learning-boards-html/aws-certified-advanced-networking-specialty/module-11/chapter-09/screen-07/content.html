Routing Policy

Geoproximity Routing: Advanced Location Control

Routes traffic based on the geographic location of your users AND your resources. You can optionally specify a "bias" to shift traffic towards a resource.

  • •Offers more granular control than simple geolocation routing.
  • •Useful for complex global deployments and capacity management.
  • •Bias allows for strategic traffic shifting to preferred regions.
Example Scenario

You have resources in `us-east-1` and `us-west-2`. By adding a positive bias to `us-east-1`, users slightly closer to `us-west-2` might still be routed to `us-east-1` to balance load or prioritize a specific data center.

Key Takeaway

Geoproximity routing combines user and resource location for traffic direction, with an optional bias to strategically influence traffic flow for advanced control.
