Weighted Routing: Distributing Traffic Proportionally

Distributes traffic to multiple resources in proportions that you specify. This is useful for load balancing, A/B testing, or gradual rollouts.

  • •Enables controlled traffic distribution across different endpoints.
  • •Perfect for testing new features with a subset of users.
  • •Facilitates safe and gradual deployment of application updates.
Example Scenario

You want to test a new application version. You set 90% weight to your stable production environment and 10% weight to the new version, allowing a small user group to experience the update.

Key Takeaway

Weighted routing provides flexible traffic distribution based on assigned percentages, making it ideal for controlled deployments, testing, and load management.
