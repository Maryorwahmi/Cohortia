Lambda@Edge overview

Run serverless functions at CloudFront events to modify requests and responses without hitting your origin.

Use cases
URL rewrites, header injection, A/B testing, custom authorization, and origin routing logic.
Design caution
Keep functions small and fast; heavy computation or large state is unsuitable for edge functions.
Key Takeaway

Lambda@Edge adds powerful customization at the edge but requires careful design to avoid reducing cache efficiency or increasing latency.

Concrete example
An origin-request function examines a feature-flag cookie and rewrites the request host header to route to a blue or green origin, enabling edge-based traffic splitting without touching the origin application.
Common mistake
Altering responses in ways that differ per viewer can fragment caches and cause a drop in hit ratios.
Pro tip
Document which headers or cookies your edge functions rely on to keep forwarding rules explicit and predictable.
