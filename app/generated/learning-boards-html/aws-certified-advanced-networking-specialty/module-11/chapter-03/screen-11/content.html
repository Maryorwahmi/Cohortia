Module 11 › Chapter 11.3 › Monitoring
Cache Hit Ratio
Measure percent of requests served by cache. Low values indicate forwarding too many attributes or fragmentation.
Origin Latency & Requests
Track request count and latency to spot origin overloads, especially after invalidations or deployments.
Errors & WAF Logs
Monitor 4xx/5xx rates and WAF logs to tune rules and detect regressions introduced by protections or deployments.
Operational pattern
Create alarms on sudden origin request increases and low cache hit ratios, and run a short post-deploy validation to ensure metrics remain within expected thresholds.
