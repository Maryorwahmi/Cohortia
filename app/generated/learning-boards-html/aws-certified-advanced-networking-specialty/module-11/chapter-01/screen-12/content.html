Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Hands-on Checklist
Step 1

Identify Regions & AZs

Use the EC2 region selector and the Launch Instance network settings to see subnets mapped to AZ labels like us-east-1a.

Reflection: Which regions host the services you depend on, and are they in proximity to your users?
Step 2

Explore CloudFront options

Create or inspect a CloudFront distribution and note the price class choices that imply regions of PoP coverage.

Reflection: Would choosing a restrictive price class increase latency for users outside the selected regions?
Step 3

List Direct Connect locations

Open Direct Connect and review the available physical locations and their associated AWS Regions to plan private on-ramps.

Try: Map the Direct Connect locations to your on-premises sites and evaluate bandwidth and redundancy options.
Key Takeaway

Console exploration reveals where AWS exposes regional choices and edge-related settings—use them to form testable design hypotheses.
