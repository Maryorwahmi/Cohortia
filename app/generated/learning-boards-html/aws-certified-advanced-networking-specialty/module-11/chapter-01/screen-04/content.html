Definition

What is an AWS Region?

A Region is a geographic area containing multiple Availability Zones and the full suite of AWS services. It provides isolation, service availability, and the basis for compliance and residency decisions.

Example: Hosting EU user data in a European region to meet residency and reduce latency.
Misconception: Adding regions automatically improves latency—only if you replicate and route traffic to those regions.
Key Takeaway

Pick regions for legal compliance and as strategic locations to host full services. Replicate and route appropriately to get latency benefits.

Supporting cards
Why isolation matters

Isolation reduces blast radius: faults, maintenance, or regulatory actions in one region don't force outages elsewhere.

Practical example

A global SaaS provider may keep customer data for Europe in eu-west-1, while user-facing APIs run in nearby regions to balance latency and redundancy.
