Overview strip
Regions
Isolated geographic areas that host AWS services for data residency and fault isolation.
Availability Zones
Distinct data centers inside a region providing redundancy and low-latency links.
Edge Locations
Points of presence for caching, DNS, and fast network ingress close to users.
Visual concept
Region
Full-service area with multiple AZs
Availability Zone
Isolated data center with redundant systems
Edge Location
Caching and network ingress near users
Key Takeaway

Choose regions for full services, AZs for redundancy, and edge locations to reduce latency and serve content closer to users.
