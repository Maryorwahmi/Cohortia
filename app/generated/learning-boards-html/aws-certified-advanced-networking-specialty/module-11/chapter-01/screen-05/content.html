Hierarchy

AZs: isolated data centers inside a Region

Each Availability Zone is one or more discrete data centers with independent infrastructure. AZs are connected by low-latency links inside a region and provide the basis for redundancy and failover.

Best practice: distribute compute and stateless tiers across at least two AZs and use managed services for cross-AZ storage replication.
Misconception: AZs do not meet geographic or legal separation requirements that regions can provide.
Design cards
Example: Web tier in AZ A and AZ B behind a load balancer; database tier using multi-AZ replica to maintain availability.
Check: For your recovery objective, is cross-AZ replication synchronous or asynchronous? That decision affects data loss risk.
Try this: List three services you would deploy multi-AZ to reduce downtime risk and explain why.
Key Takeaway

Multi-AZ deployment is the primary step for resilience within a region; complement it with multi-region planning when geography or compliance requires separation.
