Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Design Mistakes
Pitfalls
  • Relying on edge caching without optimizing origins.
  • Choosing restrictive CloudFront price classes that increase latency for some users.
  • Neglecting cache invalidation and cache-control headers, causing stale content.
  • Assuming Direct Connect coverage everywhere without verifying PoP locations.
Practical steps
Monitor cache hit ratios, origin fetch counts, and geographic latency metrics.
Select price classes and PoP coverage intentionally based on your user distribution.
Plan for invalidation strategies and origin autoscaling to handle edge misses.
Reflection

Which trade-off—cost, latency, or complexity—matters most for your application, and how will that decision shape edge and regional deployments?
