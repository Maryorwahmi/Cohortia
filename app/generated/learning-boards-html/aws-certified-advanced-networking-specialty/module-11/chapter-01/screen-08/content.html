Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Local & Wavelength Zones
Local Zone

Metro-area compute and storage

Local Zones extend a Region into a metro area to bring services closer for latency-sensitive workloads that need compute or persistent storage nearby.

Example: Financial trading systems placed in a Local Zone for single-digit millisecond response times.
Wavelength Zone

Compute at the edge of 5G networks

Wavelength embeds AWS infrastructure in a carrier network for ultra-low-latency mobile and IoT workloads tightly integrated with 5G.

Example: A mobile game offloads real-time physics computation to Wavelength for lower mobile latency.
Key Takeaway

Choose Local Zones or Wavelength when you need compute and storage physically close to users or carriers; they are not simply cache points.
