Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Active/Active vs. Active/Passive

Strategies for multi-region deployments, where all regions are active or one is standby.

Cost Optimization

Designing architectures to minimize operational expenses while meeting performance and reliability requirements.

Comprehensive Health Checks

Monitoring the health of all critical components across the entire application stack, from edge to origin.

Summary Takeaway

Effective edge architectures balance active/passive strategies with cost optimization and robust health checks to ensure continuous, efficient operation.
