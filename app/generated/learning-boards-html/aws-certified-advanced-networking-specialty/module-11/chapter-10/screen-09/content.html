Local Zones & Wavelength Zones

Bringing AWS closer to metropolitan areas and 5G networks.

Purpose & Benefits
  • Reduced Latency: Place compute and storage closer to end-users or 5G devices.
  • New Use Cases: Enable real-time applications like AR/VR, autonomous vehicles, and gaming.
  • Consistent AWS Experience: Use familiar AWS services and APIs in these extended locations.
Key Takeaway

Local Zones and Wavelength Zones offer specialized edge computing for ultra-low latency, targeting metropolitan users and 5G devices respectively.

Comparison: Local Zones vs. Wavelength Zones
Local Zones
  • Extend AWS to major metro areas.
  • Single-digit millisecond latency to city users.
  • General purpose compute/storage.
  • Use cases: media rendering, real-time gaming.
Wavelength Zones
  • Embed AWS in 5G carrier networks.
  • Ultra-low latency to 5G devices.
  • Optimized for 5G mobile edge computing.
  • Use cases: autonomous vehicles, AR/VR.
Distinction

While both reduce latency, Local Zones target broad metropolitan user bases, whereas Wavelength Zones are specifically integrated with 5G networks for mobile device applications.
