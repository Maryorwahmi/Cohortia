Holistic Edge Architecture Design

Integrating AWS services for optimal global delivery.

Key Principles
  • Lowest Latency: Deliver content and applications as close to users as possible.
  • Highest Availability: Ensure continuous operation through redundancy and failover.
  • Optimal Cost: Balance performance and reliability with cost-effective solutions.
Key Takeaway

A holistic approach to edge architecture integrates various AWS services to achieve a balance of low latency, high availability, and cost efficiency for global applications.
