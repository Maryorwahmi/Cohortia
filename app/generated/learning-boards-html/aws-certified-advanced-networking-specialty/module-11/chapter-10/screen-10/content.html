Redundancy at Every Layer

Ensuring high availability and disaster recovery.

Why It Matters
  • Prevent Single Points of Failure: Distribute components to avoid cascading failures.
  • Maintain Business Continuity: Ensure applications remain operational during outages.
  • Improve User Experience: Minimize downtime and performance degradation for end-users.
Key Takeaway

Implementing redundancy at every layer—DNS, edge services, regional endpoints, and hybrid connections—is fundamental for building highly available and resilient edge architectures.

Redundancy Layers
DNS (Route 53)

Multiple records with failover routing policies.

Edge Services (CloudFront, GA)

Distributed Edge Locations, multi-region endpoint groups.

Regional Endpoints (ALB/NLB, EC2)

Multi-AZ deployments, auto-scaling groups across AZs.

Hybrid (Outposts)

Redundant Direct Connect or VPN for Outposts service link.
