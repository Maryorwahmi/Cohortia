Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Resilient Architecture

A system designed to withstand failures and continue operating, often using redundancy and automatic failover mechanisms.

Performant Architecture

A system designed to deliver content and applications with high speed, low latency, and high throughput.

Multi-Region Deployment

Deploying application components across multiple AWS Regions for higher availability and disaster recovery.

Summary Takeaway

Designing effective edge architectures hinges on building resilient, performant systems through strategic multi-region deployments and intelligent service integration.
