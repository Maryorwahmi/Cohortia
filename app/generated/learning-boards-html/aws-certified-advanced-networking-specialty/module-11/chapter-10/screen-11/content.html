Cost Optimization Strategies

Balancing performance and reliability with expenditure.

Core Principles
  • Minimize Data Egress: Reduce data transfer out from origins through effective caching.
  • Right-size Services: Use the most appropriate service for the job, avoiding over-engineering.
  • Evaluate Trade-offs: Understand the cost-benefit ratio of latency improvements vs. expenditure.
Key Takeaway

Effective cost optimization for edge architectures involves strategic caching, careful service selection, and a clear understanding of the cost-benefit trade-offs for performance and reliability.

Service-Specific Cost Considerations
CloudFront

Optimize caching to reduce origin egress costs; select appropriate price classes.

Global Accelerator

Monitor data transfer out and hourly charges; use only when performance benefits justify cost.

Outposts

Consider significant upfront hardware investment vs. long-term data transfer savings for local processing.

Local/Wavelength Zones

Evaluate if latency benefits justify potentially higher costs compared to full region deployments.
