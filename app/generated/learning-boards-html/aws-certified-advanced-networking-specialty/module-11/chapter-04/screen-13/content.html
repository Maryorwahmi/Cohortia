Module 11 › Chapter 11.4 › Traffic Management
Operational levers
  • Traffic dials — region-level percentage control.
  • Endpoint weights — distribution inside a region.
  • Health checks — automatic reroute for failed endpoints.
Staged rollout example
Start with a 10% traffic dial to a new region, monitor health and metrics, then increase to 50% before full migration.
Failover patterns
Immediate diversion
Set the affected region’s traffic dial to zero to route traffic elsewhere without client changes.
Automate carefully
Tie traffic dial updates to deployments or observability alerts for repeatable rollback and release behavior.
Misconception check

Traffic dials are not minute-by-minute routing tools; they are best used for controlled, coarse-grained traffic shifts and rollouts.
