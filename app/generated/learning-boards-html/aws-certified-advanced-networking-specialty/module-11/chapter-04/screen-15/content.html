Module 11 › Chapter 11.4 › Practical Reminders
Pick by traffic type
Global Accelerator for dynamic/protocol-diverse, CloudFront for cacheable HTTP/S assets.
Plan controls
Define endpoint groups, traffic dials, and health checks before deployment for predictable behavior.
Combine where useful
Use CloudFront for static assets and Global Accelerator for dynamic, low-latency traffic in the same architecture.
Final reflection
When you plan global delivery, ask whether your traffic benefits more from edge caching or network path optimization, and design accordingly.
