Role of endpoint groups

Endpoint groups tie a listener to one AWS Region and contain endpoints within that region. They are the control plane for regional traffic distribution and health checks.

Traffic dial example
Set US-East to 100% and Europe to 0% during a maintenance window, or ramp Europe to 25% for testing a new release.
Misconception
Traffic dials are not instantaneous DNS-level changes; they are applied by the accelerator to the traffic it routes at the region level.
Operational controls
  • Traffic dials: percentage of traffic to an endpoint group.
  • Endpoint weights: relative weights for endpoints inside a group.
  • Health checks: automatic reroute away from unhealthy endpoints.
Key Takeaway

Use endpoint groups and traffic dials to stage releases and implement region-level failover without changing client-side configuration.
