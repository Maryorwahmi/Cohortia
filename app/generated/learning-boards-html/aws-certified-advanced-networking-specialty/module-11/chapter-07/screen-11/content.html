Routing Strategy

Implementing Outposts Routing

Directing Traffic Between On-Premises, Outpost, and Region

  • On-Premises Configuration: Direct traffic for Outpost VPC CIDR/CoIPs to the Local Gateway (LGW).

  • LGW Routing: Handles traffic for Outpost instances, directing it to on-premises or the AWS Region via the service link.

  • Dynamic Routing (BGP): Use BGP to exchange routes between your on-premises router and the LGW for resilience.

Key Takeaway

Precise routing configuration is vital for seamless and efficient traffic flow across your hybrid Outposts environment.
