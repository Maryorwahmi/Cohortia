Allocating On-Premises VLANs

Segmenting Network Traffic for Outposts Components

  • Dedicated VLANs: AWS requires specific VLANs on your on-premises network for Outposts.

  • Service Link VLAN: Essential for isolating control plane traffic between Outpost and AWS Region.

  • Local Gateway VLAN: Dedicated for traffic between Outpost resources and your on-premises network.

Key Takeaway

Proper VLAN allocation on-premises is fundamental for traffic segmentation, security, and efficient communication for Outposts components.
