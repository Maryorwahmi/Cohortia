Connectivity Option

AWS Direct Connect for Service Link

Dedicated, Private, and High-Performance Connectivity

  • Private Connection: Bypasses the public internet, offering enhanced security and consistent performance.

  • Redundancy Best Practice: Configure multiple Direct Connect connections to ensure high availability and failover for the service link.

  • On-Premises Integration: Connects via a dedicated VLAN on your local network to a customer-provided router.

Key Takeaway

Direct Connect is the preferred, robust solution for the Outposts service link, offering dedicated, private, and redundant connectivity for critical control plane communication.
