Outposts Service Link: Purpose & Requirements
AWS Outpost
AWS Region Control Plane
Service Link
Purpose

Management, monitoring, software updates, regional service access.

Requirement

Reliable, high-bandwidth, low-latency connection.

Impact of Downtime

Outpost becomes unmanageable, workloads may stop running.

Best Practice

Configure multiple connections for redundancy.

Key Takeaway

The service link is the indispensable connection for Outpost operations; its reliability directly impacts management and workload stability.

Common Mistake

Underestimating the bandwidth and latency requirements for the service link can lead to performance bottlenecks and control plane disruptions.
