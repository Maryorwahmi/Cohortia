IPsec VPN: Service Link Characteristics
Encrypted Tunnel

Secure communication over the public internet.

Variable Performance

Latency and bandwidth can fluctuate due to internet congestion.

Backup or Alternative

Suitable for less critical deployments or as a redundancy option.

Cost-Effective Redundancy

A more economical option for backup connectivity.

Connectivity Option

IPsec VPN for Service Link

Flexible Backup and Alternative Connectivity

  • Encrypted Tunnel: Provides secure communication over the public internet to the AWS Region.

  • Alternative or Backup: Can be used as a primary connection for less critical workloads or as a crucial failover for Direct Connect.

  • Performance Trade-offs: Offers less predictable performance compared to Direct Connect due to reliance on the public internet.

Key Takeaway

IPsec VPN provides a secure, flexible, and cost-effective backup or alternative for the Outposts service link, though with variable performance.
