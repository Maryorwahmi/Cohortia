Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Critical Components and Connectivity

VLANs

Virtual Local Area Networks, used to segment network traffic on the on-premises network for the service link and local gateway.

Direct Connect

A dedicated, private network connection from on-premises to AWS, highly recommended for the Outposts service link.

Routing

Configuration on both on-premises routers and the Outpost's local gateway to direct traffic between Outpost resources, on-premises networks, and the AWS Region.

Summary Takeaway

VLANs, Direct Connect, and robust Routing are foundational for establishing and managing secure, high-performance connectivity for AWS Outposts, ensuring seamless hybrid operations.
