Common Outposts Networking Mistakes

Ensuring Robust and Reliable Hybrid Connectivity

  • Underestimating Service Link: Insufficient bandwidth or high latency for the service link leads to performance issues and control plane disruptions.

  • Improper Routing: Incorrectly configured routing between Outpost and on-premises networks causes connectivity failures.

  • Solution: Meticulous planning of IP addressing, VLANs, and routing, with robust, redundant service link connectivity.

Key Takeaway

Proactive planning and robust configuration are essential to prevent critical Outposts networking issues and ensure seamless hybrid operations.
