Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Mistakes & Safeguards
Top mistakes
  • Enabling remediation before validating patterns (over-automation).
  • Granting broad IAM permissions to Lambda roles.
  • Ignoring idempotency and duplicate deliveries.
  • Poor logging and invisible failures.
Mitigations
Dry-run mode and SNS-first validation.
Scoped IAM and documented policy reasons.
Idempotent checks and DLQs for failed events.
Runbooks and a single safety switch to disable automation.
