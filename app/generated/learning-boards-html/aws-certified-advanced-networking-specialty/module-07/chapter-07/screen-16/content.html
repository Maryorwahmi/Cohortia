Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Quick Reminders
Precision
Match specific event fields and validate with actual payloads to avoid noise.
Safety
Use least-privilege IAM, dry-runs, and staged rollouts to prevent accidental disruption.
Observability
Log events, actions, and errors; publish alerts for quick incident response.
Wrap-up
Start with a small, well-instrumented automation and expand as you gain confidence and evidence of reliability.
