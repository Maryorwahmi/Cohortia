Important considerations
IAM
Ensure the caller has ec2:CreateNetworkInsightsPath, ec2:StartNetworkInsightsAnalysis, and ec2:Describe* permissions limited to necessary resources.
Cost
Analyses incur charges. Avoid high-frequency automation without scope limits; batch or sample where appropriate.
Static analysis
Results reflect current configuration. Re-run analyses after changes to keep conclusions valid.
Combine signals
Use analyzers for config state, Flow Logs for observed traffic, and CloudTrail for change history to get a full picture.
Small permissions and careful automation make these tools safe and effective in production environments.
