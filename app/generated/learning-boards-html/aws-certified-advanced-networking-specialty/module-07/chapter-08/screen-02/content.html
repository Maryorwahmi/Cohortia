Mnemonic
Think: Collect → Keep → Check → Correlate. This sequence ensures you have the data, store it correctly, validate configs, and then correlate events for security insights.
Why this matters
Unified logs reduce investigation time and enable consistent compliance reporting across accounts and regions.
Quick check
If you cannot answer "Which account has the ELB request at 03:12 UTC?" within two minutes, centralization or indexing needs improvement.
