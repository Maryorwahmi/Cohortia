Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Retention Policies
Step-by-step retention plan
  1. Classify logs by purpose: compliance, forensics, operations.
  2. Set CloudWatch retention for operational logs (30–90 days).
  3. Create S3 lifecycle rules to transition to Glacier after policy period and to expire after retention ends.
  4. Document retention in policy and enforce via orchestration templates.
Remember to encrypt S3 objects and restrict access; retention increases risk if access controls are weak.
Example Policy Tiles
CloudTrail: 7 years → Glacier
Flow Logs: 12 months in S3; 90 days in CloudWatch
ELB Logs: 1 year in S3, 90 days in CloudWatch
