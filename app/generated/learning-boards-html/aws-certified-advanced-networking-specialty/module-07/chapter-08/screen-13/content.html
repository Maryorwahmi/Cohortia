Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Safeguards
Operational Safeguards
  • Grant write-only delivery permissions to service principals; never broad read rights.
  • Harden the logging account: strict IAM, MFA, and limited human access.
  • Audit changes to logging configuration using CloudTrail and Config.
  • Maintain a runbook for log discovery, querying, and remediation steps.
Common Mistakes
Set-it-and-forget-it logging, missing cross-account strategy, ignoring cost, and failing to act on findings.
Pro Tip

Document responsibilities and integrate logs into incident workflows so findings become action, not noise.
