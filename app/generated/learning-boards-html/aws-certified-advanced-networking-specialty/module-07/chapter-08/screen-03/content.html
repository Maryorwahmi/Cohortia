Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Operational Objectives
Objective Cards
01 — Cross-account delivery
Grant the VPC Flow Logs or CloudTrail delivery principals write-only permission to the logging bucket and never grant blanket account-level read privileges.
02 — Tiered retention
Choose short CloudWatch retention for troubleshooting and S3 Glacier for multi-year compliance archives.
03 — Config rules
Enable managed rules for VPC Flow Logs, restricted SSH, and bucket logging to detect drift quickly.
04 — SIEM streaming
Use Kinesis Firehose to stream data to your SIEM, keeping an eye on costs when choosing event granularity.
Scenario Mnemonic
Remember: write-only delivery, tiered retention, continuous auditing, and actionable streaming. Apply this checklist after any architecture change.
