Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › CloudTrail Best Practices
What to enable
  • Single multi-region CloudTrail trail for management events.
  • Deliver trail to centralized S3 bucket and CloudWatch Logs for real-time analysis.
  • Enable log file integrity validation to detect tampering.
Use lifecycle policies to transition logs into colder storage after the active compliance window.
Concrete Example
A finance org keeps CloudTrail for seven years. They enable integrity validation and lifecycle to Glacier after 365 days. The log delivery bucket policy allows cloudtrail.amazonaws.com to PutObject and denies GetObject to non-admin roles.
Key Takeaway

CloudTrail is the audit trail of changes; protect it with integrity validation and controlled bucket ACLs.
