Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › SIEM Integration
Pipeline Pattern
CloudWatch Logs or S3 → Kinesis Firehose with buffering and transformation → SIEM or S3 archive.
Enrich events with tags for account, VPC ID, and application name.
Prioritize indexing for CloudTrail and Flow Logs for critical accounts.
Operational Notes
  • Monitor Firehose delivery metrics and failures.
  • Limit SIEM ingest by sampling or selective indexing.
  • Secure Firehose IAM role to write-only targets.
Key Takeaway

Streaming into SIEMs enables correlation but requires careful selection and enrichment to be cost-effective and actionable.
