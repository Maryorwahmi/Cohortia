Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Secure & Retain Logs
S3 hardening checklist
  • Enable server-side encryption and S3 bucket encryption policies.
  • Enable versioning and consider MFA delete where appropriate.
  • Restrict PutObject/Delete permissions to CloudTrail and audit roles.
Retention
Implement lifecycle rules to move logs to Glacier for long-term retention per compliance needs.
Operational notes
Monitor the S3 bucket configuration and access logs. If the bucket policy changes, investigate immediately as it can invalidate your audit trail.
Why this matters
Protected and retained logs are admissible evidence and form the backbone of post-incident analysis and regulatory compliance.
