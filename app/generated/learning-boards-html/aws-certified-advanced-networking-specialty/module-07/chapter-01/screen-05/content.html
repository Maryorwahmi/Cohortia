Core Concept

Metrics, logs, and alarms

CloudWatch collects time-series metrics and logs and uses alarms to notify or trigger automated actions. In networking, metrics such as NetworkIn and NetworkOut reveal throughput and trends.

Metric example
NetworkOut for an EC2 instance measured per minute, summed over five minutes to detect sustained egress.
Alarm action
When a metric crosses a threshold, notify an SNS topic or invoke a Lambda to automate a response.
Supporting notes
  • Choose period and statistic that match the problem: use Sum over 5 minutes for bandwidth bursts.
  • Attach alarm-actions to an SNS topic to route notifications to email, chat, or automation.
  • Tune thresholds based on baseline to avoid alert fatigue.
Concrete example
Create an alarm when NetworkOut summed over five minutes exceeds 52,428,800 bytes (50 MB). Configure the alarm to notify an SNS topic subscribed by your operations inbox.
