Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › CloudWatch Dashboard
Step plan
  1. Create dashboard named MyNetworkDashboard.
  2. Add a line widget for NetworkIn and NetworkOut scoped to the EC2 instance.
  3. Add CPUUtilization and HealthyHostCount widgets for context.
  4. Add an Alarms widget to surface current alarm states.
  5. Save and document the dashboard owner and runbook link.
Tips
- Use 1 hour for quick incidents and 24 hours to see patterns.
- Group related widgets together to reduce cognitive load.
- Keep owners and escalation steps in dashboard notes.
