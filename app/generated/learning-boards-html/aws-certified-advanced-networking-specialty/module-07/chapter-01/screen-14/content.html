Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Key Concepts
Amazon CloudWatch
Time-series metrics and alarms for performance monitoring and automation.
VPC Flow Logs
Connection-level records showing who communicates with whom and whether traffic was accepted or rejected.
AWS CloudTrail
API-level audit logs for governance, compliance, and tracing configuration changes.
Takeaway

Combine metrics, flow logs, and audit trails to detect, validate, and remediate network issues with minimal guesswork.
