What is recorded

IP-level flow records

Flow logs include source and destination IP, ports, protocol, bytes, packets, start and end times, and ACCEPT or REJECT action. They let you answer connectivity and security questions during investigation.

Use case: Connectivity troubleshooting
Confirm whether traffic reached the network interface or was denied earlier by a security group.
Use case: Security analysis
Identify unusual remote peers or lateral movement patterns by inspecting source-destination pairs over time.
Storage options
Publish flow logs to CloudWatch Logs for metric filters and real-time alerting, or to S3 for long-term archival and compliance. Many teams use both: CloudWatch for live ops and S3 for forensics.
Limitations
Flow logs summarize flows and do not capture packet payloads. For deep packet inspection, use packet capture tools in addition to flow logs.
