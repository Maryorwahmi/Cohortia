Designing alarms and avoiding alert fatigue

Combine alert sensitivity with context and central routing to keep your team effective.

State metrics
Short periods, strict thresholds, immediate alerts.
Throughput & errors
Use trend or sustained thresholds to avoid reacting to transient spikes.
Routing alerts
Treat BGPState DOWN as high priority even when link is UP.
Key Takeaway
Quality over quantity: fewer, well-tuned alerts reduce fatigue and speed response.
Operational tip
Attach runbooks and correlated on-prem evidence to alarms so responders know what to check first.
