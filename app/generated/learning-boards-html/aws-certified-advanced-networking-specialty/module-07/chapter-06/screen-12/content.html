Hands-on activity

Configure two alarms and confirm cross-domain visibility into CloudWatch and your on-prem monitoring system.

Step 1
Create TunnelState alarm with VpnId and TunnelIpAddress; validate SNS notification.
Step 2
Create Direct Connect BGPState alarm filtered by ConnectionId and VirtualInterfaceId; validate routing alerts.
Step 3
Ensure CGW logs and ISP metrics are visible in your incident system and correlate timestamps with CloudWatch alarms.
Check your understanding
If one tunnel fluctuates and the other stays stable, initial investigation should focus on customer-side connectivity and device logs.
