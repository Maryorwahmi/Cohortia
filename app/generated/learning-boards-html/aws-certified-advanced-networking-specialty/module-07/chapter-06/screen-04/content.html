Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Holistic monitoring
Core Concept

Why a holistic approach matters

Cloud-side metrics show symptoms; on-premises telemetry often shows root cause. Both are required for fast repair.

Key signals to correlate:
  • TunnelState and VPNConnectionState from CloudWatch.
  • CGW interface status, CPU, and logs from your device monitoring.
  • ISP outage or packet loss indicators for VPNs.
Key Takeaway

End-to-end visibility means correlating CloudWatch metrics with local device telemetry before escalating to providers or opening tickets.

Annotated scenario
Cloud symptom
TunnelState toggles between UP and DOWN for tunnel 1.
On-prem evidence
CGW logs show interface flaps and ISP packet loss at the same timestamps.
Action
Correlate times, raise ISP ticket, and failover traffic to second tunnel if available.
Common mistake: Relying solely on aggregate metrics like VPNConnectionState without inspecting per-tunnel TunnelState or device logs.
