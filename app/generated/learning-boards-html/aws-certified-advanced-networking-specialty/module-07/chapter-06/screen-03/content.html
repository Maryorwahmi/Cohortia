Practice Milestones

Four hands-on checkpoints to prove operational readiness.

01 Alarm for TunnelState
Create and test an alarm that alerts when a tunnel metric is DOWN.
02 Observe TunnelData
Verify traffic counters and detect imbalanced or dropped traffic patterns.
03 Direct Connect BGP alarm
Alert when the BGPState for a VIF goes DOWN even if the physical link is UP.
04 Centralize on-prem telemetry
Ensure CGW logs and ISP alerts appear in the same incident system as CloudWatch alarms.
