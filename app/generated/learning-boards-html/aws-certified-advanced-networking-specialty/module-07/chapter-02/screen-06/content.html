Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › S3 vs CloudWatch Logs
Amazon S3
Best for long-term archival, compliance, and batch processing. Logs are often partitioned by date and analyzed later with Athena or custom jobs.
When to choose S3
  • Retention and compliance requirements
  • Large-volume historical analysis
  • Lower-cost storage for archive
CloudWatch Logs
Use for real-time monitoring, filtering, metric creation, and interactive queries with Logs Insights to find REJECT entries quickly.
When to choose CloudWatch
  • Immediate troubleshooting and incident response
  • Metric filters and alarm-based notifications
  • Interactive Logs Insights queries
Tip

Consider dual publication: CloudWatch for alerts and S3 for archive to get the benefits of both worlds.
