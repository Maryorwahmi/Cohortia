Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Remember
Permissions
Validate that the flow logs role includes the required logs permissions to avoid silent failures.
Mapping
Include instance-id or use interface-id with tags to map flows to services quickly.
Destination
Use CloudWatch for real-time insight and S3 for archival; combine both when needed.
Final takeaway

Design Flow Logs with permissions, record fields, and destination goals in mind so they become a dependable part of your observability toolkit.
