Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Common Mistakes & Considerations
Common pitfalls and best practices
Baseline first
Collect representative metrics before setting thresholds to avoid false alarms.
Watch units
Bytes, bits, and packets are different — interpret each appropriately.
Limit custom metric cardinality
High-cardinality dimensions increase cost and add noise.
Tight IAM
Grant only cloudwatch:PutMetricData to publishing principals.
Key Takeaway

Good monitoring is targeted, cost-aware and secure: plan baselines, choose units and statistics wisely, and limit the scope of custom metrics to what you actually need.
