Practical Objectives

Turn knowledge into repeatable designs and a lab deliverable.

Bloom: Apply → Evaluate → Create
01
Mitigate CIDR overlap

Detect overlaps before deployment and reassign unique ranges to enable peering and Transit Gateway connections. Verified competency milestone.

02
Plan AZ-aligned subnets

Create public and private subnets per availability zone for redundancy and correct service exposure. Verified competency milestone.

03
Author SG and NACL rules

Write security group rules referencing SG IDs and NACL rules with ordered deny/allow semantics. Verified competency milestone.

04
Deliver a lab design document

Produce a CIDR map, subnet lists per AZ, and security rules as a reproducible deliverable. Verified competency milestone.
