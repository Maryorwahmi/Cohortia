Learning Objectives

Understand the skills you'll practice and why they matter.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Design multi-VPC CIDR plans

Allocate contiguous, non-overlapping private ranges across environments so peering and Transit Gateway attachments work without address conflicts. Verified competency milestone.

02
Apply VPC quotas and limits

Recognize hard limits and soft quotas that affect ENIs, SG rules, and more. Monitor and plan to avoid deployment failures. Verified competency milestone.

03
Implement SGs and NACLs

Use stateful Security Groups at the instance level and stateless NACLs at the subnet level to build layered network defenses. Verified competency milestone.

04
Formulate routing strategies

Design route tables and hub-and-spoke topologies that use Transit Gateway or peering to simplify many-to-many connectivity. Verified competency milestone.
