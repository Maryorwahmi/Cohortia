Pattern

Transit Gateway hub for shared services

Attach multiple VPCs to a Transit Gateway to centralize routing, egress, and common services while reducing peering complexity.

Example attachments
  • tgw attach — Shared Services (10.0.0.0/16)
  • tgw attach — Production (10.30.0.0/16)
  • tgw attach — Development (10.10.0.0/16)
Why use a hub
  • Simplifies many-to-many connectivity and central policy enforcement.
  • Concentrates monitoring, logging, and security services in one place.
  • Reduces operational complexity of maintaining N*(N-1)/2 peering links.
Common mistake

Assuming Transit Gateway removes the need for unique CIDR ranges; overlapping CIDRs still break routing and must be avoided.

Key Takeaway

Centralize routing and services with a hub, but plan addresses and routes explicitly to preserve predictable connectivity.
