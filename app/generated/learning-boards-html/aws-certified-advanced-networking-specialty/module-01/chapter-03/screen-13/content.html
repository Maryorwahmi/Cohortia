Architecture
Redundancy and Placement
Ensure endpoint ENIs are distributed across Availability Zones and placed for performance.
AZ Distribution
At least two ENIs in different AZs for each endpoint to tolerate AZ failure.
Subnet selection
Choose subnets close to the majority of query origination to lower latency.
Operational practices
Use tags, automate endpoint creation, and monitor endpoint status for availability metrics.
Tradeoffs
  • Centralized endpoints simplify management but can increase latency for distant clients.
  • Distributed endpoints reduce latency but add operational overhead and complexity in rule management.
  • Design should reflect your SLA tolerance for DNS latency vs operational cost.
Key Takeaway

Design endpoints with both redundancy and proximity to query sources in mind. Automate and monitor endpoint health to keep DNS reliable.
