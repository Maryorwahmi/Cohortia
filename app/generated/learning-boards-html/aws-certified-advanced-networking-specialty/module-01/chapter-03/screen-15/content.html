Route 53 Resolver
Service that extends VPC DNS to support hybrid forwarding and inbound queries.
Inbound vs Outbound Endpoints
Inbound accepts on-prem queries to AWS; outbound forwards AWS queries to external DNS under rules.
Security & Connectivity
Open UDP/TCP 53 where required, use restrictive SGs, distribute ENIs across AZs, and ensure routing.
Summary

Use endpoints and resolver rules intentionally, secure them tightly, and verify connectivity and redundancy to operate hybrid DNS reliably.
