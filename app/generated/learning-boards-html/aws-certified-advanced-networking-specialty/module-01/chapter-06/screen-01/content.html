Advanced VPC Flow Logs & Traffic Mirroring

This lesson teaches how to collect metadata with Flow Logs, stream or archive logs, query them for troubleshooting and security audits, and when to capture full packets using Traffic Mirroring for deep inspection. You will learn practical setup steps and the design patterns for centralized monitoring in large environments.

In this lesson
Flow Logs
Metadata at VPC, subnet, or ENI level for troubleshooting and auditing.
Traffic Mirroring
Capture full packet content for DPI and IDS/IPS workflows.
Centralization
Design central S3 or SIEM ingestion for scale and compliance.
