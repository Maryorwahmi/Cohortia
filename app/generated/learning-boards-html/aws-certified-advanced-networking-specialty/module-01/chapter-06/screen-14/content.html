Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Key Concepts
VPC Flow Logs
Metadata about network traffic that supports troubleshooting, security auditing, and traffic analysis.
Traffic Mirroring
Captures full packet content for deep packet inspection, IDS/IPS, and forensic analysis.
Centralized Logging
Aggregate Flow Logs and mirrored streams into central accounts or a Security VPC for scalable analysis and compliance.
Final takeaway

Use Flow Logs for broad metadata visibility and Traffic Mirroring for payload inspection, and centralize to scale and retain evidence for audits.
