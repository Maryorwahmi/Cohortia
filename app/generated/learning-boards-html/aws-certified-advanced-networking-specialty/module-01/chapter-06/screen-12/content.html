Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Common Mistakes & Safety
Permissions & policies
  • IAM role must include S3 write and trust for vpc-flow-logs.amazonaws.com.
  • S3 bucket policy must allow the Flow Logs service to put objects to the prefix.
  • Cross-account centralization requires explicit cross-account role trust and bucket permissions.
Performance & cost
  • Mirroring full packets can saturate network and CPU; use filters to reduce volume.
  • Monitor ingestion and storage costs for Flow Logs, and processing costs for mirrored traffic.
  • Provision monitoring targets and use NLBs for scale when processing mirrored streams from many sources.
Checklist
Confirm IAM and S3 policies, apply mirror filters, size mirror targets, and review retention to control costs before enabling wide-scale collection.
