Learning Objectives

Master the tools and queries you need to make network traffic visible, auditable, and actionable in AWS.

01
Configure Flow Logs
Enable Flow Logs at VPC, subnet, or ENI level and send them to CloudWatch, S3, or Firehose so logs reach the right tool.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Query Flow Logs
Use CloudWatch Logs Insights and Athena to answer incidents, detect rejected connections, and visualize traffic over time.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Implement Traffic Mirroring
Capture full packet content using mirror sources, targets, and filters for DPI, IDS/IPS, and forensics.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design Centralized Monitoring
Centralize Flow Logs or mirrored streams for scale, compliance, and cross-account analysis without losing context.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
