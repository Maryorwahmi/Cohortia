Placement & routing
Always ensure NAT Gateways are in public subnets with IGW routes and private subnets route default traffic to them.
Operational trade-offs
Balance managed scaling and cost of NAT Gateway against the customization and maintenance of NAT Instances.
IPv6 egress
Use Egress-Only IGW for IPv6-only private instances to permit outbound traffic but block inbound connections.
Remember

Route accuracy and device placement are the most common causes of NAT problems; check them first when diagnosing connectivity.
