Analyze security & cost

Compare management overhead, per‑GB or per‑attachment costs, and security surface for each method. This helps you choose where to centralize controls and where to publish services.

Endpoint policies and Security Groups are primary controls for PrivateLink. TGW route tables and associations are primary controls for Transit Gateway segmentation.

Troubleshooting checklist
Verify both VPC route tables include routes for the peer or the TGW target.
For TGW, confirm associations and propagations are set on the correct TGW route table.
Check Security Groups and NACLs for permitted traffic even after routing is correct.
