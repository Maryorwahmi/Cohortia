Essential Terminology

Recap of core concepts

AWS Certificate Manager (ACM)

A service that handles the complexity of creating, storing, and renewing public and private SSL/TLS X.509 certificates for use with AWS services.

AWS Private Certificate Authority (Private CA)

A managed service that helps you create and manage a private PKI within AWS, allowing you to issue private certificates for internal use.

SSL/TLS Certificates

Digital certificates used to establish secure, encrypted communication channels (HTTPS) and verify the identity of servers and clients.

Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)

A set of roles, policies, and procedures needed to create, manage, distribute, use, store, and revoke digital certificates.

Summary Takeaway

ACM and Private CA are foundational AWS services for managing both public and private SSL/TLS certificates, enabling robust network trust.
