Seamless Integration: ACM and Private CA

Combining public and private certificate management

ACM
Request Private Certificate

ACM requests a certificate, specifying Private CA as the issuer.

AWS Private CA

Issues the private certificate based on the request.

Internal ALB

Certificate deployed for secure internal communication.

Key Takeaway

Integrating ACM with Private CA allows you to manage private certificates with ACM's automation while leveraging Private CA's controlled trust.
