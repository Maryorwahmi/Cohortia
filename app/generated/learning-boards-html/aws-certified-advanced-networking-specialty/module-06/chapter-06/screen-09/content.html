Designing Your Private CA Hierarchy

Root CAs, Intermediate CAs, and security best practices

Root CA

The top-level authority, self-signed. Keep offline and highly secured to protect the entire trust chain.

Intermediate CA

Signed by the Root CA, used for issuing end-entity certificates. Provides an extra layer of security.

Key Takeaway

Always use an Intermediate CA for issuing certificates, keeping the Root CA offline to protect your PKI's integrity.
