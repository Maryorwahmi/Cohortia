Certificate Management: Common Mistakes

Ensuring robust and secure PKI operations

  • Self-Signed Certificates: Difficult to manage, distribute, and establish trust securely across many clients.

  • Public Certs for Internal Use: Unnecessary and can expose internal details; Private CA is the appropriate solution.

  • Poor Revocation Management: Failing to revoke compromised certificates can lead to significant security vulnerabilities.

Key Takeaway

Proactive management, proper CA hierarchy design, and robust revocation are essential for secure PKI operations.

Compromised CA

Undermines entire trust chain.

Strong IAM Policies

Restrict access to Private CA.

Pro-Tip

Regularly audit your certificate inventory and CA configurations to identify and mitigate potential security risks before they become critical.
