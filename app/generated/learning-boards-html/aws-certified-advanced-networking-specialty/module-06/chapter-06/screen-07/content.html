AWS Private Certificate Authority (Private CA)

Building your own private PKI within AWS

  • Private PKI: Create a managed private Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) for internal trust.

  • Internal Trust: Issue certificates trusted only within your organization, ideal for microservices and internal applications.

  • Managed Service: Highly available and secure, eliminating the complexities of self-managed CAs.

Key Takeaway

Private CA provides a secure, managed solution for issuing internal certificates, avoiding the pitfalls of self-signed certificates.
