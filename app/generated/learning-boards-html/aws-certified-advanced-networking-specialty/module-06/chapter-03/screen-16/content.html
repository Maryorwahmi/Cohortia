Key Concepts
Reinforcing Your Understanding of Firewall Manager
Centralized Control
Firewall Manager provides a single pane of glass for managing security policies across your entire AWS Organization.
Policy Diversity
Supports WAF, Shield Advanced, Security Groups, Network Firewall, and DNS Firewall for comprehensive security coverage.
Safe Deployment
Rigorous testing and change management are crucial to prevent unintended consequences from policy deployments.
Summary

Mastering Firewall Manager involves understanding its centralized control, diverse policy capabilities, and the critical importance of safe deployment practices like testing and change management.
