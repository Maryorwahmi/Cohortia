Best Practice
Testing and Change Management
Implement rigorous testing and change management processes to mitigate risks associated with deploying Firewall Manager policies across your organization.
  • Test policies thoroughly in staging/dev environments before production deployment.
  • Validate policy behavior, remediation actions, and unintended side effects.
  • Establish a formal change management process with approvals and rollback plans.
  • Document policies and their intended effects for auditing and troubleshooting.
  • A misconfigured policy can have wide-ranging negative impacts.
Key Takeaway

Rigorous testing in staging environments and strict change management processes are essential to mitigate risks and ensure the safe deployment of Firewall Manager policies.
