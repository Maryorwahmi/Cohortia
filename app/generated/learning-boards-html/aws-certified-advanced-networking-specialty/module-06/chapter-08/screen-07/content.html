Key Benefits of Egress Centralization

Enhancing security, management, and cost efficiency for outbound traffic.

Consistent Security Policy

All outbound traffic adheres to the same firewall rules, ensuring uniform compliance.

Reduced Attack Surface

Only the egress VPC has direct internet access, minimizing exposure to external threats.

Simplified Management

Security policies are managed from a single location, reducing operational complexity.

Cost Optimization

Shared firewall appliances and NAT Gateways can reduce overall infrastructure costs.

Enhanced Visibility

Centralized traffic logs facilitate easier auditing, threat detection, and compliance reporting.

Key Takeaway

Centralizing egress traffic significantly improves security posture, streamlines operations, and can lead to cost efficiencies by consolidating resources and management.
