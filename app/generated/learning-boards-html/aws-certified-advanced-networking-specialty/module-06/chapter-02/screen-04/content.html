Core Concept

The Importance of Edge Protection

Safeguarding Applications Where Traffic First Arrives

Why Edge Protection Matters

  • First Point of Contact: The internet-facing layer is the initial target for attacks.
  • Prevent Resource Exhaustion: Malicious traffic can overwhelm servers and databases.
  • Maintain Availability: DDoS attacks aim to disrupt legitimate user access.
  • Protect Data Integrity: Web exploits can lead to unauthorized data access or modification.
Concrete Example

Imagine a popular e-commerce site. Without edge protection, a sudden flood of fake requests (DDoS) or attempts to inject malicious code (SQLi) could bring the site down, costing millions in lost sales and reputation damage.
