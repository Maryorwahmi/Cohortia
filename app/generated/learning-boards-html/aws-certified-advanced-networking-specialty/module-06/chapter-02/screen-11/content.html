Shield Advanced Benefits

Cost Protection and the DDoS Response Team

Financial Safeguards and Expert Support During Attacks

Key Advantages

  • Cost Protection: Covers scaling charges for protected resources during DDoS attacks.
  • 24/7 DRT Access: Direct access to AWS DDoS Response Team experts for critical incidents.
  • Proactive Engagement: DRT helps investigate and mitigate attacks.
  • Peace of Mind: Reduces financial risk and provides expert support during high-stress events.
Concrete Example

During a massive DDoS attack, your ALB and EC2 instances auto-scale to handle the traffic. Without Shield Advanced, you'd pay for this increased usage. With it, AWS covers those costs, saving your business potentially hundreds of thousands of dollars.
