AWS Shield Advanced: Enhanced DDoS Protection

Sophisticated Defense for Critical Applications

Key Benefits of Shield Advanced

  • Enhanced Detection: More sophisticated capabilities against complex attacks.
  • Application-Layer (Layer 7): Stronger mitigation for HTTP floods and web exploits.
  • Broader Resource Coverage: EC2, ELB, CloudFront, Global Accelerator, Route 53.
  • Proactive Engagement: Access to AWS DDoS Response Team (DRT).
Concrete Example

A financial trading platform, a critical application, is targeted by a multi-vector DDoS attack combining SYN floods and sophisticated HTTP GET floods. Shield Advanced detects and mitigates both, ensuring continuous operation and protecting against financial losses.
