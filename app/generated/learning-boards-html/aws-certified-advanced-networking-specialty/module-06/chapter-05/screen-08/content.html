Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Create Endpoint Service
Step 1
Front your service with a Network Load Balancer
Register EC2 or container targets with the NLB so traffic from endpoint ENIs reaches your application.
Step 2
Create the VPC Endpoint Service
Point the service to the NLB and configure whether consumer requests require acceptance or can be whitelisted by account ID.
Step 3
Manage access and acceptance
Grant access to specific accounts or accept pending consumer endpoint requests. Keep permissions narrow to follow least privilege.
Security notes
  • Use NLB target controls and security groups to restrict allowed sources and ports.
  • Do not grant global access; prefer explicit account IDs or acceptance workflows.
  • Test with a consumer account to validate acceptance and traffic flow.
