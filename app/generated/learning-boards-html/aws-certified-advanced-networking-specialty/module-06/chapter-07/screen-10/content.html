GuardDuty Findings and Automated Responses

Prioritizing threats and orchestrating automated security actions

Responding to Findings

  • •
    Severity Levels: Findings are categorized (Low, Medium, High) for prioritization.
  • •
    EventBridge Integration: Findings can be sent to EventBridge for automated actions.
  • •
    Automated Actions: Trigger Lambda functions, SNS notifications, or SQS queues.
  • •
    Example: Isolate compromised EC2 instances or block suspicious IPs.
Key Takeaway

Automating responses to GuardDuty findings via EventBridge is crucial for rapid incident response and minimizing the impact of security threats.

Common Mistake

Not configuring automated responses to GuardDuty findings, leading to manual, delayed, and inconsistent incident handling.
