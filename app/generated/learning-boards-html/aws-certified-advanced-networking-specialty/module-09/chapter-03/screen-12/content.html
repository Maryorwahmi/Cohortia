Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › SSM Security
Instance profile
Attach only AmazonSSMManagedInstanceCore and any narrowly scoped additional permissions your documents require. Do not provide wide administrative rights to the instance profile.
Document & execution policies
Use IAM to restrict who can create, modify, or execute SSM Documents and associations. Apply separation of duties between operators and automation roles.
Interactive access
Prefer Session Manager over SSH for troubleshooting to avoid opening inbound ports. Session Manager sessions are logged and auditable, reducing risk during incident response.
