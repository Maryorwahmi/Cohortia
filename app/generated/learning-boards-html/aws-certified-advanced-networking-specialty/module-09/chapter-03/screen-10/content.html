Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Patch Manager
What it does
Patch Manager defines baselines, scans instances, and applies patches on a schedule. It's essential for fixing vulnerabilities in OS networking stacks and agents used for monitoring and routing.
Operational tips
  • Use maintenance windows when patches require reboots.
  • Segment patch groups to avoid mass reboots of critical network appliances.
  • Integrate with compliance reporting to track patch status.
Why it matters
Unpatched network software exposes you to known vulnerabilities that can be exploited to intercept, reroute, or disrupt traffic. Automated patching reduces that window of exposure and helps maintain network integrity across many instances.
Key takeaway
Include Patch Manager in your network automation plan to maintain a secure and consistent fleet, and plan for reboots and staged deployment to avoid operational impact.
