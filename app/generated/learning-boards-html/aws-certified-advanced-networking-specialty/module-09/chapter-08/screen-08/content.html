Hands-on Activity: Step 1

Create an SNS Topic and Subscribe

We'll use the AWS CLI to create a new SNS topic and subscribe your email address to it for receiving alerts.

Command: Create SNS Topic
aws sns create-topic --name NetworkAlerts --query "TopicArn" --output text
Expected Output (Example)
arn:aws:sns:us-east-1:123456789012:NetworkAlerts
Command: Subscribe Email to Topic
aws sns subscribe \
  --topic-arn YOUR_TOPIC_ARN \
  --protocol email \
  --notification-endpoint YOUR_EMAIL_ADDRESS
Important Note

Check your email and confirm the SNS subscription to activate notifications.

Key Takeaway

Creating an SNS topic and subscribing your email is the first step to receiving automated alerts from CloudWatch.
