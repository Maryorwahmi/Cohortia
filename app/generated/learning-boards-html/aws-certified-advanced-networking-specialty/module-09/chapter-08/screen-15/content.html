Key Concepts

Essential services for automated network operations.

Amazon CloudWatch

A monitoring and observability service providing data and actionable insights for AWS, hybrid, and on-premises resources.

CloudWatch Alarms

Objects that watch a single metric over time and perform actions based on its value relative to a threshold.

Amazon SNS

A highly available, durable, secure, fully managed pub/sub messaging service for automated notifications.

Summary Takeaway

CloudWatch, its Alarms, and SNS are foundational for building automated monitoring and alerting systems in AWS, ensuring timely detection and notification of network events.
