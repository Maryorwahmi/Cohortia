Amazon SNS: Your Alerting Hub

Amazon SNS is a fully managed pub/sub messaging service that enables automated notification delivery from CloudWatch Alarms.

  • Pub/Sub Model: Decouples publishers (Alarms) from subscribers (recipients).
  • Multiple Endpoints: Supports email, SMS, Lambda, SQS, HTTP/S, and more.
  • Reliable Delivery: Ensures alerts reach their destination even during peak loads.
