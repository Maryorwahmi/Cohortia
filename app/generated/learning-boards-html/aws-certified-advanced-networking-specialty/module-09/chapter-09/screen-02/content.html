Apply automated testing strategies for network IaC, including syntax validation, linting, and policy compliance checks. This proactive approach catches errors early, preventing costly issues in production.

Create robust rollback and change management strategies for network infrastructure deployments. This ensures business continuity and minimizes downtime in case of unforeseen issues or failed deployments.
