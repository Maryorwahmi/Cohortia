Adopting a DevOps Culture for Networking

Bridging the Gap Between Network and Development Teams

Cultural Shift Required

Moving from traditional 'ticket-based' network changes to a 'code-based' approach demands a significant cultural shift. Network engineers must embrace scripting, IaC, and version control, fundamentally changing their daily operations.

Enhanced Collaboration

Successful CI/CD for networking requires paramount collaboration between network, security, and development teams. Breaking down silos fosters shared responsibility and a more integrated approach to infrastructure management.

Key Takeaway

Adopting CI/CD for networking is a cultural journey requiring new skills, cross-team collaboration, and strong leadership support for successful transformation.

Key Enablers

  • Clear Roles: Define responsibilities for IaC development and review.
  • Coding Standards: Establish guidelines for IaC templates.
  • Training: Invest in upskilling network engineers in scripting and IaC.
  • Leadership Buy-in: Essential for driving and sustaining the change.

Common Mistake: Lack of Support

Trying to force traditional network engineers into a DevOps model without providing adequate training, resources, and leadership support often leads to resistance, frustration, and ultimately, ineffective adoption of CI/CD practices.

Example: Joint IaC Review

A network team and a security team jointly review CloudFormation templates for new firewall rules. The network team ensures functionality, while the security team verifies compliance with security policies, fostering a shared understanding and ownership of the infrastructure.
