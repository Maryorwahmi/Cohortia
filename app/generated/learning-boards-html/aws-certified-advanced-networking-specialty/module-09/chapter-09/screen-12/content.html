Robust Rollback Strategies for Network IaC

Ensuring Recovery from Failed Deployments

Importance of Rollback

Even with extensive testing, deployments can fail or introduce unforeseen issues. A clear rollback strategy is essential to revert to a previous stable state, minimizing downtime and ensuring business continuity.

CloudFormation Rollback

CloudFormation has built-in rollback capabilities. If a stack update fails, it automatically attempts to revert to the previous stable state, providing a crucial safety net for network infrastructure changes.

Key Takeaway

Always have a well-defined and tested rollback strategy to ensure rapid recovery and minimize the impact of failed network infrastructure deployments.

Terraform Rollback

Terraform rollbacks are more manual. They typically involve reverting your IaC to a previous version in your VCS, carefully managing state files, and then running `terraform apply` to revert the infrastructure. This requires careful planning and execution.

Misconception: Rollback is Automatic for All IaC

While CloudFormation offers automatic rollbacks, other IaC tools like Terraform require explicit strategies for managing state and reverting changes. Always understand the rollback mechanisms specific to your chosen IaC tool.

Example: Recovery Plan

Before deploying a new Transit Gateway attachment, the team defines a recovery plan: if the attachment fails to establish BGP peering, they will revert the CloudFormation stack to its previous state, ensuring the network remains operational.
