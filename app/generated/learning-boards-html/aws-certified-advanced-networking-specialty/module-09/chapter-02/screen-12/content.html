Best Practices Template Hygiene
Nested Stacks
Break your VPC, subnets and security groups into child templates referenced by a parent to improve reuse and manage updates safely.
Custom Resources
Use Lambda-backed custom resources for tasks CloudFormation cannot do natively, such as third-party firewall configuration, but keep them focused and tested.
Validation & CI/CD
Validate templates before deployment and integrate checks into CI pipelines so templates are reviewed and tested prior to production changes.
Key Takeaway
Modularity, automation, validation, and version control are the operational foundations that keep IaC for networking stable and auditable.
