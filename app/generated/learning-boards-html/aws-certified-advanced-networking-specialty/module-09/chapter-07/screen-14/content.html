Key Concepts: Hybrid Connectivity Services

Essential terms for automating your hybrid cloud network.

AWS Site-to-Site VPN

A secure, encrypted connection between your on-premises network and your AWS VPCs.

Customer Gateway (CGW)

An AWS resource representing your on-premises VPN device.

Virtual Private Gateway (VPG)

An AWS resource connecting your VPC to your on-premises network via VPN.

AWS Direct Connect (DX)

A dedicated network connection from your premises to AWS for higher bandwidth and lower latency.

Direct Connect Gateway (DXGW)

A global resource connecting DX to multiple VPCs in different regions or to Transit Gateways.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering these core AWS hybrid connectivity services is the foundation for building robust and automated network solutions between your on-premises infrastructure and the cloud.
