CloudFormation for Direct Connect Gateways

Automate the creation and association of Direct Connect Gateways with CloudFormation.

# CloudFormation snippet for Direct Connect Gateway
Resources:
  MyDirectConnectGateway:
    Type: AWS::DirectConnect::DirectConnectGateway
    Properties:
      DirectConnectGatewayName: MyHybridDXGW
      AmazonSideAsn: 64512 # Your AWS side ASN
      Tags:
        - Key: Name
          Value: MyHybridDXGW

  MyGatewayAssociation:
    Type: AWS::DirectConnect::GatewayAssociation
    Properties:
      AssociationGatewayId: !Ref MyVPG # Or TransitGatewayId
      DirectConnectGatewayId: !Ref MyDirectConnectGateway
      AssociatedGatewayOwnerAccount: !Ref AWS::AccountId # Or other account ID

Explanation of Resources

  • •`AWS::DirectConnect::DirectConnectGateway`: Creates a new Direct Connect Gateway.
  • •`DirectConnectGatewayName`: A descriptive name for your DXGW.
  • •`AmazonSideAsn`: The ASN used by AWS for BGP peering with your on-premises network.
  • •`AWS::DirectConnect::GatewayAssociation`: Links the DXGW to a VPG or Transit Gateway.

Concrete Example: Cross-Region Connectivity

A company has a Direct Connect connection in `us-east-1` but needs to reach VPCs in `us-west-2`. By creating a DXGW and associating it with VPGs in both regions (or a Transit Gateway), CloudFormation ensures this cross-region connectivity is established automatically and consistently, without manual console steps.

Key Takeaway

CloudFormation simplifies the provisioning and association of Direct Connect Gateways, enabling automated, centralized management of hybrid cloud connectivity across regions and accounts.

Consideration

For cross-account associations, ensure the `AssociatedGatewayOwnerAccount` is correctly specified to grant permissions.
