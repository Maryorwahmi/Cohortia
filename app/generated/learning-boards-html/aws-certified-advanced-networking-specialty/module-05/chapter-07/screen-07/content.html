High Availability
Cross-Zone Load Balancing
Distributing traffic evenly across all Availability Zones.
ALB Behavior

Always enabled by default. Distributes traffic evenly across all healthy targets in all AZs.

NLB Behavior
  • Disabled by default: routes traffic only within the same AZ.
  • Enabled: distributes traffic evenly across all healthy targets in all AZs.
  • Enabling incurs additional data transfer charges.
Key Consideration

For NLBs, enabling cross-zone load balancing enhances availability but comes with extra data transfer costs. Choose based on your availability needs and budget.
