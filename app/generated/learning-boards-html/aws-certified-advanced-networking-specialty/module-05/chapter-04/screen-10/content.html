Content Restriction

Securing Private Content with Signed URLs and Cookies

Granting time-limited access to restricted files through CloudFront.

Signed URLs

Time-limited URLs that grant temporary access to a single private file. Ideal for one-time downloads or specific content access.

Signed Cookies

Provide access to multiple restricted files for a defined period, typically used for subscription services or premium content libraries.

Key Takeaway

Signed URLs and cookies are powerful tools for securely delivering private content, ensuring only authorized users can access specific resources for a limited time.

Signed Content Access Comparison
Signed URLs
  • Scope: Single file
  • Duration: Specific time limit
  • Use Case: One-time downloads, email links
Signed Cookies
  • Scope: Multiple files/paths
  • Duration: Session-based or defined period
  • Use Case: Premium content libraries, subscription access
