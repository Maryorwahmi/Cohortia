Key Concepts: CloudFront Fundamentals

Recap of essential terms and components for content delivery.

Content Delivery Network (CDN)

A global network of servers that caches content closer to users for faster delivery.

Edge Location (PoP)

Worldwide data centers where CloudFront caches content copies to serve users with low latency.

Distribution

The CloudFront resource that defines how content is delivered, including origins and behaviors.

Origin

The source of your content, such as an S3 bucket, EC2 instance, or Application Load Balancer.

Summary Insight

CloudFront's global network and configurable distributions enable efficient and scalable content delivery from various origins.
