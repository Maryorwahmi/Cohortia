Chapter Review

Key Concepts: Availability & Internal DNS

Recapping health checks and private hosted zones.

Failover Routing

Routes traffic to a primary resource if healthy, otherwise to a secondary, ensuring high availability.

Route 53 Health Checks

Monitors the health of specified endpoints or other health checks, informing routing policies for automated failover.

Private Hosted Zone

A container for DNS records resolvable only within specified Amazon VPCs, crucial for internal service discovery.

Summary Takeaway

Effective use of failover routing, granular health checks, and private hosted zones ensures robust application availability and secure internal DNS resolution in AWS and hybrid environments.
