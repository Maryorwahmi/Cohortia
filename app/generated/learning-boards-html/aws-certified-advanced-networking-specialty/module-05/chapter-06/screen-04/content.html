Why Cross-Region Connectivity Matters

Connecting VPCs across different AWS regions is crucial for building resilient, globally distributed applications and ensuring robust disaster recovery.

Disaster Recovery

Establish standby environments in separate regions for rapid failover and business continuity.

Global Application Deployments

Serve users with low latency by deploying application components closer to them.

Shared Resource Access

Enable regional deployments to access centralized services like logging or identity management.
