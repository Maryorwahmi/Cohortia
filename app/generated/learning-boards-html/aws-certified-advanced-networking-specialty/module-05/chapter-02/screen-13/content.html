DNS Firewall Scope and Best Practices

Understanding where DNS Firewall applies and how to implement it effectively.

Scope of Protection

  • Applies to queries resolved by the VPC's Route 53 Resolver.
  • Includes queries forwarded via Outbound Endpoints.
  • **Does NOT** filter queries directly forwarded to Inbound Endpoints (on-premises to AWS).

Implementation Strategy

  • **Start with `ALERT` rules:** Monitor impact before blocking.
  • **Regularly review:** Check domain lists and CloudWatch logs.
  • **Avoid disruption:** Plan carefully to prevent blocking legitimate traffic.

Continuous Monitoring & Refinement

Use CloudWatch logs to audit and refine your DNS Firewall rules for optimal security.
