Architecture

AWS & On-prem building blocks

  • VGW: attach a Site-to-Site VPN to a VPC for direct termination.
  • Transit Gateway: central hub for many VPNs and VPCs for scalable routing.
  • Direct Connect Gateway: bridge Direct Connect circuits to VGWs or Transit Gateway.
  • Customer Gateway (CGW): represents your on-prem device in AWS; use multiple CGWs for redundancy.
On-Prem Considerations
Redundant Devices
Provision two routers or VPN devices across separate racks or sites.
Diverse ISPs
Connect CGWs to different ISPs to mitigate provider outages.
BGP
Enable BGP for automatic failover and dynamic route exchange.
IP Planning
Avoid overlapping CIDRs between on-premises and VPCs to prevent routing conflicts.
Key Takeaway
Document AWS components and on-prem devices, validate BGP and firewall rules in staging, and ensure IP addressing is conflict-free.
