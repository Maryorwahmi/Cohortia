Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › VPN vs Direct Connect
Comparison

Site-to-Site VPN

  • Pros: fast deployment, pay-as-you-go, encrypted by default, uses existing internet link.
  • Cons: variable internet performance, throughput limits per tunnel, requires on-prem VPN device.
  • Best for: moderate bandwidth, rapid deployment, backups, small branches, dev/test.
Concrete Example
A short-term migration uses VPN to securely move test traffic while a Direct Connect circuit is provisioned for production.
Comparison

AWS Direct Connect

  • Pros: dedicated private connection, consistent performance, lower latency, high throughput.
  • Cons: longer provisioning time, higher cost, requires colocation at a Direct Connect location.
  • Best for: high bandwidth, low latency, large data transfers, strict compliance.
Concrete Example
A production database replication stream uses Direct Connect for predictable latency and throughput.
Hybrid Best Practice
Use Direct Connect as primary for mission-critical performance and Site-to-Site VPN as encrypted failover to ensure business continuity when the primary circuit fails.
