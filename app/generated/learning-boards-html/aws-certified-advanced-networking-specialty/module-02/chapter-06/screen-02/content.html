Learning Objectives

Learn, Understand, Apply, Master

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Evaluate VPN Use Cases
Match Site-to-Site VPN characteristics to workload needs and decide when VPN is appropriate.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Compare VPN and Direct Connect
Contrast latency, throughput, provisioning time, and cost to choose the right connection.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design HA VPN Architectures
Use dual tunnels, multiple CGWs, and Transit Gateway for resilience against failures.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Implement Security Best Practices
Enforce strong PSKs, current IPsec parameters, strict firewall rules, and periodic audits.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Use this checklist as you practice: evaluate fit, design for failure, secure cryptography, and monitor cost drivers.
