When to centralize with Transit Gateway

Transit Gateway provides a central hub for many VPNs and VPCs, enabling simplified routing and any-to-any connectivity where appropriate.

Benefits
Simpler routing, scalable attachments, consistent management of many connections.
Considerations
Transit Gateway has costs; evaluate traffic patterns to ensure benefits outweigh expenses.
Quick Scenario
If you manage dozens of small branch VPNs, attach them to a Transit Gateway to avoid complex peering and per-VPC routing tables. Combine with Direct Connect gateway for consistent private connectivity.
Key Takeaway
Transit Gateway simplifies scale and routing, but weigh costs and traffic before consolidating.
