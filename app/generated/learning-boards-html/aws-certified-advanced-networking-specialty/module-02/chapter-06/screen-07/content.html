Security

Hardening Site-to-Site VPN

  • Use long, unique pre-shared keys per tunnel and rotate them on a schedule.
  • Configure strong IPsec parameters: AES-256, SHA256, and a strong Diffie-Hellman group supported by both ends.
  • Apply strict firewall rules and security groups that follow least privilege principle.
  • Restrict administrative access with IAM roles and limit who can manage VPN resources and CGWs.
  • Audit configurations and logs periodically to ensure cryptography and rules remain up to date.
Practical Controls
PSK Hygiene
Generate unique keys and store them securely; avoid reuse across tunnels.
Crypto Parameters
Prefer AES-256 and SHA256; review parameters as standards evolve.
Firewall Rules
Allow only necessary ports and source/destination ranges across both sides.
Audits
Schedule periodic checks of configs, logs, and key rotation status.
Example
Rotate PSKs during a maintenance window and validate both tunnels re-establish with BGP before returning to production.
