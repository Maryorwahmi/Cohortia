Hybrid Approach
Direct Connect primary, VPN as encrypted backup or for less critical links.
Disaster Recovery
Use VPN as a cost-effective replication or fallback path for DR scenarios.
AWS Direct Connect
Dedicated private link for consistent performance and high throughput.
Summary Takeaway

Choose connectivity based on performance needs, cost, and timeline; always design redundancy and monitor Data Transfer Out.
