Use Cases and Best Practices

Understand when Site-to-Site VPN is right, how to make it resilient and secure, and how to control costs in hybrid architectures.

Duration: ~10 min presentation
In this lesson
  • Identify practical VPN use cases: dev/test, DR, branch offices, temporary links.
  • Contrast VPN and Direct Connect for workload fit.
  • Design highly available VPN architectures and security controls.
Why it matters

Picking the right hybrid connectivity protects performance, cost, and continuity. VPN provides fast, encrypted options that can be hardened for production when designed carefully.

Getting Started
Think about one service that needs predictable performance. Would you use Direct Connect or VPN? Why?
Tip: small branches and short-lived projects favor VPN; mission-critical, high-throughput workloads favor Direct Connect.
