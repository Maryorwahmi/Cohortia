Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › Change Management
Process

Safe operational practices

  1. Document the change and prepare a rollback plan.
  2. Test in a staging environment that mirrors production.
  3. Verify both tunnels establish and BGP failover behavior before deployment.
  4. Schedule an approved maintenance window and communicate impacts.
  5. Audit after the change and confirm monitoring is healthy.
Common Mistakes
Overlapping IPs, weak pre-shared keys, no redundancy for on-prem devices, and leaving unused VPNs active are frequent causes of outages and unnecessary cost.
Example
A team deployed a new VPC that overlapped with on-prem IPs and caused immediate routing failures; the lesson is to validate CIDRs early.
Key Takeaway
Follow disciplined change management, test failover, and avoid address overlaps to reduce risk.
