Resilient Hybrid Connectivity — Activity

Apply the chapter patterns to design a production-ready solution.

Task checklist
Primary
Direct Connect
Justify for predictable latency and high throughput.
Backup
Site-to-Site VPN
Encrypted over internet, used as failover when Direct Connect is unavailable.
Redundancy
Multiple CGWs & ISPs
Separate devices and providers to remove single points of failure.
Security Actions
Unique PSKs, AES-256, SHA256, strict firewall rules, IAM restrictions, and audits.
Cost & Monitoring
Monitor TunnelDataOut, consolidate with Transit Gateway, and evaluate Direct Connect if DTO grows.
Deliverable
1. Primary: Direct Connect — justification
2. Backup: Site-to-Site VPN — configuration notes
3. AWS components: VGW/Transit Gateway, Direct Connect Gateway
4. On-prem: 2 CGWs, diverse ISPs, BGP config
5. Security: PSK, IPsec params, firewall rules
6. Monitoring: TunnelDataOut, tests, cost review
