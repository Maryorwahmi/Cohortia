High Availability
Dual tunnels, multiple CGWs, and diverse ISPs reduce outages.
Security Best Practices
Strong PSKs, current IPsec algos, strict firewall rules, and audits.
Cost Optimization
Monitor DTO, consolidate traffic, and evaluate Direct Connect when appropriate.
Final Takeaway

Protect connectivity with redundancy, secure it with strong cryptography and access controls, and manage costs by measuring data egress and consolidating where sensible.
