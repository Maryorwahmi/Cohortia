Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Route Propagation & BGP
How it works
When BGP is established between your on-premises device and the VGW, the CGW advertises prefixes and the VGW learns them. Enabling route propagation on a VPC route table copies those learned prefixes into the table with the VGW as the target.
Enable propagation (CLI)
aws ec2 enable-vpn-gateway-route-propagation \
  --route-table-id rtb-0fedcba9876543210 \
  --vpn-gateway-id vgw-0abcdef1234567890
Benefits & cautions
  • Automates route updates and reduces manual errors
  • Provides automatic failover when BGP withdraws prefixes
  • Requires BGP sessions to be established and healthy
Misconception: propagation does not replace verifying on-prem advertisements. You must confirm the CGW advertises the expected CIDRs.
Key Takeaway

Use propagation to simplify management and enable dynamic reachability, but always validate BGP advertisements and session state.
