Scenario values
On‑prem ASN: 65001 • On‑prem public IP: 203.0.113.10 • AWS VGW public IP (Tunnel1): 52.X.Y.Z • PSK: MyStrongPSK123 • Tunnel IP (on‑prem): 169.254.20.2/30 • AWS tunnel IP: 169.254.20.1/30 • Advertise: 10.10.0.0/16
Template
# IKE
IKE_ENCRYPTION=AES-256
IKE_HASH=SHA256
IKE_DH=Group 14
IKE_LIFETIME=28800

# IPsec
ESP_ENCRYPTION=AES-256
ESP_HASH=SHA256
ESP_PFS=Group 14
ESP_LIFETIME=3600

# Tunnel 1
TUNNEL1_AWS_VGW_PUBLIC_IP=52.X.Y.Z
TUNNEL1_PSK=MyStrongPSK123
TUNNEL1_ONPREM_TUNNEL_IP=169.254.20.2/30
TUNNEL1_AWS_TUNNEL_IP=169.254.20.1/30

# BGP
ONPREM_ASN=65001
AWS_ASN=64512
ADVERTISE=10.10.0.0/16
Guidance

After substituting values into the template, configure the device and verify IKE and IPsec states. Then enable BGP and confirm the VPC CIDRs appear in your device route table.

Check your work
  • PSKs copied without typos
  • Tunnel IPs configured as /30s
  • BGP neighbor addresses match tunnel inside IPs
