Detailed objectives

Each objective ties to a practical task: create the CGW resource, choose routing, configure two tunnels with matching IKE/IPsec, and verify routes and connectivity after the tunnels are up.

Mnemonic
IP, ASN, IKE, IPsec — remember the pillars you must specify and validate.
Assessment hint
Be prepared to explain why a static public IP is required and how BGP enables automatic failover.
