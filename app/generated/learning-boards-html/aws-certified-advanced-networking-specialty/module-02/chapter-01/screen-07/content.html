Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › IPsec & IKE
IPsec roles
  • Confidentiality: encryption of payload (ESP).
  • Integrity: verification that packets were not altered.
  • Authentication: confirming the communicating parties.
AWS Site-to-Site VPN primarily uses ESP for encryption and authentication, while IKE negotiates the cryptographic keys and SA parameters used by ESP.
Misconception check
Encryption alone does not guarantee connectivity. If IKE negotiations fail due to mismatched algorithms or lifetimes, tunnels will not establish, even if keys are present. Also confirm NAT handling so UDP 500 and 4500 are not blocked.
Key Takeaway

IKE and ESP work together: IKE negotiates keys and parameters; ESP uses them to encrypt and authenticate traffic.
