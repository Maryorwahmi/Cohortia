Validation checklist
  • Confirm public-facing IP for the on-premises VPN device; it must match the Customer Gateway entry in AWS.
  • Ensure UDP ports 500 and 4500 are allowed through firewalls and NAT devices for IKE and IPsec NAT traversal.
  • Verify matching IPsec parameters and the pre-shared key on both sides before attempting negotiation.
  • Propagate or add routes so AWS VPC route tables and on-premises routers know each other’s CIDRs.
Practical hints
Use Site-to-Site VPN for moderate bandwidth and quick deployments. Use Direct Connect where dedicated capacity and consistent latency are required. Consider VPN as a backup to Direct Connect to preserve availability.
