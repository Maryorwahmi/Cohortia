Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Connectivity Options
Site-to-Site VPN
  • Encrypted tunnels over the public internet using IPsec and IKE.
  • Quick to deploy and cost-effective for moderate bandwidth.
  • Performance subject to internet variability; ideal for backups and temporary links.
When to choose VPN: moderate bandwidth, fast setup, or backup to Direct Connect.
Direct Connect & Transit Gateway
  • Direct Connect: private dedicated links with predictable throughput and lower latency.
  • Transit Gateway: centralizes routing across multiple VPCs and on-premises attachments.
  • Typically higher setup time and cost but better performance for production workloads.
When to choose Direct Connect: sustained high throughput and low latency needs.
Comparison at a glance
Bandwidth: VPN — moderate; Direct Connect — high
Latency: VPN — variable; Direct Connect — low
Setup time: VPN — quick; Direct Connect — longer
