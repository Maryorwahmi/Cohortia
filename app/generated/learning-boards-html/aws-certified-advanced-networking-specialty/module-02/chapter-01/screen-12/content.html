Redundancy patterns
  • Two IPsec tunnels per VPN for endpoint redundancy.
  • Use VPN as backup to Direct Connect for resilience.
  • Use BGP for faster, automated route failover when possible.
Failover test
Simulate a tunnel outage, observe route withdrawal or static route change, and validate traffic uses the alternate path. Record metrics for failover time and packet loss to verify SLAs.
Why this matters
Proper HA design prevents single points of failure. Redundancy plus routing intelligence ensures your hybrid network remains available when components fail.
