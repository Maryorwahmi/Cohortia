Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
Hybrid Cloud Networking
Connects on-premises and cloud to meet compliance, migration and resilience goals.
AWS Site-to-Site VPN
Encrypted IPsec tunnels over the internet; quick to deploy and useful as primary or backup links.
VGW & CGW
VGW is the AWS endpoint attached to a VPC; CGW represents your on-premises device and optional BGP ASN.
Summary Takeaway

Match IPsec/IKE parameters, verify public IP and ports, choose routing mode intentionally, and test failover paths to ensure a reliable hybrid network.
