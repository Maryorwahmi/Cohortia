Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Enable Accelerated VPN
Step-by-step
  1. Record baseline CloudWatch metrics and application performance.
  2. Enable Accelerated VPN in the VPN creation or modification workflow.
  3. Re-run measurements and compare TunnelData and latency for improvement.
  4. Confirm tunnels and BGP sessions remain stable and update runbooks.
Validation & pitfalls
After enabling, ensure UDP ports and NAT settings still permit IKE and NAT-T. Baseline measurements let you justify the added cost. If you see no improvement, revert and investigate internet path characteristics.
Example metric: compare average tunnel latency and jitter before and after enabling.
Pitfall: enabling without testing leaves you unable to show benefit versus cost.
