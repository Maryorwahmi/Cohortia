Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Intermittent Performance
Symptoms & causes
  • Internet congestion causing latency and packet loss.
  • Tunnel flapping due to unstable transit, DPD timers, or overloaded devices.
  • MTU mismatch causing fragmentation and throughput reduction.
Diagnose & mitigate
Check device CPU/memory, tune DPD timers, test different MTU values, and consider Accelerated VPN to route traffic over the AWS backbone when internet paths are the problem.
Example: MTU lowered to 1400 resolved fragmentation and increased throughput for IPsec traffic.
