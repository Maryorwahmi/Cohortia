Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Attach VPCs & Select Subnets
Definition

VPC Attachment and TGW ENIs

Choose subnets in multiple AZs; AWS will create ENIs there to carry traffic.

Why two subnets minimum?
High availability and avoidance of single-AZ failure modes for TGW traffic ingress/egress.
What the ENI does
Acts as the TGW endpoint inside your VPC subnet for routed traffic to and from the TGW.

Example
Select subnets in AZ-a and AZ-b when creating the TGW attachment for each VPC.
Common mistake
Selecting subnets only in a single AZ reduces resilience and can cause routing issues during AZ failures.
Key Takeaway

Choose multiple subnets in different AZs so the TGW creates ENIs for high availability and predictable routing.
