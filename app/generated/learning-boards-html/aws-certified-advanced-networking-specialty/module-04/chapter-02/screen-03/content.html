Objectives: Verification & Pitfalls

Each objective paired with how to confirm success and a common pitfall to avoid.

Create TGW — Verify
  • Confirm TransitGatewayId and AmazonSideAsn in the console.
  • Check DNS Support or VPN ECMP flags in the TGW options.
Pitfall: Choosing an ASN that conflicts with on-prem networks.
Attach VPCs — Verify
  • Confirm ENIs in at least two subnets across AZs.
  • Ensure subnets are not private-only if you need reachability during testing.
Pitfall: Selecting only one AZ and reducing availability.
Routing — Verify
  • Check TGW route table propagation for attached CIDRs.
  • Add VPC route entries for remote CIDRs pointing to the Transit Gateway ID.
Pitfall: Assuming propagation alone enables VPC-to-VPC traffic.
RAM — Verify
  • Confirm the Resource Share includes the TGW and recipient account IDs or OUs.
  • Ensure recipients accept the share before creating attachments.
Pitfall: Forgetting to accept the share in the recipient account.
Practical Mnemonic

ASN → Attach → Propagate → Route → Verify
