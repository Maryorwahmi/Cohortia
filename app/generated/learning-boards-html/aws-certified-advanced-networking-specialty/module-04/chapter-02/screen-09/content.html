Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Amazon Side ASN
Definition

Amazon Side ASN

An ASN the Transit Gateway advertises during BGP peering with on-prem routers or Direct Connect gateways.

Guidance
  • Use a private ASN (64512–65534) for internal AWS TGWs.
  • Document the ASN before creating BGP sessions.
Misconception: ASN is optional for VPN-only setups. Even then, if you later add BGP or Direct Connect, ASN choice becomes important.
Concrete Example
Pick AmazonSideAsn=64512 when creating the TGW if you do not have a public ASN requirement. Record it so on-prem teams know what number the TGW will advertise when a VPN or Direct Connect BGP session is created.
Key Takeaway

Choose and document the TGW ASN early to prevent BGP conflicts when integrating with on-premises networks.
