Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Share TGW with RAM
Steps in the owner account
  1. Create Resource Share and include the Transit Gateway.
  2. Add recipient account IDs or Organization OUs as principals.
  3. Send the share; wait for acceptance.
Pitfall: Assuming attachments can be created before the recipient accepts the share. Acceptance is required.
Steps in the recipient account
  1. Accept the resource share in the RAM console.
  2. Create a Transit Gateway attachment from your VPC to the shared TGW.
  3. Associate/propagate the attachment to the appropriate TGW route table.
Key Takeaway

Centralize TGW ownership and share access with RAM; recipients must accept before attachments can be created.
