Advanced Integration

Integrating Third-Party Firewall Appliances

Deploying virtual firewalls for deep traffic inspection.

Why Third-Party Firewalls?

  • •**Deep Packet Inspection (DPI):** Analyze traffic content beyond basic headers.
  • •**Intrusion Prevention/Detection (IPS/IDS):** Identify and block malicious traffic patterns.
  • •**Advanced Threat Protection:** Protection against zero-day exploits and sophisticated attacks.
  • •**Compliance Requirements:** Meet specific regulatory or corporate compliance mandates.
Deployment Strategy

Deploy firewalls in a dedicated Security VPC, often in an auto-scaling group for high availability and scalability.
