Transit Virtual Interface (Transit VIF)

Connecting Direct Connect to DXGW

What is a Transit VIF?

A Transit Virtual Interface is a type of Direct Connect virtual interface specifically used to connect a Direct Connect connection to a Direct Connect Gateway. It's essential for enabling route exchange with the DXGW.

Key Role

Acts as the bridge between your physical Direct Connect link and the global Direct Connect Gateway.

Distinction

Different from a Private VIF, which connects directly to a single VGW. Transit VIF is for DXGW.

Key Takeaway

A Transit VIF is the specific virtual interface type required to connect your Direct Connect to a Direct Connect Gateway.
