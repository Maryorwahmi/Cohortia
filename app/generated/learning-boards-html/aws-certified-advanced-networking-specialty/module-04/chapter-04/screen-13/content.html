Common Pitfalls in Hybrid Connectivity

Avoiding Configuration Mistakes

ASN Mismatches

Incorrect or duplicate Autonomous System Numbers (ASNs) for TGW and Customer Gateway can prevent BGP peering from establishing, leading to routing failures.

Pro Tip

Always verify unique and correct ASNs for all BGP-enabled connections.

Security Group/NACL Rules

Even with correct routing, restrictive Security Group or Network ACL rules within VPCs can block traffic from on-premises networks. These act as critical firewalls.

Checklist Item

Ensure SG/NACLs permit traffic from your on-premises CIDR ranges and required ports.

Key Takeaway

Proactive management of ASNs, security rules, and IP addressing is crucial to prevent common hybrid connectivity issues.
