TGW Pricing Model: The Two Pillars

Understanding Attachment Hourly Charges and Data Processing Costs

Attachment Hourly Charge

A predictable hourly rate for each active attachment (VPC, VPN, DX Gateway, peering) to your Transit Gateway. This cost is stable and based on your network architecture.

Data Processing Charge

A variable cost incurred for every gigabyte of data processed by the Transit Gateway when it moves between attachments. This is often the more significant cost driver.

Key Idea

TGW costs are driven by predictable hourly attachment charges and variable data processing charges, with data processing often being the larger and more dynamic component.

Example Scenario

Imagine a central TGW connecting 5 VPCs. You pay 5 hourly attachment charges. If VPC-A sends 50GB to VPC-B, and VPC-C sends 20GB to VPC-D, you're charged for 70GB of data processing. The attachment cost is fixed, but data processing varies with traffic.

Key Takeaway

Focus on optimizing data flow through TGW, as data processing charges are the primary variable cost driver.
