Key Concepts: Cost Drivers & Optimization

Essential Takeaways for Managing TGW Expenses

TGW Attachment Hourly Charge

A recurring, predictable cost for each active connection to the Transit Gateway.

TGW Data Processing Charge

The variable cost incurred for every gigabyte of data that traverses the Transit Gateway between attachments.

Cost Optimization Strategies

Methods to reduce TGW-related expenses, often by minimizing data processed or optimizing attachment usage.

Summary Takeaway

Effective TGW cost management hinges on understanding both fixed attachment costs and variable data processing fees, and applying targeted strategies to minimize unnecessary data traversal.
