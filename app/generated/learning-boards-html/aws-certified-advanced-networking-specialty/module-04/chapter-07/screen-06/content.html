Cost Optimization Strategy

Strategic VPC Peering vs. TGW

Balancing Cost and Complexity for Inter-VPC Traffic

Minimize East-West Traffic

For specific high-bandwidth, low-latency VPC-to-VPC communication that doesn't need central security, consider direct VPC peering.

VPC Peering Benefit

Direct VPC peering connections do not incur Transit Gateway data processing charges, potentially saving costs for specific traffic flows.

The Complexity Trade-off

VPC peering reintroduces N*(N-1)/2 complexity for many-to-many connections, making management difficult as your network scales.

Key Idea

While TGW offers operational simplicity, targeted VPC peering can be a cost-effective alternative for very specific, high-volume inter-VPC traffic that doesn't require TGW's advanced routing or centralized security.

When to Consider Peering

Only for isolated, high-bandwidth connections between two VPCs where the traffic doesn't need to pass through a shared service VPC (e.g., a security VPC with firewalls).

Key Takeaway

Prioritize TGW for operational simplicity and advanced routing; use VPC peering only after careful cost-benefit analysis for specific traffic patterns.
