Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Objectives — Skills and Reasoning

From concept to action

Each objective maps to a concrete design or operational check you can run during network planning.

  • Use the peering formula to decide when mesh peering is no longer sustainable for your N VPCs.
  • Map attachments to ownership boundaries: which account or VPC owns a given attachment.
  • Choose when to propagate routes versus statically add them to avoid over-sharing access.
  • Plan CIDR ranges proactively to prevent routing conflicts across attachments.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY
Design readiness
You will be prepared to document a TGW deployment plan with attachments, route tables, and CIDR assignments.
