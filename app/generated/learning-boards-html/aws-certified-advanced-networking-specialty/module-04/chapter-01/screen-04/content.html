Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Why VPC Peering Doesn't Scale
Comparison

Mesh peering vs operational reality

Peering gives direct connectivity but scales poorly in connection count and operations.

# Peering count formula
N = number of VPCs
peering_connections = N * (N - 1) / 2

# Examples
N = 5  -> 5*4/2 = 10
N = 10 -> 10*9/2 = 45
N = 15 -> 15*14/2 = 105
Example
A shared-services VPC needing connections from 10 app VPCs would require 10 peering links to reach each app VPC — and additional peerings between apps if they also need east-west traffic.
Common misconception
Some assume peering remains simplest as you grow. In practice, operational overhead and route management make peering unsustainable past a small number of VPCs.
Consequences
  • Many route table entries across accounts and VPCs
  • Harder auditing and change coordination
  • No native transitive routing through a peering connection
Key takeaway

Use the peering formula as a threshold: when connections and operations become the bottleneck, consider a Transit Gateway hub.
