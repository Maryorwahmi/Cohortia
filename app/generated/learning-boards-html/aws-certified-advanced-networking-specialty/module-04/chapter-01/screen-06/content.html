Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Attachments: Types and Ownership
Definition

Attachments

Logical connections from resources into the Transit Gateway. They represent where traffic enters or leaves the TGW.

VPC attachment
Connects a VPC to the TGW so that its subnets can send and receive routed traffic.
VPN attachment
Represents a Site-to-Site VPN connection from on-premises to the TGW.
Direct Connect Gateway
Used for private, high-bandwidth connections via Direct Connect.
Operational notes
  • Attachments are created by resource owners; TGW owner controls accept/auto-accept options.
  • Document attachment ownership and who configures associations/propagations.
  • Auto-accept shared attachments is a setting to streamline multi-account setups.
Scenario
Central network account owns the TGW. App teams create VPC attachments and request acceptance. Network team manages TGW route tables and associations.
Key takeaway
Clear ownership and attachment workflows prevent delays and misconfigurations when integrating many accounts and teams.
