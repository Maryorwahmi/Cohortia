Leveraging VPC Flow Logs

VPC Flow Logs provide granular visibility into IP traffic, helping to diagnose connectivity issues.

What They Capture

Source/destination IP, port, protocol, bytes, packets, and the 'action' (ACCEPT/REJECT).

Debugging with Flow Logs

Analyze logs in CloudWatch or S3 to see if traffic reaches TGW ENIs and if it's accepted or rejected by security controls.

Key Takeaway

VPC Flow Logs are essential for deep traffic analysis; always enable them and look for 'REJECT' actions to quickly identify security group or NACL blocks.
