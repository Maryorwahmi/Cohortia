Hybrid Troubleshooting Checklist

Verify BGP status, advertised routes, NAT, and on-premises firewalls for seamless hybrid connectivity.

BGP Status & Routes

Confirm BGP peering is UP and routes are correctly advertised/propagated on both sides.

NAT & On-Prem Firewalls

Verify on-premises NAT configurations and firewall rules are not blocking traffic.

Key Takeaway

Hybrid connectivity troubleshooting requires a dual-sided approach, verifying BGP, advertised routes, NAT, and firewalls on both AWS and on-premises networks.
