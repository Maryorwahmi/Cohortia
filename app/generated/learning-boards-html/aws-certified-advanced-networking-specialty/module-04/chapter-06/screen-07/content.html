Understanding Blackhole Routes

Blackhole routes cause traffic to be silently dropped, making them challenging to detect without careful inspection.

The Impact

Traffic matching a blackhole route is discarded without any error notification, leading to connectivity failures.

Detection Strategy

Regularly review TGW route tables for any entries with a 'blackhole' target, especially for critical CIDRs.

Key Takeaway

Blackhole routes are silent killers of connectivity; proactive review of TGW route tables is essential to prevent unexpected traffic drops.
