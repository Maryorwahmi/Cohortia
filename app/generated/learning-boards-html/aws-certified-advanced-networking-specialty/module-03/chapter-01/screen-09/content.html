Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Low-Latency & Compliance
Latency-sensitive design
Direct Connect reduces jitter and provides a predictable baseline for designs that need consistent packet delivery and low latency.
Example
VoIP and streaming platforms see fewer dropped calls and smoother playback when using a private link compared with internet paths.
Compliance note
Direct Connect helps meet policies that forbid public internet transit, but encryption overlays may still be required for end-to-end confidentiality.
Design checklist
  • Confirm regulatory requirements for encryption and data residency.
  • Plan an IPsec overlay if cryptographic protection is required.
  • Design BGP failover and a backup VPN for continuity.
  • Ensure logging and audit evidence at endpoints.
Key Takeaway

Direct Connect is well suited for jitter-sensitive and compliance-sensitive workloads, but combine path control with encryption where policies require it.
