Calculation steps
  1. Total bits = 50 TB × 10^12 bytes/TB × 8 bits/byte = 400 × 10^12 bits.
  2. Effective bandwidth = advertised bandwidth × utilization.
  3. Time (seconds) = total bits ÷ effective bandwidth (bps).
  4. Convert seconds → hours → days.

Results (rounded)
100 Mbps @ 80%: Effective 80 Mbps → ~5,000,000 seconds → ~1,388.9 hours → ~57.9 days.
1 Gbps @ 80%: Effective 800 Mbps → ~500,000 seconds → ~138.9 hours → ~5.8 days.
Interpretation
A single order-of-magnitude increase in bandwidth reduces migration time by roughly the same factor. This is why Direct Connect ports or multiple circuits can be decisive for large migrations.
Key Takeaway

Always factor utilization into bandwith-based migration estimates and use Direct Connect for predictable bulk transfers.
