Component map
BGP-capable router
Your edge must speak BGP to exchange routes with AWS.
Direct Connect location & cross-connect
A physical facility and fiber connection between your device and AWS equipment.
Virtual Interface (VIF)
Logical interface over the physical link to reach VPCs or public services.
Operational checklist
  • Collect cross-connect ID, VLAN number, and your ASN.
  • Order port or hosted connection at the Direct Connect location.
  • Create private or public VIFs in the console and configure BGP on your router.
  • Validate route advertisements and traffic flow with test prefixes.
Pro tip

Prepare a short document for your colocation provider with VLAN, port speed, and cross-connect ID to avoid provisioning delays.
