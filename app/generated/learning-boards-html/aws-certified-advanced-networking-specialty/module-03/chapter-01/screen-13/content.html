Task: Compare VPN vs Direct Connect for 50 TB
Steps
  1. Find per-GB transfer rates for VPN and Direct Connect.
  2. Calculate transfer time: bits ÷ effective bandwidth (use 80% utilization).
  3. Estimate total transfer cost = GB × per-GB rate.
  4. Compare time and cost and recommend the best option.
Quick reference
Conversion used: 1 TB = 10^12 bytes; multiply TB by 8 to get bits.
Example results
100 Mbps @ 80% → ~57.9 days. 1 Gbps @ 80% → ~5.8 days. Use these numbers to judge feasibility and costs.
Reflection
If time to complete is business-critical, Direct Connect often pays back in schedule savings even if port costs are non-trivial.
Key Takeaway

Use both time and cost calculations to justify Direct Connect for large migrations; schedule improvements are often the decisive factor.
