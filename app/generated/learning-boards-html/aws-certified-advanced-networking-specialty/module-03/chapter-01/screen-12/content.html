Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Operational Steps
Step-by-step
  1. Plan capacity and pick a Direct Connect location and port speed.
  2. Order the port and collect cross-connect ID, VLAN, and port details.
  3. Coordinate physical cross-connect with the colo provider.
  4. Configure on-prem router BGP and create VIFs in AWS.
  5. Validate route exchange, traffic, and failover mechanisms.
Checklist: cross-connect ID, VLAN, ASN, BGP neighbors, VIF type, and test prefixes.
Verification & testing
Run BGP neighbor checks and confirm advertised prefixes match desired routes.
Perform small data transfers to measure steady-state throughput and latency.
Test failover by simulating link loss and validating VPN or alternate DX behavior.
Key Takeaway

A clear, shared checklist with the colo provider and networking team avoids provisioning delays and ensures predictable cutover.
