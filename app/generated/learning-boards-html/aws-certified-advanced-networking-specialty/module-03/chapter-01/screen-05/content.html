Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › DX vs VPN
Comparison table
Performance & Predictability
Direct Connect provides steady throughput and lower jitter. VPN performance depends on public internet routing and is variable.
Winner: Direct Connect
Security & Encryption
VPN provides encryption over the internet. Direct Connect provides a private path but not end-to-end encryption without an overlay.
Different strengths
Cost & Scale
Direct Connect can lower per-GB costs for sustained high-volume traffic but requires provisioning. VPN has minimal setup cost but higher per-GB internet transfer costs.
Depends on volume
Design tip: Use Direct Connect for primary traffic and VPN as a failover to combine predictable throughput and redundancy.
Concrete scenario
Your analytics cluster ingests 10 TB per day from on-prem storage. If you use VPN, daily throughput may vary and nightly jobs might overrun. Using Direct Connect helps keep ingestion within known windows.
Common mistake

Assuming Direct Connect is encrypted by default. Always plan encryption overlays if required by policy.

Key Takeaway

Match Direct Connect when you need predictability and sustained bandwidth; use VPN for quick, encrypted internet tunnels or as backup.
