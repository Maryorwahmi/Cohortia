Introduction to AWS Direct Connect (DX)
A private, dedicated network highway into AWS for predictable performance and hybrid connectivity

Direct Connect establishes a physical cross-connect between your router and AWS equipment at a Direct Connect location. It bypasses the public internet to reduce variability and support high-volume, low-latency workloads.
