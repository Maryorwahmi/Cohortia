Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › What is AWS Direct Connect?
Direct Connect — plain definition
A private physical connection into AWS that bypasses the public internet to deliver predictable bandwidth and latency.
Analogy

Think of it as your own dedicated highway into AWS, where traffic doesn't mix with general internet cars. That reduces jams and unpredictable slowdowns.

Operational example

Use Direct Connect for continuous database replication from your data center to a VPC to ensure predictable replication windows.

Common misconception

Direct Connect does not encrypt traffic by default. If encryption is required, layer an IPsec VPN over the link or use application-level encryption.

Key Takeaway

Direct Connect provides private, reliable bandwidth to AWS but requires you to add encryption if confidentiality is needed.

Components in focus
  • Direct Connect location: physical facility where AWS equipment is located.
  • Cross-connect: fiber between your device and the AWS router.
  • Virtual Interface (VIF): logical interface used to reach VPCs or AWS public services.
  • BGP: used to exchange routes between your router and AWS.
Tip: Record the cross-connect ID and VLAN when ordering a port — these are required by most colocation providers.
