Estimate transfer time
Formula to plan: Time = Total data in bits ÷ Effective bandwidth (bps). Remember to include utilization.

# Convert to bits and divide by effective bandwidth
Remember: utilization and protocol overhead reduce the effective throughput. Plan using realistic utilization values such as 0.7 or 0.8 rather than 1.0.
Key Takeaway

Use the bits-over-bandwidth formula and realistic utilization to estimate migration windows; Direct Connect shortens those windows for high-volume transfers.

Practical steps
  1. Calculate total data in bits (bytes × 8).
  2. Choose bandwidth and realistic utilization.
  3. Divide bits by effective bps to get seconds; convert to hours/days.
  4. Compare results for VPN vs Direct Connect and include costs.
Scenario
For a sustained migration, Direct Connect often makes multi-day transfers possible and predictable instead of indefinite and variable over VPN.
