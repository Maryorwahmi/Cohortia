Module 3 › Chapter 3.5 › Why Direct Connect Gateway exists
Problem: scale and cost

One VIF per region multiplies management work and costs when you operate globally.

Analogy

Think of the Direct Connect Gateway as an airport hub. Instead of separate roads into every city, flights land at one hub and passengers connect to regional carriers.

Concrete example

A single connection in us-east-1 with a DX Gateway can reach VPCs in us-west-2 and eu-central-1 via associated VGWs.

Key Takeaway

DX Gateway centralizes connectivity so you maintain a single hybrid entry point for many regions.

Definition and quick facts
  • Global resource managed from a region but usable across regions.
  • Works with Private VIFs or Transit VIFs to reach VGWs or TGWs.
  • Requires BGP peering using an Amazon-side ASN you choose.
Common misconception

A Direct Connect Gateway does not enable VPC-to-VPC transit between associated VGWs. Traffic must go to or from on-premises unless a Transit Gateway is used.
