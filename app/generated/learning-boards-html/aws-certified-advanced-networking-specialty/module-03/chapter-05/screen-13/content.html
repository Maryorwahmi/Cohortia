Module 3 › Chapter 3.5 › Common Mistakes
Frequent pitfalls
Missing attachments

VGW or TGW must be attached before association with DX Gateway.

ASN collisions

Choose an Amazon-side ASN that doesn’t conflict with on-premises ASN policies.

Route table omissions

Ensure VPC route tables have routes to on-premises via VGW or TGW.

Overlapping CIDRs

Resolve address overlaps before association; do not rely on route filters to mask the problem.

Final guidance

Use a pre-deployment checklist that includes attachment, ASN, BGP, learned prefixes, and VPC route table validation.
