Module 3 › Chapter 3.5 › Key Concepts
Direct Connect Gateway

A global hub that lets one Direct Connect reach multiple regional VPCs via VGWs or TGWs.

VGW/TGW Association

Associate VGWs or TGWs after verifying VPC/TGW attachments exist in the target region.

Amazon-side ASN

You choose the ASN for BGP; plan it to avoid conflicts with on-premises ASNs.

Takeaway

DX Gateway simplifies global hybrid connectivity but requires careful routing and addressing design.
