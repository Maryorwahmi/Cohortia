Direct Connect Gateway

Use one hybrid entry point to reach VPCs across many AWS regions and reduce operational overhead.

In this lesson: architecture, creation, routing
Problem

Multiple VIFs per region become costly and complex.

Solution

Direct Connect Gateway centralizes connections to many VGWs or TGWs.

Why it matters

Lower cost, simpler BGP peering, and regional reach with one physical connection.

Explore Key Topics
Gateway Creation

Name, choose Amazon-side ASN, and manage globally from a region.

Associations

Attach VGWs or TGWs to reach VPCs in different regions.

Routing Rules

Avoid overlapping CIDRs and understand no transitive routing by default.
