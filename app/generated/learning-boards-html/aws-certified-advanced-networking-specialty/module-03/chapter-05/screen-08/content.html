Module 3 › Chapter 3.5 › Overlapping CIDRs
Why overlaps break routing

If two networks advertise the same prefix, routers cannot choose a single correct next hop for that prefix, creating black holes or unpredictable behavior.

Operational advice

Keep a central IP registry, and validate prefixes in inventory before associating with a DX Gateway.

Misconception

Don’t assume route filters can safely resolve overlaps — the correct fix is unique addressing.

Quick checklist
  • Inventory VPC CIDRs in every region and account.
  • Compare on-premises prefixes against VPC CIDRs for overlaps.
  • Remediate overlaps by readdressing or using distinct subnets.
Key Takeaway

Unique addressing is mandatory when associating resources to a DX Gateway.
