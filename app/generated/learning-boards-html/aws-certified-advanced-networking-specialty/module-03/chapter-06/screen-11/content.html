Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Common Mistakes
Shared physical paths
Confirm separate fiber conduits and carrier handoffs to avoid a single cable cut taking both links.
Incorrect BGP policy
Misconfigured prepending or inconsistent route announcements can cause unexpected routing behavior.
No BFD
Skipping BFD leaves you with slower detection times and longer outages for sensitive apps.
Insufficient testing
Assuming failover will work without scheduled, measured tests is risky and common.
Ignoring last mile
Carrier incidents or shared handoffs are often overlooked dependencies.
Poor runbooks
Not documenting recovery steps turns planned tests into chaotic incidents.
Pro tip

Maintain a physical and logical inventory and include carrier confirmation as part of procurement and design sign off.
