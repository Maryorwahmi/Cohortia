Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › AS_PATH Prepending
Concept

Add your ASN multiple times to the AS_PATH on the secondary route advertisement to make that path look longer and less preferred by AWS.

Remember: this affects inbound traffic from AWS to your network, not your outbound selection.
Check your work
  • Verify BGP AS_PATH length on AWS-visible routes.
  • Confirm primary path has shorter AS_PATH under normal operation.
  • Simulate primary failure and verify AWS switches to secondary.
Conceptual BGP snippet
router bgp 65000
 neighbor <AWS_IP_PRIMARY> remote-as 64512
  address-family ipv4
   network 172.16.0.0 mask 255.255.0.0
  exit-address-family

router bgp 65000
 neighbor <AWS_IP_SECONDARY> remote-as 64512
  address-family ipv4
   network 172.16.0.0 mask 255.255.0.0
   neighbor <AWS_IP_SECONDARY> route-map PREPEND_AS out
  exit-address-family

route-map PREPEND_AS permit 10
 set as-path prepend 65000 65000 65000
