Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Active/Passive vs Active/Active
Active/Passive
Primary preferred; secondary used on failover. Easier to reason about inbound traffic using AS_PATH prepending.
Pros: predictability, simpler troubleshooting. Cons: standby capacity may be unused until failover.
Active/Active
Both paths carry traffic. Use when you need aggregated capacity or want distributed load across links.
Pros: better capacity utilization. Cons: requires careful policy and testing to ensure both can handle full load on failure.
Key Takeaway

Select the pattern that balances capacity needs and operational complexity, then validate with tests.
