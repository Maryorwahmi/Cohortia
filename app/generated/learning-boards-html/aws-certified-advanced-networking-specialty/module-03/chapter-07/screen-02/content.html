Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Learning Objectives
01
Identify Direct Connect cost components
Why it matters: forecast recurring spend and judge tradeoffs.

You will name port hours and data transfer out, explain when DTO is priced and when inbound transfer is free, and compute a simple monthly estimate for a 1 Gbps port with X TB out.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Read CloudWatch metrics for Direct Connect
Why it matters: detect BGP or link failures before user impact.

You will interpret ConnectionState, BGPState, and bandwidth counters, and map metric values to operational actions like opening an incident or running router checks.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Run a layered troubleshooting flow
Why it matters: root causes are found faster with a repeatable approach.

You will list physical, data-link, and BGP checks, and articulate example commands and expected outputs for each stage.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Validate route propagation and BGP state
Why it matters: connectivity requires both the link and correct routing.

You will verify BGP peering IPs, ASNs, and route tables to ensure VPC and on-prem routes are exchanged and visible where expected.
