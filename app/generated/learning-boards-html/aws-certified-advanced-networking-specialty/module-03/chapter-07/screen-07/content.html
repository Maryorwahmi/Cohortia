Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Layered Troubleshooting
Layer Checklist
  • Layer 1: Check ConnectionState, light levels, LOA-CFA, fiber cables.
  • Layer 2: Verify VLAN IDs, MAC learning, and MTU / jumbo frames.
  • Layer 3: Confirm BGPState, peer IPs, ASN, auth key, and route tables.
Concrete Scenario
If ConnectionState shows up but BGPState is down, run a BGP neighbor check on your router and verify the /30 peering IPs and ASN. If IPs or ASN mismatch, BGP won't form even when the fiber works.
Common Mistake

Assuming the link is healthy because it is up at the physical layer; always confirm BGP to validate end-to-end routing.
