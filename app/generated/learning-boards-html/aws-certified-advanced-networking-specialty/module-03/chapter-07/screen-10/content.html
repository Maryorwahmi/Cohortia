Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Physical vs Logical
Physical Indicators
  • ConnectionState down
  • High ConnectionErrorCount
  • Abnormal light levels on fiber
Logical Indicators
  • ConnectionState up but BGPState down
  • No prefixes received on BGP summary
  • Mismatched VLAN or ASN
Takeaway
Read metrics first to decide whether to engage the colo vendor for physical repairs or the network team for configuration fixes.
