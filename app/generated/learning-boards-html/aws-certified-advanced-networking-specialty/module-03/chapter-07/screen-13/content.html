Decision Factors
  • Port speed vs port-hour cost and how it affects throughput.
  • Hosted vs dedicated connections and cost/controls trade-offs.
  • DTO patterns: sustained egress benefits more from Direct Connect.
  • Redundancy: extra port-hours vs uptime requirements.
Example
Estimate DTO for nightly 50 TB transfers and compare monthly totals against higher port-hour charges for redundant ports.
Key Takeaway

Right-size ports for expected steady-state transfer and budget for redundancy when required by SLA.
