Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › VLANs & multiple VIFs
Assigning VLANs and BGP per VIF
Sequence to provision multiple logical interfaces on one physical link.
Step 1
Choose unique VLAN IDs
Each VIF requires a VLAN tag unique on the physical connection.
Step 2
Reserve /30 peering subnets
Allocate distinct /30s for each BGP adjacency between your router and AWS.
Step 3
Configure separate BGP sessions
Each VIF must have its own BGP neighbor and route advertisement policy.
Example mapping
VIF A: vlan=100, /30 169.254.10.0/30, BGP ASN 65000 → VGW. VIF B: vlan=200, /30 169.254.11.0/30, BGP ASN 65000 → TGW.
Common mistake

Reusing VLAN IDs or misconfiguring sub-interfaces on the router; this can cause traffic to be misrouted or BGP sessions to fail.
