Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › CLI parameter walkthrough
Parameter meanings
connection-id: identifies the physical Direct Connect connection that carries this VIF.
vlan: unique VLAN ID assigned to this VIF on the physical link; must not conflict with other VIF VLANs.
asn: your on-prem Autonomous System Number for the BGP session.
authKey: optional BGP authentication key for MD5 protection of the session.
amazonAddress/customerAddress: the two addresses from a /30 used for the BGP adjacency; one side for AWS, one for you.
virtualGatewayId: the VGW resource that the Private VIF will attach to.
Example checklist
  • Confirm VLAN is unique on the DX connection.
  • Verify ASN choice aligns with on-prem routing plan.
  • Reserve a /30 for peering and avoid overlapping with other links.
  • Decide if BGP authKey is required by policy.
After applying the CLI command, validate BGP adjacency and that the expected prefixes are advertised and received. If routes are missing, check VLAN mapping and BGP session details first.
