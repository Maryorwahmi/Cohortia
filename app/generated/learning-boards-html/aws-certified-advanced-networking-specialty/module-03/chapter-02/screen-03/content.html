Implementation Objectives
Prepare LOA-CFA packet for colocation
Know the port ID, rack/patch panel and cable type, and confirm the expiration date before handing to the provider.
Select location and speed
Choose a Direct Connect location near your site to minimize last-mile latency and select an adequate port speed.
Plan logical VIF mappings
Decide which VIFs you need (private, public, transit) and how they map to services and VLAN IDs.
Quick checklist
  • Obtain LOA-CFA and verify port ID and cable type.
  • Confirm LOA-CFA expiration date and delivery to provider.
  • Match VLAN ID, BGP ASN and peer IPs between AWS and on-prem.
