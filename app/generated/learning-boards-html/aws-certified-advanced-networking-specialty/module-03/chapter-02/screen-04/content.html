Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Direct Connect Location
What a Direct Connect location is
A physical facility where AWS installs routers and provides physical ports for Direct Connect.
Why it matters
Proximity affects last-mile latency and installation complexity. Choose a location that balances cost, latency and provider support.
Practical check
Confirm available port speeds and whether the colocation provider supports required patching and cross-connect procedures.
Common misconception
Not every nearby facility has identical AWS equipment or port availability; verify the exact DX location details.
