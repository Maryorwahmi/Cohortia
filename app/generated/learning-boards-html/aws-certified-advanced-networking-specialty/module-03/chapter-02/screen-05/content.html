Cross-connect workflow
From LOA-CFA to physical fiber and acceptance test.
Step 1 — Generate LOA-CFA
Create the Direct Connect connection in AWS and download the LOA-CFA containing port and patch details.
Step 2 — Deliver LOA-CFA to provider
The colocation operator uses the LOA-CFA to authorize and schedule the cross-connect.
Step 3 — Install fiber
Provider runs fiber between your patch panel and the AWS port per the LOA-CFA specifics.
Step 4 — Acceptance
Confirm link light, verify physical port IDs, and proceed to logical configuration and BGP testing.
Supporting cards
LOA-CFA contents
Port ID, patch panel location, required cable type, and expiration window.
Common mistake
Providing an expired LOA-CFA or incorrect port details delays installation significantly.
Key Takeaway

The LOA-CFA is the required authorization; verify its fields and expiration before submitting to your provider.
