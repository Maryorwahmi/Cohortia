Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Colocation Packet Walkthrough
Step-by-step packet assembly
Follow these steps to hand the provider a complete, accurate package.
Generate LOA-CFA
Create the connection in AWS to produce the LOA-CFA PDF and capture its fields.
Assemble packet
Include LOA-CFA, contact details, scheduling window, and a checklist of port and patch IDs.
Verify and submit
Confirm LOA-CFA has sufficient expiry and send to the colocation provider as a single package.
Packet contents
  • LOA-CFA PDF (port ID, patch panel, cable type, expiry)
  • Contact and scheduling window
  • Acceptance test plan (link light, BGP adjacency, route check)
Key Takeaway

A complete packet and pre-checks prevent wasted install windows and speed up the cross-connect provisioning.
