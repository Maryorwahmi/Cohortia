Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Port Speeds Comparison
Dedicated
Standard offerings: 1 Gbps, 10 Gbps, 100 Gbps.
Use cases
Large-scale migrations, constant high-throughput applications, disaster recovery links.
Hardware note
Verify NICs, router forwarding capacity, and module compatibility for the selected speed.
Hosted
Granular options: 50 Mbps, 100 Mbps, 200 Mbps, 300 Mbps, 400 Mbps, 500 Mbps, 1 Gbps, 2 Gbps, 5 Gbps, 10 Gbps.
Use cases
Startups and projects needing smaller, precisely sized links or faster provisioning.
Partner dependency
Confirm partner supports the exact increment you require and understand scaling options.
Practical check

Before ordering, run a hardware compatibility check for NICs and router throughput. Do not assume availability implies internal capability to use the bandwidth.
