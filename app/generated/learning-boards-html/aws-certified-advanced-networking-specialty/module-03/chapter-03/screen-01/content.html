Dedicated Connection
Exclusive physical port. Best for sustained high throughput and strict control.
Hosted Connection
Partner-provisioned virtual circuits for flexible, faster bandwidth options.
MACsec
Layer 2 encryption for Dedicated 10/100 Gbps where supported.
Visual Concept
Cards show trade-offs between control, cost, and speed.
Dedicated
Exclusive port. Speeds: 1 Gbps, 10 Gbps, 100 Gbps. Higher control and longer lead time.
Hosted
Partner slices of an existing port. Fine-grained speeds from 50 Mbps to 10 Gbps. Faster ordering.
Key Takeaway

Match connection type to bandwidth, control requirements, and timeline. Use MACsec for Layer 2 encryption on supported Dedicated links.
