Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Dedicated Connections
Definition
A physical Ethernet port dedicated to a single customer. Standard speeds: 1 Gbps, 10 Gbps, 100 Gbps.
Provisioning steps
  1. Order Dedicated Connection through AWS console.
  2. Receive LOA‑CFA document.
  3. Coordinate cross‑connect with colocation provider.
  4. Test link and verify bandwidth.
Example scenario
A data warehouse team orders 10 Gbps dedicated to support continuous replication. They confirmed router NICs and scheduled a colo-side cross‑connect using the LOA‑CFA.
Common mistake
Assuming dedicated will be faster to provision than hosted. Physical colocation steps usually lengthen lead times.
Key Takeaway

Use Dedicated for exclusive control and sustained high bandwidth; plan for colo coordination and longer provisioning.

Annotations
Important considerations and checklist to validate before ordering.
Control & Ownership
You manage the cross‑connect and hardware; you get exclusive access to the port.
Speed Availability
1 Gbps, 10 Gbps, 100 Gbps. Confirm on‑prem equipment supports chosen speed and jumbo frames if needed.
Ordering artifact
LOA-CFA (Letter of Authorization and Connecting Facility Assignment) - used by your colocation provider to create the cross-connect.
