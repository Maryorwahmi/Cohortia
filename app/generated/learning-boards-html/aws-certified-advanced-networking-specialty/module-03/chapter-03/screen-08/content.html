Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › MACsec
What MACsec protects
Encrypts Ethernet frames across the physical link so frames are protected before leaving premises and until they reach AWS at the DX location.
Scope & availability
Available for Dedicated 10 Gbps and 100 Gbps in select locations. Confirm via the macSecCapable flag in AWS responses.
Hardware requirements
On‑prem equipment must support 802.1AE and MACsec key exchange. Factor this into procurement and configuration plans.
Misconception
MACsec does not remove the need for application layer encryption; it secures the physical link but is complementary to IPsec or TLS.
Key Takeaway

Use MACsec for link-level protection when hardware and location support it; verify macSecCapable and device compatibility before design decisions.

Quick checklist
  • Confirm Direct Connect location supports MACsec.
  • Verify on‑prem devices support 802.1AE.
  • Plan key management and exchange procedures.
  • Keep higher-layer encryption where needed.
