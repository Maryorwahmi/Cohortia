Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Hosted Connections
How it works
A Direct Connect Partner owns the physical port; they allocate virtual segments to customers.
Bandwidth options
Typical hosted speeds include 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500 Mbps, 1 Gbps, 2 Gbps, 5 Gbps, and 10 Gbps.
When to use
Quick provisioning, smaller organizations, or when you lack presence in a Direct Connect location.
Pro tip
Ask partners for historical performance and redundancy details; measure expected jitter and latency if your workloads are latency-sensitive.
Key Takeaway

Hosted connections trade exclusive control for speed of delivery and bandwidth granularity. Vet partner SLAs before relying on them for production traffic.

Hosted connection checklist
Partner vetting
Confirm partner redundancy, expected provisioning time, typical bandwidth increments, and SLA specifics.
Operational dependency
Remember the partner controls the physical path; plan for their maintenance and incident processes.
Example decision
If you need 200 Mbps fast, and don’t have a colo presence, recommend Hosted from a vetted partner rather than a slow Dedicated order.
