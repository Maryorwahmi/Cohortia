Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Remember
Persist small settings
Use shared preferences for flags, small state, and user preferences that improve UX.
Await critical operations
Ensure getInstance and dependent writes are awaited to avoid race conditions.
Use typed setters
Match set/get types, handle nulls with defaults, and centralize keys for maintainability.
Final takeaway

Shared preferences are a pragmatic tool for simple, non-sensitive persistence. Design for async behavior and clear keys to make your app resilient and user friendly.
