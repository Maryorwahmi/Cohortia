Common mistakes and how to fix them
  • Missing references: confirm AudioSource and AudioClip assignments or use GetComponent fallback.
  • Wrong method: Play used where PlayOneShot is appropriate.
  • Too extreme pitch: small pitch ranges avoid distortion.
  • Performance: reuse AudioSources and avoid creating many temporary objects.
Mistake

Calling Play repeatedly for short SFX makes them cut each other off.

Fix

Use PlayOneShot or separate channels for intended overlap.
