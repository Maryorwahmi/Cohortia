Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Learning Objectives
Objectives — practical checks
05 — Event-driven audio

Trigger sounds on collisions, triggers, and inputs. Milestone: pickup sound plays on collision reliably.

06 — Null-safety and assignment

Guard against unassigned AudioSource or AudioClip fields with checks in Start and Update. Milestone: no null-reference errors on play.

07 — Source placement

Decide between local AudioSources and a central SoundManager for shared effects. Milestone: spatialized footsteps sound local, UI sounds are centralized.

08 — Hands-on validation

Build the footstep and door demo and confirm audio changes with movement and interaction. Milestone: footstep timing and door toggle both play expected clips.
