Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
AudioSource.Play()
Starts or restarts the assigned clip; good for loops and resumable audio.
PlayOneShot()
Plays a clip without stopping current clips on the same AudioSource, enabling overlap.
Pause / Stop
Pause preserves position; Stop halts and resets to the start.
Summary

Choose the method that matches whether you want restart, overlap, pause/resume, or cancel semantics.
