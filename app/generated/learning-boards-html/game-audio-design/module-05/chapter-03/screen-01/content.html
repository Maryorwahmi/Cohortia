Scripting Audio Playback in Unity
Make sounds respond to events, stack effects, and change properties at runtime.
In this lesson
Learn Play, Stop, Pause, and PlayOneShot, plus runtime pitch and volume control.
Practical demo
Implement footstep sounds and a door interaction in a small Unity scene.
Overview
  • Event-driven audio gives precise timing for effects like pickups or collisions.
  • PlayOneShot is the recommended approach for overlapping short effects.
  • Adjusting pitch and volume creates expressive feedback tied to game state.
Starter advice

Assign AudioSource and AudioClip references in the Inspector, and guard against nulls in your scripts.
