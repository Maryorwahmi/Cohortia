Architecture

Add an Audio Source and assign an Audio Clip

Attach to any GameObject. Assign the imported clip to the component's Audio Clip slot to make it playable.

Example: Door sound
Add Audio Source to Door, assign open.wav, and trigger playback when the door opens.
Example: Player footsteps
Attach to Player so spatial cues follow the listener and provide directionality.
Quick Checklist
  • Clip assigned to Audio Source? Verify non-null Audio Clip slot.
  • Play On Awake set correctly for ambient vs event sounds.
  • Spatial Blend set for UI (0) vs world sounds (1).
Common Mistake

Forgetting to assign the clip or leaving Play On Awake enabled for one-shot effects causes unexpected playback.
