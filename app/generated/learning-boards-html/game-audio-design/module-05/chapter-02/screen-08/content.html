Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Play On Awake & Priority
Comparison

Automatic vs Event-Driven Sounds

Play On Awake
True for ambient music that should start with the scene. False for event-triggered SFX.
Loop
Repeat the clip indefinitely when the sound must persist, such as background ambience.
Priority
Higher number → more important. Protect key sounds from being culled when many voices play.
Scenario
Crowded scene with many enemies

Raise priority on player-related sounds and important audio cues so they are less likely to be dropped by the engine.

Common Mistake

Leaving Play On Awake enabled for every sound creates a noisy, unmanageable scene. Decide which sounds must start automatically.
