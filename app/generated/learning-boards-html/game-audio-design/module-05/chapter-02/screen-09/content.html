Comparison

Loudness, Speed, and Balance

Volume
Per-source loudness from 0 to 1. Use for balancing audio layers.
Pitch
Alters playback speed and perceived pitch. Useful for variation but affects duration.
Stereo Pan
Controls left/right for 2D clips. For 3D use Spatial Blend and positioning.
Scenario
Footsteps variation

Vary pitch slightly per step to avoid repetition and adjust volume by surface type for realism.

Misconception

Changing pitch is not a free way to speed or shorten audio without altering perceived tone. DSP is needed for independent control.
