What it is

Spatialization

Transforms the source position relative to the listener into panning, level, and sometimes filtering for realistic directionality.

  • Uses source and listener positions and orientation.
  • Often paired with spatial blend to balance 2D/3D behavior.
  • Requires a correctly positioned listener to produce expected cues.
Key Takeaway

Spatialization gives directional information but must be tuned with attenuation and source levels for clarity.

Example & notes
Example

Place a looping growl at an enemy location and rotate the camera; the sound should sweep accordingly between left and right outputs.

Misconception

Assuming spatialization includes occlusion or reverb. Those effects are separate engine systems if available.

Pro tip

Use spatial blend to preview both behaviors while tuning so you don’t accidentally make a world sound feel like UI audio.
