Quick diagnostic flow
  1. Is there an active Audio Listener? If not, add or enable one.
  2. Does the Audio Source have a clip and is it enabled?
  3. Are Min/Max Distance or spatial settings making the sound inaudible?
  4. Is the audio routed or muted in a mixer group?
  5. If scripted, is the play call being invoked as expected?
Practical test

Switch a problematic source to 2D temporarily; if you hear it, the issue is spatial tuning or listener placement.

Common mistake

Assuming system volume is the issue; engine listener or source configuration is more often the cause.

Final tip

Use simple tests and change one setting at a time to quickly isolate the root cause.
