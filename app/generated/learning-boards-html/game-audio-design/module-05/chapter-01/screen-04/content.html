Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Audio Listener — the player's ears
Definition

Audio Listener

Represents where audio is heard from; typically attached to the main camera or player.

Why it matters
  • Without it, 3D audio will not be processed for the player.
  • Its position and orientation determine panning and directional cues.
Key Takeaway

Verify a single active listener on the camera or player to ensure world sounds are audible.

Concrete example & checklist
Scenario

You import sound effects and hear music but no footsteps. Check the Main Camera for an Audio Listener component.

Quick fix

Add or enable the Audio Listener on the camera, then re-test in Play mode.

Common mistake

Assuming audio clips will play without a listener. UI audio can still play because it is often non-spatialized.

Pro tip

Attach the listener to the actual player object that controls viewpoint for consistent results.
