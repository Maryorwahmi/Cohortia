Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › 2D vs 3D Audio
2D Audio

Non-spatialized playback

Plays at a constant level and panning regardless of source position. Ideal for UI, music and voiceovers.

When to use
  • HUD sounds, menu clicks, and level music.
  • When the origin should not distract or mislead the player.
3D Audio

Spatialized sounds

Positioned in world space and affected by distance, panning and filters. Use for footsteps, weapons, and environment cues.

When to use
  • Situations where direction or distance informs player decisions.
Example

A distant explosion should be 3D so the player can orient; a button click should be 2D so it remains audible regardless of in-world location.
