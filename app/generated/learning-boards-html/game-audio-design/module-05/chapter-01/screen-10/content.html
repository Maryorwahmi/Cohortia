Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Doppler Effect
Definition

Doppler effect

Perceived pitch change that occurs when source and listener move relative to one another.

Example

A passing siren rises in pitch as it approaches and lowers as it moves away. Engines simulate the same behavior for fast in-game objects.

Caution

Overusing Doppler can make sounds garbled. Moderate the effect to maintain clarity.

Key Takeaway

Use Doppler to reinforce motion cues for fast objects, but always test that speech and important sounds remain understandable.
