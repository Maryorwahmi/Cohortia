Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Audio Listener

Represents the player's "ears"; required to hear spatialized world audio.

Audio Source

Attached to a GameObject; plays a clip and provides position for spatial audio.

Spatialization

Maps positions to directional cues and volume for believable localization.

Summary takeaway

Always verify a listener is present, choose 2D for UI, 3D for world cues, and tune attenuation for the intended perceptual range.
