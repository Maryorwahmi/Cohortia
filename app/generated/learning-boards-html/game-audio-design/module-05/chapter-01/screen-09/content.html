Definition & tuning

Control how loud a sound remains at different distances using Min/Max Distance and attenuation curves.

Min Distance

Radius within which the sound is at full volume.

Max Distance

Distance where sound reaches minimum audible level or becomes inaudible.

Attenuation curve

Shape the falloff to match the character of the sound.

Example

Give an NPC whisper a Min Distance of 0.5 and Max Distance of 3 with a steep curve so it becomes quiet quickly as players step back.

Common mistake

Raising overall clip volume instead of tightening Max Distance, which makes near sounds overpowering.
