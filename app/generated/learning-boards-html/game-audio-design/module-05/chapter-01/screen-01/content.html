Introduction to Game Engine Audio Systems

Bring your sound assets into a real game environment: listeners, sources, and the rules that make audio spatial and believable.

Est. module focus: Practical integration
In this lesson

Identify Audio Listener and Audio Source, compare 2D and 3D audio, learn spatialization, attenuation, Doppler, and the basic import-to-play workflow used in common engines.

Why it matters

Correct audio setup ensures sounds are audible where they should be, improves player feedback, and prevents common silent-scene problems.

Quick takeaway

Think in pairs: every audible world sound needs a source and a listener to hear it. Choose 2D when sound is internal, 3D when it must occupy space.

Visual overview

Cards below show how listener, source, and spatial rules connect to deliver sound.

Audio Listener

Represents the player's ears. Typically attached to the camera or player.

Audio Source

Plays a clip from a GameObject. Position matters for 3D audio.

Spatial Rules

Spatialization, attenuation, and Doppler shape how a sound is perceived over distance and motion.
