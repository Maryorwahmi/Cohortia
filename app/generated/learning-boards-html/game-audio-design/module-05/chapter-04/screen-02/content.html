Learning Objectives

Skills and checkpoints for this chapter.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Understand Audio Mixer
Explain why a central mixer is needed for complex projects and how it maps to real mixing consoles. Practice: identify three benefits of central routing versus per-source control.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Create Mixer Groups
Build Music, SFX, Voice groups and route Audio Sources to them. Practice: create a nested SFX structure like Player, Enemy, Environment.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply Audio Effects
Add Reverb, Chorus, or filters to groups. Practice: apply subtle reverb to SFX and compare dry vs wet results.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Use Snapshots & Transitions
Save mixer states like Gameplay or Paused and transition smoothly. Practice: create Gameplay and Paused snapshots and test crossfades.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
