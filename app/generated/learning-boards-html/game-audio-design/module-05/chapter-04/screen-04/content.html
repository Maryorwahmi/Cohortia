Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › What is the Audio Mixer?
Core idea

An Audio Mixer routes audio through named groups, applies shared effects, and stores states that can be recalled or transitioned between.

Analogy
Think of mixer groups as channels on a desk: each channel controls multiple sound sources at once.
Common misconception
Mixers are more than volume controls: they host effects, automation, and parameter exposure for scripting.
Key Takeaway
Centralize control to scale audio management and performance.
Practical example
Create MainAudioMixer with Music and SFX groups. Route audio: assign BGM AudioSource output to Music, assign footsteps to SFX. Lower SFX group volume and hear all footsteps drop together.
Checklist
  • Create Audio Mixer asset (Project → Create → Audio Mixer).
  • Add groups for categories like Music, SFX, Voice.
  • Assign AudioSource output to the appropriate group.
Why this matters
Centralized routing reduces repetition, prevents duplicated DSP across sources, and enables game-state-driven mix changes.
