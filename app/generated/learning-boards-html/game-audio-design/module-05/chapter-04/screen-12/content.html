Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Audio Mixer
A central hub to route, process, and store mixer states for all game audio.
Audio Mixer Group
A named channel for related sounds allowing collective volume, effect control, and nesting.
Audio Effects
Processors such as Reverb and filters applied at group level to shape space and timbre.
Summary
Organize groups, apply effects where they help, and keep the mixer tidy so audio remains clear and efficient.
