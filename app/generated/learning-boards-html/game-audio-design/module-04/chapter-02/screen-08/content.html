Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Vertical vs Horizontal Techniques
Vertical Layering
  • Keeps continuity and tempo
  • Adjusts intensity by adding layers
  • Good for health, proximity, or mood shifts
Horizontal Re-sequencing
  • Swaps entire tracks or sections
  • Used when tempo or arrangement must change
  • Good for major scene changes or level transitions
Key Takeaway

Match technique to the scale of the musical change: layering for nuance, switching for dramatic shifts.
