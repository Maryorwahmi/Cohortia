Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Avoiding Phasing and Clutter
Phasing checklist
  • Export from identical DAW start point.
  • Match sample rate and bit depth.
  • Check for very similar sources panned identically.
Clutter fixes
  • Prefer subtractive mixes: reduce calm layers as tension grows.
  • Use EQ to carve space for new layers.
  • Limit always-on stems to essential elements.
Pro tip

When in doubt, mute a layer and listen. If the scene still communicates the intended mood, that layer may be unnecessary.
