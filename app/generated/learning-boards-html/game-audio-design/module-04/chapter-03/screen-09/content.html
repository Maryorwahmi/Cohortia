Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Designing a Combat Transition
Activity: Identify and compose
Follow the numbered workflow below while working in your DAW and middleware test harness.
Step 1 — Identify transition points
Listen to Exploration_Loop and mark bar 8 and bar 16 as possible EndPhrase markers. These will be mapped to the middleware cue.
Step 2 — Decide stinger or bridge
Choose a 1–2s stinger for instant drama or a 4-bar bridge to gradually introduce combat elements.
Step 3 — Compose and export
Compose Enemy_Stinger.wav in C minor at 120 BPM or create Transition_ToCombat.wav. Export and add markers inside each file if needed.
Step 4 — Implement in middleware
Map EndPhrase markers to WaitForMusicalCue calls and sequence the stinger or bridge before starting Combat_Loop.
Scenario card
Player enters combat at bar 9. The engine waits for EndPhrase at bar 16, plays a 1s stinger, then fades in Combat_Loop over 1s. This prioritizes timing over immediate reaction.
Reflection
Choose which approach better supports perceived pacing: quick stinger for punch, bridge for sustained tension. Test both in-game.
