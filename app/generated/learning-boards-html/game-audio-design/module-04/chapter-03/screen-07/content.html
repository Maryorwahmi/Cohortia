Stinger
Short flourish designed to punctuate an event and mask abrupt segment changes. Often 1–2 seconds long, tuned to match tempo and key.
Design recipe for a stinger
Keep it short, use a strong attack, match the target key and tempo, and place it at a marked transition cue so timing stays musical.
Design recipe for a bridge
Write 2–4 bars that borrow motifs from the outgoing segment and introduce instrumentation or harmony from the incoming loop.

Pro tip
Use a short stinger when you want immediate dramatic punctuation; use a bridge when you want a musical modulation or gradual textural change.
