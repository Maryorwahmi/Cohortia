Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Stinger vs Transition Segment
Side-by-side comparison
Stinger
Short, high-impact, preserves momentum. Use at dramatic events or sudden revelations.
Transition Segment
Longer bridge that gradually changes harmony or texture, used when a soft arrival is desired.
Designers should weigh frequency and context. Too many stingers reduce impact; too many bridges lessen immediacy. Use the approach that supports player experience in each scenario.
Scenario card
Boss arena entry: short stinger for high drama. Transition to safe zone: longer bridge for calm arrival. Always schedule on markers.
Check
Ask: does the event need immediate impact or a gradual change? That guides whether to use a stinger or a transition segment.
