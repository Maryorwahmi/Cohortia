Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Musical Segment
Self-contained loop, intro, outro, or stinger used for non-linear playback.
Transition
Moving from one segment to another coherently using cues, stingers or bridges.
Markers & Cues
DAW markers that middleware uses to schedule musical changes on phrase boundaries.
Takeaway

Plan transitions musically, map markers to middleware, choose the right technique for pacing, and test every transition path.
