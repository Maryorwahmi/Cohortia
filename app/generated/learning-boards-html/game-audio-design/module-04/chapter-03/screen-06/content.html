Process: Plan a rhythmic hand-off
Follow these numbered steps when designing phrase-aware transitions.
1. Mark phrase boundaries
Place DAW markers at natural ends of 4-, 8-, or 16-bar phrases you will allow transitions on.
2. Choose permissible cues
Decide whether transitions can occur on 4-bar or 8-bar boundaries and keep that consistent across segments.
3. Add pickups or lead-ins
At the end of a segment, add a short pickup to bridge to the groove of the next loop when needed.
4. Test transitions in context
Trigger transitions at each marked cue and listen for clipped beats or misaligned accents. Adjust markers or pickups as required.
Example Scenario
Loop A ends on bar 16 with a full-measure resolution. Loop B begins on the next downbeat. The middleware waits for the bar 16 marker then starts Loop B at its downbeat, or plays a one-bar pickup to introduce Loop B's rhythmic motif.
Check your understanding
If a transition feels off, ask: did the transition occur on a marked phrase end? Is a pickup needed to align accents?
