Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › What is Horizontal Re-sequencing?
Definition

Horizontal re-sequencing arranges musical segments so they can play in different orders based on gameplay. Each segment is a self-contained unit with entry and exit points, enabling the soundtrack to jump between moods or states without losing musical coherence.

Analogy
Think of segments as modular film scenes. You can reorder them, but seams need staging so edits feel natural.
Concrete Example
Exploration_Loop ends at a marked phrase. The engine waits, plays Transition_ToSafeZone, then starts SafeZone_Loop. The musical hand-off is planned, not accidental.
Key Takeaway

Compose segments to be musically whole and provide clear transition points so re-sequencing preserves immersion.

Definition Cards
Musical Segment
Self-contained section: intro, loop, outro, stinger.
Transition
Process of moving from one segment to another musically coherently.
Common misconception
Assuming loops can be cut arbitrarily. In fact, loops need exit points or markers to avoid awkward phrase breaks.
