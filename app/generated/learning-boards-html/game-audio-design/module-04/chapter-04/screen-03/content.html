Lab Checklist

Four practical checkpoints for your screen-recorded walkthrough.

Estimated lab time: 15 minutes
Step 1
Create project
Start a new Wwise or FMOD Studio project to hold assets and events.
Step 2
Import stems
Import 2–3 stems as Music Segments or timeline regions and verify synchronization.
Step 3
Assemble structure
Use a Playlist Container or Music Track to define transitions and crossfades.
Step 4
Parameter mapping
Create a State or RTPC like ThreatLevel to control which segment or layer is active.
