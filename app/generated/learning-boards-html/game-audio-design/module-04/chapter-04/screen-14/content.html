Key Concepts Part 1

What to remember

Game Audio Middleware
Tools like Wwise and FMOD Studio that centralize interactive audio authoring and runtime control.
Music Segment
A self-contained piece of music that can host multiple stems or tracks for layering.
Playlist Container / Music Track
Containers that sequence segments and define transition rules or automation for playback order.
Summary

Remember these primitives and choose segments for layering and playlists or tracks for sequencing transitions.
