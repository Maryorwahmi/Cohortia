Practical debugging and safety

Sync & sample rates
Confirm all stems share sample rate and loop points to prevent drift or clicks during playback and transitions.
Keep it simple
Author minimal transition logic first, then add layers, playlists, and RTPCs incrementally to keep debugging tractable.
Profile & backup
Use middleware profilers to monitor RTPC updates and voice counts. Save frequently and store backups of your authoring project files.
