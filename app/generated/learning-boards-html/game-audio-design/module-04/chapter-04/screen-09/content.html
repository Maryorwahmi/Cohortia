Comparison States vs Switches vs RTPCs

Choose the right control type

States (global)

Used for global conditions like Exploration or Combat that affect audio behavior across the project.

Switches (local)

Used for localized or context-specific choices like Location or Enemy_Type to choose a variation within a theme.

RTPCs (numeric)

Continuous numeric parameters like Player_Health or ThreatLevel mapped to volume, filter, or blend amounts.

Concrete example
Map Game_Intensity RTPC to the volume of Melody_Layer so it fades in as intensity climbs from 50 to 100.
Common mistake
Using States for every parameter change instead of RTPCs leads to limited control granularity.
