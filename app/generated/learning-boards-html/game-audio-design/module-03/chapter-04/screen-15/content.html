Key Concepts (Part 2)

Essential Takeaways from Advanced SFX Design

Granular Synthesis

Breaks sounds into tiny 'grains' and manipulates them to create new textures and effects.

Layering

Combining multiple distinct sound effects to create a single, richer, and more complex sound.

Modulation & Automation

Using signals or drawing changes over time to add movement, variation, and expressiveness to sounds.

Chapter Summary

These techniques are crucial for creating dynamic, immersive, and responsive sound effects, preventing repetition, and transforming simple audio into impactful game assets.
