Advanced SFX Processing (Part 1)

Transforming Simple Sounds into Impactful Assets

Granular Synthesis (Recap)

Breaks sounds into tiny 'grains' for shimmering textures, time-stretching, and abstract soundscapes.

Convolution Reverb

Uses an 'impulse response' of a real acoustic space to realistically place a sound within that environment.

Key Takeaway

Granular synthesis and convolution reverb are powerful tools for creating highly stylized, atmospheric, and spatially realistic game audio from simple source material.
