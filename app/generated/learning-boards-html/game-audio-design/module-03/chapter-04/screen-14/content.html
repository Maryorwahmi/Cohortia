Key Concepts (Part 1)

Essential Takeaways from Advanced SFX Design

Subtractive Synthesis

Shapes sound by removing frequencies from harmonically rich waveforms using filters.

Additive Synthesis

Builds sounds by combining multiple pure sine waves at different frequencies and amplitudes.

FM Synthesis

One oscillator modulates the frequency of another, creating complex, metallic, and bell-like sounds.

Chapter Summary

These synthesis methods are foundational for creating unique, impactful, and stylized sound effects, enabling precise control over sonic characteristics.
