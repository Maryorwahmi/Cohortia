Further Advanced Processing (Part 2)

Exploring Frequency Shifting, Ring Modulation, and Vocoders

Frequency Shifting

Shifts all frequencies by a fixed amount, creating dissonant, metallic, or alien effects.

Ring Modulation

Multiplies two input signals to generate new, non-harmonic frequencies, ideal for robotic or industrial sounds.

Vocoders

Imposes the spectral characteristics of one sound (e.g., voice) onto another (e.g., synth) for robotic or talking effects.

Key Takeaway

These advanced processors are powerful tools for creating highly stylized, unconventional, and unique sound effects that define a game's sonic character.

Input Sound

e.g., Voice, synth pad, noise

Processing Chain

Frequency Shifter, Ring Modulator, Vocoder

Transformed SFX

Robotic voices, alien soundscapes, complex rhythms
