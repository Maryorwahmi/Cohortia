What is Sound Synthesis?

Generating Audio Electronically from Basic Principles

Definition

Sound synthesis is the generation of audio using electronic means, allowing for the creation of sounds from fundamental waveforms rather than recordings.

Why it Matters

  • Unlocks unique, non-realistic soundscapes.
  • Offers precise control over every sonic parameter.
  • Essential for stylized and fantastical game worlds.
  • Moves beyond the limitations of recorded audio.
Key Takeaway

Sound synthesis provides ultimate control over sound creation, enabling designers to craft truly original and impactful audio assets for any game.

Basic Waveforms

Sine, Sawtooth, Square, Triangle, Noise

Synthesis Engine

Filters, Oscillators, Envelopes, LFOs

Unique SFX Output

Laser blasts, engine hums, alien voices, UI sounds
