Additive Synthesis

Building Sounds from Pure Sine Waves

Process Overview

Combines multiple pure sine waves at different frequencies and amplitudes to build complex sounds from the ground up.

Key Characteristics

  • Precise Control: Granular control over harmonic content.
  • Complex Timbres: Ideal for bell-like tones, metallic impacts, evolving textures.
  • Building Blocks: Starts with simple sine waves and layers them.
Key Takeaway

Additive synthesis offers unparalleled precision in sound design by constructing sounds from individual sine wave components, perfect for intricate and evolving timbres.
