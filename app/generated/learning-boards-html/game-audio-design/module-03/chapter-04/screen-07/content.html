FM Synthesis

Modulating Frequencies for Complex Timbres

Core Principle

One oscillator (modulator) changes the frequency of another (carrier) at audio rates, creating complex harmonic content.

Sonic Characteristics

  • Metallic & Glassy: Often produces bell-like, percussive, or shimmering tones.
  • Aggressive & Digital: Can create harsh, robotic, or alien soundscapes.
  • Dynamic: Rich harmonic changes based on modulation depth and ratio.
Key Takeaway

FM synthesis is a powerful tool for generating unique, complex, and often non-organic sounds, making it ideal for sci-fi, digital, and abstract game audio.
