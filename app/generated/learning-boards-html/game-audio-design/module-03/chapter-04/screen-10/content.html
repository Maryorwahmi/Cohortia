Modulation & Automation

Adding Movement and Expressiveness to Sounds

Modulation

Using one signal (e.g., LFO) to control a parameter of another signal, creating cyclical or rhythmic movement.

Automation

Recording or drawing changes to a parameter over time within your DAW, creating deliberate, evolving changes.

Key Takeaway

Modulation and automation are crucial for making game audio dynamic, responsive, and alive, moving beyond static sound playback.
