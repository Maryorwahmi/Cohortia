Competency Checks
  • Trim silence and confirm the start aligns with the transient of the sound.
  • Apply short fades and verify there are no clicks or abrupt jumps.
  • Normalize to a target peak and ensure no clipping occurs.
  • Use EQ to remove a muddy band and A/B the result.
Practice Reminder
Document each change you make. Notes help you replicate settings across similar sounds and speed future sessions.
Why this matters
Clear steps and documentation help you produce consistent game assets and communicate with the audio team.
