Digital Audio Workstations for SFX Editing

Refine raw recordings into polished sound effects. Learn core tools and a repeatable workflow that works in any DAW.

In this lesson
Identify DAW parts, edit clips, apply effects, export lossless SFX.
Why it matters
Game audio needs consistent, clear assets that behave predictably in the engine.
Safety note
Monitor at moderate volumes and take breaks to avoid ear fatigue.
Explore Key Topics
Core Interface
Timeline, tracks, mixer, and effect slots form the DAW backbone.
Essential Effects
EQ, compression, reverb and delay are the most used tools for SFX.
Export Workflow
Prepare, mix, and export lossless WAV or OGG with correct sample rate and bit depth.
Visual Concept
Timeline
Arrange clips over time.
Mixer
Balance levels and pan.
Effects
EQ, compression, reverb, delay.
Quick Start
Import a raw file, trim silence, add a short fade, normalize to -3 dB, then add EQ and light compression before exporting as WAV.
