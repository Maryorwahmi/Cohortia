Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Equalization (EQ)
EQ Principles
  • Cut problem bands before boosting.
  • Use narrow notches for resonances; use wide Q for tone shaping.
  • Small boosts often have large perceptual effects.
Misconception
Boosting fixes everything. Too much boost creates harshness and masks other sounds.
Concrete Example
Fixing a boxy footstep
Cut around 400–800 Hz to remove boxiness. Add a small boost near 2–4 kHz to emphasize the attack.
Key Takeaway
Tame mud first, then add presence. Always A/B and use subtle moves.
