Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Normalizing & Gain Staging
Concept

Normalize sets a clip’s peak to a chosen target. Gain staging sets levels across clips and tracks to avoid clipping and preserve dynamics when processing.

Practical Rule
Normalize after trimming and fades. Leave headroom (for example -3 dB) to allow effects to act safely.
Misconception
Loud equals better. Louder can hide detail and force over-processing.
Example
Roar processing chain
  • Trim silence, fade ends.
  • Normalize to -3 dB.
  • Reduce clip gain by 2 dB to add headroom for compression.
  • Apply light compression settings to control peaks.
Key Takeaway
Normalize with headroom, then gain-stage conservatively to preserve dynamics and avoid artifacts.
