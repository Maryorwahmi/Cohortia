Hearing Safety
Monitor at moderate levels. Take 5–10 minute breaks every hour. Ear fatigue changes perception and leads to poor decisions.
Quick Habit
Lower volume, take a short walk, then return to compare the mix with fresh ears.
Quality Checklist
  • Trim and fades cleanly applied.
  • No clipping after normalization and processing.
  • SFX tested with other game sounds or a reference mix.
  • Export settings documented and assets named clearly.
Key Takeaway
Healthy listening and contextual testing prevent mix errors and ensure assets translate into the game.
