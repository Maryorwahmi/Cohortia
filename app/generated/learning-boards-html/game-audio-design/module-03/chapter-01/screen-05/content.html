Module 03 › Chapter 3.1 › Categories of SFX
UI sounds
Short, immediate feedback. Prioritize clarity and brief duration.
Character sounds
Sync to animation, consistent timbre, support identity and physicality.
Environmental loops
Subtle, loopable, low profile. Prioritize consistency and non-fatiguing textures.
Combat/impact
Loud, pronounced transients and low-end weight to communicate force and consequence.
Example scenarios
UI: a menu select uses a quick attack, short decay, bright timbre so it’s audible without interrupting play.
Character: a heavy footstep pairs low pitch with a short tail to feel grounded and sync to walk cycles.
Environment: wind whoosh uses slowly varying dynamics and long duration loops to avoid repetition.
Combat: sword impact combines sharp transient plus resonant metallic tail to communicate both hit and weapon material.
Misconception: all impactful sounds must be loud. In practice, perception of impact can come from a distinct transient and harmonic content rather than sheer volume. Always test in the full mix.
