Module 03 › Chapter 3.1 › Designing for Context
Genre comparison
Retro / pixel: synthetic, short, arpeggiated textures fit low-fidelity visuals.
Realistic: organic recordings, nuanced body, and controlled dynamics.
Fantasy: hybrid layers that signal elements like fire, ice, or arcane energy.
Practical checklist
  • Does the SFX match visual fidelity and motion timing?
  • Does it complement or compete with music and dialogue?
  • Would a simpler texture better serve gameplay clarity?
Misconception to avoid: more detail equals better immersion. Excessive detail may mask gameplay cues and increase mixing complexity. Aim for textures that support the player’s understanding and the game’s tone.
Key Takeaway

Let genre and art style set your palette. Use constraints to make clearer, more cohesive SFX decisions.
