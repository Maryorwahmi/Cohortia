Module 03 › Chapter 3.1 › Pitfalls & Safety
Top mistakes
  • Designing in isolation from music/dialogue.
  • Layering that muddies identity.
  • Using volume increases instead of clarity improvements.
Testing & safety
Test SFX inside the engine with all audio layers active, at normal play levels. Use A/B comparisons and ask whether the player instantly understands the event. For health, take regular breaks and avoid prolonged loud listening during critical mixing sessions.
Quick checklist
  1. Does the transient communicate timing?
  2. Is the timbre distinct in the mix?
  3. Does the sound sit with music and dialogue?
Key Takeaway

Design iteratively in context, prefer clarity over loudness, and protect your hearing so your decisions stay reliable.
