Dynamics

Dynamics describe how loudness changes over time: attack, sustain, decay. Use dynamics to give motion and emphasis without relying on raw volume.

Sharp attack + quick decay: precise, crisp.
Slow attack + swell: build anticipation.
Sustained elements: ambience and texture.
Duration and pacing

Keep duration appropriate to function. Short cues keep gameplay responsive; long cues set tone but can interfere with timing-sensitive events.

Example
A charged ability that uses a rising dynamic and longer duration creates tension; the actual impact should include a strong transient so players receive immediate feedback at the collision moment.
Key Takeaway

Shape envelopes to balance anticipation and clarity. Favor clear transients for actions and measured sustain for atmosphere.
