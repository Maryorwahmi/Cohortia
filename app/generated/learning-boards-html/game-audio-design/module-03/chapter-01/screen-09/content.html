What is a transient?

The initial burst of energy that defines a sound’s start. It provides timing and often carries most of a sound’s perceived brightness and punch.

Sharp transient: immediate clarity and attack.
Soft transient: less punch, more blended with body.
Practical guidance
Preserve or craft a clear transient before adding longer textures. Use transient shaping, subtle compression, or spectral emphasis instead of only raising level.
Example
A weapon hit: clear transient for timing, then a body element to convey material. If reverb blurs the transient, reduce early reflections or use a gated tail.
Key Takeaway

Make the first 50–150 ms count. A distinct transient ensures event clarity and player responsiveness.
