Three-layer motif
  1. Identifying motif: a short, re-used sonic signature.
  2. Material body: textures that reflect source and style.
  3. Impact transient: the precise attack that defines event timing.
Example implementation
A magic system uses a short crystalline ping as motif. For a small spell the motif is high and short; for a powerful spell it’s lower and stretched, but the underlying shimmer remains to link them.
Pitfalls
Avoid applying the motif to unrelated events and making it so loud that it obscures other cues. Keep it distinctive but balanced in the mix.
Key Takeaway

Reuse a small motif across related sounds and vary supporting elements to keep recognition while preserving context.
