Module 03 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
SFX (Sound Effect)
Short audio cues that convey information, provide feedback, and build immersion.
Pitch
Perceived highness or lowness; signals size, speed, and urgency.
Timbre
The tonal color that differentiates materials and textures.
Summary
Design SFX to be clear, consistent, and tested in-context. Use pitch, timbre, dynamics, and duration deliberately to communicate function and emotion.
