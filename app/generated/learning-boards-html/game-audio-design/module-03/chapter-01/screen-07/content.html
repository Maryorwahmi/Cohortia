What is timbre?

Timbre is how a sound is colored — its harmonic and noise content plus processing. It identifies material and stylistic identity independent of pitch.

Metallic: bright harmonics, ringing tails.
Organic: breathy, inharmonic, softer attacks.
Synthetic: filtered oscillators, steady textures.
Concrete scenario
Designing a spell: combine a crystalline high shimmer for 'arcane' with a textured low rumble for mass. The contrast creates a unique timbre that signals both magic and potency.
Pro tip
Use simple EQ and gentle saturation to shape layers so the defining texture sits in the mix without being masked by other SFX or music.
Key Takeaway

Timbre gives identity. Pick textures that align with the imagined source and work in the full mix.
