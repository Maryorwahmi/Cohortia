Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Key Concepts
Licensing
Legal terms that define how a sound may be used, modified, and distributed.
Wind Screen
A cover that reduces wind noise for cleaner outdoor recordings.
Clipping
Digital distortion caused by exceeding recording headroom; it cannot be fixed after capture.
Final Takeaway

Document licenses, protect outdoor recordings, monitor levels, and use synthesis and layering to create distinct, high-quality SFX for your game.
