Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Mic Selection
Definition

Microphone choices

Shotgun / directional
Focused pickup, useful for isolating sources in noisy environments.
Omni / ambience
Captures room and environment; great for ambience and wide captures.
Condenser / studio
Clear and sensitive, often used for foley and delicate textures.
Placement & Layering
Test close and distant positions, then blend tracks to combine detail and space. Use wind protection outdoors and avoid placing mics too close to reflective surfaces.
Example: Footsteps
Record a close condens­er take for texture, and a farther omni take for room tone. Layer for a convincing, three-dimensional footstep.
Common Misconception
The most expensive mic will not fix poor placement or environmental noise. Technique matters more than sticker price.
Key Takeaway

Choose mic type for the sonic role you need, then experiment with placement and blending to control presence and atmosphere.
