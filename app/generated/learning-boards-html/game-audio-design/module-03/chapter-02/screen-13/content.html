Hands-on Activity

Mini-Foley & Found Sound

Part 1: Found Sound Field Recording
  1. Record three distinct events for 5–10 seconds each.
  2. Find a relatively quiet spot and hold the device stable.
  3. Listen back. If noisy, change position or retry.
Part 2: Mini-Foley
  1. Recreate two SFX: heavy footstep and magic sparkle.
  2. Try multiple props and techniques; record several takes.
  3. Compare takes and note the best technique for each.

Key Takeaway

Hands-on practice sharpens mic placement and performance judgment. Multiple takes and clear notes let you reproduce and layer sounds effectively in post.
