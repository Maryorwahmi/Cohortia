Field recording essentials

Equipment
Portable recorder, external mic (shotgun or omni), wind screen, headphones, extra batteries and storage.
Monitoring & Levels
Use headphones and aim for headroom; avoid clipping. Record multiple takes at varied distances.
Safety & Permissions
Check location rules and watch surroundings. Carry spare batteries and protect gear from weather.
Practical Example
Recording a car engine: choose a directional mic to isolate the engine, use a steady tripod or mic stand, record several passes—idle, rev, and drive-by—monitoring for clipping and environmental noise.
Common Mistake
Recording without a wind screen outdoors results in low-frequency rumble. Use a dead cat or foam cover for clear results.
Pro Tip
Label and log each take. Notes about distance, orientation, and ambient conditions save hours in post-production.
Key Takeaway

Careful planning, correct mic choice, and level monitoring produce clean source material that requires less editing and layering later.
