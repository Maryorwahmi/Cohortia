Learning Objectives

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Bloom: Learn ▸ Understand ▸ Apply ▸ Master
01
Identify acquisition methods
Name and compare field recording, foley, libraries, and synthesis. Knowing the options lets you pick the best tool for each SFX need.
Verified competency milestone
02
Field recording essentials
Understand recorders, mic types, wind screens, and safety. Proper technique prevents clipping and noisy takes.
Verified competency milestone
03
Foley performance & layering
Practice prop selection, timing, and recording multiple subtle layers to create convincing interactions.
Verified competency milestone
04
Use libraries & manage licensing
Assess quality, attribution needs, and commercial rights. Licensing decisions determine if a sound is safe to ship with your game.
Verified competency milestone
