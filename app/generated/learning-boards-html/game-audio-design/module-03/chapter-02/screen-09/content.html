Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Foley Layering
Process Flow

Experiment and layer

  1. Identify the physical action and its elements.
  2. Choose props that approximate materials.
  3. Record multiple takes with varied technique.
  4. Layer impact, texture, and high-frequency sparkles.
Concrete Example
Creating a monster footstep: wet sponge for squish, wooden block for impact, and paper rustle for detail. Record each independently, then align with subtle offsets for realism.
Common Mistake
Over-compressing layers early. Keep raw takes flexible; process after you find the right blend.
Key Takeaway

Layer small, focused elements and preserve slight timing variation to keep the sound organic and textured.
