Comparison

Pros vs Cons

Pro: Fast access to many high-quality recordings.
Con: Risk of generic sound if used untouched across projects.
Legal: Licenses vary—check permissions for commercial use and modification.
Concrete Example
Using a commercial thunder hit: confirm the right to include it in a shipped game, then process it—layer, EQ, and add sub-synth—to make it unique and fit your mix.
Checklist
  • Confirm commercial usage rights
  • Confirm modification allowed
  • Record license details and receipts
Key Takeaway

Treat libraries as starting points: verify licenses, then adapt and layer to keep sounds original and legally safe.
