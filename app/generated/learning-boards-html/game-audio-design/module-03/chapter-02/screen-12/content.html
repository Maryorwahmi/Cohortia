Process Flow

Assigning roles to layers

Base: Field rumble for organic body and low-end presence.
Texture: Foley for timing and surface details.
Weight: Library hit for transient attack and impact.
Character: Synthesis for unnatural modulation or pitch movement.
Mixing & Example
Align transients, use EQ to carve frequency space for each role, and add subtle time offsets for realism. Example: monster roar assembled from field rumble, foley growl, library punch, and synth sweep.
Common Mistake
Letting layers mask one another. Fix by EQ and level balancing so each layer has a clear role.
Key Takeaway

Plan layer roles, align timing, and EQ for space. Thoughtful layering yields unique, controllable SFX.
