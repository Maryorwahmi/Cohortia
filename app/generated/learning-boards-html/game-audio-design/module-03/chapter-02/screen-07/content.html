Comparison

Common mistakes vs. fixes

Noisy environment
Traffic, HVAC, crowds
Fix: reposition or use directional mic
Wind noise
Low-frequency rumble
Fix: wind screen or blimp
Clipping
Irreversible distortion when levels are too hot
Fix: monitor and reduce gain
Practical Example
You recorded a fountain but captured constant traffic. Solution: move closer to the fountain with a shotgun mic, or record earlier when traffic is quieter and label files with time and position.
Pro Tip
Record a safety pass: a low-gain long take to capture ambience, plus close high-gain takes for detail. This gives flexibility in post.
Key Takeaway

Prevent common errors with planning, protective gear, level checks, and clear logging; it saves editing time and preserves quality.
