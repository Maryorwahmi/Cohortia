Chapter Summary
Key Concepts
Essential ideas from this chapter
Multi-band Compression

Applies different compression settings to different frequency ranges of an audio signal.

Audio Quality Assurance (QA)

Systematic testing to identify, report, and resolve audio bugs and inconsistencies.

Sound Banks

Collections of audio assets and their metadata, compiled by middleware for efficient game playback.

Summary Takeaway

Effective game audio relies on precise dynamic control, rigorous quality assurance, and organized asset delivery for a professional and immersive experience.
