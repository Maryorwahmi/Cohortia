Interactive vs. Linear Media

Game audio mastering must account for dynamic, player-driven mixes, unlike fixed, pre-rendered tracks in film or music.

  • •**Linear Media:** Fixed mix, predictable playback.
  • •**Interactive Media:** Dynamic mix, player-driven changes, unpredictable scenarios.
Why it Matters: Player Experience

A consistent, impactful soundscape prevents player fatigue and enhances immersion, ensuring critical audio cues are always clear.
