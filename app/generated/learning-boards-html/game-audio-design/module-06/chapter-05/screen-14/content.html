Chapter Summary
Key Concepts
Essential ideas from this chapter
Loudness Targets (LUFS)

A standard for measuring perceived audio loudness over time, ensuring consistent volume levels.

Mastering

The final stage of audio production, processing the entire mix for optimal loudness, clarity, and impact.

Limiter

A dynamic processor that prevents audio signals from exceeding 0dBFS, preventing digital clipping.

Summary Takeaway

Finalizing and mastering game audio ensures a consistent, impactful, and bug-free player experience through careful loudness management, processing, and quality assurance.
