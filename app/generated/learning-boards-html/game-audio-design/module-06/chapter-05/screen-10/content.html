Identifying and resolving common audio bugs
The Importance of Audio QA

Rigorous testing is non-negotiable to ensure a flawless and immersive listening experience for players, preventing immersion-breaking bugs.

  • •**Player Frustration:** Bugs like missing dialogue or looping errors severely impact experience.
  • •**Immersion Break:** Glitches pull players out of the game world.
Common Audio Bugs

Sounds not playing, playing incorrectly (pitch, volume, spatialization), looping errors, missing audio, cutting out, or playing when they shouldn't.
