Advanced Voice Management

Virtual Voices: Smart Resource Allocation

Virtual voices optimize CPU load by allowing inaudible or distant sounds to exist in the audio engine without consuming full processing resources.

Reduced CPU Overhead

Sounds that are far away or occluded consume minimal CPU, only activating fully when audible.

Dynamic Transition

Virtual voices become 'real' voices when they enter the player's audible range, consuming full resources.

Example Scenario

In a large open-world game, distant battle sounds or ambient animal calls might be virtual voices until the player approaches their source.

Key Takeaway

Utilize virtual voices to efficiently manage CPU resources for a large number of potential sounds, enhancing performance in complex audio environments.
