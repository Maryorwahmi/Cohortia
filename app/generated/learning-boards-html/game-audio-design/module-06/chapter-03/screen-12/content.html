Runtime Management

Managing Audio Voices

Voice management techniques are crucial for preventing audio dropouts and controlling CPU load when many sounds play concurrently.

Polyphony

The maximum number of concurrent audio voices (sounds) allowed. Exceeding this causes dropouts.

Voice Prioritization

Assigning importance to sounds. When polyphony is reached, lower-priority sounds are culled to make room for higher-priority ones.

Consequence of No Management

Audio dropouts (sounds not playing) and high CPU spikes during intense gameplay moments.

Key Takeaway

Implement polyphony limits and voice prioritization to ensure critical sounds are always heard and CPU load is managed effectively.
