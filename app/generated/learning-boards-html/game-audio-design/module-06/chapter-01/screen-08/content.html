Creating Space with Reverb

Simulating Environments and Adding Depth

What is Reverb?

Simulates the natural reflections of sound in an environment, creating a sense of space and depth.

How it Works

A sound's direct signal is followed by early reflections and then a decaying 'tail' of diffuse reflections, mimicking real-world acoustics.

Key Parameters
  • Decay Time: How long the reverb lasts.
  • Pre-Delay: Time before reflections begin.
  • Room Size: Simulates the size of the space.
Concrete Example

A character's footsteps will sound dry and immediate in an open outdoor area, but adding a short, dense reverb will make them sound like they're walking inside a small, stone corridor.

Key Takeaway

Use reverb judiciously to create believable acoustic spaces, enhancing immersion without making the mix sound muddy or distant.

Common Mistake

Applying too much reverb to too many sounds, which can quickly wash out the entire mix, reduce clarity, and make everything sound far away and indistinct.
