Modeling Distance with Attenuation

Realistic Volume Changes Based on Proximity

What is Attenuation?

The reduction in volume of a sound as the listener moves further away from its source, mimicking real-world physics.

Why it's Crucial

Provides realistic feedback, allows players to judge distances, and enhances immersion by making the world feel tangible.

Key Parameters
  • Min Distance: Inside this range, volume is at its maximum.
  • Max Distance: Beyond this range, volume is at its minimum (or silent).
  • Roll-off Model: How the volume decreases between min and max.
Concrete Example

An enemy's growl will be loud and clear when they are close, but as they move further away, its volume will gradually decrease, allowing the player to track their approximate distance.

Key Takeaway

Implement distance attenuation to provide realistic proximity cues, making the game world feel more believable and aiding player navigation.

Common Mistake

Not applying distance attenuation, causing sounds to maintain full volume regardless of distance, which breaks immersion and makes spatial judgment impossible for the player.
