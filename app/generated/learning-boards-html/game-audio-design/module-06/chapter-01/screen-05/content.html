Mastering Gain Staging

Setting the Foundation for a Clean Mix

1

Input Levels: Set appropriate levels for raw audio assets in your DAW to prevent clipping.

2

DAW Processing: Ensure levels are healthy through all effects and bus routing within your audio software.

3

Game Engine/Middleware: Adjust final volumes and mixer group levels within the game environment.

Why it matters

Correct gain staging prevents digital clipping, maintains a high signal-to-noise ratio, and ensures a clean, undistorted audio signal throughout the entire production pipeline.

Concrete Example

Before importing a weapon fire sound effect into Unity, you ensure its peak level in your DAW is around -6dBFS. Then, in Unity, you set its AudioSource volume to a moderate level, preventing it from clipping the master mixer.

Key Takeaway

Establish healthy audio levels at every stage of your signal chain to avoid distortion and ensure a robust foundation for your mix.

Common Mistake

Turning down the master volume when the mix is too loud, instead of adjusting individual track or bus gains, which can lead to a poor signal-to-noise ratio and a weak overall sound.
