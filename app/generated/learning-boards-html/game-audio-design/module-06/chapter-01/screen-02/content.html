01

Understand Audio Hierarchy

Grasp the prioritized order of game audio elements (dialogue, SFX, music, ambient) and their relative importance in a dynamic mix. This ensures critical information is always audible.

Verified Competency Milestone
02

Apply Core Mixing Techniques

Utilize gain staging, equalization (EQ), and compression to shape individual sounds and create a balanced, clear overall mix. This is fundamental for sonic clarity.

Verified Competency Milestone
03

Implement Spatial Mixing

Utilize panning, distance attenuation, and basic 3D audio principles to create a sense of space, direction, and depth. This enhances player immersion and feedback.

Verified Competency Milestone
04

Integrate Mixing into Workflow

Understand how mixing principles apply within game engines and middleware, connecting theoretical knowledge to practical implementation. This bridges design and development.
