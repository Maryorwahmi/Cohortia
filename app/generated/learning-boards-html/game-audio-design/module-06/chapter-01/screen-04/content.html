Core Concept

The Game Audio Hierarchy

Prioritizing Sounds for Clarity and Impact

1

Dialogue: Critical narrative and instructional speech. Must be clear.

2

SFX: Player feedback, immediate threats, UI confirmations. Essential cues.

3

Music: Sets mood and pace. Should recede for critical sounds.

4

Ambient: Establishes environment. Should not demand constant attention.

Why it matters

A clear hierarchy prevents masking, ensuring players receive vital information without being overwhelmed by less important sounds. This directly impacts gameplay and immersion.

Concrete Example

During a critical cutscene, dialogue volume automatically increases, while background music and ambient sounds gently duck, ensuring the player hears every word of the narrative.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize game audio elements dynamically to maintain clarity, deliver essential feedback, and enhance player immersion.

Common Mistake

Allowing background music or ambient loops to consistently overpower critical dialogue or immediate threat sound effects, leading to player frustration and missed cues.
