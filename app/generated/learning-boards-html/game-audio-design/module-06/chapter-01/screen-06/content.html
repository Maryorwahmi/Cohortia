Shaping Sound with EQ

Carving Space in the Frequency Spectrum

What is EQ?

Equalization (EQ) adjusts the balance of frequencies in an audio signal, shaping its tonal characteristics.

Why Use EQ?

Prevents sounds from clashing, eliminates muddiness, and helps individual sounds cut through the mix.

Common Techniques
  • Cutting: Removing unwanted frequencies (e.g., low-end rumble).
  • Boosting: Enhancing desirable frequencies (e.g., presence in dialogue).
  • Filtering: Removing all frequencies above or below a certain point.
Concrete Example

To make a character's dialogue clearer, you might use an EQ to gently roll off frequencies below 150Hz, preventing it from competing with the low-end of an explosion sound effect or background music.

Key Takeaway

EQ is a powerful tool for creating sonic clarity by giving each sound its own distinct frequency range within the overall mix.

Common Mistake

Over-boosting frequencies, especially in the mid-range, which can make sounds harsh, unnatural, and contribute to a fatiguing mix rather than a clear one.
