Chapter Review

Key Concepts

Essential ideas from Cross-Platform Audio Delivery.

Platform Requirements

Technical constraints (CPU, memory, codecs, APIs) specific to different gaming platforms.

Playback Environment

The type of audio system a player uses (headphones, stereo, surround), influencing mixing decisions.

Surround Sound (5.1/7.1)

Multi-channel audio systems creating an immersive spatial sound field with multiple speakers.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding platform limitations, adapting to diverse playback environments, and leveraging multi-channel audio are foundational for cross-platform audio excellence.
