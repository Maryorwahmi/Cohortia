Contextual Audio

States: Granular Context Control

Defining Audio Behavior Based on Dynamic Gameplay Variables

What are States?

States represent different conditions in the game (e.g., 'PlayerHealth: Low', 'Location: Indoors'). They trigger specific audio behaviors and parameter changes.

Granular Control

You can define how individual audio parameters (volumes, effects, even which sounds play) change when these states are active, allowing for highly nuanced responses.

Key Takeaway

States offer a powerful way to create deeply integrated and context-aware audio that responds to the smallest shifts in gameplay.

State Examples & Effects

Player Health: Low

Heartbeat sound, urgent music, muffled enemy sounds.

Location: Indoors

Increased reverb, muffled outdoor sounds, distinct ambient loops.

States allow for dynamic audio that truly reflects the game's internal logic.
