Snapshots: Instant Mixer Configurations

Saving and Recalling Entire Audio Mixes for Game Scenarios

What is a Snapshot?

A snapshot is a saved state of your entire audio mixer, including volumes, EQs, sends, and effects. It's a 'photograph' of your mix at a specific moment.

How They're Used

Snapshots are recalled instantly via game code to smoothly transition the mix to match the current gameplay context, like entering combat or a peaceful zone.

Key Takeaway

Snapshots provide a powerful way to make global, context-specific changes to your game's audio mix with a single command.
