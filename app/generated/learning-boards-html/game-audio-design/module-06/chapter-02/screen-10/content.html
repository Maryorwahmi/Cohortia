Crafting Smooth RTPC Transitions

Avoiding Jarring Audio Changes with Careful Curve Design

Common Mistake

Making RTPC transitions too abrupt can be jarring and pull the player out of immersion. Instant changes feel unnatural.

The Solution: Automation Curves

Design gentle, interpolated curves within your audio middleware to smoothly transition parameters over time, ensuring a natural feel.

Key Takeaway

Smooth RTPC transitions are crucial for maintaining immersion and delivering a polished, professional audio experience.
