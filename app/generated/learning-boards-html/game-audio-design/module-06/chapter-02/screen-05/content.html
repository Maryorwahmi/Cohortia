Audio Ducking: Ensuring Clarity

Automatically Adjusting Volume for Critical Information

Definition

Audio ducking automatically lowers the volume of one audio element when another, more critical element, plays. It's a key technique for sound prioritization.

Classic Example

Music volume gently reduces when character dialogue begins, ensuring every word is heard clearly without the music disappearing entirely.

Key Takeaway

Ducking prevents auditory clutter, making critical sounds stand out and improving player comprehension.
