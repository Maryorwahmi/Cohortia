Skills You'll Practice

Concrete workflow skills to test formats and measure trade-offs.

Convert
Export WAV to FLAC, MP3, and Ogg and record resulting sizes for objective comparison.
Listen
Use critical listening to detect artifacts at low and high bitrates and note where perception changes.
Profile
Measure decompression CPU, memory, and latency in-engine for streaming and preloaded assets.
Activity
Follow the conversion steps using Audacity or FFmpeg and compare a 10-second WAV converted to FLAC, MP3 at 256 kbps, and Ogg at quality 3 and 8.
Outcome
A small spreadsheet listing sizes and notes on perceived quality to guide format choices later in the project.
