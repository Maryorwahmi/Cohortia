Core Concept

Lossy Compression

Uses psychoacoustic models to reduce data by removing information unlikely to be noticed by listeners.

How it saves space
By exploiting masking and frequency sensitivity, codecs remove or coarsely encode parts of the signal with minimal perceived impact.
Bitrate explained
Bitrate in kilobits per second sets how much data is stored per second of audio. Higher bitrates retain more detail.
Key Takeaway

Lossy codecs offer the best size-to-quality ratio for long assets; choose bitrates based on listening tests in context.

Comparison cards
MP3 — widely supported, good at common bitrates
Ogg Vorbis — open-source, efficient, often preferred in games
AAC — efficient quality vs MP3 at similar bitrates
Common-mistake
Over-compressing critical sounds like voice leads to audible artifacts; always audition in-game context.
