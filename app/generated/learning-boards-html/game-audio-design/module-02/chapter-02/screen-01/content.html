Audio File Formats and Compression

Understand how uncompressed, lossless, and lossy formats affect fidelity, size, and CPU load so you can make practical choices for music, dialogue, and effects.

Format families
Uncompressed • Lossless • Lossy
In this lesson
How formats change installation size, memory use, and runtime cost. Practical recommendations for music, dialogue, ambience, and UI sounds.
Why it matters
Good format choices keep download size reasonable while preserving the audio quality players notice most.
Explore Key Topics
WAV / AIFF: production masters and ultra-short, latency-critical effects.
FLAC: lossless storage for dialog and music when fidelity matters.
Ogg Vorbis / AAC: best size-to-quality for music and long loops.
Visual Concept
Size vs Quality
A conceptual scale showing uncompressed at highest quality and size, lossless reduced size, lossy much smaller with tuned quality settings.
Example
1 min stereo @ 48 kHz, 16-bit ≈ 10 MB as WAV. Lossy versions may be 1–2 MB at common bitrates.
Practical tip
Always test compressed assets in-engine to judge acceptable quality vs performance impact.
