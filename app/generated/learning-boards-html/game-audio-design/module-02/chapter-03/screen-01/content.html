In this lesson
Identify interface parts, perform fundamental edits, apply normalization, and use EQ and reverb to match game spaces.
Why it matters
Prepared audio prevents clicks, ensures consistent levels, and makes assets reusable inside a game's audio system.
Quick Orientation

Think: timeline (arrange), tracks (layers), mixer (balance), transport (playback). These are your daily tools.

Timeline & Tracks
Audio clips arranged left-to-right on tracks. Use them to layer dialogue, SFX, and music.
Mixer & FX
Faders, pan, and inserts let you shape levels and apply EQ, reverb, and other effects per track.
Transport
Playback controls and timeline navigation — essential for auditioning edits and rendering exports.
Non-destructive Editing
Edits are applied during playback or render. Originals remain safe for later adjustments.
