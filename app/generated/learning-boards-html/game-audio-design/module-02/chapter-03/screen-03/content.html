Practice & Outcomes

Concrete tasks you can perform to prove each objective.

Interface Check
Open a DAW and name the timeline, at least three track lanes, and the mixer channels. This shows you can navigate the workspace.
Edit Demo
Import a combined WAV, split dialogue and SFX, trim silence, and add a crossfade. Export the edits as a new file.
Normalize & Gain
Normalize dialogue to a target peak, then adjust track and bus levels to leave headroom and avoid clipping when adding effects.
EQ & Reverb
Apply an EQ cut to remove low rumble from SFX and a subtle reverb to dialogue. Keep processing gentle and purposeful.
Verification tip

Record your steps as notes or screenshots. Clear evidence of each edit helps you iterate and gives a reproducible workflow for future projects.
