Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Trimming, Splitting, Crossfades
Trim
Shorten the start or end of a clip to remove silence or unwanted noise before the useful sound begins.
Split
Cut a clip into segments to isolate hits, dialogue lines, or phrases for targeted processing.
Crossfade
Blend adjacent clips to remove waveform discontinuities and create seamless loops or transitions.
Concrete example
From the sample file, split dialogue and the sword clang. Trim trailing silence on the dialogue. If two clips of the same ambient file are joined, overlap by 50–150 ms and apply a short crossfade to avoid any click at the seam.
Common mistake
Overlong crossfades can smear transient detail and change perceived timing — keep fades tight for percussive sounds.
Key Takeaway
Precise, minimal edits preserve character while removing artifacts; use short fades for percussive SFX and slightly longer ones for ambient material.
