What EQ does
Boosts or cuts specific frequency ranges to shape timbre and clarity.
Common uses
  • Remove low rumble with a high-pass filter.
  • Cut muddy mid frequencies.
  • Boost presence in upper mids for clarity.
Misconception
Large boosts are not a substitute for fixing frequency clashes; cut competing elements first.
Concrete example
A sword clang sounds muddy: apply a narrow cut around 250–400 Hz to remove boxiness, then add a tempered boost around 3–6 kHz to bring out the metallic snap. Always compare bypassed and processed versions.
Key Takeaway
EQ clears space in the frequency spectrum. Prefer thoughtful cuts and modest boosts to keep sounds natural and distinct in the mix.
