Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Applying Effects & Exporting
Effect order & strategy
Prefer corrective EQ first, then creative processing like reverb. Use sends for shared spaces and inserts for unique coloration.
Export checklist
  • Confirm normalization and headroom.
  • Choose engine-expected format and sample rate.
  • Name files clearly and place in the export folder.
Concrete exercise
Apply EQ to reduce muddiness on SFX, add a subtle reverb to dialogue, then export two WAV files: edited_dialogue.wav and edited_sfx_sword.wav. Keep sample rate aligned with project settings.
Key Takeaway
Think of export as the final handoff: deliver clean, normalized files with clear names and the correct format expected by the audio implementation team.
