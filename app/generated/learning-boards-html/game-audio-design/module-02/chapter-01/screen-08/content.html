Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › ADC & DAC
ADC (Analog-to-Digital)

Capture chain

1. Anti-aliasing filter removes out-of-band frequencies.
2. Samples are taken at the chosen sample rate.
3. Each sample is quantized according to bit depth and stored.
Example
A microphone feeding an ADC at 48 kHz, 24-bit captures a high-quality source suitable for later mixing and export.
DAC (Digital-to-Analog)

Playback chain

1. Stored samples are read from the file or buffer.
2. The DAC converts numeric values into analog voltages.
3. Smoothing filters reduce stepping artifacts before amplification.
Key Takeaway
Playback cannot recreate lost information; capture well when fidelity matters, then optimize for delivery.
