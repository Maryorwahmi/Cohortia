Definition

Digital audio as numbers

Sound is reduced to samples and quantized amplitudes. Treat these two dimensions separately when diagnosing issues or choosing export settings.

Sampling (time)
How often the waveform is measured. Sets frequency fidelity.
Quantization (amplitude)
How precisely each measurement is represented. Bit depth controls dynamic range and noise.
Key Takeaway

Always state sample rate and bit depth when sharing audio; they communicate the asset's frequency and amplitude limits.

Concrete example

A recorded clap

If the clap has strong high-frequency transient, choose a sampling rate above twice the highest harmonic to avoid losing its brightness. Use a sufficient bit depth to capture the quiet decay without introducing noise.

Misconception
Higher sample rate does not substitute for proper bit depth. Frequency and amplitude axes are independent.
Question to reflect
