Process

Sampling steps

1
Measure amplitude at regular intervals
These are the raw samples that capture waveform shape across time.
2
Record the sample rate (Hz)
Higher rates capture higher-frequency detail but cost more storage and CPU.
3
Apply Nyquist to set the minimum rate
Ensure the sample rate is at least twice the highest important frequency to avoid aliasing.
Example
Record a cymbal with energy to 18 kHz. 48 kHz sampling gives safe headroom and standard compatibility with game engines and video.
Quick checks
Check for aliasing: listen for unexpected low-frequency artifacts when sample rate is too low.
Tool tip: use filters or anti-aliasing during ADC to reduce out-of-band energy before sampling.
Performance: higher sample rates increase file size linearly and can raise CPU load for processing and mixing.
Key Takeaway

Set sampling rate to cover audible content and platform needs; 48 kHz is a practical default in games.
