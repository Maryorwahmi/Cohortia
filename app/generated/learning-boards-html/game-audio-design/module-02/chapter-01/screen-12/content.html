Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Choosing Settings for In-Game Assets
Application

Decision flow

Step 1: Prioritize by perceptual importance. Mark dialogue and cinematics as high priority.
Step 2: Inspect the source for high-frequency energy and quiet dynamic details.
Step 3: Choose sample rate to meet Nyquist for highest important frequency with filter headroom.
Step 4: Use 24-bit during production; decide final bit depth based on platform and storage.
Example: Cinematic VO → record/edit at 24-bit, export at 48 kHz, 16-bit only if needed.
Example: UI SFX → export directly at 48 kHz, 16-bit to save memory.
Key Takeaway
Make choices per asset: preserve quality where players notice it, optimize where they don't.
