Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Common Mistakes & Pro Tips
Common mistakes
Exporting every asset at the highest sample rate and bit depth without assessing perceptual need.
Confusing project rate changes with export resampling and ending up with pitch or speed errors.
Using too low bit depth for quiet ambient layers, producing a noisy floor.
Pro tips
Record at 24-bit. Preserve dynamics while editing and mixing.
Use 48 kHz as a practical delivery standard for games and video sync.
Resample intentionally and verify exports by listening and checking file properties.
Key Takeaway
Be deliberate: maximize quality during production; optimize for delivery based on perceptual priority.
