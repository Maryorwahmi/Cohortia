Overview strip
Sampling
Snapshots of amplitude taken per second. Controls what frequencies you can represent.
Quantization
Rounds each snapshot to discrete amplitude steps. Bit depth sets the number of steps.
Practical choice
Use 48 kHz and 16-bit for many in-game assets; record at 24-bit for production work.
