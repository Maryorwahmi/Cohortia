Comparison

Fidelity vs resources

High quality (96 kHz, 24-bit)
Excellent capture detail but much larger files and higher CPU load.
Optimized (48 kHz, 16-bit)
Balanced fidelity and performance; widely used in games and video production.
Concrete example
A single 10-second sound at 96 kHz, 24-bit uses roughly four times the space of 48 kHz, 16-bit. Multiply that across hundreds of assets and the build size grows quickly.
Guidance
Identify critical assets (voice, cinematic SFX) and keep higher fidelity for those.
Convert ambience, loops, and UI sounds to optimized rates to save space.
Remember that playback hardware and hearing limits often make ultra-high sample rates unnecessary.
Key Takeaway

Match fidelity to the asset's perceptual importance and platform constraints rather than always choosing the highest numbers.
