Practical objectives

Skills for inspecting, resampling, and exporting audio assets with correct properties for game deployment.

Hands-on ready
Inspect
Identify sample rate and bit depth in your editor or file properties.
Compare
Note project rate vs source file and decide whether to resample before export.
Export
Choose final sample rate and bit depth that match your platform and asset needs.
Misconception check

Changing the project rate only alters playback and processing in the DAW until you resample and export. Always confirm the exported file's properties before adding it to your build.
