Comparison

WAV vs OGG vs MP3

Trade quality for size depending on role: preserve masters, compress for runtime.

WAV
Lossless, largest size, ideal for source material and critical short SFX.
OGG
Open lossy format, good quality at lower bitrates; common for game music and longer SFX.
MP3
Lossy and ubiquitous; less preferred than OGG for game runtime due to quality and licensing considerations.
Example activity
Convert a 48 kHz 24-bit WAV to OGG and MP3 at medium settings. Compare file size and listen for loss in highs or transient 'punch'. Note where each format would be appropriate in your game.
Decision guide
Key Takeaway
Keep masters lossless; export compressed formats for runtime with awareness of lost detail.
Common mistake
Applying heavy lossy compression to critical SFX and discarding the original masters.
