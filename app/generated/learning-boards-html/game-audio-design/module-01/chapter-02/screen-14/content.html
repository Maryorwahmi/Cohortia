Key Concepts

Frequency
Cycles per second determine pitch and spectral placement.
Amplitude
Magnitude maps to loudness; manage levels for clarity and comfort.
Waveform
Shape contains harmonics and defines timbre and character.
Sampling Rate
Snapshots per second; 48 kHz is practical for games.
Bit Depth
Precision per sample; 24-bit gives more dynamic range for production.
Formats & Channels
WAV masters; OGG for runtime; mono for spatial SFX, stereo for ambience.
Final takeaway

Capture with care, keep lossless masters, export appropriately, and prepare assets to be efficient and clear in the game context.
