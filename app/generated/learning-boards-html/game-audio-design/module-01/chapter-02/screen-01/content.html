Visual concept

Role
Critical UI sound
Needs pristine transient response and no compression artifacts.
Technical choice
WAV, 48 kHz, 16–24 bit
High fidelity, larger size, best for short critical SFX.
Impact
Clarity & consistency
Preserves punch and avoids masking important cues during gameplay.
Quick note
Keep your working files in lossless form. Export compressed versions for runtime but archive masters as WAV or another lossless format.
