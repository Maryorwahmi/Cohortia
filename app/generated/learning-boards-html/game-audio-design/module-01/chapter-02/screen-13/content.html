Process

Avoiding common pitfalls

A compact set of dos and don'ts to keep projects clean and predictable.

Do: Keep masters
Store WAV masters at production specs before encoding runtime copies.
Don't: Over-compress critical SFX
Lossy compression can remove transient punch needed for recognition.
Do: Standardize specs
Use a consistent sampling rate and bit depth across the project to avoid resampling issues.
Don't: Ship stereo for positionable SFX
Wasteful and reduces engine flexibility for spatialization.
Do: Test runtime
Listen to exported formats in the engine to confirm perceived quality and memory cost.
Checklist
Master WAVs, consistent sample rate, 24-bit where possible, mono for SFX, OGG for music runtime.
