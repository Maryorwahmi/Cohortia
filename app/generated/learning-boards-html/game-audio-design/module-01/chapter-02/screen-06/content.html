Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Amplitude and loudness
Definition

Amplitude

The magnitude of the wave; larger amplitudes are perceived as louder sounds.

Production tips
Maintain headroom, automate levels, and avoid normalizing everything to the same loudness.
Example scenario
In a crowded mix, duck ambient beds under dialogue to keep player attention on speech cues.
Pitfall
Overly loud SFX cause ear fatigue and may mask critical gameplay information.
Visual tools
Waveform view
Shows amplitude over time to identify peaks and quiet sections.
Loudness meters
Measure perceived loudness and help match levels across assets.
Key Takeaway
Balance amplitude for clarity and comfort; leave headroom for processing.
