Sampling rate & aliasing

Choose a sampling rate that captures the frequencies you need; prevent aliasing with filters or adequate sampling.

Rule of thumb
Sample at least twice the highest frequency you want to reproduce.
Practical setting
48 kHz aligns well with video and preserves frequencies up to about 24 kHz.
Pitfall
Mixing assets with different sampling rates without conversion can cause pitch shifts and artifacts.
Check your work
If you hear low artifacts
Confirm high-frequency content was filtered or that assets were recorded at an appropriate rate.
Key Takeaway
Prevent aliasing by filtering or using suitable sampling rates; consistency across assets avoids runtime issues.
