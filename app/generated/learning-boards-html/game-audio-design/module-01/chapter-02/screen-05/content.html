Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Frequency and pitch
Definition

Frequency

Cycles per second, measured in Hertz, that determine pitch and the placement of sound in the spectrum.

Practical rule
Use frequency separation to avoid masking: keep dialogue in mid-range, bass for explosions, highs for alerts.
Concrete example
A headshot ping emphasizes high frequencies so it remains audible above music and low rumbles.
Misconception
Boosting an overlapping frequency band always helps. In reality, it can cause masking or clipping elsewhere.
Frequency zones
Low
20–200 Hz — explosions, bass.
Mid
200–5,000 Hz — dialogue, most SFX.
High
5,000–20,000 Hz — pings, air, detail.
