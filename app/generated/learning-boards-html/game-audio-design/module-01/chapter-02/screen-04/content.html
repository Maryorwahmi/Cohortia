Core Concept

Sound as a wave

A physical vibration expressed as a time-varying pressure pattern with measurable properties.

Three descriptors
  • Frequency: cycle rate, perceived as pitch.
  • Amplitude: magnitude, perceived as loudness.
  • Waveform: shape, contributing to timbre.
Example
A UI ding is short, high-frequency, and low energy; an explosion is long, low-frequency, and high amplitude.
Key Takeaway
Knowing the physical shape of sound lets you edit for clarity and choose digital settings that preserve important details.
Visual comparator
Transient / percussive
Sharp waveform with quick attack and decay.
Sustained / pad
Smooth waveform with even amplitude.
Combined / complex
Many harmonics and a unique timbre; harder to compress without loss.
Common mistake
Treating loudness as the only metric of importance. Loud can mask other cues and tire the player.
