Practical outcomes

Tasks and checks to confirm competency.

Asset checklist
  • Consistent sampling rate (e.g., 48 kHz) across assets.
  • Working masters in WAV, exports in OGG/MP3 as needed.
  • Mono for spatialized SFX, stereo for ambient beds.
Verification tasks
Short quizzes and a hands-on conversion comparing file size and perceptible artifacts.
Mnemonic
FAR — Frequency, Amplitude, Range
A compact way to recall pitch, loudness, and timbre considerations.
Verified Competency
Export a WAV and an OGG from the same master and explain the trade-offs.
