Learning Objectives

Master the core audio decisions that shape game sound.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain sound properties
Describe frequency, amplitude, and waveform and how they affect perception. This helps you craft clear, non-masking audio layers.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe digitization
Explain sampling rate and bit depth, and why production often uses 48 kHz / 24-bit for headroom and fidelity.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Compare formats
Choose between WAV, OGG, and MP3 based on quality needs, file size, and licensing concerns for your game assets.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Configure channels
Distinguish mono, stereo and surround uses and prepare assets for engine spatialization and performance constraints.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
