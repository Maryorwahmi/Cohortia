Definition

Waveform & Timbre

The shape of the wave carries harmonic content that gives a sound its character.

Harmonics matter
Different shapes add or remove harmonics, making instruments and effects sound unique.
Editing tip
Preserve transients and harmonic detail for short, recognizable cues by avoiding excessive compression.
Key Takeaway
Timbre is central to recognition; protect waveform detail in important assets.
Analogy & examples
Sine wave
Pure tone with no harmonics; smooth and clean.
Square / complex wave
Contains harmonics that create a richer, more aggressive timbre.
Misconception
Timbre is only aesthetic; in fact, it affects detection and emotional response in gameplay.
