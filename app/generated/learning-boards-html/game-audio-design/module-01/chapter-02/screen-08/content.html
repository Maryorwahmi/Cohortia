Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Analog-to-digital conversion
Process Flow

From wave to samples

Snapshots at a fixed rate with finite precision produce digital audio; filtering prevents aliasing.

Step 1: Anti-alias filter
Remove frequencies above half the sampling rate to avoid aliasing artifacts.
Step 2: Sampling
Take amplitude snapshots at the chosen sampling rate, e.g., 48 kHz.
Step 3: Quantization
Encode each sample with a finite bit depth, e.g., 24 bits, determining precision and dynamic range.
Concrete example
Recording voice
Record at 48 kHz / 24-bit, apply anti-alias filtering, keep the WAV master, export compressed copies for runtime.
Key Takeaway
Digitization choices fix what information is available; capture carefully because you cannot regain lost detail later.
