Quick Reminders

1. Preserve masters
Keep WAV copies at production settings for future edits.
2. Standardize specs
Use consistent sampling rate and bit depth across the project.
3. Match channels
Mono for positionable SFX; stereo for wide ambiances; leave surround to the engine where possible.
Closing
Apply these rules to reduce surprises in the build and to ensure your audio remains clear, memorable, and efficient.
