Process

Record, layer, and prepare stems

  • Field recording and foley for natural textures.
  • Synthesis for otherworldly or mechanical sounds.
  • Layering multiple elements to create impact and clarity.
  • Prepare variations and metadata for middleware events.
Key Takeaway

Effective sound design is about context. Design with the game's visual and gameplay needs in mind and provide assets that are easy to implement.

Concrete example
A sword hit delivered as three stems: metallic contact, fleshy thump, and bright transient. In middleware, these are layered and a random variation selects slightly different transients per strike so repeated hits don't sound identical.
Common mistake
Using unmodified stock sounds often results in a disconnected sonic identity. Always edit and tailor sounds to the game's palette.
Pro tip

Deliver short, normalized WAV stems and include a README with suggested event names and parameter uses for the implementer.
