Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Common Pitfalls
Top Pitfalls
  • • Late audio integration — plan SFX and music earlier.
  • • Poor balance — test with all layers and on multiple devices.
  • • Repetition fatigue — create variants or procedural variation.
  • • Licensing oversight — confirm rights or create original assets.
Prevention Checklist
  1. 1. Schedule audio work early in milestones.
  2. 2. Mix in context and across playback systems.
  3. 3. Produce 3 variants for frequent cues.
  4. 4. Document event rules and priority ducks.
Reflection

Adopt practical habits early: a short checklist often prevents most common breakdowns.
