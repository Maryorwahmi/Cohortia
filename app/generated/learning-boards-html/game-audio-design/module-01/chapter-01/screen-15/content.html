Immersive Audio
Layer ambience, foley, and reactive cues to create presence.

Use distinct sounds to confirm actions and guide players.
Non-linear Audio
Design in states and events; map parameters to gameplay variables.
Summary Takeaway
Good game audio is purposeful: it supports player decisions, evokes emotion, and adapts to play. Practice listening and design with rules.
