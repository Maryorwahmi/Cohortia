Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Spatialization
Core Techniques
  • • Panning: suggest lateral direction.
  • • Attenuation: adjust level by distance.
  • • Filtering: dull high frequencies for far sources.
  • • Early reflections/reverb: suggest room size.
Practical Example
Enemy approach: low-volume, filtered growl → louder, clearer, panned toward player as it nears.
Design Cards
Speaker Tip
Check spatial cues on headphones and stereo speakers; expect different impressions and adjust levels accordingly.
Avoid
Extreme, inconsistent panning or mismatched reverb that breaks spatial coherence.
Key Takeaway

Spatial audio helps players find and prioritise sounds; tune distance and direction cues carefully for each asset.
