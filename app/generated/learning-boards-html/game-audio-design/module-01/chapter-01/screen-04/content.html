Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Immersive Audio
Definition

Immersive audio combines ambience, foley, and contextual events to make environments feel alive and believable.

  • • Base ambience establishes place and tonal character.
  • • Foley anchors actions to materials and scale.
  • • Reactive cues reward attention and cue threats or opportunities.
Example
Forest: wind ambience, leaf footsteps, distant howl—each layer signals different information and emotion.
Key Takeaway
Subtle, consistent layers create stronger immersion than loud, competing effects.
Layered Visual
Base Ambience
Continuous environmental tone setting place.
Foley & Interactive SFX
Surface-specific footsteps, doors, and objects that confirm player actions.
Reactive Cues
Warning howls, heartbeat when low health, or proximity rustles that change with state.
Key Takeaway

Design layers with purpose: ambience for place, foley for action, reactive cues for player decisions.
