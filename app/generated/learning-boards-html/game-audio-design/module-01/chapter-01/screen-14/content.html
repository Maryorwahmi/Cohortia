Pre-Release Checklist
  • • Cross-device mix test: headphones, speakers, mobile.
  • • License audit: confirm rights for all assets.
  • • Performance check: streaming, voice memory, polyphony limits.
  • • Variant coverage for frequent cues to reduce fatigue.
Playtest Prompts
Ask testers to complete tasks with audio only, report confusing cues, and note any sounds that cause discomfort or fatigue.
Key Takeaway

Polish is systematic: test widely, confirm rights, and add variation so audio remains informative and pleasant over time.
