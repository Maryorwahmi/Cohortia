Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Narrative & Emotion
Claim

Sound conveys narrative context and emotional direction, often without words.

Motif Example
A short melodic cell associated with a character that returns during key scenes.
SFX Narrative
A creaking door and children's laughter together imply memory or loss without text.
Key Takeaway

Use musical motifs and signature sounds to signal story beats and emotional shifts; avoid saturating the scene so motifs remain effective.

Analogy
Think of motifs like character leitmotifs in theatre: short, repeatable identifiers that prime player expectations and recall.
Design Prompt
Choose one character and design a two-note motif and one signature SFX. Describe how each supports that character's role.
