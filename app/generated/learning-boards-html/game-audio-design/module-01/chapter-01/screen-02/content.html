How you'll practice
Each objective includes a short listening exercise, an analysis template, and a quick design checklist to apply immediately in a prototype. Expect to listen, annotate, and propose one concrete change that improves clarity or emotion.
Assessment
Short multiple-choice questions and a practical listening submission.
Milestone
Annotate five audio cues from a 15-minute segment and explain their function.
