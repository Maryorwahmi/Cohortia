Milestones & Checks
  • Immersion
    Milestone: Identify ambient layers and explain the emotional effect they create.
  • Non-linear
    Milestone: Sketch event logic that transitions music or SFX when state changes.
  • Feedback
    Milestone: Annotate five cues, explaining the player-facing information they convey.
  • Pitfalls
    Milestone: Create a checklist to avoid late integration, imbalance, and repetition.
