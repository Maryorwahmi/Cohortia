IEnumerable / IEnumerator
Aggregate declares GetEnumerator; iterator provides MoveNext and Current.
Dispose and resources
Always Dispose enumerators that wrap streams or external handles.
Test edge cases
Verify empty, sparse, and concurrent modification behaviors with unit tests.
Final Tip

If you need custom traversals, provide additional concrete iterators rather than adding traversal responsibilities to the collection itself.
