Warning › Concurrent modification
What happens
Enumerators often carry a version stamp. If the collection's version changes during iteration the enumerator detects the mismatch and throws an InvalidOperationException.
• Safe removal strategies: iterate a copy, collect removals then remove after enumeration, or use a reverse index loop for indexed collections.
• Snapshot iterators take a copy of elements at the start to avoid mid-iteration changes but cost memory.
• Always Dispose enumerators that wrap resources.
Quick checklist
  • • Will callers modify during enumeration?
  • • Do iterators check a version stamp?
  • • Is snapshotting acceptable for your scenario?
