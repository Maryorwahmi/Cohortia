Key Concepts › What to Remember
Separate traversal
Use iterators so clients don't depend on internal storage.
Multiple iterators
Provide concrete iterators for reverse, filtered, or tree traversals.
Safe iteration
Detect concurrent modification or snapshot sequences and ensure Dispose is used when needed.
Takeaway

Design traversal as a first-class capability: implement IEnumerable/IEnumerator, provide clear traversal variants, and document modification policies so callers can rely on predictable behavior.
