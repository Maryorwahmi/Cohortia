Implementation checklist
  • Call VideoReader.Read to obtain raw data.
  • Call AudioDecoder.DecodeAudio and VideoDecoder.DecodeVideo.
  • Apply FilterProcessor.ApplyFilters when filter is provided.
  • Call AudioEncoder.EncodeAudio and VideoEncoder.EncodeVideo with format.
  • Write result with FileWriter.Write.
