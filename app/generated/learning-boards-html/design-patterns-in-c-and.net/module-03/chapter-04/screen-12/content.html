God object: symptoms and fixes
Symptoms
Large facade methods, many responsibilities, conditional branches that implement domain rules, and a lot of state.
Mitigations
Extract logic into subsystem classes, split the facade into multiple facades, and prefer dependency injection for testability.
Review practice
During code review, flag facades with complex decision trees and recommend refactoring those decisions into domain services.
Quick checklist
  • Does the facade contain heavy business logic? Refactor.
  • Could multiple facades cover different use cases? Consider splitting.
  • Is dependency injection used? Prefer it for testing.
  • Are subsystems still accessible for advanced clients? Keep them available.
