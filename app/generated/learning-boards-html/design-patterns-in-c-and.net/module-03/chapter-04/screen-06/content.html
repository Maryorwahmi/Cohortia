Analogy: dashboard as facade

The dashboard simplifies driving by presenting a few controls while hiding engine and subsystem complexity. Software facades do the same for clients interacting with many collaborating classes.

Users
Call simple operations like PlaceOrder or ConvertVideo without managing internals.
Mechanics
Have access to detailed subsystem APIs for diagnostics or advanced operations.
Misuse warning

If the dashboard or facade begins to implement detailed domain logic, it becomes a God object. Keep it thin and delegate heavy behavior to subsystem classes.

Reflective question
What controls would you expose to simplify your application's most common workflows?
