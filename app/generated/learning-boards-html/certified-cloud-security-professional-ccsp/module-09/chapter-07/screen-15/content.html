Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

International Harmonization

The process of aligning disparate national and regional laws, regulations, and standards to create a more consistent global framework.

Cross-Border Data Transfer

The movement of data from one legal jurisdiction to another, often subject to specific regulations (e.g., GDPR's Chapter V).

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

Pre-approved model clauses issued by the European Commission that can be incorporated into contracts to facilitate legal transfers of personal data outside the EEA.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding these core concepts is fundamental to navigating the legal complexities of global cloud security and ensuring compliant data operations.
