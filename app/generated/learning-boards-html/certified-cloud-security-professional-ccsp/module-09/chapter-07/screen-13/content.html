Implementing Conflict Mitigation Strategies

Data localization, encryption, and legal protections are key.

Organizations can employ a combination of technical, organizational, and legal strategies to mitigate conflicts arising from international legal requirements regarding data access.

  • Data Localization/Residency: Store data in regions with favorable legal frameworks.
  • Strong Encryption (CMK): Encrypt sensitive data with customer-managed keys for enhanced control.
  • Data Minimization & Pseudonymization: Reduce and obscure personal data to lower risk.
  • Legal Counsel & Contracts: Draft robust contracts with CSPs addressing disclosure processes and transparency.
Key Takeaway

A multi-faceted approach combining data localization, strong encryption, data minimization, and robust legal contracts is essential for managing conflicting international legal requirements in the cloud.

Data Localization

Storing data within specific geographical boundaries to align with local laws.

Example: An EU company ensures all customer data is hosted in data centers located within the EU.

Customer-Managed Keys (CMK)

Encrypting data with keys controlled by the customer, not the cloud provider.

Pro-Tip: CMK ensures that even if data is accessed, it remains unintelligible without your explicit key.
