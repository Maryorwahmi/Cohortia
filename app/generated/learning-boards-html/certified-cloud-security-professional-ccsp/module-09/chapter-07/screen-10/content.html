The EU-US Data Privacy Framework

A new mechanism for transatlantic data flows.

The EU-US Data Privacy Framework is an adequacy decision adopted by the European Commission in July 2023, providing a legal mechanism for transatlantic data transfers.

  • Adequacy Decision: Allows legal transfers from EU to certified US organizations.
  • Replaces Privacy Shield: Addresses concerns about US government surveillance that invalidated previous frameworks.
  • Enhanced Safeguards: Includes a Data Protection Review Court for EU data subjects.
  • Purpose: Restores legal certainty for transatlantic data flows and reduces compliance burden.
Key Takeaway

The EU-US Data Privacy Framework is a critical adequacy decision that facilitates legal transatlantic data transfers, offering enhanced safeguards and replacing previous invalidated mechanisms.

Transatlantic Bridge

Provides a legal pathway for data transfers between the European Union and the United States.

Example: An EU e-commerce site uses a US-based CRM certified under the DPF to manage customer data.

Addressing Past Concerns

Designed to overcome the legal challenges that led to the invalidation of previous frameworks like Privacy Shield.

Common Mistake: Confusing the new DPF with the invalidated Privacy Shield; the DPF has new safeguards.
