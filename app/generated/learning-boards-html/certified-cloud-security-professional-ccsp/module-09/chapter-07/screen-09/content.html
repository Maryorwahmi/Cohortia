Transfer Mechanism

Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)

Internal codes of conduct for intra-group data transfers.

Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs) are internal codes of conduct approved by data protection authorities, used by multinational corporations for intra-group transfers of personal data to non-EEA countries.

  • Internal Codes: Developed by a corporate group for its own data transfers.
  • Legally Binding: Establish robust data protection safeguards across all group entities.
  • DPA Approval: Require approval from data protection authorities, a complex and lengthy process.
  • Advantage: Offers a comprehensive, single solution for extensive intra-group data flows, avoiding multiple contracts.
Key Takeaway

BCRs provide a robust, legally binding framework for intra-group data transfers in multinational corporations, simplifying compliance despite a complex initial approval process.

Unified Internal Policy

A single set of rules for data transfers across all entities within a corporate group.

Example: A global automotive company uses BCRs to transfer HR data between its European and Asian offices.

Complexity vs. Efficiency

Initial implementation is complex, but offers long-term efficiency for large organizations.

Pro-Tip: Consider BCRs if your organization has frequent and extensive intra-group data transfers across many jurisdictions.
