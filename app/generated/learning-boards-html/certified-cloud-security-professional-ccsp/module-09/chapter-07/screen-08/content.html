Transfer Mechanism

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

Pre-approved model clauses for data transfers.

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) are model data protection clauses issued by the European Commission to facilitate legal transfers of personal data from the EEA to countries not deemed 'adequate'.

  • Pre-approved by EC: Standardized clauses for data exporter and importer.
  • Impose Obligations: Require data importers to uphold specific data protection and security standards.
  • Post-Schrems II: Must be supplemented by additional technical and organizational measures.
  • Purpose: Ensure data remains protected from government surveillance in the importing country.
Key Takeaway

SCCs are a foundational mechanism for cross-border data transfers, but now require supplementary measures to address risks like government surveillance, especially post-Schrems II.

The SCCs Framework

A contractual agreement between data exporter and importer, outlining data protection responsibilities.

Example: An EU company uses SCCs with a US cloud provider to process customer data.

Schrems II Impact

The ruling mandates a Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA) and supplementary measures beyond just signing SCCs.

Common Mistake: Assuming SCCs alone are sufficient without implementing additional technical safeguards.
