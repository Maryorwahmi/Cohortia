APEC Cross-Border Privacy Rules (CBPR) System

Facilitating privacy-respecting data flows in Asia-Pacific.

The APEC CBPR System is a voluntary, accountability-based framework designed to facilitate privacy-respecting data flows among participating APEC economies.

  • Voluntary Framework: Companies opt-in to demonstrate compliance.
  • Accountability-Based: Relies on a certification process by independent accountability agents.
  • Privacy Principles: Adherence to a set of APEC privacy principles.
  • Regional Scope: Simplifies data transfers among participating Asia-Pacific economies.
Key Takeaway

The APEC CBPR System offers a voluntary, certification-based framework for privacy-respecting data flows within the Asia-Pacific region, simplifying compliance for participating economies.

Certification for Trust

Companies demonstrate compliance with privacy principles through independent certification.

Example: A Japanese e-commerce company certified under CBPR can transfer customer data to a US partner without additional bilateral agreements.

Balancing Privacy & Flow

A pragmatic approach to enable data mobility while upholding privacy standards in a diverse region.

Pro-Tip: Cloud security architects must understand which regional frameworks apply to their specific data flows.
