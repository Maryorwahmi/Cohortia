Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)

Internal codes of conduct approved by data protection authorities for multinational corporations to legally transfer personal data within their corporate group across borders.

EU-US Data Privacy Framework

An adequacy decision by the European Commission allowing for legal transfers of personal data from the EU to certified organizations in the US, replacing previous frameworks.

APEC CBPR System

A voluntary, accountability-based framework for privacy-respecting data flows among participating Asia-Pacific economies.

Summary Takeaway

These advanced mechanisms and frameworks are crucial for managing complex international data transfers and ensuring robust data protection in a global cloud environment.
