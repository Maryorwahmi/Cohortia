Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)

A comprehensive framework for identifying, assessing, mitigating, and monitoring risks across an entire organization to achieve strategic objectives.

Cloud-Specific Risks

Risks unique to or significantly amplified by cloud computing, such as vendor lock-in, shared responsibility gaps, data sovereignty issues, and API vulnerabilities.

Risk Appetite

The amount and type of risk that an organization is willing to take in order to meet its strategic objectives.

Summary Takeaway

Effective cloud ERM integrates cloud-specific risks into a holistic framework, guided by the organization's risk appetite, to ensure strategic alignment and robust security.
