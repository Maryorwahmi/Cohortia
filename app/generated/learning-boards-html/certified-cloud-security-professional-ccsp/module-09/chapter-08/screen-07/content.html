Compliance and Regulatory Risk

Navigating data residency, privacy, and industry-specific regulations in the cloud.

Key Regulatory Areas
  • **Data Residency:** Legal requirements for data to remain within specific geographic borders.
  • **Privacy Laws:** Regulations like GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA governing personal data handling.
  • **Industry Standards:** Sector-specific mandates such as PCI DSS for financial data.
  • **Audit & Reporting:** Requirements for demonstrating compliance posture.
Key Takeaway

Organizations must actively manage cloud configurations and data placement to ensure compliance with all applicable data residency, privacy, and industry regulations.

Consequences of Non-Compliance
Financial Penalties

Significant fines for regulatory violations.

Legal Action

Lawsuits and legal liabilities from affected parties.

Reputational Damage

Loss of customer trust and brand credibility.

Business Disruption

Operations halted due to non-compliance issues.

Example: A healthcare provider storing patient data in a cloud region outside of HIPAA compliance, leading to significant fines and loss of patient trust.
