Vendor Dependency Risk

Understanding the challenges of relying on a single cloud provider.

Key Concerns
  • **Vendor Lock-in:** Difficulty and cost of switching providers due to proprietary services.
  • **Service Outages:** A single CSP failure can impact multiple critical applications.
  • **Limited Negotiation Power:** Reduced ability to negotiate terms and pricing.
  • **Innovation Constraints:** Dependence on a single vendor's roadmap for new features.
Key Takeaway

Mitigate vendor dependency by adopting multi-cloud strategies, using open standards, and containerization to enhance portability and resilience.

Impact of Single CSP Reliance
Vendor Lock-in

High cost & effort to switch providers.

Service Outages

Widespread disruption from a single point of failure.

Limited Flexibility

Reduced agility and strategic options.

Reduced Bargaining

Less leverage in contract negotiations.

Example: A company using a proprietary database service from one CSP finds it nearly impossible to move to another provider without a costly and time-consuming re-architecture.
