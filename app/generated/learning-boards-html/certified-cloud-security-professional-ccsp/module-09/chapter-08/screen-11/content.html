Assessment Method 1

Qualitative Assessment

Uses descriptive scales (e.g., High, Medium, Low) for likelihood and impact. Provides a quick overview and is useful for initial risk triage and categorization.

**Benefit:** Rapid categorization, expert judgment.
Assessment Method 2

Quantitative Assessment (FAIR)

Attempts to express risk in financial terms (e.g., potential monetary loss from a breach). Frameworks like FAIR (Factor Analysis of Information Risk) provide objective prioritization.

**Benefit:** Objective prioritization, resource allocation.
Prioritization Strategy

Likelihood vs. Impact Matrix

Prioritize risks by considering both the likelihood of occurrence and the potential business impact. Focus on risks exceeding the organization's defined risk appetite.

**Example:** High likelihood, high impact risks require immediate attention.
Key Takeaway

Combining qualitative and quantitative risk assessment methods, especially financial quantification, enables objective prioritization and informed resource allocation for cloud security controls.

Pro-Tip

Cloud security architects should propose mitigation strategies that are both technically effective and cost-efficient, aligning with the organization's risk appetite and budget.
