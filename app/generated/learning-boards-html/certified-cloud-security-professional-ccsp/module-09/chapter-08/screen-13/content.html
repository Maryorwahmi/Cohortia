Module 9›Chapter 9.8›The Collaborative Approach to Cloud ERM
Key Stakeholders

Diverse Perspectives for Holistic Risk

  • **IT & Security:** Technical controls, threat detection, vulnerability management.
  • **Legal:** Regulatory compliance, data privacy laws, contractual obligations.
  • **Compliance:** Industry standards adherence, audit readiness, policy enforcement.
  • **Business Units:** Operational impact, strategic objectives, data criticality.
The Challenge

Avoiding Siloed Approaches

When departments manage cloud risks in isolation, critical exposures can be overlooked, leading to unmanaged risks, inefficient controls, and misalignment with business goals.

**Consequence:** Gaps in security, compliance failures.
The Solution

Cross-Functional Partnership

Foster open communication, shared responsibility, and integrated processes across all relevant departments to ensure a comprehensive and unified approach to cloud risk management.

**Outcome:** Aligned risk posture, strategic cloud adoption.
Key Takeaway

A strong, cross-functional partnership is indispensable for effective cloud ERM, preventing siloed risks and ensuring cloud adoption aligns with overall organizational strategy and risk appetite.

Pro-Tip

Establish a Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE) to formalize cross-functional collaboration, provide governance, and guide secure cloud adoption across the enterprise.
