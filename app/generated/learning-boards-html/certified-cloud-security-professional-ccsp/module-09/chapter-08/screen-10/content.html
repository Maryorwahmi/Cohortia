Module 9›Chapter 9.8›Integrating Cloud Risks into ERM Frameworks
Step 1: Identify & Classify

Cloud Asset & Data Inventory

Identify all cloud assets (e.g., VMs, databases, storage) and the data they hold. Classify data based on sensitivity (e.g., public, internal, confidential) and criticality to business operations.

**Goal:** Understand the scope and value of cloud resources.
Step 2: Assess Risks

Cloud-Specific Risk Assessment

Conduct a comprehensive assessment using frameworks like NIST RMF or ISO 27005, adapted for cloud environments. Identify threats, vulnerabilities, and potential impacts on cloud assets.

**Example:** Misconfigured firewall (vulnerability) leading to data breach (impact).
Step 3: Map & Align

ERM Framework Integration

Map identified cloud risks to the organization's existing ERM categories and risk appetite. Ensure alignment with strategic objectives and involve business stakeholders.

**Benefit:** Holistic risk view, consistent governance.
Key Takeaway

Effective cloud risk integration requires a structured approach, adapting established risk frameworks to cloud specifics and ensuring enterprise-wide stakeholder involvement.

Common Mistake

Treating cloud risks as an isolated IT problem, rather than an enterprise-wide concern requiring business stakeholder input and alignment with overall risk appetite.
