Module 9›Chapter 9.8›Managing Cloud Costs and Shadow IT
Risk Category 1

Cost Management Risk

Uncontrolled cloud spending due to cloud sprawl, inefficient resource utilization, or unoptimized services can lead to unexpected financial burdens, impacting profitability and budget forecasts.

**Impact:** Financial losses, budget overruns.
**Mitigation:** Cost optimization, resource tagging, budget alerts.
Risk Category 2

Shadow IT Risk

Unauthorized cloud service adoption by business units bypasses security controls and compliance processes, creating unmanaged risks and potential data exposure.

**Impact:** Security gaps, compliance violations.
**Mitigation:** Cloud Center of Excellence, clear policies.
Consequences

Broad Business Impact

  • **Financial Penalties:** Fines from regulatory bodies for non-compliance.
  • **Legal Liabilities:** Lawsuits due to data breaches or service disruptions.
  • **Reputational Damage:** Loss of customer trust and brand erosion.
  • **Business Disruption:** Operational halts impacting productivity and revenue.
Key Takeaway

Proactive identification and governance of cloud spending and Shadow IT are crucial to prevent significant financial and security repercussions, ensuring cloud adoption aligns with strategic objectives.

Common Mistake

Treating cloud cost optimization as purely a finance problem or ignoring Shadow IT until a breach occurs, rather than integrating them into a holistic ERM strategy.
