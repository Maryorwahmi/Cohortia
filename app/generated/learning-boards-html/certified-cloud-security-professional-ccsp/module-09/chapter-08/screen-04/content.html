Cloud's Fundamental Shift in ERM

How cloud adoption redefines enterprise risk management.

Key Aspects of Cloud ERM Shift
  • **New Risk Categories:** Cloud introduces risks like vendor lock-in and data sovereignty.
  • **Altered Existing Risks:** Traditional risks like data breaches take on new forms due to shared responsibility.
  • **Dynamic Environments:** Continuous change in cloud requires agile risk management.
  • **Vendor Dependency:** Reliance on CSPs shifts control and introduces new supply chain risks.
Key Takeaway

Cloud adoption is a strategic shift that requires a fundamental re-evaluation and adaptation of an organization's entire Enterprise Risk Management framework.

Traditional ERM vs. Cloud ERM
Traditional ERM
  • On-premises infrastructure
  • Direct control over assets
  • Predictable risk landscape
  • Slower change cycles
Cloud ERM
  • Distributed cloud resources
  • Shared responsibility model
  • Dynamic, evolving risks
  • Rapid deployment & change
Example: A traditional data center breach is fully your responsibility. In the cloud, a breach might stem from a misconfiguration (your responsibility) or a vulnerability in the underlying cloud infrastructure (provider's responsibility).
