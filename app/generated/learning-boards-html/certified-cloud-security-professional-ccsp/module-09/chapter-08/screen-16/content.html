Module 9›Chapter 9.8›Key Concepts: Cloud Risk Management in Practice

Risk Assessment

The process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating risks to determine their likelihood and potential impact on cloud assets and business objectives.

Key Risk Indicator (KRI)

A metric used in risk management to provide an early signal of increasing risk exposure in a particular area, such as critical cloud misconfigurations.

Continuous Risk Monitoring

The ongoing process of tracking and evaluating identified risks, control effectiveness, and changes in the dynamic cloud risk landscape using cloud-native tools.

Summary Takeaway

Effective cloud risk management relies on systematic risk assessment, proactive KRI monitoring, and continuous oversight to adapt to the evolving cloud environment and maintain a strong security posture.
