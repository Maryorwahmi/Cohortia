Key Concepts: Regulations & Principles

Essential takeaways from global privacy laws and their core principles.

GDPR

Comprehensive EU law governing personal data processing, emphasizing data subject rights and accountability.

CCPA

California state law granting consumers rights to know, delete, and opt-out of the sale of their personal information.

HIPAA

US law protecting the privacy and security of Protected Health Information (PHI) and establishing ePHI standards.

Data Protection by Design & Default

Integrating privacy safeguards into system design and ensuring privacy-friendly default settings.

Chapter Summary

Mastering global privacy regulations and their practical implementation in cloud environments is crucial for compliance and building trust in the digital age.
