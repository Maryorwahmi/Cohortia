Role of Data Protection Officers (DPOs)

Guiding compliance and risk management in cloud environments under GDPR.

Independent Advisor

DPOs provide expert advice on data protection, ensuring cloud strategies align with regulations.

Liaison & Monitor

Act as a contact point for authorities and data subjects, and monitor internal compliance.

DPIA Conductor

Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk processing activities in the cloud.

Key Takeaway

DPOs are essential for translating legal privacy requirements into practical technical controls and ensuring proactive risk mitigation in cloud deployments.

DPO Responsibilities in Cloud Compliance

Advise on Cloud Privacy Strategy

Ensure cloud adoption aligns with GDPR and other relevant privacy regulations.

Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)

Identify and mitigate privacy risks for new cloud services or processing activities.

Monitor Compliance & Audit

Oversee adherence to regulations and conduct regular audits of cloud data processing.
