Global Privacy Regulations Overview

Understanding the landscape of data protection laws is crucial for cloud security.

Core Idea

The proliferation of personal data in cloud environments necessitates strict adherence to diverse global privacy regulations.

Key Regulations

GDPR (EU), CCPA (US - California), and HIPAA (US - Healthcare) are prominent examples, each with unique scopes.

Common Goal

Despite differences, all aim to protect individuals' privacy rights regarding their personal data.

Key Takeaway

Identifying the correct regulatory framework is the first step in designing compliant cloud security architectures.

Major Global Privacy Laws

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

EU law protecting personal data of EU residents. Comprehensive and far-reaching.

CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)

US (California) law granting consumers rights over their personal information.

HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)

US law protecting Protected Health Information (PHI) in healthcare.
