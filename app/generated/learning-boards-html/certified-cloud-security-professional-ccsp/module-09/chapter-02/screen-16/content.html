Key Concepts: Roles & Practices

Essential takeaways for cloud privacy implementation and compliance.

Data Subject Rights

Rights granted to individuals (e.g., access, rectify, erase, port) under privacy regulations.

Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Independent expert advising on data protection compliance and liaison with authorities.

Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

Process for identifying and minimizing data protection risks, especially for high-risk processing.

Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

HIPAA-mandated contract outlining a business associate's (e.g., CSP) responsibilities for protecting PHI.

Chapter Summary

Successfully managing cloud privacy requires understanding individual rights, leveraging expert roles like DPOs, conducting risk assessments, and establishing clear contractual agreements.
