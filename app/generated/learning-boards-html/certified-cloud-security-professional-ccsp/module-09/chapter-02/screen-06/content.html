GDPR Data Subject Rights

Empowering individuals with control over their personal data.

Core Principle

Individuals have specific legal rights regarding how their personal data is collected, processed, and stored.

Cloud Impact

Cloud environments must provide mechanisms to fulfill these rights, such as data retrieval, modification, or deletion across services.

Key Takeaway

Implementing robust processes for data subject requests is essential for GDPR compliance and building user trust.

Key Data Subject Rights Under GDPR

Right to Access

Right to Rectification

Right to Erasure ('Right to be Forgotten')

Right to Restriction of Processing

Right to Data Portability

Right to Object
