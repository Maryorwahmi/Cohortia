CCPA: Consumer Rights for California Residents

Understanding the California Consumer Privacy Act and its impact on cloud data.

Scope

Focuses on consumer rights for California residents regarding their personal information.

Cloud Implications

Requires mechanisms to identify, retrieve, delete, and manage consent for consumer data across cloud services.

Key Takeaway

Businesses must implement robust data governance and lifecycle management in the cloud to comply with CCPA requests.

Core Consumer Rights Under CCPA

Right to Know

Consumers can request information about personal data collected, sources, and business purposes.

Right to Delete

Consumers can request deletion of their personal information, with certain exceptions.

Right to Opt-Out of Sale

Consumers can prevent businesses from selling their personal information.
