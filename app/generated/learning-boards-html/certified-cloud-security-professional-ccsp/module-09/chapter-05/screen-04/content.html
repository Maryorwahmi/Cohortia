Core Concept

GRC Fundamentals in the Cloud

Aligning IT with Business Objectives, Risk, and Compliance

What is GRC?

Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) is a structured approach to aligning IT with business objectives, managing risk, and meeting compliance requirements.

  • Governance: Establishing clear policies, roles, and responsibilities for cloud usage.
  • Risk Management: Identifying, assessing, mitigating, and monitoring cloud-specific risks.
  • Compliance: Ensuring adherence to external laws, regulations, and internal policies.
Why it Matters in Cloud

Cloud's dynamic nature, shared responsibility model, and rapid change amplify the need for integrated GRC to prevent misconfigurations and ensure strategic alignment.

The GRC Pillars in Cloud

Governance

Strategic direction, policy, and accountability.

Risk Management

Identify, assess, and mitigate cloud-specific threats.

Compliance

Adherence to laws, regulations, and internal policies.

Concrete Example

A company uses GRC to ensure its cloud data storage (e.g., S3 buckets) is configured to meet GDPR requirements, preventing public access and ensuring data residency.

Key Takeaway

GRC provides a holistic framework for securely and responsibly leveraging cloud computing by aligning IT with business goals, managing risks, and ensuring compliance.
