SOC 2 Type I vs. Type II Reports

Understanding the Depth of Assurance

Why the Distinction Matters

The choice between a Type I and Type II report significantly impacts the level of assurance provided. Customers performing due diligence should always understand which type of report they are reviewing.

Key Factor

Operating effectiveness over time.

Customer Value

Type II offers stronger assurance for sustained security.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize SOC 2 Type II reports for comprehensive assurance of a CSP's control effectiveness over a period, not just at a single point in time.

Report Comparison

SOC 2 Type I

  • Describes controls at a specific point in time.
  • Assesses the suitability of control design.
  • Provides assurance that controls are appropriately designed.
  • Less valuable for ongoing due diligence.

SOC 2 Type II

  • Assesses controls over a period of time (e.g., 6-12 months).
  • Evaluates the operating effectiveness of controls.
  • Provides assurance that controls are consistently applied and effective.
  • Far more valuable for due diligence.
Common Mistake

Confusing Type I with Type II. Type I is a snapshot, Type II is a video of control effectiveness.
