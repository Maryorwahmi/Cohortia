Challenges in Auditing Cloud Environments

Navigating Dynamic and Distributed Systems

Why Cloud Audits are Different

The inherent characteristics of cloud computing, such as its dynamic nature and shared responsibility model, introduce complexities that make traditional auditing methods difficult to apply effectively.

Core Issue

Dynamic, ephemeral resources.

Visibility Gap

'Black box' nature of some CSP services.

Key Takeaway

Cloud auditing demands new approaches to address the unique challenges posed by dynamic resources, distributed data, and limited visibility into CSP infrastructure.

Key Audit Challenges

Dynamic & Ephemeral Resources

Rapid provisioning/de-provisioning of VMs, containers, and serverless functions makes capturing a consistent state difficult.

Distributed Data & Services

Data and services spread across multiple regions and availability zones complicate data governance and evidence collection.

'Black Box' Services

Limited visibility into underlying infrastructure for PaaS/SaaS, requiring reliance on CSP attestations.

Safety Note

Misinterpreting audit reports or failing to address findings can lead to significant penalties and reputational damage.
