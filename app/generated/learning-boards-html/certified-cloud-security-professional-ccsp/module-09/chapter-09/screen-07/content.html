Quantum's Challenge to Encryption

Evaluating the long-term impact on current cryptographic standards.

The Quantum Threat

Quantum computing poses an existential threat to current cryptographic standards, which rely on mathematical problems difficult for classical computers.

  • RSA & ECC: Most modern encryption algorithms are vulnerable.
  • Computational Difficulty: Quantum computers could solve these problems rapidly.
  • Long-term Confidentiality: Data encrypted today could be decrypted in the future.
Key Takeaway

The 'harvest now, decrypt later' threat model necessitates exploring post-quantum cryptography solutions today.

Harvest Now, Decrypt Later

Concept: Adversaries collect and store encrypted data today, anticipating that future quantum computers will be able to decrypt it, compromising long-term confidentiality.
