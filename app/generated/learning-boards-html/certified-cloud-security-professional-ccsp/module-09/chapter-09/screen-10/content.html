Protecting the Cloud Supply Chain

Managing third-party risks and vendor dependencies.

The Interconnected Ecosystem

Organizations rely on a complex ecosystem beyond their primary Cloud Service Provider (CSP), creating an extended supply chain.

  • Sub-processors: Vendors handling data on behalf of the CSP.
  • Third-party Software: Applications and services integrated into the cloud.
  • Open-Source Components: Libraries and frameworks used in development.
Key Takeaway

Comprehensive supply chain security requires scrutinizing every link, not just the primary CSP.

Cascading Effects of Compromise

Scenario: A vulnerability in a third-party analytics tool used by your cloud application could lead to a data breach, even if your primary CSP's infrastructure is secure.
