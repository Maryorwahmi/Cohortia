Encryption for Legal Protection

Leveraging encryption and customer-managed keys for data control.

Why Encryption Matters

Strong encryption, both at rest and in transit, renders data unintelligible to unauthorized parties, providing a fundamental layer of data protection.

Customer-Managed Keys (CMK)
  • Customer retains full control over encryption and decryption keys.
  • Keys are managed outside the direct control of the cloud provider.
  • Even if data is disclosed, it remains encrypted and inaccessible without customer keys.
  • Enhances data confidentiality and control in multi-jurisdictional scenarios.
Scenario: Legal Compulsion with CMK

A cloud provider is compelled by a legal order (e.g., CLOUD Act) to disclose customer data stored in their infrastructure.

  • Without CMK: Provider may be forced to decrypt and disclose readable data.
  • With CMK: Provider can hand over the encrypted data, but it remains unreadable to the requesting authority without the customer's keys.
  • Outcome: Customer maintains control over data confidentiality, even under legal duress.
Pro Tip

Integrate CMK strategies with your data classification scheme to apply the highest level of key control to your most sensitive data, maximizing legal protection.

Key Takeaway

Encryption with customer-managed keys is a powerful control that provides an additional layer of legal protection by ensuring data remains confidential even if legally compelled for disclosure.
