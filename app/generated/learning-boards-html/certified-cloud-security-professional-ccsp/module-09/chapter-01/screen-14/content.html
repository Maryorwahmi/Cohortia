Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Jurisdiction

The authority of a court or legal body to hear and decide cases, often complex in cloud due to global data distribution.

Data Residency

The physical location where data is stored, often mandated by regulations requiring data to remain within specific geographic borders.

Data Sovereignty

The principle that data is subject to the laws and governance structures of the nation in which it is collected or stored.

Summary Takeaway

These three concepts—Jurisdiction, Data Residency, and Data Sovereignty—are distinct yet interconnected, forming the critical legal foundation for secure and compliant cloud operations.
