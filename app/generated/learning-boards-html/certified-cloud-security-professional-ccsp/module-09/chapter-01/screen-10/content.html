Legal Challenges of Distributed Clouds

Navigating compliance in multi-cloud and hybrid cloud environments.

Multi-Cloud & Hybrid Cloud

These strategies involve distributing workloads and data across multiple public cloud providers or a mix of public cloud and on-premises infrastructure.

Amplified Complexity
  • Tracking data flows across diverse environments becomes harder.
  • Ensuring consistent application of legal requirements is challenging.
  • Each component (CSP, region, on-prem) must comply individually.
  • Interfaces between environments need secure, compliant management.
Scenario: Global Hybrid Deployment

A company uses AWS in Europe for primary apps, Azure in the U.S. for analytics, and on-premises for sensitive customer data.

  • AWS (Europe): Subject to EU laws (GDPR, local residency).
  • Azure (U.S.): Subject to U.S. laws (CLOUD Act, state privacy laws).
  • On-Premises: Subject to local national laws.
  • Challenge: Ensuring consistent compliance and secure data transfer across all three, respecting data residency and sovereignty.
Common Pitfall

Assuming a CSP's compliance certifications automatically cover all customer obligations in a multi-cloud or hybrid setup. Customer accountability remains paramount.

Key Takeaway

Multi-cloud and hybrid strategies introduce significant legal complexity, requiring meticulous data flow tracking and a granular approach to compliance across all components.
