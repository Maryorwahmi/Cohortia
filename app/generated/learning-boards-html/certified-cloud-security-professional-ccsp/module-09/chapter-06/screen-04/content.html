Core Concept

eDiscovery in the Cloud

Identifying and Preserving Electronic Evidence

What is eDiscovery?

eDiscovery is the process of identifying, preserving, collecting, reviewing, and producing Electronically Stored Information (ESI) in response to a legal request or investigation.

Key Takeaway

Cloud eDiscovery is complex due to distributed data, multi-tenancy, and the shared responsibility model, requiring specialized strategies.

Why it Matters

Ensuring legal and regulatory compliance, mitigating risks, and supporting incident response in cloud environments.

Cloud eDiscovery Challenges

Distributed Data

ESI spread across regions, services, and storage types.

Multi-Tenancy

Commingled data with other customers, raising privacy concerns.

Shared Responsibility

Limited access to underlying infrastructure by customers.

Dynamic Data

Data scaling, replication, and archiving complicate preservation.
