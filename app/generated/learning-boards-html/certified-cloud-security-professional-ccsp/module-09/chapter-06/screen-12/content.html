Maintaining Chain of Custody

Ensuring Evidence Admissibility in the Cloud

What is Chain of Custody?

The chronological documentation proving evidence has not been altered or tampered with from collection to presentation, ensuring its legal admissibility.

Key Takeaway

Secure acquisition, immutability, and strict access control are foundational for maintaining a valid chain of custody in the cloud.

Safety Note

Always assume any collected data may become legal evidence. Follow strict protocols and consult legal counsel.

Chain of Custody Principles

Secure Acquisition

Use forensically sound, read-only methods to acquire data, hashing to verify integrity.

Immutability

Store evidence in immutable storage (e.g., S3 Object Lock) to prevent alteration.

Access Control

Restrict access to forensic data using strong IAM policies to authorized personnel only.
