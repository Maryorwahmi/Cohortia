Legal Hold Challenges in Cloud

Dynamic Scaling

Data volumes constantly change, making it hard to 'freeze' all relevant ESI.

Data Replication

Copies of data exist across regions, requiring hold on all instances.

CSP Archiving

Automated archiving by CSPs can complicate data retrieval and preservation.

Ephemeral Instances

Short-lived VMs or containers make capturing data at a specific point in time difficult.

eDiscovery Process

Challenges of Legal Hold

Preserving Data in Dynamic Cloud Environments

The Core Problem

Preventing alteration or deletion of ESI when data is constantly changing, scaling, and being managed by the Cloud Service Provider.

Key Takeaway

Proactive eDiscovery policies and close collaboration with legal teams are essential to manage legal holds effectively in the cloud.

Pro Tip: Policy & Procedure

Define clear eDiscovery policies and procedures for cloud data from the outset, ensuring data can be identified and preserved effectively.
