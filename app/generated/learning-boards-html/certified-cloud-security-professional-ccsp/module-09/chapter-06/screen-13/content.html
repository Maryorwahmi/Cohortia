Chain of Custody

Logging, Hashing & Cloud Tools

Practical Steps for Evidence Integrity

Detailed Documentation

Every step of evidence handling, from collection to analysis, must be meticulously documented.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive logging, cryptographic hashing, and leveraging cloud-native/third-party tools are essential for robust chain of custody.

Common Pitfall

Failure to document every transfer or access of evidence can break the chain of custody and invalidate findings.

COC Implementation Steps

1. Detailed Logging

Maintain logs of all access and operations performed on the evidence.

2. Cryptographic Hashing

Generate and compare hashes (e.g., SHA256) to verify data integrity.

3. Cloud-Native & Third-Party Tools

Utilize tools like CloudTrail, Activity Log, Macie, and integrated third-party solutions for automation.
