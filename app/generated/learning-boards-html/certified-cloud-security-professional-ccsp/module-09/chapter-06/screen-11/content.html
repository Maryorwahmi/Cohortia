Forensic Readiness & Common Pitfalls

Designing for Effective Cloud Investigations

What is Forensic Readiness?

The state of an organization's systems, processes, and personnel being prepared to effectively and efficiently conduct a digital forensic investigation.

Key Takeaway

Proactive forensic readiness, including comprehensive and protected logging, is crucial for effective cloud investigations.

Pro Tip: Centralized Logging

Implement a centralized log management solution to aggregate, store, and protect all cloud logs from tampering and unauthorized access.

Common Logging Mistakes

Insufficient Logging

Not enabling or configuring logs comprehensively across all cloud services.

Inadequate Retention

Not retaining logs for a period long enough to meet compliance or investigation needs.

Lack of Protection

Logs not protected from unauthorized access, modification, or deletion, compromising integrity.
