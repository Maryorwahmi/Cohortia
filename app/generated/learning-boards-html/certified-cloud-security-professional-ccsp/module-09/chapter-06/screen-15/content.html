Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms to remember from this chapter

Volatile Data

Data existing only in memory, lost on system shutdown or power loss.

Chain of Custody

Documentation proving evidence integrity from collection to presentation.

Electronically Stored Information (ESI)

Any information in digital form subject to eDiscovery requests.

Forensic Readiness

Preparedness to effectively and efficiently conduct a digital forensic investigation.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive planning for forensic readiness and meticulous attention to chain of custody are vital for managing all forms of ESI in the cloud.
