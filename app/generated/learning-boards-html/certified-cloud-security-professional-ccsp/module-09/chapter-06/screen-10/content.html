Strategies for PaaS and SaaS

Leveraging Logs and Audit Trails for Evidence

Limited Access

Direct OS access is typically unavailable, making traditional disk imaging impossible.

Key Takeaway

PaaS/SaaS forensics relies on CSP-provided logs and audit trails; forensic readiness must be designed in from the start.

Example: SaaS Email

For a compromised SaaS email service, evidence comes from the vendor's audit logs, mailbox exports, and retention policies.

PaaS/SaaS Evidence Sources

Application Logs

Logs generated by the application itself, detailing user actions and system events.

Audit Trails

CSP-provided logs of management plane activities (e.g., CloudTrail, Azure Activity Log).

Managed Databases & Storage

Data stored in managed services, accessible via their APIs or export features.
