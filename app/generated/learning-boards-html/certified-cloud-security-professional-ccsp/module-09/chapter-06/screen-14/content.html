Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

eDiscovery

The process of managing Electronically Stored Information (ESI) for legal requests.

Legal Hold

A process to preserve all relevant ESI when litigation is anticipated or ongoing.

Cloud Forensics

Applying digital forensics to cloud environments to investigate security incidents.

Summary Takeaway

eDiscovery and cloud forensics are essential for legal compliance and incident response, requiring specialized approaches due to the cloud's unique characteristics.
