Ongoing Governance

Continuous Vendor Management and Oversight

Vendor management is an ongoing process to ensure compliance and security.

The Continuous Cycle

Continuous vendor management is essential for ensuring ongoing compliance and security. It's an active, iterative process, not a static one.

  • **Regular Reviews:** Periodically assess updated security reports and compliance certifications.
  • **SLA Monitoring:** Continuously monitor CSP performance against agreed-upon Service Level Agreements.
  • **Stay Informed:** Track changes to services, policies, and sub-processors to adapt your security posture.
Key Takeaway

Continuous vendor management involves regular review of CSP reports, SLA monitoring, and formal risk programs to ensure ongoing compliance and security.

Establishing Oversight

Formal Risk Program

Implement a program with periodic security assessments and vulnerability scanning of customer-managed resources.

Communication Channels

Maintain regular communication with CSP security teams for updates and incident coordination.

Critical Safety Note

Always have legal counsel review all cloud contracts, especially DPAs and BAAs, to ensure alignment with organizational risk appetite and regulatory obligations.
