Planning Your Cloud Exit Strategy

A well-defined exit strategy ensures business continuity and data portability.

Why an Exit Strategy is Essential

An exit strategy must be defined *before* migration to outline how data and applications would be migrated out of a CSP's environment. This is a proactive measure, not an expectation of failure.

  • **Business Continuity:** Ensures operations can continue even if a provider change is necessary.
  • **Data Portability:** Guarantees your ability to move your data to another environment.
  • **Contractual Leverage:** Maintains your negotiation power with the current CSP.
Key Takeaway

A cloud exit strategy, defined pre-migration, outlines data/application migration out of a CSP, ensuring business continuity, data portability, and contractual leverage.

Key Components of an Exit Plan

Data Export Formats

Define how data will be exported (e.g., open formats) to ensure compatibility with other providers.

Timelines & Costs

Estimate the time and financial resources required for a full migration, including potential egress fees.

Common Mistake

Neglecting to define an exit strategy can lead to significant challenges, unexpected costs, and business disruption if a provider change becomes necessary.
