Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Vendor Due Diligence

The process of researching and evaluating a CSP's security, compliance, financial stability, and operational capabilities before contracting.

Vendor Lock-in

A situation where a customer is dependent on a single cloud provider's proprietary technologies, making it difficult or costly to switch.

Exit Strategy

A predefined plan for migrating data and applications out of a cloud service provider's environment, ensuring business continuity and data portability.

Sub-processor

A third-party entity engaged by a cloud service provider to process personal data on behalf of the customer, often requiring customer consent or notification.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive vendor due diligence, risk mitigation, and strategic planning are crucial for maintaining control and security in cloud vendor relationships.
