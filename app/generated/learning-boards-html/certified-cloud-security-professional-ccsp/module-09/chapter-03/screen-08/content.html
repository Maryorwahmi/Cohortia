Supply Chain Security

Unpacking Sub-Processor Risks

Understanding the CSP's supply chain is vital for managing your own security posture.

What are Sub-Processors?

Sub-processors are third-party entities engaged by a Cloud Service Provider (CSP) to process personal data on behalf of the customer. They form a critical part of the CSP's supply chain.

  • **Extended Chain:** CSPs often rely on a complex network of sub-processors for various services (e.g., data centers, CDNs).
  • **Data Handling:** These entities may directly or indirectly handle your organization's data.
  • **Customer Consent:** Often requires customer consent or notification, especially for personal data.
Key Takeaway

Sub-processors are third-party entities in a CSP's supply chain; their vulnerabilities can become your vulnerabilities, necessitating careful review and oversight.

Mitigating Sub-Processor Risks

Review Agreements

Scrutinize sub-processor agreements to understand their security and compliance commitments.

Verify CSP Oversight

Confirm how the CSP ensures sub-processor compliance and security, as their vulnerabilities can become yours.

Common Mistake

Failing to review the CSP's list of sub-processors or assuming compliance without verification is a critical oversight.
