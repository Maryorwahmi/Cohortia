Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › The Quantum Computing Threat
The Threat

Large-scale quantum computers could break current asymmetric encryption algorithms (RSA, ECC) that secure communications and data using problems infeasible for classical computers. Shor's algorithm is a key quantum threat.

The Solution: QSC/PQC

Quantum-Safe Cryptography (QSC), or Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), develops algorithms resistant to both classical and quantum attacks. Standardization efforts, like the NIST PQC competition, are crucial.

Why It Matters

Data encrypted today could be vulnerable in the future. Organizations must assess their cryptographic inventory and develop migration roadmaps for QSC, especially for long-lived sensitive data, to future-proof cloud security.

Key Takeaway

Proactive planning and migration towards quantum-safe cryptography are essential to protect sensitive cloud data from future quantum computing threats.
