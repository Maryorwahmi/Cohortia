Learning Objectives Deep Dive
Expanding on key security competencies
01
Algorithm Selection

Understand ECC efficiency vs. RSA, SHA-256 for integrity, and ECDSA for signatures. Avoid choosing crypto based on familiarity; prioritize workload needs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Quantum Preparedness

Grasp the quantum threat to current crypto. Learn PQC principles and the importance of planning migration for long-lived sensitive data.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
HSM & Key Control

Analyze HSMs as roots of trust (FIPS 140-2 Level 3). Implement BYOK (key origin control) and HYOK (external key management) with awareness of operational trade-offs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Data in Use Protection

Evaluate confidential computing (TEEs) and homomorphic encryption (HE) for protecting data during active processing, understanding their advanced capabilities.
