Key Concepts
Essential takeaways from this chapter
Advanced Crypto & QSC

ECC offers efficiency; QSC prepares for quantum threats.

HSMs & Key Control

HSMs provide hardware root of trust; BYOK/HYOK offer varying levels of customer key management.

Data in Use Protection

Confidential Computing (TEEs) and Homomorphic Encryption (HE) secure data during active processing.

Summary Takeaway: Mastering these advanced concepts is vital for building secure and resilient cloud infrastructures.
