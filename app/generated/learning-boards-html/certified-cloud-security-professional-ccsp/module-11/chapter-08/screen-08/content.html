HYOK Explained

Hold-Your-Own-Key (HYOK) means the customer retains full control of the master encryption key outside the cloud provider's infrastructure, typically in an on-premises HSM or secure key store.

Maximum Control

This strategy offers the strongest control and separation of duties, as the cloud service never directly handles the master key material. It's ideal for stringent compliance and data sovereignty needs.

Operational Overhead

HYOK introduces significant operational complexity. The customer is fully responsible for the availability, backup, and disaster recovery of their external key management system. Key unavailability can lead to data inaccessibility.

Key Takeaway

HYOK provides ultimate key control but demands substantial customer investment in managing the availability and security of their external key infrastructure.
