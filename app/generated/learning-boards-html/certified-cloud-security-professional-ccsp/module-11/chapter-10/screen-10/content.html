Scenario: Compromised VM

A compromised VM is attempting to connect to external IPs on unusual ports. What is the MOST IMMEDIATE action to contain the threat?

Correct Answer: c) Modify the VM's security group to deny all inbound and outbound network traffic.

Explanation: The keyword 'MOST IMMEDIATE' points to the containment phase. Blocking all network traffic immediately isolates the compromised instance, preventing further data exfiltration or lateral movement.

Key Takeaway

In cloud incident response, immediate network isolation of a compromised resource is the most critical containment action to prevent further damage.

Incident Response Phases & Actions

01Containment

Action: Isolate the VM (e.g., block network traffic). This is the immediate priority.

02Analysis & Investigation

Action: Initiate forensic investigation. This follows containment.

03Eradication

Action: Patching the OS. This occurs after containment and analysis.

04Recovery & Post-Incident

Action: Notifying law enforcement. This is part of later phases.
