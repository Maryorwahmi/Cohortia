Strategic Pacing for the Exam

The CCSP exam demands efficient time management. Aim for approximately 1 minute and 26 seconds per question to ensure you complete all sections.

  • Monitor Your Time: Be conscious of the clock for each question.
  • Avoid Getting Stuck: If a question is too difficult, make an educated guess.
  • Mark for Review: Use the 'mark for review' feature to revisit questions later if time permits.
  • Complete All Questions: The goal is to attempt every question within the allotted time.
Key Takeaway

Efficient time management, including strategic guessing and marking for review, is crucial to complete the CCSP exam and maximize your score.

The Time Management Process

01Start with a Clear Mindset

Approach each question calmly, reading it thoroughly to understand the core problem.

02Quick Assessment & Decision

If you know the answer quickly, select it and move on. If not, assess if you can deduce it within the time limit.

03Strategic Guess & Mark

If a question is taking too long, make your best guess, mark it for review, and proceed to the next question.

04Review if Time Permits

Only revisit marked questions if you have extra time after completing the entire exam.
