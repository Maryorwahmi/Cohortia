Scenario: Strict Key Management

A company needs to encrypt data at rest with keys generated and managed exclusively by the customer, outside of the cloud provider's direct control. Which key management strategy is best?

Correct Answer: d) Hold-Your-Own-Key (HYOK) using an on-premises Hardware Security Module (HSM)

Explanation: The keywords 'exclusively by the customer' and 'outside of the cloud provider's direct control' are critical. HYOK ensures master encryption keys are generated and remain entirely within the customer's on-premises infrastructure, providing the highest level of customer control and separation from the cloud provider.

Key Takeaway

For maximum customer control over encryption keys, especially for strict regulatory compliance, Hold-Your-Own-Key (HYOK) with an on-premises HSM is the most suitable strategy.

Key Management Options Comparison

a) SSE-S3

Keys are fully managed by the cloud provider. Incorrect.

b) CMK within KMS

Customer manages key policy, but key material is in provider's KMS. Incorrect.

c) BYOK imported into KMS

Customer generates key, but imports it into provider's KMS. Incorrect.

d) HYOK using on-premises HSM

Master keys generated and remain entirely within customer's on-premises infrastructure. Correct.
