Contract Analysis

Evaluating Cloud Agreements

Analyzing vendor lock-in and exit strategy clauses to ensure long-term flexibility and control.

Vendor Lock-in Risks
  • Proprietary data formats hindering export.
  • Reliance on unique, non-standardized services or APIs.
  • High costs or contractual penalties for switching providers.
  • Technical complexity of migrating applications and data.
  • Limited vendor support during transition periods.
Key Clauses to Scrutinize
Data Portability

Ability to extract data in usable formats (e.g., SQL, CSV, JSON) with clear timelines and costs.

Exit Strategy

Defined process for migrating data and applications, including vendor support and responsibilities.

Mitigation Strategy

Carefully negotiate and understand data portability and exit strategy clauses to ensure business continuity, flexibility, and control over your cloud environment.
