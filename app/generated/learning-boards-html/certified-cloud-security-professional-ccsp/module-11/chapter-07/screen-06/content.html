Process Flow

Cloud Risk Management Lifecycle

A systematic process for identifying, assessing, mitigating, and monitoring risks associated with cloud adoption, integrated into ERM.

Key Stages
1. Risk Identification

Pinpoint potential threats and vulnerabilities in the cloud environment.

2. Risk Assessment

Analyze likelihood and impact of identified risks.

3. Risk Treatment

Decide how to address risks: mitigate, accept, transfer, or avoid.

4. Risk Monitoring

Continuously track risks and control effectiveness.

Integration

Cloud risk management should be seamlessly integrated into the organization's existing Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework to ensure a holistic approach to risk.

Tools & Techniques
  • Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM) for detecting misconfigurations.
  • Threat intelligence feeds for staying updated on emerging threats.
  • Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing.
Key Takeaway

A proactive and continuous approach to cloud risk management, integrated with ERM, is crucial for protecting organizational assets and achieving business objectives in the cloud.
