Contractual Considerations

Exit Strategies & Data Portability

Crucial clauses in enterprise agreements that ensure flexibility and mitigate the risk of vendor lock-in.

Why They Matter
  • Mitigate vendor lock-in, both technical and contractual.
  • Ensure business continuity during provider transitions.
  • Provide flexibility for cost optimization or strategic shifts.
  • Reduce disruption and minimize costs associated with data migration.
  • Maintain control over organizational data and operations.
Key Questions to Ask
  • In what formats can data be extracted?
  • What are the defined timelines for data extraction?
  • Are there any excessive costs associated with data portability?
  • What support is provided by the vendor during an exit?
  • Are there contractual limitations on migrating data or applications?
Strategic Imperative

Negotiating clear terms for data portability and exit strategies is vital for long-term flexibility, risk reduction, and maintaining control over your cloud environment.
