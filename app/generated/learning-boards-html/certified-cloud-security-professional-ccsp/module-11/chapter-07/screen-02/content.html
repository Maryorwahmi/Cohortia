Learning Objectives

Master the strategic pillars of cloud security management.

01

Establish Governance

Learn to establish and maintain a robust cloud security governance framework, ensuring consistent oversight and alignment with business objectives.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Develop Policies

Develop and implement effective cloud security policies, standards, and guidelines to translate security intent into actionable requirements.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Integrate Risk Management

Integrate cloud risk management into the enterprise risk management framework, ensuring comprehensive identification and mitigation of cloud-specific threats.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Understand Agreements

Understand cloud service agreements, focusing on exit strategies and data portability, and analyze the impact of shared assessments.
