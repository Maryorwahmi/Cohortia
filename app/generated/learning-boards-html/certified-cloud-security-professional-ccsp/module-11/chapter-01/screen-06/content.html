Cloud Service Models: IaaS, PaaS, SaaS

Defining levels of control and abstraction in the cloud.

IaaS

Infrastructure as a Service: Virtualized computing resources over the internet.

High Control

PaaS

Platform as a Service: A platform for developing, running, and managing applications.

Medium Control

SaaS

Software as a Service: Complete, ready-to-use applications over the internet.

Low Control
Key Takeaway

The choice of service model dictates the level of customer control and, consequently, the scope of their security responsibilities.
