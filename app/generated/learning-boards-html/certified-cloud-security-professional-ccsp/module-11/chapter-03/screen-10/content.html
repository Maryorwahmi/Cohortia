Data Immutability (Object Lock)

Data immutability ensures that once data is written, it cannot be altered or deleted for a specified period, providing strong protection against data tampering.

  • •Ransomware Protection: Prevents ransomware from encrypting or deleting original data.
  • •Compliance & Legal Hold: Essential for meeting regulatory requirements for data retention.
  • •Object Lock: Cloud storage features (e.g., S3 Object Lock) enable WORM (Write Once, Read Many) functionality.
Concrete Example

A financial institution uses S3 Object Lock to ensure audit logs cannot be modified for seven years, meeting regulatory compliance.

Secure Snapshot Management

Snapshots are point-in-time copies of block storage volumes. Secure management is crucial to prevent data exposure and ensure reliable recovery.

  • •Encryption: Always encrypt snapshots to protect sensitive data.
  • •Access Control: Implement strict IAM policies to control who can create, access, or restore snapshots.
  • •Retention Policies: Regularly review and enforce retention policies to avoid stale or unsecured snapshots.
Common Mistake

Leaving snapshots unencrypted or with overly permissive access, potentially exposing sensitive data.

Key Takeaway

Data immutability and secure snapshot management are vital for data resilience, protecting against ransomware, ensuring compliance, and enabling reliable data recovery.
