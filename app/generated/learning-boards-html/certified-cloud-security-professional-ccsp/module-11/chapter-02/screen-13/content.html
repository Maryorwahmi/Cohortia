Key Global Privacy Regulations

GDPR (Europe)

General Data Protection Regulation.

CCPA (California)

California Consumer Privacy Act.

HIPAA (Healthcare)

Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.

These regulations impose strict requirements on personal data handling.
Definition

Laws governing the collection, processing, storage, and protection of personal data, often including specific residency requirements.

Compliance Impact

Failure to comply can result in significant fines, legal action, and severe reputational damage for organizations.

Key Takeaway

Proactive compliance planning, including cloud region selection and data processing agreements, is essential for meeting privacy regulations.
