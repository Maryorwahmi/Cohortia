Server-Side Encryption (SSE) Options

SSE-S3

S3 manages keys and encryption. Simplest option.

SSE-KMS

AWS KMS manages keys (AWS-managed or CMK). More control.

SSE-C

Customer provides keys to S3. Customer manages keys, S3 encrypts.

The choice depends on control requirements and operational complexity.
Comparison Point

Key ownership and management responsibility shift across these options, impacting your control over the encryption process.

Example Scenario

For general purpose data, SSE-S3 is fine. For sensitive data requiring audit trails, SSE-KMS with CMK is preferred. For extreme control, consider SSE-C or client-side.

Key Takeaway

Align your SSE choice with data sensitivity and regulatory compliance needs, considering who holds the keys.
