Avoiding Common Pitfalls: Speed and Outside Knowledge
Recognize and mitigate mistakes related to reading speed and external biases.
Reading Too Quickly

Missing crucial keywords or context can lead to misinterpreting the question and selecting an incorrect answer. Always read carefully, even twice.

Bringing in Outside Knowledge

Relying on specific work experience rather than the (ISC)² Common Body of Knowledge (CBK) can lead to answers that are correct in your context but wrong for the exam.

Key Takeaway

To avoid common pitfalls, always read questions thoroughly and base your answers strictly on the (ISC)² CBK, not personal work experience.

Impact of Misreading
Question: "All are true EXCEPT..."

If 'EXCEPT' is missed, you might choose a true statement instead of the false one.

Question: "What is NOT a benefit..."

If 'NOT' is missed, you might select a benefit instead of a non-benefit.

Pro Tip

When you encounter keywords like 'NOT' or 'EXCEPT', mentally circle them or make a note to ensure you don't overlook their critical impact on the question's meaning.
