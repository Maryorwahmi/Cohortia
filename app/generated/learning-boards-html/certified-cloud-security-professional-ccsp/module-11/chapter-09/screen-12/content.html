Avoiding Common Pitfalls: Judgment and Stress
Manage your instincts and stress to maintain clear judgment during the exam.
Changing Answers Unnecessarily

Your first instinct is often correct. Only change an answer if you have a compelling, concrete reason based on re-evaluation, not just second-guessing.

Failing to Manage Stress

High stress impairs judgment and recall. Practice relaxation techniques and take short mental breaks to maintain focus.

Key Takeaway

Trust your initial, informed judgment and actively manage stress to ensure clear thinking and optimal performance throughout the exam.

Impact of Stress on Performance
Impaired Judgment

Difficulty in making sound decisions or evaluating options critically.

Reduced Focus

Struggling to concentrate on the question or scenario details.

Difficulty Recalling Information

Even well-known facts can become inaccessible under pressure.

Pro Tip

If you feel overwhelmed, close your eyes for 10-15 seconds, take a few deep breaths, and then re-engage with the question. This can help reset your focus.
