Regulatory Compliance Question

Identifying the Correct Standard

An organization is storing customer credit card data in a cloud environment. Which specific compliance standard is *most directly* applicable to ensuring the secure handling of this data?

a) GDPR
b) HIPAA
c) PCI DSS
d) ISO 27001
Explanation

PCI DSS is specifically designed for the secure handling of credit card data. While GDPR and ISO 27001 are important, they are broader in scope. HIPAA applies to health information.
