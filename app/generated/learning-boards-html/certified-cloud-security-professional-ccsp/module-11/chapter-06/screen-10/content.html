Evaluating Cloud Provider Compliance

A Practical Case Study

This scenario walks through the critical steps of selecting a cloud provider for regulated workloads, emphasizing due diligence and contractual review.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive approach combining regulatory knowledge, provider vetting, and contract negotiation is essential for compliant cloud adoption.

Action Item

Always verify provider claims through independent audit reports and clearly defined contractual terms.

Scenario Steps

1. Identify Regulations

Map applicable laws (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS) to your data and operations.

Scenario Steps

2. Due Diligence

Assess provider certifications, security posture, and ask targeted questions.

Scenario Steps

3. Contract Review

Ensure SLAs and DPAs cover data residency, sovereignty, breach notification, and audit rights.
