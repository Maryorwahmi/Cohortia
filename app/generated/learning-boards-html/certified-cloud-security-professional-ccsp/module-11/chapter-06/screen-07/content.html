Vendor Management & Due Diligence

Selecting and Overseeing Cloud Providers

Thorough vetting of cloud providers is critical to ensure they meet your security, compliance, and operational requirements throughout the service lifecycle.

Key Takeaway

Continuous monitoring and re-assessment of cloud providers are as important as the initial due diligence process.

Common Mistake

Treating vendor management as a one-time task rather than an ongoing process can lead to overlooked risks.

Due Diligence Areas

Security Posture

Assessment of physical, operational, and personnel security controls.

Due Diligence Areas

Compliance Certifications

Verification of ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, etc., through audit reports.

Due Diligence Areas

Financial Stability & Continuity

Assessing the provider's long-term viability and disaster recovery capabilities.
