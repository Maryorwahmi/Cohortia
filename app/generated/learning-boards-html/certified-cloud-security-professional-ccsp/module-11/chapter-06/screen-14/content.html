Key Concepts

Consolidating Your Knowledge

Regulatory Frameworks

GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS are legal mandates for data handling in the cloud.

Data Location

Residency and sovereignty dictate where data is stored and which laws apply.

Contracts & Audits

SLAs, DPAs, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 are vital for defining responsibilities and proving compliance.

Legal Implications

E-discovery and digital forensics require careful handling of electronic evidence in legal contexts.

Summary Takeaway

A proactive and informed approach to legal, risk, compliance, and audit is essential for secure and trustworthy cloud operations.
