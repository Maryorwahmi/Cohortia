Legal, Risk, Compliance & Audit in the Cloud

Navigating the Complex Landscape of Cloud Governance

Welcome to the essential domain of Legal, Risk, Compliance, and Audit in cloud security. This area is arguably one of the most complex and rapidly evolving aspects of managing cloud environments. It demands a thorough understanding of how global regulations intersect with cloud services and how to effectively manage the associated risks. In this module, we'll start by revisiting the critical legal and regulatory frameworks that dictate how data must be handled in the cloud, ensuring you're equipped to build and maintain secure, compliant cloud infrastructures. Understanding these principles is not just about avoiding penalties; it's about building trust, protecting sensitive information, and ensuring the long-term viability of your cloud strategy. Let's dive in and explore how to navigate this intricate landscape.
