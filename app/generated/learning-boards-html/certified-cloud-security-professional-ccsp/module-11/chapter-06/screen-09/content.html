E-discovery in the Cloud

Identifying and Producing Electronically Stored Information

Navigating e-discovery in the cloud requires understanding data location, retrieval capabilities, and legal chain of custody for electronic evidence.

Key Takeaway

Ensure your cloud contracts support forensic investigations and that your internal processes maintain the integrity of collected evidence.

Legal Consideration

The legal validity of cloud-collected evidence depends on its chain of custody, integrity, and authenticity.

Process

Identification

Locating relevant Electronically Stored Information (ESI) across cloud services.

Process

Collection

Securely retrieving and preserving ESI while maintaining its integrity.

Process

Production

Delivering ESI in a legally acceptable format as per court orders or requests.
