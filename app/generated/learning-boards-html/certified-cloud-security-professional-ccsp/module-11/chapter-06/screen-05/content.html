Core Concept

Data Residency, Sovereignty, and Privacy

Where Your Data Lives and Who Owns It

These concepts are fundamental to cloud security and compliance, dictating where data is stored, which laws apply to it, and the rights individuals have over their personal information.

Key Takeaway

Carefully select cloud regions and negotiate contracts to ensure compliance with data residency, sovereignty, and privacy laws.

Analogy

Think of residency as the house address, sovereignty as the country's laws governing that house, and privacy as the rights of people living inside.

Concept

Data Residency

The physical geographic location where data is stored. Regulations often require data to remain within specific national or regional boundaries.

Concept

Data Sovereignty

The principle that data is subject to the laws and governance of the country in which it is stored, regardless of the data owner's origin.

Concept

Privacy

Individual rights over personal data and organizational obligations to protect it, as mandated by laws like GDPR and CCPA.
