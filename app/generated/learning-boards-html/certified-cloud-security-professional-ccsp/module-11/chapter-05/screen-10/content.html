Detection & Analysis: Identifying and Scoping Incidents

Turning alerts into actionable incident intelligence

Detection Methods

  • Monitoring Alerts: From SIEM, cloud-native security services (e.g., GuardDuty, Security Center).
  • Log Analysis: Manual or automated review of logs for suspicious patterns.
  • Threat Intelligence: Leveraging external feeds to identify known threats.

Analysis Steps

  • Triage Alerts: Determine legitimacy and severity of the alert.
  • Scope & Nature: Understand what systems are affected and how the attack occurred.
  • Prioritize Response: Based on impact and severity, allocate resources effectively.
Key Takeaway

Effective detection and analysis are crucial for quickly identifying incidents and understanding their full scope to inform appropriate response actions.
