Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP)

Minimizing the Blast Radius of a Compromise

Definition

Granting a function only the minimum necessary IAM permissions to perform its specific task, and nothing more.

Why It Matters

Reduces the 'blast radius' of a compromise, limiting an attacker's ability to access or manipulate unrelated resources.

Common Mistake

Granting overly permissive IAM roles (e.g., AdministratorAccess or broad service access like s3:*).

Key Takeaway

Adhering to PoLP is fundamental for serverless security, drastically limiting the impact of a potential breach.
