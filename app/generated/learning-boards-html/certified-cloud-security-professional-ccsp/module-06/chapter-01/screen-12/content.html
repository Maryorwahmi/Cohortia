Breaking Down Silos

Eliminate traditional barriers between development, security, and operations teams to promote seamless collaboration.

Shared Responsibility

Instill a mindset where security is everyone's job, integrated into daily workflows rather than being an external gatekeeper.

Continuous Feedback & Collaboration

Establish mechanisms for ongoing communication and feedback loops to continuously improve security posture.
