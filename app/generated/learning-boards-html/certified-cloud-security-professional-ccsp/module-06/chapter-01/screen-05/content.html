Key Principle

Shifting Left: Proactive Security Integration

Moving security considerations to the earliest phases of development

Definition

Integrating security activities and testing into the earliest stages of the SDLC, such as requirements and design, rather than later phases.

Benefits

Significantly reduces the cost and effort of fixing vulnerabilities, as issues are identified and remediated when they are easiest to address.

Proactive vs. Reactive

Transforms security from a reactive 'gate' at the end of development into a continuous, embedded process throughout the entire lifecycle.

The 'Shift Left' Advantage

Early security = Lower cost, higher impact

Key Takeaway

Shifting left embeds security from the start, transforming it from a reactive fix to a proactive, cost-effective, and integral part of development.
