Risk Prioritization

DREAD: Prioritizing Identified Threats

Assessing risk based on impact and likelihood.

The DREAD Methodology

DREAD helps prioritize threats by evaluating five key factors, typically rated on a scale (e.g., 1-10), to determine an aggregated risk score.

  • Systematic Assessment: Provides a structured way to compare and rank different threats.
  • Resource Allocation: Directs security efforts towards the most critical vulnerabilities.
  • Informed Decision-Making: Helps teams make data-driven choices about mitigation strategies.

DREAD Factors

Damage

How much damage would result if the threat were exploited?

Reproducibility

How easy is it to reproduce the attack?

Exploitability

How easy is it to exploit the vulnerability?

Affected Users

How many users would be impacted?

Discoverability

How easy is it to discover the vulnerability?

Key Takeaway

DREAD provides a structured framework for assessing and prioritizing threats, enabling teams to focus mitigation efforts on the most critical risks.
