Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember.

Threat Modeling

A structured process for identifying, analyzing, and prioritizing potential security threats and vulnerabilities in a system or application.

Data Flow Diagram (DFD)

A graphical representation of the flow of data through a system, showing processes, data stores, external entities, and data flows.

STRIDE

A mnemonic for a threat categorization model (Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, Elevation of Privilege) used to identify potential threats.

Summary Takeaway

Threat modeling, visualized through DFDs and categorized by STRIDE, forms the bedrock of proactive cloud security, enabling early detection and mitigation of vulnerabilities.
