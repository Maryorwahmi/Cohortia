Securing Individual Pods

Applying Pod Security Standards and managing Service Accounts

Pod Security Standards (PSS)

Enforce Security Contexts

Apply PSS profiles (e.g., Restricted) to prevent privileged containers and restrict root user access.

Reduce Attack Surface

By limiting capabilities and privileges, PSS significantly reduces the potential impact of a container compromise.

Key Takeaway

PSS and dedicated, least-privileged service accounts are fundamental for hardening individual pods and limiting the blast radius of security incidents.

Service Account Best Practices

Dedicated Service Accounts

Create specific service accounts for each workload, rather than relying on the default, which often has broad permissions.

Least Privilege IAM Permissions

Grant only the absolute minimum IAM permissions required for the service account to perform its function.

Common Mistake

Using the default service account for all pods, which can lead to privilege escalation if a pod is compromised due to its often broad permissions.
