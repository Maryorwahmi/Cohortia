Key Concepts: Foundational Elements

Essential terms for understanding cloud continuity and recovery.

Business Continuity (BC)

Maintaining essential business functions during and after a disruption.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Restoring IT systems, data, and infrastructure after a catastrophic event.

Resilience

The ability of a system to withstand failures and continue operating.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Maximum acceptable downtime for a system after an incident.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Maximum acceptable amount of data loss after an incident.

Snapshots

Point-in-time backups of block storage volumes.

Summary Takeaway

These core concepts provide the essential vocabulary and framework for designing and evaluating effective cloud business continuity and disaster recovery strategies.
