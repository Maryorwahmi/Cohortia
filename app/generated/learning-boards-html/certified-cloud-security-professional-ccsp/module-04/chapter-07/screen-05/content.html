Defining Recovery Objectives: RTO and RPO

Setting the maximum tolerable downtime and data loss for your cloud systems.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum tolerable duration of time that a system can be down after a disaster or disruption.

  • How quickly must you recover?
  • Measured in minutes, hours, or days.
  • Influences recovery architecture.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable amount of data that can be lost from an IT service due to a major incident.

  • How much data can you afford to lose?
  • Measured in seconds, minutes, or hours.
  • Influences backup/replication frequency.
Concrete Example

An online banking application might have an RTO of 15 minutes (users can't wait longer) and an RPO of 5 minutes (losing more than 5 minutes of transactions is unacceptable). This dictates a highly available architecture with continuous data replication.

Key Takeaway

RTO and RPO are critical business drivers for your DR strategy. Define them clearly based on business impact to guide your cloud architecture and investment decisions.
