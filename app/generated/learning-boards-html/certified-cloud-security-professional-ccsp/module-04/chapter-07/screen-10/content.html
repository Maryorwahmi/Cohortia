Advanced Resilience Patterns

Immutable Infrastructure and Chaos Engineering

Proactive strategies for predictable recovery and system robustness.

Immutable Infrastructure

Infrastructure components are never modified after deployment; new components replace old ones.

  • Reduces configuration drift.
  • Simplifies recovery and rollbacks.
  • Ensures consistent environments.

Chaos Engineering

Proactively injects failures into a system to test its resilience and identify weaknesses.

  • Controlled experiments, not random.
  • Uncovers hidden vulnerabilities.
  • Builds confidence in system resilience.
Concrete Example

A team uses Chaos Monkey to randomly terminate EC2 instances in a production environment during business hours. This forces the application to prove its resilience by automatically recovering and maintaining service without human intervention.

Key Takeaway

Embracing immutable infrastructure and chaos engineering shifts your security posture from reactive to proactive, building inherently more robust and predictable cloud systems.
