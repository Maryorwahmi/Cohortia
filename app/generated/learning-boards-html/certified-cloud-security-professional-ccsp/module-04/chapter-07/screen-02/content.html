Learning Objectives: Foundational Concepts

Mastering the core principles of cloud continuity and recovery.

01

Differentiate BC, DR, and Resilience

Understand the distinct roles of Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, and Resilience in cloud computing. This skill is vital for crafting precise and effective strategies that address specific operational needs during disruptions.

02

Define & Apply RTO and RPO

Learn to define and practically apply Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) to cloud systems. This enables you to set realistic recovery targets and make informed architectural decisions that balance cost and availability needs.
