BC, DR, and Resilience: Understanding the Differences

Distinguishing between maintaining operations, restoring systems, and withstanding failures.

Business Continuity (BC)

Focuses on maintaining critical business functions during and after a disruption. It's about the business, not just IT.

  • Ensures essential operations continue.
  • May involve manual workarounds.
  • Broader scope than DR.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

The process of restoring IT systems and data after a catastrophic event. It's IT-centric.

  • Restores technology infrastructure.
  • A subset of Business Continuity.
  • Focuses on system recovery.

Resilience

The ability of a system to withstand failures and continue operating. It's about inherent system strength.

  • Built-in fault tolerance.
  • Minimizes impact of failures.
  • Proactive design principle.
Concrete Example

A regional power outage impacts your cloud data center. BC ensures customer support continues via a remote team. DR focuses on restoring your application servers in a different region. Resilience is the application's ability to automatically failover to another AZ within the same region before a full DR is needed.

Key Takeaway

While distinct, BC, DR, and Resilience are interdependent. A robust cloud strategy integrates all three to ensure comprehensive protection and continuous operation.
