Visualizing Security Posture and Avoiding Alert Fatigue
Designing Effective Dashboards and Tuning Your SIEM
The Power of Effective Dashboards
  • Provide a high-level overview of security posture.
  • Highlight critical alerts and top anomalous activities.
  • Show compliance status at a glance.
  • Enable quick prioritization of security investigations.
Scenario: Dashboard in Action

A security analyst quickly identifies a spike in failed logins from a specific region on the SIEM dashboard. Clicking into the alert reveals correlated events leading to a potential credential stuffing attack, allowing for immediate investigation.

Key Takeaway

Well-designed SIEM dashboards provide critical insights, while continuous tuning of correlation rules is essential to prevent alert fatigue and ensure accurate, actionable threat detection.

Common Mistakes & Solutions
Mistake: Ingesting All Logs Without Use Cases

Leads to 'alert fatigue' and makes it difficult to identify genuine threats amidst excessive noise.

Solution: Selective Ingestion & Defined Rules

Only ingest security-relevant logs and define clear correlation rules to focus on high-fidelity alerts.

Mistake: Not Regularly Tuning Rules

Results in high false positives, missed critical incidents, and rules becoming outdated as the environment changes.

Solution: Continuous Rule Review & Adjustment

Periodically review and adjust correlation rules to minimize false positives and adapt to environmental changes.
