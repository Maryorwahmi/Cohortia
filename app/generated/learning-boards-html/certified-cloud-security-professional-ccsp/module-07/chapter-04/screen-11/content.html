Navigating the Complexities of Cloud SIEM Operations
Addressing Cost, Compliance, and Infrastructure Maintenance
Cost Optimization

Ingesting and storing vast volumes of cloud log data can be expensive. Requires careful planning of data retention and selective ingestion of security-relevant logs.

Data Residency & Compliance

Meeting strict regulatory requirements for where security logs are stored and processed, ensuring the SIEM solution adheres to local and industry-specific mandates.

SIEM Infrastructure Maintenance

Even with cloud-native solutions, tasks like patching, scaling, ensuring high availability, and managing underlying resources still require attention and expertise.

Key Takeaway

Successfully managing a cloud SIEM requires proactive strategies to address cost, ensure compliance with data residency rules, and maintain the underlying infrastructure for optimal performance and security.
