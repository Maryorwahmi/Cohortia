What Correlation Rules Do
  • Define logic to identify patterns or sequences of events.
  • Link seemingly disparate log entries to reveal complex threats.
  • Reduce alert fatigue by focusing on high-fidelity incidents.
  • Enable proactive detection of attacks like brute-force or credential stuffing.
Example: Brute-Force Attack

A rule detects 10 failed logins to 'admin' from different IPs within 5 minutes, followed by a successful login from a new, suspicious IP. This pattern triggers an alert for a potential brute-force or credential stuffing attack.

Key Takeaway

Correlation rules are the intelligence layer of a SIEM, transforming raw log data into actionable security insights by identifying complex attack patterns.
