Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Reinforcing Core Understandings

Security Auditing

Systematically evaluating the security posture of systems and operations against established criteria.

Compliance

Adherence to laws, regulations, industry standards, and internal policies relevant to cloud operations.

Compliance Frameworks

Structured sets of guidelines and requirements, such as GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS, that guide security implementations.

Summary Takeaway

Security auditing provides the evidence needed to demonstrate compliance with various regulatory and industry-specific frameworks in the cloud.
