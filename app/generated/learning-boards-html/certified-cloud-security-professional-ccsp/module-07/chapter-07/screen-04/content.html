Core Concept

Security Auditing vs. Compliance

Understanding the Intertwined Roles in Cloud Security

Security Auditing

Systematic evaluation of cloud resources, configurations, and operations against established baselines or policies. It's the 'how' of verification.

Compliance

The act of adhering to specific laws, regulations, industry standards, and internal policies. It's the 'what' of meeting requirements.

Auditing
Compliance
Evidence & Verification

Auditing provides the necessary evidence and verification to demonstrate compliance with regulations and standards.

Key Takeaway

Security auditing is the active process of evaluating security posture, while compliance is the state of adhering to rules; auditing provides the proof for compliance.
