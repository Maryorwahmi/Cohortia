Reporting Strategy

Generating Compliance Reports: Part 1

Evidence Collection, Mapping Controls, and Initial Reporting

1

Evidence Collection

Gather logs (CloudTrail, Activity Logs), configuration snapshots (AWS Config, Azure Policy), and audit trails to prove control effectiveness.

2

Mapping Controls

Clearly map how specific cloud configurations and operational procedures meet the requirements of each compliance control (e.g., IAM policies to PCI DSS).

3

Reporting

Generate reports from cloud-native tools or SIEMs that summarize compliance status, identified non-conformities, and remediation actions taken.

Key Takeaway

A systematic approach to compliance reporting, starting with robust evidence collection and clear control mapping, is fundamental for demonstrating adherence.
