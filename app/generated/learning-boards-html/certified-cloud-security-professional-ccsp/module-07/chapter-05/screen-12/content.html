Testing and Validating Your BCDR Plan

From Theory to Proven Resilience

Why Test?

  • Identify Gaps: Uncover weaknesses in the plan before a real disaster.
  • Validate RTO/RPO: Confirm that recovery objectives can actually be met.
  • Train Teams: Prepare incident response and operations teams for execution.
  • Adapt: Ensure the plan remains relevant as the cloud environment evolves.

Cloud Advantage

Cloud environments facilitate testing through automation and the ability to spin up isolated test environments.

BCDR Testing Process

01

Define Scenarios

Simulate region outage, database failure, etc.

02

Execute Drills

Perform recovery steps in isolated environments.

03

Review & Refine

Identify gaps, update plans, and train teams.

04

Automate & Monitor

Integrate testing into CI/CD, monitor recovery metrics.

Key Takeaway

Regular BCDR testing and validation are non-negotiable for ensuring your cloud recovery plans are effective, up-to-date, and executable by your teams.
