Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Defining Acceptable Data Loss

What is RPO?

  • Definition: The maximum acceptable amount of data loss, measured in time.
  • Measurement: Time, from incident start to the last valid data backup/replication point.
  • Driver: Data criticality, compliance, and business impact of data loss.
  • Example: An RPO of 15 minutes means you can lose up to 15 minutes of data.

Why it Matters

RPO dictates backup frequency and replication strategies. A stricter RPO requires more frequent data synchronization.
