Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Defining Acceptable Downtime

What is RTO?

  • Definition: The maximum acceptable downtime for a system or application after a disaster.
  • Measurement: Time, from incident start to full operational recovery.
  • Driver: Business impact analysis (financial, reputational, operational).
  • Example: An RTO of 4 hours means systems must be fully operational within 4 hours.

Why it Matters

RTO directly influences the choice and cost of DR strategies. A stricter RTO demands more robust and often more expensive solutions.
