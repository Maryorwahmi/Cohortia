Business Continuity vs. Disaster Recovery

Distinct Roles in Cloud Resilience

Business Continuity (BC)

  • Focus: Maintaining critical business functions.
  • Scope: Organization-wide, strategic planning.
  • Goal: Minimize operational disruption and financial loss.
  • Example: Having alternative communication channels or manual processes during an outage.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

  • Focus: Restoring IT systems and data.
  • Scope: Technical, subset of BC.
  • Goal: Bring IT infrastructure back to operational state.
  • Example: Restoring databases from backups or failing over to a secondary data center.

Business Continuity

Strategic plan to keep the business running during and after a disaster.

Disaster Recovery

Technical processes to restore IT systems and data after a disaster.

Key Takeaway

BC is the overarching strategy for business resilience, while DR is the technical execution plan to restore IT systems and data after a disruptive event.
