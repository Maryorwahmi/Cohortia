RTO/RPO Drives BCDR Strategy

Balancing Cost, Complexity, and Resilience

Impact of RTO/RPO

  • Low RTO/RPO: Requires advanced, often more expensive solutions (e.g., active-active multi-region).
  • High RTO/RPO: Allows for simpler, more cost-effective strategies (e.g., backup and restore).
  • Decision Factor: Business impact analysis and risk tolerance.
  • Goal: Achieve optimal balance between recovery objectives, cost, and operational complexity.

Misconception Alert

Don't aim for near-zero RTO/RPO for all systems. Prioritize based on criticality to avoid unnecessary costs and complexity.

RTO/RPO vs. Strategy Complexity

High RTO/RPO

Key Takeaway

The chosen BCDR strategy must align with defined RTO and RPO objectives, balancing the desired level of resilience with the associated costs and operational complexity.
