Learning Objectives

Skills for Cloud Resilience

LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Differentiate BC and DR

Understand the distinct scopes of Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery in cloud environments. This skill is vital for crafting comprehensive resilience strategies that cover both operational and technical aspects.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Explain RTO and RPO

Articulate the meaning and significance of Recovery Time Objective and Recovery Point Objective. Grasping these metrics is crucial for setting realistic expectations and selecting appropriate recovery strategies.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Describe Cloud DR Strategies

Outline various cloud-native approaches for backup, restore, failover, and multi-region deployments. This objective helps you choose the right strategy for different business needs and risk appetites.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Key Idea

Master the core concepts of BC, DR, RTO, and RPO to inform strategic decisions and select appropriate cloud recovery strategies.
