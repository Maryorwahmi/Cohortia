Warm Standby Deployment

Scaled-Down, Ready-to-Scale Environment

How it Works

  • Mechanism: A scaled-down but fully functional environment runs in a secondary region.
  • Data: Data is continuously replicated to the secondary region.
  • Recovery: Resources are scaled up, and traffic is redirected during a disaster.
  • RTO/RPO: Lower than Pilot Light, typically minutes to a few hours.

Key Advantage

Offers a strong balance between cost and recovery speed, suitable for critical applications with moderate RTO/RPO.
