Method Overview

Forensics for Containerized Workloads

Due to their ephemeral nature, container forensics requires rapid and specialized collection methods.

  • •**Container Logs:** Capture application and system logs from running containers.
  • •**Container Images:** Create images of running containers to preserve their filesystem state.
  • •**Orchestrator Logs:** Analyze logs from Kubernetes or other orchestrators for management activities.
Key Takeaway

Container forensics prioritizes capturing volatile logs and images, alongside orchestrator activity, to overcome the challenge of ephemeral resources.

**Example:** A Kubernetes pod running a microservice is compromised. Before the orchestrator can restart it, an automated forensic tool captures its logs and creates an image of the running container for later analysis.

Common Mistake

Relying solely on post-incident analysis for containers. Proactive logging and automated evidence capture are essential due to their short lifespans.
