Safety Notes for Cloud IR

Ensuring secure and effective incident response operations

Test Automated Actions

Thoroughly test automated containment actions and ensure rollback mechanisms are in place.

Human Oversight

Prioritize human review for critical containment decisions, even with automation, to prevent accidental disruption.

Least Privilege Access

Ensure incident responders have least privilege access to perform their duties, reducing the attack surface.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize testing, human oversight, and least privilege in cloud IR to prevent unintended consequences and enhance security.

Regular Tabletop Exercises

Simulate various cloud incident scenarios to prepare the team and refine playbooks.

Documentation

Document every step of the IR process for compliance, lessons learned, and continuous improvement.

Continuous Improvement

Use insights from exercises and real incidents to continuously enhance your cloud security posture.

Pro-Tip

Incorporate cloud-specific attack vectors into your tabletop exercises to ensure realistic and relevant training for your team.
