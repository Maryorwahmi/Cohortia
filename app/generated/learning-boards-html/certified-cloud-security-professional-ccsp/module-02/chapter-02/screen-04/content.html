Core Concept

Virtual Private Clouds (VPCs) and Virtual Networks (VNets)

Your private, isolated network in the cloud

Definition

A logically isolated section of the cloud where you can launch resources, completely segregated from other customers' networks.

Why it Matters

Provides foundational network isolation, limiting the 'blast radius' of security incidents and ensuring data privacy.

Key Takeaway

VPCs/VNets are the essential first step in establishing a secure and isolated environment for your cloud workloads.
