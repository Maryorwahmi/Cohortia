Microsegmentation: Limiting Lateral Movement

Dividing networks into small, isolated workload segments

Definition

The practice of dividing a network into small, isolated segments, often down to individual workloads or applications.

Core Benefit

Significantly limits lateral movement for attackers, containing breaches and reducing the 'blast radius'.

Contrast

Goes beyond macro-segmentation (VPCs/subnets) by applying granular controls at the workload level.
