Governance & Policy

Administrative Controls: Policies, Procedures & Training

Establishing Secure Practices and Human Defenses

Security Policies

Formal documents outlining the organization's stance on security.

  • Acceptable Use Policy
  • Data Classification Policy
  • Incident Response Policy

Procedures & Standards

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for implementing policies.

  • Procedure for Provisioning New Cloud Resources
  • Standard for IAM Role Creation
  • Data Backup and Recovery Procedures

Security Awareness Training

Educating employees about security risks and best practices.

  • Phishing and Social Engineering Prevention
  • Data Handling Best Practices
  • Reporting Security Incidents
Key Takeaway

Administrative controls are the backbone of a secure organization, translating security objectives into actionable policies, procedures, and trained personnel, thereby significantly reducing human-related risks.
