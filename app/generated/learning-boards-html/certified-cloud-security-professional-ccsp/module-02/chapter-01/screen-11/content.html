Administrative Control

Separation of Duties

Dividing critical tasks to prevent fraud and error

Core Definition

Separation of Duties is an administrative control that ensures no single individual can complete a critical task alone, preventing fraud, error, or malicious activity.

  • • Enhances accountability and reduces insider threat risk.
  • • Requires multiple individuals for sensitive operations.
  • • Supported by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) in cloud.
Key Takeaway

Distribute critical responsibilities to avoid single points of failure and increase oversight.

Cloud Implementation

Role Separation

Separate roles like network administrator, security administrator, and application developer with distinct permissions.

Policy Creation vs. Deployment

The person who creates an IAM policy should not be the same person who approves its deployment into production.

Common Mistake

Granting a single individual or team excessive privileges that allow them to bypass critical security controls or perform conflicting actions.
