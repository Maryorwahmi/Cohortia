Understanding HIPAA

Safeguarding Protected Health Information (PHI)

HIPAA is a US law setting standards for protecting sensitive patient health information (PHI) and electronic PHI (ePHI) in cloud environments.

  • Key Requirements: Administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for ePHI, including access controls, risk assessments, encryption, and audit trails.
  • Business Associate Agreements (BAA): Essential contracts with CSPs for handling PHI, defining responsibilities.
  • Cloud Relevance: Ensure CSP has signed a BAA and your configurations meet HIPAA safeguards.

Example: A healthcare provider using a cloud EHR must have a BAA with the CSP and configure encryption and access controls for PHI.

Misconception: A CSP's general compliance certifications automatically satisfy HIPAA. Reality: A specific BAA and tailored configurations are required.

To comply with HIPAA, organizations must implement stringent security controls for PHI and secure a Business Associate Agreement with their Cloud Service Provider.
