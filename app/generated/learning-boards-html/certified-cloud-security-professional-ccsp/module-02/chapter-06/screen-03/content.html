Key Compliance Frameworks

Understanding the Standards That Govern Cloud Security

01 GDPR

EU data privacy law governing personal data of EU citizens, emphasizing rights, consent, and breach notification.

02 HIPAA

US law protecting health information (PHI), mandating security rules and Business Associate Agreements for cloud handling.

03 PCI DSS

Global standard for protecting payment card data, requiring specific technical and operational controls in cloud environments.

04 ISO 27001 & SOC 2

ISO 27001 is an ISMS standard; SOC 2 reports on CSP controls for security, availability, integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.
