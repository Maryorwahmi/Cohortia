BIA Outputs

Key Outputs of a Business Impact Analysis

Actionable insights for cloud disaster recovery planning

Critical Functions

Identifies which business processes are essential for survival and must be recovered first.

Dependency Mapping

Details the IT systems, data, and personnel that critical functions rely on.

RTO/RPO Determination

Establishes acceptable recovery time and data loss for each critical system.

Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD)

The absolute maximum time a business function can be unavailable without irreparable harm.

Key Takeaway

The BIA provides concrete metrics and priorities, ensuring your DRP is strategically aligned with actual business needs and risk tolerance.
