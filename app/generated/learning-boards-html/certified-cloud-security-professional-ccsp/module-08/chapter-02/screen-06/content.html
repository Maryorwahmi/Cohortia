DR Strategy

Pilot Light

Maintaining a minimal, scaled-down environment for faster recovery

How it Works

  • •Minimal, scaled-down environment in DR region.
  • •Essential services (e.g., databases) continuously running with replicated data.
  • •Compute resources scaled to zero or shut down.
  • •In disaster, provision compute and scale up dormant services.
Key Takeaway

Pilot Light offers a good balance between cost and recovery speed, suitable for applications needing moderate RTO and low RPO.

Example Scenario

An RDS Multi-AZ database replicating data to a standby in another region, with application servers scaled to zero until failover.

Strategy Trade-offs

Pros: Lower Cost

Reduced infrastructure costs vs. warm/hot standby.

Pros: Improved RTO

Tens of minutes to a few hours.

Pros: Low RPO

Data often continuously replicated.

Cons: More Complex

Requires more configuration and management.
