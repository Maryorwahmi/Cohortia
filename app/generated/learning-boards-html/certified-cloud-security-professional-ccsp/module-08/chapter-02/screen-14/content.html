Common Pitfalls in Cloud BCDR Planning

Avoiding critical mistakes in your disaster recovery strategy

Underestimating Costs

Failing to budget for continuous replication or standby infrastructure costs.

Failing to Test

An untested DRP is a theoretical plan that often fails in real-world scenarios.

Neglecting Data Sync

Inconsistent data replication leads to higher RPO and potential data loss.

Overlooking Dependencies

Not accounting for network or application interdependencies can cause cascading failures.

Key Takeaway

Proactive identification and mitigation of these common pitfalls are essential for building a truly resilient and effective cloud BCDR strategy.
