DR Strategy

Warm Standby

A continuously running, scaled-down duplicate for rapid failover

How it Works

  • •Fully functional, scaled-down duplicate in DR region.
  • •Key services continuously running, receiving live data.
  • •Not actively serving production traffic.
  • •In disaster, scale up resources and redirect traffic.
Key Takeaway

Warm Standby provides rapid recovery for business-critical applications, justifying its higher cost with significantly reduced RTO and RPO.

Example Scenario

Running a small cluster of EC2 instances and an RDS instance in a DR region, continuously replicating data, with Route 53 for failover.

Strategy Trade-offs

Pros: Reduced RTO

Recovery in minutes.

Pros: Low RPO

Minutes or near-zero data loss.

Cons: Higher Cost

Due to continuously running infrastructure.

Best For: Business-Critical Apps

Requires low RTO and RPO.
