DRP Elements

Cloud Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) - Part 1

Essential foundational elements for a robust recovery plan

01

Scope and Objectives

Clearly defines covered systems and sets RTO/RPO targets.

02

Roles and Responsibilities

Assigns specific duties to DR team members, ensuring accountability.

03

Communication Plan

Outlines how stakeholders will be informed during an incident.

04

Activation Criteria

Defines the specific events or thresholds that trigger the DRP.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined DRP provides a clear roadmap for recovery, minimizing chaos and maximizing efficiency during a disaster.
