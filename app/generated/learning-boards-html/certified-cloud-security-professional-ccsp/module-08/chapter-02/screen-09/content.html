Strategy Comparison

Comparing Cloud DR Strategies

Understanding the trade-offs between cost, RTO, and RPO

Backup and Restore

  • $Low Cost
  • RTOHigh (hours/days)
  • RPOHigh (last backup)

Pilot Light

  • $Moderate Cost
  • RTOModerate (tens of mins/hours)
  • RPOLow (minutes)

Warm Standby

  • $Higher Cost
  • RTOLow (minutes)
  • RPOVery Low (minutes/near-zero)

Hot Site

  • $Highest Cost
  • RTONear-Zero
  • RPONear-Zero
Key Takeaway

The optimal DR strategy is a balance of RTO, RPO, and budget, tailored to the criticality of each application. There is no one-size-fits-all solution.
