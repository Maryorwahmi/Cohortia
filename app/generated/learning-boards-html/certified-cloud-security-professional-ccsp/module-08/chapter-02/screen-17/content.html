Key Concepts: Planning & Mitigation

Essential terms for effective cloud BCDR planning

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Process to identify critical business functions and the impact of their disruption.

Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

A documented, structured approach describing how an organization can quickly resume mission-critical functions after an unplanned event.

Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD)

The maximum time a business function can be unavailable without causing irreparable harm.

Infrastructure as Code (IaC)

Managing and provisioning infrastructure through code instead of manual processes, crucial for automated DR.

Summary Takeaway

A robust cloud BCDR strategy integrates thorough analysis, detailed planning, and automation to ensure rapid, consistent, and reliable recovery from disruptions.
