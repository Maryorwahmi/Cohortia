Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identifying critical functions and the impact of their disruption

Purpose of BIA

  • •Identifies critical business functions.
  • •Maps supporting IT systems and dependencies.
  • •Quantifies financial and operational impacts of unavailability.
  • •Prioritizes recovery efforts based on business needs.
Key Takeaway

A BIA is the essential first step in cloud DR planning, providing objective data to align recovery strategies with business criticality and impact.

Why it Matters

Without a BIA, organizations risk misallocating DR resources, potentially over-protecting non-critical systems or leaving vital assets vulnerable.
