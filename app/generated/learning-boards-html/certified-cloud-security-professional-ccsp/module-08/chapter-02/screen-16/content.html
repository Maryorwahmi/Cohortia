Key Concepts: Cloud DR Strategies

Essential terms for cloud resilience

Backup and Restore

DR strategy relying on restoring data and configurations from backups to new infrastructure.

Pilot Light

DR strategy maintaining a minimal, scaled-down environment in a DR region, ready to be scaled up.

Warm Standby

DR strategy with a continuously running, scaled-down duplicate environment in a DR region, ready for failover.

Hot Site (Multi-Site Active/Active)

Most robust DR strategy, running fully active production environments in multiple regions simultaneously.

Summary Takeaway

Selecting the right cloud DR strategy involves a critical balance of cost, RTO, and RPO, tailored to the specific needs and criticality of each application.
