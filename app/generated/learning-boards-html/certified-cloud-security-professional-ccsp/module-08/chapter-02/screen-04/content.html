Core Concept

Cloud DR: Flexibility and Scalability

Leveraging the cloud's inherent advantages for disaster recovery

Beyond On-Premises Limitations

  • •No need for physical duplicate infrastructure.
  • •On-demand provisioning of resources.
  • •Pay-as-you-go model reduces capital expenditure.
Key Takeaway

Cloud DR offers unparalleled agility and cost efficiency, but requires careful alignment of RTO, RPO, and budget with chosen strategies.

Consider This

While cloud providers offer high availability for their services, your application's DR is ultimately your responsibility. Don't assume automatic protection.

Key Decision Factors

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Maximum acceptable downtime.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Maximum acceptable data loss.

Budget Constraints

Financial resources available.

Application Criticality

Impact of downtime on business.
