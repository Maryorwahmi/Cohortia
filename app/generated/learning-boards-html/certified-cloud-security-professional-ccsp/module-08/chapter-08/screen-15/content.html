More Key Concepts

Continuing our review of essential terms

VPN

Creates an encrypted tunnel over a public network for secure access.

Server-Side Encryption (SSE)

Encryption performed by the cloud service after data receipt.

Client-Side Encryption

Encryption performed by the client application before sending data.

These concepts highlight the diverse tools and strategies available for securing data, emphasizing key ownership and control.
