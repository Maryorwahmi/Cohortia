Security Best Practices

Effective Key Management and Avoiding Pitfalls

Strategies for robust encryption and common configuration errors.

Centralized Key Management

Use a Key Management Service (KMS) to centralize key generation, storage, and lifecycle management for both in-transit and at-rest encryption.

Avoid Common Mistakes

Never assume default encryption; always verify. Avoid weak or outdated algorithms (e.g., TLS 1.0/1.1) and adhere to current industry standards.

Key Takeaway

Proactive key management and adherence to modern encryption standards are critical to prevent vulnerabilities and ensure compliance in cloud environments.

Key Management Best Practices

  • •Use a KMS for centralized key management.
  • •Apply least privilege to key usage.
  • •Regularly rotate encryption keys.
  • •Enforce separation of duties for keys and data.
  • •Monitor key usage with audit logging.
  • •Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

Common Encryption Mistakes

  • •Assuming encryption is enabled by default.
  • •Using weak or outdated encryption algorithms.
  • •Failing to rotate encryption keys regularly.
  • •Inadequate access controls for encryption keys.
  • •Lack of audit logging for key usage.
  • •Not meeting specific compliance standards.
