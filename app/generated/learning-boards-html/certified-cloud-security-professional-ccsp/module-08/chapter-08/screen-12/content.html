Client-Side vs. Server-Side Encryption

Understanding key ownership and control in cloud encryption.

Client-Side Encryption

Data is encrypted by the client application before being sent to the cloud service. Full control over keys, 'zero-knowledge' to provider.

Server-Side Encryption

Data is encrypted by the cloud service after it receives the data. Simpler to implement, leverages provider's managed services.

Key Takeaway

The choice between client-side and server-side encryption hinges on key ownership, control requirements, and the balance between security and operational complexity.

Client-Side Encryption

  • •Data encrypted before leaving client.
  • •Client retains full key control.
  • •Highest confidentiality ('zero-knowledge').
  • •More complex to implement and manage keys.
  • •Ideal for highly sensitive data, strict compliance.

Server-Side Encryption

  • •Data encrypted by cloud service upon receipt.
  • •Cloud provider manages keys (often via KMS).
  • •Simpler to implement, less operational burden.
  • •Cloud provider has access to keys (though protected).
  • •Common for most data at rest, balances security/ease.
