DR Testing Methodologies

DR Testing: Tabletop & Walkthrough Exercises

Discussion-Based and Procedural Reviews for Plan Validation

1. Tabletop Exercise

A discussion-based session where key personnel walk through the DRP, identifying roles, responsibilities, and decision points. No actual systems are touched.

Pros
  • Low cost
  • Good for initial review
  • Team training
Cons
  • No technical validation
  • No performance validation

2. Walkthrough / Structured Walkthrough

A more detailed review where teams simulate the execution of specific recovery steps, often using checklists and documentation. Some technical steps might be demonstrated but not fully executed.

Pros
  • More detailed than tabletop
  • Identifies documentation gaps
Cons
  • Limited technical validation
  • No full performance test
Key Takeaway

Tabletop and walkthrough exercises are valuable for initial plan review and team alignment, but they must be complemented by more rigorous technical testing.
