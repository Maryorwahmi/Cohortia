Key Concepts: Advanced DR Testing & Review

Ensuring Comprehensive Cloud Recovery

Simulation/Functional Test

Activating the DR plan in an isolated test environment to validate technical procedures without impacting production.

Full Interruption/Cutover Test

Redirecting production traffic to the DR environment, providing the highest confidence but also the highest risk.

Post-Incident Review

A formal 'lessons learned' process to analyze incidents/tests and continuously improve future response and recovery.

Summary Takeaway

Advanced DR testing methodologies and a robust post-incident review process are crucial for achieving and maintaining high levels of cloud resilience and operational maturity.
