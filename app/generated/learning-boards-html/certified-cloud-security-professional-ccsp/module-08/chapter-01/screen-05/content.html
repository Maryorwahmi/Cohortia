Why BC/DR is Critical for Cloud Services

Mitigating risks and ensuring operational resilience

Cloud's Inherent Resilience is Not Enough

While cloud providers offer robust infrastructure, they don't eliminate the need for customer-specific BC/DR planning. Your applications and data still require protection.

  • •Cloud providers ensure underlying infrastructure resilience.
  • •Customer is responsible for application-level resilience.
  • •Outages, data corruption, and cyberattacks remain risks.
Common Misconception

Simply migrating to the cloud automatically guarantees full BC/DR. This is false; active customer planning is always required.

Consequences of Poor Planning

  • •Significant financial losses from downtime.
  • •Severe reputational damage and loss of trust.
  • •Regulatory non-compliance and potential fines.
  • •Operational disruption and reduced productivity.
Example Scenario

An e-commerce platform experiences a regional cloud outage. Without a DR plan, millions in revenue are lost per hour, and customer trust erodes.

Key Takeaway

Proactive BC/DR planning is essential in the cloud to mitigate financial, reputational, and operational risks, complementing the provider's inherent resilience.
