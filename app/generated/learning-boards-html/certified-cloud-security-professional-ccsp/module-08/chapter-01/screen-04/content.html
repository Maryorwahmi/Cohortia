Business Continuity vs. Disaster Recovery

Understanding the distinct yet complementary disciplines

Business Continuity (BC)

The overarching process of identifying potential threats and their impacts on business operations. It focuses on maintaining essential functions.

  • •Builds organizational resilience.
  • •Safeguards stakeholders, reputation, and value-creating activities.
  • •Ensures the entire business can continue operating.
Analogy

Think of BC as the entire emergency preparedness plan for a city, covering all aspects from public safety to essential services.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

A subset of BC, specifically focusing on the technological aspects of restoring IT systems and data after a disruptive event.

  • •Restores cloud-based applications and infrastructure.
  • •Focuses on technical recovery procedures.
  • •Ensures digital assets are functional again.
Analogy

DR is like the specific plan for restoring the city's power grid and communication networks after a major storm.

Key Takeaway

Business Continuity is the strategic umbrella for organizational resilience, while Disaster Recovery is the tactical plan for restoring IT systems and data.
