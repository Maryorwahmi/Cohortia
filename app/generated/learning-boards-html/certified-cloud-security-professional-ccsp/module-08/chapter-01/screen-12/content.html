Common Disasters and Disruptions in the Cloud

Preparing for a wide spectrum of potential impacts

Natural Disasters

  • •Earthquakes, floods, hurricanes affecting data centers.
  • •Regional power grid failures impacting entire cloud regions.
Impact

Can cause widespread outages across a cloud region, despite provider's physical resilience.

Technical Failures

  • •Hardware malfunctions (servers, storage).
  • •Software bugs in cloud services or customer applications.
  • •Network outages within a cloud region or Availability Zone.
Impact

Can lead to service degradation, data unavailability, or complete outages for affected resources.

Human Error

  • •Accidental deletion of critical data or resources.
  • •Misconfiguration of security groups, network settings, or access policies.
Impact

Can lead to data loss, security breaches, or service unavailability due to incorrect settings.

Malicious Attacks

  • •DDoS attacks overwhelming services.
  • •Ransomware encrypting data and demanding payment.
  • •Data breaches compromising sensitive information.
Impact

Can lead to service disruption, data loss, financial extortion, and severe reputational damage.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive BC/DR plan must account for a wide range of threats, from natural disasters and technical failures to human error and malicious attacks, tailoring strategies to mitigate specific risks.
