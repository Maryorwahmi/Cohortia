Key Concepts (Continued)

Further essential takeaways for cloud resilience

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum acceptable amount of data loss (measured in time) that an organization can tolerate during a disaster.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A systematic process to determine and evaluate the potential effects of an interruption to critical business operations.

Shared Responsibility Model

A framework outlining the security responsibilities of the cloud provider and the cloud customer.

Summary Takeaway

The Recovery Point Objective (RPO) defines acceptable data loss, while the Business Impact Analysis (BIA) informs both RTO and RPO, and the Shared Responsibility Model clarifies BC/DR roles between provider and customer.
