Setting RTO and RPO with Business Impact Analysis

Aligning recovery goals with business criticality

The Role of Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A systematic process to determine and evaluate the potential effects of an interruption to critical business operations. It identifies:

  • •Critical business functions.
  • •Resources they depend on.
  • •Impact of unavailability over time (financial, reputational, operational).
Why it matters

The BIA ensures that RTO and RPO targets are aligned with actual business needs and risk tolerance, optimizing recovery investments.

RTO/RPO Examples by Criticality

  • •**High Criticality (e.g., Banking App):** RTO and RPO near zero. Justified by severe financial and reputational impact. Requires continuous replication and high availability.
  • •**Medium Criticality (e.g., E-commerce Website):** RTO in minutes/hours, RPO in minutes. Balances cost with revenue impact.
  • •**Low Criticality (e.g., Internal HR Portal):** RTO in hours/days, RPO in hours. Tolerates longer recovery due to lower immediate business impact.
Key Takeaway

A Business Impact Analysis (BIA) is fundamental for setting appropriate RTO and RPO values, ensuring recovery strategies align with business priorities and risk tolerance.
