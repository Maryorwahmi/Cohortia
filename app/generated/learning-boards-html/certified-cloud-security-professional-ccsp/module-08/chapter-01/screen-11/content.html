Your Responsibilities *in* the Cloud for BC/DR

Ensuring resilience for your data and applications

Customer's Core Responsibilities

As the customer, you are responsible *in* the cloud for the resilience of your specific deployments:

  • •**Data:** Backups, replication, and encryption.
  • •**Applications:** High availability configuration, failover logic.
  • •**Operating Systems:** Patching, hardening, and configuration.
  • •**Network Configuration:** Security groups, routing, and VPNs.
  • •**Access Controls:** Identity and access management (IAM).
Critical Oversight

Failing to actively plan and implement these responsibilities creates significant gaps in your cloud BC/DR strategy.

Practical Implementation

  • •Configure VMs for high availability (e.g., across Availability Zones).
  • •Implement data backups and snapshots (e.g., EBS snapshots, database backups).
  • •Design cross-region replication and failover strategies for critical data and applications.
  • •Regularly test your entire DR plan to ensure effectiveness.
Example: Database Instance

You are responsible for configuring database backups, replication (e.g., RDS Multi-AZ), and restoration procedures.

Key Takeaway

Customers must actively configure and manage high availability, data protection, and failover for their specific cloud workloads to ensure comprehensive BC/DR.
