Auto-scaling: Dynamic Resource Management

Adjusting compute resources based on demand and health

Benefits of Auto-scaling

  • •**Elasticity:** Automatically scales resources up or down with demand.
  • •**Resilience:** Replaces failed or unhealthy instances automatically.
  • •**Performance:** Maintains consistent performance under varying loads.
  • •**Cost Optimization:** Prevents over-provisioning by scaling down when not needed.
Key Takeaway

Auto-scaling ensures applications maintain performance and availability by dynamically adjusting resources and self-healing from instance failures.
