Mitigating SPOFs: Redundancy at Every Layer

Designing for resilience across your cloud architecture

Key Mitigation Principles

  • •**Redundancy:** Implement duplicate components at every critical layer.
  • •**Distribution:** Spread resources across multiple AZs and regions.
  • •**Automation:** Use auto-scaling and managed services for self-healing.
  • •**Monitoring:** Continuously monitor health to detect and respond to failures.
Key Takeaway

Proactive redundancy and distribution across all layers of your cloud architecture are essential to effectively mitigate SPOFs and ensure continuous operation.

SPOF Mitigation Strategies

Redundancy by Layer

Compute

Auto Scaling Groups (Multi-AZ)

Networking

Load Balancers, Redundant DNS

Data

Multi-AZ DBs, Cross-Region Replication

Configuration

Highly Available Key-Value Stores

Common Mistake

Assuming cloud infrastructure's inherent resilience means your application is automatically highly available. Your architecture must explicitly design for redundancy.
