Managed Services for HA and FT

Offloading infrastructure complexity to cloud providers

Benefits of Managed Services

  • •**Built-in HA/FT:** Many services offer Multi-AZ deployments and automatic failover.
  • •**Reduced Overhead:** Cloud provider handles patching, scaling, and infrastructure.
  • •**Focus on Logic:** Teams can concentrate on application development, not infrastructure.
  • •**Cost-Effective:** Often more efficient than building and maintaining custom solutions.
Key Takeaway

Leveraging managed services is a strategic way to achieve high availability and fault tolerance with reduced operational burden, allowing teams to focus on core application development.

Misconception

While managed services offer built-in HA, it's still crucial to configure them correctly (e.g., enabling Multi-AZ for RDS) and design your application to utilize these features effectively.
