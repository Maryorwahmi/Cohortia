Key Lifecycle Stage 7

Key Destruction

Irreversibly eliminating keys when no longer needed

  • • Ensure keys cannot be recovered or misused after their lifecycle ends.
  • • This prevents attackers from decrypting old data or impersonating services.
  • • Cloud KMS services often provide scheduled deletion with a waiting period.
  • • Physical destruction or cryptographic erasure is required for underlying key material.
Key Takeaway

Secure and irreversible key destruction is vital to prevent future misuse and maintain data confidentiality.

Common Pitfall

Failing to ensure complete and irreversible destruction, leaving residual key material vulnerable to recovery.

STAGE 7

Key Destruction

Irreversibly delete keys to prevent recovery and misuse.

Example: Scheduled Deletion

Cloud KMS allows scheduling key deletion with a waiting period (e.g., 7-30 days) to prevent accidental loss, after which the key material is cryptographically erased.
