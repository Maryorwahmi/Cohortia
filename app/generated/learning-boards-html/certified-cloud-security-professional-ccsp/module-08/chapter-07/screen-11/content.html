Key Escrow and Access Control Principles

Balancing recoverability with the principle of least privilege

Key Escrow

Storing a copy of a cryptographic key with a third party for business continuity or compliance, requiring strong security.

Key Access Control

Strictly applying the Principle of Least Privilege to ensure only authorized users/services can use or manage keys.

Key Takeaway

Key escrow offers recoverability with strict controls, while least privilege access control minimizes key exposure.

Key Escrow

Copy of key stored securely for recovery.

  • Business continuity
  • Legal/regulatory compliance
  • Requires multi-party control & strong encryption

Least Privilege

Minimal permissions for key usage/management.

  • Granular IAM/RBAC policies
  • Prevents misuse or accidental deletion
  • Reduces attack surface
