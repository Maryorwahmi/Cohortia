Common Pitfall
Misconceptions in Cryptography
Avoiding common errors in applying cryptographic principles.
Key Takeaway

Correct application of cryptography requires understanding its limitations and proper use cases, not just its existence.

Common Mistake

Over-reliance on encryption alone without ensuring integrity, authenticity, and proper key management.

Key Areas to Clarify
Algorithm Choice, Key Management, Properties

Ensure you use strong algorithms, manage keys securely, and understand the distinct roles of encryption, hashing, and digital signatures.

Example Error
Using MD5 for password hashes
Correct Approach
Use SHA-256 or Argon2
