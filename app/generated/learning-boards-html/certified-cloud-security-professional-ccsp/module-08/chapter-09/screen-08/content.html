Core Concept

Data Sovereignty Defined

Data sovereignty is the concept that digital data is subject to the laws and governance structures of the nation where it is collected or processed. It asserts national control over data.

Why it Matters

Data Residency Requirements

Data sovereignty often leads to data residency requirements, mandating that specific data must physically reside within defined geographical boundaries to comply with local laws and regulations.

Key Takeaway

Understanding and adhering to data sovereignty and residency laws is crucial for global cloud operations and compliance.

Example

Regional Cloud Deployment

To meet data residency requirements for EU citizens, a company must deploy its cloud resources, such as databases and storage, in an AWS or Azure region located within the EU (e.g., Frankfurt, Dublin). This ensures the data physically resides in the required jurisdiction.

Common Misconception

Encryption Equals Residency

While encryption protects data confidentiality, it does not inherently satisfy data residency requirements. Data must first be physically located in the correct region. Encryption then adds a vital layer of security to that data, regardless of its location.
