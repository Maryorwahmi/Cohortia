Core Concept

Securing Complex Cloud Environments

Addressing the challenges of modern cloud workloads

The Evolving Cloud Landscape

Cloud environments now encompass a diverse mix of virtual machines, containers, and serverless functions, creating a highly distributed and dynamic attack surface.

Limitations of Traditional Security

Perimeter-based firewalls are insufficient when workloads communicate internally, allowing attackers to move freely once inside the network.

Need for Advanced Strategies

Granular controls, workload isolation, and continuous verification are essential to protect against lateral movement and reduce the 'blast radius' of a breach.

Hybrid Cloud Workloads: Diverse & Distributed
Virtual Machines
Containers (K8s)
Serverless Functions
These diverse components require a unified, granular security approach.
Key Takeaway

As cloud environments grow in complexity, advanced security strategies like micro-segmentation, service mesh, and Zero Trust become essential to protect diverse workloads and ensure resilience.
