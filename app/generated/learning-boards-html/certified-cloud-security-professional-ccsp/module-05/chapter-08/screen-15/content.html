Key Concepts

Reinforcing your understanding

Zero Trust

A security model based on the principle of "never trust, always verify," requiring continuous authentication and authorization for all access.

Lateral Movement

An attacker's technique to move deeper into a network after an initial compromise, often targeted by micro-segmentation.

Compliance Integration

Embedding security strategies to meet regulatory requirements (e.g., PCI DSS, HIPAA, GDPR) through design.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering these advanced concepts is crucial for designing, implementing, and maintaining secure and compliant cloud-native applications in today's complex environments.
