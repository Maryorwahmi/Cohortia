Image Signing and Provenance: Ensuring Integrity

Cryptographic Assurance of Origin and Tamper-Proofing

Why Image Signing Matters

  • •Integrity Verification: Confirms the image has not been altered since it was signed.
  • •Origin Assurance: Verifies the image comes from a trusted source.
  • •Supply Chain Defense: Protects against malicious injection or replacement of images.
  • •Compliance: Essential for regulatory requirements demanding verifiable software origins.
Key Takeaway

Implement image signing and track provenance to cryptographically verify image integrity and origin, crucial for defending against supply chain attacks.
