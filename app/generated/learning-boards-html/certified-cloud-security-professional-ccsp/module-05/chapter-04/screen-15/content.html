Key Concepts

Advanced Strategies for Container Security

Container Registry Security

Protecting access to image repositories through IAM, encryption, and network controls to prevent compromise.

Image Signing

Cryptographically verifying the integrity and origin of container images to prevent tampering and supply chain attacks.

Image Provenance

The verifiable history and origin of a container image, including its build process and components, for auditing and trust.

Image Lifecycle Management

Processes for updating, retiring, and auditing container images throughout their lifespan to maintain continuous security and compliance.

Chapter Summary

By implementing these advanced strategies, organizations can establish a robust security posture for their containerized applications, protecting against evolving threats and ensuring operational integrity.
