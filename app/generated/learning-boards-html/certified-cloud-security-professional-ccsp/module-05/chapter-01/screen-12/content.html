ASSET MANAGEMENT

Taming VM Sprawl

Strategies for Effective Virtual Machine Lifecycle Management

01

Effective Asset Management

Maintain a comprehensive inventory of all VMs, including purpose, owner, OS, and update status. Know what you have.

02

Regular Auditing

Periodically review VM inventory to identify stale, unused, or misconfigured VMs. Look for outdated OS or lack of ownership.

03

Decommissioning Policies

Establish clear processes for securely retiring unneeded VMs, including data wiping and proper deletion to prevent lingering risks.

04

Automated Lifecycle Management

Utilize tools to automate VM provisioning, patching, and decommissioning, ensuring consistency and reducing manual errors.

Key Takeaway

Effective asset management, regular auditing, clear decommissioning policies, and automation are key to combating VM sprawl.
