Key Concepts

Policy Management and Data Handling Techniques

DLP Policy

A set of rules that define what sensitive data to detect, where to detect it, and what actions to take when such data is identified.

False Positive

An alert generated by a DLP system for data that is not actually sensitive or does not violate policy, requiring careful tuning to minimize.

Data Redaction/Masking

Techniques used by DLP to obscure sensitive parts of data while allowing legitimate access to the rest, crucial for privacy and compliance.

Summary Takeaway

Effective DLP relies on well-defined policies, careful management of false positives, and techniques like redaction to protect sensitive data while maintaining usability.
