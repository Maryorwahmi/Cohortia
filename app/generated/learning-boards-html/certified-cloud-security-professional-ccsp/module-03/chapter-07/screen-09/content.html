Data Retention: Balancing Act

Navigating the risks of over-retention versus under-retention for optimal compliance

Risks of Over-Retention

Keeping data longer than necessary can introduce several risks and inefficiencies.

  • Increased storage costs for unnecessary data.
  • Expanded attack surface, increasing breach risk.
  • Complicates compliance with 'right to be forgotten' regulations (e.g., GDPR).
Key Takeaway

Balancing data retention means avoiding both the costs and risks of over-retention and the legal penalties of under-retention.

Risks of Under-Retention

Deleting data prematurely can lead to severe legal and business consequences.

  • Non-compliance with legal and regulatory mandates, leading to fines.
  • Inability to respond to legal discovery requests.
  • Loss of critical historical data for business intelligence or operations.
Best Practice

Regularly review and update data retention policies to align with evolving legal, regulatory, and business requirements.
