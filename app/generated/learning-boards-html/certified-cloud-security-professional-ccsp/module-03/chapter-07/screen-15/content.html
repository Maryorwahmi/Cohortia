Key Concepts

Reinforcing your understanding of secure data practices

Cryptographic Erasure

Deleting the encryption key associated with encrypted data to render the data unrecoverable.

NIST SP 800-88

Guidelines for Media Sanitization, providing standards for securely erasing data from various storage media.

Right to Be Forgotten (GDPR)

A principle allowing individuals to request the deletion of their personal data under certain circumstances.

Summary Takeaway

Secure data destruction relies on methods like cryptographic erasure, guided by standards like NIST SP 800-88, and influenced by privacy rights such as the Right to Be Forgotten.
