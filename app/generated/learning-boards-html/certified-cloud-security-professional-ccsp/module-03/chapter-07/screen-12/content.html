Other Data Destruction Methods

Overwriting and physical destruction for various storage types and shared responsibility

Methods for Different Media

Beyond cryptographic erasure, other methods are used depending on the storage type and environment.

  • Overwriting: Writing random data or zeros multiple times over storage blocks. Applicable to block storage volumes.
  • Physical Destruction: Degaussing or shredding for dedicated hardware (e.g., HSMs, physical drives).
Key Takeaway

Overwriting and physical destruction are vital for specific storage types, complementing cryptographic erasure within the shared responsibility model.

Shared Responsibility in Destruction

Customer Responsibility: Ensuring logical erasure of data and proper key management (e.g., deleting KMS keys).
CSP Responsibility: Ensuring physical security and sanitization of their underlying infrastructure.
Critical Safety Note

Once data is securely destroyed, it is gone forever. Ensure robust backups and compliance with all retention periods before destruction.
