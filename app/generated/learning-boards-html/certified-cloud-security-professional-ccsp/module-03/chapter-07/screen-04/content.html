Information Rights Management (IRM)

Embedding usage policies directly into content for persistent protection

What is IRM?

Information Rights Management (IRM), also known as Enterprise Digital Rights Management (EDRM), provides persistent protection for documents and emails.

  • Embeds usage policies directly into the content itself.
  • Ensures permissions travel with the document, regardless of location.
  • Protects data even after it leaves your immediate perimeter.
Key Takeaway

IRM provides persistent, data-centric protection, enforcing usage policies directly within content, crucial for distributed cloud environments.

IRM vs. Traditional Access Controls

Traditional Access

Perimeter-based, system-dependent

IRM

Content-embedded, persistent

  • Traditional access controls protect data only while it resides in a specific system.
  • IRM ensures specified permissions travel with the document, regardless of storage or sharing.
  • This prevents unauthorized usage even if data is downloaded or moved.
