How IRM Enforces Control

Granular permissions that travel with your data, preventing unauthorized actions

IRM Usage Restrictions

IRM embeds specific usage policies directly into documents and emails, ensuring they are enforced regardless of where the content is stored or who accesses it.

  • Permissions travel with the content.
  • Protects against unauthorized actions like printing or forwarding.
  • Crucial for data sharing in cloud environments.
Key Takeaway

IRM ensures persistent control over data usage, preventing unauthorized actions like printing or forwarding, even after content is shared.

Common IRM Restrictions

Read-Only Access
Print Disabled
No Forwarding
No Copying
Common Mistake

Relying solely on network perimeter controls. IRM provides crucial data-centric security.
