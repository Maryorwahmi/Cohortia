IRM Solutions in Cloud Environments

Protecting sensitive data across productivity suites and collaborative platforms

Seamless Integration

IRM solutions are often integrated directly with major cloud productivity suites, allowing for consistent policy application.

  • Apply sensitivity labels to documents and emails.
  • Policies are enforced even after content is shared.
  • Crucial for data-centric security in cloud environments.
Key Takeaway

Integrated IRM solutions like Microsoft Purview and Adobe Document Cloud provide persistent, data-centric protection within common cloud workflows.

Examples of IRM Integration

Microsoft 365 (Azure Information Protection / Microsoft Purview Information Protection)
Adobe Document Cloud
Example Scenario

Applying a "Confidential - Do Not Forward" label to an email prevents recipients from forwarding, printing, or copying its content.
