Data Retention Policies

Defining how long specific types of data must be kept to meet obligations

What are Data Retention Policies?

These policies define how long specific types of data must be kept by an organization.

  • Driven by legal, regulatory, and business requirements.
  • Crucial for compliance and avoiding penalties.
  • Ensures data is available when needed for audits or legal discovery.
Key Takeaway

Data retention policies are essential for compliance, defining how long data must be kept based on legal, regulatory, and business needs.

Drivers for Data Retention

Legal Requirements (e.g., financial records for 7 years)
Regulatory Compliance (e.g., medical records for decades)
Business Operations (e.g., customer support logs)
