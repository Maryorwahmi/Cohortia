Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember for data lifecycle management

Information Rights Management (IRM)

A technology that embeds usage policies into documents and emails, providing persistent protection regardless of location or recipient.

Data Retention Policy

Rules defining how long specific types of data must be kept, driven by legal, regulatory, and business requirements.

Data Destruction Policy

Rules dictating how data is securely and irreversibly deleted when no longer needed.

Summary Takeaway

IRM, data retention, and data destruction policies are foundational for comprehensive data governance and compliance in cloud environments.
