Defining Your Classification Scheme

Engage stakeholders to identify sensitive data and establish clear policies.

Key Stakeholders & Their Roles

  • Business Owners: Define data value and business impact.
  • Legal Teams: Identify regulatory and legal obligations.
  • Compliance Officers: Ensure adherence to industry standards.
  • IT/Security Teams: Implement technical controls and tools.
Key Takeaway

A successful classification scheme is a collaborative effort, aligning business needs with legal and technical requirements.

Examples of Sensitive Data Types

Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

Names, addresses, social security numbers, email addresses.

Protected Health Information (PHI)

Medical records, health insurance information (HIPAA).

Payment Card Industry Data (PCI DSS)

Credit card numbers, expiration dates, CVV codes.

Practical Tip

Start with a few broad categories and iterate. Overly complex schemes lead to user fatigue and non-compliance.
