Data Anonymization

Challenges and Risks of Anonymization

Balancing privacy with data utility

Challenge: Utility vs. Privacy

Over-anonymizing can make data useless; under-anonymizing risks re-identification.

Risk: Re-identification

Even anonymized data can be linked with external datasets to reveal identities.

Key Takeaway

Anonymization requires careful design and ongoing assessment to mitigate re-identification risks effectively.
