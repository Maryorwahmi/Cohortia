Best Practices

Building an Effective Disaster Recovery Plan

A systematic approach to ensuring business continuity.

Steps for Robust DR Implementation
1. Identify Critical Assets

Determine essential applications and data for recovery.

2. Define RTO/RPO Targets

Set clear recovery time and data loss objectives.

3. Select DR Pattern

Choose the appropriate strategy (e.g., Pilot Light, Warm Standby).

4. Automate & Test

Automate failover/failback and regularly test the plan.

Critical Warning

DR plans are only as good as their last test. Untested plans often fail when a real disaster strikes.
