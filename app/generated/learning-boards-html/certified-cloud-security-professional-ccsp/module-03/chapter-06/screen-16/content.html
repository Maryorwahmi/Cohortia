Key Concepts

Critical Metrics and Patterns for Cloud Recovery

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum acceptable downtime after an incident.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum acceptable data loss after an incident.

Cloud DR Patterns

Different strategies (Pilot Light, Warm Standby, Active/Active) for varying RTO/RPO and cost.

Summary Takeaway

RTO and RPO are critical metrics that guide the selection and implementation of cloud DR patterns to ensure business continuity.
