DR Strategies

Cloud Disaster Recovery Patterns

Choosing the right strategy based on RTO, RPO, and cost.

DR Pattern Comparison
Backup & Restore

RTO/RPO: Highest
Cost: Lowest
Description: Restore from backups in new region.

Pilot Light

RTO/RPO: Moderate
Cost: Low-Moderate
Description: Core infra replicated, ready to scale up.

Warm Standby

RTO/RPO: Lower
Cost: Moderate-High
Description: Scaled-down env. runs continuously.

Multi-Site Active/Active

RTO/RPO: Lowest
Cost: Highest
Description: Full env. runs in multiple regions.

Key Takeaway

The choice of DR pattern directly impacts recovery speed, data loss tolerance, and overall cost, requiring alignment with business objectives.
