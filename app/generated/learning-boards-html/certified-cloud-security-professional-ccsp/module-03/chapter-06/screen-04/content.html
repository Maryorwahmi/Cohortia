Core Concept

Backup, Archiving, and DR: Distinct Purposes

Understanding the fundamental differences for effective data resilience.

Data Backup

Copies for quick restoration from accidental deletion, corruption, or system failure.

Data Archiving

Long-term retention of rarely accessed data for compliance, legal, or historical reasons.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Resuming business operations after a catastrophic event like a regional outage or cyberattack.

Comparison of Data Protection Strategies
Backup

Purpose: Operational Recovery
Access: Fast
Cost: Moderate

Archiving

Purpose: Long-term Retention
Access: Slow (minutes/hours)
Cost: Low

Disaster Recovery

Purpose: Business Continuity
Access: Varies (minutes/hours)
Cost: High
