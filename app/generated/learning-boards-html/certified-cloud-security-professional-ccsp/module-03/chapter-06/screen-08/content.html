Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Maximum tolerable downtime after an incident. How fast you need to be back online.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Maximum tolerable data loss from a service. How much data you can afford to lose.

Key Takeaway

RTO and RPO are critical for defining business recovery needs and guiding the selection of appropriate DR strategies.
