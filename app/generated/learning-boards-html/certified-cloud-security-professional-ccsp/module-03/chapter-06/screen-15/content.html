Key Concepts

Foundational Terms for Cloud Data Resilience

Data Backup

Creating copies of data for restoration in case of loss or corruption.

Data Archiving

Long-term storage of infrequently accessed data for compliance or historical purposes.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

The process of resuming business operations after a catastrophic event.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the distinct purposes of backup, archiving, and DR is fundamental for building a comprehensive cloud data protection strategy.
