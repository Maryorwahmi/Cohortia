Optimizing Cloud Backup Strategies

Critical factors for effective and reliable data restoration.

Key Backup Considerations
Frequency

How often backups are taken; impacts RPO.

Retention

How long backups are kept; compliance & cost.

Storage Location

Where backups are stored; for resilience.

Testing

Regularly verify restoration capability.

Common Mistake

Assuming backups work without ever performing a full restore test. Untested backups are unreliable.
