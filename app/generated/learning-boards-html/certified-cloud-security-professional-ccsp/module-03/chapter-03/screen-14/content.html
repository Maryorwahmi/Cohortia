Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from Data Encryption in the Cloud.

Key Management Service (KMS)

A managed service for creating, storing, and managing cryptographic keys in the cloud.

Hardware Security Module (HSM)

A physical device that safeguards and manages digital keys and performs cryptographic functions.

Homomorphic Encryption (HE)

An encryption method allowing computations on encrypted data without decrypting it.

Summary Takeaway

KMS and HSMs are critical for secure key management, while HE represents the future of privacy-preserving computation in the cloud.
