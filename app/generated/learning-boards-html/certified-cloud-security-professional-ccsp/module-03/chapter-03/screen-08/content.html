Key Management

Key Management Services (KMS)

The centralized backbone for managing cryptographic keys in the cloud.

What is KMS?

  • Managed service by CSPs for cryptographic key management.
  • Allows customers to create, store, and manage keys securely.
  • Provides a secure, centralized, and auditable key management solution.
  • Essential for maintaining control over encrypted data.
Misconception

KMS is not just for storing keys; it manages their entire lifecycle and access policies.

KMS Backed by HSMs

Hardware Security Modules (HSM)

Physical devices designed to protect cryptographic keys and operations from tampering and unauthorized access.

Benefits

HSMs provide a high level of assurance for key protection, often meeting stringent compliance standards like FIPS 140-2 Level 3.

Key Takeaway

KMS, backed by HSMs, offers a critical layer of security by centralizing and protecting the management of your encryption keys.
