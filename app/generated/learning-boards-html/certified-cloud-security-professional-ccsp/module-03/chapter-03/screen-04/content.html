Core Concept

The Multi-Layered Approach to Cloud Encryption

Protecting data across its entire lifecycle: at rest, in transit, and in use.

Why Multi-Layered?

  • Different data states have unique vulnerabilities.
  • A single encryption method is insufficient for comprehensive protection.
  • Ensures confidentiality and integrity throughout the data lifecycle.
  • Meets diverse compliance and regulatory requirements.
Key Takeaway

A robust cloud security strategy mandates distinct encryption methods for data at rest, in transit, and in use to ensure continuous protection.

The Three States of Data

Data at Rest

Stored in databases, storage buckets, persistent volumes.

Data in Transit

Moving between clients and services, or between services.

Data in Use

Actively processed by an application in memory or CPU.

Common Misconception

Relying on a single encryption method for all data states leaves significant security gaps.
