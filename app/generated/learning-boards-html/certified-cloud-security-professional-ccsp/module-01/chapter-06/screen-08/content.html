Data Residency and Data Sovereignty

Legal and Regulatory Requirements for Cloud Data

Data Residency

  • Physical or geographic location where data is stored.
  • Often mandated by regulations (e.g., financial data in specific countries).
  • Requires selecting cloud regions that meet these geographical requirements.

Data Sovereignty

  • Data is subject to the laws and governance of the country where it is stored.
  • Impacts how data can be accessed, processed, or transferred across borders.
  • Requires understanding local legal frameworks and provider guarantees.
Key Takeaway

Both residency and sovereignty are crucial for compliance, dictating data storage location and legal jurisdiction, requiring careful cloud region selection.

Compliance Considerations

GDPR (Europe): Strict data protection and residency rules.

CCPA (California): Consumer privacy and data handling rules.

Industry-specific regulations (e.g., HIPAA for healthcare).
