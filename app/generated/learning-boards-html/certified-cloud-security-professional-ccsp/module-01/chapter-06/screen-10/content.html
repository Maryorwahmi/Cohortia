Cloud Supply Chain Risks

Securing Your Ecosystem of Vendors and Services

The Cloud Ecosystem

  • Primary Cloud Provider (IaaS, PaaS)
  • Third-party Software Vendors (SaaS, marketplace images)
  • Managed Service Providers (MSPs)
  • Open-source components and libraries

Risks in the Chain

  • Vulnerabilities in third-party software or images.
  • Compromise of a vendor leading to impact on your services.
  • Lack of transparency in vendor security practices.
  • Data exposure through third-party integrations.
Key Takeaway

Robust vendor management, including security assessments and continuous monitoring, is crucial for mitigating cloud supply chain risks.
