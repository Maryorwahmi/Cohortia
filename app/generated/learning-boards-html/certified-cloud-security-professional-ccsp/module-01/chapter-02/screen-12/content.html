Trade-offs: Control vs. Convenience

Balancing Flexibility, Overhead, and Security

The Control-Convenience Spectrum

IaaS

Maximum Control: Full management of OS, applications, and network. Highest flexibility.

Highest Overhead: Requires significant customer security responsibility.

PaaS

Balanced Control: Manages platform, customer controls application code. Good flexibility.

Moderate Overhead: Focus on application security and platform configuration.

SaaS

Minimal Control: Provider manages entire stack. Highest convenience.

Lowest Overhead: Focus on user access, data governance, and vendor vetting.

As you move from IaaS to SaaS, convenience increases, but control over the underlying infrastructure decreases.
Key Takeaway

The choice of cloud service model is a strategic decision balancing control, convenience, and security overhead, directly impacting risk management and compliance.

Compliance Challenge

Specific compliance requirements (e.g., OS-level logging) may be easy in IaaS but difficult or impossible in SaaS, requiring alternative controls or vendor selection.

Risk Management

Understanding these trade-offs is vital for assessing and managing overall cloud security risk, ensuring alignment with organizational security policies.
