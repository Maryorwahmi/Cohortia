Key Takeaway

The shared responsibility model is dynamic; as you move from IaaS to SaaS, more responsibility shifts to the cloud provider, but the customer always retains responsibility for their data and access management.

Customer's Constant Role

Regardless of the service model, the customer is always responsible for their data, including its classification, encryption, and access controls, as well as identity management.

Security OF vs. IN the Cloud

Provider is responsible for security OF the cloud (infrastructure), while the customer is responsible for security IN the cloud (data, applications, OS, etc., depending on model).
