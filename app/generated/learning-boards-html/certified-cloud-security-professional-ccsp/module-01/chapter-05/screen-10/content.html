Data Security: Always Your Priority

Understanding your role in protecting data across all cloud models.

Core Data Responsibilities

These duties are consistently yours, regardless of the cloud service model:

  • Data Classification: Identifying sensitivity and regulatory needs.
  • Encryption: At rest and in transit.
  • Data Loss Prevention (DLP): Policies and tools to prevent unauthorized data egress.
  • Data Backups: Ensuring recoverability and business continuity.
  • Data Residency: Meeting geographic storage requirements.
Why it Matters

Data breaches are costly and damaging. Proactive data security is paramount for compliance and trust.

Identity and Access Management (IAM)

Managing who can access what, and under what conditions:

  • Identity Management: Creating and managing user and service identities.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Assigning permissions based on job functions.
  • Least Privilege: Granting only necessary access.
  • Strong Authentication: Implementing Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Regular Access Reviews: Auditing and revoking unnecessary permissions.
Key Takeaway

Data and IAM are consistently customer-managed, requiring diligent configuration and ongoing oversight.
