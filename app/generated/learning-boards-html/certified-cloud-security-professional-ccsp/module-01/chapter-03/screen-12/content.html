Challenges in Community Clouds

Clear Responsibility
Consistent Policies
Managing Conflicts
High Trust Required

Key Governance Aspects

  • •Service Level Agreements (SLAs): Define performance and availability.
  • •Shared Security Policies: Agreed-upon security standards.
  • •Data Sharing Protocols: Rules for data access and usage.
  • •Dispute Resolution: Mechanisms for addressing conflicts.
Potential Risk

A lack of clear governance or a breakdown in trust among members can lead to security vulnerabilities, data breaches, and operational inefficiencies within the community cloud.

Key Takeaway

Effective community clouds require robust governance, clear responsibilities, and a high degree of trust among participating organizations to ensure security and collaboration.
