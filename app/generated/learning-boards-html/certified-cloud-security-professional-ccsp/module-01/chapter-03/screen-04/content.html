What are Deployment Models?

Cloud deployment models define the **ownership, scale, and access** of cloud infrastructure. They dictate how cloud resources are organized and delivered to users.

  • •Influence security architecture and control.
  • •Impact compliance posture and regulatory adherence.
  • •Shape operational management and cost structures.
Concrete Example

Imagine choosing between owning a house (private), renting an apartment (public), or a mix of both (hybrid). Each choice has different responsibilities and benefits.

Key Takeaway

Deployment models are fundamental strategic choices that dictate how cloud resources are managed and secured, impacting every aspect of cloud adoption.
