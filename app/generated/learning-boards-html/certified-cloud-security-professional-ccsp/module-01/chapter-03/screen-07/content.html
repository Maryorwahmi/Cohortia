What is a Private Cloud?

A private cloud is cloud infrastructure operated exclusively for a single organization. Resources are dedicated and not shared with other tenants.

  • •Can be managed internally or by a third party.
  • •Can be hosted on-premises or off-premises.
  • •Offers high degree of control, customization, and security.
Concrete Example

A large bank builds its own cloud infrastructure within its data centers to ensure maximum control over sensitive financial data and meet strict regulatory compliance like PCI DSS.

Key Takeaway

Private clouds provide exclusive control and customization, making them ideal for organizations with stringent security, compliance, or performance demands.
