Strategic Considerations

  • •Data Sensitivity: How critical is the data being processed?
  • •Regulatory Compliance: Specific industry or regional mandates.
  • •Existing Infrastructure: Leverage current investments or start fresh.
  • •Workload Characteristics: Predictable vs. burstable demand.
Misconception

There is no single 'best' cloud deployment model. The ideal choice is always a strategic decision tailored to an organization's unique requirements and risk appetite.

Key Takeaway

Choosing a deployment model requires balancing security, cost, control, and agility, often leading to multi-model solutions for diverse organizational needs.
