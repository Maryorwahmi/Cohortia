Core Concept

Cloud Global Infrastructure: Regions & Availability Zones

Understanding the physical foundation of cloud services

Key Components

  • Region: A distinct geographical area hosting cloud services.
  • Availability Zone (AZ): Isolated data centers within a region, designed for fault tolerance.
  • Isolation: AZs are physically separate to prevent single points of failure.
  • Resilience: Deploying across multiple AZs enhances application availability.
Key Takeaway

Regions and Availability Zones are fundamental for high availability and meeting data residency requirements in cloud deployments.
