Unique Security Risks of Cloud Adoption

While beneficial, cloud environments introduce specific security challenges that require careful management.

Data Residency & Sovereignty

Data stored across multiple geographies complicates compliance with local laws.

Multi-tenancy & Isolation

Shared infrastructure raises concerns about resource separation from other customers.

Identity & Access Management

Access to resources from anywhere makes robust IAM paramount.

Visibility & Auditing

Scale and complexity can make monitoring and detecting misconfigurations challenging.

Key Takeaway

Cloud's distributed and shared nature introduces new attack vectors and compliance complexities that demand specialized security controls.
