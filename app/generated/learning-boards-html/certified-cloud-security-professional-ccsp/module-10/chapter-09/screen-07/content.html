Definition

Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)

KRIs provide early warnings of increasing risk exposure, answering the question: "Are we becoming more vulnerable?"

Focus: Proactive identification of potential threats and vulnerabilities.

Purpose: To trigger preventative actions and risk mitigation strategies before incidents occur.

Examples of Core KRIs

Number of Publicly Exposed S3 Buckets

A rising number indicates increasing data exposure risk. (Signals misconfiguration risk)

Unused IAM Roles/Permissions

High count suggests potential for privilege escalation or unauthorized access. (Signals identity risk)

Key Takeaway

KRIs are crucial for proactive risk management, allowing you to detect and address potential issues before they become incidents.
