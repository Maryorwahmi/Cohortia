Reporting for Technical Teams

Granular, Real-time Data for Operational Efficiency

Audience Needs

Focus on Actionability

Technical teams (e.g., Cloud Engineers, SecOps) require detailed, real-time data to troubleshoot, respond to incidents, and maintain cloud security operations.

  • •Detailed logs and alerts
  • •Specific resource IDs and configurations
  • •Vulnerability reports and performance metrics
Recommended Tools & Visuals

Operational Dashboards

Tools and visualizations should support immediate action and deep dives:

  • •Grafana, Kibana, or cloud-native dashboards (e.g., CloudWatch Dashboards)
  • •Real-time graphs, alert feeds, and detailed tables
  • •Focus on operational efficiency and immediate response capabilities
Key Takeaway

Provide technical teams with granular, real-time data and actionable insights to support their operational security responsibilities.
