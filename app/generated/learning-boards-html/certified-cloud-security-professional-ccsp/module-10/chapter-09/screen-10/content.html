Analyzing and Interpreting Security Metrics

Transforming Raw Data into Actionable Insights

Step 3: Normalization & Correlation

Unifying Disparate Data

Raw data must be normalized and correlated across various cloud services and on-premises systems to create a unified view. SIEM and SOAR platforms are critical for this.

Example: Linking a failed login event from an identity provider to a network access log from a cloud firewall.

Step 4: Analysis & Interpretation

Understanding Context and Trends

Go beyond raw numbers to understand the context, trends, and implications of the metrics. A high 'failed login attempts' KRI, for instance, requires contextual understanding.

Benchmarking: Compare your metrics against industry benchmarks or internal historical data to gauge performance.

Key Takeaway

Effective analysis requires normalizing data, understanding its context, and benchmarking against relevant standards to derive true insights.
