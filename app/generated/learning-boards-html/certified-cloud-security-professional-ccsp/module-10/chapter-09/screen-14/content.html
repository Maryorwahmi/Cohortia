Avoiding Common Cloud Security Reporting Mistakes

Ensuring Metrics Provide Real Value and Clarity

"Vanity Metrics"

Reporting on metrics that look good but don't provide real insight into security posture or risk (e.g., just counting tools).

Information Overload

Providing too much raw data without analysis or context, overwhelming the audience and obscuring key insights.

Lack of Alignment

Metrics not aligning with business objectives or the organization's risk appetite, making them irrelevant to decision-makers.

Inconsistent Reporting

Using different metrics or reporting formats for different audiences or over time, leading to confusion and distrust.

Ignoring Trends

Focusing only on current numbers without showing historical trends, which can hide deteriorating or improving security posture.

Key Takeaway

Awareness of common reporting mistakes is crucial to ensure your cloud security metrics provide genuine value and actionable insights.
