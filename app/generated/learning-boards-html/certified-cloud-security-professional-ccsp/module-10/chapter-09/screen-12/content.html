Reporting for Management and Executive Leadership

Aggregated Trends and Strategic Business Impact

Management Needs (CISO, Dept. Heads)

Overall Posture & Progress

Management needs aggregated, trend-based information to understand overall security posture, resource allocation, and progress against objectives.

  • •KPIs and KRIs with trends over time
  • •Compliance status and initiative effectiveness
  • •Executive summaries and traffic light indicators (R/A/G)
Executive Needs (CEO, Board)

Strategic Risk & Business Impact

Executive leadership requires high-level, strategic insights into overall risk exposure, compliance, and the business impact of security.

  • •Concise, non-technical reports focusing on "so what?"
  • •Financial and reputational implications of risks
  • •Strategic value of security investments against risk appetite
Key Takeaway

Tailor reports to each audience, providing granular operational data for technical teams and high-level strategic insights for leadership.
