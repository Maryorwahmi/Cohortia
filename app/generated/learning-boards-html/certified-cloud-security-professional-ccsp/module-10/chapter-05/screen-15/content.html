Key Limitations to Understand

Limited Scope

Reports only cover the CSP's responsibilities ("security *of* the cloud"), not the customer's configurations or applications ("security *in* the cloud").

Point in Time

Even Type 2 reports, covering a period, are snapshots. The CSP's environment might change between audit cycles.

Proprietary Information

Full reports are often under NDA and may not be publicly accessible, requiring a formal request from the CSP.

Interpretation Required

Customers must interpret how CSP controls contribute to their own compliance and identify any gaps.

Key Takeaway

Never assume CSP reports guarantee your compliance; always integrate them into your own due diligence and audit efforts.
