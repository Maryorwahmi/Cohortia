Key Steps in Audit Preparation

01

Define Scope and Objectives

Clearly understand what the audit will cover (e.g., specific cloud services, applications, data types) and the criteria against which it will be assessed (e.g., PCI DSS, internal policy).

Why it matters

A well-defined scope prevents scope creep and ensures audit efforts are focused on critical areas, saving time and resources.

02

Identify Stakeholders

Determine who needs to be involved, including cloud security team, IT operations, legal, compliance, and business owners.

Pro Tip

Create a RACI matrix (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) to clarify roles and responsibilities for each audit phase.
