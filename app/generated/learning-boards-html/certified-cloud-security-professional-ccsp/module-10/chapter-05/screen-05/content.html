Module 10›Chapter 10.5›Internal vs. External Audits

Internal Audits

Conducted By

Organization's own internal audit team or designated personnel.

Purpose

Provide management with independent assessment of internal controls, identify weaknesses, and ensure compliance with internal policies.

Example

An internal team reviews cloud resource tagging and cost allocation to ensure adherence to internal governance policies.

External Audits

Conducted By

Independent third-party auditors.

Purpose

Required for regulatory compliance (e.g., PCI DSS, HIPAA) or for demonstrating assurance to customers and stakeholders (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001).

Key Takeaway

Internal audits strengthen internal controls; external audits provide credible assurance to outside parties.
