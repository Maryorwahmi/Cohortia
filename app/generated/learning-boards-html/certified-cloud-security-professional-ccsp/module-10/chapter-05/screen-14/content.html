Common CSP Audit Reports

SOC 2 Type 2 Report

Provides detailed information and assurance about a CSP's controls relevant to security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy over a period.

ISO/IEC 27001 Certification

Demonstrates that a CSP has implemented an Information Security Management System (ISMS) meeting the requirements of the ISO 27001 standard.

PCI DSS Attestation of Compliance (AOC)

For CSPs handling payment card data, this report confirms their adherence to PCI DSS requirements.

FedRAMP Authorization

For CSPs providing services to U.S. federal agencies, indicating compliance with government security requirements.

Key Takeaway

CSP audit reports are essential for understanding the security posture of the cloud provider, but they do not cover customer responsibilities.
