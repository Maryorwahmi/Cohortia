Essential Preparation Steps

03

Gather Documentation

Collect all relevant policies, procedures, architectural diagrams, shared responsibility matrix, data flow diagrams, and previous audit reports.

Why it matters

Comprehensive documentation provides auditors with a clear understanding of your controls, streamlining the audit process.

05

Pre-Audit Self-Assessment

Conduct an internal review or mock audit to identify and address potential gaps before the official audit.

Pro Tip

Use automated compliance tools to regularly scan your cloud environment for misconfigurations and policy violations.
