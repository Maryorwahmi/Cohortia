Ongoing Security

Continuous Monitoring of Vendor Security

Security posture evolves, so should your oversight

01

Regular Reviews

Schedule periodic reviews of vendor security, typically annually or semi-annually, based on criticality. This involves re-requesting updated audit reports, certifications, and security questionnaires to ensure ongoing compliance and security effectiveness.

  • Annual or semi-annual assessments.
  • Request updated audit reports.
  • Verify continued compliance.
02

Threat Intelligence Integration

Integrate vendor information into your threat intelligence program. Monitor news, dark web forums, and security advisories for any reported vulnerabilities or breaches affecting your CSPs. This proactive approach helps anticipate and respond to emerging threats.

  • Monitor public security advisories.
  • Track vendor-specific vulnerabilities.
  • Proactive risk identification.

Dynamic Security

A vendor's security posture is not static. New threats emerge, and internal changes occur. Continuous monitoring ensures that your risk assessment remains current and effective over the entire lifecycle of the vendor relationship.

Key Takeaway

Continuous monitoring, through regular reviews and threat intelligence, is essential to adapt to evolving vendor security postures and emerging threats.
