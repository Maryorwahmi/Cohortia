Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Vendor Security

Vendor Management

The systematic process of identifying, assessing, mitigating, and monitoring risks associated with third-party providers.

Third-Party Risk

The risk introduced to an organization by its reliance on external vendors, including Cloud Service Providers (CSPs).

Due Diligence

A comprehensive investigation of a vendor's security, financial stability, and operational capabilities before engagement.

CSA CAIQ

A standardized questionnaire from the Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) to assess a cloud provider's security controls.
