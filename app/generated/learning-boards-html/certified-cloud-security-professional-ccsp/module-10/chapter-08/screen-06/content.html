Due Diligence Process

Phase 1: Defining Requirements & Initial Vetting

Laying the groundwork for secure partnerships

01

Requirement Definition

Clearly define your organization's security, compliance (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS), performance, and business continuity requirements. This step ensures that all subsequent evaluations are aligned with your specific needs and risk appetite.

  • Specify data sensitivity and criticality.
  • Outline regulatory and industry standards.
  • Define performance and availability SLAs.
02

Initial Vetting

Conduct an initial review of potential vendors to assess their reputation, financial stability, and basic security posture. This helps in quickly identifying and filtering out unsuitable candidates before deeper engagement.

  • Check public reputation and news.
  • Review financial health indicators.
  • Examine publicly available security certifications and privacy policies.

Tailored Assessment

The due diligence process should be customized based on the criticality and sensitivity of the data or services being outsourced. A one-size-fits-all approach is insufficient for effective risk mitigation.

Key Takeaway

A strong due diligence process begins with clearly defined requirements and initial vetting to filter potential vendors effectively.
