Due Diligence Process

Phase 3: Contractual Safeguards & Validation

Legal and technical verification before commitment

01

Contractual Review

Involve legal and security teams to scrutinize the Cloud Service Agreement (CSA), Service Level Agreement (SLA), and Data Processing Agreement (DPA). Ensure critical clauses on data ownership, incident response, audit rights, and data deletion are adequate.

  • Verify data ownership and privacy.
  • Define incident response and notification.
  • Secure audit rights and data deletion.
02

Proof of Concept (PoC) / Pilot

For complex services, conduct a PoC or pilot to validate technical capabilities and security controls in a controlled environment. This practical step verifies that the vendor's claims hold true in a real-world scenario.

  • Validate technical capabilities.
  • Test security controls in practice.
  • Reduce deployment risks.
03

Risk Acceptance/Mitigation

Based on the assessment, identify residual risks and determine if they are within the organization's risk appetite. If not, negotiate additional controls or consider alternative vendors to bring the risk to an acceptable level.

  • Identify and quantify residual risks.
  • Align with organizational risk appetite.
  • Negotiate controls or seek alternatives.
Key Takeaway

Comprehensive due diligence concludes with robust contractual safeguards, practical validation through PoCs, and clear risk acceptance or mitigation strategies.
