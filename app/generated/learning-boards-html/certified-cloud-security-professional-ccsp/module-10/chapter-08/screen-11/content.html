Ongoing Security

Advanced Vendor Monitoring Techniques

Leveraging tools and contractual rights for oversight

03

Security Ratings Services

Utilize third-party security rating services (e.g., BitSight, SecurityScorecard) that continuously monitor and score vendors' external security posture. These provide early warnings of deteriorating security based on publicly observable data.

  • Objective, real-time security scores.
  • Early warning for deteriorating posture.
  • Based on publicly observable data.
04

Performance Monitoring

Monitor the performance and availability of cloud services as per the SLA. Downtime or performance degradation can often indicate underlying security or operational issues, prompting further investigation.

  • Verify SLA adherence.
  • Detect potential security/operational issues.
  • Proactive issue identification.
05

Change Management & Audit Rights

Require vendors to notify you of significant changes to their security controls or infrastructure. Additionally, exercise contractual audit rights where appropriate, or leverage the ability to request additional documentation for deeper scrutiny.

  • Stay informed of vendor changes.
  • Maintain contractual audit capabilities.
  • Ensure transparency and accountability.
Key Takeaway

Advanced monitoring techniques, including security ratings and contractual rights, provide comprehensive and proactive oversight of vendor security posture.
