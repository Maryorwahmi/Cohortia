Due Diligence Process

Phase 2: In-depth Security Assessment

Evaluating vendor controls and posture

01

Security Questionnaires

Send detailed security questionnaires to assess their controls. These often leverage industry standards like the CSA CAIQ or NIST SP 800-171, providing a structured way to gather information on their security practices.

  • Standardized frameworks (CSA CAIQ).
  • Covers various security domains.
  • Initial self-assessment by vendor.
02

Audit Reports

Request and review third-party audit reports such as SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, or PCI DSS AOC. These reports offer independent verification of a vendor's controls, providing a more objective view of their security posture.

  • Independent verification of controls.
  • Understand scope and limitations.
  • Crucial for high-assurance services.

Verification is Key

Do not rely solely on a vendor's self-attestations. Always seek independent verification through audit reports and certifications to ensure their stated controls are actually effective.

Key Takeaway

A robust security assessment combines detailed questionnaires with independent audit reports to verify a vendor's security posture.
