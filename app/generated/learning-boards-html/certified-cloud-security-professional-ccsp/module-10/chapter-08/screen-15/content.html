What to Remember

Critical Takeaways for Cloud Security Professionals

Security Ratings Services

Third-party services that continuously monitor and score vendors' external security posture based on publicly observable data.

Cloud Exit Strategy

A pre-planned approach for migrating data and applications away from a cloud service provider.

Data Portability

The ability to easily move data from one cloud provider to another, or back on-premises, in a usable format.

Proactive Risk Management

A continuous and strategic approach to identifying, assessing, mitigating, and monitoring risks from third-party vendors.

Chapter Summary

Effective vendor management, continuous monitoring, and robust exit strategies are paramount for mitigating third-party cloud risks and ensuring organizational security and compliance.
