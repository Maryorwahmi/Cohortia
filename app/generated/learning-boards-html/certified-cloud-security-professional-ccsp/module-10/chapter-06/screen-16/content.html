Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential ideas to remember

Risk Categorization

Grouping similar risks together (e.g., strategic, operational, compliance, financial, security, vendor) for easier management and reporting.

Holistic Approach

Managing cloud risks as an integral part of the overall enterprise risk landscape, rather than in isolation.

Continuous Monitoring

Regularly tracking new threats, changes in cloud architecture, and evolving regulatory requirements to keep ERM relevant.

Summary Takeaway

Effective cloud risk management relies on proper categorization and a holistic approach, ensuring continuous monitoring and integration into the broader enterprise risk landscape for comprehensive protection.
