Cloud Risk Categories

Strategic and Operational Cloud Risks

Understanding business alignment and daily operations

Strategic Risks

  • Business Alignment: Cloud adoption not aligning with overall business strategy.

  • Vendor Lock-in: Dependence on a single cloud provider, limiting flexibility and increasing exit costs.

  • Benefit Realization: Failure to achieve expected benefits from cloud investments.

Example

A company's long-term strategy is to be multi-cloud, but a specific project commits to a single vendor for a critical workload, creating a strategic vendor lock-in risk.

Operational Risks

  • Misconfigurations: Errors in setting up cloud services, leading to vulnerabilities or data exposure.

  • Service Outages: Disruptions to cloud service availability, impacting business continuity.

  • Inadequate Incident Response: Failure to effectively respond to security incidents in cloud environments.

Key Takeaway

Strategic risks impact long-term business goals and flexibility, while operational risks affect the daily functioning and security of cloud services.
