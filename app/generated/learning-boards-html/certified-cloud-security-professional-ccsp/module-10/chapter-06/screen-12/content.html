Integration Strategies

Cross-Functional Collaboration & Centralized Reporting

Fostering shared understanding and executive visibility

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Communication Channels: Establish regular meetings and working groups among diverse teams.

  • Multiple Perspectives: Ensure cloud risks are understood from technical, business, legal, and compliance viewpoints.

  • Aligned Mitigation: Coordinate mitigation strategies with overall business objectives.

Example

Cloud security teams collaborate with legal to assess data residency risks for a new cloud service, ensuring compliance and technical controls are aligned.

Centralized Risk Reporting

  • Enterprise-Level Feed: Cloud risk reports must integrate into overall ERM reporting mechanisms.

  • Consolidated View: Dashboards should show how cloud risks contribute to the overall enterprise risk profile.

Key Takeaway

Cross-functional collaboration ensures comprehensive understanding of cloud risks, while centralized reporting provides executive leadership with a unified and actionable view of the organization's risk landscape.
