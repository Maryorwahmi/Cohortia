Cloud Risk Categories

Compliance and Financial Cloud Risks

Navigating regulations and managing costs

Compliance/Regulatory Risks

  • Legal Obligations: Failure to meet legal, regulatory (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA), or contractual obligations.

  • Data Residency: Non-compliance with data sovereignty requirements for sensitive data storage.

Example

Storing customer data in a cloud region that does not comply with local data protection laws (e.g., GDPR) can lead to significant fines and legal repercussions.

Financial Risks

  • Unexpected Costs: Inefficient resource utilization, over-provisioning, or lack of cost optimization leading to budget overruns.

  • Breach Impact: Financial consequences of security breaches, including fines, legal fees, and lost revenue.

Key Takeaway

Compliance risks stem from regulatory non-adherence, while financial risks involve direct monetary impacts from cloud operations or security incidents.
