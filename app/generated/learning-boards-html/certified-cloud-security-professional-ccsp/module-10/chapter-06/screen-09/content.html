Cloud Risk Categories

Security and Vendor/Third-Party Cloud Risks

Protecting data and managing external dependencies

Security Risks

  • Data Breaches: Unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction of sensitive data.

  • Cyber Threats: Malware, denial-of-service attacks, or other cyber threats specific to cloud environments.

Example

A misconfigured firewall rule in a cloud network allows unauthorized access to a database, leading to a data breach and potential loss of customer trust.

Vendor/Third-Party Risks

  • Provider Security: Risks from the cloud service provider's security posture, financial stability, and operational practices.

  • Supply Chain: Vulnerabilities introduced by third-party components or services used by the cloud provider or your organization.

Key Takeaway

Security risks focus on direct cyber threats, while vendor risks highlight the critical dependencies and potential vulnerabilities introduced by external cloud providers and third parties.
