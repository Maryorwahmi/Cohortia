Risk Documentation

Documenting Cloud Risks: The Cloud Risk Register

A specialized tool for consistent risk management

Cloud Risk Register Elements

  • Description: Clear explanation of the cloud risk.

  • Impact & Likelihood: Potential consequences and probability of occurrence.

  • Existing Controls: Mitigation strategies already in place.

  • Residual Risk: The level of risk remaining after controls are applied.

Example Scenario

A 'misconfigured S3 bucket' risk is documented, noting its high impact if sensitive data is publicly exposed. Existing controls like 'least privilege access' and 'regular audits' are listed, leading to a calculated residual risk level.

Key Takeaway

The Cloud Risk Register is a vital tool for systematically documenting, assessing, and tracking cloud-specific risks, ensuring consistent management and alignment with enterprise risk objectives.
