Integrating Cloud Risks into Enterprise Risk Management

A Holistic Approach to Cloud Security and Business Resilience

This chapter will guide you through understanding Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) and its crucial role in cloud computing. You'll learn how to identify, categorize, and integrate cloud-specific risks into your organization's existing ERM framework, fostering better decision-making and enhancing overall resilience.

Key Idea

ERM provides a comprehensive, organization-wide approach to identifying, assessing, and managing all risks that could affect strategic objectives, moving beyond siloed views.
