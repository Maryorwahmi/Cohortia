Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential ideas to remember

Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)

A comprehensive, organization-wide approach to identifying, assessing, and managing all risks that could affect strategic objectives.

Risk Appetite

The amount and type of risk that an organization is willing to take in pursuit of its strategic objectives.

Cloud Risk Register

A specialized component of an enterprise risk register, detailing cloud-specific threats, vulnerabilities, and associated risks.

Summary Takeaway

ERM provides a holistic framework for managing all organizational risks, including cloud-specific ones, guided by the organization's risk appetite and documented through tools like the Cloud Risk Register.
