Integration Strategies

Standardized Methodology & Harmonized Risk Appetite

Ensuring consistency in cloud risk assessment

Standardized Risk Assessment Methodology

  • Consistency: Cloud risk assessment must align with the organization's overall ERM methodology.

  • Common Scales: Use identical scales for likelihood and impact across all risk types.

  • Cloud Risk Register: Utilize specialized tools to feed cloud-specific data into the enterprise register.

Common Mistake

Treating cloud risks as an exception and applying different assessment standards can lead to an inconsistent and ineffective overall risk posture.

Harmonized Risk Appetite

  • Enterprise-Wide View: Cloud risks must be evaluated against the organization's overall risk appetite.

  • Boundary Adherence: Ensure cloud deployments remain within defined risk tolerance levels.

Key Takeaway

Consistent methodology and a harmonized risk appetite are foundational for integrating cloud risks, ensuring that assessments are comparable and aligned with overall enterprise risk tolerance.
