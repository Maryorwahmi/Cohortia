The Continuous Journey of a Cloud Security Policy

Understanding the iterative stages from initiation to ongoing review and updates.

1. Initiation & Planning

Identify need, define scope, identify stakeholders.

2. Development & Drafting

Draft content based on best practices, regulations, and risk profile.

3. Review & Approval

Stakeholder review for accuracy, completeness, and alignment; formal approval.

4. Dissemination & Communication

Communicate policies effectively through training and accessible portals.

5. Implementation & Enforcement

Translate policy into technical controls and operational procedures; use Policy as Code.

6. Monitoring & Audit

Continuously monitor for compliance through logging and reviews.

7. Review & Update

Periodically review and update policies to remain relevant and effective.
