Key Pillar

Pillar 2: Risk Management

Identifying, assessing, and mitigating cloud-specific risks

Core Activities

  • Identifying cloud-specific threats and vulnerabilities.
  • Assessing the likelihood and impact of potential risks.
  • Implementing controls to mitigate identified risks.
  • Continuously monitoring the cloud risk posture.

Types of Cloud Risks

Technical Vulnerabilities
Supply Chain Risks
Regulatory Risks
Operational Risks

Why it's Critical

  • Protects sensitive data and intellectual property.
  • Ensures business continuity and resilience.
  • Supports informed decision-making regarding cloud adoption.
Key Takeaway

Proactive cloud risk management is crucial for protecting assets and maintaining business continuity in dynamic cloud environments.

Common Pitfall

Assuming the cloud provider is solely responsible for all risks, neglecting the customer's role in the shared responsibility model.
