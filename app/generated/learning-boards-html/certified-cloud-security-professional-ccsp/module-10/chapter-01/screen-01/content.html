Introduction to Cloud Governance

Establishing Secure, Compliant, and Efficient Cloud Operations

As organizations increasingly migrate their operations to the cloud, traditional IT governance models often prove insufficient. Cloud governance provides a robust framework of policies, processes, and controls to ensure secure, compliant, and efficient use of cloud resources, aligning cloud initiatives with overall business objectives and risk appetite.

Key Takeaway

Effective cloud governance is essential to prevent security vulnerabilities, compliance failures, spiraling costs, and operational inefficiencies in cloud adoption.
