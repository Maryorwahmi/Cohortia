Common Pitfalls in Cloud Governance

Learning from mistakes to build a stronger framework

Pitfall 1: On-Premises Mindset

Applying outdated policies and controls designed for traditional data centers to dynamic cloud environments.

Pitfall 2: Lack of Stakeholder Involvement

Failing to involve IT, security, legal, finance, and business units, leading to ignored or ineffective policies.

Pitfall 3: Unclear Roles & Responsibilities

Not clearly defining duties within the shared responsibility model, creating security gaps.

Pitfall 4: Neglecting Automation

Relying on manual processes for governance in a rapidly changing cloud landscape, making efforts cumbersome.

Key Takeaway

Proactively addressing these common pitfalls by adopting a cloud-native, collaborative, and automated approach is crucial for effective cloud governance.
