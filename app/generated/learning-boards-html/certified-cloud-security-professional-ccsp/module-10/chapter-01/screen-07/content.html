Key Pillar

Pillar 3: Compliance Management

Ensuring adherence to legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations

Core Requirements

  • Adherence to legal frameworks (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).
  • Compliance with industry regulations (e.g., HIPAA, PCI DSS).
  • Meeting contractual obligations with partners and customers.
  • Maintaining robust audit capabilities for verification.

Key Activities

Continuous Monitoring
Regular Audits
Policy Enforcement
Reporting & Documentation

Why it's Critical

  • Avoids legal penalties and fines.
  • Protects organizational reputation and customer trust.
  • Ensures data privacy and security standards are met.
Key Takeaway

Effective compliance management requires continuous monitoring and robust audit capabilities to demonstrate adherence to all relevant obligations.

Common Pitfall

Treating compliance as a one-time checklist rather than an integrated, ongoing part of cloud operations.
