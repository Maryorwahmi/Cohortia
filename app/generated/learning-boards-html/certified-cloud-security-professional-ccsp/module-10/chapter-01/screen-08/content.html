Key Pillar

Pillar 4: Architecture & Design Governance

Building secure, resilient, and scalable cloud solutions from the outset

Core Principles

  • Security by Design: Integrating security from the initial planning stages.
  • Resilience: Architecting for high availability and disaster recovery.
  • Scalability: Designing solutions that can grow with demand.
  • Adherence to Enterprise Architecture: Aligning with organizational standards.

Key Activities

Threat Modeling
Security Reviews
Compliance Checks
Reference Architectures

Why it's Critical

  • Prevents costly reworks and security vulnerabilities.
  • Ensures solutions are inherently secure and compliant.
  • Supports long-term operational stability and efficiency.
Key Takeaway

Robust architecture and design governance ensures cloud solutions are inherently secure, resilient, and compliant from their inception.

Common Misconception

Believing security can be 'bolted on' later, rather than integrated into the initial design phase.
