Key Concepts

Essential Regulations and Data Principles

GDPR

EU law on data protection and privacy for individuals within the EU and EEA, with global impact.

HIPAA

U.S. law protecting the privacy and security of protected health information (PHI), requiring BAAs.

PCI DSS

Global standard for organizations handling branded credit cards, a contractual security obligation.

Summary Takeaway

These regulations are foundational for cloud compliance, each demanding specific controls and contractual agreements to protect sensitive data.
