Navigating Cross-Border Data Transfers

Ensuring Compliant Data Movement Across Jurisdictions

Key Transfer Mechanisms (e.g., GDPR)

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

Pre-approved contract templates for data transfers.

Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)

Internal codes of conduct for multinational groups.

Adequacy Decisions

EU Commission deems a country's protection adequate.

Derogations

Specific exceptions for certain transfers (e.g., explicit consent).

Key Takeaway

Cloud architectures must be designed to manage cross-border transfers compliantly, often by selecting specific cloud regions or implementing robust encryption.

Cloud Architecture for Compliance

  • •Region Selection: Choose cloud regions that align with data residency and transfer requirements.
  • •Data Localization: Configure services to keep data within specific geographical boundaries.
  • •Encryption & Anonymization: Implement strong encryption for data at rest and in transit, and anonymize data where possible.
  • •Data Flow Mapping: Understand and document all data flows to identify potential cross-border transfers.

Example Scenario

A European company uses a global CRM system hosted in the US. To transfer customer data from Europe to the US, they must implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with their cloud provider. They also encrypt the data before transfer and ensure the US cloud region has adequate security controls to protect the data.
