HIPAA: Securing Protected Health Information

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is a U.S. law protecting the privacy and security of Protected Health Information (PHI).

  • •Applies to 'Covered Entities' (healthcare providers, plans) and 'Business Associates' (CSPs handling PHI).
  • •Mandates specific administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for PHI.
  • •Requires a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with CSPs that process PHI.
Key Takeaway

Cloud customers must ensure CSPs sign a BAA and implement robust controls like encryption and access management to protect PHI in the cloud.

Key HIPAA Safeguards

  • •Administrative: Security management processes, workforce training, contingency plans.
  • •Physical: Facility access controls, workstation security, device and media controls.
  • •Technical: Access controls, audit controls, integrity controls, transmission security (e.g., encryption).

Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

A legally required contract between a HIPAA covered entity and a business associate (like a CSP) that handles PHI. It outlines how the business associate will protect PHI and comply with HIPAA rules.

Why it matters:

Ensures shared accountability and defines specific security obligations for PHI in the cloud, preventing legal exposure for both parties.
