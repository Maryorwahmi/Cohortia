Key Concepts

Data Governance and Compliance Frameworks

Data Residency

The physical location where data is stored, often mandated by regulations.

Data Sovereignty

Data is subject to the laws of the country in which it is stored, regardless of ownership.

Cross-border Data Transfer

Moving data across national borders, subject to strict regulatory requirements (e.g., SCCs).

Summary Takeaway

Understanding these data governance concepts is fundamental for designing compliant cloud architectures and mitigating legal risks.
