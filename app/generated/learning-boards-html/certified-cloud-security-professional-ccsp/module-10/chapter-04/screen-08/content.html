SOX and CCPA/CPRA: Financial & Consumer Privacy

Ensuring Accountability and Consumer Rights

Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)

A U.S. federal law mandating practices in financial record keeping and reporting for public companies.

  • •Requires accurate financial reporting and internal controls (ICFR).
  • •Cloud systems handling financial data need robust access controls, change management, and audit trails.
  • •Ensures data integrity and accountability for financial records.
Key Takeaway

Cloud systems supporting financial reporting must have strong controls to demonstrate SOX compliance and prevent fraud.

CCPA / CPRA: California Privacy Laws

U.S. state laws granting California consumers extensive rights regarding their personal information.

  • •Similar to GDPR, includes rights to know, delete, and opt-out of data sale.
  • •Organizations handling California consumer data in the cloud need data mapping and deletion capabilities.
  • •Focuses on 'personal information' and consumer control over data.
Key Takeaway

Cloud deployments must support data mapping, access controls, and deletion capabilities to comply with California's consumer privacy laws.
