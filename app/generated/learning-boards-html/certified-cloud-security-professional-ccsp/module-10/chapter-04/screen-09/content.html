Data Residency vs. Data Sovereignty

Understanding Where Your Data Lives and Whose Laws Apply

Data Residency

Refers to the physical location where data is stored. Many regulations require data to be kept within specific geographical boundaries.

  • •Physical Location: Where data centers are physically situated.
  • •Regulatory Mandates: Laws (e.g., national banking laws, specific GDPR types) requiring in-country storage.
  • •Cloud Customer Action: Verify CSP data center locations and select compliant regions.
Key Takeaway

Ensuring data physically resides in a compliant region is a fundamental step in meeting many regulatory requirements.

Data Sovereignty

Implies that data is subject to the laws of the country in which it is stored, regardless of the data owner's nationality.

  • •Legal Jurisdiction: Data is governed by the laws of the host country.
  • •Government Access: Foreign governments may access data under their national security laws (e.g., CLOUD Act).
  • •Challenge: Protecting sensitive data from foreign legal demands.
Key Takeaway

Data sovereignty highlights the risk of foreign government access to data, necessitating careful consideration of legal frameworks.
