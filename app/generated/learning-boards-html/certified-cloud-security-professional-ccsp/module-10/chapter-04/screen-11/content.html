Strategies for Cloud Compliance (Part 1)

Leveraging Cloud-Native and Third-Party Solutions

Cloud-Native Compliance Tools

Services offered by CSPs to monitor resource configurations against compliance benchmarks, automate remediation, and generate reports.

  • •AWS Config: Evaluates configurations against desired states.
  • •Azure Policy: Enforces organizational standards and assesses compliance at scale.
  • •Google Cloud Security Command Center: Centralized security and risk management platform.
Key Takeaway

Cloud-native tools provide continuous, automated monitoring and reporting, crucial for maintaining an up-to-date compliance posture.

Third-Party Compliance Software

Specialized tools that provide continuous compliance monitoring, risk assessments, and reporting across multi-cloud environments.

  • •Multi-Cloud Visibility: Centralized view of compliance across different CSPs.
  • •Automated Assessments: Continuous scanning for misconfigurations and policy violations.
  • •Customizable Reporting: Generate reports tailored to specific regulatory frameworks.
Key Takeaway

Third-party solutions are vital for consistent compliance management and reporting in complex multi-cloud or hybrid environments.
