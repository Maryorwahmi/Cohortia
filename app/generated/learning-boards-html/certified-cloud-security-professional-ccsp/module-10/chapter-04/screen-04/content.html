The Cloud Compliance Challenge

When data and applications move to the cloud, they become subject to a complex web of legal and regulatory requirements from multiple jurisdictions. This creates significant challenges for organizations.

  • •Data is no longer confined to a single physical location, making its legal jurisdiction ambiguous.
  • •Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) operate globally, introducing their own jurisdictional laws.
  • •Failure to comply can lead to severe penalties, including hefty fines and reputational damage.
Key Takeaway

Cloud compliance demands a proactive approach to understand and manage legal obligations across multiple, potentially conflicting, jurisdictions.

Common Misconception

Many believe CSPs handle all compliance. In reality, the customer retains significant responsibility for 'compliance in the cloud'.

Organization's Home Country Laws

Initial set of regulations governing the business operations.

Cloud Service Provider's Jurisdiction

Laws of the country where the CSP is headquartered or operates.

Data Storage Location Laws

Regulations of the country where data is physically stored or processed.
