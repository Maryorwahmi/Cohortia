Strategies & Shared Responsibility Model

Policy as Code, DLP, and the Compliance Partnership

Policy as Code

Implementing compliance policies through code (e.g., Open Policy Agent, Terraform) ensures consistent and automated enforcement during provisioning.

Benefit:

Automates compliance, reduces human error, and scales across dynamic cloud environments.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Tools to identify, monitor, and protect sensitive data in use, in motion, and at rest within cloud environments.

Benefit:

Prevents unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information, crucial for data privacy regulations.

Audit Logging and Monitoring

Robust logging (e.g., AWS CloudTrail, Azure Monitor) is essential for demonstrating compliance and providing evidence during audits.

Benefit:

Provides an immutable record of activities, critical for forensics and proving adherence to controls.

Introducing: Shared Responsibility Model

A framework defining the security and compliance obligations of the cloud service provider (CSP) and the customer.

Why it matters:

Crucial for understanding who is accountable for what, preventing compliance gaps and misattributions of responsibility.
