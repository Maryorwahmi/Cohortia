Cloud Risk Management Frameworks

Navigating Security in the Dynamic Cloud Landscape

This chapter provides a systematic approach to identifying, assessing, and mitigating cloud-specific risks, ensuring organizations can confidently leverage cloud benefits while minimizing exposure to potential harm.

Key Takeaway

Effective cloud risk management is about understanding your risk posture and making informed decisions, not eliminating all risk.
