Key Stages of ISO 27005 Risk Management

From context establishment to continuous monitoring.

1

Context Establishment

Define the scope, boundaries, and risk criteria for your information security management system (ISMS). This sets the foundation for all subsequent risk activities.

Example: Defining the scope for a specific cloud application migration.
2

Risk Assessment

A three-part process: Identify (discover risks), Analyze (understand nature and level), and Evaluate (compare against criteria to determine acceptability).

Example: Identifying data sovereignty risk, analyzing its impact, and evaluating its acceptability.
3

Risk Treatment

Select and implement appropriate controls to modify risks to an acceptable level. This involves choosing mitigation strategies based on the risk assessment results.

Example: Implementing encryption for data at rest and in transit in the cloud.
4

Risk Acceptance

Formally decide to accept the residual risks that remain after treatment. This decision is typically made by management based on the organization's risk appetite.

Example: Documenting and accepting the low residual risk of a non-critical system.
Key Takeaway

ISO 27005 emphasizes a systematic, iterative process for managing information security risks, ensuring continuous alignment with business objectives and risk criteria.
