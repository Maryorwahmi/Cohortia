Understanding Cloud Risk Management

A systematic approach to securing dynamic cloud environments.

What is Cloud Risk Management?

It's the systematic process of identifying potential threats and vulnerabilities specific to cloud environments, assessing their likelihood and impact, and implementing controls to mitigate them to an acceptable level. It's about making informed decisions to protect assets and ensure business continuity.

Identify: Pinpoint cloud-specific threats and vulnerabilities.

Assess: Evaluate likelihood and impact of identified risks.

Mitigate: Implement controls to reduce risks to an acceptable level.

Why It's Crucial in the Cloud

  • •
    Dynamic Nature: Cloud environments change rapidly, introducing new vulnerabilities.
  • •
    Shared Responsibility: Clear understanding of security duties between CSP and customer is vital.
  • •
    Third-Party Dependencies: Reliance on CSPs means their security posture directly impacts yours.
  • •
    Continuous Evolution: Threat vectors and cloud services are constantly evolving, requiring adaptive strategies.
Key Takeaway

Cloud risk management is a continuous, adaptive process focused on informed decision-making, not total risk elimination.
