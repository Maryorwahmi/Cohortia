ISO/IEC 27005 for Information Security Risk

International guidelines for managing information security risks.

1

Context Establishment

Define scope, boundaries, and risk criteria for the ISMS.

2

Risk Assessment

Identify, analyze, and evaluate information security risks.

3

Risk Treatment

Select and implement controls to modify risks to an acceptable level.

4

Risk Acceptance

Formally decide to accept residual risks after treatment.

5

Communication & Consultation

Share risk information and engage with stakeholders.

6

Monitoring & Review

Continuously oversee and review the risk management process.

Key Takeaway

ISO 27005 provides a flexible, business-driven framework for managing information security risks, crucial for integrating cloud security into a broader ISMS.
