Cloud Risk Management within Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)

A holistic approach to organizational risk.

Why Integrate?

Cloud risks are not isolated; they are a component of the broader enterprise risk landscape. Integration ensures a holistic view and consistent management across the organization.

Example: A cloud misconfiguration could have cascading effects on on-premises systems.

Key Integration Strategies

  • •
    Aligning Risk Appetites: Ensure cloud risk tolerance matches overall ERM.
  • •
    Consistent Methodologies: Use similar risk assessment approaches for all risks.
  • •
    Centralized Reporting: Incorporate cloud risks into enterprise-level dashboards.
  • •
    Cross-functional Collaboration: Foster teamwork between cloud security, ERM, legal, and business units.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • •
    Siloed Assessments: Conducting cloud risk assessments without considering broader business impacts or interdependencies.
  • •
    Stagnant Assessments: Failing to regularly update risk assessments as cloud deployments evolve or new threats emerge.
Key Takeaway

Effective cloud risk management requires seamless integration into the organization's ERM program for a unified, consistent, and resilient security posture.
