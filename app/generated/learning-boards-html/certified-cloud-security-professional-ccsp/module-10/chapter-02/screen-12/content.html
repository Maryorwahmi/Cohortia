Leveraging the CSA CCM for Cloud Security Assessment

Practical steps for evaluating CSP security posture.

1

Review CCM Domains

Understand the 17 domains (e.g., Application Security, Data Security) to grasp the scope of controls.

2

Map Controls

Identify which CCM controls are relevant to your specific cloud services and data, tailoring the assessment.

3

Assess CSP Compliance

Evaluate how a CSP addresses each relevant control, often via their STAR assessment or direct audit.

4

Identify Gaps

Pinpoint areas where the CSP's controls or your own responsibilities fall short of requirements.

5

Develop Mitigation Strategies

Implement additional controls or negotiate with the CSP to address identified security gaps.

Real-World Connection

The CCM is invaluable for due diligence during CSP selection and continuous monitoring of third-party risk.

Key Takeaway

Applying the CSA CCM systematically enables thorough assessment of CSP security, ensuring robust third-party risk management and compliance.
