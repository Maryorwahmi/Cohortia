Key Concepts

Advanced Frameworks and Enterprise Integration

CSA Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM)

A comprehensive framework of 197 control objectives across 17 domains, specifically designed for cloud security.

CSA STAR

A program providing a public registry of cloud providers' security capabilities based on the CCM and other standards.

Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)

A company-wide process to identify, assess, and manage risks that could affect the achievement of organizational objectives.

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging cloud-specific frameworks and integrating cloud risk into ERM ensures a comprehensive, transparent, and resilient security posture across the entire enterprise.
