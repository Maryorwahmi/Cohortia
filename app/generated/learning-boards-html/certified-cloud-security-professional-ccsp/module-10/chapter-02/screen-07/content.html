Preparing and Categorizing with NIST RMF

Laying the groundwork for effective risk management.

1

Prepare

This step involves understanding the organizational context, mission, and risk appetite. It sets the stage by defining the scope and identifying key stakeholders for risk management activities.

Example: Defining the acceptable level of data loss for a critical application.
2

Categorize

Classify information systems and data based on the potential impact (low, moderate, high) if compromised. This directly influences the selection of appropriate security controls.

Example: Classifying a system handling PII as 'High' for confidentiality impact.
3

Select

Choose an initial set of security controls from NIST SP 800-53 based on the system categorization. These controls are then tailored to the specific organizational and system context.

Example: Selecting access control and encryption controls for a high-impact system.
Key Takeaway

Accurate preparation and categorization are fundamental to NIST RMF, ensuring that risk management efforts are aligned with organizational objectives and system criticality.
