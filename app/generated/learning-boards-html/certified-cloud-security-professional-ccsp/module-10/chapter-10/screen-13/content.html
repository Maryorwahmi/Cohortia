The Principle of Least Privilege
Ensuring entities only have the permissions necessary to perform their function.
Minimal Permissions

Grant only the essential privileges required for a user, program, or process to operate.

Reduced Attack Surface

Limits the damage an attacker can do if an account or service is compromised.

Ongoing Review

Requires continuous monitoring and adjustment of access controls and policies.

Key Takeaway

Least privilege is a foundational security control that significantly reduces risk by limiting potential damage from breaches.
