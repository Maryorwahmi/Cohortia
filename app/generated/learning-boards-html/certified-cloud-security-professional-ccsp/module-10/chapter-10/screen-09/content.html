Cryptographic Risk

Quantum computers can break current encryption algorithms (RSA, ECC), threatening data security.

Post-Quantum Crypto (PQC)

Mandate adoption of quantum-resistant algorithms to secure future communications and data.

Future Integration

Plan for QKD, QaaS, and governance of quantum workloads as the technology matures.

Key Takeaway

Proactive governance is needed to transition to quantum-resistant cryptography and secure future cloud environments.
