CSA Components

Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Guaranteed Performance and Security Commitments

What an SLA Specifies

The Service Level Agreement (SLA) is a critical component of a CSA that explicitly defines the guaranteed level of service a CSP will provide.

  • •Uptime & Availability: Guaranteed service accessibility (e.g., 99.9% uptime).
  • •Performance Metrics: Specific benchmarks for speed, latency, and throughput.
  • •Security Incident Response: Defined timelines and processes for handling security events.
  • •Remedies for Non-Compliance: Compensation or service credits for unmet guarantees.

Example: Incident Notification Clause

A strong SLA might state: "CSP will notify Customer of any confirmed security incident affecting Customer Data within 24 hours of discovery, via designated communication channels."

  • •Benefit: Enables timely customer response and compliance with regulatory notification requirements.
  • •Mistake: Vague language like "without undue delay" is insufficient and a red flag.
Key Takeaway

SLAs provide measurable guarantees for service performance and security, including critical incident response timelines. Explicit terms are vital for accountability.
