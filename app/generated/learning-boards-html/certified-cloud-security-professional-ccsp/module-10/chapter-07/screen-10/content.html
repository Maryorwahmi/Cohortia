Critical Clauses

Data Location, Incident Response, and Audit Rights

Ensuring Data Residency, Timely Breach Notification, and Accountability

Key Considerations

  • •Data Location: Specify where data is stored and processed to meet residency requirements.
  • •Incident Response: Define CSP's detection, response, and notification responsibilities.
  • •Audit Rights: Clarify customer's right to audit or rely on third-party reports.
Common Mistake

Assuming prompt incident notification without explicit timelines in the SLA can lead to compliance failures and delayed internal response.

Incident Response Flow

1

Detection: CSP identifies a security incident.

2

Analysis: CSP investigates the scope and impact.

3

Notification: CSP informs customer within agreed timelines (e.g., 24 hours).

4

Remediation: CSP works to resolve the incident and restore services.

Key Takeaway

Explicit clauses for data location, incident response timelines, and audit rights are essential for meeting compliance, ensuring timely breach management, and verifying CSP security.
