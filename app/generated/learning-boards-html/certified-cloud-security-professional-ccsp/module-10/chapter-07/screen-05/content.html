CSA Components

Master Service Agreement (MSA) and Terms of Service (ToS)

The Overarching Legal Framework for Cloud Engagements

Defining the MSA/ToS

The Master Service Agreement (MSA) or Terms of Service (ToS) is the foundational legal document that sets the general terms and conditions for all cloud services provided by a CSP.

  • •General Terms: Overarching legal principles governing the relationship.
  • •Intellectual Property: Defines ownership of data and software.
  • •Liability: Specifies responsibilities and limitations for both parties.
  • •Dispute Resolution: Outlines processes for addressing conflicts.
  • •Termination Clauses: Details conditions for ending the agreement.

Example: Liability Clause

A typical MSA might state: "Provider's total liability for any claim arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim."

  • •Impact: Limits CSP's financial responsibility.
  • •Consideration: Assess if this limit aligns with your organization's risk appetite for potential damages.
Key Takeaway

The MSA/ToS establishes the fundamental legal terms for cloud services, covering IP, liability, and dispute resolution. Careful review is essential to understand overall risk.
