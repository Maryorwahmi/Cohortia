CSA Components

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) and Technical Appendices

Defining Permitted Actions and Specific Security Controls

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

The AUP defines what the customer is permitted and prohibited from doing with the cloud services.

  • •Purpose: Prevents misuse, illegal activities, and harm to CSP infrastructure.
  • •Examples: Prohibits spamming, DDoS attacks, hosting illegal content.
  • •Consequence: Violations can lead to service suspension or termination.

Technical Appendices / Security Addendums

Detailed documents outlining specific security controls, certifications, and operational procedures implemented by the CSP.

  • •Content: Encryption standards, access controls, network security, physical security.
  • •Certifications: Lists maintained security certifications (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2).
  • •Importance: Provides granular technical assurance and validates compliance.
Key Takeaway

The AUP governs acceptable usage to prevent misuse, while Technical Appendices provide crucial details on the CSP's specific security controls and certifications, ensuring technical alignment.
