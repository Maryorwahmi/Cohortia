Critical Clauses

Data Deletion, Portability, and Liability

Planning for Exit Strategies and Managing Risk

Key Considerations

  • •Data Deletion: Secure and timely deletion of data upon contract termination.
  • •Data Portability: Ability to easily export data in a usable format for migration.
  • •Compliance Certifications: CSP's maintained security certifications (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2).
  • •Liability & Indemnification: Limits of responsibility for both parties, especially in breaches.

Example: Exit Strategy Clause

A robust CSA might state: "Upon termination, CSP will provide Customer with all Customer Data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format within 30 days, and securely delete all remaining copies within 90 days, providing proof of deletion."

  • •Benefit: Prevents vendor lock-in and ensures data availability for migration.
  • •Mistake: Vague terms like "commercially reasonable timeframe" for deletion are insufficient.
Key Takeaway

Explicit clauses for data deletion, portability, and liability are crucial for a smooth exit strategy, maintaining data control, and managing financial risk in cloud engagements.
