Negotiation Strategies

Negotiating Security Requirements with CSPs

Advocating for Your Organization's Unique Security Needs

01

Early Engagement

Involve legal, security, and procurement teams early to identify issues and ensure comprehensive review.

02

Define Requirements Clearly

Articulate specific security, compliance, and data residency needs using frameworks like CSA CCM.

03

Leverage Third-Party Reports

Use SOC 2, ISO 27001 reports as a baseline, but identify and address contractual gaps.

04

Custom Security Addendums

Negotiate specific controls, audit rights, or incident response procedures for enterprise deals.

05

Plan for Exit Strategy

Negotiate terms for data portability, deletion timelines, and migration assistance to avoid vendor lock-in.

Key Takeaway

Proactive negotiation, clear requirement definition, and leveraging all available tools are crucial for tailoring CSAs to your organization's specific security and compliance needs.
