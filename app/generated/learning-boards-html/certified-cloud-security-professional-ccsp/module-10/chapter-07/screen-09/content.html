Critical Clauses

Security Guarantees and Data Ownership

Ensuring Explicit Protections and Retaining Control of Your Data

Security Guarantees

Look for explicit statements about the security controls implemented by the CSP.

  • •Specificity: Details on encryption standards, access controls, network security.
  • •Red Flag: Vague language like "industry-standard security" is insufficient.
  • •Importance: Ensures CSP's security measures align with your requirements.

Data Ownership

The CSA must clearly state that the customer retains ownership of their data.

  • •Clarity: Customer retains all rights, title, and interest in their data.
  • •Scrutiny: Any clauses suggesting CSP gains rights to customer data should be challenged.
  • •Benefit: Maintains legal and ethical control over your organization's intellectual property and sensitive information.
Key Takeaway

Demand explicit security guarantees and unequivocal data ownership clauses in CSAs to ensure robust protection and maintain full control over your organization's data.
