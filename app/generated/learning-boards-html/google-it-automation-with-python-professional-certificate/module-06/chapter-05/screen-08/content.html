Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Print vs Logging
Print
Quick to use, unstructured output, no levels, no timestamps, hard to filter or redirect reliably in production.
Logging
Structured messages with levels, timestamps, handlers, and formatters; suitable for unattended scripts and audits.
Quick Example
Print: print("Error: something went wrong")
Logging: logging.error("Something went wrong: %s", error, exc_info=True)
