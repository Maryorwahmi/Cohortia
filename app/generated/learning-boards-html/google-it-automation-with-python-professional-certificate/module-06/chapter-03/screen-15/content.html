Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › What to Remember
Use SSL/TLS
Encrypt connections before authenticating to protect credentials and message content.
Compose for compatibility
Provide plain-text and HTML parts and attach files as binary MIME parts.
Protect secrets & limit noise
Use environment variables or app passwords and design notifications to avoid alert fatigue.
Final Reflection
Before automating, ask whether the notification will help someone act. If not, consider a digest or lower-severity channel.
