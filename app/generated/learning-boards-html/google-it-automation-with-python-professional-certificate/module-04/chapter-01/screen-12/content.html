Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Common Mistakes & Tips
Pitfalls
  • Broadly catching exceptions without logging.
  • Silently skipping errors and continuing with invalid state.
  • Not including contextual information in error messages.
Practical Tips
  1. Log errors with stack traces and context.
  2. Validate inputs and data before operations.
  3. Use finally for cleanup and resource release.
