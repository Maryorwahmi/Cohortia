What to Remember
tottime vs cumtime
tottime excludes subcalls; cumtime includes them. Use cumtime first to find hotspots.
Representative runs
Profile with realistic inputs to surface true bottlenecks.
Measure, change, re-measure
Save profile files to prove improvements and avoid guesswork.
Final takeaway

Targeted debugging plus evidence-driven profiling makes fixes faster and ensures optimization effort is well spent.
