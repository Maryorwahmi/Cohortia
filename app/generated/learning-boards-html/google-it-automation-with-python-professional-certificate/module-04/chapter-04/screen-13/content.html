Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Mistakes & Practices
Comparison
Common mistakes
  • Optimizing without data.
  • Profiling with tiny or non-representative inputs.
  • Ignoring I/O waits because CPU time appears low.
Practical rules
  • Measure first, change second, measure again.
  • Save profile files to compare changes.
  • Sort by cumulative time, then inspect callers and callees.
Final reflection
When diagnosing performance, treat profiling as an experiment: hypothesis, measurement, intervention, and verification. This discipline keeps optimizations focused and effective.
