Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Benefits & Challenges
Scalability
Scale resources to match traffic and avoid over-provisioning.
Example: auto-scaling servers for a promotional event.
Cost-effectiveness
Pay-as-you-go reduces capital expense but requires active cost control.
Example: shutting down non-production environments overnight.
Reliability & Reach
Provider redundancy and global regions improve uptime and latency.
Example: deploying instances closer to users in different regions.
Challenges
  • Cost drift from idle resources
  • Security and compliance complexity
  • Potential vendor lock-in
Mitigations
  • Enable cost monitoring and alerts
  • Enforce least privilege and encryption
  • Design portability where necessary
