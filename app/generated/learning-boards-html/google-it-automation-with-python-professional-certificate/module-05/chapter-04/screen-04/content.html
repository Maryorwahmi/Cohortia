Core Concept

What is Cloud Computing?

On-demand delivery of compute, storage, and services over the internet. Consume what you need and stop when you don't.

Concrete example
Launch a VM for a short campaign to handle peak traffic, then decommission it to avoid idle costs.
Common misconception
Cloud is not just remote servers; it includes managed services, global distribution, and operational guarantees.
Key Takeaway
Cloud is an operational and economic model: flexible resource rental plus managed services.
Analogy

Renting vs Building

Build (On-premises)
  • Buy servers and maintain hardware
  • Large upfront costs
Rent (Cloud)
  • Pay-as-you-go
  • Scale on demand
Why this matters: renting shifts effort from hardware ops to software and architecture, enabling faster innovation when managed carefully.
