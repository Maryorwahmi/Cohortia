request = compute_v1.DeleteInstanceRequest(project=project_id, zone=zone, instance=instance_name)

operation.wait()
if operation.error:
    print("Error deleting instance")
else:
    print("Instance deleted successfully")
After wait, re-run your list script to confirm the instance is gone. Logging the operation helps auditing and troubleshooting.
Safety checklist
  • Confirm exact instance name and zone before delete.
  • Prefer targeted deletes over bulk pattern deletions for safety.
  • Log operations and check for operation.error after wait.
Pro Tip

Key Takeaway

Treat deletion as a deliberate, reversible step in development; verify and log to reduce risk and surprise billing.
