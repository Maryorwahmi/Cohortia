Common mistakes vs mitigations
Hardcoded secrets
Mitigation: use Secrets Manager or Parameter Store and grant least privilege.
No error handling
Mitigation: add retries, try-except, and dead-letter queues to capture failures.
Over-alerting
Mitigation: tier alerts by severity and route low-priority ones to passive channels.
Operational tips
• Rotate credentials regularly and avoid embedding them in code repositories.
• Monitor delivery success rates and set alerts for failed notification deliveries.
• Keep enrichment lightweight and prioritize the most actionable fields.
Key Takeaway

Small operational controls — secrets management, retries, and testing — greatly improve alert reliability.
