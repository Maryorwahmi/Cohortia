Process Flow
SNS subscription patterns
Design subscriptions that match your delivery needs and failure handling.
  1. Configure CloudWatch alarm to publish to SNS topic.
  2. Add subscribers: email/SMS for people, HTTP/S for webhooks, Lambda for enrichment.
  3. Grant SNS permission to invoke Lambda functions and confirm endpoint validation for webhooks.
  4. Test delivery and configure retries or dead-letter queues for failed messages.
Example scenario
A critical alarm publishes to a 'critical' topic with a PagerDuty webhook and a Lambda that posts enriched details to Slack.
Subscriber types
Email / SMS
Simple human notifications. No payload transform.
HTTP/S (Webhook)
Direct POST to a third-party endpoint. Confirm expected JSON format.
Lambda
Enrich, filter, and forward to multiple destinations.
Test checklist
Publish sample SNS messages, verify subscription receipt, inspect Lambda logs, and validate webhook payload parsing.
Pitfall
Assuming webhooks accept raw SNS JSON; many require reformatting via Lambda.
