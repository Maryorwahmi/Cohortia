Core Concept
AWS Chatbot
Bring alerts and simple AWS actions into Slack or Teams to speed triage and coordination.
• Configure Chatbot to subscribe to SNS topics or CloudWatch alarms for channel notifications.
• Allow limited commands via IAM role to run diagnostics from the chat client.
• Use Chatbot for operational visibility and quick fixes; use Lambda for complex integrations and PagerDuty for escalation.
When to use Chatbot
Operational alerts and quick, low-risk remediation commands from chat.
When not to
Critical escalation workflows that require formal incident tracking and on-call paging.
Practical notes
• Map SNS topics to specific chat channels so teams receive only relevant alerts.
• Use fine-grained IAM roles to restrict which commands run from chat.
• Combine Chatbot notifications with PagerDuty for critical incidents that require formal escalation.
Why it matters

Chat-based alerts reduce context switching and enable immediate triage, but choose the right channel for severity.
