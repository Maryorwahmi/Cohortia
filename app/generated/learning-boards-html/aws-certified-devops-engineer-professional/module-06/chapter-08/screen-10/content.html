Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Multi-Site Active-Active
How it works

Multiple regions serve traffic simultaneously. Replication keeps data synchronized so a region failure causes near-immediate reroute to healthy regions.

Use global traffic management and synchronous or near-synchronous data replication depending on consistency needs.
Design session management and database sharding to avoid write conflicts across regions.
Operational concerns
  • Higher operational overhead and cost.
  • Complex consistency and conflict resolution for distributed writes.
  • Requires robust monitoring and automation for routing and capacity management.
Key takeaway

Active-active offers the fastest possible recovery but demands architecture and process maturity to manage complexity and cost.
