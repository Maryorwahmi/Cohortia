Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › DR vs BCP
Definition
Disaster Recovery

Recover infrastructure and data after catastrophic outages. Focus on RTO and RPO and the technical steps to restore systems.

Business Continuity

Ensure essential organizational functions continue during and after disruption. Includes manual processes, stakeholder communications, and compliance steps.

Analogy

DR replaces a broken engine on a plane; BCP reroutes passengers and flight operations so service continues while repairs happen.

Example scenario

A regional outage makes your production region unreachable. DR actions restore a database replica in another region and reconfigure DNS. BCP ensures customer support follows a scripted manual process to accept orders and financial teams log transactions until systems are live.

Common mistake
Treating DR as purely technical and not rehearsing cross-functional continuity steps.
Key takeaway
Design DR playbooks that integrate with BCP so both technical and organizational recovery happen in concert.
