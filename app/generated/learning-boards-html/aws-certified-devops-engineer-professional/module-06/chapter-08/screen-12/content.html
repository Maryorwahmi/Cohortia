Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › AWS Global Accelerator
Why use it

Global Accelerator routes traffic over AWS’s global network and can reroute users quickly when endpoints become unhealthy, improving both availability and performance.

It complements DNS-based failover by reducing latency and making endpoint selection faster and more deterministic.
Operational notes
  • Use when global user experience and near-instant failover are priorities.
  • Still require health checks and integration with your DR automation.
  • Consider cost and ensure endpoints are prepared to absorb traffic when rerouted.
Key takeaway

Global Accelerator speeds recovery and reduces user-visible impact when you have multi-region endpoints and strict availability needs.
