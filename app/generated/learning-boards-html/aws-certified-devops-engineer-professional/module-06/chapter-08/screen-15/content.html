Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Key Concepts — Actions
Route 53 Failover

Use health checks and primary/secondary records to automate DNS failover; tune TTLs to reduce visible delay.

AWS Global Accelerator

Employ when you need faster global failover and consistent low-latency routing across regions.

Common pitfalls

Untested plans, missing dependencies, long DNS TTLs, and lack of automation are frequent causes of failed recoveries.

Final takeaway
Define measurable goals, pick the minimal effective DR pattern, automate failover, and validate through realistic drills so recovery works when it matters most.
