Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Warm Standby
Pattern

Maintain a fully functional duplicate environment with lower capacity. Continuous replication keeps data current so scaling is a capacity change rather than a rebuild.

Run essential services behind a load balancer, with a small number of instances in the DR region.
Replicate databases with read replicas or other replication tools and keep configuration templates in sync.
Automate scaling to increase capacity and route traffic during failover.
When to choose

Choose warm standby when short downtime is critical but fully active multi-site is too costly. It balances cost and recovery speed well for many business-critical services.

Key takeaway

Warm standby gives fast recovery with reduced cost compared to active-active, but automation and configuration drift checks are essential.
