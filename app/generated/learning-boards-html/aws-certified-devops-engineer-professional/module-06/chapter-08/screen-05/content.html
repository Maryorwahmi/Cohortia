Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › RTO & RPO
Definitions
Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Maximum allowable downtime before service must be restored. Drives how quickly you must scale or redirect traffic.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Maximum acceptable data loss measured in time. Determines replication frequency and backup cadence.

Example

An e-commerce checkout requiring less than two minutes downtime and under 30 seconds of data loss will push you to a warm standby or active-active design, with near-real-time replication.

Mapping to strategies
  • High RTO/RPO tolerance → Backup and Restore (hours to days).
  • Moderate RTO, low RPO → Pilot Light (minutes to hours, minutes of data loss).
  • Low RTO, low RPO → Warm Standby (minutes, seconds to minutes of data loss).
  • Near-zero RTO/RPO → Multi-Site Active-Active (near-zero downtime and data loss).
Key Takeaway

RTO and RPO come from the business. Use them to choose the right AWS DR pattern and justify cost trade-offs.
