These are progressive goals: from understanding terms to applying automated failover in real incidents.

01
Differentiate DR and BCP

Distinguish technical recovery tasks from organizational continuity actions and explain why both are needed during major incidents.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Master RTO & RPO

Compute acceptable downtime and data loss, and use those numbers to select the appropriate DR pattern and cost profile.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Evaluate DR strategies

Compare Backup, Pilot Light, Warm Standby and Multi-Site Active-Active for RTO/RPO, cost and operational complexity.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Automate & test failover

Configure Route 53 health checks, use Global Accelerator where appropriate, and run DR drills to validate real RTO/RPO.
