Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Key Concepts
Disaster Recovery

Recover data and restore business functionality after catastrophic events.

Business Continuity Planning

Ensure essential business functions can continue during and after a disaster.

RTO & RPO

RTO: maximum downtime; RPO: maximum data loss. Both drive DR strategy selection.

Takeaway
Capture RTO and RPO for each application and use them as pass/fail criteria during DR drills and architecture reviews.
