Strategy comparison table
Backup & Restore
Cost: Low. RTO/RPO: Hours→Days. Use: non-critical workloads.
Pilot Light
Cost: Low→Medium. RTO: Tens minutes→Hours. Use: critical core services with some lead time.
Warm Standby
Cost: Medium. RTO: Minutes. RPO: Seconds→Minutes. Use: important services requiring quick recovery.
Multi-Site Active-Active
Cost: High. RTO/RPO: Near-zero. Use: mission-critical services with strict SLAs.
Choose by risk

Compute impact and map to cost you are willing to pay for recovery speed.

Dependencies

Include third-party services and data stores when estimating RTO/RPO.

Test often

A documented but untested plan is likely to fail; schedule regular DR drills.
