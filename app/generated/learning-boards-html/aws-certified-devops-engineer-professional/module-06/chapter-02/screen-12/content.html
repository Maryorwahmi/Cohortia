Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Direct Actions vs EventBridge
Direct CloudWatch Actions
Attach SNS, Auto Scaling, or EC2 actions directly to an alarm for immediate, simple responses.
Pros and cons:
  • Fast and straightforward to configure.
  • Limited orchestration and enrichment capabilities.
EventBridge Routing
Use for flexible patterns, enrichment, multi-target routing, and complex automation workflows.
Pros and cons:
  • Centralizes automation patterns and supports many targets.
  • Requires designing event patterns and targets, slightly more upfront work.
Example hybrid

Send immediate pager notifications via SNS for critical alarms while also emitting an EventBridge event for enrichment, ticketing, and analysis.
