Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Principle of Least Privilege
Grant only needed permissions to minimize exploitation if credentials are compromised.
IAM Roles
Identities with attachable policies that can be assumed by trusted principals.
Cross-Account Access
Allow a principal in one account to assume a role in another using trust policies and conditions.
Summary takeaway

Combine narrow roles, MFA enforcement, and cross-account trust to enable secure, auditable incident response.
