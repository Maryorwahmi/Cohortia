Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Multi-Factor Authentication
Require an additional factor for assume-role to protect elevated access.
Service Control Policies (SCPs)
Organization-level policies that apply preventive restrictions to all principals in scoped accounts.
AWS Security Token Service (STS)
Issues temporary credentials for assumed roles, limiting exposure from compromised keys.
Summary takeaway

Combine MFA, STS, and SCPs for layered protection and forensic integrity during incident response.
