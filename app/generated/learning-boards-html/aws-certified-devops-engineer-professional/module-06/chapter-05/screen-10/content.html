Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Common Mistakes
Over-privileged roles
Resist granting broad rights for speed; instead pre-create narrow roles for common tasks.
Lack of MFA
Require MFA both on user login and in trust policies for role assumption.
Long-lived credentials
Use STS temporary credentials and avoid embedding keys in automation.
No audit trail
Enable CloudTrail for assume-role events and privileged API calls for forensics.
Neglecting SCPs
Apply SCPs to protect critical resources regardless of IAM permissions.
Mitigation checklist
  • Permission reviews and narrow roles.
  • MFA in trust policies and on users.
  • STS temporary sessions and logged assume-role events.
