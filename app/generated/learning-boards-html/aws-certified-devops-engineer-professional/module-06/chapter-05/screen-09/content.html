Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › SCPs vs IAM Policies
Service Control Policies
Apply at organization or OU scope and limit the maximum permissions available in member accounts.
Use case:
Deny s3:DeleteBucket in production OU to protect audit data.
IAM Policies
Attach to identities or resources to grant or deny actions within the SCP boundary.
Use case:
Grant ec2:RebootInstances for a diagnostic role in the production account.
Design guidance
  • Use SCPs as conservative guardrails for sensitive OUs.
  • Keep IAM policies focused and minimal inside SCP constraints.
  • Test SCP effects before applying to production to avoid accidental service denial.
