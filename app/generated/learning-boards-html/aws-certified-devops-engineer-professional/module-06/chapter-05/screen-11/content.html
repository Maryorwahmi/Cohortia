Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Break-Glass Procedure
Steps to design
  1. Define strict approval workflow and authorized approvers.
  2. Enforce MFA and multi-party authorization for requests.
  3. Issue short STS sessions and require detailed justification.
  4. Log and alert on every assume-role and privileged action.
Operational controls
  • Approval system integrated with identity provider or ticketing.
  • Immediate notification to security and compliance teams.
  • Post-use review and revocation procedures.
Drill recommendation
Regularly simulate break-glass use to validate approvals, MFA prompts, and log collection.
