Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Permissions & logging
Execution role checklist
  • lambda:InvokeFunction for each Lambda Task
  • sns:Publish for SNS publish Tasks
  • ssm:StartAutomationExecution for SSM Automation Tasks
  • Least privilege: limit ARNs and actions to what the playbook needs
Logging & retention
Stream Lambda logs to CloudWatch, retain execution history for the period your post-incident reviews require, and link executions to ticketing or artifact stores for long-term analysis.
Operational advice
Automate smoke tests in staging that validate success and failure paths. Simulate common errors and confirm Retry and Catch behavior. This prevents surprises during a real incident.
Misconception check
Don't grant broad admin permissions to the execution role; scoped permissions and cross-service least privilege reduce blast radius.
