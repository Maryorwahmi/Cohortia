Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Tool selection
Step Functions
Best for multi-step, conditional, concurrent workflows that need audit trails and retries.
Lambda
Good for single, short tasks; not ideal for complex branching or long-running orchestration.
SSM Automation
Effective for sequential operations on one or a few resources; lacks broad branching and parallel orchestration features.
Decision rule
If you need visual branching, retries, parallel work and an execution history, choose Step Functions. For simple, single-resource tasks, prefer SSM or Lambda.
