Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Implementation reminders
Scoped IAM
Give only the permissions necessary to the Step Functions execution role and task roles.
Test error paths
Simulate failures and verify Retry, Catch and notification behaviors in staging.
Keep machines modular
Split very complex workflows into smaller state machines or reusable subflows for clarity and easier testing.
Summary: secure execution roles, thorough testing, and modular designs make Step Functions playbooks effective and maintainable.
