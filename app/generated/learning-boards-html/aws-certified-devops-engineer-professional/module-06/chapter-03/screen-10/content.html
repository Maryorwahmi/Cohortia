Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Manual vs Automated
Manual Intervention
  • Pros: context-aware decisions, flexible troubleshooting.
  • Cons: slower MTTR, inconsistent steps, human fatigue.
When to prefer: unique incidents or when you lack confidence in automated rollbacks.
Automated Remediation
  • Pros: fast, repeatable, reduces toil, auditable.
  • Cons: risk if untested, requires least-privilege role configuration.
When to prefer: routine transient failures like service restarts or instance reboots that are testable and reversible.
