Verification checklist
  • Confirm the document appears under Systems Manager → Documents.
  • Observe the Automation execution history for step outputs and status.
  • Verify the EC2 instance restarted and the service is running.
  • Record errors and add additional logging or retries as needed.
Common Mistake

Using broad managed policies in the Automation role for speed. Instead, attach only specific permissions needed by the runbook and expand only after validated needs.
