MTTR — timeline
MTTR is the elapsed time from detection to restoration. Reduce it by improving detection, analysis, containment, eradication, and recovery.

Practical levers to reduce MTTR
  • Tune alarms and reduce noisy alerts
  • Centralize logs so queries are fast
  • Automate safe containment actions
  • Practice runbooks in drills
Example
If a manual rollback takes thirty minutes, automating that rollback as a documented runbook step can shave that time and reduce human error, cutting MTTR substantially for that incident class.
