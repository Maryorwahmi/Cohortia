Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Preparation & Drills
Roles and communication
Define who does what and how to talk during an incident to keep actions coordinated and auditable.
On-call responder
Executes runbook steps and reports status.
Incident commander
Makes decisions on escalation and communications.
Communications owner
Manages stakeholder and customer messaging using templates in the playbook.
Drill checklist
Confirm access to logs and Systems Manager, verify alarms trigger notifications, execute runbook steps, and capture timing metrics for MTTR improvements.
After-action
Run a short post-drill review to capture gaps, update runbooks, and assign fixes. Treat drills like real incidents to get realistic practice.
Key Takeaway
People and practice are as important as tooling—test both regularly to ensure readiness.
