Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Runbooks & Playbooks
Runbook (short, executable)
A runbook contains step-by-step instructions for known incidents and should be tested.
Elements
  • Trigger condition and verification
  • Immediate containment steps
  • Safe diagnostic commands and expected outputs
  • Resolution criteria and rollback actions
Example
High 5xx runbook: capture logs, check recent deployments, restart unhealthy instances, monitor error rate until it returns to baseline.
Playbook (coordinated response)
A playbook coordinates cross-team actions, communications, and decision points for complex incidents.
Elements
  • Decision trees for ambiguous symptoms
  • Escalation contacts and legal/comms templates
  • References to multiple runbooks and checklists
Example
Security playbook: run initial containment runbook, then notify security, legal, and external communications per severity levels.
Pro tip
Keep runbooks short, machine-executable where possible, and keep playbooks focused on coordination, not long procedures.
