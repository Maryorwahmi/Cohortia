Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Common Mistakes
Top mistakes
These recurring issues lengthen incidents and are straightforward to fix.
Insufficient logging
Centralize logs so responders don't spend time gathering evidence.
Untested runbooks
Runbooks must be executed in drills to verify steps and permissions.
Unclear roles
Assign an incident commander and communications owner to avoid duplication.
No post-incident review
Capture lessons and update playbooks to prevent recurrence.
Mitigations
Practical steps to close gaps and improve response speed.
Centralize and retain logs
Use CloudWatch Logs or S3 with query patterns for fast retrieval.
Practice runbooks
Schedule game days and simple exercises to validate steps and permissions.
Automate routine snapshots
Capture diagnostic data automatically when an alarm fires to preserve evidence.
