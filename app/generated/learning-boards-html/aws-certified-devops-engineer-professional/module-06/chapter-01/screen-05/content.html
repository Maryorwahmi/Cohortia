Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Incident Lifecycle
Repeatable process
Follow a clear sequence from preparation to review so teams respond reliably and learn from incidents.
1. Prepare
Define roles, runbooks, IaC, and practice drills.
2. Detect & Analyze
Use CloudWatch, CloudTrail, and GuardDuty to confirm events and scope impact.
3. Contain
Limit blast radius while preserving forensic data.
4. Eradicate
Remove cause: roll back, patch, or revoke credentials.
5. Recover
Restore services and validate customer flows.
6. Post-Incident
Review, update runbooks, and schedule fixes to prevent recurrence.
Example: 5xx error storm
A CloudWatch alarm detects high 5xx rate. The on-call team uses CloudTrail to check for recent deployments, isolates the new nodes, rolls back the deployment, and then runs the post-incident review to add pre-deploy checks to the playbook.
Supporting cards
Preserve evidence

Automate safe actions
