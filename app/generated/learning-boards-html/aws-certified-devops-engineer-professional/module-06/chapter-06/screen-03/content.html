Practice checklist
  • Redact sensitive entries and restrict access to forensic logs before sharing a report.
  • Reconstruct a precise timeline from detection to mitigation with timestamps and actor actions.
  • Use RCA techniques such as the five whys and identify contributing factors like missing alerts or runbooks.
  • Create measurable action items with owners, deadlines, and verification steps; track outcomes until verified.
