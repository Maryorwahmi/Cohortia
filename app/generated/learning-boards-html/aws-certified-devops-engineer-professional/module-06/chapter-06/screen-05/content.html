Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Blameless Culture
Process steps
1.
Detect and stabilize the incident, prioritize safety and containment.
2.
Collect and preserve forensic data: CloudTrail, Config snapshots, CloudWatch logs.
3.
Schedule the post-mortem quickly while details are fresh and invite stakeholders.
4.
Reconstruct timeline, run RCA, produce action items with owners and deadlines.
5.
Track and verify completion; update runbooks and automation as needed.
Practical checklist
  • Declare the review blameless at the start.
  • Limit distribution of sensitive logs; redact where necessary.
  • Capture decisions and rationale, not just technical facts.
  • Convert findings into tracked tickets with owners and verification steps.
Common misconception
Blamelessness is not lack of accountability. Accountability is implemented through owners and verification, not blame.
