Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Reporting
Report structure
Summary: brief impact and timeline.
Timeline: ordered events with timestamps and evidence links.
RCA and contributing factors: documented with supporting logs or config snapshots.
Action items: owners, deadlines, acceptance, verification.
Lessons learned: what will change in process or automation.
Appendix: Athena queries, CloudTrail extracts, and redacted log samples.
Distribution and storage
Store reports centrally with access controls and versioning.
Limit distribution of raw forensic data; provide redacted extracts for wider audiences.
Track action item status in your task system and include report links.
