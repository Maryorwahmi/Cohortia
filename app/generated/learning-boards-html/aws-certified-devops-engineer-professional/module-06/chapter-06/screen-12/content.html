Root cause vs contributing factors
Root cause: the underlying defect that allowed the incident to start (e.g., bad deployment artifact).
Contributing factors: conditions that increased impact or delayed resolution (e.g., missing alarms, unclear runbooks).
Action: tag each finding as corrective or preventative and assign owner and verification steps.
Common remedial actions
  • Update or add CloudWatch alarms for better detection.
  • Improve logging and add identifiers to trace requests across services.
  • Update runbooks and automation documents with verified steps.
  • Schedule training or drills to ensure the team can execute runbooks under pressure.
