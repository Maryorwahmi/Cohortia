Post-Incident Review
Structured process to understand incidents and produce measurable fixes.
Blameless Culture
Focus on systemic change, not individual blame, to surface full facts and enable learning.
Track & Verify
Assign owners, set deadlines, and require verification steps so fixes are completed and proven.

Combine CloudTrail, Config, CloudWatch Logs, and Athena to build reproducible evidence and use a blameless process to turn findings into tracked improvements.
