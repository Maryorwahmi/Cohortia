Pilot Light Strategy

Minimal infrastructure ready for scale-up

Concept

A minimal version of your infrastructure is kept running in a DR region, ready to be scaled up to full capacity during a disaster.

Typical RTO/RPO

RTO: Tens of minutes. RPO: Minutes (due to continuous data replication).

Example Services

Small EC2 instances, RDS Cross-Region Read Replicas, minimal networking.

Trade-offs

Higher cost than Backup & Restore, but significantly lower RTO/RPO. Requires automation for efficient scale-up.

Key Takeaway

Pilot Light provides a good balance for applications needing faster recovery than basic backup, by keeping core components warm and ready.
