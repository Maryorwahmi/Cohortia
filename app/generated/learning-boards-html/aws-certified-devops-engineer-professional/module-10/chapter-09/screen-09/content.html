Multi-Site (Active-Active) Strategy

Highest availability with near-zero RTO/RPO

Concept

Your application is fully deployed and actively serving traffic in multiple regions simultaneously. If one fails, the others seamlessly take over.

Typical RTO/RPO

RTO/RPO: Seconds or less (near zero downtime and data loss).

Traffic Management

AWS Route 53 (Latency-based or Geolocation Routing) directs users to the nearest healthy region.

Trade-offs

Highest cost and most complex, especially for data consistency across regions.

Key Takeaway

Active-Active is for mission-critical applications demanding continuous operation, but requires meticulous design for data synchronization.
