AWS Disaster Recovery Strategies

A spectrum of options for RTO, RPO, and cost

Lower Cost / Higher RTO/RPOHigher Cost / Lower RTO/RPO

Backup and Restore

Highest RTO/RPO, lowest cost.

Pilot Light

Minimal infra, moderate RTO/RPO, higher cost.

Warm Standby

Scaled-down, low RTO/RPO, higher cost.

Multi-Site (Active-Active)

Near-zero RTO/RPO, highest cost.

Key Takeaway

The choice of DR strategy depends on your application's criticality, acceptable RTO/RPO, and budget. Each strategy offers a different balance of these factors.
