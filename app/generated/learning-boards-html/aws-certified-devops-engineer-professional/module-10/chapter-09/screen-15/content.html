Key Concepts: Core DR Principles

Essential terms for disaster recovery

Disaster Recovery (DR)

The process of preparing for and recovering from events that disrupt business operations, such as regional outages.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum acceptable delay for restoring business functions after a disaster.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum acceptable amount of data loss that can occur during a disaster.

Summary Takeaway

DR, RTO, and RPO are foundational concepts that guide the design and implementation of resilient cloud architectures, ensuring business continuity.
