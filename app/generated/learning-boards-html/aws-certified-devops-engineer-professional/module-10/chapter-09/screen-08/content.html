Warm Standby Strategy

Scaled-down but fully functional environment

Concept

A scaled-down but fully functional version of your application is running in the DR region, ready to scale up and shift traffic during a disaster.

Typical RTO/RPO

RTO: Minutes. RPO: Seconds to minutes (due to near real-time replication).

Example Services

Scaled-down Auto Scaling Groups, RDS Cross-Region Read Replicas, Aurora Global Database.

Trade-offs

Higher cost than Pilot Light, but significantly lower RTO/RPO. Requires careful synchronization and automation for failover.

Key Takeaway

Warm Standby is suitable for critical applications requiring rapid recovery, balancing cost with high availability and minimal data loss.

Warm Standby vs. Pilot Light

Pilot Light

  • Minimal core infrastructure
  • Data replicated
  • Requires full scale-up
  • Higher RTO (tens of minutes)

Warm Standby

  • Scaled-down, functional environment
  • Data replicated
  • Requires scale-up and traffic shift
  • Lower RTO (minutes)

Failover Process

Primary region fails; DR region scales up and takes over traffic. Data is already synchronized, minimizing loss.
