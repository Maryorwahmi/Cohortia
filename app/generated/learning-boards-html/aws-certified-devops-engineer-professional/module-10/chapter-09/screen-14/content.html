Strategic Planning

Architecting for Resilience

Aligning DR with business objectives

Understand Application Criticality

Identify which applications are mission-critical and what their specific RTO and RPO requirements are. This drives strategy selection.

Define Data Requirements

Determine the maximum acceptable data loss (RPO) for each dataset and ensure appropriate replication mechanisms are in place.

Evaluate Cost vs. Resilience

Balance the cost of implementing and maintaining a DR strategy against the business impact of downtime and data loss.

Automate & Test Continuously

Automate failover processes and regularly test your DR plan to ensure it functions as expected during a real disaster.

Key Takeaway

A successful DR strategy is a continuous effort that aligns technical solutions with business objectives, balancing resilience, cost, and operational complexity.
