Active-Active Deployment: Data Management

Synchronizing data for global consistency

Amazon Aurora Global Database

Provides a single Aurora database spanning multiple AWS regions, enabling fast local reads and rapid disaster recovery for relational workloads.

Global relational consistency

DynamoDB Global Tables

Automatically replicate data across regions, providing multi-region active-active capabilities with low latency for NoSQL databases.

Multi-master NoSQL replication

Custom Solutions & Challenges

  • For other databases, complex multi-master replication or sharding may be needed.
  • Data Consistency: The most complex aspect, especially for write operations and conflict resolution.
  • Latency: Minimizing replication latency between regions is crucial for near-zero RPO.
Key Takeaway

Achieving data consistency in Active-Active deployments requires specialized services or complex custom solutions, making it the most challenging aspect of this strategy.
