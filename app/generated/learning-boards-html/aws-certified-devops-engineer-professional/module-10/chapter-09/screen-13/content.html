Avoiding Common DR Pitfalls

Essential considerations for robust disaster recovery

Not Testing DR

A DR plan is useless if not regularly tested. Conduct full DR drills (game days) to validate RTO/RPO.

Incomplete Replication

Forgetting to replicate all critical data (e.g., S3 buckets, EFS, configuration data in Parameter Store).

Manual Failover Dependencies

Relying on manual steps during a disaster increases RTO and human error. Automate as much as possible.

Underestimating RTO/RPO

Setting unrealistic targets without the architecture to support them. Align targets with business criticality.

Key Takeaway

Proactive testing, comprehensive data replication, automation, and realistic goal-setting are crucial for a resilient DR strategy.
