Design Principle

Evolutionary Database Design

Adopting small, incremental, and backward-compatible changes

Core Tenets

  • • Advocates for small, incremental schema changes.
  • • Prioritizes backward compatibility at every step.
  • • Avoids large, monolithic, and risky database updates.
  • • Changes are broken down into safer, manageable steps over multiple deployments.
Why it Matters

Minimizes risk of data loss and downtime, enabling continuous delivery for databases.

Traditional vs. Evolutionary

Big Bang Updates(High Risk)
Incremental Changes(Low Risk)
Analogy

Think of it like renovating a house while people are still living in it – you make small, non-disruptive changes rather than tearing everything down at once.

Key Takeaway

Evolutionary database design is crucial for safe, continuous schema evolution by prioritizing small, backward-compatible steps.
