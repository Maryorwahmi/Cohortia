The A/B Testing Process

From hypothesis to data analysis.

Iterative Improvement

A/B testing is a continuous cycle of experimentation and learning to optimize product features.

Data-Driven Decisions

Ensures that product changes are based on empirical evidence rather than assumptions.

Risk Mitigation

Small-scale experiments reduce the risk of rolling out ineffective or detrimental features to all users.

5 Steps of A/B Testing

1. Define a Hypothesis
2. Identify Metrics
3. Split Traffic (Feature Flags)
4. Collect Data
5. Analyze Results

Example: Test a new onboarding flow by tracking user completion rates for each version.

Key Takeaway

A structured A/B testing process, from hypothesis to analysis, is crucial for making statistically sound, data-driven decisions in product development.
