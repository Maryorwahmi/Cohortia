ALB Weighted Target Groups

Distributing traffic for canary releases.

How it Works

  • 1An ALB listener forwards requests to multiple target groups.
  • 2Each target group is assigned a specific weight, determining traffic distribution.
  • 3New versions are deployed to a 'canary' target group with a small weight.
  • 4Weights are adjusted to gradually shift traffic or instantly rollback.
Key Takeaway

ALB weighted target groups provide a simple, effective mechanism for controlled traffic shifting, making canary deployments accessible for many applications.

Visualizing Traffic Flow

Example Scenario

A new payment gateway integration is deployed. Initially, 1% of traffic is routed to the canary target group. If no errors or latency spikes are observed after 24 hours, the traffic is gradually increased to 10%, then 50%, and finally 100%.
