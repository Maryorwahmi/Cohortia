Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential terms from this chapter

Deployment Group

A set of instances, Auto Scaling Groups, or ECS services targeted for a deployment.

Deployment Configuration

Defines how traffic is shifted (e.g., all-at-once, linear, canary) and other deployment parameters.

Lifecycle Hooks

Scripts or functions executed by CodeDeploy at specific stages of a deployment.

Summary Takeaway

Effective blue/green deployments rely on carefully configured Deployment Groups, precise Deployment Configurations, and powerful Lifecycle Hooks for automation and validation.
