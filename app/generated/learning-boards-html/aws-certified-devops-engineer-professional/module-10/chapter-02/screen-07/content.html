Rollback and Retention for EC2 Deployments

Ensuring a safety net after traffic has shifted to green

Safety Mechanism

Blue Environment Retention

After traffic shifts to 'green', the original 'blue' environment is retained for a pre-defined period as a safety net.

Rapid Recovery

Instant Rollback Capability

If issues are detected in 'green' post-shift, CodeDeploy can instantly revert traffic back to the stable 'blue' environment.

Key Takeaway

The retention period for the blue environment is crucial for post-deployment validation and provides a rapid, low-risk rollback option, minimizing service disruption.

Rollback Flow

Green Environment (Live)

New App Version, Serving Traffic

Blue Environment (Retained)

Old App Version, Ready for Rollback

Critical Period

Monitor green environment closely during retention period.
