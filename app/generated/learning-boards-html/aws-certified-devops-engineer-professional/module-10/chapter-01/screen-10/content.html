Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Automation & Observability
Automation
Pipelines and automated gates
  • CI/CD pipelines enforce repeatable deployment steps and provide rollback hooks.
  • Automated gates evaluate observability signals before proceeding to the next stage.
  • Test rollback paths regularly to ensure fast recovery when needed.
Observability
Logs, metrics, and traces that matter
  • System metrics: error rate, latency, resource utilization.
  • Business metrics: transaction success rate, checkout conversions.
  • Synthetic checks and distributed traces to narrow root causes quickly.
Pro tip: connect your pipeline to observability so promotion occurs automatically only when signals are within gate thresholds.
