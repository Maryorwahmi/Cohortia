Architecture

Two parallel environments and a switch

Green environment is deployed and fully validated before traffic shifts. Rollback is a traffic switch back to blue.
AWS example: CodeDeploy blue/green for ECS or Lambda; use a load balancer or DNS change to shift traffic between environments.
Cost trade-off: maintaining two environments increases infrastructure spend while green is validated.
Stateful service concerns
Databases cannot simply be duplicated. Typical options are replication, carefully planned migrations with backward-compatible schema changes, or shared databases that accept both old and new schemas during transition.
Common mistake
Assuming environment parity equals data safety; always plan data migration and replication strategies before switching traffic.
