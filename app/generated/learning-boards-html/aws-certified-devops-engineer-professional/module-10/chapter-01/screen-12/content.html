Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Design Checklist
Compatibility
Ensure APIs and schemas accept both old and new formats or use feature flags.
Testing
Add integration and canary-targeted tests that run as part of the pipeline.
Automated gates
Tie progression to meaningful metrics with automatic halt/rollback on anomalies.
Rollback readiness
Prepare scripts and test the rollback path regularly to ensure fast recovery.
Observability
Capture system and business metrics, traces, and logs for both old and new versions.
Rehearsal
Run tabletop exercises and dry-run deployments to validate procedures and timing.
Key Takeaway: checklists convert strategy into reliable operational steps your team can execute under pressure.
