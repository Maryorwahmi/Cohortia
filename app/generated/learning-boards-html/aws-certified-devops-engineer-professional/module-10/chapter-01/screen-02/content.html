Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Learning Objectives
01
Differentiate strategies

Explain all-at-once, rolling, blue/green, and canary deployments and their core trade-offs in risk and downtime. Knowing these differences helps you select the least risky approach for production workloads.

02
Evaluate trade-offs

Assess downtime, blast radius, and operational complexity for each strategy so you can justify an approach to stakeholders and design mitigations.

03
Map to AWS services

Identify which AWS primitives implement each strategy, such as ASGs, ECS/EKS, ALB weighted routing, Lambda aliases, and CodeDeploy. This makes designs actionable.

04
Apply automation & observability

Design CI/CD automation and observability (logs, metrics, traces) so rollouts are consistent and failures are detected quickly for safe rollback.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE • Demonstrate a chosen strategy in a simulation and explain rollback steps.
