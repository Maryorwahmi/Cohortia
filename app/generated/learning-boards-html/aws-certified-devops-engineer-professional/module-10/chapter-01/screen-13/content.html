Rollback patterns
  • Blue/green: traffic switch back to blue for instant rollback.
  • Canary: divert canary traffic away to limit impact to a small group.
  • Rolling: reverse the rolling update, which takes time and coordination.
  • All-at-once: redeploy previous version or restore from snapshot; downtime likely.
Blast radius
Blast radius is the scope of impact from a failed release. Smaller blast radii reduce customer exposure and simplify incident response. Canary and well-configured blue/green approaches minimize blast radius when implemented with automation.
Pro tip: automate rollback triggers on critical observability signals to limit the human reaction time that otherwise expands blast radius.
