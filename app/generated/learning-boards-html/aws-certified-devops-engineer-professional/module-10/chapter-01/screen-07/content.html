Comparison

Stage traffic to a small subset and expand

Start with a tiny percentage of users, monitor, then gradually increase. AWS tools: ALB weighted routing, App Mesh, Lambda aliases.
Typical progression: 1% → 5% → 20% → 100% with automated health gates at each step.
Observability focus: error rate, latency distribution, and business metrics specific to the feature being released.
Example scenario
Route 1% of production traffic to the canary and monitor SLOs. If the canary’s error rate stays within acceptable thresholds, increase to 5%. If an anomaly appears, divert that traffic back to the stable release immediately.
Misconception
Assuming a canary will reveal every issue; some problems only appear at full scale or under specific user behaviors not present in the canary sample.
