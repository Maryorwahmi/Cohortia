Explore Key Topics
Three quick entry points to the chapter's core ideas and their AWS mappings.
All-at-once
Fast but risky; suitable for non-critical environments.
Blue/Green
Zero-downtime switches; requires duplicate environments and planning for state.
Canary
Controlled rollout to a small traffic subset with fast rollback if needed.
