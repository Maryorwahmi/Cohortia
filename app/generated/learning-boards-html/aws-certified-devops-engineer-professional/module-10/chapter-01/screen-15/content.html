Canary deployment
Small traffic sample for early detection; needs automated gates and strong monitoring.
Backward compatibility
Allow old and new versions to interoperate during transitions to avoid errors.
Observability
Logs, metrics, and traces provide the signals to validate rollouts and trigger automation.
Final takeaway

Combine strategy, compatibility engineering, automation, and observability to make deployments predictable and safe.
