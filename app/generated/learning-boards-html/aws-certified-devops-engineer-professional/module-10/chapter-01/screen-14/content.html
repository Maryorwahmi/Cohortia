Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Key Concepts
All-at-once deployment
Deploy to all instances simultaneously; fast but high risk and downtime.
Rolling deployment
Update in batches with health checks; requires backward compatibility during overlap.
Blue/Green deployment
Run two environments and shift traffic for zero downtime and instant rollback.
Summary

Each pattern balances risk, downtime, and cost differently. Use automation and observability to make any pattern safe.
