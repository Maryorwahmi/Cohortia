Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Trade-offs
All-at-once
Fast, low complexity, highest risk, low cost.
Rolling
Lower downtime, compatibility required, moderate complexity.
Blue/Green
Zero downtime, instant rollback, higher infrastructure cost.
Canary
Minimized blast radius, highest monitoring needs, greatest complexity.
Decision rule
Prioritize minimizing blast radius and rollback speed for high criticality systems; accept higher cost and complexity when business impact is large.
