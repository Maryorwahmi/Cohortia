Best Practices

Integrating Testing & Monitoring

The essential safety net for successful progressive rollouts.

Automated Testing

Execute various tests against the new version during initial traffic shifts:

  • •Integration Tests: Verify component interactions.
  • •Smoke Tests: Basic functionality checks.
  • •Performance Tests: Assess load handling.

Comprehensive Monitoring

Utilize AWS services for deep observability:

  • •CloudWatch: Metrics and logs for application health.
  • •AWS X-Ray: Distributed tracing for performance bottlenecks.
  • •Custom Alarms: Trigger automated rollbacks on critical issues.
Common Mistake

Relying solely on basic health checks without deep application-level metrics or automated rollback mechanisms can lead to widespread issues.

Key Takeaway

Robust automated testing and comprehensive monitoring with CloudWatch alarms are non-negotiable for ensuring the safety and reliability of progressive deployments.
