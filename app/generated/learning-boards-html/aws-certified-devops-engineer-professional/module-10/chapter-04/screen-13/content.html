Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Progressive Deployment

A deployment strategy that gradually introduces new software versions to users, minimizing risk and enabling rapid rollbacks.

Linear Deployment

A CodeDeploy traffic shifting option where traffic is shifted in equal increments over a specified time period.

Canary Deployment

A CodeDeploy traffic shifting option where a small percentage of traffic is shifted first, followed by the remaining traffic after a wait period.

Summary Takeaway

Progressive deployments, including linear and canary strategies, are crucial for safe and controlled software releases, minimizing risk and ensuring stability.
