Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

More essential terms to solidify your understanding.

CloudWatch Alarms

Used in CodeDeploy to monitor metrics during a deployment and automatically trigger rollbacks if thresholds are breached.

Kubernetes Rolling Update

The default deployment strategy in Kubernetes, where Pods are gradually replaced with new versions, controlled by maxUnavailable and maxSurge.

Service Mesh (EKS)

Tools like Istio or AWS App Mesh that provide advanced traffic management, observability, and security for microservices in Kubernetes.

Summary Takeaway

CloudWatch alarms, Kubernetes rolling updates, and Service Meshes are critical for robust monitoring, controlled rollouts, and advanced traffic management in modern cloud deployments.
