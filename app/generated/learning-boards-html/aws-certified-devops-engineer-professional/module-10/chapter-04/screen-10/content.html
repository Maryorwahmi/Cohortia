EKS Advanced Traffic Management

Fine-Grained Control with Service Mesh & Ingress

Implementing sophisticated canary and blue/green strategies in EKS.

Service Mesh

Tools like Istio or AWS App Mesh provide an infrastructure layer for managing microservice communication.

  • •Weighted routing for canary releases.
  • •Header-based routing for specific user segments.
  • •Enhanced observability and security.

Ingress Controllers

Such as Nginx Ingress Controller or ALB Ingress Controller for EKS.

  • •Configured to split traffic between service versions.
  • •ALB weighted target groups for canary deployments.
  • •Manages external access to services within the cluster.
Key Takeaway

Service Meshes and Ingress Controllers provide the sophisticated traffic management capabilities needed for advanced progressive deployments in EKS.
