Safety Mechanism

CloudWatch Alarms for Automated Rollback

Ensuring stability with proactive monitoring during progressive deployments.

How Automated Rollbacks Work

  • •CodeDeploy monitors the new 'green' environment during traffic shifts.
  • •CloudWatch alarms are configured to watch critical metrics (e.g., 5xx errors, latency).
  • •If an alarm triggers, CodeDeploy automatically reverts traffic to the 'blue' environment.

This mechanism is configured in the CodeDeploy Deployment Group, linking specific CloudWatch alarms.

Key Takeaway

CloudWatch alarms provide an essential automated safety net, triggering immediate rollbacks to prevent widespread impact from deployment issues.

appspec.yaml snippet
version: 0.0
Resources:
  - TargetService:
      Type: AWS::ECS::Service
      Properties:
        TaskDefinition: <TASK_DEFINITION_ARN>
        LoadBalancerInfo:
          ContainerName: "your-container-name"
          ContainerPort: 80
Hooks:
  AfterAllowTestTraffic:
    - Location: scripts/run_integration_tests.sh
      Timeout: 600
      RunAs: root
# Note: Traffic shifting and rollback alarms are configured
# in the CodeDeploy Deployment Group, not directly in appspec.yaml.
Example: High CPU Alarm

Configure a CloudWatch alarm for CPUUtilization > 50% on the new ECS service. If triggered during a deployment, CodeDeploy will automatically roll back.
