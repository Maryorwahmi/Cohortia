Core Concept

Sharing — Knowledge and Eventing

Make knowledge, runbooks, and operational state visible and actionable across teams.

Shared repositories
Store code, IaC, and runbooks together so teams can review and update artifacts collaboratively.
Centralized dashboards
Shared CloudWatch dashboards provide a common operational picture for developers and operators.
Event-driven alerts
SNS or EventBridge notify stakeholders of important events and enable rapid coordination.
Key Takeaway

Sharing requires maintenance: keep dashboards and runbooks current and make notifications actionable.

Concrete scenario

A change to architecture stored in a repo triggers EventBridge notifications and an updated dashboard entry, helping stakeholders validate impact and adjust runbooks.

Misconception
Simply storing files centrally is not enough; sharing must be paired with discoverability, ownership, and maintenance practices.
