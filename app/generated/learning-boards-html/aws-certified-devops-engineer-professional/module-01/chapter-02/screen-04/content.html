Core Concept

The CALMS Framework — Overview

A single framework to align people, process, and tools for DevOps success.

Culture
Collaboration and shared responsibility; the foundation for trusting automation and feedback.
Automation
Remove repetitive tasks across delivery and operations with repeatable processes.
Lean
Deliver small changes, reduce waste, and optimize flow through the pipeline.
Measurement
Collect metrics and logs to validate changes and detect issues quickly.
Sharing
Make knowledge and artifacts accessible so teams learn and iterate together.
Key Takeaway

Pillars must be combined: automation without culture or measurement has limited value.

Example scenario

A team adopts CloudFormation templates and deploys more often in small batches. They measure latency with CloudWatch and share runbooks in the same repository that holds the templates. This combination reduces lead time and improves recovery speed after incidents.

Common misconception
Installing automation tools by itself will not create DevOps — culture and measurement must accompany tools for lasting change.
