Definition

Culture — The Foundation

Collaboration, trust, and shared responsibility enable faster learning and safer operations.

Concrete example
Developers and operators share a CloudWatch dashboard and maintain a common CodeCommit repository. When an alarm fires, both teams can see traces and logs and collaborate on a fix.
Misconception
Culture is not changed by a mandate or a new tool alone; it requires small structural practices and shared rituals.
Key Takeaway

Concrete shared artifacts — dashboards, repos, runbooks — reinforce cultural shifts and improve incident outcomes.

Practical signs of healthy culture
  • Shared ownership of code and operational dashboards.
  • Blameless postmortems and continuous improvement.
  • Teams can update runbooks and IaC as part of standard work.
