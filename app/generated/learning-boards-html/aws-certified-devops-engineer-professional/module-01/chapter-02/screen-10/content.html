Core Concept

Lean Practices in DevOps

Optimize flow, reduce batch sizes, and remove non-value work to deliver faster and safer.

Small changes
Frequent, small deployments reduce risk and make problems easier to isolate and fix.
On-demand environments
CloudFormation and CDK enable quick provisioning and teardown for testing, avoiding wasted resources.
Microservices and serverless
Lambda and container services promote independent deployments and smaller batch sizes.
Common misconception

Speed without measurement is not lean. You must validate each change with metrics and tests.

Example

A team splits a large feature into multiple small deployments behind feature flags. Each deploy is tested and monitored independently, allowing safe rollback and fast iteration.

Why it matters
Smaller batch sizes reduce cognitive load during reviews and make deployments more predictable.
