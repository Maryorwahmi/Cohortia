Hierarchy

RTO and RPO mapping

Define the acceptable downtime and data loss, then choose designs that meet those goals: Multi-AZ, cross-region replication, or cold standby options based on constraints.

Key elements
Multi-AZ, Auto Scaling, ALB/NLB, Route 53 policies, backups, and recovery runbooks tied to RTO/RPO.
Design trade-offs
Higher availability often increases cost and operational complexity; choose the least complex design that meets objectives.
Concrete example

Requirement: near-zero downtime. Choose Multi-AZ compute with Auto Scaling, ALB health checks, and automated failover procedures. For full region loss protection, add cross-region replication and tested failover runbooks.

Key Takeaway

Translate stated RTO/RPO goals into specific architecture choices and justify them by cost, complexity, and recovery assurance.
