Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Learning Objectives
Checkpoints
Timed practice

Run three 75-question, 180-minute practice exams. Track which domains consume the most time and which question types slow you down.

Domain focus

Assign study hours proportional to domain weightings, adding extra labs for SDLC Automation and Incident Response.

Question triage

Practice eliminating clearly wrong answers and marking ambiguous items to avoid spending too long on a single question.

Pitfall review

Create a list of common traps — misread constraints, overvaluing rare features, and ignoring operational overhead.

Study Plan Snippet
Example week: 8 hours SDLC Automation labs, 5 hours IaC tasks, 4 hours monitoring dashboards, 6 hours scenario practice and full timed mock. Adjust based on practice exam results.
Mini-check
Can you justify a single architectural choice by trade-off: cost, availability, security, or operational complexity? If yes, you are ready to move from reading to applied practice.
