Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Time Management & Question Triage
Checklist
  1. Identify core problem and explicit constraints (cost, availability, security).
  2. Scan answers to eliminate clearly wrong choices.
  3. If uncertain, mark and move on; return during review time.
  4. Reserve 20–30 minutes at the end to revisit marked items.
Practice rule
Aim for an average near two minutes and twenty-four seconds but allow buffer for long scenarios and review.
Concrete example

Long question asks for most cost-effective highly available design. Label constraints: COST, AVAIL. Find options meeting availability, eliminate ones with unnecessary cross-region duplicates for cost, then compare remaining for operational overhead.

Key Takeaway

Practice triage and review as a repeating routine: it protects your score and reduces time pressure during the exam.
