Definition

Format, duration, and score

Multiple-choice and multiple-response; ~75 questions; 180 minutes; passing 750/1000. Use these numbers to design pacing and review strategy.

Pacing math
180 min / 75 q ≈ 2 min 24 sec per question. Use as target but adjust for long scenarios.
Scoring
Aim for consistent accuracy across domains; marking uncertain items helps protect final score.
Example

A long scenario asks for the most highly available option. Instead of comparing every architecture detail, identify key availability controls: Multi-AZ, Auto Scaling, and load balancing. Eliminate single-AZ or non-redundant options quickly, then compare remaining choices for cost and operational complexity.

Key Takeaway

Turn timing into a routine: triage, answer, mark, and reserve review time to protect your score.

Common mistakes
  • Spending too long on one scenario and running out of review time.
  • Answering before identifying the constraint axis (cost, security, availability).
  • Choosing a technically valid but suboptimal option that doesn't match the stated requirement.
Check your approach
Strategy card
If you can eliminate two incorrect answers quickly, mark and return with more time to compare the remaining choices carefully.
Pro tip
Create a shorthand in practice exams to tag constraints like COST, AVAIL, SEC so you can scan stems faster under time pressure.
