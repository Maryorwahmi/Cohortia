Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Credential hygiene
Cleanup process
  1. Audit IAM users and keys for age and usage.
  2. Rotate or disable old keys immediately.
  3. Replace broad permissions with scoped policies.
  4. Delete test users after confirming no dependencies.
Document your rotation schedule and automate reminders. Treat inactive credentials as potential exposures until removed.
Common mistake
Leaving learning users or keys in the account and assuming they won't be abused later.
Key Takeaway

Make cleanup part of the workflow: rotate, restrict, and delete credentials you no longer need.
