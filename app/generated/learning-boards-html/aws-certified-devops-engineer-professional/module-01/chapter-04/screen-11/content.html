What are quotas?
Quotas limit the number of resources or concurrent operations in your account and region. They prevent abuse and ensure service stability.
Example quota
Maximum number of CloudFormation stacks per region — hit it and stack creation will fail.
Action
Use Service Quotas console to view and request increases for soft limits.
Pro tip
Schedule quota increase requests before heavy runs. Include region, resource type, and expected scale in the request form to speed approval.
Key Takeaway

Service quotas can block automation. Inspect them and request increases in advance when planning large operations.
