Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Deploy & Rollback
Deploy stage responsibilities
Apply validated artifacts, monitor health, and enable rollback if needed.
Deployment options
CodeDeploy, CloudFormation, or direct artifact hosting for static sites.
Gradual releases
Canary or linear deployments reduce blast radius by shifting small traffic percentages first.
Rollback strategies
Keep immutable artifacts and tags to revert quickly to a known-good version on failures.
Key Takeaway

Treat deploy as a risk-managed operation. Use gradual rollouts and automated rollback triggers to protect production.

Example decision
Use canary for user-facing APIs and immediate rollback on health alarms. Use full replace for simple static assets.
