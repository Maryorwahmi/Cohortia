Comparison

EC2 vs Containers vs Serverless

EC2 (Virtual machines)

Maximum control, more patching and maintenance required; use when custom OS-level control is necessary.

Containers

Portable packaging for microservices, pairs with an orchestrator for scaling and lifecycle management.

Serverless

Minimal infrastructure management, ideal for event-driven tasks and short-lived workloads.

Concrete scenario

A batch job with heavy CPU use may be best on containers or VMs, while a webhook handler that runs briefly is a natural fit for functions. Consider operational cost, scaling needs, and team expertise.

Common misconception

Serverless eliminates the need for monitoring and governance. It reduces some tasks but still requires observability and deployment controls.

Key Takeaway

Match compute to the workload's lifecycle and your team's operational capacity for sustainable delivery.
