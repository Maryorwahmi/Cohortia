Comparison

Blue/Green · Canary · Immutable

Blue/Green

Two environments, quick traffic switch, simple rollback by reverting traffic.

Canary

Gradual exposure to limit blast radius while observing key metrics before full promotion.

Immutable

Create new infrastructure for each release, eliminating in-place changes and configuration drift.

Concrete example

A canary rollout begins with 5% traffic, monitoring error rate and latency. If metrics are stable, promotion continues; if not, rollback happens while the rest of traffic remains unaffected.

Common mistake

Choosing a complex rollout without monitoring or rollback mechanisms increases risk; choose a strategy you can operate reliably.

Key Takeaway

Select a deployment strategy that matches your observability, rollback capability, and organizational readiness.
