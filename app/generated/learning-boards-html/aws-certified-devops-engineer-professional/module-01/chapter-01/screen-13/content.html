Architecture

Redundancy, Failover, and DR

Redundancy

Use multiple availability zones and redundant services to survive component failures.

Automatic failover

Automate detection and traffic shift so recovery is fast and repeatable.

Disaster recovery

Plan for region-level outages with defined recovery time and data preservation strategies.

Concrete example

A service deployed across multiple zones with a health-checked load balancer automatically shifts traffic away from failed instances, while periodic DR drills rehearse multi-region recovery procedures.

Common mistake

Relying on manual recovery steps that are seldom tested; automated failover and rehearsal are essential to prove resilience.

Key Takeaway

Design for failure: automate failover, test recovery plans, and match the architecture to recovery objectives.
