What SCPs do

SCPs set a permission boundary for all principals in an account or OU. They prevent actions regardless of IAM policy allowances, acting as a preventive control at scale.

Example policy intent
Deny ec2:AuthorizeSecurityGroupIngress when the ingress rule contains 0.0.0.0/0 on critical ports, or deny S3 bucket creation without server-side encryption.
Design tips
  • Test SCPs in a non-production OU before broad roll out.
  • Keep SCPs narrowly focused on high-risk actions to avoid blocking legitimate automation.
  • Pair SCPs with Config rules and CI checks for layered protection.
