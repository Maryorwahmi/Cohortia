Introduction to CDK

Use programming languages to model and provision AWS resources with reusable constructs and synthesis to CloudFormation.

Estimated time: 15–25 minutes
This lesson shows how CDK replaces verbose templates with constructs, how to initialize a project, and how CDK generates deployable CloudFormation. It emphasizes practical steps you will perform locally and explains why synthesis and bootstrapping exist.
In this lesson
