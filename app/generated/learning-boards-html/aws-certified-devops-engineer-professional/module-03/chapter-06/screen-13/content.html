Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Lab Walkthrough
Instructor steps (summary)
  1. Deploy the template with the failure parameter enabled to trigger a custom resource error.
  2. Open the CloudFormation Events and find the FAILED event and status reason.
  3. Fix the template (disable the failing resource) and redeploy until UPDATE_COMPLETE.
  4. Enable termination protection, attempt deletion to observe the protection, then disable and delete.
  5. Verify retained resources and snapshots behave as expected after deletion.

Follow the Events log and CloudWatch logs for custom resource Lambdas for precise error information.
Key takeaway
This lab ties together diagnosis (Events and logs), recovery (fixing the template and redeploying), and protection (DeletionPolicy and termination protection) to demonstrate safe operational routines for IaC.
