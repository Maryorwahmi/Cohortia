Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Key Concepts
Declarative IaC
Describe desired state; CloudFormation keeps the internal record and applies changes to reach that state.
Automatic rollback
CloudFormation reverts failed changes automatically, but rollbacks can fail and require manual recovery.
Protection policies
Use DeletionPolicy and termination protection to prevent accidental data loss and stack deletion.
Takeaway

Read Events first, design idempotent handlers, and apply retention protections so deployments and rollbacks remain predictable and safe.
