Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Termination Protection
Why enable it
Prevents accidental deletions, requiring explicit disabling before a delete can proceed. Use for production stacks that hold critical infrastructure.
Operational notes
  • Toggling is deliberate — plan automation accordingly.
  • Combines well with DeletionPolicy for layered protection.
Practice workflow
After a successful deployment, enable termination protection for the stack. When you must delete the stack, disable protection explicitly, then proceed with deletion. Confirm retained resources exist after deletion if DeletionPolicy was used.
Key Takeaway

Stack termination protection adds a deliberate safety step before destructive actions, reducing accidental loss in production.
