Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Idempotency & Roles
Idempotency checklist
  • Detect existing resources before creating new ones.
  • Make update handlers converge to a stable configuration.
  • Make delete handlers tolerant of partial state.
Permissions checklist
  • Use least-privilege for deployment roles.
  • When Access Denied appears, add only the minimal permission required.
  • Avoid granting broad administrative rights for production deployments.
Why this matters
Combined, idempotency and proper permissions make deployments resilient to retries and reduce the impact of failures or rollbacks on production systems.
