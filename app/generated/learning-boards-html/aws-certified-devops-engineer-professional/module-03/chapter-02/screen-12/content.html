Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Common Mistakes & Best Practices
Top Mistakes
  • Not uploading nested templates to S3.
  • Defining Conditions but not applying them.
  • Skipping Change Sets in production.
Best Practices
  • Centralize region constants in Mappings.
  • Keep child stack outputs minimal and documented.
  • Use Stack Policies to protect critical resources.
Operational Tips
  • Integrate change set review into CI approvals.
  • Use descriptive logical IDs for policy targeting.
  • Test nested stacks independently before composing.
