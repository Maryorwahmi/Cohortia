Intrinsic Functions
Built-in helpers for string assembly, substitution, conditional values, and lookups.
Mappings
Static lookup tables for region or environment-specific values, retrieved with FindInMap.
Conditions
Logical gates that determine whether resources or properties are created.
Summary

Use these constructs together to create templates that work across regions, minimize risk, and are easier for teams to maintain.
