CloudFormation for Complex Architectures and Cross-Stack References
Design modular stacks, share values safely, extend CloudFormation with Lambda-backed custom resources, and deploy consistently across accounts and regions with StackSets.
In this lesson
Plan multi-tier stacks, use Export and Fn::ImportValue, build custom resources for unsupported actions, and automate multi-account deployments with StackSets.
Why it matters
These patterns let teams scale infrastructure management while keeping ownership and lifecycle boundaries clear.
Explore Key Topics
Exports & Imports
Share VPC IDs and subnet lists across stacks with Export and Fn::ImportValue, but manage deletion order carefully.
Custom Resources
Extend provisioning with Lambda for actions CloudFormation can't do, ensuring idempotent behavior and clear error handling.
StackSets
Deploy consistent baseline stacks to many accounts/regions from a single administrator account using a StackSet.
Concept Map
Stacks, Exports, Lambda, StackSets
