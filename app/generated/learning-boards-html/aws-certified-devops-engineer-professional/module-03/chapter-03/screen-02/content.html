Design multi-tier stacks
Decompose networks, applications, and data into focused CloudFormation stacks to enable reuse and clear ownership.
Mnemonic: Layered cards = separation of concerns

02
Use Cross-Stack References
Export outputs and retrieve them with Fn::ImportValue to share resource identifiers between stacks, honoring region scope and lifecycle order.
Mnemonic: Export → shared key, Import → direct lookup
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Integrate Custom Resources
Invoke Lambda from CloudFormation to perform non-native provisioning operations and return success/failure events to the stack lifecycle.
Mnemonic: ServiceToken = Lambda bridge
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Deploy across accounts with StackSets
Use StackSets and the service-managed permission model to roll out templates to multiple accounts and regions from an administrator account.
Mnemonic: StackSet = single template, many targets
