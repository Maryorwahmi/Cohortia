Module 03 › Chapter 3.5 › Key Concepts
L3 Constructs
Opinionated patterns that reduce boilerplate and enforce defaults.
Custom Constructs
Reusable components that encode organizational standards and outputs.
Aspects
Traversal-based rules for tagging, validation, and governance across constructs.
CDK Pipelines
Automate synth, test, and deploy stages with cross-account support and approval steps.
CfnInclude
Import existing CloudFormation templates for incremental migration; included templates remain raw CFN.
Summary
Choose patterns for speed, constructs for policy, aspects for governance, pipelines for automation, and CfnInclude for safe migration.
