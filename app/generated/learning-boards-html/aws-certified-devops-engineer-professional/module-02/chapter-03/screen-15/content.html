What to Remember
Scope Targets
Use tags or Auto Scaling groups to keep deployments predictable as infrastructure changes.
ValidateService
Use strong end-to-end checks that verify functionality, not just process health.
Monitor & Rollback
Integrate CloudWatch alarms with canary or blue/green strategies to enable automated rollback on regressions.
Final thought
Treat deployments as guarded operations: define checks, monitor meaningful signals, and ensure quick rollback paths so users are protected during releases.
