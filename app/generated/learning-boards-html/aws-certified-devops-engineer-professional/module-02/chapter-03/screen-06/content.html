Comparison
Deployment Configurations
AllAtOnce
Fastest; updates every target simultaneously. Use for non-critical or quickly reversible changes.
OneAtATime
Safest; updates one target at a time. Use for stateful services or risky migrations.
HalfAtATime
Balanced speed and safety; updates groups of targets concurrently.
Example decision
If you have 10 stateless web servers behind an ALB with health checks, HalfAtATime or OneAtATime will reduce risk of widespread failure while keeping reasonable deployment time.
Checklist before choosing
  • Is the service stateful or stateless?
  • Are client sessions sticky or short-lived?
  • How long does a rollback take?
  • Do you have robust health checks and monitoring?
Pro tip: Combine a conservative deployment configuration with ValidateService checks and CloudWatch alarms to automate safe rollbacks.
