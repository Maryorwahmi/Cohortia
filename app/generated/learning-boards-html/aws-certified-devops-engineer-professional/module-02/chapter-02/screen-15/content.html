Artifacts
Build outputs stored in S3 or pushed to ECR for subsequent pipeline stages.
Caching
Use S3 for shared caches and local caches for project-scoped speed; verify cache restores in logs.
Service role & logs
Grant least privilege to the CodeBuild service role and use CloudWatch logs to find failing commands and permission issues.
Final takeaway

Define your build recipe, secure your environment, validate cache behavior by re-running builds, and use logs to fix the smallest failing step.
