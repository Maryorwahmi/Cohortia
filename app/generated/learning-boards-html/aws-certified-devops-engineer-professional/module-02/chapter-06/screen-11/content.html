Comparison › Notification Strategies
Catch-all channel
Pros: simple to configure and guarantees delivery. Cons: high noise, harder to prioritize and escalate critical incidents.
When to avoid
Do not use for production-critical pipelines where timely action matters.
Granular topics + Lambda
Pros: route alerts by severity, format messages, and integrate with different channels like Slack or PagerDuty. Cons: more configuration and maintenance.
Best practice
Use per-pipeline or per-severity topics and a Lambda to enrich and route to third-party integrations.
Key Takeaway

Design channels to minimize noise and enable fast, appropriate responses. Use escalation and runbooks linked to each alert.
