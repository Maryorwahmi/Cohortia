Process Flow › CloudWatch Alarms

Alarm lifecycle

Metric → Threshold → Action. Choose statistic and period, then attach SNS or Lambda targets for notifications or remediation.

Example alarm
Metric: PipelineExecutionFailed, Statistic: Sum, Period: 5m, Threshold: >= 1 → Action: publish to MyPipelineAlertsTopic
Pro-tip
Use composite alarms or anomaly detection to reduce noise for metrics that fluctuate often.
Key Takeaway

Design alarms with realistic thresholds and tested targets so alerts are meaningful and enable timely action.

Notification targets
  • SNS topic to email or mobile channels.
  • Lambda for custom remediation or ticket creation.
  • Auto-scaling or CodeDeploy rollback hooks for automated fixes.
Common-mistake
Tying rollbacks to noisy metrics. Test rollback triggers in staging to avoid cascading failures during transient problems.
