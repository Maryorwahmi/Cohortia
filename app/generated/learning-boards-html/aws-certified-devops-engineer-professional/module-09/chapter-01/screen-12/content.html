Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › ECS vs EKS
ECS with EC2 or Fargate
ECS integrates tightly with AWS. EC2 launch type lets you manage instances; Fargate removes server management and runs containers serverlessly. ECS is often quicker to adopt for teams focused on AWS services.
EKS (Kubernetes)
EKS is a managed Kubernetes control plane, useful when you need the Kubernetes API, community tooling, or portability. It requires Kubernetes expertise and usually more operations effort.
Decision checklist
  • If you want AWS-native simplicity and less ops, prefer ECS with Fargate.
  • If you need Kubernetes features or multi-cloud portability, choose EKS.
  • Balance team expertise against required features before committing.
Reflective question: Does your team need Kubernetes-specific features, or will serverless containers with Fargate meet your deployment needs?
