Graviton, Image Lifecycle, and Auto Scaling

Advanced Strategies for Container Cost Efficiency on AWS

Graviton Processors

Utilize Graviton-based EC2 instances or `arm64` Fargate tasks for better price-performance, often leading to lower compute costs.

ECR Image Lifecycle Policies

Automatically delete old or untagged container images from ECR to reduce storage costs and keep repositories clean.

Robust Auto Scaling

Configure auto-scaling for ECS services or EKS node groups to match capacity with demand, scaling out during peaks and in during low periods.

Key Takeaway

Combining Graviton, ECR lifecycle management, and intelligent auto-scaling creates a highly cost-efficient and responsive container environment.

Container Cost Optimization Flow

Adopt Graviton

Switch to ARM-based processors for better price/performance.

Implement ECR Lifecycle

Automate deletion of old container images to save storage.

Configure Auto Scaling

Dynamically adjust resources based on demand to optimize costs.
