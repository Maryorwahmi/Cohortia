Containers vs. Serverless: Making the Right Choice

Decision Factors for Different Application Requirements

Containers (ECS/EKS/Fargate)

  • Long-running processes
  • Stateful applications (more control)
  • Specific runtime requirements (custom OS, GPU)
  • Predictable, high-volume workloads
  • Lift-and-shift migrations
  • Existing Kubernetes expertise

Serverless (Lambda)

  • Event-driven workloads
  • Intermittent or unpredictable workloads (scales from zero)
  • Microservices architecture
  • Rapid prototyping and development
  • Cost optimization for variable loads (no idle costs)
Key Takeaway

The choice between containers and serverless is not absolute; it depends on workload characteristics, operational overhead, and specific technical requirements.
