Canary Deployments & Rollback

Roll out changes gradually and monitor production metrics to decide whether to advance or revert.

Canary: shift a small percentage, observe behavior, then increase traffic if healthy.
Rollback: use CloudWatch alarms to trigger automated reversion via CodeDeploy or Lambda.
Misconception: canaries do not replace testing; they provide real traffic validation.
Checklist for Canary
  • Define initial traffic percentage and observation window.
  • Set CloudWatch alarms for error rates and latency that should trigger rollback.
  • Automate rollback and document manual rollback steps as a fallback.
Example: shift 5% of traffic for 15 minutes; if 5xx rate exceeds the threshold, CodeDeploy reverts traffic immediately to the previous version.
