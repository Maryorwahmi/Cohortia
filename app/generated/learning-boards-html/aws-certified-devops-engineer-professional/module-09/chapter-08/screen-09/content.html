Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Canary deployments with CodeDeploy
Canary pattern
Shift a small percentage of traffic to a new version, monitor alarms, then continue or rollback automatically.
Step 1: Publish new Lambda version after deployment.
Step 2: CodeDeploy updates alias to route a percentage to new version.
Step 3: Alarms are monitored; if healthy, traffic increases; if not, rollback occurs.
Pro Tip

Use a mix of error rate and invocation-based alarms to detect both overt failures and silent regressions such as missing output or empty responses.

Example rollout plan
Canary10Percent5Minutes: shift 10% for 5 minutes, evaluate alarms, then proceed. CodeDeployDefault.LambdaCanary10Percent5Minutes is a deploy config that matches this behavior.
Key Takeaway

Canaries give you a safety net: incremental exposure and automatic rollback keep production stable during change.
