Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Key Concepts — What to Remember
Serverless CI/CD
Automated pipeline that builds, tests, and deploys SAM/CDK and Lambda artifacts.
Canary Deployments
Gradually shift traffic with aliases and CodeDeploy and rollback on alarms.
Observability
CloudWatch logs/metrics and X-Ray traces for detecting and diagnosing issues.
Keep these concepts in mind when designing pipelines: immutable artifacts, layered tests, monitored rollouts, and least-privilege roles. They together reduce risk and increase delivery speed.
