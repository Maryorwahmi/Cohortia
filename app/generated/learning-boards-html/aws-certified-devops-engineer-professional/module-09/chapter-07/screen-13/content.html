Comparison › SAM vs CDK
SAM — declarative
Concise YAML for serverless resources, tight integration with sam local commands, and straightforward mapping to CloudFormation.
When to use: small to medium serverless apps that benefit from readable YAML and fast local iteration.
CDK — programmatic
Write infrastructure as code in Python or TypeScript, enabling constructs, loops, and object-oriented patterns for reusable architectures.
When to use: complex systems where reuse, abstraction, and programmatic generation reduce duplication.
Both generate CloudFormation under the hood. Choose the tool that best matches your team's workflow, and keep templates or stacks in version control as the source of truth for deployments.
