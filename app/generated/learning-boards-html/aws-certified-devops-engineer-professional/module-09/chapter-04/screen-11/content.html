Module 9 › Chapter 9.4 › Worker Node Options
Managed Node Groups
You manage EC2 instance types, lifecycle, and any node-level software. Use when you need host-level access or specialized networking and daemonsets.
Optimization options: use Spot for fault-tolerant workloads and right-size instances for pod density.
Fargate
Serverless compute for Pods removes instance management and isolates workloads. Ideal for bursty tasks and simpler operational footprint but not for all node-level requirements.
Cost consideration: evaluate Fargate pricing against EC2 Spot and reserved options based on workload profile.
Practical guidance
Map each workload to a compute model: critical services with steady load may favor optimized EC2 instances; isolated microservices or occasional jobs can benefit from Fargate's simplicity.
