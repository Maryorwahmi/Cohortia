Module 9 › Chapter 9.2 › Key Commands & Security
docker login
Use aws ecr get-login-password piped into docker login --password-stdin to avoid leaking tokens.
docker tag & push
Tag with <account>.dkr.ecr.<region>.amazonaws.com/repo:tag and push to upload layers to ECR.
scanOnPush
Enable scanning on push to detect vulnerabilities early and feed findings back into your pipeline decisions.
Final summary

Follow secure authentication patterns, tag and push correctly, automate lifecycle management, and integrate scanning into CI/CD to keep your image registry secure and manageable.
