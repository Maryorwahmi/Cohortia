Auto-scaling policy example
Scale-out when average CPU > 70% over a short period. Scale-in when CPU < 50% to reduce cost. Set min and max tasks to control bounds.
Min tasks: 1
Desired tasks: 2
Max tasks: 10
Health check tuning
Choose a health endpoint that reports application readiness. Set interval and unhealthy threshold so transient startup delays don't trigger replacement.
  • Health check path: / (or /health)
  • Interval: 30 seconds, Timeout: 5 seconds
  • Healthy threshold: 2, Unhealthy threshold: 2
Key Takeaway: Match health checks and scaling policies to your app's startup and load characteristics to avoid unnecessary restarts or cost spikes.
