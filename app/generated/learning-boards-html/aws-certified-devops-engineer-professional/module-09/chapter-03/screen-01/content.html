Orchestrating Containers with Amazon ECS and Fargate
Deploy containerized apps without managing EC2, integrate with ALB, and enable service auto-scaling.
In this lesson
You will create a Fargate cluster, author a task definition pointing at an ECR image, create a service attached to an ALB, and configure auto-scaling and health checks.
Why it matters
Fargate removes server management, speeds deployments, and allows teams to focus on application logic and scaling policies rather than node maintenance.
