Fargate vs EC2
Fargate: serverless and pay-per-task. EC2: self-managed hosts and deeper control.
ALB & Health Checks
Use target-type ip for Fargate and configure health checks to protect traffic routing.
Takeaway

ECS with Fargate simplifies running containers at scale. Combine correct task definitions, ALB integration, and autoscaling to build resilient services while adhering to least-privilege IAM.
