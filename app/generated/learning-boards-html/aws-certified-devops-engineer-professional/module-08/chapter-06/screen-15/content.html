Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › What to Remember
Centralize Logs
Keep CloudTrail logs encrypted and centralized for reliable audit evidence.
Encode Rules
Store managed and custom rules in source control and test before wide deployment.
Aggregate Findings
Use Security Hub to normalize findings and reduce alert fatigue through prioritization.
Final takeaway
Start small, automate one rule, validate it in staging, then expand coverage and add safe remediation where appropriate.
