Identity and Access Management for DevOps

Practical rules for identities, policies, temporary credentials, and organizational guardrails.

Estimated time: 10–15 min
Why this chapter matters
Small permission mistakes lead to large breaches. We'll keep your CI/CD and services secure while enabling automation.
Approach
Start with least privilege, use roles and temporary credentials, audit regularly, and apply SCP guardrails for multi-account safety.
