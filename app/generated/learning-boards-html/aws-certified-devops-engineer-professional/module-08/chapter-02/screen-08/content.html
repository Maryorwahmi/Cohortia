Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › IAM Access Analyzer
What Access Analyzer Does
It inspects resource-based policies and computes whether external access is possible, producing findings you can review and act on.
Example Finding
An S3 bucket policy allows Principal "*". Access Analyzer marks the bucket as publicly accessible and shows the statement to remediate.
Action
Review the flagged statement, scope the resource ARN, add conditions, or change the principal to remove unintended access.
Operational Tips
  • Include Access Analyzer checks in your regular policy audits and CI validation.
  • Prioritize findings that expose resources to anonymous or external accounts.
  • Document remediation steps and track reoccurring policy misconfigurations.
Key Takeaway

Use Access Analyzer findings to close accidental access paths quickly, and integrate checks into your deployment lifecycle.
