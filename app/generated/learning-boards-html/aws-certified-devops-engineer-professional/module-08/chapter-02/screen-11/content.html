Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Common Mistakes
Top Mistakes
  • Overly broad permissions (s3:* or ec2:*) granted for convenience.
  • Hardcoding access keys into code, images, or CI scripts.
  • No MFA for users and root account not hardened.
  • Ignoring Access Analyzer and CloudTrail findings.
Remediation Actions
Create automated scans to detect embedded keys, require policy reviews for changes, enforce MFA in IAM, and apply SCPs for critical guardrails. Use CloudTrail as the single source of truth for investigations.
Warning

Avoid quick fixes that broaden permissions. Always prefer iterative tightening and automated checks.
