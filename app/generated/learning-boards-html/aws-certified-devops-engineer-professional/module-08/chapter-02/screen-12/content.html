Least Privilege
Grant only the permissions required for the task and iterate by testing missing actions.
Roles & Temp Creds
Prefer roles for services and CI/CD to eliminate long-lived access keys.
Guardrails & Detection
Use SCPs, Access Analyzer, and CloudTrail to prevent and detect unintended access across accounts.
Summary

Combine least privilege, role-based access, and organization guardrails for a secure DevOps identity strategy.
