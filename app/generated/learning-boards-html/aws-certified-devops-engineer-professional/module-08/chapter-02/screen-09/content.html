Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Service Control Policies
What SCPs Do
SCPs limit the maximum permissions that can be granted to identities within member accounts; they do not grant permissions themselves.
Example
Deny ec2:RunInstances for certain instance types in development OUs to control cost and risk.
Caution
An overly broad SCP can break automation; always test and iterate in a sandbox OU first.
Operational Steps
  1. Inventory actions required by automation across accounts.
  2. Draft a restrictive SCP that denies only the highest-risk actions.
  3. Apply the SCP to a test OU, monitor failures, and refine exceptions.
  4. Roll out the SCP to production OUs with documented exceptions.
