Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Centralized Logging
Design for protected logs
Centralize logs, encrypt, version, and limit administrators to protect evidence integrity.
Key controls
  • Separate logging account with minimal admin access.
  • S3 encryption, versioning, and MFA delete where policy allows.
  • Automated alerting on configuration changes and unexpected deletions.
Retention & compliance
Define retention by regulation and business needs. Automate lifecycle transitions to cheaper storage while meeting preservation requirements.
Why it matters
If logs can be altered, forensic timelines break down. Centralized, immutable logs are the backbone of trustworthy investigations and compliance reporting.
