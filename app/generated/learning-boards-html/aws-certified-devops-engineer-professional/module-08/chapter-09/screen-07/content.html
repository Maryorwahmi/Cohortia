What to check after automation
  • Are EBS snapshots present and immutable?
  • Were security groups replaced with the isolation group?
  • Was the finding updated in Security Hub or ticketing system?
Common mistake
Giving broad IAM permissions to remediation functions makes your automation a new attack surface. Grant only what is necessary.
