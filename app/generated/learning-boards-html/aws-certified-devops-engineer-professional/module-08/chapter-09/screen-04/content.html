What is a playbook?
A concise, repeatable step list for a particular incident type. It reduces ambiguity under pressure.
Example: EC2 compromise
  1. Mark incident and notify incident commander.
  2. Create EBS snapshot of attached volumes.
  3. Replace security groups with isolation group.
  4. Collect CloudTrail and VPC Flow Logs for the instance.
Living document
Version your playbooks, run tabletop exercises, and update after drills or incidents so steps remain accurate and executable.
Common mistake
Playbooks that lack clear ownership or testing lead to confusion and slow response during real incidents.
Playbook anatomy
  • Scope and triggers — what counts as this incident type.
  • Roles — who is the incident commander, technical lead, comms lead.
  • Steps — ordered, testable actions with checkpoints.
  • Evidence preservation — logs, snapshots, and where they are stored.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Short, tested playbooks with clear ownership enable fast, consistent containment while preserving evidence for forensics.
