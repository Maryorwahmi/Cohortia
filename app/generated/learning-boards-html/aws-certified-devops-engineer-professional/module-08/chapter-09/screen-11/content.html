Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Communication
Roles and notifications
Establish who decides, who acts, and who communicates during incidents.
Incident commander
Owns decisions, escalations, and external notifications.
Technical lead
Directs containment, forensic collection, and remediation steps.
Communications lead
Crafts messages for stakeholders and ensures legal/regulatory review when needed.
Checklist
  • Secure, auditable communication channels.
  • Pre-approved notification templates and escalation maps.
  • Real-time incident log with timestamps and actors.
