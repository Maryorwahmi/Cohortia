Quick scenario
A GuardDuty finding shows an EC2 instance communicating with a known malicious IP. Your automation should isolate the instance, snapshot its volumes, and notify the security team while preserving logs.
Why isolate
Isolating prevents further malicious activity and limits lateral movement while forensics are collected.
Why snapshot
A snapshot preserves disk state so investigators can examine evidence without altering the original system.
Starter checklist
  • Enable GuardDuty in each region and forward findings.
  • Centralize CloudTrail logs in a separate logging account and enable encryption/versioning.
  • Create tested playbooks and automation documents for high-severity events.
