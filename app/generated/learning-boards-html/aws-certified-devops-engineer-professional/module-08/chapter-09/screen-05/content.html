Incident Response Phases
A clear sequence reduces confusion and protects evidence.
Preparation
Enable logging, train teams, write playbooks, and configure detection sources like GuardDuty and CloudWatch.
Identification
Confirm an event via findings and alarms. Validate scope before taking containment actions.
Containment
Isolate affected resources and take snapshots to preserve evidence while stopping further activity.
Eradication
Remove the root cause: patch, remove malicious artifacts, or revoke credentials as needed.
Recovery
Restore services from known-good images or validated snapshots and monitor closely for recurrence.
Post-Incident Analysis
Recreate a timeline with CloudTrail and VPC Flow Logs, document lessons learned, and update playbooks.
Key Takeaway

Follow phases deliberately: containment preserves evidence, eradication removes the threat, and post-incident analysis closes the loop for continuous improvement.
