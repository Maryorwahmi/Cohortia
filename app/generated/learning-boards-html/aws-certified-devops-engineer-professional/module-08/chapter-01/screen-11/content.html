Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Least Privilege and IAM
Definition

Design minimal permissions

Create narrowly-scoped roles and automate policy reviews to prevent privilege escalation and limit impact from compromise.

Practical pattern
Start with least permissions, test in an isolated environment, and add only required permissions. Use role assumption for cross-account access.
Key Takeaway

Least privilege reduces attack surface. Make policy scoping and automated review part of your DevSecOps pipeline.

Example & Common Mistake
Concrete example
Assign a Lambda role permission to read a specific S3 bucket ARN instead of granting full S3 access. Validate during test runs and tighten as needed.
Common mistake
Using overly-permissive managed policies or wildcards for speed, which creates unnecessary exposure and harder remediation later.
