Comparison
Data Classification
Translate sensitivity into concrete encryption and access policies.
Public
Low sensitivity. AWS-managed encryption is acceptable; monitoring is minimal.
Confidential
Requires KMS with customer-managed keys, stricter IAM and audit trails.
Restricted
Highest controls: dedicated key policies, rotation schedules, and limited decryption principals.
Policy mapping
For each classification, define: key ownership (AWS vs. customer), rotation cadence, required network protections (VPC endpoints, TLS), and audit expectations.
