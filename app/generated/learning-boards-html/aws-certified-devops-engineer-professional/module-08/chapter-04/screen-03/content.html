Learning Objectives — Practical outcomes
Translate objectives into deployable controls and automated checks.
Policy Enforcement
Create bucket policies that deny uploads lacking KMS encryption, then test via CLI to confirm Access Denied for non-compliant uploads.
Automated Renewals
Use ACM to reduce certificate maintenance and attach managed certs to ALBs and distributions to keep connections secure.
Rotation & Audit
Rotate keys with KMS rotation or Secrets Manager and rely on CloudTrail to provide audit records for key usage and changes.
