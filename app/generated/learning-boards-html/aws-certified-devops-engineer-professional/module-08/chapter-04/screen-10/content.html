Core Concept
VPC Endpoints and private paths
Use endpoints to keep traffic inside AWS while maintaining TLS for security.
Benefits
  • Traffic between VPC and services remains on the AWS network.
  • No need for internet gateway, NAT, or public IPs for service access.
  • Reduces attack surface when combined with IAM and resource policies.
Example
A backend service uploads logs to S3 using a gateway VPC endpoint. The service uses TLS and a bucket policy that requires KMS encryption, keeping data private and encrypted both in transit and at rest.
Misconception

VPC endpoints do not replace TLS. They reduce exposure but you must still use encrypted channels and access controls.
