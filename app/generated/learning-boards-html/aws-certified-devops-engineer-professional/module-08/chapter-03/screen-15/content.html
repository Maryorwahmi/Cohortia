Secret Management
SecureString or Secrets Manager keep secrets out of source and enable rotation.
Least Privilege
Grant CI roles only the exact actions and resource ARNs they need to operate.
Image Scanning & Testing
SAST/SCA in build, ECR scanning on push, and DAST in staging provide layered detection.
Summary

Centralize secrets, tighten permissions, and scan artifacts — together they turn the pipeline into a security gate.
