Attack surface: third-party components

Base images, libraries, plugins, and CI tools expand your pipeline's trust boundaries. Vet and control these components.

Inventory
Track base images and dependencies used in your builds and container images.
Rebuild and scan
Rebuild third-party images under your account and scan before trusting them.
Pin and update
Pin versions, track CVE feeds, and trigger rebuilds when critical issues arrive.

Practical steps

  • Use SCA to detect vulnerable dependencies.
  • Scan base images and store trusted snapshots in a private registry.
  • Limit plugin permissions and audit their usage.
Misconception
Assuming widely used open-source images are safe by default; they still require scanning and provenance validation.
