Module 08 › Chapter 8.7 › Continuous Scanning & Remediation
Continuous scanning patterns
  • Schedule periodic Inspector scans for running instances and images.
  • Trigger scans in CI after image builds or dependency updates.
  • Centralize findings and prioritize by severity and business context.
Automated remediation example
EventBridge can route high-severity Inspector findings to automation that isolates a host or queues a rebuild. Only automate safe, reversible actions and keep human approval for risky steps.
Checklist before automation
  • Is the action reversible or non-destructive?
  • Are tests and smoke checks in place after remediation?
  • Is the owner team notified and able to override?
