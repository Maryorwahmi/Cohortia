Module 08 › Chapter 8.7 › Common Mistakes & Safety
Top mistakes
  • Manual patching at scale instead of automation.
  • Ignoring Inspector or SCA findings.
  • Using outdated AMIs and not rebuilding images.
  • Broad permissions for SSM and automation roles.
Safety notes
  1. Test patches and images in non-production first.
  2. Limit automated remediation to reversible steps.
  3. Record audit trails for compliance and forensics.
Pro tip

Keep a clear rollback plan tied to each automation so you can recover quickly from regressions.
