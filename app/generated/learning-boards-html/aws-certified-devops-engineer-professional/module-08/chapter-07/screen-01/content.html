Vulnerability Management and Patching Strategies
Automate patching, scan images and instances, and bake secure golden AMIs that reduce drift and simplify rollbacks.
In this lesson

Understand Patch Manager, Inspector, and Image Builder and how they integrate into CI/CD to keep your fleet secure.

Why it matters

Unpatched systems are an easy attack vector. Automation reduces human error, enforces baselines, and enables rapid, tested updates.

Explore Key Topics
Patch Manager
Define baselines, groups, and maintenance windows to automate OS updates.
Amazon Inspector
Automate vulnerability scans for EC2 and ECR images and act on findings early.
EC2 Image Builder
Bake golden AMIs so new instances start patched and hardened.
