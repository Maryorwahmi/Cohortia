Achieving Fault Tolerance

Explore patterns and AWS services that prevent cascading failures and keep your application available under partial failures.

Duration: short module
In this lesson

Practical patterns: circuit breakers, retries, idempotency, SQS/SNS, Lambda DLQs, graceful degradation and backpressure.

Why it matters

Preventing cascading failures reduces user impact and operational toil during incidents.

Explore key topics
Circuit Breakers

Retries & Backoff
Use exponential backoff with jitter to avoid synchronized retries.
SQS, SNS & DLQ
Decouple producers and consumers, and capture failed messages for inspection.
