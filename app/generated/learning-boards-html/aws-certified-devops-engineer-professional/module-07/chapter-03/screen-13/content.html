Comparison

Bad assumptions vs good operational practice

Bad assumption
  • Dependencies won’t fail.
  • Retries alone fix all transient issues.
  • No need to monitor DLQs or queue depth.
Better practice
  • Assume failures and provide fallbacks.
  • Use backoff, jitter, and circuit breakers together.
  • Instrument queues and DLQs and alert on unusual growth.
Action item

Add metrics and alerts for queue depth and DLQ counts.

Action item

Ensure critical operations are idempotent before enabling retries.

Action item
