Common failure causes
Map causes to controls and recovery techniques.
Hardware & network
Mitigate with multi-AZ redundancy and load balancing.
Software bugs
Mitigate with testing, canaries, and graceful degradation.
Human error
Mitigate with automation, IaC, and change controls.
Regional disasters
Mitigate with cross-region backups and DR architectures.
Concrete scenario
A misconfigured deployment causes a region-wide outage of a microservice. Restoring service required a tested rollback and a cross-region backup to restore data to a warm standby, highlighting the need for rehearsed runbooks.
Misconception
Backups alone are not recovery. You must test restores and automate recovery steps to meet RTO targets.
Pro Tip

Schedule DR rehearsals and include business stakeholders so RTO/RPO expectations remain realistic.
