Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › HA vs FT vs DR
High Availability
Minimize downtime using redundancy across AZs and automated failover.
Trade-offs: moderate cost, possible brief failovers.
Fault Tolerance
Continuous operation with zero data loss using active-active replication and strong consistency.
Trade-offs: higher cost, design complexity, potential latency impact.
Disaster Recovery
Restore operations after regional disasters using isolated recovery targets and defined RTO/RPO.
Trade-offs: variable cost depending on DR model; accepts some downtime or data loss based on targets.
Guidance
  • Use mixed strategies per component: HA for UI, FT for payment systems, DR for backups and recovery.
  • Define RTO and RPO before picking architecture; they are the primary constraints.
  • Test DR plans regularly — untested plans often fail when needed most.
