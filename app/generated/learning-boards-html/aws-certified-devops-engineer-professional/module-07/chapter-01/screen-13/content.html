Activity: RTO/RPO Analysis
Propose targets for three applications and justify them.
E-commerce Website
Suggested RTO: 15 minutes. Suggested RPO: 5 minutes. Justification: direct revenue and transactional integrity demand minimal downtime and near-zero data loss. Architecture: multi-AZ, synchronous or frequent replication, hot standby.
Inventory Management
Suggested RTO: 4 hours. Suggested RPO: 1 hour. Justification: operational disruption impacts fulfillment but can tolerate short outages. Architecture: multi-AZ with warm standby or scheduled replication.
Marketing Analytics
Suggested RTO: 24 hours. Suggested RPO: 24 hours. Justification: daily reports can be re-generated; backups are cost-effective. Architecture: daily snapshot backups and restore process.
Deliverable: Document your targets and provide short justifications and an outline of the architecture and tests you will run to validate the RTO and RPO.
Key Takeaway

RTO and RPO choices must be justified by business impact and validated through rehearsals and tests.
