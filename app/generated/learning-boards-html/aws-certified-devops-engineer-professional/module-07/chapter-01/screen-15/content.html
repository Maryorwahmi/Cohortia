Regions & AZs
Use AZs for HA, Regions for DR boundaries.
RTO & RPO
RTO = recovery speed, RPO = tolerated data loss window.
Mitigation Patterns
Multi-AZ, autoscaling, replication, backups, and cross-region DR.
Final Reflection
Which components of your system should be HA, FT, or DR? Use RTO/RPO and cost trade-offs to decide and schedule tests to validate the plan.
