Resilience decision flow
A practical sequence to align technical choices with business needs.
Step 1: Elicit impact
Ask stakeholders about revenue, user experience, and compliance requirements.
Step 2: Set RTO/RPO
Translate impact into measurable recovery objectives.
Step 3: Map options
Compare backup/restore, pilot light, warm standby, and active-active costs and benefits.
Example trade-offs
A warm standby may meet a 30-minute RTO at lower cost than full active-active. For systems where every transaction matters, multi-region active-active may be justified despite higher expense.
Misconception
Assuming cheaper equals sufficient. Cost must be weighed against potential revenue losses and reputational damage.
Pro Tip

Document assumptions, run cost models, and present options with quantified RTO/RPO and estimated monthly cost differences.
