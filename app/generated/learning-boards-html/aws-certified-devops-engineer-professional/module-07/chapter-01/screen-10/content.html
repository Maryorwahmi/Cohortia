Regions, AZs, and Edge Locations
The physical topology you rely on for availability and disaster recovery.
Region
A geographic area isolated from other regions; used for disaster recovery boundaries.
Availability Zone (AZ)
Physically separated data centers within a Region, connected with low-latency networking; used for HA.
Edge Location
Caches content and serves DNS closer to users; improves latency, not a substitute for AZs in resilience.
Example design choices
Use multi-AZ within a Region for HA. For DR, replicate to another Region; decide whether you need warm standby or full active-active based on RTO/RPO.
Misconception
Confusing edge caching with resilience. Edge locations reduce latency but do not hold authoritative compute or storage for failover.
Key Takeaway

Align your HA and DR boundaries with AZ and Region isolation. Use edge locations for performance, not as a DR substitute.
