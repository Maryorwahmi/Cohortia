Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › HA Patterns
HA pattern flow
A step-by-step operational sequence to sustain service.
1
Multi-AZ placement
Distribute instances and storage across AZs to prevent single-facility failure.
2
Load balancing & health checks
Route traffic to healthy instances and remove unhealthy ones automatically.
3
Autoscaling
Maintain capacity and replace failed instances without manual steps.
4
Managed replication for state
Use Multi-AZ databases or replicated storage to keep state available during failover.
Concrete example
An ALB directs users to EC2 instances in two AZs. Autoscaling maintains three healthy instances. When one AZ suffers a hardware failure, the autoscaling group launches replacements in the remaining AZ and the ALB routes traffic away from the unhealthy targets.
Misconception
Incorrect health check tuning can cause healthy instances to be marked unhealthy, triggering unnecessary scale actions. Tune checks and cooldowns carefully.
Key Takeaway

Combine placement, load balancing, autoscaling, and managed replication for resilient, self-healing services.
