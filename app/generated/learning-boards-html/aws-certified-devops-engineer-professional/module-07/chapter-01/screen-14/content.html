Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Key Concepts
High Availability
Redundancy and failover across AZs to minimize downtime.
Fault Tolerance
Design for zero downtime and zero data loss via active-active replication.
Disaster Recovery
Restore operations in another region guided by RTO and RPO.
Summary Takeaway
Design resilience to match business impact: set measurable RTO/RPO, choose appropriate HA/FT/DR patterns, and test your plans regularly to ensure they work when needed.
