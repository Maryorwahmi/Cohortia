Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Key Concepts
Backup & Restore
Regular backups stored cross-region; restore infrastructure after disaster. High RTO/RPO, low cost.
Pilot Light
Minimal core resources and replicated data ready to scale. Moderate cost with reduced RTO/RPO.
Warm Standby
A running, scaled-down environment with continuous replication for fast scaling and low RTO/RPO at higher cost.
Summary
Match your application's tolerance for downtime and data loss to the least-costly strategy that meets those targets, then test it regularly.
