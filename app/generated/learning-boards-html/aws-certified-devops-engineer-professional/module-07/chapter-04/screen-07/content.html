Architecture

Warm Standby

A fully provisioned, smaller-capacity environment runs continuously in the DR Region with data replication in place so it can be scaled to full capacity when needed.

Components
Smaller EC2 fleets or smaller instance sizes, ALBs, continuous DB replication, and synchronized static assets.
When to choose
  • Business-critical applications that tolerate only minutes of downtime.
  • When you need fast scale-up without full active-active cost.
Key Takeaway

Warm Standby offers fast recovery with continuous costs. Always include scale-up validation and failover drills in your test plan.
