Services

S3 · RDS · DynamoDB

S3 Cross-Region Replication

Replicate static assets to another Region for low RPO of object data and fast availability on failover.

RDS read replicas

Replicate relational data and promote a replica to primary during recovery to reduce RPO. Promotion requires orchestration and validation.

Considerations
  • Verify replication configuration and monitor lag.
  • Include parameter groups, roles, and secrets in the DR Region.
  • Test promotion and validation steps as part of DR drills.
Key Takeaway

Replication lowers RPO, but promotion steps, configuration, and integrity checks are required to achieve reliable recovery.
