Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Key Concepts
Multi-Site Active/Active
Fully active deployments across Regions for near-zero downtime and data loss, at the cost of complexity and expense.
RTO / RPO
RTO is maximum acceptable downtime; RPO is maximum tolerable data loss. Both must be agreed with stakeholders before choosing a strategy.
Test & Validate
A DR plan must be practiced hands-on, include data integrity checks, and document credential access in the DR Region.
Final Reflection
Which applications in your environment should be grouped together for the same DR strategy, and how will you prove recovery meets the agreed RTO and RPO?
