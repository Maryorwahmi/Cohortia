Learning Objectives

Build the skills to evaluate DR strategies against measurable recovery targets and cost constraints.

01
Evaluate DR strategies

Assess Backup & Restore, Pilot Light, Warm Standby, and Multi-Site Active/Active for a given application and explain why each is suitable or not.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Analyze RTO / RPO trade-offs

Translate business requirements into acceptable recovery time and data loss targets and show how cost rises as targets tighten.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Select services & patterns

Map S3 replication, RDS replicas, and DNS failover to a chosen DR approach and describe configuration considerations for each tier.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Plan and test recovery

Create a written DR plan with validation steps, credential management, and a testing cadence to ensure the strategy works in practice.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
