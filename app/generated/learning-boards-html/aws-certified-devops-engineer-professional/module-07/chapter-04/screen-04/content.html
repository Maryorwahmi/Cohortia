Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › DR vs HA
Definition

Disaster recovery

DR prepares for region-level failures using cross-region backups, replicas, and documented recovery steps. It focuses on restoring service under catastrophic conditions, not just single-server faults.

Concrete example

An Auto Scaling group in multiple AZs survives AZ failure but still needs a cross-region plan if the entire Region fails. Backups or a replica in another Region are required for full recovery.

Common misconception

Assuming AZ redundancy equals disaster recovery. AZ redundancy won't help if the Region's control plane or connectivity is impacted.

How to think about it
  • RTO: Maximum acceptable recovery time after a disaster.
  • RPO: Maximum acceptable data loss measured in time.
  • Choose the least complex strategy that satisfies both targets and your budget.
Key Takeaway

Disaster recovery is about region-level resilience: align RTO and RPO with a strategy and test it regularly.
