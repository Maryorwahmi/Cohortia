RTO, RPO, and Cost

Use a three-factor comparison to assign a strategy: how long can the business tolerate downtime, how much data loss is acceptable, and how much ongoing cost can you budget?

Quick guide
Backup & Restore: high RTO/RPO, low cost. Pilot Light: moderate RTO/RPO, moderate cost. Warm Standby: low RTO/RPO, higher cost. Active/Active: near-zero RTO/RPO, very high cost.
Decision flow
  1. Obtain RTO and RPO targets from stakeholders.
  2. Map targets to the strategy matrix to shortlist approaches.
  3. Estimate cost and pick the least expensive that meets targets.
Key Takeaway

Always align technical choices to measurable business targets and avoid over-engineering every application for the highest resilience level.
