Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Backup & Restore
Definition

Backup & Restore

Regularly back up data and store backups in another Region. Restore data and provision infrastructure only after a disaster.

When to use
Non-critical workloads that can accept hours or days of downtime and some data loss.
Automation tips
  • - Keep infrastructure-as-code templates in the DR Region.
  • - Pre-build AMIs and scripts to speed provisioning.
  • - Test restores on a schedule to validate backups.
Trade-offs
  • RTO: Hours to days.
  • RPO: Hours, depending on backup frequency.
  • Cost: Low ongoing cost, primarily storage.
Key Takeaway

Backup & Restore is economical for non-critical systems, but automation and repeated restore testing are necessary to make it reliable.
