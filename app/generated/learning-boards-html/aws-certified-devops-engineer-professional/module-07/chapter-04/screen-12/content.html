Runbook contents
  • Recovery steps for each tier with exact commands and templates.
  • Credential access plan and IAM roles in the DR Region.

Testing cadence

Schedule hands-on restores periodically and after any architecture change. Document lessons learned and update the runbook immediately.

Key Takeaway

A DR plan that is not tested frequently will likely fail during a real event. Practice real restores and refine your runbook after each drill.
