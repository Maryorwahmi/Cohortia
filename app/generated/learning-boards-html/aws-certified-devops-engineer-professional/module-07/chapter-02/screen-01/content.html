Designing for High Availability with AWS Services
Build redundancy and automatic failover across compute, data, storage, and DNS.
In this lesson

You will learn how ASGs and ELBs work together, how Multi‑AZ deployments protect your databases and caches, how S3 durability and CRR provide durable storage, and how Route 53 health checks enable DNS failover.

Why it matters

Downtime costs users, revenue, and trust. Proper HA design reduces both unplanned outages and manual recovery time by using automation and distributed placement.

Data

Storage

DNS
Route 53 health checks and failover routing ensure users reach healthy endpoints and support multi‑region strategies.
