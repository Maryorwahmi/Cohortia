Objective validation checklist
  • Deploy ASG+ALB across two AZs and manually terminate one instance to confirm automatic replacement.
  • Enable RDS Multi‑AZ and observe failover to the standby during a simulated primary failure.
  • Turn on S3 versioning and perform an overwrite then recover the previous version.
  • Create Route 53 health checks that probe the application’s /health endpoint and test failover by disabling the target.
Common validation pitfalls
  • Checking only host status instead of application health can mask service failures.
  • Hardcoded IPs or single VPN connections can create hidden single points of failure.
  • Assuming read replicas provide automatic failover—read replicas are for scaling or DR, not primary HA.
Pro tip
Automate deployments using CloudFormation or CDK so your HA configuration is repeatable and auditable.
Check your understanding
After a failover, how would you verify RDS is writable and your application can resume normal traffic? Consider both DNS and connection pooling impacts.
