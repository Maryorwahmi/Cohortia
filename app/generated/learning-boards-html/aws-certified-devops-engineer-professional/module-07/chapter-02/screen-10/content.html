Definition
Amazon S3 durability and replication
Objects are stored across AZs; use versioning and cross‑region replication for stronger protection and DR.
Best practices
  • Enable versioning to recover from accidental deletes or overwrites.
  • Use CRR to replicate objects automatically to another Region for disaster recovery.
  • Combine lifecycle policies with versioning to manage storage costs while preserving recoverability.
Validation step
Enable versioning on a test bucket, upload an object, overwrite it, then restore the previous version. Then enable CRR and verify that new versions are present in the destination bucket.
Common misconception
Assuming replication is instantaneous and includes prior versions; CRR copies new objects and versions after the rule is active, so plan accordingly.
Key takeaway

S3’s durability plus versioning and CRR form a powerful combination for object protection and cross‑region recovery.
