Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
ASG & ELB
ASGs replace unhealthy instances and maintain capacity while ELBs route traffic only to healthy targets to preserve user experience.
Multi‑AZ Data Services
RDS and ElastiCache Multi‑AZ protect databases and caches by provisioning standby replicas and enabling automatic failover.
S3 & Route 53
S3 durability plus versioning and CRR support data protection; Route 53 health checks and failover routing steer traffic to healthy endpoints.
Summary takeaway

Design HA across layers: compute, data, storage, and DNS. Use meaningful health checks, automate deployments, and test failover to ensure predictable recovery.
