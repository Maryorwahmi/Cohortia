Definition
RDS Multi‑AZ
Synchronous standby replicas that enable automatic failover within a Region.
How it's provisioned
  • A primary instance and a synchronous standby in a separate AZ.
  • Writes are synchronously replicated to the standby; upon failure, RDS promotes the standby to primary.
  • Failover is automatic but not instant — test reconnection behavior of your client drivers.
Contrast with read replicas
Read replicas replicate asynchronously and are primarily for read scaling or cross‑region DR. They are not guaranteed to be up to date for automatic failover and require manual promotion unless part of a managed failover plan.
Validation step
In a test environment, force a failover and measure the time for the standby to become primary and how your application reconnects. Tune client retry logic accordingly.
Key takeaway

Multi‑AZ is the recommended configuration for RDS when automatic failover and minimized data loss are required within a Region.
