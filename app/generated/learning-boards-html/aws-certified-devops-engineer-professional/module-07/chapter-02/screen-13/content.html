Pitfalls vs Best Practices
Pitfall
  • Hardcoded IPs or single VPN links.
  • Health checks that verify only host up/down.
  • Assuming read replicas equal automatic failover.
Best Practice
  • Design for redundancy across AZs and services.
  • Use app-level health checks and tune thresholds.
  • Automate with templates and test failover regularly.
Verification
Simulate failures in staging and observe ALB deregistration, ASG replacement, and RDS failover so you can quantify RTO and RPO.
Operational
Monitor and alert on health check failures, ASG replacements, and failover events to ensure quick detection and response.
Scenario analysis
If you have a single VPN to on‑prem systems and it fails, your application may be unreachable even if all AWS components are healthy. Remove single external dependencies or introduce redundant network paths to avoid this class of failure.
Key takeaway

High availability succeeds when architecture, health checks, automation, and verification work together. Address hidden single points of failure and practice failovers regularly.
