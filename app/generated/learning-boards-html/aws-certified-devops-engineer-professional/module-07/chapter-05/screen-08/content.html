Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Vaults & Lifecycles
Vaults

Secure and organize backups

Vaults act as containers for recovery points and control encryption and access boundaries. Use separate vaults for differing compliance or retention requirements.
Example
Production vault with KMS key A and 90-day retention; archive copies retained in a different vault with longer retention and separate key.
Lifecycle

Transition to colder storage

Lifecycle rules let you move backups to archival storage or delete them after a defined period. This reduces cost while preserving long-term recovery points when required.
Ensure KMS grants exist for cross-Region copies, or copies may be unreadable in the destination vault.
Key takeaway: Combine vaults, encryption and lifecycle to meet both security and cost requirements.
