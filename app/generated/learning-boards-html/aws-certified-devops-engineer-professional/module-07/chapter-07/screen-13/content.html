Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Decision Matrix
Decision flow
  1. Define RTO and RPO targets and acceptable cost.
  2. Characterize write patterns: single-region, multi-region concurrent, or sharded.
  3. Map data stores to replication options (Global Tables, CRR, RDS warm standby).
  4. Choose Active/Active when multi-master replication is feasible; otherwise choose Active/Passive.
  5. Test failover procedures and monitor replication and health checks continuously.
Example mappings
Global social app
Active/Active with DynamoDB Global Tables and Global Accelerator for low latency and fast failover.
Relational billing system
Active/Passive with warm standby RDS because of strict consistency needs.
Final thought

Document the chosen strategy and rehearse failover; the best architecture is the one you can operate reliably under stress.
