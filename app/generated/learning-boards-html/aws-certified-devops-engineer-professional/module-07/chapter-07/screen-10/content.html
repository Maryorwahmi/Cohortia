How Global Tables work
Multi-Region, multi-master replication managed by DynamoDB with eventual consistency.
Benefits
  • Local read and write performance in each Region.
  • Managed replication reduces operational overhead.
  • Good fit for high-volume, low-latency global writes.
Limitations
Eventual consistency across Regions; designs must tolerate brief divergence and implement conflict resolution where necessary.
Operational tips
  • Instrument replication lag monitoring.
  • Use item versioning or last-writer-wins when acceptable.
  • Test conflict scenarios and reconciliation logic before production.
Key Takeaway

Global Tables are ideal when you need multi-region writes with managed replication and can tolerate eventual convergence.
