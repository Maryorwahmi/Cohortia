Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Warm Standby Architecture
What differs from Pilot Light
Compute runs continuously at reduced capacity and is connected to replicated data so scaling up is mostly about capacity, not provisioning.
Warm Standby steps
  • Keep Cross-Region read replica active for the database.
  • Run a scaled-down Auto Scaling Group with desired capacity tuned for warm operation.
  • On failover, promote the replica, scale ASG to production, and update Route 53 failover records.
Example CloudFormation snippet
Resources:
  DRAppASG:
    Type: AWS::AutoScaling::AutoScalingGroup
    Properties:
      LaunchConfigurationName: !Ref DRLaunchConfig
      MinSize: '2'
      MaxSize: '10'
      DesiredCapacity: '2'
      VPCZoneIdentifier:
        - !Ref DRSubnet1
        - !Ref DRSubnet2
Benefits & Risks
  • Faster RTO than Pilot Light because compute is already running.
  • Higher ongoing cost than Pilot Light, but lower than active-active.
  • Must maintain parity in configuration, AMIs, and deployment pipelines to avoid drift.
Key Takeaway
Use Warm Standby when you need sub-hour RTO and can budget continuous, reduced-cost compute in the DR Region.
