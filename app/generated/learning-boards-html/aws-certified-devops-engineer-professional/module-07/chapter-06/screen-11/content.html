Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Pilot Light vs Warm Standby
Quick comparison
Pilot Light
Low ongoing cost, higher RTO, data replicated; compute launched on failover from IaC templates.
Warm Standby
Higher ongoing cost, lower RTO, scaled-down functional app runs continuously and scales up on failover.
Decision factors
  • RTO requirement: shorter RTO pushes you toward Warm Standby.
  • RPO tolerance: both depend on replication lag and strategy.
  • Budget constraints: Pilot Light saves money but increases provisioning steps on failover.
Key Takeaway
Choose based on RTO/RPO, automate the steps that are slowest, and rehearse to verify your assumptions.
