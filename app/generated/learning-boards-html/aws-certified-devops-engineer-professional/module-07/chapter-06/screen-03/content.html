Practical verification and drills
Steps to verify replication and recoverability
Verify Replication
Query primary and read replica endpoints. Confirm data appears on the replica after writes to the primary, and note replication lag.
Scale Warm Standby
Scale ASG desired capacity from warm values to production values, validate ALB health checks and performance under increased load.
Failback Planning
Plan reversing steps: re-sync data, re-point DNS back to primary, and re-run IaC to reconcile drift. Test these steps in a sandbox.
