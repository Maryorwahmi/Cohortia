Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Key Concepts
Pilot Light DR
Minimal compute; continuous data replication for ready recovery.
Warm Standby DR
Scaled-down functional app runs continuously for faster failover.
IaC & Automation
CloudFormation/CDK and Systems Manager/Lambda enable repeatable provisioning and orchestration.
Summary Takeaway

Design DR to meet required RTO and RPO. Use replication for data durability, IaC for provisioning, Route 53 for traffic control, and automation for safe, repeatable failovers.
