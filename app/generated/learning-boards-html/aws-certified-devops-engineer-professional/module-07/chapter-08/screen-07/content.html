Experiment Templates

Templates bundle targets, actions, stop conditions, and roles into a repeatable experiment definition.

Targets
Scoping to instances, ASGs, or tags.
Actions
What fault to inject: terminate, latency, throttle.
Stop conditions
CloudWatch alarms or metrics that halt the experiment.
Safety checklist
  • Define explicit stop conditions for user impact and system metrics.
  • Use least-privilege IAM roles for FIS actions.
  • Start with COUNT(1) or a small selection mode to limit blast radius.
Pre-built templates

Use templates for common scenarios like terminating an instance in an ASG, then adapt them to your environment with filters and stop conditions.

Common mistake

Running experiments without stop conditions or with overly broad targets can cause unplanned outages.
