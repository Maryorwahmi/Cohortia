Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Why Regular DR Testing Matters
Definition

DR testing is the process of executing recovery steps under controlled conditions to ensure RTO and RPO targets are met and runbooks work.

Concrete example
Database failover drill
Simulate primary DB failure, measure failover time, verify client connections and data integrity within RPO.
Common mistake
Rare testing
Testing only once a year or never leaves assumptions unvalidated and increases incident risk.
Key Takeaway

Make tests frequent, measurable, and part of your deployment cadence so recovery is practiced and repeatable.

Guided checklist
  • 1. Identify critical components and their RTO/RPO targets.
  • 2. Design a test to force the recovery path (e.g., instance termination, promote replica).
  • 3. Define success metrics and monitoring alarms.
  • 4. Run tests with a controlled blast radius; start in staging.
  • 5. Update runbooks and automate frequently repeated steps.
Why it matters

Testing reduces surprise, improves response time, and creates evidence you can present to auditors and stakeholders that DR works.
