Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Lab Walkthrough
Prerequisites
  • An Auto Scaling Group with at least one running EC2 instance.
  • An IAM role for FIS with permissions to terminate instances and describe ASGs.
  • A CloudWatch alarm to act as a stop condition.
Steps summary
  1. Create an FIS experiment template scoped to your ASG with COUNT(1).
  2. Add stop conditions for user-impacting metrics.
  3. Start the experiment and monitor EC2 and ASG behavior.
  4. Review results, update runbooks, and document lessons learned.
Observation checklist
  • Did the ASG launch a replacement to preserve desired capacity?
  • Did the ALB health checks continue to route traffic correctly?
  • Did stop conditions trigger (if configured)?
  • Were any unexpected errors or latency spikes observed?
Key Takeaway

Start small, observe carefully, and convert findings into improvements to runbooks and monitoring.
