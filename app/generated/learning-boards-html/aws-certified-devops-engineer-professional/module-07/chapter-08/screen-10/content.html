Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Stop Conditions
With Stop Conditions
  • Define CloudWatch alarm for error rate or latency.
  • Experiment automatically halts on alarm firing.
  • Minimizes customer impact and allows safe learning.
Example
Stop experiment if 5xx rate > 1% for 2 minutes.
Without Stop Conditions
  • Experiment continues even if user experience degrades.
  • Risk of cascading failures and prolonged outage.
  • Difficult to attribute impact and recover quickly.
Risk
No automatic halt increases blast radius and operational risk.
Key Takeaway

Always attach stop conditions that reflect customer impact. They enable safe, repeatable experiments and preserve production stability.
