Key Concepts

Essential ideas from this chapter.

Amazon ElastiCache

A fully managed in-memory caching service (Redis or Memcached) for application-level caching.

Connection Pooling

Managing a pool of reusable database connections to reduce overhead and improve responsiveness.

Proactive Testing

Load testing and chaos engineering to identify and address performance and resilience issues early.

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging managed services for caching and connection pooling, combined with rigorous proactive testing, is essential for building and maintaining resilient, high-performance applications.
