EBS Management

Managing EBS Snapshots and Eliminating Waste

Automating backups and identifying orphaned volumes for significant cost savings.

EBS Snapshot Lifecycle

Snapshots are incremental backups stored in S3. Implement lifecycle policies (e.g., via AWS Backup) to automatically delete old or unnecessary snapshots, preventing cost accumulation.

Automated Deletion

Unattached EBS Volumes

These are volumes no longer connected to an EC2 instance but still incurring charges. They often result from EC2 termination without 'Delete on Termination' set for data volumes. Identify and delete them.

Orphaned Resources
Key Takeaway

Proactive management of EBS snapshots through lifecycle policies and diligent identification and deletion of unattached volumes are crucial for minimizing EBS costs.

Safety Note

Always verify an EBS volume is truly unattached and not needed before deleting it. Deleting a volume is irreversible and can lead to data loss if not handled with extreme care.
