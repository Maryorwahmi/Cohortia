Core Concept

The Hidden Costs of Cloud Storage

Understanding how per-GB costs accumulate significantly over time, especially with large datasets and long retention periods.

1

Per-GB Costs Accumulate: While individual storage costs are low, they multiply rapidly with data volume and retention, leading to substantial overall expenses.

2

Misaligned Storage Classes: Storing infrequently accessed data in expensive, high-performance tiers is a common and costly mistake.

3

Orphaned Resources: Unattached EBS volumes or unmanaged S3 buckets continue to incur charges without providing value.

4

Lack of Automation: Manual management of data lifecycles is prone to errors and often misses optimization opportunities.

Concrete Example

The Unseen Log Archive

Imagine a company storing 50TB of application logs in S3 Standard. These logs are only accessed for compliance audits once a year. Keeping them in S3 Standard, designed for frequent access, costs significantly more than moving them to S3 Glacier Deep Archive, which is optimized for long-term, infrequent retrieval. Without a lifecycle policy, this oversight can cost thousands of dollars annually.

Key Takeaway

Proactive identification and optimization of storage resources are essential to prevent hidden costs from accumulating and impacting your cloud budget.

Common Mistake

One Size Fits All

Assuming all data should reside in the default, highest-performance storage class (e.g., S3 Standard, gp2 EBS) without considering actual access patterns. This leads to overspending on storage that doesn't require such performance or availability.
