EBS Optimization

Right-Sizing EBS Volumes for Cost Efficiency

Choosing the optimal volume type for your EC2 workloads to balance performance and cost.

gp3 Volumes (Recommended)

General-purpose SSD, offering a lower cost per GB than gp2. Allows independent provisioning of IOPS and throughput, making it highly flexible and cost-effective for most workloads.

General Purpose SSD

gp2 Volumes (Legacy)

Previous generation general-purpose SSD. Performance (IOPS) scales with volume size, which can lead to over-provisioning and higher costs if only high IOPS are needed. Migrate to gp3 for savings.

Legacy SSD

io1/io2 Volumes

Provisioned IOPS SSD, designed for I/O-intensive workloads like large databases. Offers the highest performance but at a significantly higher cost. Use only when extreme performance is critical.

High Performance SSD

st1/sc1 Volumes

Throughput Optimized HDD (st1) and Cold HDD (sc1). Designed for large, sequential workloads (st1) or infrequently accessed data (sc1) where lowest cost is paramount. Not suitable for boot volumes.

HDD Storage
Key Takeaway

Regularly review and right-size your EBS volumes, prioritizing gp3 for general-purpose workloads, to ensure you're not overpaying for performance you don't need.
