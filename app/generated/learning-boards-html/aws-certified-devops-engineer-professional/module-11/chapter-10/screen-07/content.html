Definition › FinOps Principles
FinOps: collaboration and accountability
Turn cost visibility into team decisions and continuous optimization.
Four principles
  • Collaboration: finance and engineers making trade-offs.
  • Visibility: provide detailed cost dashboards to teams.
  • Optimization: automate right-sizing and lifecycle policies.
  • Accountability: assign cost ownership to teams or projects.
Common mistake
Treating FinOps as chargeback alone undermines collaboration and learning between teams.
Concrete scenario
Give a product team a filtered Cost Explorer view and weekly anomaly alerts. The team discovers a test cluster left running and shuts it down, reducing waste. Accountability means the team documents the change so it doesn’t recur.
Pro tip
Embed cost signals in pull requests or pipeline checks so developers see trade-offs when changing infrastructure.
