Continuous Optimization Loop
A repeatable five-step cycle that prevents drift and reduces waste.
1
Monitor
Collect metrics, logs, and cost data continuously from CloudWatch and Cost Explorer.
2
Analyze
Use dashboards to find anomalies, waste, and performance bottlenecks.
3
Optimize
Right-size resources, add lifecycle policies, and consider purchasing commitments.
4
Govern
Enforce rules and policies to keep improvements from regressing.
5
Automate
Trigger remediation and CI/CD checks so governance becomes self-sustaining.
Concrete example
Detect a cost spike in Cost Explorer, trace to an untagged test cluster, right-size or terminate unused instances, add a Config rule to prevent untagged launches, and automate a notification to the owning team.
