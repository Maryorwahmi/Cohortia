Comparison › Common Mistakes vs Safeguards
Common mistakes
Policies without communication, overly broad denies, and unenforced rules create friction and drift.
Mistake: No communication
Teams are surprised by failing deployments and learn to bypass controls instead of adopting them.
Mistake: Too restrictive
Broad SCPs or automatic remediation that block valid work cause operational risk.
Safeguards
Start with detection, run policies in staging, and pair automation with notifications and exceptions.
Safeguard: Staged rollout
Apply policies first in a dev OU and measure impact before attaching to production OUs.
Safeguard: Notification + override
Notify owners and provide a do-not-stop or exception tag to avoid accidental disruption.
