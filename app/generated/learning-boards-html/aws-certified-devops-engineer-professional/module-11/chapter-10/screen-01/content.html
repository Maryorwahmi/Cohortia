Implementing Cost and Performance Governance
Make cost and performance a continuous, automated part of your DevOps workflow so you avoid drift and surprise spend.
In this lesson
You will learn tagging enforcement, AWS Config rules, SCP guardrails, FinOps principles, and how to embed cost checks into CI/CD.
Why it matters
Continuous governance prevents policy drift, ensures accurate cost allocation, and turns cost-awareness into a team habit rather than a late-stage cost-cutting exercise.
Explore Key Topics
Tagging Enforcement
Standardize required tags and use Config to detect missing tags.
SCP Guardrails
Prevent costly operations across accounts with organization-level deny policies.
FinOps & Automation
Bring finance, engineering, and product together and automate remediation to enforce outcomes.

Note: Governance must be communicated, tested, and iterated to avoid overly restrictive controls that block legitimate work.
