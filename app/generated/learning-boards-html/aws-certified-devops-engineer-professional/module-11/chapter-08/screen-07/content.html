CloudWatch: Your Observability Hub

Centralized monitoring for AWS resources and applications.

The Foundational Monitoring Service

AWS CloudWatch is the primary monitoring and observability service, providing data and actionable insights across your entire AWS environment.

  • •Comprehensive Data: Collects metrics from virtually all AWS services (EC2, S3, Lambda, etc.).
  • •Actionable Insights: Helps understand resource utilization, identify trends, and respond to operational events.
  • •Centralized View: Simplifies monitoring by consolidating data, reducing the need for multiple third-party tools.

Why It Matters: Proactive Operations

CloudWatch enables proactive identification of performance degradation, allowing DevOps teams to address issues before they impact users, ensuring high availability and reliability.

Example: EC2 CPU Monitoring

You can easily view the CPU utilization metric for your EC2 instances, set alarms if it exceeds a threshold, and even trigger Auto Scaling actions to maintain performance.

Key Takeaway

CloudWatch is your essential first line of defense for monitoring, providing the visibility needed to maintain healthy and efficient AWS workloads.
