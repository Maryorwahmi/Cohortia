Cost Explorer vs. AWS Budgets
Understanding the distinct roles of analysis and control.
Key Takeaway

Cost Explorer analyzes past and forecasts future spending, while AWS Budgets proactively monitors and enforces spending limits with alerts and actions.

AWS Cost Explorer

**Role:** Analysis & Visibility

Looks backward (historical data), analyzes trends, forecasts future spending. Helps identify cost drivers and optimization opportunities.

AWS Budgets

**Role:** Control & Enforcement

Looks forward (monitors current/projected spend), enforces limits via alerts and automated actions. Prevents budget overruns.
