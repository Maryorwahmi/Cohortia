Core Concept
AWS Cost Explorer: Your Financial Dashboard
Visualize and understand your AWS costs and usage over time.
Key Takeaway

AWS Cost Explorer offers granular visibility into your AWS spending, allowing for detailed analysis, custom reporting, and future cost forecasting.

Why it Matters

Without detailed cost visibility, optimizing your AWS spend is impossible. Cost Explorer provides the insights needed to make informed decisions and prevent budget overruns.

Example Scenario

High data transfer costs detected.

Using Cost Explorer, filter by 'Data Transfer' usage type, group by 'Region' and 'Service' to identify specific sources of egress traffic, such as an S3 bucket serving large files directly to the internet without CloudFront.

Common Pitfall

Simply reviewing the total monthly bill without drilling down into specific services, regions, or tags. This prevents identifying the actual cost drivers and opportunities for optimization.
