What to Remember
Core principles for effective AWS cost management.
Visibility

Use Cost Explorer to understand where your money is going and forecast future spend.

Control

Implement Budgets with alerts and actions to proactively manage and enforce spending limits.

Optimization

Integrate cost management into DevOps for continuous improvement and maximum value from AWS.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive cost management through visibility (Cost Explorer) and control (Budgets) is essential for efficient and profitable AWS operations.
