Configuring Budget Alerts and Actions
Automate responses to cost thresholds.
Key Takeaway

Budget Actions enable automated responses, such as stopping resources, when cost thresholds are met, providing a critical layer of automated cost control.

Example Scenario

A development environment budget hits 100% of its monthly limit. A budget action automatically stops all non-essential EC2 instances tagged `Environment: Dev` to prevent further charges.

Alert Types
Actual Threshold: Triggered when actual costs exceed the limit.
Forecasted Threshold: Triggered when costs are projected to exceed the limit.
Safety Note

Exercise extreme caution with budget actions that modify or terminate resources. Always test thoroughly in non-production environments and ensure the associated IAM role has minimal necessary permissions.
