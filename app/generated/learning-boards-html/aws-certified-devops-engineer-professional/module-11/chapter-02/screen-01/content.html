Leveraging AWS Budgets and Cost Explorer for Visibility
Understand and control your AWS spending with powerful native tools.

Welcome! In the world of cloud computing, especially with a service as dynamic as AWS, understanding where your money is going is not just good practice – it's essential for operational excellence and profitability. Without clear visibility into your AWS spending, any efforts to optimize costs can feel like guesswork. This chapter introduces you to two of AWS's most powerful tools designed to bring clarity and control to your cloud finances: AWS Cost Explorer and AWS Budgets. We'll explore how these services work together to provide deep insights into your historical costs, forecast future spending, and set up proactive alerts to prevent unexpected overruns. By the end of this module, you'll be equipped to navigate your AWS bill with confidence and implement strategies for continuous cost optimization, ensuring your cloud investments are both efficient and effective.
