Key Concepts
Essential takeaways for cost visibility and control.
AWS Cost Explorer

Visualizes and analyzes AWS costs and usage over time, enabling detailed cost driver identification.

AWS Budgets

Sets custom budgets for costs, usage, and reservations, providing proactive monitoring and alerts.

Budget Actions

Automated responses triggered by Budgets when thresholds are met, such as stopping resources to prevent overspending.

Summary Takeaway

Combine Cost Explorer's insights with Budgets' proactive controls and automated actions to achieve continuous cost optimization and maintain financial health in your AWS environment.
