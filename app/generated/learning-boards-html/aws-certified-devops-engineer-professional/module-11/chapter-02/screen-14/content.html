Key Concept
Reservation Utilization and Coverage
Maximizing value from Reserved Instances and Savings Plans.
Key Takeaway

Monitoring reservation utilization and coverage with AWS Budgets ensures you maximize discounts from RIs and Savings Plans by aligning commitments with actual usage.

Why it Matters

Underutilized reservations mean wasted money. Proactive monitoring helps adjust commitments to match workloads, unlocking significant cost savings.

Key Metrics
Utilization: Percentage of purchased reservations actively used.
Coverage: Percentage of eligible on-demand usage covered by reservations.
Example

Purchased RIs for `m5.large` but running `t3.medium` instances. This leads to low utilization for `m5.large` RIs and low coverage for `t3.medium` usage, missing potential savings.
