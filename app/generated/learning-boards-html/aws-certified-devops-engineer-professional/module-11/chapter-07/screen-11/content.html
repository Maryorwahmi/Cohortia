Global Cost Strategy

Reducing Inter-Region Data Transfer Expenses

Design regionally, compress data, and choose efficient connectivity options.

01

Minimize Cross-Region Traffic

Design applications to be as regional as possible, only replicating data or sending traffic across regions when absolutely necessary for business continuity or global reach.

02

Data Compression

Compress data before transferring it across regions to reduce the overall volume, thereby lowering transfer costs. This is highly effective for large datasets.

03

Direct Connect vs. VPN

For large, consistent data transfers between on-premises and AWS, AWS Direct Connect can be more cost-effective than VPN over the public internet.

Key Takeaway

Proactive architectural design and smart data handling are essential to control the high costs associated with inter-region data transfer.
