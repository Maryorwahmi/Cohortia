Global Architecture Costs

The High Cost of Cross-Region Data

Data transfer between AWS regions is significantly more expensive than within a single region.

Why Inter-Region Transfer Costs More

  • •
    Infrastructure Overhead: Requires dedicated, high-bandwidth global network infrastructure.
  • •
    Increased Latency: Data travels longer distances, impacting performance and potentially increasing retries.
  • •
    Common Scenarios: Disaster recovery replication, multi-region application communication, global data synchronization.
Key Takeaway

Always evaluate the necessity and volume of inter-region data transfer, as it can quickly become a major cost center.
