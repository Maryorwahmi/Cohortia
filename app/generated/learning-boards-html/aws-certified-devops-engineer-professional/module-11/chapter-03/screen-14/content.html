Holistic Strategy

Comprehensive EC2 Cost Optimization

Combining strategies for maximum savings

Synergistic Approach

The greatest cost savings come from combining multiple optimization strategies, not relying on a single technique. Each strategy reinforces the others.

Continuous Improvement

Cost optimization is an ongoing process. Regularly monitor, analyze, and adapt your strategies to your evolving infrastructure and workload patterns.

Beyond Cost: Efficiency & Resilience

Optimized architectures are not just cheaper; they are often more efficient, performant, and resilient, leading to better overall system health.

Integrated Optimization Pillars

Right-Sizing (Compute Optimizer)

Strategic Purchasing (On-Demand, RIs, SPs, Spot)

Dynamic Scaling (Auto Scaling Groups)

Non-Production Management (Scheduled Stop/Start)

Key Takeaway

Achieve maximum EC2 cost savings by integrating right-sizing, strategic purchasing, dynamic scaling, and non-production environment management into a continuous optimization strategy.
