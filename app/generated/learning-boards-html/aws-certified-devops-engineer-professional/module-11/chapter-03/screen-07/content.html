EC2 Purchasing Models: An Overview

Choosing the right financial commitment for your workloads

Impact on Costs

The purchasing model can dramatically alter your EC2 bill. Moving from On-Demand to a commitment-based option can yield savings of up to 75-90%.

Aligning with Workloads

Each model is best suited for different workload characteristics: unpredictable, stable, flexible, or fault-tolerant. Misalignment leads to overspending.

Common Mistake: Defaulting to On-Demand

Many users default to On-Demand for all instances, missing out on significant savings for predictable or interruptible workloads that could use RIs, Savings Plans, or Spot.
