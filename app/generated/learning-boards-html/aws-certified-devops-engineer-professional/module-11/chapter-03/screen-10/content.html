Dynamic Scaling

Auto Scaling Groups for Cost Optimization

Dynamically adjusting capacity to minimize idle resources

Elastic Capacity

ASGs automatically add or remove EC2 instances based on demand, ensuring you only pay for the capacity actively being used. This prevents idle resources from incurring costs.

Combining with Spot Instances

For even greater savings, use ASGs with a mixed instances policy to leverage Spot Instances for flexible parts of your workload, while maintaining a baseline with On-Demand or RIs.

Application Design

ASGs are most effective for stateless applications that can gracefully handle instances being added or removed. This ensures high availability and cost efficiency.

ASG Cost Optimization Flow

Monitor workload demand

Scale out instances during high demand

Scale in instances during low demand

Optimize costs by paying only for needed capacity

Key Takeaway

Auto Scaling Groups dynamically adjust EC2 capacity to demand, minimizing idle resources and optimizing costs, especially when integrated with Spot Instances.
