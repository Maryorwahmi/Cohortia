Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Visibility: Without tags vs. With tags
Without tags
Billing is opaque and costly to analyze.
Symptoms
  • Single large invoice with no owner mapping
  • Delayed cost allocation and manual spreadsheets
  • No quick way to identify high-cost services
Short-term remedy
Add basic reporting and run manual audits to identify expensive resources.
With tags
Billing becomes a set of filters that point to owners and projects.
Benefits
  • Slice costs by Project, Owner, or Environment
  • Automate alerts for untagged or high-cost resources
  • Assign clear financial accountability to teams
Practical enforcement
Require tags at provisioning and use Config rules or tagging APIs to detect exceptions and remediate automatically.
Checkpoint

If you can't answer "Which project paid for these resources?" within minutes, your environment lacks actionable visibility.
