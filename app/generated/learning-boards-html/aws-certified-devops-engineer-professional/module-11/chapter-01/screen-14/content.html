Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Key Concepts
Cost Optimization
Reduce cloud spend while maintaining performance through visibility and actions.
Waste
Over-provisioned, idle, or forgotten resources that add avoidable cost.
Resource Tagging
Key-value labels that enable cost allocation and owner accountability.
Takeaway
Start with a small, enforced tagging policy, automate detection and remediation, and include cost checks in IaC and reviews to shift from reactive cleanup to proactive cost-aware engineering.
