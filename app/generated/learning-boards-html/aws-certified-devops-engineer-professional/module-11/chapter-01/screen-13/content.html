Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Integrating cost awareness
Daily engineering habits
Embed cost checks into existing workflows so they are low friction.
Design reviews include cost
Require a short note on expected ongoing cost for significant infrastructure changes.
PR checklist item
Add a cost impact question to pull requests to surface potential bill increases early.
Regular reporting
Share tagged cost reports so teams see the monetary impact of their systems.
Concrete team actions
  1. Define owner and project tags and publish the taxonomy.
  2. Add required tags to IaC templates and CI checks.
  3. Automate scans and remediation for temporary resources.
Reflection
Which habit will you adopt this week: a PR cost checklist, an IaC default, or a scheduled scan? Pick one and apply it to a small scope first.
