Fargate Cost Savings

Harnessing Fargate Spot and Graviton Processors

Significant discounts for fault-tolerant and ARM-compatible workloads.

Fargate Spot Instances

Fargate Spot capacity offers up to 70% discounts for fault-tolerant tasks. It's ideal for workloads that can handle interruptions, such as batch processing or data analytics jobs.

  • • Up to 70% discount
  • • Best for fault-tolerant workloads
  • • Consider mixed On-Demand/Spot strategies
Key Takeaway

Leveraging Fargate Spot for appropriate workloads and adopting Graviton processors for compatible containers are powerful strategies to achieve substantial cost reductions in your containerized applications.

Graviton Processors for Fargate

Similar to Lambda, running Fargate tasks on ARM-based Graviton2 processors can provide significant price-performance improvements. Ensure your container images are compatible with ARM architecture.

  • • Improved price-performance
  • • Requires ARM-compatible container images
  • • A direct path to cost savings

Example: Batch Processing with Spot

A nightly data processing job can be configured to run entirely on Fargate Spot. If a Spot instance is reclaimed, the job can simply restart from its last checkpoint or be rescheduled. This fault-tolerance makes it an ideal candidate for the deep discounts offered by Spot capacity, significantly reducing the operational cost of the batch workload.
