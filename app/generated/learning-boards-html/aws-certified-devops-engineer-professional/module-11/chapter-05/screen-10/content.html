Cost-Saving Features

Aurora Serverless & Automatic Storage

Aurora offers unique features that enable significant cost savings, especially for variable workloads.

  • Aurora Serverless: Automatically starts, scales, and shuts down based on demand. Scales to zero when idle, meaning no cost when not in use. Ideal for intermittent or unpredictable workloads (dev/test, infrequent apps).
  • Automatic Storage Scaling: Aurora storage automatically scales up to 128 TB. You only pay for the storage you use, eliminating the need for manual over-provisioning.
Concrete Example

A staging environment database that is only active during business hours can leverage Aurora Serverless to scale to zero overnight, drastically reducing costs.

Key Takeaway

Aurora Serverless and automatic storage scaling provide unparalleled cost efficiency for variable and intermittent database workloads.
