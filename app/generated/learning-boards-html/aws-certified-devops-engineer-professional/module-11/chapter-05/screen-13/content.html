Core Principle

Balancing the Database Trilemma

Optimizing databases involves inherent trade-offs between cost, performance, and availability. Strategic decisions are key.

Cost

Minimize expenditure on compute, storage, and data transfer. Often involves trade-offs with performance or availability.

Performance

Ensure fast query execution, low latency, and high throughput. May require more expensive resources.

Availability

Maintain continuous operation and resilience to failures. Often achieved through redundancy, which increases cost.

Concrete Example

For a mission-critical e-commerce platform, high availability and performance might justify a Multi-AZ RDS deployment with Provisioned IOPS, even if it's more costly.

Key Takeaway

Effective database optimization requires understanding the trilemma and making informed, context-specific trade-offs that align with business priorities.

Decision Matrix: Balancing Priorities

Production Workloads

  • Prioritize: Availability, Performance
  • Cost: Optimize within performance/availability needs

Dev/Test Environments

  • Prioritize: Cost Efficiency
  • Performance/Availability: Accept lower tiers, scale down/stop when idle

Key Considerations

  • Application criticality and RTO/RPO
  • Traffic patterns (predictable vs. spiky)
  • Data retention policies
  • Budget constraints
