Purchasing Options

Commitment for Cost Savings

Reduce RDS costs significantly by committing to usage over time, similar to EC2.

Reserved Instances (RIs)

Up to 70% discount for 1 or 3-year commitments on specific instance types. Ideal for stable, predictable workloads.

Savings Plans

Flexible commitment to an hourly spend, offering discounts across compute services. Good for evolving instance needs.

Common Mistake

Not utilizing RIs or Savings Plans for stable production workloads, leading to higher on-demand costs.

Key Takeaway

Strategic purchasing options can drastically cut RDS compute costs, especially for consistent workloads.

Multi-AZ vs. Single-AZ Cost Trade-offs

Multi-AZ Deployment

High availability, disaster recovery. Synchronous replication. Doubles instance cost. Essential for production.

Single-AZ Deployment

Lower cost, less resilient. Tolerates some downtime. Suitable for non-production environments.

Read Replicas

Offload read-heavy queries from primary. Improves performance, but adds cost for additional instances.
