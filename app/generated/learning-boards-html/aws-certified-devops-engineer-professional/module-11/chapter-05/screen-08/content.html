Core Concept

Choosing Your DynamoDB Capacity

DynamoDB offers two capacity modes, each with distinct pricing and scaling behaviors, impacting your overall cost.

Provisioned Capacity

  • Specify RCUs/WCUs.
  • Cost-effective for predictable, consistent workloads.
  • Pay for what you provision, even if unused.

On-Demand Capacity

  • Automatically scales capacity.
  • Pay per request.
  • Ideal for unpredictable, spiky, or new workloads.
Common Mistake

Over-provisioning capacity in Provisioned mode for highly variable workloads, leading to wasted spend on unused RCUs/WCUs.

Key Takeaway

Align your DynamoDB capacity mode with your application's traffic patterns to achieve optimal cost efficiency.
