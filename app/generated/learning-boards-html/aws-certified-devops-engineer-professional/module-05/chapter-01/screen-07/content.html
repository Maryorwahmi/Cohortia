Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Storage Resolution
Standard Resolution
One-minute granularity. Best for steady-state metrics and dashboards where minute-level insights are sufficient.
High Resolution
One-second granularity. Use for fast spikes or short-duration events requiring immediate detection.
Recommendation
  • Start with standard resolution and test detection windows.
  • Promote only critical, bursty metrics to high-resolution.
  • Be mindful of increased data points and cost with high-resolution.
Key Takeaway

Match resolution to detection needs: frequency and business impact determine whether one-second granularity is justified.
