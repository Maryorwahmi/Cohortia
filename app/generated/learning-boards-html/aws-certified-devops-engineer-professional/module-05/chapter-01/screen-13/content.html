Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Designing Composite Alarms
Step-by-step
  1. Identify noisy alarms and candidate corroborating signals.
  2. Write a clear AlarmRule using AND/OR/NOT for logic.
  3. Test state transitions in staging by simulating child alarms.
  4. Integrate actions with SNS, runbooks, and paging systems.
  5. Iterate and tune thresholds after initial monitoring.
Example checklist
  • Do child alarms have compatible dimensions and periods?
  • Are ARNs and regions correct in AlarmRule?
  • Are actions enabled and pointing to the right SNS topics?
  • Has the on-call runbook been updated to mention composite logic?
Key Takeaway

Composite alarms improve signal quality when carefully designed, tested, and integrated with incident response practices.
