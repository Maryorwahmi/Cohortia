Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Custom Metrics
Publish application signals with deliberate namespaces, units, and dimensions.
Metric Math
Compute error rates and percentiles on-platform; ensure compatible metrics and periods.
Composite Alarms
Combine child alarm states with AND/OR/NOT to reduce noise and focus critical alerts.
Cross-Account Monitoring
Share metrics to a monitoring account with explicit links and least-privilege roles.
Design Principle
Model metrics for questions you will ask and verify before alerting.
Security Note
Follow least-privilege when granting monitoring access to avoid over-exposure of operational data.
Summary
Publish meaningful metrics, compute derived signals, reduce noise with composite alarms, and centralize observability securely.
