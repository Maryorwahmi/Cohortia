Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › What are Custom Metrics?
Definition
Custom metrics let you publish counters, gauges, and timers from your application into CloudWatch so you can monitor business and operational events not covered by AWS defaults.
Namespace
Group related metrics to avoid collisions and simplify queries.
Dimensions
Slice metrics by attributes like Region, InstanceId, or Stage.
Unit
Use Count, Milliseconds, Bytes to keep statistics meaningful.
Resolution
Choose standard or high-resolution based on how quickly values change.
Concrete example
Publish OrderCount and FailedOrderCount in namespace WebStore with Dimension Region=us-east-1. Later you can compute error rate per region.
Why it matters
Default metrics often lack business context. Custom metrics let you answer operational questions directly from CloudWatch without additional instrumentation or third-party metrics stores.
Common mistake
Over-indexing dimensions. High-cardinality dimensions can make queries slow and increase costs. Model for queries you will actually run.
Key Takeaway

Design metrics for the operational questions you need to answer; choose namespaces and dimensions deliberately.
