Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Backtest and historical data
Why history matters

Historical data reveals patterns like daily peaks, weekends, and monthly cycles. The backtest uses this history to train and validate the detector's baseline.

  • Short history: baseline noisy, more false positives.
  • Representative history: captures seasonality and peak behavior.
  • Missing data: causes gaps and misalignment in intervals.
Pro Tip: include recent weeks of normal operation; avoid including large incident windows as the only history.
Validation steps
Run backtest and check anomaly rate over historical period
Inspect baselines for expected peaks and troughs
Test with a synthetic anomaly to confirm detection
Misconception: more history is always better. While more data helps, include representative data rather than many irrelevant months that hide recent operational changes.
