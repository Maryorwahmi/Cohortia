Proactive Anomaly Detection with Amazon Lookout for Metrics

Learn how machine learning discovers unusual patterns in time-series data, cuts alert noise, and points you to the likely root cause so you can act fast.

Why this matters

Static thresholds miss context. ML-based detectors learn normal behavior, reduce false positives, and surface subtle regressions before users notice them.

Who benefits

DevOps engineers, SREs, and incident response teams who need timely, actionable alerts across services and regions.
