Keep header names exact and timestamps consistent. Place historical files under a historical prefix and new files under live.
CloudFormation snippet (metric set)
Resources:
  MyAnomalyDetector:
    Type: AWS::LookoutMetrics::Detector
    Properties:
      AnomalyDetectorName: MyApplicationPerformanceDetector
      AnomalyDetectorConfig:
        AnomalyDetectorFrequency: PT5M
      MetricSetList:
        - MetricSetName: ApplicationMetrics
          MetricList:
            - MetricName: ErrorRate
              AggregationFunction: AVG
            - MetricName: Latency
              AggregationFunction: AVG
          DimensionList:
            - ServiceName
            - Region
          TimestampColumn:
            ColumnName: timestamp
            ColumnFormat: "yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss"
          MetricSource:
            S3SourceConfig:
              RoleArn: arn:aws:iam::123456789012:role/LookoutMetricsS3AccessRole
              TemplatedPathList:
                - s3://your-metrics-bucket/app-metrics/.../metrics.csv
Remember to replace the role ARN and bucket paths with your values and grant least privilege.
