Best Practices
Sampling and cost optimization
A practical guide to choose rates and roll out tracing safely.
Start conservative
Begin with low global rates and increase for specific endpoints after validating value.
Prioritize errors
Create rules to capture 100% of error traffic or set higher fixed rates for 5xx responses.
Use reservoirs
Guarantee some traces per second during spikes for critical services to avoid blind spots.
Key Takeaway

Design sampling rules around business-critical paths and error conditions, validate in staging, and monitor ingestion trends to balance visibility and cost.
