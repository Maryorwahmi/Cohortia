Architecture

Choose the right target for remediation

Match target complexity to the remediation need and design handlers to be safe to rerun.

Lambda
Short-lived handlers for inspection and immediate fixes, such as attempting to restart an EC2 instance and logging details.
Step Functions
Orchestrate multi-step remediation: isolate, collect forensic data, patch, and validate. Good for conditional workflows and retries.
SNS & SQS
SNS for notifications to ops, SQS for buffering and decoupling processing when you need durability or rate control.
Design constraint: idempotency. Ensure your target actions can be repeated without harmful side effects because events may be retried or delivered more than once.
Scenario
EC2 instance stops unexpectedly. Option A: Lambda checks health and restarts. Option B: Step Functions capture logs, snapshot volumes, notify team, then decide to restart.
Safety checklist
  • Make actions idempotent.
  • Log inputs and outputs for audit.
  • Keep retries and backoff in mind.
  • Buffer noisy events with SQS if needed.
Key Takeaway

Start with the simplest target that meets your SLA; move to orchestration for multi-step or conditional remediation while ensuring idempotency.
