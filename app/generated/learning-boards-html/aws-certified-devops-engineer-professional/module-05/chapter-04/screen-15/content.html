Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › What to Remember
Dead-Letter Queue
SQS queue that stores messages SNS failed to deliver for later inspection and replay.
PagerDuty Integration
Use HTTPS subscription or Lambda transform to ensure the payload meets PagerDuty's expected schema.
Lambda-based Logic
Use Lambda to enrich, route, or transform alerts, but treat it as a critical, monitored component.
Final takeaway: combine structured topics, filter policies, DLQs, and optional Lambda transforms to build alerting pipelines you can trust.
