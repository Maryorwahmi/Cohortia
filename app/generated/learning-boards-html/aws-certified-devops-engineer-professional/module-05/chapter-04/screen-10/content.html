Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Lambda Alerting
Use cases
  • Message enrichment with context from DynamoDB or config stores.
  • Payload transformation to match PagerDuty or internal APIs.
  • Conditional routing: forward to different topics or endpoints based on content.
Operational checklist
  • Grant minimal IAM permissions to the Lambda role.
  • Emit structured logs and enable monitoring/alarms on errors.
  • Attach a DLQ for messages that the Lambda cannot process.
Analogy
Think of Lambda as a triage nurse: it enriches an alert and decides which specialist to call.
