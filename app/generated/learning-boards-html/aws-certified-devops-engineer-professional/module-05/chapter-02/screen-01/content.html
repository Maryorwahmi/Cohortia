Centralized Logging
Collect logs, run interactive queries, stream events for processing, and deliver to analytics stores.
What you'll do
Install agents, build subscription filters, decode events in Lambda, and stream to Firehose for storage.
Why it matters
Real-time visibility reduces MTTD and MTTI, while durable delivery supports audits and analytics.
In this lesson
Agent setup, Logs Insights, subscription filters, Lambda processing, and Firehose delivery configuration.
Collection
Agents and auto-collected AWS service logs.
Analysis
Logs Insights queries to find and aggregate events.
Delivery
Subscription filters to Lambda/Firehose and Firehose delivery to S3/Redshift/OpenSearch.
Visual Concept
CloudWatch Logs
Aggregates log groups and streams; apply retention and access control.
Subscription Filter
Filters events and streams them to Lambda or Firehose for processing or storage.
Kinesis Firehose
Buffers, transforms, compresses, and delivers to S3, Redshift, or OpenSearch.
