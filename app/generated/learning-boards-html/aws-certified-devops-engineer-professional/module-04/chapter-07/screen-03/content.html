Lab walkthrough
Lab steps (conceptual)
Step 1
Design CloudFormation template
Keep it focused and modular, expose only safe parameters.
Step 2
Create Product
Upload the template as a product and attach helpful documentation.
Step 3
Add to Portfolio
Group products for teams and set portfolio access controls.
Step 4
Apply Constraints
Use template constraints to limit parameters and launch constraints to assign a provisioning role.
Step 5
Test in Non-Prod
Verify tags, security groups, and that the launch role works as intended.
Step 6
Publish & Monitor
Roll out the portfolio and watch provisioned product statuses for errors or drift.
Checkpoint: each template should enforce tagging and produce predictable resources, and each launch role should follow the principle of least privilege.
