Module 4 › Chapter 4.7 › Launch Constraints
What a launch constraint does
When a product is launched, Service Catalog assumes the specified IAM role and uses its permissions to create resources. End-users need only permission to launch the product itself.
Best practices
  • Grant the launch role only the permissions necessary to provision resources declared in the template.
  • Test the role in non-production to ensure no missing permissions or excess privileges.
  • Use tagging and IAM condition keys to limit resource scope where possible.
Illustrative scenario
Developers can launch a web server product. They do not need ec2:RunInstances. Service Catalog assumes a launch role that has ec2:RunInstances and necessary tagging permissions to provision the instance on their behalf.
Misconception check: granting end-users product launch permissions does not give them underlying resource privileges when a proper launch role is used.
