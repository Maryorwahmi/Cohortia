Module 4 › Chapter 4.7 › Key Concepts
AWS Service Catalog
Create and manage a catalog of approved IT services for self-service provisioning.
Product
An IT service defined by a CloudFormation template representing a single deployable unit.
Portfolio
A collection of products organized for teams, roles, or environments to control access.
Summary

Design products carefully, group them into portfolios for access control, and use constraints and launch roles to enforce governance while enabling self-service.
