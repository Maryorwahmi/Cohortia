Module 4 › Chapter 4.7 › What is Service Catalog?
Definition
AWS Service Catalog lets administrators publish approved IT services as products. End-users can then browse and launch these products without owning direct permissions to the underlying AWS resources.
Analogy
Think of it as an internal app store: products are apps, portfolios are shelves, and constraints are the store policies.
Concrete Example
A "Development Web Server" product that provisions an EC2 instance with required tags and a security group. Developers supply only safe parameters like key pair and environment name.
Key Takeaway

Service Catalog decouples who requests infrastructure from who actually provisions it, enabling governed self-service.

Misconceptions & Quick Checks
  • Service Catalog is not a permissions shortcut; it requires careful IAM in launch roles.
  • It does not replace CloudFormation; products are usually CloudFormation templates.
  • Avoid exposing every possible template parameter to users; keep the UX simple.
Why it matters
Faster provisioning for teams and consistent infrastructure reduce operational friction and improve compliance.
