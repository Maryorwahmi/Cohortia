Module 4 › Chapter 4.7 › Best Practices
Keep templates simple
Modular templates are easier to maintain and test. Avoid exposing unnecessary parameters to users.
Least privilege launch roles
Grant only required permissions to the launch role. Excess privileges defeat the purpose of centralized provisioning.
Test in non-production
Validate constraints and roles in a sandbox portfolio before broad rollout.
Document each product
Provide concise instructions on expected parameters, outputs, and troubleshooting steps.
Enforce tagging
Include required tags in templates to support cost allocation and inventory tracking.
Regular reviews
Periodically review portfolios and remove stale or irrelevant products.
