Module 4 › Chapter 4.7 › Learning Objectives
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain Service Catalog
Describe the purpose of AWS Service Catalog and its benefits for self-service provisioning and governance.
You will learn how Service Catalog centralizes approved templates and reduces manual approvals. This matters because it speeds development and reduces inconsistent configurations.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Define Products & Portfolios
Identify how to model services as products and group them into portfolios for role-based access.
You will be able to design a product that maps to a specific infrastructure need, and place it in a portfolio that grants access only to the right teams.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Configure Constraints
Apply template and launch constraints to enforce parameter limits and centralized provisioning roles.
You will practice creating a template constraint to limit instance types and defining a launch role with minimum permissions to provision the product.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Empower End Users
Enable developers to browse and launch approved products while monitoring provisioned product status.
You will walk through the end-user workflow conceptually and learn how status and errors are surfaced so operations can maintain governance while teams self-serve.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
