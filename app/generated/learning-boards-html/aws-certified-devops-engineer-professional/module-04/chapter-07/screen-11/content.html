Module 4 › Chapter 4.7 › Provisioned Product Lifecycle
Typical statuses
Available
Provisioning finished successfully and resources are ready.
Under Change
Product update or change is being applied via CloudFormation.
Error
Provisioning failed; inspect CloudFormation events and permissions.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • Check CloudFormation events for the failing resource.
  • Verify the launch role has the exact permissions required.
  • Confirm template constraints did not block provided parameter values.
Governance actions
  • Inspect tags for cost allocation and inventory.
  • Review provisioned resources for drift and security posture.
