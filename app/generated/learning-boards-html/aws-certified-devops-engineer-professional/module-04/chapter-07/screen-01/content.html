Why this matters
Self-service provisioning reduces wait times and shadow IT while ensuring resources are created according to security, cost, and tagging policies. Service Catalog centralizes approved configurations and enforces governance through constraints and controlled launch roles.
Internal app store metaphor
Imagine a catalog where developers choose a vetted product like a development web server. They supply a few parameters, and Service Catalog provisions the resources using an IAM launch role. This decouples who requests from who can create resources.
