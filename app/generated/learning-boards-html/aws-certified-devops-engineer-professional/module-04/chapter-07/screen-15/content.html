Module 4 › Chapter 4.7 › Constraints & Launch
Constraint
A rule that governs how products are launched, implemented via template or launch constraints.
Template Constraint
Limits parameter choices at launch time to enforce cost and compliance policies.
Launch Constraint
Specifies the IAM role Service Catalog assumes to provision resources, centralizing permissions.
Final takeaway

Combine focused templates, sensible template constraints, and least-privilege launch roles to enable safe self-service provisioning while preserving governance.
