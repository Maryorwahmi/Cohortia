Launch steps (conceptual)
  1. Browse portfolios shared with your account to find the desired product.
  2. Select the product and enter only the allowed parameters, such as key pair or environment name.
  3. Submit the launch request; Service Catalog assumes the launch role to provision resources.
  4. Monitor the provisioned product status and troubleshoot with operations if an error appears.
Status & troubleshooting
Provisioned products show statuses like Available, Under Change, or Error. If a launch fails, check template constraints, launch role permissions, and CloudFormation error messages. Administrators should provide clear logs and contact points.
Pro Tip

Provide concise product documentation so end-users know which parameters to supply and what the expected outputs are.
