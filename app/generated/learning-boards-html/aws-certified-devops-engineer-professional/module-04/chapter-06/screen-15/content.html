Managed Rules
Pre-built checks for typical compliance needs; low maintenance.
Custom Rules
Lambda-backed rules for organization-specific policies; require testing.
Remediation Actions
Automated fixes triggered by rules; validate in non-production and use least privilege.
Final Takeaway
