Why it matters

The recorder defines what you can audit. Bad scope choices mean blind spots or unexpected costs.

Scope Trade-offs
Wide scope (all resources)
Pros: Complete visibility for audits. Cons: Higher storage, more evaluations, and potential cost.
Targeted scope
Pros: Lower cost and less noise. Cons: Possible blind spots if you miss a resource type.
Quick validation
Make a small configuration change, then open the resource's timeline in Config to confirm a new configuration item was recorded and contains expected attributes.
