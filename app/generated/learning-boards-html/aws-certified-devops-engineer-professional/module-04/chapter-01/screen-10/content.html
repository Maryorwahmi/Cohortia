Definition

IAM Instance Profile

An IAM role attached to an EC2 instance that grants the SSM Agent permission to call Systems Manager APIs and related services.

Checklist
  • Attach AmazonSSMManagedInstanceCore or equivalent.
  • Confirm role allows sending logs and inventory when needed.
  • Use least privilege and avoid embedding long-lived credentials.
Common pitfalls
  • No instance profile attached at launch — instance will not register as managed.
  • Custom policy missing specific SSM permissions results in permission denied during commands.
  • Assuming SSM works without a role — it relies on role-based permissions.
Example

Launch an EC2 with AmazonSSMManagedInstanceCore attached. If it does not appear in Managed instances, verify the role and agent service are both present.
