Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Mistakes & Safety
Top pitfalls
  • Missing or incorrect IAM service roles and instance profiles.
  • Non-idempotent recipes that fail on re-run.
  • Deploying untested cookbooks to production stacks.
Safe practices
  • Test recipes in a development stack and include acceptance checks.
  • Keep cookbooks/modules in version control and tag releases.
  • Plan rollback steps and handle errors in recipes gracefully.
Warning

Recipe failures during lifecycle events can prevent instances from coming online. Always test lifecycle flows and verify IAM permissions before scaling up a stack.
