Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › When to Use OpsWorks
Benefits
  • Managed Chef/Puppet reduces master maintenance.
  • Stacks model application lifecycle events cleanly.
  • Tight mapping of recipes to deployment stages improves repeatability.
Trade-offs
  • Requires Chef/Puppet expertise and cookbook/module testing.
  • Less flexible for ad-hoc operational tasks compared to Systems Manager.
  • Operational changes still required for recipe errors and permissions.
Prototype
Build a dev stack and validate lifecycle hooks and recipes before production.
Measure
Compare operational effort with managed master vs running your own servers.
Decide
Select OpsWorks when stack automation or managed masters match your constraints and skills.
