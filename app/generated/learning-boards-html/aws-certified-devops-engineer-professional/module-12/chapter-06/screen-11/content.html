Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › VPC Flow Logs
Why flow logs
They provide recorded evidence of network traffic and the actions applied, which is essential for identifying blocked or rejected traffic during an incident.
Example Logs Insights query
fields @timestamp, @message
| filter logStatus = "OK" and action = "REJECT"
| sort @timestamp desc
| limit 20
Replace filtering fields with dstAddr or dstPort to narrow results to the instance and service you care about.
Practical steps
  1. Ensure flow logs are enabled for the VPC or subnet and targeting CloudWatch Logs.
  2. Run a Logs Insights query for REJECT actions in the incident time window.
  3. Correlate srcAddr and dstAddr with your client and instance IPs to find the blocker.
Misconception
Flow logs do not show packet payloads; they show metadata. Use them to prove presence and action, then use instance logs for payload-level investigation.
