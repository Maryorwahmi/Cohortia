When to use Lookout
Use Lookout when you have multi-dimensional business or operational datasets and need automatic correlation and diagnosis across sources.
Capabilities
  • Connects to S3, RDS, Redshift, Kinesis, and supported SaaS tools.
  • Detects anomalies across dimensions and correlates signals.
  • Provides diagnostics to help identify likely root causes.
Concrete example
Lookout finds a sales drop and ties it to a spike in errors for a particular product category and region, pointing your investigation to a targeted subsystem or third-party issue.
Operational note

Provide clean, well-partitioned data and allow learning time. Use Lookout to augment, not replace, domain expertise when diagnosing anomalies.
