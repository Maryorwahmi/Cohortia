Choose resolution by use case
High-resolution (1s) for rapid detection or debugging; standard resolution for typical operational dashboards and long-term cost control. Prioritize high resolution for metrics that require immediate action.
Aggregation patterns
  • Aggregate in-process to reduce API calls.
  • Publish percentiles or aggregated counts instead of raw events.
  • Use dimensions to slice only when meaningful.
Concrete example
During a flash sale, payment latency might spike for short bursts. Enable high-resolution for PaymentGatewayLatency metric for quicker detection. For daily revenue totals, standard resolution suffices.
Common mistake

Setting all metrics to high resolution increases costs and yields little extra signal. Select based on actionability.
