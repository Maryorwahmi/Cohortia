Action Item Example
Add DB connection pre-warming during instance startup. Owner: DB team. Acceptance: p99 latency stays below 300ms during replacement events in follow-up experiment.
Iteration Cycle
After fixes, run targeted experiments to validate each remediation. Use the same steady-state metrics and keep blast radius small until proven safe. Track change over time to show increased confidence for stakeholders.
Misconception

A single successful experiment does not guarantee future resilience. Regular, automated experiments and monitoring are required to maintain confidence.

Why it matters

Closing the loop ensures chaos engineering leads to improved observability, better incident response, and fewer customer-facing outages over time.
