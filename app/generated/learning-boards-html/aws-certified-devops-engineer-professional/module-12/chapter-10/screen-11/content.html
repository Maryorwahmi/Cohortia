Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › IAM for FIS
Role Design Pattern
Service role scoped to experiment resources with minimal actions.
Minimal Actions
  • ec2:DescribeInstances
  • ec2:TerminateInstances (scoped ARNs)
  • cloudwatch:DescribeAlarms
Scoping Resources
Prefer explicit resource ARNs for your ASG, instances, or RDS instances. Avoid giving blanket access to all resources in the account.
Sample policy fragment (illustrative)
{
  "Effect": "Allow",
  "Action": [
    "ec2:DescribeInstances",
    "ec2:TerminateInstances",
    "cloudwatch:DescribeAlarms"
  ],
  "Resource": ["arn:aws:ec2:us-east-1:123456789012:instance/*"]
}
Scope the Resource value to the exact ARNs used by your experiment in production to minimize blast radius.
Operational Controls
Audit who can create or edit FIS templates. Restrict template creation to a small set of engineers and require reviews. Keep a change log linking template versions to experiment runs.
Risk note

If the IAM role is too broad, an attacker or misconfigured experiment could terminate large swaths of infrastructure. Least privilege reduces that risk.

Pro tip

Use separate roles for staging and production experiments. Validate role permissions in a safe sandbox before granting production access.
