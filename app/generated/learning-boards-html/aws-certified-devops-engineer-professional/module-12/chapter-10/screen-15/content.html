Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Key Concepts
AWS Fault Injection Simulator
Managed service for creating experiments that target AWS resources with controlled actions and stop conditions.
Experiment Template
A declarative definition of targets, actions, stop conditions, and the IAM role used for an experiment.
Hypothesis
A testable prediction about how the system will behave under the injected fault that can be validated with metrics.
Quick Summary

Treat experiment templates and stop conditions as first-class artifacts: review them, restrict who can change them, and link each experiment to measurable steady-state metrics for traceable, auditable resilience testing.
