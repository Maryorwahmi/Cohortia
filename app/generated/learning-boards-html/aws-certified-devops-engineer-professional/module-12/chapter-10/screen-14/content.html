Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Key Concepts
Chaos Engineering
Experimenting on systems to build confidence in their resilience under turbulent conditions.
Steady-State Behavior
The measurable normal state of the system defined by metrics like success rates and latency.
Blast Radius
The scope of impact an experiment can have; keep it as small as possible to start.
Summary Takeaway

Treat chaos engineering as a measured, iterative discipline: hypothesis, small blast radius, automated safety, careful analysis, and remediation.
