Design and execution steps
Follow this plan for every experiment you run.
Step 1
Define steady state
Document baseline SLIs and thresholds used to judge success.
Step 2
Formulate hypothesis
State the expected behavior when the fault is injected.
Step 3
Choose blast radius
Limit scope to one instance, one AZ, or small traffic percentage.
Step 4
Inject fault
Use FIS or other tools to perform the action safely and repeatably.
Step 5
Observe & verify
Compare observed metrics to your hypothesis and watch stop conditions closely.
Step 6
Rollback & document
Revert changes if alarms fire and record findings for remediation.
Scenario card
Example task: terminate a single EC2 instance in an ASG tagged MyFISInstance and ensure the ASG restores capacity while HTTP success rate stays above 99%.
Key Takeaway

A pre-run checklist and a clear rollback plan are as important as the injection itself.

Safety Reminder

Notify on-call engineers, set CloudWatch alarm stop conditions, and grant FIS only the permissions it needs.
