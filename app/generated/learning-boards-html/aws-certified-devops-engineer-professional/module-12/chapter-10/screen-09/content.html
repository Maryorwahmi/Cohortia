Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Analyzing Experiment Results
Hypothesis
A clear, measurable prediction to test.
Example: "Terminate one instance; success rate stays ≥ 99% and p99 latency < 300ms." Record the exact metrics and threshold values for later comparison.
Observed Outcome
Measured behavior during the experiment window.
Capture time-series charts and raw logs. Note transient spikes and recovery times. Tag logs with experiment id for traceability.
Deviation & Root Cause
Where the system diverged and why.
Link anomalies to components: load balancer, ASG latency, DB queue growth, or insufficient IAM permissions affecting automation. Prioritize fixes by user impact.
Evidence Panel
Snapshots, charts, and notes you use to judge the experiment.

During experiment
Success rate: 98.5% (2 min dip)
p99 latency: 420ms (spike)
Interpretation: short-lived latency spike exceeded hypothesis threshold and caused a small, brief customer impact. Root cause traces show slow DB connection provisioning on replacement instance.
Concrete Example
Action item: pre-warm database connections during instance launch, tighten health check thresholds to avoid routing traffic to under-prepared instances, and add a CloudWatch alarm for p95 latency during scaling events.
Key Takeaway
Careful comparison of hypothesis and evidence turns a failed expectation into concrete improvements that raise real resilience.
