Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Benefits & Mistakes
Benefits
  • • Increased resilience: find and fix failure modes before customers do.
  • • Improved observability: experiments highlight blind spots and missing alerts.
  • • Better incident response: practice reduces reaction time and confusion.
  • • Reduced downtime and potential cost savings from optimized fault tolerance.
Example Outcome

An experiment that exposed DB connection exhaustion led to connection pool tuning and an immediate reduction in errors under load.

Common Mistakes
  • • No hypothesis or steady-state: tests become noise and give no actionable insight.
  • • Missing stop conditions: experiments can escalate into real outages.
  • • Overly broad permissions for FIS: unnecessary risk from accidental actions.
  • • Large blast radius too early: lessons should come from small, controlled scope.
Action

After each experiment produce a short report: hypothesis, observations, whether it held, and 2–3 prioritized remediation tasks.
