Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Learning Objectives
Why these objectives matter
Shared language
A lifecycle and severity scale prevent confusion under pressure by giving everyone the same definitions.
Consistent prioritization
Severity criteria let you allocate on-call resources and communication channels quickly.
Runbooks as training aids
A good playbook is both a checklist and a teaching document for less experienced responders.
Safe automation
Automate routine remediations, but always test and constrain with least-privilege IAM roles.
