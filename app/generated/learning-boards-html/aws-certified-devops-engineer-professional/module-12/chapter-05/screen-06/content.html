Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Runbook Structure
Runbook template
Incident Title/Type
A concise name, e.g., "High CPU on Production EC2 Instance".
Trigger Conditions
Which alarms or symptoms start the runbook, with precise thresholds.
Initial Triage Steps
Quick checks to verify the incident and gather context, such as metric snapshots and recent deployments.
Mitigation & Troubleshooting
Ordered, tested actions to restore service and commands to gather diagnostics.
Communication & Escalation
List contact roles, channels, and update cadence. Define who to escalate to and when.
Resolution & Post-Mortem
Clear criteria to close the incident and a requirement to perform a post-mortem when thresholds are met.
Pro Tip
Keep the primary mitigation steps short and tested. Put longer diagnostics as appendices.
