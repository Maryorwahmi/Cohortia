Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Severity & Impact
Severity levels
Severity 1 (Critical)
Major outage, widespread customer impact, or data loss — immediate all-hands response.
Severity 2 (High)
Key functionality impaired, significant customer impact, urgent response required.
Severity 3 (Medium)
Limited impact with workaround; schedule remediation.
Severity 4 (Low)
Cosmetic or minor issues; non-urgent fixes.
Impact assessment criteria
  • Number of affected users or percentage of traffic impacted.
  • Financial cost or revenue impact per minute or hour.
  • Data integrity concerns: risk of loss or corruption.
  • Compliance and legal implications for the business.
Practical example
A payment API that fails for 10% of users may be Severity 2 because it affects revenue, whereas a visual layout bug for many users may be Severity 4.
Common mistake
Setting severity by vocality rather than measured impact; codify thresholds to avoid arguments during incidents.
