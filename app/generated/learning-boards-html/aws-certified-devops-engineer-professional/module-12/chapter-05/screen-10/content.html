Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Communication & Escalation
Communication plan essentials
Who to notify
Incident channel, on-call engineer, service owner, and business stakeholders as needed.
What to say
Impact summary, actions taken, next steps, ETA, and links to diagnostics or runbook sections.
When to escalate
If no resolution in X minutes or if user impact grows beyond defined thresholds.
Message cadence
Initial ack, after triage, after mitigation, and on resolution. Use short templates to save time.
Template example
"Ack: Investigating [service], impact [short description], current action: [what we are doing], ETA: [if known]."
