Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Safety & Pitfalls
Common mistakes
  • Outdated runbooks that don't reflect current deployments.
  • Automation roles with excessive IAM permissions.
  • Automations that perform irreversible or destructive actions without approvals.
  • No testing of Automation documents in non-production environments.
Example hazard
An automation that terminates instances as a fix may reduce capacity further if the Auto Scaling Group configuration is incorrect. Test and validate first.
Mitigation checklist
Use least privilege, reversible steps, execution logs, approval gates for high-risk actions, and scheduled drills.
