Common Mistakes
Operational Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them
Practical steps to reduce common operational risks.
Underdocumented runbooks
Keep runbooks concise, versioned, and linked to alarms. Update them after incidents.
Untested restores
Regularly test backup and restore procedures in a safe environment to confirm they work.
Noisy alerts
Reduce alert noise by tuning thresholds and applying anomaly detection to avoid fatigue.
Key Takeaway

Address documentation, testing, and alert quality iteratively to build reliable operational capability.
