Core Concept
What is Operational Excellence?
The pillar that ensures systems run to deliver business value and improve over time.
Importance: Aligns ops work to business outcomes
Definition & claims
  • Run and monitor systems to deliver business value.
  • Automate operational tasks to reduce manual errors and toil.
  • Continuously improve practices through testing and learning.
Common misconception
Thinking monitoring alone is operational excellence. Monitoring is necessary but insufficient without automation and tested runbooks.
Concrete example
A CloudFormation stack that provisions an EC2 instance, a CloudWatch dashboard, and a HighCPU alarm demonstrates operational excellence by making monitoring and alerts part of the deployable artifact.
Key Takeaway

Holistic operational practices — not a single tool — create resilient and maintainable systems.
