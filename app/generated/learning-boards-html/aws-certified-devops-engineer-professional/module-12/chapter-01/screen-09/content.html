Definition
Runbooks and Playbooks as Living Documents
Short, clear procedures that are tested and versioned.
Good runbook structure
  1. Trigger condition and verification steps.
  2. Immediate mitigation actions that are safe to run.
  3. Escalation criteria and post-incident steps.
Store runbooks in the code repo and reference them from alerts for versioned guidance.
Example: HighCPU runbook (summary)
  • Verify alarm state and recent CPU trend for five minutes.
  • Check Auto Scaling activity and instance health checks.
  • Execute safe mitigation (scale out or restart service) and notify team if unresolved.
Key Takeaway

Concise, tested runbooks reduce decision time and avoid escalation noise during incidents.
