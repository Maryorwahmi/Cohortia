Core Concept
Why post‑mortems must lead to action
Convert findings into prioritized, verifiable work so incidents do not repeat.
SMART checklist
  • Specific: name the metric or resource to change.
  • Measurable: define how success is proven.
  • Time‑bound: provide a deadline.
Common pitfall
Recording many vague items without prioritization causes post‑mortem fatigue and no real improvement.
Key takeaway
Turn one past post‑mortem note into a SMART task and define how you'll verify it.
Example — vague vs SMART
Vague: “Improve monitoring.”
SMART: “Implement CloudWatch anomaly detection for CPUUtilization on all production EC2 instances by Nov 30th, owner: Jane Doe; verify by simulating high CPU in staging.”
Reflection prompt
Which of your team's post‑mortem items could be made measurable this week?
