Implementing Continuous Improvement from Post‑Mortem Findings
Translate action items into prioritized automation, verification, and cultural change so incidents lead to lasting reliability.
Why this chapter matters
Post‑mortems without follow‑through repeat failures. This chapter makes follow‑through concrete and measurable.
Practical focus
You will see examples for IaC changes, CI pipeline gates, verification methods, and cultural practices to sustain improvement.
Explore Key Topics
SMART Action Items
Make tasks Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time‑bound so they can be tracked.
CI/CD Integration
Embed validation steps and tests into pipelines to catch regressions before production.
Tracking & Verification
Use a dedicated backlog, regular reviews, and verification criteria to ensure action items are complete and effective.
From finding to automation
Examples show how a missing index or misconfigured security group becomes IaC and CI checks.
Measure and verify
Define how each improvement will be proven effective, and track metrics like incident recurrence.
