Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Constructing an Accurate Timeline
Why timelines matter

Timelines anchor hypotheses to evidence and reveal causal ordering. Accurate times reveal delays in detection or rollback completion that change the RCA outcome.

Sources to collect
  • Monitoring alerts and metric graphs
  • Deployment and CI/CD logs
  • Request traces and application logs
  • Change logs, chat history, and runbook actions
Avoid relying on memory; always attach the artifact location for each timeline entry.
Timeline builder (example rows)
13:50 — Deploy v1.2.0 (CI/CD build #234) — link: deploy-log-234
14:05 — SearchServiceLatency alarm — metric snapshot attached
14:20 — New heavy query appears in logs — log excerpt attached
Practical tip
Annotate each entry with the artifact location and a one‑line claim: what this proves. This makes the timeline verifiable by anyone reading the report later.
Key Takeaway

Machine timestamps and artifact links make timelines objective and trustworthy; they are the backbone of a strong RCA.
