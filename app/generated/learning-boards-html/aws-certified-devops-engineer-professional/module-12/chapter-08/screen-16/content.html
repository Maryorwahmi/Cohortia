Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Key Concepts — Practical
Attach Evidence
Every timeline entry and RCA claim should reference logs, metrics, or artifacts for verification.
SMART Actions
Make action items specific, measurable, assigned, and verifiable to ensure completion and impact.
Protect Data
Redact or restrict sensitive details and use controlled channels for security issues.
Final Takeaway

Small, disciplined practices — evidence attachments, SMART verification, and data protections — make post‑mortems trustworthy, actionable, and safe to share.
