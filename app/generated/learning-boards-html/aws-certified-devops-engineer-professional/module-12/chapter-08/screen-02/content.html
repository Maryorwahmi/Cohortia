Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Learning Objectives
01
Verified Competency Milestone
Explain Blameless Post‑Mortems

You will describe why post‑mortems focus on systems, not people, and how this approach creates psychological safety. This skill matters because candid reporting reveals real causes and systemic gaps.

Mnemonic: Venn — Safety ∩ Transparency = Learning
02
Verified Competency Milestone
Apply Structured RCA Techniques

You will use the 5 Whys and Fishbone diagram to trace symptoms back to systemic causes and propose durable fixes. This matters because surface fixes often allow recurrence.

Mnemonic: Timeline → Why → Fix
03
Verified Competency Milestone
Compose a Complete Post‑Mortem Report

You will assemble incident title, timestamps, timeline, impact, RCA, contributing factors, lessons, and SMART action items so the report is actionable and auditable.

Mnemonic: Title → Time → RCA → Actions
04
Verified Competency Milestone
Facilitate Blameless Meetings

You will practice preparing timelines, grounding conversations in data, encouraging diverse perspectives, and ensuring action items with owners and deadlines.

Mnemonic: Prepare → Data → Assign
