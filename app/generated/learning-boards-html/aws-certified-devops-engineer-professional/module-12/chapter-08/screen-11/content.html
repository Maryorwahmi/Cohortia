Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Facilitating Meetings
Before the meeting
  • Distribute preliminary timeline and artifacts in advance.
  • Set ground rules: blameless, evidence‑first, timeboxed.
  • Invite diverse participants with relevant context.
During the meeting
Walk the timeline, label hypotheses, capture evidence gaps, avoid deep debugging, and keep actions timeboxed. The facilitator redirects blame to processes and asks, "What in our system allowed this?"
After the meeting
Publish the final report, assign SMART actions with owners and deadlines, and schedule verification milestones to confirm mitigations worked.
Key Takeaway

A prepared facilitator keeps the meeting productive, blameless, and focused on turning findings into verifiable actions.
