Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Common Pitfalls & Safety
Mistakes to avoid
  • Skipping reviews for minor incidents.
  • Focusing on immediate fixes rather than systemic causes.
  • Allowing blame to dominate the discussion.
  • Creating action items without owners or verification steps.
Sensitive information
Redact customer data and restrict security vulnerability details. When sharing broadly, use a sanitized summary and keep sensitive artifacts in restricted storage with clear access rules.
Governance checklist
Include incident threshold, required attachments, SMART actions, owner field, and verification milestone in the report template to prevent process slippage.
Key Takeaway

Use a template and governance rules to avoid skipped reviews, unowned actions, and unsafe sharing of sensitive details.
