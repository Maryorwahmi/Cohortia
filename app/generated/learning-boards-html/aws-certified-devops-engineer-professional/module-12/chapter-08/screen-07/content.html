Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › RCA Techniques
Technique
5 Whys
Fast, iterative questioning to reach systemic causes. Best for a single causal chain.
Fishbone
Visual categorization of causes across People, Process, Tools, Environment, Metrics, Design. Best for multi‑factor incidents.
Chronological Analysis
Reconstructs the exact sequence to expose timing and dependency issues.
How to combine methods
  • Start with chronological reconstruction to anchor events.
  • Use 5 Whys to trace the most likely causal chain from timeline cues.
  • Use Fishbone to enumerate other contributing factors and mitigate secondary risks.
Key Takeaway

Choose RCA methods based on incident complexity: combine timeline, 5 Whys, and Fishbone to reach systemic and comprehensive fixes.
