Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Writing a Post‑Mortem
Report anatomy
Metadata: title, ID, start/detect/mitigation/resolution times, duration.
Timeline: timestamped events with artifact links.
RCA & Contributing factors: method, evidence, and separate listing of contributors.
Lessons & Actions: SMART items with owners and due dates.
Verification plan: how you will confirm fixes worked.
SMART action example
Add DB query performance stage to CI that fails builds with queries scanning >5s on a realistic dataset — owner: DevOps — due: 14 days. Verification: run three representative deploys showing <5s queries for top 10 searches.
Storage & discoverability
Store post‑mortems in a version‑controlled wiki or Git repository with tags for service and incident type. That ensures traceability and auditability.
Key Takeaway

Clear sections, artifact links, SMART actions, and a verification plan make reports reusable and effective for preventing recurrence.
