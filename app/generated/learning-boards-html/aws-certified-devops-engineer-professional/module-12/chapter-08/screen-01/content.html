Conducting Effective Post‑Mortems & Root Cause Analysis

Learn how to extract systemic improvements from incidents using a blameless process, structured RCA techniques, and actionable follow‑up.

Why this matters

Post‑mortems convert costly downtime into permanent improvements. A consistent, blameless process reduces recurrence and increases team learning velocity.

What you'll master
  • Design and store a complete post‑mortem report.
  • Apply 5 Whys and Fishbone techniques to find systemic causes.
  • Facilitate blameless meetings that produce SMART action items.
