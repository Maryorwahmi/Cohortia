Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Practice Reminders
Start small
Automate low-impact, reversible tasks first and prove reliability before expanding.
Least privilege
Grant narrow permissions to automation roles to limit blast radius of mistakes.
Observe & throttle
Log runs, notify operators, and add circuit breakers to prevent loops and cascades.
Final check

Automation is a tool for resilience when implemented with staged testing, minimal privileges, and strong observability.
