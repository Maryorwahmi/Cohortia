Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Mistakes & Safety
Common Mistakes
  • Automating high-impact changes without approval gates
  • No throttles or circuit breakers on retries
  • Insufficient testing in representative environments
  • Overly broad IAM roles for automation agents
Safety Patterns
  • Use throttles and retry limits to avoid loops
  • Add approval stages for destructive remediations
  • Grant least-privilege IAM, scoped to resources
  • Log every run and expose results to operators
Check

Before enabling automation, run simulations and confirm that rollback and escalation procedures are documented and tested.
