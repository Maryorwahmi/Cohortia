Automating Remediation and Self-Healing
Why automation, what you will practice, and a safety-first mindset
What you'll do
Design remediation flows, author SSM Automation documents, and link Config rules to automated actions.
How it helps
Reduce toil, lower mean time to recovery, and ensure consistent, auditable fixes for repeatable problems.
Safety note
Always stage automation: test in non-production, use least-privilege roles, and add throttles or circuit-breakers.
