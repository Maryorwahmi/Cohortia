Core Concept › Pitfalls & Best Practices
Pitfall: partial instrumentation
Missing segments from a service make traces incomplete and obscure the root cause.
Pitfall: noisy annotations
Over-annotating or indexing large objects increases cost and reduces search relevance.
Best practice
Standardize annotations, patch SDKs, validate collector health, and tune sampling rules based on observed needs.
Concrete check
After deployment, confirm that a few test traces include both the front-end and backend segments, that annotations are present, and that the collector logs show no dropped records.
