Comparison › Service Map vs Trace Timeline
Service Map
A topology view that surfaces suspicious nodes and edges by aggregating latency and error data. Use it to pick a candidate service to investigate further.
When to use
  • When you need a quick overview of where latency clusters in the architecture.
  • When you want to detect new or unexpected service connections.
Trace Timeline
A per-request timeline showing segments and subsegments with exact durations and any recorded exceptions or annotations. This is where you confirm causality.
When to use
  • To verify which operation contributes the most latency for a single request.
  • To inspect recorded exceptions and custom metadata for context.
Concrete scenario
A service map highlights a database node with high latency. Inspecting a trace shows the Lambda segment has a nested DB subsegment that consumes most time, pointing to query tuning rather than compute scaling.
