Sampling strategies
  • Fixed percentage sampling for baseline telemetry.
  • Rule-based sampling to always capture slow or error responses.
  • Path-based sampling to always trace critical endpoints.
Operational trade-offs
Trace less to reduce cost, but ensure you still capture the signal for intermittent failures. Increase sampling for error responses or for features under investigation.
Concrete rule
Trace 10% of all requests, but always trace requests with response time greater than 500ms or those that return 5xx status codes.
