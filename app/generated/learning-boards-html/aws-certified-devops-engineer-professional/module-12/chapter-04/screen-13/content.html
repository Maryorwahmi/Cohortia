Assessment › Troubleshooting Scenario
Scenario

Intermittent latency spikes in an application that includes API Gateway, Lambda, and DynamoDB. Service metrics are normal.

Task
  1. Instrument the API, Lambda, and DynamoDB calls so traces include segments for each hop.
  2. Invoke the flow repeatedly and collect traces with sampling rules tuned to capture slow responses.
  3. Analyze traces to identify the segment that contributes the most latency and propose a remediation.
Submission
Provide an exported trace ID, a short explanation of the offending segment, and one recommended fix such as query optimization, index creation, or caching.
