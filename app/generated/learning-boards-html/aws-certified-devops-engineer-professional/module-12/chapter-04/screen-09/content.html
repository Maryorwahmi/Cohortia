Process Flow › OTel → ADOT → X-Ray
Collector deployment patterns
  • Sidecar per pod/task for isolation and per-service configuration.
  • DaemonSet on Kubernetes nodes for resource sharing and simplicity.
  • Agent on EC2 for host-level collection.
Operational checks
Confirm receiver config matches your SDK export settings, verify exporter targets X-Ray, and watch collector logs to detect dropped spans. Test with a small traffic sample before full production rollout.
Example
Deploy ADOT as a sidecar in an EKS pod. Configure the app’s OTel SDK to send traces to localhost where the sidecar receives and forwards them to X-Ray.
