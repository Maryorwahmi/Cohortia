Comparison
Kinesis Data Firehose
Managed delivery to OpenSearch, S3, or Redshift with buffering and simple transforms.
Best when
You want low operational overhead and simple delivery to supported destinations.
Features
Buffering, compression, basic Lambda transforms, S3 error backup.
Tradeoffs
Less flexible for complex per-record routing or strict ordering.
Kinesis Data Streams
Low-level stream with shards and custom consumers for advanced processing and ordering guarantees.
Best when
You need fine control over partitioning, ordering, or custom end-to-end processing.
Features
Shards, retention windows, custom consumers like Lambda for rich transformations.
Tradeoffs
More operational overhead to manage consumers and ordering semantics.
Key Takeaway

Pick Firehose for simple delivery pipelines and Data Streams when you require custom processing or strict ordering guarantees.
