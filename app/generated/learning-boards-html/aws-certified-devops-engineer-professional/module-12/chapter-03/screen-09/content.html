Definition
Retention, Access, Cost
Balance operational needs with compliance and budget by using retention windows, S3 archival, and least-privilege access.
Retention
Set retention rules in CloudWatch or OpenSearch and archive older data to S3 with lifecycle transitions.
Access
Use IAM roles, resource policies, and OpenSearch fine-grained controls to limit who can read or modify logs.
Cost
Filter non-essential logs, compress payloads, and move cold data to cheaper storage classes.
Example policy
Keep 30 days in OpenSearch for quick queries, archive three years to S3 with lifecycle to Glacier for compliance, and revoke index read access for non-investigators.
Key Takeaway

Automate retention and lifecycle transitions and verify IAM policies to control cost and maintain compliance.

Cost optimization tactics
  • Filter verbose debug logs in production at source to reduce volume.
  • Compress records in Firehose to lower transfer and storage costs.
  • Use S3 lifecycle rules to move older logs to Glacier tiers.
  • Right-size OpenSearch nodes and consider UltraWarm for infrequent queries.
