Reliable, structured logging turns operational noise into searchable signals you can alert on, analyze, and archive efficiently.
In this lesson

Why it helps
Structured fields let you query by event types, user, or service quickly during incidents.
Safety note
Never log PII or secrets in plain text; sanitize or redact before sending logs to aggregation services.
