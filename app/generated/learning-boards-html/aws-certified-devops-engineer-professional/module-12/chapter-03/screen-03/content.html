Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › Practical outcomes
Verifiable milestones
  • Emit consistent JSON logs from a Lambda and see structured fields in CloudWatch Logs.
  • Configure a Firehose delivery stream to deliver logs to OpenSearch or S3 with buffering and compression.
  • Create an OpenSearch index pattern and find fields like level and event in the dashboard's Discover view.
  • Apply retention and lifecycle policies to balance compliance and cost.
Mnemonic
Emit → Stream → Index → Archive
This sequence helps teams remember the pipeline and its verification steps.
