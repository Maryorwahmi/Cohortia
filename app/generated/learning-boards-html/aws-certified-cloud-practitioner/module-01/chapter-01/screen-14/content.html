Key Concepts
Cloud Computing
On-demand IT resources over the internet with pay-as-you-go pricing.
Five Characteristics
On-demand, broad access, pooled resources, rapid elasticity, and measured service.
Deployment Models
Public, Private, Hybrid — pick for scale, control, or a mix.
Service Models
IaaS, PaaS, SaaS — who manages infrastructure through application layers.
Benefits
Agility, elasticity, cost model, global reach, and managed services.
Challenges
Vendor lock-in, shared security, and cost management needs governance.
Final takeaway

Cloud changes how teams buy and operate IT: it provides speed and scale, but success requires governance, architecture, and cost controls.
