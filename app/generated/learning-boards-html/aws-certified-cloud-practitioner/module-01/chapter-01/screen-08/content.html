Deployment Models Overview
Public Cloud
Owned and operated by a third party, accessible to many customers.
Benefits: scale, cost efficiency, broad managed services. Trade-offs: less direct control, potential vendor lock-in.
Private Cloud
Operated for a single organization, on-premises or hosted by a third party.
Benefits: control, customization, data locality. Trade-offs: higher operational burden and cost.
Hybrid Cloud
A combination of private and public infrastructures made to interoperate.
Benefits: best of both worlds; Trade-offs: integration complexity and governance challenges.
Concrete Example
Sensitive payroll in private cloud; customer-facing storefront in public cloud to scale globally; hybrid connectivity for data transfer and DR.
Key Takeaway
Choose a model by matching data sensitivity, need for scale, and willingness to manage infrastructure.
