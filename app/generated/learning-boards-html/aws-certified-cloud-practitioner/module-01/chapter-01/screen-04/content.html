Core Concept
What is Cloud Computing?
The on-demand delivery of IT resources over the internet with pay-as-you-go pricing.

Definition: Provision computing resources and services on demand, billed for usage, and accessible over standard networks.

Utility analogy: Like electricity — no plant ownership, just consumption and metering.

Concrete example: Launch a virtual machine and object storage to host a website without buying hardware.

Misconception: Cloud is more than remote hosting; it includes automation, metering, and pooled resources enabling elasticity.

Example
Provision a VM, attach storage, deploy the app, and scale on demand during traffic spikes.
Key Takeaway
Cloud shifts costs to operational spend and enables faster experimentation.
