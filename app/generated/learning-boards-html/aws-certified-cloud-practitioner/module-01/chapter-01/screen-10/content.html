Private Cloud Deep Dive
Why choose private cloud?
  • • Full control over data location and infrastructure configuration.
  • • Can be tailored for strict regulatory or performance needs.
  • • Enables consistent operations when public clouds are not permitted.
Trade-offs
  • • Greater operational burden: capacity planning, maintenance, and upgrades.
  • • Often higher per-unit costs and less breadth of managed services.
  • • Misconception: private cloud guarantees security — operations and architecture still matter.
Concrete example
A hospital runs patient records and key services inside a private cloud to meet compliance and control requirements.
Operational note
Plan for staffing, patching, and capacity growth when selecting private cloud.
Key Takeaway
Private cloud gives control but requires investment in operations and planning to succeed.
