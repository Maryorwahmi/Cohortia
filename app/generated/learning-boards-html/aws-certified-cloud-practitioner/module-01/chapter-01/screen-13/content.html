Benefits and Challenges of Cloud Adoption
Key benefits
  • • Cost model shifts to OpEx with pay-as-you-go billing.
  • • Agility: provision environments in minutes to accelerate development.
  • • Elasticity: scale automatically to handle peaks and troughs.
  • • Global reach with regional deployments to reduce latency.
Key challenges
  • • Vendor lock-in if you heavily depend on proprietary services.
  • • Shared responsibility for security requires organizational controls.
  • • Cost management needs tagging, budgets, and automation to avoid surprises.
Governance strategy
Use tagging, budgets, automated lifecycle policies, and identity controls to reduce risk and optimize spend.
Concrete example
Schedule non-production instances to stop outside business hours and enforce tags to track ownership and cost center.
Key Takeaway
Plan governance and architecture intentionally; cloud is an enabler, not a panacea.
