Common cost pitfalls
Unexpected charges usually come from unused resources, over-provisioning, and data-transfer or multi-region replication without planning.
• Leaving non-production resources running.
• Not rightsizing instances and storage classes.
• Forgetting to use reserved pricing for predictable workloads.
Management strategies
  • • Rightsize based on observed metrics.
  • • Automate shutdown of test/dev environments after hours.
  • • Use tags and reports for team-level accountability.
  • • Apply reserved or savings plans where usage is stable.
Common Mistake

Treating cost control as a one-time effort. It requires ongoing monitoring and governance tied to organizational processes.
