Scenario recap
5 physical servers (m5.large equivalent)
10 TB storage
IT staff 20% time
Annual costs:
  Hardware: $10,000
  Licenses: $2,000
  Data center: $5,000
  Staff (20%): $16,000
  Backup: $3,000
Step 1: List on-premises cost categories. Step 2: Map each to AWS service categories. Step 3: Identify three qualitative benefits to include in your presentation.
Mapping hints
  • • Hardware → Compute instances or managed compute services.
  • • Storage → Object or block storage and backup snapshots.
  • • Data center utilities → Eliminated, replaced by service fees.
  • • Staff time → Operational shift to cloud tasks; include transition time.
Key Takeaway

Walk stakeholders through assumptions and show how utilization and managed service choices change the numerical outcome.
