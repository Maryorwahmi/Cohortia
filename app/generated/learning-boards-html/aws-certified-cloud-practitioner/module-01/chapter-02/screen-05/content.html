Benefit from massive economies of scale
Large providers lower per-unit costs and deliver managed services customers can’t economically build themselves.
Shared investments: hardware, power optimization, and operational automation reduce unit costs.
Access to enterprise-grade redundancy and features without building your own data centers.
Managed services like backups and databases reduce engineering overhead for customers.
Example

A small company gains access to resilient storage and managed snapshots without buying multiple redundant sites, because the provider spreads those infrastructure costs across many customers.

What to include in TCO
  • • Cost savings from pooled purchasing and lower power/hardware per unit.
  • • Value of managed services that otherwise require in-house engineering.
  • • Avoided capital and ongoing operational costs for redundancy and disaster recovery.
Misconception
Not every workload sees the same percentage of savings. High-compute or highly specialized systems may require tailored analysis, but many general workloads benefit substantially from shared infrastructure investments.
