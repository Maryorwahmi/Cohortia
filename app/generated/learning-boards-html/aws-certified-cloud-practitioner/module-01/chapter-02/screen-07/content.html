Increase speed and agility
Provision resources in minutes and use managed services to reduce setup time and operational burden.
• Rapid provisioning shortens feedback loops for product and platform teams.
• Managed services reduce setup and maintenance work, enabling focus on business logic.
• Infrastructure-as-code keeps faster provisioning reproducible and auditable.
Example
A team spins a test environment for a prototype in hours. That speed lets them try multiple designs, learn which yields better performance, and iterate more rapidly.
Practical guidance
  • • Use managed databases and storage to remove routine maintenance work.
  • • Automate provisioning with templates to keep speed repeatable and controlled.
  • • Measure experiment velocity: number of deploys or prototypes per quarter.
Key Takeaway

Speed alone isn’t the goal; speed paired with automation and governance increases safe experimentation and quicker business outcomes.
