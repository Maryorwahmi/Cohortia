Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
CapEx → OpEx
Shift from upfront hardware purchases to pay-for-use services that improve cash flow and reduce stranded assets.
Elasticity
Scale up or down to match demand and avoid over-provisioning; automation and monitoring are essential to capture value.
TCO & Shared Responsibility
Include direct and indirect costs in TCO and remember the security tasks you retain as the customer.
Summary
Use these three lenses—financial model, operational design, and security roles—when you recommend migration strategies and present TCO analysis to stakeholders.
