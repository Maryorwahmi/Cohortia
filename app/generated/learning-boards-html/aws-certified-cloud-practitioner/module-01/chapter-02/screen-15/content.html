CapEx / OpEx
CapEx: upfront hardware spending. OpEx: ongoing, pay-as-you-go service charges.
Economies of Scale
Large-scale operations lower per-unit costs and enable shared investments in features.
Elasticity & TCO
Elasticity aligns cost with demand; TCO must list direct and indirect costs and state assumptions.
Final thought
Combine numeric TCO analysis with qualitative benefits to create a balanced migration recommendation that stakeholders can act on.
