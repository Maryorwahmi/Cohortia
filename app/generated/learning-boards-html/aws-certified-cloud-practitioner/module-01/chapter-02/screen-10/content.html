Understand TCO for cloud migration
Compare hardware, software, facilities, staff, and backup costs to equivalent cloud services and support.
• On-premises items: hardware amortization, licenses, data center, network, staff, backup.
• Cloud items: compute, storage, networking, managed services, support plans, and recurring operational fees.
• Include qualitative gains, like faster deployments and reduced maintenance.
A simple TCO exercise
On-premises annual costs:
  Hardware: $10,000
  Licenses: $2,000
  Data center: $5,000
  Staff (20%): $16,000
  Backup: $3,000
Map each to AWS services and estimate annual service charges for comparison.
Key Takeaway

Be specific about assumptions. Savings appear or disappear depending on utilization, managed services chosen, and which costs are included.
