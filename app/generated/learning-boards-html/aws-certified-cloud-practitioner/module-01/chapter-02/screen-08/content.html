Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › No Data Center Maintenance
Stop spending money running and maintaining data centers
Delegate facilities, hardware, and physical security to the provider and reassign team effort to product and cloud automation.
• Eliminate direct facility costs: power, cooling, and space.
• Avoid hardware refresh cycles and associated procurement burden.
• Reduce time spent on physical troubleshooting and hardware maintenance.
Example
A small operations team spends less time replacing failed drives and more time automating deployments and improving performance after migration.
Operational trade-offs
  • • Facilities and hardware tasks are eliminated, but cloud configuration and access control remain your responsibility.
  • • Measure time saved on maintenance and reassign to cloud engineering work.
  • • Ensure governance and automation to keep speed sustainable.
Key Takeaway

Moving away from data center maintenance frees resources for differentiation, but teams must adopt cloud best practices to manage their new responsibilities.
