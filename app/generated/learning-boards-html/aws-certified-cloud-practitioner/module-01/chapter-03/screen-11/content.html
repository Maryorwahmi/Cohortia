Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Common mistakes
Top mistakes
  • Single-AZ deployments to reduce cost but increase outage risk.
  • Neglecting data residency and compliance constraints for stored data.
  • Assuming Edge Locations change where data is stored.
Contrast

Short-term savings from single-AZ choices often lead to longer outages and higher recovery costs; plan for resiliency up front.

Checks to catch mistakes
Verification steps
  1. Confirm multi-AZ distribution for critical services.
  2. Document data residency requirements and record the Region choice.
  3. Run failover drills and measure recovery RTO and RPO.
Key takeaway

Prevention and testable documentation are more cost-effective than reactive fixes after an outage.
