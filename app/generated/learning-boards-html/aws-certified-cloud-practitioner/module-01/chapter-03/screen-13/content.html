Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Data center
Physical components
  • Racks of compute and storage hardware.
  • Redundant power, cooling, and networking.
  • Physical security and operational processes.
Design implication

Because AZs contain multiple data centers, distribute components across AZs rather than relying on a single facility.

Operational note

AZ naming is account-specific; do not hard-code AZ letters into automation without discovery logic. Instead, use logical groupings and tags in your infrastructure-as-code tooling.

Key takeaway

Design for AZ-level independence and use automation to map AZ identities rather than relying on fixed names.
