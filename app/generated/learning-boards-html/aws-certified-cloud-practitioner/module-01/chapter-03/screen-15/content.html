Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Terms
Availability Zone

Isolated data center locations within a Region used to provide redundancy and fault isolation.

Data Residency

The physical location where data is stored to meet local legal or policy requirements.

High Availability

The ability of a system to remain operational and accessible most of the time through redundancy and failover.

Final takeaway

Keep Region, AZ, and Edge distinctions clear. Use them together to meet latency, availability, and regulatory goals.
