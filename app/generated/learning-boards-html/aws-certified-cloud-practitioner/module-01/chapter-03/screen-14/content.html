Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
AWS Region

Geographic area for resources and data residency; isolated from other Regions.

Availability Zone

Independent data center locations within a Region used for fault isolation and high availability.

Edge & Regional Caches

Caching layers that speed content delivery via CloudFront; they do not change origin data residency.

Summary takeaway

Design with Region, AZ, and Edge tiers together. Document choices and test failover to keep systems resilient and performant.
