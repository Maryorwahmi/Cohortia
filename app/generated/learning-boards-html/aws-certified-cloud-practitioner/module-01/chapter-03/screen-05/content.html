Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Choosing a Region
Decision factors
Latency

Place resources close to users to reduce response time and improve experience.

Data residency

Legal or regulatory requirements that mandate where data may be stored.

Service availability

Confirm that the services and instance types you need exist in the Region.

Cost

Pricing varies slightly — include cost in your total cost of ownership analysis.

Quick checklist
  1. Confirm compliance and data residency needs for the workload.
  2. Choose the nearest Region to the largest user base when legal constraints allow.
  3. Verify required services and instance families exist in the Region.
  4. Estimate costs and operational impact; document the choice rationale.

Example decision

North American app: choose us-east-1 or us-west-2 for proximity. If a managed service you need exists only in us-east-1, select that and document why.
