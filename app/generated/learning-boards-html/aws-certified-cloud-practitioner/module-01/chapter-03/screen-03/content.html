Practice & Assessment

Concrete tasks and verification steps ensure you can make region and availability decisions in real projects.

Checkpoint
Region selection task

Choose a Region for a North American user base and justify it based on latency and services. Milestone: selected Region with reasoning.

Checkpoint
Multi-AZ design

Sketch how web servers and databases span at least two AZs and explain failover. Milestone: annotated diagram or list of components per AZ.

Checkpoint
Edge caching decision

Recommend CloudFront edge caching for global assets and explain cache tiers. Milestone: concise policy for which assets to cache.

Assessment
Common mistakes to avoid

Identify and explain why single-AZ deployments and ignoring data residency are risky. Milestone: corrected architecture with multi-AZ and regional data placement.
