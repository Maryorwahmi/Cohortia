On-Demand Instances

Pay for compute by the hour or second with no long-term commitment. Best for unpredictable or short-lived workloads.

Why choose On-Demand
  • Immediate launch without contracts or upfront commitments.
  • Useful for development, testing, or ad-hoc tasks that start and stop frequently.
  • Simplifies billing during experimentation phases or one-off workloads.
Concrete example
A QA environment that runs during business hours and is terminated overnight.
Avoid this mistake
Leaving predictable production services on On-Demand when Reserved or Savings Plans would reduce cost.
Check your understanding

Key Takeaway

Use On-Demand when you need agility and no contractual commitment; avoid it for long-lived predictable workloads.
