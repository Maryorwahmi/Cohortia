Comparison Savings Plans

Savings Plans

Commit to a dollar-per-hour spend for 1 or 3 years to receive discounts across compute services.

Why choose Savings Plans
  • Broad coverage across EC2, Fargate, and Lambda.
  • Not tied to instance family or region, so workload changes are easier.
  • Offers substantial discounts similar to RIs but with more flexibility.
Decision checklist
Pick Savings Plans if you can commit to spend but need to retain flexibility across instance types and compute services.
Concrete example
A company with sustained compute needs wants the option to move some load to containers or serverless later without losing discounts.
Key Takeaway

Savings Plans provide a flexible way to commit spend and get discounts across multiple compute services, reducing lock-in to specific instance attributes.
