Spot Instances

Use spare capacity at deep discounts when your workload can tolerate interruptions.

How Spot works
  • You bid for unused EC2 capacity and pay the Spot price while it remains below your bid.
  • A short interruption notice is given if AWS reclaims capacity.
  • Best for stateless, distributed, or checkpointable workloads like batch processing.
Concrete example
Large data analysis job that can checkpoint progress and resume after interruption to gain steep cost savings.
Design considerations
Build retry and checkpoint logic. Use Spot for the flexible fraction of workloads and keep critical baseline capacity on On-Demand or Reserved.
Common mistake
Using Spot for stateful databases that cannot recover from abrupt termination.
