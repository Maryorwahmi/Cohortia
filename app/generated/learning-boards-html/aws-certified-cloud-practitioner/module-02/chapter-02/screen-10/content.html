EBS Volume Types

Choose volume types based on IOPS, throughput, durability, and cost tradeoffs.

General Purpose
gp2 / gp3
Balanced price and performance; gp3 lets you scale IOPS independently of capacity.
Provisioned IOPS
io1 / io2
Designed for I/O-intensive databases that require consistent high performance and durability.
HDD Options
st1 / sc1
st1 for high-throughput processing, sc1 for cold, infrequent access at lowest cost.
Practical guidance
  • Start with gp3 for most workloads; measure latency and throughput to decide on io2 or st1.
  • Use io2 for mission-critical databases that need guaranteed IOPS and higher durability.
  • Choose st1 and sc1 where throughput or cost is the dominant concern and latency is less critical.
Key Takeaway

Match volume type to workload I/O profiles; avoid expensive Provisioned IOPS unless you truly need guaranteed performance.
