EC2 Pricing Models

Tradeoffs between flexibility, commitment, and cost determine which model suits your workload.

Definition & Decision Guide
  • On-Demand: pay for seconds or hours with no commitment; best for unpredictable or short-lived tasks.
  • Reserved Instances: billing discounts for 1–3 year commitments matched by instance attributes.
  • Savings Plans: commit to $/hour for 1–3 years; discounts apply across compute services and are more flexible.
  • Spot Instances: bid on spare capacity for deep discounts; instances may be interrupted with a short warning.
Common mistake
Picking Spot for critical stateful services that cannot tolerate interruptions.

Key Takeaway

Choose the pricing model that aligns with availability needs and cost goals: predictability favors commitment, tolerance for interruption favors Spot.
