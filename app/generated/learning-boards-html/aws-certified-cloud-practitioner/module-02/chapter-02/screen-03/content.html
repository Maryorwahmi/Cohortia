Learning Objectives — Practical Skills

These objectives pair with short scenario exercises so you can validate your ability to choose pricing and storage patterns under real constraints.

Scenario Practice
Map workloads to pricing models
Try recommending On-Demand, RI/Savings Plan, or Spot, and justify the choice.
Storage Mapping
Match data to EBS, Instance Store, or S3
Explain persistence needs and access patterns that drive the decision.
Autoscaling
Define min, desired, max
Use CPU or custom metrics to scale out and in while keeping costs controlled.
