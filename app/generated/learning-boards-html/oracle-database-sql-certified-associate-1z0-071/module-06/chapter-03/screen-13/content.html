Quick reference cards
Three practical one-line reminders.
Verify filters
Run SELECT with your WHERE before DELETE to confirm affected rows.
TRUNCATE = irreversible
TRUNCATE issues implicit commits; it cannot be rolled back.
Watch triggers & FKs
DELETE fires triggers and enforces FKs; TRUNCATE bypasses triggers and may be restricted by FKs.
Bottom line

Match your removal method to the need for rollback, trigger execution, and space reclamation to avoid costly mistakes.
