Key Concepts
Three concise cards to remember the essentials.
DELETE
DML, supports WHERE, fires triggers, and is reversible before commit.
TRUNCATE
DDL, removes all rows, resets high-water mark, issues implicit commits, and cannot be rolled back.
Safety
Always verify with SELECT, backup before major removals, and avoid truncating when rollback is required.
Summary takeaway

Match the command to your need for rollback, triggers, and space reclamation: that decision prevents most removal mistakes.
