Safety guidelines
A checklist of practices to prevent accidental loss and ensure maintainability.
Verify before delete
Always run SELECT with your WHERE to confirm rows to be removed.
Use transactions
Perform deletions inside transactions and commit only after verification.
Backup and review
Take backups before mass removal and have peer review for high-impact changes.
Batch for scale
For very large deletes, process rows in batches to limit undo and lock duration.
Avoid mixing
Do not mix TRUNCATE with uncommitted DML you do not want committed, since TRUNCATE issues implicit commits.
Final thought

Treat data removal as an operation with potential for irreversible impact. When in doubt, favor reversible DELETEs and strong verification.
