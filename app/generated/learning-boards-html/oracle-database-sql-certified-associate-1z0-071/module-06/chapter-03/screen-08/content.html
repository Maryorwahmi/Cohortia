High-water mark and storage
Know whether deletion affects logical data only or also physical allocation.
After DELETE
Rows are removed, but the high-water mark remains. The table's allocated blocks can still be considered used by the database until overwritten or explicitly resized.
Implication
Space may not be reclaimed, potentially leading to inefficient storage usage.
After TRUNCATE
High-water mark resets and data segments are deallocated. The database recognizes freed space immediately, improving performance for future inserts.
Benefit
Faster reuse of space and reduced allocated storage after a full clear.
Practical note

If storage reclamation matters, prefer TRUNCATE for temporary tables, or plan post-DELETE maintenance to reclaim space.
