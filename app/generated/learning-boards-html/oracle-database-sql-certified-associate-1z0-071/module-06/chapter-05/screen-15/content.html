Design
Pick keys and constraints that express core business rules directly in the schema.
Migrations
Clean data, test ALTER TABLE operations in staging, and measure validation time.
Operations
Disable for bulk loads if needed, but include re-enable and validation steps in your process.
Final thought

Treat constraints as contract statements about your data. When designed, managed, and monitored carefully, they make your data reliable and your systems safer.
