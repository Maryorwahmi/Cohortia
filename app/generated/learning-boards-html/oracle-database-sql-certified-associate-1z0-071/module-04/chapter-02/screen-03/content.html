Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Learning Objectives
01
Differentiate WHERE and HAVING
Explain that WHERE restricts rows before grouping and HAVING restricts groups after aggregation, and why aggregates cannot be used in WHERE.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Combine WHERE, GROUP BY, HAVING
Create layered filters such as employees hired after a date, grouped by department, then filtered by average salary.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Group by multiple columns
Summarize by combinations such as department and job to get granular totals or averages per role per department.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Avoid over-granulation
Recognize when adding extra GROUP BY columns creates groups that are too small to answer the original business question.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
