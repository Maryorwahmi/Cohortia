Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › What to Remember
Pick grouping
Decide which column combination represents the summary entity you need.
Avoid ORA-00979
Ensure every non-aggregated SELECT column is in GROUP BY or remove it.
Filter stages
WHERE filters rows first; HAVING filters after aggregation and can use aggregates.
Final thought

Always map the business question to a grouping level and then choose WHERE or HAVING appropriately to get the intended summary.
