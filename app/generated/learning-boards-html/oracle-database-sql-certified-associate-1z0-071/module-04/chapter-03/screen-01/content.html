Understanding Different Types of Joins

Combine related rows across tables to form meaningful datasets for reports and analysis. Learn to avoid accidental cross joins and choose the right outer join to include unmatched rows.

In this lesson
Why joins exist, Cartesian product, INNER JOIN with ON and USING, and LEFT/RIGHT/FULL OUTER JOIN behavior with NULLs.
Coming up
Examples with employees and departments, common mistakes, and practice queries to try in your lab.
Explore Key Topics
Cartesian Product
Every row pairs with every other row; often accidental and large in size.
INNER JOIN
Returns only matching rows using a join condition like department ID equality.
OUTER JOINs
LEFT, RIGHT, and FULL preserve unmatched rows from one or both tables by filling with NULLs.
