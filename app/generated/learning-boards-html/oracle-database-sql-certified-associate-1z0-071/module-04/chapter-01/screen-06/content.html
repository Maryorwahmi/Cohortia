Module 04 › Chapter 4.1 › SUM and AVG with NULLs
Behavior

SUM ignores nulls; AVG ignores nulls in numerator and denominator

Decide whether NULL means unknown or zero. If it should be zero, replace it first with NVL or COALESCE.

• SUM totals only present numeric values; NULLs are not added.
• AVG computes the mean using only non-null entries by default.
• NVL(column, 0) or COALESCE(column, 0) can convert NULL to zero before aggregation.
Example scenario
To compute average commission treating NULL as zero, substitute NULLs first. Otherwise, the average divides by fewer employees and may misrepresent the overall rate.
-- Visual queries (run these in your SQL client)
-- Average commission excluding NULLs
SELECT AVG(commission_pct) FROM employees;

-- Average commission treating NULL as zero
SELECT AVG(NVL(commission_pct, 0)) FROM employees;

-- Total salary (NULLs ignored)
SELECT SUM(salary) FROM employees;
Check Your Understanding

Compare the two average results and ask whether NULL represents missing data or a true zero for your business question.
