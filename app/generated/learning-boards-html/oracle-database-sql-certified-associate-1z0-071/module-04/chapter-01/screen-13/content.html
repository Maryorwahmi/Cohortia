Module 04 › Chapter 4.1 › Mini Practice Checklist

Practice steps

Step 1
Run COUNT(*) and COUNT(column). Record differences.
Step 2
Run SUM and AVG on salary. Note null handling.
Step 3
Run MIN and MAX. If you need rows, run a lookup for matching values.
Step 4
Run COUNT(DISTINCT job_id) to find unique job categories.
Step 5
Compute AVG with NVL or COALESCE to treat NULL as zero and compare results.
Step 6
Prepare a one-paragraph explanation of what changed and why.
Key Takeaway

A short, repeatable checklist ensures your aggregates are correct and your interpretation is defensible.
