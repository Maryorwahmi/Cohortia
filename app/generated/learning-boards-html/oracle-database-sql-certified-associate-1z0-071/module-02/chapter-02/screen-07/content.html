Logical operators
AND
Both conditions must be true. Use to narrow results.
OR
At least one condition true. Use to broaden results.
NOT
Negates a condition; use for exclusion.
Examples & notes
When you mix AND and OR, precedence can change results. Use parentheses to group terms explicitly, for example require A and either B or C by writing A and (B or C). Test subexpressions independently to avoid logic mistakes.
Check your logic by evaluating each clause on its own before combining.
Pro-tip: parenthesize complex expressions for readability, even when precedence would give the desired result.
