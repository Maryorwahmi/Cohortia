Architecture snapshot
Routing Engine (RE)
Runs routing protocols, CLI, and system processes. Acts as the control plane.
Example: OSPF or BGP processes update routing tables here.
Packet Forwarding Engine (PFE)
Specialized hardware that forwards packets at wire speed based on programmed tables.
Example: ASICs forward traffic even if a control process restarts.
