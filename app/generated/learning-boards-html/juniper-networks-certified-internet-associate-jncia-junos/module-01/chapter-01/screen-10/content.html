Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Juniper Platforms
MX Series
Universal routing platforms for service provider and enterprise routing.
Use case: high-scale routing, MPLS.
SRX Series
Services gateways that combine routing with integrated security functions.
Use case: firewalling, VPN termination.
EX Series
Campus and data center access-layer Ethernet switches.
Use case: campus access, PoE, VLANs.
QFX Series
High-performance switches for data center and cloud fabrics.
Use case: spine/leaf, high throughput fabrics.
Guidance
Choose a platform based on workload and feature needs. Junos knowledge transfers across these platforms, but hardware capabilities determine scale and performance.
