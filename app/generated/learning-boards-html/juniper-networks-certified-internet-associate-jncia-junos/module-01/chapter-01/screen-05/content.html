Architecture

Modular processes derived from FreeBSD

Why modular?
  • Limits the impact of a single process failure.
  • Makes debugging targeted to the offending process.
  • Enables safer, incremental upgrades of components.
Concrete example
Routing daemons update tables on the RE; the PFE uses those tables without running the routing processes itself.
Misconception: modular means slow — in reality it prevents broad outages and supports high availability.
Process callouts
Routing daemons, CLI shell, system management processes, each isolated for stability.
Pro tip

When troubleshooting, identify the failing process in logs to avoid chasing unrelated symptoms.
