Key Concepts
What to Remember
Three core practices that reduce risk and simplify operations.
Hostname
A unique descriptive name for device identification.
Root Password
Protect the highest-privilege account with a strong password.
Commit
Validate and activate staged changes; use commit check and commit confirmed when appropriate.
Summary Takeaway

Follow the draft-validate-activate pattern: stage edits in candidate, validate them, use timed commits for remote changes, and rely on rollback to recover quickly when needed.
