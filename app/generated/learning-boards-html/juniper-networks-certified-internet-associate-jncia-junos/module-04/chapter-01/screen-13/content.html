Longest Prefix Match
Most specific route matching the destination is selected.
Preference
Ranks trustworthiness of route sources; lower is preferred.
Metric
Protocol-specific cost used to prefer paths inside the same protocol.
Reflection prompt
Pick a route from show route and explain: why it is active, what its next-hop is, and whether you trust it based on preference and protocol.
