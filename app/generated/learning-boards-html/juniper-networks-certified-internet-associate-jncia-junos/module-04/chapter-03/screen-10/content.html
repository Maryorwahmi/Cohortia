Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › DR / BDR
Why DR/BDR exist

To prevent an explosion of adjacencies on multi-access networks by centralizing LSA origination and adjacency maintenance on the DR and BDR.

DR: elected router that originates the network LSA and maintains adjacencies with others.
BDR: standby router that takes over if the DR fails.
DRothers: form full adjacencies only with DR and BDR, not with each other.
Election rules

Routers use priority to elect DR and BDR; when priorities are equal the highest Router ID decides the winner. This ensures deterministic selection.

Key takeaway

DR/BDR simplify multi-access OSPF behavior and limit the number of adjacencies required to synchronize LSDBs.
