Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › IGP vs EGP
Interior Gateway Protocols (IGP)
Used inside an autonomous system. Examples in our source: OSPF, IS-IS, EIGRP, RIP. These protocols exchange topology or reachability to ensure internal routing works efficiently.
Focus
Fast convergence and consistent internal topology view.
Why use IGP
Internal reachability and efficient path selection inside a single administrative domain.
Exterior Gateway Protocols (EGP)
Used between autonomous systems. The primary example is BGP, which connects different administrative domains like ISPs and large organizations.
Focus
Policy-driven inter-AS routing and reachability exchange.
Why use EGP
To carry routes between different administrative entities, incorporating routing policies and filtering.
Key takeaway

Use IGPs for internal routing and EGPs for inter-AS policy and reachability; they solve different problems.
