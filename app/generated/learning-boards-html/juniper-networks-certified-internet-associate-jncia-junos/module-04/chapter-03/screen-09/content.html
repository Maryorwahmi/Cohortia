Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Adjacency Process
Adjacency states
Routers progress: Down → Init → 2-Way → ExStart → Exchange → Loading → Full. Full means LSDBs are synchronized and routing exchange can proceed.
Hello: discover neighbors and agree on timers.
Exchange/Loading: synchronize LSAs so LSDBs match.
Full: adjacency complete; SPF can compute routes using consistent data.
Troubleshooting
If neighbors do not reach Full, check Hello and Dead intervals, authentication settings, and network type. Mismatches are the most common cause.
Check

Confirm timers and authentication match on both sides of an adjacency before investigating deeper state issues.
