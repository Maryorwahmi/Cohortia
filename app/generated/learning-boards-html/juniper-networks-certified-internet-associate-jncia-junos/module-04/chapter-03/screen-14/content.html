Dynamic vs Static
Dynamic routes adapt and scale; static routes do not recover from failures automatically.
DV vs LS
DV = table exchanges; LS = LSAs and SPF for a full topology map.
OSPF Essentials
Areas limit impact, Area 0 is backbone, and DR/BDR reduce adjacency churn on LANs.
