Dynamic Routing Concepts and OSPF
Understand why we use dynamic protocols, how they differ, and the foundations of OSPF.
In this lesson

Explore dynamic routing benefits, IGP vs EGP, distance-vector vs link-state, and OSPF building blocks like areas and DR/BDR.

Why it matters

Large networks need automatic route sharing and fast adaptation to failures; static routes can cause blackholes when links fail.

Quick preview

You will learn conceptual distinctions and specific OSPF roles so you can reason about protocol choice and basic troubleshooting.
