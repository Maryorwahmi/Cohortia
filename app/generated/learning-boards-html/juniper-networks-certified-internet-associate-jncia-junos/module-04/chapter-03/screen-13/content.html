Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Dynamic Routing
Routers discover topology and adapt routes automatically, improving scalability and fault tolerance.
DV vs LS
Distance-vector shares tables and can be slow; link-state floods LSAs and builds full topology maps for SPF.
OSPF Basics
Areas limit LSA scope, Area 0 is the backbone, and DR/BDR reduce adjacencies on multi-access segments.
Final takeaway

Use these three checks—scope, mechanism, structure—when choosing protocols and diagnosing problems.
