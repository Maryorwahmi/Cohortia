Hierarchical routing with areas

OSPF divides the domain into areas to contain LSA flooding and reduce the scope of SPF recalculations. This improves scalability and performance on routers.

Area 0
The backbone. All other areas must connect to Area 0.
Non-backbone areas
Contain LSA scope and limit SPF for local changes.
Area Border Router
Connects areas and advertises inter-area routes.
Design guidance

Avoid putting everything into Area 0; instead, partition the network logically to reduce LSDB size and SPF frequency. Use ABRs to summarize where appropriate.

Key Takeaway

Areas are about containment and scale; Area 0 remains the hub for inter-area routing.
