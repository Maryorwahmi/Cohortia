Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts — Terms & Risks
Next-Hop
The IP of the adjacent router that receives forwarded packets.
Stub Network
A network with a single entry/exit point, a good candidate for static routing.
Blackhole
When traffic is routed to a next-hop that cannot forward it further, causing packet loss.
Safety reminder
Before finishing a change: commit, show route protocol static, and ping the next-hop. These three steps catch the most common static-route failures.
