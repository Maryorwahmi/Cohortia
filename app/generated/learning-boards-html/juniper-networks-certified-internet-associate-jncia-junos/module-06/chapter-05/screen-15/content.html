Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Quick Reference
Match Proposals
Ensure encryption, hash, DH group and lifetimes match on both peers for both Phase 1 and Phase 2.
Bind & Route
Bind the vpn to st0 and route protected networks via st0 so packets enter the encrypted tunnel.
Verify & Log
Use SA and interface commands, and review logs for authentication or negotiation failures.
Keep this checklist handy when you configure or troubleshoot VPNs. It reduces time-to-resolve and helps you adopt secure defaults.
