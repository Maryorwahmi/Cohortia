Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Phase 2 Rekeying
Phase 2 Sequence
  1. 1. Match an IPsec proposal that defines ESP/AH, encryption and integrity algorithms.
  2. 2. Optionally negotiate Perfect Forward Secrecy with a DH group for a fresh key exchange.
  3. 3. Agree on a lifetime for the IPsec SA and create the tunnel that will protect traffic.
  4. 4. When lifetime nears expiry, the peers perform rekeying to renew the SA.
Why Lifetimes Matter
Lifetimes limit exposure from a compromised key. Shorter lifetimes increase rekeying but reduce risk.
PFS Advantage
PFS ensures new DH exchange for each SA so compromise of one key cannot decrypt others.
Common Misstep
Setting mismatched lifetimes or disabling PFS on one peer while enabled on the other will cause Phase 2 negotiations to fail.
Example Negotiation
Peer A proposes: ESP / AES-256-CBC / HMAC-SHA-256 / PFS group14 / lifetime 3600s. Peer B must accept an identical proposal and either accept PFS group14 or fail the match. If both accept, an IPsec SA is created and traffic flows over the secured tunnel.
Verification Tip
Use the device's SA show commands to verify active lifetimes and whether PFS is applied for a given SA.
Check Your Understanding
If Phase 2 fails, list the three top items you would compare on both peers: proposal algorithms, PFS setting, and lifetime values.
