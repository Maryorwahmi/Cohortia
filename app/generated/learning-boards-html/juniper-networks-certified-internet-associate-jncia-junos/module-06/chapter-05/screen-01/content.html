Introduction to VPNs and Junos IPsec
Secure tunnels, IKE phases, and Junos configuration building blocks.
In this lesson

Understand VPN purpose, IKE and IPsec phases, ESP vs AH, and how Junos maps proposals and policies to real tunnels.

Why it matters

VPNs protect confidential traffic across untrusted networks and allow distributed teams to access shared resources safely.

Common pitfall

Mismatched cryptographic parameters across peers will prevent tunnel formation even when interfaces and routing are correct.

Concept map
Site-to-site → IPsec → Junos elements
Site-to-site
Always-on network-to-network tunnels, transparent to users.
IPsec
Framework of protocols: IKE negotiates keys, ESP carries encrypted traffic.
Junos
Proposals, policies, gateways, and st0 tie configuration to a tunnel interface.
