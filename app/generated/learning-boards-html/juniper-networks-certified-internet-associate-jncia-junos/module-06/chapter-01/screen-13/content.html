Planning HA: Addressing, Timers, Paths

Operational detail makes HA resilient in practice.

Addressing
Place IPs on logical aggregated interfaces and document allocations to avoid configuration drift that breaks failover behavior.
Protocol timers
Tune routing and LACP timers for the desired failover speed, balancing responsiveness against stability.
Path independence
Ensure backup paths do not share the primary physical conduit. Validate with cable and path diversity tests.
Practical check
Draw the physical and logical paths and confirm no single device or cable is critical to both primary and backup routes.
