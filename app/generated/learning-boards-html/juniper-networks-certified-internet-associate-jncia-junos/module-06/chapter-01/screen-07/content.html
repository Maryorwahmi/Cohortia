Graceful Routing Engine Switchover

A four-step process that keeps control and forwarding consistent during an RE switch.

1. Detect
Failure detection triggers the switchover sequence.
2. Promote
Standby RE becomes active, using synchronized control state.
3. Activate
Control plane processes continue without full restart when possible.
4. Verify
Confirm routing and forwarding tables are valid and traffic is uninterrupted.
Example
A standby RE with synchronized OSPF adjacencies takes over without neighbor flaps, keeping data flows alive.
Misconception
GRES alone isn’t a substitute for planned testing and verifying routing timers and state replication.
