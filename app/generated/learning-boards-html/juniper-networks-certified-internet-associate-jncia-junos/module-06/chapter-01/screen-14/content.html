Key Concepts

Three core reminders from the chapter.

High Availability (HA)
Systems designed to remain operational and minimize downtime.
Stateful Synchronization
Replicates session and NAT state so connections survive failover.
Link Aggregation (LAG)
Bundles links for increased bandwidth and redundancy; use LACP for negotiation.
Summary Takeaway

Combine hardware redundancy, software readiness, stateful synchronization, and routine testing to achieve reliable HA in Junos networks.
