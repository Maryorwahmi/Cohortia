Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP)

LACP negotiates bundle membership and monitors link health for predictable failover.

How LACP works
  • Exchanges periodic control messages to confirm membership and link health.
  • Can operate in active or passive mode; active initiates negotiation.
  • Removes failing links from the bundle so traffic moves to healthy members.
Example
Enable LACP with fast periodic messages to ensure quick reaction to link flaps during a failure test.
Misconfiguration trap
Mismatched LACP settings on either side or inconsistent interface speeds can prevent successful aggregation. LACP helps detect these issues but cannot correct mismatched physical configuration.
Pro tip
Use active LACP on one side and fast periodic timers when you need rapid detection and removal of failed member links.
