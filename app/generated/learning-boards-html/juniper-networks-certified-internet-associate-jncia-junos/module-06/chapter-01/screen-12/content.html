Testing and Common Mistakes

A checklist to validate HA and avoid common traps.

Test checklist
  1. Document expected switchover behavior and metrics to monitor.
  2. Perform controlled failovers for RE, PFE, and link failures.
  3. Verify session continuity and review state tables post-failover.
  4. Restore and re-run tests regularly after changes.
Common design mistakes
  • Relying on hardware redundancy without state replication.
  • Backup paths that share a single physical dependency with primary paths.
  • Skipping verification of routing timers and LACP settings.
