Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › How Policies Are Evaluated
Step 1
Identify source and destination zones
The device determines the from-zone and to-zone for the traffic before evaluating any policies.
Step 2
Evaluate policies top to bottom
Within that zone pair, the first policy whose match conditions are satisfied is applied and processing stops.
Step 3
Implicit deny if no match
If no policy matches, the traffic is denied by default, which is a security best practice.
Pro tip
When adding rules, place narrow denies or specific permits above broader permits, and document policy order to avoid surprises.
Concrete scenario
You need to block a single compromised host from reaching the internet. Create a deny policy that matches that host and place it above a general permit all policy; otherwise the deny will be shadowed.
Common mistake
Adding a specific deny below a permissive rule causes the deny to never match. Policy order, not presence alone, determines effect.
