Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Upgrade planning
Pre-upgrade checks
  • Read release notes for prerequisites and known issues.
  • Confirm disk space and platform compatibility.
  • Backup the active configuration to local or remote storage.
  • Schedule a maintenance window and notify stakeholders.
After the reboot, run verification checks against routing tables and services. Document the upgrade and any corrective actions so future upgrades are smoother.
Why this matters
Skipping these steps increases the risk of prolonged outages and complicates vendor-assisted recovery.
