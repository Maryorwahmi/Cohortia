Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Troubleshooting Methodology
Steps
  1. Define the problem: scope and impact.
  2. Gather information: show commands, syslog, traceoptions, ping, traceroute, monitor traffic.
  3. Analyze the data and identify patterns.
  4. Formulate a hypothesis based on evidence.
  5. Test the hypothesis with controlled changes or targeted checks.
  6. Implement the fix if confirmed and verify.
  7. Document the incident, solution, and lessons learned.
Practical tip
Collect logs and important outputs early; they often contain the clue that accelerates analysis.
