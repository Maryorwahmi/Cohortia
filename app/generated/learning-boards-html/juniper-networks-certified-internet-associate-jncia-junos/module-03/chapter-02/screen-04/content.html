Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › What is syslog in Junos OS?
Definition
Syslog: structured event captures
Syslog records events with a facility and a severity so administrators can filter, store, and forward messages appropriately for auditing and troubleshooting.
Analogy
Think of syslog as an indexed library: facility is the section, severity is the label that determines urgency.
Misconception
Logging everything at debug is useful forever — no. Keep verbose logs short-lived.
Example syslog line
Jun 21 10:30:05 R1 rpd[1234]: RPD_OSPF_NBRDOWN: OSPF neighbor 192.168.1.2 is down
Facility & Severity
Facilities classify the origin, such as kernel, daemon, or security. Severity ranks urgency from emergency to debug. Use those fields to decide what to store locally versus forward remotely.
Key takeaway
Choose forwarding rules by facility and severity so remote collectors get high-value events and local files retain operational detail.
