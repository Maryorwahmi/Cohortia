Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Key Concepts
Syslog
Standard protocol for device event messages; use facility and severity filters to control what you collect and forward.
Traceoptions
Local, detailed debugging for specific processes or protocols; always bound file sizes and rotate files.
Rotation & Limits
Use size and files parameters to manage disk usage and avoid uncontrolled growth from verbose logging.
Summary takeaway
Design logging to be selective, bounded, and reversible: forward what you need, capture detailed traces briefly, and always clean up traceoptions after the task.
