Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
ping
Tests reachability and measures round-trip time using ICMP echo requests and replies.
traceroute
Reveals the route packets take by sending probes with increasing TTL and observing time-exceeded replies.
ICMP
The protocol used for echo and time-exceeded messages that support ping and traceroute diagnostics.
Summary
Remember: ping checks reachability, traceroute finds the failing hop, and ICMP is the mechanism that carries those diagnostic messages.
