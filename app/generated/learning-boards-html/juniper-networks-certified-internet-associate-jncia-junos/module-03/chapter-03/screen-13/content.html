Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
TTL
Limits packet lifetime and is the mechanism traceroute manipulates to discover intermediate hops.
SSH vs Telnet
SSH is encrypted and for management; telnet is plaintext and only for controlled lab or quick port checks.
Monitor traffic filters
Always scope captures by host, port, or protocol to avoid overwhelming output and to find relevant packets quickly.
Final takeaway
Follow the evidence: use the right tool in sequence, narrow your scope with filters, and document outputs so you can explain and repeat your diagnosis.
