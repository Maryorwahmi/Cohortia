Common mistakes and operational hygiene
Simple routines reduce risk across your device fleet.
Do not share accounts; use unique users so actions are attributable.
Avoid overuse of super-user; assign operator or custom classes where appropriate.
Disable or remove inactive accounts and run periodic account audits.
Document any temporary privilege changes and remove them when work is complete.
Audit step
List users, classes, and last login times to identify stale accounts for removal.
Pro tip
Use centralized authentication so audits and policies apply consistently across devices.
Checklist
  • Unique accounts for each admin
  • Least privilege assigned
  • Regular account reviews
Key Takeaway
Operational routines like audits and timely disabling of accounts keep your environment resilient and auditable.
