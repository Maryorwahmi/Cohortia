Local accounts vs. remote authentication
Compare trade-offs to pick the right approach for your network.
Local users
Stored on the device, simple to configure, ideal for small sites or emergency fallback.
Remote servers
Centralized management with consistent policies, easier audits, better scalability.
Best practice
Configure authentication-order so the device knows whether to try remote servers first and when to fall back to local accounts.
Example scenario
Large deployments with hundreds of devices benefit from RADIUS or TACACS+ to enforce a single password policy.
Pitfall
Relying solely on remote servers without a local fallback can lock you out if network issues occur; keep local accounts as a safe fallback.
