'from' statement
Defines all conditions a route must meet; conditions are ANDed.
route-filter vs prefix-list
Use route-filter for length semantics and prefix-list for reusable literal prefix sets.
BGP attributes
neighbor, as-path, community, origin — define globally and test propagation.
Takeaway

Precise matches, reusable definitions, and thorough verification are the pillars of safe routing policy design.
