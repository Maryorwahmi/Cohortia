Configuring Routing Policies — Match Conditions
Use 'from' statements to precisely select routes by prefix, protocol, and BGP attributes so that policy actions affect exactly the routes you intend.
In this lesson
Match by prefix, protocol, neighbor, as-path, community; common mistakes and a practice lab.
Why it matters
Routing policies control reachability and traffic flow. Wrong matches cause outages or leak routes; precise matches ensure predictable behavior.
Explore Key Topics
Prefix matching
route-filter, exact/longer/upto, and prefix-length-range.
Protocol & neighbor
Match by protocol (OSPF, BGP) or a specific neighbor address.
BGP attributes
as-path, community, origin — use global definitions for reuse.
