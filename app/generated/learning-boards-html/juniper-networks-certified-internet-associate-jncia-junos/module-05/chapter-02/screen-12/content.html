Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Verification & Troubleshooting
Verify attributes
Use route inspection to determine whether a policy should match before changing policies.
Useful commands
show route <prefix> detail — view protocol, AS path, communities. show route protocol <protocol> — list routes from a protocol.
Troubleshooting steps
  1. Confirm the route has the attribute you expect.
  2. Check that referenced objects exist and names are correct.
  3. Verify term order and logical consistency of 'from' conditions.
Key Takeaway

Inspect route attributes first. Policy mismatches are often caused by missing attributes or typos, not by policy engine bugs.

Example verification
show route 192.168.10.0/24 detail
  # Inspect protocol, AS path, communities
show route protocol bgp
  # List BGP learned routes
Use the output to confirm that the attributes you match are present before assuming the policy is incorrect.
Safety tip
Test in a simulated environment or apply policies in a staging context before committing to production to avoid accidental route leaks or blackholes.
