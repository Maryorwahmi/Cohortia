Policy Anatomy
policy-statement contains ordered terms. Each term has from and then clauses.
• from: match conditions like prefix, protocol, AS-path, community.
• then: actions such as accept, reject, or set attributes.
• term without from: matches everything and must be ordered intentionally.
Example terms
accept-ospf: from protocol ospf then accept. reject-others: then reject (catch-all).
Visual: Term Stack

Term: accept-internal
from: prefix 10.0.0.0/8 → then: accept
Term: reject-others
no from → then: reject (explicit catch-all)
Key takeaway
Structure your terms so specific matches appear before catch-alls and document the intent with clear term names.
