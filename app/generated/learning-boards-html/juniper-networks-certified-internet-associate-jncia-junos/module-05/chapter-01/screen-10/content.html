Implicit reject explained
If a route does not match any term, Junos rejects it by default.
• This default prevents unintended acceptance but can cause surprises.
• Always add an explicit final term to document the intended default behavior.
• Example pattern: term accept-ospf then accept; term reject-others then reject.
Practical safeguard
Use a final explicit catch-all term to make your policy self-explanatory and avoid accidental blackholes.
Example
set term default-accept then accept — this overrides the implicit reject if your intent is to accept unmatched routes.
