Practical Skills
Apply policies safely and verify behavior in a testable workflow
Task 1
Create ACCEPT-OSPF-ONLY
Define two terms: accept OSPF and reject others. Commit in lab and check the RIB.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Task 2
Apply import/export
Attach the policy at the protocol or routing-instance application point and observe route behavior to ensure intent.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Task 3
Order terms safely
Place specific matches before general ones. Use a final accept term if you intend to accept unmatched routes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Task 4
Test and rollback
Validate policy effects in a lab or maintenance window and keep a rollback plan to avoid blackholes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
