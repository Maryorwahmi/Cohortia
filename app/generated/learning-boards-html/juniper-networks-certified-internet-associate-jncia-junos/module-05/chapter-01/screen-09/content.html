Module 05 › Chapter 5.1 › Sequential Processing
Process: First-match-wins
Terms are evaluated in order; the first term that matches determines the action.
• Step 1: Evaluate Term 1's from conditions.
• Step 2: If match, apply then actions and stop; otherwise evaluate next term.
• Step 3: If no term matches, implicit reject occurs unless you provide an explicit catch-all.
Example
Term1: reject 10.0.0.0/8 — Term2: accept 10.0.0.0/16 — A route 10.1.1.0/24 will be rejected by Term1.
Design tips
Place specific rejects or accepts before general rules. Reserve final terms for intended defaults and document them clearly.
Check your order
Review term order after writing policies. If a route behaves unexpectedly, check earlier terms for accidental broader matches.
Question
When would you intentionally place a broad reject earlier than a specific accept?
