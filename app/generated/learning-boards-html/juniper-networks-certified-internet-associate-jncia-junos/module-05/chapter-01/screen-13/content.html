Module 05 › Chapter 5.1 › Applying Policies
Attachment points
Apply policies under protocol configuration or routing-instance import/export statements.
• Protocol level: attach as import/export for that neighbor or group.
• Routing instance: attach policies for VRF-specific behavior.
Safety practices
Test in lab, apply to limited peers first, use monitoring and counters, and have a rollback plan.
Apply incrementally
Attach policies to one peer and observe before wider rollout.
Monitor
Use policy match counters or syslog to confirm expected matches after commit.
