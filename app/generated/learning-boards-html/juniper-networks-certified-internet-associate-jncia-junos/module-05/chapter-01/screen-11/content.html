Ordering pitfalls
Simple order mistakes cause large outages; review and test carefully.
Mistake
Placing a broad reject before a specific accept, which prevents the specific rule from ever matching.
Mistake
Forgetting final action and assuming the policy defaults to accept.
Practice
Test each term in isolation in a lab, and simulate overlapping matches to confirm the winning term.
Tip
Name terms descriptively (accept-ospf, reject-10-net) so reviewers understand intent at a glance.
