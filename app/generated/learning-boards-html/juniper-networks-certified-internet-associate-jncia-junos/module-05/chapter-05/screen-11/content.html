Best Practices › Ordering & Testing
Ordering rule
Put specific matches first. Example: deny a problematic host before accepting its subnet if you need the exception to apply.
Explicit disposition
Make final actions explicit: choose accept or discard rather than rely on implicit behavior. It clarifies intent and reduces surprises.
Testing checklist
  • Test in a lab environment before production.
  • Use show firewall and show firewall policers to verify counters and policer activity.
  • Monitor traffic on interfaces to observe packet flow during rollout.
Key takeaway
Test, verify counters, and always have a rollback plan when applying new or changed filters.
