Introduction to Firewall Filters
Understand how Junos OS inspects packets, applies ordered rules, and enforces an implicit default-deny posture to protect network resources.
Filter at a glance
Ordered terms inspect packets and apply an action on first match.
Stateless
Each packet is checked alone; connections are not tracked.
Implicit deny
Anything not matched is discarded by default.
In this lesson: terms, match criteria, actions, sequencing, input/output application, and a safe CLI template for protecting a management interface.
