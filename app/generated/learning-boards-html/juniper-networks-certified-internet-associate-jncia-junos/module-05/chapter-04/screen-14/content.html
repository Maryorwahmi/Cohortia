Key Concepts
Three compact reminders to keep your filters safe and effective.
Firewall Filter
A set of ordered terms that control packet flow based on match conditions and actions.
Stateless Filter
Each packet is evaluated independently; plan symmetric rules for two-way traffic.
Implicit Discard
Packets that match no term are discarded by default; explicit permits are required for allowed traffic.
Summary Takeaway

Design filters with narrow matches and intentional ordering, stage with observation, and always verify before committing to production.
