Why it matters
Operational scenarios
Filters answer practical needs from security to traffic management in production networks.
Management protection
Allow only management hosts to reach SSH or SNMP on router interfaces.
Protocol restriction
Block risky protocols from user networks while permitting business-critical services.
Traffic shaping & monitoring
Policer and count allow you to rate-limit noisy flows and observe traffic before enforcement.
Concrete example
Prevent accidental BGP peering by matching only known neighbor source addresses, and discard others. This avoids session hijacks and configuration mistakes.
Key Takeaway

Filters provide focused control at the packet level and are essential for securing router services and shaping network behavior.
