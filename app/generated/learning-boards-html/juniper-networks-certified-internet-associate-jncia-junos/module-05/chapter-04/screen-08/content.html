Definition
'then' actions
Actions determine whether packets are allowed, dropped, reported, or rate-limited when a term matches.
accept
Permit the packet to continue processing beyond the filter point.
discard
Silently drop the packet. Use when you want stealthy denial.
reject
Drop and send an ICMP unreachable; useful for debugging but reveals information.
Observation and rate control
Use 'count' and 'log' to measure matches without changing behavior, and 'policer' to limit throughput of matched traffic.
then count
then log
then policer police-1
Practical pattern

Stage rules: count and log first, review results, then change to discard or accept as operational needs require.
