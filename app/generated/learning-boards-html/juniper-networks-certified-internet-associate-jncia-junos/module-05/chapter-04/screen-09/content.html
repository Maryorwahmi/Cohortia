Process Flow
Sequential processing
Order your terms intentionally because the first matching term wins.
Rule ordering
Specific deny rules should be placed before general accept rules to ensure they are applied.
Implicit discard
Any packet that doesn't match a term is discarded by default; this enforces a deny-all posture.
Scenario
If term 1 discards traffic from a specific source and term 2 accepts port 80, a packet from that source to port 80 will be discarded because the first match applied.
Common mistake

Placing broad accept rules early creates accidental permissions and bypasses later denies.

Remember to test with 'count' and logs, and consider adding an explicit final 'then accept' only if you really intend to permit unmatched traffic.
