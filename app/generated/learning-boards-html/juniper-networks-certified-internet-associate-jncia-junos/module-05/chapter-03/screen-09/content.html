Definition
as-path prepend
Prepend AS numbers on export to make the route appear longer to others and therefore less preferred.
set policy-options policy-statement PREPEND-AS term 1 then as-path prepend "65000 65000"
set policy-options policy-statement PREPEND-AS term 1 then accept
Best practice: prepend on exports when you want inbound traffic reduced on a specific link, but test for ripple effects and avoid loops.
Verification
Check the AS_PATH seen by your neighbor and the subsequent propagation with show route advertising-protocol bgp <neighbor> and inspect AS_PATH fields.
Common mistake

Over-prepending can make a route so unattractive that it is effectively unreachable from large parts of the Internet; be conservative and test.
