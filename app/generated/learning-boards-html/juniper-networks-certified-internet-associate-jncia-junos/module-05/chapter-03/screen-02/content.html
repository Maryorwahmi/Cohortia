Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Learning Objectives
01
Accept & Reject
Configure explicit accept or reject so routes behave as intended.
Mnemonic: think 'gatekeeper' — accept opens the gate, reject closes it. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Policy Flow
Use next-term or next-policy to control evaluation order and composition.
Mnemonic: a conveyor belt — next-term keeps items on the same belt, next-policy moves to the next belt. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
BGP Attribute Edits
Add or change local-pref, community, AS path, MED, origin, and next-hop to influence routing.
Mnemonic: tag and tune — tag with communities, tune with preference values. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Safety & Scope
Recognize attribute scope and test changes in lab before production.
Mnemonic: internal vs external — not all attributes travel across neighbors. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
