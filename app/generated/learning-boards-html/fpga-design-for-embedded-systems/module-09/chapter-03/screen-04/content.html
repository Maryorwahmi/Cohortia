Definition

System Requirements

  • ADC: MAX 10, 12-bit, up to 1 MSPS.
  • Capture: trigger-based 256 samples, circular buffer in dual-port BRAM.
  • DSP: 16-tap FIR or FFT option.
  • Comms: UART at 115200 to PC terminal.
  • UI: Button 1 start/stop, Button 2 change filter/display.
  • Processor: NIOS II for control and UART handling.
Concrete Example
A clear spec entry:
Capture Window
256 samples at 500 kSPS, trigger when ADC crosses threshold, send captured block over UART as ASCII waveform or numeric values.
Why it matters
Concrete numbers let you choose BRAM size, CPU loop timing, and whether filtering must be hardware-accelerated to meet throughput.
Interface Table
List base addresses and data widths for ADC, UART, BRAM, GPIO.
Performance
Declare clock constraints (50 MHz) and acceptable latency for filter output.
Trigger
Define threshold crossing behavior, pre/post trigger samples, and capture confirmation via LED.
