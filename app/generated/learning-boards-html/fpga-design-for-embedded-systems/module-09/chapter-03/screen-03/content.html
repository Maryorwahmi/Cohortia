Verified Competencies

Verification Milestone
ModelSim testbenches with waveforms for ADC read, buffer wrap-around, and filtered output. These form the evidence for correctness.
Timing & Synthesis
Quartus compile results with timing reports and resource usage. Ensure the design meets the 50 MHz clock constraint and fix any setup violations.
Board Debug
Use JTAG to load software, step through the NIOS II application, monitor LEDs, and observe signals with a logic analyzer to verify the ADC and trigger paths.
Documentation
A report with block diagrams, code excerpts, test results, and a short demo video showing input, filtered output, and UART traces to the PC terminal.
