Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › adc_interface & circular_buffer
adc_interface
Provide a stable data_valid signal and align sample timing to your system clock. Debounce or synchronize any asynchronous ADC-ready flags and tag each sample with a counter for trigger logic.
Signals
  • sample_out[11:0]
  • data_valid
  • write_enable
circular_buffer
Implement 256-depth dual-port BRAM with independent read/write pointers. Use modulo arithmetic and a sample count for full/empty detection.
Testbench idea
Drive a repeating ramp and random glitches from the ADC, assert trigger at threshold, and verify captured 256 samples and wrap-around behavior in ModelSim waveforms.
Common Mistake
Reading and writing pointers without synchronization across clock domains can produce corrupted reads. Either keep a single clock domain for ADC path or add synchronizers when crossing domains.
