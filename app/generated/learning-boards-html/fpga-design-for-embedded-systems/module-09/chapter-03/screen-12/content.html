JTAG and NIOS II
Load the bitstream, then download the ELF to NIOS II via JTAG. Use breakpoints to confirm peripheral initialization and ISR behavior.
Quick Check
Blink an LED in the timer ISR to verify the timer and IRQ are functioning before complex capture tests.
Logic Analyzer
Probe adc_interface, BRAM addr/data, and fir_filter handshakes. Capture the trigger event and confirm 256 sample writes then readback.
Record Evidence
Save terminal logs, waveform screenshots, and a short video showing potentiometer movement, capture, and UART output for the report and rubric.
