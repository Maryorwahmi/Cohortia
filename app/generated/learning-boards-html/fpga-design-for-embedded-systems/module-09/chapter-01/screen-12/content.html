Key Concepts
Three compact reminders of what matters.
GPIO Controller
Pins are registers; use clocked updates and input synchronization for reliability.
UART & SPI
UART needs correct baud sampling; SPI offers full-duplex with dedicated clock and chip select lines.
BRAM & SDRAM
Infer BRAM from RTL arrays; use vendor SDRAM IP for refresh and burst transfers.
Takeaway

Choose the right interface for your device, verify timing in simulation, and confirm behavior with small hardware tests.
