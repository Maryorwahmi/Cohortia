GPIO
Pins become registers in your HDL. Use a small controller to read buttons and drive LEDs for immediate hardware feedback.
Serial Links
UART for simple byte streams; SPI for faster full-duplex transfers. I2C trades speed for fewer wires and bus arbitration complexity.
Memory & ADC
BRAM for local dual-port reads; SDRAM via controller IP for larger storage. MAX 10 ADC captures analog sensors directly in fabric.
