SPI vs I2C
Compare wires, timing, and implementation complexity.
SPI — Key Points
  • Signals: SCLK, MOSI, MISO, CS.
  • Full duplex; master controls clock and chip select.
  • Simpler HDL: generate clock edges and shift registers.
Example: Read flash memory with a burst transfer while SCLK toggles at high frequency.
I2C — Key Points
  • Signals: SDA and SCL with open-drain drivers and pull-ups.
  • Supports multiple masters and slaves with addressing and ACK bits.
  • HDL must implement start/stop detection and arbitration handling.
Common Mistake: forgetting to model open-drain behavior or neglecting bus capacitance effects on speed.
Choose SPI
Fast, full-duplex transfers for flash and sensors.
Choose I2C
Multiple low-pin-count devices, slower but flexible bus.
Key Takeaway

Match the bus to the device’s speed, wiring constraints, and multi-device requirements before writing HDL.
