Learning Objectives
Learn the interfaces, implement memory, use ADC, and verify with testbenches.
01
GPIO & Basic Verilog
Configure FPGA pins as inputs or outputs and map them to registers. This lets you read buttons and drive LEDs directly from HDL.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
UART & Serial Timing
Implement a UART transmitter and receiver including baud generation and bit shifting. Validate with a logic analyzer and testbench timing checks.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
SPI & I2C Tradeoffs
Use SPI for fast full-duplex links and I2C where fewer wires and multi-master support are needed. Understand open-drain arbitration for I2C.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Memory & ADC Basics
Infer BRAM for fast local memory, use SDRAM through an IP controller, and read analog signals with the MAX 10 ADC via Avalon or HAL calls.
Verified Competency Milestone
