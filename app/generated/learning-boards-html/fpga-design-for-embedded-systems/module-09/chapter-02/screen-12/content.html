Module 9 › Chapter 9.2 › FPGA vs MCU
FPGA Strengths
  • Parallelism: many operations at once.
  • Deterministic timing and low-latency responses.
  • High sample-rate DSP and MHz-range control loops.
Microcontroller Strengths
  • Faster development for control logic and UI tasks.
  • Lower cost for simple low-rate tasks.
  • Easier software debugging and toolchains for algorithm tweaks.
Design Question
Estimate operations per sample on the MCU and compare with FPGA sample-per-clock capability. Choose the platform that meets timing and development constraints.
