Module 9 › Chapter 9.2 › Overview
FPGA-Based Signal Processing and Motor Control
High-throughput FIR filters, PWM timing, encoder decoding, and hardware PID loops
What you'll learn

How to implement FIR filters using shift registers and parallel multipliers, how to build PWM and encoder readers, and how to design a PID loop in hardware.

Why it matters

FPGA parallelism yields deterministic timing and high sample throughput, key for control stability and high-frequency DSP where microcontrollers fall short.
