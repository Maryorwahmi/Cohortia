Parallel Throughput
Convert loops to parallel hardware to reach one sample per clock after latency fill.
DSP & Pipelining
Use DSP tiles for multiplies and pipeline adder stages to meet frequency targets.
Synchronized Integration
Sync ADC and encoder signals, clamp accumulators, and use a processor for non-time-critical tasks.
