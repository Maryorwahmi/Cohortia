FIR Filter Verification
  • Apply an impulse to check tap responses and coefficient signs.
  • Apply a step to observe settling and DC gain.
  • Measure throughput: one output per clock after pipeline fill.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Motor Control Verification
  • Simulate PWM levels and compute frequency from counter size.
  • Feed synthetic A/B encoder waveforms to confirm counting and direction.
  • Step-test PID and observe overshoot and settling time.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Why this order matters

Design then verify. A correct functional simulation gives confidence before synthesis and mapping to DSP blocks or board-level testing. This reduces hardware debug iterations and helps reason about timing, latency, and resource usage.
