Filter Tests
  • Impulse response: output sequence equals coefficients.
  • Step response: check DC gain and settling time.
  • Coefficient sign checks and overflow assertions.
Control & PWM Tests
  • PWM duty vs high-time measurement.
  • Encoder waveform injection and position verification.
  • PID step tests: measure overshoot, settle, and steady-state error.
Practical Tip
Include assertions in your testbench for range checks and timing invariants. Run worst-case input rates to find timing or synchronization bugs early.
