Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Place & Route
What the fitter does
Assigns logic to physical sites and routes nets. Reports show critical paths and routing congestion.
Placement
Map each element to device coordinates.
Routing
Connect nets through programmable interconnects.
Reports
Show timing, congestion, and placement heat maps.
Practical Responses
  • Floorplan hot modules to keep related logic nearby.
  • Add pipeline registers to break long paths.
  • Adjust constraints or pick a larger device if necessary.
Misconception: a single compile will find the optimal placement; in practice, guided constraints or architectural changes are needed for consistent timing success.
