Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Timing Simulation
Why run timing sim?
To validate behavior with actual delays, detect setup/hold violations and glitches caused by placement and routing.
Use case
A high-speed handshake that fails under routing delay indicates the need for extra synchronization or pipeline stages.
Limitations & Tips
  • Timing sim is slower and may be impractical for large testbenches.
  • Focus it on critical scenarios identified by STA.
  • Combine with hardware probes for final verification.
Misconception: timing sim finds all hardware failures. It helps a lot but cannot replace real-world testing under varied conditions.
