Program and measure
Use JTAG to load the .sof for development and test the design with LEDs, serial consoles, oscilloscopes, and logic analyzers to confirm behavior.
Hands-on task
Implement a 1 Hz LED blink using a counter on a 50 MHz clock, program the board, and measure the blink period with a stopwatch for verification.
Debug checklist
  • Confirm .qsf pin assignments match the board silk-screen.
  • Check clock source and frequency settings.
  • Verify power rails and IO standards.
Misconception: a blink rate close to expected means all timing is fine. It may hide other domain issues; still run STA and targeted timing simulations for critical paths.
