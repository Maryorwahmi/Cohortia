Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Synthesis
What synthesis does
  • Convert RTL into LUTs, flip-flops, and specialized blocks.
  • Optimize logic and report inferred resources.
  • Emit a technology-mapped netlist for fitting.
Synthesis Report Tips
Check resource summary, max fanout warnings, and inferred memory/dsp usage to ensure mappings match expectations.
Example issues
A poorly written multiplier might consume many LUTs rather than a DSP block. Detect this in the report and refactor to instantiate vendor primitives when appropriate.
Misconception
Assuming synthesis optimizes all constructs optimally. You must read reports and guide synthesis with constraints or RTL adjustments.
