Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Map & Planner
Technology Map Viewer
Shows LUTs, FFs, carry chains and how the generic netlist maps to device primitives after technology mapping. Use it to spot inefficient logic implementations.
Chip Planner
Displays physical placement on the die and the routing between used CLBs and I/O. Useful for diagnosing placement‑related timing issues and verifying pin placements.
When to inspect
  • After fitting when timing issues remain unresolved.
  • To verify whether critical paths are placed close together.
  • To locate clustered resource usage that may impact routing.
Key Takeaway

Use both viewers together to align logical mapping with physical placement, and then apply targeted RTL changes or floorplanning to improve timing.
