Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › What to Remember
1. Project & Sources
Verify .qpf/.qsf and that sources are added and saved before compiling.
2. Device Selection
Choose the exact MAX 10 part for DE10‑Lite so pin names and resources align with the board.
3. Read Reports
Flow, Fitter, and Timing reports together indicate resource and timing health.
4. Use Viewers
RTL Viewer for synthesis verification; Technology Map and Chip Planner for mapping and placement inspection.
5. Programming
Use Programmer with USB‑Blaster and select .sof for active programming or .pof for flash persistence.
Final question

If the Flow Summary shows over 100% logic usage, what remediation steps can you take: optimize RTL, swap to hard blocks, or select a larger device?
