Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › RTL Viewer
What it shows
A technology‑independent netlist showing registers, combinational logic, and connectivity. Use it to verify synthesis produced the structure you intended from your HDL.
When to use
  • After Analysis & Synthesis to confirm module structure.
  • To check reset and clocking behavior represented by flip‑flops.
  • To spot unintended logic resulting from coding mistakes.
Checklist
  • Does the RTL netlist reflect your module hierarchy?
  • Are registers and reset paths present as expected?
  • Do combinational blocks match intended logic expressions?
Common-mistake

Expecting to see technology-mapped LUTs in the RTL Viewer. Use Technology Map Viewer for post‑mapping details.
