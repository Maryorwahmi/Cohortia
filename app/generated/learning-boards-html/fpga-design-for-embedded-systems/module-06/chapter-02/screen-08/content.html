Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Compilation Reports
Flow Summary
Shows totals: logic elements, registers, memory bits, DSP blocks. Use this first to check capacity.
Fitter Summary
Displays resource distribution after placement and routing and provides an fmax estimate for the design.
Timing Analyzer
Performs static timing checks and reports slack and failing paths for each clock domain.
Actionable checks
  • If Flow Summary shows >100% utilization, consider design optimization or larger device.
  • If Fitter shows low slack or low fmax, explore critical path restructuring or reduce logic depth on the clocked path.
  • If Timing Analyzer reports negative slack, identify failing nets and constraints, then iterate RTL or constraints.
Common-mistake

Treating reports in isolation. Read all three reports together to understand whether issues are resource or timing related, and choose the right remediation path.
