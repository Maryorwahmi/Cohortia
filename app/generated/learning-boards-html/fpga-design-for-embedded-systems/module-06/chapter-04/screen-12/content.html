Module 7 › Chapter 6.4 › ModelSim vs Quartus
Comparison at a glance
ModelSim
  • Full-featured debugging (breakpoints, tracing)
  • Self‑checking testbenches and coverage
  • Best for complex and automated verification
Quartus Simulator (VWF)
  • Built‑in, quick startup
  • Graphical waveform drawing for small tests
  • Good for fast sanity checks and demos
Practical guideline

Use VWF to check a hypothesis quickly. For repeatable verification, write a self‑checking testbench and use ModelSim to run it under scripted conditions and collect coverage.

Key Takeaway

Pick speed for quick checks and depth for assurance. Use both tools as complementary parts of verification.
