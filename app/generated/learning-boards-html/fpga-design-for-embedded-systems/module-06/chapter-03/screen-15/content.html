Inferred latch detection
Look for latches caused by incomplete assignments and fix HDL with defaults.
Post-Mapping vs Post-Fitting
Use mapping for resource choices and fitting for placement-dependent timing data.
Resource report interpretation
Correlate LEs, registers, memory and DSP usage to choose optimizations or devices.
Final takeaway

Make visualization a repetitive practice: inspect, act, and verify across RTL Viewer, Technology Map Viewer, Chip Planner, and reports to reliably improve designs.
