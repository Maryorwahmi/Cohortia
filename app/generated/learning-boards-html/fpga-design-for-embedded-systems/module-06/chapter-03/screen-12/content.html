ALU lab steps
Follow these steps and capture screenshots or notes for your report.
Step 1 — RTL Viewer
Compile to Analysis & Synthesis, trace the ADD signal, and confirm adders and multiplexers are present. Note any unexpected latches.
Step 2 — Technology Map
Count LEs, registers, DSPs and memory used by the ALU in Post-Mapping. Record totals for your report.
Step 3 — Chip Planner
Search for the ALU instance, locate its LEs on the die, and note any routing congestion or clustering that may affect timing.
Report items: logical structure observed, LE and register counts, physical location screenshot or notes, and a short optimization recommendation such as adding a pipeline stage or targeting DSPs.
