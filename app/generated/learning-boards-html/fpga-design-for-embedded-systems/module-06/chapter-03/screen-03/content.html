Lab checklist and assessment
Follow these steps and capture evidence for your report.
Trace ADD in RTL Viewer
Confirm the adder and multiplexer structure and identify any unexpected latches or counters.
Count LEs in Map Viewer
Record LEs, registers, DSPs and memories used for the ALU and note opportunities to use DSPs instead of LUT multipliers.
Locate in Chip Planner
Find placement, look for routing congestion, and note if manual placement could shorten critical paths.
Assessment example: given an RTL screenshot with a latch, identify the coding cause and propose an HDL fix such as adding a default assignment or completing all case branches. Submit a short report linking screenshots to your findings.
