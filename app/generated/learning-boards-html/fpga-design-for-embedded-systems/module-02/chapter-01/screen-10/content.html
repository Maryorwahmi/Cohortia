Routing architecture
Horizontal and vertical channels contain multiple wires of varied lengths. Switch matrices at intersections provide programmable connectivity between wire segments.
Short wires: local links
Medium wires: regional spans
Long wires: global routes
Concrete impact
Each switch and wire segment adds delay. A long routed net will typically traverse multiple switch matrices and wire segments, and that cumulative delay can dominate the critical path.
Warning

Routing is the main bottleneck for timing. Pay attention to placement and the number of switch matrices a net crosses.
