Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Wire Lengths
Short wires
Used for neighboring CLBs, minimal switch crossings, low delay and low area per connection.
Best for: local combinational signals and short data paths.
Long wires
Span large distances with fewer hops but occupy larger routing resources and can add skew or delay if misused.
Best for: global signals and nets that must cross many regions.
Practical guideline
  • Keep timing-critical nets local to avoid multiple switch matrices.
  • Reserve long wires for signals that must reach across the device.
  • Interpret routing congestion and delay reports to adjust placement.
