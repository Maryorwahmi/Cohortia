Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Advantages of SRAM-based FPGAs
Comparison — Why choose SRAM

Key advantages

  • Unlimited reprogramming: No practical limit to rewrite cycles.
  • Fast reprogramming: Device reconfigures in milliseconds to seconds.
  • Latest features: High densities and fastest transceivers appear first on SRAM parts.
Concrete scenario
Imagine a team exploring multiple accelerator topologies. They rebuild and reload bitstreams many times per day. SRAM-based FPGA speed and infinite rewrites enable rapid iteration and validation.
Security note
Because the bitstream often crosses external memory, protect it with encryption and authentication to avoid reverse-engineering or tampering.
Key takeaway
SRAM is the right choice for flexible, feature-rich, and high-performance designs where frequent updates are expected.
