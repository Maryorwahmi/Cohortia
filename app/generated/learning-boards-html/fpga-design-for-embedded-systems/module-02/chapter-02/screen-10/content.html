Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Trade-offs
Reprogrammability
  • SRAM: Unlimited rewrites.
  • FLASH: Limited erase/program cycles.
  • Anti-fuse: One-time.
Power & Density
  • SRAM: Standby power and external storage impacts.
  • FLASH: Low standby power, lower density in many families.
  • Anti-fuse: Very low standby power, higher cost per gate.
Security & Reliability
  • SRAM: Vulnerable during external load unless encrypted.
  • FLASH: Internal storage reduces exposure to interception.
  • Anti-fuse: High tamper resistance and radiation immunity.
Concrete decision example
For a machine learning prototype that changes daily, choose SRAM. For a brake controller in aircraft, prefer anti-fuse or FLASH for instant-on and resilience. Consider cost, vendor availability, and reprogramming needs together.
