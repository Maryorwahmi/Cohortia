Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
SRAM-based FPGA
Volatile storage; unlimited reprogramming; external bitstream required at power-on.
FLASH-based FPGA
Embedded non-volatile configuration; instant-on and lower standby power; limited reprogram cycles.
Anti-fuse FPGA
One-time programmable links; extreme reliability and security; no post-program updates.
Summary takeaway
When selecting a configuration technology, weigh reprogrammability, startup time, power, security, and cost against your system requirements and document your decision rationale.
