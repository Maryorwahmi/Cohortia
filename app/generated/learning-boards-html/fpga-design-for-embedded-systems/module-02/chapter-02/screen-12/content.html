Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Choosing the right technology
Decision checklist
  1. How often will you update the design?
  2. Is instant-on required at power-up?
  3. Are radiation or tamper resistance critical?
  4. What are cost and vendor availability constraints?
Scenario examples
Prototype ML accelerator: SRAM for rapid changes.
Automotive ECU: FLASH for instant-on and internal storage.
Aircraft brake controller: Anti-fuse or FLASH for reliability and instant availability.
Key takeaway
Match the technology to documented system priorities and verify that startup, security, and update processes meet operational requirements.
