Four practice checkpoints
01 — Datasheet table
Create a table comparing LUTs/LEs, BRAM Kbits, DSPs, I/O, ADC, PLLs, cost.
02 — Device choice write-up
For each sample project, give the dominant constraint and justify the family choice.
03 — Prototype on DE10-Lite
Build a minimal design, program the MAX 10 device, and confirm toolchain support.
04 — Reflection
Compare estimated resource usage to actual after synthesis and revise your estimation method.
Quick checklist
  • Record LUT/LE and BRAM totals from datasheets.
  • Count DSP blocks and confirm multiplier needs for signal processing.
  • List required I/O and any high-speed transceivers.
  • Note board features: ADC, MicroSD, headers, and power method.
Key Takeaway

Structured practice with datasheets and a short prototype reveals the gap between estimates and real resource use.
