Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Worked Example
Problem
Design needs: 5,000 logic elements, 50 Kbits memory, 20 multipliers, 40 I/O pins.
Apply the selection rules: shortlist devices that meet or exceed each numeric requirement, then choose the most cost-effective one. The chapter recommends MAX 10 as the best fit in this scenario because it satisfies logic, BRAM, DSP, and I/O needs while remaining low-cost.
Why not over-specify?
Selecting a family with far greater capacity increases BOM cost and power without adding necessary capability. Prototype to confirm the choice before committing to a final module.
Key Takeaway

Match capacity to need and validate with a prototype; MAX 10 is often the most cost-effective pick for modest embedded requirements.
