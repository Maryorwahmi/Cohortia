Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Development Boards & DE10-Lite
What boards provide
  • Power and voltage regulation for the FPGA and peripherals.
  • Programming connector (JTAG) and USB power interface.
  • On-board peripherals that speed prototyping: ADCs, sensors, memory slots.
Prototype advantage
Validate hardware choices and toolchain flows before committing to a production module or custom board.
DE10-Lite highlights
The DE10-Lite includes the Intel MAX 10 10M50DAF484C7G, 64 MB SDRAM, Arduino-compatible header, MicroSD slot, accelerometer, ADC, and a 10-pin JTAG connector. It is USB-powered and supported by Quartus Prime Lite for MAX 10 devices.
Key Takeaway

Use DE10-Lite to validate designs intended for MAX 10 devices and to practice the full FPGA toolchain with real peripherals.

Prototype → Production trade-off
Development boards include extras for convenience. When you move to production, consider a custom carrier or production module that removes unneeded peripherals to save cost and space while retaining the validated FPGA device and interfaces.
