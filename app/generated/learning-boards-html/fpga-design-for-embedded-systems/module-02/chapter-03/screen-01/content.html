Family Snapshot
Quick comparison
Intel — MAX 10
Low-cost, embedded flash, ADC, used on DE10-Lite. Example device: 10M50 with 50k logic elements and 144 DSP blocks.
AMD/Xilinx — Artix-7
Low-cost, low-power; used on Basys 3 and Nexys A7 development boards and suitable for learning projects.
High-performance lines
Stratix, Versal, Virtex, Agilex target networking, AI, and data-center acceleration where transceivers and DSP capacity scale up.
