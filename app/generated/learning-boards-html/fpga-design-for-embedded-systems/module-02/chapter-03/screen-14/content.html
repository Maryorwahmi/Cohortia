Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
Family tiers
Match family to cost, processor integration, or high-speed needs.
Separate specs
Count LEs/LUTs, BRAM, DSPs, I/O, PLLs, and transceivers individually.
Prototype early
Use DE10-Lite or similar to validate assumptions and toolchain before production.
Takeaway

Keep family fit, precise counts, and early prototypes as your selection checklist for every FPGA project.
