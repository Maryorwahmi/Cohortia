Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › MAX 10 vs Artix-7
Intel MAX 10 (DE10-Lite)
Embedded flash, ADC, 10M50DAF484C7G example: 50k logic elements, 1,638 Kbits of M9K, 144 DSP blocks. Instant-on and on-board peripherals simplify prototyping.
Xilinx Artix-7
Low-cost, low-power family used on Basys 3 and Nexys A7 boards. Well-suited for learning and cost-sensitive embedded projects.
Practical advice
If you need instant-on and an on-chip ADC, MAX 10 simplifies integration. If you prefer Xilinx tooling and existing class boards, Artix-7 provides a solid learning platform. Try implementing a UART bridge on both to compare resource use and toolchain flow.
Key Takeaway

For course work the DE10-Lite MAX 10 board provides convenient peripherals and flash-based configuration; Artix-7 boards remain excellent alternatives depending on tooling and board features.
