How to use these objectives
Work through simulations or small HDL exercises: capture signals with a D flip-flop, implement a 4-bit counter, and construct a SIPO shift register. For timing, plug numbers into the timing equation to compute the maximum safe clock frequency and then test a faster clock to observe metastability in simulation.
Mnemonic: Capture -> Store -> Move
Practice: 4-bit Johnson counter design
Use the validated milestones to check competency with simulation traces or simple testbenches.
