Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Timing Equation & Metastability
Equation
T_clock must be greater than tco + tpd + tsu for correct sampling across register boundaries.
Worked numbers: tco=3 ns, tpd=8 ns, tsu=2 ns → T_clock > 13 ns → f_max ≈ 76.9 MHz.
Consequence: running at 100 MHz violates setup and risks metastability or wrong captures.
Mitigations & Practice
Options: lengthen clock period, shorten combinational paths by adding registers, or restructure logic. In safety-critical paths, use synchronizers and allow extra settling time for asynchronous inputs to reduce metastability risk.
Practice
Identify the worst-case path in your design, compute tpd, include tco and tsu, and decide whether to pipeline or reduce logic depth.
Question
If a path violates timing by 2 ns, where would you insert a register to split the combinational delay and how does that change the equation?
