Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Quick Reference
Design
Use registers to form pipeline stages; avoid clock gating; use clock enable instead.
Verification
Simulate counters and shift registers. Compute T_clock constraints and verify timing reports after synthesis.
Timing
Apply T_clock > tco + tpd + tsu. If violated, add registers or slow the clock.
Final Thought

Sequential building blocks are simple, but timing interplay determines whether stitched designs behave reliably on real FPGA hardware.
