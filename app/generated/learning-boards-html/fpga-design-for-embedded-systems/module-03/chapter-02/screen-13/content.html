Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Key Concepts
Flip-flops
D captures on edge; T toggles; JK can set/reset/toggle but D is preferred in FPGAs.
Registers & Counters
Registers group flip-flops; counters sequence values and modulo-N counters wrap after N states.
Shift Registers & Johnson
SIPO and PISO move bits across clocks; Johnson counters use inverted feedback to yield 2N states.
Takeaway

Design with D flip-flops, group into registers, apply the timing equation to set clock rates, and use shift or Johnson counters where sequence or decoding simplicity is needed.
