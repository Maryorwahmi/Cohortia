Combinational Logic: Gates, Adders, Multiplexers, and Decoders
Core building blocks for arithmetic and selection logic, and how they map to FPGA primitives.
In this lesson

We will define combinational circuits, inspect adders from half to carry-lookahead, explore multiplexers as universal logic, and see decoders and encoders in practice.

Why it matters

Understanding these blocks helps you write HDL that synthesizes to efficient LUT and carry-chain usage, improving speed and area on FPGAs.
