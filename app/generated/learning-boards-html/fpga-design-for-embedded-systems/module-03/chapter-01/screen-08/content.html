Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Ripple-Carry Adder
Process
Chain full-adders so each Cout becomes the next Cin. Worst-case delay is when a carry travels across all stages.
Step 1: Place N full-adders for N bits.
Step 2: Connect each stage's Cout to next stage's Cin.
Step 3: Evaluate worst-case carry propagation from LSB to MSB.
Concrete example
A 4-bit ripple adder has four stages. If the LSB produces a carry, it may ripple through all four stages causing accumulated delay proportional to stage count.
Common mistake

Assuming vendor carry chains eliminate all ripple delay. They reduce per-stage delay but logical propagation remains sequential across bits.
