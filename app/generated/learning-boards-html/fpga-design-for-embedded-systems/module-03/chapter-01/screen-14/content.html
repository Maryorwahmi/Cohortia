Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Combinational = Stateless
Outputs depend only on current inputs; design for stable sampling by sequential logic.
Adder architectures
Ripple is simple; lookahead and carry chains give lower latency for wide adders.
Selection primitives
Multiplexers, decoders, and encoders implement routing and address selection; MUX trees underlie LUT behavior.
Summary

Design with clear combinational intent, choose the right adder for your timing needs, and use MUX/decoder primitives to structure selection and routing efficiently.
