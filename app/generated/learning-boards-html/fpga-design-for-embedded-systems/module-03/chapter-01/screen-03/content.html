Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Implementation Objectives
Translate HDL to FPGA primitives
Practice writing arithmetic and selector logic in Verilog so synthesis maps it efficiently to LUTs and carry chains. Inspect RTL visuals to confirm mapping.
Choose adder architectures
Decide when ripple-carry is acceptable and when you should use carry lookahead or vendor primitives for fast arithmetic.
Mnemonic
Write intent in HDL, then verify mapping — think: describe, synthesize, inspect.
Pro-tip
Small changes in HDL style can guide synthesis toward carry chains or LUT packing; test and inspect with your vendor tools.
