LUTs
LUTs store truth-table entries for functions of k inputs. Many gate networks compress into single LUTs after synthesis.
Carry chains
Dedicated fast links that forward carry signals between adjacent arithmetic cells. They lower latency for adders compared to LUT-only chaining.
Concrete check
Synthesize a small adder and open the RTL viewer. Look for grouped arithmetic primitives and labeled carry nets to confirm synthesis used carry hardware.
