Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Full-Adder
Equations
Sum = A XOR B XOR Cin. Carry-out = (A AND B) OR (Cin AND (A XOR B)). Express these in HDL to allow synthesis optimizations.
Inputs: A B Cin
Example: A=1, B=1, Cin=0 -> Sum=0, Cout=1
A=1, B=1, Cin=1 -> Sum=1, Cout=1
Chaining into adders
Connect Cout of LSB adder to Cin of next stage. Four full-adders chained produce a 4-bit ripple-carry adder where the carry propagates sequentially.
Common mistake

Assuming chaining won't introduce substantial delay. Ripple carry delay grows with bit width unless you use lookahead or vendor carry chains.
