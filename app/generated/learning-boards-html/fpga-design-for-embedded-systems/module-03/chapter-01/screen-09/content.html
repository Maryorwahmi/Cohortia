Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Carry-Lookahead
Core signals
Generate G = A AND B. Propagate P = A XOR B. Carry-out: Cout = G OR (P AND Cin).
Group carries by computing block G and P, then combine to get faster multi-bit carry resolution.
Example — 4-bit block
Compute G_i and P_i for each bit. Then compute block generate: G_block = G3 OR (P3 AND G2) OR (P3 AND P2 AND G1) ... This yields carries with fewer gate levels than ripple.
Key Takeaway

Lookahead trades extra logic for much lower worst-case delay, and maps well to FPGA carry and LUT resources for wide adders.
