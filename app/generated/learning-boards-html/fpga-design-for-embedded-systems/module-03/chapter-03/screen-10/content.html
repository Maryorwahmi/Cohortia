Module 4 › Chapter 3.3 › Estimating LUT Count: 6-input AND
Step-by-step estimate
  1. Identify distinct inputs: A, B, C, D, E, F. That's six inputs.
  2. Map up to four inputs into one LUT: compute X = A AND B AND C AND D.
  3. Use a second LUT for Y = X AND E AND F. Total at least two LUTs.
Assessment idea

Ask: How many 4-input LUTs are needed for a 6-input AND? Answer: At least two.

Compare with adder

A 4-bit adder often maps to one LUT per bit for sum with carry handled by the carry chain, so the adder can use fewer LUTs than a similarly wide gate-only expression.

Key Takeaway

Estimate resource use by counting distinct inputs and knowing the LUT width; verify with the Technology Map Viewer.
