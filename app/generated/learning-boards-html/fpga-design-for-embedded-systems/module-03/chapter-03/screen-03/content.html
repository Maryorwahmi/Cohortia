Verification checklist
  • Generate a synthesis report and locate the generic netlist in the RTL Viewer.
  • Open the Technology Map Viewer and count LUTs and registers used for a function.
  • Synthesize a 4-bit adder and compare its LUT count to a 4-input AND example.
  • Confirm carries are shown as dedicated chains in the map viewer.
Common misconception

Do not assume a complex expression always fits in one LUT. If the number of distinct inputs exceeds the LUT width, the function will be split across LUTs and require routing between them.

Try this

Synthesize a 5-input AND and observe that two LUTs are used. Then synthesize a 4-input AND and confirm it fits in a single LUT.
