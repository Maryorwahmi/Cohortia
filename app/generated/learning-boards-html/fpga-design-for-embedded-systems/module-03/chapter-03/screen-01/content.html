From Logic Equations to LUTs

Follow the path from HDL to gates, then from gates to device primitives. Learn how LUTs, CLB registers, and carry chains implement your design on silicon.

Orientation

You will practice reading synthesis reports and comparing RTL schematic output to the device-specific technology map. This makes it easier to predict LUT count, spot wide logic that must be split, and confirm that inferred flip-flops landed in CLB registers.

In this lesson
Examples: 4-input AND, 5-input AND, adders with carry chains.

Visual Concept
LUT + FF
A device logic element combines a 4-input LUT and a flip-flop. Mappers pack logic into LUT truth tables and assign registers to the flip-flop.
Carry Chain
Dedicated fast connections between adjacent LEs implement arithmetic carry with lower delay than general routing.
Key Takeaway

Synthesis and mapping are distinct steps. Read synthesis reports and use viewers to confirm how your code became LUTs and registers.
