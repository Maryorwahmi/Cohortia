Module 4 › Chapter 3.3 › Synthesis Lab: 4-bit Comparator
Steps
  1. Write a Verilog module that outputs A>B, A==B, and A<B for 4-bit inputs.
  2. Synthesize for your target device and open the RTL Viewer to inspect inferred gates and registers.
  3. Open the Technology Map Viewer and count LUTs and flip-flops used.
  4. Synthesize a 4-bit adder and compare resource usage and presence of carry chains.
Checklist
  • Confirm inferred flip-flops appear as CLB registers in the map viewer.
  • Note where multi-input logic is split across LUTs.
  • Capture screenshots of both viewers documenting LUT counts.
Key Takeaway

Hands-on synthesis and viewer inspection is the fastest way to connect HDL style to mapped hardware resources.
