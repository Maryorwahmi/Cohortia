Module 4 › Chapter 3.3 › Flip-Flops, CLB Registers, and Clock Networks
Definition

Sequential mapping

A D flip-flop inferred in HDL is mapped to a register in the CLB. The global clock network distributes the clock to all registers with low skew.

Concrete scenario

You create a rising-edge clocked assignment; the synthesis tool infers a D flip-flop. Mapping assigns it to a CLB register and ties it to the clock network.

Points to check
  • Confirm registers in the Technology Map Viewer match your inferred flip-flops.
  • Verify clock enables and resets are routed through supported dedicated nets.
  • Watch for placement that separates producer and consumer registers, which can create timing pressure.
Pro tip

Use timing reports and the technology map to correlate registers with timing paths and adjust constraints to help the placer.
