Module 4 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Synthesis

HDL is converted into a generic gate netlist and inferred flip-flops before device mapping.

Technology Mapping

Generic gates are grouped into LUTs and registers according to device primitives and LUT input limits.

Carry Chains

Dedicated fast links between adjacent LEs make multi-bit arithmetic faster than gate-only implementations.

Summary takeaway

Inspect both RTL and Technology Map viewers after synthesis to confirm how your HDL was mapped, then use placement and routing reports to close timing.
