Major Vendors
AMD (Xilinx): Artix-7, Kintex-7, Virtex-7, Zynq-7000, Zynq UltraScale+, Versal — broad range to heterogeneous platforms.
Intel (Altera): MAX 10, Cyclone V, Arria 10, Stratix 10, Agilex — from low-cost to high-performance datacenter parts.
Lattice Semiconductor: iCE40, MachXO, ECP — low-power, small form factors for edge devices.
Microchip (Microsemi): IGLOO, ProASIC, SmartFusion — radiation-tolerant and security-focused families.
Selection checklist
  • • Does the family include hard processors if you need SoC features?
  • • Are high-speed transceivers required for your interfaces?
  • • How many DSP blocks and block RAM are available for signal processing?
  • • What are the power and package constraints?
Concrete example
For a low-power IoT sensor node evaluate Lattice iCE40 or a small Artix device; for data-center acceleration evaluate Stratix or Agilex families.
