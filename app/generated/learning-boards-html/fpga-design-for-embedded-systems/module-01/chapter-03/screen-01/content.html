The History and Evolution of Programmable Logic

From PROM lookup tricks to modern heterogeneous FPGAs — learn the inventions, firms, and trends that shaped programmable logic.

This chapter examines key milestones and engineering insights. You will see how increasing logic density and integrated hard IP transformed FPGAs into full SoC platforms, enabling signal processing, embedded processors, and AI acceleration.

  • Trace the device lineage from PROM → PAL → CPLD → FPGA.
  • Understand Ross Freeman’s routing-network insight and why it scaled.
  • Recognize generational trends that enabled SoC integration and AI accelerators.
