Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › PALs: Efficient Programmable Logic
Core Concept

Programmable AND, fixed OR

PALs used a programmable AND array feeding a fixed OR array. Sharing product terms across outputs reduced silicon compared to a full lookup memory.

• Replaced many discrete gates with a single device.
• Efficient for multiple Boolean functions using shared terms.
• Targeted at glue logic and small state machines.
Concrete scenario
A control board with multiple related output signals can compute shared product terms once and feed them to several outputs, saving silicon and wiring complexity.
Historical note

MMI’s PAL architecture and handbook became standard reference material for generations of digital designers because it showed how to structure programmable logic for practical engineering use.

Common misconception
Do not confuse PALs with general reconfigurable arrays. PALs are constrained in architecture to gain efficiency, not breadth of routing or scale.
Key Takeaway

By structuring programmability, PALs offered a practical balance between flexibility and silicon efficiency that PROMs could not match.
