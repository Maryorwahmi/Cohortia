Activity

Create a decade timeline

For 1970s, 1980s, 1990s, 2000s, 2010s and 2020s list the key invention, lead company, and one representative device. Note what changed architecturally each decade.

• 1970s: PROM — lookup-table origin.
• Late 1970s: PAL — shared product terms, efficient glue logic.
• 1980s: CPLD and FPGA births — reprogrammability and routing fabrics.
• 1990s–2010s: memory, DSP, hard cores, and SoC integration.
Assessment prep
Compare the XC2064 to a modern part in four metrics: logic cells, block RAM, DSP count, and transceivers. Use those comparisons to explain capability growth driven by process scaling.
Study tip
Focus on architectural differences rather than raw numbers: what each metric enables in system design.
