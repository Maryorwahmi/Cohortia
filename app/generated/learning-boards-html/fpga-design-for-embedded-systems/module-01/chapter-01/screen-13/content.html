Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Activity
Hands-on task
Find three real FPGA applications and write one paragraph per app explaining what the FPGA does, why it outperforms a processor for that role, and what would change with a processor.
Assessment idea
Match five application descriptions to the correct platform: FPGA, ASIC, Microcontroller, GPU, or General-Purpose Processor. Example: a weekly-changing video codec prototype maps to FPGA.
Try this: For each chosen application identify the dominant constraint — latency, throughput, cost, or reconfigurability — and justify your platform choice in one sentence.
