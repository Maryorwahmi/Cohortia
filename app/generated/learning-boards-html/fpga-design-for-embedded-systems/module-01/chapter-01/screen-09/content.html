Reconfigurable hardware
Change device behavior by loading a new bitstream instead of fabricating new silicon.
Prototyping: Iterate algorithm hardware weekly.
Field updates: Fix bugs or add features after deployment.
Concrete example
A team testing a new video codec can load new accelerator designs onto an FPGA each week to measure real throughput and power, find optimization opportunities, and avoid an expensive ASIC re-spin.
