Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Deterministic timing
Predictable latency
Designers compute or measure propagation and pipeline delays so end-to-end response is guaranteed.
Why it matters
Safety and control systems require consistent timing to maintain stability and correctness.
What to check
Timing reports, clock domain crossings, and pipeline balancing.
Scenario
A radar signal path must process samples and produce results within a fixed window. An FPGA pipeline gives a fixed latency and low jitter, enabling deterministic control decisions.
Key takeaway: Determinism is achieved by designing explicit hardware pipelines and validating timing end-to-end.
Common mistake: Ignoring clock domain crossings that introduce unexpected latency and metastability.
