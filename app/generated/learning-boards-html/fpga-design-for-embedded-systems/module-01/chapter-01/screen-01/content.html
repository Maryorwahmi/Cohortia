What Are FPGAs and Why Do They Matter?
Programmable hardware that implements digital circuits via a bitstream rather than software instructions.

FPGAs configure logic blocks and routing to behave like a custom chip. This makes them ideal when parallel work, precise timing, and reconfigurable designs are required.

Orientation
This lesson describes how programming an FPGA creates hardware, how that grants parallelism and determinism, and where those properties are applied in industry. Keep the traffic-analogy in mind: multiple lanes moving together is parallel hardware, while a single controller directing cars resembles sequential processors.
