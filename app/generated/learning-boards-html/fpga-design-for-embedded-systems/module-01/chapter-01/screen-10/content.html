Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Advantages & Disadvantages
Advantages
  • Parallelism: many operations run concurrently for throughput.
  • Reconfigurability: update hardware behavior via bitstreams.
  • Deterministic timing: known latencies for real-time tasks.
  • Rapid prototyping: test hardware iterations quickly.
Disadvantages
  • Higher per-unit cost than ASICs for large volumes.
  • Greater power consumption than optimized ASICs.
  • Steeper hardware learning curve than software development.
Common mistake: Choosing FPGA without estimating production volume and power budget.
