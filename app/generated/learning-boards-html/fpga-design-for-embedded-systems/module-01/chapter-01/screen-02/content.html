Why these objectives matter
Clear objectives let you evaluate whether to prototype in an FPGA, to buy an ASIC, or to implement a faster software path. These skills are practical: you will use them to estimate design time, cost trade-offs, and the fit of FPGAs in embedded systems.
Mnemonic: TRADE
Timeline: Define → Compare → Prototype → Decide
Scenario: Choose FPGA when you need parallelism and reconfigurability with predictable timing.
