Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Selection Checklist
Step 1
Rate algorithm churn
Frequent change → FPGA. Stable and standard → ASIC or ASSP.
Step 2
Assess startup needs
Instant-on deterministic signals → CPLD for glue logic.
Step 3
Estimate volume and NRE
High volume and tight power → ASIC. Low volume or rapid launch → FPGA or ASSP.
Step 4
Consider hybrids
Structured ASIC or eFPGA when you need reduced NRE or selective post-silicon flexibility.
Concrete Scenario

A startup with limited prototype funds and a tight timeline should prototype on FPGA; if sales reach 100,000 units and design is fixed, evaluate ASIC migration for cost efficiency.

Check

Did you calculate NRE versus per-unit cost across expected volumes? If not, do it before committing to ASIC.

Key Takeaway

Follow the checklist and let dominant constraints determine the platform rather than assumptions about device superiority.
