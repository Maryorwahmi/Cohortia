Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › ASSP
Definition

Off-the-shelf chips for common functions

ASSPs implement a particular function for many customers, reducing per-customer development cost and shortening time-to-market.

  • • Not programmable by the customer; integrate as fixed-function components.
  • • Economical for standard interfaces such as USB or Ethernet.
  • • Immediate availability avoids long custom development cycles.
Concrete Example
USB controller chip

A ready-made USB controller avoids the need to design and validate USB protocol logic yourself.

Misconception

Thinking ASSPs are inferior because they are fixed. When the function matches your requirement, they are the fastest and cheapest option.
