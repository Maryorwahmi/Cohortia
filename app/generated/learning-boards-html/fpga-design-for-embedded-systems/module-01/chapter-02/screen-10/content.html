Structured ASIC

Lower NRE than full custom

Pre-diffused base layers with customizable metal layers reduce mask steps and cost, offering a midway point between FPGA and full ASIC.

Good when you want lower upfront cost than a full ASIC but better per-unit cost than an FPGA for certain volumes.
eFPGA

Embedded reconfigurable fabric

Place FPGA fabric inside an ASIC to keep selective hardware blocks reprogrammable after production, combining flexibility with efficiency.

Useful when most of the chip is fixed but a few functions require post-silicon updates.
Concrete Example

An SoC with embedded FPGA fabric for a small accelerator that may be tuned post-production.

Misconception

Believing hybrids are always easier. Integration and partitioning complexity can increase design effort.

Key Takeaway

Hybrids let you trade some flexibility for lower NRE or add limited configurability inside fixed silicon—use them where appropriate.
