Microcontroller — very low cost, weeks to market, low performance, very low power.
CPLD — medium flexibility, deterministic timing, low cost for glue logic, instant-on.
FPGA — high flexibility and parallelism, moderate cost at low volume, weeks to market.
ASSP — fixed function, immediate availability, low development effort.
ASIC — none flexibility, very high performance, low power and low per-unit cost at high volume, very high NRE and long schedule.
Concrete Scenario

If your team must iterate algorithms weekly with limited budget, choose FPGA for the prototype. If the product will sell millions and power is critical, evaluate ASIC migration once the design stabilizes.

Common Mistake

Optimizing only for per-unit cost without considering NRE and development time can lead to wrong platform choices.

Key Takeaway

Let the dominant constraint in your project drive the platform selection, not assumptions about which device is 'best'.
