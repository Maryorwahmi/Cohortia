Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › ASICs
Definition

Custom silicon for a single purpose

Designed and manufactured for one function, ASICs achieve the best possible power and performance per unit at scale.

  • • Highest performance and lowest power per unit in high volumes.
  • • Very high NRE cost and long time-to-market, typically many months to years.
  • • Chosen when product volumes and margins justify mask and tooling costs.
Key Takeaway

Move to ASIC only when volume and fixed requirements offset the heavy upfront development cost and schedule.

Concrete Example
High-volume consumer SoC

When production reaches millions of units and power efficiency is critical, a custom ASIC becomes cost-effective despite large NRE.

Misconception

Assuming ASICs are always better. For prototypes and low-volume products, the long schedule and high NRE make programmable devices more sensible.
