Distinguish device definitions

You will name and explain microcontrollers, CPLDs, FPGAs, ASSPs and ASICs and their typical uses in embedded systems. Knowing definitions helps you map requirements to options.

Explain trade-offs

You will compare cost, performance, power, flexibility, and time-to-market for each platform and know which factors dominate a decision.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Select devices by scenario

You will identify when to use CPLD, FPGA, or ASIC based on factors like instant-on needs, algorithm change frequency, and volume targets.

04
Describe hybrid options

You will describe structured ASICs and embedded FPGA fabric, and explain how they reduce NRE or add post-silicon flexibility.
