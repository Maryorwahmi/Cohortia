Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Clock Generation
Clock snippet
process
begin
  clk <= '0'; wait for 5 ns;
  clk <= '1'; wait for 5 ns;
end process;
This creates a 10 ns period clock. The process repeats forever because it lacks a terminating wait.
Best practices
  • Keep clock generation in its own process to avoid coupling with stimulus timing.
  • Match wait for delays to the intended clock frequency for deterministic behavior.
  • Use rising_edge checks in stimulus for synchronization with clocked DUTs.
Check
Make sure clock period aligns with your DUT timing assumptions.
