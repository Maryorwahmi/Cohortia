Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Timing and Termination
Examples
Use wait for 10 ns between test vectors. End the process with wait; to halt further stimulus.
If you see repeated tests, check for a missing final wait or a process that re-enters unintendedly.
Misconceptions
  • Do not assume wait for guarantees ordering of concurrent processes; it only advances time in the calling process.
  • Do not rely on waveform observation alone; assert statements give machine-checkable verification.
