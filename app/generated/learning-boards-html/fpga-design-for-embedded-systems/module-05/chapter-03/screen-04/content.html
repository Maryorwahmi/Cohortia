Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › What is a Testbench?
Definition & Role

A testbench is not synthesizable RTL. It declares signals that represent DUT ports, instantiates the DUT, generates stimulus, and checks outputs automatically.

Concrete example
The adder bench assigns a and b vectors, waits 10 ns, then asserts the expected sum and carry values.
Key Takeaway

Write the bench as an executable specification: stimulus, checks, termination.

Structure snapshot
Entity tb_name is end entity tb_name; architecture declares signals and processes.
Instantiate DUT with generic map and port map, or declare a component and instantiate that.
Use processes for stimulus, clocks, resets, and assertions.
Misconception check: do not design the testbench to be synthesized or to include unnecessary synthesis pragmas.
