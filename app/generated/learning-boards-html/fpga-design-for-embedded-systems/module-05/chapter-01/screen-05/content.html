Entity: the interface
Declare ports and generics. This is the contract other modules rely on.
library IEEE;
use IEEE.STD_LOGIC_1164.ALL;

entity and_gate is
  port(
    A : in std_logic;
    B : in std_logic;
    Y : out std_logic
  );
end entity and_gate;
The entity shows names, directions, and types. No internal signals belong here.
Architecture: the behavior
Describe how outputs are driven from inputs and internal signals.
architecture behavioral of and_gate is
begin
  Y <= A and B;
end architecture behavioral;
Concrete example
A logical AND implemented as a concurrent assignment, suitable for synthesis.
Common mistake
Putting internal signals in the entity or reading internal nets from outside the module.
