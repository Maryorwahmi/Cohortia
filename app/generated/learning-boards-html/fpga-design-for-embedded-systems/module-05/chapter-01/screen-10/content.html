Why conversions matter
Explicit conversions prevent ambiguous arithmetic and make your intent visible to reviewers and tools.
If A and B are std_logic_vector, use SUM <= std_logic_vector(unsigned(A) + unsigned(B)); or better: declare A and B as unsigned to avoid repeated casts.
Practical tip
Declare numeric ports as unsigned or signed so operations are direct and intention is clear at the interface.
Check

Run a waveform simulation to confirm numeric behavior and carry handling after conversions.
