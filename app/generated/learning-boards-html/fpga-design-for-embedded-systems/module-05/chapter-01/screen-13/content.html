What to Remember
Entity & Architecture
Interface vs implementation; change architecture without altering the contract.
STD_LOGIC & NUMERIC_STD
Use std_logic for signal realism and numeric types for arithmetic with explicit conversions.
Generics
Parameterize width and behavior for reusable, adaptable modules.
Final takeaway

Follow explicit typing, declare libraries at the top, and use generics to make designs safe, testable, and reusable.
