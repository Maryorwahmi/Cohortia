Verilog
Fast prototyping; familiar to software teams; pairs with SystemVerilog for verification.
VHDL
Strong typing and enumerated states improve maintainability and waveform readability.
Mixed Projects
Always validate widths, signedness, and reset/clock semantics when instantiating across languages.
Final note
Capture your language choice and the reasons behind it for future teams. That short rationale prevents unnecessary rewrites and ensures consistent integration practices.
