Module 6 › Chapter 5.4 › Port Compatibility
Checklist
A simple checklist reduces mixed-language errors.
  • • Confirm bit widths match exactly between ports.
  • • Verify signed vs unsigned intent and add conversions.
  • • Confirm MSB/LSB indexing conventions and bus ordering.
  • • Match reset polarity and clock domain names.
Concrete wrapper pattern
Create an explicit wrapper that declares the types, adds conversion functions where needed, and documents signedness and polarity so the mapping is reproducible.
Common mistake
Assuming a width match implies identical semantics. Signed arithmetic and slice ordering must be checked separately.
