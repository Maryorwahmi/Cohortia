Context
SystemVerilog for verification
SystemVerilog adds verification constructs, assertions, and richer testbench features that speed validation.
Why it matters
If your project reuses verification IP, SystemVerilog support in your toolflow reduces integration effort.
Concrete example
You may write your FPGA RTL in Verilog or VHDL but use a SystemVerilog testbench to generate complex stimulus and assertions to exercise corner cases.
Misconception
SystemVerilog changes what can be synthesized. It primarily affects verification, not the synthesized structures.
