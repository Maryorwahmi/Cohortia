Module 6 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Verilog
Concise and C-like, well-suited for rapid prototyping and SystemVerilog verification.
VHDL
Verbose and strongly typed; enumerated types and strict typing aid maintainability and debugging.
Mixed Design
Supported by modern tools, but always verify widths, signedness, and reset/clock semantics when instantiating across languages.
Summary takeaway
Choose the language that best fits your team's skills, toolchain, and the project's lifetime maintenance needs. Validate mixed-language mappings with explicit wrappers and tests.
