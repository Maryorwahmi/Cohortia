Pitfalls & Checks
  • Check sensitivity lists and conditional coverage to avoid inferred latches.
  • Verify arithmetic types; prefer unsigned/signed for + and - operations.
  • Decide between synchronous and asynchronous resets and confirm behavior in waveform.
  • Test signal vs variable timing with small, focused process examples.
Practical tests
Create short test cases that toggle inputs and examine whether outputs change immediately or only after a clock edge, and whether states transition as expected.
Check your understanding
Ask: does this block require combinational or sequential modeling, and which VHDL construct best expresses that intent?
