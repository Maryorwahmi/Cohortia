rising_edge(clk)
Preferred clock-edge detection for sequential logic and registers.
Signals vs Variables
Variables update immediately; signals update after the process finishes.
Enumerated FSMs
Use enumerated types and separate register/next-state for readable state machines and easier debugging.
Final takeaway
Follow clear VHDL patterns: use concurrent forms for combinational logic, clocked processes for sequential logic, and disciplined verification to avoid common synthesis pitfalls.
