Common Mistakes
Drivers & Types
Conflicting drivers and mixing reg/assign contexts cause synthesis or compile errors.
  • Do not drive a reg with a continuous assign statement.
  • Avoid multiple active drivers on the same net unless explicitly resolved by tri-state logic.
  • Reserve integer and simulation-only constructs for testbenches.
Concrete Example
Declaring output reg out and later writing assign out = expr; will cause a compile error. Use wire for assign-driven outputs.
Error Pattern
output declared as reg but continuously assigned elsewhere — change the declaration or use procedural assignment.
Pro Tip

Run quick synthesis or linting checks early; tool feedback catches type and driver conflicts before debugging waveforms.
