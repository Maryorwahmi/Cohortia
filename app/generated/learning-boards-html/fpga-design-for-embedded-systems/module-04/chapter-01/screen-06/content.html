Comparison
wire
Represents a net driven by continuous assignments or module outputs; no storage.
  • Driven by assign or module port outputs.
  • Used for combinational paths and module interconnects.
  • Cannot be assigned inside procedural always blocks.
Example
assign y = a & b; makes y a wire reflecting the gate output.
reg
Used inside procedural blocks to hold values across statements; becomes storage when used with clocked always blocks.
  • Assigned inside always blocks.
  • Can represent registers when updated on clock edges.
  • Not driven by continuous assign statements.
Misuse to avoid
Declaring an output as reg and then driving it with assign causes a compile error. Use consistent assignment contexts.
Key Takeaway

Choose wire for connectivity and reg for procedural storage; the assignment context determines synthesis behavior.
