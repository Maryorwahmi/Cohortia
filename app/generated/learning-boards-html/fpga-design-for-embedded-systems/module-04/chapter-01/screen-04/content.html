Core Concept
Verilog models hardware
Statements describe continuous relationships and storage that exist simultaneously on silicon.
Key points
  • assign creates a continuous connection, like a wire driving a gate.
  • always at a clock edge models registers that store values.
  • There is no single sequential execution order across the entire design.
Analogy
Think of Verilog as an electrical schematic described in code: wires, gates, and storage are declared rather than executed.
Key Takeaway

Design in Verilog by mapping each statement to a physical element and reason in terms of signals and timing.

Concrete Example
module and_gate (
  input wire A,
  input wire B,
  output wire Y
);
  assign Y = A & B;
endmodule
This assign statement declares a persistent AND gate. Whenever A or B change, Y reflects the new result immediately in the model.
Misconception
Do not treat always blocks or assigns like functions to be executed; they represent concurrent hardware behavior.
