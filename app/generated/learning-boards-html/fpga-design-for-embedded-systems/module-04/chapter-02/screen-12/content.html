Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Concatenation & Replication
Examples
// concatenation captures carry and sum
assign {cout, sum} = a + b + cin;

// sign extend 8-bit a into 16-bit extended
assign extended = {{8{a[7]}}, a};
Concatenation and replication become wires in hardware and are efficient ways to manage bit widths and carry signals.
Practical notes
  • Use replication for sign extension to preserve signed arithmetic semantics.
  • Concatenation is useful to return multiple fields from an arithmetic expression.
  • Both are compile-time structural constructs and do not add sequential cost.
Key Takeaway

Concatenate to form multi-field outputs and replicate bits to sign-extend safely — both are synthesis-friendly wiring techniques.
