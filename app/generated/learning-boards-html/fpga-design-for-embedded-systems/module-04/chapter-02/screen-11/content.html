Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Operator groups
Arithmetic

+, -, *, /, % — division and modulo are hardware-heavy for non-powers-of-two.

Bitwise

&, |, ^, ~ — operate per-bit across vectors; synthesize to arrays of gates.

Logical

&&, ||, ! — produce single-bit true/false, commonly used in if statements.

Reduction

&a, |a, ^a — collapse a vector to a single bit, useful for parity or detect-all-ones.

Comparison

==, !=, <, >, <=, >= and simulation-only ===, !== for X/Z aware comparisons.

Shift & Concatenation

<<, >>, <<<, >>> for shifts; {a,b} for concat and {N{a}} for replication.

Key Takeaway

Group operators conceptually and prefer efficient constructs like shifts instead of division when possible.
