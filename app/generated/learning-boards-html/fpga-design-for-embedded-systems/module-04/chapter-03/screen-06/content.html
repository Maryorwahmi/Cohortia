Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Registers & enables
Enabled register pattern
If enable is asserted, the register captures input on the rising clock; otherwise it holds its value.
always @(posedge clk) begin
  if (en)
    q <= d;
end
Synthesis maps en to clock enable on the FPGA's flip‑flops rather than gating the clock signal.
Analogy
Think of enable as a gate that allows the register to listen to its input on the clock edge instead of turning the clock on and off.
Verification scenario
Run a test where en toggles: when en is low the output remains constant across clock edges; when en goes high the output follows the data vector on the next rising edge.
Common mistake
Gating the clock net at RTL to implement enable logic. Keep the clock net intact and express enabling in flip‑flop logic.
Key Takeaway

Use en inside the clocked block so the FPGA’s flip‑flop clock enable is used; never gate the clock at RTL for this purpose.
