What to add and measure

Essential signals

Clock, reset, DUT outputs, enables, and internal registers that contribute to the output. These reveal whether control and data paths align in time.

Measurements

Use waveform cursors to measure edge-to-edge delays and verify that outputs change at the expected time relative to clock edges.

Misconception

Console logs alone rarely provide the sequence-of-events needed to understand why a value changed; waveforms show the timeline.

Pro tip

Start with a focused set of signals, then add neighbors in the hierarchy only when you need more context to trace the fault.
