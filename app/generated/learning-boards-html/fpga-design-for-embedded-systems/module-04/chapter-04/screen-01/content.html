Verilog Testbenches & ModelSim

Learn how to construct top-level Verilog testbenches, generate clock and reset signals, create stimulus, and verify results using ModelSim's waveform viewer and simulation controls.

In this lesson

Write a self-checking testbench, run it in ModelSim, and interpret waveforms to pinpoint logic or timing faults.

Why it matters

Simulation exposes functional and timing issues long before you commit to silicon or load the FPGA, saving development time and preventing hardware mistakes.
