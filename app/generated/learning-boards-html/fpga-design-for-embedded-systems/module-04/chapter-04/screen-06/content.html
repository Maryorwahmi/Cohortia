Clock Generation

Pattern A: always toggle
reg clk = 0;
always #5 clk = ~clk;  // 10 ns period

This is concise and commonly used in testbenches for a stable square clock.

Pattern B: initial with forever
initial begin
  clk = 0;
  forever #5 clk = ~clk;
end

Equivalent to the always pattern; useful when you want to set initial conditions explicitly before starting the clock.

Misconception

Do not drive the clock from logic in the DUT. Keep the testbench clock independent so timing is predictable and measurement is simple.

Check your understanding

Measure the time between consecutive rising edges in the waveform to confirm the period equals your intended value.
