Lab Walkthrough

4-bit ALU testbench tasks

Starter pattern
module tb_alu;
  reg [3:0] a, b;
  reg [2:0] opcode;
  wire [3:0] result;
  wire overflow;
  alu4 uut(.A(a), .B(b), .OP(opcode), .Y(result), .OVF(overflow));
  // Apply vectors and self-checks
endmodule
Deliverable

A testbench that prints PASS/ERROR for each case, plus ModelSim waveform screenshots for at least two edge cases.

Hints

Include overflow and sign edge cases, initialize inputs, and ensure reset and clock are present if the ALU is sequential.

Checkpoints
  • All opcodes exercised
  • PASS/ERROR output for each test
  • Waveform captures for edge cases
Reflection

After passing tests, consider what internal signals were most useful to trace and why they helped identify faults.
