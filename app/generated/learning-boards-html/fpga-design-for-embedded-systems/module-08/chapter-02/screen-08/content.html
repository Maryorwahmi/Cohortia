Comparison Hard vs Soft vs Firm
Choose the right IP type for your constraints
Hard IP
Best performance, fixed silicon, tied to device family, low power.
Soft IP
HDL source, portable and modifiable, performance depends on synthesis.
Firm IP
Technology-mapped netlist, optimized for family, needs place-and-route.
Decision checklist
  • Does the function need maximum throughput or lowest power? If yes, prefer hard IP.
  • Do you require source-level changes or portability? If yes, prefer soft IP.
  • Are you targeting a single family and want optimized results? Consider firm IP.
Concrete example
DDR3 controller on a tight schedule → vendor hard or firm IP. UART where you may tweak behavior → soft IP.
Misconception check
Don't assume license cost alone dictates choice; engineering time and verification risk often outweigh upfront fees.
