Trade-offs Buy vs Build
Cost, time, performance, flexibility, and risk
Structured evaluation checklist to justify acquiring an IP core or designing custom logic.
  • Time: How many engineer-weeks will a custom design require versus acquiring IP?
  • Cost: Compare license fees to estimated engineering cost and opportunity cost.
  • Performance: Can custom logic meet throughput and latency targets?
  • Flexibility: Do you need to modify internals or keep source access?
  • Risk: Is the function complex (like DDR) where vendor verification reduces risk?
  • Portability: Will the IP travel across future device families?
Concrete example
DDR3 controller: acquire IP because of complex timing calibration and high verification cost. Custom build only if you have specialized timing expertise and schedule slack.
Decision artifact
Create a short justification memo listing trade-offs and selecting buy or build based on measurable constraints.
