IP Cores and Third-Party IP Acquisition
Pre-verified logic blocks, decision trade-offs, and practical steps to add vendor or third-party IP to your FPGA project.
Practical payoff
Ship complex subsystems faster and reduce verification burden.
Hard IP: silicon blocks, best perf, no changes.
Soft IP: HDL sources, portable and customizable.
Firm IP: tech-mapped netlist, mid ground.
In this lesson
You will learn IP types, sources, trade-offs, and a practical IP Catalog workflow in Quartus and Vivado. A short hands-on activity prepares you to generate and inspect an IP instance.
Coming up
  • IP types and definitions
  • IP Catalog workflow
  • Cost-benefit and hands-on exercise
Key Takeaway

IP cores let you reuse proven logic, saving time and risk. Choose hard, soft, or firm IP based on performance, flexibility, and portability needs.
