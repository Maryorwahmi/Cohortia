Definition Firm IP
Technology-mapped netlists and practical trade-offs
Firm IP is supplied as a synthesized netlist targeted at a specific FPGA family. It reduces mapping work but expects place-and-route and final verification within your project flow.
  • Pre-mapped to device primitives for performance gains.
  • Less portable than soft IP; tied to vendor family.
Example
A DSP pipeline provided as a firm netlist optimized for a vendor's DSP slices, offering improved performance over an HDL version while maintaining some deployment flexibility within the family.
Integration caution
Confirm that the firm IP's targeted device family matches your product roadmap; migrating to a different family may force a redesign.
