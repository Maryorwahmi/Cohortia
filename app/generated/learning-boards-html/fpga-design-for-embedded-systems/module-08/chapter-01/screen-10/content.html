Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Decision Process
A 5-step selection process
  1. Capture functional and non-functional requirements and rank them by priority.
  2. Map requirements to processor attributes: OS support, ISA features, power, and performance.
  3. Check FPGA resource availability for soft-core implementations.
  4. Prototype the highest-risk path (synthesis of soft-core or baseline hard-core software).
  5. Document measured metrics and complete a weighted matrix to finalize the choice.
Example mapping
Linux + OpenCV → hard-core ARM. Simple periodic control → NIOS II/e soft-core.
Common pitfall
Relying only on theoretical clock numbers; always prototype with representative workloads.
Key Takeaway

A structured process reduces bias: prioritize requirements, prototype, and measure before finalizing your processor choice.
