Why this matters

Choosing a soft or hard processor changes whether you can customize instruction set, how much FPGA area you consume, and whether you can run full operating systems. That decision shapes hardware/software partitioning for your product.

Quick decision strip
If you need deep customization, choose a soft-core. If you need high performance and OS support, choose a hard-core. Many designs use both: soft cores for control and hardware acceleration for heavy compute.
