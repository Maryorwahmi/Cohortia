Activity steps
  1. Define the workload for each scenario: control, video, IoT.
  2. Collect spec points for NIOS II/f and ARM Cortex-A9 that matter to your workloads.
  3. Fill a comparison table and annotate trade-offs.
  4. Write a one-page recommendation per scenario with measured reasoning.

Common mistake
Comparing only highest clock speeds without workload profiling or resource availability checks.
Pro tip
Prototype a kernel of your workload on evaluation boards or small synthesized soft-core configs to collect real metrics.
Key Takeaway

Ground decisions in measured metrics and workload profiling to choose the correct processor class for each scenario.
