Module 8 › Chapter 7.3 › Key Concepts
Pipelining
Registers shorten stage delays to raise fmax; expect higher cycle latency.
Resource sharing
Multiplexing inputs to one block saves area when operations are non-overlapping.
Retiming
Move registers across logic to balance delays; validate interface sampling.
Summary Takeaway
Optimization is iterative: pick the technique that addresses the measured bottleneck, apply it, and verify resource and timing reports to confirm benefit.
