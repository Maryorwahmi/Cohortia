Practice & Verification

Concrete lab outcomes and what to check in waveforms and CDC reports.

Lab: synchronizer + Gray counter + FIFO
Lab steps
  1. Implement two-FF synchronizer in Verilog and simulate asynchronous toggles.
  2. Write a 4-bit Gray counter in the source clock, sample it from the destination clock, and verify single-bit transitions.
  3. Build and simulate a dual-clock FIFO and verify pointer synchronization and data order.
  4. Run CDC analysis to find unsynchronized crossings and document fixes.
Verification tips
Capture waveforms across clock domains to see metastable behavior resolve.
Use short, randomized timing for the asynchronous toggles to stress the synchronizer.
