Learning Objectives

Four skills to practice and verify in a short lab.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define metastability

Explain why a flip-flop may output an indeterminate voltage when sampled near the clock edge and how that affects downstream logic.

Mnemonic: think of a ball balanced on a ridge — it will fall, but unpredictably.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify CDC points

Locate signals that cross between different clock domains and classify them as single-bit or multi-bit crossings.

Mnemonic: map clocks like country borders; every crossing needs paperwork.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Implement two-FF synchronizer

Write and simulate a two-flip-flop synchronizer for a single-bit asynchronous input, and verify stability at the destination clock.

Mnemonic: two gates of review reduce risk.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Choose multi-bit CDC method

Decide between Gray code, a dual-clock FIFO, or a handshake based on data width, throughput, and coherence needs.

Mnemonic: wide data needs a buffer or guaranteed single-bit transitions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
