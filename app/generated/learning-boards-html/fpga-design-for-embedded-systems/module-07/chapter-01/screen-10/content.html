Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › I/O Delays
Input delay example
set_input_delay -clock {clk} -max 5.000 [get_ports {data_in}]
set_input_delay -clock {clk} -min 1.000 [get_ports {data_in}]
Max models latest valid arrival for setup; min models earliest arrival for hold analysis.
Output delay example
set_output_delay -clock {clk} -max 3.000 [get_ports {data_out}]
Models the time the external device needs the data before its next clock edge.
Concrete scenario
An ADC that updates 4 ns after the clock implies set_input_delay -max 4.0 so your FPGA sampling logic meets the real-world arrival.
Misconception
Assuming zero external delay makes internal timing appear easier than it is; always model I/O.
Check
Use datasheet numbers for external devices to populate -max and -min I/O delays accurately.
