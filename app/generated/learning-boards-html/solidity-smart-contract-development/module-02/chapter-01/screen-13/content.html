What to Remember
Three quick rules to guide your next contract.
Choose representation
Match bytes32, bytes, or string to your data and cost constraints.
Be explicit with Ether
Use address payable and check low-level call results for safe transfers.
Keep operations bounded
Avoid unbounded loops; prefer mappings, pagination, and batched updates.
