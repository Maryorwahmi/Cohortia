Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › What to Remember
Remove unnecessary copies
Iterate storage or use calldata instead of copying large arrays into memory.
Mark qualifiers explicitly
Make storage/memory/calldata explicit in parameters and locals to avoid surprises.
Use constant & immutable
Promote fixed and deployment-time values out of storage to save gas on reads.
Final takeaway

Small, deliberate choices about data location yield safer contracts and meaningful gas savings. Apply the checklist and measure the impact.
