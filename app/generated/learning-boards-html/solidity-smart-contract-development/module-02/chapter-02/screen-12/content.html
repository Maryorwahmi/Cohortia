Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Arithmetic
+, -, *, /, % — arithmetic in 0.8.0+ reverts on overflow to prevent silent corruption.
Comparison
==, !=, <, >, <=, >= — produce booleans used to gate behavior and validate inputs.
Logical & Short-circuit
&&, ||, ! — combine checks and short-circuit; order operands by cost and avoid side effects in operands.
Summary

Know the operator categories, test aggressively for invalid values, and design flow to minimize gas while preserving clarity.
