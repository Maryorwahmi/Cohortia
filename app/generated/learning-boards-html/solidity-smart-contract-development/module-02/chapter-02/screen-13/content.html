Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Bounded loops only
Ensure loops have clear upper bounds or avoid them to prevent out-of-gas failures.
Mappings over scans
Use mapping(address => bool) for membership checks instead of iterating large arrays.
Fail early
Validate inputs and revert on invalid conditions before doing work to keep state consistent.
Final takeaway

Design for gas from the start: choose data structures and flow that scale and keep contracts secure and reliable.
