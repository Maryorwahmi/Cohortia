How we'll practice these skills

Hands-on observation and short assessments build confidence without deploying contracts on mainnet.

Explorer Activity
Inspect a well-known address to read balance, transactions, and contract code tabs. Observe Gas used and transaction fee fields.
What to look for: Gas used, Gas price, function signatures where visible.
Reflection: Why did gas vary between similar transactions?
Assessment snippets
Short multiple-choice checks that ensure you can explain Gas and account initiation rules.
