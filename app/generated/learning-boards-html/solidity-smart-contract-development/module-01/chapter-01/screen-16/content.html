What to Remember

Three compact reminders for rapid recall and debugging.

Pillars
Decentralization · Immutability · Transparency — the foundation of blockchain trust.
Gas
Gas measures computation and is paid in Ether. Always estimate Gas for state-changing operations.
Accounts
EOA signs and initiates; Contract Account executes when called. Keep initiation flow in mind.
Final prompt

Ask yourself for each on-chain event: who signed it, how much Gas was used, and does the explorer show contract code?
