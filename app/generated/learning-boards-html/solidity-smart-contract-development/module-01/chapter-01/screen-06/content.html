Comparison

Proof of Work

Security through computational difficulty; miners expend energy to produce blocks.
  • High energy consumption
  • Attacker needs majority hash power
  • Strong historical track record for censorship resistance
Concrete example
A miner invests in specialized hardware and electricity to win block rewards by solving puzzles.

Proof of Stake

Security through economic stake; validators lock funds and can be penalized for misbehavior.
  • Lower energy usage
  • Attacker needs majority stake and risks slashing
  • Different assumptions about finality and validator behavior
Concrete example
Validators stake ETH and are randomly selected to propose blocks; misbehavior can lose part of their stake.
Key Takeaway

Both mechanisms secure the ledger but use different resources and incentives; understanding them clarifies network behavior you depend on as a developer.
