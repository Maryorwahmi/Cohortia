Process Flow

How consensus keeps a ledger consistent

Steps that lead from transaction broadcast to confirmed block.

1
Broadcast
A transaction is signed and shared with peers for validation.
2
Validate
Nodes check signatures, balances, and basic rules before accepting the tx into a block.
3
Propose & Decide
A proposer creates a block; consensus rules determine which proposal becomes canonical.
4
Append & Propagate
Once chosen, the block is appended and distributed so nodes update their ledger copy.
Misconception
Consensus is not just about preventing double-spend; it also sets how forks resolve and how final a transaction is.
Comparing PoW and PoS
Proof of Work (PoW)
Miners compete to solve puzzles; secure but energy-intensive.
Proof of Stake (PoS)
Validators are selected based on stake; more energy-efficient with different economic penalties for misbehavior.
Key Takeaway

Consensus rules determine who can append blocks and shape the security and resource trade-offs of the network.
