Assessment Idea

Quick checks: Gas and account initiation

Two short multiple-choice questions to confirm core operational knowledge.

Question 1
Which best describes Gas? It represents computational effort and is paid in ETH. This prevents spam and compensates validators.
Question 2
Which account initiates contract deployment? An Externally Owned Account signs and sends the deployment transaction.
Why this matters

These rules help you interpret explorer fields correctly and design transactions and deployment flows with clarity.
