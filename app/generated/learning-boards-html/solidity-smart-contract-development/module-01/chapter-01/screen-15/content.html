Key Concepts

Three compact concept cards to carry forward as quick references.

Blockchain
A distributed, immutable ledger that records linked blocks across nodes.
Consensus & EVM
Consensus decides canonical blocks; the EVM runs contract code deterministically across nodes.
Ether, Gas, Accounts
Ether pays for Gas; EOAs sign transactions; Contract Accounts run code when called.
Summary takeaway

These concepts form your mental map for reading transactions, estimating costs, and designing secure, gas-aware contracts.
