Definition

Ether and Gas

Gas is the unit for work; Ether pays for Gas and transfers value.

How fees are computed
Fee = Gas used × Gas price. Gas price is set by the transaction sender and reflects network demand.
Why Gas exists
To prevent spam, ration resources, and compensate validators for computation.
Key Takeaway

Design contracts to minimize gas-heavy operations like large loops and unnecessary storage writes to avoid high user costs.

Concrete example
A simple ETH transfer consumes a small fixed amount of Gas. Writing an array of items to contract storage consumes much more Gas per item.
Pro tip
Favor calldata and events for large data instead of repeated storage writes to reduce Gas costs where possible.
Common mistake
Assuming a testnet transaction uses the same Gas price as mainnet; Gas price depends on network demand and can vary widely.
