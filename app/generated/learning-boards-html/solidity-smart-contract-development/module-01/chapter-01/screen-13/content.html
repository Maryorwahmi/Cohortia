Benefits and challenges of building on Ethereum

A balanced view helps you design systems that fit the platform's strengths and limits.

Benefits
  • Censorship resistance
  • Composability with existing protocols
  • Transparent, verifiable behavior
Challenges
  • Variable Gas costs and throughput constraints
  • Immutable contracts and upgrade complexity
  • High security burden; bugs can be costly
Concrete planning tip
Design for Gas efficiency, clear failure modes, and upgrade or governance paths to handle necessary changes safely.
Developer example
A live contract storing large arrays may be costly to use; consider off-chain storage or batching to reduce on-chain writes.
Key Takeaway

Match your application's needs to the platform: use Ethereum when trustless, composable, and censorship-resistant properties outweigh cost and complexity.
