Szabo's vending machine
A guiding analogy for smart contracts
Analogy explained
You insert money and select an item; the machine's mechanism enforces the transaction without human agents.
Why it matters
The analogy shows how clearly measurable conditions can be automated and trusted without central authorities.
Modern realization
Ethereum gives a global execution environment so vending-machine logic can run at scale and be verified by anyone.
Concrete prompt
Identify a process you use that is repeatable and measurable; that makes it a candidate for smart contract automation.
Key Takeaway

Szabo's vending machine explains why automation works when conditions are measurable and removes intermediaries for repeated transactions.
