Where smart contracts shine
Common domains and a practical reserve
DeFi
Lending, borrowing, AMMs, and composable money protocols.
NFTs
Unique asset ownership tracked and transferred by contract functions.
DAOs
Governance rules and treasury operations encoded in contracts.
Supply Chain
Recording custody, provenance, and transfer events immutably.
Escrow example
Buyer deposits funds; contract holds them until delivery confirmation, then releases funds automatically.
Cost note
Complex interactions incur gas; design for efficiency and consider off-chain work when appropriate.
Key Takeaway

Pick smart contracts when rules are measurable and automation increases trust or efficiency; watch for gas and security costs.
