Smart vs. Legal Contracts
Key differences to guide design choices
Enforcement
Smart: executed by network. Legal: enforced by courts/agents.
Ambiguity
Smart: requires measurable conditions. Legal: handles subjectivity.
Intermediaries
Smart: often remove intermediaries. Legal: depend on them.
Remedies
Smart: implementable as code paths. Legal: flexible, discretionary remedies.
Concrete scenario
Escrow example: a smart contract can hold funds and release them when both parties confirm delivery, eliminating a human escrow agent and fees.
Misconception
Smart contracts are not a substitute for legal contracts when interpretation or damages need human judgment.
Key Takeaway

Choose smart contracts for precise, automatable agreements; use legal contracts alongside them when subjectivity matters.
