Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Learning Objectives
01
Identify access control vulnerabilities

Recognize missing onlyOwner or open public setters. Practice by scanning functions and asking who should call them.

Mnemonic: "Who and What"
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Apply modifier-based controls

Implement onlyOwner and test that only intended addresses can perform admin actions. Understand require checks and zero-address guards.

Mnemonic: "Guard the Setter"
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Understand DoS via unbounded loops

Learn why iterating over growing arrays can exceed block gas limits and how that makes functions unusable.

Mnemonic: "Bound the Loop"
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Prevent DoS via pull & batch patterns

Use pull payments, batching, or off-chain tallying to avoid single-transaction work that can fail due to gas limits.

Mnemonic: "Pay Pull, Break Batches"
