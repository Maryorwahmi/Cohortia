Core Concept
Denial of Service
When contract logic becomes unusable due to gas limits or failing external dependencies.
Why it happens: unbounded loops and fragile external calls.
Practical sign: a function that reverts only once an array grows beyond a certain size.
Defense: pull payments, batch processing, and pause controls.
