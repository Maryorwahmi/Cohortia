Key Concepts
Access Control
Mechanisms that restrict who can call functions or change state.
onlyOwner
A simple modifier restricting a function to the contract owner address.
RBAC
Assign named roles to addresses for granular, multi-admin permissions.
Denial of Service
Attacks or conditions that make a contract’s functions unusable.
Unbounded Loops
Loops over growing collections that can exceed block gas limits and revert.
Pull Payment System
Recipients withdraw owed funds themselves, preventing large push loops.
Summary

Make permissions explicit and design operations to remain callable as your system scales.
