Process Flow
RBAC steps
  1. Define roles that reflect responsibilities, e.g., MINTER_ROLE, PAUSER_ROLE.
  2. Grant roles to addresses or multisigs at deployment or via governance.
  3. Check role membership in function modifiers before executing sensitive logic.
  4. Revoke or rotate roles when keys are retired.
Tip: Assign roles to multisigs or governance contracts, not to single personal keys.

Takeaway
Design roles to minimize privileges and attach them to governance-friendly addresses. RBAC increases clarity and reduces single-point-of-failure risk.
