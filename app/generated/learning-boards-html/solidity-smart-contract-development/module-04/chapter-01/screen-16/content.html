Integer safety & testing

Integer Overflow
Arithmetic exceeding a type's max wraps to the minimum in older compilers.
Solidity 0.8.0+
Arithmetic now reverts on overflow/underflow, preventing silent wrap-around.
SafeMath
Use checked arithmetic in older compilers to revert on dangerous operations.
Closing takeaway

Combine ordering, guards, compiler checks, and exploit tests to create durable contract security.
