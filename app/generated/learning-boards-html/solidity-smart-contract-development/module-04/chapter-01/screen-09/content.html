Comparison › call vs transfer

External call mechanisms and risk

transfer
Forwards small fixed gas; can break with complex recipients but historically limited reentrancy capability.
send
Similar to transfer but returns a boolean instead of reverting; requires explicit checks.
call{value:...}()
Forwards all remaining gas; flexible but requires careful ordering and guards to prevent reentry.
Key rule: choose the mechanism for compatibility, but never treat gas limits as a security pattern. Always order state updates and consider a reentrancy guard.
