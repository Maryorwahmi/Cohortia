Core Concept
What happens during a professional audit
A structured sequence of review steps that together offer comprehensive coverage.
Audit Steps
  1. Manual Code Review: Line-by-line inspection to find logic and Solidity-specific pitfalls.
  2. Static Analysis: Automated scans to surface common patterns and candidate vulnerabilities.
  3. Dynamic Analysis / Fuzzing: Stress inputs and explore state to reveal edge cases.
  4. Economic/Game Theory Review: Analyze incentives and attack profitability.
  5. Documentation & Reporting: Prioritized findings and remediation suggestions.
Key Takeaway

An audit combines automated breadth with human insight; prepare clear specs and tests so auditors can focus on your critical properties.

Example: Audit checklist
A sample audit will flag visibility issues, reentrancy points, outdated compiler versions, and economic edge cases, then rank them by severity.
Common-mistake
Assuming an audit is a one-time guarantee. Audits help but do not eliminate risk; plan follow-ups after major changes.
Pro tip
Provide example transactions and a short threat model to help auditors target tests and economic analysis.
