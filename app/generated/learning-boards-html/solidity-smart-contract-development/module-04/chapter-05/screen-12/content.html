Common Pitfalls
Limits and common mistakes
Know the blind spots of each technique and avoid overconfidence.
Over-reliance
Assuming one audit or one tool makes the contract fully secure.
Ignoring warnings
Not triaging static analysis output or documenting dismissals wastes future reviewers' time.
Incomplete specs
Formal verification proves only what you specify; missing properties mean gaps remain.
Remedy
Use layered defenses, keep triage history, and treat audits and proofs as targeted investments aligned with value-at-risk.
