Upgradeability Patterns & Security

Preserve state, update logic, and run secure upgrades with UUPS and monitoring.

Why this matters

Immutability prevents on-chain fixes. Upgradeability lets you evolve without migrating users. We will keep address continuity and state safety as central goals.

What you’ll see
  • Proxy pattern anatomy and UUPS specifics.
  • Solidity initializer patterns and storage safety rules.
  • Hardhat deploy and upgrade scripts using OpenZeppelin helpers.
  • Post-deployment monitoring and incident response steps.
