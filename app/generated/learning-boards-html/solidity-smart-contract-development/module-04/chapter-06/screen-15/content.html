Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Quick Reference
Upgrade safety
Only apply upgrades that pass storage-layout checks and are reviewed by a multisig or governance process.
Initializer rule
Always use initializer functions for proxy deployments and ensure they cannot be called again unexpectedly.
Operate & respond
Run automated alerts, time-lock upgrades, and a rehearsed incident response plan linked to emergency pause mechanisms.
Final takeaway

Upgradeability lets chains evolve responsibly, but only when paired with strong governance, careful storage practices, and continuous monitoring.
