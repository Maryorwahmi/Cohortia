Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Incident Response
Immediate steps
  1. Validate alert and collect transaction/state snapshots.
  2. Trigger emergency pause if it exists and is safe to do so.
  3. Notify core team and escalate via on-call channels.
Investigation & remediation

Identify root cause, prepare patched implementation if upgradeable, and test upgrades in a controlled environment before applying to mainnet.

Communication plan

Prepare messages that outline what happened, what is affected, and the immediate next steps. Keep updates frequent and factual to maintain trust.

Post-mortem

Document lessons learned, adjust monitoring thresholds, and update the upgrade governance and technical controls accordingly.
