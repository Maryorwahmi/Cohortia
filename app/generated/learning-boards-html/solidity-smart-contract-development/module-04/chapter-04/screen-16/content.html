Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Testing patterns
Isolation
Use beforeEach and fresh deployments to avoid cross-test contamination.
Paired assertions
Assert events and the resulting state or balance together.
Adversarial tests
Include attacker contracts and large-input tests to prove defenses.
Final takeaway

Make testing part of the design process. Tests are not an afterthought; they are your strongest guardrail against costly, irreversible errors in smart contract systems.
