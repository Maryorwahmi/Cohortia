Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Signers & connect
Pattern
Get signers in beforeEach, then call contract.connect(signer).functionName(...) to simulate a transaction from that account. Use this to test onlyOwner modifiers and multi-user interactions.
Example
After deploying as owner, use connect with addr1 and expect a protected call to revert with the correct message.
Why it matters
  • Verifies access control and authorizations.
  • Simulates user roles without redeploying contracts.
  • Supports realistic multi-account scenarios for economic flows.
Misconception
Assuming the deployer is always the caller hides many authorization bugs; explicitly test non-deployer callers.
