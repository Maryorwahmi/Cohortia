Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Hardhat
Local network and tooling for compiling, deploying, and testing contracts quickly.
Waffle
Chai matchers for contract-specific assertions like events, reverts, and balance changes.
Mocha & Chai
Test runner and assertion library that structure and express expected behavior in tests.
Summary Takeaway

Use the Hardhat + Waffle stack with focused, isolated tests and adversarial scenarios to build a reliable safety net around your smart contracts.
