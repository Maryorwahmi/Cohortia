Researching Hardhat plugins
Identify plugins that address real gaps in your workflow, then validate them safely before integration.
• Step 1: Inventory your needs — testing helpers, gas reports, verification, or deployment utilities.
• Step 2: For each candidate plugin, note what it does and a practical scenario where it helps.
• Step 3: Test in isolation in a small repo or branch to verify behavior and configuration work.
Assessment guidance
The chapter's suggested assessment items test understanding of Hardhat Network benefits and the plugin system. Use short, scenario-based questions to evaluate practical judgment.
Example assessment
Which Hardhat feature best supports fast, deterministic local testing? Answer: Hardhat Network. Explain why and contrast with public testnets.
Key Takeaway

Evaluate plugins pragmatically: match to need, test in isolation, and integrate incrementally.
