Comparison
Hardhat vs Truffle vs Foundry
Hardhat
Developer-friendly, JavaScript/TypeScript-first, extensible plugins, built-in Hardhat Network and debugging tools.
Truffle
A long-standing framework with its own conventions; may feel less integrated with modern JS tooling compared to Hardhat.
Foundry
Rust-based toolchain that emphasizes performance and low-level control, offering a different development paradigm.
Decision note
Match tools to team expertise: choose the environment that minimizes ramp-up and supports your testing and deployment needs.
Key Takeaway

Frameworks differ by ergonomics and focus; select based on team skills and project priorities rather than hype.
