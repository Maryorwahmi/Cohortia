Pitfalls and how to avoid them
Practical traps that cause build or collaboration problems.
Editing node_modules
Avoid direct edits. Fork or patch and link if a change is required.
Committing cache/artifacts
These are reproducible; prefer generating them in CI rather than committing to Git.
Solidity version mismatch
Ensure hardhat.config.js's solidity setting is compatible with contract pragma lines to avoid compile failures.
Final checklist
Verify .gitignore, commit package-lock.json, avoid editing node_modules, keep artifacts regenerated in CI, and align pragma and configured solidity versions.
