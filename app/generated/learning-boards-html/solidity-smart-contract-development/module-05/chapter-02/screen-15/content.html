What to Remember
Artifacts & Best Practices
Short reminders of outputs and safe habits.
node_modules/
Contains installed packages; usually excluded from version control.
artifacts/ & cache/
Compiled outputs and build cache generated by npx hardhat compile.
Commit & Ignore
Commit package.json and package-lock.json; ignore node_modules and, depending on workflow, artifacts and cache.
Final takeaway: prefer local devDependencies and npx, commit manifests and locks, and treat generated artifacts as build outputs rather than source.
