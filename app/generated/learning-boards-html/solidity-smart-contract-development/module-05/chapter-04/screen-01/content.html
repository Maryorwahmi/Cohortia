Writing Your First Smart Contract in Hardhat
A hands-on walk-through of Counter.sol and the Solidity building blocks you will use every day.
In this lesson
Create contracts/Counter.sol, add pragma and SPDX, declare state, write constructor and functions with safe checks, and run simple compile tests in Hardhat.
Why it matters
Correct syntax and visibility determine both safety and usability of your contract. Small mistakes cause compilation or runtime failures that cost gas and developer time.
