Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › What to Remember
uint256
A common unsigned integer type used for counters and balances.
private / public
Visibility controls who can access functions or state at the Solidity level.
require()
Use for validation checks and to revert with a helpful reason string on failure.
Final tip
Develop with tests and small iterative deployments; explicit declarations and checks save time and reduce risk when you move from local to live networks.
