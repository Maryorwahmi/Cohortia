Comparison

Warnings vs Errors

Warning
Non-fatal advice; review and fix if needed to avoid logical pitfalls.
Optimizer
Reduces gas cost by transforming compiled output; affects bytecode shape and debugging.
When to enable optimizer

Enable it for production deployments when gas cost matters. Keep iterations without optimization to speed development debugging, then test with optimization before deploying.

Practical guideline

  • Address warnings early; they often reveal logical or compatibility issues.
  • Test with optimizer enabled before production to confirm behavior and gas profiles.
  • Change optimizer settings deliberately; note that bytecode can differ between runs.
Misconception
Higher optimizer runs always reduce total cost. They may change runtime profiles and complicate debugging.
Key Takeaway
Treat warnings seriously and choose optimization for production after testing, not by default during development.
