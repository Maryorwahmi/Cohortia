Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Quick Reference
Await everything

Await contract.deploy(), transactions, and view reads used after state changes.

Isolate tests

Use beforeEach to deploy fresh instances so tests don't interfere with one another.

Assert reverts & events

Use to.be.revertedWith and event assertions to confirm failure reasons and emitted signals.

Final takeaway

Tests are your contract’s living spec. Keep them focused, deterministic, and reflective of realistic user and attacker behaviors.
