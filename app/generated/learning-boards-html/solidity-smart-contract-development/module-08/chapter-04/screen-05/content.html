Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Address & ABI
Contract Address

A hexadecimal identifier returned at deployment. Keep the exact address for the network where you deployed.

Quick sanity check

If a call reverts, confirm the address matches the network shown in your wallet. A wrong network is the most common cause of read failure.

ABI (Application Binary Interface)

A JSON array describing public functions and events. Hardhat writes this into artifacts after compilation.

Why update it

If you change your contract and redeploy, update the frontend ABI to match or Ethers.js won't know about new functions.
