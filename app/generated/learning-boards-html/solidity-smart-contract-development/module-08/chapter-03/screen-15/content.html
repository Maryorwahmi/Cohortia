What to Remember
Three operational reminders for daily development.
Signer
Used for signing; always obtained after user connects and never reveals private keys.
Event Listeners
accountsChanged, chainChanged, disconnect — attach and remove them to stay in sync with the wallet.
Network Validation
Always check chainId or network name to ensure your contracts are being called on the intended chain.
Final Tip

Test connection flows in a real browser with MetaMask on a test network, simulate rejections and network switches, and use the lifecycle pattern to make connection behavior predictable.
