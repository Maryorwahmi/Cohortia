Learning Objectives

What you'll be able to do and why it matters for a reliable dApp.

01
Differentiate calls vs transactions

Explain why view/pure calls do not change state or cost gas, while nonpayable and payable functions require transactions and fees.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Send transactions from the frontend

Use a Signer to instantiate a contract for write operations and implement a function that triggers a signed transaction.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Manage confirmations & receipts

Call tx.wait() to obtain a TransactionReceipt and check receipt.status to determine whether the transaction succeeded.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Handle gas, errors, and UX

Estimate and handle gas, parse revert messages, and provide pending/confirmed/failed UI states to keep users informed.
