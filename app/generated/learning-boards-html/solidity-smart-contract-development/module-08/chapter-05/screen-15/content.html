What to Remember

Pay attention to signer
Only sign with a connected account; otherwise reads only.
Await confirmations
Use tx.wait() and check receipt.status before trusting updates.
Handle failures
Distinguish user cancellations, reverts, and insufficient funds with clear messages.
Quick Tip

Test write flows on a testnet and intentionally trigger errors to improve your messages and UX before mainnet release.
