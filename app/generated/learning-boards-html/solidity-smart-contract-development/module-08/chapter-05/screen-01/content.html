Writing Data: Sending Transactions

How to get a signer, send state-changing calls, await confirmations, and surface clear feedback to users.

In this lesson

Sign a transaction from the user's wallet, handle mining with tx.wait(), and refresh state after confirmation.

Why it matters

Writes change on-chain and cost gas. Good UX and error handling prevent confusion and lost funds.
