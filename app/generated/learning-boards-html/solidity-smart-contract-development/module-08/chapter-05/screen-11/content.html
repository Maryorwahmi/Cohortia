UX Patterns for Transactions

Pending

Disable inputs and show tx.hash and an estimated wait message.

Success

Refresh contract data and show confirmation with block number for transparency.

Failure

Show a clear error, suggest checking balance or trying again, and log details for debugging.

Check your understanding

If you show an optimistic update, what step must you still perform to guarantee correctness? (Hint: reconcile with on-chain state after tx.wait.)
