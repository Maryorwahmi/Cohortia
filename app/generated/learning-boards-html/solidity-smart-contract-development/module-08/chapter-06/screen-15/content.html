Smart Contract Events
On-chain logs that external apps can listen to for real-time updates.
emit keyword
Used in Solidity to create a log entry during a transaction.
indexed keyword
Makes parameters filterable by blockchain nodes for efficient subscriptions.
Summary
Design events with the future filters you need, emit them at state changes, and subscribe with contract.filters in the frontend to build responsive, efficient dApps.
