Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Re-fetch vs Targeted
Re-fetch everything
Pros: authoritative, simple. Cons: extra latency, network load, poor UX if frequent.
Example: after an event, call a full fetchContractData to refresh all fields displayed in the UI.
Targeted update
Pros: faster perceived response, lower load. Cons: must ensure eventual consistency.
Example: use newGreeting from the event to update the greeting display immediately, then schedule a quick confirm read of greet() if needed.
Design rule
Prefer immediate targeted updates for responsiveness and confirm with lightweight reads for correctness. Avoid full re-fetches on every event unless necessary.
