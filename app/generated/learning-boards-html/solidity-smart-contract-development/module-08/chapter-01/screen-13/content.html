Hands-on Activity

Analyze three dApps and prepare a short report

For each dApp identify network, contract purpose, frontend stack, wallet interaction, and decentralization benefits.

Report structure
  1. One-paragraph summary.
  2. Architecture bullets: network, contracts, frontend, wallet.
  3. Two evidence points and trade-off notes.
What to look for
  • Does the UI use a known framework?
  • Does it use indexers or direct node reads?
  • Which wallets are supported?
Evaluation tips
Judge benefits: immutability, censorship resistance, and shared trust. Note where centralization improves UX and why that trade-off was likely made.
Assessment idea: include a short reflection: where did decentralization materially improve the product, and where did it introduce constraints?
