Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › What to Remember
Transaction UX
Always show pending and final states and manage optimistic updates carefully.
Indexers vs Nodes
Use indexers for fast queries, but verify important facts against the blockchain.
Framework choice
Select a UI stack that supports predictable state and integrates with your Web3 tooling.
Reflective Prompt

Choose a real-world scenario and decide: does decentralization materially improve trust or should a hybrid design be used for better UX and cost control?
