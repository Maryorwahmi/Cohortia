Set the contenthash
Use the ENS manager to paste your IPFS CID into the domain's contenthash record and confirm the update.
Steps:
  1. Connect the wallet that owns the ENS name.
  2. Open the domain's records and choose contenthash.
  3. Paste the CID you received from your pinning service and save the change.
  4. Confirm the on-chain transaction and wait for block confirmation.
Practical note

Setting contenthash requires signing a transaction and paying gas. Keep your ENS keys secure and consider automating updates through a CI/CD step that calls a verified contract method or script.

Why it matters

ENS makes your dApp discoverable with a human-readable name while remaining decentralized because the name points to an immutable CID.

Misconception

ENS does not host your files. It is a pointer. If no node pins the CID, the content may be unavailable even if the ENS record points to it.
