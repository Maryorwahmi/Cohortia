Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Key Concepts
IPFS
A content-addressed, peer-to-peer file system that produces CIDs for immutable assets.
Decentralized Hosting
Hosting frontend assets on a distributed network to avoid single points of failure and censorship.
Pinata
A pinning service that keeps your IPFS content available without running your own node.
Summary
Together these concepts enable a resilient, content-addressed frontend that you can publish, verify, and point at with ENS for a human-friendly experience.
