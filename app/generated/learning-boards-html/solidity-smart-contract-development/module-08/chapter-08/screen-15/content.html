Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Operational Reminders
CID = Artifact
Record and publish the CID for each release; use it to verify exact deployed bundles.
Protect secrets
Keep Pinata keys in CI secrets and .env files locally; never bundle them into the frontend.
Verify runtime
After publishing, test on gateways, connect a wallet, and run sample contract calls to confirm behavior.
One-sentence takeaway
Treat publishing as a release process: build, pin, verify, and update pointers or documentation so users always reach the correct, pinned artifact.
