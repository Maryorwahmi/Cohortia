Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Clarity & Consistency
Clarity
Use simple language, show gas estimates, and explain irreversible actions clearly.
Consistency
Maintain the same labels, colors, and interaction patterns for wallet and transaction states everywhere in the app.
Responsiveness
Ensure layouts and flows work across desktop, tablet, and mobile wallets, and test common viewports.
Example audit
Review copy for ambiguous terms, verify all transaction buttons disable while pending, and ensure color contrast meets accessibility requirements.
Key Takeaway
Don’t assume polish equals usability. Test for clarity, consistency, and responsiveness to reduce user risk.
