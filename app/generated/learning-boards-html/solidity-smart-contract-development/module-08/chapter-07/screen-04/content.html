Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › dApp UX challenges
Definition
dApp UX challenges are the interface problems that arise because users must connect wallets, wait for confirmations, and manage irreversible on-chain changes.
Wallet Interaction
Users approve transactions outside your UI. Your app must reflect approvals, rejections, and missing accounts.
Gas & Latency
Transactions take time and cost money. Show costs and prevent duplicate sends.
Immutability
Once on chain, state changes are final. Confirm intent and provide clear receipts.
Concrete Example
A token transfer UI should show estimated gas, require confirmation, display a pending indicator, and show the transaction hash with a link to a block explorer. If it fails due to insufficient allowance, show the revert reason and next steps.
Key Takeaway

Design for failure modes and delays. Clear feedback prevents mistakes and builds user trust.

Common Mistake

Assuming instant confirmation and not disabling repeat actions leads to duplicate transactions and wasted gas.
