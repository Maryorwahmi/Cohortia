Comparison › Gas and best practices
require / revert
Refund remaining gas, good for input validation. Short messages ok in dev; refactor repeated messages to custom errors for production.
assert
Signals internal bug; consumes remaining gas. Use sparingly and document the invariant clearly.
Custom Errors
Lower revert data gas cost and provide structured parameters for tests and tooling.
Events
Cheaper than storage but still cost gas. Keep payloads small and index only what you query.
Practical workflow
Use readable messages in dev; refactor to custom errors and concise events before deployment to optimize gas and observability.
