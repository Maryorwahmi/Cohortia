Architecture › Escrow contract

State & roles

  • • depositor: address that creates the escrow and sends funds.
  • • beneficiary: address that receives funds on release.
  • • arbiter: trusted address that can release or refund funds.
  • • amount: the Ether held in escrow; isReleased: whether funds were moved.
Constructor checks
Use require to prevent zero addresses and assign depositor to msg.sender. These are deployment-time validations that immediately revert for invalid parameters.
Design note
Use custom errors for role violations and repeated checks to save gas in production.
