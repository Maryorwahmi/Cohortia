Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Practice Reminders
Governance
Restrict upgrade control with multisig and time delays to reduce centralization risk.
Proxy-first interactions
Always call initialize and other functions through the proxy so state resides in the proxy.
Testing & Audit
Simulate upgrades, check storage layout, and audit delegatecall and fallback behavior.
Final Prompt
Before choosing upgradeability, weigh whether preserving state and address justifies the additional design, testing, and governance burden.
