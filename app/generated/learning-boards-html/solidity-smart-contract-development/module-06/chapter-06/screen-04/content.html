Understanding Gas in Ethereum

The fundamental pricing mechanism for EVM computation

What is Gas?

Gas is a unit of computational effort required to execute operations on the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM).

Gas Cost & Fees

Each EVM opcode has an associated gas cost. Total gas consumed determines the transaction fee paid in Ether.

Impact on DApps

High gas costs negatively impact user experience, adoption, and the feasibility of DApp functionalities.
