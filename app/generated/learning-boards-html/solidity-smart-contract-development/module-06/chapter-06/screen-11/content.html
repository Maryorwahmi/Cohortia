Performance Tooling

Introducing `hardhat-gas-reporter`

Analyzing gas costs with your Hardhat tests

Integrates with Hardhat

A plugin that works seamlessly with your existing Hardhat test suite to provide gas cost analysis.

Detailed Gas Reports

Provides a breakdown of gas consumption for each function called within your tests.

Identify Gas Guzzlers

Helps pinpoint which functions are most expensive, guiding your optimization efforts effectively.

Why Use a Gas Reporter?

Quantify Impact

Measure the exact gas savings from your optimization efforts.

Prioritize Work

Focus on optimizing the functions that consume the most gas.

Regression Detection

Catch unintended gas cost increases introduced by new code changes.
