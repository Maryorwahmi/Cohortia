Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter

Gas

The unit of computational effort required to execute operations on the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM).

Gas Cost

The amount of Ether (or native token) paid for the gas consumed by a transaction.

SSTORE

The EVM opcode for writing data to persistent storage, one of the most expensive operations.

Summary Takeaway

Gas is the lifeblood of Ethereum, and understanding its costs, especially for operations like SSTORE, is fundamental to effective gas optimization.
