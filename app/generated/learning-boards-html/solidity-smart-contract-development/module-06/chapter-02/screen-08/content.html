Technique
Signers & connect()
Use getSigners to obtain accounts, then contract.connect(signer) to send transactions from that account. This is how you test owner-only functions and multi-user interactions. 
const [owner, user] = await ethers.getSigners();
await contract.connect(user).someAction();
Concrete scenario
Test updateOwner by calling updateOwner(newAddr) from owner and from a non-owner. The non-owner call should revert with the owner-only require message.
Key Takeaway

Explicitly connect the signer you intend to simulate to avoid mistaken assumptions about msg.sender.
