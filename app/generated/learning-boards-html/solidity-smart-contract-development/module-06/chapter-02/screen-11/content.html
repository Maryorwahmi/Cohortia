Assessment Idea
Verify withdraw reverts on insufficient balance
Arrange a contract state with balances[msg.sender] equal to 100, then attempt withdraw(200) and assert the call reverts with the message "Insufficient balance". Finally, re-check the balance is unchanged.
await expect(myContract.connect(owner).withdraw(200))
  .to.be.revertedWith("Insufficient balance");

expect(await myContract.balances(owner.address)).to.equal(100);
Key Takeaway

Assert precise revert messages and verify invariants to ensure safe failure behavior.
