Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Exercise
Tasks
  1. Write at least two invariants for increment and two for decrement, including explicit preconditions.
  2. Describe a fuzzing scenario for decrement that would find an edge case a unit test might miss.
  3. Explain why implementing these properties in Hardhat/Waffle would require external tooling or integration.
Example invariants
increment: count_after > count_before; increment never decreases count. decrement: if count_before > 0 then count_after < count_before and count_after ≥ 0.
Example fuzz scenario
Random initial counts including boundary values, mixed increment/decrement sequences, and repeated calls to detect ordering or require-guard failures.
Reflection

A clear counterexample is the best teacher: it tells you the precise inputs and steps that broke your invariant.
