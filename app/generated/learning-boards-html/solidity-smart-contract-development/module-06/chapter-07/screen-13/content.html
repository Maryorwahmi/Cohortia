Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Quick Rules
1. Baseline Tests
Keep clear unit tests for documented behavior and regressions.
2. Focused Properties
Express invariants precisely and include preconditions.
3. Selective Fuzzing
Prioritize high-risk functions and run short fuzz budgets in CI, longer runs offline.
Final thought

A mix of unit tests, properties, and fuzzing gives the best defenses against unexpected inputs and state transitions.
