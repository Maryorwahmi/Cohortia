Definition
Testing revert conditions
Force failing inputs and assert exact error messages to prove guards exist and behave correctly.
Why this matters
  • Confirms preconditions are enforced and specific errors are returned.
  • Prevents silent failures where a different guard reverts the call.
  • Makes contract behavior explicit for off-chain error handling.
Common mistakes
Using to.be.reverted instead of checking the message, and forgetting to await state-changing calls. Both lead to brittle or misleading tests.
// test snippet (visual example)
await expect(ownable.connect(addr1).transferOwnership(addr2.address))
  .to.be.revertedWith("Ownable: caller is not the owner");

await expect(ownable.connect(owner).transferOwnership(ethers.constants.AddressZero))
  .to.be.revertedWith("Ownable: new owner is the zero address");
Check your understanding

If a test uses to.be.reverted and it passes, what additional assertion would you add to confirm the revert reason? Try asserting the exact message with to.be.revertedWith.
