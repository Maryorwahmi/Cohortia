ERC-20 testing: transfer and transferFrom
Starter assertions
After deployment, assert totalSupply equals owner balance and that a Transfer from address(0) was emitted.
Negative cases
  • Transfer more than balance reverts with specific message.
  • Transfer or approve to zero address reverts.
  • transferFrom without allowance reverts.
// transfer test example
await expect(token.connect(owner).transfer(addr1.address, 100))
  .to.emit(token, "Transfer")
  .withArgs(owner.address, addr1.address, 100);

await expect(token.connect(addr1).transfer(addr2.address, 1000000))
  .to.be.revertedWith("ERC20: transfer amount exceeds balance");
Example scenario

Approve owner→spender then have spender call transferFrom; assert allowance decreased and Transfer event emitted with correct args.
