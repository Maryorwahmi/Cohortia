Mock Contracts: Design and Scope

Keep mocks focused on the interface and behaviors your tests require.

ERC-20 mock essentials
  • balance mapping and balanceOf method
  • transfer, approve, transferFrom and allowance
  • emit Transfer and Approval events when actions occur
Keep constructor minting and a simple transfer logic for predictable tests.
Oracle mock essentials
  • setter to control price values
  • getter used by consumer contracts
  • ability to simulate stale or erroneous responses
Design negative responses to verify your contract's revert paths and error handling.
