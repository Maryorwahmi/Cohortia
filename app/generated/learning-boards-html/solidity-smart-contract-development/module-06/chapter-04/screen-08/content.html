Mocks vs Forking Mainnet

Tradeoffs when testing external dependencies.

Mocks
  • Pros: deterministic, fast, and under your control.
  • Pros: easy to force edge cases and errors.
  • Cons: may diverge from real implementations if over-simplified.
Best for regular integration and unit tests where behavior is simple and known.
Forking
  • Pros: interact with real deployed contracts and current state.
  • Pros: useful for end-to-end verification against established protocols.
  • Cons: slower, requires network config, and can be brittle.
Use sparingly for final integration checks that require real protocol behavior.
