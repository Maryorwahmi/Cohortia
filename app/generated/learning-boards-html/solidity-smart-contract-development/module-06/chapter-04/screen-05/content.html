Deploy Order and beforeEach Fixtures

A reliable beforeEach deploys contracts in sequence and prepares accounts.

Deployment steps
Step 1 — Signers
Call ethers.getSigners and assign owner, user1, user2.
Step 2 — Deploy Token
Deploy MyToken with initial supply and owner address.
Step 3 — Deploy Vault
Deploy Vault and pass MyToken.address into the constructor.
Step 4 — Prepare Accounts
Transfer tokens to user accounts and assert balances before tests run.
Guards and assertions
  • Await deployment and call deployed() to ensure the contract is ready.
  • Assert dependent contract stores the provider address you passed.
  • Keep fixture scope narrow to speed up the suite and reduce coupling.
Mistake to avoid: deploying contracts in the wrong order or forgetting await leads to flakey tests that fail intermittently.
