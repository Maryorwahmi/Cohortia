Key Concepts

What to Remember

Integration Testing
Deploy and test contracts together to validate real interactions and event sequences.
Signer Management
Use ethers.getSigners and connect() so txs come from the intended account and msg.sender is correct.
Mocks & Failures
Create minimal mocks and include negative tests to check revert paths and error handling.
Summary Takeaway

Integration testing with well-designed fixtures, explicit signer usage, and targeted mocks gives you confidence in multi-contract systems before deployment.
