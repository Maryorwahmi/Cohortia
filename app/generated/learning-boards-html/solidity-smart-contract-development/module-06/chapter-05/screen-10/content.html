When to use verbose

Escalate to verbose traces when you need to inspect opcodes, gas usage, or low-level faults that are not visible from prints or simple stack traces.

➜ npx hardhat test --verbose
... opcode trace ...
  0x...    0 PUSH1 0x80    gas: 100
  0x...    1 SSTORE        gas: 5000
  ...
  REVERT (returned)
Reading tips
  • Find where REVERT or invalid opcode appears in the trace.
  • Map opcodes back to the Solidity line indicated by the stack trace.
  • Check gas usage spikes to spot expensive loops or repeated storage access.
Key Takeaway

Verbose traces are your microscope for EVM-level diagnosis — use them after higher-level checks narrow the suspect region.
