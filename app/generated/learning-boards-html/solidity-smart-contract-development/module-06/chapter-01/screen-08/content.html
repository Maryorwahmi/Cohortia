End-to-end scope

E2E tests check full flows and external integrations under realistic conditions. Use them for high-value flows and user journeys.

What E2E validates
  • Front-end to contract interaction and transaction flows.
  • Gas and ordering issues that only appear in full transactions.
  • Integration with off-chain services oracles, relayers, or wallets in end-to-end scenarios.
Limitations
E2E is slower, more brittle, and less diagnostic than unit tests. Use E2E sparingly and pair with strong unit/integration coverage.
Practical rule
Prioritize E2E for fund movement and critical UX flows; test logic primarily with unit and integration tests.
Example

An E2E deposit test signs a transaction via a wallet simulation, sends it to a forked network, and confirms the balance change on-chain.
