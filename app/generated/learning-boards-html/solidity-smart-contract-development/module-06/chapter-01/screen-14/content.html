Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Immutability
Once deployed, smart contract code cannot be changed. This makes testing essential before deployment.
Unit Testing
Tests single functions or components in isolation to verify logic and edge cases.
Integration Testing
Validates interactions between contracts and reveals cross-contract side effects.
Summary

Prioritize tests that protect fund flows and contract interactions; treat immutability as the reason tests are your most important safety measure.
