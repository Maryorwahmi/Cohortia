Fee selection for critical transactions
EIP-1559 fields let you set a max and priority fee to influence inclusion speed.
// Example override to speed a transaction
await contract.someFunction({
  gasLimit: 200000,
  maxFeePerGas: ethers.utils.parseUnits('100', 'gwei'),
  maxPriorityFeePerGas: ethers.utils.parseUnits('2', 'gwei')
});
  • Default estimators are usually sufficient for normal operations.
  • Raise fees only for time-sensitive transactions to avoid unnecessary costs.
  • Set gasLimit to prevent out-of-gas errors on complex calls.
Common mistake
Using a very large maxFee without understanding the network may lead to unexpectedly high costs; monitor replaced transactions and fee refunds carefully.
Why it matters

Choosing appropriate fees balances speed and cost; it is an operational decision for production dApps.
