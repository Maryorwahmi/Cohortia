Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › State-changing Transactions
Three-step process
  1. Ensure Contract initialized with a Signer capable of paying gas.
  2. Send the transaction by calling the state-changing method.
  3. Await tx.wait() to obtain the TransactionReceipt and confirm the block inclusion.
// send and await
const tx = await counter.connect(signer).increment();
console.log('tx hash:', tx.hash);
const receipt = await tx.wait();
console.log('mined in block', receipt.blockNumber);
Why awaiting matters
Without awaiting the receipt, subsequent reads or dependent actions may observe the previous state and cause logic errors or failed assertions.
Common mistake
Not awaiting tx.wait() or assuming immediate finality. Always handle asynchronous confirmation and potential reorgs in robust systems.
Key Takeaway

A transaction response is only the submission. The receipt confirms the change and includes gas and event details important for verification.
