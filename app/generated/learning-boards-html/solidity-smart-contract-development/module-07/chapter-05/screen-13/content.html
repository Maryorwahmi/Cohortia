Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Common Mistakes
Missing signer
State-changing calls need a signer. Initialize the contract with a signer or connect before calling.
Not awaiting tx.wait()
Leads to race conditions. Always await the receipt before reading updated state.
Wrong ABI or address
Use the artifact ABI from your compile step and verify the deployed address for the target network.
Network mismatch
Ensure --network matches where the contract was deployed. Otherwise interactions fail or go to the wrong chain.
Unit conversion errors
Always use ethers.utils.parseEther to convert ETH amounts to wei before sending value fields.
Insufficient logging
Log tx.hash and receipt fields to quickly diagnose failures or unexpected gas costs.
