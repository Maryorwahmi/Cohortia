Contract Instance
Address + ABI produce the ethers.js Contract used for interactions.
Provider vs Signer
Provider reads state without gas. Signer signs transactions and pays gas for writes.
Transaction Lifecycle
tx response on submit; await tx.wait() to get receipt with gas, block, and logs.
Bottom line

Load ABI from artifacts, choose provider or signer correctly, await receipts, and automate safely with scripts and tasks.
