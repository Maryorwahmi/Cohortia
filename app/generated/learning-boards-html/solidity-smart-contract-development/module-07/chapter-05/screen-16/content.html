Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Quick Reference
Load ABI from artifacts
Avoid manual ABI mismatches by requiring artifacts compiled by Hardhat.
Choose Provider or Signer
Provider for reads, Signer for writes and sending ETH.
Await the receipt
Use tx.wait() to confirm transactions, inspect gasUsed and events.
Final thought

Treat interactions as scripted operations: verify inputs, sign explicitly, await confirmations, and log results for auditing and debugging.
