Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Gas Fees
Definition
Gas fees and computational cost

Gas is paid in ETH to cover the computational work of transactions and deployments. Complex operations cost more gas.

Deployment vs transaction
Both cost gas. Deployment gas is paid once; transaction gas is ongoing.
Network differences
Mainnet uses real ETH and fluctuating prices; testnets use faucet ETH and may not match Mainnet congestion.
Concrete example
A large contract with many storage writes can cost significantly more to deploy than a minimal implementation. Simple refactors like packing variables lower gas usage.
Pro tip
Generate gas reports during deployment scripts and compare results between local, Sepolia, and a Mainnet estimate before finalizing design.
Key Takeaway

Gas shapes design decisions. Use staging to estimate costs and optimize expensive operations before Mainnet.
