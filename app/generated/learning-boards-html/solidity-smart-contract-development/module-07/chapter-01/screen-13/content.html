Why testnets matter for integrations
  • Wallet permissions and UI flows are network-aware.
  • Faucets supply test ETH so you can sign and pay gas.
  • Oracles and relayers may behave differently on public networks.
Concrete steps
Connect a test wallet to Sepolia, request faucet ETH, and run your full UI flows to validate signatures, pending transactions, and timeouts in a real public environment.
Key Takeaway

Validate wallets and external services on a public testnet to reduce integration surprises on Mainnet.
