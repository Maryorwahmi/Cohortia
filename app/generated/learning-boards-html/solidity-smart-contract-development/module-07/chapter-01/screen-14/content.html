Deployment readiness hierarchy
Audit
Formal review for contracts handling value.
Gas budgeting
Estimate deployment and recurring transaction costs and include margin for spikes.
Monitoring & response
Prepare alerts, transaction tracing, and a plan for incidents.
Practical tip
Consider a phased rollout and use upgradeable patterns if you need emergency fixes, while understanding their trade-offs and security implications.
Key Takeaway

Operational readiness reduces risk: audit, budget, monitor, and plan for upgrades or response.
