What they are
Public Testnets

Public networks like Sepolia and Goerli mirror Mainnet behavior but use test ETH so you can deploy and test without monetary risk.

Sepolia
Recommended long-term public testnet for staging and integration testing.
Goerli
Operational but being deprecated; avoid starting new projects here.
Concrete example
After passing local tests, deploy the same contract bytecode to Sepolia, fund a test wallet from a faucet, and exercise UI flows to validate wallet interactions and timing under public network conditions.
Common mistake
Assuming testnet gas and miner behavior exactly match Mainnet. Testnets help but do not perfectly replicate all production conditions.
Key Takeaway

Use Sepolia as your staging ground to verify integrations and reduce risk before Mainnet deployment.
