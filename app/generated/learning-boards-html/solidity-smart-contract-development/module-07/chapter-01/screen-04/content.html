Definition
Ethereum Mainnet

The live, public blockchain where transactions use real ETH and contracts are globally visible and immutable.

Use case
Production dApps and assets carrying real value.
Risk
Irreversible cost and exposure to attacks if not fully tested.
Misconception
Treating Mainnet as a testing ground ignores economic incentives and real users; it is not a sandbox.
Concrete example
Deploying a token contract on Mainnet will require ETH for deployment and each token transfer will cost gas. Any error becomes public and costly to fix.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Mainnet is for production. Only deploy there after robust local and testnet verification, gas budgeting, and audits.

Quick safety checklist
  • Use a production wallet with planned funding.
  • Confirm configuration in hardhat.config.js and .env.
  • Run a final audit and gas estimate before deploying.
