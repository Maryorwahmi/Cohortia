Learning Objectives
Four measurable skills to practice and verify as you work through the chapter.
01
Differentiate network types
Explain Mainnet, public testnets, and local networks and why each exists for development and production.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use public testnets safely
Request test ETH from a faucet, deploy contracts, and validate wallet and service integrations without real value at risk.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Run local development networks
Use Hardhat, Ganache, or Anvil for instant confirmations, resettable states, and fast unit testing loops.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Manage gas and deployment safety
Understand gas fees, budget deployments, and prevent accidental Mainnet transactions with separate test wallets and configs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
