Key Concepts
Three concise cards to keep in mind when deploying contracts.
Deployment Script
Automates factory creation, deploy, awaiting confirmation, and logging address and transaction hash.
ContractFactory
A deployer bound to bytecode and ABI; use it to create Contract instances via deploy().
Troubleshooting
Check balance, key, RPC, constructor args, and ensure you awaited deployed() to avoid race conditions.
Summary
Treat deployment scripts as repeatable, verifiable artifacts: write them, run them, log their outputs, and use a checklist to resolve failures.
