Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › What to Remember
Never hardcode secrets
Use .env and dotenv and add .env to .gitignore.
Validate RPC and chainId
Confirm the url is correct and chainId matches the target network.
Isolate keys per environment
Use distinct testnet keys; prefer hardware wallets or managed services for Mainnet.
Final action: pick one of these and apply it to your repo now to harden your deployment posture.
