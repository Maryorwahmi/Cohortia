Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Build the Simple Counter dApp
Starter file highlights
  • Include ethers.js via CDN before your script.
  • Use window.ethereum.request to connect the wallet on button click.
  • Create provider and signer, then instantiate contract with ABI and address.
  • Bind getCount, incrementCounter, and decrementCounter to UI elements.
What to observe
When you open the page with MetaMask on Sepolia, click Connect Wallet and accept the prompt. Click Increment and watch MetaMask ask for confirmation. After confirming, wait for the transaction to be mined and see the count update. Use console logs for debugging but provide friendly on-page status messages for users.
Why this lab matters

It ties together detection, connection, provider/signer creation, contract instantiation, and transaction lifecycle management into a single, testable page.
