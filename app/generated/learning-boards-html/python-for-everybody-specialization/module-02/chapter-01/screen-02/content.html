Learning Objectives
Progress from understanding what a list is to confidently manipulating lists and avoiding common pitfalls.
01
Define lists and mutability
Explain ordered storage and mutable behavior; know why order matters and what mutability allows.
Mnemonic: numbered shelf
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Access via indexing & slicing
Use zero-based indices, negative indices, and slices with start:end:step to retrieve items and subsequences safely.
Mnemonic: start is inclusive, end is exclusive
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Add, remove, and modify elements
Practice append, insert, del, remove, and pop; understand when operations change the list in place versus returning a new list.
Mnemonic: append extends; insert places
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Iterate, search, and avoid common errors
Use loops, enumerate, count, and index safely. Recognize IndexError, ValueError, and pitfalls of modifying while iterating.
Mnemonic: loop over a copy when mutating
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
