Comma rules
(item,) for single-element tuples; () for empty tuple; commas matter more than parentheses.
Immutable container
You cannot assign to tuple indices; the container is fixed, though inner objects may still be mutable.
Pack & Unpack
Use tuple packing for returns and swapping, and unpack to assign elements to named variables for clarity.
Final takeaway

Tuples are a small, powerful tool: learn their syntax, embrace immutability when helpful, and use packing and unpacking to keep your code readable.
