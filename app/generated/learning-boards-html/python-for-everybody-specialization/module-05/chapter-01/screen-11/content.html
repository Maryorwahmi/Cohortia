Comparison

Greedy vs Non-greedy

• Greedy quantifiers try to match as much as possible.
• Non-greedy quantifiers end as soon as the remainder of the pattern can match.
• Use non-greedy when you need the shortest match between delimiters.
Example confusion
When parsing delimited segments, unexpected long matches usually mean your quantifier is greedy; switch to non-greedy and retest.
Quick diagnostic
  1. Check the captured substring length — is it larger than expected?
  2. Look for quantifiers like star or plus adjacent to dots or wide classes.
  3. Try the non-greedy alternative and compare results on sample data.
Pro tip

Testing patterns on representative lines quickly reveals greedy behavior and helps you choose the correct quantifier.
