Definition

Data visualization explained

Visualization maps data to visual properties so trends and anomalies appear immediately. It’s both an exploratory tool and a communication channel.

Concrete example
Monthly visitor numbers in a table are hard to scan. A line plot reveals direction and rate of change at a glance.
Misconception
Visualization is decoration. In reality, the wrong chart type can mislead; choose plots to answer questions, not to beautify data.
Why it matters
Decision-makers rely on clear visuals. A single well-labeled chart can drive action faster than a long table.
Analogy

Maps vs spreadsheets

Think of visualization like a map: it shows spatial relationships at a glance. A spreadsheet is like an address list — useful, but not a map.

Check your understanding
  • What question are you trying to answer with your data?
  • Does your intended plot type show that relationship clearly?
  • Are labels present so others can understand the plot without the raw data?
Key Takeaway

Visualization is a core analysis tool that makes patterns obvious; choose the plot that directly answers your question.
