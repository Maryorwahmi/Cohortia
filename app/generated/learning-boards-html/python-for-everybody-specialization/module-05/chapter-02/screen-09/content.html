Process Flow

From CSV to confident DataFrame

Load with pd.read_csv, then run a short inspection sequence to verify rows, columns, dtypes and missing values.

Quick checklist
  • df = pd.read_csv('file.csv')
  • df.head() to preview rows
  • df.info() to check types and non-null counts
  • df.describe() for numeric summaries
Why it matters
Catching wrong dtypes early prevents downstream calculation errors and confusing results.
