Common mistakes and how to avoid them

Short checklist of frequent issues and practical remedies to keep your analysis correct and reproducible.

Series vs DataFrame
Remember which type a selection returns; shape mismatches often cause downstream errors.
loc vs iloc
Check df.index to choose label or position-based indexing to avoid off-by-one or unexpected slices.
SettingWithCopyWarning
If you intend to modify a filtered subset, use df_filtered = df[mask].copy() before assignment to avoid surprises.
Check dtypes early
Run df.info() to catch numeric columns that were parsed as objects, then clean and convert prior to math.
NaN propagation
When combining Series with different indices, missing labels produce NaN; plan fills or alignments accordingly.
Practical habit
After each major transformation, run head or a quick aggregation to verify the result matches expectations.
