Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Precedence
Evaluation order
Parentheses, exponentiation, multiplication/division/floor/modulo, addition/subtraction. Left-to-right when precedence ties.
Without parentheses
2 + 3 * 4 evaluates multiplication first, producing 14. This follows standard precedence rules.
With parentheses
(2 + 3) * 4 forces addition first, producing 20. Parentheses remove ambiguity and improve readability.
Pro tip
Use parentheses to express logical grouping even when you know the precedence. This makes intent clear for reviewers and future you.
