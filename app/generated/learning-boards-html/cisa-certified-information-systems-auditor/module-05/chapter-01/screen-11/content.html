BCDR Lifecycle Phase 3 & 4

BCDR Program Lifecycle: Risk & Strategy

Assessing threats and developing tailored recovery approaches.

Common Mistake

Adopting generic strategies without tailoring them to specific risks and impacts identified in earlier phases.

Key Takeaway

Effective BCDR strategies are built upon a thorough understanding of risks and tailored to meet specific recovery objectives.

Mid-Stages of the BCDR Lifecycle
03

Risk Assessment

Identify potential threats and vulnerabilities to critical assets, assessing their likelihood and impact to inform mitigation.

04

Strategy Development

Design appropriate recovery strategies based on BIA and risk assessment, including alternate sites, data backup, and communication plans.

These phases translate analysis into actionable recovery plans.
