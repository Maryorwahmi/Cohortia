BCDR Lifecycle Phase 1 & 2

BCDR Program Lifecycle: Policy & BIA

The foundational steps for establishing and understanding an organization's resilience needs.

Common Mistake

Skipping a thorough BIA leads to misaligned recovery objectives and overlooked dependencies.

Key Takeaway

Strong policy and a detailed BIA are crucial for defining the scope and priorities of the entire BCDR program.

First Steps in the BCDR Lifecycle
01

Policy and Program Management

Establish the overarching BCDR policy, assign responsibilities, and secure executive sponsorship. This sets the strategic direction.

02

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identify critical business functions, their dependencies, and the impact of disruption. Define Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs).

These phases lay the groundwork for effective resilience planning.
