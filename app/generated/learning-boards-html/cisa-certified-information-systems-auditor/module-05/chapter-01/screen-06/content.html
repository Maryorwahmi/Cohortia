Definition

Disaster Recovery (DR): Restoring IT Systems

Focusing on the tactical recovery of technology infrastructure and data after a disaster.

Analogy

Like fixing the flat tire itself, getting the original wheel back in working order.

Example Scenario

Restoring servers from backups, reconfiguring network devices, and bringing applications online at an alternate site.

Key Takeaway

DR is a tactical plan for IT recovery, a critical component of the broader BC strategy.

Disaster Recovery (DR) Overview
Primary Objective

Restore IT services to operational state

Scope

IT infrastructure, systems, data

Key Question

"How do we restore the technology that enables the business after a disaster?"
