BC vs. DR: Understanding the Relationship

Differentiating the strategic 'what' from the tactical 'how' in organizational resilience.

BC: The 'What'

Focuses on the overall business, ensuring critical functions continue during disruption.

DR: The 'How'

Focuses on IT, detailing how systems and data will be restored after a disaster.

Key Takeaway

DR is a critical subset of BC; BC is the strategic umbrella, DR is the technical recovery plan.
