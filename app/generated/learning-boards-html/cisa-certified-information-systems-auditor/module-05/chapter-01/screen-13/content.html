Auditor's Responsibilities

The IT Auditor's Role in BCDR Evaluation

Providing independent assurance on BCDR program alignment and effectiveness.

Audit Concern

An outdated BCDR policy (e.g., 5 years old) may not reflect current business processes or technology, making the program ineffective.

Key Takeaway

Auditors are crucial for validating BCDR program effectiveness, ensuring it's integrated with ERM and continuously improved.
