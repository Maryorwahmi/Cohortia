BCDR Lifecycle Phase 5-8

BCDR Program Lifecycle: Plan, Test, Maintain & Train

The ongoing operational phases ensuring BCDR readiness and effectiveness.

Common Mistake

Neglecting ongoing phases, treating BCDR as a one-off project, leads to obsolete and ineffective plans.

Key Takeaway

Continuous testing, maintenance, and training are vital to ensure BCDR plans remain effective and personnel are prepared.

Operational Stages of the BCDR Lifecycle
05

Plan Development

Document detailed procedures for BC and DR, outlining roles, responsibilities, and step-by-step actions for scenarios.

06

Testing and Exercise

Regularly validate plans through various exercises to identify gaps, refine procedures, and ensure personnel readiness.

07

Maintenance and Review

Periodically update plans to reflect changes in the business environment, technology, or regulatory landscape.

08

Training and Awareness

Educate employees on their roles and responsibilities within the BCDR framework to ensure preparedness.
