Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Planning & Lifecycle

Crucial elements for developing and managing BCDR programs.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable amount of data (measured in time) that can be lost from an IT service due to a major incident.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A process that identifies and evaluates the potential effects of an interruption to critical business operations.

BCDR Program Lifecycle

The structured approach to developing, implementing, testing, and maintaining an organization's BCDR capabilities.

Summary Takeaway

RPO, BIA, and the BCDR lifecycle are crucial for comprehensive planning, impact assessment, and continuous improvement of resilience strategies.
