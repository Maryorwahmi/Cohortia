Business Continuity (BC) Overview
Primary Objective

Maintain critical business functions

Scope

Processes, people, facilities, technology

Key Question

"How do we keep the business running, even at reduced capacity, when something goes wrong?"

Definition

Business Continuity (BC): Keeping the Business Running

Understanding the strategic approach to maintaining critical operations during disruption.

Analogy

Like having a spare tire to continue driving after a flat, even if slower.

Example Scenario

If a data center fails, customer service shifts to a remote team using cloud VoIP to continue support.

Key Takeaway

BC is a holistic strategy ensuring the entire organization's critical functions persist, minimizing disruption impact.
