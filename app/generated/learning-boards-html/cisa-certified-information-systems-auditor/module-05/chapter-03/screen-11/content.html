Audit Procedures

Auditing the BCP: Part 1

Reviewing documentation and engaging key personnel

Review BCP Documentation

Assess the plan's structure, content, and alignment with the BIA. Look for clear roles, responsibilities, activation criteria, and comprehensive recovery procedures.

Audit Focus

Verify completeness, clarity, and coverage of all critical business functions.

Interviews with Key Personnel

Speak with CMT members, business unit managers, and BCDR coordinators to assess their understanding of the plan, roles, and confidence in its effectiveness.

Audit Focus

Ascertain understanding, identify training gaps, and gauge confidence in the plan's viability.

Example Scenario

Assessing Recovery Procedures

During documentation review, an auditor finds a recovery procedure for a critical system. They then interview the system owner to confirm their understanding of the steps, required resources, and estimated recovery time, cross-referencing with the documented plan.
