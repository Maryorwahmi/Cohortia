Operational Strategies

Maintaining Operations: Part 1

Practical approaches during a disruption

Manual Workarounds

Temporary manual procedures to sustain operations when automated systems are unavailable (e.g., paper forms for orders).

Audit Focus

Verify documentation, staff understanding, and testing of manual procedures.

Alternate Work Sites

Relocating staff to pre-arranged facilities (hot, warm, or home offices) to continue operations. BCP details logistics and IT connectivity.

Audit Focus

Verify site viability, accessibility, and adequate provisioning for critical functions.

Example Scenario

Order Entry System Failure

If an electronic order entry system fails, a manual workaround might involve customer service representatives taking orders via phone and recording them on pre-printed paper forms, which are then manually processed later. This ensures business continuity for sales.
