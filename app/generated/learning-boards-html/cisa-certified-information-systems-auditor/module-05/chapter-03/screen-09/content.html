Operational Strategies

Maintaining Operations: Part 2

Strategic prioritization and external dependencies

Resource Prioritization

Prioritizing which functions and systems receive resources first during a crisis, guided by the BIA and RTOs.

Audit Focus

Verify clear prioritization, communication, and alignment with business criticality.

Third-Party Agreements

Leveraging SLAs with vendors (cloud, MSPs) for critical services, ensuring alignment with RTOs and RPOs.

Audit Focus

Review contracts to ensure alignment with organizational RTOs/RPOs and adequate support.

Example Scenario

Cloud Service Provider SLA

If a company relies on a cloud provider for its critical e-commerce platform with an RTO of 2 hours, the auditor must verify that the cloud provider's Service Level Agreement (SLA) guarantees restoration within or less than 2 hours, including penalties for non-compliance.
