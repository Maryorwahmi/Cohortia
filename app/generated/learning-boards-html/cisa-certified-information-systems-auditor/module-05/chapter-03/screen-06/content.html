BCP Structure

Essential BCP Components: Part 2

Ensuring operational continuity across the enterprise

04

Business Unit Recovery Plans

Specific procedures for each critical business unit to continue essential functions, including resources, interdependencies, and workarounds.

Audit Focus

Verify practicality, resource allocation, and alignment with BIA.

05

Resource Requirements

Identification of critical resources for recovery: personnel (with cross-training), facilities, equipment, software, and vital records.

Audit Focus

Confirm availability, configuration, and adequacy of all identified resources.

06

Communication Strategy

A detailed plan for internal and external communications during and after a disruption, including pre-approved messages and media relations.

Audit Focus

Evaluate redundancy, clarity, and consistency of communication channels and messages.
