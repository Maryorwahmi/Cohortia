Audit Procedures

Auditing the BCP: Part 3

Ensuring ongoing readiness through training and maintenance

Review Training & Awareness Records

Verify that employees receive adequate BCP training and that awareness programs are conducted regularly. Check attendance and relevance.

Audit Focus

Ensure comprehensive training and awareness programs are in place and effective.

Analysis of BCP Maintenance Procedures

Confirm formal processes for reviewing and updating the BCP periodically, with proper documentation, version control, and approval.

Audit Focus

Verify dynamic processes for continuous improvement and adaptation to changes.

Common Pitfall

Static BCPs

A common pitfall is treating BCPs as static documents. Auditors must ensure there is a dynamic process for continuous improvement, driven by lessons learned from tests, real incidents, or changes in the business environment.
