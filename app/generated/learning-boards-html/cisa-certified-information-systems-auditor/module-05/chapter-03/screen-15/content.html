Chapter Summary

Key Concepts to Remember: Part 2

Essential definitions for Business Continuity Planning

Alternate Work Sites

Pre-arranged locations or facilities where employees can relocate to continue critical operations if their primary workplace is unavailable.

Communication Strategy

The plan for disseminating information to internal staff, customers, stakeholders, and the public during a crisis.

Manual Workarounds

Temporary, non-automated procedures used to maintain essential business functions when automated systems are unavailable.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts represent practical strategies and communication frameworks vital for an organization's operational continuity during disruptions.
