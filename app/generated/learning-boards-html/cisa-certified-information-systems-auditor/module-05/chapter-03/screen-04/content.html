Core Concept

The Business Continuity Plan (BCP)

Your strategic roadmap for resilience during disruption

What is a BCP?

A comprehensive plan guiding an organization through disruptive events, ensuring essential business processes continue to operate until full recovery. It's a strategic roadmap, not just a technical document.

Key Takeaway

A BCP integrates people, processes, facilities, and technology to maintain resilience, extending far beyond IT disaster recovery.

BCP Scope & Purpose
  • •**Strategic Roadmap:** Guides the entire enterprise through disruptions.
  • •**Ensures Continuity:** Maintains essential business processes, even at reduced capacity.
  • •**Holistic Approach:** Integrates people, processes, facilities, and technology.
  • •**Auditor's Role:** Assess robustness, actionability, and alignment with risk appetite and regulations.
Common Misconception

A Poor Plan is Worse Than None

A poorly constructed BCP can create a false sense of security, leading to confusion and chaos during an actual incident, potentially worsening the outcome. It's crucial for plans to be well-defined and tested.
