Audit Procedures

Auditing the BCP: Part 2

Verifying resources and communication readiness

Verification of Resource Availability

Confirm that identified alternate sites, equipment, and personnel are available and configured as stated in the plan (e.g., site visits, contract reviews).

Audit Focus

Ensure resources are genuinely accessible, functional, and align with BCP requirements.

Assessment of Communication Strategy

Evaluate internal and external communication plans, including contact lists, notification systems, and media protocols. Ensure redundancy in channels.

Audit Focus

Verify multiple communication channels and consistent messaging to prevent breakdowns.

Example Scenario

Alternate Site Visit

An auditor conducts a physical visit to a declared 'warm site' to confirm that essential equipment (desks, computers, network connectivity) is present, functional, and sufficient to support the number of staff designated to relocate there during an incident.
