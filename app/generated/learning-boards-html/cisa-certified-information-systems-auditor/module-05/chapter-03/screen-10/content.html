Critical Factors

Crisis Management & Human Factors

Beyond technology: people, communication, and decision-making

The Human Element

A BCP must address staff well-being, communication, and decision-making under stress. Neglecting these can undermine even the best technical plans.

Key Takeaway

Effective crisis management relies on clear authority, established decision protocols, and robust communication, not just technical recovery.

Crisis Management Team (CMT) Focus
  • •**Clear Authority:** Defined decision-making power during a crisis.
  • •**Established Protocols:** Pre-defined procedures for incident response.
  • •**Robust Communication:** Strategy for internal and external messaging.
  • •**Auditor's Verification:** Look for CMT training, meeting schedules, and chain of command.
Critical Safety Note

Off-site Contact Information

Ensure emergency contact information is up-to-date and stored off-site, not solely within systems that might be unavailable during a disaster. This prevents critical communication failures.
