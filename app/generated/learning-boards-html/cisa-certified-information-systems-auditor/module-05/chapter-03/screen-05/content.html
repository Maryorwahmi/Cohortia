BCP Structure

Essential BCP Components: Part 1

Laying the foundation for effective crisis response

01

Executive Summary & Scope

High-level overview defining the plan's purpose, scope, objectives, and identifying the authority (e.g., Board approval).

Audit Focus

Verify executive approval and clear definition of scope.

02

Activation & Notification Procedures

Detailed steps for declaring a disaster, escalating incidents, and notifying key personnel, stakeholders, and external parties (e.g., call trees, contact lists).

Audit Focus

Assess clarity, completeness, and redundancy of communication channels.

03

Crisis Management Team (CMT)

Identifies CMT members, their roles, responsibilities, and decision-making authority for overall incident coordination and strategic decisions.

Audit Focus

Verify clear roles, responsibilities, and decision-making protocols.
