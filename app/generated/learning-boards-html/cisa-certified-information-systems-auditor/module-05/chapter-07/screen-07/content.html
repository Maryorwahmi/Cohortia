Payment and Critical Infrastructure

Securing Cardholder Data and Critical Operations

PCI DSS and Other Sector-Specific BCDR Requirements

PCI DSS: Cardholder Data Environment

  • • **Scope:** Applies to entities processing credit card data.
  • • **Requirement:** Maintain a BCDR plan for continuity of Cardholder Data Environment (CDE) operations.
  • • **Key Aspect:** Regular testing of recovery plans is mandatory.
  • • **Auditor Focus:** Verify testing procedures, documentation, and gap remediation.

Industry-Specific Regulations

Financial Services

Regulations like Basel III and Dodd-Frank Act impose stringent BCDR requirements.

Telecommunications

Often require mandatory testing and reporting for network continuity.

Critical Infrastructure

Sectors like energy and water have specific resilience and recovery mandates.

Key Takeaway

BCDR plans must align with industry-specific regulations like PCI DSS and other sector mandates to ensure specialized data protection and operational continuity.
