Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter

GDPR

EU law on data protection and privacy, impacting BCDR through requirements for data availability, integrity, and rapid restoration of personal data.

HIPAA

US law requiring healthcare entities to protect patient health information, including BCDR for ePHI.

SOX

US law requiring internal controls over financial reporting, indirectly supported by BCDR.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding these core regulations is fundamental for ensuring BCDR plans are legally compliant and effective in protecting sensitive data and financial integrity.
