Regulatory Deep Dive

GDPR: Protecting Personal Data in BCDR

Ensuring Data Availability, Integrity, and Resilience for EU Citizens

Key GDPR Requirements for BCDR

  • • **Article 32:** Mandates data protection by design and default, including availability, integrity, and resilience.
  • • **Rapid Restoration:** BCDR plans must ensure quick recovery of personal data post-incident.
  • • **Data Minimization:** Back up only data that is truly necessary.
  • • **Pseudonymization:** Implement measures to process data without direct identification.
  • • **Secure Deletion:** Ensure mechanisms for secure and permanent data removal.
  • • **Data Subject Rights:** Maintain rights (e.g., erasure) even in backup copies and during recovery.

Auditor's Focus for GDPR Compliance

Backup Procedures

Verify data minimization and pseudonymization are applied to backups.

Recovery Processes

Ensure rapid restoration of personal data and secure deletion mechanisms.

Data Subject Rights

Verify mechanisms to address rights like erasure even in backup copies.

Key Takeaway

GDPR mandates that BCDR plans prioritize the rapid and secure restoration of personal data, upholding data protection principles throughout the recovery lifecycle.
