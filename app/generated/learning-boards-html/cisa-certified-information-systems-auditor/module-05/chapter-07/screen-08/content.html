Meeting Service Level Agreements

The Impact of SLAs, RTOs, and RPOs on BCDR Planning

Understanding Contractual Obligations

  • • **Service Level Agreements (SLAs):** Define uptime guarantees, RTOs, and RPOs.
  • • **Recovery Time Objective (RTO):** Maximum tolerable downtime for a system or service.
  • • **Recovery Point Objective (RPO):** Maximum tolerable data loss measured in time.
  • • **Consequences:** Failure to meet these can lead to financial penalties, contract termination, and reputational damage.

Auditor's Role in Contractual Compliance

Contract Review

Review SLAs and other contracts to identify BCDR-related commitments.

Capability Alignment

Ensure BCDR plans and capabilities align with contractual RTOs and RPOs.

Verification

Verify technical and procedural means to achieve contractual commitments.

Key Takeaway

Contractual obligations, especially SLAs, are critical BCDR drivers; auditors must verify that recovery capabilities meet these binding commitments.
