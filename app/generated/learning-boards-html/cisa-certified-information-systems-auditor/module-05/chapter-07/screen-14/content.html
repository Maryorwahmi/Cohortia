Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter

PCI DSS

Standard for organizations handling credit card data, including BCDR requirements for the CDE.

Contractual Obligations

BCDR requirements stipulated in agreements with customers, vendors, and partners (e.g., SLAs).

Data Residency

The physical location where data is stored, a critical consideration for compliance with data privacy laws.

Ethical Considerations

Principles guiding BCDR decisions, such as fairness, transparency, and accountability, especially during resource prioritization and communication in a crisis.

Summary Takeaway

A robust BCDR program integrates technical resilience with strict adherence to contractual obligations, data residency rules, and ethical principles for responsible crisis management.
