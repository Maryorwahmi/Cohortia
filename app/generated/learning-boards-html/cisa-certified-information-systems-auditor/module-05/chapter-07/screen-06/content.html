Sector-Specific Mandates

Healthcare and Financial Reporting Compliance

HIPAA's Contingency Plans and SOX's Internal Controls

HIPAA: Protecting ePHI

  • • **Security Rule:** Requires covered entities to have a contingency plan.
  • • **Plan Components:** Data backup, disaster recovery, and emergency mode operation plans.
  • • **Objective:** Ensure availability and integrity of electronic protected health information (ePHI).
  • • **Auditor Focus:** Verify documentation, testing, and specific ePHI addressing.

SOX: Supporting Financial Integrity

Internal Controls

Requires publicly traded companies to maintain controls over financial reporting.

BCDR's Indirect Role

Ensures continuity and integrity of systems and data for financial reporting.

Auditor's Assessment

Evaluate how BCDR controls contribute to SOX Section 404 effectiveness.

Key Takeaway

HIPAA and SOX demonstrate how BCDR is essential for sector-specific compliance, protecting sensitive data and ensuring the integrity of critical business processes.
