Ethical BCDR

Navigating Ethical Dilemmas in Disaster Recovery

Prioritization, Transparency, and Avoiding New Privacy Risks

Core Ethical Principles in BCDR

  • • **Fairness:** Prioritize recovery based on pre-defined, justifiable criteria, not ad-hoc decisions.
  • • **Transparency:** Communicate honestly and openly during a crisis, avoiding misinformation.
  • • **Accountability:** Ensure clear responsibility for decisions made during a disaster.
  • • **Privacy Protection:** Avoid creating new privacy risks during recovery (e.g., insecure environments).

Auditor's Ethical Assessment

Prioritization Framework

Assess if the BCDR plan includes ethically justifiable criteria for resource allocation.

Communication Protocols

Verify plans for honest and transparent communication during a crisis.

Privacy Risk Mitigation

Ensure BCDR plans do not inadvertently introduce new privacy vulnerabilities.

Key Takeaway

Ethical considerations are vital in BCDR, guiding fair prioritization, transparent communication, and proactive measures to prevent new privacy risks during crisis recovery.
