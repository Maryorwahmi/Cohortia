CISA Audit Focus

Assessing BCDR Compliance: The Auditor's Mandate

From Policy Review to Third-Party Assurance

The Auditor's Multifaceted Role

  • • **Identify Landscape:** Pinpoint all applicable laws, regulations, and contracts.
  • • **Review Policies:** Examine BCDR policies and procedures for explicit compliance.
  • • **Verify Implementation:** Confirm compliance requirements are actively put into practice.
  • • **Analyze Test Results:** Ensure BCDR tests validate compliance scenarios.
  • • **Assess Third-Party Assurance:** Review vendor audit reports (e.g., SOC 2) for BCDR controls.

Example: Verifying GDPR Compliance

Policy Check

Does the DRP explicitly state compliance with GDPR Article 32?

Evidence Review

Review backup encryption logs and data residency attestations.

Test Validation

Confirm tests include scenarios for restoring regulated data and notification timelines.

Key Takeaway

The auditor's role is to systematically review, verify, and validate all aspects of BCDR to ensure full compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
