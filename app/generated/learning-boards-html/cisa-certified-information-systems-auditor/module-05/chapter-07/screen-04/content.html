Core Concept

Beyond Technical Recovery

Understanding the Broader Impact of BCDR Compliance

The Compliance Imperative

  • • Organizations operate within a complex web of legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
  • • These mandates profoundly impact BCDR plan design, implementation, and auditing.
  • • Non-compliance can lead to severe penalties, reputational damage, and legal liabilities.
  • • CISA auditors must ensure BCDR programs meet both technical and compliance standards.

Consequences of Non-Compliance

Legal Penalties

Fines and legal action for violating data privacy or industry-specific laws.

Reputational Damage

Loss of customer trust and public image due to compliance failures.

Financial Liabilities

Compensation, legal fees, and operational costs from non-compliance.

Operational Disruption

Extended recovery times or inability to resume critical functions.

Key Takeaway

BCDR is a holistic discipline; technical recovery must be seamlessly integrated with legal, regulatory, and ethical compliance to ensure true organizational resilience.
