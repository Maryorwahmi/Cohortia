Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Disaster Recovery Planning

Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

A detailed plan for restoring IT systems, applications, and data after a disruptive event.

Hot Site

A fully equipped, operational off-site data center that can take over operations immediately.

Warm Site

A partially equipped off-site data center with basic infrastructure, requiring some setup and data loading.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the DRP and various recovery site options is crucial for aligning IT recovery strategies with an organization's RTOs and budget.
