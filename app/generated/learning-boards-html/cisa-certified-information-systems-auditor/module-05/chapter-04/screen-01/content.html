Chapter 5.4 Overview

Developing and Auditing the Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

Safeguarding IT Infrastructure and Data Against Disruptive Events

The Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) is the technological counterpart to the Business Continuity Plan (BCP), detailing specific steps to restore IT infrastructure, systems, and data after a disruptive event. As a CISA auditor, your role is to ensure the DRP is a living, actionable plan that can genuinely bring critical IT services back online within defined Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs).

Key Idea

The DRP is a technical plan to restore IT infrastructure, systems, and data after a disaster, ensuring alignment with RTOs and RPOs.
