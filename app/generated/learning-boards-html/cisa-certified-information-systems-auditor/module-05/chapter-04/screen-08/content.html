Recovery Strategy

Warm and Cold Sites

Balancing Cost and Recovery Time Objectives

Warm Site: Partial Readiness

  • •Partially equipped facility with basic infrastructure.
  • •Requires additional setup and data loading.
  • •Moderate RTO (hours to a day).
  • •Lower cost than a hot site.

Cold Site: Basic Facility

  • •Basic facility with power, cooling, network, but no hardware/software.
  • •Requires significant time for procurement and installation.
  • •Highest RTO (days to weeks).
  • •Least expensive option.

Example: Small Retailer vs. Archival Agency

A small retailer with an RTO of 12 hours might opt for a warm site. In contrast, a government archival agency with an RTO of 72 hours, prioritizing long-term data retention over immediate operational urgency, could effectively use a cold site.

Auditor's Focus: RTO Alignment

Auditors must critically evaluate if the chosen site type genuinely supports the organization's defined RTOs. A cold site for a 4-hour RTO, for instance, is a critical mismatch and a significant audit concern.

Key Takeaway

Warm and cold sites offer cost-effective recovery, but auditors must ensure their RTO capabilities align precisely with business requirements to avoid critical recovery failures.
