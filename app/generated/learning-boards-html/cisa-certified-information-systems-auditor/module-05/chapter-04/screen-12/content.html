Data Resilience

Data Replication and Snapshots

Advanced Strategies for Low RPO and Quick Recovery

Data Replication: Near Real-Time

  • •Real-time or near real-time copying to a secondary location.
  • •Synchronous (zero data loss, higher latency) or Asynchronous (minimal data loss, lower latency).
  • •Ideal for very low RPOs.

Snapshots: Point-in-Time Copies

  • •Point-in-time copies of a system's state.
  • •Useful for quick recovery from minor issues.
  • •Not a substitute for off-site backups for disaster recovery.

Auditor's Focus: Backup Security & Testing

Auditors must verify that backup media are stored securely off-site, regularly tested for restorability, and encrypted. They also ensure backup administrators follow the principle of least privilege and that backup systems are hardened against cyber threats.

Common Mistake: Untested Backups

A critical error is failing to test backups. Many organizations discover their backups are corrupted or incomplete only during an actual disaster, rendering their recovery efforts futile. Regular restore tests are non-negotiable.

Key Takeaway

Replication and snapshots enable rapid recovery for low RPOs, but comprehensive data resilience requires secure, off-site, encrypted, and regularly tested backups.
