Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Data Recovery and Auditing

Cold Site

A basic facility with power and cooling, requiring significant time to procure and install hardware and software.

Cloud Recovery

Utilizing cloud infrastructure (public or private) for system and data replication and recovery.

Full Backup

A complete copy of all selected data.

Summary Takeaway

Effective DRP auditing requires a deep understanding of various recovery site types, cloud considerations, and diverse backup strategies to ensure data resilience.
