Recovery Strategy

Choosing the Right Recovery Site

Balancing RTO, RPO, and Cost Considerations

Recovery Site Importance

  • •Directly impacts Recovery Time Objective (RTO).
  • •Auditors must assess alignment with RTOs and budget.
  • •A mismatch can lead to recovery failure or excessive costs.

Hot Site: Immediate Readiness

  • •Fully equipped, operational data center.
  • •Hardware, software, network connectivity ready.
  • •Lowest RTO (minutes to a few hours).
  • •Most expensive option.

Example: Global Financial Trading Firm

A firm with an RTO of 15 minutes for its trading platform would require a hot site. Any downtime or data loss leads to immediate, severe financial and reputational damage, justifying the high cost for near-instant recovery.

Consideration: Cost vs. RTO

While a hot site offers the fastest recovery, its high operational cost means it's typically reserved for the most mission-critical systems where even minutes of downtime are unacceptable. Auditors must verify this trade-off is justified.

Key Takeaway

Hot sites provide the fastest recovery for critical systems but come with the highest cost, demanding careful alignment with RTOs and budget.
