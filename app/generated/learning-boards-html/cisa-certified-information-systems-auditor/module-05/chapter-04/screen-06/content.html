DRP Structure

Components of a Comprehensive DRP (Part 2)

Procedures, Sites, Testing, and Vendor Management

5. Recovery Procedures

Step-by-step instructions for recovering each critical system, including hardware, software, network, data, and application testing. These must be highly granular.

6. Backup and Restoration Procedures

Details on backup schedules, storage locations (on-site, off-site), media, retention policies, and specific data restoration steps. Essential for RPO adherence.

7. Recovery Site Information

Details about the chosen recovery site(s), including physical location, access procedures, infrastructure, and connectivity. Ensures readiness and accessibility.

8. Testing and Maintenance

Procedures for regularly testing the DRP and updating it to reflect changes in the IT environment. An untested plan is a significant risk.

9. Vendor and Third-Party Contacts

List of critical vendors (e.g., hardware, ISPs, cloud providers) and their emergency contact information. Crucial for external dependencies.

Key Takeaway

A DRP's operational strength comes from detailed recovery steps, robust backup plans, clear site information, consistent testing, and accessible vendor contacts.
