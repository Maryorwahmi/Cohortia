Auditor's Role

Evaluating Integrated Policies and Communication

Assessing the consistency and clarity of response plans and communication channels.

Review Policies & Procedures

Ensure IR, BCDR, and emergency management policies are consistent, mutually supportive, and define clear escalation paths.

Interview Key Personnel

Confirm understanding of integrated approach and roles with IR leads, BCDR coordinators, and emergency management staff.

Assess Communication Channels

Verify robust, redundant channels for internal teams and external stakeholders during all phases of an incident or emergency.

Verify Escalation Paths

Confirm that trigger points for escalating from IR to BCDR are clearly defined and understood, preventing delays.

Common Oversight

Separate communication plans for IR, BC, and DR that are not aligned. This leads to confusion and delays during a crisis, hindering a coordinated response.

Key Takeaway

Auditors play a crucial role in ensuring that an organization's resilience policies and communication strategies are integrated and clearly understood across all levels.

The Auditor's Lens

Auditors provide assurance by verifying that the organization's documented plans translate into effective, coordinated action. This involves:

  • Identifying inconsistencies in policies.
  • Confirming personnel awareness.
  • Assessing communication effectiveness.
