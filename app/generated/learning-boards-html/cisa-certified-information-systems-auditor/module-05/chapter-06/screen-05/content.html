Incident Response: Immediate Tactical Reaction

IR focuses on the immediate actions taken to identify, contain, eradicate, and recover from security incidents or specific operational failures.

What is Incident Response (IR)?

  • •Immediate, tactical reaction to security incidents (e.g., malware, data breach) or operational failures (e.g., server crash).
  • •Scope is typically narrower and more immediate than Business Continuity (BC) or Disaster Recovery (DR).
  • •Primary goal: Minimize damage, eradicate the threat, and restore normal operations quickly.
Key Takeaway

Incident Response is the immediate, tactical first line of defense against specific security incidents or operational failures, aiming to minimize damage and restore operations quickly.

IR in Action: Server Crash

A critical server experiences an unexpected crash. The IR team immediately isolates the server, diagnoses the root cause (e.g., faulty patch), and works to restore it from a recent backup or rollback the change. Their focus is on the specific server and the immediate incident.

Misconception: IR vs. BCDR

IR is often confused with BCDR. While related, IR is about the immediate, tactical response to a specific event, whereas BCDR deals with broader, sustained disruptions affecting entire business functions or IT infrastructure.
