Chapter Summary

Essential Concepts for Incident Response

Key definitions and frameworks to remember for effective incident and emergency management.

Incident Response (IR)

Immediate actions to identify, contain, eradicate, and recover from a security incident or operational event.

IR Lifecycle

Structured phases of incident response: Preparation, Detection & Analysis, Containment/Eradication/Recovery, and Post-Incident Activity.

Emergency Management

Broader framework for prevention, preparedness, response, and recovery for all types of emergencies, often with external coordination.

Summary Takeaway

Incident Response, Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, and Emergency Management are synergistic disciplines that form a comprehensive organizational resilience strategy, requiring seamless integration and continuous improvement.
