Incident Response Lifecycle: Preparation and Detection

The IR lifecycle follows a structured approach, starting with proactive preparation and continuous detection and analysis.

Phase 1

Preparation

  • •Develop policies, plans, and procedures for incident handling.
  • •Establish an IR team and acquire necessary tools (SIEM, EDR).
  • •Conduct regular training and awareness programs.
Phase 2

Detection and Analysis

  • •Monitor systems for anomalies and identify potential incidents.
  • •Analyze scope, impact, and root cause of detected events.
  • •Utilize tools like IDS, SIEM, and threat intelligence feeds.
Key Takeaway

A robust IR program is built on thorough preparation and relies on effective detection and analysis to identify and understand incidents early, enabling timely response.
