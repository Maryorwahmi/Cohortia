Emergency Management Frameworks

Coordinating a comprehensive response to all types of major disruptions, beyond IT.

Prevention/Mitigation

Actions to reduce the likelihood or impact of an emergency (e.g., structural reinforcement, security upgrades).

Preparedness

Developing plans, training, and resources for effective response (e.g., emergency drills, supply stockpiles).

Response

Actions taken during an emergency to save lives, protect property, and meet basic needs (e.g., evacuation, first aid).

Recovery

Actions to restore normal operations and conditions after an emergency (e.g., rebuilding infrastructure, psychological support).

Real-World Connection

A regional power outage impacts both IT systems and physical facilities. An emergency management framework dictates how the IT DR team, BC team, and facilities management coordinate efforts and communicate with external responders like utility companies.

Key Takeaway

Emergency management frameworks ensure a coordinated, multi-faceted response to any major disruption, integrating IT-specific plans with broader organizational and external efforts.

Integrating with BCDR & IR

Auditors assess how BCDR and IR plans fit into this larger framework, looking for:

  • Consistent communication protocols.
  • Coordinated efforts across teams.
  • Clear lines of authority.
  • Integration with external responders.
