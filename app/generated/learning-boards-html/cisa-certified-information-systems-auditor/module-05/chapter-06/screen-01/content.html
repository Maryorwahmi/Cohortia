Incident Response and Emergency Management

Building a Resilient Defense Against Disruptions

In this chapter, you will learn how Incident Response (IR), Business Continuity (BC), and Disaster Recovery (DR) plans must integrate to form a comprehensive resilience strategy. We'll explore the structured phases of incident response and the broader scope of emergency management, preparing you to audit these critical organizational capabilities.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive organizational resilience requires a synergistic and well-integrated strategy encompassing Incident Response, Business Continuity, and Disaster Recovery.
