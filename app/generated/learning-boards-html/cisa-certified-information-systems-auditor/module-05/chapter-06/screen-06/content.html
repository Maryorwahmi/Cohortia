Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery: Sustaining Operations

BC and DR are activated when an incident escalates, focusing on maintaining essential business functions and restoring IT systems.

Business Continuity (BC)

  • •Activated when normal operations cannot be maintained due to an incident.
  • •Focuses on keeping essential business functions running, potentially at alternate locations or with manual workarounds.
  • •Goal: Maintain critical services and processes to minimize business impact.
Key Takeaway

BC ensures essential business functions continue during a disruption, while DR focuses on restoring the underlying IT systems and data after a major outage.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

  • •The technical component of BC, specifically dealing with IT systems and data restoration.
  • •Involves restoring servers, networks, applications, and data from backups after a major outage.
  • •Goal: Bring IT infrastructure back online to support business operations.

Example: Data Center Outage

A regional power outage impacts a primary data center. The BC plan activates to shift critical business processes to a secondary location or manual operations. Simultaneously, the DR plan focuses on restoring IT systems at a designated recovery site from backups.
