The Integrated Resilience Strategy

Incident Response (IR), Business Continuity (BC), and Disaster Recovery (DR) are not isolated functions but interconnected components of a holistic resilience framework.

Synergistic Relationship

These three disciplines work together, each supporting the others to ensure comprehensive organizational resilience against various disruptions.

Common Mistake: Siloed Approach

Treating IR, BC, and DR as separate entities leads to confusion, delays, and conflicting priorities during an actual incident, hindering effective response.

Key Takeaway

Effective organizational resilience hinges on the seamless integration of IR, BC, and DR plans, avoiding siloed approaches that lead to confusion and delays.
