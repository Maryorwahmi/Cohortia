Auditor's Role

Assessing Integration Through Testing and Compliance

Verifying that IR, BCDR, and emergency management plans work together effectively under pressure.

Review Test Scenarios

Examine BCDR and IR test scenarios to ensure they simulate realistic threats and integrated escalation paths.

Evaluate Post-Incident Reviews

Review reports from actual incidents or exercises to assess collaboration, challenges, and lessons learned.

Verify Regulatory Compliance

Ensure incident reporting requirements (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA) are integrated into IR plans and fulfilled.

Holistic Assurance

Provide assurance that the organization is prepared for a full spectrum of disruptions with integrated response capabilities.

Integrated Test Scenario Example

A test begins with a cyberattack (IR) that escalates to system failover (DR) and business process continuity (BC), demonstrating the seamless transition between plans.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must go beyond documentation, verifying through testing and compliance that integrated response plans are effective and resilient in practice.

The Value of Integrated Testing

Integrated testing reveals critical gaps that individual plan tests might miss. It ensures:

  • Smooth escalation between plans.
  • Coordinated team efforts.
  • Accurate RTO/RPO validation.
  • Effective communication under stress.
