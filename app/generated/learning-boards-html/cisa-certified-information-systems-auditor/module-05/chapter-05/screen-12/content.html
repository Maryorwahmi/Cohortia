BCDR Training and Awareness Programs

Tailoring Education to Ensure Everyone Knows Their Role in a Crisis

Why Training is Critical
  • •Perfect plans are useless if personnel lack knowledge.
  • •Ensures personnel readiness and effective plan execution.
  • •Reduces confusion and panic during actual disruptions.
Tailored Audiences
  • •Executive Management: Strategic oversight, decision-making.
  • •Crisis Management Team: Incident command, communication.
  • •IT DR Team: Hands-on technical recovery procedures.
  • •Business Unit Staff: Workarounds, alternate site procedures.
  • •General Employees: Basic emergency awareness.
Key Takeaway

Effective BCDR training is a continuous process, tailored to specific roles, and essential for transforming theoretical plans into actionable resilience.

Training Program Example

Annual BCDR Training Schedule

Q1

Executive Briefing: Strategic decision-making, communication protocols. (Awareness)

Q2

IT DR Team Workshop: Hands-on system restoration, network configuration. (Technical)

Q3

Business Unit Drills: Manual workarounds, alternate site procedures. (Procedural)

Q4

All-Staff Awareness: Emergency procedures, communication channels. (General)

Auditor's Check

Verify regular training, effectiveness assessment (quizzes/drills), documented participation, and cross-training for critical personnel.
