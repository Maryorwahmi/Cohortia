Walkthrough Exercises: The Foundation of BCDR Testing

Verbally Reviewing Plans to Identify Logical Flaws and Familiarize Personnel

Key Characteristics
  • •Verbal review of BCP/DRP steps.
  • •No actual systems activated.
  • •Focus on discussion, roles, and procedures.
Benefits
  • •Familiarizes staff with the plan.
  • •Identifies logical flaws and inconsistencies.
  • •Improves communication and coordination.
Key Takeaway

Walkthroughs are essential for initial plan validation and team alignment, serving as a cost-effective first step in a comprehensive testing strategy.

Tabletop Exercise Scenario

Discussing a Data Center Outage

1

Scenario Introduction: Power outage at primary data center. Discuss immediate response steps.

2

Team Roles & Responsibilities: Each team (IT, Business, Comms) outlines their initial actions based on the BCP.

3

Identify Gaps: Discussion reveals conflicting contact lists and an unclear decision-making matrix for failover.

4

Action Items: Documented tasks to update contact lists, clarify roles, and schedule follow-up training.

Auditor's Check

Verify clear objectives, documented attendance, and a report detailing issues and action items from the walkthrough.
