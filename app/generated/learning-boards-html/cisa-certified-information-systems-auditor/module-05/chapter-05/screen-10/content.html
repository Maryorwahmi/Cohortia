Interpreting Test Results: Beyond Just "Passing"

Extracting Actionable Insights to Drive Continuous Improvement

Auditor's Focus Areas
  • •Were defined RTOs/RPOs achieved?
  • •Were critical business functions maintained?
  • •Were deficiencies in plan/resources/training identified?
  • •Was a post-test review conducted and documented?
  • •Were corrective actions assigned, tracked, and verified?
Common Mistake

Identifying issues during a test but failing to implement the necessary changes, rendering the test ineffective and providing a false sense of security.

Key Takeaway

The true value of BCDR testing comes from a rigorous post-test analysis that leads to documented lessons learned and verified corrective actions, ensuring continuous improvement.

BCDR Test Report Analysis

Online Banking System Failover - Key Findings

1

Database failover completed in 1.5 hours, meeting RTO.

2

Application server provisioning took 3.75 hours, exceeding 2-hour target.

3

Network connectivity to payment gateway failed due to incorrect firewall rule.

4

Business users unable to log in for 45 mins due to unannounced SSO change.

5

Communication log shows 20-minute delay in notifying business unit.

Auditor's Conclusion

Despite meeting the overall RTO, several critical gaps were identified, indicating a need for significant plan updates and coordination improvements.
