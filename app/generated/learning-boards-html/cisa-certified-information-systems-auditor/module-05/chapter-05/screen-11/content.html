BCDR Plan Maintenance: Staying Current

Adapting to Evolving Business, Technology, and Regulatory Landscapes

Why Maintenance Matters
  • •Business environment, technology, and regulations constantly evolve.
  • •Static plans quickly become obsolete and ineffective.
  • •Ensures the plan reflects current organizational reality.
Key Processes
  • •Periodic Review & Update: At least annually or upon significant changes.
  • •Change Management Integration: BCDR plan review for all critical IT/business changes.
  • •Documentation Control: Version control, change logs, and formal approvals.
Key Takeaway

Continuous BCDR plan maintenance is essential to ensure its ongoing relevance, accuracy, and effectiveness in a dynamic operational landscape.

Maintenance Scenario

Cloud Migration Impact on DRP

1

Change Event: Critical application migrates from on-premise servers to a new cloud provider.

2

Trigger: Change management process flags this as a BCDR-relevant change.

3

DRP Review: DRP is reviewed to assess impact on recovery procedures, responsibilities (shared responsibility model), and RTO/RPO.

4

Update & Approval: DRP updated with new cloud-specific recovery steps, contact info, and formally approved.

Auditor's Check

Verify formal processes for periodic review, integration with change management, and robust documentation control for BCDR plans.
