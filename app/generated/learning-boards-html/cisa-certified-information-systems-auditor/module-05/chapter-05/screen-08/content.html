Full Interruption Tests: The Ultimate Validation

End-to-End Failover to a Recovery Site for Comprehensive Assurance

Key Characteristics
  • •Actual failover of production systems to recovery site.
  • •Operations run from the alternate location.
  • •Most comprehensive and disruptive test type.
Benefits & Challenges
  • •Validates entire BCDR process end-to-end.
  • •Confirms technical recovery and business continuity.
  • •Requires meticulous planning due to high disruption risk.
Key Takeaway

Full interruption tests offer the highest assurance but demand rigorous planning and auditor oversight to manage inherent risks and validate true resilience.

Live Test Scenario

Online Banking System Failover

1

Objective: Failover online banking to DR site, operate for 4 hours, meet 4-hour RTO.

2

Execution: Production traffic redirected. Business users access services from DR site.

3

Findings: Failover completed in 3.5 hours (met RTO). Minor performance degradation observed. Communication delays to business units.

4

Action: Optimize DR site performance, refine communication protocols, schedule re-test.

Auditor's Scrutiny

Scrutinize test plan, observed performance against RTO/RPO, and the post-mortem analysis for comprehensive assurance.
