The Auditor's Role: Evaluating BCDR Program Adequacy

Providing Assurance Through Comprehensive Review of Testing, Maintenance, and Training

Key Audit Responsibilities
  • •Review BCDR test plans, reports, and outcomes against RTO/RPO.
  • •Examine evidence of corrective actions from identified deficiencies.
  • •Assess formal maintenance procedures and change management integration.
  • •Review BCDR training records and awareness programs.
  • •Interview personnel to gauge understanding and confidence.
Audit Objective

To provide assurance that the BCDR program is practical, current, and executable, not merely theoretical.

Key Takeaway

A CISA auditor's comprehensive evaluation of BCDR testing, maintenance, and training is crucial for validating an organization's true resilience and readiness.

Auditor's Evaluation Framework

Ensuring BCDR Program Effectiveness

1

Test Validity: Are tests comprehensive, relevant, and do they measure RTO/RPO?

2

Actionable Outcomes: Are test findings leading to documented and implemented corrective actions?

3

Plan Currency: Are BCDR plans regularly reviewed, updated, and integrated with change management?

4

Personnel Preparedness: Is training adequate, regular, and does it cover all critical roles?

Audit Opinion

Based on these evaluations, the auditor forms an opinion on the overall adequacy and effectiveness of the organization's BCDR program.
