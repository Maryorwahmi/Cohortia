Key Concepts

Essential Terms for BCDR Readiness

BCDR Testing

The systematic process of validating BCDR plans and ensuring personnel readiness.

Walkthrough (Tabletop)

A discussion-based test where participants verbally review the BCDR plan step-by-step.

Simulation Exercise

A test where participants execute parts of the plan in a simulated environment without impacting live operations.

Summary Takeaway

These initial concepts define the foundational approaches to validating BCDR plans and preparing teams for potential disruptions.
