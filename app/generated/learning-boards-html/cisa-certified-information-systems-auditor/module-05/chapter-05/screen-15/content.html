Key Concepts

Critical Elements of BCDR Program Management

Full Interruption Test

The most comprehensive test, involving actual failover to a recovery site and operation from the alternate location.

BCDR Maintenance

The ongoing process of reviewing, updating, and improving BCDR plans and documentation.

BCDR Training

Educating employees on their roles, responsibilities, and procedures within the BCDR framework.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts emphasize the continuous effort required to maintain an effective and resilient BCDR program, ensuring sustained operational readiness.
