Audit Procedures

Evaluating BIA and Risk Assessment Processes

Verifying documentation, stakeholder approval, and regular updates.

Auditor's Evaluation Methods

  • •Documentation Review: Examine BIA reports, risk registers, and BCDR plans for completeness and accuracy.
  • •Personnel Interviews: Engage business owners, IT managers, and BCDR coordinators to confirm understanding and application.
  • •Data Verification: Cross-reference information to ensure consistency and support for identified impacts and objectives.

Key Audit Considerations

  • •Formal Approval: Ensure BIA and risk assessment are formally approved by relevant stakeholders.
  • •Regular Review & Update: Verify processes are updated annually or after significant organizational changes.
  • •Alignment with BCDR: Confirm BIA outputs (RTOs, RPOs) directly feed into BCDR strategy development.
Key Takeaway

Auditors play a vital role in ensuring BIA and risk assessment processes are robust, current, and effectively integrated into BCDR strategies, safeguarding organizational resilience.
