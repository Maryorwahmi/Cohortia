Process Flow

Mapping Business Functions and Processes

The initial step: understanding the organizational landscape.

01

Identify All Functions

List all business functions and processes, from core operations to support activities. This includes everything that contributes to the organization's mission.

02

Engage Stakeholders

Conduct interviews with department heads, managers, and subject matter experts. Their insights are crucial for accurate identification.

03

Document Dependencies

Map interdependencies between functions and processes. Understand how the disruption of one might affect others.

04

Verify Completeness

Ensure all critical and supporting functions are captured. A common mistake is overlooking non-IT related processes.

Why this step matters

This initial mapping provides the essential context for all subsequent BIA activities. Without a clear and accurate understanding of these functions, any analysis of impact or recovery planning will be built on a shaky foundation, potentially overlooking critical areas.

Key Takeaway

A thorough identification of all business functions, including dependencies, is crucial for a robust BIA and effective BCDR planning.
