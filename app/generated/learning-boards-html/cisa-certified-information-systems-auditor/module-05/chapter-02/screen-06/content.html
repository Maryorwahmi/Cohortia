Impact Analysis

Quantifying Disruption: Financial, Operational, Reputational, Legal

Evaluating potential consequences across various domains.

Financial Impact

  • •Loss of revenue or sales.
  • •Contractual penalties or fines.
  • •Increased operational costs during recovery.

Operational Impact

  • •Inability to deliver products or services.
  • •Production halts or delays.
  • •Service degradation and backlog accumulation.

Reputational Impact

  • •Damage to brand image and public trust.
  • •Loss of customer loyalty and churn.
  • •Negative media coverage and public perception.

Legal/Regulatory Impact

  • •Non-compliance with industry regulations.
  • •Legal liabilities and lawsuits.
  • •Potential loss of operating licenses or certifications.
Key Takeaway

A comprehensive impact assessment considers all potential consequences, not just financial, to accurately prioritize recovery efforts.
