Strategic Alignment

BIA and Risk Assessment: A Combined View for Prioritization

Identifying threats and vulnerabilities to inform BCDR strategies.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

  • •Identifies critical business functions.
  • •Determines potential impact of disruption.
  • •Establishes recovery objectives (RTO, RPO, MTD, WRT).
  • •Answers: What is critical and how quickly to recover.

Risk Assessment

  • •Identifies potential threats (e.g., cyberattack, hardware failure).
  • •Assesses vulnerabilities and likelihood of threats.
  • •Evaluates potential impact of specific risks.
  • •Answers: What could go wrong and how likely is it.
Key Takeaway

Integrating BIA and risk assessment provides a comprehensive view, enabling organizations to prioritize risks and develop targeted, effective BCDR strategies.
