Core Concept

The Cornerstone of BCDR: Business Impact Analysis

Systematically identifying critical functions and potential disruption impacts.

What is a Business Impact Analysis (BIA)?

The BIA is a systematic process to identify critical business functions and processes, determine the potential impact of their disruption, and establish recovery priorities. It answers: what needs protection, why it needs protection, and how quickly it needs to be recovered.

  • •Identifies Criticality: Pinpoints functions essential for organizational survival.
  • •Quantifies Impact: Assesses financial, operational, reputational, and legal consequences of disruption.
  • •Establishes Priorities: Guides the allocation of resources for recovery planning.
Key Takeaway

A robust BIA is indispensable for effective BCDR, ensuring resources are aligned with true business needs and risks.

Why is BIA Paramount for Auditors?

As a CISA auditor, evaluating the BIA is critical because it directly informs the entire BCDR strategy. A flawed BIA can lead to:

Misallocated Resources: Investing in non-critical systems while critical ones remain vulnerable.

Inadequate Recovery: Failure to restore critical capabilities within acceptable timeframes.

Organizational Failure: Unacceptable consequences leading to severe financial or reputational damage.
