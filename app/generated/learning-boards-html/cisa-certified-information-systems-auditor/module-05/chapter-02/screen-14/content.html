CHAPTER RECAP

Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A systematic process to determine and evaluate the potential effects of an interruption to critical business operations.

Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD)

The maximum amount of time a business process can be inoperative before the organization suffers unacceptable consequences.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum acceptable duration of time for a business function to be restored to an operational state after an incident.

Summary Takeaway

BIA, MTD, and RTO are foundational for understanding business criticality and setting realistic recovery targets in BCDR planning.
