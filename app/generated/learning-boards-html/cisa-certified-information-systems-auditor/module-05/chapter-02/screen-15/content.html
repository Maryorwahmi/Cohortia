CHAPTER RECAP

Key Concepts

More essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time, representing the age of files or data to be recovered.

Work Recovery Time (WRT)

The time needed to configure a recovered system, perform data synchronization, and conduct integrity checks to make it fully operational.

Risk Assessment

The process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating risks to an organization's assets and operations.

Summary Takeaway

RPO, WRT, and Risk Assessment are crucial for defining data protection, ensuring full system readiness, and proactively addressing threats in BCDR.
