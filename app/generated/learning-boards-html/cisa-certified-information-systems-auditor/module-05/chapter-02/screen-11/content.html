Auditor's Focus

Validating BIA Metrics: Accuracy and Justification

Ensuring RTOs, RPOs, and MTDs are rigorous and aligned with business needs.

Verification Steps

  • •Review Documentation: Examine BIA reports, impact assessments, and metric definitions.
  • •Interview Stakeholders: Confirm agreement and understanding of metrics with business owners.
  • •Scrutinize Methodology: Ensure RTO/RPO determination is based on rigorous analysis, not arbitrary decisions.
  • •Check Justification: Verify that metrics are supported by evidence of potential business impact.

Common Mistakes to Identify

  • •Too Aggressive: RTOs/RPOs set unrealistically low, leading to excessive costs.
  • •Too Loose: RTOs/RPOs set too high, resulting in unacceptable downtime or data loss.
  • •Inconsistencies: A critical system having an RTO longer than its MTD, indicating a fundamental planning flaw.
Key Takeaway

Auditors must ensure BIA metrics are robust, justified, and consistent to prevent misaligned recovery strategies and ensure true resilience.
