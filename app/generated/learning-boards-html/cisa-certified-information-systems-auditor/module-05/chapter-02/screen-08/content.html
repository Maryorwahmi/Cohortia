Critical Metric

Setting the RTO: Target for Restoration

The targeted duration to restore a business process after a disaster.

What is Recovery Time Objective (RTO)?

RTO is the targeted duration of time and a service level within which a business process must be restored after a disaster to avoid unacceptable consequences. It's the 'how quickly' a system needs to be back online.

  • •Targeted Duration: The goal for system or process restoration.
  • •RTO ≤ MTD: Must always be less than or equal to the Maximum Tolerable Downtime.
  • •Drives Strategy: Directly influences the choice of recovery solutions and technologies.
Key Takeaway

RTO defines the recovery speed, acting as a critical target that must align with business needs and MTD constraints.

Example: Critical System Recovery

Scenario: A critical system has an MTD of 4 hours.

RTO Setting: The RTO is set at 2 hours.

Benefit: This provides a 2-hour buffer, ensuring recovery before MTD is reached.
