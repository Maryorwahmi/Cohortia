The Guardrails: Policies, Standards, and Procedures

Translating strategy into enforceable operational rules.

High-Level

IT Policies

Mandatory statements defining rules and principles for IT resource use and management.

Example: "All sensitive data must be encrypted at rest and in transit."
Specific

IT Standards

Specific requirements for implementing IT policies, detailing acceptable technologies or configurations.

Example: "Use AES-256 encryption for data at rest."
Detailed

IT Procedures

Detailed step-by-step instructions for performing specific IT-related tasks or processes.

Example: "Steps for configuring AES-256 encryption on a database server."
Key Takeaway

Policies, standards, and procedures form a critical hierarchy that translates strategic intent into actionable, enforceable operational controls, ensuring consistent and secure IT practices.
