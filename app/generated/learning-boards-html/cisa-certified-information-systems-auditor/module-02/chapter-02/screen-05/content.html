Crafting the IT Roadmap

From Business Vision to Actionable IT Initiatives.

Step 1

Business Vision & Goals

Understand organizational direction.

Step 2

Translate to IT Objectives

Define measurable IT targets.

Step 3

Develop IT Initiatives

Create actionable projects.

Step 4

Execute & Review

Implement and monitor progress.

Example Scenario

Business Goal: Expand into new international markets. IT Objective: Establish scalable, secure global cloud infrastructure. IT Initiatives: Multi-region cloud deployment, international data privacy compliance.

Key Takeaway

A structured IT strategic planning process ensures that technology efforts are always aligned with and directly contribute to the organization's overarching business goals.
