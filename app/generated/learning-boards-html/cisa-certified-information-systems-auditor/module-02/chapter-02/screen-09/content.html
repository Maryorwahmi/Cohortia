Assessing Investment & Portfolio Controls

Ensuring transparent, value-driven IT spending.

Project Selection & Criteria

Are there clear, documented criteria for project approval, and are they consistently applied across all IT initiatives?

Audit Question: "Are project approval criteria clear and consistent?"

Benefit & Cost Tracking

Is there a mechanism for tracking project benefits and costs post-implementation to assess actual ROI and value delivery?

Audit Question: "How are project benefits and costs tracked post-implementation?"

Controls Against 'Pet Projects'

What controls are in place to prevent initiatives without strategic justification from consuming valuable resources?

Audit Question: "What controls prevent non-strategic projects?"

Common Mistakes

Lack of clear prioritization, inconsistent evaluation criteria, or failure to terminate underperforming projects.

Impact: Resource drain and missed opportunities.
Key Takeaway

Auditors play a vital role in ensuring IT investment and portfolio management processes are robust, transparent, and effectively align IT spending with strategic business objectives.
