Key Concepts

Essential Definitions for CISA

IT Portfolio Management

Centralized management of processes, methods, and technologies to analyze and collectively manage current or proposed IT projects based on key characteristics.

Enterprise Architecture (EA)

A conceptual blueprint defining the structure and operation of an organization, bridging the gap between business strategy and IT execution.

IT Policies, Standards, Procedures

The hierarchical operational embodiment of IT strategy: Policies (rules), Standards (requirements), and Procedures (step-by-step instructions).

Summary Takeaway

These concepts provide the framework for governing IT, ensuring strategic alignment, efficient operations, and robust risk management across the organization.
