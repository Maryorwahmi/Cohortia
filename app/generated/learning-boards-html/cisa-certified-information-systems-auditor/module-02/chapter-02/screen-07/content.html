Smart Spending: IT Investment Management

Maximizing value from every technology dollar.

Core Definition

The process of selecting, prioritizing, and managing IT projects and assets to maximize their value contribution to the business.

Strategic Alignment

Ensures investments are made in projects that best align with strategic objectives and offer the highest return on investment (ROI).

Resource Optimization

Manages limited resources (budget, personnel, time) by prioritizing projects based on strategic fit and risk profile.

Key Takeaway

Effective IT investment management ensures that every technology investment is a deliberate step towards achieving business goals, preventing wasteful spending.
