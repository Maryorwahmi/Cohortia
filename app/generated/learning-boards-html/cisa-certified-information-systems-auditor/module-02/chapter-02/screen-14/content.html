Key Concepts

Core Ideas to Remember

IT Strategy

A comprehensive plan outlining how information technology will be used to achieve an organization's overall business objectives.

Strategic Planning Process

The systematic procedure for developing an IT strategy, including defining vision, mission, goals, objectives, and initiatives.

IT Investment Management

The process of evaluating, selecting, and funding IT projects and assets to align with strategic goals and maximize value.

Summary Takeaway

Effective IT strategy, planning, and investment management are crucial for ensuring technology drives business value and achieves organizational objectives.
