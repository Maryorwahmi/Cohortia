Auditing the Strategic Planning Process

Verifying robustness, collaboration, and documentation.

Formal Process

Is there a documented, systematic procedure for IT strategic planning, ensuring consistency and accountability?

Audit Question: "Is the IT strategy documented and formally approved?"

Regular Review

How often is the IT strategy reviewed and updated to remain relevant with business and technological changes?

Audit Question: "How frequently is the IT strategy reviewed and updated?"

Stakeholder Involvement

Who are the key business and IT leaders involved in the strategy's development and approval?

Audit Question: "Who are the key stakeholders involved in its development and approval?"
Key Takeaway

A CISA ensures the IT strategic planning process is well-governed, collaborative, and dynamic, actively guiding IT investments to support business objectives.
