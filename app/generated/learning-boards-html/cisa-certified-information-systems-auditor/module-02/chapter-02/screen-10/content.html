The Blueprint: Understanding Enterprise Architecture

Bridging business strategy and IT execution with a holistic view.

Core Definition

A conceptual blueprint defining the structure and operation of an organization, bridging business strategy and IT execution.

Holistic View

Encompasses processes, information, applications, and technology infrastructure to provide a complete organizational picture.

Key Benefit

Ensures new IT initiatives fit into the existing landscape without creating silos or unnecessary complexity, promoting integration.

Key Takeaway

EA is essential for creating a coherent, adaptable technological ecosystem that directly supports and enables the organization's strategic direction.

Enterprise Architecture Layers
Business LayerProcesses & Organization
Data LayerInformation & Data Assets
Application LayerSoftware Systems & Services
Technology LayerInfrastructure & Hardware
Analogy

Just as an architect designs a building with blueprints for structure, plumbing, and electrical systems, EA provides a blueprint for an organization's IT and business components.
