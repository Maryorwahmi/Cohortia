EA for Decision-Making and Audit Insights

Using the blueprint to avoid silos and adapt to change.

Strategic Insights

EA documentation provides insights into an organization's strategic direction, integration approach, and adaptability to future business needs.

Avoiding Fragmentation

Helps prevent new IT initiatives from creating silos or unnecessary complexity, ensuring a cohesive IT landscape.

Auditor's Assessment

Assess if EA is actively used for decision-making and regularly updated to reflect changes in business and technology.

Key Takeaway

A well-maintained and actively used EA is a critical tool for ensuring IT investments are strategically sound, integrated, and adaptable, providing valuable audit evidence.

EA Impact on IT Landscape
With EAIntegrated, Efficient, Adaptable
Without EAFragmented, Redundant, Inefficient
Decision-MakingStrategic & Informed
Adaptability to ChangeHigh & Proactive
Example of Poor EA

If EA shows a fragmented application landscape with numerous redundant systems, it indicates inefficiencies and a lack of strategic oversight in past IT investments, leading to higher costs and complexity.
