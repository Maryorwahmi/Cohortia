Verifying Operational Adherence

Assessing comprehensiveness, communication, and enforcement.

Comprehensiveness & Currency

Are IT policies, standards, and procedures comprehensive, covering all relevant areas, and are they kept current with evolving technology and risks?

Audit Focus: Document Review

Communication & Enforcement

Are these documents effectively communicated to relevant personnel, and are they consistently enforced across the organization?

Audit Focus: Interviews & Training Records

Risk of Inconsistency

Inconsistent application or outdated procedures can expose an organization to significant risks, undermining even a sound IT strategy.

Impact: Security breaches, compliance failures.

Compliance Testing

Auditors conduct compliance testing by reviewing logs, configurations, and interviewing personnel to verify adherence to operational controls.

Audit Focus: Evidence & Verification
Key Takeaway

A CISA's audit ensures that IT policies, standards, and procedures are not just documented, but are actively and consistently enforced, providing robust operational integrity and risk mitigation.
