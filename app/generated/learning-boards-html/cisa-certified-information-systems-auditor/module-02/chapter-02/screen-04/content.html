The Foundation: Aligning IT with Business Strategy

Ensuring IT drives organizational value, not just cost.

Core Principle

IT effectiveness is measured by its ability to seamlessly support and enable the overarching business strategy.

CISA's Role

Assess if IT strategy is clearly defined, communicated, and integrated with business objectives.

The Risk of Misalignment

Without alignment, IT risks becoming a cost center, pursuing initiatives that are technically sound but strategically irrelevant.

Key Takeaway

Strategic IT alignment ensures every technological endeavor contributes directly to the organization's mission and vision, maximizing value.
