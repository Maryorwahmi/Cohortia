Framework Step 1

Proactive Risk Identification

This stage involves actively discovering potential threats and vulnerabilities that could impact IT assets. It's about looking for problems before they manifest.

  • **Threats:** External or internal events that could cause harm.
  • **Vulnerabilities:** Weaknesses in systems or processes that threats can exploit.
  • **Assets:** What needs protection (data, hardware, software, reputation).
Key Takeaway

Thorough risk identification is the bedrock of effective IT security, preventing future issues by uncovering potential problems early.

Methods for Risk Identification
**Asset Inventories:** Comprehensive lists of all IT assets.
**Vulnerability Scans:** Automated tools to find system weaknesses.
**Threat Intelligence:** Information on current and emerging threats.
**Personnel Interviews:** Gathering insights from key staff.
Common Mistake

Incomplete Asset Inventory

Failing to maintain a comprehensive and up-to-date register of all IT assets can lead to critical systems or data being overlooked in risk assessments.
