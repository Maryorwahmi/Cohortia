Core Concept

What is IT Risk?

IT risk is the business risk associated with the use, ownership, operation, involvement, influence, and adoption of IT within an enterprise. It encompasses threats and vulnerabilities impacting confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information and IT systems.

  • It's a business risk, not just a technical one.
  • Impacts confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA).
  • Requires a comprehensive management approach.
Key Takeaway

IT risk is a multifaceted business concern, requiring auditors to consider its broad impact across an organization's operations and objectives.

Data Breach Impact

A data breach exposing customer PII leads to regulatory fines (compliance risk), loss of customer trust (reputational risk), and potential legal costs (financial risk). This single event demonstrates the interconnectedness of IT risk types.
