Key Concepts Continued

Further essential terms for your CISA knowledge

Risk Analysis

The process of understanding the nature of risk and determining the level of risk (likelihood and impact).

Qualitative & Quantitative Assessment

Methods using descriptive terms (qualitative) or numerical values (quantitative) for likelihood and impact.

Risk Evaluation, Appetite & Tolerance

Comparing assessed risks against the organization's willingness to pursue/retain risk (appetite) and acceptable variation (tolerance).

Summary Takeaway

Understanding risk analysis and evaluation, including qualitative, quantitative, appetite, and tolerance, is vital for making strategic risk management decisions.
