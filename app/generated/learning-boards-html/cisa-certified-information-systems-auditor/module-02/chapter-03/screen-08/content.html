Assessment Method

Qualitative Risk Assessment

This method uses descriptive scales (e.g., "low," "medium," "high") for likelihood and impact. It's often quicker and less resource-intensive, useful for initial assessments and prioritizing risks.

  • **Descriptive Scales:** Uses terms like Low, Medium, High.
  • **Quick & Efficient:** Ideal for initial assessments and prioritization.
  • **Less Data-Intensive:** Useful when precise numerical data is unavailable.
Key Takeaway

Qualitative assessment provides a practical, rapid overview of risks, enabling effective prioritization without extensive data requirements.

Example Scenario

Phishing Attack Assessment

An organization assesses the risk of a successful phishing attack:

  • **Likelihood:** Medium (due to frequent attempts and human error).
  • **Impact:** High (potential for data breach, financial loss, reputational damage).
Auditor's Focus

Consistency & Justification

Auditors must review the qualitative methodology to ensure ratings are consistent across the organization and are justified by clear criteria, preventing subjective biases.
