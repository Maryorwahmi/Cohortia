Strategic Integration

Why Integrate IT Risk?

Integrating IT risk management into overall enterprise governance is paramount because IT risks are not isolated; they often have broader business implications that affect strategic objectives and overall organizational health.

  • **Holistic View:** Considers IT risks alongside all other enterprise risks.
  • **Effective Resource Allocation:** Ensures resources are optimally distributed.
  • **Strategic Alignment:** Connects IT risk management to business objectives.
Key Takeaway

Integrated IT risk management ensures IT is a strategic enabler, not just a technical function, by aligning risk decisions with overall business goals.

Common Mistakes & Auditor Notes

Treating IT Risk as Technical Only

Ignoring business implications and failing to involve business stakeholders in risk assessments.

Outdated Risk Register

Failing to update the risk register to reflect current threats, vulnerabilities, or business changes.

Confirmation Bias

Auditors must independently verify risk assessments, not just rely on management's presented data, to avoid skewed perceptions.
