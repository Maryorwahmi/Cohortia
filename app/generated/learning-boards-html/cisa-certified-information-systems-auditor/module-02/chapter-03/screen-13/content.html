Framework Step 5

Continuous Risk Monitoring

Risk monitoring is the continuous process of tracking identified risks, reviewing the effectiveness of controls, and identifying new risks. It ensures the risk management program remains dynamic and relevant.

  • **Ongoing Process:** Not a one-time check, but a continuous loop.
  • **Effectiveness Review:** Verifies if controls are working as intended.
  • **New Risk Identification:** Adapts to evolving threat landscapes.
Key Takeaway

Effective risk monitoring is essential for maintaining a responsive and adaptive risk management program that continuously protects organizational assets.

Key Activities
**Tracking Risks:** Maintaining an up-to-date risk register.
**KRI Reporting:** Regular reports to management using Key Risk Indicators.
**Action on Findings:** Ensuring management responds to monitoring results.
Common Mistake

Ineffective Action

Monitoring data is collected but not translated into actionable improvements, leading to a stagnant and ineffective risk management program.
