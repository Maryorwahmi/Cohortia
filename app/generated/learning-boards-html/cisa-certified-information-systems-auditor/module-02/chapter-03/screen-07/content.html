Understanding Risk Analysis

Risk analysis is the process of understanding the nature of risk and determining the level of risk by assessing two key factors: **likelihood** and **impact**.

  • **Likelihood:** The probability or frequency of a risk event occurring.
  • **Impact:** The severity of the consequences if the risk event occurs.
  • **Purpose:** To prioritize risks and inform treatment decisions.
Key Takeaway

Accurate risk analysis is crucial for prioritizing security efforts, ensuring resources are allocated to address the most significant threats.

Risk Analysis Components
**Likelihood:** How often could it happen?
**Impact:** How bad would it be?
Common Pitfall

Underestimating Risk Factors

Failing to accurately assess either the likelihood or impact of a risk can lead to misprioritization and inadequate protective measures, leaving critical assets vulnerable.
