Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

IT Risk

The business risk associated with the use, ownership, operation, involvement, influence, and adoption of IT within an enterprise.

Risk Management Framework

A structured, cyclical approach to identifying, assessing, treating, and monitoring risks to protect assets and achieve objectives.

Risk Identification

The proactive process of finding, recognizing, and describing potential threats and vulnerabilities that could impact IT assets.

Summary Takeaway

Effective IT risk management is a continuous, structured process that begins with understanding and identifying risks, guided by a comprehensive framework.
