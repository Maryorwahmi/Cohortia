Assessment Method

Quantitative Risk Assessment

This method assigns numerical values to likelihood (e.g., annual probability) and impact (e.g., monetary loss), allowing for a more precise calculation of risk, often expressed as Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE).

  • **Numerical Values:** Uses probabilities and monetary figures.
  • **Precise Financial Understanding:** Quantifies potential losses.
  • **Data-Intensive:** Requires reliable data for accurate calculations.
Key Takeaway

Quantitative assessment provides a clear financial basis for risk management decisions, justifying investments and prioritizing controls based on monetary impact.

Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)

ALE Formula & Example

**ALE = ARO × SLE**

  • **ARO (Annualized Rate of Occurrence):** Annual probability of a risk event.
  • **SLE (Single Loss Expectancy):** Monetary loss from a single occurrence.

**Example:** Data breach with 10% annual probability (ARO = 0.1) and $1,000,000 impact (SLE).
ALE = 0.1 × $1,000,000 = **$100,000**

Auditor's Focus

Data Accuracy & Assumptions

Auditors must scrutinize the data sources and assumptions underpinning quantitative models to ensure their validity and prevent misleading risk calculations.
