Risk Evaluation

Risk evaluation involves comparing the assessed risk levels against the organization's predefined **risk appetite** and **risk tolerance** to determine if the risk is acceptable.

  • **Decision Point:** Determines if a risk requires treatment or can be accepted.
  • **Governance Role:** Heavily influenced by strategic direction from the Board.
Key Takeaway

Risk evaluation ensures that risk-taking decisions align with the organization's strategic objectives and its willingness to accept risk.

Key Concepts

Risk Appetite

The amount and type of risk an organization is willing to pursue or retain in the pursuit of its objectives. A high-level, strategic statement.

Risk Tolerance

The acceptable variation around risk appetite, often expressed in specific metrics or thresholds. More operational and measurable.

Auditor's Focus

Alignment Verification

Auditors must verify that stated risk appetite and tolerance are clearly defined, communicated, and that actual risk acceptance aligns with these parameters.
