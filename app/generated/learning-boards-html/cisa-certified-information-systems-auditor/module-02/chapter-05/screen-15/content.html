Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Recovery Objectives & Strategies

Understanding the metrics and approaches for effective recovery.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum tolerable duration a business process can be down following a disaster or disruption.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable amount of data that can be lost from an IT service due to a major incident.

Recovery Site Types & Testing

Includes Hot, Warm, and Cold Sites for recovery, and methods like Tabletop Exercises and Full-Scale Simulations for validation.

Summary Takeaway

RTO and RPO define recovery targets, while various site types and testing methods provide the practical means to achieve these objectives and ensure plan effectiveness.
