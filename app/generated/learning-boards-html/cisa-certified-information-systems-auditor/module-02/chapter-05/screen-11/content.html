Process Step

Plan Development: Documenting the Details

Creating clear, concise, and actionable BCP and DRP documents.

Key Elements of the Plan

  • •Activation procedures and criteria.
  • •Communication plans (internal & external).
  • •Roles, responsibilities, and contact lists.
  • •Step-by-step recovery procedures for systems.
  • •Vendor and third-party contact information.
Why Clarity Matters

During a crisis, clear, concise, and actionable plans minimize confusion and expedite recovery, preventing further damage.

Auditor Focus & Common Pitfalls

  • •**Auditor:** Review completeness, clarity, and coverage of critical functions/scenarios.
  • •**Pitfall 1:** Vague or incomplete plans that lack specific procedures.
  • •**Pitfall 2:** Plans that don't align with current infrastructure or business processes.
  • •**Pitfall 3:** Lack of clear ownership or approval for plan content.
Example

A DRP should include a detailed checklist for server restoration, specifying boot order, network configurations, and application dependencies.

Key Takeaway

Effective BCP/DRP documentation must be comprehensive, clear, and actionable, providing a reliable guide for personnel during a disruption.
