BCP vs. DRP: A Critical Distinction

Understanding the scope of Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Planning.

Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

  • •Focuses on maintaining critical business functions.
  • •Encompasses people, processes, and technology.
  • •Ensures the *business* keeps running during and after disruption.
  • •Broader, strategic approach to organizational resilience.
Example Scenario

During a regional power outage, BCP ensures sales teams can work remotely, customer service can reroute calls, and essential product delivery processes continue, even if the main office is down.

Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)

  • •Specifically addresses recovery of IT systems and infrastructure.
  • •Focuses on data, applications, servers, and networks.
  • •Ensures *systems* are restored and operational.
  • •Subset of BCP, tactical and technology-focused.
Example Scenario

Following a server room flood, DRP outlines the steps to restore data from backups, bring up replacement servers at a recovery site, and re-establish network connectivity for critical applications.

Key Takeaway

BCP ensures the entire business continues, while DRP focuses on restoring the underlying IT infrastructure. Both are essential for comprehensive organizational resilience.
