Process Flow

Risk Assessment & Strategy Development

Identifying threats and outlining recovery approaches.

Risk Assessment

  • •Identifies potential threats (e.g., cyberattacks, natural disasters).
  • •Identifies vulnerabilities to critical resources.
  • •Informs the development of effective recovery strategies.
Example

Identifying a single data center as a vulnerability to a regional power grid failure.

Strategy Development

  • •Outlines approaches to recover critical functions/systems.
  • •Decides *how* RTOs and RPOs will be achieved.
  • •Includes options like hot, warm, or cold recovery sites.
Recovery Site Types

**Hot Site:** Fully equipped, operational in hours (highest cost).
**Warm Site:** Partially equipped, needs more setup (balanced cost/speed).
**Cold Site:** Basic infrastructure, needs significant setup (lowest cost).

Key Takeaway

Risk assessment identifies threats and vulnerabilities, which then guide the development of recovery strategies, including the selection of appropriate recovery site types.
