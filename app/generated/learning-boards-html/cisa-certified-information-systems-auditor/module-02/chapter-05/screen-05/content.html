The BCP/DRP Development Lifecycle

A systematic approach to building organizational resilience.

01

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identify critical functions & impacts.

02

Risk Assessment

Identify threats & vulnerabilities.

03

Strategy Development

Outline recovery approaches.

04

Document detailed procedures.

05

Testing & Maintenance

Validate and update plans.

06

Continuous Improvement

Refine based on lessons learned.

Key Takeaway

BCP/DRP development is a continuous cycle of analysis, strategy, planning, testing, and maintenance, ensuring ongoing organizational resilience.
