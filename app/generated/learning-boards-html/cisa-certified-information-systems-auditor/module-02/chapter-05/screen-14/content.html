Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Foundations of Resilience

Essential terms and definitions from this chapter.

Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

The overall process to identify threats and impacts, building organizational resilience to safeguard interests and value-creating activities.

Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)

A subset of BCP, focused specifically on the recovery of IT systems, applications, and data after a disaster or disruption.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A process that identifies critical business functions and the impact a specific disaster or disruption would have on them.

Summary Takeaway

BCP provides the overarching framework for organizational resilience, DRP focuses on IT recovery, and BIA is the critical analysis that informs both.
