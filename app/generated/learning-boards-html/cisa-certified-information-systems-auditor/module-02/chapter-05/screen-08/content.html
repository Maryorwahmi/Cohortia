Key Metric

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Defining the maximum tolerable data loss for IT services.

What is RPO?

  • •Maximum tolerable amount of data loss.
  • •Defines how current recovered data needs to be.
  • •Determined by Business Impact Analysis (BIA).
Impact

Directly influences data backup, replication, and synchronization strategies to minimize data loss.

Example & Auditor Focus

  • •**Example:** Real-time trading system: RPO of seconds. Static archival system: RPO of hours/days.
  • •**Auditor:** Verify RPOs are realistic, aligned with business needs, and supported by adequate data protection.
  • •**Misconception:** RPO is not the frequency of backups, but the *maximum acceptable data loss*.
Why it Matters

An unrealistic RPO can lead to insufficient data protection, resulting in significant, unrecoverable data loss during a disruption, impacting compliance and operations.

Key Takeaway

RPO defines the maximum acceptable data loss, guiding backup and replication strategies to ensure data currency after an incident.
