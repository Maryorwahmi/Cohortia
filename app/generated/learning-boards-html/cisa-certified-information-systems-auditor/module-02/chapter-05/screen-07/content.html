Key Metric

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Defining the maximum tolerable downtime for business processes.

What is RTO?

  • •Maximum tolerable duration of downtime.
  • •Target time for restoring a business process/system.
  • •Determined by Business Impact Analysis (BIA).
Impact

Directly influences the choice of recovery strategies and the required investment in recovery solutions.

Example & Auditor Focus

  • •**Example:** Real-time trading system: RTO of minutes. Archival system: RTO of days.
  • •**Auditor:** Scrutinize RTOs for realism, alignment with business needs, and formal approval.
  • •**Misconception:** RTO is not the actual recovery time, but the *objective* or *target*.
Why it Matters

Miscalculated RTOs can lead to either overspending on recovery solutions or insufficient recovery capabilities, exposing the organization to significant risk.

Key Takeaway

RTO defines the maximum acceptable downtime for a business process, directly impacting recovery strategy and investment decisions.
