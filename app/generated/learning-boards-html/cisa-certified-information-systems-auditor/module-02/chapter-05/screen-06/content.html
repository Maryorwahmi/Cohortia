Definition

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identifying critical functions and the impact of their unavailability.

What is BIA?

  • •Identifies critical business functions.
  • •Determines resources these functions depend on (IT, personnel, facilities).
  • •Quantifies financial and operational impact of unavailability over time.
Purpose

To understand the potential consequences of disruption and prioritize recovery efforts based on business criticality.

Key Outcomes

  • •Identifies maximum tolerable downtime for each function.
  • •Helps determine Recovery Time Objective (RTO).
  • •Helps determine Recovery Point Objective (RPO).
  • •Provides data for risk assessment and strategy development.
Auditor Focus

Verify BIA thoroughness, regular updates, and realistic RTOs/RPOs approved by business owners. Miscalculations can lead to inadequate recovery.

Key Takeaway

The BIA is the foundational step, identifying critical business functions and quantifying the impact of their unavailability to guide effective recovery planning.
