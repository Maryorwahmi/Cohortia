COBIT Principles

Integrated Framework & Holistic Approach

A Unified and Comprehensive View of IT Governance

Applying a Single Integrated Framework

COBIT can be used as a single, overarching framework to integrate various IT-related standards, frameworks, and practices within an organization, ensuring consistency and avoiding redundancies.

Auditor's Focus

Look for evidence of a unified approach to IT governance rather than disparate, uncoordinated frameworks.

Enabling a Holistic Approach

Effective IT governance requires considering multiple 'enablers' that influence how IT is governed and managed, including principles, processes, organizational structures, culture, information, services, and people.

Common Mistake

Focusing on only one or two enablers while neglecting others, leading to gaps in governance effectiveness.

Key Takeaway

COBIT's integrated and holistic principles ensure comprehensive and consistent IT governance across the enterprise.
