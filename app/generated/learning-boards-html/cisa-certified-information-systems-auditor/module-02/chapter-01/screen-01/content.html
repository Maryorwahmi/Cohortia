Foundations of IT Governance

Directing Technology for Business Success

Effective IT governance is the bedrock for ensuring an organization's technology investments are aligned with its strategic objectives, deliver measurable value, and manage associated risks. It's about establishing clear decision-making processes and accountability structures that drive IT performance.

Key Idea

IT governance ensures technology aligns with business goals, manages risks, and optimizes value delivery.

Core Principle

Strategic Alignment

Ensuring IT strategy is inextricably linked to overall business strategy.

Key Outcome

Value Delivery

Optimizing IT costs and proving the value of IT investments.

Critical Area

Risk Management

Understanding and mitigating IT-related risks to an acceptable level.
