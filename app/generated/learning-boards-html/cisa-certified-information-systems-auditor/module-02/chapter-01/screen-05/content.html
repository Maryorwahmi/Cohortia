Five Core Components

A Framework for Decision-Making and Accountability

Strategic Alignment

Ensuring IT strategy is inextricably linked to business strategy, translating business objectives into IT initiatives.

Value Delivery

Optimizing IT costs and proving the value of IT, ensuring that IT delivers the promised benefits.

Risk Management

Identifying, assessing, and mitigating IT-related risks to an acceptable level.

Resource Management

Optimal investment in, and proper management of, critical IT resources: applications, infrastructure, information, and people.

Performance Measurement

Tracking and monitoring strategy implementation, project completion, and service delivery against established goals and metrics.

Key Takeaway

These five components are interdependent and crucial for a holistic and effective IT governance framework.
