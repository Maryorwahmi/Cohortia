Strategic Alignment & Value Delivery

Ensuring Technology Drives Organizational Goals

Strategic Alignment

This component ensures that IT strategy is inextricably linked to overall business strategy. It involves translating business objectives into specific IT initiatives and ensuring that IT investments directly support the organization's goals.

Example

A company's goal to expand into new markets leads to IT initiatives for developing localized software versions and setting up regional data centers.

Value Delivery

This focuses on optimizing IT costs and proving the value of IT investments. It ensures that IT delivers the promised benefits, whether through cost savings, increased revenue, or improved efficiency.

Misconception

Value delivery is not just about cutting IT costs; it's about maximizing the benefits and returns from IT investments.

Key Takeaway

Without strategic alignment, IT efforts can be misdirected; without value delivery, IT investments may not yield expected returns.
