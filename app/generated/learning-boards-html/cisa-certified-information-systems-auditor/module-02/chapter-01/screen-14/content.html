Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

IT Governance

The system by which the current and future use of IT is directed and controlled to support organizational objectives.

COBIT

A comprehensive framework for IT governance and management, providing an end-to-end business view of enterprise IT.

Strategic Alignment

Ensuring IT strategy and initiatives are directly linked to and support the overall business strategy and objectives.

Summary Takeaway

Effective IT governance is foundational for aligning technology with business goals, managing risks, and optimizing value.
