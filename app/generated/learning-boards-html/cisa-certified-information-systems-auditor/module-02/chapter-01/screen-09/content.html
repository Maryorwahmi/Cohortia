COBIT 2019: A Holistic Approach

The Leading Framework for Enterprise IT Governance

What is COBIT?

COBIT (Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies) is a comprehensive framework for IT governance and management. It helps organizations create optimal value from IT by balancing benefits realization, risk optimization, and resource utilization.

Key Focus

Provides an end-to-end business view of the governance of enterprise IT.

Five Key Principles

  • Meeting stakeholder needs
  • Covering the enterprise end-to-end
  • Applying a single integrated framework
  • Enabling a holistic approach
  • Separating governance from management
Auditor's Perspective

These principles are crucial for evaluating the effectiveness and comprehensiveness of an organization's IT governance practices.

Key Takeaway

COBIT 2019 offers a structured, holistic framework to ensure IT governance aligns with business objectives and delivers value.
