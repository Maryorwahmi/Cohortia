Who Governs IT?

Defining Accountability Across the Organization

Board of Directors

Holds ultimate accountability for IT governance, setting strategic direction and managing enterprise IT risks.

IT Steering Committee

Translates board directives into IT strategies, overseeing major IT investments and projects.

CIO / CISO

Responsible for operational execution of IT strategy and security programs, reporting to governance bodies.

IT Management

Implements policies and procedures defined by governance bodies, managing day-to-day IT operations.

Key Takeaway

Clear definition and effective performance of these roles are crucial for robust IT governance and accountability.
