Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Core Principles in Practice

Value Delivery

Optimizing the value from IT by ensuring that IT investments deliver the promised benefits and are cost-effective.

Risk Management

Identifying, assessing, and mitigating IT-related risks to an acceptable level to protect the organization.

Resource Management

The optimal allocation and use of IT resources, including infrastructure, applications, information, and personnel.

Summary Takeaway

These practical components are essential for the effective implementation and continuous improvement of IT governance within any organization.
