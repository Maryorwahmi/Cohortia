Performance Measurement

Tracking Progress and Ensuring Accountability

What it Involves

  • Monitoring IT strategy implementation.
  • Tracking IT project completion rates and budget adherence.
  • Evaluating service delivery against Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
  • Reporting on key performance indicators (KPIs) relevant to business objectives.
Purpose

Provides feedback to ensure IT objectives are being met and identifies areas for improvement.

Practical Application

For example, if an organization sets a goal to reduce IT operational costs by 15% within a year, performance measurement involves regularly reviewing financial reports and IT expenditure data to track progress towards this target. It also includes assessing the impact of any cost-saving initiatives on service quality.

Common Mistake

Measuring too many irrelevant metrics or failing to link performance data back to strategic business outcomes.

Key Takeaway

Effective performance measurement is essential for accountability, demonstrating IT value, and driving continuous improvement.
