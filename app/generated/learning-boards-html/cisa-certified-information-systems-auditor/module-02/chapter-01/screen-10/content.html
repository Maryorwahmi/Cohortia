COBIT Principles

Meeting Needs & Covering the Enterprise

Ensuring IT Delivers Value Across the Organization

Meeting Stakeholder Needs

This principle ensures that IT processes and services are actually delivering what the business requires. It involves assessing whether IT is responsive to the diverse needs of all stakeholders.

Auditor's Focus

Reviewing Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and user satisfaction surveys to verify IT service delivery and relevance.

Covering the Enterprise End-to-End

This principle implies that IT governance extends beyond the IT department itself, encompassing all IT-related assets and processes across the entire organization, including third-party providers.

Auditor's Focus

Examining vendor contracts and third-party audit reports to verify comprehensive coverage of IT-related assets and processes.

Key Takeaway

COBIT ensures IT is relevant to business needs and that all IT assets, internal and external, are under governance control.
