Core Concept

What is IT Governance?

Guiding Technology for Organizational Success

Definition

IT governance is the system by which the current and future use of IT is directed and controlled. It involves evaluating and directing the use of IT to support the organization and monitoring this use to achieve plans.

Why it Matters

  • Ensures IT investments align with business strategy.
  • Optimizes resource allocation and value delivery.
  • Manages IT-related risks effectively.
  • Promotes accountability and compliance.
Analogy

The Ship's Captain & Crew

Imagine IT Governance as the Captain, setting the strategic course and ensuring the ship (organization) reaches its destination safely and efficiently.

IT Management is the Crew, executing the daily tasks to sail the ship according to the captain's directives.

Key Takeaway

IT governance is a strategic, not just technical, function that ensures IT supports and extends business objectives.
