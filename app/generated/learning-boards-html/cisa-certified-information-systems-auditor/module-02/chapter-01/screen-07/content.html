Resource & Risk Management

Optimizing Assets and Mitigating Threats

Resource Management

This involves the optimal investment in, and the proper management of, critical IT resources, including applications, infrastructure, information, and people.

Example

Ensuring adequate staffing of IT security teams and efficient allocation of cloud computing resources to meet demand.

Risk Management

This requires understanding, identifying, assessing, and mitigating IT-related risks to an acceptable level, protecting the organization from potential threats.

Misconception

The goal is not to eliminate all IT risks, but to reduce them to an acceptable level that aligns with the organization's risk appetite.

Key Takeaway

Effective resource management ensures IT assets are optimized, while robust risk management protects the organization from IT-related threats.
