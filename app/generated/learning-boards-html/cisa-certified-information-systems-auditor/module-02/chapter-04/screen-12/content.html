Scenario: Improving Phishing Awareness
Designing a Targeted Training Plan

Let's apply these concepts to a practical scenario. Imagine 'GlobalData Corp,' a multinational company, is facing a significant challenge with employee awareness regarding phishing attacks. Despite having a policy in place that prohibits clicking suspicious links, recent internal phishing simulations reveal a concerningly high click-through rate. As a CISA or security professional, you'd need to design a plan to address this. Our three-step plan focuses on revamping training, implementing varied simulations, and establishing feedback loops.

First, we'll revamp training content and delivery. This means developing new, engaging, and interactive modules that use real-world phishing examples relevant to GlobalData Corp's industry. We'll move away from lengthy annual sessions to monthly micro-learning modules, perhaps delivered via a gamified platform, incorporating interactive quizzes. Auditors would verify this by reviewing content, checking platform reports for engagement, and interviewing employees. Second, we'll implement targeted and varied phishing simulations. These will be monthly, using different attack vectors like email and SMS, and themes relevant to specific departments. Those who click will receive immediate, in-context remedial training. Auditors will track click-through rates, simulation diversity, and remedial training effectiveness. Third, we'll establish a continuous feedback and improvement loop. This involves creating an easy way for employees to report suspicious emails and analyzing these reports to identify new threats. Sharing anonymized success stories will also reinforce positive behavior. Auditors would examine incident response logs for reported phishing attempts and how feedback informs program updates. This structured approach ensures continuous improvement and a more resilient defense against phishing.

Key Takeaway

A three-step plan involving revamped training, targeted simulations, and feedback loops is essential for improving employee phishing awareness and reducing click-through rates.

Phishing Mitigation Plan
Step 1: Revamp Training

Engaging, relevant micro-modules, gamified platform, monthly delivery.

Step 2: Targeted Simulations

Varied vectors/themes, immediate remedial training for clicks.

Step 3: Feedback Loop

Easy reporting, threat analysis, sharing successes.
