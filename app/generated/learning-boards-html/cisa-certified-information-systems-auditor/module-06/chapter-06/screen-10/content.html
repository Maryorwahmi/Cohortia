IRP Phases 4 & 5

Eliminating Threats and Restoring Operations

Removing the Root Cause and Returning to Normalcy

Eradication: Key Actions

  • •Remove Root Cause: Patch vulnerabilities, fix misconfigurations.
  • •Clean Systems: Remove malware, backdoors, and malicious files.
  • •Change Credentials: Reset passwords for all compromised accounts.
  • •Strengthen Defenses: Implement new controls to prevent recurrence.
Key Takeaway

Eradication ensures the threat is fully removed, while recovery focuses on safely restoring business operations, emphasizing integrity verification and continuous monitoring.

Recovery: Bringing Systems Back Online

Restore from Backups

Use clean, verified backups to restore data and systems.

Verify System Integrity

Ensure systems are free from compromise before going live.

Monitor for Recurrence

Maintain heightened vigilance post-recovery to detect new threats.
