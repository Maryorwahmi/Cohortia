IRP Phase 1

Building a Foundation: The Preparation Phase

Readying for the Inevitable

Key Elements of Preparation

  • •Policies & Procedures: Define roles, responsibilities, and incident handling steps.
  • •Tools & Resources: Assemble forensic toolkits, secure communication, backup systems.
  • •Team Training: Ensure IR team is skilled and regularly rehearses the plan.
  • •Communication Plan: Establish internal and external contact lists and notification protocols.
Key Takeaway

Effective preparation is the bedrock of incident response, ensuring that an organization has the necessary framework, resources, and trained personnel to face security incidents.
