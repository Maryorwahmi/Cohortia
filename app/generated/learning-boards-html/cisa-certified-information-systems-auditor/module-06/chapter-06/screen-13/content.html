Auditor's Focus

Evaluating SecOps and Incident Response Capabilities

Ensuring Readiness and Resilience

Why Audit SecOps?

  • •Validate Controls: Ensure security controls are effective in practice.
  • •Assess Readiness: Determine the organization's ability to respond to incidents.
  • •Ensure Compliance: Verify adherence to regulatory requirements and internal policies.
  • •Identify Gaps: Uncover weaknesses in processes, technology, or personnel.
Key Takeaway

Auditing SecOps and IRP provides critical assurance that an organization can effectively detect, respond to, and recover from security incidents, protecting assets and maintaining resilience.

Auditor's Evaluation Steps

1Review SecOps Policies & Procedures
2Assess SIEM Implementation & Use
3Examine Incident Response Plan (IRP)
4Audit Incident Logs & Reports
5Review Vulnerability Management Program
6Evaluate Security Awareness Training & Interviews
