Module 6 Overview

Security Operations and Incident Management

Proactive Defense and Rapid Response to Cyber Threats

In the dynamic world of cybersecurity, organizations must not only prevent attacks but also possess the capability to detect, respond to, and recover from security incidents. This chapter focuses on the ongoing functions of monitoring, threat detection, and the structured approach to managing incidents, ensuring business resilience.

Key Idea

Effective security operations and a robust incident response plan are essential for detecting, containing, and recovering from inevitable cyber incidents, ensuring business continuity.
