The Incident Response Plan (IRP)

A Structured Approach to Cyber Incidents

Core Objectives of an IRP

  • •Detect: Identify security events and confirm incidents.
  • •Contain: Limit the scope and impact of the incident.
  • •Eradicate: Remove the root cause of the incident.
  • •Recover: Restore affected systems and data to normal operations.
  • •Learn: Analyze the incident to prevent future occurrences.
Key Takeaway

An IRP is a critical blueprint for managing security incidents, ensuring a systematic and effective response that minimizes damage and supports business continuity.
