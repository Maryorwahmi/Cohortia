IRP Phase 6

Continuous Improvement: The Lessons Learned Phase

Evolving Defenses Through Post-Incident Analysis

Key Activities

  • •Post-Incident Review: Analyze the incident's timeline, impact, and response.
  • •Identify Weaknesses: Pinpoint gaps in controls, processes, or technology.
  • •Implement Improvements: Update policies, enhance training, refine SIEM rules.
  • •Document Findings: Record lessons learned and track remediation efforts.
Key Takeaway

The Lessons Learned phase is crucial for transforming incidents into opportunities for growth, ensuring the organization continuously strengthens its security posture and resilience.
