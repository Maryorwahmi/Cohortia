Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Phases of Incident Response

Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, Lessons Learned.

Vulnerability Management

The continuous process of identifying, assessing, and remediating security vulnerabilities.

Security Awareness Training

Educating employees about security risks and best practices to reduce human error.

Summary Takeaway

A holistic approach to security operations, encompassing incident response phases, vulnerability management, and security awareness, is vital for organizational resilience.
