IRP Phases 2 & 3

Detecting and Limiting Impact

From Event to Incident, From Incident to Control

Identification: Key Actions

  • •Monitor Systems: Continuously watch for suspicious activity.
  • •Analyze Logs: Review SIEM alerts and system logs for anomalies.
  • •Confirm Incident: Verify if a security event is a true incident.
  • •Scope Assessment: Determine the extent and nature of the compromise.
Key Takeaway

Rapid and accurate identification is crucial to trigger the incident response, while effective containment prevents further damage and limits the incident's overall impact.

Containment: Limiting the Damage

Isolate Systems

Disconnect compromised devices from the network.

Block Malicious IPs

Update firewalls to prevent communication with attackers.

Take Systems Offline

Temporarily shut down critical services if necessary.
