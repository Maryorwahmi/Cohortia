Core Concept

The Imperative of Security Operations (SecOps)

Beyond Prevention: Monitoring, Detection, and Response

What is SecOps?

  • •Continuous Monitoring: Ongoing surveillance of IT environment for security events.
  • •Threat Detection: Identifying suspicious activities and potential breaches.
  • •Incident Response: Structured actions to contain, eradicate, and recover from incidents.
  • •Analysis: Investigating alerts to distinguish true threats from false positives.
Key Takeaway

SecOps ensures an organization can actively defend against and recover from cyber incidents, complementing preventative measures with continuous vigilance and rapid response.

SecOps: The Active Defense Cycle

Monitor

Constant surveillance of systems and networks.

Detect

Identify anomalies and potential security events.

Analyze

Investigate alerts to confirm incidents and scope.

Respond

Execute actions to contain, eradicate, and recover.

SecOps is a continuous cycle, not a one-time setup. It requires constant vigilance and adaptation.
