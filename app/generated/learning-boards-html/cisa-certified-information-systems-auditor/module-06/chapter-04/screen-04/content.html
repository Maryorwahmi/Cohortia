Understanding Identity and Access Management

The Gatekeeper of Digital Resources

What is IAM?

A security discipline ensuring the right individuals/entities have the right access to the right resources at the right time and for the right reasons.

Why it Matters

Protects information assets, enforces least privilege, and supports compliance requirements, preventing unauthorized access and data breaches.

Auditor's Focus

Auditors assess IAM controls to identify weaknesses that could lead to security incidents or regulatory non-compliance.

Key Takeaway

IAM is a comprehensive security discipline that acts as a digital gatekeeper, crucial for protecting assets, enforcing least privilege, and maintaining compliance across an organization's IT landscape.
