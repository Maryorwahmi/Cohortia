Auditing IAM Systems: Policies and Accounts

Ensuring Robust Identity Lifecycle Management

01

Review IAM Policies

Ensure policies are comprehensive, align with business needs, and cover the entire identity lifecycle.

02

Examine User Account Management

Verify proper procedures for account creation, modification, and timely de-provisioning of terminated employees.

03

Assess Authentication Controls

Confirm strong password policies, MFA implementation, and secure credential storage.

Key Takeaway

Initial IAM audit procedures focus on reviewing comprehensive policies, verifying robust user account lifecycle management, and assessing the strength of authentication controls to ensure foundational security.
