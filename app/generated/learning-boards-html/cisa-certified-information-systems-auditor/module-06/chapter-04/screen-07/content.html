Authorization: What Are Your Permissions?

Defining Access Rights to Resources

  • Determines Access: Defines what an authenticated user can do.
  • Based on Roles: Permissions assigned to users or their job roles.
  • Least Privilege: Ensures minimum necessary access.
  • Auditor's Focus: Review proper configuration and enforcement.
Concrete Example

A 'Finance User' is authorized to view financial reports but not to modify system configurations, preventing unauthorized changes to critical infrastructure.

Authorization Flow
User Identity
Permissions
Resource Access

Authorization ensures that only permitted actions are taken on specific resources, aligning access with job responsibilities.

Key Takeaway

Authorization is the process of defining and enforcing what an authenticated user is allowed to do, crucial for maintaining the principle of least privilege and preventing unauthorized actions.
