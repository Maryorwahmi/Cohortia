Authentication: Are You Who You Say You Are?

Methods for Proving Your Digital Identity

  • Verifies Identity: Confirms the user is legitimate.
  • Three Factors: Something you know, have, or are.
  • MFA: Combines two or more factors for stronger security.
  • Auditor's Role: Assess strength and implementation of controls.
Common Misconception

Relying solely on passwords, even strong ones, is insufficient. Passwords can be stolen or guessed, making MFA essential for robust security.

Authentication Factors

Something You Know

Password, PIN, Secret Question

Something You Have

Smart Card, Token, Phone

Something You Are

Fingerprint, Facial Recognition

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Combines 2+ factors for enhanced security

Key Takeaway

Authentication is the process of verifying identity using various factors, with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) providing a crucial layer of defense against unauthorized access.
