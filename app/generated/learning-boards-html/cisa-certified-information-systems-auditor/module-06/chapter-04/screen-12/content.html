Identifying Common IAM Vulnerabilities

Weaknesses That Threaten Digital Security

Weak Passwords

Easily guessed or cracked, providing simple entry points.

Lack of MFA

Compromised passwords grant full access without a second factor.

Excessive Privileges

Users have more access than required, violating least privilege.

Orphaned Accounts

Active accounts of former employees, ripe for exploitation.

Privilege Escalation

Attackers gain higher access than authorized, leading to compromise.

Insecure Provisioning

Accounts created with excessive default privileges or not de-provisioned timely.

Key Takeaway

Identifying and mitigating common IAM vulnerabilities like weak passwords, lack of MFA, excessive privileges, and orphaned accounts is crucial for preventing unauthorized access and data breaches.
