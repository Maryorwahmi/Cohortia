Avoiding Common IAM Pitfalls

Proactive Measures for Stronger Security

Common Mistake: Neglecting De-provisioning

Leaving active accounts for former employees or contractors (orphaned accounts) creates significant security risks, as they can be exploited for unauthorized access.

Risk Implication

Unauthorized access, data breaches, and compliance violations if orphaned accounts are compromised.

Common Mistake: Privilege Creep

Users accumulate more permissions over time than needed for their current role, violating the principle of least privilege and expanding the attack surface.

Risk Implication

Increased potential for insider threats, accidental data exposure, and easier lateral movement for attackers.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must diligently identify and remediate common IAM pitfalls like neglected de-provisioning (orphaned accounts) and privilege creep to ensure robust access management and security assurance.
