Accounting: Tracking User Activities

Logging and Auditing User Actions

  • Tracks Actions: Records user activities and resource access.
  • Logs Events: Captures login attempts, file access, system changes.
  • Crucial For: Forensic analysis, incident response, compliance.
  • Auditor's Role: Verify log completeness, accuracy, and review.
Real-World Impact

Without proper accounting logs, it's impossible to determine who accessed sensitive customer data after a breach, hindering investigation and legal compliance.

Key Takeaway

Accounting, or auditing, is the process of logging all user activities and resource access, providing essential data for security investigations, incident response, and demonstrating regulatory compliance.
