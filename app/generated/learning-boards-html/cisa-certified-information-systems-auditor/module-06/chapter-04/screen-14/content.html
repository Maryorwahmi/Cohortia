Auditing IAM Systems: Authorization and Privileges

Verifying Access Rights and Activity Monitoring

04

Audit Authorization Mechanisms

Review ACLs, role assignments, and group memberships to enforce least privilege and prevent SoD conflicts.

05

Analyze Access Logs

Check for unauthorized attempts, unusual activity, and ensure logs are retained and regularly reviewed.

06

Review PAM Solutions & UARs

Ensure privileged accounts are managed, and periodic user access reviews are performed by business owners.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive IAM auditing extends to verifying authorization mechanisms, analyzing access logs, and reviewing privileged access management and user access reviews to ensure ongoing security and compliance.
