The AAA Pillars of IAM

Authentication, Authorization, and Accounting

Authentication

Verifying a user's identity: "Are you who you say you are?"

Authorization

Determining permissions: "What are you allowed to do?"

Accounting

Tracking user activities: "What did you do?"

Key Takeaway

The AAA pillars—Authentication, Authorization, and Accounting—form the sequential and interconnected framework for verifying identity, granting permissions, and tracking user actions within IAM systems.
