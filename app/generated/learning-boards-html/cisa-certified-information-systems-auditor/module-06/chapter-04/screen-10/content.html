Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Streamlining Permissions with Job Functions

  • Role-Centric: Permissions grouped into roles.
  • User Assignment: Users assigned to roles based on job function.
  • Scalability: Simplifies management in large organizations.
  • Least Privilege: Enforces minimum necessary access.
Example

A new employee is assigned the 'Marketing Specialist' role, automatically gaining access to all marketing tools and files without individual permission grants.

RBAC Hierarchy

Roles

(e.g., HR Manager, DB Admin)

Permissions

(e.g., Read, Write, Execute)

Users

Key Takeaway

RBAC simplifies access management by assigning permissions to roles, which users inherit, making it scalable and effective for enforcing least privilege in enterprise environments.
