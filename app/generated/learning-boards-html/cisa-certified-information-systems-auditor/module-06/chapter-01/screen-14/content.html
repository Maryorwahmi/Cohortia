Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from Information Security Governance

InfoSec Governance

The system by which an organization directs and controls information security activities to achieve its objectives.

CISO

The senior executive responsible for an organization's information security program.

InfoSec Policy

High-level, mandatory statements outlining management's commitment and requirements for information security.

Summary Takeaway

Effective information security is driven by strong governance, led by the CISO, and guided by clear, high-level policies.
