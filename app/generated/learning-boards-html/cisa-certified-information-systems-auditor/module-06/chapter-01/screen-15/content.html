Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from Information Security Governance

InfoSec Standard

Mandatory requirements specifying technologies, configurations, or methods to implement policies.

InfoSec Procedure

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for performing specific tasks to comply with policies and standards.

InfoSec Guideline

Non-mandatory recommendations and best practices for secure behavior.

Summary Takeaway

The documentation hierarchy (standards, procedures, guidelines) provides granular direction, while Segregation of Duties ensures critical tasks are distributed for enhanced control.
