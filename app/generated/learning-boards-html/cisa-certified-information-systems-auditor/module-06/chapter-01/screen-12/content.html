Control Principle

Segregation of Duties and Personnel Awareness

Ensuring checks and balances in security operations.

What is Segregation of Duties (SoD)?

SoD is a control principle designed to prevent fraud and error by distributing critical tasks among different individuals. It ensures no single person can authorize, implement, and review a critical change, creating a system of checks and balances.

Key Takeaway

SoD and continuous awareness training are vital for mitigating internal risks and ensuring compliance.

SoD in Practice

Separate Roles

Different individuals for authorization, implementation, and review of critical tasks.

Reduced Risk

Minimizes opportunities for fraud, error, and unauthorized access.

Personnel Awareness & Training

  • Security Awareness Training: Regular training for all employees.
  • Role-Specific Training: Tailored training for technical and specialized roles.
  • Certifications: Evidence of necessary skills for security professionals.
