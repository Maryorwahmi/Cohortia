Documentation Deep Dive

Guidelines: Recommendations and Best Practices

Offering flexible advice for secure behavior.

What are Guidelines?

Guidelines offer non-mandatory recommendations and best practices. They provide flexibility while still promoting secure behavior, often used when strict adherence isn't feasible or when a situation requires judgment.

Key Takeaway

Guidelines are flexible recommendations that promote security awareness and best practices without being strictly mandatory.

Key Characteristics of Guidelines

Non-Mandatory

Offers advice, not strict rules, allowing for flexibility.

Best Practices

Promotes secure behavior and informed decision-making.

Contextual Advice

Useful when strict rules are impractical or require judgment.

Common Mistake: Outdated Documentation

A frequent audit finding is outdated policies or procedures that no longer reflect the current technological environment or regulatory landscape, leading to control gaps.
