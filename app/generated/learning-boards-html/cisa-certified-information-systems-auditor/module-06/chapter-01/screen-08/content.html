Standards: Implementing Policies with Requirements

Defining the mandatory technical and procedural requirements.

What are Standards?

Standards provide mandatory requirements for implementing policies, often specifying technologies, configurations, or methods. They translate high-level policy statements into actionable, enforceable rules for 'how' security is achieved.

Key Takeaway

Standards are mandatory, specific requirements that bridge policies to practical implementation, ensuring consistency.

Key Characteristics of Standards

Mandatory

Compliance is required to meet policy objectives.

Specific Requirements

Details technologies, configurations, or methods.

Enforce Policy

Provides the 'how-to' for meeting high-level policy objectives.

Example: Password Standard

Dictates minimum password length (e.g., 12 characters), complexity (e.g., uppercase, lowercase, number, special character), and rotation frequency (e.g., every 90 days).
