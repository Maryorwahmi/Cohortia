Key Characteristics of Procedures

  • Detailed Steps: Granular, explicit instructions.
  • Task-Specific: Focus on how to perform a single task.
  • Mandatory: Must be followed for compliance.
  • Consistency: Ensures tasks are performed uniformly.

Auditor's Focus

Auditors verify that procedures are accurate, up-to-date, and consistently followed by personnel. Deviations can indicate control weaknesses.

Key Takeaway

Procedures are the 'how-to' guides, ensuring specific tasks are performed consistently and compliantly.
