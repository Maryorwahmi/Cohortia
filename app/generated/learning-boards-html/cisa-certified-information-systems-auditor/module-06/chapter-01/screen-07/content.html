Policies: High-Level Directives

Understanding the foundational statements of security intent.

What are Policies?

Policies are high-level, mandatory statements that articulate management's intent and commitment to information security. They define the 'what' and 'why' of security, setting the overall strategic direction without detailing implementation specifics.

Key Takeaway

Policies are the top-level security directives, mandatory and strategic, reflecting management's commitment.

Key Characteristics of Policies

Mandatory

Compliance is required for all personnel and systems within scope.

High-Level

Focus on principles and objectives, not granular technical details.

Management Intent

Reflects the organization's commitment and strategic approach to security.

Example: Acceptable Use Policy

States that all company assets must be used for legitimate business purposes. It sets the expectation for employee conduct without specifying technical configurations.
