Auditor's Role

Verifying Risk Treatment and Oversight

Ensuring Controls and Management Buy-in

Key Audit Steps (Part 2)

  • Examine Treatment Plans: Check for effective control implementation and formal acceptance of residual risks.
  • Verify Monitoring & Review: Confirm regular risk re-assessment, periodic control testing, and program responsiveness.
  • Check Management Buy-in: Look for evidence of senior management involvement and approval in risk decisions.

Auditor's Tool: Risk Register

Use the risk register to verify that identified risks, assessments, and treatment plans are current and tracked for completion.

Key Takeaway

Auditors provide assurance that risk treatment is effective, monitoring is continuous, and management actively supports the risk program, ensuring robust protection of information assets.

Auditor's Checklist: Action & Oversight
04

Examine Risk Treatment Plans

Are controls effectively implemented? Are residual risks formally accepted by management? Verify that actions align with strategy.

05

Verify Monitoring and Review

Are risks regularly re-assessed? Are controls tested periodically? Is the program responsive to changes in the threat landscape?

06

Check for Management Buy-in

Is there evidence of senior management involvement and approval for risk decisions? This indicates strategic commitment to security.
