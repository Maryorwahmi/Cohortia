Threat Landscape

Navigating the Modern Threat Landscape

Understanding Common Information Security Threats

Key Threat Categories

The digital world faces a variety of sophisticated and evolving threats:

  • Malware: Viruses, ransomware, spyware.
  • Phishing/Social Engineering: Exploiting human trust.
  • DoS/DDoS Attacks: Overwhelming systems.
  • Insider Threats: Malicious or negligent internal actions.
  • APTs: Long-term, targeted attacks.
Key Takeaway

Organizations must continuously monitor for a diverse range of threats, from widespread malware to targeted APTs and often-overlooked insider risks, to maintain a strong security posture.

Common Threat Types

Malware

Viruses, ransomware, spyware disrupting systems.

Phishing

Tricking users to reveal sensitive information.

DoS/DDoS

Overwhelming systems to make them unavailable.

Insider Threats

Malicious or negligent actions by employees.
