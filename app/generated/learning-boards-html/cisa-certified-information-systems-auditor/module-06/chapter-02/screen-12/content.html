Auditor's Role

Auditing the Risk Management Framework

Assessing the Foundation of Security Decisions

Key Audit Steps (Part 1)

  • Review Framework: Check for defined methodology (e.g., ISO 27005, NIST SP 800-30) and its appropriateness.
  • Assess Identification: Verify comprehensive asset inventories and regular review of threat/vulnerability sources.
  • Evaluate Analysis & Evaluation: Examine soundness of methodology, consistency of risk ratings, and clear definition of business impacts.
Key Takeaway

A robust risk management program starts with a well-defined framework, comprehensive risk identification, and a sound, consistent analysis and evaluation process, all of which auditors must critically assess.

Auditor's Checklist: Foundation
01

Review Risk Management Framework

Is there a defined methodology (e.g., ISO 27005, NIST SP 800-30)? Is it appropriate for the organization's context?

02

Assess Risk Identification

Are asset inventories comprehensive? Are threat and vulnerability sources regularly reviewed and updated?

03

Evaluate Risk Analysis and Evaluation

Is the assessment methodology sound? Are risk ratings consistent and justified? Are business impacts clearly defined?
