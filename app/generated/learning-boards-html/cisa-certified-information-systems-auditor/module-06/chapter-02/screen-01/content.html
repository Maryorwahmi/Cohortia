Chapter 6.2

Risk Management and Threat Landscape

Navigating Information Security for IT Auditors

This chapter provides a comprehensive overview of information security risk management, defining key concepts like threats, vulnerabilities, and impacts. It details the systematic process of identifying, assessing, and treating risks, explores the modern threat landscape, and outlines the critical steps an IT auditor takes to evaluate an organization's risk management program for effectiveness and compliance.

Key Idea

IT auditors ensure organizations systematically identify, assess, and treat information security risks to protect assets and maintain resilience.
