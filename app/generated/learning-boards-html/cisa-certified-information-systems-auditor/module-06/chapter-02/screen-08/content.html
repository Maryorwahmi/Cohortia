Detailed Approaches to Managing Risk

Choosing the Right Response for Each Risk

Strategic Responses to Risk

Organizations employ various strategies to modify risks to an acceptable level:

  • Mitigation: Reduce likelihood or impact.
  • Acceptance: Consciously take no action.
  • Avoidance: Eliminate the risk-causing activity.
  • Transfer: Shift financial consequences to another party.
Key Takeaway

The choice of risk treatment strategy depends on a careful assessment of risk severity, cost-benefit, and the organization's overall risk appetite, requiring formal documentation.

Treatment Options

Mitigation

Implement controls to reduce risk likelihood or impact. (e.g., patching software)

Acceptance

Consciously decide to take no action. (e.g., low impact, high cost)

Avoidance

Eliminate the activity causing the risk. (e.g., decommissioning a system)

Transfer

Shift financial consequences to a third party. (e.g., cyber insurance)
