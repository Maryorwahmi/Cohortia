Risk Management Process

Step 3 & 4: Evaluation and Treatment

Prioritizing and Responding to Identified Risks

Key Activities

  • Risk Evaluation: Comparing estimated risk levels against criteria to determine significance and prioritize.
  • Risk Treatment: Selecting and implementing measures to modify risks using strategies like mitigation, acceptance, avoidance, or transfer.

Auditor's Focus

Verify that risk criteria are clear and consistently applied, and that treatment plans are effective with formally accepted residual risks.

Key Takeaway

Effective risk evaluation ensures proper prioritization, while strategic risk treatment ensures appropriate responses are implemented and residual risks are managed.

Prioritizing and Acting
03

Risk Evaluation

Compare analyzed risk levels against predefined criteria to determine their significance. This helps prioritize which risks need immediate attention. For example, a 'high' risk might trigger an urgent security patch.

04

Risk Treatment

Select and implement measures to modify risks. This includes strategies like mitigation (reducing likelihood/impact), acceptance (taking no action), avoidance (eliminating the risk source), or transfer (shifting risk, e.g., insurance).
