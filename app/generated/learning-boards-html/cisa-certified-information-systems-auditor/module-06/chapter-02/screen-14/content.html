Chapter Summary

Core Risk Terminology

Essential Definitions for IT Auditors

Information Security Risk

The potential for loss or harm to an organization's information assets due to a threat exploiting a vulnerability.

Threat

A potential cause of an unwanted incident, which may result in harm to a system or organization (e.g., malware attack).

Vulnerability

A weakness in an asset or control that can be exploited by one or more threats (e.g., unpatched software).

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the distinct roles of risk, threats, and vulnerabilities is foundational for any IT auditor to effectively assess and manage an organization's security posture.
