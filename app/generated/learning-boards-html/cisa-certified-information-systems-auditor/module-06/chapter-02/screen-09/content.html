Risk Management Process

Step 5: Monitoring and Review

Ensuring Continuous Effectiveness and Adaptability

Key Activities

  • Track identified risks and monitor risk controls.
  • Identify new risks and changes in the threat landscape.
  • Ensure the process is continuous, regularly updated, and responsive.

Auditor's Focus

Assess whether the risk management process is dynamic, responsive to changes, and if controls are regularly tested and updated.

Key Takeaway

Continuous monitoring and review are essential to maintain the relevance and effectiveness of a risk management program in a dynamic threat landscape.

The Continuous Loop
05

Risk Monitoring and Review

This ongoing step involves tracking identified risks, monitoring the effectiveness of implemented controls, and identifying new or emerging risks. It ensures the risk management program remains relevant and effective over time.

Adaptation and Improvement

Based on monitoring results, the organization adapts its risk strategy, updates controls, and refines its risk assessment methodologies to respond to changes in the threat landscape or internal environment.
