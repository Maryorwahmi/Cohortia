Vulnerability Landscape

Identifying Common Vulnerabilities

Weaknesses Exploited by Modern Threats

Key Vulnerability Categories

Weaknesses in systems or controls that can be exploited by threats:

  • Unpatched Software: Known security flaws.
  • Misconfigurations: Default settings, open ports.
  • Weak Access Controls: Insufficient authentication/authorization.
  • Lack of Encryption: Data exposed in transit or at rest.
  • Poor Coding Practices: Application-level flaws.
  • Human Error: Accidental mistakes by users.
Key Takeaway

Proactive identification and remediation of vulnerabilities through regular scanning, secure configurations, and user training are essential to prevent threat exploitation.
