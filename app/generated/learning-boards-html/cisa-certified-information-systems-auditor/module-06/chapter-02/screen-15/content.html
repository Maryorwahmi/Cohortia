Chapter Summary

Risk Management in Practice

Strategies and Tools for Effective Oversight

Impact

The magnitude of harm that could result from the exploitation of a vulnerability by a threat (e.g., financial loss).

Risk Management Process

The systematic application of management policies and practices to identify, analyze, evaluate, treat, monitor, and review risk.

Risk Register

A document used to record, track, and manage risks, including their identification, assessment, and treatment plans.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk management relies on understanding potential impacts, following a systematic process, and utilizing tools like the risk register for comprehensive tracking and oversight.
