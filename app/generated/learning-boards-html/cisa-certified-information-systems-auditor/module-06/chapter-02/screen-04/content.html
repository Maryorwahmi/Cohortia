Understanding Information Security Risk

Managing Potential Loss to an Acceptable Level

What is Information Security Risk?

Information security risk is the potential for loss or harm to an organization's information assets. This harm can manifest as financial loss, reputational damage, or operational disruption.

  • It's a systematic process, not a one-time event.
  • Focuses on managing risk to an acceptable level, not eliminating all risk.
  • Forms the basis for all security decisions and control implementations.
Key Takeaway

Risk management is a continuous, strategic process to protect information assets by reducing potential harm to an acceptable level, guided by systematic identification and treatment.
