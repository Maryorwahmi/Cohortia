Risk Management Process

Step 1 & 2: Identification and Analysis

Discovering and Understanding the Nature of Risks

Key Activities

  • Risk Identification: Discovering, recognizing, and describing risks by identifying assets, threats, and vulnerabilities.
  • Risk Analysis: Understanding the nature of risks and determining the level of risk, using qualitative or quantitative methods.

Auditor's Focus

Review asset inventories for completeness and assess the consistency and appropriateness of risk analysis methodologies.

Key Takeaway

Thorough risk identification and sound analysis are foundational for effective risk management, enabling informed decisions on risk prioritization and treatment.

The First Two Steps
01

Risk Identification

Systematically discover and describe risks. This includes identifying all critical assets, potential threats, and existing vulnerabilities. For example, reviewing a server inventory to ensure all production systems are listed.

02

Risk Analysis

Understand the nature of identified risks and determine their level. This can be qualitative (e.g., High, Medium, Low) or quantitative (e.g., estimated financial loss). An auditor would check if the analysis methodology is consistent and justified.
