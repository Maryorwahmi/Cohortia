Deployment & Maintenance: Sustaining Security

Ensuring ongoing security post-deployment and throughout the system's life.

Deployment: Focus on secure configuration management and hardening servers before launch.

Maintenance: Implement regular patching, vulnerability management, and continuous monitoring.

Auditor's Role: Review configuration baselines, patch management, and incident response procedures.

Key Takeaway

Secure deployment and continuous maintenance are critical for protecting systems against evolving threats throughout their operational lifespan.

Concrete Example

After deploying a new server, the IT team ensures all default passwords are changed, unnecessary network ports are closed, and a regular patching schedule is established. This prevents common attack vectors and keeps the system secure over time.

Common Mistake

Neglecting to apply security patches promptly or failing to regularly review system configurations can leave systems vulnerable to known exploits, even if they were securely designed and coded.

Why it Matters

The threat landscape is constantly changing. Robust deployment and maintenance practices ensure that systems remain protected against new vulnerabilities and evolving attack techniques, preserving their integrity and confidentiality.
