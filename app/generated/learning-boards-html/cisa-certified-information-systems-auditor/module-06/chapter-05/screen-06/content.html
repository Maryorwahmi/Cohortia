Network Defense

Firewalls: The Network Gatekeepers

Controlling traffic flow based on security rules.

Firewall Fundamentals

  • Barrier: Sits between trusted (internal) and untrusted (external) networks.
  • Rule-Based: Filters traffic based on predefined security policies.
  • Monitor & Control: Inspects packets and decides to allow or deny them.
  • Logging: Records traffic and blocked attempts for auditing and incident response.

Types of Firewalls

Packet-Filtering Firewall

Examines individual packets based on IP addresses, ports, and protocols. Simple and fast, but lacks context.

Stateful Inspection Firewall

Tracks the state of active connections, making intelligent decisions. More secure and efficient than packet filters.

Key Takeaway

Firewalls are the first line of defense, enforcing access policies and preventing unauthorized network communication.

Audit Focus

Auditors must verify firewall rules are restrictive, necessary, and regularly reviewed to prevent 'any-any' rules or outdated policies.
