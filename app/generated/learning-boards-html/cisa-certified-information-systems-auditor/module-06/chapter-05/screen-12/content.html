Endpoint Configuration

Configuration Hardening and Mobile Device Management

Securing systems through baselines and managing mobile access.

Configuration Hardening

  • Reduce Attack Surface: Disable unnecessary services and close unused ports.
  • Secure Baselines: Apply predefined secure configurations.
  • Proactive: Minimizes vulnerabilities from the initial setup.

Mobile Device Management (MDM)

Policy Enforcement

Manages and secures mobile devices, enforcing policies like strong passwords and encryption.

Remote Capabilities

Includes remote wipe and application management for lost/stolen devices or compliance.

Key Takeaway

Hardening and MDM are crucial for minimizing vulnerabilities and securing the increasingly diverse range of endpoints in an organization.

Audit Focus

Auditors verify that secure baselines are applied and that MDM policies are comprehensive and enforced across all mobile devices.
