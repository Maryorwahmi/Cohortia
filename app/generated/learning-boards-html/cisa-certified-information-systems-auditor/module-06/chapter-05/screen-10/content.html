Endpoint Protection

Securing Individual Devices: Anti-Malware and EDR

Protecting laptops, desktops, and servers from malicious software.

Anti-Malware / Antivirus

  • Purpose: Detect, prevent, and remove malicious software.
  • Detection: Primarily signature-based, identifying known threats.
  • Audit Check: Up-to-date definitions, regular scans, centralized management.

Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)

Advanced Monitoring

Continuously monitors endpoints for suspicious activity and collects telemetry data.

Threat Response

Provides capabilities for advanced threat detection, investigation, and automated response (e.g., isolation).

Key Takeaway

EDR offers a more proactive and comprehensive defense against modern threats compared to traditional anti-malware solutions.

Misconception

Traditional antivirus alone is often insufficient against sophisticated, fileless, or zero-day attacks.
