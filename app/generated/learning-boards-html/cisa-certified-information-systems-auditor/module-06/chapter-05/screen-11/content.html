Endpoint Hardening

Host-Based Firewalls and Patch Management

Essential controls for securing individual endpoints.

Host-Based Firewalls

  • Local Protection: Controls network traffic specific to an individual device.
  • Granular Rules: Can restrict application-level communication.
  • Mobility: Provides protection even when devices are outside the corporate network.

Patch Management

Vulnerability Remediation

Systematic process of applying software updates to fix security vulnerabilities.

Critical Control

One of the most effective ways to prevent exploitation of known software flaws.

Key Takeaway

Timely patch management and host-based firewalls are non-negotiable for maintaining a strong endpoint security posture.

Audit Focus

Auditors must verify the existence of a robust, automated patch management process and consistent host-based firewall configurations.
