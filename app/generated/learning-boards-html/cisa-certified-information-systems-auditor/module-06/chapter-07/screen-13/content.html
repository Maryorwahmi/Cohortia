Common Audit Points in IAM
Identifying Weaknesses in Access Management

Common IAM audit findings include inadequate de-provisioning, weak privileged account controls, lack of regular access reviews leading to privilege creep, and insufficient password policies.

Key Takeaway

Thorough auditing of IAM processes is essential to identify and remediate vulnerabilities that could lead to unauthorized access.

Frequent Finding

Inadequate de-provisioning and privilege creep are persistent vulnerabilities that require continuous attention and process improvement.

IAM Audit Points
De-provisioning, Privileged Access, Reviews
