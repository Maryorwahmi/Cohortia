Managing the Identity Lifecycle
From Onboarding to Offboarding

The identity lifecycle involves provisioning (granting access), de-provisioning (revoking access), and regular access reviews. Prompt de-provisioning and periodic reviews are critical to prevent unauthorized access and privilege creep.

Key Takeaway

A robust identity lifecycle management process is essential for maintaining security and compliance by ensuring access is granted appropriately and revoked promptly.

Common Audit Finding

Incomplete or delayed de-provisioning of accounts for terminated employees is a frequent and serious security vulnerability.
