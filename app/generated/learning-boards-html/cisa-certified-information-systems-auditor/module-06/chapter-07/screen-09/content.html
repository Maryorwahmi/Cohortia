Security Monitoring
The Role of Security Monitoring
Continuous Vigilance for Threats

Security monitoring provides continuous visibility to detect, analyze, and respond to security incidents. It acts as a crucial complement to IAM, identifying threats that may bypass preventative controls.

Key Takeaway

Effective security monitoring is essential for timely threat detection and incident response, minimizing potential damage and downtime.

Audit Focus

Auditors assess the scope of log collection, retention policies, SIEM configuration, alert tuning, and the responsiveness of the security operations team.
