Verifying Identity: The AAA Principles
Authentication, Authorization, and Accounting

The AAA principles are fundamental to IAM: Authentication verifies who you are (e.g., password, MFA), Authorization determines what you can do (e.g., read, write), and Accounting logs your actions for audit trails.

Key Takeaway

The AAA framework provides a structured approach to managing and securing access by confirming identity, defining permissions, and tracking activities.

Audit Focus

Auditors examine authentication strength, authorization policies (like least privilege), and the completeness and retention of accounting logs.
