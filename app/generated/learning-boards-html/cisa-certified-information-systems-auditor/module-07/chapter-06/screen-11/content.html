Overcoming Alert Fatigue and False Positives

Strategies for effective SIEM tuning and rule refinement.

1. Baseline Normal Activity

Understand the Norm

Establish what constitutes 'normal' network and system behavior to better identify anomalies.

2. Refine Correlation Rules

Increase Precision

Adjust rule logic and thresholds to reduce false positives while maintaining detection capability for true threats.

3. Suppress Irrelevant Alerts

Focus on Criticality

Implement processes to suppress or deprioritize alerts that are consistently non-malicious or low-impact.

Example: Tuning a Login Rule

A correlation rule detects 'multiple failed logins from a single source IP followed by a successful login from a different IP within 5 minutes.' Tuning involves adjusting the number of failed attempts or time window, or adding exceptions for known legitimate activities.

Key Takeaway

Continuous SIEM tuning, including baselining and rule refinement, is vital to minimize false positives, prevent alert fatigue, and ensure critical threats are not missed.
