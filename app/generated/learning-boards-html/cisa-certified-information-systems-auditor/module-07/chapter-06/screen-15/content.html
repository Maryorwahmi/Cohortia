Key Concepts

Understanding SIEM challenges and data integrity.

Alert Fatigue

A state experienced by security analysts when they receive an excessive number of alerts, leading to missed critical incidents.

False Positive

An alert generated by a SIEM that indicates a security incident when none exists, contributing to alert fatigue.

Log Integrity

The assurance that log data has not been altered or tampered with since its creation, crucial for forensic and compliance purposes.

Summary Takeaway

Addressing alert fatigue and false positives through effective SIEM tuning, and ensuring log integrity, are vital for reliable security monitoring.
