Core Concept

The Role of SIEM and Log Management

Centralizing visibility for effective incident response.

Foundation of Defense

Log management and SIEM are the 'eyes and ears' of an organization's security, enabling detection, analysis, and response to incidents.

CISA Auditor's Task

Assess an organization's ability to leverage IT system data for effective security monitoring and incident response.

Log Lifecycle Evaluation

Audit the entire process: log generation, collection, aggregation, analysis, retention, and secure disposal.

Key Takeaway

A robust SIEM and log management system is essential for centralized visibility, correlating events, and transforming raw data into actionable security intelligence.
