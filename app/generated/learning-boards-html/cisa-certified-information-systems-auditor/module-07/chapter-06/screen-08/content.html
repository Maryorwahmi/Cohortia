Example: Account Compromise Detection

A SIEM correlates multiple failed VPN login attempts with a successful login from an unusual geographic location to trigger an alert for a potential account compromise.

Key Takeaway

SIEM's ability to aggregate, normalize, and correlate disparate log events is crucial for identifying complex security incidents that individual logs cannot reveal.
