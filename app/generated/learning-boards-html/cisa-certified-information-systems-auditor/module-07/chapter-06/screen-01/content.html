SIEM and Log Management Audit

Ensuring Vigilant Security Monitoring and Incident Response

In this chapter, you will learn how to evaluate an organization's Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) solutions and log management processes. We'll cover the entire lifecycle of security logs, from generation to analysis, and explore how these systems contribute to effective threat detection and incident response. This knowledge is crucial for any CISA auditor aiming to strengthen an organization's defensive capabilities.

Key Idea

SIEM and log management provide the essential 'eyes and ears' for an organization's defense, turning disparate data into unified, actionable security insights.
