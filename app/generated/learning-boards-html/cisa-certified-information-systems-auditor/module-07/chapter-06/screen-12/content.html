Auditor's Toolkit

Formulating SIEM and Log Management Audit Steps

A systematic approach to verifying security controls.

01

Review SIEM Architecture

Understand how logs are collected, parsed, and stored within the SIEM system.

02

Examine Correlation Rules

Verify rules are relevant to the organization's threat landscape (e.g., ransomware, data exfiltration).

03

Analyze Alert Logs

Review a sample of SIEM alerts to assess legitimacy, investigation speed, and resolution.

04

Test Log Integrity

Inquire about and verify mechanisms protecting logs from tampering (e.g., WORM storage, digital signatures).

05

Assess Retention Policies

Ensure logs are retained for the required duration, considering legal, regulatory, and business needs (e.g., PCI-DSS).

Key Takeaway

A structured audit approach, covering architecture, rules, alerts, integrity, and retention, provides assurance that SIEM and log management effectively support security posture.
