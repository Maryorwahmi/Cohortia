Core Concept

Data Encryption on Endpoints

Full Disk Encryption (FDE) protects data at rest on laptops and mobile devices, making it unreadable if the device is lost or stolen, provided recovery keys are managed securely.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must verify that FDE is enforced, and crucially, that recovery keys are securely stored and managed to ensure data accessibility and prevent unauthorized access.

Audit Focus
  • Verify FDE is enabled on all required laptops and mobile devices.
  • Confirm secure storage and management of FDE recovery keys.
  • Check for established key escrow procedures for business continuity.
  • Assess if data classification policies dictate encryption requirements.
Critical Consideration

The security of recovery keys is paramount. If keys are lost, data is inaccessible; if they are compromised, encryption is useless.
