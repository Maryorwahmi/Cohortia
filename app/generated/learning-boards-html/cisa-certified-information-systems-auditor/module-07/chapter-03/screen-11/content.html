Formulating Audit Procedures

Developing clear, actionable audit steps to verify compliance with endpoint and mobile security policies, ensuring a systematic and thorough assessment.

Key Takeaway

Well-defined audit procedures are essential for a systematic and effective assessment of endpoint and mobile device security controls and policy compliance.

Steps in Formulation
  • Define audit scope and objectives based on policies and regulations.
  • Identify specific controls and policies to be tested (e.g., anti-malware, MDM).
  • Determine evidence required (e.g., reports, console access, device checks).
  • Outline specific actions for evidence collection and verification.
  • Plan for documentation of steps, evidence, and findings.
  • Ensure procedures are SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).
Example Procedure

To audit anti-malware: Request management console reports for signature updates and scan logs, then verify on a sample of endpoints that the software is active and current.
