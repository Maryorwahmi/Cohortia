Core Concept

Mobile Device Management (MDM)

MDM enables organizations to centrally manage and secure mobile devices, enforcing policies, managing apps, and enabling remote actions like wiping lost devices.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must verify that MDM solutions are effectively implemented and policies are rigorously enforced to protect corporate data on mobile devices, especially in BYOD environments.

Audit Focus Areas
  • Enrollment and provisioning security.
  • Policy enforcement (passcodes, encryption, app restrictions).
  • Functionality of remote wipe/lock capabilities.
  • Application management and approval processes.
  • Data containerization for BYOD scenarios.
Common Mistake

Deploying MDM without fully utilizing its security features or rigorously enforcing policies, such as requiring passcodes on personal devices.
