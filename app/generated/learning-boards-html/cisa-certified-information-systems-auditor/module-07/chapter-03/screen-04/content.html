Core Concept

Endpoint Security Fundamentals

Endpoints are the devices users interact with daily, making them critical targets. Auditors must verify foundational controls to protect against threats that bypass network defenses.

Key Takeaway

Robust endpoint security is essential because these devices are often the last line of defense and the most accessible entry points for attackers. Comprehensive auditing ensures these defenses are effective and up-to-date.

Why It Matters
  • Endpoints are direct access points for users and data.
  • They are frequently outside the network perimeter, increasing exposure.
  • Compromised endpoints can lead to widespread system breaches.
  • Effective controls protect against malware, phishing, and unauthorized access.
Common Pitfall

Relying solely on basic anti-malware without advanced detection or layered defenses is a significant risk. Modern threats require more sophisticated protection mechanisms.
