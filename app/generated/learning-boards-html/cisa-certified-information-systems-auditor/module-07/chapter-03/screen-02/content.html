Learning Objectives

What You Will Be Able to Do

01
Evaluate Endpoint Controls
Assess anti-malware, host intrusion prevention, and data encryption effectiveness to ensure robust device-level protection against threats.
02
Assess MDM Solutions
Examine Mobile Device Management (MDM) implementations, policy enforcement, and BYOD security for mobile fleets.
03
Identify Security Risks
Recognize common vulnerabilities and threats associated with mobile devices and remote endpoints in various operational contexts.
04
Formulate Audit Procedures
Develop specific, actionable audit steps to verify compliance with endpoint and mobile security policies effectively.
