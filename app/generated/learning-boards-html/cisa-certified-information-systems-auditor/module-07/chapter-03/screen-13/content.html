Key Concepts

What to Remember

Endpoint Security

Protects networks by securing client devices like laptops and servers with controls like anti-malware and HIPS.

MDM & Containerization

MDM manages mobile devices; containerization isolates corporate data on BYOD devices.

Audit Procedures

Systematic steps to verify compliance with security policies and identify vulnerabilities in device controls.

Comprehensive auditing ensures data protection across all organizational endpoints and mobile devices.
