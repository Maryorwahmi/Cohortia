Core Concept

Anti-Malware and EDR

Anti-malware detects known threats, while EDR provides continuous monitoring and behavioral analysis to identify novel and sophisticated attacks that signature-based methods miss.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must verify that organizations use up-to-date anti-malware and ideally supplement it with EDR for comprehensive threat detection and response capabilities.

Audit Focus
  • Verify anti-malware deployment and up-to-date signatures.
  • Check scan frequencies and remediation rates.
  • Inquire about EDR capabilities for advanced threat detection.
  • Review EDR reports for suspicious activities and incident response actions.
Common Mistake

Relying solely on signature-based anti-malware without advanced detection methods like EDR leaves systems vulnerable to zero-day and polymorphic threats.
