Practical DLP Audit Scenario

A Step-by-Step Walkthrough for PCI Compliance

An organization processes customer credit card data ('Confidential - PCI'). Their DLP solution prevents this data from being emailed externally or copied to unencrypted USB drives. Here's how you'd audit it:

Key Takeaway

Apply audit steps to a real-world scenario to verify DLP and data classification effectiveness for compliance.

01Review DLP Policies

Verify rules target credit card numbers (e.g., regex patterns) and the 'Confidential - PCI' tag.

02Test DLP Effectiveness

Conduct simulated tests (with authorization) to email dummy credit card numbers or copy them to USB drives.

03Examine Incident Logs

Review DLP logs for blocked attempts and alerts. Confirm security personnel review and act upon these incidents.

04Interview Users/Admins

Discuss their understanding of data classification and DLP policies, and any challenges they face.

05Review Data Storage

Verify 'Confidential - PCI' data is stored only in approved, secure locations, not on unencrypted shares.
