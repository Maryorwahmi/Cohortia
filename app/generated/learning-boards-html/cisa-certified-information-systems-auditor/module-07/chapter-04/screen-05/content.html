Data Classification: Categorizing Information by Sensitivity

Understanding Data Classification Levels and Enforcement

Data classification is the process of categorizing information based on its sensitivity, value, and the impact its compromise would have on the organization. It's the essential first step before implementing protection measures.

  • •
    Purpose: To determine appropriate protection measures, access controls, and handling procedures.
  • •
    Key Question: What data is sensitive, how sensitive is it, and what are the consequences of its compromise?
Key Takeaway

Data classification assigns sensitivity levels to information, guiding appropriate protection measures.

Public

Information freely available to anyone, with no restrictions on disclosure.

Internal Use Only

Intended for internal organizational use; not for public disclosure.

Confidential

Sensitive business information; unauthorized disclosure could cause harm.

Restricted / Top Secret

Highly critical data; severe impact if compromised, often legally protected.
