Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Technologies and processes designed to prevent sensitive information from leaving an organization's control, whether intentionally or accidentally.

Data Classification

The process of categorizing data based on its sensitivity, value, and regulatory requirements to determine appropriate protection measures.

Data Exfiltration

The unauthorized transfer of data from a computer or network, representing a significant security breach risk.

Summary Takeaway

DLP and data classification are fundamental to preventing unauthorized data loss and ensuring compliance with security policies.
