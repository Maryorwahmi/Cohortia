Core Concept

The Evolving Digital Landscape

Opportunities and Challenges of Emerging Technologies

New technologies introduce both incredible opportunities and novel security challenges. Auditors must move beyond traditional security paradigms to address the complexities of cloud, IoT, AI/ML, and sophisticated adversaries.

Opportunities

Increased efficiency, scalability, new business models.

Challenges

Novel attack vectors, complex risk profiles, shared responsibilities.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must continuously adapt their knowledge and methods to address the security implications of new technologies.

Cloud Computing

Shared responsibility, data residency

Internet of Things

Device sprawl, firmware vulnerabilities

AI/ML Systems

Data bias, model integrity, explainability

Advanced Threats

Persistent, sophisticated, targeted
