AI/ML Security and Ethical Considerations

Protecting Models and Ensuring Fair Outcomes

  • • Data Security & Privacy: Securing training data and protecting sensitive information.
  • • Model Integrity: Protection from adversarial attacks (e.g., data poisoning, evasion).
  • • Bias & Fairness: Ensuring models are free from discriminatory bias.
  • • Explainability: Ability to understand and audit model decisions.

Example Scenario

An AI model used for loan applications must be audited to ensure its training data is unbiased and its decisions are explainable to prevent unfair lending practices.

Key Takeaway

AI/ML systems introduce risks related to data security, model integrity (adversarial attacks), bias, and explainability, requiring specialized audit focus.

AI/ML Risk Vectors

Data, Model, Bias, Explainability.

Ethical Implications

Ensuring fairness and preventing discriminatory outcomes.
