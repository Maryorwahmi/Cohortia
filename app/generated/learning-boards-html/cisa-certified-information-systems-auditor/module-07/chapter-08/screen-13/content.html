Audit Procedures

Auditing for APT Preparedness

Assessing an Organization's Resilience Against Advanced Threats

  • • Threat Intelligence: Active use of relevant feeds.
  • • Proactive Defense: EDR, sandboxing, behavioral analytics.
  • • Detection Capabilities: SIEM/SOC for subtle IoCs.
  • • Incident Response: Trained IR team, robust forensics.
  • • Security Awareness: Training against social engineering.

Audit Recommendation

Verify regular penetration testing and red teaming exercises that simulate APT tactics.

Key Takeaway

APT preparedness audits must cover threat intelligence, proactive defenses, detection, incident response, and security awareness, with continuous testing.

APT Defense Pillars

Intelligence, Proactive, Detect, Respond, Awareness.

Continuous Adaptation

Security posture must evolve to counter advanced threats.
