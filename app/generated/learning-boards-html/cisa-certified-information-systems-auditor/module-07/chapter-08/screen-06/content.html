Audit Procedures

Auditing Cloud Security Posture and IAM

Verifying Configuration and Access Controls in Cloud Environments

  • • CSPM: Verify continuous monitoring for misconfigurations and compliance.
  • • IAM: Ensure least privilege policies and MFA enforcement.
  • • Review: Examine CSPM reports and IAM policies for adherence to best practices.

Common Mistake

Granting overly broad IAM permissions or failing to enforce MFA significantly increases risk.

Key Takeaway

Effective cloud auditing requires verifying robust CSPM and IAM configurations to prevent misconfigurations and unauthorized access.

Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM)

Continuous monitoring for misconfigurations and compliance.

Identity and Access Management (IAM)

Least privilege and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) enforcement.
