Key Concepts

Threats and Technologies to Master

Internet of Things (IoT)

Network of physical objects with sensors and software for data exchange.

AI/ML Security

Protecting AI/ML systems from attacks and ensuring ethical operation.

Advanced Persistent Threat (APT)

Sophisticated, prolonged cyberattack with undetected network access.

Summary Takeaway

IoT, AI/ML security, APTs, and red teaming are critical areas for auditors to understand in the evolving threat landscape.
