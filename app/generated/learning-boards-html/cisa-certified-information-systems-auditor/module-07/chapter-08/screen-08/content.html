Audit Procedures

Auditing Cloud Logging, Monitoring, and Common Mistakes

Ensuring Visibility and Avoiding Pitfalls in Cloud Security

  • • Logging: Verify cloud activity logs are enabled, collected, and integrated with SIEM.
  • • Monitoring: Ensure alerts are configured for suspicious activities.
  • • Shared Responsibility: Confirm the organization understands and fulfills its part.

Common Mistake

Assuming the cloud provider handles all security, leading to misconfigurations and exposed resources.

Key Takeaway

Robust cloud logging and monitoring are essential, and auditors must ensure organizations understand and fulfill their shared security responsibilities.

Logging and Monitoring

Cloud activity logs integrated with SIEM for detection.

Auditors must verify customer's understanding and fulfillment of security duties.
