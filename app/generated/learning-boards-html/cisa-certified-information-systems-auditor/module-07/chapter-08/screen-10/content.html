Audit Procedures

Auditing IoT Security Controls

Securing the Interconnected Device Ecosystem

  • • Device Inventory: Comprehensive tracking of all IoT devices.
  • • Network Segmentation: Isolating IoT devices from critical corporate data.
  • • Authentication: Strong, unique credentials and secure management platforms.
  • • Firmware Security: Regular and secure application of firmware updates.
  • • Data Privacy: Protection of data collected by IoT devices.

Example Scenario

Review network diagrams and firewall rules to confirm smart building sensors are on a separate VLAN from employee workstations.

Key Takeaway

IoT audits must cover device inventory, network segmentation, strong authentication, firmware security, and data privacy to mitigate unique risks.

IoT Security Pillars

Inventory, Segmentation, Auth, Firmware, Data Privacy.

Proactive Strategy

Verify a robust IoT security strategy is in place.
