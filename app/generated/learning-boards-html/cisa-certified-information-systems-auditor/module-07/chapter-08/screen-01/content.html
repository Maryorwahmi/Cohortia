Auditing Emerging Technologies and Advanced Threats

Navigating the Evolving Digital Landscape for Robust Security

The digital landscape is constantly evolving, introducing both incredible opportunities and novel security challenges. This chapter focuses on how CISA auditors must remain vigilant, understanding the impact of innovations like cloud, IoT, and AI/ML on an organization's risk profile, and how to audit the controls designed to protect them.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must adopt a forward-thinking strategy to assess and secure new technologies and evolving threat landscapes.
