Module 7›Chapter 7.7›IR Plan Phases
01

Preparation

This phase ensures IR teams are adequately trained and equipped with tools like forensic workstations and threat intelligence feeds. It's about building a strong foundation before an incident occurs.

  • Adequate team training and skill development.
  • Availability of necessary tools and resources.
  • Up-to-date threat intelligence feeds.
02

Detection & Analysis

This phase focuses on how incidents are identified (e.g., SIEM alerts, user reports) and the procedures for initial triage and analysis. Rapid and accurate detection is paramount.

  • Mechanisms for incident detection (SIEM, EDR, user reports).
  • Clear procedures for initial triage and severity assessment.
  • Avoiding alert fatigue through effective prioritization.
Example Scenario

Phishing Simulation Response

An organization conducts a phishing simulation. The IR team's ability to quickly detect reported suspicious emails and initiate analysis demonstrates effective preparation and detection capabilities. An auditor would review the response time and accuracy.

Key Takeaway

Robust preparation and efficient detection mechanisms are the bedrock of an effective incident response, enabling swift action and minimizing initial impact.
