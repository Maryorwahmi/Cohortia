05

Recovery

Procedures to restore affected systems and data to normal operation. This includes restoring from backups and bringing services back online safely.

  • Restoring data from clean, verified backups.
  • Reconfiguring and hardening affected systems.
  • Verifying RTOs and RPOs are met for critical systems.
06

Post-Incident Activity

Conducting a post-mortem analysis to identify lessons learned and improve future response. This is crucial for continuous security posture improvement.

  • Performing root cause analysis.
  • Updating the IR plan and security controls.
  • Sharing lessons learned across the organization.
Common Mistake

Skipping Lessons Learned

Failing to conduct a post-mortem analysis and integrate lessons learned means the organization is likely to repeat the same mistakes, hindering long-term resilience.

Key Takeaway

Recovery focuses on restoring operations, while post-incident activities drive continuous improvement, ensuring the organization learns and adapts from every incident.
