Key Concepts to Remember (Part 2)

Metrics and exercises for effective incident management

Tabletop Exercise

A discussion-based exercise where participants talk through their roles and responsibilities during a simulated incident.

Red Teaming

A simulated attack conducted by an independent team to test the effectiveness of an organization's security controls and incident response.

MTTD & MTTR

Mean Time To Detect (MTTD) and Mean Time To Respond (MTTR) are metrics measuring the average time to identify and resolve security incidents.

Incident Playbook

A detailed, step-by-step guide for handling specific types of security incidents, ensuring consistent and effective responses.

Summary Takeaway

Testing mechanisms and performance metrics are crucial for validating and continuously improving an organization's incident response capabilities.
