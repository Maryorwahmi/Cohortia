Auditor's Checklist for Ransomware Response

  • Detection: How quickly was the attack detected? Were SIEM/EDR alerts effective?

  • Containment: Were affected systems immediately isolated? Was spread limited?

  • Communication: Were stakeholders informed according to the IR plan?

  • Recovery: Were backups restored? Were RTO/RPO met for critical systems?

  • Post-Mortem: Was root cause analysis performed? Were lessons learned incorporated?

Scenario Context

Ransomware Attack

A company experiences a ransomware attack, encrypting critical data and systems. This scenario tests the organization's entire incident response capability from detection to recovery and learning.

Audit Focus

End-to-End Effectiveness

The audit should verify that each phase of the IR plan was executed effectively, identifying any gaps or weaknesses that could be exploited in future incidents.

Key Takeaway

A ransomware attack provides a comprehensive test case for an organization's IR and SOC capabilities, highlighting the importance of a well-executed, end-to-end response.
