Methods for Testing IR Plans

  • Tabletop Exercises: Discussion-based simulations to review roles, responsibilities, and procedures.

  • Simulated Attacks (Red Teaming): Independent teams attempt to breach defenses to test IR capabilities.

  • Actual Incident Reports: Reviewing real-world incident handling to identify strengths and weaknesses.

Auditor's Focus

Evidence of Testing

Auditors must verify that IR plans are not just documented but actively tested. Look for reports, lessons learned, and subsequent plan updates.

Example Audit Question

Phishing Simulation Review

"What were the results of the last phishing simulation, and how did the IR team respond to reported suspicious emails?" This reveals practical readiness.

Key Takeaway

Regular testing and continuous refinement are essential for an IR plan to be truly effective, moving it from theoretical to practical readiness.
