Elements of an Effective IR Plan

  • Roles & Responsibilities: Clearly defined duties for all team members involved in incident handling.

  • Communication Protocols: Who to inform, when, and through what channels (internal/external).

  • Technical Procedures: Step-by-step guides for handling specific incident types (e.g., malware, data breach).

  • Alignment: Must align with industry best practices like NIST SP 800-61 and ISO 27035.

Auditor's Focus

Verifying the Plan

As an auditor, you must verify the IR plan's completeness, currency, and alignment with established industry best practices. An outdated or incomplete plan is a significant vulnerability.

Common Mistake

Paper-Only Plans

A plan that exists only on paper and has never been tested is a critical weakness. It creates a false sense of security and leads to chaotic responses.

Key Takeaway

A robust IR plan is a living document, constantly updated and aligned with best practices, serving as the organization's critical guide during security incidents.
