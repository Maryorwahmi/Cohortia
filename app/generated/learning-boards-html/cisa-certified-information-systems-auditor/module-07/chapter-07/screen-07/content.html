03

Containment

Steps taken to limit the scope and impact of an incident. This involves isolating affected systems or blocking malicious activity to prevent further damage.

  • Isolating compromised systems from the network.
  • Blocking malicious IP addresses or domains.
  • Disabling compromised user accounts.
04

Eradication

Identifying and eliminating the root cause of the incident. This ensures the threat is completely removed and cannot re-emerge, preventing future attacks.

  • Removing malware and malicious artifacts.
  • Patching exploited vulnerabilities.
  • Rebuilding compromised systems from trusted sources.
Common Pitfall

Premature Recovery

Attempting to recover systems before complete eradication of the threat can lead to re-infection and prolonged downtime. Thoroughness is key.

Key Takeaway

Effective containment and thorough eradication are crucial for stopping an incident's spread and eliminating its root cause, preventing recurrence.
