Core Concept

Federated Identity Management

Enabling seamless and secure access across multiple service providers.

Key Principles

  • •**Single Authentication:** Users authenticate once with a trusted Identity Provider (IdP).
  • •**Multiple Services:** Access various Service Providers (SPs) without re-entering credentials.
  • •**Trust Relationships:** Secure agreements define how IdP and SPs interact.
  • •**Improved User Experience:** Reduces password fatigue and simplifies access.
Key Takeaway

Federated identity allows users to authenticate once with an IdP to access multiple SPs, improving user experience and centralizing security.

Common Misconception

Federated identity does not mean sharing your password with every service. Instead, the IdP verifies your identity and then sends a secure, cryptographically signed assertion to the Service Provider, confirming your authentication without exposing your credentials.
