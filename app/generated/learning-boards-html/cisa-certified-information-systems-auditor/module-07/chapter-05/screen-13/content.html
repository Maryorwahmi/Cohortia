Lifecycle Management

Auditing the Identity Lifecycle

Ensuring secure provisioning, review, and de-provisioning of accounts.

Why Lifecycle Management is Key

  • •**Prevents Unauthorized Access:** Ensures access is always appropriate and current.
  • •**Mitigates Orphan Accounts:** Eliminates dormant accounts that pose security risks.
  • •**Enforces Least Privilege:** Access is granted and revoked based on current roles.
  • •**Ensures Compliance:** Meets regulatory requirements for access control.
Key Takeaway

Auditing the identity lifecycle ensures secure provisioning based on job roles, regular access reviews, and timely de-provisioning to prevent orphan accounts and privilege creep.

Identity Lifecycle Stages

1. Provisioning

Account creation & initial permissions.

2. Review

Regular access rights recertification.

3. De-provisioning

Timely account disablement/removal.

Common Mistake: Orphan Accounts

Failing to promptly de-provision accounts for terminated employees or contractors creates "orphan accounts." These dormant accounts can be exploited by attackers, as they often retain access rights and are less likely to be monitored.
