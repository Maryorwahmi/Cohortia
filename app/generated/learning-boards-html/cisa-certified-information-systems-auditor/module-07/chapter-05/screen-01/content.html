Identity and Access Management (IAM) Advanced Auditing

Securing Access in Complex IT Environments

This chapter focuses on advanced IAM concepts like federated identity, Single Sign-On (SSO), Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and Privileged Access Management (PAM). You will learn to audit these sophisticated systems to ensure secure design, implementation, and management, crucial for preventing data breaches in modern IT landscapes.

Key Takeaway

Inadequate Identity and Access Management is a leading cause of data breaches, making advanced IAM a high-priority area for CISA auditors.

Explore Key Topics
Federated Identity

Multi-Factor Auth
