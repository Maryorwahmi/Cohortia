Audit Procedures

Auditing MFA Implementation

Assessing factor strength, widespread implementation, and recovery processes.

Key Audit Considerations

  • •**Widespread Implementation:** Verify MFA for all users, especially privileged accounts and remote access.
  • •**Factor Strength:** Evaluate the robustness of chosen factors (e.g., FIDO2 vs. SMS).
  • •**Enrollment Process:** Ensure secure and verified user enrollment for MFA.
  • •**Recovery Procedures:** Assess the strength of account recovery to prevent bypasses.
Key Takeaway

Auditing MFA involves verifying widespread implementation, assessing factor strength (e.g., FIDO2 over SMS), and ensuring robust enrollment and recovery processes to prevent bypasses.

Common Mistake: Weak Recovery Procedures

Allowing account recovery based on easily guessable personal information or insecure channels can completely undermine MFA. An attacker could bypass MFA by exploiting a weak recovery process, even if the primary factors are strong.
