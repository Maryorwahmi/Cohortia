Digital Signature

A cryptographic mechanism verifying message authenticity, integrity, and ensuring non-repudiation.

Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)

A system of policies, procedures, and components (like CAs) for managing digital certificates and public-key cryptography.

Key Management

The comprehensive set of processes for generating, storing, distributing, archiving, and destroying cryptographic keys securely.

Summary Insight

Effective PKI and rigorous key management are essential for maintaining digital trust and ensuring the long-term security of cryptographic controls.
