The Foundation of Digital Trust

Understanding Cryptographic Controls in Auditing

Beyond Encryption: Cryptography is a discipline that underpins multiple security principles, not just data scrambling.

Auditor's Role: Evaluate appropriate application, robust implementation, and ongoing management of controls.

Mitigating Risks: Ensure cryptographic solutions effectively reduce risks to confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity.

Concrete Example

An auditor verifies that a database encrypting sensitive customer data uses strong algorithms and that the encryption keys are securely stored and regularly rotated, not just that encryption is 'on'.

Key Takeaway

Cryptography is fundamental for digital trust, and auditors must assess its comprehensive implementation, not just its presence.

The CIA Triad + Non-Repudiation

Confidentiality

Prevent unauthorized access to data.

Integrity

Ensure data has not been altered.

Authenticity

Verify identity of users/systems.

Non-Repudiation

Prevent denial of actions.
