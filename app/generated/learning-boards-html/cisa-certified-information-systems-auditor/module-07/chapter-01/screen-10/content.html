Key PKI Audit Areas

  • Certificate Policies (CP) & Practice Statements (CPS): Verify completeness, currency, and alignment with security objectives.
  • CA System Security: Assess logical controls, operating system hardening, and application security of CA servers.
  • Physical & Environmental Controls: Review access to CA facilities, power, cooling, and fire suppression.
  • Personnel Adherence: Verify that staff follow documented procedures for certificate issuance, renewal, and revocation.
Key Takeaway

A thorough PKI audit assesses policy alignment, system security, physical safeguards, and human processes to ensure digital trust.

Example: Auditing CA Configuration

# Check CA configuration for certificate templates
Get-CACertificateTemplate | Select-Object Name, ValidityPeriod, RenewalPeriod

# Check CRL distribution points
Get-CACRLDistributionPoint | Select-Object URI, PublishPeriod

# Check OCSP configuration
Get-CAOCSPConfiguration | Select-Object Name, Enabled

These conceptual PowerShell commands illustrate how an auditor might inspect an Active Directory Certificate Services (AD CS) CA to verify configuration settings related to certificate templates, revocation lists, and online status protocols. This helps ensure that the PKI is configured according to policy and best practices.

Pro-Tip

Always review the CA's root key protection mechanisms. Compromise here undermines the entire PKI's trustworthiness.
