Evaluate Cryptographic Controls

Learn to assess if various cryptographic controls, like encryption algorithms and protocols, are appropriately applied and effectively implemented to meet security objectives. This skill ensures data protection mechanisms are robust and fit for purpose.

Assess PKI Effectiveness

Develop the ability to assess the effectiveness of Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) components and processes, including Certificate Authorities and certificate lifecycle management. This ensures the integrity and trustworthiness of digital identities and communications.
