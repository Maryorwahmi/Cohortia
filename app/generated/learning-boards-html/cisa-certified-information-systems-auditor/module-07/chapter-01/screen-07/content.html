Hashing Algorithms

Verifying Data Integrity with Unique Digital Fingerprints

One-Way Function: Transforms data into a fixed-size string (hash value) that cannot be reversed.

Sensitivity: Even a minor change in input data produces a drastically different hash value.

Auditor's Tool: Used to verify the integrity of files, software, and logs by comparing hash values.

Concrete Example

To verify the integrity of a critical system file, an auditor computes its SHA-256 hash and compares it against a known good hash. If they differ, the file has been tampered with.

Key Takeaway

Hashing is crucial for detecting data tampering, providing a reliable method to ensure the integrity of digital assets.
