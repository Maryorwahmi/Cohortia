Certificate Revocation Mechanisms

Certificate Revocation List (CRL)

A periodically published list of revoked certificates. Relying parties download and check this list.

  • Batch processing
  • Potential for stale information
  • Requires regular updates
Online Certificate Status Protocol (OCSP)

Provides real-time status checks for individual certificates by querying an OCSP responder.

  • Real-time status
  • Less bandwidth intensive
  • Requires online connectivity

Auditor's Focus for Revocation

  • Timeliness of Revocation: Verify that compromised certificates are revoked immediately as per policy.
  • Relying Party Checks: Confirm that systems using certificates are effectively checking CRLs or OCSP for status.
  • Configuration & Accessibility: Verify that OCSP responders are correctly configured, accessible, and responsive.
Key Takeaway

Effective certificate revocation is as critical as issuance; auditors must ensure timely and verifiable invalidation of compromised certificates.
