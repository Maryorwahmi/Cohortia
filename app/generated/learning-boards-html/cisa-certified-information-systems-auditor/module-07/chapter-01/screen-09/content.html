The PKI Ecosystem

Certificate Authority (CA)

A trusted entity that issues, revokes, and manages digital certificates, binding public keys to identities.

Digital Certificates

Electronic documents that verify the ownership of a public key, signed by a CA to ensure authenticity.

Certificate Policy (CP)

A high-level document outlining the rules for certificate issuance, management, and usage.

Certificate Practice Statement (CPS)

A detailed document describing the specific procedures a CA uses to implement its CP.

PKI Certificate Issuance Process

1User requests certificate
2CA verifies identity
3CA issues signed certificate
4User receives and installs certificate
Key Takeaway

PKI establishes trust for digital identities and secure communication, with the CA as its central, trusted component.
