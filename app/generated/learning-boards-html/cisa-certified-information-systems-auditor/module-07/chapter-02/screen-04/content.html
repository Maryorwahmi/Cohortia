Core Concept

Foundations of Network Security Architecture

Understanding the strategic importance of network design in protecting information assets.

Why Network Architecture Matters

  • •Protection: Safeguards information assets from unauthorized access, misuse, modification, or denial.
  • •Risk Mitigation: Ensures security devices are strategically placed and configured to mitigate identified risks.
  • •Policy Enforcement: Verifies that security policies are effectively enforced across the network.

CISA Auditor's Role

As an auditor, you assess not just the presence of security devices, but their strategic placement, configuration, and ongoing management. This holistic view ensures effective enforcement of security policies and risk mitigation.

Key Takeaway

A well-designed network architecture with strategically placed and configured controls is the bedrock of effective information security, going beyond mere device presence.
