Network Segmentation Principles

Dividing networks to limit the impact of security breaches.

What is Network Segmentation?

Network segmentation divides a computer network into multiple smaller, isolated segments. This prevents free communication between all devices, enhancing security.

  • •DMZ: For public-facing services.
  • •Production: Critical operational systems.
  • •Development: Software creation and testing.
  • •User Networks: End-user workstations.

Principle of Least Privilege

Traffic between segments must be strictly controlled, allowing only necessary communication. This minimizes potential attack vectors and limits lateral movement for attackers.

Benefits of Segmentation

Limits 'Blast Radius' of Breaches
Enhances Security Posture
Improves Performance & Management
Key Takeaway

Network segmentation is crucial for containing breaches and enforcing granular access control, significantly reducing overall risk.
