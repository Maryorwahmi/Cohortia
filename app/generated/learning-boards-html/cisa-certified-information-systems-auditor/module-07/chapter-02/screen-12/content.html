VPN Audit

VPN Tunneling and Security Audit

Assessing split vs. full tunneling and user access controls.

Split Tunneling

  • •Definition: Corporate traffic via VPN, internet traffic directly from local network.
  • •Concern: Malicious traffic can bypass corporate security controls.
  • •Risk: Potential backdoor into the corporate network if local machine is compromised.

Full Tunneling

  • •Definition: ALL traffic (corporate & internet-bound) routes through the VPN.
  • •Benefit: Ensures all traffic is subject to corporate security policies.
  • •Recommendation: Generally preferred for enhanced security.

Additional VPN Audit Points

Strong Ciphers & Key Lengths: Verify cryptographic strength.
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Enforce for remote users.
User Provisioning/De-provisioning: Ensure accounts are managed properly.
Key Takeaway

Auditors must prioritize 'full tunneling' for VPNs to ensure all traffic is secured by corporate policies, alongside verifying strong cryptographic settings and robust user authentication.
