Secure Connectivity

Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) Fundamentals

Establishing secure, encrypted communication channels over untrusted networks.

What is a VPN?

A VPN creates a secure, encrypted connection (a 'tunnel') over a less secure network, typically the internet. It extends a private network across a public network.

  • •Remote Access: Enables secure connection for remote users.
  • •Site-to-Site: Connects distributed offices securely.
  • •Data Protection: Ensures confidentiality and integrity of data in transit.

Why it Matters for Auditors

Auditors must verify VPN configurations to ensure strong encryption, robust authentication, and proper access controls, as VPNs are critical for protecting remote access and distributed operations.

Key VPN Components to Audit

Cryptographic Protocols: IPsec, SSL/TLS VPNs.
Authentication Mechanisms: Multi-factor authentication (MFA).
Access Controls: Policies applied to VPN users.
Key Takeaway

VPNs are essential for secure remote access and data protection. Auditors must verify strong cryptographic protocols, robust authentication, and appropriate access controls to ensure their effectiveness.
