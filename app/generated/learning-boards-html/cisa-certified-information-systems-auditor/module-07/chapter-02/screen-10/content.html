Intrusion Detection and Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS)

Monitoring and actively blocking malicious network activities.

IDS: Intrusion Detection System

  • •Monitors: Observes network traffic for suspicious patterns or attack signatures.
  • •Alerts: Generates notifications when threats are detected.
  • •Passive: Does not interfere with traffic flow; acts as an alarm.

IPS: Intrusion Prevention System

  • •Monitors & Prevents: Actively blocks or drops malicious traffic in real-time.
  • •Active: Can terminate connections, block IP addresses, or quarantine threats.
  • •Inline Deployment: Typically sits in the direct path of network traffic.

Auditor's Verification Points

Review alert logs for critical incidents.
Analyze false positive rates and tuning.
Confirm regular signature updates.
Verify SIEM integration and incident response.
Key Takeaway

IDS/IPS are crucial for detecting and preventing intrusions. Auditors must ensure they are actively monitored, updated, and integrated into incident response workflows to be effective.
