Perimeter Defense

Firewalls: The Network Gatekeepers

Controlling traffic flow at the network perimeter.

Firewall's Core Function

  • •Gatekeeper Role: Monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic.
  • •Rule-Based: Operates based on predefined security rules and policies.
  • •First Line of Defense: Protects internal networks from external threats.

Why it Matters for Auditors

Auditors must understand firewall types and capabilities to assess if the deployed solution is appropriate for the organization's risk profile and effectively configured to enforce security policies.

Types of Firewalls

Packet-Filtering: Basic IP/port inspection.
Stateful Inspection: Tracks connection state.
Next-Gen (NGFW): Deep packet inspection, app awareness, IPS.
Key Takeaway

Firewalls are essential perimeter defenses, acting as gatekeepers. Auditors must understand their types and capabilities to ensure proper deployment and configuration.
