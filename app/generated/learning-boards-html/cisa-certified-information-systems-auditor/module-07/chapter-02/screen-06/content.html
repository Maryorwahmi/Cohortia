Audit Procedure

Auditing Network Segmentation Implementation

Verifying logical and physical boundaries through configuration review.

Key Verification Points

  • •VLANs (Virtual Local Area Networks): Confirm logical separation within physical switches.
  • •Subnetting: Verify division of IP address ranges into smaller, isolated networks.
  • •Routing Configurations: Examine rules governing traffic flow between segments.
  • •Firewall Rules: Crucial for controlling inter-segment traffic based on least privilege.

Common Audit Finding: Overly Permissive Rules

A frequent issue is the presence of 'any-to-any' rules or broad access control lists between segments. This effectively negates the security benefits of segmentation, allowing unauthorized lateral movement.

Example: Verifying VLAN Isolation

Review switch configurations (e.g., `show vlan brief`).
Examine router ACLs for inter-VLAN traffic filtering.
Confirm traffic is restricted based on business need.
Key Takeaway

Auditing segmentation involves deep dives into VLANs, subnets, and routing rules to ensure strict, least-privilege enforcement, preventing 'any-to-any' bypasses.
