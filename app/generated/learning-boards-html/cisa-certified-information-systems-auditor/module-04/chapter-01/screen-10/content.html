ITIL Focus Area

Key Processes in Service Operation

The ITIL Service Operation domain includes critical processes for managing day-to-day IT service delivery and ensuring service levels are met.

SERVICE OPS
Incident Management
Problem Management
Request Fulfilment
Access Management
Event Management
Example Scenario

An auditor reviewing Incident Management would check for proper logging, categorization, prioritization, and escalation procedures to ensure swift resolution of IT service disruptions.

Key Takeaway

Effective implementation of ITIL Service Operation processes is crucial for maintaining the availability, performance, and security of IT services.
