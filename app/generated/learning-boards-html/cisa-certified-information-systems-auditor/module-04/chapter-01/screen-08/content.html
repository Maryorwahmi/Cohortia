Common Pitfall

Avoiding the 'Cost Center' Trap

Underinvestment in IS operations, driven by a 'cost center' mentality, inevitably leads to higher long-term costs due to incidents and recovery efforts.

Short-Term View

Cost Center Approach

  • Underinvestment in staff training
  • Limited automation
  • Lack of redundant systems
  • Focus on immediate savings
→ Higher Long-Term Costs
Strategic View

Investment Approach

  • Continuous staff development
  • Extensive automation
  • Robust redundant systems
  • Focus on resilience & efficiency
→ Lower Long-Term Costs
Example Scenario

A company postpones server upgrades to save money. A critical server fails, causing a 3-day outage and data loss, costing far more in lost revenue and recovery than the upgrade itself.

Key Takeaway

Strategic investment in IS operations is a proactive measure that builds resilience, reduces risks, and ultimately lowers total cost of ownership.
