Key Concepts

Core Definitions for IS Operations

IS Operations Management

Day-to-day activities to maintain IT infrastructure, applications, and data stability, availability, and performance.

Service Level Agreement (SLA)

A contract specifying services, performance targets, and responsibilities between a service provider and a customer.

ITIL

A widely adopted framework providing best practices for IT service management, aligning IT services with business needs.

Summary Takeaway

These foundational concepts are essential for understanding how IT services are managed and for effectively auditing an organization's operational maturity.
