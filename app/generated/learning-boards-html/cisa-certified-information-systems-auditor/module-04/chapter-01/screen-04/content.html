Core Concept

Defining IS Operations Management

IS Operations Management encompasses the day-to-day activities required to maintain the stability, availability, and performance of IT infrastructure, applications, and data.

Key Aspects
  • Stability: Systems operate consistently without unexpected errors.
  • Availability: Services are accessible to users when needed.
  • Performance: Systems respond efficiently and quickly.
Key Takeaway

Effective IS operations are crucial for business continuity, ensuring IT services consistently meet agreed-upon service levels.

Stability

Consistent, error-free operation.

Availability

Accessible when and where needed.

Performance

Efficient and responsive systems.

Example Scenario

A company's e-commerce website must be stable to prevent crashes, available 24/7 for global customers, and performant to handle peak traffic, directly impacting sales and customer satisfaction.
