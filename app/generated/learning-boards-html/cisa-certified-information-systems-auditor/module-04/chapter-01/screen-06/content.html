Operational Processes

Essential Day-to-Day Activities

IS operations involve a myriad of activities to maintain IT service levels, from system monitoring to help desk support.

System Monitoring

Proactive health and performance checks.

Job Scheduling

Automating tasks for efficiency.

Backup & Recovery

Data protection and restoration.

Patch Management

Applying security updates and fixes.

User Account Mgt.

Controlling access permissions.

Help Desk Support

Front-line user assistance.

Example Scenario

A financial institution relies on automated job scheduling to process end-of-day transactions. Any failure in this process could lead to incorrect account balances and regulatory non-compliance.

Key Takeaway

Each operational activity is interdependent; a weakness in one area can compromise overall IT service delivery and business continuity.
