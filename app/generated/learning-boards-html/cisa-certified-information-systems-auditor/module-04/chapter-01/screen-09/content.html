Leveraging ITIL for IS Operations

ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library) is a widely adopted framework providing best practices for IT service management, aligning IT services with business needs.

ITIL Benefits
  • Comprehensive set of best practices.
  • Covers entire IT service lifecycle.
  • Provides a common language and processes.
  • Benchmark for operational maturity.
Key Takeaway

Familiarity with ITIL's key processes is highly beneficial for auditors to assess an organization's IT service management effectiveness.
