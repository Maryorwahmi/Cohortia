Audit Deep Dive

Scrutinizing Operational Documentation and Data

IS auditors conduct a deep dive into operational policies, procedures, and performance metrics to assess effectiveness and efficiency.

Policies
  • Are they current?
  • Clearly communicated?
  • Consistently enforced?
Procedures
  • Are they complete?
  • Accurate and detailed?
  • Adhered to by staff?
Performance Metrics
  • Uptime statistics
  • Incident resolution times
  • Resource utilization reports
Example Scenario

An auditor finds that a 'critical incident response' procedure is outdated and staff are unaware of its latest version, leading to delayed incident resolution.

Key Takeaway

Thorough review of documentation and data provides objective evidence of operational health and identifies areas for control improvement and optimization.
