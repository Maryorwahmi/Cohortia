Auditor's Perspective

The IS Auditor's Review Approach

The IS auditor's role extends beyond merely checking for the presence of controls; it involves assessing their effectiveness and efficiency.

Key Audit Focus
  • Effectiveness: Do controls achieve their intended purpose?
  • Efficiency: Are controls operating without undue cost or delay?
  • Beyond Compliance: Provide actionable insights for optimization.
Key Takeaway

A control's presence is insufficient; auditors must verify its active contribution to risk mitigation and operational objectives.

Example Scenario

An organization has a firewall, but if its rules are outdated or misconfigured, it's ineffective. If managing it requires excessive manual effort, it's inefficient.
