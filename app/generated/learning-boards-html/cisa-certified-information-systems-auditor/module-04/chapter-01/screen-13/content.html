Risk Management

Identifying and Mitigating Operational Risks

Operational risks arise from inadequate processes, people, systems, or external events, potentially impacting business objectives. Auditors must identify and mitigate these risks.

System Outages

Disruption of critical services.

Data Corruption

Loss or integrity compromise.

Security Breaches

Unauthorized access or disclosure.

Audit Safety Note

Always conduct audit activities, especially those involving live systems or data, in a non-disruptive manner, ideally in test environments or during scheduled maintenance windows, and with proper authorization and supervision.

Key Takeaway

Proactive identification of operational risks and adherence to non-disruptive audit practices are essential for building resilient and secure IS operations.
