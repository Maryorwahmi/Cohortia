Key Concepts

Understanding Operational Risks and Processes

Operational Risk

Risk of loss from inadequate or failed internal processes, people, systems, or external events.

Event Management

ITIL process monitoring events in IT infrastructure to detect and escalate exceptions or significant occurrences.

Incident Management

ITIL process focused on restoring normal service operation quickly and minimizing adverse business impact.

Problem Management

ITIL process aiming to identify the root causes of incidents and prevent their recurrence.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts are vital for understanding how organizations manage and mitigate risks in their IT operations, a key area for IS auditors.
