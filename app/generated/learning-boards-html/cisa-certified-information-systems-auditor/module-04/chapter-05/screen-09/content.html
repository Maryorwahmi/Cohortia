Evaluating Structured Approaches to Growth

Auditors evaluate the methodology used for capacity planning, looking for evidence of a structured approach that considers both organic growth and specific business initiatives.

Auditor's Review Focus
  • Methodology: Is there a structured, systematic approach?
  • Realism: Are forecasts based on sound data and assumptions?
  • Regularity: Are plans and forecasts regularly updated?
  • Approval: Are plans approved by relevant stakeholders?
Example Scenario

If an organization plans to double its online user base, the auditor expects a detailed capacity plan for web servers, databases, and network infrastructure.

Key Takeaway

A robust capacity planning methodology, regularly reviewed and approved, is critical for ensuring IT infrastructure can reliably support future business demands.

Capacity Planning Evaluation Steps

1. Review Planning Methodology
2. Analyze Forecasts & Reports
3. Verify Stakeholder Approval
4. Confirm Alignment with Growth
