Why Performance and Capacity Matter

Efficient IS operations are not just about keeping systems running; they are about ensuring systems perform optimally and have the capacity to meet current and future business demands.

Auditor's Role

Provide assurance that IT resources are effectively managed to support business objectives.

Prevent Bottlenecks

Avoid congestion points that impede data flow and degrade system performance.

Optimize Resource Allocation

Prevent costly over-provisioning (wasted capital) or under-provisioning (system failures).

Key Takeaway

Proactive performance monitoring and capacity planning are essential for business continuity, user satisfaction, and efficient IT expenditure.
