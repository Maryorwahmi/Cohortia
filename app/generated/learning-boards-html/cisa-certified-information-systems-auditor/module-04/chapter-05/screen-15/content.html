Key Concepts

Essential terms for Performance Monitoring and Capacity Planning

Key Performance Indicator (KPI)

A measurable value that demonstrates how effectively a company is achieving key business objectives.

Performance Monitoring

The continuous observation and measurement of IT system resources and application behavior to ensure optimal operation.

Capacity Planning

The process of determining the production capacity needed by an organization to meet changing demands for its products or services.

Summary Takeaway

KPIs provide the metrics, performance monitoring provides the real-time data, and capacity planning provides the foresight to ensure IT systems are always ready for business demands.
