Performance Monitoring and Capacity Planning

Ensuring Optimal IS Operations and Future Scalability

Efficient IS operations are not just about keeping systems running; they are also about ensuring systems perform optimally and have the capacity to meet current and future business demands. As an IS auditor, your role is to provide assurance that the organization is effectively managing its IT resources to support business objectives, prevent bottlenecks, and avoid costly over-provisioning or under-provisioning.

Estimated Time: 15-20 minutes

Core Focus

Performance Monitoring

Continuously collect and analyze data on system resource utilization and application response times.

Capacity Planning

Predict future resource requirements and ensure IT infrastructure can scale to meet those needs.
