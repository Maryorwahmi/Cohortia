Consequences of Neglecting Performance and Capacity

Inadequate performance monitoring and capacity planning can lead to significant operational and financial risks for an organization.

Key Risks Identified
  • System Outages: Due to resource exhaustion (CPU, memory, disk).
  • Slow Application Response Times: Leading to user frustration and lost productivity.
  • Customer Dissatisfaction: Directly impacting business reputation and revenue.
  • Inefficient Resource Allocation: Costly over-provisioning or critical under-provisioning.
  • Unexpected Costs: From emergency upgrades or lost business opportunities.
Auditor Safety Note

Always consider the context of performance data (e.g., scheduled batch jobs vs. peak hours). Look for trends and correlations, not isolated data points.

Key Takeaway

Proactive performance and capacity management are crucial for mitigating risks that can lead to operational failures, financial losses, and reputational damage.

Risk Impact Spectrum

Low: Minor Performance Glitches
Medium: Customer Dissatisfaction
High: System Outages & Financial Loss
