Key Concepts

Advanced terms for Performance Monitoring and Capacity Planning

Resource Utilization

The percentage of time a resource (e.g., CPU, memory, disk I/O) is actively being used.

Bottleneck

A point of congestion in a system that impedes the flow of data or processes, leading to performance degradation.

Trend Analysis

The process of examining data over time to identify patterns or trends, often used in capacity planning to predict future resource needs.

Summary Takeaway

By understanding resource utilization, identifying bottlenecks, and performing trend analysis, auditors can provide deeper insights into IT operational efficiency and future readiness.
