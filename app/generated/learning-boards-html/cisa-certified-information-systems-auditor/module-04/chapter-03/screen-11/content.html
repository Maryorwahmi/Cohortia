Auditing Monitoring Effectiveness
  • Assess scope of monitoring tools and data collected.
  • Evaluate alert thresholds and their relevance.
  • Review processes for alert generation, escalation, and resolution.
  • Verify integration with incident response procedures.

Key Takeaway

Robust network monitoring and a well-defined incident response plan are crucial for maintaining network availability and security, and auditors must validate their effectiveness.
