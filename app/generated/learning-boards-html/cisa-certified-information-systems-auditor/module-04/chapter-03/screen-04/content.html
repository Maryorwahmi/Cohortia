Core Concept

Network Infrastructure: The Digital Foundation

Enabling Communication, Data Transfer, and Resource Access

What is Network Infrastructure?

The interconnected hardware and software components that facilitate communication, data transfer, and resource sharing across an organization.

  • Includes physical devices like routers, switches, and cables.
  • Encompasses logical configurations, protocols, and services.
  • Essential for all modern Information Systems operations.
Auditor's Focus

IS auditors evaluate network architecture, device configurations, security controls, monitoring, and incident response capabilities to ensure data confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

Risk of Poor Management

A poorly secured or managed network is a significant attack vector, leading to data breaches, service disruptions, and non-compliance issues, directly impacting business operations.

Key Takeaway

Network infrastructure is the critical foundation for all IS, and auditors must ensure its security, integrity, and availability to protect organizational assets.
