Detecting and Preventing Malicious Activity

Understanding IDS and IPS Capabilities

Intrusion Detection System (IDS)

Monitors network traffic for suspicious activity and generates alerts. Passive, does not block traffic.

Intrusion Prevention System (IPS)

Monitors traffic and actively blocks or prevents detected malicious activity in real-time. Active defense.

Auditor's Considerations for IDS/IPS

  • Deployment & Configuration: Verify proper placement and settings to maximize coverage.
  • Signature Updates: Ensure signatures are regularly updated to detect new and evolving threats.
  • Alert Review & Response: Assess if alerts are consistently reviewed, prioritized, and acted upon by security personnel.
  • False Positives: Evaluate mechanisms for tuning to minimize false positives and maintain operational efficiency.
Common Pitfall

Outdated signatures or unaddressed alerts render IDS/IPS ineffective, leaving the network vulnerable to emerging threats.

Key Takeaway

IDS and IPS are essential for real-time threat detection and prevention, requiring vigilant auditing of their configuration, signature updates, and operational effectiveness.
