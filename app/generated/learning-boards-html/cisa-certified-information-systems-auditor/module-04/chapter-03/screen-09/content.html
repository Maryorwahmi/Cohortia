Secure Connectivity

Virtual Private Networks (VPNs): Safe Remote Access

Encrypting Connections Over Untrusted Networks

What is a VPN?

A technology that creates a secure, encrypted connection (a 'tunnel') over a less secure network, such as the internet, allowing remote users to access private network resources as if they were local.

  • Protects data confidentiality and integrity during transit.
  • Essential for secure remote work and business continuity.
  • Masks user's IP address and location from external observers.
Auditor's Checklist for VPNs
  • Strong Encryption: Verify robust, current encryption protocols are in use.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Ensure MFA is enforced for all VPN access.
  • Comprehensive Logging: Check for detailed logs of VPN connections, including user, time, and duration.
  • Access Controls: Confirm that VPN access is granted based on the principle of least privilege.
Common Mistake

Relying solely on username and password for VPN access significantly increases the risk of unauthorized network entry if credentials are compromised.

Key Takeaway

VPNs provide secure remote access, but their effectiveness depends on robust encryption, strong authentication, and thorough logging, all of which auditors must verify.
