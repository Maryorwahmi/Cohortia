The Interconnected World of IT Service Management

Understanding Incidents, Problems, and Changes

Foundation of Stability: These three processes are the bedrock for maintaining reliable and secure Information Systems operations.

Auditor's Mandate: Assess if processes are well-defined, consistently followed, and effective in minimizing adverse impacts.

Risk Mitigation: Proper management prevents prolonged outages, data corruption, and security vulnerabilities.

Key Takeaway

Effective IT service management relies on the interconnected processes of incident, problem, and change management to ensure system stability and security.
