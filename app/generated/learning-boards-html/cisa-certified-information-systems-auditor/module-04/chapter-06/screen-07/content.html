Core Concept

Problem Management: Proactive Prevention

Moving Beyond Incidents to Root Cause Analysis

Reactive vs. Proactive: Incident management is reactive; problem management is proactive, seeking underlying causes.

Problem Definition: The unknown cause of one or more incidents, requiring investigation beyond immediate fixes.

Auditor's Role: Evaluate the process for identifying problems and conducting thorough root cause analysis.

Key Takeaway

Problem management proactively identifies and eliminates the root causes of recurring incidents through systematic analysis, enhancing long-term system stability.

Problem Management Process Steps

Step 01

Problem Identification

Problems are identified through trend analysis of recurring incidents, major incident reviews, or proactive analysis of system data.

Example

Multiple incidents reported about slow database queries on the same server over a week.

Step 02

Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

Formal methodologies (e.g., 5 Whys, Fishbone diagrams) are used to systematically investigate and diagnose the underlying cause of the problem.

Methodology

The '5 Whys' technique involves asking 'why' repeatedly to peel back layers of symptoms and uncover the root cause.
