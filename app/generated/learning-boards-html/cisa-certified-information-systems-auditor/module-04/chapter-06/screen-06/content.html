Process Flow

Incident Management: Investigation, Resolution, and Closure

Structured Steps to Service Restoration

Step 03

Categorization & Prioritization

Incidents are classified by type and prioritized based on business impact and urgency, often guided by SLAs, to ensure critical issues are addressed first.

Auditor Focus

Verify that prioritization aligns with organizational risk appetite and service level agreements.

Step 04

Investigation & Diagnosis

A structured approach is used to diagnose the root cause of the incident, involving data collection, analysis, and expert consultation.

Pro-Tip

Effective diagnosis often involves collaboration across different IT teams and leveraging knowledge bases.

Steps 05-06

Resolution, Recovery & Closure

Incidents are resolved efficiently, service is restored within RTOs, and then properly documented and closed, with user verification if applicable.

Common Mistake

Focusing solely on quick resolution without proper documentation or analysis, leading to recurring incidents.

Key Takeaway

A structured approach from categorization to closure ensures efficient incident resolution, proper documentation, and prevents recurrence.
