Key Concepts: Process and Tools

Understanding the frameworks and mechanisms for effective IT service management.

Incident Management

Process for managing the lifecycle of all incidents, aiming to restore service quickly.

Problem Management

Process for managing the lifecycle of all problems, focusing on root cause analysis and prevention.

Change Management

Process for controlling the lifecycle of all changes to the IT environment to minimize risk.

Summary Takeaway

These three management processes—Incident, Problem, and Change—are interdependent and crucial for maintaining the stability, security, and overall health of an organization's IT services.
