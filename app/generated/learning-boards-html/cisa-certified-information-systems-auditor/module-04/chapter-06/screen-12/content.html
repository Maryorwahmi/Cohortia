The Auditor's Role: Ensuring Process Effectiveness

Assessing incident, problem, and change management for stable and secure operations.

Incident Management Review

  • Review incident logs and resolution times.
  • Assess escalation procedures and adherence to SLAs.
  • Verify proper documentation and closure.

Problem Management Assessment

  • Evaluate root cause analysis (RCA) methodologies.
  • Check for evidence of recurring incident prevention.
  • Verify documentation of workarounds and Known Errors.

Change Management Audit

  • Review change logs and CAB meeting minutes.
  • Verify authorization, testing, and rollback plans.
  • Assess post-implementation reviews for completeness.
Key Takeaway

Auditors play a crucial role in independently verifying that IT service management processes are effectively implemented and adhered to, ensuring the overall stability and security of information systems.
