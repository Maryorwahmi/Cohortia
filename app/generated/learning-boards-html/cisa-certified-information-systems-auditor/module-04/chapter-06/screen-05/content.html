Process Flow

Incident Management: Restoring Service Swiftly

From Detection to Initial Logging

Step 01

Detection

Incidents are identified through monitoring tools (e.g., system alerts) or user reports (e.g., help desk calls). Prompt detection is key to minimizing impact.

Example

A server monitoring tool triggers an alert for high CPU utilization, or a user reports they cannot access a critical application.

Step 02

Logging

All detected incidents must be accurately recorded in a central system, such as a help desk ticketing system, capturing essential details for tracking.

Why it matters

Proper logging ensures a clear audit trail, facilitates communication, and provides data for future problem analysis.

Incident Definition

An incident is an unplanned interruption to an IT service or a reduction in the quality of an IT service. The goal is to restore service as quickly as possible.

Common Mistake

Failing to log all incidents, especially minor ones, can obscure recurring issues and hinder effective problem management.

Key Takeaway

Rapid detection and accurate logging are the critical first steps in incident management, enabling quick service restoration and minimizing business impact.
