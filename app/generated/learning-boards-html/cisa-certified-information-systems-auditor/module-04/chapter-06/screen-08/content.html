Process Flow

Problem Management: Workarounds, Known Errors, and Permanent Fixes

From Temporary Solutions to Lasting Resolutions

Step 03

Workarounds & Known Errors

Temporary solutions (workarounds) are documented, and a Known Error Database (KEDB) is maintained to provide quick fixes while permanent solutions are developed.

Benefit

Reduces the impact of recurring incidents by providing immediate, documented temporary fixes.

Step 04

Problem Control & Error Resolution

Permanent solutions are developed, thoroughly tested, and implemented, often through the change management process, to eliminate the root cause.

Auditor Focus

Verify that permanent solutions are properly tested and authorized before deployment.

Concrete Example: Recurring Server Issues

If the same server repeatedly experiences memory issues, problem management should investigate the root cause (e.g., faulty hardware, memory leak in an application) rather than just restarting it each time. This leads to a permanent fix, like replacing the RAM or patching the application.

Common Mistake

Failing to move beyond workarounds to implement permanent solutions, leading to continuous resource drain and instability.

Key Takeaway

Problem management ensures temporary workarounds lead to permanent, controlled solutions, preventing recurring incidents and enhancing system stability.
