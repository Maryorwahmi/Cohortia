Key Concepts: Data and Recovery

Essential terms for understanding data protection and site recovery.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable amount of data that can be lost from an IT service due to a major incident.

Full Backup

A complete copy of all selected data, offering the simplest restoration but consuming the most storage.

Differential Backup

A backup that copies all data that has changed since the last full backup, growing in size over time.

Key Takeaway

RPO guides backup frequency, while full and differential backups are core data protection methods. Hot sites provide rapid recovery for critical systems.
