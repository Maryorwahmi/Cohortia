Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and Recovery Objectives

Identifying critical processes and defining acceptable recovery targets.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

  • •Identifies critical business processes and dependencies.
  • •Assesses potential impacts of disruptions (financial, reputational, legal).
  • •Foundation for setting recovery objectives.

Example: A BIA for a hospital identifies patient care systems as critical, with severe impact if unavailable for even short periods.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO) & Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

  • •RTO: Maximum acceptable downtime after an incident.
  • •RPO: Maximum acceptable data loss from an IT service.
  • •Must be realistic, management-approved, and achievable by strategy.

Example: An online banking system might have an RTO of 1 hour and an RPO of 15 minutes, requiring highly robust recovery solutions.

Common Mistake

Setting aggressive RTO/RPO targets without adequate budget or technical capabilities to meet them, leading to a false sense of security.
