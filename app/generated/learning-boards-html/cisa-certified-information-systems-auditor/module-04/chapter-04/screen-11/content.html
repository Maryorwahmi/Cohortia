Evaluating a Disaster Recovery Plan Excerpt

Applying audit principles to a real-world DRP scenario.

Application: E-commerce Web App (Criticality: High)RTO: 4 hoursRPO: 1 hourRecovery Site: Warm Site in a different city.Data Replication: Daily file-level replication of database backups to the warm site.Server Recovery: Manual installation of OS and application software, then restore data from replicated backups.Network Connectivity: VPN tunnel established manually after site activation.Testing: Annual tabletop exercise.

Scenario: You are an IS auditor reviewing this DRP excerpt for a critical e-commerce application. Identify inconsistencies or weaknesses.

Auditor's Questions/Concerns

  • •RPO Mismatch: Daily replication vs. 1-hour RPO (potential 23-hour data loss).
  • •RTO Mismatch: Manual OS/software installation vs. 4-hour RTO (likely too long).
  • •Insufficient Testing: Annual tabletop for critical app (functional/full-scale needed).
  • •VPN Setup: How long does manual VPN take? Included in RTO?

Conclusion: The DRP excerpt shows significant inconsistencies between stated objectives and recovery strategies, indicating a high risk of failure.

Key Takeaway

A DRP must be internally consistent, with recovery strategies and testing frequency realistically supporting the defined RTO and RPO objectives.
