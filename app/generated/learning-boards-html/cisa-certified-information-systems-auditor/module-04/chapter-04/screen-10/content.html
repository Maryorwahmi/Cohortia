The Imperative of Regular Testing

Validating BCP and DRP effectiveness through consistent exercises.

Types of Tests

  • •Tabletop Exercises: Discussion-based, review roles and procedures.
  • •Functional Tests: Simulate partial outages, test specific components.
  • •Full-Scale Simulations: Actual failover to recovery sites, comprehensive testing.

Example: An annual full-scale DRP test involves shutting down primary systems and activating the warm site to verify recovery.

Auditing Test Results

  • •Review test plans, participant lists, and results reports.
  • •Identify deficiencies and track corrective actions.
  • •Verify plans are updated based on test findings and IT changes.

Example: An auditor reviews a DRP test report, noting a critical application failed to start, and verifies the remediation plan's implementation status.

Common Mistake

Infrequent testing, testing only a subset of systems, or failing to update plans based on test results, leading to ineffective recovery capabilities.
