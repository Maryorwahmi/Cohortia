Audit Best Practice

Auditing DRP Test Results and Remediation

Verifying that identified deficiencies are addressed and plans are improved.

Reviewing Test Reports

  • •Examine post-test reports for identified deficiencies and successes.
  • •Verify that all critical systems and processes were adequately tested.
  • •Assess the accuracy and completeness of the reporting.

Example: An auditor reviews a DRP test report that clearly outlines a failure to restore a specific application and the root cause.

Remediation and Updates

  • •Verify that remediation plans are developed for all deficiencies.
  • •Track the implementation of corrective actions and their effectiveness.
  • •Ensure DRPs are updated to reflect test findings and environmental changes.

Example: The auditor confirms that the application failure from the test was resolved by a software patch, and the DRP was updated to include the new procedure.

Common Mistake

Failing to implement corrective actions identified during testing, or neglecting to update plans, renders the testing effort largely ineffective.
