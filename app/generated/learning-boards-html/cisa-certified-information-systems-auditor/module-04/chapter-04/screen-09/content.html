Recovery Infrastructure

Choosing the Right Recovery Site

Matching recovery site capabilities to RTO/RPO requirements.

Hot Site

  • •Fully equipped, real-time data replication.
  • •Operational within hours.
  • •Lowest RTO/RPO, highest cost.

Example: A financial trading platform requires a hot site to ensure near-zero downtime and data loss.

Warm Site

  • •Partially equipped, some data replication.
  • •Operational within hours to a day.
  • •Moderate RTO/RPO, moderate cost.

Example: An e-commerce website might use a warm site, tolerating a few hours of downtime for setup.

Cold Site

  • •Basic facility (power, connectivity only).
  • •Requires significant setup time (days/weeks).
  • •Highest RTO/RPO, lowest cost.

Example: Archival data or non-critical internal applications might use a cold site, tolerating longer recovery times.

Common Mistake

Selecting a recovery site type that does not align with the RTO and RPO requirements of the critical systems it is meant to protect.
