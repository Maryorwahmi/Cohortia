BCP vs. DRP: Distinct but Integrated

Understanding the different scopes of business continuity and disaster recovery.

Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

  • •Focuses on maintaining ALL critical business functions.
  • •Encompasses non-IT aspects: personnel, facilities, processes.
  • •Goal: Keep the entire business operational during disruption.

Example: A regional power outage affects an entire office building. BCP ensures staff can work remotely, critical calls are rerouted, and essential services continue.

Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)

  • •Specifically focuses on IT systems and infrastructure recovery.
  • •Involves restoring hardware, software, data, and networks.
  • •Goal: Get IT systems back online and operational.

Example: A data center fire destroys servers. DRP outlines steps to restore systems at an alternative site, recover data, and re-establish network connectivity.

Key Takeaway

BCP is the overarching strategy for business continuity, while DRP is the IT-specific plan that supports it. Both are essential and must be integrated.
