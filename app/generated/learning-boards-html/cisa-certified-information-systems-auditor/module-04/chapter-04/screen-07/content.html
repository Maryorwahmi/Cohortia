Backup Logistics

Secure Backup Storage and Retention

Ensuring backups are protected, accessible, and compliant.

Storage Locations

  • •On-site: Convenient, but vulnerable to localized disasters.
  • •Off-site: Essential for geographical diversity, protects against site-specific events.
  • •Cloud: Scalable, accessible, often encrypted, but requires internet.
  • •Air-gapped: Physically isolated, best protection against ransomware.

Example: A company uses daily cloud backups for operational data and monthly air-gapped tape backups for long-term archives.

Encryption & Retention

  • •Encryption: Protects data confidentiality on backup media, both in transit and at rest.
  • •Retention Policies: Define how long backups are kept, driven by legal, regulatory, and business needs.
  • •Must be documented, implemented, and regularly reviewed.

Example: Customer data backups are encrypted with AES-256 and retained for 5 years to comply with industry regulations.

Common Mistake

Relying solely on on-site backups, making them vulnerable to the same localized disaster that affects primary systems.
