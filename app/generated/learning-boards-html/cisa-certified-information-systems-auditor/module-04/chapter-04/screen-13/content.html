Key Concepts: Planning and Objectives

Essential terms for understanding system resiliency.

Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

A holistic process to identify threats and impacts, building organizational resilience across all business operations.

Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)

A documented process to recover and protect an organization's IT infrastructure in the event of a disaster.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum tolerable duration a system, application, or network can be down after a disaster or failure.

Key Takeaway

BCP provides the overarching strategy for business resilience, DRP handles IT recovery, and RTO defines acceptable downtime, all critical for audit assurance.
