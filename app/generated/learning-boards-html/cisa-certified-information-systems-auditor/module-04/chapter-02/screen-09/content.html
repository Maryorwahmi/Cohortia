Recovery Validation

Ensuring Recoverability of Data

It's not enough to have backups; they must be regularly tested to ensure they are recoverable and can restore data to a consistent state within defined objectives.

  • Test Restores: Crucial for verifying backup integrity.
  • Recovery Time Objective (RTO): Max tolerable downtime.
  • Recovery Point Objective (RPO): Max tolerable data loss.
  • Documentation: Evidence of tests and RTO/RPO adherence.
Common Mistake

Assuming backups are working without ever performing a test restore. This can lead to a rude awakening during an actual disaster, finding backups are unusable.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must request and review evidence of recent test restores and verify that RTO and RPO targets are being met to ensure effective data recovery.
