Recognize Database Vulnerabilities

Identify common vulnerabilities and risks in database environments. This includes understanding threats like SQL injection, weak authentication, and misconfigurations to prevent potential breaches.

Apply Audit Principles

Reinforce foundational audit principles like independence and objectivity. This ensures your assessments are unbiased, credible, and adhere to professional standards, enhancing the value of your audit findings.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Ensure Data Availability

Understand the mechanisms and controls necessary to guarantee that authorized users can access data and information systems whenever required, minimizing downtime and business disruption.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08

Protect Data Confidentiality

Understand the methods and controls for protecting sensitive data from unauthorized disclosure. This involves implementing encryption, access restrictions, and other measures to maintain privacy and compliance.
