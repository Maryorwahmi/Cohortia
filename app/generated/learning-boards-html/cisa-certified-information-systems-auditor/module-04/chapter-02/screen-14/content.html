Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Data Integrity

The accuracy, completeness, and validity of data throughout its lifecycle.

Data Availability

The assurance that authorized users can access data and information systems when needed.

Data Confidentiality

The protection of data from unauthorized disclosure.

Least Privilege

Users and applications are granted only the minimum necessary permissions for their functions.

Chapter Summary

This chapter emphasized the critical role of IS auditors in ensuring robust data management and database administration, focusing on controls for data integrity, availability, and confidentiality, evaluating security configurations, assessing backup and recovery strategies, and highlighting common vulnerabilities.
