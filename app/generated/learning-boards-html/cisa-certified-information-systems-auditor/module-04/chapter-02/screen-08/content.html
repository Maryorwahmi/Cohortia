Strategy Components

Building a Resilient Backup Plan

A comprehensive backup strategy is vital for data availability and business continuity. It ensures that data can be restored after system failures, corruption, or other incidents.

  • Backup Types: Full, Differential, Incremental.
  • Frequency: How often backups are performed.
  • Storage Locations: On-site, Off-site, Cloud.
  • Retention Periods: How long data must be kept.
Why it Matters

Without a robust backup strategy, organizations risk catastrophic data loss, prolonged downtime, and severe financial and reputational damage in the event of a disaster.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must verify that backup strategies are clearly defined, documented, and align with the organization's recovery objectives and compliance requirements.
