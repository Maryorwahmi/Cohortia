Core Concept

The CIA Triad for Data

Data is the lifeblood of any modern organization. Its effective management and protection are paramount for business continuity and regulatory compliance. The CIA Triad defines the three core security goals.

  • Integrity: Data is accurate, complete, and valid.
  • Availability: Authorized users can access data when needed.
  • Confidentiality: Data is protected from unauthorized disclosure.
Common Mistake

Assuming data is protected without verifying controls for all three aspects of the CIA triad. A failure in one can compromise the others.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must ensure controls are in place and operating effectively to safeguard data integrity, availability, and confidentiality throughout its lifecycle.

Real-world Example

A failure in data integrity (e.g., corrupted customer orders) can lead to erroneous business decisions, while a breach in confidentiality (e.g., leaked customer data) can result in significant financial and reputational damage.
