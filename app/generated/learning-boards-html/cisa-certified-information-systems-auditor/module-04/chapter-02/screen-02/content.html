Learning Objectives

Skills You'll Master in This Chapter

Bloom's Taxonomy:
Learn Understand Apply Master
01

Identify Critical Data Controls

Learn to pinpoint essential controls for data integrity, availability, and confidentiality. This skill ensures you can verify that data is accurate, always accessible, and protected from unauthorized disclosure.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Evaluate Database Security

Develop the ability to assess database security configurations and access management. This includes reviewing user roles, privileges, and authentication mechanisms to prevent unauthorized access.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Assess Backup & Recovery

Gain expertise in evaluating data backup, recovery, and retention strategies. This ensures business continuity by verifying that data can be restored effectively and complies with legal requirements.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Understand DBA Role

Grasp the auditor's role in reviewing database administration activities and documentation. This includes verifying patching, performance tuning, and the quality of operational records.
