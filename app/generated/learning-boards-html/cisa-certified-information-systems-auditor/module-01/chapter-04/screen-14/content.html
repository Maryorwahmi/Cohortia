Key Concepts: Techniques and Frameworks

Essential methods and structures for effective IT auditing.

Evidence Collection Techniques

Methods like Inquiry, Observation, and Inspection for gathering audit evidence.

Advanced Evidence Techniques

Re-performance (independent execution) and Analytical Procedures (data relationship study).

CCCE Framework

Condition, Criteria, Cause, Effect – a structured approach for articulating audit findings.

Summary Takeaway

Applying a diverse set of evidence collection techniques and structuring findings with frameworks like CCCE ensures comprehensive, clear, and actionable audit results.
