Continuous Improvement

Ensuring Remediation: Follow-up Activities

Verifying the implementation and effectiveness of corrective actions.

Key Aspects of Follow-up

  • •
    Verify implementation of agreed-upon recommendations.
  • •
    Ensure identified control weaknesses are effectively remediated.
  • •
    Involves reviewing updated policies, re-testing controls, or examining new configurations.
Key Takeaway

Follow-up activities are essential to verify remediation and drive continuous improvement in IT controls, demonstrating the long-term value of the audit function.

Verification of Remediation

Confirming that corrective actions have been implemented as planned.

Re-testing Controls

Performing audit procedures again to ensure controls are now effective.

Continuous Improvement Cycle

Follow-up closes the loop, fostering ongoing enhancement of the control environment.
