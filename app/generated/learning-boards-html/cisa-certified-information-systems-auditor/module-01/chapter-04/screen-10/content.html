Reporting Findings

Structuring Audit Findings: The CCCE Framework

Clearly articulating control weaknesses using Condition, Criteria, Cause, and Effect.

The CCCE Framework

  • •
    Condition: What is the specific issue observed? (The 'what')
  • •
    Criteria: What should be happening? (The 'standard')
  • •
    Cause: Why did the issue occur? (The 'why')
  • •
    Effect/Impact: What is the risk or consequence? (The 'so what')
Key Takeaway

The CCCE framework ensures audit findings are clear, objective, and provide a complete picture of the issue, facilitating effective remediation.

Example Finding: MFA Not Enforced

Condition: Multi-factor authentication is not enforced for administrative access to the critical database.

Criteria: Company policy requires MFA for all administrative access to critical systems.

Cause: Oversight during system configuration.

Effect/Impact: Increased risk of unauthorized access and data breach.

Why CCCE Matters

This structured approach helps management quickly grasp the problem, understand the expected standard, identify the root cause, and recognize the potential impact, leading to more effective and timely corrective actions.
