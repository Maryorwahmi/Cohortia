Core Concept

The Foundation of Audit: Evidence Collection

Understanding the principles of sufficient, reliable, and relevant audit evidence.

Key Principles of Audit Evidence

  • •
    Sufficiency: Is there enough evidence to support the finding? Quantity matters.
  • •
    Reliability: Is the evidence trustworthy? Source and nature are key.
  • •
    Relevance: Does the evidence directly pertain to the audit objective?
Key Takeaway

Sufficient, reliable, and relevant evidence forms the bedrock of credible audit findings.

The Evidence Triangle
Example: Access Controls Audit
To assess overall control effectiveness, an auditor needs to review logs for a representative sample of users over a significant period, cross-reference with access policies, and interview relevant personnel. A single log entry is insufficient.
