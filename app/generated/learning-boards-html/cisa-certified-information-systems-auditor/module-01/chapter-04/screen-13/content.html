Key Concepts: Evidence and Documentation

Essential terms for collecting and recording audit information.

Audit Evidence

Information collected to support audit findings and conclusions; must be sufficient, reliable, and relevant.

Audit Working Papers

The official documentation of the audit, including procedures, evidence, findings, and conclusions.

IT Audit Report

A formal document communicating audit findings, conclusions, and recommendations to stakeholders.

Summary Takeaway

A successful IT audit relies on collecting high-quality evidence, meticulously documenting all procedures and findings, and clearly communicating results through a formal report to drive action.
