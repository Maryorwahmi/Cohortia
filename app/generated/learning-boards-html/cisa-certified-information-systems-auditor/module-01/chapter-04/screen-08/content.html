Audit Documentation

The Backbone of the Audit: Working Papers

Understanding the purpose and essential components of audit working papers.

Purpose of Working Papers

  • •
    Demonstrate proper audit planning and execution.
  • •
    Provide support for audit report findings and conclusions.
  • •
    Facilitate review by supervisors and external parties.
Key Takeaway

Working papers are the official record, validating audit execution, supporting findings, and ensuring accountability and reviewability.

Audit Program Steps

Documenting procedures performed.

Evidence Collected

Screenshots, log excerpts, interview notes.

Analysis & Findings

Detailed descriptions of issues identified.

Security Note

Working papers contain sensitive info; secure storage is vital.
