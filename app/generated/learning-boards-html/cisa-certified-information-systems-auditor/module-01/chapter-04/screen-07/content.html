Practical Application

A Holistic Approach to Evidence Collection

Combining multiple techniques for a comprehensive audit of a patch management process.

Patch Management Audit Scenario

  • •
    Inspect: Review patch management policy and procedures.
  • •
    Observe: Watch the patching process in action.
  • •
    Review: Examine patch deployment logs and reports.
  • •
    Interview: Speak with system administrators about their procedures.
  • •
    Re-perform: Conduct a vulnerability scan on sample systems to verify patch effectiveness.
Key Takeaway

Effective audits combine multiple techniques to verify controls from various angles, building a comprehensive and reliable assessment.
