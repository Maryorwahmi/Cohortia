Evidence Collection Techniques

Gathering Evidence: Inquiry, Observation, and Inspection

Exploring foundational methods for collecting audit evidence.

Core Evidence Collection Methods

  • •
    Inquiry (Interviews): Asking questions to understand processes and policies. (Mistake: Don't rely solely on verbal statements.)
  • •
    Observation: Directly watching activities being performed, like data center entry.
  • •
    Inspection (Document Review): Examining documents, records, and physical assets. Often the most reliable.
Key Takeaway

Combining inquiry, observation, and inspection provides a robust view of controls, with inspection often yielding the most reliable evidence.

Inquiry in Action

Interviewing IT managers about their incident response procedures.

Observation Example

Watching a system administrator perform a daily backup operation.

Inspection Scenario

Reviewing access logs for a critical database or physical security policies.
