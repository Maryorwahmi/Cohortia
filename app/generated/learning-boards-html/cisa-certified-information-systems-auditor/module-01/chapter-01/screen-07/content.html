IT Controls: The Foundation of Assurance

Policies and Procedures for Reliable IT Operations

IT controls are policies, procedures, practices, and organizational structures designed to provide reasonable assurance that business objectives are achieved and that undesired events are prevented, detected, or corrected. They are the bedrock of effective IT governance and risk management.

Key Takeaway

Well-designed and operating IT controls are essential for mitigating risks and ensuring the reliability of information systems.

Example Controls
  • Access controls (e.g., passwords, MFA).
  • Change management procedures.
  • Data backup and recovery processes.
  • Segregation of duties.
  • Security awareness training.

Purpose of IT Controls

Ensuring objectives are met and risks are managed.

Preventive

Stop errors or fraud before they occur (e.g., access controls).

Detective

Identify errors or fraud that have occurred (e.g., system logs).

Corrective

Fix errors or mitigate fraud once detected (e.g., data recovery).

Directive

Guide actions towards desired outcomes (e.g., policies).
