Key Concepts

Remember These Core Ideas

IT Audit

Systematic examination of IT systems for control effectiveness and compliance.

Assurance Services

Independent services improving information quality for decision-making.

IT Controls

Policies and procedures ensuring objectives are met and risks managed.

Risk Management

Identifying, assessing, and mitigating IT-related risks.

CISA

Certified Information Systems Auditor: expert in IT audit, control, and security.

Compliance

Adherence to laws, regulations, and standards.

These core concepts form the bedrock of understanding IT auditing and assurance, guiding your journey as a CISA professional.
