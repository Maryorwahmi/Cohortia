Introduction to IT Auditing and Assurance

Understanding the Foundation of Trust in Digital Systems

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts of IT auditing and assurance, defining its purpose, distinguishing it from related disciplines, and highlighting its benefits and the role of the CISA professional.
