Scenario: Auditing an E-commerce Platform

Applying IT Audit Principles to Protect Transactions

Auditing an e-commerce platform involves identifying business objectives, assessing risks like data breaches and non-compliance, and defining audit areas such as PCI DSS compliance and application security.

Key Takeaway

A structured approach, from business objectives to specific audit areas, is key to effectively auditing complex systems like e-commerce platforms.

Audit Focus Areas
  • Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) compliance.
  • Application security controls (e.g., preventing XSS, SQL injection).
  • Data privacy controls (e.g., GDPR compliance).
  • Secure transaction processing.
  • System availability and performance.

E-commerce Audit Steps

A practical approach to defining audit scope.

1. Business Objectives

Define goals (e.g., process orders, protect data).

2. Risk Identification

Identify threats (e.g., data breaches, non-compliance).

3. Audit Areas

Determine focus (e.g., PCI DSS, app security).

4. Control Evaluation

Assess existing controls.
