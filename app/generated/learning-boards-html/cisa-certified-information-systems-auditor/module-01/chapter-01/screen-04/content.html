Core Concept

Defining IT Auditing

A Systematic Examination for Assurance

IT auditing is a systematic examination of an organization's information technology infrastructure, applications, data, operations, and processes. Its primary goal is to evaluate the effectiveness of IT controls, ensure data integrity, assess compliance with relevant laws and regulations, and identify potential risks to information assets.

Key Takeaway

IT auditing provides assurance that IT systems are functioning correctly, securely, and in compliance with requirements.

Example

Auditing a customer database involves checking data accuracy, access controls, and backup procedures.

Key Components of an IT Audit

Focus areas for systematic examination.

Infrastructure

Servers, networks, hardware.

Applications

Software, databases, operating systems.

Data

Integrity, confidentiality, availability.

Processes

Operations, change management, security.
