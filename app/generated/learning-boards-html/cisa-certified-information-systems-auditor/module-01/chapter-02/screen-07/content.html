Performance Standards (1200 Series)
Effective Audit Execution

These standards provide the practical framework for conducting IT audit engagements, ensuring thoroughness and effectiveness.

Standard 1201: Engagement Planning

Requires a clear plan defining audit objectives, scope, and methodology, aligned with organizational goals.

Standard 1202: Risk Assessment in Planning

Mandates a risk-based approach, focusing audit efforts on areas with the highest potential impact to the organization's information assets.

Standard 1203: Performance and Supervision

Guides evidence collection, requiring sufficient and reliable information, and ensures proper supervision of audit staff.

Concrete Example
Auditing Access Controls

When auditing access controls for a critical financial application, an auditor must first plan the audit (1201) to understand the system's purpose and controls. Then, they must assess risks (1202), identifying potential threats like unauthorized access or data breaches. Based on this, they would collect evidence (1203) by reviewing access logs, user permissions, and configuration settings, ensuring the data gathered is sufficient and reliable to support their findings, rather than just accepting verbal assurances.

Key Takeaway

Effective audit execution relies on thorough planning, a risk-based approach, and diligent evidence collection, as guided by Performance Standards.
