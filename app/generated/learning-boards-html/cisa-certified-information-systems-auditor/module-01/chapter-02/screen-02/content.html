Learning Objectives
Mastering the ISACA IT Audit and Assurance Standards
01
Recognize Importance
Understand why professional standards are crucial for the credibility and effectiveness of IT auditing practices worldwide.
02
Identify Standards Categories
Differentiate between General, Performance, and Reporting standards, grasping their distinct roles in the audit process.
03
Explain Code of Ethics
Understand how ISACA's Code of Professional Ethics guides auditor conduct, integrity, and responsibility.
04
Apply Independence & Objectivity
Apply the core principles of independence and objectivity in real-world IT audit scenarios to ensure unbiased assessments.
