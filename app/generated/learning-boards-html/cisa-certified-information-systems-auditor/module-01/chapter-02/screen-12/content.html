Practical Application
Access Control Audit Scenario

Let's see how ISACA standards guide an audit of access controls for a financial application.

General Standards (1000 Series)

Independence & Objectivity: Confirm no prior involvement in system design/management. Maintain unbiased attitude.

Performance Standards (1200 Series)

Planning (1201): Define scope and objectives. Risk Assessment (1202): Focus on risks like unauthorized access. Evidence (1203): Review logs, permissions; avoid hearsay.

Reporting Standards (1400 Series)

Reporting (1401): Clearly state objectives, scope, findings (e.g., excessive privileges), conclusions (risk of data modification), and actionable recommendations (e.g., quarterly access reviews).

Outcome
Credible and Impactful Audit

By systematically applying ISACA's standards, the auditor ensures the review is thorough, objective, and provides valuable insights. This leads to a credible audit report that management can use to strengthen the organization's security posture and compliance.

Key Takeaway

Integrating ISACA's General, Performance, and Reporting standards ensures a comprehensive, objective, and impactful IT audit process.
