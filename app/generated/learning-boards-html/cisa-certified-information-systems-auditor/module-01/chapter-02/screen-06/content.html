General Standards (1000 Series)
Independence and Objectivity

The General Standards set the foundational principles for IT auditors, focusing on their personal and professional conduct. Key among these are independence and objectivity, ensuring unbiased assessments.

Standard 1001: Organizational Independence

The IT audit function must be independent of the activities it audits. This prevents conflicts of interest, ensuring impartial reviews.

Standard 1002: Professional Independence

Auditors must maintain an unbiased mental attitude, approaching engagements with objectivity and avoiding undue influence or preconceived notions.

Scenario
Auditing Your Own Work?

Imagine you were a key developer on a new customer relationship management (CRM) system. Six months after its successful launch, you are asked to lead the audit of its security controls. According to Standard 1001, this situation presents a direct conflict of interest, compromising your organizational independence. A different auditor, not involved in the CRM's development, should conduct this review to ensure objectivity.

Key Takeaway

Independence and objectivity are paramount; auditors must avoid conflicts of interest to ensure unbiased and credible audit findings.
