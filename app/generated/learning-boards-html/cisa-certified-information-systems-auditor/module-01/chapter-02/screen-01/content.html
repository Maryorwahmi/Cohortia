ISACA IT Audit and Assurance Standards
The Foundation of Trust and Quality in IT Auditing
In the dynamic world of information technology, the role of an IT auditor is paramount. These standards serve as the essential blueprint and ethical compass for our profession, ensuring consistency, quality, and credibility in every audit performed. Adhering to these guidelines is not just about compliance; it's about building and maintaining the trust that is the cornerstone of IT auditing.
