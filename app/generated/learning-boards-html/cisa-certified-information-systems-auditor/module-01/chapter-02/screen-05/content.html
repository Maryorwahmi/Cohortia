Standard Structure
General, Performance, and Reporting Standards

ISACA organizes its IT Audit and Assurance Standards into three distinct categories, each addressing a critical facet of the audit lifecycle:

General Standards (1000 Series)

Focus on the auditor's qualifications, competence, independence, objectivity, and ethical conduct. They set the foundation for professional integrity.

Performance Standards (1200 Series)

Guide the actual execution of audit work, including planning, risk assessment, supervision, and evidence collection, ensuring efficiency and effectiveness.

Reporting Standards (1400 Series)

Dictate how audit findings are communicated, ensuring reports are clear, objective, constructive, and timely for management action.

Hierarchy Visual
Standards Framework

Imagine a pyramid:

  • Top: General Standards (Who you are, how you behave)
  • Middle: Performance Standards (What you do, how you do it)
  • Bottom: Reporting Standards (How you communicate results)

This structure ensures a complete and compliant audit process from start to finish.

Key Takeaway

Understanding the General, Performance, and Reporting standards is key to conducting a compliant and effective IT audit.
