Ethical Framework
Core Principles of Conduct

The ISACA Code of Professional Ethics guides IT auditors to act with integrity, competence, and responsibility.

Uphold Standards

Support and implement appropriate standards and procedures in practice.

Due Diligence & Care

Perform duties competently and conscientiously, exercising professional judgment.

Honesty & Lawfulness

Serve stakeholders lawfully and honestly, maintaining integrity in all dealings.

Confidentiality

Protect confidential information unless legally required to disclose.

Competence

Maintain and enhance professional knowledge and skills through continuous learning.

Ethical Dilemma
Confidential Information

An auditor discovers sensitive employee performance data during a review of HR system access logs, which is unrelated to the audit's scope of system security. According to the Code of Ethics, the auditor must maintain confidentiality and not disclose this information, as it was obtained incidentally and is outside the audit's objectives. This principle ensures trust and protects privacy.

Key Takeaway

The ISACA Code of Professional Ethics provides a vital ethical compass, guiding auditors to act with integrity, competence, and confidentiality.
