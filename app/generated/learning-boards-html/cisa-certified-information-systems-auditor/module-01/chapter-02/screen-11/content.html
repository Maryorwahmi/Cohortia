Application in Practice
Ensuring Unbiased Audits

Actively applying independence and objectivity is crucial for maintaining the integrity and credibility of IT audit work.

Organizational Independence (1001)

Avoid auditing areas you were involved in developing or managing. Example: Not auditing a system you helped build.

Professional Independence & Objectivity (1002)

Maintain an unbiased mental attitude. Avoid personal relationships or stakes that could influence judgment. Seek objective evidence.

Scenario
Disaster Recovery Audit

An auditor is assigned to review the data center's Disaster Recovery (DR) plan. To ensure organizational independence, they confirm they had no role in creating or recently modifying the plan. To maintain professional objectivity, they ensure personal relationships with the DR team manager do not influence their assessment, focusing solely on evidence like DR test results and documented procedures.

Key Takeaway

Consistently applying independence and objectivity principles ensures that audit findings are fair, accurate, and defensible.
