What to Remember
Consolidating Your Knowledge of IT Audit Standards
ISACA Standards
General, Performance, and Reporting standards form the core framework for IT audits.
Performance Standards
Emphasize planning, risk assessment, and evidence collection for effective audit execution.
Reporting Standards
Ensure clear, objective, and actionable communication of audit findings to management.
Code of Ethics
The ethical compass guiding competence, integrity, confidentiality, and professional conduct.
Independence & Objectivity
Crucial for unbiased audits; independence is positional, objectivity is mental.
Summary Takeaway

Adherence to ISACA's standards and ethical principles is fundamental for IT auditors to ensure credible, effective, and trustworthy audits.
