Key Concepts

More essential terms to solidify your understanding

Likelihood

The probability of a specific IT risk event occurring.

Impact

The magnitude of harm that could result if a specific risk event occurs.

IT Audit Program

A detailed, step-by-step plan outlining the specific procedures and tests to be performed during an IT audit.

Engagement Letter

A formal document that outlines the terms, objectives, scope, responsibilities, and deliverables of an audit engagement.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts provide the framework for assessing, planning, and formalizing IT audit engagements, ensuring clarity and effectiveness.
