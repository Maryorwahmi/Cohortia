Risk Identification

Identifying Common IT Risks

IT risks can be categorized into various types, each posing a unique threat to an organization's information assets and business objectives.

  • Technical Risks: Vulnerabilities in hardware, software, or network.
  • Operational Risks: Failures in processes, people, or internal systems.
  • Compliance Risks: Non-adherence to laws, regulations, or industry standards.
Key Takeaway

A comprehensive understanding of technical, operational, and compliance risks is essential for effective IT audit planning.

Risk Categories & Examples

Technical Risks

Unpatched software, weak encryption, insecure configurations, network vulnerabilities.

Operational Risks

Inadequate change management, lack of disaster recovery plan, insufficient training, human error.

Compliance Risks

Failure to meet GDPR, PCI DSS, HIPAA, or other regulatory requirements.

Real-World Impact

GDPR Non-Compliance

Failing to comply with GDPR data protection regulations can lead to significant fines, reputational damage, and loss of customer trust, directly impacting business objectives.
