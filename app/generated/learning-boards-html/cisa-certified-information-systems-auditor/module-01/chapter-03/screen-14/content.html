Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Audit Planning

The initial phase of an IT audit where objectives, scope, resources, and methodology are defined.

Risk-Based Auditing

An audit approach that prioritizes efforts on areas posing the highest risk to information assets and business objectives.

IT Risk

The business risk associated with the use, ownership, operation, involvement, influence, and adoption of IT within an enterprise.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering these foundational concepts is crucial for any CISA to conduct effective, efficient, and value-driven IT audits that protect organizational assets.
