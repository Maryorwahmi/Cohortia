Risk Assessment

Quantifying Risk: Likelihood and Impact

For each identified IT risk, auditors assess its likelihood (probability of occurrence) and its potential impact (magnitude of harm) to prioritize audit efforts effectively.

  • Likelihood: How probable is the risk event? (e.g., Low, Medium, High)
  • Impact: What would be the consequences if the risk occurs? (e.g., Financial, Reputational, Operational)
Key Takeaway

Prioritizing risks based on both likelihood and impact ensures that critical vulnerabilities receive appropriate audit attention and resource allocation.
