Practical Application

Audit Procedures in Practice

When a risk assessment highlights a specific vulnerability, the audit program includes targeted procedures to gather evidence and test controls related to that risk.

  • Targeted Testing: Procedures are designed to directly address identified risks.
  • Evidence Gathering: Focuses on collecting verifiable information.
  • Control Verification: Assesses the effectiveness of existing controls.
Key Takeaway

Effective audit procedures are specific, measurable, and directly linked to assessing and mitigating identified IT risks.

Scenario: Data Integrity Risk

Audit Procedures for a Database

1. Review Change Management Logs

Verify authorized and documented modifications to database schema or data.

2. Test Data Validation Controls

Check if the system prevents incorrect or malicious data input.

3. Reconcile Sample Data

Compare data between source and target systems to ensure consistency.

4. Interview DBAs on Backup/Recovery

Assess the robustness of data restoration mechanisms.

Common Pitfall

Generic Procedures

Using generic audit procedures without tailoring them to specific risks can lead to missed vulnerabilities and wasted effort on low-risk areas.
