IT Audit Planning and Risk Assessment

Laying the Groundwork for Effective IT Audits

This chapter will guide you through the essential phases of planning an IT audit, from understanding the business context to developing a risk-based approach and formalizing the engagement. You'll learn to identify critical IT risks and craft audit programs that deliver maximum value.

Key Takeaway

Effective IT audits begin with meticulous planning and thorough risk assessment, setting the direction and scope for the entire engagement.

Audit Planning

Define objectives, scope, and resources.

Risk Assessment

Identify, analyze, and prioritize IT risks.

Audit Program

Develop detailed procedures and tests.
