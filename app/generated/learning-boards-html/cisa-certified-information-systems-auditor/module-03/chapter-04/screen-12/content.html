Defect Management: Tracking and Resolution

A structured approach to identifying, prioritizing, and resolving software defects.

Defect Lifecycle

  • •Logging: Documenting all identified defects.
  • •Tracking: Monitoring the status of each defect.
  • •Prioritizing: Ranking defects based on severity and business impact.
  • •Resolving: Fixing the identified issues.
  • •Verifying: Retesting to confirm the fix.

Auditor's Review

  • •Assess: Thoroughness of testing.
  • •Evaluate: Effectiveness of the resolution process.
  • •Verify: Critical bugs are prioritized and retested.
Key Takeaway

A well-managed defect process is crucial for ensuring that identified issues are systematically addressed and verified, minimizing residual risk.

Risk Alert

Lack of formal defect prioritization can lead to critical security vulnerabilities or business-impacting bugs being delayed or unaddressed.
