Risk Management

Common Testing Pitfalls and Their Consequences

Understanding the risks of inadequate testing and quality assurance.

Common Pitfalls

  • •Insufficient test coverage (missing critical scenarios).
  • •Using unrealistic test data.
  • •Skipping security testing.
  • •Inadequate defect management.
  • •Pressure to 'pass' testing prematurely.
  • •Lack of independent testing.

Severe Consequences

  • •Critical bugs halting business operations.
  • •Severe security vulnerabilities leading to data breaches.
  • •Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
  • •Significant financial losses due to system failures.
  • •Reputational damage and loss of customer trust.
Key Takeaway

Untested or poorly tested software poses significant risks, making the CISA's assurance over testing and QA vital for organizational safeguarding.
