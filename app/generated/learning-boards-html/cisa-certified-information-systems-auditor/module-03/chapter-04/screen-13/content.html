Auditor's Responsibilities

The Auditor's Role in Testing and QA

Providing assurance over the integrity and effectiveness of software quality processes.

01

Review Test Plans

  • •Assess completeness, coverage, and alignment with requirements.
02

Verify Independence & UAT

  • •Ensure testing is independent; UAT by actual business users.
03

Assess Test Data & Defect Logs

  • •Confirm data adequacy; examine defect tracking and resolution.
04

Evaluate Security Testing & Sign-off

  • •Review security test results; confirm formal UAT sign-off.
Key Takeaway

The auditor's comprehensive review of testing and QA processes is essential for ensuring system reliability, security, and compliance.
