System and User Acceptance Testing: Validating Requirements

Ensuring the complete system meets specifications and user needs.

01

System Testing

  • •Focus: Complete, integrated system.
  • •Purpose: Evaluate compliance with specified requirements (functional, non-functional, performance).
  • •Example: Testing an e-commerce site from user login to product purchase and order confirmation.
02

User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

  • •Focus: End-users or business representatives.
  • •Purpose: Validate the system against actual business needs and scenarios.
  • •Example: Business users performing typical daily tasks in the new system to confirm it supports their workflows.
Key Takeaway

UAT sign-off is a critical control point, indicating business acceptance and readiness for deployment.

Auditor's Focus

Auditors verify UAT is conducted by actual business users, not just the project team, to ensure true business validation.
