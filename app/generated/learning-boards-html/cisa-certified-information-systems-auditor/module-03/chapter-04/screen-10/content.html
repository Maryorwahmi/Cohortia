Testing Techniques

Regression, Black-box, and White-box Testing

Maintaining functionality and varying levels of internal knowledge.

01

Regression Testing

  • •Purpose: Ensure new changes don't break existing functionality.
  • •Method: Retesting previously passed test cases.
  • •Benefit: Maintains system stability and prevents unintended side effects.
02

Black-box Testing

  • •Knowledge: No knowledge of internal structure or code.
  • •Focus: Inputs and outputs, user interface.
  • •Analogy: Testing a black box without knowing its internal mechanisms.
03

White-box Testing

  • •Knowledge: Full knowledge of internal structure, code, and design.
  • •Focus: Code paths, internal logic, data flows.
  • •Analogy: Testing a transparent box where all internal workings are visible.
Key Takeaway

Choosing the right testing technique depends on the objective and the level of internal system knowledge available.
