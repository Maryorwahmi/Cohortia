Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Quality Foundations

Essential terms for understanding software quality.

Quality Assurance (QA)

Process-oriented activities focused on preventing defects and ensuring quality throughout development.

Quality Control (QC)

Product-oriented activities focused on identifying and fixing defects, primarily through testing.

Unit Testing

Testing individual software components or modules in isolation to ensure correct function.

Summary Takeaway

QA, QC, and Unit Testing are fundamental for building and verifying software quality from the ground up.
