Test Management

Essential Test Planning and Environments

Laying the groundwork for effective and reliable testing.

Test Plan

  • •Outlines: Scope, objectives, resources, schedule, entry/exit criteria.
  • •Defines: Test cases, expected results, required data.
  • •Importance: Ensures structured and comprehensive testing.

Test Data Management

  • •Requirement: Representative of production data.
  • •Security: Often anonymized or sanitized for sensitive information.
  • •Importance: Ensures realistic test scenarios without privacy risks.

Test Environment

  • •Isolation: Separate from development and production.
  • •Configuration: Closely mirrors the production environment.
  • •Importance: Ensures accurate and reproducible test results.
Key Takeaway

A well-defined test plan, secure test data, and a realistic test environment are non-negotiable for reliable testing outcomes.
