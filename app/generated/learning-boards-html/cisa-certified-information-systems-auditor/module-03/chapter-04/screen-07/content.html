Performance Testing: Under Pressure

Evaluating system responsiveness, stability, and scalability under various load conditions.

Load Testing

  • •Purpose: Assess system behavior under expected user load.
  • •Goal: Confirm system handles typical workload without degradation.

Stress Testing

  • •Purpose: Push system beyond normal limits (extreme load).
  • •Goal: Identify breaking points and recovery mechanisms.

Scalability Testing

  • •Purpose: Determine system's ability to handle increasing workloads.
  • •Goal: Assess performance with added resources (e.g., more servers).
Key Takeaway

Adequate performance testing is crucial for critical systems to prevent outages and ensure business continuity under peak demand.
