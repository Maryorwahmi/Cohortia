Core Concept

QA vs. QC: Proactive Prevention vs. Reactive Detection

Understanding the distinct focuses of Quality Assurance and Quality Control.

Quality Assurance (QA)

  • •Process-Oriented: Focuses on the methods and procedures used to create the software.
  • •Proactive: Aims to prevent defects from occurring in the first place.
  • •Goal: Build quality into the system from the start.
  • •Example: Defining coding standards, conducting design reviews, establishing clear requirements.
Key Takeaway

QA sets the stage for quality by preventing issues, while QC validates the outcome by finding and fixing them.

Quality Control (QC) & Testing

  • •Product-Oriented: Focuses on the actual software product.
  • •Reactive: Aims to identify and fix defects after they have occurred.
  • •Goal: Verify the product meets specified requirements.
  • •Example: Running test cases, debugging code, performing user acceptance testing.
Common Misconception

Confusing QA and QC can lead to a reactive-only approach, missing opportunities to prevent costly defects early in the SDLC.
