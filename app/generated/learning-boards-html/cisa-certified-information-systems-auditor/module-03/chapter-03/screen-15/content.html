Key Concepts

More essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Segregation of Duties (SoD)

Divides critical functions among different individuals to prevent fraud or error.

Secure Coding Standards

Guidelines for writing code that minimizes security vulnerabilities from the outset.

Change Management

Formal process for controlling changes to IT systems, ensuring authorization, testing, and documentation.

Summary Takeaway

Implementing specific controls like SoD, secure coding, and robust change management is crucial for maintaining system integrity and security throughout its lifecycle.
