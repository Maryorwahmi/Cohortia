Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Preventive Controls

Controls designed to stop errors or unauthorized actions from occurring (e.g., SoD, secure coding).

Detective Controls

Controls designed to identify errors or unauthorized actions after they have occurred (e.g., audit trails, vulnerability scans).

Corrective Controls

Controls designed to correct errors or recover from incidents (e.g., backup and recovery, incident response).

Summary Takeaway

A robust control framework integrates preventive, detective, and corrective measures to create a comprehensive defense-in-depth strategy for information systems.
