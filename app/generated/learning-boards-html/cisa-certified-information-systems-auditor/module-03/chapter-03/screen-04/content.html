What are Controls?

Controls are mechanisms implemented to ensure the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of data and systems, mitigate risks, and ensure compliance with policies and regulations.

  • They act as safeguards against errors, irregularities, and unauthorized actions.
  • Crucial for building trustworthy and reliable information systems.
  • A core responsibility for CISAs to evaluate their effectiveness.
Key Takeaway

Effective controls are fundamental for protecting organizational assets, ensuring business continuity, and maintaining regulatory compliance in all IS endeavors.

Three Pillars of Control

Preventive Controls

Stop errors or unauthorized actions from occurring.

Detective Controls

Identify errors or unauthorized actions after they occur.

Corrective Controls

Correct errors or recover from incidents once detected.
