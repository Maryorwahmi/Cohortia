What are Detective Controls?

These controls are designed to identify errors, irregularities, or unauthorized actions after they have occurred. They provide an alert or evidence of a deviation.

  • Focus on monitoring and identifying anomalies.
  • Crucial for uncovering issues that bypass preventive measures.
  • Provide the necessary information for corrective actions.
Pro-Tip

Regular review of detective control outputs (e.g., logs, scan reports) is as important as the controls themselves to ensure timely identification of issues.

Key Takeaway

Detective controls are essential for visibility into system activities and for triggering appropriate responses when security incidents or errors occur.

Examples of Detective Controls

Audit Trails & Logging

Recording all significant system events and user activities for review and forensic analysis.

Code Reviews

Independent examination of source code by peers to identify bugs, vulnerabilities, or non-compliance.

Vulnerability Scanning & Pen Testing

Automated or manual tests to identify security weaknesses in applications and infrastructure.
