Evaluating Control Effectiveness

A CISA's role involves not only identifying controls but also evaluating their design and operating effectiveness throughout the SDLC.

  • Assess if controls are well-designed to mitigate specific risks.
  • Verify if controls are functioning as intended in practice.
  • Provide assurance that systems are trustworthy and compliant.
Example Scenario

Reviewing change requests to ensure proper initiation, approval by the Change Advisory Board (CAB), testing, and documentation before deployment.

Key Takeaway

The CISA's comprehensive evaluation of controls is critical for identifying weaknesses and ensuring the overall security and reliability of information systems.

CISA's Evaluation Activities

Review Documentation

Examine policies, procedures, and system configurations for control design.

Interview Personnel

Gather insights from staff on control implementation and perception.

Test Controls

Perform direct tests (e.g., examining access logs) to verify operating effectiveness.
