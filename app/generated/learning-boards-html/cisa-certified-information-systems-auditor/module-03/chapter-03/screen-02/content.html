Learning Objectives

What you'll achieve by the end of this chapter.

Bloom's Taxonomy:
LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Classify Control Types

Identify and classify various types of controls (preventive, detective, corrective) applicable throughout the IS acquisition and development lifecycle. This skill is crucial for understanding how different controls address specific risks at various stages.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Integrate Controls into SDLC

Understand the critical importance of integrating controls directly into the design and development processes of information systems. This ensures security is built-in, not bolted on, leading to more resilient systems.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
