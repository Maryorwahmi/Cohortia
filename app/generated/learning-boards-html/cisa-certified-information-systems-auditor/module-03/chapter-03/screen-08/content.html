Module 3›Chapter 3.3›SDLC Controls: Requirements & Design

Requirements Phase Controls

Controls in this phase ensure that security and functional requirements are clearly defined and agreed upon by all stakeholders.

  • Formal User Sign-off: Ensures all requirements, including security, are reviewed and approved.
  • Traceability Matrix: Links requirements to design, development, and testing, ensuring comprehensive coverage.
Why it Matters

Clear, approved requirements prevent scope creep and ensure security is considered from the very beginning of the project.

Design Phase Controls

Controls here focus on 'security by design,' embedding security into the system's architecture and components.

  • Security Architecture Reviews: Experts scrutinize the system design for potential vulnerabilities.
  • Data Classification: Categorizing data by sensitivity to ensure appropriate handling and protection.
  • Encryption Standards: Incorporating encryption for sensitive data in transit and at rest.
Real-world Connection

Designing with security in mind reduces the cost and complexity of fixing vulnerabilities later in the development cycle.

Key Takeaway

Proactive control integration in requirements and design phases is foundational for building secure, compliant, and resilient information systems.
