What are Preventive Controls?

These controls are designed to stop errors, irregularities, or unauthorized actions from occurring in the first place. They are proactive measures.

  • Focus on preventing issues rather than detecting or correcting them.
  • Implemented early in the process or system design.
  • Reduce the likelihood of risks materializing.
Common Mistake

Relying solely on detective or corrective controls without sufficient preventive measures can leave systems vulnerable to initial breaches or errors.

Key Takeaway

Strong preventive controls are the most cost-effective way to manage risk, as they avoid the need for costly detection and recovery efforts.

Examples of Preventive Controls

Segregation of Duties (SoD)

Prevents single individuals from having end-to-end control over critical processes, reducing fraud and error risk.

Secure Coding Standards

Guidelines for developers to write inherently secure code, minimizing vulnerabilities from the start.

Access Controls

Restricting access to sensitive data or production environments based on user roles and permissions.
