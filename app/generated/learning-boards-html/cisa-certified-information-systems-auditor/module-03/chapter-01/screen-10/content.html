Project Management Methodologies: Agile

Iterative Development for Evolving Requirements

Agile Characteristics

  • •Iterative and incremental approach.
  • •Emphasizes flexibility, collaboration, rapid delivery.
  • •Ideal for evolving requirements and quick feedback.
  • •Frameworks: Scrum, Kanban.

Example: Developing a new mobile application with changing user needs.

Audit Implications

  • •Assess controls in iterative processes (stand-ups, sprint reviews).
  • •Verify product owner effectiveness and user story definition.
  • •Ensure security is integrated (DevSecOps).
  • •Beware of 'Wagile' projects lacking true agility.

Misconception: Agile means no documentation; it means just-enough, just-in-time documentation.

Key Takeaway

Agile methodologies offer flexibility and rapid delivery for evolving projects, requiring auditors to focus on embedded process controls and continuous stakeholder engagement.
