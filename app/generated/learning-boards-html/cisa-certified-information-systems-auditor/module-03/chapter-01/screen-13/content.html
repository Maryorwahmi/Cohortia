Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Business Case

A document justifying a project, detailing problem, solution, strategic alignment, costs, benefits, and risks.

Return on Investment (ROI)

A performance measure evaluating the efficiency or profitability of an investment.

Waterfall Methodology

A linear, sequential project management approach where each phase must be completed before the next.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts form the bedrock of understanding IT project initiation, justification, and traditional management approaches, crucial for any CISA.
