Key Components of a Business Case - Part 3

Exploring Alternatives and Feasibility

06

Alternative Analysis

Explores other potential solutions, including the option of doing nothing, to demonstrate that the chosen path is the optimal one.

  • •Consider multiple viable solutions.
  • •Include 'do nothing' option.

Example: Upgrade existing system vs. new acquisition.

07

Feasibility Study

Confirms the project's practicality across various dimensions: technical, operational, economic, legal, and schedule.

  • •Assess technical, operational, economic, legal, schedule feasibility.
  • •Ensures project is achievable within constraints.

Example: Does the organization have the technical expertise?

Auditor's Focus

Verify that alternatives were genuinely explored and that feasibility studies are thorough and realistic, preventing impractical or suboptimal project choices.
