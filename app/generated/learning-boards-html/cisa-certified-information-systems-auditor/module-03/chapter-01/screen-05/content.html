Key Components of a Business Case - Part 1

Defining the Problem, Solution, and Strategic Alignment

01

Problem Statement

Clearly articulates the organizational need or opportunity the project addresses. Ensures the project solves a real issue, not just a technical desire.

Example: "Manual data entry leads to 15% error rate and 20 hours/week wasted."

02

Proposed Solution

Details how the new system or acquisition will resolve the identified problem. Should be clear and concise, outlining the approach.

Example: "Implement automated cloud-based data validation system."

03

Strategic Alignment

Demonstrates how the project contributes to the organization's overarching goals and mission. Ensures the project supports broader business objectives.

Example: "Supports 'Digital Transformation' initiative to improve operational efficiency."

Auditor's Focus

Verify that the problem is real, the solution is appropriate, and the project genuinely supports strategic objectives, preventing misdirected investments.
