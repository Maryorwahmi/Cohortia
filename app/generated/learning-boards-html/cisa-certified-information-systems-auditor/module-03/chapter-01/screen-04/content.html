The Foundation of IT Projects: The Business Case

Justifying IT Investments for Strategic Alignment and Value

What is a Business Case?

A formal document that justifies the undertaking of a project, detailing the problem it solves, the proposed solution, strategic alignment, costs, benefits, and risks.

  • •Provides primary justification for IT projects.
  • •Outlines problem, benefits, and resource consumption.
  • •Forces critical thinking before resource commitment.
Why it Matters for CISA

Ensuring Strategic Alignment & Value

Auditors verify that IT investments contribute to organizational goals and deliver tangible value, preventing 'pet projects' or 'runaway projects' that lack clear purpose or consume excessive resources without expected returns. This ensures accountability and responsible resource allocation.

Consequence of Absence

Risk of Misaligned or Failed Projects

Without a clear business case, projects can lack strategic alignment, leading to wasted resources, missed objectives, and systems that do not meet genuine business needs. This can result in significant financial losses and operational inefficiencies for the organization.

Key Takeaway

A well-developed business case is the cornerstone of responsible IT investment, providing clear justification and strategic direction for all projects.
