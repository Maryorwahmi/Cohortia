Common Pitfalls and Audit Vigilance

Identifying and Mitigating Business Case and Project Management Weaknesses

Business Case Mistakes

  • •Overly optimistic benefit projections.
  • •Underestimating costs.
  • •Failing to identify all relevant risks.
  • •Lacking strategic alignment.

Example: Promising high ROI without accounting for training costs.

Project Management Pitfalls

  • •Scope creep (uncontrolled changes).
  • •Inadequate risk management.
  • •Poor communication.
  • •Lack of adherence to methodology ('Wagile').

Example: Project claiming Agile but lacking frequent feedback.

Key Takeaway

Auditors must be vigilant in identifying common business case and project management pitfalls early to prevent project failure, financial losses, and misaligned IT systems.
