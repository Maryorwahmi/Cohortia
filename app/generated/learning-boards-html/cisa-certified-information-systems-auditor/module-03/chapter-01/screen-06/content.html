Key Components of a Business Case - Part 2

Financial Justification and Risk Assessment

04

Cost-Benefit Analysis

Quantifies tangible (e.g., reduced costs, increased revenue) and intangible (e.g., improved satisfaction) benefits against all project costs.

  • •Quantify tangible & intangible benefits.
  • •Include all project costs (dev, hardware, training, maintenance).

Metrics: ROI, NPV, IRR

05

Risk Assessment

Identifies potential project risks (technical, operational, financial, compliance) and proposes mitigation strategies to address them proactively.

  • •Identify technical, operational, financial, compliance risks.
  • •Propose clear mitigation strategies.

Example: Data migration complexity, user adoption issues.

Auditor's Focus

Verify assumptions in financial calculations, challenge optimistic projections, and ensure comprehensive risk identification with realistic mitigation plans.
