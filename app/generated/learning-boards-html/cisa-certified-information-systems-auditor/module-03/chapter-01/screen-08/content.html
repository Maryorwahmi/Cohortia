Project Management Fundamentals

Guiding Projects to Meet Requirements and Deliver Outcomes

What is Project Management?

The application of knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques to project activities to meet the project requirements.

  • •Ensures projects are controlled and on track.
  • •Delivers expected outcomes and value.
  • •Crucial for managing IT investments responsibly.
Auditor's Perspective

Assessing Project Control & Progress

Auditors leverage their understanding of project management to evaluate whether projects are being executed effectively, risks are being managed, and objectives are being met. This ensures accountability and helps prevent project failures.

Risk of Poor Management

Budget Overruns & Missed Objectives

Inadequate project management can lead to uncontrolled changes, resource misallocation, and a lack of clear direction, resulting in projects that exceed budgets, miss deadlines, and fail to deliver the intended business value.

Key Takeaway

Effective project management is vital for translating a justified business case into a successful IT solution, ensuring control and value delivery.
