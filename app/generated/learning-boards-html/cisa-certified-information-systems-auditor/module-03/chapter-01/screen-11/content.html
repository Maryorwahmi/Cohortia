Establishing Effective Project Governance

Directing and Controlling Project Success and Accountability

What is Project Governance?

The framework for directing and controlling a project, including roles, responsibilities, and decision-making processes.

  • •Ensures accountability and timely decisions.
  • •Provides strategic oversight.
  • •Involves project sponsor, manager, steering committee.
Auditor's Review

Assessing Structure & Implementation

Auditors review project charters, organizational structures, reporting lines, and decision-making processes. They verify active engagement of steering committees and adequate authority/resources for project managers.

Risk of Weak Governance

Lack of Accountability & Project Drift

Weak governance can lead to unclear responsibilities, delayed decisions, and projects losing strategic alignment, increasing the risk of failure and inefficient resource utilization.

Key Takeaway

Robust project governance is essential for providing direction, control, and accountability, ensuring projects remain aligned with strategic goals and achieve success.
