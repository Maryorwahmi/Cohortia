Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Agile Methodology

An iterative and incremental project management approach focused on flexibility, collaboration, and rapid delivery.

Project Governance

The framework by which project decisions are made, including roles, responsibilities, and decision-making processes.

Scope Creep

The uncontrolled expansion of a project's requirements without corresponding adjustments to time, cost, or resources.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts highlight modern project management approaches, the importance of structured oversight, and a critical risk to manage for project success.
