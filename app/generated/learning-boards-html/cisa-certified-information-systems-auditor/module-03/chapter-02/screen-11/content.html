Methodology Spotlight

Agile Methodologies

Embracing flexibility and iterative development

Core Principles of Agile

  • •**Individuals & Interactions:** Over processes and tools.
  • •**Working Software:** Over comprehensive documentation.
  • •**Customer Collaboration:** Over contract negotiation.
  • •**Responding to Change:** Over following a plan.

CISA Audit Focus in Agile

  • •**Process Effectiveness:** Evaluating iterative cycles and feedback loops.
  • •**Transparency:** Visibility of product backlog and progress.
  • •**Integrated Security (DevSecOps):** Security activities throughout development.
  • •**Stakeholder Engagement:** Regular reviews and acceptance.
Key Takeaway

Agile offers flexibility for evolving requirements, requiring auditors to focus on continuous feedback, process transparency, and integrated security practices.
