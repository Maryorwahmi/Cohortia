Waterfall Phases 5 & 6

Deployment & Maintenance

Bringing the system live and ensuring its longevity

Deployment (Go-Live)

  • •**Installation:** System moved to production.
  • •**Data Migration:** Transferring existing data.
  • •**User Training:** Educating end-users.

Maintenance & CISA Audit Focus

  • •**Deployment Plan:** Verification of procedures, including rollback.
  • •**Data Migration Integrity:** Ensuring data is transferred accurately.
  • •**Security Hardening:** Production system security configuration.
  • •**Change Management:** Paramount control for all modifications (authorized, tested, documented).
Key Takeaway

Auditors ensure secure deployment with rollback plans and robust change management for ongoing maintenance, preserving system integrity and security post-go-live.
