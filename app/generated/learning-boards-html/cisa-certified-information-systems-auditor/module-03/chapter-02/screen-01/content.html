System Development Life Cycle (SDLC)

Phases, Methodologies, and Audit Considerations

The SDLC provides a structured framework for managing the development, acquisition, and maintenance of information systems. For a CISA, understanding the SDLC is paramount as it dictates how controls are designed, implemented, and managed throughout a system's existence.

Key Idea

The SDLC is the foundational process for building and maintaining secure, reliable, and effective information systems, directly impacting an organization's risk posture.

Structured Phases

Sequential steps for system development.

Diverse Methodologies

Waterfall, Agile, Prototyping, RAD.

Integrated Controls

Security and quality built-in.

Audit Perspective

Evaluating risk and compliance.
