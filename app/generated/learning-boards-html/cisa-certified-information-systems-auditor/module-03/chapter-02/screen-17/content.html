Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

Maintenance Phase

The ongoing phase of supporting, enhancing, and fixing the system after deployment.

Waterfall SDLC

A linear, sequential SDLC model where each phase must be completed before the next begins.

Agile SDLC

An iterative, incremental SDLC model emphasizing flexibility, collaboration, and rapid delivery.

Scrum

A popular Agile framework that uses short development cycles called sprints.

Kanban

An Agile method focused on visualizing workflow and continuous delivery.

Prototyping

An SDLC approach that involves creating working models of the system to gather feedback.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding diverse SDLC methodologies and their specific audit considerations is crucial for a CISA to effectively assess project management and risk mitigation strategies.
