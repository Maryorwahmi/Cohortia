Common SDLC Mistakes & CISA Evaluation

Avoiding pitfalls and assessing methodology appropriateness

Frequent SDLC Pitfalls

  • •**Skipping Phases:** Inadequate testing, rushed requirements.
  • •**Insufficient User Involvement:** System doesn't meet needs.
  • •**Poor Documentation:** Hinders maintenance and understanding.
  • •**Weak Change Management:** Unauthorized/untested modifications.
  • •**Late Security Integration:** Costly to fix fundamental vulnerabilities.

CISA's Evaluation Role

  • •**Methodology Appropriateness:** Is the chosen SDLC suitable for project characteristics (e.g., regulatory compliance, complexity, requirement stability)?
  • •**Control Effectiveness:** Are controls adequately designed and operating effectively within each phase?
  • •**Risk Mitigation:** Does the SDLC effectively mitigate risks to system integrity, confidentiality, and availability?
Key Takeaway

Auditors must identify common SDLC mistakes and critically evaluate the chosen methodology and its controls to ensure effective risk mitigation and system success.
