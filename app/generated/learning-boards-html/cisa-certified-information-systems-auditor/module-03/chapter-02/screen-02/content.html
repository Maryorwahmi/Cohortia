Describe SDLC Phases

Articulate the distinct phases of the traditional System Development Life Cycle and their interdependencies. This skill is vital for understanding the structured approach to system development and identifying key control points.

Differentiate SDLC Methodologies

Distinguish between various SDLC methodologies, including Waterfall, Agile, Prototyping, and RAD. This enables you to understand different project approaches and their implications for control implementation.
