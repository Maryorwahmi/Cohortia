Core Concept

The SDLC Framework

A structured approach to system development and its CISA relevance

What is the SDLC?

The System Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a structured framework for managing the development, acquisition, and maintenance of information systems. It ensures a systematic approach from conception to retirement.

  • •Provides a roadmap for IT projects.
  • •Ensures systems meet business requirements.
  • •Facilitates the integration of security and quality controls.

Why it Matters for a CISA

For a CISA, understanding the SDLC is paramount because it dictates how controls are designed, implemented, and managed throughout a system's existence. It's the foundation for auditing system integrity.

  • •Identifies control points in each phase.
  • •Assesses risk mitigation strategies.
  • •Ensures compliance with policies and regulations.
Key Takeaway

The SDLC is the essential framework for building secure and effective systems, and a CISA's role is to ensure its proper implementation and control integration to mitigate organizational risks.
