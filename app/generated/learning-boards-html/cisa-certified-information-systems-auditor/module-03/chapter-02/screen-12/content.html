Agile Frameworks

Scrum and Kanban

Popular iterative and flow-based approaches

Scrum: Iterative Sprints

  • •**Sprints:** Short, time-boxed iterations (2-4 weeks).
  • •**Product Owner:** Represents business needs, manages backlog.
  • •**Sprint Reviews:** Regular demos to stakeholders for feedback.

Kanban: Continuous Flow

  • •**Visualize Workflow:** Use of visual boards to track tasks.
  • •**Limit Work in Progress (WIP):** Focus on completing tasks before starting new ones.
  • •**Continuous Delivery:** Smooth, ongoing release of value.
Key Takeaway

Scrum and Kanban offer distinct Agile approaches; auditors must adapt their focus to assess controls within sprints or continuous workflow, respectively.
