Other Methodologies

Prototyping, RAD, and Spiral

Alternative approaches for specific project needs

Prototyping

  • •**Working Model:** Create early versions for feedback.
  • •**Risk Reduction:** Refine requirements early.
  • •**Audit Focus:** Formal capture of feedback, change control.

RAD (Rapid Application Development)

  • •**Rapid Iteration:** Emphasizes speed and quick delivery.
  • •**Specialized Tools:** Often uses tools to accelerate development.
  • •**Audit Focus:** Effectiveness of rapid cycles, quality of accelerated development.

Spiral Model

  • •**Combines Elements:** Waterfall + Prototyping.
  • •**Risk Management:** Explicit focus at each iteration.
  • •**Audit Focus:** Risk assessment and mitigation strategies.
Key Takeaway

Different methodologies suit different projects; auditors must understand each approach's unique controls and risks to provide effective oversight.
