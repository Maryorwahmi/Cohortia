Waterfall Phase 1

Requirements Gathering/Analysis

Defining what the system needs to do

Key Activities

  • •**User Needs:** Understanding what users require.
  • •**Functional Specifications:** What the system *does*.
  • •**Non-Functional Requirements:** Performance, security, usability.

CISA Audit Focus & Controls

  • •**Completeness & Clarity:** Are all requirements defined and unambiguous?
  • •**Stakeholder Involvement:** Were all relevant parties consulted?
  • •**Documentation & Approval:** Are requirements formally documented and signed off?
  • •**Traceability:** Can requirements be traced to business objectives?
Key Takeaway

Thorough requirements gathering is critical; auditors ensure completeness and formal approval to prevent costly issues and ensure the system meets its intended purpose.
