Methodology Spotlight

Traditional Waterfall SDLC

A sequential approach for stable requirements

Key Characteristics

  • •**Sequential Flow:** Each phase must be completed before the next begins.
  • •**Rigid Structure:** Low tolerance for changes once a phase is complete.
  • •**Extensive Documentation:** Detailed records at each stage.
  • •**Predictable:** Clear milestones and deliverables.

When to Use Waterfall

  • •**Clear, Stable Requirements:** Projects where needs are well-understood upfront.
  • •**Regulatory Compliance:** Industries requiring strict adherence and audit trails.
  • •**Low Change Tolerance:** Projects where mid-course corrections are costly or impossible.
Key Takeaway

Waterfall is best for projects with fixed requirements and high regulatory oversight, providing a clear, auditable path, but it struggles with evolving needs.
