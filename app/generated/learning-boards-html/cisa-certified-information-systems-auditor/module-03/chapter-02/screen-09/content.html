Waterfall Phase 4

Testing Phase

Rigorous validation to ensure quality and security

Types of Testing

  • •**Unit Testing:** Individual component verification.
  • •**Integration Testing:** Modules working together.
  • •**System Testing:** Entire system against requirements.
  • •**Performance Testing:** System responsiveness and stability.
  • •**Security Testing:** Vulnerability scanning, penetration testing.
  • •**User Acceptance Testing (UAT):** End-user validation of business needs.

CISA Audit Focus & Controls

  • •**Test Plans & Coverage:** Are tests comprehensive and documented?
  • •**Defect Management:** How are defects identified, tracked, and resolved?
  • •**UAT Sign-offs:** Formal approval from business users.
  • •**Security Test Results:** Review of vulnerability and penetration test findings.
Key Takeaway

Auditors ensure rigorous testing, including security and UAT, to validate system quality and security, preventing costly post-deployment issues.
