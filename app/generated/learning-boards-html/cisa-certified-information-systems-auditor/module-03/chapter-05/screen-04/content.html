Core Concept

The Implementation Phase: From Development to Live Use

The implementation phase marks the transition of a new or acquired information system from development and testing into live operational use. This is a high-risk phase, requiring meticulous planning and controls.

  • • High-Risk Transition: Any missteps can lead to significant business disruption, data loss, or security incidents.
  • • CISA's Role: Verify that the transition is controlled, secure, and minimally disruptive.
  • • Comprehensive Planning: Develop a detailed implementation plan, communication strategy, and a crucial rollback strategy.
Key Takeaway

Meticulous planning, including a robust rollback strategy, is essential to mitigate risks and ensure a smooth, secure system go-live.

Process Flow: Implementation Planning
1

Develop Implementation Plan: Outline all tasks, timelines, resources, and responsibilities for deployment.

2

Create Communication Strategy: Inform stakeholders about timelines, impacts, and support channels.

3

Design Rollback Strategy: Plan to revert to a previous stable state in case of deployment failure.

4

Define Contingency Measures: Prepare for unexpected issues and potential disruptions.

Scenario: Rollback Plan

During a new CRM system deployment, a critical integration fails. The pre-approved rollback plan allows the team to quickly revert to the old CRM, minimizing customer impact and data loss while troubleshooting.
