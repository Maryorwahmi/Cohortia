System Lifecycle: From Go-Live to Ongoing Support

Mastering Implementation, Review, and Maintenance for CISA Success

This chapter guides you through the critical stages of an information system's journey: from its initial deployment into live operation, through a comprehensive review of its performance and benefits, and into its long-term maintenance. We'll explore the auditor's role in ensuring these transitions are controlled, secure, and aligned with business objectives, focusing on key strategies and paramount controls.

Key Takeaway

Effective implementation, thorough post-implementation review, and robust maintenance with strong change management are critical for system integrity, security, and business value.
