Change Control

Change Management: Execution, Documentation, and Auditor Oversight

After approval, changes must be thoroughly tested, carefully implemented, and meticulously documented to maintain system integrity.

  • • Testing: Rigorous testing in non-production environments to prevent errors.
  • • Implementation: Deployment of the change, often with a crucial rollback plan.
  • • Documentation: Updating all relevant system documentation and audit trails.
Key Takeaway

Auditor oversight of testing, implementation, and documentation ensures adherence to change management, critical for system stability and security.

Change Management Process (Execution & Oversight)
4

Testing: Thorough testing of the change in a non-production environment.

5

Implementation: Deployment of the change to production, with a rollback plan.

6

Documentation: Updating system documentation, user manuals, and audit trails.

7

Auditor Review: Reviewing logs, observing CAB, and sampling changes for adherence.

Critical Safety Note

Weak change management, especially for 'emergency' changes, is a major source of security vulnerabilities and system instability, leading to potential breaches or operational failures.
