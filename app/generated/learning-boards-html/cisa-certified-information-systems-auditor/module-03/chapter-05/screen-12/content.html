System Lifecycle

Maintenance and Operations: Sustaining System Health

The maintenance phase involves continuous support, enhancement, and adaptation to ensure systems remain functional, secure, and relevant.

Corrective Maintenance

Fixing bugs and defects identified during live operation.

Adaptive Maintenance

Modifying the system to adapt to changes in its environment (e.g., new OS, regulations).

Perfective Maintenance

Enhancing functionality or performance based on user feedback or new business requirements.

Preventive Maintenance

Proactive measures to prevent future problems (e.g., refactoring code, applying patches).

Key Takeaway

Understanding the four types of maintenance is crucial for ensuring system longevity, security, and continued alignment with business objectives.
