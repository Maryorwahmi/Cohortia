Post-Implementation Review (PIR): Assessing Project Success

A Post-Implementation Review (PIR) is a critical audit activity to independently assess whether a new system has met its original objectives and delivered expected benefits.

  • • Independent Assessment: Evaluates project success, efficiency, and control effectiveness.
  • • Benefit Realization: Confirms if anticipated financial and non-financial benefits were achieved.
  • • Control Effectiveness: Verifies controls operate as designed in the live environment.
Key Takeaway

The PIR is vital for accountability, identifying lessons learned, and ensuring IT investments provide tangible value to the organization.

PIR Purpose & Benefits
  • Assess if original objectives were met.
  • Evaluate efficiency and effectiveness of implementation.
  • Identify lessons learned for future projects.
  • Ensure accountability for IT investments.
Common Mistake

Skipping the PIR leads to missed opportunities for learning, lack of accountability, and potential repetition of project failures in the future.
