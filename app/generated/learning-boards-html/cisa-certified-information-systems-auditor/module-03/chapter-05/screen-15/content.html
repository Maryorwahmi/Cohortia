Operational Controls

Other Key Operational Controls and Common Mistakes

Beyond change management, several other processes are vital for robust IS operations, and auditors must be aware of common pitfalls.

Key Operational Controls

  • • Incident Management: Responding to and resolving system outages or security incidents.
  • • Problem Management: Root cause analysis for recurring incidents.
  • • Configuration Management: Maintaining accurate records of system components and configurations.
  • • Release Management: Planning and controlling the movement of releases to production.

Common Mistakes & Risks

  • • Inadequate Planning: Poor implementation plans, neglecting rollback.
  • • Poor Data Migration: Integrity issues, data loss.
  • • Weak Change Management: Unauthorized/untested changes, bypassing controls.
  • • Skipping PIR: Missed learning, lack of accountability.
Key Takeaway

A CISA's continuous oversight of all operational controls and awareness of common pitfalls are essential for ensuring ongoing system integrity, security, and value.
