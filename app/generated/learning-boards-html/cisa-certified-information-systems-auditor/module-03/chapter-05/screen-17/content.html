Key Concepts: Maintenance & Controls

Essential Terms for CISA Success

System Maintenance

Ongoing activities to support, enhance, and fix a system (corrective, adaptive, perfective, preventive).

Change Management

A formal process for controlling all modifications to an IT system, ensuring they are authorized, tested, and documented.

Change Advisory Board (CAB)

A group responsible for reviewing and approving changes to IT systems.

Summary Takeaway

Robust system maintenance and stringent change management processes are fundamental for ensuring the long-term integrity, security, and operational value of IT assets.
