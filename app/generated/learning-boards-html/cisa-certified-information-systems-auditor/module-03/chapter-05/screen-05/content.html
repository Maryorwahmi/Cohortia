Data Migration: Ensuring Integrity and Completeness

Data migration involves the extraction, transformation, and loading (ETL) of data from old systems to the new one. This process is critical for the success of any new system.

  • • ETL Process: Extract data from source, transform it for the new system, and load it into the target.
  • • High Risk: Errors can lead to corrupted data, incomplete records, or data loss.
  • • Auditor's Role: Verify integrity and completeness of migrated data.
Key Takeaway

Auditors must actively verify data migration integrity through reconciliation and sampling to prevent data quality issues in the new system.

Process: Data Migration Assurance
1

Review Migration Plan: Assess strategy, tools, and validation procedures.

2

Verify Data Extraction: Ensure all required data is extracted from source systems.

3

Assess Data Transformation: Confirm data is correctly formatted and mapped for the new system.

4

Validate Data Loading: Ensure data is accurately loaded into the target system.

5

Reconcile & Sample: Compare source and target data, and sample records for integrity and completeness.

Misconception Check

Simply moving data is not enough. The critical step is ensuring data quality, consistency, and integrity throughout the ETL process to avoid future operational issues.
