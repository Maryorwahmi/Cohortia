Key Concepts: Implementation & Review

Essential Terms for CISA Success

System Implementation

The process of deploying a new or acquired system into a live operational environment.

Cutover Strategy

The approach used to transition from an old system to a new one (e.g., Big Bang, Phased, Parallel, Pilot).

Post-Implementation Review (PIR)

An independent assessment after go-live to evaluate project success, benefit realization, and control effectiveness.

Summary Takeaway

Effective implementation and thorough post-implementation review are crucial for validating IT investments and ensuring systems meet their intended objectives.
