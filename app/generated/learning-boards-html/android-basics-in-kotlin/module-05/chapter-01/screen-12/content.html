Scenario answers
  1. Dark mode preference: Shared Preferences. Reason: single boolean, tiny and quick to read/write. Risk: avoid storing complex state here.
  2. User-created recipes: Room database. Reason: structured records with queries and sorting. Risk: migration work for schema changes.
  3. Large recipe cover photo: Internal storage. Reason: private offline access and removed on uninstall. Risk: counts against device storage.
  4. Temporary debug log: Internal cache/file. Reason: temporary and private; system may clear when needed. Risk: don't rely on cache for long-term data.
  5. Shopping list: Room for robust operations, or a small local file/prefs for extremely minimal lists. Reason: frequent updates and queries benefit from a database. Risk: overkill if the list is always tiny.
