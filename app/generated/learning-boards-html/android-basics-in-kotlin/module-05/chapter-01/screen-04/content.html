Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Why data persistence matters
Definition

Persistence means data survives beyond a single run of your app. It is how preferences, user files, and records remain available after restarts.

Concrete example
A to-do list that keeps tasks between sessions so users rely on the app to track work.
Common misconception
Only big apps need persistence. In fact, even a small preference or one high score is meaningful to users.
Why it matters
Good persistence improves UX, prevents data loss, and enables offline features.
Quick checklist
  • Who needs the data?
  • How large is it?
  • Will you query or sort it?
  • Is it sensitive or shared?
Example scenario
Game high score: simple integer persisted as a preference is efficient and appropriate; no database needed.
Key Takeaway

Design persistence around data shape and user expectations to avoid future refactors.
