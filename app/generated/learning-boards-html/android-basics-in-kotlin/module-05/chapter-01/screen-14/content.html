Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Data Persistence
Keeping data available after app closes or the device restarts.
Shared Preferences
Key-value store for small primitives like booleans and strings.
Internal Storage
Private files removed on uninstall; no runtime permission needed.
Quick summary
Keep these three in mind as the foundation: persistence, small key-value settings, and private files. They guide many routine decisions.
