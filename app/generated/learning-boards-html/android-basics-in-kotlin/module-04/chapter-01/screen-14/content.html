Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Activity
A single focused screen or task the user performs in your app.
Activity Lifecycle
Callbacks guide initialization, visibility, and cleanup of an activity.
Back Stack
A LIFO stack that controls the order of navigation and what Back does.
Summary takeaway
Design screens as activities, map work to lifecycle callbacks, and always verify activity declarations in the manifest to keep apps predictable and crash-free.
