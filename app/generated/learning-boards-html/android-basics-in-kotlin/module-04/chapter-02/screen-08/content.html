Process Flow › What happens when startActivity runs
Back stack behavior

startActivity places the new activity on top of the back stack. The previous activity remains under it and will be resumed when the top is popped.

Lifecycle sequence example when starting SecondActivity:
  • MainActivity: onPause(), onStop()
  • SecondActivity: onCreate(), onStart(), onResume()
  • Press back: SecondActivity is destroyed, MainActivity: onRestart(), onStart()
Design note: Use finish when you intentionally want to remove the previous activity and prevent returning.
Common misconception

Students sometimes think startActivity replaces the caller. It does not destroy the caller unless finish is called.

Key Takeaway

Understand lifecycle transitions and the back stack before deciding to call finish.
