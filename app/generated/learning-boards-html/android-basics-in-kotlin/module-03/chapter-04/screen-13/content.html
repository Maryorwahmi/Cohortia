Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Guidelines, Barriers, Chains
Guidelines
Invisible anchors at fixed dp or percentage positions. Constrain views to a guideline to keep consistent alignment across varying screen sizes.
Barriers
A barrier adapts to its largest child, creating a dynamic anchor that other views can use to stay aligned when contents resize.
Chains
Group sibling views into a chain to distribute space with styles like spread, spread_inside, and packed. Chains control distribution without extra wrappers.
When to use

Start with parent and sibling constraints; add Guidelines, Barriers, or Chains when you need percentage alignment, adaptive anchors, or controlled distribution among siblings.
