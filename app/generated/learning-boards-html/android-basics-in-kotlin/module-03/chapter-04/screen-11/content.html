Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Avoid common mistakes
Under-constraining
Missing a horizontal or vertical constraint makes a view under-constrained. The editor warns and the view may appear at (0,0) at runtime. Fix by adding the missing anchor.
Conflicting constraints
Impossible combinations, like fixed width plus tight opposing anchors, will be flagged. Use 0dp or remove fixed dimensions to resolve.
Over-constraining
Redundant constraints add maintenance burden. Keep only what is necessary to express position and size.
Quick checklist
  • Does each view have horizontal and vertical anchors?
  • Are there editor warnings or conflict messages?
  • Would MATCH_CONSTRAINT (0dp) solve the sizing problem?
