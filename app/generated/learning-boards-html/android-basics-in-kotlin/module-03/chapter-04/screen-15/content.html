Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts — Part 2
0dp (MATCH_CONSTRAINT)
Makes a view expand to fill the space defined by its constraints, not zero pixels.
Baseline Constraint
Aligns text baselines of TextViews for consistent rows.
Under-constraining
A missing horizontal or vertical constraint that causes unpredictable layout behavior; the editor flags this.
Final takeaway
ConstraintLayout gives you relationship-based control: define anchors, use 0dp for flexible sizing, and rely on the editor to catch under-constrained or conflicting setups.
