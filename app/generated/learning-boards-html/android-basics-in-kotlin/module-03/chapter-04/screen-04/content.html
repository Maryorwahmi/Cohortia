Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Why prefer ConstraintLayout
Comparison at a glance
LinearLayout/RelativeLayout: often needs nesting for complex layouts, increasing measurement passes.
ConstraintLayout: flat relationships let views align to parent or siblings without extra containers.
Performance: fewer nested groups often means fewer layout passes and better responsiveness.
Misconception
Some think ConstraintLayout is harder. It has more attributes, but using constraints removes nesting and simplifies maintenance for complex UIs.
Key Takeaway

Choose ConstraintLayout when you need flexible alignment and want to avoid nested ViewGroups that hurt performance.

Concrete scenario
You have a title, input, and button centered on many screen sizes. With LinearLayouts you might wrap groups to center; with ConstraintLayout set start/end and top constraints and margins so the same views adapt without extra wrappers.
Check your understanding
  • Can each view be given one horizontal and one vertical constraint?
  • Would adding another ViewGroup solve alignment or increase complexity?
