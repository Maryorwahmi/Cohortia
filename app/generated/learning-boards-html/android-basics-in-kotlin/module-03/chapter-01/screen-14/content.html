Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
View
Draws content and handles input events, like TextView and Button.
LinearLayout
Stacks children and uses layout_weight combined with 0dp to distribute space proportionally.
RelativeLayout
Positions children relative to parent or siblings using id-based rules for flexible arrangements.
Quick takeaway

Choose the simplest container that expresses your layout, use dp and sp, and avoid unnecessary nesting to keep performance healthy.
