Module 03 › Chapter 3.3 › Benefits vs Drawbacks

Benefits

  • Simplifies client interactions with a single endpoint.
  • Centralizes authentication, authorization, logging, and monitoring.
  • Improves resilience via caching, rate limiting, and circuit breakers.
Example benefit

Clients receive aggregated responses instead of multiple independent calls.

Drawbacks & Mitigations

  • Single point of failure: mitigate with many gateway instances and load balancing.
  • Operational complexity: use clear route policies and modular filters to limit drift.
  • Potential latency: monitor and optimize aggregation and filter costs.
Design rule

Keep business logic in services; use the Gateway for cross-cutting concerns only.
