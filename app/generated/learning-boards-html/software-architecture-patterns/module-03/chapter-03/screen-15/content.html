Module 03 › Chapter 3.3 › What to Remember
Centralize Concerns

Auth, logging, and rate limiting are good Gateway responsibilities.

No Business Logic

Keep domain rules in services to avoid a mini-monolith Gateway.

Design for Ops

Ensure high availability, monitoring, and horizontal scalability for the Gateway.

Final Thought

When adding a feature to the Gateway, ask whether it simplifies clients or merely moves complexity into a single operational surface.
