Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Broker responsibilities
Definition

What an event broker does

  • Accept events from publishers and persist them durably so they survive restarts.
  • Deliver events to interested subscribers, supporting retries and acknowledgements.
  • Support replay or seek so recovering subscribers can catch up to the latest state.
  • Provide features like fan-out to multiple consumers while keeping brokers free of business logic.
Operational note: choice of broker affects semantics. Document the delivery and ordering guarantees you rely upon.
Common brokers (examples)
The chapter mentions Apache Kafka, RabbitMQ, and AWS SQS/SNS as brokers that provide durable delivery and differing feature sets suitable for EDA.
Common mistake
Embedding business routing or transformations into the broker turns it into a hard-to-maintain central authority; avoid this.
Check

List the broker guarantees your system requires and ensure your service contracts and tests reflect them.
