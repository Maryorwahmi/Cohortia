Monitoring and debugging Sagas
Make the distributed flow reconstructable and alert on stuck state

Attach a correlation ID to every message and log that ID with structured logs. This lets you join records across services.

Use distributed tracing to visualize the end-to-end timeline. For choreography, aggregate events into an event store or a Saga dashboard.

Alert on unusually long Saga durations, repeated compensation failures, and high retry rates so operators can act before data drifts become permanent.

Playbook items
  1. Trace the correlation ID to reconstruct the Saga timeline.
  2. Check orchestrator state (if present) for current Saga step and retries.
  3. Rerun compensations or escalate if automated retries fail repeatedly.
Why it matters

Without correlation and tracing, diagnosing a failed Saga becomes manual and slow, increasing mean time to repair and customer impact.
