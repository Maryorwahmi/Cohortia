Saga Pattern for Distributed Transactions
Manage multi-step business processes across services by sequencing local transactions and compensations.
Problem
ACID cannot span independent databases without tight coupling and 2PC issues.
Pattern
A Saga sequences local ACID transactions and defines compensations for failures.
Result
Eventual consistency with decoupled services and explicit failure handling.
In this lesson
You will learn why distributed ACID is impractical, what a Saga is, how compensation works, and the trade-offs between choreography and orchestration.
