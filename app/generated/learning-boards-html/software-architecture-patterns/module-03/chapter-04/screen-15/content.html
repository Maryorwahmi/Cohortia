Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Remember
Saga = Sequence + Compensation
Local ACID per service, compensations for failures, eventual consistency.
Choreography vs Orchestration
Event-driven decentralization vs central orchestrator control; weigh complexity and observability.
Implementation needs
Idempotency, durable messaging, outbox pattern, correlation IDs, and tracing are essential.
One-sentence summary
Use Sagas to manage distributed business transactions by sequencing safe local operations and designing compensations, while investing in idempotency and observability.
