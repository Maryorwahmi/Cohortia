Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Saga Pattern
Sequence of local transactions with compensations to achieve eventual consistency.
Choreography vs Orchestration
Choreography uses events; orchestration uses a central controller. Each has trade-offs.
Compensation & Idempotency
Design compensations as business operations and make all handlers idempotent for safe retries.
Summary takeaway
Sagas enable resilient, decoupled multi-step business processes by sequencing local transactions and providing carefully designed compensations, but they demand idempotency, durable messaging, and strong observability.
