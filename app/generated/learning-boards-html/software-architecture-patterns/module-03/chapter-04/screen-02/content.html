Learning Objectives
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain limits of ACID across services
Describe why two-phase commit and global transactions create coupling and latency across microservices, and why they are often impractical.
Verified competency milestone
02
Define the Saga pattern
Articulate Sagas as sequences of local transactions with compensations and explain the idea of eventual consistency.
Verified competency milestone
03
Differentiate choreography and orchestration
Explain how event-driven choreography distributes control versus an orchestrator that commands participants, including pros and cons of each.
Verified competency milestone
04
Design Sagas and identify challenges
Create sequences and compensation steps, and recognize complexities like idempotency, retries, and observability needed to debug Sagas.
Verified competency milestone
