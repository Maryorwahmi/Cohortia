Choreography-based Saga
Event-driven, decentralized coordination

Each service publishes domain events and other services subscribe and react. No central orchestrator exists.

Example flow: OrderCreatedEvent → PaymentService processes payment → PaymentProcessedEvent → InventoryService decrements stock → InventoryDecrementedEvent → ShippingService schedules shipment.

Pros: less coupling, no orchestrator to maintain. Cons: harder to debug, especially for long-running or branching workflows.

Design considerations
  • Design clear events and include correlation IDs so the entire Saga can be traced.
  • Ensure every handler is idempotent; messages may be delivered more than once.
  • Use centralized logging or distributed tracing to reconstruct the flow for debugging.
Pro-tip

For small, linear Sagas prefer choreography. For complex workflows, pair choreography with strong observability or consider orchestration.
