Sagas for long-running transactions
Sequence local transactions and compensations instead of distributed atomic commits.
Choreography
Services publish events and other services react. No central coordinator. Good for simple sagas where steps map naturally to events.
Orchestration
A dedicated orchestrator tells services which local transaction to run next. Easier to reason about complex flows but introduces a coordinator component.
Design checklist
  • Define local transactions and compensating actions
  • Ensure idempotency and retry behavior
  • Prefer eventual consistency where acceptable
