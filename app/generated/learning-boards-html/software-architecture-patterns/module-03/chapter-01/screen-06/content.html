From local commit to system-wide consistency
Choose between immediate sync calls and asynchronous eventual consistency patterns.
Step 1: Local transaction
Service performs a local commit inside its own database and enforces local business rules.
Step 2: Publish domain event
Publish an event such as OrderCreatedEvent describing the change, for other services to consume asynchronously.
Step 3: Subscribers react
Interested services update their local state, trigger workflows, or send notifications, eventually converging the system.
When to use sync APIs
When correctness must be immediate and you can accept tighter coupling and latency sensitivity.
When to use events
When autonomy, scalability, and decoupling are more important and temporary inconsistency is acceptable.
