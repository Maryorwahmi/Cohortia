Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Concepts
Resilience
The system's ability to recover and continue functioning, possibly in degraded mode.
Circuit Breaker
Stops calling a failing service after repeated errors and probes for recovery with test requests.
Bulkhead
Isolates resources into compartments so one slow dependency doesn't exhaust global capacity.
Final takeaway

Combine containment patterns with monitoring and fallbacks to reduce incident impact and buy time for root cause fixes.
