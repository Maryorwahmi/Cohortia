Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › What to Remember
Expect Failures
Design timeouts and bounded retries as a first line of defense.
Stop Repeats
Use circuit breakers and meaningful fallbacks to avoid wasting resources.
Partition Resources
Use bulkheads to contain resource usage per integration and avoid global exhaustion.
Final action

Pick one dependency, add a circuit breaker or bulkhead, instrument it, and iterate based on the metrics you observe.
