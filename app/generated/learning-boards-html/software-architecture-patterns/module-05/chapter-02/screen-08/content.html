Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Service Registry
Registry contents
Name, instance ID, IP, port, metadata tags, and health status are typical registry fields.
Registration
Service announces its network location at startup.
Heartbeats
Periodic signals to indicate the instance is alive.
Deregistration
Explicit removal during graceful shutdown or after heartbeat timeouts.
Operational note
Because many services depend on the registry, ensure it is highly available and monitored. Consider replication, backups, and health endpoints so discovery queries remain reliable.
Key Takeaway
Keep registry metadata current via heartbeats and ensure the registry itself is resilient to avoid cascading failures.
