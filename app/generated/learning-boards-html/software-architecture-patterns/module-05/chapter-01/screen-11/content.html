Trade-off Analysis When to adopt microservices
A pragmatic checklist for architecture decisions
Benefits to confirm
  • Independent scaling needs across components.
  • Multiple teams requiring independent release cycles.
  • Clear bounded contexts that minimize semantic coupling.
Costs to plan for
  • Monitoring, tracing, and logging across services.
  • Service discovery, deployment automation, and operational expertise.
  • Complexity in debugging and ensuring consistency across services.
Practical rule
Start with a modular monolith and extract services incrementally when you see stable boundaries and have operational capacity.
Key Takeaway

Make adoption decisions based on team scale, domain boundaries, and readiness to manage operational overhead. Incremental extraction reduces risk.
