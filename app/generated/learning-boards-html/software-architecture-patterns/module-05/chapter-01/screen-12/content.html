Deployment Practice Monitoring, Deployment, and Observability
Essential operational systems for microservices
Observability stack
  • Structured logs with correlation IDs for requests.
  • Metrics for latency, error rates, and saturation.
  • Distributed tracing to follow requests across services.
Practical note
Plan your correlation IDs early so logs, metrics, and traces can be joined reliably during incidents.
Deployment and resilience
  • Independent pipelines for each service to allow isolated deploys and rollbacks.
  • Health checks, circuit breakers, and service discovery to route around failures.
  • Runbooks and alerts focused on user-impacting degradations.
Common mistake
Delaying observability until after services are in production makes incident response slow and costly.
Key Takeaway

Invest in tracing, logging, metrics, and deployment automation before you have many services. These tools make distributed systems observable and maintainable.
