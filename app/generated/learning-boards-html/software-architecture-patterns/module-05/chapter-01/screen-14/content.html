What to Remember Three practical reminders
A compact checklist for architects and engineers
1. Start modular
Prefer a modular monolith and extract services when boundaries stabilize.
2. Own your data
Service data ownership prevents hidden coupling and keeps services autonomous.
3. Observe and automate
Invest in tracing, metrics, and deployment pipelines early to manage distributed complexity.
Final takeaway

These three checks help you make pragmatic architecture decisions that balance development speed with operational cost.
