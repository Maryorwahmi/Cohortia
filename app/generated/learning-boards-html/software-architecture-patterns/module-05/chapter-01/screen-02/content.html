Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define distributed systems

Explain why multiple machines can present as a single coherent system and list core advantages like scalability and resilience. This helps you recognize when distribution is necessary.

Verified Competency Milestone
02
Explain microservices vs monoliths

Describe how microservices split responsibility into independently deployable services and contrast that with a single monolithic deployable. This distinction directs design decisions about modularity and deployment.

Verified Competency Milestone
03
Identify microservice principles

Recognize autonomy, data ownership, loose coupling, bounded contexts, and polyglot choices as defining characteristics of microservices and why each matters to teams and operations.

Verified Competency Milestone
04
Understand trade-offs

Articulate the costs of operational complexity, monitoring needs, and debugging challenges against benefits like independent scaling and team autonomy. This balance guides architecture choices.
