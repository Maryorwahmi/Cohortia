Distributed Systems & Microservices

Understand why systems move from single deployables to clusters of services, the benefits this brings, and the operational trade-offs that influence architecture decisions.

In this lesson
Definitions, benefits, trade-offs, and practical decomposition steps.
Why it matters
When software grows, architecture drives your ability to change, scale, and isolate failures. This chapter gives practical judgement about when to adopt microservices.
Monolith
Single deployable that can be simple to start with but harder to scale and change as it grows.
Microservices
Independent services aligned to business capabilities for autonomy and independent scaling.
Operational cost
More infrastructure and monitoring are needed; plan for that early if you adopt microservices.
Quick takeaway

Microservices trade operational complexity for autonomy and independent scaling. Evaluate needs before adopting.
