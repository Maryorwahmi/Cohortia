Comparison Monolith vs Microservices
Understand practical trade-offs to guide choice
Monolithic architecture
Single deployable, shared codebase and often a single database. Easier to start and debug locally; lower operational surface area for small teams.
When to choose
Small team, limited complexity, rapid initial development.
Risk
Becomes hard to change and scale if it grows without modular design.
Microservices architecture
Independent services aligned to business capabilities, each owning its own data and deploy pipeline. Supports team autonomy and independent scaling.
When to choose
Large domain complexity, multiple teams, need for independent release cycles or heterogeneous technologies.
Operational cost
Requires monitoring, discovery, deployment automation, and tracing across services.
Key Takeaway

Choose the architecture that fits team size and domain complexity. Avoid premature decomposition and watch for distributed monoliths.
