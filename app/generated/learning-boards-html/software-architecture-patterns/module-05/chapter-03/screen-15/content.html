Idempotency
Use requestId keys and persistent lookup to avoid duplicate side effects.
Dead-Letter Queues
Route repeated failures to DLQs and alert for manual handling.
Monitoring
Alert on queue backlog, consumer lag, and DLQ growth to detect issues early.
Final Takeaway

Design with purpose: choose messaging patterns selectively, build safety nets, and instrument your system so asynchronous communication increases reliability rather than hiding failures.
