Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Sync vs Async
Comparison
Synchronous (Blocking)
  • Caller waits for immediate response; tight coupling.
  • Failures propagate quickly; risk of cascading outages.
  • Simple to reason about for immediate results.
Asynchronous (Messaging)
  • Caller sends message to broker and continues; loose coupling.
  • Improves resilience and allows independent scaling.
  • Requires design for eventual consistency and visibility.
Concrete Example: OrderService posts PaymentRequest to a payment_queue and returns acceptance to the user; PaymentService processes the queue asynchronously.
Key Explanations
Misconception
Asynchronous is not synonymous with faster processing. It trades immediate response for decoupling and resilience; processing can still be delayed.
When to choose
Use synchronous calls for immediate user-facing confirmation. Use async when you want fault isolation and background processing.
Key Takeaway: Choose patterns by failure domain and user expectations; design compensations for eventual completion.
