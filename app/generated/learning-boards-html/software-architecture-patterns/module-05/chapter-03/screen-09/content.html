Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Durability & DLQ
Durability
Configure the broker to persist messages to disk and replicate if supported so messages survive broker restarts. Durable messages protect critical flows like payments.
Dead-Letter Queue (DLQ)
Route messages that exceed retry limits or trigger permanent errors to a DLQ for manual review and remediation.
Monitoring
  • Alert on queue backlog growth and consumer lag.
  • Track DLQ rates and create tickets for recurring failures.
  • Monitor latency from publish to consume for SLAs.
Common Mistake: Not surfacing DLQ entries to on-call teams. Unseen DLQ growth causes silent data loss in downstream processes.
Key Takeaway: Durability plus DLQs plus monitoring form the safety net that keeps message-driven systems operable under failure.
