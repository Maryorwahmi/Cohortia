Objective Checklist
  • Event schema: orderId, userId, itemsPurchased, totalAmount, timestamp — each field supports downstream processing and auditing.
  • Subscribers: InventoryService, NotificationService, AnalyticsService — independent reactions reduce coupling.
  • Broker choice: match requirements for durability, retention and fan-out; justify why a chosen broker fits.
  • Operational safety: dead-letter queues, retries, idempotency strategies, and backlog monitoring.
Milestone: Draft an event with example JSON fields for OrderCreated.

Why this matters
Concrete definitions and safety practices let teams operate event-driven systems with less disruption. They make incident response and debugging more predictable.
