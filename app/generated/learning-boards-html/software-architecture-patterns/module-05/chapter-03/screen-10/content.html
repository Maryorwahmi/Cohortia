Retry Strategies
Use exponential backoff and cap retries to avoid thrashing. After max attempts, move to DLQ and alert operators for manual inspection.
Misconception: Retries alone solve failures. Without idempotency, retries can cause duplicate side effects and incorrect state.
Key Takeaway: Combine idempotent consumer logic with sensible retries and DLQ routing to maintain correctness under redelivery.
