Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Consistency Models
Strong consistency
Reads always return the latest write. Best for financial operations and critical state changes.
  • Guarantees immediate correctness after a write.
  • Often requires coordination that can reduce availability.
  • Use when business correctness cannot tolerate divergence.
Analogy
Bank balance updates should be strongly consistent to avoid overdraws.
Eventual consistency
Replicas converge over time. Good for high-read scenarios and when temporary divergence is tolerable.
  • Enables higher availability and performance at scale.
  • Requires reconciliation and careful error handling.
  • Design compensations or validation for operations that must be correct.
Analogy
Social media counts may lag briefly but correct themselves quickly.
Example
Validation at purchase time: even with eventual consistency in caches, validate stock with InventoryService for the final commit.
Misconception
Eventual consistency is not permanent inconsistency; it requires designed convergence and compensations.
Key Takeaway

Match consistency to the business requirement: use strong when correctness is essential, eventual when latency and availability matter more.
