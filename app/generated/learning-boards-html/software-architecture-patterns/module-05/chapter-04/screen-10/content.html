Compensating transactions
Compensations undo or correct the business effects of prior steps when a saga fails partway through.
  • Identify the business invariant to restore after failure, e.g., inventory consistency, payment reversal.
  • Compensations are triggered by failure events and executed as local transactions by the owning service.
  • They may not restore pre-transaction state perfectly but must return the system to a safe, consistent state.
Design note
Document compensating flows alongside forward flows so teams understand rollback implications.
Idempotency pattern
Use correlation IDs and processed-record tables so repeated deliveries are detected and ignored or handled safely.
Example
Reserve stock: create reservation record keyed by correlationId. If reservation exists, return success rather than duplicating reservation.
Key Takeaway

Design compensations and idempotency together to ensure sagas handle failures and retries without corrupting state.
