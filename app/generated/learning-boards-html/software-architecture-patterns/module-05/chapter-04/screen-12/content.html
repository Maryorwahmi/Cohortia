Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Database per Service
Each microservice owns its datastore, enforcing autonomy and enabling polyglot persistence.
Consistency Models
Strong consistency gives immediate correctness; eventual consistency allows scalability with temporary divergence.
Saga Pattern
Sequence local transactions, publish events, and run compensations on failures to preserve business invariants.
Summary

Design for autonomy, choose consistency according to correctness needs, and coordinate multi-service processes with well-designed sagas that include idempotency and compensations.
