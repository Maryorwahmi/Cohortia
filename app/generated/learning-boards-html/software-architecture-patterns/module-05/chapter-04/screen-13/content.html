Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › What to Remember
Compensating transaction
An operation that counteracts a previous successful step to maintain business invariants.
Idempotency
Design handlers so repeated events do not cause duplicate effects.
Correlation id
A unique identifier included in events to trace a saga across services.
Final Takeaway

Design for autonomy, instrument for observability, pick the right consistency guarantees, and use sagas with compensations and idempotency to manage distributed business processes.
