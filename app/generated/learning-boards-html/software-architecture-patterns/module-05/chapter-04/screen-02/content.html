01
Identify distributed data challenges
You will list the problems that arise when data is shared across services, such as coupling, schema constraints, and deployment friction. This matters because identifying the right boundary prevents future outages and rigid teams.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain database-per-service
You will describe what it means for a service to own its database and why this enables polyglot persistence and independent schema evolution. Example: Product uses a document store while Payment uses a relational store.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Draw consistency trade-offs
You will contrast strong and eventual consistency and decide which to use based on correctness, latency, and user expectations. Misconception: eventual consistency is 'broken' — it's a deliberate, often acceptable choice.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Describe the Saga pattern
You will explain how sagas sequence local transactions and use compensations to maintain business-level atomicity without distributed ACID. Example flow: OrderCreated → StockReserved → PaymentProcessed → OrderConfirmed.
