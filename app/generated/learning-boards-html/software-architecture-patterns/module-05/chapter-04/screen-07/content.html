Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Distributed Transactions & 2PC
Process
Two-phase commit overview
2PC coordinates multiple participants with a prepare and commit phase. It's blocking and couples availability to all participants' health.
  1. Coordinator asks participants to prepare. Participants lock resources and respond ready/not-ready.
  2. If all ready, coordinator instructs commit; otherwise instructs rollback. Participants then commit or rollback.
  3. Locks and coordination can block progress; failures require complex recovery protocols.
Why it's problematic
It reduces availability because every participant must be responsive and accept locks for the duration of the protocol.
Example scenario
An Order coordinator waits on Inventory and Payment. If Payment is slow or partitioned, the whole transaction blocks.
Alternative
Use sagas: sequence local commits and define compensating actions when steps fail.
Consideration
If strict global atomicity is absolutely required, weigh the operational costs carefully before using 2PC.
Key Takeaway

Avoid distributed ACID unless necessary; prefer sagas for multi-service business processes to improve availability and reduce coupling.
