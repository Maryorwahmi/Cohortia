Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Storytelling & Language
Structure
  1. State the problem and desired outcome.
  2. Explain the proposed design at a level the audience needs.
  3. List benefits, risks, and the specific decision you're asking for.
Analogy Example
Microservices are like city districts: each has a function, local governance, and shared roads. This highlights boundaries and coordination needs without code detail.
Common Pitfalls
  • Leading with technology details instead of business impact.
  • Using unexplained jargon that excludes stakeholders.
  • Assuming diagrams speak for themselves.
Key Takeaway

Clear narrative plus a single well-chosen analogy makes complex designs accessible and actionable.
