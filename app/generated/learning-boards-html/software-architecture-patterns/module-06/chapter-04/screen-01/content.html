Communicating Architecture to Stakeholders
Turn diagrams into decisions by tailoring views, language, and the narrative to each audience.
In this lesson

You will learn how to choose the right diagrams (C4, UML), shape messages for developers, operations, and executives, and avoid common communication failures.

Why it matters

A design that cannot be understood will not be implemented correctly. Clear communication secures alignment, budgets, and faster delivery.

Key Takeaway

Choose the smallest diagram and the clearest language that answers the stakeholder's decision question.
