Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Choosing Diagrams
Decision → Diagram
  • Who interacts with the system? → Context diagram (C4 Level 1).
  • What are the main deployable pieces? → Container/Deployment diagram.
  • How do parts collaborate for a flow? → Component or Sequence diagram.
Rule
Start with the smallest view that answers the decision and expand only for those who ask.
Practical Example
For a new Recommendation Service, show a Context view to product stakeholders, a Container view to technical leads, and a Component/Sequence view to implementing developers.
Misinterpretation Risk
A diagram without the decision it supports invites confusion about its purpose.
Tip
Label diagrams with audience and the decision question they answer.
