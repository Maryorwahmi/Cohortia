Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › The Architect's Communication Role
Claim
Design is not complete until stakeholders can make decisions informed by it.
Context Statement
Concise sentence describing the problem and the decision required.
Benefits & Risks
List the expected improvements and the operational or cost trade-offs.
Ask for Decision
End with a specific question to guide stakeholder action.
Key Takeaway

Always pair a context sentence with the minimal diagram and a short list of business impacts to enable decisions.

Example: Adding a Cache
Explain expected latency drop, added complexity, and cost. State the decision: approve cache funding and operational monitoring?
Why this matters
Without the why and the measurable impact, stakeholders cannot prioritize the change against other work.
Common Mistake
Presenting low-level design before explaining the business problem and expected outcome.
Pro Tip
Start with one slide: problem, impact, ask. Use subsequent slides for more detail as requested.
