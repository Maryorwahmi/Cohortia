Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › Standard ADR Structure
Hierarchy of Sections
Title, Status, Date
Clear identifier and current state for quick scanning and filtering.
Context
Define the problem, constraints, and relevant NFRs that make this decision necessary.
Decision
A short, specific statement of the chosen solution. Avoid ambiguity.
Alternatives & Rationale
Show other options and the trade-offs that led to your choice.
Consequences
List benefits, risks, and required follow-up actions or monitoring.
Practical guidance
  • Keep each ADR focused on a single decision so history stays searchable.
  • Reference requirement IDs or ticket numbers in Context to link to specs.
  • When listing alternatives, include short reasons for rejection — not a long essay.
Misconception

An ADR is not a design cookbook. It should not list every implementation step; focus on the decision, trade-offs, and impacts.
