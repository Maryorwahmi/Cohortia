Why these objectives matter
  • ADRs reduce repeated debates by making trade-offs explicit and retraceable.
  • Consistent structure lets new team members find the why quickly.
  • Living ADRs keep documentation aligned with evolving architecture.
Practical tip: pick one past decision you regretted and write a short ADR for it as practice.
