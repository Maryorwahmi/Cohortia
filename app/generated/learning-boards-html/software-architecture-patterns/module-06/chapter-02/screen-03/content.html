Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › Learning Objectives
Applied Skills
  1. Select decisions that affect multiple teams, NFRs, or are costly to reverse.
  2. Draft an ADR that links requirements to the chosen solution with clear trade-offs.
  3. List alternatives and concrete consequences, including follow-up actions.
Assessment Idea

Complete the ADR exercise for the notification service database: choose two candidate databases, pick one, and produce a full ADR. Your submission should make the rationale and consequences explicit and show why the alternatives were rejected.
