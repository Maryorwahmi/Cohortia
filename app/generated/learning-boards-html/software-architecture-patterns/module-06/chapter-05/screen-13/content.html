Repayment Timeline

A simple three-sprint plan

Small, measurable steps
Sprint 1
  • Spike to estimate Item B refactor
  • Rotate any exposed keys (Item A) immediately
  • Add unit tests around payment logic
Sprint 2
  • Implement modular payment adapters
  • Refactor small components and add integration tests
  • Begin encapsulating old UI components
Sprint 3
  • Complete adapter extraction and tests
  • Perform release with monitoring and rollback plan
  • Review backlog for remaining runway items
Metrics to watch

Lead time for changes, number of production incidents, and time spent on urgent fixes versus planned work.

Prioritization Matrix
High Impact / High Risk

Address immediately — security, data integrity, critical scalability bottlenecks.

High Impact / Low Risk

Schedule early but can be buffered into sprints.

Low Impact / High Risk

Contain and monitor; avoid large rewrites unless payoff is proven.

Low Impact / Low Risk

Tackle opportunistically with Boy Scout Rule improvements.

Quick Rule

If a change takes less than a day and improves design, do it now.
