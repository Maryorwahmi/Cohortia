Skills You'll Practice

Convert theory into team habits
Practice routines
Visibility
Track debt as backlog items with clear impact statements so product owners can prioritize work rationally.
Prioritization
Rank debt by development speed impact and business risk. Focus first on items that block feature delivery or create outages.
Dedicated Time
Allocate sprint capacity or use timeboxes for refactoring, following the Boy Scout Rule to make small continuous improvements.
Safety Nets
Invest in automated tests to give confidence during refactors and reduce the risk of regressions when repaying debt.
