Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Quick Takeaways
Make Debt Visible
Document impact, expected repayment, and owners so debt is managed intentionally.
Prioritize by Impact
Fix what blocks delivery or creates risk first; schedule the rest incrementally.
Small, Continuous Steps
Prefer many small refactors backed by tests over one large risky rewrite.
Final Prompt

Pick one technical debt item you can improve this week. Make it visible, estimate one small change, and schedule it. Small steps compound into a healthy architecture.
