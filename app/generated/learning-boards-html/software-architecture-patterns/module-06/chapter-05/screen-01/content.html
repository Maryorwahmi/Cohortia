Evolving Architecture and Technical Debt

Learn why architecture must change, how shortcuts become debt, and practical ways to keep systems adaptable over time.

Systems live in changing environments. Requirements, scale, and tech stacks move, and a rigid design becomes costly. We will view architecture as a living artifact that needs continuous care.

Technical debt is the implied rework from choosing a faster, limited solution today. It accrues interest in the form of slower development, higher bugs, and brittle systems.

This lesson focuses on identifying debt, deciding whether it is prudent, and applying repeatable patterns to manage repayment over time.
