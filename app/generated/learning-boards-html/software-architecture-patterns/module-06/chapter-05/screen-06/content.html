Prudent versus Reckless Debt

Prudent Debt
  • Intentional shortcut to meet a business goal.
  • Documented trade-offs and risks.
  • Scheduled repayment in the backlog.
  • Often paired with tests or fallbacks to limit risk.
Example: an MVP data sync replaced in the next quarter after planned refactor.
Reckless Debt
  • Accidental or careless shortcuts.
  • Not tracked or visible to stakeholders.
  • No plan or time allocated to repay it.
  • Leads to compounding costs and brittle systems.
Example: scattering hardcoded credentials and never rotating them or moving to secrets management.
Decision Questions
Was this intentional? Is it visible? Is there a repayment plan?
Practical Step
Convert each known shortcut into a backlog debt item with impact and estimate.
Check
If an item lacks a plan, treat it as high risk until proven otherwise.
