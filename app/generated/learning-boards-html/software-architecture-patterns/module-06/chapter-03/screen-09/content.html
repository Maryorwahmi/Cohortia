Definition
ATAM (lightweight)
Scenario-based trade-off analysis adapted for practical use.
Scenario creation
Define realistic scenarios that stress top NFRs and use them to evaluate alternatives.
Identify sensitivity points
Find attributes where small changes have large impacts and treat them as risks.
Beginner's note
You don't need the full ATAM process to gain value; scenario thinking alone brings clarity.
Example scenarios
  • Scalability: traffic spikes tenfold for one hour.
  • Resilience: one availability zone becomes unreachable.
  • Security: a compliance audit requires encryption and audit trails.
Key Takeaway

Scenario-based evaluation surfaces trade-offs and operational implications that a raw score may hide.
