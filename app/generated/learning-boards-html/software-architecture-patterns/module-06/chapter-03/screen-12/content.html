Risk & Trade-off
Common Mistakes
Recognize recurring errors and follow practical mitigations.
1. Ignoring critical criteria
Create an NFR checklist and validate it with stakeholders.
2. Biased scoring
Use peer review or independent scoring to surface bias.
3. Not involving stakeholders
Run short workshops to define criteria and weights together.
Mitigations
  • Require one-line evidence per score.
  • Perform sensitivity analysis on key weights.
  • Document and revisit decisions when new information appears.
Key Takeaway

Mitigations require modest process steps that greatly increase the reliability and defensibility of the final decision.
