Involving Diverse Perspectives
Make scoring more accurate and decisions more durable by involving the right people early.
Who to involve
Developers, operations, security, product, and any team that will operate or be impacted by the choice.
How to involve them
  • Workshops to define criteria and weights.
  • Subject matter experts validate scores with evidence.
  • Asynchronous review for wider feedback.
Pitfall
Inviting stakeholders only after the matrix is complete reduces buy-in and misses early input.
Workshop template
A short agenda you can use:
  1. Present alternatives and context (10 minutes).
  2. Collect and agree on criteria tied to requirements (20 minutes).
  3. Assign weights and capture rationale (15 minutes).
  4. Score alternatives and note evidence (20 minutes).
  5. Discuss results and next steps (15 minutes).
Key Takeaway

Early, structured stakeholder involvement improves score quality, reveals risks, and increases buy-in for the chosen architecture.
