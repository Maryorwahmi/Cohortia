Practical Technique
Validate Assumptions
Turn claims into verifiable evidence tied to scores to improve matrix reliability.
Attach one-line evidence per score: benchmark, POC, or team experience.
Assign experts to review criteria with the highest risk or weight.
Prioritize testing for criteria that are both high-weight and high-uncertainty.
Evidence note examples
  • "Throughput 10k msg/s observed in internal POC"
  • "Team completed prior project on this provider last year"
  • "Vendor supplied compliance summary and audit report"
Common mistake

Accepting vendor claims without representative tests or expert review.
