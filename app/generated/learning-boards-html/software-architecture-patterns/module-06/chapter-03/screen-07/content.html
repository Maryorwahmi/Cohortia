Definition
Weighted Scoring
Assign more influence to criteria that matter most for the project's success.
Choosing weights
Start from top NFRs and discuss weights with stakeholders; document justification.
Sensitivity check
Change important weights and observe whether rankings flip; if they do, require stronger justification.
Misconception
Assuming weights are trivial and need no discussion; they encode strategic priorities, so treat them carefully.
Quick method
  1. List top 3–5 NFRs for the decision.
  2. Assign weights 1–5 where 5 is critical, 1 is minor.
  3. Record who agreed on each weight and why.
Key Takeaway

Weights turn priorities into measurable influence. Validate and test them to avoid hidden decision drivers.
