Process Flow
Build the Matrix
A repeatable six-step workflow to prepare and evaluate alternatives.
1. Identify Alternatives
List all viable options and include edge alternatives to test assumptions.
2. Define Criteria
Choose measurable traits tied to NFRs and business constraints.
3. Assign Weights
Reflect priorities with a consistent scale and document rationale.
4. Score Alternatives
Bring experts to score; record evidence for each score.
5. Calculate Totals
Multiply scores by weights and sum to rank alternatives.
6. Review & Iterate
Validate assumptions, add missing criteria, and discuss qualitative trade-offs.
Practical example: quick checklist
  • Document one source of evidence per score: benchmark, case study, or team feedback.
  • Normalize weight sums if you want percentages, or keep raw weights but be consistent.
  • Use peer review: independent rescoring highlights biased judgments.
Common mistake

Skipping peer review and assuming scores are neutral when familiarity or preference drove them.

Key Takeaway

Follow the six steps and include evidence and peer review to make scores trustworthy and useful for discussion.
