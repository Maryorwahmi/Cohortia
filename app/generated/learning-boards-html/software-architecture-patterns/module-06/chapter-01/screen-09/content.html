Process
Make trade-offs visible and measurable
Decision table example
Create a compact table: rows are NFRs and constraints, columns are alternatives. For each cell, note impact and estimate cost or benefit.
Quantify impacts
Translate statements into numbers where possible: latency target, cost per month, or estimated developer-hours to support.
Mitigations and acceptance criteria
Record mitigations for negative impacts and set measurable acceptance criteria to validate the decision in production.
Key Takeaway

Explicit trade-offs and measurable acceptance criteria let teams evaluate and revisit decisions without guessing.
