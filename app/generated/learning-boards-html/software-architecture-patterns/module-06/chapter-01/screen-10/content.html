Definition
Analysis Paralysis
A state where excessive study prevents decision-making and blocks progress.
Why it happens
Unclear priorities, too many options, or fear of being wrong.
How to avoid
Set deadlines, limit alternatives, require measurable evaluation criteria, and document decisions.
Quick checklist
  • Limit to 2–3 alternatives
  • Timebox research to a fixed period
  • Make a defensible, documented choice and iterate later
Key Takeaway

Pragmatic decisions that are documented and iterated beat waiting for a perfect answer that never arrives.
