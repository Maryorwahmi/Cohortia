Architecture
Organizational & Business Constraints
Team skills
Evaluate whether your team can build and operate a proposed stack, and estimate ramp-up effort if not.
Budget
Include infrastructure and operational costs over time, not just initial development expenses.
Compliance
Legal and regulatory requirements can restrict architectures; surface these early.
Scenario
A small team with limited ops budget should avoid architectures that demand 24/7 SRE coverage early on.
Practical tip
Annotate each alternative with estimated training time, monthly infrastructure cost, and compliance gaps.
Key Takeaway

An architecture that matches the organization's skills and constraints is more likely to succeed than a technically optimal but impractical one.
