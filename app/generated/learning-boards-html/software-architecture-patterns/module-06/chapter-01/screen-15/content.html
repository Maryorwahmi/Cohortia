Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Architectural Decision
A choice about components and interactions that shapes qualities and evolution.
Functional vs NFRs
Functional = what; NFRs = how well. NFRs usually drive the architecture.
Trade-offs
Every choice implies sacrifices; make them explicit and measurable.
Summary

Use a concise decision record and a repeatable evaluation process to make defensible architectural choices that align technology with business and team realities.
