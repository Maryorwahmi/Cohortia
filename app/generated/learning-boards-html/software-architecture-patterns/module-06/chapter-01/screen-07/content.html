Comparison
Monolith
Simpler operations, faster initial development, easier end-to-end testing. Can become harder to scale specific components and slower for parallel team work.
Pros
  • Lower operational overhead
  • Faster integration and testing
  • Lower initial cost
Cons
  • Harder to scale parts independently
  • Potentially larger codebase coupling
Comparison
Microservices
Enables independent deploys and per-service scaling. Requires mature DevOps, observability, and careful design of service boundaries to avoid distributed complexity.
Pros
  • Independent scaling and releases
  • Smaller service codebases for focused teams
  • Resilience via isolation
Cons
  • Operational complexity
  • Higher monitoring and networking needs
Key Takeaway

Match architecture style to your prioritized NFRs and team readiness; document why you chose the style.
