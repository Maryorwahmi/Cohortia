Process Flow
Six steps to make and justify a decision
1. Problem
2. Requirements
3. Alternatives
4. Evaluate
5. Select
6. Document
Evaluation checklist
  • Map each alternative to prioritized NFRs.
  • Estimate implementation and operational cost.
  • Assess team capability and training needs.
  • Identify single points of failure and recovery strategies.
Practical constraints
Limit to three realistic alternatives and set a decision deadline. This reduces the chance of analysis paralysis and forces evidence-based trade-offs.
Example
Problem: Choose product catalog store. Requirements: fast reads, horizontal scale, flexible attributes. Alternatives: relational, document store, search-backed cache.
Key Takeaway

A repeatable process reduces bias and makes trade-offs explicit. Timebox evaluations and require documented rationale.
