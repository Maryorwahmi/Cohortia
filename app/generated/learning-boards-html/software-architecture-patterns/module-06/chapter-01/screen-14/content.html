Pro Tip
Stakeholders & Team Readiness
Who to involve
Developers, operations, security, QA, and product owners all bring essential perspectives to decisions.
Readiness checks
Assess training needs, tooling readiness, and required runbooks before committing to an architecture that demands operational change.
Review cadence
Plan to re-evaluate the decision after measurable production data or at milestone releases.
Decision review
Present the concise decision card and solicit feedback; capture any operational concerns as risks to be tracked.
Operational plan
Include monitoring, alerting, and runbooks in the decision documentation to ensure the team can support production behavior.
Key Takeaway

Decisions succeed when stakeholders agree on trade-offs and the team is prepared to operate the chosen architecture.
