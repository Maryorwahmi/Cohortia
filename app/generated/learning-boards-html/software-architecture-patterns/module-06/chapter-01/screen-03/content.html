Practical guidance
Make decisions that your team can operate and maintain. If your team lacks a skill, factor the training and hiring cost into the architecture choice. Timeboxing can force a pragmatic early decision rather than indefinite study.
Scenario
A three-month deadline favors simpler deployments; postpone a distributed architecture until you can staff operations.
Pitfall
Ignoring team skill leads to fragile systems and long maintenance cycles.
