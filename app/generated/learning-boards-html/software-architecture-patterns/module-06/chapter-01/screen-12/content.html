Process
Documenting Architectural Decisions
Essential fields
  • Problem statement
  • Prioritized requirements and constraints
  • Alternatives considered
  • Chosen option and rationale
  • Trade-offs and acceptance criteria
Best practices
Keep records concise, versioned, and discoverable. Link to tests or spike results that informed the choice. Revisit decisions at significant milestones.
Example decision card
Problem: Select product catalog datastore. Requirements: fast reads, horizontal scale, flexible attributes. Chosen: document database with search index. Rationale: schema flexibility and read performance; trade-off: eventual consistency for some queries; acceptance: 95th percentile read latency & monthly cost cap.
Key Takeaway

A concise, versioned decision record preserves context and enables safer evolution of the architecture.
