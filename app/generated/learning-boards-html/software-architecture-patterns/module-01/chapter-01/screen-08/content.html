Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Architectural Trade-offs
Balancing quality attributes
List tensions, evaluate options, and choose with business alignment.
Trade-off template
  1. Name attributes in tension.
  2. Describe architectural options that favor each attribute.
  3. List consequences for cost, operations, and future change.
  4. Recommend a prioritized approach and a fallback plan.
Concrete contrast
Performance vs maintainability: optimize hot paths with caching and tuned queries when necessary, but document and isolate optimizations so code remains readable and testable.
Warning
Trying to maximize every quality attribute usually leads to unnecessary complexity and cost.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize attributes based on business needs, document trade-offs, and plan fallbacks.
