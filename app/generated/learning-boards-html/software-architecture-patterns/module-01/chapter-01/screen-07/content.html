Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)
Quality attributes
Performance, scalability, security, reliability, maintainability, usability.
Why they matter
NFRs drive architecture because they determine constraints on components and interactions. They often require trade-offs and measurable targets.
Performance
Latency and throughput targets.
Scalability
Handling increased load gracefully.
Security
Protecting data and access.
Concrete example
Requirement: support 10,000 concurrent users with median response time under 300ms. Architectural choices: caching, sharding, autoscaling, and CDN for static assets.
Misconception
Treating NFRs as optional or vague leads to unstated expectations and late rework.
Key Takeaway

Translate NFRs into measurable targets early and use them to guide structural decisions.
