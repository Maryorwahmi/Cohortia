Decision process
A practical six-step flow to make and record architectural choices.
  1. Gather functional and non-functional requirements with measurable targets.
  2. Identify constraints such as budget, regulations, or legacy systems.
  3. Generate options and map each to quality attributes.
  4. Evaluate consequences, including operational and cost impacts.
  5. Select an approach and define fallback plans.
  6. Document rationale, assumptions, and review triggers.
Example
Choose managed message queue to reduce ops effort; document cost and vendor trade-offs.
Common mistake
Skipping documentation or clear metrics leads to hidden assumptions and technical debt.
Check
Define one metric that will trigger a decision review in production.
Key Takeaway

Use a documented decision process with measurable targets to avoid ad hoc choices that cost more later.
