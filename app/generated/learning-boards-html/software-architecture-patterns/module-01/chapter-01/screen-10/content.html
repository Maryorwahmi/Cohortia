Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Monolith vs Microservices
Monolith
Single deployable unit. Pros: simpler local development, fewer cross-service operational concerns, and easier testing. Cons: harder to scale parts independently and potential for slowed team velocity on large codebases.
Microservices
Multiple small services with independent deploys. Pros: independent scaling and team ownership. Cons: increased operational complexity, network issues, and data consistency challenges.
When to prefer monolith
Small teams or early-stage products where speed matters.
When to prefer microservices
Large domains with independent scaling needs and multiple teams.
Misconception
Microservices are not a cure-all; they require strong operational maturity.
Key Takeaway

Choose structure based on team size, evolution expectations, and operational readiness, not trends.
