Security
Protecting data and operations
Plan authentication, authorization, encryption, and auditing as architecture concerns, not just implementation details.
Encryption at rest and in transit, tokenization, and strict access controls are common tactics.
Include key management and recovery plans as part of design.
Regulatory Constraints
GDPR, HIPAA, PCI and others
Regulatory requirements dictate data handling, logging, retention, and consent — treat these as mandatory architecture drivers.
Example: PCI mandates affect how payment data is stored and who can access it, steering design toward tokenization and dedicated services.
Mistake: addressing compliance late creates costly refactors. Instead, map data flows early against regulations.
