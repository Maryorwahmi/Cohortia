Elicitation Techniques
Interviews • Workshops • QAWs
Engage stakeholders with scenario-based questions to reveal hidden expectations and trade-offs.
Interviews: short, focused sessions with business and technical stakeholders to capture needs.
Use cases and user stories: capture functional scope and attach quality attributes per scenario.
QAWs: structured workshops to prioritize NFRs using scenarios and stakes.
Prioritization & Validation
Risk analysis • Prototypes
Rank drivers by impact and risk, and build prototypes to test critical assumptions before committing to architecture.
Risk analysis: identify high-impact failures and drivers that mitigate them.
Prototyping: small implementations to validate performance, scalability, or integration assumptions.
Output: prioritized driver list with acceptance criteria for each item.
Activity
Use interviews and a miniature QAW to convert vague requests into prioritized, measurable drivers for a single critical feature.
