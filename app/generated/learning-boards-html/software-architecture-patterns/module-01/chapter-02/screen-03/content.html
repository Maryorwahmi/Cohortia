From drivers to decisions
Practical steps to make drivers actionable and testable
Measure it
Convert quality desires into numbers or scenarios, for example: payment latency < 2s for 99% of transactions.
Mnemonic: Target → Test
Map to tactics
Select architecture tactics that directly address the driver: caching for performance, partitioning for scalability, encryption for security.
Mnemonic: Driver → Tactic
Prioritize
Rank drivers by business impact and risk. Ask "what happens if this fails?" to separate critical from nice-to-have.
Mnemonic: Impact over preference
Validate
Use prototypes or proofs of concept to test assumptions before committing to a full design.
Mnemonic: Test small, learn fast
