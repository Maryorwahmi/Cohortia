Architectural Drivers
The combined set of requirements and constraints that guide design.
Quality Attributes
Performance, scalability, security, reliability, maintainability — make them measurable.
Constraints
Business, technical, and regulatory limits that must be respected in design.
Summary
Keep a prioritized, measurable driver list. Use it to filter architecture options and validate assumptions with prototypes.
