Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Constraints — Comparison
Business
Budget, timeline, policies
Example: a $200,000 infrastructure cap constrains cloud choices and feature rollout.
Technical
Legacy, languages, team skills
Example: required integration with a legacy mainframe or a team skilled only in Python limits technology choices.
Regulatory
Legal and compliance mandates
Example: GDPR or PCI may require data residency, encryption, and retention controls that dictate architecture.
Practical step
Create a constraint checklist and score candidate architectures against it. Ask whether a constraint is negotiable and what mitigation is possible.
