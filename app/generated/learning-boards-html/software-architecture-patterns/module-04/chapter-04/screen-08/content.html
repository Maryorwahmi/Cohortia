Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Micro-Frontends
Definition

Micro-Frontends

Break a frontend monolith into smaller, independently deployable units owned by teams.

Motivation

Reduce build times, lower merge conflicts, and let teams choose appropriate stacks for their slice.

Dashboard example

Sales charts, engagement metrics, and inventory tables as separate micro-frontends composed by a shell.

Common misconception

Micro-frontends do not remove the need for design consistency; shared style guides or component libraries are still necessary.

Practical notes
Team ownership

Each micro-frontend is a small cross-functional unit responsible for code, tests, and deployment.

Independent deployment

Deploy individual UI pieces without rebuilding or redeploying the whole application.

Key Takeaway

Micro-frontends scale teams and deployments but require shared governance for UX and dependencies.
