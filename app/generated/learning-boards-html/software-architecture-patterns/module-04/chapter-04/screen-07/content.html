Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › SPA vs MPA
SPA

App-like interactivity

  • Client-side routing and rendering.
  • Faster transitions after initial load.
  • Rich, dynamic UI components and real-time features.
When to choose

Complex dashboards, single-page tools, and apps requiring low interaction latency.

Traditional MPA

Server-rendered pages

  • Server builds full HTML per navigation.
  • Generally faster first paint and simpler SEO.
  • Less client-side complexity and smaller JS payloads.
When to choose

Marketing sites, documentation, and content-heavy pages where first-load and SEO are critical.

Practical insight

You can combine approaches: server-render critical pages and hydrate into an SPA for dynamic sections to get both fast first load and fluid interactions.
