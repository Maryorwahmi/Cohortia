UI State Management
Track loading, error, empty, and loaded states explicitly and centralize shared state when necessary.
Testability
Isolate data access and rendering so you can test logic with mocks and rendering with deterministic inputs.
Why it matters
Patterns avoid tangled code, reduce regression risk, and let teams work independently on visuals, data, and business logic.
Parting question

Pick one UI you worked on and imagine replacing its view. Can you do it without touching business logic? If not, where would you refactor first?
