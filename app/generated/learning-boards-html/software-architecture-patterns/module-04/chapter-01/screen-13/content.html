Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Common Challenges
Top pitfalls
  • Tangled handlers mixing DOM, business, and data logic.
  • Duplicated business rules across multiple UI components.
  • Scattered state causing inconsistent views.
Practical tip
After refactor, add a small unit test for the extracted function to lock in its behavior and prevent regressions.
Before / After analogy
Before: tangled wires and pipes. After: separate systems with clear labels and accessible components. The 'after' is easier to maintain and test.
Key Takeaway
Small refactors that extract responsibilities pay off quickly in fewer bugs and faster feature work.
