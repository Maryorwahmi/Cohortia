Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Validation & Interaction
What belongs in the UI
Immediate checks like required fields, numeric formats, and simple range limits. These give quick feedback and keep the UI responsive.
What to delegate
Business rules that require cross-data checks, persistence, or complex calculations should be handled by the application layer.
Example scenario
An order form should validate email format in the UI, but check credit limits and discounts in the application layer and show results returned from that layer.
Common mistake
Duplicating business validation across several views leads to inconsistency and maintenance overhead.
