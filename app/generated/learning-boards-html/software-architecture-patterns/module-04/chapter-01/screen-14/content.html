Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Presentation Patterns
Architectural blueprints for the UI layer that promote separation of concerns.
Separation of Concerns
Divide UI responsibilities into display, data, and interaction to reduce coupling and speed development.
Layered Architecture
Presentation is the top layer; it should delegate business rules and data access to lower layers to remain replaceable.
Summary

When you structure the UI with clear responsibilities, development becomes faster, testing becomes simpler, and the system adapts more easily to change.
