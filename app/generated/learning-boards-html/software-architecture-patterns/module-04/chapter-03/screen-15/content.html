Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Quick Reminders
Presenter
Holds presentation logic and updates the View through a contract for testability.
Passive View
Displays data and forwards events. Keep it dumb.
ViewModel
Exposes properties and commands; binding keeps UI and state in sync.
Final takeaway

Design for testability: isolate presentation logic in Presenter or ViewModel, use interfaces or binding, and keep the Model focused on business rules.
