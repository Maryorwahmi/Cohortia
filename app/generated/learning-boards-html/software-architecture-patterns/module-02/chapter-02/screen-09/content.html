Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Trade-offs

Control & Consistency

Client-server simplifies centralized policies and strong consistency via a central data store.

When to choose

Applications requiring audits, strict authentication, or authoritative data sources.

Watch out for

Single points of failure unless you add redundancy and failover mechanisms.

Resilience & Distribution

P2P favors availability and distributed capacity, but complicates security and consistency.

When to choose

Applications that need censorship resistance, decentralized control, or to leverage user resources.

Watch out for

Discovery, validation, and uneven performance from heterogeneous peers.

Reflection

Which trade-off matters most for your application: control or distribution?
