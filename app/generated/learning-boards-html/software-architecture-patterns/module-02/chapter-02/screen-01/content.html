Client-Server vs Peer-to-Peer

Two distinct architectural choices for how components communicate, each with different trade-offs for control, scale, and resilience.

In this lesson
Client-Server

Central server(s) respond to client requests using a request-response model and often maintain authoritative data.

Peer-to-Peer

Nodes act as both clients and servers, sharing resources without a central authority and favoring decentralization.

Why it matters

Choosing between these patterns affects how you design for updates, security policies, data consistency, and how the system recovers from failures.
