Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Business Logic Layer
What it owns
  • Domain validations and business rules.
  • Orchestration of operations and service calls.
  • Creation of domain entities and state transitions.
Key takeaway
Centralize rules to avoid duplication across UI or persistence code.
Concrete scenario
In the order example the service checks stock, computes price, creates an order object, saves it through a repository, and notifies the inventory service to deduct stock.
Pro Tip
Write unit tests for services that mock repositories, ensuring business behavior without touching the database.
Common Mistake
Duplicating rules in controllers or repositories leads to inconsistent behavior.
