Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Database Layer
What it includes
  • DBMS, schemas, tables, indexes.
  • Storage-specific optimizations and integrity constraints.
  • Backups, replication, and maintenance responsibilities.
Note
Use constraints for data integrity but keep domain policy in the business layer.
Design question
When is it acceptable to denormalize for performance? When you can guarantee consistent updates through the DAL and tests.
Scenario
Storing a computed 'average rating' speeds reads but requires careful updates when new ratings arrive; the DAL must manage consistency.
