Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Advantages vs Disadvantages
Advantages
  • Clear separation of concerns and easier maintenance.
  • Improved testability through isolated layers and mocks.
  • Reusability of business logic across multiple presentation channels.
Disadvantages
  • Added indirection and potential performance overhead.
  • Risk of over-engineering for small projects.
  • Requires discipline to avoid erosion and leaky abstractions.
Decision hint
Choose layering when the system will grow or must be maintained by a team; leaner options suit short-lived utilities.
