Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Presentation Layer
Responsibilities
  • Translate user actions into commands and requests.
  • Display data and format output for users.
  • Perform minimal input validation; delegate policy checks to business logic.
Misconception
Putting heavy business rules in controllers for quick delivery reduces reuse and testability.
Concrete example
In an Online Recipe Book a web form that collects title, ingredients, and instructions belongs to Presentation; the form calls a RecipeService to validate and persist the recipe.
Analogy
Reception: greet and translate requests.
Key Takeaway
Keep views thin and delegate rules out.
