Assessment question
Which approach is most robust for a Trap that should damage a player once every 3 seconds?
Correct Answer
Use a validated hit check plus a player-specific os.time based cooldown stored per UserId.
Why
This avoids blocking waits inside handlers and scopes cooldowns per player for multiplayer safety.
Reflection

When designing interactions, ask whether the action should be passive, clicked, or explicitly prompted. Your choice should prioritize player intent and clear feedback.
