Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Learning Objectives
01 Validate Touched
Confirm collisions are from player characters using parent, Humanoid, and player lookup.
Why it matters: prevents false positives from falling objects. VERIFED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Implement Debounce
Use per-player timestamps or tables to avoid multiple rapid triggers from one standing player.
Why it matters: preserves fairness and prevents runaway effects. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Configure Prompts
Set ActionText, ObjectText, HoldDuration and keys for clear player guidance.
Why it matters: creates intentional interactions and reduces accidental triggers. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 Choose Correct Tool
Decide between Touched, ClickDetector, and ProximityPrompt based on intent and UX needs.
Why it matters: right tool leads to better player experience. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
