Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Interaction Comparison
Touched
Passive proximity triggers.
Use for damage zones, auto-teleporters, and environmental effects where simply entering an area should cause the action.
ClickDetector
Direct clicking interactions.
Good for desktop-oriented clickable items and simple pickups where a click is intuitive.
ProximityPrompt
Guided, deliberate interactions.
Best for chests, NPC dialogs, and quest actions—provides clear UI and requires player input to commit.
Decision checklist
Ask: Should the action be automatic, clicked, or explicitly prompted? Match the choice to player expectation to avoid accidental triggers and improve UX.
