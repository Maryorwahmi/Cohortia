Touched vs ClickDetector.MouseClick

Touched (collision)
  • Fires when two parts physically contact.
  • Handler receives the touching Part (otherPart or hit).
  • Best for pressure plates, traps, or area triggers.
Watch out: Non-player objects can trigger Touched. Validate that otherPart belongs to a character before acting.
ClickDetector.MouseClick (explicit click)
  • Fires when a player clicks a part that has a ClickDetector.
  • Handler receives the Player who clicked.
  • Best for interacting with objects that require deliberate input.
Design tip: Use MouseClick for menus, chests, or buttons where you want clear player intent.
