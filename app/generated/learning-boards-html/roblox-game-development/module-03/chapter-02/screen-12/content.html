Key Concepts
Three compact reminders for reliable click interactions.
ClickDetector
Parent to a BasePart to make it clickable by players.
MaxActivationDistance
Controls how far a player can be to click an object.
MouseClick & player
MouseClick passes the Player instance so you can tailor behavior to the clicking player.
Summary

These concepts let you build clear, player-aware click interactions and avoid common setup errors in Roblox Studio.
