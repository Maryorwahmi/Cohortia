Quick Reference
Three short reminders while you code and test.
Parenting
Attach ClickDetector to a BasePart, not a Model.
Existence checks
Use FindFirstChild to avoid nil errors and warn clearly during testing.
Player argument
MouseClick passes the Player instance; use it for player-specific logic.
Parting thought

These quick checks will keep your clickable interactions robust and predictable while you iterate and test in Roblox Studio.
