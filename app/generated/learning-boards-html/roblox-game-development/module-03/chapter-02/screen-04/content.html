ClickDetector vs Touched
Choose based on user intent and gameplay design.
Touched (Proximity)
  • Fires on physical contact or overlap.
  • Good for automatic pickups and collision-based mechanics.
  • Fast, low-friction interactions.
Example: a health pickup disappears when a player walks through it.
Misconception
Touched is not always ideal for intentional UI actions.
ClickDetector (Deliberate Input)
  • Requires hover and click; shows a hand cursor on hover.
  • MouseClick passes the Player object to your handler.
  • Ideal for buttons, levers, and collectibles that require intent.
Example: a door button that opens when a player clicks it.
Design tip
Use a transparent part with ClickDetector to make complex models clickable.
Key takeaway

Decide between automatic and deliberate interactions by asking whether the player should act intentionally; choose ClickDetector for intent-driven UX.
