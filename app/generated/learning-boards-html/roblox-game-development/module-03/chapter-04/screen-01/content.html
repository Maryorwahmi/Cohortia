Building Simple Game Mechanics with Events

Combine ProximityPrompt, ClickDetector, and Touched events with simple boolean state and TweenService to make interactive doors, collectibles, and traps that behave reliably and feel responsive.

Interactive Door
Use a ProximityPrompt, tween the door's position, and debounce interaction until tween.Completed fires.
Collectible Item
Use ClickDetector or Touched, set a collected flag, update leaderstats, and remove the item after a short delay.
Trap & Reset
Trigger damage with Touched, animate the trap, and use a reset timer or tween to re-enable it safely.
Why this chapter matters

These patterns are the foundation of interactive levels. Small safe scripts let you iterate quickly and avoid frustrating race conditions when multiple players interact at once.
