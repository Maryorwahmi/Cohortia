Key Concepts
Remember these three core ideas.
Publishing
Uploading a place and making it discoverable; set Access to Public.
Game Settings
Permissions, Monetization, Security, and Avatar choices control behaviour and revenue.
Community Engagement
Collect feedback, communicate updates, and host events to retain players.
Takeaway

Publishing is the start of a loop: publish, configure, promote, and engage. Repeat these steps to grow and maintain your audience.
