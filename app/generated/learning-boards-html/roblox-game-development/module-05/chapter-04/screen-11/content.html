Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Community Engagement
Solicit feedback
Make feedback channels obvious and easy to use.
In-game forms
A simple feedback form can surface bugs and improvement ideas directly from players.
Roblox Group & Forum
Use your Group for announcements and the Developer Forum to attract other creators' attention.
Prioritize and communicate
Triage bug reports, fix critical issues first, and post a changelog so players know you’re working on their feedback.
Events & updates
Plan a mix of updates: fixes, quality-of-life, and new content or events.
Event idea
Host a weekend event with double rewards to re-engage players after a new feature launch.
Changelog tip
Keep changelogs short and highlight user-facing improvements. Link to the Group for discussion.
Key Takeaway

Engagement is ongoing work. Build feedback channels, prioritize issues, and show players you’re listening through visible updates and events.
