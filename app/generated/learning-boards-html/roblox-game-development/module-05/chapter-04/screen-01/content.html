Publishing Your Game and Community Engagement
Prepare your game for players, set essential settings, and begin community building.
In this lesson
Publish correctly and configure permissions so players can join.
Coming up
Monetization and security options that affect functionality.
Why it matters
Promotion and updates keep players engaged and help your game grow.
Launch checklist
Quick actions before you press go
  • Publish from Studio and set Access to Public.
  • Review Monetization and Security tabs in Game Settings.
  • Create a Roblox Group and prepare a short announcement.
  • Plan your first three updates with changelogs.
