Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Avatar Settings
Avatar types
Choose R6, R15, or both depending on animation needs and gameplay.
R6
Simpler rig with six joints. Easier to animate for basic movement and older assets.
R15
More joints, smoother motion, but requires additional animation work and testing.
Accessory and body part rules
Control allowed attachments to avoid clipping or gameplay issues.
Example and test
Test with a variety of avatar combinations to ensure animations and collisions are stable.
Scenario: platformer control
If jump arcs depend on a character height, test both R6 and R15 players to ensure fairness and avoid clipping through platforms.
Key Takeaway

Avatar settings shape player experience. Choose and test them according to your gameplay mechanics and document the choice for players.
