What to Remember
Three concise reminders for a successful launch.
Prepare visuals & metadata
Title, description, icon, and thumbnail affect discovery.
Verify settings
Permissions, APIs, and monetization must match your game’s needs.
Engage continuously
Promotion, feedback, and frequent updates keep players coming back.
Final Thought

Publishing is the start of player relationships. Prepare carefully, test, and keep iterating with your community.
