Permissions and Access
Make sure players can join and collaborators have correct roles.
Access: Private vs Public
Private blocks players; Public allows everyone to join. This setting is often the reason a new game appears unplayable to others.
Collaborators and roles
Ensure team members have the intended permissions for testing and publishing. Misconfigured roles can block updates or hides builds.
Common mistake
Sharing a link before changing Access to Public — players cannot join even though the page exists.
Quick verification
Steps to confirm joinability after you publish.
  1. Set Access to Public in Game Settings → Permissions.
  2. Open the game in a browser with a non-developer account and attempt to join.
  3. Confirm server-side features initialize correctly for that account.
Key Takeaway

Access controls are simple to change but easy to forget. Test with another account immediately after publish.
