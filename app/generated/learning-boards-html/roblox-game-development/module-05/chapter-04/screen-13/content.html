Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Update Planning
Plan updates and backups
A timeline approach to keep the game fresh and safe.
Week 1 — Hotfix and QoL
Address critical bugs reported at launch and small quality-of-life improvements.
Week 3 — New cosmetic + event
Release a small cosmetic and a weekend event to encourage returns.
Week 6 — Balance and polish
Adjust mechanics based on play data, push small improvements, and hotfix regressions.
Backups: Save copies in Studio and keep changelog entries for each published version so you can revert if needed.
Avoid stale content
Games without updates lose players. Even small content or balance tweaks signal activity.
Testing and rollback
Test updates and keep versions so you can revert a problematic release quickly.
Key Takeaway

A sustainable update cadence and backups protect players’ experience and keep your community engaged over time.
