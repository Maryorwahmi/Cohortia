Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Pitfalls & Security
Major pitfalls
  • Granting items on the client — insecure and exploitable.
  • Returning NotProcessedYet incorrectly — causes retries and confusion.
  • Not recording PurchaseId — breaks idempotency.
Design pitfall

Overly intrusive monetization degrades player experience. Aim for optional value.

Security checklist
Wrap MarketplaceService calls in pcall.
Persist PurchaseId values before returning PurchaseGranted.
Queue offline purchases for later processing.
Warning

Client-based grants and missing idempotency are the most common causes of lost revenue and unhappy players.
