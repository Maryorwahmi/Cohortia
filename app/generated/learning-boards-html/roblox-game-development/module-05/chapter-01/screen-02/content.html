Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Learning Objectives
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Enhance Visual Aesthetics
Use Lighting and post-processing to set mood and readability.

Skill: Configure Lighting properties and effects. Why it matters: careful lighting draws attention and improves clarity. Milestone: produce a scene with balanced brightness and one post-effect enabled.

02
Integrate Immersive Audio
Manage music and SFX using Sound objects and SoundService.

Skill: Insert looping background music and button SFX. Why it matters: audio communicates action and tone. Milestone: create a looped track at comfortable volume and one interaction sound.

03
Design Responsive UI
Use UDim2 scale/offset and layout components to adapt to screens.

Skill: Build a HUD that scales and repositions. Why it matters: players use many device types. Milestone: HUD remains readable when emulating phone and desktop sizes.

04
Apply UI/UX Principles
Clarity, feedback, and hierarchy in interface design.

Skill: Prioritize information and provide feedback on actions. Why it matters: good UX reduces confusion and increases retention. Milestone: add a clear settings button with feedback when pressed.
