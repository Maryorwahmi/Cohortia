Definition
Game Polishing

The process of refining visual, audio, and interactive elements to improve immersion and usability.

Why it matters
  • Encourages longer play sessions and returns.
  • Makes rules and feedback clearer to players.
  • Boosts perceived quality even with simple mechanics.
Key Takeaway

Polish is measurable: change one element, test, and see if player behavior improves.

Analogy & Example
Analogy
Polish is like UI on a website: small spacing, color contrast, and feedback determine whether users complete tasks.
Concrete Example
Enable a BloomEffect and set a low-intensity music loop. Players notice objectives faster and feel the game's tone.
Common Mistake
Applying many visual effects at once without testing performance or readability.
