Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Camera Controls
WASD, right-click look, scroll wheel and F to focus — move fluidly around your scene.
Part
A single 3D object such as Block, Sphere or Cylinder with properties like Size and Position.
Select Tool
Pick and confirm objects before transforming; use Explorer selection and bounding box to verify.
Summary Takeaway

Develop camera fluency, name and manage parts, and always confirm your selection — these habits will save hours of debugging.
