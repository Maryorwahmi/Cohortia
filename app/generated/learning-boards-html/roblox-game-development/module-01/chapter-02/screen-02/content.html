These objectives guide practice: learn the controls, understand parts and tools, apply transforms, and reach a mastery milestone by grouping and testing your build.

01
Camera Navigation
Learn right-click look, WASD movement, scroll zoom, Q/E vertical shift, and F focus. Practice by centering on a part and flying around it.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Parts & Shapes
Identify Block, Sphere, Cylinder, Wedge and CornerWedge and insert a part from the Model tab. Name it descriptively in Explorer.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Select & Move
Use Select to highlight objects then Move to align along X, Y or Z using colored gizmo arrows. Verify by moving only along the Y-axis.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Scale, Rotate, Properties
Scale uniformly and per-axis, rotate around X/Y/Z rings, and edit Size, Position, Color, Material and Anchored to control behavior.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
