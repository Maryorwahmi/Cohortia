Comparison

Scale vs Rotate

Scale changes Size; Rotate changes Orientation. Both are necessary to position and shape parts correctly.

Scale (Ctrl/Cmd + 3)
Use spheres on faces and corners to adjust dimensions per axis or uniformly.
Rotate (Ctrl/Cmd + 4)
Use colored rings to spin parts around X, Y, Z; orientation values are measured in degrees.
Concrete Example
Scale a Block to 4 × 0.25 × 4 to form a floor tile, then rotate 45° on Y to lay it diagonally. Confirm placement from multiple camera angles.
Visuals & Tips
Misconception
Scaling does not change a part's orientation or pivot. Rotation changes facing but not size dimensions.
Group Effects
When scaling a grouped Model, children scale relative to the group's bounding box center. Ungroup to adjust individuals.
Pro Tip
Make small test adjustments and orbit the camera to check how scale and rotation interact visually with nearby parts.
Try This

Scale and rotate a Block to create a diagonal floor tile and verify that Size or Orientation changed as expected.
