Move / Scale / Rotate
Use these tools to position, size and orient parts precisely; memorize their shortcuts for speed.
Axis Colors
Red = X, Green = Y, Blue = Z. Rely on gizmo colors, not screen orientation, for accurate transforms.
Anchored
Anchored=true fixes parts in place; Anchored=false lets physics move them. Check before testing.
Final Takeaway

Before you end a session, run this checklist: correct active tool, proper selection, sizes and positions, anchored settings, and grouping for organization.
