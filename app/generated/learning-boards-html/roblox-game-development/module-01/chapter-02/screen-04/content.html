Core Concept

Camera Navigation

Practice: insert a Part, select it, press F to focus, then use right-click plus WASD to fly around it. Use scroll wheel to adjust distance.

Step 1 — Look & Fly
Hold right-click to look; press W/A/S/D to move horizontally while looking in the direction you face.
Step 2 — Vertical & Zoom
Use Q and E for vertical adjustments and scroll wheel to zoom in and out smoothly.
Step 3 — Focus
Select a part in Explorer or Viewport and press F to center and zoom to it. This regains orientation instantly.
Key Takeaway

Camera shortcuts let you inspect geometry rapidly. Use F whenever you lose your place.
