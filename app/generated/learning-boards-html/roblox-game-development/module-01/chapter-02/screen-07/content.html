Process Flow

Move Tool

Constrain movement along an axis using colored arrows or move freely on a plane using the center handle. Shortcut: Ctrl/Cmd + 2.

Step 1 — Activate
Press Ctrl/Cmd + 2 to enable the Move gizmo when a part is selected.
Step 2 — Axis Moves
Drag red, green or blue arrows to move along X, Y, Z respectively. Use the center to drag on a plane.
Step 3 — Verify
Rotate the camera and confirm the part sits where intended from other angles. Use F to refocus if needed.
Check
If movement feels off with multi-selection, adjust individual parts or group them to standardize transforms.
Illustration: Axis Gizmo
Concrete Example
Place a Sphere on top of a Cylinder. Select the Sphere, activate Move, drag the green arrow up until it sits flush on the cylinder’s top surface.
Common Mistake
Dragging without checking axis orientation; camera foreshortening can make arrows hard to interpret. Reframe or press F to recenter.
Pro Tip
Use the Move center for planar adjustments and axis arrows for exact vertical or horizontal placement.
