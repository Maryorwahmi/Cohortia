Grouping & Models

Group parts to form Models that act as one object for transforms and Explorer organization.

Create a Group
Select parts, press Ctrl/Cmd + G or click Group in the Model tab. A Model node appears in Explorer.
Edit Children
Expand the Model in Explorer to select and edit individual parts, or Ungroup to separate them.
Group Caveat
Transforms on a group use the group's center; multi-part behavior may differ from per-part transforms. Check after grouping.
Concrete Example: Table Model
Step 1 — Build Parts
Create a Block for the tabletop and four Cylinder legs. Position legs at the corners of the tabletop.
Step 2 — Group
Select all five parts, press Ctrl/Cmd + G to make TableModel. Now move or rotate the table as a single unit.
Step 3 — Test
Run Play to ensure parts are Anchored if the table should remain static, or leave unanchored if it should respond to physics.
Key Takeaway

Grouping simplifies transforms and organization, but always verify Anchored and transforms after grouping.
