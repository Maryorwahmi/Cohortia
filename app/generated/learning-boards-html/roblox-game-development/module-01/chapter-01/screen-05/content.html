Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Launch & Sign In
Home Screen: What to expect
Templates, recent projects, and learning links. Your avatar or username shows when signed in.
Confirm: your username or avatar appears on the Home screen — this indicates Studio is linked to your Roblox account.
If sign-in fails: check internet connectivity and credentials. Two-step verification prompts must be completed if enabled on the account.
Misconception check: saving locally does not publish; publishing requires a linked account and network access.
Concrete Example
After signing in, visit My Games. If you have published places they will appear here. New users will see an empty list until their first publish.
Key Takeaway
Always confirm account linkage before creating or publishing a project. This avoids orphaned uploads and permission issues later.
