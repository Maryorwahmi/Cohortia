Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Properties & Toolbox
Properties Panel
Displays editable attributes of the selected object like Size, Position, Color, and Material.
Example: change Material to "Plastic" and observe the visual update in the Viewport immediately.
Pro tip: numeric fields accept precise values for consistent scaling and placement.
Toolbox
Search and insert community models and plugins to accelerate building.
After inserting an asset, inspect its children in Explorer to find any scripts or nested parts.
Misconception: toolbox assets are safe without inspection. Always check for scripts and understand what they do.
Key Takeaway: Properties for precise control, Toolbox for reusable content — combine them carefully.
