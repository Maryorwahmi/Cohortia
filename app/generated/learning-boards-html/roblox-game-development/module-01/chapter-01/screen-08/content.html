Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Explorer & Viewport
Explorer: Hierarchy
Workspace is the root for visible objects. Expand nodes to find parts, models, and scripts.
Example: Workspace → Baseplate. Selecting Baseplate in Explorer highlights it visually in the Viewport and enables property edits.
Misconception: changes in Viewport are separate; in truth, moving or renaming parts in the Viewport updates Explorer immediately.
Viewport: Visual Editing
Use tools to move, rotate, and scale parts. Visual feedback confirms selection and transformations.
Practice: click a part in Viewport and watch its node highlight in Explorer — this verifies the link.
Key Takeaway: Explorer helps you find objects, Viewport helps you manipulate them; use both together for efficient workflows.
