Tabs at a glance
Home, Model, Test, and View each group tools for a phase of development.
Home
Select, insert, basic transforms.
Model
Scale, align, group, and snapping.
Test
Play and simulate your game within Studio.
Concrete Example
Insert a Part from Home, adjust its size in Model, then switch to Test to play and observe physics. This shows the full edit-check loop.
Key Takeaway
Remember the edit-check loop: Home → Model → Test. Relying on Test ensures behaviors work as expected under simulation.
