Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Install Roblox Studio
Installation Steps
  1. 1. Download the installer from the official site and open it.
  2. 2. Follow the installer prompts and complete setup.
  3. 3. Launch Roblox Studio and sign in with your Roblox account.
  4. 4. Confirm the Studio home screen and your account avatar or name appear.
Example
After install, opening Studio should show templates and a Home view. Seeing your account name proves Studio is linked to your profile and ready for publishing.
Troubleshoot
If Studio won’t open: ensure you have an active internet connection and that your account credentials are correct. Some installers are blocked by security tools; try briefly disabling them if safe.
Misconception: Studio is not fully usable offline — publishing and initial account verification require internet access.
Key Takeaway
A successful install ends with Studio launching and your account visible on the Home screen.
