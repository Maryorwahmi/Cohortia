Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Open, Search, Preview, Insert
Step-by-step
  1. Open Toolbox from the View tab.
  2. Select the right category (Models, Decals, Audio, Meshes, Plugins).
  3. Search with a specific term like 'tree' and preview thumbnails.
  4. Insert chosen asset and immediately inspect in Explorer.
  5. Use Move, Scale, Rotate to place and rename the model descriptively.
Common Mistake
Bulk-inserting many models without inspecting increases the chance of importing malicious or messy content.
Practical Example
Search 'tree', preview two thumbnails, insert the preferred tree, then rename it to 'OakTree1' and move it to an appropriate location on your Baseplate using the Move tool. Expand the model in Explorer and scan for any Script objects.
Key Takeaway
Preview before inserting, then inspect and rename immediately to avoid confusion later.
