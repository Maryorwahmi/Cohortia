Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Learning Objectives
Bloom Progression
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Follow the steps to move from recognition to confident use and organization of assets.
01
Describe the Toolbox
Explain categories and when to choose Models, Decals, Audio, Meshes, or Plugins. Verified Competency Milestone
Mnemonic: category Venn — visuals, sound, and geometry.
02
Insert and Preview
Search, preview thumbnails, and insert assets safely. Verified Competency Milestone
Mnemonic: search → preview → insert.
03
Inspect for Safety
Open Explorer, find unexpected Script objects, and remove or reject risky assets. Verified Competency Milestone
Mnemonic: expand then scan.
04
Organize with Folders
Create named folders like 'Scenery' and 'Furniture' to keep Workspace readable. Verified Competency Milestone
Mnemonic: folders = findability.
