Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › What is the Toolbox?
Definition
The Toolbox is an in-studio repository of community assets: models, decals, audio, meshes, and plugins you can insert into your game.
Why use it?
  • Speeds up prototyping and scenery creation.
  • Provides ready-made mechanics or decorations to learn from.
  • Offers meshes and audio assets that are otherwise time-consuming to create.
Concrete Example
Insert two tree models and a bench to populate a park. Then rename each model to 'OakTree1' and 'Bench1' to keep the Explorer readable.
Asset Types at a Glance
Model
Grouped parts and objects; may include scripts.
Decal
2D image applied to a Part surface.
Audio / Mesh / Plugin
Audio creates Audio objects; Meshes are imported geometry; Plugins extend Studio tools.
Misconception check: Toolbox assets are not always production-ready. Inspect and optimize before publishing.
