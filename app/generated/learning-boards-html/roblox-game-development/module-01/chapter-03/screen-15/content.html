Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Preview
Click thumbnails and 3D previews in Toolbox before inserting to avoid irrelevant or low-quality assets.
Inspect
Expand models in Explorer and remove unexpected Script objects. Test unknown assets in an empty place.
Organize
Use descriptive names and folders like 'Scenery' and 'Furniture' to keep your Workspace tidy.
Final Takeaway

A short routine—preview, inspect, organize—saves time and protects your project as it grows.
