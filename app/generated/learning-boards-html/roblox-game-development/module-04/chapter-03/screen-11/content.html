Testing Edge Cases
Test checklist
  • Run Roblox test purchases for both Developer Products and Game Passes.
  • Simulate player disconnect during a purchase and verify receipt persistence.
  • Force DataStore failures and ensure ProcessReceipt returns NotProcessedYet.
  • Confirm idempotency by replaying the same PurchaseId.
Example test scenario
1. Initiate test purchase for 500 coins. 2. While purchase is pending, disconnect player. 3. Confirm ProcessReceipt is called and that receiptInfo is saved. 4. On next join process the saved receipt and persist coins.
Pro Tip

Keep a small test account to run repeatable purchase flows and verify your DataStore entries after each test.
