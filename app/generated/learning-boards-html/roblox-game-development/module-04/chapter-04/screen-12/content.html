What to Remember

Key Concepts

Three compact reminders to guide your implementation choices.

Tweening
Engine-driven interpolation for smooth transitions across properties.
CFrame
Use a single CFrame target to animate translation and rotation together reliably.
Server vs Client
Authoritative movement on server; client-only for cosmetic polishing and distributed load.
Takeaway

Design tweens for both feel and correctness: pick the right properties, the right TweenInfo, and the right execution context.
