Practical outcomes

Concrete builds that show how to apply tweens in gameplay: platforms, doors, and combined visual effects.

Moving Platform

Two Waypoint parts, an anchored platform, and alternating tweens form a predictable back-and-forth movement that players can stand on.

Rotating Door

Use CFrame.Angles for a 90-degree opening motion and disable clicks while the tween plays to avoid spamming effects.

Fade & Sink

Tween Transparency and Position/CFrame together; set CanCollide false before sinking to avoid physics issues.
