Starter structure
StarterGui → ShopScreen (ScreenGui) → ShopFrame (Frame) → ShopTitle, ItemScrollFrame (ScrollingFrame) → ItemTemplate (hidden).
ItemTemplate
Contains ItemIcon (ImageLabel), ItemName (TextLabel), BuyButton (TextButton). Keep Visible = false so you clone and populate at runtime.
Animation & events
Animate the ShopFrame with TweenService when opening and closing. Fire BuyItemEvent to ReplicatedStorage on purchase clicks.
