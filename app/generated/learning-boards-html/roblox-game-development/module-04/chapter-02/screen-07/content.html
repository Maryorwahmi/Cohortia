Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › UIGridLayout & PageLayout
UIGridLayout
Arrange tiles into a predictable grid. Set CellSize and Padding and use Scale when you need proportional resizing.
Example
CellSize = UDim2.new(0.33, -10, 0.25, 0) for a three-column layout with small horizontal padding.
UIPageLayout
Use for tabbed or paged content. PageLayout supports transitions and is useful for onboarding or multi-screen menus.
Practical note
Combine PageLayout with buttons or swipe gestures and animate transitions with TweenService for polish.
Accessibility reminder
Keep tile contents readable with TextScaled and avoid dense grids on small screens. Test with narrow aspect ratios to ensure tap targets remain reachable.
