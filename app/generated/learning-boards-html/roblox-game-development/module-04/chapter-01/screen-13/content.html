Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › What to Remember
Use pcall
Catch failures and implement retries instead of letting scripts error out.
SetAsync vs UpdateAsync
SetAsync overwrites. UpdateAsync modifies atomically and handles concurrent edits.
Serialize smartly
Keep saved data small and use ID-based representations to minimize size and complexity.
Closing question
As you implement your own persistence, which items will you store by ID and which will you store as full values? Plan your schema before coding.
