Tables: the Powerhouse

Create, read, and organize game data: arrays, dictionaries, and nested objects powered by curly braces.

In this lesson

Create and initialize tables; choose array-like vs dictionary-like; iterate safely and build nested structures.

Why it matters

Tables represent inventories, settings, and dynamic lists. Good table design reduces bugs and simplifies iteration logic.
