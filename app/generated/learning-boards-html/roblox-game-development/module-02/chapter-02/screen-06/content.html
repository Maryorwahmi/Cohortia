Operators

Comparison & Logical Operators

  • == equal to • ~= not equal to
  • < less than • > greater than
  • <= less or equal • >= greater or equal
  • and, or, not combine boolean results
Example

Allow action when health above zero and not frozen.

Precedence Tip

Use parentheses to group conditions and remove ambiguity.

Concrete Snippets
-- Combined checks
if playerHealth > 0 and not isFrozen then
  -- allow movement
end

-- Parentheses clarify intent
if (playerCoins >= cost and hasKey) or isAdmin then
  -- allow passage
end
Misconception

Assuming and/or behave the same; they do not. and requires both sides true, or needs just one. Evaluate with examples or parentheses to be sure.
