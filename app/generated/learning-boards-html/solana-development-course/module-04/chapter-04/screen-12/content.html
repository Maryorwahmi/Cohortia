Key Concepts Summary
Anchor fetch
Fetches and deserializes using your program's IDL, returning typed objects like anchor.BN for big integers.
React hooks
useState stores fetched values; useEffect triggers fetches on dependency changes and must be stabilized to avoid loops.
Update strategy
Re-fetch after your transactions for most flows; use onAccountChange for live multi-client updates with care.
One-line takeaway
Safe deserialization, stable hook patterns, and mindful updates are the foundation of reliable DApp UIs.
