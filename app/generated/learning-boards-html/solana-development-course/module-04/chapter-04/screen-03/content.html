Practical success criteria
Reliable display
UI consistently shows the correct on-chain value after transactions and on refresh.
Good feedback
Show loading states, disable actions during pending RPC calls, and present clear errors.
Efficient updates
Re-fetch after transactions for most cases; subscribe for high-frequency changes carefully.
Quick checklist
  1. Use Anchor fetch for typed data when possible.
  2. Convert anchor.BN to a safe number before rendering.
  3. Call your fetch after transaction confirmation to avoid stale UI.
