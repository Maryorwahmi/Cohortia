Architecture Update strategies
Re-fetch after transaction
Call fetchCounter after rpc() confirmation. Best for actions initiated by this client and for conserving RPC usage.
Good for: single-user flows; low RPC cost
Subscribe with onAccountChange
Use connection.onAccountChange(publicKey, callback) to receive immediate notifications when account data changes.
Good for: collaborative apps, real-time feeds. Mind RPC quotas and cleanup of subscriptions.
Implementation note
Combine approaches: re-fetch after your own transactions and subscribe only if you need immediate external updates. Always remove subscriptions on cleanup to avoid leaks.
