Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Rent & Freeze
Rent exemption

Accounts that are not rent-exempt can be reclaimed and closed. For program-owned storage, allocate sufficient SOL to remain rent-exempt to avoid accidental closure.

Practical measure
When creating accounts, calculate rent exemption and fund accordingly. Verify rent exemption status where account permanence is required.
Freeze authority

SPL Token accounts can be frozen by a freeze authority. Vaults should avoid giving freeze authority to a single key; prefer None or a multisig to prevent unexpected freezes.

Key Takeaway

Plan account funding and authority settings at creation time to prevent administrative or network-level failures later.
