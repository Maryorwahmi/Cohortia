CPI pre-flight checklist
Pre-validate
  • Ownership: account.owner == expected program ID.
  • Writeability: accounts that will be mutated must be writable.
  • Signer: ensure the authority is a proper signer or a correctly derived PDA with invoke_signed.
  • Structure: use Pack to deserialize SPL token accounts and confirm mint matches.
Concrete risk

If you pass an attacker-controlled account as a destination, they can receive funds meant for another recipient. If you pass an invalid type, the callee may panic and cause denial of service.

Pro Tip

Validate ownership and structure before calling the callee; log clear messages to ease audits and tests.
