Process Flow › Logging strategy
Development vs Production
  • • Development: verbose msg! to trace state and root causes.
  • • Production: minimal, essential logs; avoid heavy tracing in hot paths.
  • • Never log secrets or private keys.
Practical controls
Gate detailed logging with a debug flag or only enable it in non-mainnet clusters. Document which error codes are permanent and which may change as you evolve the program.
Real-world connection
A clear logging policy reduces operational surprises and helps your dApp team triage incidents quickly.
