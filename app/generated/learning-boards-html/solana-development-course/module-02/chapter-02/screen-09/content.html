Lamports explained
Lamports are the smallest unit of SOL. Fees are usually a few thousand lamports — generally tiny compared to a whole SOL — but they must be available in the sender account.
Common mistakes
  • Sending nearly your entire balance and leaving no lamports for fees.
  • Assuming fees cannot increase when executing heavy computations.
Practical checklist before sending
  1. Check your account balance in SOL and lamports.
  2. Estimate the fee or observe the fee printed by the CLI.
  3. Ensure the sender retains enough lamports for the fee after the transfer.
Warning
Insufficient lamports cause transaction rejection even when the transfer amount is available.
