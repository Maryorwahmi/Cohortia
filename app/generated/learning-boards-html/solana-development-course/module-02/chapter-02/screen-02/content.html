Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Learning Objectives
01
Deconstruct a Transaction
Identify signatures, message header, account keys, recent blockhash, and instructions.
Skill: Read and explain a transaction’s fields. Why it matters: you will avoid replay attacks and invalid messages. Mnemonic: imagine a sealed envelope with a stamp, sender list, and a note inside.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain Instructions & Programs
Describe how instructions call programs and list account metas and data.
Skill: Map an instruction to a program_id and its accounts. Why it matters: program calls must be authorized and target the right accounts. Visual mnemonic: a program as a function and instruction as its call signature.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Sign & Send Transactions
Understand which keypairs sign and how validators process a signed transaction.
Skill: Sign a transaction locally and broadcast it to devnet. Why it matters: unsigned or incorrectly signed transactions are rejected. Mnemonic: signatures are approval stamps from account owners.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Handle Transaction Fees
Identify lamports, compute fees, and avoid insufficient-funds errors.
Skill: Ensure sender account balance covers both transfer amount and fee. Why it matters: small balances can fail even when sending small amounts. Mnemonic: think of lamports as cents for gas.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
