Transaction — Atomic Bundle
Group related instructions so the runtime executes them all or none to preserve state consistency.
Message & Account Keys
Header, account list, recent blockhash and instructions direct validator verification and parallelization.
Fees in Lamports
Transactions pay validators in lamports; always ensure fees are covered to avoid rejection.
Takeaway

Design transactions thoughtfully: include the right accounts, request the right permissions, leave lamports for fees, and inspect signatures to verify execution.
