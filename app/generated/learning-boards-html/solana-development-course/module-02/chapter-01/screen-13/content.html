Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
Account
A storage object identified by a public key, owned by a program and holding data and lamports.
Owner (Program)
The program assigned as owner is the only actor allowed to modify an account's data.
Rent Exemption
One-time lamport deposit based on account size which ensures the account persists without ongoing rent.
Summary
Design accounts by deciding owner and persistence needs up-front. Secure private keys and make critical accounts rent-exempt to protect your application's state.
