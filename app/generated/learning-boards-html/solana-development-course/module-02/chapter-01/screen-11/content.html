Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Rent & Rent-Exemption
Why rent exists
Rent prevents unlimited on-chain storage by requiring a lamport deposit that corresponds to the account's size. Without sufficient balance, accounts can be reclaimed.
Rent-exempt deposit
If an account holds the required amount for its size, it becomes rent-exempt and will persist indefinitely without recurring charges. SDKs typically compute this amount for you.
Concrete guidance
  • Estimate account size before creating it so you can compute rent-exempt balance.
  • Make critical state accounts rent-exempt to avoid accidental deallocation.
  • When modifying data size, re-check rent-exemption and top up if necessary.
Key Takeaway
Treat rent-exemption as a one-time storage deposit that guarantees persistence. Plan deposits based on account size.
