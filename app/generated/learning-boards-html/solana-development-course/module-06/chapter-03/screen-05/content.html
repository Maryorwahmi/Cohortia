Definition

WebSocket subscriptions

Subscriptions allow the RPC to push messages to your client when specific accounts or logs change. They are more efficient and immediate than periodic polling.

API: connection.onAccountChange(publicKey, callback, commitment)
Example: subscribe to a counter PDA and update state when the account changes.
Misconception: subscriptions alone are enough. Always fetch initial state before relying on updates.
Subscription flow
  1. Initial fetch: program.account.counter.fetch(pda) to populate UI.
  2. Set up listener: connection.onAccountChange(pda, callback, 'confirmed').
  3. Deserialize: use program.coder.accounts.decode to read account data.
  4. Cleanup: connection.removeAccountChangeListener(id) on unmount.
Key Takeaway

Subscriptions provide timely updates. Use an initial fetch, then subscribe, and always remove the listener on cleanup.
