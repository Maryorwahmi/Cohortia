Learning Objectives
Skills to deploy, secure, and release a Solana DApp.
01 Deploy to Clusters
Deploy Anchor programs to devnet, testnet, and mainnet-beta and manage cluster configuration. Practically, you will use anchor deploy with the provider.cluster flag and fund the deployer for mainnet.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Key Management
Protect deployer keys, prefer hardware wallets or encrypted vaults, and never commit keypairs to Git. Learn immediate steps to rotate keys and recover from leaks.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Security Patterns
Adopt ownership checks, signer validations, checked arithmetic, and conservative mutability to reduce attack surface.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 Release Readiness
Plan audits, monitoring, documentation, and emergency upgrade procedures before mainnet-beta rollout to protect users and maintain trust.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
