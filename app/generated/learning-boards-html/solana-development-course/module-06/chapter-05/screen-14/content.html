What to Remember

Additional Essentials

Authority management, audits, monitoring, and an emergency playbook complete your readiness checklist.
Upgrade Authority
Keep upgrade keys in hardware wallets or multi-sig and rotate if compromise is suspected.
Auditing
Use professional auditors and a public bug bounty to catch issues you may miss.
Monitoring & Ops
Alert on errors, spikes, and balance changes. Rehearse an emergency playbook with clear roles and messages.
Final Takeaway

Authority, audit, and operational readiness are as important as code correctness. Prepare people and processes as well as technical fixes.
