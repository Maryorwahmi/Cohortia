What to Remember

Key Concepts

Three compact reminders to carry into every deployment.
Key Management
Keep deployer and upgrade keys secure, prefer hardware wallets or multi-sig and never commit keys to Git.
CPI & State Order
Finalize and serialize state before CPIs to avoid reentrancy-like exploits and inconsistent invariants.
Safe Arithmetic
Use checked_add, checked_sub, and explicit errors. Avoid silent wrapping in release builds.
Summary Takeaway

Secure keys, verify ownership and signer authority, and make arithmetic explicit. These three guardrails prevent the most damaging deployment mistakes.
