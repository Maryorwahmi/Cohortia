Compare Blockchain Performance Metrics
Collect recent TPS and fee data for Solana, Ethereum, and Bitcoin and reflect on suitability for high-volume apps.
Instructions: look up recent average TPS and average transaction fees for each chain, record them in a simple table, and answer the reflection prompt: which chain would you choose for a high-volume, low-value payment system and why? Focus on patterns: throughput capability and fee stability under load are key factors.
