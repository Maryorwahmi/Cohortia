Where Solana excels
High-throughput, low-fee, low-latency applications that can benefit from parallel execution.
High-frequency DeFi: rapid trades and order updates need low latency and tiny fees to be practical.
Real-time gaming: many frequent state updates per player require fast confirmation and low cost.
NFT marketplaces: high-volume minting and transfers benefit from low costs and high throughput.
Payments: global, low-value transfers are economical when fees are fractions of a cent.
Design tip
Structure state to minimize overlapping writes so Sealevel can maximize parallelism.
Key Takeaway

Match Solana to workloads that need many fast, low-cost operations and design programs to avoid shared account contention.
