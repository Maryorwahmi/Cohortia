Gulf Stream
A mempool-less forwarding protocol that routes transactions to validators ahead of finalization.
Gulf Stream reduces the time transactions spend waiting by proactively pushing them toward the validators expected to process them. This decreases latency and helps prevent bottlenecks that would otherwise occur in a global mempool model.
Concrete example: A client transaction is forwarded to the anticipated validator, allowing speculative scheduling and earlier execution when possible.
Misconception: Forwarding speeds delivery but does not replace PoH’s role in establishing canonical order for finality.
Why this helps
Less queuing and earlier routing reduce latency spikes and keep fees low by avoiding concentrated bursts waiting in a mempool.
Key Takeaway

Routing transactions early shortens confirmation time and pairs with PoH and Sealevel to improve end-to-end throughput.
