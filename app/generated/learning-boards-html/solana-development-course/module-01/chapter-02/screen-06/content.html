Tower BFT
A PoH-aware consensus protocol that reaches faster finality.
Tower BFT builds on PBFT ideas but measures voting and timeouts against the PoH clock. Because validators share a verifiable order, they need fewer message rounds to detect and resolve conflicts. This reduces the latency to finality compared to consensus layers that must establish both order and agreement through heavier messaging.
Concrete example: Validators vote using PoH positions. If a conflicting block appears, Tower BFT refers to the PoH order to choose which view to finalize more quickly.
Misconception: Tower BFT does not replace PoH. PoH is the ordered timeline; Tower BFT is the agreement layer that uses that timeline to speed consensus.
Impact for developers
Faster finality means your transactions confirm quickly and applications can proceed on reliable state sooner, improving user experience for time-sensitive apps.
Key Takeaway

Tower BFT leverages PoH’s ordering to reduce coordination and reach ledger finality faster than protocols that must separately establish timestamps.
