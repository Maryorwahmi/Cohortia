Transactions per second and cost
Throughput and fees are outcomes of architectural choices about ordering, execution, and system flow.
Older chains often process around 15 to 30 transactions per second and can experience high fees during congestion. Solana aims to support thousands and often reaches tens of thousands of transactions per second because PoH, Sealevel, and system optimizations lower coordination and allow parallelism. Fees on Solana are typically fractions of a cent for many operations, making it viable for frequent, small-value transactions.
Comparison snapshot
Use TPS and fee behavior to match platform to workload. High TPS and low fees favor streaming, gaming, and high-frequency markets.
Key Takeaway

Measure fit by both throughput and transaction semantics: Solana’s stack is optimized to lower per-transaction cost while supporting high concurrency.
