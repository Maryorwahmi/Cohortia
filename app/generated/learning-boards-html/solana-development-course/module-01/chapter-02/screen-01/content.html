Why Solana? Performance by design
An orientation to the architectural choices that deliver high throughput and low cost.

Solana tackles the blockchain trilemma by introducing a verifiable sequence of events called Proof of History and layering a set of system optimizations. This reduces coordination costs between validators and unlocks parallel execution and pipelined processing, enabling far greater transactions per second with very low fees.
