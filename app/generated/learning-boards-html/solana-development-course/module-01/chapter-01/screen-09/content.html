Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Consensus Mechanisms: PoW vs PoS
Proof of Work
Miners expend computational effort to solve puzzles; the first to solve proposes the next block. Security comes from the cost of computation; an attacker must outcompute honest miners.
Proof of Stake
Validators stake currency as collateral and are chosen to create blocks based on stake and other selection rules. Misbehavior risks losing stake, aligning incentives economically.
Misconception check: Proof of Stake is not simply "pay to win"; selection and slashing rules are designed to maintain security while avoiding large energy costs.
Comparison card
Cost model
PoW: computational energy. PoS: economic stake.
Attack resistance
Both make attacks costly, but by different resource models—compute vs stake.
Environmental impact
PoW typically uses more energy; PoS reduces computational waste by using stake instead.
Key Takeaway

Consensus is the mechanism that replaces central control with economic or computational cost, keeping the ledger honest.
