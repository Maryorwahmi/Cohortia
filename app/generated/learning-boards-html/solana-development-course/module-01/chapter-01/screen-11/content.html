Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Transparency and Pseudonymity
What transparency gives you
Public auditability of transactions, enabling anyone to inspect and verify the ledger's integrity and flows of assets.
Pseudonymity explained
Addresses are identifiers, not names. If an address is linked to a person via outside data, the ledger becomes a forensic trail.
Misconception check: visibility does not equal identity. Transparency helps audit but may expose activity if off-chain identifiers connect addresses to people.
Concrete scenario
A public grant ledger enables donors and auditors to confirm spending matches stated objectives. Donor names remain off-chain, keeping a level of privacy while preserving public accountability.
Key Takeaway

Transparency enables auditability; pseudonymity protects casual identity but not guaranteed anonymity when combined with off-chain information.
