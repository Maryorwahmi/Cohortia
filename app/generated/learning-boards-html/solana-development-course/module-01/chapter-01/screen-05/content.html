Role of Nodes
  • Store copies of the ledger for redundancy and auditability.
  • Broadcast and relay transactions across the peer-to-peer network.
  • Validate incoming transactions against protocol rules.
Node types
Full nodes keep entire history; light clients query instead of storing everything; validators or miners propose and confirm blocks depending on the consensus design.
Consider: running a full node contributes to decentralization but requires more resources than a light client. Which trade-offs matter for your project?
Propagation & Validation Flow
Step 1
A user broadcasts a transaction to nearby peers.
Step 2
Nodes relay the transaction, increasing its visibility across the network.
Step 3
Validators check rules and, once consensus selects the block, nodes append it to their copy.
Key Takeaway

Nodes both protect and publish the ledger. Different node roles balance resource requirements with contribution to network security.
