Quick Mnemonics
Define
Ledger = Shared Log
Say: "A blockchain is a distributed ledger of linked blocks."
Pillars
D + I + T
Decentralization, Immutability, Transparency — the three guards of integrity.
Hash & Consensus
Fingerprint + Vote
Hash = fingerprint of data. Consensus = network agreement on the next block.
Misconceptions
Not Anonymous, Not Magical
Pseudonymity is not the same as privacy; immutability is technical, not legal.
