Comparison › init vs mut vs signer
init

Creates a new account. Requires payer to fund creation. Use when you need a fresh account owned by the program.

Example: #[account(init, payer = user, space = 8 + 8)]
mut

Marks that the instruction will modify account data. Without mut, writes fail. Use for any account you intend to update.

Example: #[account(mut)] pub counter_account
signer

Requires the account to sign the transaction. Use for authorizing payments or proving control of a keypair.

Example: pub user: Signer<'info>
Common pitfall
Assuming init makes the created account a signer during creation — it does not. The payer must be a signer to authorize lamport spending.
