Key Concepts › What to remember
Solana Account
Address, owner, lamports, and data bytes — the single storage primitive for program state.
Program-Derived Address (PDA)
Deterministic address derived from seeds and program ID; no private key, used for program ownership.
Anchor #[account]
Maps Rust structs to bytes, adds an 8‑byte discriminator, and applies constraints like init and seeds.
Summary Takeaway

Design account ownership, seed choices, and space intentionally; Anchor enforces contracts but does not remove the need for careful planning.
