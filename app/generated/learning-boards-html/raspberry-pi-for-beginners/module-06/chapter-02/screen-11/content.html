Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › QoS & Reliability
Conceptual comparison
Low assurance
Lower overhead, occasional loss acceptable, suitable for frequent telemetry.
Higher assurance
Stronger delivery guarantees, potential duplicates and extra traffic, suitable for critical commands.
Practical guidance
  • Use lightweight delivery for sensor streams where samples update frequently.
  • Consider stronger delivery or acknowledgement for commands that must not be missed.
  • Plan for duplicate handling if you use stronger delivery settings.
Key Takeaway

Match QoS and delivery patterns to the message type: telemetry tolerates loss, commands may require stronger assurances and duplicate handling.
