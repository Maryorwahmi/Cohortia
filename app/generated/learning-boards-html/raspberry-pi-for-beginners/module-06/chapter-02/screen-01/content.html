IoT Messaging with MQTT

Use Mosquitto on a Raspberry Pi, publish sensor data, and subscribe to commands to control GPIO devices using paho‑mqtt in Python.

What you'll understand
Broker role, topic naming, publishing from Python, subscribing to control GPIO.
Why it matters
MQTT is resilient to unreliable networks and keeps bandwidth low—perfect for field sensors on a Pi.
