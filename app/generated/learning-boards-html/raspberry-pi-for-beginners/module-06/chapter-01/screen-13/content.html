Practical reminders

Three actionable items to guide your next practice session.

Virtual environments
Isolate project packages to avoid version conflicts and to make development reproducible.
Network testing
Bind to 0.0.0.0 and use the Pi’s IP and port to confirm access from other devices on your Wi‑Fi.
Interaction choices
Use simple GET route triggers for prototypes; add POST/JS when you need a responsive UI.
Final task

Build the three-button Flask app: turn LED on, turn LED off, and read sensor. Test it from your phone to confirm end-to-end behavior.
