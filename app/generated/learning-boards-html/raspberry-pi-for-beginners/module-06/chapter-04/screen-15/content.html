Module 7 › Chapter 6.4 › What to Remember — Part 2
Keep Updated
Use apt update and apt upgrade regularly to apply security fixes to packages and the OS.
Disable Unused Services
Stop and remove packages and units you do not need to reduce attack surface and maintenance effort.
Least Privilege & HTTPS
Run services with minimal permissions and use TLS for web interfaces to protect data in transit.
Final takeaway

Security is an ongoing practice: apply the basics now, then maintain them with routine updates and attention to services and privileges.
