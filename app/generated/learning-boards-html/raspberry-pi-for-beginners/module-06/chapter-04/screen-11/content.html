Module 7 › Chapter 6.4 › Disable unnecessary services
Why minimize services?
Each service can contain bugs and require updates. Removing what you do not use reduces the number of components to secure and monitor.
Example
Choose Raspberry Pi OS Lite for headless projects to avoid desktop packages and services you do not need.
How to disable
Use systemctl stop and systemctl disable for services, and remove packages with apt when not needed. Always test functionality after disabling.
Common mistake
Disabling services without checking dependencies. Verify that removing one service does not break another required by your project.
