When to use PWM and how to be cautious

PWM is ideal for dimming LEDs, motor speed control, and servo positioning. Use gpiozero to simplify the work; switch to hardware PWM pins if you need stable timing under load.
Do
Use resistors with LEDs, test step sizes, and try gpiozero helpers first.
Don't
Don't power motors from the Pi without checking current limits; avoid skipping resistors.
Final takeaway

PWM gives you fine control over devices with digital pins. Tune duty, frequency, and pin choice to match the device's needs.
