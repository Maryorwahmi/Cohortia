Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Assessment idea and quick check
Assessment checklist
Short tasks that confirm concept understanding and practical ability.
Question
If a PWM signal has a duty cycle of 25%, what percent of full power does the load receive on average?
Answer: 25%
Practical task
Run the three-step PWMLED script and verify LED brightness at values 0, 0.5 and 1.
Tune exercise
Double the fade speed by halving the sleep delay or increasing step size; observe trade-offs in smoothness.
Servo check
Use Servo.min, Servo.mid and Servo.max to demonstrate pulse-width positioning and explain pulse timing in your own words.
Key Takeaway

Practical short tasks are the fastest way to verify understanding: compute, run, tune, and explain.
