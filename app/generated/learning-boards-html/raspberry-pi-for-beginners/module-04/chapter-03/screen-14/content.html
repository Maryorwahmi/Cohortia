Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Duty cycle
Percentage of ON time that sets average power to a load.
gpiozero helpers
PWMLED and Servo provide simple interfaces for fading and positioning.
Hardware PWM pins
GPIO 12, 13, 18, 19 provide stable, low-jitter PWM for precision tasks.
Summary takeaway

Use PWM to control power over time. Start with gpiozero, choose hardware PWM when you need precision, and always protect your hardware with correct wiring.
