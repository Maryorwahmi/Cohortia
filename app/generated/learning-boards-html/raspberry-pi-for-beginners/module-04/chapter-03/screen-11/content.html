Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Common mistakes and troubleshooting
Missing resistor
Always include a current-limiting resistor with LEDs to avoid damage.
Visible stepping
Use smaller step increments and ~20ms delays for smooth fades.
CPU-induced jitter
If signals wobble under load, consider hardware PWM or reduce background tasks.
Servo jitter
Check power supply, pulse consistency, and try hardware PWM if motion is unstable.
Safety note

Avoid powering motors or servos directly from the Pi's 3.3V rail without verifying current limits. Use external power when needed and common ground.

Key Takeaway

Isolate variables, test components individually, and use hardware PWM for time-critical signals.
