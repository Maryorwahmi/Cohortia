Concept
Duty cycle
Percentage of time a pin is HIGH within each cycle. Controls average power without changing voltage.
Concept
Servo pulses
Pulse width, not duty percent, positions a servo. Typical pulses are around one to two milliseconds every 20 milliseconds.
