Key Concepts — Part 2

Verify power & ground

Confirm 3.3V at VREF and tied grounds to ensure meaningful ADC conversions.

Use gpiozero

The MCP3008 class returns normalized values; multiply by 3.3 to get volts or by 1023 to get raw counts.

Calibrate sensors

Record min and max ADC readings under known conditions and map values to a percentage scale.

Final tip

Before running projects, do a three-point check: power rails, SPI enablement, and a terminal demo that shows live ADC values while moving the sensor.
