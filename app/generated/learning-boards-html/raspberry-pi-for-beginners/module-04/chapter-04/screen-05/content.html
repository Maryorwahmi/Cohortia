ADC Math

Resolution and VREF

Formula

Voltage = (raw_value / 1023) × VREF. For a 10-bit ADC, divide by 1023, not 1024, for correct full-scale mapping.

Step size

With VREF = 3.3V, step = 3.3 / 1023 ≈ 0.00322 V, or about 3.2 millivolts per step.

Worked example
If raw = 512, voltage ≈ 512 / 1023 × 3.3 ≈ 1.65 volts. This is the midpoint of the reference range and a useful sanity check when powering a potentiometer from 3.3V.
Why stability matters
A noisy or drifting VREF results in incorrect voltages even with high resolution.
Misconception
Higher bits don't fix a poor sensor or noisy wiring; they only increase numerical precision.
Check
Measure VREF with a multimeter before trusting conversions.
Visual
A vertical bar divided into 1024 steps labeled 0 and 1023 with VREF at top to visualize discrete steps across the voltage range.
Key Takeaway

Compute volts with the ADC formula and always verify your VREF and grounding for accurate readings.
