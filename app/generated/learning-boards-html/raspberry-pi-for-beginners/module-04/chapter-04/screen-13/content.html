Key Concepts — Part 1

Analog vs Digital

Analog sensors output continuous voltages; the Pi needs an ADC to read those values numerically.

ADC Resolution & VREF

Resolution sets steps, VREF sets scale; volts = raw / 1023 × VREF for a 10-bit ADC.

MCP3008 + SPI

MCP3008 has eight channels and communicates via MOSI, MISO, SCLK, and CS; tie VREF to 3.3V when using a Pi.

Takeaway

Keep these three concepts in mind as you wire, code, and calibrate to prevent common errors and ensure accurate readings.
