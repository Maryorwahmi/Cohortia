Wiring

MCP3008 → Raspberry Pi mapping

  • MCP3008 VDD → Pi 3.3V
  • MCP3008 VREF → Pi 3.3V
  • MCP3008 AGND → Pi GND
  • MCP3008 DGND → Pi GND
  • MCP3008 CLK → Pi GPIO 11 (SCLK)
  • MCP3008 DOUT → Pi GPIO 9 (MISO)
  • MCP3008 DIN → Pi GPIO 10 (MOSI)
  • MCP3008 CS → Pi GPIO 8 (CE0)
After wiring, check VREF with a multimeter and ensure grounds are common. Tight breadboard connections and a stable power rail reduce noise and measurement errors.
Common mistakes
  • Using 5V for VREF while Pi runs at 3.3V.
  • Not tying analog and digital grounds together.
  • Loose breadboard or jumper wires causing intermittent reads.
Verification steps
  1. Power the Pi and measure the 3.3V rail at VREF.
  2. Confirm all grounds are common.
  3. Enable SPI in raspi-config and reboot.
  4. Run a quick read script and watch for stable values.
Key Takeaway

Correct wiring and verification eliminate the most common causes of bad ADC readings: incorrect VREF, missing grounds, and loose connections.
