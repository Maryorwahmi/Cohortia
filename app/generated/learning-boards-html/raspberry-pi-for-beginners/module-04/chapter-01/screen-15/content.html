What to Remember
Voltage
Pi uses 3.3V logic; avoid exposing GPIO to 5V.
Resistors
Always include current-limiting resistors for LEDs and compute safely.
Drivers
Use transistors or motor drivers for high-current devices and separate supplies with common ground.
Parting thought
A brief safety routine prevents most hardware failures and keeps your Raspberry Pi projects progressing confidently.
