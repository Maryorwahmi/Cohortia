3.3 volt logic and interfacing options
Understand the trade-offs between voltage dividers and level shifters.
Voltage divider
Two resistors reduce voltage for simple, unidirectional signals. Watch out for input impedance and speed limits.
Level shifter
Designed for reliable bidirectional communication and predictable thresholds; safer for many use cases.
When to use divider
Simple sensor outputs at low speed and one direction only.
When to use shifter
Bidirectional buses, higher speed communications, or unknown input impedance.
Quick safety rule
If unsure, pick a level shifter or choose a three point three volt device to avoid risk.
