Learning Objectives
Skills and knowledge to practice in the lab.
01
Explain GPIO behavior
Describe how pins act as inputs or outputs, what HIGH and LOW mean at 3.3 volts, and why this matters for circuit design.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use numbering & gpiozero conventions
Choose BOARD or BCM numbering consistently and write code that maps to the physical pins you wire on the breadboard.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Build LED circuits with resistors
Assemble an LED in series with a current-limiting resistor on a breadboard and verify orientation and brightness safely.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Respect voltage and current limits
Calculate resistor values with Ohm's Law, avoid applying 5 volt signals directly, and use drivers for high current demands.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
