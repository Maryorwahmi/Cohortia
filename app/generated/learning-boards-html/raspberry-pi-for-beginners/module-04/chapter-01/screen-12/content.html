Safe wiring practices and powering up
A practical checklist to avoid common hazards when working with GPIO.
Pre-power checklist
  • Power off before wiring.
  • Verify pin numbering and connections.
  • Confirm resistor is in series with LED.
Wiring hygiene
Use short, tidy jumpers, avoid stray exposed wires, and use shrouded connectors for 5V lines.
Common hazard
Accidentally connecting 5V to a GPIO pin or hot-plugging devices can permanently damage the Pi.
Pro Tip

Keep a printed pinout and wiring checklist at your bench to reduce human error when wiring and testing.
