Learning Objectives — Lab Focus
Concrete skills to practice in your breadboard session.
LED Orientation
Identify anode (long leg) and cathode (short leg) and place LED so current flows from pin to ground.
Resistor Bands
Read color bands to pick a common 220 ohm resistor as a safe, readily available choice for indicator LEDs.
Power Safety
Always power off before connecting wires, verify wiring twice, and never expose GPIO to voltages above 3.3 volts.
