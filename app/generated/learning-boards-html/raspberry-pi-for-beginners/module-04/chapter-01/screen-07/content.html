Wiring the LED and resistor
A safe, repeatable assembly flow for your breadboard session.
Step-by-step
  1. Power off the Pi.
  2. Confirm numbering: BOARD or BCM and locate chosen pin.
  3. Place LED with legs in separate rows; long leg toward GPIO.
  4. Place resistor in series with LED in the same current path.
  5. Connect jumper from GPIO to LED/resistor row and jumper from cathode to ground.
  6. Inspect wiring and then power on to test.
Verification
  • Measure GPIO voltage when HIGH; expect about 3.3 volts.
  • Measure voltage across resistor to compute actual current.
  • If values differ, power down and re-check connections and resistor value.
Key Takeaway

Follow the checklist and verify voltages before and after powering to avoid damage.
