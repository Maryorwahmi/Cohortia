GPIO current limits and external drivers
Understand when a transistor or driver is required to protect the Pi.
Per-pin and total limits
Pins can source or sink around 16 mA each; keep total GPIO current below about 50 mA.
When to use drivers
Use drivers or transistors for motors, many LEDs, or any load that needs more current than a pin can safely provide.
NPN transistor
A small GPIO current controls a larger current from an external supply to the load.
Motor driver module
Provides direction control and protects against back-emf from motors.
Practical rule
If the device draws more than the per-pin safe current, use an external driver and separate power source with a common ground.
