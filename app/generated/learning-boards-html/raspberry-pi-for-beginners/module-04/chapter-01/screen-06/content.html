Resistor selection using Ohm's Law
A step-by-step process to compute a safe series resistor for an LED.
Steps
  1. Identify supply voltage: 3.3 volts for GPIO.
  2. Find LED forward voltage, for red ~1.8 volts.
  3. Compute resistor voltage: supply minus LED drop.
  4. Choose desired current, e.g., 10 mA.
  5. Compute R = V divided by I and pick the nearest higher standard resistor.
Worked example
3.3 minus 1.8 is 1.5 volts. At 10 milliamps, R = 1.5 divided by 0.01 equals 150 ohms. Use 220 ohms for a safer current of 6.8 milliamps.
Tip
If unsure, pick a larger resistor to reduce current and protect the GPIO pin and LED.
Key Takeaway

Use the supply minus LED forward voltage as the resistor voltage, then divide by desired current to find R.
