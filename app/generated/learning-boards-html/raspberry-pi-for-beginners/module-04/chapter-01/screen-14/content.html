Key Concepts
GPIO modes
Pins are inputs or outputs at 3.3 volts; configure modes consciously.
Resistor & Ohm's Law
R equals voltage across resistor divided by desired current; choose a safe standard value.
Safety limits
Avoid 5V on GPIO, keep per-pin and total currents within safe ranges, and use drivers when needed.
Takeaway
A careful wiring routine plus proper component selection protects your Pi and extends the range of projects you can build.
