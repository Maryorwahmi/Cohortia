Build and measure: LED on GPIO 17
A guided lab: assemble the circuit, calculate current, and verify with a multimeter.
Step checklist
  1. Power off and locate GPIO 17 using BCM numbering.
  2. Place LED and 220 ohm resistor in series between GPIO 17 and ground.
  3. Power on and set GPIO 17 HIGH.
  4. Measure voltage across resistor; I equals V divided by R.
  5. Compare measured current with calculated expected current.
Expected calculation
With 3.3 V supply and 1.8 V LED drop, resistor sees 1.5 V. At 220 ohms, expected current ≈ 6.8 mA.
Key Takeaway

Measure and compare; real components vary and measurement confirms safe operation.
