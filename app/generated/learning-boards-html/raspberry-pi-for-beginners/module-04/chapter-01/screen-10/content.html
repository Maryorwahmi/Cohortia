Switching higher currents with an NPN transistor
Let the transistor carry the heavy current; the GPIO only provides the control current.
Wiring pattern
  • Emitter to ground.
  • Collector to load; load to external positive supply.
  • GPIO to base through a base resistor.
  • Share common ground between Pi and external supply.
Protection
Add a flyback diode across inductive loads and a base resistor to limit GPIO current into the transistor base.
Key Takeaway

Transistors let your GPIO control larger currents safely; always include proper resistors, diodes, and a common ground.
