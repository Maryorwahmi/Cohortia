Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Enable & Detect
Enable I2C in raspi-config and use i2cdetect to find display addresses like 0x27 or 0x3C.
Choose the right display
Character LCDs for text; SSD1306 OLEDs for text plus small graphics like bar graphs.
Format & throttle
Limit decimals, clear or pad lines, and update only when needed to avoid flicker and artifacts.
Final takeaway

Combine correct wiring, detection, library selection, and formatting strategy to build reliable, readable display interfaces.
