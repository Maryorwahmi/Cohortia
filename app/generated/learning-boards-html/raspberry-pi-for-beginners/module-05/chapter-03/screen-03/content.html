Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Objectives — Practical
Hands-on activity
Connect a DHT22 and display temperature and humidity on a 16x2 LCD. Update every two seconds, then swap to an OLED and render a humidity bar graph.
Milestone: detect device address with i2cdetect and display formatted temp with two decimal places.
Milestone: render a simple horizontal bar to show humidity as a percent on the OLED.
Formatting discipline
Use f-strings to limit decimals and clear old text before writing new text to avoid leftover characters on character LCDs.
Key Takeaway

Formatting and controlled updates make displays readable and stable in real projects.
