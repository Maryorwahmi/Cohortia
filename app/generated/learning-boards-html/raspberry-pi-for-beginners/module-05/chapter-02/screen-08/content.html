Why 17150?
Speed of sound ≈ 343 m/s. Convert to cm/s: 343 * 100 = 34300 cm/s. For a round-trip echo divide by two → 17150. Multiply pulse duration in seconds by 17150 to get distance in centimeters.
Microsecond view
Alternatively, multiply microseconds by 0.034 and divide by two to get centimeters; both approaches are equivalent.
Concrete assessment
If Echo lasts 1500 microseconds, compute distance: 1500 * 0.034 / 2 = 25.5 cm. This reuseable calculation validates your implementation.
When precision matters
Temperature changes the speed of sound. For higher precision, measure air temperature and recalculate the constant accordingly.
Key Takeaway
17150 is a practical constant for converting seconds to centimeters for most classroom and hobby projects.
