Filtering recipe
  1. Add timeouts to every wait for Echo transitions to prevent hangs.
  2. Reject values outside expected bounds and retry sampling when necessary.
  3. Calculate median of three to five samples to remove occasional spikes.
  4. Use averaging when noise is small and latency is acceptable.
Retry strategy
If a measurement fails validation, take two or three more samples and use the median. If repeated failures occur, signal an error rather than forwarding bad data to actuators.
Example: median of three
Read distances three times: [24.7, 25.1, 1000.0]. The median is 25.1 cm; the large spike is ignored, producing a stable result for the application.
Common mistake
Trusting a single raw reading can lead to false alarms or incorrect control decisions in your project.
Key Takeaway
Time out safely, check bounds, and filter readings so your program responds predictably to real-world conditions.
