Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Troubleshooting
Quick diagnostic steps
  1. Power & ground: ensure 5V to VCC and shared GND with the Pi.
  2. Voltage check: confirm Echo through divider ≈3.3V when high.
  3. Pin mapping: verify TRIG and ECHO pins match your code.
  4. Compare gpiozero vs manual timing to reveal hidden timing issues.
Behavioral symptoms
No echo: check wiring and divider. Spikes: shorten wires and filter. Scale errors: check your multiplier and divide-by-two step.
Logging tip
Log raw pulse_duration values at known distances to identify offset vs scatter and guide your fixes.
Key Takeaway
Systematic logging and comparing library vs manual methods reveal whether issues are wiring, timing, or environmental.
