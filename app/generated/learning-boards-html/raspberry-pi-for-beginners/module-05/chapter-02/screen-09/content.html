Practical accuracy factors
  • Minimum reliable distance ~2 cm; very close objects often confuse the return timing.
  • Maximum advertised range ~400 cm, but measurement quality drops at longer distances.
  • Surface type and angle strongly affect reflections and therefore the returned pulse.
  • Electrical noise and long wires can add jitter to start/stop detection.
Practical recommendation
For a parking sensor, restrict operation to the reliable band (e.g., 10–200 cm) and filter readings; this keeps behavior predictable and responsive.
Two use-case comparisons
Parking sensor
Focus on 20–200 cm, fast updates, median filter, buzzer feedback.
Range finding
Accept slower sampling, perform averaging, and validate against known targets for calibration.
Key Takeaway
Match your expected distance band to the sensor's reliable range and use simple filtering to stabilize readings in real projects.
