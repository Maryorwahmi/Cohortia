Definition

Variants and their purpose

Different modules provide trade-offs in optics and features. Choose based on field of view, low-light needs, and whether a swappable lens or autofocus is required.

Standard / Wide-angle
Compact modules for general capture and wider scenes.
Infrared-capable
Works with IR light for night or low-light recording.
Camera Module 3 (autofocus)
Adds autofocus for changing subject distances.
High Quality Camera
Interchangeable lenses for precision imaging experiments.
Concrete example

If you want to monitor a garden bed from a short distance with minimal wiring, a wide-angle standard module covers the area and fits compactly. For nighttime wildlife monitoring with infrared illumination, choose an IR-capable module. For lens experiments like macro or telephoto, choose the High Quality Camera and pick a lens to match the focal length you need.

Key Takeaway

Match the camera’s optical features to your project rather than assuming one module fits all tasks.
