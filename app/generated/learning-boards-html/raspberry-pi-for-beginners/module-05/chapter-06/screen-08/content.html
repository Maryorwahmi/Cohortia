Example

Minimal video recording

from picamera2 import Picamera2

camera = Picamera2()
camera.start_and_record_video("video.mp4", duration=10)
This records a short MP4 clip. Use short clips to check encoding and playback before experimenting with resolution and bitrate settings.
Considerations
  • Video demands more CPU and storage than stills.
  • Monitor file sizes when recording long or high-resolution clips.
  • For continuous recording, design a rotation or streaming approach.
Common mistake

Assuming video settings are identical to stills and not checking storage limits or CPU load for longer captures.
