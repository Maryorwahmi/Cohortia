Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Standard vs continuous-rotation servos
Standard servo
Moves to and holds a specific angle between its mechanical limits. Use when you need accurate positioning and holding torque.
Use case
Pan-tilt camera or robotic arm joint.
Control
Signal maps to angle; servo holds position.
Continuous-rotation servo
Rotates continuously; control input changes speed and direction rather than angle. Use for small-drive wheels and robots.
Use case
Drive wheels at variable speed without separate motor controller.
Control
Signal maps to speed/direction; cannot reliably hold a fixed angle.
Practical note
If you need both continuous motion and precise stopping, combine a continuous motor with an encoder and a control loop, or use a standard servo if holding position is primary.
