Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Servo: precise angle
Use PWM and gpiozero's Servo methods to move and test min, mid, max positions.
DC motor: use a driver
Drivers handle motor current and direction; control speed with PWM and share ground with the Pi.
Relay: safe switching
Relays let you control separate high-voltage circuits. Follow full electrical safety practices when switching mains.
One-sentence summary

Choose the right actuator, use the correct interface and power, tie grounds, and always verify wiring for safe and reliable motion.
