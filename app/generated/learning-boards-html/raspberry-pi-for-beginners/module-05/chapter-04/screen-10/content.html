Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › External power requirements and common ground
Step-by-step power plan
  1. Estimate motor stall and continuous current and select a supply with margin.
  2. Connect motor supply negative to driver ground and Pi ground.
  3. Connect motor supply positive to the driver V+ terminal.
  4. Attach logic pins from Pi to driver, then power the logic and motor supplies in order.
Noise and stability tips
Add decoupling capacitors across motor power to smooth spikes and reduce electrical noise. Secure connections and avoid long thin wires for high-current paths to reduce voltage drops.
Warning

Never connect motor positive directly to Pi power rails if the motor draws significant current; this risks damaging the board and causing instability.
