Concept map
How servo, motor driver, and relay connect to the Pi and power.
Servo
Signal pin to PWM-capable GPIO; power and ground from supply.
Motor driver
Driver takes logic signals from Pi, power from external supply, grounds tied together.
Relay module
Low-voltage control lets the relay switch a separate high-voltage circuit safely when wired correctly.
Key Takeaway

Actuators convert electrical signals into motion. Always use proper drivers and separate power for motors, and respect safety when switching mains with relays.
