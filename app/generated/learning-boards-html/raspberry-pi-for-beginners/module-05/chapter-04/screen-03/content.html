Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Learning Objectives (Detailed)
Servo check
Run a sweep: min → mid → max. Smooth motion and no power dips indicate correct PWM wiring and supply.
DC motor check
Drive forward at low speed, stop, and reverse. No Pi resets implies correct driver and external power plan.
Relay check
Toggle a known low-voltage load first and observe clean switching. Inspect insulation before moving to mains.
Ground & power check
Confirm shared ground between Pi and driver, and that motor supply voltage meets motor rating but does not feed back into logic lines.
Tip
Label wires and disconnect power before changing wiring.
Common mistake
Powering a motor directly from a GPIO pin can damage the Pi and is not a valid control method.
Why it matters
These checks protect your hardware and ensure repeatable behavior when you move from prototypes to projects.
