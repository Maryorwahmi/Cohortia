Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › DC motors and why drivers are needed
Why a driver?
A motor driver provides higher current paths, direction logic, and accepts PWM for speed. It prevents high motor currents from flowing through Raspberry Pi pins.
Driver inputs
Logic pins from the Pi control direction and enable lines. PWM on the speed input controls motor velocity.
Motor power
Motor power comes from an external supply connected to the driver’s motor V+ terminal; size the supply for stall and continuous current.
Concrete example
Using an L298N: connect Pi GPIOs to the driver logic inputs, motor supply to the L298N VCC, tie the grounds, and use the enable pin with PWM to adjust speed. Then test forward and backward directions at low speed first.
Misconception to avoid
A small DC motor connected briefly might appear fine but can draw high current when stalled. Size the driver and power supply for peak draws and test under load cautiously.
