Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Relay modules and safe switching
How a relay works
A low-voltage signal energizes a coil or optocoupler to change the relay contacts and connect or disconnect the external circuit. The Pi controls the coil via a driver or module input.
Module features
Look for optocouplers and flyback diodes on microcontroller modules to protect the Pi from spikes.
Safe-practice checklist
  • Only work on mains wiring if trained and de-energized.
  • Secure terminals, use proper insulation, and mount the relay in an enclosure.
  • Test switching with low-voltage loads before applying mains.
Safety reminder
Relays switch the circuit but do not remove the hazard of the switched voltage. Treat switched circuits with full electrical safety practices.
