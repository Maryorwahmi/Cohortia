Practical Notes
  • Use Adafruit DHT or gpiozero DHT device for timed reads.
  • Return values may be None on timing failures; do not unwrap without checks.
  • Keep a short delay between attempts to avoid bus contention.
Common Mistake

Calling a timing-sensitive read from multiple threads or processes without coordination can cause failed reads or corrupted results.
