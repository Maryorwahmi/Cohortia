Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › MCP3008 Flow
Process

Read and calibrate an LDR via MCP3008

Step 1 — Wiring
LDR and 10k resistor between 3.3V and GND. Junction to MCP3008 channel. Connect SPI pins and common ground.
Step 2 — Enable SPI and libraries
Enable SPI in Raspberry Pi OS and install an MCP3008 helper or use spidev to read channels from Python.
Step 3 — Record baselines
Capture raw ADC values in dark and bright conditions to compute min and max for mapping.
Step 4 — Map to percent
percent = (raw - min) / (max - min) * 100, then clamp to zero and 100.
Terminal example
ADC read channel 0: 28 ADC read channel 0: 990 Mapped percent: 97%
Troubleshooting

If readings are stuck at zero or full scale, check wiring to MCP3008 and ensure SPI is enabled and the channel is correct.
