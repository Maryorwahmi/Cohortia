Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › LDR & ADC
Definition

LDR with voltage divider

Create a divider using the LDR and a fixed 10k resistor, then read the junction with an MCP3008 channel to obtain a usable numeric value.

Why the ADC is needed

The Pi's GPIO pins are digital only. The MCP3008 converts analog voltage into a digital count the Pi can read over SPI.

Key Takeaway

Record dark and bright ADC baselines, then map raw counts into a percentage for consistent readings across setups.

Concrete Example
ADC raw values: 20 in darkness, 1020 in bright light. Mapped percent = (raw - min) / (max - min) * 100
Common misconception

Assuming raw ADC values are directly comparable across different LDRs or resistor choices. Always calibrate your specific circuit and environment.

Practical tip: start with a 10k fixed resistor and test bright and dark baselines before deciding to change resistance for a different reading range.
