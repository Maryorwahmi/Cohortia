Environmental Sensors

Practical wiring, terminal-first examples, and robust error handling for DHT, LDR, and PIR sensors on the Raspberry Pi.

In this lesson

Wire sensors, run terminal examples, and add calibration and retry logic so your scripts tolerate noisy readings.

Hands-on focus

Connect a DHT22 to GPIO four and a PIR to GPIO eighteen, read values, and log them to the console every two seconds with safe checks.

Explore Key Topics
DHT Sensors

Timed single-wire protocol for temperature and humidity; handle timing and retries.

LDR & ADC

Use a voltage divider and MCP3008 to read analog light levels and map to percent.

PIR Motion

Detect motion as a digital event; prefer event-driven handlers over polling.
