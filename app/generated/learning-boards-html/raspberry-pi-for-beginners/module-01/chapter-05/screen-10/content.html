Warning

Voltage and current rules

Protect the Pi by respecting logic voltage and current constraints when wiring external devices.

Voltage rule
GPIO operate at 3.3V logic. Do not apply 5V to GPIO pins; use level shifting when needed.
Current limits
Each GPIO can source/sink ~16 mA. Total current across GPIO pins should remain under ~50 mA.
Concrete example
If an LED requires 20 mA, do not power it directly from a single GPIO. Instead, control the LED using a transistor with the LED powered from 3.3V or 5V supply as appropriate, and share ground with the Pi. For a motor, use a motor driver and external power supply; the GPIO will only provide the control signal, not the motor current.
Common Mistake

Directly powering high current devices from GPIO pins leads to failures. Use drivers and external supplies.
