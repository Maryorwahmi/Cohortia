Module 2 › Chapter 1.5 › I2C, SPI and UART Pins
Core Concept

Hardware bus pins and uses

I2C
Pins 3 & 5 (BCM 2 & 3). Two‑wire bus often used for sensors and small displays. Built‑in pull‑ups present.
SPI
Multiple pins for clock, data and chip selects, commonly used for displays and high‑speed peripherals.
UART
Pins 8 & 10 (BCM 14 & 15). Provide serial transmit and receive for consoles and serial devices.
Concrete example
To connect an I2C temperature sensor, use pins 3 and 5 for data and clock, share ground, and use 3.3V for VCC. For a SPI display use the SPI chip select and clock pins so data moves reliably at higher speeds than software bit‑banging can guarantee.
Tip
If multiple devices share a bus, ensure unique chip selects or addresses to prevent bus conflicts.
Key Takeaway

Choose hardware bus pins for reliable timing and avoid repurposing them when you need the bus functionality.
