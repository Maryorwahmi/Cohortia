Process Flow

Choose pins with a simple checklist

1
Confirm power and ground
Pick a 3.3V or 5V pin as needed and a ground pin; measure with a multimeter to be safe.
2
Select a GPIO or special pin
For simple I/O pick a GPIO like GPIO 17 (physical 11). For I2C or SPI choose the dedicated pins.
3
Check PWM needs
For precise PWM use hardware pins: GPIO 12, 13, 18 or 19. Software PWM is possible on other pins but less precise.
4
Verify numbering and test
Confirm BOARD vs BCM to place wires correctly and test power lines before connecting fragile components.
Example scenario
To read an I2C temperature sensor, choose pins 3 and 5 for SDA and SCL, share ground, and use 3.3V for power.
Misconception
Picking adjacent pins without checking labels can put signals on 5V instead of a GPIO and damage the chip.
