Quick Checklist

Numbering
Confirm BOARD vs BCM to place wires correctly.
Voltages
Measure 3.3V and 5V rails before connecting devices.
Current & drivers
Use level shifters and external drivers for high current or 5V interfacing.
Final takeaway

Simple checks—confirm numbering, measure rails, and use proper drivers—prevent most hardware problems and make projects reliable.
