Learning Objectives

Master practical skills for wiring and understanding the 40‑pin header.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify power, ground, and GPIO pins

Locate 3.3V, 5V and ground pins and distinguish GPIO lines so you connect components to the correct rails. This prevents miswiring and damage.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Distinguish BOARD and BCM numbering

Recognize when a tutorial uses physical pin numbers or Broadcom GPIO numbers, and convert between them reliably.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Recognize I2C, SPI, UART, PWM pins

Know which pins provide hardware support for common buses and PWM so you choose the right lines for sensors and actuators.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply safe voltage and current limits

Follow 3.3V logic rules, use level shifting for 5V devices, and avoid drawing too much current from GPIO pins.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
