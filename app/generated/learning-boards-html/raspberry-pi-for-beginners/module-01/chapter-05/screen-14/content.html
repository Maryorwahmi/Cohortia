Key Concepts

Power pins
Two 5V and two 3.3V pins supply small devices; choose the correct rail for the component.
Ground pins
Eight grounds provide common reference; always share ground with external supplies.
GPIO lines
Twenty‑six configurable pins for inputs and outputs used by code to interact with hardware.
Summary

Remember these categories and the hardware pins for buses and PWM when planning projects.
