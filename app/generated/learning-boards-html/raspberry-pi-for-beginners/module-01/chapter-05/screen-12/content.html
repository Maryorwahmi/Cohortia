Hands‑On Activity

Safe multimeter checklist

1
Power the Pi and set meter
Set your multimeter to DC voltage and power the Pi normally.
2
Find a ground
Place the black probe on a GND pin to establish reference before measuring voltages.
3
Measure rails
Touch the red probe to 3.3V and 5V pins to confirm expected voltages relative to ground.
4
Verify pin mapping
Blink a BCM GPIO in code and observe the corresponding physical pin toggling between 0 and 3.3V.
Safety note

Avoid shorting pins with probes. Use a printed pinout to guide probe placement and work slowly.
