Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Key Concepts
OS Variants
Desktop vs Lite and 32‑bit vs 64‑bit — choose according to GUI needs and hardware capability.
Imager & Preconfig
Use Raspberry Pi Imager to write the image and supply hostname, credentials, Wi‑Fi, and SSH before first boot.
raspi-config & Interfaces
Enable SPI, I2C, UART, camera, VNC, and other settings; many require a reboot to apply.
Quick Takeaway
Preconfigure and verify your installation so the Pi boots ready for headless or GUI workflows, then use raspi-config for hardware interfaces.
