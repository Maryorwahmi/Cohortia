Card & Reader Checklist
  • Choose capacity and endurance appropriate for your project (logs, OS, apps).
  • Use a compatible and reliable USB card reader if necessary.
  • Confirm the system detects the card before writing the image.
  • After writing, allow verification to finish and use safe eject.
Boot Prep
Insert the prepared microSD card into the Raspberry Pi, connect power, and let the first boot complete. If you preconfigured Wi‑Fi and SSH, you can boot headless and connect remotely.
Example
Use a 16GB high‑endurance card for a small server to reduce wear and risk of corruption.
Pitfall
Removing the card before verification finishes can damage the filesystem; always safe eject first.
Key Takeaway

Choose quality cards and complete the write+verify cycle. Physical prep avoids most mysterious boot failures.
