Model Timeline

A compact timeline showing major releases and their practical effects.

Pi 3 family
Introduced broad Wi-Fi and Bluetooth integration, good for IoT and sensor projects that need wireless connectivity.
Pi 4 Model B
Brought desktop-capable features: dual 4K support, higher RAM options up to 8 GB, and USB-C power for better performance and peripherals.
Pi 5
Latest standard form factor with a more powerful CPU, PCIe expansion, fan connector, and higher power needs suitable for media and compute-heavy tasks.
Zero family
Introduced a tiny, low-cost form factor for space- and power-constrained projects; great for battery monitors and hidden cameras.
Concrete example
Media center

A Pi 5 or Pi 4 is appropriate because dual 4K outputs and higher RAM support desktop-class playback and multiple services.

Misconception
Newer isn't always better for small, battery-powered tasks; a Pi Zero can be the correct tool for the job.
Key Takeaway

Use the timeline to map model features to your project's performance, size, and power requirements.
