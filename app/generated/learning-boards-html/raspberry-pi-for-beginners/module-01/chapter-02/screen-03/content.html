Verified Competency Milestones

Work through these practical checks to confirm you can apply model specifications to projects.

Comparison Table
Create a table with CPU, RAM, ports, networking, power input and approximate price for three models.
Example: Pi 5 — Cortex-A76 2.4 GHz, up to 8 GB, dual 4K HDMI, USB 3.0, PCIe, 5V 5A.
Project Recommendations
Assign a model to three sample projects and justify by size, power, and ports.
Example: Portable plant monitor — Pi Zero 2 W for small size and low power.
Board Labeling
Identify SoC, RAM, microSD slot, USB/Ethernet controller, camera and display connectors, and 40-pin GPIO.
Practice: point to each component on a board photo and name its function.
Key Takeaway

Practical checks — table, recommendations, board labeling, power estimate — confirm you can choose the correct Pi for your project.

Quick Power Guide
Estimate 5V 3A for Pi 4 typical use; 5V 5A for Pi 5 under full load or with high-power USB devices and PCIe expansion.
