Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Pi Zero Family
Definition

Pi Zero 2 W and Pi Zero W

Ultra-compact boards with modest CPUs and limited ports. Excellent for projects where enclosure size and battery life are decisive.

Zero 2 W specs
Quad-core Cortex-A53 CPU and 512 MB RAM, built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, single micro-USB for peripherals.
Use cases
Portable plant monitors, motion cameras, and compact environmental loggers.
Concrete example
Battery wildlife camera

Choose Pi Zero 2 W for small size, integrated wireless to offload images, and lower power draw than full-size boards.

Common Mistake
Expecting many full-size peripherals to plug in directly. Plan for adapters or powered hubs when you need extra USB devices.
Key Takeaway

Zero boards are the right choice when enclosure size and battery life beat raw throughput and many built-in ports.
