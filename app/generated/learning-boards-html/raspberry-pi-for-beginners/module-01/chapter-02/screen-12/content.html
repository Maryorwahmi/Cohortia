Assessment Idea

Recommend a model for a constrained project

Write your decision and justification. Use three constraints and reference specific board features in your explanation.

Prompt
A battery-powered wildlife camera must fit in a small waterproof case, run for long periods, and upload images intermittently over Wi‑Fi. Which Raspberry Pi model would you recommend and why? Include the main constraints and explain trade-offs.
Expected answer
Pi Zero 2 W or Pi Zero W due to small size, integrated wireless, and lower power use. Explain why larger boards' power and space needs are disadvantages.
Key Takeaway

A good hardware recommendation explains constraints, matches them to specific board features, and justifies why alternatives are less suitable.
