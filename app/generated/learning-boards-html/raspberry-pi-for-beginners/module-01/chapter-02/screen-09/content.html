Checklist to choose a model

Follow these steps to translate project constraints into a model selection.

1
Assess performance needs
Do you need desktop-class CPU and GPU? If yes, consider Pi 5; if no, smaller models may suffice.
2
Decide on RAM
Multiple services or databases need 4 or 8 GB; headless single-purpose scripts may work with 512 MB to 1 GB.
3
Check connectivity needs
Require wired Gigabit and many USB 3.0 devices? Lean Pi 4 or Pi 5. Need built-in wireless for remote deployment? Pi 3 or Zero 2 W may suffice.
4
Estimate power and size
Battery operation favors Zero; sustained high-load tasks require higher-amperage supplies and possible cooling on Pi 5.
Concrete scenario
Smart mirror

A Pi 4 with higher RAM handles a browser-based dashboard and local services without the Pi 5's higher power needs.

Common Mistake
Focusing only on raw CPU; ports and RAM often determine whether the device can run your full stack.
Key Takeaway

Use a checklist: performance, RAM, connectivity, power, size, cost. Follow the branches to arrive at the suitable model for your project.
