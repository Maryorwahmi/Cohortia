Trade-offs matter
Choose by performance, power, ports and size. The single dominant constraint should guide selection.
GPIO continuity
The 40-pin GPIO header is preserved across modern models, enabling reuse of many accessories.
Power first
Verify power input and current requirements early; Pi 5 needs a 5V 5A supply in full-load scenarios.
Summary

Filter models by your project's most important constraints, confirm GPIO compatibility, and check power needs before finalizing hardware choices.
