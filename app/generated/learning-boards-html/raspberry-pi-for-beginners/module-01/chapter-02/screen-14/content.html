Pi 5
Highest performance, PCIe and dual 4K — plan for a 5V 5A supply and possible cooling.
Pi 4
Balanced choice with up to 8 GB RAM and modern ports — ideal for versatile home projects and light servers.
Pi Zero
Tiny and low-power for battery or space-limited uses like portable monitors or wildlife cameras.
