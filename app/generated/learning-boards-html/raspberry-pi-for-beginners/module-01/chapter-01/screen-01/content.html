What Is Raspberry Pi and Why It Matters

An approachable single-board computer that combines a Linux OS, network and multimedia ports, and hardware I/O to make learning and building affordable and flexible.

In this lesson
Define the Pi and its components. Compare it with Arduino and desktops. Review use cases that show its value.
Why it matters
Because it gives you a full computer plus direct hardware control at low cost, it lowers barriers to hands-on learning and prototyping.
Coming up
Practical comparisons, the SoC and GPIO concepts, and how to choose the right platform for a project.

Key takeaway
The Raspberry Pi combines a full operating system with hardware I/O, making it uniquely suited for learning and connected projects.
