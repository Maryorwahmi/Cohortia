Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Common Use Cases
Education
Used in classrooms to teach programming, networking, and electronics with low cost and hands-on examples.
Prototyping & Home Automation
Quick proof-of-concept builds and home hubs that run automation platforms and manage devices on a local network.
Media, Gaming & Robotics
From media centers and retro emulation to robots that need local compute and GPIO-based motor control.
Edge & IoT
Collect sensor data and run lightweight inference or preprocessing at the edge before sending to the cloud.
Misconception
Avoid thinking use cases are only for hobbyists. Many applications are practical and deployed where cost and size are constraints.
