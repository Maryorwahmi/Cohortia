Platform roles
Pi for OS tasks and networking; microcontrollers for low-power, real-time control.
GPIO limits
Use drivers and relays for motors and high-current devices; GPIO is for logic-level control.
Common applications
Education, prototyping, home automation, media, retro gaming, robotics, and edge computing.
Final takeaway
Use the Pi where its combination of OS, networking, GPIO, and low cost best solves the project constraints.
