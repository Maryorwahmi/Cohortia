Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › How to Choose
Checklist
  1. Do you need a full OS, file storage, or networking services?
  2. Do you require real-time control or ultra-low power?
  3. Is high performance or expandability required?
Concrete mapping
Answering yes to the first implies Pi, yes to the second implies microcontroller, and yes to the third implies desktop.
Example decision
For a camera-based robot that needs image processing and motor control, combine a Pi for vision and a microcontroller for low-level motor timing.
Misconception
Avoid defaulting to the most powerful option. Simpler platforms often meet needs with lower cost and power use.
