Concept

GPIO basics

GPIO pins provide simple digital inputs and outputs that let the Pi interact with sensors and actuators at logic levels.

Read sensors
Drive signals
Use with drivers
Controlled by software
Concrete scenario
A Pi detects motion via a PIR sensor and triggers a camera script to record video and upload a short clip to a server.
Power note
GPIO pins are logic-level only. For motors or high-current devices, use external drivers or relays that the Pi controls with GPIO signals.
Misconception
Do not power motors directly from GPIO. Use the Pi to send control signals and let a driver handle the current and voltage requirements.
