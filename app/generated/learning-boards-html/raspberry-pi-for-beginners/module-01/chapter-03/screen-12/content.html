Symptoms and immediate actions
Match observed behavior to a focused test and fix.
Insufficient power signs
  • Lightning-bolt low-voltage icon
  • Unexpected reboots or freezes
  • USB devices disconnecting under load
  • Slow or failed writes leading to corruption
Overheating signs
  • Sudden performance slowdown during long tasks
  • Thermal throttling indicators in logs or decreased clock speeds
  • Higher fan noise as temperatures climb
Quick test
Swap to a known-good supply and short cable, then remove USB devices to see if stability returns.
When to add cooling
If slowdown occurs under sustained load, add heatsink or fan and re-test performance stability.
Key Takeaway

Symptoms guide tests: reduce load, swap supply/cable, and add cooling to confirm the root cause.
