What is thermal throttling?
Automatic CPU speed reduction to prevent overheating and protect hardware.
Heatsink: passive cooling for the main chip; low cost and effective for short bursts.
Fan / active cooler: recommended for continuous loads and for Pi 5 with its built-in fan connector.
Case choice affects airflow. Some cases have metal fins for passive cooling; others include mounts for fans.
Misconception: light tasks never need cooling — ambient heat or sustained medium loads can still trigger throttling.
Scenario
During a long video encoding task, the CPU temperature rises and the board reduces clock speed, causing the job to take longer. Adding a fan maintains speed and shortens runtime.
Key Takeaway

Prevent throttling with a heatsink for general use, and add an active cooler for sustained or intensive workloads.
