Practice mnemonics and verification
Short memory aids and confirmed milestones to build confidence.
01
Mnemonic: SD, Power, Case, IO

Quick check: Do the parts match the Pi model and planned project before powering up.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Cross-checked parts list with device model.
02
Mnemonic: stores, powers, protects, connects

Use this phrase to explain accessory function when planning a build or ordering parts.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Explain each accessory role to a peer.
03
Mnemonic: 5V and enough amps

Rehearse choosing the supply: match 5 volts and the manufacturer’s recommended current for your model.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Pick a supply rated for your Pi model.
04
Mnemonic: Spot the bolt

Practice recognizing the low-voltage icon and the first step: replace the cable or supply before deeper troubleshooting.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Fix a simulated low-voltage case by swapping the cable.
