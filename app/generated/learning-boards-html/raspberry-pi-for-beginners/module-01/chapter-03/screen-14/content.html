Key Concepts
Remember these to avoid common pitfalls.
microSD class
A1/A2 and adequate capacity keep the OS responsive and reliable.
Official vs third-party power
Prefer supplies rated for 5V and required amps to avoid undervoltage.
Voltage drop
Long or thin cables cause drops and trigger the low-voltage warning; swap the cable first when troubleshooting.
Summary

Focus on storage performance, proper power, and cooling to build a dependable Pi setup.
