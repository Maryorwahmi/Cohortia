Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Power Supplies
Definition & Model Guidelines
Supply must provide stable 5V and enough current for board and attached devices.
Pi 5: recommended 5V 5A supply for heavier setups and official active coolers.
Pi 4: commonly paired with 5V 3A USB-C supplies for general use.
Pi 3: 5V 2.5A micro-USB supply. Pi Zero: 5V 2A usually sufficient.
Checklist & Example
Checklist: choose short cable, rated supply, and avoid weak phone chargers for initial power-up tests.
Symptom to watch: lightning-bolt low-voltage icon; immediate action: swap to the official or a higher-rated supply and use a thicker, shorter cable.
Common Mistake

Assuming the phone charger can supply stable voltage under Pi load. Voltage drop is the usual culprit behind instability.

Key Takeaway

Match 5 volts and model-recommended amps; prefer the official supply and a short cable.
