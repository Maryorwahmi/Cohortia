Make your shopping list
Include essentials, cooling choices, and at least five GPIO items.
Core items
  • Raspberry Pi model
  • A1/A2 microSD, 16–64 GB
  • 5V power supply rated for model (amps per specification)
  • Short, high-quality USB cable
  • Case matching cooling needs
GPIO starter pack
  • Breadboard
  • Jumper wires
  • LEDs and resistors
  • One basic sensor (light or temperature)
  • Optional: camera or touch display for planned projects
Assessment idea
Total the price for two suppliers and note trade-offs between cost and quality.
Tip
Prefer a short cable and rated supply over a cheaper long cable to avoid early problems.
Key Takeaway

Buy purposefully: essentials first, accessories later based on the projects you will build.
