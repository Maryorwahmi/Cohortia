Four-step diagnostic
A repeatable process to isolate power-related faults.
Step 1: Check cable
Replace long or thin cables with a short, thick cable to reduce voltage drop.
Step 2: Swap supply
Use an official or correctly rated supply for your Pi model and test again.
Step 3: Remove USB loads
Disconnect unneeded USB devices or use a powered hub to isolate draw.
Step 4: Inspect SD
Check for file-system errors and re-image if corruption persists.
Example
If the Pi reboots when a USB drive mounts, a weak supply or unpowered hub is likely the cause.
Misconception
Many assume the board is faulty, but cables and supplies are more commonly at fault.
Key Takeaway

Follow the simple steps in order to find the easiest fix and protect your data.
