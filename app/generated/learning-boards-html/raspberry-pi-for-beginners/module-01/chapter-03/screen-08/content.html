Passive vs Active
Compare the two main cooling approaches and their trade-offs.
Passive case
Silent, uses metal or fins for heat dissipation, best for light or intermittent workloads.
Active case
Includes fan or active cooler; recommended for continuous heavy use or Pi models with high thermal output.
Decision checklist
  • Will you run the Pi continuously or intermittently?
  • Do you need access to the GPIO header, camera connector, or ports?
  • Is quiet operation a priority?
  • Does the case match your Pi model and any active cooler connectors?
Key Takeaway

Choose a case that provides the right balance of cooling, access, and build quality for your project.
