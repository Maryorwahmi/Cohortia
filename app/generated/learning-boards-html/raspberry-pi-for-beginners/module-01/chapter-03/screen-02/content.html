Learning Objectives
Skills to select parts and verify safe operation
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
List essential accessories

Identify the microSD, power supply, case, and input devices needed to start. This matters because correct parts prevent common setup failures.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Can enumerate a starter kit for your model.
02
Explain accessory roles

Describe why the microSD holds the OS, why cases protect circuits, and when keyboard and monitor are optional for headless use.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Can justify accessory choices for a project.
03
Explain power requirements

Understand voltage, current, and power ratings and how they influence stability for your Pi model.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Can select a supply rated for 5V and the correct amperage.
04
Spot symptoms and respond

Recognize low-voltage warnings, random reboots, or SD corruption and know immediate fixes like changing the cable or supply.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Diagnose and fix a low-voltage warning.
