Voltage, Current, and Power
A simple relationship you can use when picking a supply.
Definition
Voltage: the required five volts. Current: the maximum amps the Pi and peripherals draw. Power (watts) = volts × amps.
Practical example
5V × 3A = 15W. If peripherals push draw above 3A, voltage may drop and instability can occur.
Misconception
Assuming connector type alone guarantees supply suitability. Always check voltage stability under load and rated amperage.
Checklist
Match 5V and choose a supply rated above expected peak current for a safety margin.
Key Takeaway

Use simple volts × amps arithmetic to ensure your supply can handle peaks and avoid voltage drop.
