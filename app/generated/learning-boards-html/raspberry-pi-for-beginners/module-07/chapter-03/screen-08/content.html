Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › GPIO Wiring
Pin numbering & orientation
Confirm your code's numbering scheme and each component's orientation.
BCM vs Physical
gpiozero uses BCM numbering by default. Always confirm whether your reference diagram uses BCM or board numbers to avoid wrong-pin wiring.
LED orientation & resistor
An LED has an anode and cathode. Use a series resistor and confirm the LED polarity if it doesn't light.
Concrete scenario
You set GPIO 17 high but nothing lights. Check that the physical pin wired corresponds to BCM 17, measure the voltage when toggled, and verify the LED orientation and resistor value. Swap parts to separate wiring from components.
Common mistake
Using board numbering while the code expects BCM, or forgetting the resistor in series with an LED.
