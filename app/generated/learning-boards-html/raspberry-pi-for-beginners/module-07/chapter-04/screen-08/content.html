Module 8 › Chapter 7.4 › Sensor Comparison
Temperature & Humidity
DHT22
Higher accuracy and wider range; good when precise readings matter.
DHT11
Lower cost and simpler, suitable for basic room monitoring where tight precision is not required.
Light sensing
LDR + MCP3008 (ADC)
Inexpensive and flexible but needs extra wiring and conversion in code.
Digital light module
Provides direct digital readings and simpler integration when available.
Practical advice
Match sensor choice to the precision you need and the wiring you are prepared to implement. Test sensors in isolation first to avoid surprises.
