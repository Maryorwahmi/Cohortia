Module 8 › Chapter 7.4 › Reminders
Document everything
README, wiring diagram, backup steps, and demo instructions are required for grading.
Use systemd
Prefer systemd for auto-start and automatic restart behavior; document the service file.
Safety first
Always use current-limiting resistors and appropriate drivers when switching higher-voltage or higher-current devices.
Final Takeaway
A complete capstone combines reliable code, safe wiring, and clear documentation — prepare a short demo that proves each requirement.
