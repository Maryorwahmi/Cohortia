Comparison › Image vs rsync
Full image
Byte-for-byte copy including OS, partitions, and installed packages. Slow to create but simple to restore.
  • Pros: exact replica, simple restore.
  • Cons: large files, not incremental.
  • When: before major changes, migrations, or long-term archival.
Rsync
File-level sync that transfers only changed files. Fast for regular backups and efficient over network links.
  • Pros: incremental, space-efficient, configurable.
  • Cons: not a full system snapshot; requires careful selection of files.
  • When: nightly backups of project folders and data.
Combined approach
Keep periodic full images and daily rsync mirrors. Test restores from both types and log where copies are stored.
