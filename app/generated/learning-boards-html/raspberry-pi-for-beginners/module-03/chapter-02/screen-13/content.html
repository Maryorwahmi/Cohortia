Mini Project: Summary Reporter
A complete workflow to practice the chapter's skills on your Pi.
Step 1: Collect
Append five numeric readings into a list, keeping them as floats for calculations.
Step 2: Compute
Compute average with sum / len, and use min and max to find extremes.
Step 3: Package
Create a dictionary with device id, timestamp, and numeric statistics stored under clear keys.
Step 4: Present
Use an f-string to print a human-friendly summary, rounding only for display. Keep raw numbers in the dictionary for later checks or exports.
Check your work

Does your printed summary include a label and units, and does your dictionary keep numeric values rather than formatted strings?
