Comparison operators
  • Equality: ==
  • Inequality: !=
  • Less than / greater than: <, >
  • Less or equal / greater or equal: <=, >=
Use case
Threshold checks for control logic: turn on devices when values cross limits.
Logical operators
and, or, not combine boolean values. Use parentheses and named boolean variables to keep expressions readable.
Scenario
If temperature > threshold and not maintenance_mode then activate cooling. Breaking conditions into named checks makes the intention clear and easier to test.
Common Mistake

Comparing floats for equality or writing overly complex combined expressions that become hard to debug. Prefer small named checks.
