Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › cd & Navigation
Move through folders
Relative and absolute paths, parent and home shortcuts, and safe navigation practices.
Relative vs absolute
Relative paths are relative to your current directory. Absolute paths begin at root and always point to the same location.
Shortcuts
Two dots move up one level. Tilde returns you to your home directory. These are fast and predictable.
Example sequence
Example navigation (visual only)
alex@raspberrypi:~/projects/demo $ cd ..
alex@raspberrypi:~/projects $ cd ~
alex@raspberrypi:~ $
