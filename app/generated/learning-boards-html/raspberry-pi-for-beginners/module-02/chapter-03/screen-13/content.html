Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
Least privilege
Daily tasks use a normal account; limit administrative use to necessary moments.
Safe user management
Append groups to grant rights, then verify membership and re-login to apply.
Processes and IDs
Identify the exact process by its ID before stopping or restarting it to avoid collateral impact.
Summary: combine safe account practices, precise process identification, and short health checks to keep a Raspberry Pi secure and reliable.
