Users, Processes, and System Monitoring
Secure accounts, inspect running programs, and monitor health on your Pi.

This chapter guides you from creating and limiting accounts, through live process inspection, to managing services and reading resource status. We emphasize safe habits: use a non-administrator account for day-to-day work and elevate only when necessary. You'll practice commands that reveal who is on the system, which programs are running, and whether the device is overheating or low on disk.

Concrete examples and short exercises give immediate feedback, for instance creating a guest account, verifying group membership, watching a process with a live monitor, and restarting a service that fails to start on boot.
