Networking, SSH, and VNC
Make a Pi reachable, transfer files safely, and use remote graphical sessions when you need a desktop.
Why this chapter matters

A networked Pi becomes a server, a remote workstation, or an IoT node. Learning these basics prevents avoidable downtime and security problems.

Real examples you’ll do
