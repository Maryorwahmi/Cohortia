Owner & Group

Every file has an owner and a group that determine default access. Think of ownership as who controls a file and group as a team membership.

Permission Bits

Three sets of permissions for owner, group, and others: read, write, execute. They behave slightly differently for directories.

Commands

Use ls -l to inspect, chmod to change permissions, and chown to change ownership. sudo is required for administrative changes.
