Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
What to Remember
Owner & Group
Who controls and who shares a file.
Permission Bits
r, w, x for owner, group, others.
ls -l
Inspect type, perms, owner, group, size, time, name.
Use these concepts in combination: inspect, plan the minimal change, apply chmod or chown as needed, and verify. These steps form the habit that protects your Pi while letting you share and run code as intended.
