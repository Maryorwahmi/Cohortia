Thin-Client
Minimal client logic, server handles business rules and rendering. Pros: easy deployment and centralized updates. Cons: higher server load and sensitivity to network latency.
Thick-Client
Client carries significant logic and rendering. Pros: responsiveness and offline capability. Cons: complex deployment and potential logic duplication.
Decision Factors
Consider network reliability, device capability, security needs, offline requirements, and deployment cadence when choosing thin or thick clients.
