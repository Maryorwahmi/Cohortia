Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Fat Service
What to Watch For
  • • Large classes with many public methods.
  • • High cyclomatic complexity and low cohesion.
  • • Multiple unrelated responsibilities like validation and persistence mixed together.
Refactor Pattern
Split responsibilities into validator, orchestrator, and repository adapter. Compose them in a thin orchestration layer that coordinates work.
Key Takeaway

Prefer focused services and composition so each unit is small, testable, and replaceable.
