Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Benefits of Serverless
Primary benefits
  • Reduced operational overhead — no server patching or provisioning.
  • Automatic scaling — handle spikes without manual interventions.
  • Pay-per-execution — cost-efficient for variable workloads.
  • Faster time to market — focus on business logic and integrate managed services.
Concrete scenario
A gallery app uses functions to generate thumbnails only on upload. During low traffic the app costs little; when viral sharing occurs functions scale automatically to handle the load.
Misconception
Assuming serverless removes the need for monitoring. In reality, you need specialized tracing and logging for distributed functions.
Pro tip
Track invocation patterns and memory usage early to estimate cost and spot hotspots.
