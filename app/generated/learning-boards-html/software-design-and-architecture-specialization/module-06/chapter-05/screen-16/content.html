Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Processing Unit (PU)
A self-contained node combining business logic, in-memory data, and messaging.
In-Memory Data Grid
Distributed RAM-based store replicated across PUs for fast, local access.
Replication
Copying data across nodes for availability; requires careful consistency management.
Final takeaway

Choose architecture by matching non-functional requirements to pattern strengths: serverless for variable workloads and low ops, SBA for predictable low latency and very high throughput.
