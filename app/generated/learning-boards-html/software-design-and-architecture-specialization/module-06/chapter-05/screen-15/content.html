Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Serverless Architecture
Provider-managed servers allowing developers to focus on code.
Function-as-a-Service
Event-triggered functions that scale automatically and bill per execution.
Backend-as-a-Service
Managed backend services for auth, DB, and storage without hosting servers.
Summary takeaway

Use these core terms to evaluate trade-offs across performance, cost, and operational responsibilities.
