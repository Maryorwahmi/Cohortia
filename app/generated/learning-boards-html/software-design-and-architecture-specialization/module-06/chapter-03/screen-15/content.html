Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
Event-Driven Architecture
Components publish and subscribe to events, enabling loose coupling and asynchronous processes.
Eventual Consistency
Different parts of a distributed system converge over time; design for duplicates and delays.
Microkernel Architecture
A minimal core exposes extension points so independent plugins add or remove functionality safely.
Summary: Choose EDA when you need cross-service loose coupling and scalability; choose Microkernel when you need feature extensibility inside a product. Both require careful design of contracts, observability, and compatibility rules.
