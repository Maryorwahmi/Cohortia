Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Quick Checklist
Events as contracts
Include eventId, type, timestamp, payload, and source so consumers have a reliable contract.
Idempotent consumers
Deduplicate by eventId and use atomic writes to avoid duplicate side effects on retries.
Minimal core & plugin safety
Provide a stable API, manifest, sandboxing, and version checks to protect the core.
Use this checklist in design reviews: state your chosen pattern, list the contracts and guarantees you need, and document failure and compatibility strategies before implementation.
