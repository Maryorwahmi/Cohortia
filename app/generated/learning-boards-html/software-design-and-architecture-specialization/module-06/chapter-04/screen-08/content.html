How a Saga works
Split a cross-service workflow into local transactions. On failure, execute compensating transactions to revert effects.
Example sequence
  1. Order Service creates an order.
  2. Payment Service attempts charge.
  3. Inventory Service reserves stock.
  4. If payment fails, compensating actions cancel order and release stock.
Styles
Choreography (event-driven) or Orchestration (central coordinator).
Key Takeaway

Use sagas to maintain eventual consistency, but design compensating actions and idempotency carefully.
