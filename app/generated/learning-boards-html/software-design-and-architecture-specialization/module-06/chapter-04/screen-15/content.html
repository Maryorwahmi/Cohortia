Module 6›Chapter 6.4›Key Concepts
Microservices Architecture
A collection of small, independently deployable services each aligned to a business capability.
Bounded Context
An explicit domain boundary where a service's model applies, reducing domain coupling.
Decentralized Data
Each service owns its data store to maximize autonomy and optimize storage choices.
Summary

Adopt microservices when its benefits for scale and autonomy outweigh the increased operational complexity.
