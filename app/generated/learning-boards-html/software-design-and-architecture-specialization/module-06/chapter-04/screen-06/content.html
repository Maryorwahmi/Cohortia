Module 6›Chapter 6.4›What Microservices Buy You
Advantages
Microservices separate concerns so you can tune scale, availability, and development processes per capability.
Scalability
Scale only the services that need capacity.
Resilience
Failures can be contained using timeouts and circuit breakers.
Velocity
Small teams can deploy independently and iterate faster.
Heterogeneity
Choose the best stack and database per service.
Practical example
If product search drives traffic spikes, you can scale the catalog service independently while keeping other services lean.
Key Takeaway

Microservices give targeted control over scale and ownership but require investment in automation and observability to realize their benefits.
