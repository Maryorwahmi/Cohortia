Module 6›Chapter 6.4›Monolith vs Microservices
Monolith
Single deployable unit
All logic and data in one process and database. Easy to test end-to-end and maintain transactional integrity.
  • Simple deployment and debugging.
  • ACID transactions across features.
  • Can become a coordination bottleneck as teams grow.
Key Takeaway

Good initial choice for small teams and simple applications due to lower operational cost.

Microservices
Multiple independent services
Split by business capability, each with its own datastore and lifecycle. Enables focused scaling and independent releases.
  • Independent deployment and scaling per service.
  • Teams own services end-to-end.
  • Requires service discovery, tracing, and orchestration.
Key Takeaway

Powerful for large systems where autonomy and scaling matter; prepare for distributed complexity.
