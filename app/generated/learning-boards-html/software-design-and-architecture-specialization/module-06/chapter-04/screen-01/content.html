Microservices Architecture
Structure systems as many small services that are independently deployable and owned by small teams.
In this lesson
Learn core characteristics, trade-offs, common infra patterns, and a practical decomposition of a bookstore monolith into services you can deploy independently.
Why it matters
Microservices enable focused scaling, faster releases, and team autonomy. They demand stronger operational practices such as tracing, service discovery, and resilient communication patterns.
Explore Key Topics
Core Characteristics
Small services, bounded contexts, decentralized data, independent deploys.
Advantages & Trade-offs
Scalability and team autonomy vs distributed complexity.
Infrastructure Patterns
API Gateway, service discovery, container orchestration, and Saga.
System View
Example services
  • User Service — accounts and auth.
  • Catalog Service — product data and stock info.
  • Shopping Cart — ephemeral cart storage.
  • Order Service — transactional order lifecycle.
  • Payment Service — external payments and events.
Interactions
Services communicate via lightweight APIs or message events. Resilience patterns such as retries and circuit breakers prevent cascading failures.
Note
Independent data stores avoid single-database bottlenecks but require eventual consistency strategies.
Practical tip
Adopt microservices when team size, velocity, and scaling needs outweigh operational cost.
