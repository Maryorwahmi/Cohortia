Readiness checklist
  • Measurable scaling needs on specific components.
  • Teams structured for end-to-end ownership.
  • Investment in CI/CD, orchestration, monitoring, and tracing.
  • Willingness to accept eventual consistency where necessary.
Practical advice
Start with a modular monolith and split services when you have clear operational and business justification.
Reflection

Which two criteria from the checklist would you prioritize for your project: scale or team autonomy?
