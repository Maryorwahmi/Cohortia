Module 6›Chapter 6.4›Defining Characteristics
Service architecture
Each service encapsulates a bounded context and data. Services communicate via lightweight APIs or messages and can be deployed independently.
Small & Autonomous
Focused on one capability, owned by one team.
Bounded Contexts
Separate domain models avoid cross-cutting domain complexity.
Decentralized Data
Each service manages its own persistence and schema.
Independent Deploy
Ship a service without coordinating a full-system release.
Resilience & heterogeneity
Services are designed to fail independently and can use different technologies. Operational tooling is essential to make this work at scale.
Key Takeaway

Design service boundaries around business capabilities and plan for data consistency and resilience from the start.
