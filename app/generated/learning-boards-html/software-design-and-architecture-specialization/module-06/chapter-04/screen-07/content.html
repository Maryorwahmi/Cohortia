Module 6›Chapter 6.4›Distributed-System Complexity
Key challenges
Networked services are inherently less predictable than local calls. Design for failure and build observability from day one.
  • Latency & failures: use timeouts, retries, and circuit breakers.
  • Service discovery & load balancing: dynamic registration and routing are essential.
  • Testing & debugging: distributed tracing and contract tests help find cross-service issues.
Common-mistake
Assuming existing operational processes for monoliths are enough. Microservices typically require more automation and tooling.
Warning

Without investment in tracing, monitoring, and pipelines, debugging and operating microservices becomes slow and error-prone.
