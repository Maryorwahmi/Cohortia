Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Key Concepts
Caching
Store frequently accessed data in faster locations to reduce latency and backend load.
Load Balancing
Distribute incoming traffic across instances to prevent overload and improve availability.
Circuit Breaker
Prevent repeated calls to a failing dependency so systems can recover and avoid cascading failures.
Quick summary

Use these tactics thoughtfully together: measure first, choose the right tactic for the problem, and verify results with monitoring and tests.
