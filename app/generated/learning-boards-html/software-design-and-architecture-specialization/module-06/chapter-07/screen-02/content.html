Learning Objectives
Master practical tactics and verification for key quality attributes.
01
Apply performance tactics
Use caching, load balancing, and async processing to lower latency and increase throughput. You will propose when each tactic matters and note common pitfalls like over-caching.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Design for scalability
Distinguish vertical versus horizontal scaling and design stateless services and sharded data to support growth without major rework.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Integrate security by design
Embed authentication, authorization, encryption, least privilege, input validation, and monitoring into the architecture, not bolted on later.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design for maintainability & reliability
Make systems modular, testable, observable, and redundant so they are easier to evolve and keep available under failure.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
