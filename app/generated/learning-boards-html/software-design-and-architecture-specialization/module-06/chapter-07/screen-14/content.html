Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Key Concepts
Measure then optimize
Profile systems and apply tactics where they address measured bottlenecks.
Design for scale
Prefer stateless services, load balancers, and sharding for sustainable horizontal growth.
Security & reliability by design
Embed encryption, least privilege, redundancy, circuit breakers, and DR testing into architecture.
Summary

Balance competing attributes by aligning tactics with business priorities, instrument outcomes, and iterate based on measurement.
