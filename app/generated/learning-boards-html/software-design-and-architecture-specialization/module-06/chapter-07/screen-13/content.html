Reliability and a hands-on Payment Gateway exercise
Design tactics to keep payments flowing under failure.
Reliability tactics
  • Redundancy: active-active across availability zones or regions.
  • Fault tolerance: retries with exponential backoff and idempotent operations.
  • Circuit breakers: stop calls to failing processors to avoid cascading outages.
  • Graceful degradation: offer partial functionality under stress.
DR and testing
Regularly test failover and recovery paths. An untested disaster recovery plan is not a plan.
Payment Gateway: starter tactics
Performance: async processing for non-critical tasks, in-memory cache for routing configuration, connection pooling. Reliability: active-active across zones, circuit breakers for external processors, idempotent operations for retries. Security: TLS for all channels, tokenization of card data, strict least-privilege for service credentials, and audit logging.
