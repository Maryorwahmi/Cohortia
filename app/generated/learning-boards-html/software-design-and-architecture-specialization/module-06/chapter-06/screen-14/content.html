Process
Measuring QAs
Choose metrics and verification methods that map directly to your SMART statements. Collect data, compare to targets, and iterate on design or operations.
Tools & methods
Load testing for performance, uptime monitoring for availability, penetration testing for security, and usability studies for UX.
ATAM
A structured evaluation that highlights sensitivity and trade-off points to guide design improvements.
Practical guidance
  • Map each metric to a SMART QA statement.
  • Automate tests and monitoring to detect regressions early.
  • Run ATAM or similar reviews before major design commitments.
Key Takeaway

Tie measurements directly to QA statements and use structured reviews to expose risks and trade-offs.
