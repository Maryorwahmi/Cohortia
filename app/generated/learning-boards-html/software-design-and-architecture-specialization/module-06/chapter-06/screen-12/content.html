SMART Quality Attributes
Specific
Name the operation or data the QA covers.
Measurable
Pick a metric like p95 latency, throughput, or uptime percentage.
Achievable & Relevant
Confirm the target is realistic and aligns with business goals.
Time-bound & Verifiable
Include the measurement context and verification method such as load tests or audits.
Examples
Performance
"The search API shall return results within 300ms for 95% of requests under a steady load of 5,000 concurrent queries."
Security
"All customer PII shall be encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2+; annual penetration testing shall produce no critical findings."
Key Takeaway

SMART QAs enable test plans and clear acceptance criteria for architecture reviews.
