Definition
Functional Requirements
Describe specific behaviours or actions the system must perform. Captured as user stories or use cases.
Example
"The system shall allow users to upload documents."
Why it matters
Functional stories define scope and acceptance criteria for features.
Definition
Non-Functional Requirements (Quality Attributes)
Specify qualities, constraints, and measurable targets such as performance, availability, and security.
Example
"Files up to 50 MB shall upload within 5 seconds for 95% of attempts under expected network conditions."
Why it matters
Provides measurable targets that drive architecture, testing, and operational choices.
Quick practice
Pick a feature you know and write one functional sentence and one measurable QA sentence. This small habit avoids vague acceptance criteria and simplifies design trade-offs.
