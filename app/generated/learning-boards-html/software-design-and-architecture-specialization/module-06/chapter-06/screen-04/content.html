Core Concept
What are Quality Attributes?
Measurable properties describing how well a system performs its functions. They direct architectural choices and validation activities.
Metaphor — the car
Functional: the car drives. QA: it is fuel-efficient, safe, and easy to repair. That how-well view shapes design decisions.
Common misconception
Thinking QAs are optional polish. They are requirements that must be prioritized and validated like any other requirement.
Examples
Functional → Non-functional
"Process payments" (functional) → "Process payments within 2 seconds for 99% of transactions" (performance QA).
Why it matters
A feature that works but is too slow or insecure will not satisfy users or business goals; QAs prevent that disconnect.
Check your understanding
Try converting a feature you know into one measurable QA using percentiles or uptime targets.
Key Takeaway

Quality attributes answer 'how well' and must be measurable so architects can validate designs.
