Architecture
Reasoning about Trade-offs
List priorities, estimate impacts, and choose designs that align with business goals. Use SMART QAs and tests to validate choices.
Banking example
Prioritize security and reliability even if it costs some latency. Design for strong encryption, audits, and replication.
Game example
Prioritize low latency and horizontal scaling; accept faster iteration and more operational complexity.
Decision checklist
  • Rank QAs by business priority.
  • Estimate architectural impact and cost for each option.
  • Create SMART targets for the prioritized QAs.
  • Plan validation and monitoring to confirm the chosen trade-off.
Key Takeaway

Document priorities and validate trade-offs with measurable tests so stakeholders understand the chosen compromises.
