Quality Attributes
Non-functional requirements that determine how well a system performs under real conditions.
In this set of slides you will: name common QAs, learn how to write measurable statements, and practice reasoning about trade-offs that influence technology choices and deployment.
Why it matters
  • QAs influence architecture, not just features; they guide deployment, redundancy and testing.
  • Vague QAs block validation; measurable statements enable testing and trade-off analysis.
