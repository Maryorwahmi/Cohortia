Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Learning Objectives — Applied Skills
Skills to demonstrate
  • Translate a functional requirement into one or more measurable QAs.
  • Choose metrics and tests to validate QA claims under expected load.
  • Explain architectural trade-offs with evidence-based reasoning.
  • Draft SMART QA statements suitable for architecture reviews.
Micro-metrics
Examples of measurable targets you will use: response time percentiles, transactions per second, uptime percentage, encryption standards, test coverage thresholds.
Milestone
Prepare two SMART QA statements and a test plan for one of them.
