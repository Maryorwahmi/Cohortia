Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Application & Business Layers
Application Layer
• Orchestrates workflows and composes domain operations.
• Validates request formats and coordinates transactions.
Business Logic Layer
• Encapsulates domain rules, entities, and invariants.
• Independent of UI and persistence technologies.
Example: Application layer sequences order placement; business logic enforces discount rules and inventory checks.
Key Takeaway

Keep orchestration separate from domain rules so business logic stays reusable and testable across different workflows.
