Introduction to Architectural Patterns
Why blueprints for systems matter and how Layered Architecture gives structure.
In this lesson

Define architectural patterns, contrast them with design patterns, explore categorization criteria, and walk a layered architecture example with an e-commerce scenario.

Why this matters
Patterns give teams a shared vocabulary and predictable trade-offs, reducing onboarding time and design ambiguity.
