Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Separation of Concerns
Break the system into distinct sections where each addresses a single concern to reduce overlap and coupling.
Modularity
Design components so they can be separated and recombined with clear interfaces to support change and reuse.
Dependency Direction
Enforce that higher layers call only the layer immediately beneath them to avoid tight coupling and abstraction leaks.
Final takeaway

Use layered patterns and these principles to make architecture decisions that support maintainability, testability, and clear team communication.
