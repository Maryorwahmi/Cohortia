Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › How Patterns Are Categorized
Categories
  • Structural: organize subsystems and responsibilities.
  • Distributed: patterns for multi-node interaction and resilience.
  • Data-focused: patterns for storage, caching, and access.
  • Domain-specific: tailored to particular problem spaces.
Use case: Choose a category based on the primary operational concerns, such as responsiveness or cross-node scaling.
Practical filter
Pick the top quality attributes, then use categories to reduce candidate patterns. Follow with a trade-off evaluation for each candidate.
Key Takeaway

Categorization is a practical heuristic to focus pattern selection on the system attributes that matter most.
