Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Architectural Pattern
A reusable system-level organization that guides subsystem boundaries and interactions.
Design Pattern
A localized solution used inside modules to solve recurring coding problems.
Layered Architecture
Presentation, application, business logic, and data access with strict adjacency to reduce coupling.
Summary

Use patterns deliberately: match them to your system goals, centralize domain rules, and preserve layer boundaries to make systems easier to change and test.
