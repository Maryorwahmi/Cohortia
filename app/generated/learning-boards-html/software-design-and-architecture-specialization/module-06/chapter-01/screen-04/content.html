Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › What is an Architectural Pattern?
Definition
Architectural Pattern
A high-level, reusable solution describing system structure and relationships between major components to satisfy functional and quality goals.
• Generalizes structure across systems so teams share a vocabulary.
• Focuses on subsystem boundaries, not class-level mechanics.
• Guides trade-offs: maintainability, scalability, latency, and testability.
Analogy: A blueprint for a house guides room placement and load-bearing walls.
Example: Layered Architecture prescribes presentation, application, business, and data layers.
Common misconception
Patterns are not guarantees of success. They require appropriate context and trade-off analysis. Applying a pattern without reasoning can create unnecessary constraints.
Quick check
  • Does this pattern clarify responsibilities?
  • Does it align with the system's quality goals?
  • What trade-offs does it impose?
Key Takeaway

Architectural patterns shape system structure and give teams a shared language for making deliberate design trade-offs.
