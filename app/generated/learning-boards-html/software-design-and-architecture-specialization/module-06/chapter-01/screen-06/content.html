Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Why Apply Architectural Patterns?
Benefit Map
  • Consistency: repeatable architecture that reduces mental overhead.
  • Communication: a shared vocabulary for design discussions.
  • Predictable trade-offs: known strengths and weaknesses help decision-making.
Example: Onboarding a new engineer is faster when the system follows a familiar pattern like Layered Architecture.
Pitfall: Applying patterns blindly can introduce unnecessary constraints or latency.
Decision process
1) Identify the primary quality attributes you need. 2) Shortlist patterns that support those attributes. 3) Evaluate known trade-offs. 4) Monitor after adoption.
Key Takeaway

Apply patterns deliberately: choose them for the system-level qualities you need, not for convenience alone.
