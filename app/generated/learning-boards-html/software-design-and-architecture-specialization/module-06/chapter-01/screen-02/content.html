01
Define architectural patterns
Explain what an architectural pattern is and how it generalizes solutions across systems. This matters because it helps you reason at system scale and communicate design intent clearly.
Verified competency milestone
02
Explain benefits of patterns
Describe how patterns bring consistency, reduce ambiguity, and help teams make trade-offs explicit. This supports maintainability and team alignment.
Verified competency milestone
03
Categorize patterns
Identify criteria like structural, distributed, or data-focused concerns to choose a pattern that fits requirements and constraints.
Verified competency milestone
04
Recognize Layered Architecture
Map responsibilities to presentation, application, business logic, and data access layers, and understand the benefits and pitfalls of strict layering.
Verified competency milestone
