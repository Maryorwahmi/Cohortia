Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › SLOs & Error Budgets
Definitions
SLI: measurable indicator such as success rate or latency. SLO: desired target for an SLI. Error budget: allowable unreliability derived from the SLO.
Example SLI/SLO
email_delivery_success_rate with SLO 99.9% over 7 days.
Using error budgets
If the budget is spent, pause risky releases and focus on reliability work.
Architectural implications
  • Emit accurate metrics for the chosen SLIs from every component.
  • Design for fault isolation and graceful degradation to preserve SLOs.
  • Implement retries, circuit breakers and backpressure with observability around them.
