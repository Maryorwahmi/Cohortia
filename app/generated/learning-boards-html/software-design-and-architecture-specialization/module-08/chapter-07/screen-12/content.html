Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Architecture Patterns
Microservices
Pros: independent deploys, small teams. Cons: increased tracing and ops complexity.
Serverless
Pros: reduced infra toil. Cons: cold starts, vendor constraints, and observability challenges.
Monolith
Pros: simple operations early. Cons: slows independent delivery and scaling.
Design tip

Match pattern to team and operational maturity. If you pick microservices, invest up front in observability and automation.
