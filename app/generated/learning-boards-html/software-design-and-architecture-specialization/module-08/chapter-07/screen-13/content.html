Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Pipeline Security
Secrets and Access
Use a secrets store and grant pipelines only the permissions they need. Avoid hardcoding credentials in code or IaC files.
Audit and Review
Keep an auditable trail of pipeline actions and require peer review for changes that affect production access.
Practical checklist
  • Secrets in vaults with dynamic credentials.
  • Least-privilege pipeline roles and scoped tokens.
  • Automatic validation of IaC plans and policies before apply.
Reflection
If your pipeline credentials were revoked, how quickly could teams restore safe deployment capability?
