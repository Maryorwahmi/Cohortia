Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Observability
Instrument for diagnosis

Choose SLIs tied to user experience and emit supporting metrics that enable root-cause analysis. Use structured logs and distributed tracing to connect events across services.

Metrics
Counters and histograms for success rates and latencies.
Logs
Structured logs with correlation identifiers for fast search.
Traces
End-to-end traces to find where latency or errors originate.
Concrete example
For NotificationService emit email_send_attempts, email_send_successes, email_send_failures and a latency histogram. Use request IDs so logs and traces are correlated for any failed request.
