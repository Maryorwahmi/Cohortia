Comparison

Benefits

Reduced operational overhead — provider handles servers and scaling.
Cost efficiency — pay only for execution time and invocations.
Automatic scaling — functions scale transparently on demand.
Faster time to market — focus on code and business logic.

Trade-offs & cautions

Vendor lock-in
Providers have different APIs and event shapes. Portability requires abstraction and effort.
Cold starts
Initial invocation after inactivity can cause latency; mitigate with provisioned concurrency where needed.
Limits and observability — providers enforce memory, time, and concurrency limits and you must instrument functions for traces and metrics.
Key Takeaway

Choose serverless when reduced ops and pay-per-use economics match your workload profile; prepare for vendor-specific trade-offs.
