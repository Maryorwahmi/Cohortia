Example metrics for TransactionService
  • transaction_processing_rate (Counter)
  • transaction_failure_rate (Counter)
  • transaction_commit_latency_seconds (Histogram)
These metrics let you derive SLIs like success rate and latency percentiles for SLOs.
Common mistakes
  • High-cardinality labels without need
  • No aggregation strategy or retention policy
  • Alerting on non-user-impacting internal signals
Concrete example & takeaway
If commit latency percentiles rise, investigate slow database spans. Always map metrics to an SLI so alerts focus on customer experience rather than internal noise.
