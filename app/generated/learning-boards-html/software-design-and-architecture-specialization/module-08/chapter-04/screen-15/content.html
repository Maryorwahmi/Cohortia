Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › Key Concepts
Event Sourcing
Persist every change; replay events to reconstruct state and enable auditability.
CQRS
Separate write and read models so each can be optimized and scaled independently.
Operational principles
Eventual consistency, idempotency, retries, and DLQs keep pipelines reliable under failure.
Final takeaway

Design events as facts, choose patterns to match needs, and add operational safeguards so your event-driven system behaves predictably at scale.
