Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › Error Handling

Typical flow

1. Consumer attempts processing. 2. On failure, apply bounded retries with backoff. 3. After retries exhausted, route to DLQ for inspection.
Include event identifiers and correlation metadata to trace and diagnose errors.
On DLQ items, provide tooling to inspect, correct, and reinsert events safely in an idempotent way.
Operational tip
Alert on rising DLQ counts and long retry latencies. Provide playbooks for common error classes like bad data or downstream outages.
Concrete scenario
ShippingService receives OrderPlaced but fails due to invalid address format. Retries fail; event lands in DLQ. Operator inspects payload, corrects address, and requeues event for idempotent reprocessing.
Key Takeaway

Automate retries, push irrecoverable failures to DLQs, and keep reprocessing safe and observable.
