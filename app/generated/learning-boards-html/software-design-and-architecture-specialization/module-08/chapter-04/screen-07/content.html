Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › Brokers vs Streaming
Message Brokers
Examples
RabbitMQ, ActiveMQ, Amazon SQS/SNS (as broker-like services)
• Flexible routing and queue semantics for work distribution.
• Good for transactional message handoffs and complex routing rules.
• Typically shorter retention; not designed primarily as a long-lived event store.
Event Streaming Platforms
Examples
Apache Kafka, Amazon Kinesis — treat events as durable, ordered streams.
• Durable log for replay and historical processing.
• High throughput and partitioning for parallel consumption.
• Strong ordering guarantees within partitions; consumers manage offsets.
Decision checklist
Consider ordering, retention and replay, throughput, routing complexity, and operational overhead. Streaming platforms excel at replay and persistence; brokers excel at routing and queue semantics.
