Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › Event Sourcing

How it works

Ledger of events
OrderCreated → ItemsAdded → PaymentProcessed → OrderPlaced
• Audit trail and temporal queries are native because every change is recorded.
• Replay events to rebuild read models or repair state after bugs.
• Use snapshots to avoid expensive full replays for large aggregates.
Practical notes
Plan for schema evolution and migration strategies for older events. Keep events as small, clear facts to ease transformations.
Key takeaway
Event sourcing gives you a full history and rebuildability, at the cost of replay complexity and versioning responsibilities.
