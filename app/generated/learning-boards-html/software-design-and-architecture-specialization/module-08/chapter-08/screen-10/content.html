Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Carbon-Aware Deployment
Process

Annotate, schedule, measure

  1. Classify jobs by urgency and allowable delay.
  2. Collect carbon-intensity signals from provider or grid API.
  3. Schedule flexible jobs to low-carbon windows or greener regions.
  4. Record and report carbon metrics per job.
Example

Nightly model training moved to region A reduced estimated carbon by 40% while keeping delivery times within SLA because training was not user-facing.

Key Takeaway

Small scheduling changes and clear SLAs let you shift heavy workloads to greener times without harming users.

Architecture

Carbon-aware control plane

A lightweight control plane annotates and schedules jobs, integrates carbon signals, and enforces SLAs.

Job Registry
Stores job metadata: urgency, expected runtime, data locality, and allowed delay.
Carbon Signal Adapter
Normalizes provider or grid carbon intensity for the scheduler to consume.
Scheduler
Decides when and where to run jobs while respecting SLAs and data residency.
Pitfalls
  • Ignoring compliance constraints when moving workloads across regions.
  • Over-deferring jobs that actually require faster completion.
  • Not tracking results, so improvements are invisible.
