Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Edge Operations
Use Cases

When to choose edge

  • Autonomous vehicles and tight control loops
  • Industrial control where safety requires local decisions
  • AR/VR and tactile applications demanding low latency
  • Sensor fleets producing high raw bandwidth
Device capability matrix
Classify devices by compute, memory, and network, and map workloads accordingly so devices receive feasible software updates and tasks.
Operational checklist
  1. Secure identity & attestation for each device
  2. OTA update pipeline with staged rollout
  3. Local health telemetry and centralized aggregation
Challenges

Heterogeneity, security, and updates

Devices differ in hardware and network reliability. Design the management plane to push compatible artifacts only to capable devices, and use canary rollouts. Ensure cryptographic identity and signed updates to prevent compromise.

Fallback policies

Define graceful degradation paths: cached responses, limited feature sets, and operator alerts. These keep service available even in degraded conditions.

Human ops

Surface clear operational signals so teams can triage edge incidents quickly; avoid black‑box devices without telemetry.
