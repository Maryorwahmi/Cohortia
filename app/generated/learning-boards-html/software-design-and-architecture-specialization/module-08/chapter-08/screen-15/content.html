Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › What to Remember
Localize Critical Work

Keep latency-sensitive, privacy-critical, or high-bandwidth processing near the sources using edge patterns and gateway logic.

Design for Adaptation

Build control planes, feedback loops, and observability so systems can safely auto-tune and recover from failures.

Prioritize Sustainability

Measure energy, optimize hot paths, and use carbon-aware scheduling to make tangible reductions in environmental impact.
