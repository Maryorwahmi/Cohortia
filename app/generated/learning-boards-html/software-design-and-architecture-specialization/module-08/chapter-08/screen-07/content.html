Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Quantum Computing
Definition & Scope

Quantum's domain of impact

Quantum promises asymptotic improvements for certain problems. For architectures, this implies hybrid systems where classical services orchestrate inputs and handle fallback logic while quantum resources solve narrowly defined kernels.

Example domain

Combinatorial optimization, specific simulation tasks, and potential cryptographic impact are realistic early targets for quantum acceleration.

Misconception

Quantum is not a universal speedup. Assess whether problem structure matches quantum algorithms before designing an integration path.

Hybrid architecture snapshot
Classical control plane

Validate inputs, manage retries, and interpret quantum outputs. Also provide fallback when quantum access is unavailable.

Quantum service

Expose a well‑defined API for specific kernels. Expect queuing, special provisioning, and security controls around keys and data sent for processing.

Design question: Which computations lose correctness if results are delayed due to quantum queueing? Use that to set SLAs.
