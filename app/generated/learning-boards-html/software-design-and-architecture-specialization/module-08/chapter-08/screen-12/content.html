Architecture

Data Mesh components

A platform that enables domain teams to produce discoverable data products with contracts, lineage, and SLAs.

Domain Data Product
API, schema, SLAs, and owner metadata for each dataset provided by a domain team.
Data Catalog
Discoverable registry with lineage, quality scores, and access controls.
Platform Tools
Templates, CI/CD, and self-service pipelines to help domains publish products reliably.
Governance model

Federated policy and automated checks ensure data quality while giving domains ownership over their products.

Concrete example

The orders domain publishes an 'orders' product with schema, sample queries, SLAs for freshness, and a contact owner. Analytics teams consume it directly instead of building custom ingestion.

Common mistake

Skipping platform investment: without discovery, lineage, and templates, federated ownership leads to fragmentation rather than speed.

Key Takeaway

Data Mesh is organizational and technical: invest in a platform so domain ownership accelerates reliable data delivery.
