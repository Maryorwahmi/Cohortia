Definition

Edge computing explained

Bring compute and storage near data sources to meet latency, bandwidth, and privacy constraints while still integrating with cloud services for heavy lifting.

Latency-sensitive workloads
Autonomous control, AR/VR, and industrial safety require sub‑second decisions.
Bandwidth & privacy
Filter, aggregate and retain sensitive data locally to reduce cost and risk.
Example

Traffic sensors perform local analytics to adjust lights instantly, only sending aggregated metrics to the cloud for trend analysis.

Key takeaway

Place compute based on where timely decisions are required; cloud remains central for global view and heavy compute.
