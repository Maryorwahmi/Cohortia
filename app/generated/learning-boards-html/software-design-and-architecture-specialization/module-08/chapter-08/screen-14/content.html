Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Key Concepts
Edge Computing

Bring processing close to data sources to lower latency and bandwidth, and design for intermittent connectivity and security.

Adaptive Systems

Use observability and control loops to enable self-healing and self-optimization, but ensure safe fallbacks and transparency.

Sustainability

Treat energy and data footprint as measurable requirements: optimize code, storage, and deployment to reduce carbon impact.

Summary
Future-proof architectures balance locality, adaptability, performance, and sustainability while integrating new runtimes and AI with governance and measurement.
