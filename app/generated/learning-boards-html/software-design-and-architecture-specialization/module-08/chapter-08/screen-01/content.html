Future Trends in Software Architecture

A concise orientation to near‑term changes architects must understand: distributed edge patterns, nascent quantum effects, adaptive and self‑healing systems, plus sustainability as a design constraint.

In this lesson

Understand what each trend is, why it changes architectural decisions, and how to begin adding patterns to your designs.

These trends intersect. Design choices that lower latency may raise energy use, and adaptivity can mitigate intermittency at the edge.
