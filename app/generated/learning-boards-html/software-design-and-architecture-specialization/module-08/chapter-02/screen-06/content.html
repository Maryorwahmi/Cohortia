Module 08 › Chapter 8.2 › Monolith vs Microservices
Comparison: When to choose
Monolith
  • Simple deployment and local debugging
  • Works well for small teams and tight feature coupling
  • Fewer operational tools required
Microservices
  • Independent scaling and deployments
  • Technology diversity and team autonomy
  • Requires orchestration and observability investments
Concrete example

If product search has 10× read traffic and complex indexing, extract it as a service to scale independently and choose a search-optimized datastore, while keeping order logic in a separate service with strict consistency.

Misconception

Microservices automatically speed development. Without automation and governance, they increase coordination overhead and can slow teams down.

Key Takeaway

Adopt microservices when technical and organizational needs justify the operational investment; otherwise iterate the monolith.
