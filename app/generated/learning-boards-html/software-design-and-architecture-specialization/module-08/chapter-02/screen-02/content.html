Learning Objectives

These competencies prepare you to design, operate, and evolve microservice systems responsibly.

01
Articulate core principles

Explain service componentization, domain-oriented design, and decentralized governance so teams can choose right-sized boundaries.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Evaluate benefits

Assess independent scaling, resilience, and faster deployment cycles against added operational overhead using an e-commerce scenario.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify challenges

Recognize distributed data, monitoring, security, and the risk of a distributed monolith; prepare mitigation strategies.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply best practices

Design decomposition, choose communication patterns, and implement observability and CI/CD practices to operate microservices at scale.
