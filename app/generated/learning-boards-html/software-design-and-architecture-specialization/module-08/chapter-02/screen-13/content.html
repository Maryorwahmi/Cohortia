Process: Incremental extraction steps
  1. Identify a bounded capability and its data ownership.
  2. Extract an API or adapter and implement a new service behind it.
  3. Route a subset of traffic to the new service and observe behavior.
  4. Iterate, migrate data or publish events, and expand traffic as confidence grows.
Example priority

Choose a high-load read path, such as product search, for early extraction. It often yields clear operational benefits and lower transactional complexity.

Practical tip

Keep the API contract stable during migration and use feature flags or gateway routing to control traffic split safely.

Pitfall

Extracting data without a synchronization or ownership plan leads to divergence and operational headaches.
