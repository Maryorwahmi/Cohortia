Module 08 › Chapter 8.2 › What is a Microservice?
Core definition

A microservice is a unit of modularity built around a business capability. It is independently deployable, owns its own data, and communicates via network APIs or messages.

Example
Product Catalog Service

Manages product metadata, search, and read models; emits product events on updates.

Common mistake
Nano-services

Too many tiny services increase latency and operational overhead.

Key Takeaway

Design services around business capabilities and ensure each can be developed and operated end-to-end by one team.

Annotated example: Product Catalog
Responsibility
Provide product search, details, and indexing
Owned data
Product catalog store and search index
Communication
Expose REST for reads, publish events for updates
Operational
Independent CI/CD, health checks, metrics, and logs
