Example simulation

Simulate the checkout flow: if the cart needs product info and pricing and causes many synchronous calls, consider co-locating read models or using events to reduce synchronous dependencies.

Sizing heuristic

Group functions that change together and that share transactional needs into the same service. Extract when different teams require independent release cadence or scaling.

Avoid

Don't split a single transaction across many services without a plan for eventual consistency.
