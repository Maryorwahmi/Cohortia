Architecture: Polyglot persistence
Services choose storage technologies aligned with their access patterns. Decentralized ownership reduces coupling but introduces distributed consistency concerns.
Order Service
Relational DB

Strong transactions for payments and invoicing.

Product Catalog
Search DB

Optimized for search and read performance.

Practical step

Publish domain events on state changes so other services can update their local views asynchronously and avoid direct queries into another service’s store.

Example: Event propagation

When a product price changes, the product service updates its store and emits a product.updated event. Inventory and pricing caches subscribe to that event and update their local stores asynchronously, accepting short windows of inconsistency.

Pitfall

Using a shared database breaks autonomy and coordination becomes a manual, error-prone process.
