Process: Core principles
  1. Componentization via services: services are the unit of modularity and communicate over the network.
  2. Organize by business capability: domains like inventory, orders, or users become owned services.
  3. Products not projects: teams own lifecycle and operations for continuous delivery.
  4. Decentralized data and governance: services pick stores and tooling that fit their needs.
Example

A payments team chooses a strongly available key-value store and specialized SDKs, while a catalog team uses a search-optimized database. Teams still agree on API contracts and tracing formats to interoperate.

Common Pitfall

Decentralization without standards leads to integration pain; agree on contracts and observability formats early.
