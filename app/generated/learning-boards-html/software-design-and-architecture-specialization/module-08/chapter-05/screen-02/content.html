Learning Objectives
Concrete skills to design, deploy, and operate ML systems in production.
01
Recognize integration challenges
Identify lifecycle, drift risks, and cross-team workflows so you can plan for ongoing model maintenance.
02
Choose deployment patterns
Pick online, batch, embedded or remote serving based on latency, throughput and coupling trade-offs.
03
Design MLOps pipelines
Automate ingestion, training, validation, and deployment to ensure reproducible model delivery.
04
Manage data & features
Use feature stores and data versioning to avoid training/serving skew and support audits.
