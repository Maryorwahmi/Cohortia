Architecture Serving strategies
Containers, serverless, and scaling choices
Match serving platform to traffic and resource needs, and bake safe rollout practices into deployments.
Containerized services
Good for predictable traffic, large models, and rich runtime dependencies; use orchestration for autoscaling.
Serverless
Cost-efficient for spiky, low-volume workloads but may suffer cold starts and runtime limits.
Rollout strategies
Canary, shadow, and A/B testing allow safe promotion and validation of new models under real traffic.
Key Takeaway

Plan for resource constraints and rollout safety: test memory, cold starts, and include health checks to maintain reliability at scale.

Concrete scenario
A Flask service containerized and deployed on Kubernetes with horizontal pod autoscaling based on request latency and CPU.
Common pitfall
Ignoring model memory requirements can lead to pod crashes under scale; set resource limits and test at load.
Try this
Estimate memory and startup time for your model and choose a serving platform that supports those constraints.
