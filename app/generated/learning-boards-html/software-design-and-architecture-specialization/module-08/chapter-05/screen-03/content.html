Operational objectives
Define monitoring, retraining, and governance plans for ML in production.
01
Monitoring & alerting
Track model metrics and input distributions. Alerts should be actionable and tied to thresholds or tests.
02
Model registries
Manage model versions, metadata, and stage transitions so deployments are reproducible and auditable.
03
Retraining triggers
Use schedules, performance thresholds, or hybrid triggers to keep models current without unnecessary churn.
04
Governance & security
Ensure secure access, protect sensitive training data, and comply with privacy rules when designing pipelines.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Quick mnemonic
Monitor, Version, Retrain, Secure — a compact checklist for production readiness.
Common pitfall
Assuming stable performance without monitoring. Models degrade as data distributions change.
Assessment hint
When proposing a retraining trigger, link it to a measurable metric such as a drop in CTR or a statistical drift test.
