Design Patterns and MLOps: Bringing ML into Production
Practical architectural choices, operational practices, and the lifecycle that make ML reliable and scalable.
In this lesson
Map business needs to deployment patterns, design MLOps pipelines, and plan monitoring and retraining to manage model drift.
Why it matters
ML systems are data-dependent and change over time; architecture plus operations keep them accurate and safe.
Explore Key Topics
Deployment Patterns
Online, batch, embedded, and remote models—choose based on latency and coupling needs.
Feature Stores
Centralize feature computation and serving to prevent data skew between training and inference.
Monitoring & Retraining
Detect data or concept drift, then trigger retraining or rollback policies when performance drops.
Architecture
Data → Feature Store → Training → Registry → Serving
Common pattern
Use a dedicated prediction service for online inference, and batch jobs for periodic scoring.
