Avoid anti-patterns

Distribution without autonomy recreates monolithic constraints and adds network fragility.

Observability first

Tracing, logging, and monitoring are prerequisites for safe distributed operations.

Evolve intentionally

Start simple, measure, and extract only when clear criteria and ops are in place.

Final takeaway

Architecture choices are trade-offs. Use measurable criteria, invest in observability, and plan evolvability to reduce risk.
