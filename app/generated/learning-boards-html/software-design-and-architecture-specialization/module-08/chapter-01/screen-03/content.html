Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Learning Objectives

Applied skills to evaluate architectures

You will practice decision-making with real constraints so recommendations are actionable.

01
Analyze trade-offs

Score and justify choices across speed, scalability, and operational cost. Use evidence rather than intuition to choose an architecture.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify coupling patterns

Recognize tight vs loose coupling and predict system fragility and evolution speed based on those patterns.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Assess operational needs

Evaluate monitoring, tracing, and DevOps maturity required to run distributed services reliably.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Build a decision matrix

Combine business needs and technical risk to arrive at a recommended architecture for an initial phase and future growth.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Use case
Startup example

You will apply these objectives to a startup building a learning platform and produce a short recommendation for the initial phase.
