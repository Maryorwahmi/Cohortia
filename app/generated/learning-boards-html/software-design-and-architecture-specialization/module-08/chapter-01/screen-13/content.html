Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Key Concepts
Monolithic Architecture

All components bundled into a single deployable unit; simple to ship and manage early on.

Distributed System

Multiple independent components communicating over a network; enables targeted scaling and isolation.

Trade-off Analysis

Use a matrix to weigh speed, scalability, team size, and ops maturity, then plan staged evolution.

Summary

Choose the architecture that addresses your measured constraints now, and design clear extraction and observability plans for the future.
