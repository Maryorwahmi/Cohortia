Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Operational Complexity
Definition

Monitoring, tracing, deployment

  • Logging: events and errors per service instance.
  • Monitoring: metrics, health checks, and SLAs.
  • Tracing: request paths across services to locate latency or failures.
Practical tip

Instrument critical user flows first: payments, login, and content delivery. Use traces to reduce mean-time-to-detect and mean-time-to-repair.

Scenario

A payment request times out. In a monolith you inspect one process; in a distributed system you consult traces to identify the slow component, then logs and metrics to find the cause and scope.

Common mistake

Assuming monitoring is optional. Without it, debugging distributed failure modes becomes slow and risky.

Key Takeaway

Invest in observability and CI/CD ahead of heavy decomposition. Operational readiness dictates how many services you can safely run.
