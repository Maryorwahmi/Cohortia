Process

Five-step decision flow

  1. 1. Gather constraints: business goals, expected growth, SLAs, team size.
  2. 2. Evaluate team & ops maturity: CI/CD, monitoring, automation.
  3. 3. Score the factors: development speed, scaling, complexity, ops overhead.
  4. 4. Prototype & measure: instrument hotspots before splitting.
  5. 5. Plan extraction points: define safe seams for later decomposition.
Practical rule

Prefer a simple, modular monolith early and extract services where clear, measurable bottlenecks or ownership boundaries exist.

Example recommendation

For a small startup with three developers and limited DevOps, a modular monolith maximizes speed. Plan to extract heavy-traffic or independently evolving features later.
