Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › The Software Architect's Responsibilities
Role breakdown

Strategic responsibilities

  1. Translate business goals into technical requirements and constraints.
  2. Define major components and interactions to meet prioritized ilities.
  3. Choose core technologies and justify trade-offs.
  4. Mentor teams and resolve cross-cutting conflicts.
  5. Document architectural decisions and rationale for future maintainers.
Concrete example
An architect selects an event streaming system for cross-service communication to meet throughput goals and documents the reasoning for teams to follow.
Pitfall
Focusing on local implementation details instead of preserving architectural integrity across teams.
Skills and habits
  • Trade-off analysis and clear written justification.
  • Communication: translating technical constraints for stakeholders.
  • Prioritization of non-functional requirements and incremental validation.
Reflection prompt
Identify one architectural decision you would document now for a past project, and write the short rationale you would save for future teams.
