Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Architectural Decision Brainstorm
Activity

Map NFRs to high-level decisions

  1. Identify 3–5 critical non-functional requirements for the platform.
  2. For each, propose one high-level architectural decision that addresses it.
  3. Write a short rationale explaining how the decision meets the requirement.
  4. Note one trade-off or downside for each decision.
  5. Rank the decisions by risk and pick one to validate with a small prototype or test.
Worked example
NFR:
Scalability to support 100,000 concurrent users.
Decision:
Adopt a microservices architecture with cloud auto-scaling for key components.
Rationale & Trade-off:
Allows independent scaling but increases operational complexity and requires orchestration.
Validation prompt
For your riskiest decision, plan one small experiment that reduces uncertainty: a prototype, a load test, or a cost estimate exercise.
