Architecture impacts

Teams, deployment, and data

  • Team structure: services can align to autonomous teams owning features end-to-end.
  • Deployment: more components mean more pipelines, orchestration, and monitoring needs.
  • Data: distributed components require strategies for consistency and transaction management.
Example
Microservices enable independent deploys but require orchestration and monitoring investments.
Misconception
Assuming a technical pattern solves organizational problems without adjusting team structure and processes.
Decision checklist
  • What team changes will this architecture require?
  • What deployment and operational investments are needed?
  • How will data consistency be handled across boundaries?
Reflection
List three operational implications of your chosen architecture and estimate which one will require the largest investment.
