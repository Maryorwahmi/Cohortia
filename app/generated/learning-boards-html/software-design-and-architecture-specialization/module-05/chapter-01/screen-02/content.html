Distinguish architecture and design

Mnemonic: blueprint vs interior

Understand the architect's role
Translate business goals to technical structure, make core technology choices, and guide teams without losing the big picture.
Mnemonic: planner, not just coder
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Link decisions to non-functional requirements
Identify how choices affect scalability, security, performance, and maintainability so you can evaluate risk early.
Mnemonic: the "ilities" checklist
Verified Competency Milestone
How to use these objectives
Treat these as checkpoints: after reading each lesson, try to explain the idea aloud and map it to a real system you know. That active mapping helps transfer abstract architecture ideas into practical judgment.
Reflection prompt
Pick an app you use daily. For each objective, write one sentence explaining an architectural choice that enables a key feature: scale, uptime, or security.
