Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Why Architecture Matters: The 'Ilities'
Core idea

Non-functional requirements drive architecture

Identify which system qualities are most important, then choose structures that satisfy them. Each priority creates different constraints and trade-offs.

  • Scalability: partition services to scale independently.
  • Reliability: add redundancy and failure isolation.
  • Maintainability: reduce coupling and clarify boundaries.
  • Security: enforce separation and least privilege.
Concrete example
To meet high scalability, partition services so the video streaming component can scale separately from the user profile service.
Trade-off note
Splitting services increases deployment and operational complexity, which can raise cost and coordination overhead.
Key Takeaway

Rank the 'ilities' early and choose architecture to address them; that reduces late-stage rework and risk.

Checklist
  • List the most critical non-functional requirements for your product.
  • For each, propose one high-level architectural decision that would address it.
  • Record one downside or trade-off for each decision.
