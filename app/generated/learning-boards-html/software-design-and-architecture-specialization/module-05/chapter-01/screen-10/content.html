Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Monoliths vs Microservices
Comparison
Monolith
  • Single deployable unit
  • Simpler testing and deployment
  • Tighter data consistency
  • Harder to scale pieces independently
Microservices
  • Independent services and deploys
  • Allows targeted scaling
  • Requires orchestration and monitoring
  • Adds distributed data complexity
Concrete scenario
For a large video streaming function, microservices let you scale streaming without scaling unrelated components like course management.
Operational checklist
  • Can your team operate multiple deployments?
  • Do you have monitoring and orchestration capabilities?
  • Is the business benefit of independent scaling clear and justified?
