Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Software Architecture
The fundamental organization of a system embodied in components, relationships, and design principles.
Architectural Decisions
High-level choices about structure and properties that impact non-functional requirements.
Non-Functional Requirements
Qualities like scalability, security, performance, and maintainability that drive architectural choices.
Summary takeaway
Architecture should be documented, validated early, and tied explicitly to prioritized non-functional requirements to reduce long-term risk.
