Practice checklist
  1. Define architecture and draw a simple component diagram capturing major parts and their relationships.
  2. Contrast an architectural choice with two concrete design decisions that follow from it.
  3. Write a short rationale for a technology choice tied to an NFR such as low latency.
  4. Map each NFR to one architectural decision and note a possible downside for each choice.
Verification tips
  • Keep exercises short and focused: a single paragraph or a one-box sketch is enough for verification.
  • Use a real or imagined product, like an online learning platform, to ground decisions in realistic constraints.
  • Share your brief rationale with a peer or mentor to check whether it communicates the architectural intent clearly.
