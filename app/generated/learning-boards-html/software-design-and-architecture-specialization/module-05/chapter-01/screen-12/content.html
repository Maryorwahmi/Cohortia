Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Documenting and Communicating Architecture
Documentation essentials
  • Component diagrams showing boundaries and interactions.
  • Architectural decision records capturing choices, rationale, and trade-offs.
  • Annotated NFRs linked to the decisions they drive.
Concrete format
Short records: decision title, chosen option, rationale, expected impact, and known downsides. Keep them concise so teams read and share them.
Key Takeaway

Document decisions with rationale and link them to prioritized qualities to keep teams aligned and decisions durable.

Communication practices
  • Share decision records in a common repository and reference them in code reviews.
  • Use diagrams to communicate boundaries during onboarding and planning.
  • Keep documentation concise and linked to work items so it stays relevant.
Reflection
Choose one past architectural decision and draft a one-paragraph record that explains the why, the chosen option, and the main trade-off.
