Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Making Architectural Decisions
Decision steps
  1. Gather and prioritize non-functional requirements.
  2. Propose candidate architectures or styles to meet priorities.
  3. Analyze benefits, costs, and operational complexity for each candidate.
  4. Prototype or validate the riskiest assumptions early.
  5. Document the chosen decision and the rationale for future teams.
Concrete example
To support massive concurrency, one candidate is microservices with cloud auto-scaling; evaluate operational complexity and team readiness before committing.
Trade-off matrix
Benefit
Independent scaling, team autonomy.
Cost
Increased deployment complexity and operational overhead.
Validation tip
Prototype the highest-risk component or run a small-scale simulation to verify the chosen approach before full adoption.
