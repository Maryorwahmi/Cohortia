Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Utility Tree — Building
Structure
Top-level attributes then specific, measurable scenarios beneath each, ordered by importance.
Example fragment
Performance → Page response under 300 ms for 95% of requests.
Availability → Failover completes within 30 seconds, 99.99% monthly uptime.
Keep it short
Prioritize the top 5–8 scenarios so evaluation time targets the most critical business risks.
Using the tree
  1. Elicit attributes from stakeholders.
  2. Generate measurable scenarios for each attribute.
  3. Prioritize scenarios in collaboration with stakeholders.
  4. Use top scenarios to drive the ATAM analysis.
Check
Is your top scenario testable and time-bounded? If not, refine it until concrete.
