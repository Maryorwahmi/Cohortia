Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › From Findings to Recommendations
Prioritize
Rank risks by business impact and likelihood, and focus recommendations on the highest-priority items.
Recommendation template
Risk statement → Proposed mitigation → Trade-offs → Measurable success criteria → Estimated effort/cost.
Example recommendation
Mitigation: Deploy CDN and edge replication to mitigate growth risk. Trade-offs: extra cost, added deployment complexity. Success: 95% of viewers see less than 3 seconds buffering during peak events.
Key Takeaway
Recommendations must be measurable and prioritized; otherwise teams cannot translate findings into action.
