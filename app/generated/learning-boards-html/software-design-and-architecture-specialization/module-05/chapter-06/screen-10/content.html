Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Trade-off Analysis
Two-column trade-off list
Benefits
  • Improved throughput
  • Better availability
  • Stronger security
Costs / Trade-offs
  • Higher operational complexity
  • Increased latency in some paths
  • Greater maintenance expense
Key Takeaway

Document trade-offs clearly so decisions map to stakeholder priorities and acceptable compromises.

Sensitivity points
Look for single choices that disproportionately affect outcomes, such as central datastore, synchronous contracts, or third-party dependency choices.
Exercise
Pick one sensitivity point in your design. List 2 mitigations and the trade-offs each introduces.
