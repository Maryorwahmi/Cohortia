Evaluating Software Architecture
Assess architecture early to expose risks, trade-offs, and sensitivity points that affect non-functional requirements.
Orientation

This chapter teaches you to use scenario-based evaluation to check whether an architecture meets critical quality attributes. We'll focus on SAAM and ATAM and practice finding trade-offs using a utility tree and scenario walkthroughs.

In this lesson
Find risks, prioritize quality attributes, and document trade-offs before implementation.
