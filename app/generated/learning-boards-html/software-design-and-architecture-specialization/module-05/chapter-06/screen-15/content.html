Stakeholder alignment
Include product, security, operations, and performance voices to reveal relevant priorities and constraints.
Measurable scenarios
Use numbers and time bounds so analysis yields actionable findings.
Iterate often
Re-run evaluations when architecture or business priorities change to keep risk profiles current.
