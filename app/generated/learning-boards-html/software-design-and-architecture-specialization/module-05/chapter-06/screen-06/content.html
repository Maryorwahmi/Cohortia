Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › ATAM — Key Steps
ATAM sequence
1. Presentation of ATAM — set expectations and process.
2. Presentation of the Architecture — business drivers and proposed design.
3. Identification of Architectural Approaches — key patterns and decisions.
4. Elicitation of Utility Tree — prioritize measurable quality attribute scenarios.
5. Analysis of Approaches — test approaches against prioritized scenarios.
6. Identification of Trade-offs, Sensitivity Points, Risks & Non-Risks.
7. Presentation of Results — share findings and recommendations.
Notes and examples
A utility tree example: prioritize 'high availability' then break into scenarios like 'failover within 30s' or '99.99% uptime per month'. These measurable scenarios guide analysis.
Common mistake
Skipping stakeholder elicitation or using vague scenarios weakens the entire analysis.
Pro-tip
Make scenarios measurable and time-bounded to produce actionable results.
