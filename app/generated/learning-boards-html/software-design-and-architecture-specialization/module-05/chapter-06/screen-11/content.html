Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Scenario-Based Risk ID
Starter scenarios
Growth: 10x concurrent viewers during a live event within 12 months.
Failure: Primary video server fails during peak hours; system must continue serving content for 5 minutes.
Risks identified
Growth: single server bottleneck causing buffering and outages. Failure: single point of failure causing service disruption.
Mitigations & trade-offs
Growth mitigation: Add CDN and edge caching. Trade-off: added cost and complexity.
Failure mitigation: Replicate servers and implement failover. Trade-off: increased replication and operational overhead.
Check
Do your mitigations include measurable success criteria (e.g., 95% of viewers experience less than 3s buffering)? If not, refine them.
