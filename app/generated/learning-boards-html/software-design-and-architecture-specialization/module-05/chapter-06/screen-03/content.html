Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Learning Objectives — Applied
Demonstrable tasks
Each objective maps to a short artefact you can present in a review meeting.
Scenario list
At least 4 scenarios: one use case, one growth, one change, one failure. Make them measurable and time-bounded.
Utility tree sketch
Break attributes into prioritized scenarios with acceptance criteria to focus analysis.
Risk report
List sensitivity points, likely failures, and whether they are risks or non-risks.
Mitigation suggestions
High-level architectural changes mapped to scenarios and trade-offs.
Tip
Focus on measurable scenarios. A vague "scale well" scenario is less useful than "support 10x concurrent users in 6 months."
