Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Evaluation Checklist
Session checklist
  1. Gather business drivers, constraints, and architecture diagrams.
  2. Invite a cross-functional stakeholder set and an independent evaluation team if possible.
  3. Elicit scenarios and build a prioritized utility tree.
  4. Analyze top scenarios, identify sensitivity points and trade-offs.
  5. Deliver prioritized findings with measurable recommendations and estimated effort.
Session tip
Limit the session to the top 5 scenarios to keep analysis focused and actionable.
Reflection
Which stakeholder view will you prioritize in the next evaluation, and how will you measure success?
