Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Architectural Evaluation
Systematic process to assess suitability, find risks, and guide decisions before coding.
SAAM vs ATAM
SAAM focuses on modifiability; ATAM analyzes trade-offs and produces a utility tree and risk profile.
Utility Tree
A prioritized, measurable breakdown of quality attributes into scenarios that drive analysis.
Summary

Evaluate early with prioritized scenarios, record trade-offs, and produce measurable recommendations to guide stakeholders.
