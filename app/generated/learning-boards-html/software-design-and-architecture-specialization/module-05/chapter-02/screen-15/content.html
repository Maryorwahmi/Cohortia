Monolithic
Best for small, quick projects; redeploy whole app for changes.
Layered
Separate presentation, logic, and data; improves testing and maintainability.
Client-Server
Centralize services on servers; build resilient clients for network variability.
Final takeaway

Use this guide as a checklist: pick the style that best matches your constraints for delivery speed, scale, and security.
