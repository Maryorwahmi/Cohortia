Architectural Styles and Patterns
Foundational choices that shape how components interact, evolve, and scale.

Architectural styles provide repeatable structures for organizing components and their interactions. They are not implementation details but guides that influence testing, deployment, and team structure.

This chapter focuses on three: Monolithic, Layered (N-tier), and Client-Server. Each offers trade-offs between initial simplicity, separation of concerns, distribution, and scalability.
