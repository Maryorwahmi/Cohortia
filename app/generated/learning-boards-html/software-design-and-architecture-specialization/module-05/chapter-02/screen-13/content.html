Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Scenario Activity
Scenario 1
Small internal tool for a five-person team with no expected growth.
Style: Monolithic. Reasoning: Rapid development and low operational overhead make a single deployable unit appropriate.
Scenario 2
Online banking requiring UI, business rules, and secure database separation.
Style: Layered. Reasoning: Separation supports rigorous testing, security isolation, and maintainability.
Scenario 3
Mobile app that queries product data and places orders via a backend.
Style: Client-Server. Reasoning: Centralized services and heterogeneous clients are best handled by a networked server.
