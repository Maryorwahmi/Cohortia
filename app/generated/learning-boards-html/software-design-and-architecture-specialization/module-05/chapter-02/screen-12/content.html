Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Choosing an Architecture
Decision checklist
  1. Is quick delivery more important than long-term scaling?
  2. Do you need strict separation for security or compliance?
  3. Will clients be heterogeneous and require networked services?
  4. What is the expected growth and peak load profile?
Scenario guidance
Small internal tool: Monolithic for rapid development and minimal ops.
Online banking: Layered for separation of concerns, maintainability, and security isolation.
Mobile app with backend: Client-server to centralize data and allow thin clients.
Key Takeaway

Align architecture with the constraints that most affect success: delivery speed, security, and scaling needs.
