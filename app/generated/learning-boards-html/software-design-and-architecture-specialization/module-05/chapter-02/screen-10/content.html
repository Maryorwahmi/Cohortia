Client-server basics

Clients send requests; servers respond and manage shared resources like databases, files, or services. Clients may be thin (UI only) or thick (local processing).

Benefits
  • Centralized control for security and backups.
  • Heterogeneous implementations across clients and servers.
  • Flexible client capabilities depending on needs.
Concerns and mistakes
  • Server bottlenecks if not scaled properly.
  • Network latency affecting client experience.
  • Neglecting authentication and authorization.
Warning

Assuming perfect network conditions leads to fragile clients; always plan for timeouts and retries.
