Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts
Architectural Style
A reusable structural solution for common problems; defines component organization and interactions.
Monolithic
All components deployed as a single unit; easy to start, harder to scale selectively.
Layered
Presentation, Business Logic, Data Access; promotes separation of concerns and testability.
Client-Server
Clients request services; servers provide centralized data and control. Enables heterogeneity, requires network planning.
Separation of Concerns
A design principle recommending distinct sections of a program address separate concerns. It underpins layered architecture and helps localize change and testing.
Takeaway

Match architecture to constraints: pick the style that aligns with your project's needs for speed, scale, and security.
