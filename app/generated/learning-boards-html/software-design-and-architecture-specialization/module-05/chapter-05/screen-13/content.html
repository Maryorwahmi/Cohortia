Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
ADR quick template
Title: Clear, short description of the decision.
Status: Accepted, proposed, deprecated.
Context: Why this decision is under consideration.
Decision: The chosen solution and brief justification.
Alternatives: Other options considered and why they were rejected.
Consequences: Positive and negative outcomes to watch for.
Example excerpt
Title: Use PostgreSQL for primary data storage. Context: Need ACID guarantees for orders. Decision: Use PostgreSQL for its transactional guarantees and extensibility. Alternatives: MySQL, NoSQL solutions. Consequences: Better consistency, some operational complexity.
Practice

Draft an ADR for a message queue choice and store it with your repo. Link it to a related pull request.
