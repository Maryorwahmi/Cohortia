Sample questions

  • Which quality is addressed by keeping the system operational when a server fails? Explain availability and fault tolerance.
  • Which quality improves with microservices and auto-scaling? Describe added operational concerns.
  • Trade-off prompt: low latency vs strong encryption—propose mitigations and estimate costs.
Grading guidance

Look for precise naming, measurable targets, and reasoned trade-off analysis. Answers should include mitigation options and potential secondary impacts on cost or maintainability.
