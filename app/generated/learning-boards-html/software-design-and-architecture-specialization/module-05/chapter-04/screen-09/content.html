Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Reliability
Definition

Availability, fault tolerance, recoverability

Design systems to continue operating under component failures and recover quickly when outages occur.

Reliability patterns
  • Redundancy and multi-zone deployment
  • Health checks and automated failover
  • Circuit breakers and bulkheads
Example

Multiple service instances across zones with a load balancer and automated failover preserve availability when a node or zone fails.

Checks & exercises
Chaos testing

Intentionally fail components to verify failover, alerting, and recovery. This reveals hidden dependencies and recovery gaps.

Common mistake

Assuming replication alone equals reliability. Without health checks, failover automation, and recovery playbooks, replication can give a false sense of safety.
