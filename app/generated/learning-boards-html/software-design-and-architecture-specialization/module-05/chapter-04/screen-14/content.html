Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
NFRs
Attributes like performance, scalability, security, maintainability, reliability, and usability describe how a system behaves under real conditions.
Metrics
Measure latency percentiles, throughput, and availability to convert qualities into actionable targets for architecture decisions.
Trade-offs
Architects must balance conflicting attributes and document impacts, costs, and mitigations clearly for stakeholders.
Summary

Use measurable targets and scenario-based trade-off analysis to design architectures aligned with business priorities.
