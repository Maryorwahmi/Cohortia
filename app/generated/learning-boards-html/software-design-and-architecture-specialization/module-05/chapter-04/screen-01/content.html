Architectural Qualities

Explore the non-functional attributes that shape architecture: performance, scalability, security, maintainability, reliability, and usability. Learn how choices move these levers and why trade-offs are inevitable.

In this lesson

Define key quality attributes, examine measurement approaches, and practice analyzing trade-offs for real design decisions such as a trading platform or medical records system.

Why it matters

Ignoring NFRs produces systems that are slow, insecure, hard to change, or expensive to run. Good architecture balances priorities against business goals.
