Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Activity

Task checklist

  1. Name your top three qualities for the trading platform and justify them.
  2. For each, identify one conflicting quality and explain the tension.
  3. Describe at least one mitigation and estimate its effect on latency, cost, or complexity.
Example snippet

Priority: low latency. Conflict: security. Mitigation: selective encryption and hardware offload to reduce CPU overhead while protecting critical fields.

What to deliver

A short memo: prioritized qualities, conflicts, measurable impacts, recommended mitigations, and an ordering of options by cost and operational risk.

Pro tip

Quantify expected latency change, cost delta, and complexity increase for each mitigation where possible to make recommendations persuasive.
