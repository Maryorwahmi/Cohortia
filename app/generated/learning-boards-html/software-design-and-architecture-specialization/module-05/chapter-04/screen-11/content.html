Pair examples

Performance vs Security

Encrypting all data increases CPU and latency. Mitigations: selective encryption, hardware offload, or encrypting at boundaries.

Scalability vs Consistency

Relaxing consistency enables higher availability and scale. Mitigations include compensating logic and eventual consistency patterns.

Maintainability vs Performance

Abstraction eases change but can add overhead. Profile hot paths and keep performance-critical code streamlined.

Decision checklist

  • State the prioritized qualities and why they matter to the business.
  • List architectural options and the expected impact on each quality.
  • Estimate cost and operational complexity for each option.
  • Propose mitigations for weakened qualities.
Common mistake

Failing to document trade-offs and their expected operational impacts makes future changes risky and stakeholder conversations vague.
