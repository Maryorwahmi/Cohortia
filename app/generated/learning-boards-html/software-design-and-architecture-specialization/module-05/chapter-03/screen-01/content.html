SOA contrast
Coarse-grained services, often an ESB, centralized governance.
Explore Key Topics
Microservices enable independent deployments and scaling. They are best when business domains can be separated and teams can own services.
Event-Driven Architecture decouples producers and consumers via events and brokers, improving extensibility but introducing eventual consistency.
SOA differs mainly in granularity and centralized communication; microservices favor decentralization and smaller services.
