Microservices Architecture
An application composed of small, independently deployable services, each owning a specific business capability and its data.
Event-Driven Architecture
Systems communicate by publishing and subscribing to events via a broker, enabling loose coupling and extensibility.
Service-Oriented Architecture
An earlier style with coarser services and often centralized integration through an ESB; contrasts with microservices' decentralization.
Summary takeaway
Choose patterns to match domain boundaries and team maturity, and invest in observability and deployment automation so the architecture’s benefits are realized in practice.
