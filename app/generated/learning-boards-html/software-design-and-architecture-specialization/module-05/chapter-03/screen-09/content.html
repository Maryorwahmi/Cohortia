Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Microservices vs SOA
Microservices
  • Fine-grained services, each owning data and capabilities.
  • Decentralized governance and technology diversity.
  • Lightweight communication, independent deployment.
SOA
  • Coarse-grained services, often enterprise scoped.
  • Centralized governance and an Enterprise Service Bus for integration.
  • Focus on reuse across organizational boundaries.
Example
SOA: ESB handles transformations. Microservices: each service handles its own integration or uses events to decouple transformations.
Misconception
Thinking SOA is obsolete. It’s a different set of trade-offs, still relevant for certain enterprise governance models.
Key takeaway
Choose based on desired granularity, governance model, and organizational readiness for operational complexity.
