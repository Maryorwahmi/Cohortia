Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Assessment: Refactor Decision
Decision criteria
  • Team autonomy: Do teams need independent release cycles?
  • Bounded contexts: Can the domain be split into cohesive services?
  • Operational maturity: Are CI/CD, monitoring, and tracing in place?
Prototype one service and measure the operational cost. If the prototype shows acceptable overhead and clear gains in deployment velocity, scale the approach cautiously.
Checklist
  1. Sketch service boundaries.
  2. Plan data ownership and migration.
  3. Build CI/CD and observability for one service.
Key takeaway
Treat the migration as an operational program, not a code rewrite. Operational capabilities enable the architectural benefits.
