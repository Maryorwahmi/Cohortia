Message Broker
Routes published events to subscribers and can provide durability, but does not automatically handle idempotency or business logic.
Loose Coupling
Design services and events so components can be developed and deployed independently, relying on contracts rather than implementations.
Ordering & Idempotency
Plan for event ordering when necessary and implement idempotent handlers to tolerate duplicates and retries.
Final thought
Use these compact concepts as a checklist during design reviews: broker, coupling, and delivery guarantees keep distributed systems understandable and reliable.
