Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Advantages of Microservices
Benefits
Independent deployment reduces coordination overhead and enables faster releases.
Selective scaling lets you allocate resources only where needed, lowering cost for variable loads.
Technology heterogeneity permits choosing optimal tools per service, improving fit for purpose.
Concrete example
If order volume surges, scale the Order Service independently rather than the entire platform, keeping user and catalog services stable.
Operational costs
  • Distributed tracing, logging, and monitoring become essential.
  • Service discovery, authentication, and network resilience are required engineering work.
  • Cross-service consistency requires patterns like sagas or compensation logic.
Common mistake
Splitting too early leads to tight runtime coupling and complex deployments.
Pro tip
Invest in CI/CD, observability, and failure handling before you split a monolith.
Key Takeaway

Microservices deliver autonomy and scalable deployments but require substantial operational investment to manage communication, consistency, and observability.
