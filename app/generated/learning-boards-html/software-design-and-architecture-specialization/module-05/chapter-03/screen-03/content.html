Verification tasks
Analyze Microservices
Task: Decompose a monolith endpoint into services, list owned data, and propose independent deploy steps.
Understand EDA
Task: Publish an OrderCreated event to a mock broker and list subscribers with actions.
Compare with SOA
Task: Create a short comparison table for granularity, governance, and typical tooling.
Identify Use Cases
Task: Choose a feature, argue for microservices or EDA, and name at least one operational control required.
Tip: Keep tasks small and concrete. Small victories build the confidence to handle operational complexity later.
Visual mnemonic
Think of the objectives as a four-step checklist: Decompose, Publish, Compare, and Choose. Each step maps to a small deliverable you can verify in a demo or design document.
