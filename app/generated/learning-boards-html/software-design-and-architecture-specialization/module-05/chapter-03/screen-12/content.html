Essential tooling
  • Distributed tracing to connect events and calls across services.
  • Centralized logging and metrics to correlate incidents and monitor health.
  • Service discovery and API contracts to manage dynamic endpoints and compatibility.
  • CI/CD and deployment orchestration for safe, independent service releases.
Event broker ops
Durability, dead-letter queues, replay mechanisms, and consumer lag monitoring are essential for reliable EDA operations.
Example
If consumers fall behind, alert on lag and plan to replay events from durable storage so state can be reconstructed.
Why it matters

Operational investment converts architectural freedom into reliable, observable systems. Without it, microservices and EDA create more problems than they solve.
