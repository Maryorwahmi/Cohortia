Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Locks vs Atomics vs Volatile
Locks (synchronized)
Guarantee mutual exclusion for arbitrary critical sections; easy to reason about for complex invariants.
Pros: flexible, clear semantics.
Cons: contention, risk of deadlock if order is inconsistent.
Atomic types
Lock-free atomic operations for common patterns like counters or references. Fast and safe for those operations only.
Pros: efficient, no locks.
Cons: limited to supported atomic operations.
Volatile
Ensures visibility and ordering of a single variable across threads, but does not make compound actions atomic.
Pros: lightweight visibility guarantees.
Cons: not a substitute for locks or atomic operations.
Key Takeaway

Choose the smallest synchronization primitive that satisfies your correctness requirement to keep design simple and scalable.

Check your understanding
Would you use a lock or an atomic type to update a pair of related fields that must remain consistent? Prefer a lock to protect the invariant across both fields.
