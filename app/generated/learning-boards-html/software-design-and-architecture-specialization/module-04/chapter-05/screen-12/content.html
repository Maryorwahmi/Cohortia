Design: high-volume ingestion to slow database
Use Producer-Consumer with a bounded BlockingQueue to decouple ingestion from writes and provide backpressure.
Producer: ingests quickly, enqueues items, may block when full.
Buffer: bounded queue, enforces backpressure and synchronization.
Consumer: dequeues and writes to DB at its own pace, can retry on failure.
Key Takeaway

Explain roles, queue sizing trade-offs, safety notes like idempotency, and what to monitor: queue length and consumer lag.
