Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Safety Notes
Immutability
Favor objects that do not change after creation. They are inherently thread-safe and simplify reasoning.
Minimize critical sections
Keep synchronized blocks as small as possible to reduce contention and lower deadlock risk.
Higher-level primitives
Use BlockingQueue, executors, and other concurrent utilities instead of raw locks when appropriate.
Visibility and ordering
Use volatile, synchronized, or atomics to ensure updates by one thread are visible to others.
Lock ordering & timeouts
Establish global lock order and use timeouts to detect potential deadlocks and allow recovery.
Testing habit
Write reproducible concurrency tests and run them repeatedly under stress to reveal timing bugs.
Practical note
High-throughput paths may need specialized approaches; measure and prefer lock-free primitives for hot counters or metrics.
