Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Producer-Consumer Pattern
Pattern anatomy
Producers, a shared buffer, and consumers cooperate. The buffer enforces synchronization and backpressure when bounded.
Producer: creates items, enqueues them, may block if buffer full.
Shared Buffer: a queue with blocking semantics for full or empty states.
Consumer: dequeues items and processes them, may block if buffer empty.
Design choices
Decide bounded vs unbounded, the capacity, and how to handle blocking or rejection when full.
Bounded: predictable memory, backpressure
Unbounded: simpler but risk of OOM
Blocking behavior: natural coordination
Key Takeaway

Use a bounded blocking queue to decouple rates and apply backpressure instead of busy waiting.
