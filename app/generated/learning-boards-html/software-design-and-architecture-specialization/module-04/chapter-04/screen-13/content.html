Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Applying Patterns
Step 1: Identify
Find the largest conditional or ordered checks that drive behavior or processing stages.
Step 2: Choose
If behavior varies by internal mode → State. If pipeline of independent checks → Chain.
Step 3: Extract
Pull out one branch as a state or handler, add tests, then repeat until all logic is refactored or clear trade-offs appear.
Final thought

The goal is clearer responsibilities and easier extension. Use tests to ensure behavior stays correct while you replace conditionals with states or handlers.
