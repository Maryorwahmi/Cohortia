Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Chain Structure
Handler Interface
abstract Logger

Declares setNextLogger and logMessage. Keeps a LogLevel and reference to the next handler in the chain.

Concrete Handlers
ConsoleLogger, FileLogger, EmailLogger

Each implements write() with handler-specific behavior. They decide based on level whether to write and may forward after writing.

Client
Chain builder & caller

Construct the chain by linking handlers and send requests to the first handler to start processing.

Design question
Should a handler stop propagation after handling? Decide based on whether multiple handlers should process the same request.
Operational tip
Ensure chains do not silently drop requests: include a default rejection or final logger to capture unhandled requests.
