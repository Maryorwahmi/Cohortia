Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Anti-patterns
Over-engineering
Don't extract a strategy for behavior that will not change; watch maintenance cost vs benefit.
Poor interface design
Avoid large, unfocused interfaces that force strategies to implement irrelevant methods.
Context coupling
Never let the context downcast or rely on concrete strategy internals; that defeats the purpose of decoupling.
Common Mistake

Creating too many micro-strategies that differ by one constant often increases cognitive load more than it helps.
