Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Benefits & Pitfalls
Benefits
  • • Reduces branching and central complexity.
  • • Enables runtime algorithm selection.
  • • Follows Open/Closed Principle: add strategies without modifying Context.
  • • Improves testability by isolating algorithms.
Pitfalls
  • • Over-engineering: strategy for a single stable behavior.
  • • Poor granularity: strategies too broad or too narrow.
  • • Leaking implementation details through the interface.
Pro Tip

If adding a new algorithm causes edits to multiple classes, Strategy can reduce that friction. If not, consider simpler alternatives.
