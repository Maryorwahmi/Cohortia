When readers release locks, the writer acquires writeLock and executes its update alone, blocking further readers until done.

Misconception & trade-off

Believing read locks defer all complexity. Fairness settings prevent writer starvation but may reduce throughput. Choose based on acceptable write latency.

Key Takeaway

Model runtime arrival patterns to decide lock fairness and expected write latency.
