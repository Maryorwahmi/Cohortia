Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › When to use DI
Pragmatic DI rules
Balance benefit and complexity when introducing DI.
When to inject
External services, databases, and algorithms that may change or must be mocked for tests.
Constructor vs setter
Constructor for required dependencies. Setter for optional or late-bound services.
Composition root
Centralize wiring at application startup so composition is explicit and testable.
Small interfaces
Keep abstractions focused to keep implementations and mocks simple.
Avoid over-abstraction
Don't create interfaces for trivial classes that are stable and unlikely to change.
Key Takeaway

Use DI strategically for boundaries that change or require tests, and keep wiring explicit and centralized.
