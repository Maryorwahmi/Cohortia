Practical benefits of DIP and DI
How applying the principle changes your development lifecycle.
Loose coupling
Business code depends on interfaces not implementations, limiting impact when infrastructure changes.
Testability
Inject mocks or stubs to test business logic quickly and deterministically.
Extensibility
Add new implementations without touching high-level modules.
Key Takeaway

Prefer small, stable contracts and use DI to supply implementations; it simplifies tests and future changes.
