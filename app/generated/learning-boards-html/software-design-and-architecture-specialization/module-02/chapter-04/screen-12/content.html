Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Assessment: PaymentProcessor
Assessment idea — Payment gateway
Identify DIP violation and propose a refactor using an abstraction and injection.
Violation
PaymentProcessor creates StripeGateway directly, coupling business logic to a specific provider.
Refactor
Define IPaymentGateway.process_payment(amount). Implement concrete gateways and inject the chosen implementation into PaymentProcessor via the constructor.
Testing
Inject a stub gateway to simulate success and failure paths reliably in unit tests.
Evaluation checklist
Did you identify the concrete dependency? Did you propose a focused interface? Did you show injection at composition time?
Key Takeaway

Good answers remove direct instantiation, introduce a small interface, and inject implementations so business logic does not change when providers change.
