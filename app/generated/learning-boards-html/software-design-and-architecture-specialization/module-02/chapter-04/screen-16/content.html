Key Concepts — Continued
Three short cards to finish.
Abstraction
An interface or abstract class defining required behavior for dependents.
Inversion of Control
Delegate creation and lifecycle of dependencies to an external composer or framework.
Dependency Injection
A technique to supply dependencies from outside, commonly via constructors, enabling IoC.
Takeaway

Use abstractions and injection to keep business logic stable, testable, and easy to extend by adding implementations instead of changing policy code.
