Key Concepts
Three compact reminders of core ideas.
Dependency Inversion Principle
High-level and low-level modules should depend on abstractions.
High-level Module
Contains business logic or policy; depends on contracts rather than implementations.
Low-level Module
Implementation detail like database access or I/O that should implement abstractions.
Summary

When in doubt, introduce a small interface at the boundary and inject implementations at wiring time to keep business logic stable and testable.
