Common mistakes and safety guidance
Practical traps to watch for when applying DIP and DI.
Direct instantiation
High-level modules creating low-level objects creates tight coupling and brittle designs.
Injecting concrete classes
If you inject a concrete type you still depend on a detail. Define a small interface instead.
Over-engineering
Avoid creating interfaces for objects that are simple and unlikely to change.
Service locator anti-pattern
A global locator hides dependencies and makes testing more difficult.
Safety note

Use DI where dependencies are likely to change or require stubbing for tests. Keep interfaces focused and centralize wiring at composition time.
