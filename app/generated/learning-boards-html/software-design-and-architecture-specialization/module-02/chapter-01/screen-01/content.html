Reviewing Core OOP Principles

Understand the four foundational OOP concepts and how they combine to produce modular, flexible, and maintainable systems. This chapter links theory to a Python payment example you will inspect.

In this lesson

Define each principle, inspect code that uses them, and practise a small design for a payment processor.

Why it matters

Good OOP reduces bugs, clarifies intent, and lets teams extend systems without fragile changes.

Common pitfalls

Watch for overexposed state, deep inheritance chains, and confusing abstraction with obfuscation.
