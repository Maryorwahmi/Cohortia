Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Key Concepts
Design by Contract
Formal assertions of preconditions, postconditions, and invariants inside trusted code.
Defensive Programming
Validate and handle external inputs, provide safe defaults, and recover gracefully from errors.
Layered approach
Combine boundary validation with internal contracts to balance resilience and debuggability.
Final takeaway

Place checks where they belong: assert internal contracts to find developer bugs quickly, and defend at boundaries to handle the messy real world.
