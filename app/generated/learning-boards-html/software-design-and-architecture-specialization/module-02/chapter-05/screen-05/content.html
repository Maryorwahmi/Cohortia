Process: Defining Preconditions
  1. Identify the minimal conditions required by the operation, e.g., amount > 0.
  2. Document the expectation in the method contract and in unit tests that cover violations.
  3. Use assertions to fail fast during development and testing, making the origin of a bug obvious.
  4. Keep checks narrow: avoid over-validating internal data that trusted modules already guarantee.
Example
deposit(amount) asserts amount > 0. A unit test that passes -1 confirms the assertion triggers and pinpoints the bad caller.
Supporting notes
  • Why it matters: Precise responsibility reduces duplicated checks and narrows debugging scope.
  • Misconception: Preconditions do not replace boundary validation for untrusted clients.
  • Pro tip: Combine boundary validation with internal assertions for layered safety.
