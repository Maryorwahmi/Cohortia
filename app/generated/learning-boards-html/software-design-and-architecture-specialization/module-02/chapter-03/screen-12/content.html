Fat interface smell
IMultiFunctionDevice forces all devices to implement unrelated operations leading to dummy methods on some implementations.
Solution: split into IPrinter, IScanner, IFaxMachine and let implementers choose what they need.
Composition approach
Advanced devices can be composed from small components each implementing a narrow interface, making each concern replaceable and testable independently.
Result: clients that only need printing depend only on the printing contract and are unaffected by changes to scanning or fax.
