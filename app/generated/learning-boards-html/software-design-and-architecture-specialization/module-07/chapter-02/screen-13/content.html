Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Mistakes & Tips
Common mistakes
  • Vague attributes without measurable response measures.
  • Omitting environment or assumptions that affect evaluation.
  • Allowing one stakeholder to dominate priorities.
Pro tips
  • Treat exploratory scenarios as experiments to reveal limits, not hypotheticals to dismiss.
  • Use a simple scoring matrix for impact and likelihood to justify prioritization.
  • Refine scenarios after prototyping — scenarios are living artifacts.
Key Takeaway

Enforce structure and measurable responses to make scenarios useful for evaluation and design.
