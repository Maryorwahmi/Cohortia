Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Prioritizing Scenarios
Prioritization rubric
Evaluate each scenario for business impact and likelihood. Use a simple score or matrix to separate must-address scenarios from lower-priority ones.
High impact
Scenarios affecting revenue, safety, or major user flows rank high.
Likelihood
Practical frequency of occurrence or exposure.
Refinement checklist
  • Does the scenario include environment and response measure?
  • Are assumptions explicitly recorded?
  • Can we write acceptance tests or probes from it?
  • Does it map to components or interfaces to evaluate?
Result
A compact, prioritized scenario set suitable for architecture probes, risk analysis, and design trade-offs.
