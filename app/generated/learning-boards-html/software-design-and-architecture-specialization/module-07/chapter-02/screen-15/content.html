Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
Quality Attribute
A measurable, non-functional characteristic such as performance or availability that guides design choices.
QAW
A facilitated workshop to elicit, structure, and prioritize scenarios from diverse stakeholders for evaluation.
Scenario Structure
Use Source, Stimulus, Artifact, Environment, Response, Response Measure to make scenarios testable.
Summary
Well-formed, prioritized scenarios are the single most effective bridge between stakeholder concerns and architecture decisions. Keep them measurable and revisit them as the design evolves.
