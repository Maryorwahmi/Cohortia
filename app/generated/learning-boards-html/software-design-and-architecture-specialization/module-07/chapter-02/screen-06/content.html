Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Eliciting Scenarios
Operational (Use Case)
Describes normal system behavior under typical conditions. Example: a user logs in and sees their dashboard within two seconds.
Purpose
Validate everyday performance and correctness.
Growth (Evolutionary)
Describes how the system must adapt to predictable changes. Example: support a 50% increase in concurrent users within six months without major redesign.
Purpose
Assess architecture readiness for expansion and change.
Exploratory (Stress)
Describes behavior under extreme or failure conditions. Example: automatic failover within 30 seconds after a primary database failure.
Purpose
Reveal resilience, failure modes, and recovery expectations.
Facilitation note
Ensure the QAW solicits at least one scenario of each type. Skipping exploratory cases leaves critical risks undiscovered; skipping growth cases hides long-term costs.
