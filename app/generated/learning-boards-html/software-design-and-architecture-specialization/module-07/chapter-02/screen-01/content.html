Quality Attribute Workshops and Scenario Design
Turn non-functional goals into measurable scenarios so architecture decisions are grounded in real expectations and trade-offs.
In this lesson
Define quality attributes, run a QAW that elicits scenarios, write structured scenarios, and use scenarios to evaluate architecture choices.
Why it matters
Without scenarios, non-functional needs are vague. Scenarios let teams measure success and spot conflicts early, reducing costly rework.
