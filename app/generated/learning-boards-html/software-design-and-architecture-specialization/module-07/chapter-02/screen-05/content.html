Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › QAW Overview
Workshop flow
Step 1
Introduce quality attributes and the goal of the workshop.
Step 2
Brainstorm attribute candidates and propose scenario ideas with context.
Step 3
Refine scenarios to include measures and environmental conditions.
Step 4
Prioritize scenarios by impact and likelihood.
Key facilitation tips
  • Encourage concrete examples, not abstract labels. Ask "how will we measure success?"
  • Include diverse stakeholders so operational and business perspectives appear.
  • Prevent vague acceptance: require environment and response measures for each scenario.
  • Use prioritization to focus subsequent design and evaluation work.
Outcome
A prioritized, measurable scenario set that architects can use to probe design.
Common mistake
Accepting generic attributes without scenario context, which leaves design choices unconstrained.
