ATAM Simulation: Global IoT Ingestion
Analyze the centralized queue under burst conditions
Scenario
Sensors burst to 10x rate. Artifact: centralized message queue. Environment: global, high-latency regions. Response: all data ingested <0.1% loss, <5s latency.
Walkthrough questions
What happens on queue backlog? How is persistence guaranteed? Does backpressure propagate to sensors or producers?
Identified Risk
Centralized queue as single point of failure for availability and throughput.
Sensitivity Point
Broker throughput and partition configuration strongly affect latency under burst.
Trade-off
Regional sharding reduces latency but raises operational complexity and consistency concerns.
Recommended next step
Prototype regional ingestion gateways or add queue replication and measure latency and loss under load.
