Phase 3: Test, Prioritize & Report
Consolidate findings and recommend concrete next steps
Brainstorm
Ensure all risks, sensitivities, and trade-offs are captured with evidence from walkthroughs.
Prioritize
Rank items by severity and likelihood to focus mitigation efforts.
Report
Deliver a short, actionable report linking findings to business drivers and owners.
Recommended action example
Introduce regional ingestion points and replicate queues to mitigate centralized-queue availability risk.
Common mistake
Delivering a long, unranked list that stakeholders cannot operationalize.
