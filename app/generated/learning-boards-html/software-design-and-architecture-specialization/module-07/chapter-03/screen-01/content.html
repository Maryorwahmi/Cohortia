Architecture Trade-off Analysis Method (ATAM)
Formal, scenario-driven evaluation that links architectural decisions to quality attributes
ATAM helps teams move beyond opinion by using prioritized scenarios and a structured walkthrough. It surfaces where architecture supports business goals and where it risks failure. Expect concrete recommendations and prioritized risks at the end.
