Interpreting an ATAM Finding
Classify a finding and recommend a reasoned response
Prompt (summary)
In-memory DB yields ultra-low latency but complicates durability and recovery after crashes.
Task
Classify the finding, justify your classification, and propose a mitigation that balances attributes.
Example answer
Classification: Trade-off. Mitigation: add periodic durable checkpoints or a write-ahead log to restore durability with limited latency impact.
Misconception to avoid
Do not label this as a sensitivity point; it is a deliberate trade-off that sacrifices one attribute for another.
