Phase 1: Presentation & Understanding
Set expectations, business drivers, and architecture context
Step 1
Present ATAM
Lead explains goals, timebox, roles, and deliverables to align expectations.
Step 2
Present Business Drivers
Business stakeholders describe priorities that determine scenario importance.
Step 3
Present Architecture
Architect explains key components, patterns, and assumptions for the evaluation to inspect.
Practical tip
Record assumptions and confirm a prioritized set of quality attributes before analysis begins.
Common mistake
Skipping a clear business-driver statement leads to unfocused scenario selection.
