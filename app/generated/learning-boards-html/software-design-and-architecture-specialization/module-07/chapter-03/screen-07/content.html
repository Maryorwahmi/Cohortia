Identify Architectural Approaches
Catalog the major design choices and expected impacts
Approach: Centralized Queue
Impact on availability: single point of failure. Impact on performance: potential bottleneck for global traffic.
Approach: Asynchronous Messaging
Impact on performance: decouples producers and consumers, but sensitive to broker latency.
Approach: Caching Layer
Impact on latency: improves read time but introduces cache consistency trade-offs.
Approach: Replicated Database
Improves availability but raises data consistency concerns and complexity in failover.
Practical activity
Make a two-column chart: approach vs expected quality impact. Use it as a checklist during scenario walkthroughs.
