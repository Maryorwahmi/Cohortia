Brainstorm & Prioritize Scenarios
Make scenarios measurable and rank by business impact
Scenario template
Source, Stimulus, Artifact, Environment, Response, Response Measure.
Example
Global IoT burst: 10x normal rate, centralized queue artifact, high-latency regions, response: all data ingested, measure: <0.1% loss, <5s latency.
Prioritization
Rank scenarios by business impact and likelihood. Focus analysis on top-ranked items to keep sessions productive.
Do
Include an explicit response measure for every scenario.
Don't
Write vague goals like 'the system must be fast' without a measure.
Check
Confirm scenario priorities with business owners before analysis.
