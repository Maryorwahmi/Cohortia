Module 07 › Chapter 7.6 › Key Concepts
Architectural Decision Record
A short document capturing a single significant architectural decision and its rationale.
ADR Structure
Title, Status, Context, Decision, Options, Rationale, Consequences — follow this order to tell the story.
Knowledge Preservation
ADRs keep reasoning accessible across team turnover and architecture evolution.
Summary: Capture the why, keep ADRs concise, store them with code, and update status when decisions evolve.
