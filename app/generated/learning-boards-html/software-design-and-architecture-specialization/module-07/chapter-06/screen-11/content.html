Module 07 › Chapter 7.6 › Writing Clear, Concise ADRs
Style guidelines
  • Be explicit about assumptions in Context.
  • List only viable alternatives in Options Considered.
  • Make Rationale explain why the option fits now.
  • State Consequences in operational terms.
Example wording
Instead of 'may increase complexity,' write 'introduces a new database type and requires two weeks of training for three engineers.'
Common mistake
Writing ADRs that try to be specs or long histories rather than concise decision records.
