Module 07 › Chapter 7.6 › Consequences & Operational Impact
Why consequences matter
Consequences translate an abstract choice into operational realities that teams must manage.
Positive consequence example
Improved geospatial query performance enabling richer mapping features.
Negative consequence example
Requires two weeks of training and increases operational complexity with a new database type.
How to write consequences
  • Be specific and measurable where possible.
  • Separate operational costs from development impacts.
  • Link to related ADRs or migration plans when appropriate.
Check your consequences
If someone can estimate time or risk from your consequence statements, you are providing useful guidance for future decisions.
