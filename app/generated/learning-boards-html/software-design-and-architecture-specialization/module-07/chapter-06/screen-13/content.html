Module 07 › Chapter 7.6 › Common Mistakes & Maintenance
Common mistakes
  • Too verbose: burying the decision in long history.
  • Too vague: missing measurable consequences or assumptions.
  • Over-documentation: ADRs for trivial preferences.
Maintenance practices
Mark old ADRs as Superseded with a link to the new ADR. Keep statuses current and review ADRs periodically so they remain relevant.
Quick rule
Create a new ADR for changes; don't delete or overwrite historical reasoning.
