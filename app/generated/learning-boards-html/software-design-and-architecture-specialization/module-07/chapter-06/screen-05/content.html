Module 07 › Chapter 7.6 › Typical ADR Structure
Linear writing flow
Use this ordered template to reduce reader friction and make assumptions explicit.
1
Title & Status
A precise name and a clear status so readers know if the decision is current.
2
Context
What problem and constraints led to this decision. Include measurable expectations if possible.
3
Decision & Options
State the decision clearly and list alternatives considered so readers see the trade space.
4
Rationale & Consequences
Explain why this option fits and record expected positive and negative impacts to guide future change.
Example sequence
Title: Use PostgreSQL. Status: Accepted. Context: high write loads, geospatial queries. Decision: adopt PostgreSQL. Options: MySQL, MongoDB, CockroachDB. Rationale: PostGIS, indexing. Consequences: migration work and training.
Check your writing
  • Is the decision unambiguous?
  • Are alternatives listed, even briefly?
  • Do consequences include operational impacts?
