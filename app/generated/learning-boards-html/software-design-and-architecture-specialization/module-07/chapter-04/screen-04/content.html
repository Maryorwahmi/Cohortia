Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Architecture Review Board
Definition
Standing committee for design reviews

An ARB reviews proposals, enforces standards, and shares lessons across teams.

How an ARB runs
  • Periodic meetings with concise proposal artifacts.
  • Present decisions, key trade-offs, and risks.
  • Board provides feedback and guidance.
Common mistake
Allowing the ARB to act as a gatekeeper that delays innovation instead of advising teams.
Example & Guidance
Scenario: Presentation layer accessing database
An ARB questions a direct database call from UI code, prompting the architect to show how business rules will be preserved by inserting a service layer.
Pro tip
Prepare a short decision record highlighting alternatives and expected impacts to speed review.
Key takeaway
Use ARBs for guidance and alignment; keep them advisory and outcome-focused.
