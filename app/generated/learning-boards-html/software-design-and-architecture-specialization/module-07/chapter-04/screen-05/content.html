ARB Review — Step-by-step
A compact process to make reviews efficient and actionable.
1. Pack
Distribute concise artifacts
Goals, constraints, key decisions, and data points.
2. Present
State problems and trade-offs
Focus on three to five specific questions for the board.
3. Discuss
Probing and targeted feedback
Surface operational and cross-project impacts.
4. Record
Capture outcomes
Actions, owners, and decision status: approve, conditionally approve, or revisit.
Example outcome
Add caching, evaluate consistency impacts, owner assigned for follow-up test.
Common mistake
No follow-up on conditions; decisions fade and causes drift.
Key takeaway
Time-box, prepare, and record outcomes to make ARBs productive.
