Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Technique Comparison
Compare strengths & weaknesses
Pick the right mix based on risk, cost, and time.
ARB
Strength: collective wisdom. Weakness: potential slowdown if not advisory.
Checklist
Strength: speed and consistency. Weakness: limited discovery.
Simulation
Strength: explore trade-offs. Weakness: fidelity depends on assumptions.
Prototype
Strength: concrete evidence. Weakness: cost and risk of misinterpretation.
Static Analysis
Strength: continuous enforcement. Weakness: requires rule definition and upkeep.
Practical pattern
Use checklists for baseline, ARB for policy, prototypes for high-risk, simulation for capacity, static checks for ongoing protection.
Misconception
A single technique will not catch all classes of risk—composition is the solution.
Key takeaway
Combine techniques thoughtfully to reduce the most important risks for your project.
