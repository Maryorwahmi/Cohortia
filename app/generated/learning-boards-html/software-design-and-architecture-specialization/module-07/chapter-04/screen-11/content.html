Design a focused checklist
Sample items grouped by quality attribute to adapt for your team.
Scalability
  • Are services designed stateless where possible? (Yes/No/N-A)
  • Is there a data partitioning or sharding strategy? (Yes/No/N-A)
Resilience
  • Are circuit breakers used to prevent cascading failures? (Yes/No/N-A)
  • Is service discovery configured to route around failures? (Yes/No/N-A)
Maintainability
  • Are services independently deployable and versioned? (Yes/No/N-A)
  • Is centralized logging and monitoring in place? (Yes/No/N-A)
Security & Data
  • Is inter-service communication secured? (Yes/No/N-A)
  • Does each service own its data store or is data ownership clear? (Yes/No/N-A)
How to use
Use in ARB pre-checks, sprint gating, and onboarding templates. Mark uncertain answers for prototype or simulation follow-up.
Pitfall
Avoid vague questions. Prefer measurable and evidence-backed answers.
Key takeaway
A clear checklist speeds reviews and surfaces the highest-uncertainty items for targeted experiments.
