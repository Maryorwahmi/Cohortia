Select and Combine Techniques
A quick decision framework to match technique to risk.
Decision Flow
  1. Identify the highest-risk quality attribute to protect.
  2. Estimate cost and expertise required for each technique.
  3. Choose minimal combination to reduce that risk effectively.
  4. Plan for continuous enforcement where needed.
Example mix
For a high-criticality service: ATAM if available, ARB for governance, prototype for latency, and static analysis in CI for long-term integrity.
Common pitfall
Treating a prototype as production-ready leads to hidden technical debt. Use prototypes for learning, not shipping.
Checklist
  • Is the technique aligned with the primary risk?
  • Is the required effort justified by the potential impact?
  • Will feedback be actionable and timely?
