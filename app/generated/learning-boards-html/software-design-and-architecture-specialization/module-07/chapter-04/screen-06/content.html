Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Checklists & Questionnaires
Definition
Actionable, categorized questions

Organize items by quality attribute and make them answerable with Yes/No/N-A to enable rapid evaluation.

Security sample items
  • Are all external interfaces authenticated?
  • Is data encrypted at rest and in transit?
  • Are common web vulnerabilities considered?
Reliability sample items
  • Are circuit breakers in place for remote calls?
  • Is there a failover or retry strategy?
Good practice
Keep items specific and tied to measurable outcomes rather than vague recommendations.
When to use
First pass reviews, ARB pre-checks, or as templates for onboarding teams.
Limitations
They only catch what is listed; novel interactions or subtle trade-offs may be missed.
Example: missing performance interaction
A checklist may verify caching exists but miss that cache invalidation leads to expensive rebuilds under peak load.
Key takeaway
Use checklists for speed and consistency; pair them with targeted experiments for unknown risks.
