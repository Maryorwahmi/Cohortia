Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Why communication and evolution matter
Living documentation: a working definition
Documentation that is discoverable, accurate, and maintained as part of normal development activity.
Why it matters
Prevents mismatches between design and implementation, reduces incident handling time, and helps onboarding.
Common mistake
Equating volume with quality. Big, unmaintained docs cause more harm than a few maintained artifacts.
Key Takeaway

Focus on discoverability and maintenance cycles. Small, frequent updates beat large, stale documents.

Concrete scenario
A service changes how it authenticates users. Without an updated diagram or runbook, deployments and incidents use old steps and cause friction.
Actionable sample
Require an ADR and a small deployment note in the pull request when authentication logic or endpoints change.
Check your understanding
Does your team have a checklist item in pull requests to update relevant docs? If not, add one for a week and observe the effect.
