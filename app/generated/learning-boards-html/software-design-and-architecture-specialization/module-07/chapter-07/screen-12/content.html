Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Fostering a documentation culture
Practical roles
  • Lead by example: architects publish ADRs and update diagrams themselves.
  • Provide templates: ADR skeletons, diagram folders, PR checklists.
  • Train: short demos and mentoring to lower the barrier to contribute.
Lightweight enforcement
Use small rules that are easy to follow: PR checklist items, CI rendering, and occasional documentation review meetings. Avoid heavy processes that discourage contributors.
Key Takeaway

Make it easy and expected to document; tool support and small habits win over heavy governance.
