Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Automated generation vs manual diagrams
Manual diagrams
Good for quick communication and brainstorming but usually produce binary artifacts that are hard to diff and maintain.
When to use
Sketching, whiteboard captures, early brainstorming. Convert to versioned text if the artifact will be kept.
Automated generation
Text-based definitions that render into consistent visuals, suitable for diagrams that evolve with code.
When to use
Architecture views, sequence flows, and diagrams tied to ongoing development. They support diffs, reviews, and CI rendering.
Key Takeaway

Prefer text-defined diagrams for maintainability; only keep manual images for transient or presentation tasks unless converted later.
