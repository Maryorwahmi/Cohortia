Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › E-commerce: Database Trade-off
Scenario
Transactional DB optimized for checkout
High write performance for checkout but limited support for complex analytical queries. Evaluation shows analytics could block or slow operational workloads.
Mitigation options
  • Introduce a separate analytics store and ETL pipeline.
  • Use a data warehouse for complex queries and reporting.
  • Denormalize specific tables or add read replicas for analytics load.
