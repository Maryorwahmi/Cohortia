Introduction to Architectural Evaluation
Why we inspect designs before building: uncover risks, protect quality attributes, and enable confident decisions.

Architectural evaluation is a proactive review process. It highlights where a design may fail to meet quality goals and clarifies trade-offs so teams can plan mitigation or choose a different approach.

In this lesson
You will learn why evaluation matters, how it surfaces risks, who should participate, and when to use informal checks versus formal methods.

Concept Map
Risks • Sensitivity • Trade-offs • Stakeholders
Risk
A design decision that could cause failure or unmet requirements.
Sensitivity
A small change that would create a large impact on a quality attribute.
Trade-off
Improving one attribute may reduce another; decisions must be explicit.
Stakeholders
Diverse voices ensure operational, business and developer concerns are addressed.
