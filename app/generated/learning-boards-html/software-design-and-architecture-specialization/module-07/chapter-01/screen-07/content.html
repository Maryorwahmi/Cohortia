Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Stakeholders in Evaluation
Architects
Assess overall design rationale, constraints, and alignment with quality attributes.
Developers
Raise implementation complexity, technology limitations, and developer productivity concerns.
Operations
Highlight deployment, monitoring, scaling and maintenance impacts of design choices.
QA
Focus on testability, defect isolation and verification strategies tied to architecture.
Project Managers
Consider timeline, budget and delivery risk when trade-offs are proposed.
Business Reps
Ensure architecture aligns with strategic goals, feature priorities and non-functional expectations.
Mapping exercise
Map stakeholder concerns to architectural implications: e.g., BI needs → different data store or analytics pipeline; operations concerns → add observability and automated scaling.
Key Takeaway

Active stakeholder participation turns subjective worries into documented inputs and mitigation plans.
