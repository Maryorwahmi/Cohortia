Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Risks, Sensitivity, and Trade-offs
Step 1
Collect scenarios
Identify expected loads, failure modes, and security threats to drive the evaluation.
Step 2
Map design to scenarios
Trace how architectural elements behave under each scenario to surface risks and sensitivity points.
Step 3
Document trade-offs
Record the consequences of choices and propose mitigations or acceptance criteria.
Example scenario
Microservices for scalability
Evaluation finds distributed transactions introduce complexity and operational overhead — a trade-off between scalability and maintainability. Options include eventual consistency patterns, compensating transactions, or simplifying the architecture.
Key Takeaway

Scenario-driven evaluation turns abstract qualities into concrete risks and trade-offs you can manage.
