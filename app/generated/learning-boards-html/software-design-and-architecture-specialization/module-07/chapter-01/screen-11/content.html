Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Common Challenges and Pitfalls
Pitfall
Evaluation in isolation
Missing operations and business input can leave critical concerns unaddressed.
Pitfall
Ignoring non-functional requirements
Treating functionality as sufficient often produces fragile systems in production.
Pitfall
Late evaluation
Delaying review makes fixes expensive and risky; early checks are more cost-effective.
Practical mitigations
  • Make reviews collaborative and scenario-driven.
  • Prioritize findings by likelihood and impact.
  • Translate top findings into verification tasks.
Key Takeaway

Structured, timely, and prioritized evaluations produce actionable findings rather than an overwhelming list of concerns.
