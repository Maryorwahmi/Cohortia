Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Informal Reviews vs Formal Methods
Informal Reviews
Quick checks and team discussions
  • Fast feedback, low overhead, great for catching obvious design issues.
  • Supports continuous alignment between design and implementation.
  • May miss deep trade-offs or subtle sensitivity points without scenarios.
Formal Methods
Structured analysis for high-stakes systems
  • Evidence-backed, scenario-driven, and resource intensive.
  • Ideal when quality attributes are mission-critical or regulated.
  • Produces a documented report with prioritized risks and mitigations.
Decision guidance
Use informal reviews for rapid learning and continuous improvement. Escalate to formal methods when the cost of failure, regulatory needs, or complexity demands rigorous assessment. Combining both gives the team speed plus assurance.
Key Takeaway

Match the method to the risk profile: speed and frequency versus depth and evidence.
