Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Non-functional Requirements
Key attributes
  • Performance — response times and throughput.
  • Security — resistance to attacks and protection of data.
  • Maintainability — ease of change and understanding.
  • Scalability — ability to grow with load.
  • Reliability — availability and graceful failure handling.
Prioritization
Rank attributes by business impact and use that ranking to select scenarios and focus evaluation effort where it matters most.
Example: Security & DoS
A design that ignores denial-of-service vectors may seem functionally complete but is fragile in production. Evaluation forces explicit scenario consideration and mitigation planning such as rate limiting and monitoring.
Key Takeaway

Make quality attributes explicit, rank them, and use that list to drive scenario-based evaluations.
