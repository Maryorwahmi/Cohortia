Architectural Evaluation — a precise definition
A systematic analysis focused on quality attributes and risk mitigation.
Core claim
It is not just comments — it is structured inquiry into whether the architecture will meet its quality goals.
Concrete example
Reviewing a checkout flow may reveal a synchronous payment call that will block throughput under peak load.
Key Takeaway

Treat evaluation as proactive risk management: surface issues before they are costly to fix.

Definition card
Systematic process
Collect scenarios, identify risks and sensitivity points, analyze trade-offs, and document findings with stakeholders.
Misconceptions
  • Only for large systems — false, small systems still face costly late changes.
  • Architects alone should evaluate — false, diverse stakeholders reveal different risks.
