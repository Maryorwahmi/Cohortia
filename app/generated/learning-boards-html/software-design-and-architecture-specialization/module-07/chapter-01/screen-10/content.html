Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Stakeholder Identification Activity
Scenario brief
Smart Home Energy Management System collects device data, analyzes consumption, recommends savings, and offers remote control via mobile app. It must be available, secure, and scalable.
Template
1) Stakeholder group. 2) Primary concerns. 3) Architectural implications. Repeat for at least five groups.
Example entry
End-Users / Homeowners
Concerns: ease of use, privacy. Implications: simple API for mobile, encryption at rest and transit, fine-grained access control.
Exercise output
Produce a stakeholder list with mapped concerns and three verification tasks, such as data privacy threat modeling, availability drills, and analytics load tests.
Key Takeaway

Good evaluations translate stakeholder concerns into explicit architectural requirements and verification plans.
