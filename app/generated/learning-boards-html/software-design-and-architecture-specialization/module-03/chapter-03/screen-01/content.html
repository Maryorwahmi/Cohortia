Constructing Complexity: The Builder Pattern

When a single object needs many optional parts or alternative representations, Builder helps you assemble it safely and readably.

Why it matters

Telescoping constructors and scattered setters lead to unreadable code and inconsistent state. Builder centralizes assembly and supports fluent construction and multiple representations.

This chapter includes
  • Roles: Product, Builder, Concrete Builder, Director
  • Fluent APIs and method chaining
  • Comparison with Factory patterns and common mistakes
