Director example
Recipe: Standard Report
  1. set_title("Standard Business Report")
  2. set_header("Confidential")
  3. add_section("Introduction to Q3 performance.")
  4. add_table("Sales Data Summary")
  5. set_footer("Generated on 2023-10-27")
  6. set_format("PDF")
Benefits
Encapsulates ordering rules, reduces duplication, and documents construction intent.
When to skip
Skip Director for one-off constructions or when clients require full ad-hoc control.
Pro Tip

Directors help testing: verify that a director produces expected results across different builders without rewriting step sequences in each test.
