Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Builder Roles
Architecture overview
Product
Holds assembled parts and final state.
Example: Report with title, sections, tables, charts, footer, and format.
Builder
Defines construction steps (interface).
Contract used by Directors and clients to assemble a product in stages.
Concrete Builder
Implements Builder and assembles a specific representation.
Example: PdfReportBuilder formats sections with PDF-specific markup or tags.
Director (Optional)
Encodes recipes for common configurations.
Example: construct_standard_report calls a sequence of builder steps for a standard report.
Concrete example
PdfReportBuilder
Implements set_title by formatting text for PDF and returns self for chaining. Holds an internal Report object until get_report returns a complete product.
Misconception check
Do not let Product construct itself; shifting assembly into Product couples construction with representation and reduces reuse.
