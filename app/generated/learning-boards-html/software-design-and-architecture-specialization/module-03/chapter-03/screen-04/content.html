Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › When construction becomes complex
Problem patterns
  • Telescoping constructors: many overloads cause unreadable calls and parameter-order risks.
  • Post-construction setters: allow inconsistent state if required parts are not set.
Concrete example
A Report with title, header, multiple sections, tables, charts, footer, and format — passing all options to one constructor becomes unreadable; scattered setters risk missing required pieces.
Key Takeaway

If assembly order or optional parts make construction error-prone, prefer Builder to enforce correct assembly and clarity.

Analogy
Think of assembling a custom sandwich: ingredients must be chosen and layered in a deliberate sequence. You wouldn’t hand someone separate bowls of ingredients and hope they assemble them correctly. Builder is the sandwich chef that applies the recipe reliably.
