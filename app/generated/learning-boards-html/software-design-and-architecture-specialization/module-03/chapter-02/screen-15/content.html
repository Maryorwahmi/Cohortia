Quick Reference
Three compact concept cards for quick scanning
Abstract Product
An interface or abstract class that declares operations for a product type.
Creator Roles
Abstract Creator declares factory methods; Concrete Creator implements them to return concrete products.
Trade-offs
Abstract Factory eases family consistency; Factory Method eases adding single-product variants. Choose based on needs.
Final prompt

Scan your codebase for places that construct multiple platform-specific classes. Those are the best candidates for refactoring into a Factory Method or Abstract Factory.
