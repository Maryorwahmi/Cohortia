Unlocking Flexibility: Design Patterns and the Singleton

Understand why patterns give teams a shared language, the three GoF categories, and how the Singleton provides a single point of access while introducing trade-offs.

In this lesson

Explore pattern taxonomy, a deep dive into the Singleton, thread-safety, and a logger implementation activity that demonstrates race condition prevention.

Why it matters

Patterns improve maintainability and communication; Singletons solve single-instance needs such as config or logging but can hide dependencies if misused.

Creational

Focus on object creation strategies for flexibility and reuse.

Structural

Compose classes and objects to form larger systems with stable interfaces.

Behavioral

Define effective communication and responsibility between objects.
