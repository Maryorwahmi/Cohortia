Learning Objectives

A progressive Bloom path from concept to application and mastery.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain pattern purpose

Describe how patterns capture reusable design knowledge and why they improve team communication and maintainability. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

02
Differentiate pattern categories

Recognize Creational, Structural, and Behavioral patterns and choose the right family for a design problem. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

03
Implement Singleton safely

Write a Singleton that controls instantiation and protects initialization in concurrent environments. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

04
Evaluate appropriateness

Assess when a Singleton introduces harmful coupling and when dependency passing or injection is preferable. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
