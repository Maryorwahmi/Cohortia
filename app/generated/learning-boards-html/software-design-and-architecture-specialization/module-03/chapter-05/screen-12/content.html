Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Best Practices
Design rules
Small Component API
Include only operations common to both leaves and composites.
Composite-only management
Place add/remove on Composite to avoid meaningless implementations on leaves.
Focused Facades
Expose a few meaningful client tasks, not every subsystem method.
Anti-pattern: Interface Bloat
Putting methods in Component that only apply to composites forces leaves into awkward implementations and violates interface segregation.
Anti-pattern: God Facade
A facade that duplicates business logic or re‑exposes every method reduces clarity rather than increasing it.
Key Takeaway

Follow ownership and interface rules to keep trees and facades predictable and testable.
