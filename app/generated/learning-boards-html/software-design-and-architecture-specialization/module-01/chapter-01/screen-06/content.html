Technical debt
Shortcuts now that increase future rework and complexity.
Concrete example
Duplicating logic across modules to ship quickly multiplies future fixes and increases bug risk.
Why it accumulates
Untracked shortcuts, unclear ownership, and postponed refactors let debt grow unnoticed.
Pro tip
Mark shortcuts in your backlog with an estimate of future work required to repay them.
Common misconception
Debt is not proof of bad work. It is a deliberate trade-off that must be acknowledged and scheduled for repayment.
Risk note

If left unmanaged, technical debt increases debugging time and reduces the team's ability to deliver new features quickly.
