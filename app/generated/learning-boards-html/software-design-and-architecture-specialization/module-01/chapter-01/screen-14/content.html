Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Assessing design
Sample question
A team misses deadlines and has frequent critical bugs. Which cause is most likely? Poor design leading to high technical debt.
Explanation
Poor design increases complexity and coupling, making new features risky and time-consuming, which explains frequent bugs and missed deadlines.
Why design is an investment
  • Reduces maintenance costs by making code easier to understand and fix.
  • Speeds future feature development through modular, extensible design.
Key Takeaway

Use symptoms to diagnose design problems and recommend specific, measurable refactors.
