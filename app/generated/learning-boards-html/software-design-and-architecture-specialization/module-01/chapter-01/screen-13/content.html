Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Reflection Activity
Activity steps
  1. Identify one well-designed aspect and describe its benefit.
  2. Identify one poorly designed aspect and record its cost.
  3. Propose one concrete, estimated change to reduce the cost.
Example change: extract validation into a single module, add unit tests, and remove duplicated logic in three files. Estimated effort: two days. Expected benefit: faster fixes and fewer regressions.
Why this works
Targeted, measurable refactors are easier to prioritize and show clear ROI, preventing backlog pile-up.
Key Takeaway

Practical reflection identifies the highest-impact refactors and turns design learning into scheduled improvements.
