Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Software Design
The process of defining architecture, components, interfaces and system characteristics.
Technical Debt
The implied cost of future rework from choosing easy short-term solutions now.
Maintainability
The ease with which a system can be modified to correct faults or adapt to change.
Summary

Use these concepts to evaluate systems: design reduces future cost; debt must be managed; maintainability preserves team speed.
