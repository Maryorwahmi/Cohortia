Reflective checklist
  1. Pick one well-designed aspect and note its benefit.
  2. Pick one poorly designed aspect and record the cost.
  3. Write one concrete change you can apply today.
Example mapping
Monolithic logging utility → extract interface + two implementations. Benefit: easier testing and platform-specific behavior without changing callers.
Why this matters
Reflection converts abstract design qualities into targeted refactors. That makes your improvements measurable and reduces future technical debt.
