ReportWorkflow: a thin coordinator
Compose cohesive modules and sequence them without adding new responsibilities.
Behavior
Call formatter to create content, then pass it to printer, email sender, and archiver. Do not implement formatting or email logic here.
Misconception
Coordinator is not a dumping ground
If you find business logic in the orchestrator, extract it to the appropriate module.
Testing benefit
You can unit test orchestration by replacing modules with fakes, keeping tests focused on sequencing and error handling rather than internal behavior.
Architecture sketch
ReportFormatter
ReportPrinter
EmailSender
ReportArchiver
These modules are loosely coupled. The workflow composes them and manages the sequence but not their internal concerns.
Key Takeaway

Keep the orchestration layer thin and focused on coordination, not implementation detail.
