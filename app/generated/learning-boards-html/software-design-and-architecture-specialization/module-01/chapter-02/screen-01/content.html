Abstraction and Encapsulation
Simple contracts and protected state enable components to evolve independently and stay correct.
In this lesson
Learn how to define contracts with interfaces and protect state with encapsulation. See examples and a refactor task to practice.
Why it matters
Abstraction gives flexibility to swap implementations. Encapsulation safeguards invariants so bugs stay local and tests remain reliable.
Quick takeaway

Use abstraction to hide how something works. Use encapsulation to control who can change it.
