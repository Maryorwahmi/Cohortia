Smells
Global state, internal modifications, and flag-driven control passing are indicators of high coupling.
Small refactors
Extract minimal interfaces, inject them, and add focused tests to validate contracts.
Architecture
Adopt messaging or service boundaries when independent deployment, scaling, or failure isolation is required.
