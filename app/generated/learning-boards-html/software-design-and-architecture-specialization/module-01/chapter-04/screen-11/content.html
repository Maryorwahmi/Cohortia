When to use messaging and service boundaries

Architectural decoupling gives independent evolution at the cost of extra infrastructure and coordination.

Event-driven
Publish events and let independent consumers handle them. Reduces compile-time and deployment coupling.
Message queues
Queues add resilience and time decoupling but require schema versioning and monitoring.
Service boundaries
Define clear service APIs so teams own independent slices of functionality.
Advice
Start with local decoupling via interfaces, then adopt messaging where independent deployment and scaling are required.
Pitfall
Don't introduce messaging for its own sake; operational costs and eventual consistency must be justified.
