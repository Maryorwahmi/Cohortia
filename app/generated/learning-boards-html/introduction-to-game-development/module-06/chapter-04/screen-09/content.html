Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Running Playtest Sessions
Session Templates
Use a short, repeatable script so sessions are comparable.
  1. Intro and consent.
  2. Provide README and tasks.
  3. Ask tester to think aloud during play.
  4. Observe quietly, take notes.
  5. Debrief: short interview and form completion.
Moderated vs Unmoderated
Moderated: deep observation and follow-ups. Unmoderated: larger sample, less depth.
Think-aloud tip
Encourage natural commentary; avoid leading questions that shape the player's behavior.
Check your understanding
After a session, can you state the top two usability failures observed in one sentence?
