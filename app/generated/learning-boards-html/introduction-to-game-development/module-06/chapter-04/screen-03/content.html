Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Learning Objectives
Objective to Milestone
  1. Recognize playtesting — Give two examples of issues uncovered by playtesting and why early tests reduce cost.
  2. Identify test types — Map usability, bug, balance, and fun-factor tests to simple goals.
  3. Prepare a build — Export a Unity standalone with scenes and basic player settings configured.
  4. Collect feedback — Produce a 6–10 question survey with rating and open responses.
Why these matter
Milestones turn abstract goals into checks you can complete before moving on. This keeps iteration fast and focused.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY
Deliver a build folder and a linked feedback form that a peer can use within 10 minutes.
