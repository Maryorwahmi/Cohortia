Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Designing a Feedback Form
Survey Structure
Keep it short. Mix scales and open responses.
Rating items
3–5 scale questions for quick comparison across sessions.
Open responses
3 questions that invite concrete examples and suggestions.
Sample questions
  • Controls intuitiveness: 1–5
  • Enjoyment: 1–5
  • Most frustrating part: (open)
  • Most enjoyable moment: (open)
  • Bugs encountered: please describe steps
Practical example
Ask the same rating items across multiple playtests so you can chart improvements numerically.
Key Takeaway
Short, targeted questions produce the most actionable feedback.
