Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Types of Playtesting
Usability vs Bug
Usability examines player understanding; bug testing finds reproducible technical errors.
Usability
Goal: Can players learn controls and objectives? Measure with task completion and confusion notes.
Bug
Goal: Are there crashes or glitches? Produce step-by-step reproduction notes and environment details.
Balance vs Fun-factor
Balance checks fairness and challenge; fun-factor observes player enjoyment and memorable moments.
Balance
Goal: Is any strategy dominant? Watch outcomes and win rates across players.
Fun-factor
Goal: Do players enjoy key moments? Use open questions and observation notes for emotional response.
Practical tip
For a small prototype, run usability and fun tests together: they reveal whether the core mechanics are both understandable and enjoyable.
