Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Launch Instructions
What to include
  • How to open the build (filename and folder).
  • Controls (example: WASD to move, Space to jump).
  • What you want testers to focus on (example questions).
  • How to submit feedback (where to send the form).
Sample README (visual)
Launch: Open MyGame_Playtest_Build_v1/MyGame.exe
Controls: WASD = move  |  Space = jump
Goal: Reach the green flag in Level 1
Focus: Is the jump responsive? Rate controls 1-5
Feedback: Complete the feedback form in the folder after play.
Pro Tip
A short script reduces tester confusion and keeps sessions comparable.
