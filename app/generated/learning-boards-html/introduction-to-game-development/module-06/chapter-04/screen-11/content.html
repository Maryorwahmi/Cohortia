Example case
Five testers report a confusing door that won't open; this is high frequency and usually a quick fix to input mapping or collider setup—move it to the top of the backlog.
Misconception
Not all votes are equal. Weight feedback by tester diversity and alignment with your design goals.
Action
Select one high-impact, low-effort fix to implement before the next short playtest cycle.
