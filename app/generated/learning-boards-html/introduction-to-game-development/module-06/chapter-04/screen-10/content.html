Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Collecting & Organizing
Categorize feedback
Use categories: Bug, Usability, Balance, Suggestion.
Bug reports
Include steps to reproduce, platform, and screenshots or logs if available.
Usability & Balance
Capture intent and where the player got stuck or exploited a mechanic.
Prioritization grid
Map items by impact (player experience) and effort (developer time). Fixes in the high-impact/low-effort quadrant first.
Storage
Use a simple spreadsheet or issue tracker with columns: category, description, steps, severity, frequency, proposed action.
Key Takeaway
Order your backlog by player impact and implementation cost to keep iteration practical.
