Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Interpreting Feedback
From comment to hypothesis
Turn repeated qualitative comments into a concise hypothesis you can test with a small change.
Example
Observation: testers call jump "floaty." Hypothesis: increase gravity or lower jump impulse will feel tighter. Test: change one value and run 3 short sessions.
Experiment design
Change one variable at a time, use the same rating items, and compare before/after results numerically and qualitatively.
Misconception
Applying many fixes at once makes it impossible to know which change caused improvement.
Reflective question
What one variable will you change to test your highest-priority issue?
