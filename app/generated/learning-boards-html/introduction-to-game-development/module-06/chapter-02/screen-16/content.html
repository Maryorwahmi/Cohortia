Import wisely
Choose Decompress On Load for SFX, Streaming for long tracks to balance latency and memory.
PlayOneShot for SFX
Use for overlapping short effects; avoid Play when overlap is required.
Use the Mixer
Group Music and SFX for centralized volume control and effects.
Actionable next step

Implement a MusicManager and a SoundEffectPlayer in your scene, route them to mixer groups, and test overlap and spatial behavior during playtesting.
