AudioSource properties and usage

Configure sources for behavior you need: looping music, instant SFX, or 3D positional audio.

Play On Awake & Loop
Use for background music to auto-start and repeat when scenes load.
PlayOneShot vs Play
PlayOneShot allows overlapping instances; Play replaces current playback on that source.
Volume & Pitch
Use these to match audio levels and to add variety via pitch randomization.
Practical example
Add an AudioSource to Player, uncheck Play On Awake, expose an AudioClip field in a script, and call PlayOneShot when the player jumps to allow multiple jump sounds to overlap during rapid actions.
Common mistake
Using a single AudioSource with Play for every SFX will interrupt previously playing sounds and feel brittle.
Key Takeaway

AudioSource settings determine whether a clip behaves like an ambient layer or an instant event; choose accordingly.
