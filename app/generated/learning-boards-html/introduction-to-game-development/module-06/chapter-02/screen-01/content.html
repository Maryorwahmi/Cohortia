Immersive Audio: Sound Effects and Music Integration

Practical steps for importing clips, configuring sources, and designing a controllable game soundscape.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson

Import audio, connect AudioSource components, and route audio with an Audio Mixer to control music and SFX independently.

Why it matters

Sound provides feedback and emotion; small audio choices influence perceived responsiveness and atmosphere.

Quick win

Add a MusicManager with a looping AudioSource to create immediate background mood.

Common pitfall

Using Play for short, overlapping effects will cut earlier sounds; prefer PlayOneShot for SFX.

Explore Key Topics
AudioClip Import
Choose Decompress On Load for short SFX, Streaming for long music tracks.
AudioSource Usage
PlayOneShot for overlapping SFX; Play or Loop for background music.
Audio Mixer
Group SFX, Music, and Voice to control volume and apply effects centrally.
Visual Concept
AudioClip
Imported file configured for load and compression.
AudioSource
Attaches to GameObjects; plays AudioClips and exposes spatial settings.
AudioListener
Receives audio; usually attached to the Main Camera to match player position.
Getting started

Import a short effect and a looping music file. Create a MusicManager object for the music and add an AudioSource for SFX on the player. Use PlayOneShot for coin or jump sounds.
