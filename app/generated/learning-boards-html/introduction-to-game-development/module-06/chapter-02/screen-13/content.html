Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Play() vs PlayOneShot()
Comparison

Choose the right playback method

Play()
Starts the assigned clip on the AudioSource and will interrupt any current playback on that source. Use for exclusive sounds where you do not want overlap.
When to use
  • Long or exclusive clips like single ambient streams or unique actions.
PlayOneShot()
Plays a clip immediately without stopping sounds already playing on the same source, allowing overlap. Ideal for SFX like coins, jumps, and repeated impacts.
When to use
  • Short, repeatable SFX that should not cut each other off.
Misconception
Using many Play calls across identical sources to simulate overlap is inefficient; PlayOneShot is a cleaner solution.
