Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Player Settings
Identity fields
Company Name and Product Name become metadata in the built application and stores. Use consistent, final values before release to avoid confusing players or reviewers.
Icon guidance
Provide appropriately sized images for target platforms. Windows commonly needs .ico; other platforms accept PNGs with multiple resolutions.
Splash and Presentation
Customize the splash screen or choose to disable Unity splash if your license allows. Configure default resolution, fullscreen mode, and window resizability to match your design goals.
Practical tip
If your UI is not responsive, avoid enabling resizable windows. For mobile, lock orientation to portrait or landscape depending on your gameplay.
