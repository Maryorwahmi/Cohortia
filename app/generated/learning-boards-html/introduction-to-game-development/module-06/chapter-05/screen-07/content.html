Quick optimization checklist
  • Texture audit: resize and enable compression where appropriate.
  • Remove unused assets or scenes and clear references to them.
  • Disable unnecessary AudioSource and Light components.
  • Move heavy per-frame calculations out of Update when possible.
  • Use sprite packing for 2D projects and LODs for 3D models.
Concrete example
A single high-resolution texture can dominate build size. Reducing a 4096 texture to 1024 and compressing it often yields big savings with little visible change in gameplay contexts.
Pro tip
Iterate: make one optimization, rebuild, and compare build size and runtime behavior to validate benefits.
Don't do this
Avoid global deletion without backups. Keep a clean branch or a copy of the project before large asset removals.
