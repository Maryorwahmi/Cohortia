Optimization
Reduce textures, remove unnecessary assets, and avoid expensive per-frame calculations to improve player experience.
WebGL Hosting
Must be served over HTTP. Local file access often blocks data loads. Use a simple server or a hosting platform for reliable playback.
Distribution
Zip the full build, include instructions and requirements, and test extraction before uploading to your chosen platform.
Takeaway: tidy packaging and clear instructions save time for you and your players. Test everything one last time before sharing.
