Learning Objectives

These objectives map to hands-on milestones and testable outcomes.

01
Identify broken authentication
Recognize weak passwords, predictable reset tokens, and missing MFA. This lets you prioritize fixes that stop credential-based takeover.
Verified competency milestone
02
Understand session attacks
Explain session hijacking and fixation and why token randomness, rotation, and cookie flags matter to prevent impersonation.
Verified competency milestone
03
Explain broken access control
Differentiate vertical and horizontal escalation and IDOR and identify missing server-side authorization checks.
Verified competency milestone
04
Exploit and mitigate in lab
Use Burp Suite techniques to test IDOR and session flaws and propose server-side fixes like token randomness and authorization checks.
Verified competency milestone
Bloom bar: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
