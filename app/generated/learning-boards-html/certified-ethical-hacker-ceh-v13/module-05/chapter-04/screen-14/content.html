Key Concepts — Part 1

Three compact reminders you should be able to explain and demonstrate.

Broken Authentication
Flaws in verifying identity such as weak passwords or predictable reset tokens that enable account takeover.
Session Management
Maintaining authenticated state with secure, rotated, and invalidated session tokens to prevent hijack and fixation.
Broken Access Control
Failing to enforce what users can do, leading to vertical or horizontal escalation and IDOR.
Summary

These fundamentals guide detection, exploitation, and mitigation. Always pair findings with exact server-side recommendations.
