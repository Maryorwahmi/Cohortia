Module 05 › Chapter 5.5 › Android vs iOS Security Models
Android

Open distribution, sideloading permitted, and more customization options increase attack surface and make integrity controls essential.

Risk: Sideloading

Apps installed outside the official store may include malicious or repackaged code.

Risk: Rooted Devices

Root access allows reading of app private data and bypassing of platform protections.

iOS

Stricter review processes and sandboxing reduce some risks, but insecure coding patterns still expose data.

Risk: Logical Flaws

An app with weak auth or insecure storage is vulnerable despite OS controls.

Risk: Jailbreak

A jailbroken device negates many platform protections, exposing app data.

Example

A hardcoded API key inside an APK can be extracted regardless of whether the device is Android or iOS.

Misconception

Believing iOS is invulnerable. Platform protections help but do not replace secure development.

Key Takeaway

Tailor your testing strategy to platform characteristics; prioritize integrity and storage controls on Android and logic flaws across both platforms.
