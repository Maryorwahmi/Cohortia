Module 05 › Chapter 5.5 › Learning Objectives — Practical Skills
Hands-on checks
  • Search decompiled Smali for strings like password, api_key, or secret to find hardcoded values.
  • Inspect usages of SharedPreferences and SQLiteDatabase for unencrypted storage patterns.
  • Test TLS validation and attempt a MitM where certificate validation is disabled.
  • Try to repackage an app in a lab to understand tampering risk and missing integrity checks.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Reporting

For each finding include the location, reproduction steps, impact assessment, and a mitigation recommendation such as encrypting tokens, sideload prevention, or certificate pinning.

Safety

Always obtain permission and keep test artifacts isolated; unauthorized analysis of third-party apps is unethical and may be illegal.

Practice lab

Use intentionally vulnerable sample apps in a controlled environment to practice the APK analysis workflow safely.
