Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Key Concepts
Static Analysis

Decompile and inspect APK/IPA to find exposed components, permissions, and hardcoded data.

Dynamic Analysis

Observe runtime behavior with Burp and Frida to validate and reproduce vulnerabilities.

Emulator & ADB

Use emulators and ADB for safe testing, pushing binaries, forwarding ports, and collecting logs.

Takeaway

The right combination of static reconnaissance, interception, and targeted instrumentation yields reliable, reproducible findings — always test ethically and document fully.
