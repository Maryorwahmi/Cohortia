Core Concept

Methodical testing definition

A repeatable sequence of steps that ensures full coverage, reduces missed checks, and documents verification for reproducibility.

Common workflow
  1. Reconnaissance and mapping of endpoints and parameters.
  2. Automated scanning for known issues.
  3. Targeted manual testing for logic and design flaws.
  4. Reporting and retesting fixes.
Key Takeaway

Discipline scales: a good methodology helps you find systemic issues, not just random bugs.
