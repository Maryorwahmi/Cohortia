Definition

Insecure Design

Flaws rooted in architecture and design that lead to security gaps regardless of bug-free code.

Design example

A monolithic service that holds broad privileges where a single compromise yields total access.

Threat modeling

Identify critical assets and attack paths early in design to reduce long-term risk.

Testing notes
  • Ask whether a design failure can be mitigated by configuration or requires redesign.
  • Look for single points of privilege or overly broad trust boundaries.
  • Verify whether least-privilege and separation of duties are enforced in design.
Key Takeaway

Design-level fixes reduce the need for brittle implementation workarounds later on.
