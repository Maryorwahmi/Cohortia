Learning Objectives

These outcomes focus on reasoning, description, and practical application of the OWASP Top 10.

01
Understand structured methodology

You will explain why consistent steps prevent gaps and how they improve coverage in a test plan.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain OWASP Top 10 purpose

You will describe how the Top 10 serves as an awareness and test-prioritization document.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify each Top 10 category

You will be able to name and briefly describe each category and its impact on tests.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Map Top 10 to assessments

You will use the Top 10 as a checklist to guide reconnaissance, scanning, and manual verification prioritization.
