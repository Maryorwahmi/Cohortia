Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Practical Reminders
Authorized testing

Only test on systems you own or that explicitly permit testing, like DVWA or dedicated lab environments.

Capture baseline

Measure normal responses and latency so you can detect boolean or timing differences during blind tests.

Validate fixes

After remediation, rerun your proofs to confirm the vulnerability no longer reproduces and note any remaining gaps.

Final takeaway

Good security work combines careful testing, clear remediation, and validation to ensure vulnerabilities are closed responsibly.
