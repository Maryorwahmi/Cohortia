Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Reconnaissance
Local network scanning
Use nmap with service detection and a full port sweep to reveal ports and banners. Keep timing moderate on fragile devices.
Example command
sudo nmap -sV -p- -T4 192.168.1.0/24
This returns open ports, identified services, and version banners useful for profiling.
Safety note
Limit scan intensity and document scope. For battery devices, prefer passive discovery or brief active probes.
Public reconnaissance
Shodan lets you search for exposed services and device types. Use queries to understand public exposure but never connect without permission.
Example Shodan queries
Search by port, product name, or keywords to find public-facing devices and common configurations.
Pro Tip

Record a compact profile: address, ports, banners, inferred model, and next safe checks. That profile powers the verification steps.
