Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Safety & Ethics
Safety first
Obtain written permission and define scope and rollback plans before testing. Know what normal operation looks like to avoid causing harm.
Before you test
  • Confirm written authorization and scope.
  • Plan for backups and rollback steps for firmware or settings.
  • Use the least intrusive checks first and monitor device stability.
Responsible disclosure
Provide reproduction steps, sanitized outputs, and remediation recommendations to vendors. Avoid public disclosure until fixes are available or coordinated.
Key Takeaway

Ethical testing protects users. Always minimize impact and follow disclosure best practices to help vendors fix issues responsibly.
