Default Credentials
Factory usernames and passwords often go unchanged and enable immediate compromise.
MQTT Broker
A misconfigured broker can allow anonymous subscribe and publish, leading to monitoring or control of devices.
nmap & Shodan
Essential discovery tools: nmap for local scanning and Shodan for public exposure mapping. Use responsibly.
Final takeaway: prioritize high-impact, fixable issues, test under permission, and produce clear, actionable reports that vendors can implement.
