Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Default Credentials
Stepwise verification
  1. Confirm service and banner to identify likely device model.
  2. Attempt a single default credential such as common factory pairs.
  3. Capture evidence: banner, prompt, and any accessible read-only outputs.
  4. Stop further changes and prepare documentation and remediation suggestions.
Example Telnet check
telnet 192.168.1.50
# Try usernames like admin, root with common passwords.
If you find valid credentials, do not pivot or change settings. The goal is to confirm the vulnerability, collect evidence, and recommend remediation such as forcing unique defaults and disabling insecure services.
Common mistake
Repeated rapid login attempts can lock devices or cause instability. Limit to a single, documented attempt per account unless authorized for brute-force testing.
