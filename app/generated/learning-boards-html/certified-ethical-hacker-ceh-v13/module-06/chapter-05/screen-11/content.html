Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Lab: nmap & Shodan
Local scan sequence
Identify your network range and run a moderate full-port scan to collect banners and services.
Starter command
sudo nmap -sV -p- -T4 192.168.1.0/24
Capture output and extract candidate devices for further low-impact checks.
Public exploration
Use indexed searches to understand product exposure and common ports. Never attempt to access devices you do not own.
Example Shodan query ideas
Search by port or product name to see typical exposure patterns for a device model and common misconfigurations.
Safety checklist

Confirm authorization, limit scan intensity, backup firmware if needed, and stop if devices show instability.
