Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Assessment Scenario
Scenario steps
  1. Run a moderate nmap scan to find open ports and banners.
  2. Attempt a single default Telnet login to verify access and capture evidence.
  3. Subscribe to a benign MQTT topic or publish a harmless test message to confirm broker permissions.
  4. Document commands, sanitized outputs, and recommended remediations.
Example evidence to collect: nmap output showing open ports, Telnet banner and prompt, MQTT subscription confirmation, and topic names. Recommended fixes include forcing unique credentials, disabling insecure services, and enabling broker authentication and TLS.
Grading focus
Clear, minimal evidence with safety-conscious methods and practical remediation suggestions.
