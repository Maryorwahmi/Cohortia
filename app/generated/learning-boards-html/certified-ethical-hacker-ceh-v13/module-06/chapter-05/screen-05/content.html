Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Communication Protocols
Protocol comparison
Wi‑Fi
IP-based, broad discovery with nmap. Look for HTTP, SSH, Telnet, MQTT ports.
Bluetooth LE
Requires proximity. Use BLE-specific tools to enumerate services and characteristics.
Zigbee / Z‑Wave
Mesh networks needing radios and care; discovery can require joining networks and special hardware.
Application protocols
MQTT
Brokered pub/sub. Misconfiguration can allow anonymous subscribe/publish and control of devices.
CoAP
Lightweight REST-like protocol for constrained devices; may lack strong auth.
Practical note
Choose tools based on protocol and avoid aggressive scans on battery-powered or mesh devices to prevent disruption.
