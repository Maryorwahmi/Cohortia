IoT (Internet of Things)
Physical objects with sensors and software that exchange data on networks.
IoT Architecture
Devices, gateways, cloud, and apps form the stack; test where data crosses trust boundaries.
Communication Protocols
Wi‑Fi, BLE, Zigbee, MQTT, CoAP — each determines discovery and attack methods.
Summary: Use architecture and protocol knowledge to prioritize discovery and safe verification.
