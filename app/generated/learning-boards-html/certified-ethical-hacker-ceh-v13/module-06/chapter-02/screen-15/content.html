Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
Passive Reconnaissance
Gather information by listening to wireless frames without sending traffic.
Active Reconnaissance
Send probes or deauth to elicit responses; more effective but more detectable and disruptive.
airodump-ng Fields
BSSID, PWR, CH, ENC, CIPHER, AUTH, ESSID and station listings are essential for prioritization.
Hidden SSID
Hiding SSID in beacons doesn't prevent discovery; probe and association frames reveal the ESSID.
Channel Focus
Narrowing to a channel and BSSID increases capture relevance and reduces file noise.
Ethics & Safety
Always obtain authorization for active tests and avoid disrupting legitimate network operations.
Summary Takeaway
Combine passive-first collection, informed channel targeting, client observation, and rigorous authorization to produce high-value, ethical reconnaissance results.
