Learning Objectives
Progress from passive listening to focused captures and topology mapping.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Passive and Active Recon
Practice listening-only captures and when minimal, authorized probing is useful. Knowing the difference reduces detection risk and preserves client operations.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use airodump-ng Effectively
Interpret BSSID, PWR, CH, ENC, CIPHER, AUTH and clients to prioritize targets. Knowing columns makes captures actionable.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Reveal Hidden SSIDs
Capture probe requests or authorized deauthentication-triggered reauth to obtain hidden ESSIDs; hiding SSID alone is not secure.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Map Topology & Channels
Use signal strength, channel usage, and walking surveys to place APs and find interference. Channel focus speeds targeted captures.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
