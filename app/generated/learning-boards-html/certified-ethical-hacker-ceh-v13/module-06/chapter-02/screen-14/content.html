Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Common Mistakes
Mistakes that reduce recon quality
Learn the pitfalls and the concrete fixes to keep captures reliable and ethical.
Mistake: Not in monitor mode
Fix: Confirm and document the monitor interface name before scanning.
Mistake: Conflicting services
Fix: Stop network managers that may change channels or reclaim the radio during capture.
Mistake: Stopping too early
Fix: Allow longer capture windows and monitor clients for sporadic probe behavior.
Mistake: Ignoring clients
Fix: Track station lists as primary reconnaissance artifacts; clients often reveal hidden network details.
Key Takeaway

Avoid simple setup errors, be patient, and treat clients as valuable sources of information while always working ethically.
