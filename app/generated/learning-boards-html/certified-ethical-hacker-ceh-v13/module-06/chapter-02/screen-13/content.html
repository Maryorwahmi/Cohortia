Build a topology from captures
Use RSSI and channel data to infer AP placement and coverage overlap.
Steps to map
  1. Collect PWR and channel readings for APs from airodump-ng across multiple locations.
  2. Record waypoints during a walking survey and note signal variations.
  3. Plot readings roughly on a floorplan or map to estimate AP placement and coverage.
Why it matters
Mapping helps you decide where to position a capture device, anticipate client movement, and identify APs placed in insecure or exposed positions.
Key Takeaway

Combine signal measurements and channel info to build a practical map used to prioritize reconnaissance and testing.

Practical notes
  • Stronger PWR generally means closer proximity but obstructions can alter readings.
  • Channels reveal interference patterns; overlapping channels reduce capture clarity.
  • Document the time and location of readings to support later remediation recommendations.
Common mistake: assuming PWR alone tells you exact location. Use multiple readings and waypoints for a reliable map.
