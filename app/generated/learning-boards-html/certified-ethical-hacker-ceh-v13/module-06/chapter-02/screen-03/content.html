Objectives — Practical Focus
Use focused capture parameters, save targeted packet logs, and combine signal observation with captures.
Targeted Capture & Saving
Learn to target a specific BSSID and channel and save the capture file for offline analysis, which increases efficiency and preserves privacy of unrelated networks.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Avoid Setup Mistakes
Ensure monitor mode is active, stop conflicting services, and wait sufficiently long to capture ground-truth traffic before concluding a network is inactive.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mapping & Signal Awareness
Combine RSSI and channel observations to infer AP placement and coverage; this helps plan physical reconnaissance or targeted captures later.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Ethics & Minimizing Disruption
Prioritize passive gathering; only use active, potentially disruptive techniques when explicitly authorized and necessary for the engagement.
