Station list: actionable intelligence
Capture and analyze station entries to reveal probes, associations, and movement between APs.
What to record
  • Station MAC and observed PWR to estimate proximity.
  • The AP BSSID the station is associated with, to see relationships.
  • Probed ESSIDs and packet counts to infer activity patterns.
Concrete scenario
A client that frequently probes for a hidden network is likely to provide the ESSID in its probe requests. Observing this client over time reveals the hidden name without active interference.
Pro-tip
Follow clients with strong PWR for best capture results, especially when trying to capture handshakes.
Mistake
Ignoring clients reduces your chances of seeing the frames that reveal hidden SSIDs or handshakes.
