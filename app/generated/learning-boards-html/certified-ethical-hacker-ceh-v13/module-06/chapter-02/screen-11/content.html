Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Safety & Scope
Work ethically and avoid disruption
Practical guidance for staying within scope and protecting operations while testing.
Authorization
Verify explicit permission for active techniques. Record what you are allowed to run and when.
Non‑disruptive priority
Prefer passive capture. If active tests are needed, schedule a window or notify stakeholders.
Document everything
Log commands, timestamps, interfaces, and capture filenames for repeatability and audit trails.
Safety in the field
When performing physical surveys, be aware of location safety and avoid drawing attention during war driving or walking audits.
Key Takeaway

Reconnaissance is valuable only when performed ethically and within scope. Protect operations and document your work.
