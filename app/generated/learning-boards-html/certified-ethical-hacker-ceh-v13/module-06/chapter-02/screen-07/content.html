Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Channel Hopping & Focus
Channel workflow
Sequence to move from discovery to focused capture for a target AP.
1
Scan broadly
Run airodump-ng with channel hopping to discover APs across the band and note BSSID and CH.
2
Select channel
Choose the AP channel (e.g., 6) and restart airodump-ng with the -c flag to focus on that frequency.
3
Filter by BSSID
Add the --bssid filter to capture only frames for the target AP, keeping file sizes small and relevant.
Key Takeaway

Channel-focused captures increase the chance of collecting critical frames and reduce post-capture analysis time.

Why channel focus helps
  • Reduces captured noise from unrelated APs on other channels.
  • Improves probability of seeing time-sensitive frames like handshakes from your target.
  • Smaller capture files are faster to analyze and transfer.
Regional note
Non‑overlapping channel sets differ by region. Knowing local channel plans informs which channels to prioritize.
Common mistake: forgetting that network managers or other processes can change your interface channel; ensure your monitor interface remains locked when focusing.
