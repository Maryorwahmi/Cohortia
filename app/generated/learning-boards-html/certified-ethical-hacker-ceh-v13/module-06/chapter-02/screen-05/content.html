airodump-ng: What the output means
Learn to read the columns that tell you which APs and clients are most useful to target.
Important columns
  • PWR: signal strength; higher values indicate closer devices and better capture chances.
  • Beacons and #Data: volume of observed frames; indicates activity and a chance to capture handshakes.
  • CH: channel the AP uses; helpful for focused captures with the -c flag.
  • ENC / CIPHER / AUTH: encryption and authentication details to assess target strength.
Example interpretation
An AP with strong PWR, active data frames, and WPA2/CCMP is a prime target for a focused capture to try to obtain a handshake.
Key Takeaway

Reading airodump-ng columns informs whether to focus on a given AP or to follow clients for revealing frames.
