Learning Objectives

From scope to reporting: measurable cloud pen-test skills

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define scope & obtain authorization
Learn to document which accounts, regions, and services are in scope and why written permission from client and provider matters.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Enumerate with CLIs and auditors
Use aws cli, az cli, and auditors like ScoutSuite to list buckets, users, roles, and find misconfigurations quickly.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify misconfigurations & IAM risks
Recognize public storage, permissive policies, and trust relations that enable escalation paths.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Report with remediation & proof
Document impact, proof-of-concept, and prioritized fixes so defenders can act quickly.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
