Comparison

High-impact vulnerability categories

IAM Privilege Escalation
Look for wildcard actions or trust policies that allow role chaining and escalate privileges.
Misconfigured Storage
Public buckets or permissive ACLs can leak secrets and data that enable further compromise.
Network Misconfigurations
Security groups or NSGs with broad inbound rules expose management interfaces or services.
Concrete example
A user policy allowed s3:GetObject and iam:ListUsers. By listing users and scanning public buckets, an attacker could find credentials stored in a bucket and then use them to escalate privileges. Proof includes the policy document and the bucket ACL output.
Fix recommendations
  • Apply least privilege to IAM policies; avoid '*' in actions or resources.
  • Enable public access block and tighten bucket ACLs.
  • Restrict security groups to known IP ranges and remove open management ports.
Key Takeaway

Group findings by category and provide both evidence and precise remediation steps to defenders.
