Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Cloud Logging
Definition
Audit logs and managed detection

Audit logs record management API actions. Managed detectors analyze those logs and telemetry to surface suspicious behavior like role assumption chains or mass data reads.

Checklist
  • Is multi-region audit logging enabled and retained?
  • Are logs aggregated to a central account or SIEM?
  • Is role-assume and console-login activity monitored and alerted?
  • Are logs protected from tampering and restricted in access?
Example
GuardDuty flagged unusual data access after a role assumption. Investigation used CloudTrail events to map the API calls and time stamps, enabling quick revocation of the compromised role.

Key Takeaway

Logging and detection are only useful when enabled, centralized, and monitored. Verify configuration, retention, and alerting during assessments.
