Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Remember
Confirm Scope

A clear SOW and provider notifications prevent accidental provider-impacting activity and legal exposure.

Enumerate Read-Only

Use CLI and auditing tools in read-only mode to gather evidence without changing state.

Least Privilege

Replace '*' actions and resources with specific, minimal permissions and add conditions where possible.

Final Thought

A safe, prioritized, and evidence-backed approach reduces risk and increases the likelihood that findings are remediated quickly.
