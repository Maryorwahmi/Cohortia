Key Concepts
Three compact concept cards to keep top of mind.
Evil Twin / Rogue AP
Fake APs mimic ESSIDs to intercept traffic or harvest credentials.
WPS PIN Weakness
The PIN is validated in halves, making brute-force attacks feasible; disable WPS.
WPA2-Enterprise Attacks
Rogue RADIUS and EAP phishing succeed if clients do not verify server certificates; prefer EAP-TLS.
Summary Takeaway

Protect authentication channels, disable risky convenience features, deploy WIDS/WIPS, and require written permission for any real-world testing.
