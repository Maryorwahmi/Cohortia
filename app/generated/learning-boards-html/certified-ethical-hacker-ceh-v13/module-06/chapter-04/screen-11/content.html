Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › Wireless DoS
Comparison
Denial of Service (DoS) Attacks
Differentiate between targeted deauths and high-volume beacon or auth floods.
Targeted deauth
Low-volume but effective disruption of specific clients or a single AP.
High-volume floods (mdk3)
Beacon floods and authentication floods create many fake APs or overwhelm radios and clients.
Detection & response
Deploy WIDS/WIPS with automated thresholds, correlate alerts with client logs, and use channel management to mitigate impact.
Incident checklist
If you detect a DoS: capture the offending MACs and frame rates, switch affected APs to alternate channels, increase logging, and validate whether an attacker is within range or spoofing from afar. Hardware resilience and WIDS rules together reduce the attack surface.
Key Takeaway
Availability attacks require automated detection, hardware quality, and channel management to limit user impact.
