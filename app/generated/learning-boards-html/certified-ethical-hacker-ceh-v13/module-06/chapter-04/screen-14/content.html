What to Remember
Three short defense-focused statements to carry forward.
Mutual Authentication
Clients must validate server certificates; prefer EAP-TLS where possible.
Disable WPS
WPS lowers brute-force cost; remove or lock it down and monitor WPS logs.
Detect & Respond
Use WIDS/WIPS and logging to spot deauth floods, rogue APs, and suspect RADIUS activity.
Final note

Always run demonstrations in isolated test environments, capture only test credentials, and obtain written permission before any real-world testing.
