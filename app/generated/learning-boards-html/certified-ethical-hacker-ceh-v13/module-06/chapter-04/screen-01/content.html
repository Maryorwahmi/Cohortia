Advanced Wireless Attacks & Defenses
Why attackers mimic trusted networks, how setup conveniences weaken security, and how networks can fight back.

In this lesson you will see concrete attack flows, a terminal lab to build a controlled Evil Twin, the WPS PIN weakness and tools that exploit it, and enterprise attack vectors like rogue RADIUS and EAP phishing. Every demo is paired with explicit defenses.

The lab steps use monitor-mode wireless adapters and simple DHCP/NAT. Defenses emphasize configuration hardening: disable WPS, require certificate validation, and deploy WIDS/WIPS to detect floods and rogue APs.
