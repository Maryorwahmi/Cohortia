Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › WPS PIN Weakness
Vulnerability
WPS PIN Structure
Why an 8-digit convenience PIN becomes an easy brute-force target.
Mechanics
The AP validates the PIN in two parts. Because of this split, the total attempts are the sum of the two halves rather than the product of eight digits, dramatically reducing the work factor.
Tools that exploit it
Reaver automates the brute-force flow and pixiewps can exploit implementation weaknesses to speed recovery.
Key Takeaway
Disable WPS. If that’s impossible, require lockout after failed PIN attempts and monitor for repeated WPS probes.
Visual breakdown
Effective search space
Because the PIN is validated in halves, the effective attempts are on the order of ten thousand instead of one hundred million in many cases.
Administrator checklist
  • Disable WPS on all APs.
  • Enable lockout and rate limiting for WPS operations.
  • Monitor logs for repeated WPS attempts.
Reflection
If WPS is enabled on a critical AP, how quickly can it be disabled and alerts created? That is the remediation you should recommend.
