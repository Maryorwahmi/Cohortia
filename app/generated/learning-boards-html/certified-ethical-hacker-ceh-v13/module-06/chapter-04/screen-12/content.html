Best Practices
Defensive Strategies & Common Mistakes
Concrete steps to reduce risk and common operator pitfalls to avoid.
Immediate configuration actions
  • Disable WPS on all APs.
  • Enforce server cert validation in enterprise supplicants.
  • Prefer EAP-TLS and client certificates where possible.
Monitoring & detection
Deploy WIDS/WIPS, create alerts for deauth spikes, WPS negotiation attempts, and duplicate ESSIDs, and correlate with client logs.
User training
Teach staff to verify certificates, treat unexpected captive portals with suspicion, and use VPNs on unknown networks.
Common operator mistakes
  • Assuming proximity prevents attacks.
  • Forgetting to disable WPS after deployment.
  • Not logging WPS or authentication anomalies for later forensics.
Remediation checklist
Disable WPS; enforce cert validation; deploy WIDS/WIPS; patch APs; require MFA; document lab tests and obtain written permission.
