Definition
WPA/WPA2-PSK fundamentals
The four-way handshake exchanges nonces and authentication data to derive session keys; a captured handshake enables offline PSK verification.
Concrete example
Capture the four-way handshake when a client connects or reconnects. With that capture, an attacker can test candidate passwords offline against the recorded material.
Misconceptions
  • Capturing a handshake does not immediately reveal the PSK.
  • Offline attacks only succeed if the PSK is in the wordlist or guess set.
  • A long, random PSK resists practical dictionary attacks.
Key Takeaway

WPA2 shifts the attacker’s task from intercepting weak IVs to finding the PSK; strong PSKs stop offline attacks.
