Comparison
WEP vs WPA/WPA2-PSK
WEP
Attacked via IV collection and ARP replay. Low difficulty in a lab due to design flaws.
WPA/WPA2-PSK
Attacked by capturing handshake and performing offline dictionary attacks; success depends on PSK strength.
Practical advice
  • Always deprecate WEP; it is not secure for any modern deployment.
  • Use strong PSKs or enterprise authentication to defend WPA/WPA2 networks.
  • Confirm capture completeness before attempting expensive offline attacks.
