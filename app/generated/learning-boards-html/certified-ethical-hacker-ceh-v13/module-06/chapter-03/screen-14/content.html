Best Practices
Common mistakes and mitigation
Troubleshoot captures first; harden networks with protocol upgrades and strong keys.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • WEP: confirm high IV counts and injection success.
  • WPA2: confirm the complete WPA handshake in the capture.
  • Wordlists: use appropriate lists; weak lists lead to false negatives about key strength.
Hardening recommendations
  • Remove WEP from service; it is insecure by design.
  • Use long, random PSKs or enterprise authentication to prevent dictionary attacks.
  • Include capture validation in test reports so results are reproducible.
Key Takeaway

Most failed attacks are capture problems or strong keys. Document and validate every capture before claiming a network is secure or insecure.
