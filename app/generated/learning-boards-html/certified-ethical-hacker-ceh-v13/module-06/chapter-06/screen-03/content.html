Objective verification

Each practice objective includes a specific verification step you can perform in a test environment.

Inspect IAM
Read policies, find wildcard principals, check for console access without MFA. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Check storage ACLs
Verify bucket policy and public access settings. Never download unknown data. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Audit network rules
Look for security groups and firewall rules allowing broad inbound access. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Confirm logging
Check for activity logs and retention settings to determine detection capabilities. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Safety and scope

Always work in a scoped engagement or a dedicated lab. Use temporary credentials with least privilege for tests, and avoid modifying or downloading unknown customer data.

Common assessor mistake
Focusing on provider infrastructure rather than customer configurations. Most breaches result from customer-side misconfigurations and application vulnerabilities.
