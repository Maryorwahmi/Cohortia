Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Open Storage Buckets
Problem

Public buckets expose sensitive data

Buckets with public read or list permissions can leak backups, configs, and keys. Discovery is straightforward; responsible handling is not optional.

Detection checklist
  • Check bucket ACL and policy for public principals.
  • Look for IsPublic: true indicators in policy status.
  • Record types of files visible without downloading content.
Command example
aws s3api get-bucket-acl --bucket my-sensitive-data-bucket
aws s3api get-bucket-policy-status --bucket my-sensitive-data-bucket
If ACL shows READ for AllUsers or policy status IsPublic true, treat as critical and report with remediation.
Assessor caution
Do not download or alter data. Observing directory listings is sufficient for proof of exposure; follow disclosure policies and preserve evidence.
Key Takeaway

Public buckets are discoverable and serious. Document exposure without interacting and recommend restricting access to authorized identities.
