Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Logging & Monitoring
Definition

Audit logs and detection

Logs record who did what and when. Without them, attackers can act unnoticed and incidents become harder to investigate.

Checks
  • Confirm activity logs like CloudTrail or Activity Log are enabled
  • Verify retention and centralization policies
  • Check alerting on critical events
Example
Missing logs hide privilege escalation
If IAM changes are not logged or retained, an attacker can escalate privileges and erase traces. Recommend enabling audit logs and alerts for IAM policy changes and key creations.
Misconception
Assuming short log retention is sufficient. Retain logs long enough for investigation and correlate events centrally.
Key Takeaway

Verify logs are enabled, retained, and monitored. Recommend centralization and alerts for security-critical events.
