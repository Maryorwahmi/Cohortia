Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Assessment Priorities
Process

Prioritize and avoid harm

  1. Map who controls each security layer.
  2. Inspect IAM and public data exposures first.
  3. Confirm logging and retention for detection.
  4. Report with prioritized remediation and evidence.
Common mistakes
  • Testing provider-managed infrastructure in-scope
  • Downloading or modifying customer data without authorization
  • Assuming a configured control implies monitoring and response
Key Takeaway

Use ownership mapping and impact scoring to prioritize findings. Always verify scope and avoid actions that could disrupt services or expose you legally.
