Search for public buckets safely

Use public search patterns and authorized CLI checks to confirm exposure without downloading or changing data.

Example commands (illustrative)
aws s3api get-bucket-acl --bucket my-sensitive-data-bucket
aws s3api get-bucket-policy-status --bucket my-sensitive-data-bucket
If policy status shows IsPublic true, document the finding and follow disclosure practices. Do not download files.
Safety checklist
  • Confirm scope and authorization before testing.
  • Use temporary least-privilege credentials.
  • Do not download, change, or delete any data you encounter.
  • Document evidence and recommend immediate policy changes.
Pro tip

When in doubt, stop and re-confirm scope. Responsible disclosure builds trust and avoids legal exposure.
