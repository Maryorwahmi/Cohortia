Assessment Focus

Common Vulnerabilities and Misconfigurations

Identify the misconfigurations that turn protocol strengths into real weaknesses.

WEP IV reuse
Capturing repeated IVs enables statistical key recovery.
Weak PSKs
An easily guessable passphrase makes WPA2-PSK vulnerable to offline attacks.
Enterprise misconfigs
Poorly configured RADIUS or EAP methods expose credential paths.
Example remediation
Migrate away from WEP, enforce strong PSKs, and migrate to WPA3 or WPA2-Enterprise with strong EAP methods and certificate validation.
Reporting
Document evidence and suggest prioritized fixes that reduce exposure to rapid compromise.
