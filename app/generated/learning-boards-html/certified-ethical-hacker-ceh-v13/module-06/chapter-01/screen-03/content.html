How you will use these skills

From planning the engagement to verifying adapter capabilities, these objectives support safe, effective wireless assessments.

Choose tools by standard
Example: older 2.4 GHz networks may be 802.11b/g and often have weaker protections; choose capture tools that handle that traffic.
Verify adapter features
Example: confirm monitor mode and injection before launching an assessment to avoid surprises.
Document permissions
Example: written permission clarifies scope and prevents legal exposure during live tests.
