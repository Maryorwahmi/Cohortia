Setting Up and Testing Adapter Capabilities

A concise, permission-first lab routine to validate your tools before testing.

Step list
  1. Connect adapter and confirm with lsusb.
  2. Identify interface with iwconfig.
  3. Stop network-manager and wpa_supplicant.
  4. Enable monitor mode with airmon-ng and verify iwconfig shows Mode:Monitor.
  5. Optionally test injection with aireplay-ng -9 when authorized.
  6. Restore monitor mode and restart services; document findings.
Example command
sudo airmon-ng start wlan0
Common error
Not passing the USB device to a VM or neglecting to stop network services are the most frequent causes of failure.
Key Takeaway

Validate hardware and document results before any intrusive testing to maintain reproducibility and legal compliance.
