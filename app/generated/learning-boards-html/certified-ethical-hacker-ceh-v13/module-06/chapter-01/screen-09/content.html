Definition

Packet Injection — Capabilities and Limitations

Injection enables active tests but depends on chipset and driver support; always validate before relying on it.

Concrete example
Use aireplay-ng -9 on wlan0mon to test packet injection; a successful test indicates the adapter can craft and send frames.
Common misconception
Assuming monitoring support implies injection capability—many adapters monitor only.
Ethical note
Active injection can disrupt services; use it only under explicit permission and within scope limits.
Chipset example
Realtek RTL8812AU and Atheros AR9271 are examples mentioned as commonly supported chipsets; confirm driver support in your environment.
Pro tip

Keep a short list of validated adapters and drivers in your lab notes to avoid last-minute hardware issues.
