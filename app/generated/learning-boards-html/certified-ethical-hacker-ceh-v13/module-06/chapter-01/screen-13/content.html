Ethics & Legal

Legal and Ethical Boundaries

Explicit written permission and a defined scope protect both testers and clients from legal and operational harm.

Get written permission
Define allowed techniques, time windows, and excluded systems before testing.
Limit tests
If injection is disallowed, do only passive reconnaissance and reporting.
Restore services
Plan for restoration and document any changes you make during testing.
Reflection

Consider how you would document scope and consent for a wireless test at your organization to avoid accidental disruption or legal exposure.
