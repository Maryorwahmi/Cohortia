Workflow steps
Scope

Scope: Confirm authorization and targets.
Non-invasive: Run DNS, read-only SNMP, ldapsearch where allowed.
Capture: Save commands, timestamps, and sanitized outputs.
Assess: Determine impact and need for deeper probing.
Escalate: Request approval before noisy or intrusive actions.
Checklist
  • Have written permission and a clear scope.
  • Prioritize low-noise enumeration first.
  • Record and sanitize all findings before including them in reports.
