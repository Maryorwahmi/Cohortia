Get authorization
Always confirm scope and permission before running active enumeration.
Check defaults
Look for default SNMP community strings, anonymous SMB, and unrestricted zone transfers.
Document & redact
Save command, timestamp, and sanitized output; recommend prioritized fixes.
Final Tip

Small pieces of enumeration data often combine to form a complete attack map. Collect them responsibly and communicate their impact clearly to defenders.
