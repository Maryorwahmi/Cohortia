Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › NTP Enumeration
Command
ntpq -c peers 192.168.1.20
Peers output shows connected NTP clients and their IPs; internal ranges help map the network.
Why it matters
  • Internal IPs found here can link hosts found via DNS or SMB.
  • Use NTP clues to expand your scanning range in an authorized lab.
  • Defend by restricting queries and hiding client lists from unauthenticated requests.
