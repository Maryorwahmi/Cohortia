Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Scanning vs Enumeration
Definition

What enumeration is

Enumeration is the deliberate querying of services to extract users, shares, device info, hostnames, or peers. It builds the context scanning cannot provide.

Analogy
Scanning finds the doors. Enumeration looks through unlocked doors to list rooms and people — information that directly affects next steps.
Common misconception
Some treat enumeration as passive. In fact it actively queries services and is noisier — treat it with authorization and care.
Concrete example
From scan to enumeration
# scan shows port 445 open
nmap -p 445 192.168.1.105

# enumeration lists shares and users
enum4linux -U 192.168.1.105
enum4linux -S 192.168.1.105
Key takeaway
Enumeration turns open ports into actionable findings like valid usernames or sensitive shares.
Check your understanding
If you discover a username through enumeration, how would that affect password attacks or social engineering planning?
