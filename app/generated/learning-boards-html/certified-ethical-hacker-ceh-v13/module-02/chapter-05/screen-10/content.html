How Tor works

Traffic is routed through multiple volunteer relays with layered encryption. Each relay only removes one layer and knows the next hop, not the original source and final destination together.

Strengths

Strong indirection and end-to-end anonymity for supported protocols.

Practical limits
  • Exit nodes are commonly blacklisted by targets.
  • High latency makes active scans slow and often unreliable.
  • Some protocols and port scans do not work through Tor as expected.
When to use Tor

Prefer Tor for anonymous browsing or lightweight checks where anonymity is the priority and speed is secondary.

Key Takeaway

Tor is powerful for anonymity but has exit-node and latency trade-offs that limit its use for heavy scanning.
