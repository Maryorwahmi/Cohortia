Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Key Concepts
proxy_dns
When enabled, DNS requests are routed through the proxy chain to prevent DNS-based leaks to your ISP.
Nmap compatibility
Use -sT for proxy-routed scans; -sS requires raw sockets and often fails through proxies.
Documentation
Record proxy lists, modes, timestamps, and verification outputs to make scans reproducible and auditable.
Final takeaway

Anonymity is layered and testable. Build your stack deliberately, verify it, and document results so your scanning is realistic, ethical, and defensible.
