Learning Objectives

Learn, understand, apply, and verify anonymity techniques for scanning.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Anonymity & Obfuscation

Explain why you mask identity during active reconnaissance and what risks you avoid by doing so. Verify by describing two real leak vectors.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
VPN Fundamentals

Describe how a VPN encrypts traffic and masks your IP, and why provider logging policies affect anonymity. Show a verification step using a public IP check.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Proxy Chains

Configure and verify proxychains to route traffic through one or more proxies. Confirm functionality by checking the public IP via proxychains.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Proxy Types & Use

Differentiate HTTP and SOCKS proxies and select the right type for Nmap or other TCP/UDP tools. Include a short mnemonic for protocol fit.
