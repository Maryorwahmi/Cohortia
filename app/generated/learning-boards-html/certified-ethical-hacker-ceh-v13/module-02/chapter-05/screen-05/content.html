Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › VPNs
How it works

A VPN encrypts all traffic between you and a remote server. The remote server forwards your traffic and therefore presents its IP to the destination.

Concrete example

Connect your Kali VM to a VPN and use a public IP check; the service will show the VPN server IP, confirming outbound masking.

Misconception

Assuming a VPN hides everything. DNS or split tunnels can still leak, and providers may keep logs.

Key Takeaway

Use reputable providers and verify DNS behavior; treat VPNs as the base layer of an anonymity stack.

Practical checks
  • Before connecting, run a public IP check and record the value.
  • After connecting to the VPN, repeat the IP check and confirm it changed to the VPN server IP.
  • Test DNS resolution paths to ensure queries go through the VPN and not your local resolver.
  • Consider time-stamping and documenting the VPN endpoint used for later auditability.
Analogy

A VPN is like sending a sealed envelope to a postal hub; the hub delivers the letter and appears as the sender to recipients.
