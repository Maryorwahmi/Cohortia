Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › TCP Connect vs SYN
TCP Connect (-sT)
Performs a full three-way handshake. Good when you cannot use privileged raw sockets or when you need to confirm a service accepts real connections.
nmap -sT 192.168.1.100
When to choose
Use when you lack privileges or must validate a complete connection.
SYN Scan (-sS)
Sends SYN, receives SYN-ACK, sends RST. Faster and often generates fewer full-connection logs, but typically requires elevated privileges.
sudo nmap -sS 192.168.1.100
When to choose
Use when you have privileges and want a quicker inventory with fewer completed handshakes.
Key Takeaway

Pick the scan that fits your access level and your tolerance for being logged on the target system; both scans have valid uses.
