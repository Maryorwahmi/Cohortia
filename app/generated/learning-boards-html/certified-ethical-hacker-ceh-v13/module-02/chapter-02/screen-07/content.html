How UDP differs

Because UDP has no handshake, scanners rely on application responses or ICMP messages. Absence of a response may mean the port is open and the service ignored the probe, or that a firewall dropped the packet.

sudo nmap -sU 192.168.1.105 -p 53,67,68,123,161
Example interpretation
No response on 161 -> reported as open|filtered; follow-up is required to confirm SNMP presence.
Common mistake
Assuming no response means closed. It may be filtered or lost; never assume without follow-up probes.
Practical tip
Expect UDP scans to be slower; plan for timeouts and consider targeted probes for likely services to reduce ambiguity.
Key Takeaway

Treat UDP results conservatively and follow up on open|filtered responses with application-specific probes in a lab.
