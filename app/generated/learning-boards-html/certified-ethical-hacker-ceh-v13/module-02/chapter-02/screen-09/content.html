Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Interpreting Nmap Port States
Port states
Open
Service is accepting connections; pursue version detection and service checks.
Closed
Host replied but no service listens on the port.
Filtered
A firewall or filter blocked probes; use different techniques to investigate.
Unfiltered
Response eliminates filtering but doesn't confirm open; rare.
Open|Filtered
Nmap cannot tell if the port is open or filtered, common with UDP.
Closed|Filtered
Rare; indicates ambiguity often from special scans.
Example output
PORT    STATE       SERVICE
22/tcp  open        ssh
80/tcp  open        http
123/udp open|filtered ntp
Misconception
Filtered does not equal closed; it usually means a defensive device is interfering with probes.
Key Takeaway

Let port states guide your follow-up: different states suggest different probe types or lab confirmation.
