Port Scanning Techniques & Tools

Understand how Nmap finds services, what open and filtered ports mean, and how to choose scans that balance speed and stealth.

In this lesson
Live commands • Result interpretation • Practical lab
Why it matters
Every open port is a potential entry point. Accurate scanning helps you prioritize defensive or offensive testing safely.
Tools
Nmap provides flexible scan types, version detection, OS fingerprinting, and scripting to automate checks.
Explore Key Topics
TCP SYN (-sS)
Half-open, fast, stealthier when you need fewer logs.
UDP (-sU)
Probes connectionless services; expect slower, ambiguous results.
Stealth (FIN/XMAS/NULL)
Manipulate TCP flags to provoke different responses; less reliable on modern stacks.
Visual concept
Scan types grouped by detectability
Fast & stealthy
SYN scan: half-open, reduces full-connection logs while remaining effective.
Full connection
Connect scan: reliable but more visible; use when you cannot run privileged SYN scans.
UDP & Stealth
UDP is slow and ambiguous. FIN/XMAS/NULL may bypass naive filters but are often blocked today.
