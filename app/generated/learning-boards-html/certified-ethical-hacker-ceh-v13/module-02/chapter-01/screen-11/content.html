Stepwise Workflow
A repeatable sequence to minimize risk and improve accuracy.
1
Passive Recon
Collect public and observed traffic data.
2
Obtain Authorization
Written scope, duration, and permitted tools.
3
Low-intensity probes
ARP, ICMP, and TCP pings as needed.
4
Escalate carefully
SYN scans, UDP probes, and fingerprinting after validation.
Key Takeaway
A documented, phased approach reduces false positives and operational risk while producing verifiable results.
