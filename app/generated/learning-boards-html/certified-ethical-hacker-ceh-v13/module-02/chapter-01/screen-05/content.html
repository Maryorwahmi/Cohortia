Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Active vs Passive Scanning
Active Scanning
Direct interaction with the target to elicit responses and precise information.
  • Examples: ping sweeps, SYN scans, OS fingerprinting.
  • Pros: detailed host and port state, specific service identification.
  • Cons: higher chance of detection by IDS/IPS and logging systems.
Pro tip
Begin with low-intensity scans and escalate only when necessary and authorized.
Passive Scanning
Observe traffic or public data without sending probes to reduce detectability.
  • Examples: packet sniffing, log analysis, public asset discovery.
  • Pros: stealthier and less disruptive to operations.
  • Cons: less likely to reveal closed or silent hosts and specific port states.
Key Takeaway
Combine passive observation with targeted active probes when you need confirmation.
