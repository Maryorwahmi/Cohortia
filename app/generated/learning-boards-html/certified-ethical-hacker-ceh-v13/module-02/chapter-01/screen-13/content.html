Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Legal and Ethical Considerations
Authorization and Scope
Written permission is required; scope, duration, and allowed techniques must be explicit.
Required items
  • Written permission naming assets and time window.
  • Allowed scan types and intensity limits.
  • Emergency contacts and stop conditions.
Risks
Unauthorized or aggressive scans can cause legal exposure and operational outages. Always err on the side of caution.
Key Takeaway
Permission, scope, and safety measures are not optional; they are central to professional, ethical scanning.
