Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › How you'll use these skills
Concrete Outcomes
What you will be able to do after studying this chapter.
Plan reconnaissance with intent
Choose passive observation to gather initial intel, then escalate to active scans only when authorized and necessary.
Perform safe host discovery
Use ARP and TCP pings where ICMP is blocked; verify ambiguous results by alternative methods.
Document and report
Record commands, timestamps, and results so scans are reproducible and auditable.
Avoid disruption
Start with low-intensity scans, monitor effects, and stop if services show instability.
