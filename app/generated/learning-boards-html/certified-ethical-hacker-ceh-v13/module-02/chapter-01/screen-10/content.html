OS Fingerprinting Overview
Compare network replies against known patterns to infer OS family and likely versions.
How it helps
Prioritize exploits and tests by targeting OS-specific vulnerabilities when confident of the fingerprint.
Caveat
Firewalls and intrusion prevention systems can alter packets, producing incorrect fingerprints.
Example
If network replies match patterns for a specific Unix stack, focus research on patches and CVEs relevant to that system family, but validate with banners and service probes.
