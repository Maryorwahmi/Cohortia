Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Best Practices for Effective, Stealthy Scans
Practical Guidelines
A hierarchical view of conservative to deeper scan methods.
Start
Passive, ARP, ICMP where allowed.
Then
TCP pings to common ports when ICMP is blocked.
Finally
Targeted SYN/UDP scans and fingerprinting for confirmed hosts.
Key Takeaway
Be methodical: escalate only when needed, verify ambiguous signals, and keep scans gentle and documented.
