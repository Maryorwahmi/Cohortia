Permission & Scope
Written authorization and clearly defined scope are required.
Interpret Protocols
ICMP, TCP, and UDP responses mean different things; corroborate ambiguous results.
Scan Conservatively
Rate-limit probes, escalate carefully, and log everything to avoid disruption.
Final Thought
Effective scanning is about quality, not noise. Use methods that give you reliable answers while protecting the systems you test.
