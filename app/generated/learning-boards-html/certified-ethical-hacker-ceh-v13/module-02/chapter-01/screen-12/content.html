Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them
Frequent Errors
Four mistakes to watch for and practical mitigations.
Scanning without permission
Always obtain explicit written authorization defining scope and duration.
Overly aggressive scans
Use rate limits and smaller probe sets to avoid DoS and alarms.
Misinterpretation
Verify with alternate probes; don’t assume no reply equals offline.
Poor documentation
Log commands, timestamps, and results so findings are reproducible and defensible.
Key Takeaway
Good process protects assets and the tester: permission, restraint, verification, and documentation are essential.
