Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What is Network Scanning?
Definition
Active probing to discover live hosts, open ports, and services for penetration testing planning.
Analogy
Like testing doors and windows of a building to find which ones are unlocked and which have alarms.
Why it matters
  • Identifies attack surface: hosts, ports, services.
  • Guides vulnerability research on discovered services.
  • Informs safe, scoped penetration testing steps.
Concrete example
Finding port 22 open suggests SSH; the next step is to identify the SSH service version to check for known vulnerabilities.
Common misconception
A single scan result is definitive. In reality, filtered or transient responses require verification with alternative techniques.
Key Takeaway
Use scanning to produce verifiable intelligence; always cross-check and document results.
