Competency checklist
Apply these skills in the lab and in real assessments.
CVE Mapping
Locate CVE IDs and summarize risk and exploitability for each finding.
Scan Tuning
Choose scan profiles and schedule updates to balance accuracy and load.
Verification
Manually confirm high-impact results before remediation.
Remediation
Recommend patches, configuration changes, or compensating controls with prioritization.
Mnemonic
Map CVE → Verify → Prioritize → Remediate.
Practical tips
  • Keep vulnerability feeds current to reduce false negatives.
  • Prefer credentialed scans for internal systems when authorized.
  • Document verification steps alongside any critical finding.
