Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Nessus vs OpenVAS
Side-by-side
Nessus
Commercial scanner with a large plugin library, polished reports, and enterprise support. Often used where vendor support and compliance reporting are required.
OpenVAS / GVM
Open-source scanning engine with an active feed. Good for labs and smaller teams; requires feed and config management for best accuracy.
Choose by need:
  • Budget and licensing constraints.
  • Need for vendor support or advanced reporting formats.
  • Comfort with managing updates and tuning scan profiles.
Example trade-off
If you must produce regulator-ready reports quickly and need vendor-backed validation, a commercial scanner can save time. For repeatable lab practice and cost-sensitive projects, OpenVAS provides strong scanning capabilities with more hands-on maintenance.
Practical tip
Run both tools when possible; differences in feeds can surface otherwise missed findings and reduce false negatives.
Misconception
Open-source does not equal inaccurate. Properly updated feeds and tuning yield reliable results.
Key Takeaway

Choose tools based on support needs, budget, and report requirements. Use multiple tools and manual verification when stakes are high.
