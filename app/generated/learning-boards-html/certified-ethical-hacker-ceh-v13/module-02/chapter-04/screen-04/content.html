Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Scanning vs Enumeration vs Vulnerability Scan
Definition
Precise terms for each reconnaissance stage and their outputs.
  • Network scanning: finds live hosts and open ports.
  • Enumeration: collects service details and banners.
  • Vulnerability scanning: matches observed data to known CVEs.
Common misconception
Scanners do not find every vulnerability; they detect known patterns. Manual verification and multiple techniques are essential.
Key Takeaway

Treat scanning as stages of increasing detail: each step builds context for accurate vulnerability assessment.

Illustrative pipeline
Scan
Identify live hosts and open ports. Example output: 192.168.1.105:80 open.
Enumerate
Collect service banners: Apache 2.2.8 reported.
Scan for Vulns
Cross-reference Apache 2.2.8 with CVE entries to identify known issues.
Example scenario
A public server shows an old banner; scanner flags multiple CVEs — verify patch status manually before escalating.
Check your understanding
Why might a scanner report a vulnerability that isn't exploitable in your context? Consider network exposure and authentication requirements.
