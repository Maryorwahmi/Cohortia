Verify CVEs
Use CVE details to confirm exploitability and required conditions.
Prioritize by exposure
Public-facing services often need faster remediation than isolated lab systems.
Credentialed scans
Use when authorized to reduce false negatives and confirm patches.
Final guidance
Document verification steps, include rollback plans in remediation, and treat scans as part of a continuous discovery and hardening cycle.
