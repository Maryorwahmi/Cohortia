Core Concept Legal and Ethical Boundaries
Rules of Engagement essentials
  • Authorized IP ranges and time windows for active tests.
  • Permitted techniques (e.g., light scans, no DoS testing) and banned actions.
  • Escalation contacts and incident handling instructions.
  • Data handling and reporting requirements for sensitive findings.
Example scenario
A signed RoE allows external port scans on specified ranges between 02:00 and 04:00; you must avoid internal host probing even if public records hint at internal IPs.
Misconception
Publicly visible data is always permissible to collect without policy review. It may still require consent or careful handling.
Warning

Unauthorized active reconnaissance can be treated as an attack. Always confirm written permission and scope before probing.
