What to Remember Key Concepts
Footprinting
The initial evidence collection that enables focused testing.
Passive vs Active
Passive is low-risk and public; active interacts with systems and must be authorized.
Rules of Engagement
Define scope, permitted techniques, and approvals to limit legal and operational risk.
Final Takeaway

Collect, corroborate, and document intelligence, then map it to a prioritized, approved plan for safe testing.
