Footprinting and Reconnaissance: The Foundation
Gather the right intelligence to guide every later step in an ethical test.

Footprinting is the deliberate collection of information about a target to plan safe, focused testing. It reduces noise, highlights likely weaknesses, and prevents wasted effort.

This lesson emphasizes legal boundaries, contrasts passive and active approaches, and shows how specific data points shape the subsequent phases of a penetration test.

In this lesson
Define, compare, and apply reconnaissance
Passive reconnaissance — public records, OSINT
Active reconnaissance — scans and probes (authorized only)
Outcome — focused tests, actionable findings
Explore Key Topics
Passive Recon
