Summary & Strategy Plan from reconnaissance
Prioritization template
- Finding description, source, confidence.
- Suggested test and required approval level.
- Expected impact and mitigation advice.
Concrete example
Host 198.51.100.5 — Apache 2.4.x; source: banner + archived page; confidence: medium. Suggested: light version scan and manual check. Approval: external scanning only; no exploitation without explicit sign-off.
Common-mistake
Submitting raw logs without suggested actions. Always translate evidence into recommended, scoped steps.
Key Takeaway

Deliver reconnaissance as a prioritized, approved plan that directs safe scanning and verification.
