Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Clearing Tracks
What defenders need to know
Clearing tracks hides evidence. Describe indicators, not operational recipes for attackers.
Indicators: log gaps, unexpected timestamp changes, or unusual process restarts.
Mitigations: centralized immutable logs, strong access controls, and alerting on suspicious deletions.
Reporting guidance
Include detection points and recovery steps, avoid publishing scripts or procedures that attackers could use.
Scenario
Describe the evidence gap and recommend how the security team can confirm and recover without exposing methods.
Consider: How would immutable logging change an investigation after an attacker clears tracks?
