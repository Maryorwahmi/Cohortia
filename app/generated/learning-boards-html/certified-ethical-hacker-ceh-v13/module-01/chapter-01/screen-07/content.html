Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Reconnaissance & Scanning
Reconnaissance
Collect public information to map the environment and identify likely entry points.
Sources: DNS, web pages, certificates, and public code repositories.
Goal: Build a list of candidates to probe during scanning.
Example
Find a subdomain and a mention of a legacy app version in a public file.
Scanning
Confirm which hosts and services are reachable and worth testing further.
  • Port discovery and service enumeration.
  • Version identification to prioritize likely vulnerabilities.
  • Respect rate limits and RoE to avoid disruption.
Key Takeaway

Reconnaissance guides scanning; scanning confirms what to test. Do both carefully and document findings.
