Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Legal & Ethical Responsibilities
Key duties
Authorization, confidentiality, and documented conduct form the legal and ethical baseline.
Authorization: Signed RoE before testing.
Confidentiality: Secure handling of findings and limited distribution.
Conduct: Avoid prohibited actions and escalate critical issues per plan.
Common mistakes & controls
Mistakes include scope creep, data exfiltration, and poor documentation. Controls: clear RoE, logs, and emergency contacts.
Action
Keep a tamper-proof activity log and use secure channels for all communications and reports.
Reflection prompt: How would you document a critical finding while preserving confidentiality?
