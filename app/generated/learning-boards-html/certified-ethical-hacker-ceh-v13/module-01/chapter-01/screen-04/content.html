Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What is Ethical Hacking?
Working definition
Identify vulnerabilities with explicit permission to improve security.

Ethical hacking combines technical testing and professional rules. You use reconnaissance, scanning, and controlled exploitation to evaluate defenses. Permission and intent separate ethical work from criminal hacking.

Misconception: good intent alone does not equal legal permission. Always secure written authorization before testing.

Key Takeaway

Definition requires both authorization and a defensive purpose; document scope before testing.

Analogy: Digital Safety Inspector
An inspector tests fire doors to find failures before a real emergency. Ethical hackers test systems to prevent real breaches.
Role
Find weaknesses, recommend fixes, protect users.
Responsibility
Avoid harm, maintain confidentiality, follow RoE.
