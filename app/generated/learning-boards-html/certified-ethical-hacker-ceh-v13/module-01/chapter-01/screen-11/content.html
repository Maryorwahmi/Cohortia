Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Hacker Types
White Hat
Authorized, defensive testing with a remit to improve security.
Grey Hat
Ambiguous authorization; disclosure practices can cross ethical lines.
Black Hat
Unauthorized and malicious activity for personal gain or disruption.
Misconception: hats are not about tools. The same techniques can be used by any hat; authorization and intent are the difference.
Reflect on how you would explain these distinctions to non-technical stakeholders.
