Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Value for Organizations
Why organizations hire ethical hackers
Find and fix vulnerabilities, meet compliance, and reduce the chance of damaging breaches.
Reduce breach risk through prioritized remediation.
Support regulatory requirements with documented assessments.
Improve security culture by demonstrating real risks and fixes.
Concrete illustration
Finding a SQL injection and fixing it prevents a potential compromise of customer data, financial loss, and compliance violations.
Key Takeaway

Ethical testing converts unknown risks into prioritized, actionable work that defenders can execute.
