CEH Phases
A sequence to make testing thorough and repeatable.
Reconnaissance
Gather public information to map targets and identify likely entry points.
Scanning
Probe for live hosts, open ports, and services to focus exploitation efforts.
Gaining Access
Exploit vulnerabilities to demonstrate impact, using safe proof-of-concept techniques.
Maintaining Access
Show persistence mechanisms an attacker might use, documented for defenders.
Clearing Tracks
Describe how an attacker could hide evidence so defenders can detect and remediate.
Practical checklist
Use the phases as a running checklist: document findings at each stage and avoid moving forward without confirming prerequisites.
Avoid skipping reconnaissance — it leads to noisy and unfocused scans.
Maintain clear documentation of each exploit used and its proof-of-concept limitations.
Always link findings back to impact and remediation suggestions for stakeholders.
Reflect: Which phase will you practice first in the lab, reconnaissance or scanning?
