What is Maintaining Access?
Persistence techniques an attacker might use to stay within a network.
Examples: hidden accounts, scheduled jobs, startup scripts.
Ethical rule: Do not create persistence in production unless RoE explicitly authorizes an approved test lab.
Defensive guidance
Recommend steps to detect and remove persistence, such as auditing accounts, startup entries, and scheduled tasks.
Scenario
Document hypothetical persistence an attacker could use and include exact remediation actions.
Question: Which logs would you recommend defenders monitor to detect persistence?
