Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
White / Grey Hats
White-hat: authorized; grey-hat: operates in a legal grey area when disclosing without permission.
Rules of Engagement
The formal authorization and scope document that protects client and tester.
Vulnerability & Exploit
A vulnerability is a weakness; an exploit is the method or code that takes advantage of it.
Final takeaway
Use precise terminology and documented authorization to make your findings actionable and protect everyone involved.
