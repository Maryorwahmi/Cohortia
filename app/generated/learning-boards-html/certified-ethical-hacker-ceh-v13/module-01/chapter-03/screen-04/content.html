Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › What is OSINT
Definition

OSINT is collecting and analyzing publicly available information. It stays passive and focuses on connecting facts to form useful hypotheses.

Example
A PDF indexed by Google references an internal project. A LinkedIn post from a staff member names the project and date. Together they show scope and personnel.
Common mistake
Treating a single public fact as conclusive without cross-checking other sources.
Key Takeaway

OSINT is passive and evidence-based: collect multiple public signals to form validated intelligence you can report.

Practical checklist
  • Record source URL and capture timestamp.
  • Cross-check names, dates, and file metadata.
  • Note whether data was intentionally published or leaked.
  • Keep activities within authorization and scope.
