Ethical checklist
  • Confirm scope and written permission before targeted queries on non-test domains.
  • Capture timestamps and full URLs for every finding.
  • Annotate confidence and suggest mitigations for defenders.
Common mistakes
Treating an exposed fact as permission to access, failing to document provenance, and over-relying on a single source are frequent errors that make reports unusable.
Reporting template
Query, captured URL, timestamp, raw output, interpretation, confidence level, and mitigation recommendation.
