Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Social media analysis
What to collect
  • Public names and job titles with profile URLs and timestamps.
  • Images that reveal equipment or layout, noting privacy context.
  • Public posts mentioning projects, events, or vendor names.
Concrete scenario
A LinkedIn job post lists a new cloud migration project. A public Instagram photo from an employee shows a whiteboard with the same project name and a date. Together, these public clues form a timeline and identify responsible teams.
Misconception
Not all social posts are reliable or recent—always capture the date and cross-check with other public records.
Lab task
Find one public staff profile on example.com and record the profile URL, role, and one corroborating public item that links the person to an organizational project.
