Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Maltego vs Shodan
Maltego
Graphical link analysis aggregating WHOIS, DNS, social handles, and domain links to visualize relationships and enable attribution hypotheses.
Use-case
Connect an email to a domain and to public social profiles to identify responsible parties or related assets.
Shodan
Search engine for internet-connected devices; indexes banners to reveal open ports, services, and device types reachable from the internet.
Use-case
Discover exposed webcams, routers, or misconfigured services that could be reachable without authentication.
Comparison takeaway
Maltego = relationship graphs for attribution and link-following. Shodan = asset discovery for exposed services. Use each tool where its data model supports your investigative hypothesis and always remain passive unless scanning is explicitly permitted.
