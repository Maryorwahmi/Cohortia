Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) Techniques

Learn to collect public data responsibly: search operators, social media analysis, domain and DNS lookups, and OSINT tools.

In this lesson
How to find and interpret public facts without active scanning, and why that protects you legally while producing high-value intelligence.
Why it matters
Passive footprinting limits risk, preserves evidence, and produces actionable leads for later in-scope testing.
Explore Key Topics
Google Dorking
Crafted search operators to find exposed pages and files.
WHOIS & DNS
Registration and DNS records reveal ownership and infrastructure.
Maltego & Shodan
Visualization and device indexing for link and asset discovery.
Visual summary
Public sources → structured facts → linked hypotheses
Search
Advanced operators that reduce noise and surface sensitive files or pages.
Records
WHOIS and DNS records map registrants and infrastructure.
Social
Public posts reveal personnel, events, and technology hints.
Tools
Maltego links facts; Shodan indexes device banners and services.
Key Takeaway

Start with passive collection to build an evidence-backed profile. Combine multiple public signals before forming actionable hypotheses.
