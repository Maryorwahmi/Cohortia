Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Automation & theHarvester
Automated collection pattern
Run an aggregator to collect candidate emails, subdomains, and hostnames, then validate the highest-priority results manually and capture source evidence.
Example step
theHarvester -d example.com -b google
Review the output, then validate an email or subdomain with separate queries before recording it as evidence.
Workflow tip
Prioritize manual checks for high-risk findings, and maintain a provenance column for each aggregated item in your report.
Lab task
Run an aggregator on example.com to create a candidate list, then validate two candidates manually and record the verification steps and sources.
