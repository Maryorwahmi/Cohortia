Safe example queries
Find PDFs mentioning confidential
site:example.com filetype:pdf confidential
Why: PDFs commonly hold reports and contracts that may reveal sensitive project details.
Locate possible admin login pages
site:example.com inurl:admin intitle:login
Why: Admin panels may be forgotten or misconfigured and reveal management endpoints.
Search spreadsheets for passwords
site:example.com filetype:xls "passwords"
Why: Spreadsheets sometimes store credentials or lists that are high priority to report.
Practice routine
Run queries only on permitted domains, capture URL and timestamp, save screenshots, and write a short rationale for each finding.
Check your understanding
For each dork you run in the lab, record one defender action that would mitigate the exposure revealed by the result.
