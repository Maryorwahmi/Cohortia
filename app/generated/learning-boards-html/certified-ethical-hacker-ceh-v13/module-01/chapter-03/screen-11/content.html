Interpreting a banner
A typical Shodan result shows IP, open ports, service name, and banner text that may indicate device type or software version—record all fields and recommend mitigations.
Sample output fields
  • IP: the address of the device.
  • Port: open service port, e.g., 80 or 554.
  • Service: protocol or product string.
  • Banner: product/version text or welcome message.
Reporting checklist
  • Record IP, port, and full banner text.
  • Note geographic or ISP context if available.
  • Propose mitigations: restrict access, patch, or change defaults.
