Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Key Concepts
DNS Enumeration
Query A, MX, NS records to find hosts, mail servers, and authoritative name servers.
Host Discovery
Use nmap -sn and ARP where appropriate to find live hosts within verified ranges.
TCP vs UDP
TCP scans are handshake-based and clearer; UDP scans are connectionless and often ambiguous.
Summary: Combine DNS leads, verified network ranges, and protocol-aware scanning while maintaining strict authorization, documentation, and minimal impact.
