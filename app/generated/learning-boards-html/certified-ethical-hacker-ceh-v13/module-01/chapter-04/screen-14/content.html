DNS & Traceroute
dig example.com A

dig example.com MX

dig example.com NS

traceroute google.com  # or tracert on Windows
Observe answer sections, TTLs, and authoritative servers. Record outputs to show what you queried and what responses you received.
Host Discovery (Lab)
nmap -sn 192.168.1.0/24
nmap -oN scan-results.txt -p 1-1024 target-in-lab
Use -oN or -oA to capture raw outputs. Keep notes explaining why you ran each command and the time you ran it.
