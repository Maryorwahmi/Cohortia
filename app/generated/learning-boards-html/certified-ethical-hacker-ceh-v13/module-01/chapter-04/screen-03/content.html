Practice & Safety

Tools are powerful; scope and documentation make them ethical.

Practice safely
Use example.com or lab networks for live commands. Simulate nmap -sn in your virtual lab before real tests.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Document every step
Capture timestamps, exact commands and outputs, and client approvals to prove compliance with RoE.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Choose low-impact scans
Begin with host discovery and non-intrusive scans. Escalate only with permission and monitoring for impact.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Respect time and scope
Avoid peak-hours scanning unless authorized, to reduce the risk of disrupting production systems.
