How traceroute works

Traceroute increments TTL to discover each hop and measures round-trip time. Use it to infer topology, not to assert ownership of intermediate devices.

• Use traceroute or tracert depending on platform. • Expect asterisks where routers don't reply. • Record hop IPs and latencies for topology mapping.
Command Example
traceroute google.com
# On Windows: tracert google.com
Interpretation: groups of hops can indicate ISP boundaries; sudden latency jumps may signal choke points or long-haul links.
