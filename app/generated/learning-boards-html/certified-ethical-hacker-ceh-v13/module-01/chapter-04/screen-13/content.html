Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Common Mistakes and Safety Considerations

Safety checklist

• Confirm scope and written permission before any active probe. • Start with host discovery and narrow port ranges. • Avoid peak business hours unless allowed. • Keep an audit trail of commands and outputs with timestamps.

If something goes wrong

Stop scanning immediately, record what you did, and contact your client. Continued attempts risk damage, detection, and legal consequences.
Common error: Assuming stealth absolves you of reporting. Always document and report any anomalies.
Key Takeaway

Discipline, documentation, and communication are the central safety tools for ethical active reconnaissance.
