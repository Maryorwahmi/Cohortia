Definition

What DNS tells you

A, MX, and NS records point to addresses and services. Use them to find potential attack surface but verify later.

• A records reveal IPv4 endpoints you can test for open ports.
• MX records expose mail infrastructure and priorities used for routing.
• NS records can lead you to other authoritative servers or secondary zones.
Key Takeaway

Treat DNS enumeration as a prioritized list of leads to guide further discovery; always log command outputs for auditability.

Command Examples
Try these in a safe lab or against public examples like example.com.
dig example.com A

dig example.com MX

dig example.com NS

dig axfr @ns1.example.com example.com
If axfr succeeds, you may receive a full zone dump. That indicates a misconfigured name server and yields many hostnames quickly. If it fails, proceed to discover hosts via other records and observations.
