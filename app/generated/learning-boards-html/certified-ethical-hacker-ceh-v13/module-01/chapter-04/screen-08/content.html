Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Port Scanning: TCP vs UDP
Comparison

TCP

Connection-oriented; scans like SYN are informative and faster to interpret. Common TCP ports: 22, 80, 443, 21.
• SYN scan shows SYN-ACK for open ports. • Often logged by IDS but can be tuned for stealth. • Good first step for service identification.
Comparison

UDP

Connectionless; many services don’t respond to unexpected traffic, making silence ambiguous. Common UDP ports: 53 (DNS), 161 (SNMP).
• Requires careful payloads and timing. • Often slow and produces false negatives. • Use only if needed and authorized.
When to prefer TCP
Initial service discovery and version detection.
When to consider UDP
Targeted probes for DNS, SNMP, or other UDP-only services with explicit scope approval.
Misconception
No response from UDP does not equal closed; it may be filtered or simply non-responsive to that payload.
