Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Computer Virus
Attaches to programs or documents and needs user action to execute and spread.
Worm
Self-replicating malware that spreads across networks by exploiting vulnerabilities without human action.
Boot Sector Virus
Infects the Master Boot Record or boot sector and may load before the OS.
Summary

Classify by propagation method, then choose controls: endpoint for viruses, network patching and segmentation for worms.
