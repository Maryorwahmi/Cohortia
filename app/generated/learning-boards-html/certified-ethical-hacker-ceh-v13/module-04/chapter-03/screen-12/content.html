Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Detection & Prevention
Architecture

Layered controls for both threats

Endpoint
Antivirus, EDR, allowlisting, integrity monitoring.
Network
Segmentation, firewalls, IDS/IPS, rate-limiting of scanning.
Management
Patch management, centralized logging, IR playbooks.
Testing & validation
Run controlled scans and simulated infections in isolated labs to verify detection and containment.
Key Takeaway

Multiple defensive layers reduce single-point failures and slow both viruses and worms, buying time for containment.
