Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Ethics & Mistakes
Common mistakes
  • Labeling any spread as a virus without checking for autonomous scanning.
  • Relying only on signatures against polymorphic or stealth threats.
  • Running intrusive tests without permission or isolation.
Ethical hacker practice
Simulate attacks in isolated labs, document remediation, and coordinate with operations for safe testing windows.
Checklist
Get written authorization before scanning.
Isolate the test environment from production.
Provide clear, prioritized remediation guidance to ops teams.
