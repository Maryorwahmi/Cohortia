Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Worms: Autonomous Propagation
Characteristics

Exploit, scan, deliver

Worms autonomously exploit vulnerabilities, scan for new victims, and install payloads that can range from nuisance to catastrophic.

Network exploitation
Target specific protocols or services like SMB or RPC.
Scanning
Rapid probing for other vulnerable hosts increases spread.
Payload
May install backdoors, encrypt files, or perform DDoS actions.
Key Takeaway

Patch public-facing services and segment networks to reduce worm attack surface and stop lateral scanning.
