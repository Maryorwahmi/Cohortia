Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Worm Propagation Steps
Process

Exploit → Scan → Propagate → Payload

1. Exploit
Leverage unpatched services or weak creds.
2. Scan
Active network probing to find other targets.
3. Propagate
Copy or trigger remote execution on new hosts.
4. Payload
Install backdoors, encrypt, or disrupt services.
Monitor
Watch for scanning patterns and unusual service requests.
Harden
Patch services and restrict network exposure.
Contain
Isolate infected hosts quickly to stop lateral spread.
