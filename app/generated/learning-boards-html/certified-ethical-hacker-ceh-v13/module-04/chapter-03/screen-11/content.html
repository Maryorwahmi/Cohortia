Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Historical Worms
Notable outbreaks
1988
Morris Worm — exploited sendmail and weak passwords; accidental widespread disruption.
2001
Code Red — IIS buffer overflow, site defacement and DDoS actions.
2003
Slammer — rapid SQL worm; infected many hosts in minutes causing outages.
2010
Stuxnet — targeted ICS and SCADA, physical sabotage example.
2017
WannaCry — SMB exploit combined worm and ransomware with major real-world impact.
Lesson

When a worm pairs a high-impact exploit with automated scanning, the resulting speed requires rapid containment and broad patching to prevent widespread damage.
