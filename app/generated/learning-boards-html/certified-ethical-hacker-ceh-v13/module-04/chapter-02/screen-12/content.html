Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Common Pitfalls
Frequent mistakes
  • Production analysis: Running samples on live systems risks spread.
  • No snapshot: You lose the ability to revert and reproduce results.
  • Overreliance on static: Strings and imports are leads, not proof.
  • Ignoring anti-analysis: Some malware hides behavior in VMs or under debuggers.
Mitigations
Always isolate and snapshot.
Correlate Procmon and Wireshark timestamps.
Document raw outputs and your interpretation.
