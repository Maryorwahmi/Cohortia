Learning Objectives

Apply and demonstrate skills that translate directly to assessments and incident response.

01
Identify the ethical hacker role

Explain safe analysis practices and how ethical hackers inform defenses without causing harm or spreading malware.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Classify incidents from reports

Given incident descriptions, determine the most likely malware type and justify your reasoning for triage decisions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Map controls to attacker goals

Link attacker objectives like data theft or extortion to appropriate mitigations and incident plans.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Recognize misconceptions

Identify and correct common misunderstandings, such as assuming payment resolves ransomware or confusing viruses with other malware families.
