Infection Mechanisms and Common Vectors

Common vectors
Phishing and malicious attachments
Exploited vulnerabilities in services
Bundled or malicious installers
Drive-by downloads and browser exploits
Practical guidance
Map telemetry to determine the earliest observable event. If the earliest event is an unusual outbound connection, suspect remote command channels; if the earliest is a user action, pursue phishing as the vector.
Example investigation steps
  1. Collect logs and timestamps to identify first-moment activity.
  2. Compare user reports and telemetry to narrow initial vector.
  3. Isolate affected systems and preserve evidence for forensics.
Key Takeaway

Finding how malware first gained access is the most important step in cleaning and preventing further compromise.
