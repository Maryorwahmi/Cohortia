Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Spyware & Adware
Spyware

Covertly monitors and records user activity to collect credentials, keystrokes, and sensitive data for theft or espionage.

  • Includes keyloggers and screen capture tools.
  • Often bundled or installed via drive-by downloads.
  • Requires careful forensics to determine exfiltrated data.
Key Takeaway

Spyware threatens confidentiality; monitor for unexpected input capture and suspicious outbound connections.

Adware

Displays or downloads advertising; may track browsing behavior and redirect searches, sometimes bundled with free apps.

  • Can degrade performance and user experience.
  • May collect identifiers that erode privacy.
  • Some adware is legitimate; focus on consent and transparency.
Scenario
A user installs a free tool and then sees their homepage changed, excessive pop-ups, and search redirects to unknown engines.
