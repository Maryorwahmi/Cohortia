Key Concepts

Operational reminders to use during incident response and assessments.

Ransomware Defense
Tested offline backups and recovery plans remove leverage from extortion attempts.
Rootkit Response
Use integrity checks and offline analysis; removal may require full system rebuilds.
Botnet Disruption
Detect correlated host behavior and cut command channels to reduce scale and resilience.
Final summary: match your remediation to the attacker goal — backups for extortion, integrity for stealth, and telemetry correlation for distributed threats.
