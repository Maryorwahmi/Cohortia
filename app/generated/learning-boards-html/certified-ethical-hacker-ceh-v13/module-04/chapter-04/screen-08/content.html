Common spyware functions
Keylogger
Records keystrokes to capture credentials and private data.
Screen scraper / screenshot capture
Takes periodic screenshots to record visual information.
Browser hijacker
Modifies browser settings to redirect traffic or capture sessions.
Audio/Video surveillance
Activates webcams and microphones to record conversations or surroundings.
Key Takeaway

Spyware is stealthy; combine process forensics, registry checks, and historical network data to detect it.

Privacy and impact
Collection of credentials, personal documents, and recordings can enable identity theft, fraud, or extortion. The long-term presence of spyware increases the scope of compromise.
Investigation tip
Preserve volatile memory and review historical logs to uncover implants that erased traces from active directories.
