Trojans, Ransomware, and Spyware
How deception, encryption, and surveillance differ and why each matters to an ethical hacker.
Trojans: deception to gain execution and backdoors
Ransomware: encrypts data, demands payment, may exfiltrate first
Spyware: covert data collection like keyloggers or screen capture
In this lesson

You will compare the three malware classes, meet real subtype examples, and learn concrete mitigations ethical hackers use to test organizational defenses.

Quick takeaway

Each malware family has a distinct goal: access, extortion, or surveillance. Defenses must align to those goals.
