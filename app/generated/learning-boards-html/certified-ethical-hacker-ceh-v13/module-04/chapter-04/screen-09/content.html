Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Mitigations
Defense layers
User education
Phishing awareness and download verification reduce initial compromise.
Endpoint protection
EDR and AV detect suspicious post-execution activity.
Patch management
Keep remote services and software updated to close exploit windows.
Backups & recovery
Immutable, offline backups allow recovery without paying ransom.
Controls for specific threats
Whitelisting: blocks unknown executables, effective against Trojans and ransomware.
Network segmentation: limits lateral movement after initial compromise.
Least privilege: reduces what malware can access once running.
Ethical hackers validate these controls with realistic, non-destructive simulations and report gaps to be remediated.
