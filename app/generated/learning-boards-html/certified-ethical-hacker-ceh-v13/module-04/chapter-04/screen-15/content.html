Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Ransomware encryption
Strong symmetric/asymmetric encryption makes offline recovery dependent on backups.
Double extortion
Attackers may both encrypt and exfiltrate data, adding pressure through publication threats.
Spyware impact
Stealthy collection of credentials and personal data that enables identity theft and fraud.
Closing takeaway

Prioritize backups, detection tuning, and forensic readiness. Validate one high-value control in your environment this week.
