Demonstrable skills
These skills are validated through lab scenarios and artifact analysis.
Analyze a ransom note and extract payment, timeline, and unique IDs.
Simulate a RAT in an isolated VM and capture network indicators.
Detect a keylogger using process and registry forensics.
Assessment tip

When you report findings, include the threat type, detection evidence, recommended containment steps, and a short restoration plan tied to backups or system rebuilds.
