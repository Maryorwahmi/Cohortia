Prevent
  • Phishing simulations and user training

Detect
  • EDR and behavioral rules
  • Network monitoring for beaconing and exfiltration
  • Regular startup and registry audits
Recover

  • Incident playbooks and forensic procedures
  • Rehearsed restoration drills
