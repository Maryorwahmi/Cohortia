Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Ethical Hacker Role
Test
Simulate RAT connections, downloader fetches, and non-destructive encryption of test files in lab environments to validate controls.
Advise
Prioritize containment, evidence preservation, and recovery steps based on attack impact and evidence collected.
Validate
Run recovery rehearsals and review whitelisting rules and segmentation to ensure the organization can recover without paying ransom.
Ethical constraints: always avoid running destructive payloads on production and document your simulation scope clearly.
