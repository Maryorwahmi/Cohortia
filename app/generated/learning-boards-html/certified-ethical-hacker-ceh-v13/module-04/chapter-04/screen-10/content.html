Task
Use the simulated ransom note and follow these steps to produce an incident starter report.
1. Identify
List threat, payment, timeline, and identifiers.
2. Triage
Isolate systems, capture images, and collect logs for forensics.
3. Advise
Immediate advice: do not pay, preserve evidence, notify response and law enforcement.
