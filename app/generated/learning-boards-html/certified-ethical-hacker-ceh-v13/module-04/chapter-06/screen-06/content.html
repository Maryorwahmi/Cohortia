Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Firewalls, IDS & IPS
Core Definitions
  • Firewall: Rule-based traffic control; NGFW adds application context.
  • Host-based firewall: Machine-specific network restrictions.
  • IDS: Monitoring and alerting for suspicious activity.
  • IPS: Detection plus active prevention actions to block attacks.
Example
An exploit tries to scan and exploit hosts. IDS alerts the team; IPS blocks the connection and prevents further spread.
Operational Cards
Audit Rule Checklist
Document rules, review open ports, and remove legacy exceptions to reduce attack surface.
False Positive Management
Tune IDS/IPS to avoid alert fatigue; ensure actionable alerts route to responders.
Key Takeaway

Use layered network controls plus regular audits. IPS can stop propagation, but tuning and testing are essential to avoid gaps.
