Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Key Concepts
Defense-in-Depth
Multiple overlapping controls reduce single points of failure.
Endpoint Protection
Combine AV and EDR for known and unknown threat detection and response.
Awareness & Policy
Training, call-back policies, MFA, and least privilege reduce social engineering success.
Takeaway

Layer technical controls with people and process, then verify with tests and rehearsals. Prioritize visibility, backups, and training first for immediate resilience.
