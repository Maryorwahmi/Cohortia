Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Email & Web Gateways
Email Gateway
Scans for malicious attachments, phishing indicators, and suspicious senders. Quarantines or blocks dangerous messages before delivery.
Example
A malicious attachment is identified and quarantined, preventing user exposure.
Web Gateway
Blocks access to known malicious sites and prevents drive-by downloads. Can enforce browsing policies and filter content by risk category.
Key Takeaway

Gateways lower the volume of attacks reaching users. Combine them with training, MFA, and incident workflows for maximum effect.
