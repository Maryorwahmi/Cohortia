Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Defense-in-Depth
Definition & Analogy
Think of defense-in-depth as concentric rings of protection. Each ring is a different control type so an attacker must bypass multiple different mechanisms to reach critical assets.
Example Scenario
A phishing email bypasses a filter, an employee clicks, MFA stops credential abuse, segmentation prevents lateral spread, and immutable backups enable recovery.
Common Mistake
Relying on many tools of the same type, such as multiple signature-only AVs, rather than diverse controls that fail differently.
Key Takeaway

Design layers with different capabilities: prevention, detection, response, and recovery. Verify them with tests and drills.

Layered Cards
1
Perimeter
Firewalls, NGFW, email and web gateways to block known attacks.
2
Host
Endpoint protection, EDR, whitelisting and patching.
3
Application
Input validation, application filters, and secure configuration.
4
People & Process
Training, policies, incident response, and verification exercises.
