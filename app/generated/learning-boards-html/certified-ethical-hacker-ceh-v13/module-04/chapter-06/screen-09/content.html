Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Whitelisting vs Blacklisting
Whitelisting
Only approved applications may run. Strong protection against unknown malware when maintained correctly.
When to prefer
Critical servers or locked-down workstations where administrative overhead is acceptable.
Blacklisting
Blocks known malicious applications. Easier operationally but misses unknown threats unless updated continuously.
Operational Trade-off
Lower administrative burden but higher residual risk from unknown executables.
Key Takeaway

Use whitelisting for high-value endpoints and blacklisting as a complementary control elsewhere. Maintain clear change control for application updates.
