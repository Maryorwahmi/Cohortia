Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Policies & Controls
Key Controls
Multi-Factor Authentication
Adds an extra verification factor so stolen credentials are less useful.
Least Privilege
Limit accounts to minimum permissions to reduce attacker impact if accounts are compromised.
Network Segmentation
Isolate critical systems so attackers cannot easily move laterally after a breach.
Policy Examples
  • Information handling: define data classification and sharing rules.
  • Physical security: badge usage and visitor controls to prevent tailgating.
  • Incident reporting: how to report suspicious emails or access attempts.
Key Takeaway

Policy plus enforced technical controls and audits form the organizational backbone that limits attacker effectiveness and helps detection.
