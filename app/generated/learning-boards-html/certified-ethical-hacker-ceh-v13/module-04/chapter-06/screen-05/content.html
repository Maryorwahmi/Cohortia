Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Endpoint Protection
Concepts
Antivirus: signature and heuristic scanning. EDR: telemetry, behavior analytics, investigation and containment capabilities.
Concrete Example
A novel ransomware variant encrypts files rapidly. AV without a signature may miss it. EDR flags abnormal file encryption activity and triggers containment.
Common Mistake
Deploying agents without an operational process to investigate alerts leads to missed incidents despite telemetry.
Pro-Tip
Keep detection rules and telemetry retention tuned so EDR can reconstruct timeline for investigations.
Comparison Panel
Antivirus
Fast signature matching, good for known malware. Needs frequent definition updates.
EDR
Behavioral detection, investigation data and response actions for unknown threats.
Key Takeaway

Use both: antivirus for rapid blocking of known threats, EDR for visibility and response to advanced behaviors.
