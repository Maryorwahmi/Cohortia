People & Process: Training, policies, MFA.
Why layers matter
If a phishing attack bypasses email filters, trained employees, MFA, segmented networks, and immutable backups work together to limit damage and recover systems without paying ransom.
Key Takeaway

Combine technical controls, policy, and training. Test them regularly so layers remain effective against evolving threats.
