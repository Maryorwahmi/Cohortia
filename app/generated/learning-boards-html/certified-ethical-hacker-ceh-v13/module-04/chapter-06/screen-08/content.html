Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Data Backup & Recovery
3-2-1 Rule
Maintain three copies of data, store them on two different types of media, and keep one copy offsite or air-gapped to prevent simultaneous compromise.
Immutable Backups
Ensure backups cannot be modified or deleted by attackers to preserve a reliable restore point.
Example
When ransomware encrypts primary data, an isolated backup allowed restore without paying ransom.
Restore Testing
Regularly test backup restores to validate integrity and recovery procedures within defined time objectives.
Operational Considerations
Keep backups segregated from production networks and limit access. Document recovery steps and prioritize critical systems for sequential restoration.
Key Takeaway

Backups are only useful if isolated, immutable, and tested. Make recovery plans explicit and practiced so restoration is reliable under pressure.
