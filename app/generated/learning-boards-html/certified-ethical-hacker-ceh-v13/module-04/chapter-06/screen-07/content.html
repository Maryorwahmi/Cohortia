Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Patch Management
Patch Process Steps
  1. Inventory assets and map criticality.
  2. Prioritize patches by risk and exposure.
  3. Test patches in staging environments.
  4. Deploy in waves with rollback plans.
  5. Verify deployment and monitor for issues.
Historical Example
WannaCry exploited a patchable vulnerability. Organizations that delayed patching suffered widespread impact.
Operational Controls
Automation with Controls
Automate scan and deployment but include staged test waves and documented rollbacks to avoid outages.
Third-party Software
Include firmware and vendor applications in inventory; they can be an overlooked vector.
Key Takeaway

Patching reduces exploit windows. Prioritize critical updates, automate carefully, and verify with tests and rollbacks.
