Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Security Awareness Training
Training Elements
  • Phishing simulations with immediate feedback.
  • Practical tips: hover links, inspect sender, verify unusual requests by call-back.
  • Physical security: tailgating and shoulder surfing awareness.
  • Reporting mechanisms and no-reprisal policies.
Scenario Card
Send a simulated phishing email and provide tailored retraining to staff who clicked, reinforcing correct verification practices.
Operational Advice
Make reporting simple: a dedicated address or report action. Provide constructive feedback and training resources rather than punitive measures to encourage reporting.
Key Takeaway

Regular, realistic simulations and supportive retraining change behavior and supply early threat intelligence to defenders.
