Map the lever
Identify which psychological principle an attack uses and design a targeted defense.
Match defenses to technique
Use MFA, filters, and whitelisting where technical mitigations apply; use training and procedures for human behaviors.
Test ethically
Obtain permission, run realistic simulations, report sensitively, remediate, and re-test to measure improvement.
Final takeaway

Social engineering blends psychology with method. Reduce risk by mapping attacks to levers, applying layered defenses, and measuring improvement through ethical testing.
