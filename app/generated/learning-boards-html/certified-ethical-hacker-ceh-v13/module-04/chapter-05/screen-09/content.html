Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Tailgating & Shoulder Surfing
Tailgating

Gaining unauthorized physical access by following someone with legitimate access, often by exploiting courtesy or misdirection.

Mitigations
  • Enforce badge checks and visitor escort policies.
  • Use turnstiles, manned reception, or anti-tailgating doors where feasible.
  • Train employees to refuse tailgaters and report incidents.
Shoulder Surfing

Observing sensitive inputs directly by looking over someone's shoulder or using cameras and reflective surfaces to capture data entry.

Practical steps
  • Privacy screens for laptops and mobile devices.
  • Encourage shielded input for PINs and sensitive entry.
  • Design workspaces to reduce observation lines of sight.
Key Takeaway

Physical social engineering is simple to test and often cheap to fix with policies, training, and modest controls.
