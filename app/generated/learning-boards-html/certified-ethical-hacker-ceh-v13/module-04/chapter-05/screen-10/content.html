Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Assessments
Assessment architecture
Authorize & Scope
Written permission, allowed techniques, excluded groups.
Design Scenarios
Realistic, contextual, and single-principle focused for clearer measurement.
Execute Carefully
Record metrics, avoid harm, debrief participants.
Report Sensitively
Focus on systems and training needs, not blame.
Remediate
Targeted training, policy updates, technical hardening.
Re-test
Measure improvement and validate controls.
Concrete metric examples
  • Phishing click-through rate and credential submission rate.
  • Number of successful tailgates in a test window.
  • Rate of disclosure during vishing scenarios.
Key Takeaway

Ethical tests reveal process and policy gaps. Measure, remediate, and re-test to reduce human risk over time.
