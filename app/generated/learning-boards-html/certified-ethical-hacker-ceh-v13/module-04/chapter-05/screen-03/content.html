Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Learning Objectives
Practical outcomes
Phishing analysis
Identify sender anomalies, urgent language, generic salutations, and misdirected links through a checklist approach.
Scenario classification
Map a case to a technique: is this pretexting, quid pro quo, or simple curiosity baiting?
Ethical testing
Define permission, scope, and safe reporting for simulated phishing and physical tests.
Mitigation planning
Translate weaknesses into concrete controls: MFA, filtering, policies, and technical hardening.
Assessment checklist
  1. Obtain written authorization and define scope.
  2. Use realistic but safe simulations for email and phone testing.
  3. Record outcomes, focus on training needs, not punishment.
  4. Recommend layered mitigations and repeat tests to measure progress.
