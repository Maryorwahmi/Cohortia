Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Key Concepts
Social Engineering
Psychological manipulation of people to reveal information or take actions.
Common Techniques
Phishing, pretexting, baiting, quid pro quo, tailgating, and shoulder surfing.
Defenses
Combine awareness, verification policies, MFA, filtering, and physical controls.
Summary

Map attack techniques to psychological principles, test ethically, and deploy layered mitigations to reduce human-driven risk.
