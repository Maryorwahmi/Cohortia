Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Preventing Social Engineering
Education & Policy
  • Regular, scenario-based awareness training and simulations.
  • Clear verification procedures for identity and sensitive requests.
  • Policies for removable media, visitor access, and reporting suspicious activity.
Cultural step
Encourage a culture where verification is normal and refusing a request is supported, not punished.
Technical controls
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Blocks access even if credentials are stolen.
Email filtering & gateway security
Reduces phishing volume reaching users.
Application whitelisting & disable autorun
Prevents execution from unknown removable media.
Physical controls
Access cards, CCTV, reception checks to reduce tailgating.
Key Takeaway

No single control is sufficient. Combine training, procedures, and technical defenses to reduce successful social engineering attacks.
