Password Attacks: The Human Element

Exploiting Weak and Reused Credentials

Why Passwords Remain Vulnerable

  • •**Pervasive Problem:** Weak and reused passwords are still common.
  • •**Human Factor:** Users choose memorable, simple, or reused credentials.
  • •**Path of Least Resistance:** Often easier than exploiting complex technical flaws.
  • •**Initial Foothold:** Provides a starting point for further system compromise.
Key Takeaway

Password attacks exploit human tendencies towards weak or reused credentials, making them a consistently effective method for initial system access.
