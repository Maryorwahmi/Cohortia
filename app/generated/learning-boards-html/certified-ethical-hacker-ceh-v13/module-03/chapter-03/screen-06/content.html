Brute-Force Attacks

Systematic Trial-and-Error

How Brute-Force Works

  • •**Method:** Systematically tries every possible character combination.
  • •**Guaranteed Success:** Eventually finds the correct password.
  • •**Drawbacks:** Incredibly time-consuming for complex passwords; easily detected by security measures.
Common Mistake

Failing to implement rate-limiting on login attempts makes brute-force attacks significantly easier and faster for attackers.

Dictionary Attacks

Targeted Wordlist Guessing

How Dictionary Attacks Work

  • •**Method:** Uses a list (dictionary) of common words, phrases, and leaked passwords.
  • •**Efficiency:** More efficient than brute-force for common passwords.
  • •**Customization:** Dictionaries can be tailored with target-specific terms.
Key Takeaway

Dictionary attacks leverage human predictability in password choices, making them a highly effective and efficient method against common or easily guessable passwords.
