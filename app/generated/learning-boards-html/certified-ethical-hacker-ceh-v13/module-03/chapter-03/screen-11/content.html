Social Engineering

Manipulating Individuals for Access

Why It's Effective

  • •**Human Element:** Exploits trust, curiosity, or fear.
  • •**Bypasses Technical Defenses:** Targets people, not systems.
  • •**Path of Least Resistance:** Often easier than complex technical exploits.
Key Takeaway

Social engineering leverages human psychology to gain access, making security awareness training a crucial defense against these non-technical attacks.

Common Social Engineering Tactics

Phishing Emails

Pretexting (Role-Playing)

Baiting (Temptation)

Scenario: Phishing for Credentials

An attacker sends an email disguised as an urgent security alert from the company's IT department. The email contains a link to a fake login page, prompting employees to enter their credentials, which are then harvested by the attacker.
