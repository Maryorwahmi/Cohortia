Practical Skills & Safety
How to run targeted attacks in a lab and interpret results without causing harm.
Prepare Wordlist
Create a concise list with likely passwords instead of huge public lists for lab work.
Throttle & Scope
Throttle attempts, set time windows, and target lab IPs to avoid lockouts and alerts.
Analyze Output
Confirm success lines in tool output and validate with an actual login before further actions.
Key Takeaway

Responsible testing balances realism with restraint. Plan, throttle, and verify before escalating activity.

Common Pitfall
Blindly replaying huge public lists will generate noise and can lock accounts; use targeted lists instead.
