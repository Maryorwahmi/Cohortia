Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Shell & Initial Checks
What a shell gives you
A command interface to inspect the environment, processes, permissions, and users for escalation clues.
  • Verify identity: whoami and id.
  • Check working directory: pwd and ls -la.
  • List processes and services for high-privilege binaries.
Safety and documentation
Do not run destructive commands. Collect outputs, timestamps, and paths to support remediation steps and later escalation testing.
Concrete commands to run
whoami
id
pwd
ls -la
ps aux | head -n 10
Key Takeaway

Treat the shell as a reconnaissance instrument: collect evidence carefully and plan escalation steps from observed data.
