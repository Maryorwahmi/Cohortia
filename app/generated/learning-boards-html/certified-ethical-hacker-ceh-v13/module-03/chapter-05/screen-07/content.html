Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Dictionary Attacks
Why wordlists work
Humans choose predictable passwords; curated lists turn human bias into attacker efficiency.
Sources for lists
Public leaks, reconnaissance, product names, and internal naming conventions supply high-value entries.
Mutations
Append digits, swap letters for symbols, and vary capitalization to mimic user habits.
Concrete example
echo "password" > passwords.txt
echo "123456" >> passwords.txt
echo "msfadmin" >> passwords.txt
echo "toor" >> passwords.txt
echo "admin" >> passwords.txt
A short, targeted file like the one above often succeeds quickly in controlled lab VMs.
Key Takeaway

Targeted, contextual lists with smart mutations outperform blind large lists in most lab and engagement scenarios.
