System Hacking
Gaining initial access, common password techniques, and early post-exploit steps.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson

Learn practical ways attackers establish a foothold and how to replicate safe, authorized tests in a lab environment.

Why it matters

Initial access is the gateway to deeper compromise. Ethical testers must know these techniques to verify and remediate risks.

Safe practice

Always operate in a scoped lab with permission and be aware of account lockouts and detection mechanisms.

Common pitfall

Using huge wordlists without restraint can trigger alerts and lockouts; prefer targeted lists and throttling.
