Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Actionable Reminders
Verify
Always confirm tool output with a real login or command to avoid false positives.
Targeted Lists
Use contextual wordlists and mutations rather than giant public lists for precision and safety.
Throttle
Limit threading and speed to avoid lockouts and noisy detection in real environments.
Prioritize fixes
Default credentials and open services are quick wins to remediate and reduce attack surface.
Document
Record commands, outputs, and timestamps so defenders can reproduce and remediate reliably.
Final reflection

When you find a foothold, ask: what proof will I collect, and how will I recommend a remediation that eliminates the root cause?
