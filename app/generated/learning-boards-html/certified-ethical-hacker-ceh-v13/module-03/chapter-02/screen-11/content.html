Warning › Safe scanning
Operational risks
  • IDS/IPS alerts and noise from high-volume scanning.
  • Potential DoS on fragile or legacy systems from aggressive probes.
  • Unexpected behavior from authenticated checks against production services.
Practice

Start with discovery scans, reduce parallelism, schedule off-peak, and have a contact and pause procedure ready.

Pre-scan safety checklist
Authorization
Written permission with clear scope and allowed times.
Configuration
Use non-intrusive defaults, conservative timing, and test on staging first.
Monitoring
Watch target health and have an immediate stop plan if services degrade.
