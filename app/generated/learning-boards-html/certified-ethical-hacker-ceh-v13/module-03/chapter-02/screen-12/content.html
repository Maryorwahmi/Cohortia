Strategy › Scan & prioritize
Structured approach
Define scope, schedule scans, triage by impact, and validate critical issues before remediation.
Scope & authorization
Document in-scope assets and get written permission that includes timing and allowed activities.
Schedule & safety
Prefer off-peak scans, discovery first, and conservative timing settings for production systems.
Prioritization
Combine severity, CVE exploitability, and business impact to rank remediation work.
Practical checklist
  1. Define scope and obtain written authorization.
  2. Run discovery scans and then targeted credentialed scans.
  3. Validate top findings manually and document proof before remediation.
