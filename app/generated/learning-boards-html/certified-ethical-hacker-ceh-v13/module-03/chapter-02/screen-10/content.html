Core Concept › False Positives / Negatives
False Positives
Scanner flags that are incorrect for the target; often due to banner inference or backported fixes.
Validation steps
  • Confirm running version through authenticated checks or config files.
  • Review vendor patch notes for backported fixes.
  • Document why a finding is a false positive for the report.
False Negatives
Missed real issues, often due to custom apps, zero-days, or scanner coverage gaps.
Mitigation
  • Complement automated scans with manual penetration testing and logic checks.
  • Use specialized web tools for application logic vulnerabilities.
  • Keep scanner feeds updated to reduce missed known issues.
Documenting decisions

Record why a finding was dismissed or confirmed so remediation priorities remain traceable and auditable.
