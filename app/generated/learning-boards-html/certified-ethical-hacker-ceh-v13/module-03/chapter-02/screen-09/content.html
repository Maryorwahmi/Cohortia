Core Concept › Report interpretation
Reading report elements
  • Severity label: Critical, High, Medium, Low, Info — use as triage cues.
  • CVE reference: lookup exploitability, affected versions, and vendor fixes.
  • Remediation advice: often generic; verify applicability to your config.
Key Takeaway

Treat reports as prioritized hypotheses; validate critical items manually and consider asset impact when scheduling fixes.

Example analysis
Suppose a scan flags a critical CVE in a service on a customer database server. Verify the service version, check for vendor backports, and plan an emergency patch if exploitability is confirmed.
Misconception
Do not treat 'Critical' as automatic production reboot; assessment may reveal compensating controls or mitigations already in place.
Pro tip
Filter reports by severity and CVE, then focus manual validation on the top three critical or high findings that affect production assets.
