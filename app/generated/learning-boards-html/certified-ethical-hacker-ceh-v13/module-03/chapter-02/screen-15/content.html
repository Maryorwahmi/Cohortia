Key Operational Concepts
Three compact cards with quick operational definitions.
NVTs
Pattern files OpenVAS uses to match known vulnerabilities.
Credentialed Scan
Authenticated scan that provides deeper, more accurate results when authorized.
False Positive / Negative
False positive: reported but not exploitable. False negative: real issue missed by the scanner.
Final takeaway

Combine automated scanning with manual validation, safe planning, and prioritized remediation to make scans truly useful in real engagements.
