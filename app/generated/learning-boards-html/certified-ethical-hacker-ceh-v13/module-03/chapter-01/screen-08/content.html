Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › CVE and CVSS: Identifiers and Severity
CVE

Canonical vulnerability identifiers

Each CVE entry names a public vulnerability, giving you a stable reference to research impact and fixes.

Use it to
Link scan findings to public advisories, vendor patches, and proof-of-concept evidence.
CVSS

Scoring severity and urgency

CVSS converts exploitability and impact into a numeric score so teams can triage remediation work.

High score example
Remote exploit, no authentication, high impact on confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
Context matters
Environmental and temporal metrics change priority for a given organization.
Check your understanding
Record CVE and CVSS together: CVE shows what and where; CVSS helps decide how fast to fix it. Always note environmental factors that alter urgency.
