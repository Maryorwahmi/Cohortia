Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Legal and Ethical Considerations
Permission
Obtain explicit written authorization with scope, allowed methods, and contact points.
Data handling
Treat discovered sensitive information with confidentiality and share only with authorized stakeholders.
Emergency controls
Include stop conditions and rollback steps so testing can be halted immediately if instability occurs.
Common mistakes
  • Scanning out-of-scope assets.
  • Failing to validate scanner output and reporting false positives.
  • Not documenting commands, evidence, and timelines.
