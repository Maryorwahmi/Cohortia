whois and dig examples

# Domain registration details
whois example.com

# DNS records: A, MX, NS, TXT
dig example.com A

dig example.com MX

dig example.com NS

dig example.com TXT
What to look for
  • Nameservers and registrar names that suggest hosting providers or misconfigurations.
  • MX records that reveal mail servers and possible administrative hosts.
  • TXT records like SPF that indicate email policies and potential exposure.
Practical checklist
  • Save command output as evidence.
  • Note any subdomains or exposed files for later validation.
  • Mark items that require owner permission before active testing.
Reflection
If whois shows an old registrar contact, or dig reveals unexpected MX hosts, consider whether these indicate misconfiguration worth validating later with consent.
