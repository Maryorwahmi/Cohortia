Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Stages of a Vulnerability Assessment
Stage 1

Planning and scope

Define targets, methods, time windows, and obtain written permission.

Protects testers and owners from legal and operational risk.
Stage 2–3

Info gathering & scanning

Combine passive recon with scoped active scans to collect evidence of weaknesses.

Use tools and manual checks to populate the findings list.
Stage 4–5

Validation & Reporting

Manually validate to remove false positives, then report with CVSS and remediation guidance.

Reports should include prioritized, actionable steps, not just raw scanner logs.
Deliverable
Executive summary and technical appendix.
Evidence
Screenshots, logs, and CVE links for each validated finding.
Prioritization
Use CVSS and business context to order remediation.
