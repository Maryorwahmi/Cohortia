Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Interpreting Reports
Report Anatomy
• Description: what the scanner detected and why it flagged it.
• Evidence: headers, version strings, or probe outputs cited by the scanner.
• Severity/CVSS: numerical and qualitative assessments of impact.
• References: CVE IDs and remediation steps if available.
Example
A finding lists CVE-2023-XXXX, CVSS 9.0, and shows a package version string. Use that CVE to verify affected versions before acting.
Interpretation checklist
  • • Confirm presence of CVE IDs and note exact affected versions.
  • • Verify evidence quoted by the scanner on the actual host.
  • • Consider network segmentation and access controls when weighing impact.
  • • Prioritize by exploitability, not just CVSS alone.
Key Takeaway

Use CVSS and CVE as tools in triage, then validate findings and consider environment-specific exploitability.
