Prioritization Template
1. Sort by CVSS severity (Critical → Low).
2. Check CVE details and exploit availability.
3. Assess asset exposure and criticality.
4. Assign remediation priority with documented rationale.
Example
A critical CVSS finding on a segmented internal host may be lower risk than a high CVSS issue on a public web server with known public exploits.
Common pitfalls
  • • Ignoring business impact and asset value when assigning fixes.
  • • Focusing only on the highest CVSS numbers without checking exploitability.
  • • Failing to record the prioritization rationale for future audits.
Pro tip

Include a remediation ETA and a verification step in each priority item so fixes are measurable and testable.
