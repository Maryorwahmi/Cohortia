Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Assessment Approach & Reporting
Report structure
Demonstration
Reproduce the issue or show read-only evidence such as hash mismatches or file permission problems.
Impact
Explain confidentiality, integrity, or authentication risks in business-relevant terms.
Remediation
Give prioritized, practical fixes, configuration changes, and verification steps.
Key Takeaway

Translate technical findings into concise demonstrations and prioritized remediation so owners can act quickly and verify fixes.
