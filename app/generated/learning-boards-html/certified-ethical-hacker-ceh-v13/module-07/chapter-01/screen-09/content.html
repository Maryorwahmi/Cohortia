Signature architecture
Step 1
Hash the message
Compute a digest using a collision-resistant hash.
Step 2
Sign the digest
Encrypt the digest with the signer's private key to create the signature.
Step 3
Verify and validate
Decrypt the signature with the public key and compare to a new digest; validate the certificate chain for trust.
Testing tip
Recompute digests and verify chain; attempt to locate the private key or demonstrate improper verification accepting weak certificates.
Key Takeaway

Digital signatures guarantee data integrity and authorship when the signing key and certificate trust chain are themselves secure.
