Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Collision Resistance & Weak Hashes
Side-by-side: Strong vs Weak
Strong hash (example)
SHA-256 / SHA-3: collision-resistant and suitable for integrity checks and modern signatures.
Weak hash (example)
MD5 / SHA-1: known collision attacks make them unsafe for security-critical use.
Practical impact
If a signature or integrity check relies on a weak hash, an attacker could produce an alternate payload with the same digest, bypassing validation and enabling forgery.
Key Takeaway

Detect deprecated hashes and migrate critical checks to modern collision-resistant algorithms to preserve integrity guarantees.
