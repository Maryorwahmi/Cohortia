Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Key Concepts
Symmetric keys
Fast bulk encryption; protect the shared secret or confidentiality is lost.
Asymmetric keys
Solves distribution and enables signatures, but relies on private key protection and trusted certificates.
Hashing & salting
Use collision-resistant hashes and per-password salts; hashing does not provide secrecy.
Summary

Good cryptography requires correct primitive choice, secure implementation, and rigorous lifecycle management to be effective in the real world.
