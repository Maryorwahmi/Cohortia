Practical checks
Symmetric
Detect hardcoded keys and insecure exchange
Look for embedded secrets, weak randomization, and unprotected key files. Verify whether an asymmetric mechanism is used for secure exchange.
Asymmetric
Check key sizes and certificate chains
Confirm keys are strong and private keys are access-controlled; ensure CAs are trusted and chains validate correctly.
Hashing
Identify weak hashes and missing salts
Replace MD5 or SHA-1 where used for integrity or password storage and verify unique random salts for each password.
