Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › AES vs DES
Side-by-side comparison
AES
Modern symmetric cipher, strong with appropriate key sizes and modes. Suitable for bulk encryption when keys are protected.
DES
Legacy cipher with small key size; should not be used for security-critical confidentiality.
Assessment focus
Identify deprecated ciphers in configurations and recommend migration. Verify encryption modes and inclusion of integrity checks such as authenticated encryption modes.
Key Takeaway

Algorithm choice and parameters matter; deprecate weak ciphers and ensure proper modes and key sizes for secure encryption.
