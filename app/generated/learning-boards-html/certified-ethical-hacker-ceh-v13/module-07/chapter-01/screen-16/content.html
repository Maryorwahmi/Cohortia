Protect keys
Secure generation, storage, rotation, and limited access to prevent compromise.

Check chain integrity and key sizes to avoid impersonation and fraudulent trust.

Ensure password hashes use unique salts and algorithms like bcrypt, scrypt, or Argon2.
Final takeaway
