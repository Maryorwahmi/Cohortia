Cryptographic Attacks and Tools

Explore how attackers target cryptography through weak passwords, stored hashes, and predictable plaintext. Learn ethical techniques to test, report, and remediate these risks without violating trust.

Brute-force
Systematically try keys or passwords to find a match; effective against short or simple secrets.
Rainbow tables
Precomputed hash-to-password maps that speed up cracking when salts are absent.
Tools
Hashcat and John the Ripper let testers simulate attacks and verify defenses under authorization.
Quick orientation

We prioritize ethical testing: obtain permission, scope your work, use non-destructive methods where possible, and report findings with actionable remediation steps.
