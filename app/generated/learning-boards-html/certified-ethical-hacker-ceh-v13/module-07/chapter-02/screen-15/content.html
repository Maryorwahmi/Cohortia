What to Remember › Quick Reference
Map artifacts
Choose attacks by what you observe, not by a guess of math-level failure.
Document tests
Save format, lists, rules, and hardware for reproducibility and remediation validation.
Prioritize defenses
Salts, slow KDFs, throttling, and key management reduce practical attacker success quickly.
Final takeaway
Ethical cryptographic testing is about revealing operational weaknesses and enabling prioritized fixes. Use the right method, document it, and help your client reduce real-world attack success.
