Anatomy of a detailed finding

1
Finding ID & Name
Unique identifier and short descriptive title.
2
Affected Assets & Description
Exact hosts, URLs, and parameters involved.
3
Proof of Concept (PoC)
Concrete, reproducible evidence: commands, screenshots, or logs.
4
Risk, Impact & Remediation
CVSS score, practical impact, and prioritized, specific fixes.
Concrete Example Card
Example: What a PoC looks like

Include the exact request, payload, and observed response so engineers can reproduce the issue and verify the fix.

Key Takeaway

Each finding should be a self-contained ticket that an engineer can act on and verify independently.
