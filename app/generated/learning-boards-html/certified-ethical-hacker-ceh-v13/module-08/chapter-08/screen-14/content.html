Security & Process

Confidential handling and continuous improvement

Practical checklist to close engagements responsibly.
Secure Delivery
Encrypt reports in transit, use authenticated delivery, and limit distribution to need-to-know recipients.
Artifact Cleanup
Remove temporary files, captured logs, and tool outputs from client and tester systems after acceptance.
Continuous Cycle
Integrate retests and automated scans into change windows so fixes remain effective over time.
Final Takeaway

Handle reports securely, document verification, and treat remediation as an ongoing process to sustain security improvements.
