Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Key Concepts
Penetration Test Report
Primary deliverable documenting findings, risk, and remediation.
Detailed Findings
Include description, affected assets, PoC, CVSS, impact, and remediation.
Remediation & Retest
Assign owners, schedule fixes, and verify with focused retests.
Summary: A report must be clear, evidence-backed, and tied to a remediation lifecycle. Secure delivery and prompt debriefing increase the chance that findings are fixed and verified.
