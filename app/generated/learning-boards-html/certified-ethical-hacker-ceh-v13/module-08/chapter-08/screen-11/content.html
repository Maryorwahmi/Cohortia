Common Mistakes

What goes wrong and how to fix it

Simple corrections raise the report's value dramatically.
Bad vs Good
Bad
  • Long, jargon-heavy text
  • Vague fixes
  • No prioritization
Good
  • Concise executive summary
  • Actionable checklists
  • Clear priorities and owners
Evidence standards
Include reproducible PoC items: the exact request, minimal screenshot, and logs/command output. This prevents disputes over exploitability and speeds fixes.
Prioritization
Use CVSS as a baseline, then apply business context to prioritize. Mark items as Critical / High / Medium / Low with justifications.
Timeliness

Deliver reports promptly and schedule the debrief while the findings are still fresh to preserve momentum.

Key Takeaway

Clarity, evidence, prioritized recommendations, and timely delivery make a report useful and actionable.
