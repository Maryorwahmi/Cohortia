Definition

Purpose of the Executive Summary

Provide leadership with a concise understanding of risk, impact, and recommended priorities without technical detail.

Scope
Dates, systems tested, and exclusions
Top Risks
High-level impacts and business consequences
Recommended Roadmap
  • Immediate mitigations for critical issues
  • Short-term tactical fixes
  • Longer-term strategic controls
Misconception: Avoid including PoC logs or raw exploits in this section. Those belong to the detailed findings and appendices.
Example Executive Summary snippet
Summary

Testing of the customer portal between 2023-09-01 and 2023-09-05 found three critical issues that could allow unauthorized data access. Recommended immediate actions include patching the authentication service and reviewing database access privileges.

Key Takeaway

Write the executive summary to enable a business decision: accept, remediate, or budget for mitigation.
