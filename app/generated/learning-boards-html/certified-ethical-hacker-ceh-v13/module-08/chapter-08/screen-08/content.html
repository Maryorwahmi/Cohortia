Post-Engagement

Debriefing & client alignment

A structured walkthrough to confirm findings, assign fixes, and agree the next steps.
Agenda
  • Confirm scope and critical findings
  • Demonstrate PoC evidence and impact
  • Agree owners, timelines, and retest dates
Communication tips
  • Frame technical risk in business terms, not jargon.
  • Separate immediate critical actions from longer-term projects.
  • Invite questions and record decisions in writing.
Debrief flow
1
Overview & business impact
Explain top risks in plain language and quantify impact when possible — lost revenue, downtime, exposure of customer data.
2
Technical evidence & PoC review
Show concise proof that the issue is exploitable and what an attacker could achieve.
3
Actions, owners, retest
Agree who fixes what, by when, and when you'll verify the fix.
Key Takeaway

A short, two-way debrief that assigns owners and schedules verification turns findings into measurable security improvements.
