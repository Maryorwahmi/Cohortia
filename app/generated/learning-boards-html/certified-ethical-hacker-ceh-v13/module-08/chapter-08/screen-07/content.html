Comparison

PoC vs CVSS

Use PoC to prove exploitability and CVSS to quantify severity. Both are needed for prioritization.

Proof of Concept (example excerpt)
Finding ID: PT-WEB-001 - SQL Injection on Login Page
Description:
The login page at https://www.example.com/login.php is vulnerable to SQL Injection.
PoC Steps:
1. Go to https://www.example.com/login.php
2. In the username field, enter: ' OR 1=1 -- -
3. In the password field, enter any password
4. Submit and observe authentication bypass and dashboard access
(Include screenshot of successful bypass and sample SQLMap output)
CVSS Example
Vector: AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H
Base Score: 9.8 — explains network exploitability, low complexity, no privileges, and high impacts on confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
Common Mistake

Treating CVSS alone as priority. Always factor in asset criticality and business impact when ordering remediation.
