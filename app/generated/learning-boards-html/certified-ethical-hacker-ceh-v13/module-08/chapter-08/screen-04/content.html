Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Report Structure
Core Concept

Sections by audience

A clear separation between executive and technical sections prevents confusion and speeds decisions and fixes.

Executive Summary
Scope, top 3 risks, business impact, high-level recommendations.
Scope & Methodology
Dates, agreed targets, and testing approach for transparency.
Detailed Findings
Per finding: PoC, assets, CVSS, impact, remediation.
Key Takeaway

Organize the report by audience: executive decisions and technical actions each need their own focused section.

Visual: Report component map
Exec
Top risks & roadmap

Management uses this to prioritize resources and timelines.

Risk Assessment
Risk matrix & overall posture

Summarizes the organization's exposure based on findings.

Technical Findings
Detailed, reproducible evidence

Includes exact assets, PoC, and remediation steps for engineers.

Example: What an executive needs vs an engineer
Executive
  • Top 3 vulnerabilities
  • Business impact statements
  • High-level remediation priorities
Engineer
  • Affected assets and endpoints
  • PoC steps and output
  • Exact remediation steps
