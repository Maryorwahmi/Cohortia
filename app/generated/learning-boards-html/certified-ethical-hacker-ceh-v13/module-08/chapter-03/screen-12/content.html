Module 08 › Chapter 8.3 › Detection vs Prevention
Prevention
Patching, MFA, least privilege, network segmentation, and admin control hardening reduce initial access and escalation opportunities.
Example

Network segmentation prevents an adversary with one host's credentials from easily reaching critical servers.

Common mistake

Relying solely on perimeter blocks while lacking internal logging and EDR leaves blind spots.

Detection & Response
Behavioral analysis, EDR, continuous logging, and proactive hunting reveal stealthy campaigns missed by signature tools.
Example

EDR alerts for unusual process behavior combined with network telemetry can expose lateral movement even when malware signatures are absent.

Key Takeaway

Combine prevention and detection. Instrument systems deeply so you can find adversaries who bypass perimeter controls.
