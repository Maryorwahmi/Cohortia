Advanced Persistent Threats

APTs are prolonged, targeted intrusions that prioritize stealth and persistence. Learn the lifecycle, tools, and defender strategies you can apply in enterprise environments.

In this lesson
Map the APT lifecycle, common tools, and practical detection steps you can apply in threat hunting.
Hands-on
Simulate lateral movement and persistence in an isolated lab, following strict authorization and safety guidelines.
Outcome
Recognize patterns of stealthy activity and recommend layered defenses that reduce dwell time.
Visual overview
APT lifecycle cards with TTP highlights
Reconnaissance
OSINT, social media mapping, and targeted profiling to build attack plans.
Initial Compromise
Spear-phishing and zero-day exploits deliver dropper tools or backdoors.
Lateral Movement
Credential theft and remote execution expand reach within the network.
Exfiltration
Small, encrypted chunks over legitimate channels to avoid DLP detection.
Key Takeaway

APTs focus on stealth and long-term access; effective defense combines visibility, behavior analysis, and proactive hunting.
