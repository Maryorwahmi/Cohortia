Reconnaissance

Gathering public context to shape attacks

OSINT and profiling identify people, tech stack, and internal processes used to craft convincing lures and choose attack timing.

Examples of OSINT
  • Employee roles and public email addresses
  • Public repositories revealing software versions
  • Press releases indicating active projects
Defender focus

Harden email gateways, increase phishing awareness, and monitor for suspicious attachments or domains referencing internal terms.

Spear-phishing illustration
A crafted email references a real vendor invoice number and includes a malicious document exploiting a vulnerability. The victim opens it and executes a dropper that installs a backdoor.
Phishing sample — safe visualization
Subject: Updated invoice for Project Orion From: accounts@trusted-vendor.example Body: Please review the attached invoice and confirm receipt. Signed, Jane Doe, Vendor Accounts
Key Takeaway

Small authentic details make lures effective; reduce what is public and train users to verify unexpected requests.
