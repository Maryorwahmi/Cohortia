Module 08 › Chapter 8.3 › Privilege Escalation
Concept

Privilege escalation and credential theft

Once inside, attackers aim to increase privileges to access sensitive systems and avoid being confined to one host.

Memory credential extraction

Tools may read authentication material from memory to obtain passwords or hashes that allow immediate reuse.

Defensive steps
  • Enable LSA protection and similar OS hardening features.
  • Reduce number of privileged local accounts.
  • Monitor suspicious process access to credential stores.

Common mistake

Assuming zero-days are required for escalation; often stolen credentials or misconfigurations are enough.
