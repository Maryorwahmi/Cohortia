Learning Objectives

Master these skills so you can identify, investigate, and recommend controls for APT activity.

01
Define APTs
Formally describe what makes an attack an APT and why persistence changes detection priorities.
Mnemonic: Long-term mission focus
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Map the lifecycle
Identify reconnaissance, compromise, escalation, lateral movement, persistence, C2, and exfiltration phases and their goals.
Mnemonic: Recon → Reach → Remove
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Recognize TTPs
List common tools and techniques like credential dumping, PsExec, DNS tunneling, and stealthy persistence mechanisms.
Mnemonic: Tools, Techniques, Patterns
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Detect & respond
Apply behavioral detection, logging, EDR, and threat hunting to find and remove stealthy adversaries.
Mnemonic: Look for behavior, not signatures
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
