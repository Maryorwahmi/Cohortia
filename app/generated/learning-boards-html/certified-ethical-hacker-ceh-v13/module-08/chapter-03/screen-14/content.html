Module 08 › Chapter 8.3 › Key Concepts
Advanced Persistent Threat
A targeted, stealthy campaign focusing on long-term access for espionage or theft.
Reconnaissance
Public and internal data collected to craft targeted lures and exploit paths.
Initial Compromise
Spear-phishing, zero-days, or social engineering used to gain the first foothold.
Summary

Keep visibility, instrument internal telemetry, and practice safe, authorized simulations to improve detection and reduce dwell time.
