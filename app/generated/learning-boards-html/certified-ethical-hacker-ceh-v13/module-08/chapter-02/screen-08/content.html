Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Deploying a Honeypot
Process

Plan → Isolate → Instrument → Monitor

  1. 1. Plan objectives and choose interaction level based on data needs.
  2. 2. Isolate network paths and enforce outbound controls to prevent escape.
  3. 3. Instrument logging, session capture, and centralized analytics for evidence.
  4. 4. Monitor continuously and prepare a reporting template for suspected interactions.
Scenario card
Example: Research lab honeypot
A university security lab deploys multiple low-interaction services and centralizes logs to analyze automated scanning campaigns without risking live systems.
Pro tip

Only emulate services attackers expect to find; unrealistic combinations can reveal the deception and reduce engagement.
