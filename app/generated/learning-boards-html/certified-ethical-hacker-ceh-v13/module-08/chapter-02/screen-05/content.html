Core Concept

Low-interaction honeypots

They emulate a small set of services, give canned responses, and record automated scans and simple exploits with minimal overhead.

  • • Easy to deploy and maintain, low resource cost.
  • • Lower risk because fewer live services run.
  • • Captures high-volume, automated activity and malware samples.
  • • Limited visibility into complex attacker behavior.
Examples
Dionaea
Emulates multiple services like FTP and SMB to collect malware samples.
Kippo / Cowrie (SSH)
Presents a fake SSH login and records authentication attempts and command sequences without giving a real shell.
Key Takeaway

Low-interaction systems are ideal for broad telemetry and sample gathering, but expect simulated outputs that limit behavioral analysis.
