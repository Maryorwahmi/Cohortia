Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Key Concepts
Honeypot
Decoy system that attracts attackers to collect intel.
Low vs High Interaction
Low-interaction is light and safe; high-interaction yields deeper insight but needs isolation.
Deception Fabric
Networks of decoys, fake credentials, and decoy data to mislead attackers.
One-line takeaway

Use deliberate, contained deception to learn attacker methods, but always corroborate detection signals and follow ethical rules when testing.
