Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Threat Intelligence
From logs to action

Collect, enrich, analyze, and respond

Collect
Centralize timestamps, session records, and network captures.
Enrich
Add geolocation, known signatures, and tool context to raw events.
Analyze
Triage noise versus meaningful campaigns, focus on persistent or novel behaviors.
Respond
Apply findings to detection rules, firewall updates, and patch prioritization.
Concrete example
SSH brute-force campaign
Multiple decoy SSH logins from similar IP ranges reveal a credential stuffing campaign. After enrichment, defenders add detection rules and blocklists based on observed patterns.
Key Takeaway

Honeypot telemetry becomes valuable when enriched and acted upon to harden defenses and update detection logic.
