Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Ethics & Reporting
Principles
When you suspect a honeypot, pause active exploitation. Preserve evidence, document banners, outputs, and timestamps, and report findings to the client with recommended next steps.
Risks
  • • Exposure of tools and infrastructure.
  • • Legal or operational issues from unintended escalation.
  • • Loss of trust if engagement rules are violated.
Reporting checklist
  • • Save nmap output and raw banners.
  • • Save command outputs and timestamps.
  • • Note any timing, TTL, or filesystem anomalies.
  • • Recommend coordination with the client's security team.
