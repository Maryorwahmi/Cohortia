Core Concept

High-interaction honeypots

These provide a complete environment so attackers can execute real tools and workflows, producing rich forensic evidence.

  • • Reveal post-exploitation behaviors like privilege escalation.
  • • Require virtualization, segmentation, and active containment.
  • • Provide richer telemetry at higher operational cost and risk.
Deployment considerations
Plan network segmentation, monitoring, and legal authority. High-interaction honeypots must be contained to prevent escape into production or the Internet.
Common mistake

Deploying a high-interaction honeypot without isolation or monitoring increases the risk of an attacker using it to reach real assets.
