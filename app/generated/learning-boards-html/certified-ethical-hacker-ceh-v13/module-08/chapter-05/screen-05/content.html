Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › Passive Recon
Definition

Passive Reconnaissance

Collect publicly available information that reveals infrastructure and personnel links without contacting the target systems directly.

WHOIS & DNS
Use whois and dig to find registrant details, name servers, MX records, and TXT entries that hint at services.
Archives & Search
Wayback snapshots and Google dorking can reveal forgotten pages or exposed config files.
Device indexes
Services like device search engines list exposed hosts and open ports without scanning them yourself.
Practice example
Simulate a whois lookup and DNS queries for examplecorp.com to collect registrant and MX records. Note any surprising subdomains or legacy hosts discovered via archive snapshots.
Misconception
Passive recon is weak. In truth, it often uncovers high-value leads and points to likely attack paths without raising alarms.
Check your understanding
List three OSINT sources you would query for a new target and explain what each reveals.
Key Takeaway

Start with public sources. They are low risk and often point directly to targets you will later validate if authorized.
