Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › WHOIS & DNS
Commands
whois examplecorp.com

dig examplecorp.com MX

dig examplecorp.com NS

dig examplecorp.com TXT
Run these against lab domains. Record registrant fields, name servers, mail hosts, and any TXT entries that may indicate mail or verification configurations.
Interpreting results
  • Name servers point to DNS authority and subdomain delegation.
  • MX records identify mail infrastructure and relay providers.
  • TXT entries can reveal SPF or other verification strings worth checking for misconfiguration.
Common mistake
Assuming TXT entries are always security-related. Many are benign; document and then research their purpose before escalation.
Key Takeaway

Capture the record type, value, and timestamp for every DNS result — context is crucial for later vulnerability mapping.
