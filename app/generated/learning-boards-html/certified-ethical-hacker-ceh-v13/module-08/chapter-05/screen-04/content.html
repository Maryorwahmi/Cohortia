Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › Scope & ROE
Definition

Scope

A precise list of authorized targets and explicit exclusions. It prevents accidental testing outside agreed boundaries.

Example scope
Include: webapp examplecorp.com and its database host. Exclude: HR systems and payroll servers.
ROE essentials
  • Authorized IP ranges and domains
  • Permitted techniques and forbidden actions
  • Testing hours and emergency contact
Key Takeaway

Get a signed ROE before any scanning. It protects both the tester and the client and clarifies acceptable actions.

Common mistakes

Rushing signature, leaving targets implicit, or not documenting forbidden techniques are frequent errors. These lead to legal risk or operational impact.

Practical template
Targets:
  - examplecorp.com (web)
  - 203.0.113.0/28 (API hosts)
Forbidden:
  - DoS tests
  - Social engineering without explicit approval
Contacts:
  - Client SOC: soc@examplecorp.com
  - Emergency: +1-555-0100
Signature: ____________________
Reflection
Ask whether every asset you might touch is listed. If not, update the scope before testing.
