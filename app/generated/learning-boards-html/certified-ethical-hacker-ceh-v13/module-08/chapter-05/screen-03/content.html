Practice & Safety Objectives

Every command you run has operational impact. These objectives teach you how to avoid that impact while learning.

01
Simulate OSINT in a lab
Practice whois, dig, and archive browsing against example domains to build skill without risk.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Stealthy scanning
Use SYN scans and narrow port ranges in authorized environments to limit noise and disruption.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Document findings
Record discovered hosts, banners, and potential CVEs with context and risk level.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Escalation protocol
Follow ROE contacts and timing when reporting critical or disruptive findings.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
