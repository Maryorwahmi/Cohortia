Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › ExampleCorp Lab
Activity

Passive then active recon on ExampleCorp

Passive checklist
  • whois examplecorp.com
  • dig examplecorp.com MX / NS / TXT
  • Check Wayback snapshots for old pages
Active checklist (lab only)
  • dig examplecorp.com A to find IP
  • nmap -sS -p 1-1000 <IP>
  • nmap -sV -p <open_ports> <IP>
At the end of the activity, produce a short report with three prioritized test targets, the evidence for each, and whether further verification requires explicit client approval. This replicates a professional workflow and trains you to document confidently.
Reflection
Which passive finding most changed your priorities, and what active check would you execute next with client permission?
Key Takeaway
Simulated labs are the safest way to combine passive and active reconnaissance and practice documenting findings and priorities.
