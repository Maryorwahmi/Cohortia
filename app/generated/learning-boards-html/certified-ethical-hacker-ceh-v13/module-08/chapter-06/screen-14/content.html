Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Key Concepts
Scanning
Identify live hosts, ports, and services as candidates for testing.
Enumeration
Extract detailed service, user, and configuration data to form actionable leads.
Verification
Confirm scanner findings manually to reduce false positives before reporting.
Takeaway: Use tools like Nmap, enum4linux, snmpwalk, dig, and gobuster thoughtfully, validate outputs, and prioritize safety and authorization.
