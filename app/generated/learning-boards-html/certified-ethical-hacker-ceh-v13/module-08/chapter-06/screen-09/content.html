Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › SMB Enumeration
Common commands
enum4linux -a <target_IP>
smbclient -L <target_IP> -N
The enum4linux -a option runs an automated suite that pulls user and share information. smbclient lists available shares and can connect to them if accessible.
What to look for
  • Share names that imply backups, admins, or development resources.
  • Accessible files with credentials, scripts, or configuration data.
  • User and group names that suggest weak account patterns.
Verification step
If a share is listed, attempt to connect and list files with smbclient to confirm access levels, documenting any access or denial messages.
Key Takeaway

SMB enumeration often reveals the most direct paths to sensitive data; always validate with manual connection attempts and respect scope.
