Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Flag-based & UDP Scans
FIN / Xmas / Null
These scans send unusual TCP flag combinations. Closed ports should reply with RST, open ports often ignore the packet. Results vary across OS and firewall configurations, so interpret with care.
Misconception
Assuming a successful flag-scan always indicates an exploitable service is incorrect. It only suggests a response characteristic that requires further probing.
UDP Scanning
UDP services may not respond even when open. Look for lack of ICMP port unreachable messages and use protocol-specific probes to confirm services like DNS, SNMP, or others.
Practical note
Expect slower UDP scans, use retransmissions and consider following up with targeted application queries for confirmation.
Key Takeaway

Flag-based scans and UDP probes give extra coverage but are prone to variability; always validate their findings with service-specific checks.
