Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › SYN vs Connect
SYN (-sS)
Sends SYN, listens for SYN/ACK or RST. On SYN/ACK it sends RST to avoid completing the session. Stealthier in some logs, preferred when raw packet privileges exist.
Connect (-sT)
Uses the OS TCP stack to complete the three-way handshake. Works without raw sockets but is more visible and leaves connection logs on the target.
When to choose which
  • Prefer SYN for stealth and speed when permitted.
  • Use Connect if SYN is blocked or privileges are insufficient.
Risks and misconceptions
  • Neither scan is invisible; both can be flagged by IDS/IPS depending on rules and thresholds.
  • SYN is not a proof of exploitation potential — follow up with version detection and enumeration.
Example
If you cannot run raw sockets on your attacker VM, run a Connect scan and accept the added visibility while documenting the choice in your report.
