Module 08 › Chapter 8.7 › Post‑Exploitation Explained
Primary goals
  • Privilege escalation to determine maximum attacker capability.
  • Persistence demonstration to show long‑term access risk.
  • Evidence collection for remediation and risk reporting.
  • Safe restoration and cleanup of any modifications.
Concrete example
Privilege check

Run whoami or getuid, then enumerate SUID files or service configurations. Record paths and explain why they could be abused to gain higher privileges.

Key takeaway

Post‑exploitation is investigative: document what an attacker could do and how defenders can block those steps.
