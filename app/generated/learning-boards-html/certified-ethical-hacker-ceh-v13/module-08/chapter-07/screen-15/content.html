Privilege Escalation

Techniques to move from user to administrator/root, requiring careful verification and reporting.

Maintaining Access

Persistence vectors like accounts and startup modifications show long‑term risk but must be reversible.

Covering Tracks

Demonstrate how attackers might erase traces and then restore systems; coordinate all such activities with stakeholders.

Final takeaway

Show what an attacker could do, demonstrate detection gaps, and always leave systems restored and documented for the client.
