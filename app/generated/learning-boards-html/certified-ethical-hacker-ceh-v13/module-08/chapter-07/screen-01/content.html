Exploitation & Post‑Exploitation

Learn how testers convert vulnerability evidence into access, then expand and persist that access while remaining safe and reversible.

In this lesson

You will validate exploits, use Metasploit to deliver payloads, perform privilege escalation techniques on Windows and Linux, and learn persistence and clean‑up practices.

Why it matters

Proving exploitability demonstrates real business risk and gives guidance for prioritized remediation.

Quick Takeaway

Exploitation validates impact; post‑exploitation shows what an attacker could do next.

Concept map

From exploit to persistence

Exploit

Post‑Exploit
Escalate, persist, exfiltrate, then restore state.
