Aggressive vs Conservative
Aggressive

Full compromise with high‑privilege payloads. Shows maximum impact but increases crash and detection risk.

Conservative

Low‑impact proof‑of‑concepts that confirm exploitability while limiting side effects.

Checklist
  • Confirm scope and rollback options.
  • Verify exploit preconditions and compatibility.
  • Prefer non‑destructive payloads for evidence.
  • Record outputs and avoid repeated noisy runs.
Scenario card
Service crash example

An exploit module designed for a different OS patch level caused an application crash. The tester did not snapshot the VM and restoration took significant time. This could have been avoided by validating versions and testing on a clone first.

Key takeaway

Plan for safety: snapshots, limited attempts, and low‑impact proofs unless full compromise is authorized.
