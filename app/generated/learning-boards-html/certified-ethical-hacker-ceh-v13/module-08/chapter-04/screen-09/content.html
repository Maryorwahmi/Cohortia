Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › NIST SP 800-115
NIST Testing Approach
Plan, execute, and report with documentation that supports governance and audit requirements.
Planning
Define objectives, scope, and resources for repeatable tests.
Execution
Perform tests under controlled conditions with logged evidence.
Post-testing
Analyze, report, and feed findings into risk remediation planning.
When to use NIST
For organizations requiring auditable, governance-aligned testing procedures and traceability.
Key Takeaway
Use NIST for disciplined testing that feeds formal risk management and compliance processes.
