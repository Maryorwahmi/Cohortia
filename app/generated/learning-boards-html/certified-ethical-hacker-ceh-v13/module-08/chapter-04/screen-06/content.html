Methodologies at a Glance
Each methodology offers a structure you can document and follow during an engagement. Below are short descriptions and recommended uses.
OSSTMM
Comprehensive coverage across domains with an emphasis on measurable metrics and operational security.
PTES
Seven-phase standard focused on execution and clear deliverables: pre-engagement to reporting.
NIST SP 800-115
Technical guide used for formal testing programs and alignment with governance and risk management.
OWASP WSTG
Detailed web application test cases and checklists for authentication, input validation, and business logic.
Practical Note
Document the methodology used in the report so stakeholders know what was tested and how findings were validated.
Key Takeaway
Select frameworks to match the target domain and reporting needs for repeatable outcomes.
