Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Authorization & ROE
Key Documents
SOW: engagement scope and deliverables. ROE: permitted techniques, timing, contacts, and prohibited activities.
Scope
Define IP ranges, domains, systems, and explicit out-of-scope targets.
Permitted Techniques
Document whether exploitation, social engineering, or DoS are allowed or forbidden.
Timing & Contacts
Testing windows and emergency contact procedures for critical findings.
Authorization
Signed authorization is required to make tests lawful and defensible.
Practical Guidance
  • Preserve evidence and communications to show authorized activity.
  • If uncertain, pause and confirm authorization rather than guess.
  • Notify emergency contact immediately for critical, impactful findings.
Key Takeaway
ROE and SOW are the contract that protect both client and tester—never test without them.
