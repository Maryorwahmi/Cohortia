ROE Interpretation Practice
Translate clauses into actions: when to stop, who to call, what to document.
  • Identify in-scope IPs and services and verify contact details.
  • Recognize forbidden activities and confirm allowed testing windows.
  • Document critical findings and notify emergency contact immediately.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Selecting Testing Types
Decide between black-box, gray-box, and white-box to meet client goals.
Black-box: no prior knowledge; simulates external attacker.
Gray-box: partial knowledge; balances depth and realism.
White-box: full knowledge; supports thorough internal testing.
