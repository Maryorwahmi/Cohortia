PTES Seven Phases
  1. Pre-engagement Interactions: Define scope, ROE, contacts, timing, and prohibited activities.
  2. Intelligence Gathering: Passive and active information collection to identify targets.
  3. Threat Modeling: Map possible attack paths and prioritize targets.
  4. Vulnerability Analysis: Combine automated scans and manual review.
  5. Exploitation: Attempt to prove vulnerabilities under ROE constraints.
  6. Post-Exploitation: Document impact, evidence, and cleanup.
  7. Reporting: Deliver executive summary, technical details, and remediation steps.
Pre-engagement Example
ROE: No DoS, no social engineering, defined IP ranges, and emergency contact for critical issues.
Common Mistake
Skipping detailed pre-engagement leads to accidental out-of-scope testing and legal exposure.
