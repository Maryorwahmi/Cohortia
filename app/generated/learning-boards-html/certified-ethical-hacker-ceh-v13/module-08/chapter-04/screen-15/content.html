Vulnerability Assessment
Automated discovery and prioritization of potential issues without exploitation.
Exploitation Rules
Only exploit when ROE permits; otherwise document and notify emergency contact.
Testing Types
Black-box, gray-box, white-box — record which you used and why for clarity in reporting.
