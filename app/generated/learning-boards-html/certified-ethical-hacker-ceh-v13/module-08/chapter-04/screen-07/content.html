OSSTMM Overview
Measure security with operational metrics across multiple domains rather than only listing flaws.
Domains Covered
Physical, human, wireless, telecoms, and data networks.
Measurement
Emphasizes quantifiable attack surface and verified events to demonstrate progress.
Example
A wireless audit measures number of verified secure vs insecure channels before and after remediation.
Key Takeaway
OSSTMM is best when you need measurable, repeatable security metrics across domains.
