Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Pen Test vs Vulnerability Assessment
Comparison
Vulnerability Assessment
  • Automated scanning, broad coverage, lists potential issues.
  • Prioritizes by signature-based risk and CVSS scores.
  • Good for continuous monitoring and inventorying assets.
Example
A scanner reports 150 potential issues across web servers and endpoints, useful for patch planning.
Comparison
Penetration Test
  • Manual exploitation attempts, depth-oriented, demonstrates impact.
  • Finds logical, chained, and business logic flaws automated tools miss.
  • Validates controls and incident response with realistic scenarios.
Example
A tester chains a misconfiguration and a business logic bug to access customer records, proving a tangible breach path.
Common Mistake
Expecting a pen test to list every vulnerability found by scanners; they serve different purposes.
Pro Tip
Run assessments frequently and schedule pen tests periodically or before major releases for proof of remediation.
Key Takeaway
Use scans for breadth and pen tests for proof and prioritized action plans.
