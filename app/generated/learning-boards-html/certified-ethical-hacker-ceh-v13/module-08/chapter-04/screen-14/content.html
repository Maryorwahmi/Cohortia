Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Key Concepts
Penetration Testing
Simulated attack that attempts exploitation to show real-world impact.
Authorization & ROE
Written permission and clear rules of engagement protect both parties and define allowed techniques.
Methodologies
OSSTMM, PTES, NIST, and OWASP provide structure—choose per target and reporting needs.
Summary Takeaway

Use pen tests to prove impact, secure written authorization, and follow an agreed methodology so findings are actionable and defensible.
