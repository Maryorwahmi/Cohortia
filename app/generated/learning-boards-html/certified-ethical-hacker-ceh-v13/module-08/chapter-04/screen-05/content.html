Goals of a Penetration Test
Attacker Perspective
Reveal how far an attacker can move and what assets are at risk.
Find Chained Flaws
Uncover logical sequences that lead to exploitation where single findings may not.
Validate Controls
Confirm whether defenses actually block realistic attacks.
Prioritized Remediation
Produce actionable recommendations by impact, not just severity scores.
Concrete Example
A pen test demonstrates a chain: an exposed service + weak auth + missing patch leads to admin access. That finding moves to the top of the remediation list.
Key Takeaway
Pen tests convert noise into prioritized, proven risk that security teams can act on.
