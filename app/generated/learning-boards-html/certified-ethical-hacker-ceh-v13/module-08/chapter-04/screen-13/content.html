Sample ROE (simplified)
Client: Example Corp
Engagement: External Network Penetration Test
In-Scope: 203.0.113.0/28; www.examplecorp.com; mail.examplecorp.com; ns1.examplecorp.com
Out-of-Scope: internal networks not listed; third-party hosted services; social engineering; physical assessment
Permitted: reconnaissance, vulnerability scanning, controlled exploitation, limited post-exploitation (no data modification)
Prohibited: Denial of Service, social engineering
Start: Nov 6, End: Nov 17; Testing hours: Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00
Emergency Contact: John Smith (+1-555-987-6543)
Use this sample to answer the activity questions in the lesson guide.
Activity Questions & Guidance
  1. Which IPs/domains are authorized? Answer with the listed ranges and hostnames.
  2. Which activities are forbidden? Identify DoS and social engineering and avoid them.
  3. Who is the emergency contact and how to notify? Call John Smith immediately for critical findings.
  4. If a third-party cloud service shows a vulnerability, should you exploit it? No — it is out-of-scope; document and notify the client instead.
Key Takeaway
ROE directs immediate actions: document findings, preserve evidence, and contact the emergency person before any disallowed exploitation.
