Definition
Mistakes to avoid
Noisy scans, missing captures, and single-technique reliance are common problems that reduce report value and increase risk.
Do: start quietly and escalate
Begin with stealth scans and small tests, then increase aggressiveness only with authorization and monitoring.
Do: collect reproductions
Capture packets and logs from the network and the target host to validate results.
Pro tips
  • Record timestamps and device configurations so defenders can reproduce your findings.
  • Encode payloads incrementally to trace which encoder introduced detection changes.
  • For anomaly tests, provide baseline traffic statistics and time windows used by the detector.
Key Takeaway

Careful planning, low-noise execution, and clear evidence create the most useful and responsible evasion tests.
