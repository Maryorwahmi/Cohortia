Measure and document detection behavior
Collect Wireshark captures, compare scan outputs, and log which device flagged or blocked traffic. Reproducibility is the test of a useful finding.
Mnemonic
Capture → Compare → Report
Risk
Fragmentation can cause instability; test in a VM lab.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Ethical and legal constraints
Always obtain written authorization and follow rules of engagement. Unauthorized evasion attempts are illegal and unethical.
This objective ensures your findings can be used to improve security without causing harm or legal exposure.
