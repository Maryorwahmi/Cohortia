Core Concept
Definitions
Clear definitions help you target tests to the right control.
Firewall
Filters traffic by IP, port, protocol; may be packet-filtering or stateful and sometimes inspects application layer.
Intrusion Detection System (IDS)
Monitors traffic for suspicious patterns and alerts; can be signature or anomaly based.
Intrusion Prevention System (IPS)
An IDS that can block traffic inline when it detects malicious activity.
Inspection behaviors
  • Packet filters check headers and ports; they may not track connection state.
  • Stateful devices track handshakes and sequence numbers, which affects scans that avoid full handshakes.
  • Signature systems match byte patterns; obfuscation can defeat them.
  • Anomaly systems analyze volume and behavior over time; slow-and-low attacks try to stay below thresholds.
Example
A stateful firewall may ignore a FIN or Null scan response pattern, while a signature IDS looks inside payload bytes and would only match a known exploit string.
Common Mistake
Assuming all devices reassemble fragments or decrypt TLS for inspection; they might not.
