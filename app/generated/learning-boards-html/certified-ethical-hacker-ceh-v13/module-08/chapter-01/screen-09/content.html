Process Flow
Slow-and-Low & Mimicry
Design tests that operate within baseline thresholds or mimic legitimate application traffic to evaluate anomaly sensitivity.
Slow-and-Low example
Send incomplete HTTP headers spaced over minutes to exhaust connection slots without producing a volume spike.
Mimicry example
Place a SQL payload inside a normal-looking web form POST that matches legitimate field names and timing.
Validation checklist
  • Establish a baseline of normal traffic patterns and time windows.
  • Run the slow test and record whether the anomaly detector flags it over the same windows.
  • Compare detector sensitivity and tune your reporting to show time-based evidence of evasion or detection.
Key Takeaway

Anomaly systems require contextual baseline data; proving an evasion needs careful, time-based evidence and comparison to normal behavior.
