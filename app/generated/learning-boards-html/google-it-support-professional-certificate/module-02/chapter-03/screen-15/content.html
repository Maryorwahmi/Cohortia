Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Quick Reference
Classify the issue
Decide whether it is a system, application, driver, or hardware problem before taking steps.
Check drivers
Update or reinstall manufacturer drivers when devices underperform or behave erratically.
Prioritize updates
Apply security patches promptly; plan firmware upgrades and backups before applying them.
Final takeaway
These three checks will speed troubleshooting and reduce unnecessary hardware replacement. Keep them handy when triaging incidents.
