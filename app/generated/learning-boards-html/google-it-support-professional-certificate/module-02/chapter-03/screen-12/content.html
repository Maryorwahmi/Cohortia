Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Updates & Security
Security benefits
  • Patches close known vulnerabilities and reduce risk of compromise.
  • Regular updates improve stability and fix crashes caused by bugs.
  • Compatibility updates prevent conflicts with newer hardware or software.
Firmware considerations
Firmware updates can be critical but risky; always follow manufacturer guidance and ensure power stability during the update.
Policy tip
Enable automatic updates for critical patches, stage major updates, and treat firmware updates as scheduled maintenance events.
Key Takeaway
Updates protect systems. Prioritize security patches, test other updates, and handle firmware carefully to avoid bricking devices.
