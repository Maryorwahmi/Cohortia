Input Devices
Keyboards, mice, touchscreens, microphones, and webcams send data to a computer.
Troubleshooting steps
  • Confirm cable or wireless connection and power to the device.
  • Test with a known-good device to isolate whether the issue is the peripheral or the host port.
  • Check OS device settings and driver status before replacing hardware.
Output Devices
Monitors, printers, and speakers present information. Some devices, like touchscreens, are both input and output.
Common checks
  • Ensure monitor input selection matches the connected cable type.
  • Confirm printer is online and drivers are installed.
  • Check audio output selection and mute states for speakers or headphones.
