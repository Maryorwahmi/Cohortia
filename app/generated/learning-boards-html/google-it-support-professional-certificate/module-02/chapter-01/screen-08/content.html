Motherboard
Main circuit board that connects CPU, memory, storage, and peripherals via sockets and connectors.
Key roles
  • Houses CPU socket, RAM slots and expansion slots.
  • Provides ports for USB, audio, network and video outputs.
  • Includes the chipset that coordinates communication and data flow between components.
Diagnostic example

If several unrelated ports or drives fail, the motherboard or its power delivery may be the underlying cause; test with a minimal hardware configuration to isolate the issue.

Annotation
Slots & sockets
CPU socket, DIMM slots for RAM, SATA / M.2 connectors for storage, and PCIe slots for expansion.
Pro Tip
Record the motherboard model and chipset before attempting upgrades to ensure compatibility with CPUs and RAM generations.
