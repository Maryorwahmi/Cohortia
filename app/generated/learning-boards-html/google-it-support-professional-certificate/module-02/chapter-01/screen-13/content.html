Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
Hardware
The physical components making up a computer system.
CPU & RAM
CPU executes instructions; RAM is the fast, volatile workspace they use.
Storage
Non-volatile HDDs and SSDs retain data; SSDs are faster and more durable.
Summary Takeaway

When troubleshooting, use a component checklist: identify the symptom, map it to CPU, memory, storage, power, motherboard, or I/O, then perform targeted checks and document findings.
