Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What to Remember
Power First
Verify power cables, outlet, and PSU capacity before deeper diagnostics.
RAM Matters
Check memory usage for slow systems—adding RAM often fixes responsiveness issues faster than replacing drives.
Document Everything
Record motherboard model, drive types, and port layout to guide upgrades and troubleshooting.
Final Thought

Use these heuristics to prioritize checks and avoid unnecessary component swaps; efficient triage saves time and reduces risk.
