Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Storage Devices
Hard Disk Drive (HDD)
Magnetic platters that spin; offers large capacity at lower cost but slower access and mechanical fragility.
  • Good for bulk storage and backups.
  • Listen for unusual noises that indicate imminent failure.
  • Lower cost per gigabyte, slower throughput than SSD.
Solid State Drive (SSD)
Flash-based storage with faster read/write speeds, better durability, and lower power use compared to HDDs.
  • Ideal for operating systems and frequently used applications.
  • Improves boot times and general responsiveness.
  • Typically costs more per gigabyte than HDDs.
Use SSD
For OS, apps, and files needing fast access.
Use HDD
For archival backups and large media collections.
Immediate Action
If an HDD makes noise, back up data and schedule replacement; mechanical failure risk is high.
