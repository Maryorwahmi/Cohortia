Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Virtual Component Identification
Activity Steps
  1. Find a clear internal components diagram or safely power down and open a desktop to inspect components.
  2. Identify at least five internal parts and note their primary function in a two-column table.
  3. Inspect front and back panels to list at least five ports and the devices they accept.
  4. Create a short report or table describing each component and one common symptom linked to it.
