Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Ports & Connectors
USB
General-purpose for peripherals and external drives; carries power and data.
HDMI / DisplayPort
Transmit video and audio to monitors and displays.
Ethernet
Wired network connection for stable, low-latency networking.
Audio Jack
Headphones and speakers; check mute and output selection when audio is missing.
Troubleshooting Pattern
Identify port shape, confirm cable type, reseat connections, and swap known-good cables before replacing hardware.
Common Mistake
Confusing onboard video and discrete graphics outputs. If nothing displays, check the port tied to the active graphics adapter.
