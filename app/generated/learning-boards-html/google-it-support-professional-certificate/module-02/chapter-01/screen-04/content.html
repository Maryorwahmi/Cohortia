Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What is Hardware?
Definition
Hardware is the set of physical components of a computer system, from chips to cables and external peripherals.
  • Internal examples: CPU, RAM, storage drives, motherboard, PSU.
  • External examples: keyboard, mouse, monitor, printer, network cable.
  • Why it matters: Hardware faults often show as software symptoms; a physical check can reveal the root cause.
  • Misconception: Small parts like cables or connectors are not minor — they can cause system failures.
Key Takeaway

Always include a physical inspection when troubleshooting; hardware faults are often visible and fixable.

Analogy
Computer as a kitchen
Hardware are the stove, fridge, and utensils. Software are the recipes and instructions the chef follows.
Concrete Example
Boot failure check
Confirm power cable and PSU; ensure RAM modules are seated; check monitor cable and input selection.
Quick Mistake
Assuming software first
Don't reinstall software before ruling out a loose cable or failing drive; physical fixes are often faster.
