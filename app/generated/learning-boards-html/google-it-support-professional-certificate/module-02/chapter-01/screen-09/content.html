Power Supply Unit
Converts AC to DC and provides power rails to internal components; proper wattage is essential for reliability.
Checklist for PSU issues
  • Confirm power cable and outlet are functional.
  • Check for loose connectors to drives and motherboard.
  • Verify adequate wattage for high-performance components.
  • Observe for strange noises, smells, or visible damage—stop and back up data immediately if present.
Safety
Always unplug the system before opening the case. Capacitors in the PSU can hold charge and present a shock risk even when the unit is off.
Example
Random shutdowns during heavy load often indicate the PSU can't supply enough power; test with a known-good PSU if available.
Key Takeaway

Power issues can mimic many other faults. Verify power delivery early in the troubleshooting sequence.
