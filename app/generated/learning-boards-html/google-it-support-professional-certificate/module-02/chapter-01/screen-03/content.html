How Objectives Apply on the Job
Slow performance with many apps
Check RAM usage and whether the system is swapping to the drive. Consider adding memory before replacing drives.
No display from a desktop
Verify video cables and ports. If cables are good, test RAM seating and GPU output to isolate the cause.
Random shutdowns
Confirm PSU wattage and cable seating. An undersized or failing PSU can cause unexplained instability.
Documenting hardware
Create a two-column list of components and primary functions as a baseline for support and inventory tasks.
Quick Mnemonic
Think: Brain (CPU), Counter (RAM), Pantry (Storage), Circuit (Motherboard), Power (PSU), Hands & Eyes (I/O).
