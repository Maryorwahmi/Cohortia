Common Mistakes and Safety Practices
Simple safeguards that prevent major failures
Best practices
Top mistakes
  • Editing protected system files without backups.
  • Rebooting immediately rather than diagnosing runaway processes.
  • Installing unverified drivers without a rollback plan.
Practical safety steps
Create backups, document changes, use staging environments, and prefer scripted steps for repeatability.
Scenario card
You must update a driver on a production workstation: first back up critical files, note existing driver version, create restore points or backups, and schedule downtime. If problems occur, restore to the documented good state.
Reflection

Which safety step would you prioritize for a remote server update: backup, staging, or documentation? Consider the impact on users and recovery speed.
