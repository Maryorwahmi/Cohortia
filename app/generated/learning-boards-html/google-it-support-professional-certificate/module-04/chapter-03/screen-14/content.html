User Account
An identity used for login and assigning privileges to access resources.
Permissions (r, w, x)
Owner, group, others with read, write, execute bits and octal mapping for chmod.
NTFS Inheritance & Effective Rights
Windows ACL inheritance affects child objects; effective permissions combine all sources.
Summary takeaway
Apply least privilege, verify as the user, and regularly audit accounts to keep systems secure and predictable.
