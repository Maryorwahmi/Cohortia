Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Mistakes & Safety
Top mistakes
  • Granting excessive permissions rather than minimal rights.
  • Using weak passwords or skipping policy enforcement.
  • Mismanaging inheritance and applying unintended access.
  • Leaving dormant accounts active.
Safe workflow
  1. Create account with minimal rights.
  2. Add to a narrow group for necessary access.
  3. Verify access as the user and check effective permissions.
  4. Document and schedule audits for removal if not needed.
Key takeaway
Test permissions after changes and prefer removing access over opening broad permissions.
