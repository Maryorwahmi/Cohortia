Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › chown & chgrp
Commands
sudo chown new_owner:new_group /path/to/file sudo chgrp itsupport /opt/shareddata
After changing ownership, verify with ls -l to ensure the correct owner and group are set before adjusting permission bits.
Example & caution
Example: sudo chown root:itsupport /opt/shareddata then sudo chmod 770. Caution: do not chown system files or change owners of OS-managed files without understanding the impact.
Pro tip
Document ownership changes and test access as the target group to confirm intended behavior.
