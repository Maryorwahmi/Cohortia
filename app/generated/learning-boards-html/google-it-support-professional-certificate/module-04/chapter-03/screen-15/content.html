Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › What to Remember
Least Privilege
Grant minimal rights
Avoid Full Control; restrict to necessary operations.
Verify
Test as the user
Use id, ls -l, or Effective Permissions to confirm real access.
Use Groups
Scale access with groups
Assign permissions to groups rather than individuals for easier management.
Final takeaway
Consistent account policies, group-based permissions, and routine audits make user management reliable and secure.
