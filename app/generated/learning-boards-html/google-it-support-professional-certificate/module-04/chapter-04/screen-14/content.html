Key Concepts

Troubleshooting Methodology

A repeatable six-step approach that guides safe diagnosis and repair.

System Logs

Event Viewer, /var/log, and journalctl provide timestamped evidence and event IDs for root-cause analysis.

Disk & Maintenance

Use chkdsk/fsck carefully, avoid defragging SSDs, and keep updates, backups, and restore points current.

Summary

Combine a methodical workflow with logs, safe disk tools, and routine maintenance to reduce downtime and resolve incidents efficiently.
