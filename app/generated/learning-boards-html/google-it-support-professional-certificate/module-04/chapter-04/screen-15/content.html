Quick Reference

Document everything

Logs, commands, timestamps, and outcomes are the most valuable artifacts after a fix.

Back up first

Before disk repairs or major changes, ensure a tested backup exists to avoid data loss.

Test minimally

Make small, reversible changes and verify results before proceeding to broader actions.

Final Thought

These simple, repeatable habits make troubleshooting predictable and let teams learn from each incident.
