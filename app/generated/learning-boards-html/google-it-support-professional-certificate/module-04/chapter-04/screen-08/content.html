journalctl, tail, and /var/log

Use commands to filter recent errors and to follow logs live while reproducing a problem.

Example commands
journalctl -p err -p warning --since "24 hours ago"
tail -f /var/log/syslog
journalctl -u apache2
Useful system checks
uname -a
cat /etc/os-release
free -h
Teaching Visual — Scenario Card
Service failure example
A web service fails after a deploy. Run journalctl -u the-service to get its error output and tail -f the main syslog while reproducing to capture related kernel or auth events.
Misconception
Seeing many warnings does not mean all are relevant. Use timestamps and service names to isolate the entries that match the incident.
Key Takeaway

Use targeted journalctl filters and live-tail commands to collect the exact log lines and times that demonstrate the failure; include those lines in your documentation.
