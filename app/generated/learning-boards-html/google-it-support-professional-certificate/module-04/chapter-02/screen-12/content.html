Key Concepts
Three compact reminders you can carry into the lab and daily support work.
Hierarchy
Know top-level folders and mount points to find files quickly.
Permissions
Inspect attributes and permission bits before changing files or ownership.
Process control
Monitor resource use and prefer graceful termination; force only when necessary.
Final takeaway

A verification-first workflow prevents data loss and reduces system instability: verify, operate, verify.
