Regulation Snapshot
GDPR
Protects personal data for EU residents; drives data handling rules and breach notification.
HIPAA
Sets privacy and security standards for patient health information in the U.S.
PCI DSS
Standards for organizations that process branded payment cards.
Example: Mapping Policies to Regulation
To meet GDPR you might enforce a strong password policy, a data handling policy that specifies encryption and retention, and an access review schedule. Documented steps show auditors you acted systematically, not ad hoc.

Common Misconception
Compliance is not the same as full security. Use compliance as a baseline and add controls for human risks and operational realities.
Key Takeaway

Know which regulations apply and map specific policies and controls to those requirements, then document and review them regularly.
