Trusted Sources
Subscribe selectively: vendor advisories, government alerts, and established security blogs provide reliable, verifiable information you can act on.
  • Vendor advisories for your deployed software and hardware.
  • Threat intelligence feeds for indicators and active campaigns.
  • Community and professional organization updates for context and tactics.
Routine & Responsibilities
Turn information into tasks: assign reviewers, triage items by impact, and log follow-up actions so alerts drive change rather than noise.

Example: Rapid VPN Vulnerability
An advisory reports a critical flaw in a VPN appliance. Your team evaluates exposure, applies a vendor patch, and enables a temporary firewall rule for affected versions. Documenting the timeline gives auditors and leadership a clear record of response.
Key Takeaway

A small, trustworthy set of feeds plus a repeatable review process helps you catch and act on threats before they reach users.

Common Mistake
Relying on too many unverified social sources creates noise. Verify before escalating and maintain a short list of trusted channels.
