Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Security Policies
What is a Security Policy?
A formal set of rules and procedures that describe how information and systems must be protected and how employees are expected to behave.
Password Policy
Defines complexity, rotation, and reuse to reduce credential theft.
Data Handling Policy
Classifies and prescribes storage, transmission, and disposal rules for sensitive data.
Remote Work Policy
Specifies VPN, encryption, and network requirements for off-site work.
Policy to Practice — Guided Steps
Turn each policy into measurable actions you can verify and coach users on.
Example
Password policy: set minimum length to 12, enable complexity, and prevent reuse of last 5 passwords.
Common Mistake
Making policies too complex or not reviewing them regularly, which leads to user workarounds and non-compliance.
Pro Tip
Write a one-sentence user-friendly rationale for each policy to improve buy-in and reduce resistance.
Check Your Understanding
Choose a policy and name two configuration settings that enforce it. If you can't, break the policy into smaller steps until you can.
Key Takeaway

Policies must be actionable and reviewed. Your job is to make them understandable and enforceable.
