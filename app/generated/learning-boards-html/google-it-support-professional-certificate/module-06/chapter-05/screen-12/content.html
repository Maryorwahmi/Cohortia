Security Tip of the Week: Stop and Think Before You Click
Phishing messages often create urgency or request login details. Hover over links to see their real address and check the sender carefully. If anything looks odd, don’t click.
Action: Forward the message to security@example.com or use the mail client's report button.
Why it works
Short, clear behavior + reporting reduces risk and helps IT respond quickly.
