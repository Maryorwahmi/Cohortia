Simulated Phishing — A Comparison
Compare simulation versus reporting to see how they complement one another.
Simulated Phishing
  • Tests real behavior under controlled conditions.
  • Highlights training needs and provides targeted feedback.
  • Should be followed by immediate coaching, not punishment.
Reporting Mechanisms
  • Converts employees into sensors for suspicious activity.
  • Requires simple channels and quick responses.
  • Non-punitive response encourages future reports.
Concrete Example
Send a simulated phishing message and log reports. Those who report get recognition; those who click get immediate coaching and a short remedial module.
Design Checklist
  • Keep simulations realistic but harmless.
  • Ensure reporting is a one-click or one-forward action with clear contacts.
  • Follow up quickly with coaching and system checks.
Common Mistake

Punishing users for clicks reduces future reporting and hides real incidents. Focus on learning and improvement.

Key Takeaway

Simulations find gaps; reporting finds real incidents. Both are needed and must be handled supportively.
