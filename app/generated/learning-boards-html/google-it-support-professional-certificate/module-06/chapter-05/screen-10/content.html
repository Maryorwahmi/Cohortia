Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Physical Security
Core Controls
  • Access Controls — locked rooms, keycards or biometrics restrict entry.
  • Surveillance — cameras provide evidence and deterrence.
  • Environment — UPS, cooling, and power monitoring protect equipment.
  • Asset Management — tag hardware and keep inventory logs.
Scenario & Actions
If a laptop is stolen, first confirm device encryption and remote-wipe capability. Notify security and log the incident. Asset tags and inventory help confirm ownership and expedite recovery or insurance claims.
Key Takeaway

Physical security is a core layer; without it other controls can be bypassed. Treat doors, power, and inventory with the same seriousness as passwords and patches.

Pro Tip
Simple measures like enforcing screen locks, labeling equipment, and occasional physical audits reduce risk significantly and are easy for IT Support to help enforce.
