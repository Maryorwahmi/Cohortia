Designing an Awareness Program
A mix of training, reminders, simulations, and easy reporting turns awareness into defensible behavior.
Step 1 — Baseline Training
Deliver a core module on phishing, passwords, and reporting. Keep it interactive and measure completion.
Step 2 — Micro-Learning
Send short tips or posters that reinforce single behaviors, such as locking your screen or verifying attachments.
Step 3 — Simulated Testing
Run controlled phishing emails to identify gaps. Provide targeted coaching to those who click, not punishment.
Step 4 — Reporting & Feedback
Make reporting simple and respond quickly so users learn that reports lead to improvement, not blame.
Concrete Example
Simulated phishing: send a harmless fake email with a plausible subject. Track who clicks and immediately enroll those users in a short coaching session that explains why the message was suspicious.
Common Mistake
Treating training as a one-time event rather than an ongoing cycle of learning and testing.
Key Takeaway

Reinforcement and feedback are the keys to turning knowledge into safe daily habits.
