Network Security Fundamentals
Protecting data in motion: practical controls and how they reduce risk

This chapter focuses on the controls you'll use daily: firewalls to control traffic, VPNs to secure remote access, wireless hardening to protect Wi‑Fi, segmentation to limit impact, and IDS/IPS to detect and stop threats. Each lesson includes clear configuration examples and common mistakes to avoid.
