Change Defaults
Always change router and device admin credentials and disable WPS to remove trivial attack vectors.
Least Privilege
Deny incoming by default, allow outgoing, and open only necessary ports like SSH.
Isolate & Monitor
Place guests and public services on separate VLANs or a DMZ and use IDS/IPS to detect issues.
Final Takeaway
These three practices reduce most common network risks and give you reliable steps to secure user devices and services during support tasks.
