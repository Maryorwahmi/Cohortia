Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts — What to Remember
Firewalls & Rules
Enforce least privilege: deny incoming by default and open ports only as needed.
VPNs
Provide encrypted tunnels and mutual authentication for remote access; pair with endpoint security.
Segmentation & Monitoring
Use VLANs and DMZs to isolate resources and IDS/IPS to detect and respond to suspicious activity.
Summary Takeaway
Combine least-privilege firewall rules, encrypted VPN tunnels, wireless hardening, and network segmentation to create layered defenses that are practical to manage and effective at reducing risk.
