Comparison
IPsec, OpenVPN, WireGuard
IPsec
Strong, widely supported; often used for site-to-site; can be complex to configure and debug.
OpenVPN
Flexible client options; runs over TLS and handles NAT well.
WireGuard
Modern, simple, and fast; smaller codebase and simpler key management.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • Verify user credentials or keys and client configuration match server settings.
  • Check whether local or network firewalls are blocking VPN ports or protocols.
  • For IPsec, review phase negotiations and certificate validity.
  • For OpenVPN/WireGuard, confirm tunnel interface is up and routes are applied.
Key Takeaway

Understand which protocol is in use and collect logs, client versions, and exact error messages before escalating.
