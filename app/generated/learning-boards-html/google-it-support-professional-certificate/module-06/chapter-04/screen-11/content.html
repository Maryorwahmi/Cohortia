Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › DR vs BC
Disaster Recovery (DR)
Focuses on restoring servers, networks and data to a working state after a major event.
Includes restoration steps, server rebuilds, and data recovery using backups.
Often technical, run by IT and focused on making systems functional again.
Business Continuity (BC)
Focuses on keeping essential business functions running during and after disruption.
Includes alternative work locations, communication plans, and manual workarounds.
Ensures the organization can operate while DR teams restore full technical capability.
Key Takeaway
DR restores IT; BC keeps the business operating. Both must align on priorities set by RTO and RPO.
