Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Preparation
What preparation includes
  • Policies and a documented IR plan with roles and contacts.
  • Detection tools and logging that capture suspicious events reliably.
  • Runbooks and training so IT Support can execute initial steps without hesitation.
  • Backups and verification routines aligned to business recovery needs.
Practice & Checklist
Tabletop Exercise
Walk through a fictional incident step-by-step to reveal missing contacts or unclear responsibilities.
Checklist Sample
Identify source of alert, gather initial logs, isolate suspect system while preserving evidence, and notify IR lead.
Key Takeaway
Practice beats tools. A trained team with clear runbooks recovers faster than a team with unpracticed tools.
