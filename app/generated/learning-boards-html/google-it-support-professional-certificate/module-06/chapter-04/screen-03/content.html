Practical Skills
Isolate, preserve, restore, and document with confidence.
Recognize Indicators
Unusual logins, spikes in outbound traffic, unexpected file changes or user reports often signal incidents.
Safe Isolation
Disconnect a device to stop spread, but avoid wiping logs or re-imaging before evidence is preserved.
Restore & Verify
Restore from backups and confirm files and services work as expected; a restore test validates your backups.
Hands-on lab
Simulate isolation using simple OS commands, then reconnect and verify restoration in a controlled VM.
Practicing these steps reduces hesitation during real incidents, which limits damage and speeds recovery.
