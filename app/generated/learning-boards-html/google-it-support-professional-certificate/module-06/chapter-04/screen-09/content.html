Preservation actions
Steps to keep evidence intact until specialists arrive.
  • Do not re-image or delete logs without explicit instruction.
  • Secure log files and note original locations and timestamps.
  • Isolate systems but keep them powered when asked by investigators.
  • Record who handled which artifacts and when.
Chain of Custody
Document the seizure, custody, control, transfer and analysis of any digital evidence.
Example entry
Copied file 'auth.log' from host 'WS-42' at 2023-11-12T08:15Z. Copied by Lisa. Stored on secure share 'IR-evidence'.
Key Takeaway
Careful preservation and detailed notes keep forensic options available and support credible investigations.
