Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Eradication & Recovery
Eradication
Remove malware, patch exploited systems, and close persistence mechanisms.
Steps: identify root cause, remove malicious files, update systems, and scan to confirm cleanliness.
Role of IT Support: apply patches, re-image when instructed, and prepare clean backups for recovery.
Recovery
Restore services from trusted sources and validate functionality.
Restore: use clean backups, reconfigure services, and run verification tests.
Verification: check data integrity, user access, and service performance before declaring full recovery.
Key Takeaway
Cleaning and restoring are distinct; both require verification to ensure the incident is fully resolved.
