Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › RTO & RPO
Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
Maximum acceptable downtime for a system. Drives recovery procedures.
Short RTOs require fast failover, automation, and tested staff processes.
Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
Maximum acceptable data loss measured in time. Determines backup frequency.
For an RPO of one hour, backups or replication must capture data at least every hour.
Example
An e-commerce order database with RTO 4 hours and RPO 1 hour requires hourly backups and a recovery plan that restores services within four hours.
Key Takeaway
Set RTO and RPO with business owners, then design backups and recovery procedures to meet those targets and test them regularly.
