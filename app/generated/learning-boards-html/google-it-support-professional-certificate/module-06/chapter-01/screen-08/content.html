Availability strategies
  • Redundancy and load balancing to prevent single points of failure.
  • Regular backups and tested restores to recover from data loss.
  • Monitoring and alerting to detect early issues.
  • Maintenance planning to minimize downtime during updates.
Misconception: backups equal availability. Backups support recovery, but only tested restores and redundancy ensure true availability.
Scenario
Server outage during peak hours
Recognize degraded response, open an incident ticket, route traffic to redundant nodes if available, and coordinate with network operations for recovery.
Key Takeaway

Availability requires planning and practice: redundancy plus tested recovery keeps services reliable.
