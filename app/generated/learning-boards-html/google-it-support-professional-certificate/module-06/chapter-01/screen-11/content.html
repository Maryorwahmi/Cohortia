Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Viruses vs Worms vs Trojans
Virus
Attaches to files and needs user action to spread. Response: clean or remove infected files, scan backups for signs of infection.
Worm
Self-replicates across networks by exploiting vulnerabilities. Response: isolate network segments, patch vulnerable services, and remove infected hosts.
Trojan
Disguised as legitimate software to trick users. Response: remove the software, rotate credentials, and check for backdoors.
Concrete scenario
A Trojan installer opens a remote access channel; after cleanup, change passwords and check for unauthorized accounts. For a worm outbreak, immediately isolate and patch to stop propagation.
Key Takeaway

Identify the family to guide containment: user-action threats, self-spreading network threats, or deceptive payloads require different responses.
