Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Threat Actors
Script Kiddies
Use publicly available tools; often opportunistic and less sophisticated. Response: basic containment and cleanup.
Hacktivists
Motivated by ideology; may deface sites or leak information. Response: involve communications and security teams.
Organized Crime
Financially motivated, often use ransomware or data theft. Response: isolate systems, preserve evidence, and notify leadership.
Nation-State
Highly skilled actors pursuing espionage or disruption. Response: immediate escalation to specialist teams and possibly authorities.
Insider Threat
Current or former employees who misuse access, intentionally or accidentally. Response: audit logs, limit access, and follow HR/legal procedures.
Why this matters
Knowing motive shapes containment and who to notify: technical teams, legal, communications, or authorities.
