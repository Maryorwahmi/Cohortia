Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Ransomware, Spyware, Adware
Ransomware
Encrypts files and demands payment. Response: isolate, preserve evidence, restore from clean backups, and notify leadership.
Spyware
Secretly captures data and credentials. Response: scan, remove, reset affected credentials, and investigate for further access.
Adware
Displays unsolicited ads and may track behavior. Response: remove software, check for bundled threats, and educate users on safe downloads.
Practical checklist
Isolate affected hosts, preserve logs, remove malware, restore from verified backups, rotate credentials, and communicate clearly with stakeholders.
Key Takeaway

Classify the malware to match cleanup and recovery steps: availability, confidentiality, or nuisance impacts require different priorities.
