Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Quick Definitions
IT Security
Protecting information systems from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction.
Threat Actor
An individual or group that poses a threat to information systems with specific motives.
Malware
Software designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to systems.
Phishing
Fraudulent electronic communication intended to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information.
DoS
An attack that makes a resource unavailable by overwhelming it from one source.
DDoS
A DoS attack launched from many compromised systems at once, increasing scale and complexity.
Final takeaway

Keep short, accurate definitions ready so you can report incidents quickly and clearly to your security team.
