Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Phishing Analysis
Step 1 — Check sender domain
Confirm the actual domain matches the expected official domain and watch for added segments like extra country codes.
Step 2 — Inspect links
Hover to reveal link targets. If the visible link and destination differ, do not click; report the email.
Step 3 — Evaluate language
Watch for urgent or threatening phrasing and poor grammar—both are common clues of phishing attempts.
Step 4 — Handle attachments carefully
Do not open unexpected attachments; scan them safely or verify the sender first.
Step 5 — Verify out-of-band
Confirm requests via a known phone number or official ticket before taking sensitive actions like changing credentials or transferring funds.
Example
Fake Google security alert
Sender domain includes extra country code and link points to a suspicious domain. Report, block, and verify via official account settings—not the email link.
Common mistake
Trusting the display name or logo. Always inspect the underlying headers and URLs.
Key Takeaway

A simple, consistent checklist reduces the chance of falling for phishing and makes reporting efficient.
