Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Confidentiality
Core controls
  • Strong authentication and MFA to reduce account takeover risk.
  • Role-based access controls to limit who can view or change data.
  • Encryption of data at rest and in transit to protect against stolen media or interception.
  • Audit logging to track who accessed what and when.
Pro tip
When resetting credentials, verify identity using a second channel such as a company phone number or ticketed request.
Concrete example
Temporary contractor access
Grant time-limited access with an expiration date, apply the least privilege, log activity, and revoke access when the task is done.
Common mistake
Leaving shared credentials or failing to revoke temporary accounts creates prolonged exposure to sensitive data.
Key Takeaway

Confidentiality requires both technical controls and disciplined account and access procedures.
