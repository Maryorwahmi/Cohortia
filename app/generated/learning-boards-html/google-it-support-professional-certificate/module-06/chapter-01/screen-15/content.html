Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › DoS / DDoS
Attack profile
DoS: single source floods a service. DDoS: many compromised hosts coordinate to overwhelm the target, often via botnets.
Signs to watch
  • Sudden traffic spikes and connection saturation.
  • Slow or unresponsive services across many users.
  • Many simultaneous connections from unusual IP ranges.
Initial support actions
  1. Confirm and document symptoms and affected services.
  2. Collect logs and traffic snapshots for security teams.
  3. Isolate internal failures to rule out misconfiguration.
  4. Escalate to network and security teams for mitigation.
Concrete example
Web app overwhelmed by distributed traffic
Many different IP addresses generate a flood of requests causing timeouts for legitimate users. Action: preserve logs and escalate for network-level mitigation.
Common misconception
Assuming any outage is an internal misconfiguration. Distinguish by examining traffic patterns and IP diversity.
Key Takeaway

Quick detection, evidence collection, and escalation are essential—DDoS mitigation is usually a network-team task.
