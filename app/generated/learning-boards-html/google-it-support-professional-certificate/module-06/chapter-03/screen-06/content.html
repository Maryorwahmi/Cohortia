Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › MFA Implementations
Comparison table
TOTP (authenticator apps)
Secure, works offline, widely supported; requires device protection and enrollment steps.
Push notifications
User-friendly, fast approval; depends on device and vendor security.
SMS codes
Easy to use but vulnerable to SIM swap and interception; avoid for high-risk accounts.
Hardware security keys
Strongest protection against phishing; requires provisioning and user training.
Tip: Use risk-based policies to assign stronger factors where it matters most.
Concrete scenario
An administrator account should require a hardware key or push plus a strong password. A general staff account may use an authenticator app. Never rely on SMS for administrator access.
Misconception

All MFA is equal. In reality, methods have different threat models and trade-offs; choose based on risk.
