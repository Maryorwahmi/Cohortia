Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Data Loss Prevention
What DLP does
  • Monitors data leaving the organization across email, cloud, and removable media.
  • Detects sensitive patterns like payment numbers or confidential templates.
  • Blocks, alerts, or logs transfers based on policy.
DLP is often managed by security teams, but support staff should know how it impacts users and common troubleshooting steps.
Concrete example
An employee attempts to upload a spreadsheet with customer account numbers to a public cloud folder. DLP policy detects the pattern and either blocks the upload or quarantines it for review. Support may assist by explaining policy, submitting an exception request, or guiding anonymization steps.
Pro-tip

Tune DLP rules gradually and provide clear exception processes to reduce user disruption and false positives.
