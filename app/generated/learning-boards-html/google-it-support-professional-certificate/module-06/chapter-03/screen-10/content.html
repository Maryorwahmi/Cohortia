Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Endpoint Security
Component cards
Antivirus / Anti-malware
Signature-based detection and removal of known threats.
Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR)
Continuous monitoring, detection of suspicious behaviour, and investigation tools.
Host-based firewalls
Controls network traffic per device to limit exposure.
Device control & App whitelisting
Prevent unauthorized media and restrict allowed applications, respectively.
Example incident response
Scenario:
EDR flags unusual process creation. Support triages the alert, isolates the endpoint, runs forensic checks, cleans the system, and applies missing patches to close the exploited vector.
Key Takeaway

Endpoints must be protected with layered controls and monitored so incidents are detected and contained quickly.
