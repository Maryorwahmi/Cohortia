Protecting Systems and Data

Practical steps every IT support professional can take to reduce risk.

This chapter focuses on controls you will enable or manage: who can access systems, how data is encrypted and moved, how software updates remove vulnerabilities, how endpoints stay protected, and how DLP prevents sensitive leaks. The content mixes conceptual definitions, a short terminal demonstration, and hands-on tasks that are safe for learning environments.

Keep in mind the three properties we protect: confidentiality, integrity, and availability. Each control targets one or more of those properties, and understanding those links helps you prioritize efforts in a real environment.
