Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Hands-on Activity
Part 1 — MFA setup (Google Account)
  1. Open account security settings and choose 2-Step Verification.
  2. Select authenticator app, scan the QR code with your device, and enter the generated code.
  3. Add backup codes or recovery options and test sign-in with the second factor.
Do this on your personal account or a test account; observe how login prompts change after enabling MFA.
Part 2 — Disk encryption (conceptual)
Steps:
Create a virtual disk file, initialize it with LUKS, open the mapped device, format, mount, write a test file, then unmount and close. Confirm the file is inaccessible without opening the LUKS container.
Safety note

Do not run disk encryption commands on a production system. Use disposable VMs or lab images for learning.
