Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Switches: Internal Network Traffic
Network Switch
Connects devices within a LAN; forwards frames to specific MAC addresses.
Switches intelligently direct local traffic, reducing collisions and unnecessary broadcasts. Managed switches add VLANs and monitoring. When troubleshooting, check link lights, port speed/duplex mismatches, and VLAN assignments before assuming higher-layer faults.
Quick checks
  • Verify physical link LEDs and cabling integrity.
  • Check port speed and duplex settings for mismatches.
  • Confirm VLAN assignments for segmentation issues.
Useful tip
Use port mirroring to capture traffic for deeper inspection when needed.
Key Takeaway

Switches manage internal LAN traffic by MAC address; check port status and VLANs when local connectivity fails.
