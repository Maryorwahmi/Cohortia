Understanding IT Infrastructure Components
Identify servers, storage and networking elements that support applications and users.

IT infrastructure combines the physical hardware, the software it runs, and the network that connects everything. Good infrastructure planning balances performance, sharing needs, and growth. Expect to compare types of servers and storage depending on workload and to check network devices for correct routing and segmentation.

Why this matters

Correctly matching server, storage, and network choices reduces outages, improves backup and recovery, and controls cost. Small missteps like underestimating growth or confusing NAS and SAN change performance and manageability.
