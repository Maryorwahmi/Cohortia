Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Routers: Connecting Networks
Router
Connects networks and forwards packets based on IP addresses.
Routers decide where traffic should go outside the local subnet. They often implement NAT and firewalling. When internal devices cannot reach the internet but can see each other, the router or its configuration is the likely cause.
Troubleshooting steps
  • Confirm device IP, gateway, and subnet mask settings.
  • Ping the gateway, then an external IP, then a hostname to isolate DNS issues.
  • Use traceroute to find where packets stop beyond the LAN.
Misconfiguration risk
Applying firewall or NAT changes without verification can block services for many users.
Key Takeaway

When external access fails but local traffic works, inspect router configuration and routing diagnostics before wider changes.
