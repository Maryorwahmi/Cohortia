NAS vs SAN
NAS = file-level sharing; SAN = block-level, high-performance storage for databases and virtualization.
Switch vs Router
Switches: forward within LAN by MAC. Routers: forward between networks by IP and manage external access.
Troubleshooting rule
Reproduce, test network, inspect compute, then check storage. Document and use rollback plans for changes.
Final takeaway

Use these distinctions and a layered troubleshooting approach to make clear, cost-effective infrastructure recommendations and to resolve support issues faster.
