Definition & Comparison

CRAC/CRAH vs layout strategies

CRAC / CRAH Units
Specialized HVAC units that regulate temperature and humidity for server rooms.
Hot Aisle / Cold Aisle
Rack arrangement that separates hot exhaust from cold intake to improve cooling efficiency.
Containment Strategies
Physical barriers that prevent hot and cold air mixing and increase CRAC efficiency.
Practical Guidance
  • Design for redundancy such as N+1 CRAC units so cooling persists during maintenance or failure.
  • Implement hot-aisle containment to reduce cooling load and prevent hotspots.
  • Avoid broken airflow paths: seal cable cutouts, organize cables, and manage open floor tiles.
  • Monitor inlet temperatures and humidity to detect performance degradation early.
Example
Installing cold-aisle containment around a high-density compute cluster reduced inlet temperatures, lowering CRAC runtime and improving hardware reliability.
Key Takeaway

Efficient airflow and redundant cooling protect hardware and reduce energy costs; poor airflow is a common and avoidable cause of thermal events.
