Asset Management

Know what you own and where it lives

  • Record model, serial, rack, and PDU connections for each device.
  • Update inventory after moves, replacements, and firmware changes.
  • Use tracking to support capacity planning and maintenance scheduling.
Change Control

Plan, approve, implement, verify

A formal change process includes scope, testing, approvals, a maintenance window, and verification steps. This prevents surprises and documents lessons learned.

Example
Firmware update on a storage controller is scheduled with a rollback plan, approved by stakeholders, and validated after the update to confirm service continuity.
Key Takeaway

Document assets and use change control to make infrastructure work predictable and recoverable.
