Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts
Data Center
A facility that houses servers and systems with controlled power, cooling, and security for continuous operation.
Uninterruptible Power Supply
Provides instantaneous backup power to bridge short outages and support graceful transfers to longer backup sources.
Generator
A long-running backup power source that keeps systems running during extended utility outages.
Summary
Remember how these elements connect: the facility hosts equipment, UPS handles instant interruptions, and generators sustain longer outages.
