Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Operational Challenges

Common Challenges

  • Capacity planning shortfalls: insufficient power, cooling, or rack space for growth.
  • Energy efficiency trade-offs and missed opportunities to lower operating cost.
  • Insufficient disaster recovery planning or untested recovery procedures.

Practical Responses

Regular reviews
Schedule capacity and energy audits to forecast needs and identify efficiency gains.
Staged expansion
Grow infrastructure in planned stages with documented headroom to avoid emergency retrofits.
Key Takeaway

Proactive planning and regular testing reduce the risk and cost of operational failures.
