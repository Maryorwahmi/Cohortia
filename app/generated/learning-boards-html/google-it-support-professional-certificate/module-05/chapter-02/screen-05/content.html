Utility to Rack: the power chain

1
Utility Power
Primary grid power feeding the facility; subject to outages and fluctuations.
2
UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply)
Immediate backup that bridges brief interruptions and provides clean power to sensitive gear.
3
Generator
Long-term backup for extended outages; starts after UPS holds systems steady.
4
PDU (Power Distribution Unit)
Distributes conditioned power to racks and provides monitoring of per-outlet draw.
Practical Notes
  • Always calculate total rack draw and include growth headroom to prevent overloaded circuits.
  • Expect inrush currents on new equipment; specify UPS and breakers accordingly.
  • Use dual UPS paths and dual PDUs for critical servers to remove single points of failure.
  • Monitor per-PDU metrics to detect imbalances and intervene before trips occur.
Example scenario
A web host uses dual PDUs and a redundant UPS so maintenance on one PDU or a UPS module doesn't interrupt traffic. This design keeps services reachable during component replacement.
Key Takeaway

Layered power and measured sizing are essential to prevent outages and allow predictable expansion.
