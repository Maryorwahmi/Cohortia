Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Objectives — Practical

Apply design choices with confidence

These competencies let you size systems with headroom and document changes so teams can deliver high availability.

Sizing & Redundancy
Estimate total power draw and add capacity for future growth; recommend UPS and generator layers and PDU distribution per rack.
Cooling & Airflow
Use CRAC/CRAH units and hot aisle/cold aisle containment to avoid hotspots and improve efficiency.
Security & Ops
Select physical controls and a clear change control process to reduce risk from human error and unauthorized access.
Assessment Prep
You should be able to answer questions on UPS purpose, the benefit of hot/cold aisle containment, and steps in change control. A short design exercise asks you to name three critical elements and justify redundancy choices.
