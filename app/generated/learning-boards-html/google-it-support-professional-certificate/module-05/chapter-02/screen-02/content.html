Learning Objectives

Build competency in the primary systems and practices that keep data centers operational.

Define data center role

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Use asset tracking, change control, and capacity planning to reduce outages and support growth with predictable resources.
