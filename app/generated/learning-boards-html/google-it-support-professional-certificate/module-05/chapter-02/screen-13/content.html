Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts
Power Distribution Unit
Distributes conditioned power to rack outlets and provides monitoring to avoid overloads.
Cooling & Hot/Cold Aisle
A configuration and equipment that separate cold intake air from hot exhaust to improve cooling efficiency.
Change Control
A documented workflow for planning, approving, implementing, and verifying infrastructure changes to reduce risk.
Final Takeaway
Monitoring, redundancy, and disciplined operations are the practices that turn smart design into reliable service delivery.
