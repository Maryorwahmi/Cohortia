Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Monitoring & Fire Suppression
Detection

Sensors and early warnings

  • Temperature and humidity sensors detect trends before failures occur.
  • Smoke and water-leak sensors provide immediate critical alerts for danger.
  • Integrate alerts with operations channels and automated paging for fast response.
Protection

Fire suppression suited for electronics

Data centers typically use inert gas suppression agents rather than water to extinguish fires without damaging equipment. Systems are integrated with detection to reduce accidental discharges and are tested on a maintenance schedule.

Example
A humidity spike flagged by sensors led technicians to discover a cooling fault; repairs prevented component failure and a potential fire scenario.
Key Takeaway

Active monitoring with appropriate suppression protects equipment and allows timely human intervention before disasters unfold.
