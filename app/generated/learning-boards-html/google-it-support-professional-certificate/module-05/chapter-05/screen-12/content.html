Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Cloud Audit Logs
What to look for
  • Changes to IAM bindings or role grants.
  • Creation, deletion, or modification of network/firewall rules.
  • Resource creation or deletion near the incident time.
Filtering by principal and time window often surfaces the responsible change. Record the audit entry ID when escalating.
Example
A sudden outage correlates with a firewall rule edit logged in Audit Logs. Reverting or adjusting the rule restores access. Document the change and why it was reverted.
Common mistake

Ignoring audit entries because they look administrative; even intended changes can cause unexpected failures.
