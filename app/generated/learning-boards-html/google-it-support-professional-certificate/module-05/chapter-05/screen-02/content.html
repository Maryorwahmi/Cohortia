These objectives move you from recognizing monitoring and logging to acting on alerts, investigating logs for root cause, applying IAM controls, and following a repeatable troubleshooting flow.
Objectives at a glance
01
Monitor resources
Create dashboards and alert policies so you know when CPU, memory, or network exceed safe thresholds.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Search logs
Filter and export logs to locate errors, boot messages, and audit events relevant to incidents.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Secure with IAM
Apply least privilege and review roles for users and service accounts to prevent unauthorized access.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Troubleshoot methodically
Follow a checklist: define the problem, check changes, inspect health, review logs, verify network, and check permissions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
