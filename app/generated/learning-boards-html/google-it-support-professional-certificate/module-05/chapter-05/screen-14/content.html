Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Security Best Practices
Least Privilege
Assign minimal roles required. Prefer specific compute or storage roles over editor or owner.
Encryption & MFA
Encrypt data at rest and in transit; require multi-factor authentication for users to reduce credential risk.
Audit & Rotate
Regularly audit IAM, rotate keys, and review Audit Logs to catch unexpected changes early.
Common pitfalls
  • Over-permissioned service accounts.
  • Publicly exposed storage without access controls.
  • Ignoring alert tuning leading to alert fatigue.
