Managing and Troubleshooting Cloud Environments
How monitoring, logging, IAM, and a methodical approach reduce downtime and security risk in GCP.
This chapter teaches you to read dashboards, search logs for root causes, validate network reachability, and verify permissions. We focus on repeated checks and avoiding mistakes like skipping audit logs or giving broad roles.

Why this matters
Visibility plus controlled access reduces mean time to repair and prevents security incidents. Monitoring spots issues, logs explain them, and IAM ensures only authorized actors can change systems.
