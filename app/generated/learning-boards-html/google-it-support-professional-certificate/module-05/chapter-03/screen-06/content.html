Resource pooling & rapid elasticity

Providers pool shared resources to serve multiple customers and allow rapid scaling of capacity.

Resource pooling
Dynamic assignment of physical and virtual resources to optimize utilization across customers.
Rapid elasticity
Scale resources quickly to match workload changes — up or down — often automated by policies.
Measured service
Monitoring and metering usage enable transparent billing and capacity planning for both providers and consumers.
Scenario
An online store uses pooled storage for images, auto-scales web servers during a promotion, and receives usage reports that show peak hours and costs.
Key Takeaway

Pooling and elasticity increase efficiency; measured service gives the metrics to govern spending and capacity.

Misconception
Pooling does not eliminate the need for security or compliance reviews; isolation controls remain important.
