Benefits and challenges

Balance agility and costs against governance and vendor considerations.

Benefits
  • Cost savings via pay-as-you-go
  • Global scale and fast deployment
  • Performance and reliability through managed infrastructure
  • Improved productivity as teams focus on innovation
Challenges
  • Potential vendor lock-in
  • Data sovereignty and compliance constraints
  • Need for new cloud operational skills
Concrete example
A business moves email to a cloud provider and gains global access and lower local IT overhead but must implement account governance and check data residency rules.
Key Takeaway

Assess each workload for cost profile, compliance needs, and required controls before selecting a cloud approach.
