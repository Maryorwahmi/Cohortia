Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Software as a Service (SaaS)
Definition

SaaS: provider-managed software

A complete application you use over the internet while the provider manages all infrastructure and platform operations.

Provider examples
Gmail, Salesforce, Dropbox, Google Workspace.
When to choose
When you need a ready-made application with minimal IT overhead and quick deployment.
Operational note
Even with SaaS, organizations must manage user accounts, access controls, and compliance settings.
Key Takeaway

SaaS minimizes infrastructure work; evaluate administrative and integration controls before adoption.

Misconception
Provider-managed does not mean hands-off: governance, access, and data policies remain your responsibility.
