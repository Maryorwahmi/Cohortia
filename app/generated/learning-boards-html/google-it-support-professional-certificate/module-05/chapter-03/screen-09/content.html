Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Platform as a Service (PaaS)
Definition

PaaS: managed platform

Provider manages platform components so you can focus on developing and deploying applications.

Provider examples
Google App Engine, AWS Elastic Beanstalk, Heroku.
When to choose
When you want fast development cycles and to delegate platform management.
Analogy
PaaS is a furnished apartment: essentials are provided, you add your belongings and live comfortably.
Key Takeaway

PaaS reduces infrastructure management and speeds developer productivity while retaining application-level control.

Misconception
PaaS doesn’t mean zero configuration: frameworks, environment variables, and add-on services still require decisions.
