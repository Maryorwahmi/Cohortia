Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)
Definition

IaaS: raw infrastructure

You rent virtual machines, storage, and networks and manage the OS and applications yourself.

Provider examples
Google Compute Engine, Amazon EC2, Microsoft Azure Virtual Machines.
When to choose
When you need OS-level control, custom configurations, or to migrate existing VMs with minimal change.
Analogy
IaaS is an empty apartment: you bring and manage everything inside.
Key Takeaway

Use IaaS when you need maximum control over the OS and software stack while outsourcing hardware operations.

Misconception
IaaS doesn’t mean no provider responsibility; the provider still ensures hardware reliability and virtualization layers.
