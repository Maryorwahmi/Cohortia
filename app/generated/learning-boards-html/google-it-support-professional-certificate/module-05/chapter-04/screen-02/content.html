Learning Objectives

These four competency milestones guide your practice and verification as an IT support professional working with GCP.

01
Identify major cloud providers

Recognize AWS, Azure, and GCP service families and their common strengths. This helps you route issues to the right platform quickly.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explore compute services in GCP

Describe Compute Engine, App Engine, and Cloud Functions and pick the right level of control for a workload.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Understand GCP storage options

Match Cloud Storage, Persistent Disk, and managed databases to use cases like backups, boot disks, and transactional data.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Describe networking services

Explain VPCs, load balancing, DNS, CDN, and secure connections for hybrid networks so you can troubleshoot connectivity.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
