The big three and why focus matters

AWS, Azure, and GCP offer comparable service families. As a support professional, targeted fluency on one provider accelerates diagnosis and action.

  • Map incidents to services: Identify whether a problem involves compute, storage, or networking before you act.
  • Console vs CLI: Learn where to check metrics in the Console and which CLI commands you will use to reproduce or fix issues.
  • Misconception: Providers are not identical; names and defaults differ and those differences affect troubleshooting steps.
Key Takeaway

Choose one major provider to master for faster, more accurate support work. Knowing service families is more important than memorizing every product.

Practical example

If a user reports a slow application, first ask whether it's running on a VM, a managed platform, or serverless. That maps the problem to Compute Engine, App Engine, or Cloud Functions respectively and narrows diagnostics.

Common mistake

Trying the same troubleshooting script across all providers can waste time because consoles and default metrics differ.
