PSU: stable power delivery
Converts AC to DC and supplies rails to all components; choose proper wattage and connectors for reliability.
Symptoms of PSU issues
Intermittent power loss, failure to boot under load, unexplained reboots.
Replacement notes
Match connectors and wattage; choose a reliable brand to avoid instability.
Example
A gaming PC that reboots during load likely needs a higher wattage PSU or has failing capacitors in the existing unit.
Safety steps
Unplug before opening, use an anti‑static wrist strap, and avoid touching pins on components. Even small static discharges can damage chips.
Key Takeaway

Stability and safety go together: verify power capacity for system demands and always ground yourself before hardware work.

Quick safety checklist
  • Unplug the system before opening the case.
  • Use an anti‑static wrist strap connected to the chassis.
  • Inspect PSU and capacitors visually for bulging or leakage.
Common mistake
Failing to unplug or ground can cause personal injury or component damage; never skip this step.
