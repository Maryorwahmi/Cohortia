Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Storage Comparison
HDD
Spinning platters, magnetic storage. Good capacity per dollar but slower and mechanically fragile.
  • Pros: High capacity, lower cost.
  • Cons: Slower read/write, vulnerable to shock, moving parts wear out.
  • Failure signs: grinding sounds, SMART warnings.
SSD
Flash memory, no moving parts. Faster, durable, energy efficient, available as SATA or NVMe.
  • Pros: High speed, more durable, better boot and app load times.
  • Cons: Higher cost per gigabyte for large capacities.
  • Interface note: NVMe over PCIe is faster than SATA SSDs.
Scenario
A user complains about long boot times and slow application launches. Replacing an HDD with an SSD, preferably NVMe when supported, is the most likely effective upgrade.
