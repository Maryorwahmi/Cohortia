Key Concepts — Quick Reference
A short triage routine to carry into everyday support work.
1. Processes
List and inspect for high CPU or duplicates to find runaway tasks.
2. Memory & Swap
Check for low free RAM and heavy swap activity that slow performance.
3. Drivers & Permissions
Confirm correct drivers and sufficient permissions before changing device or file state.
Final Thought

A clear, short diagnostic routine helps you act quickly and safely. Practice until it becomes your habit.
