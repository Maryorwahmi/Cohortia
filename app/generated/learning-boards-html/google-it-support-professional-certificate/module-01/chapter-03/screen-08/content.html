Device Management
Device Drivers & Peripheral Management
Drivers let the OS control hardware safely and reliably.
Driver Role

Drivers act as translators between OS service calls and specific hardware protocols. They handle I/O operations and present a consistent interface to higher-level software.

Example
When printing, the printer driver converts the OS print request into the printer's page commands so the device can render the document.
Troubleshooting Checklist
  • Identify the device in the OS and confirm it is recognized.
  • Verify a matching driver is installed and check driver version if available.
  • Look for device errors or conflicts in the system logs.
Warning

Installing unsigned or incorrect drivers can destabilize the system. Use vendor-provided drivers when possible and keep backups before major changes.
