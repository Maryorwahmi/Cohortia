Translate Objectives into Practice
Concrete actions that show competency
Checklist Routine
  • Log ticket details and ask clarifying questions.
  • Try simple fixes: reset credential, check cables, restart app.
  • Record outcome and next steps.
Escalation Notes
  • Summarize symptoms, steps tried, and user impact.
  • Include screenshots or error text when available.
  • State why Tier 2 or 3 expertise is needed.
Impact Measurement
Note resolution times and how downtime affected user tasks to show the business value of support.
Empathetic Script
Ask a clarifying question, paraphrase the problem, explain the next step, and confirm when resolved. Avoid jargon.
Practise these steps on mock tickets or with a peer. The habit of documenting and explaining preserves time and prevents repeat calls.
