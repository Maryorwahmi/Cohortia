Avoiding Common Pitfalls
Protect user tasks and handle data per policy.
User-centered vs Technician-centered
Always confirm how the issue affects the user's task. Fixing a symptom without ensuring task completion leaves the user stuck.
Data Handling Safety
Verify identity, avoid sharing credentials, use approved tools, and log any access or transfers of sensitive information.
Practical rule
Ask: What is the user's immediate task? Confirm completion before closing the ticket.
Common mistake
Logging in to test without authorization or not recording the steps you took. Both undermine trust and traceability.
