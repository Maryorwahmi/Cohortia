Escalation Decision Rules
Use evidence and scope to route tickets efficiently.
Tier 1 → Tier 2
Escalate when standard checks fail, when logs indicate deeper issues, or when system-level tools are required.
Tier 2 → Tier 3
Escalate when infrastructure, server, or database intervention is necessary or multiple users are affected.
Escalation package checklist
  • User impact and urgency.
  • Exact errors, screenshots, and timestamps.
  • Steps tried and their results.
Common mistakes
Spending too long trying to fix beyond your scope or escalating without key evidence. Both slow resolution.
