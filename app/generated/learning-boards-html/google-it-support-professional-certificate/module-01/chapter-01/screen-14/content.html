Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
IT Support
Technical assistance enabling user productivity and safety.
Help Desk (Tier 1)
Entry-level fixes, fast resolution, and documentation.
Escalation
Route unresolved or specialised problems to higher tiers with clear evidence.
Summary Takeaway

Support is a user-centred practice: fix problems, communicate clearly, document steps, and escalate when necessary to protect productivity and data.
