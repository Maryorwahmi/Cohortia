Speak So Users Can Act
Empathy, clarity, and confirming understanding reduce friction.
Empathy Script
"I understand this is blocking your work. Tell me exactly what happened and I'll walk you through the next steps." Then paraphrase to confirm.
Clear Instructions
Give numbered steps, say what will happen, and ask the user to confirm after each step.
Example
Guide a user through a printer reset: tell them which cable to check, what light to expect, and confirm the print queue afterwards.
Common mistake
Using technical terms without translation. Replace jargon with plain descriptions so users follow along.
