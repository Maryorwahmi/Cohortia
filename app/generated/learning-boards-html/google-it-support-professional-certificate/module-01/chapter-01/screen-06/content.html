A Four-Step Troubleshooting Pattern
Describe → Hypothesise → Test → Resolve/Document
Checklist
  • Reproduce or record the symptom precisely.
  • Choose one hypothesis to test at a time.
  • Run a targeted test and record the result.
Concrete Example
App crash: note error, check recent updates, restart app, capture logs, then escalate with steps tried if unresolved.
Common mistake
Changing many settings at once and hoping the problem disappears. That prevents learning which change fixed it.
Pro-tip
Take screenshots or copy exact error text; clear evidence accelerates diagnosis and escalation.
