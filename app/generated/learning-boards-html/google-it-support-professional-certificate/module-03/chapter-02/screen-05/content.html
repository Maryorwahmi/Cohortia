Definition
Layer 1 — Physical Layer
Handles physical transmission of raw bits across a medium.
Common elements
Ethernet cables, fiber optics, RJ45, link lights, radio signals, and data rates.
Troubleshooting tip
  • Check that cables are plugged in and link lights are on.
  • Try a known-good cable or a different switch port.
  • For Wi‑Fi, confirm association and signal strength.
Common mistake
Assuming hardware failure before verifying cables and power—most physical issues are simpler than they appear.
Step checks (process)
1. Visual check
Verify cables, link LEDs, power to network devices.
2. Replace cable
Swap a known-good cable to rule out damage or bad terminations.
3. Port test
Move the cable to a different switch port or test the port with another device.
Key Takeaway

Layer 1 faults are found quickly with simple physical checks; start here to remove obvious causes.
