Mnemonic cards
PAN
Personal devices: Bluetooth or cable to one user. Test: pair a headset.
LAN
Local area: office or home Wi‑Fi and switches. Test: list devices on the same subnet.
WAN
Wide area: connects LANs across distance. Test: trace route between branches.
Verification steps
  1. Label device photos with port types and expected role.
  2. Sketch a simple topology showing where the modem, router, switch, and WAP sit.
  3. Identify cable types and note run lengths for cabling decisions.
