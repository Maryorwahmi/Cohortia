Standards & bands
802.11n: often dual-band, reliable for common tasks.
802.11ac: higher throughput on 5 GHz.
802.11ax (Wi‑Fi 6): improved efficiency and performance in dense environments.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • Check RSSI or signal strength from the client.
  • Review channel congestion and adjust if necessary.
  • Confirm the client is on the expected SSID and security mode.
Concrete example
Weak signal at the edge of coverage

If users near the edge of a wireless network report slow speeds, verify whether the AP's transmit power, antenna orientation, or channel planning are appropriate. Adding a WAP near the users or adjusting roaming settings can solve the problem.

Misconception
A single high-powered AP always fixes coverage — placement and channel planning are often more important.
Key takeaway
Measure signal and channel occupancy before changing hardware; targeted adjustments are usually sufficient.
