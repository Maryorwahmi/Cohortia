Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Learning Objectives
01
Explain network fundamentals

Describe what a network is and why it matters. Being able to explain networks helps you guide users and prioritize fixes.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate PAN, LAN, WAN

Identify typical sizes and use cases. Knowing the scope of a network guides the choice of tools and the likely failure points.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify network devices

Recognize routers, switches, hubs, modems, and WAPs and state their primary functions so you can assemble and diagnose networks correctly.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Match cabling and topology

Choose between Ethernet and fiber, and reason about star, bus, or mesh topologies when troubleshooting or designing networks.
