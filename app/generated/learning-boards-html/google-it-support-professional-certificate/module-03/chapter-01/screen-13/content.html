Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Network
A collection of devices sharing resources and exchanging data.
Devices
Router, switch, modem, and WAP each have distinct roles in moving and bridging traffic.
Cabling & Topology
Ethernet for LANs, fiber for backbones; topology affects fault domains and troubleshooting steps.
Summary takeaway

Work methodically: identify devices and ports, confirm physical links and addressing, and use topology to narrow the scope of faults before changing configurations.
