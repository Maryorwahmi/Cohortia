Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Hands‑On Activity
Given items
  • Cisco Catalyst 2960 — multiple Ethernet ports (switch).
  • Netgear Nighthawk R7000 — WAN port, LAN ports, antennas (wireless router).
  • Arris SURFboard SB6183 — coax connector and Ethernet port (modem).
  • CAT6 cables and single-mode fiber (for future backbone).
  • TP‑Link TL‑WA801N — single Ethernet port, power adapter (WAP).
Logical connection plan
  1. Connect Arris modem to ISP coax outlet and power it on.
  2. Ethernet from modem to router WAN port.
  3. Ethernet from router LAN to switch uplink port.
  4. Connect clients to switch with CAT6. Connect WAP to switch for extra Wi‑Fi coverage.
  5. Power sequence: modem → router → switch → WAP; verify link lights and IP addresses.
Example expected outputs
Switch: clients have local connectivity; ping between clients should succeed.
Router: shows a WAN IP from the modem; clients get private IPs and internet access via NAT.
WAP: broadcasts SSID and allows wireless devices to join the LAN.
Common mistake
Testing devices before confirming the modem-to-router WAN link; always confirm upstream first.
Key takeaway
Identify devices by ports, wire modem→router→switch→WAP, power in order, and verify link and addressing to confirm success.
