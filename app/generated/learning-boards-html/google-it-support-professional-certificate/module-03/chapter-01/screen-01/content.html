Learn how devices connect, the hardware that makes it happen, and the choices that affect speed, coverage, and reliability.

What you'll understand

How network types, devices, cabling, and topology combine to carry data. Practical checks you can do first when troubleshooting.

Why this matters

Networks are the foundation of every IT service. Small misconfigurations or the wrong cable choice can cause major outages or slowdowns. Learn to spot root causes quickly.
