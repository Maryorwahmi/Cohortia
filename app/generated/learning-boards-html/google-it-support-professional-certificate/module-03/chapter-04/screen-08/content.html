Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Troubleshooting methodology
Seven-step flow
1. Identify the problem
Gather symptoms, timings and what changed.
2. Establish a theory
Is it physical, software, local or widespread?
3. Test the theory
Use tools to confirm or refute the hypothesis.
4. Plan action
Consider effects and rollback options.
Continue the flow
5. Implement or escalate
Make the change or hand off with evidence.
6. Verify and prevent
Confirm full functionality and consider preventive steps.
7. Document
Record symptoms, tests, actions and outcomes for future reference.
Example
If tracert shows the path failing at an ISP hop, gather tracert output, contact the ISP and include timestamps and affected destinations when escalating.
Key Takeaway

A methodical approach reduces mistakes and shortens repair time. Test before you change.
