Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › netstat — Inspect active connections
Command example
netstat -an // lists all connections and listening ports numerically
Use cases
  • Identify unexpected listening ports before allowing remote access.
  • Detect many outbound connections that may indicate heavy usage or malicious traffic.
  • Correlate ports with services when troubleshooting connectivity to an application.
Scenario
A workstation shows slow performance. netstat -an reveals many established outbound connections. Combine with process listing to locate the application responsible and decide whether to terminate or escalate to security.
Misconception
Netstat alone doesn't name processes on all systems without extra flags or permissions; match ports to processes with task tools.
Key Takeaway

Netstat provides a snapshot of connections and listening ports; use it to detect anomalies and map ports to processes for action.
