Operator reference and examples
Arithmetic examples
sum_val = 10 + 5
product = 6 * 7
fraction = 10 / 4    # true division -> float
whole = 10 // 4      # floor division -> int
remainder = 10 % 4   # modulo
power = 2 ** 8       # exponentiation
Applied scenario
Calculate a 20% discount on a price and split the result among three people using true division so each gets a fair share.
Remember to convert any text inputs to numbers first when computing prices or amounts.
Misconception
Using floor division expecting fractional results produces truncated values and may silently change your logic.
Pro tip
Use parentheses to make combined expressions explicit and avoid relying on operator precedence to communicate intent.
Key Takeaway
Pick operators based on the numeric type and result you need; convert inputs before arithmetic to be safe.
