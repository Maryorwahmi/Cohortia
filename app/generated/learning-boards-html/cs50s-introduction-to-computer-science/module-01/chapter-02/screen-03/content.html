Practice tasks
Small, focused practice helps you internalize the compile-and-run loop and common syntax patterns.
Personalized Greeting Activity
Change the print statement to show a custom greeting, recompile, and observe output differences when you add or remove the newline sequence.
Assessment checkpoint
Explain what happens if the include line is removed and why the compiler complains about an unknown function.
Quick tips
  • Always save before you compile.
  • Read compiler messages from top to bottom; they often point to the first place parsing failed.
  • Small edits and recompile cycles are normal — iterate quickly.
