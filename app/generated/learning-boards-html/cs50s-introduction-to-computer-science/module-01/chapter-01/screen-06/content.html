Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Binary → Decimal
Step-by-step

Multiply and add

  1. Write powers of two under each digit (2^0, 2^1, 2^2 …).
  2. Multiply each binary digit (0 or 1) by its corresponding power of two.
  3. Sum all non-zero products to get the decimal value.
Worked example
1101 binary
= 1×8 + 1×4 + 0×2 + 1×1
= 8 + 4 + 0 + 1
= 13 decimal
Common mistake

Misaligning powers of two or skipping zeros leads to wrong sums. Mark every position explicitly.

Reverse check
To verify, convert back by subtracting largest powers of two: start with the largest power ≤ decimal, mark a 1, subtract, and continue. This confirms accuracy.
Practice prompt
Convert 11010 to decimal and check your steps.
Takeaway
A reliable method avoids guesswork and builds fluency for larger encodings.
