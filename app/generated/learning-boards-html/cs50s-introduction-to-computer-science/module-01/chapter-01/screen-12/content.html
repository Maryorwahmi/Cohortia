Binary Conversion Challenge

Work each problem carefully and use the reverse conversion to check your work.

1. Convert 11010 to decimal
Label powers 16,8,4,2,1 → multiply and sum.
2. Convert 27 to binary
Use largest-power subtraction to place ones and zeros.
3. How many values with 6 bits?
Compute 2^6 to find the number of unique states.
4. 24-bit color question
With 8 bits per channel, what is the maximum decimal per component?
Check
Convert answers back to verify correctness.
Watch out
Off-by-one errors occur when mistaking 2^n for the maximum value representable.
Extension
Consider how many colors are possible with 24-bit color: 2^24 total combinations across RGB channels.
