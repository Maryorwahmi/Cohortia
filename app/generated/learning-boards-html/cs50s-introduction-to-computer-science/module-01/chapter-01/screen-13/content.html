Common mistakes and how to catch them

Binary anxiety
Fix: label powers of two and practice small conversions to build confidence.
RAM vs Storage
Fix: verify persistence by saving and restarting; assume volatility unless proven otherwise.
Off-by-one
Fix: remember range is 0 to 2^n − 1; compute small examples like 8 bits → 255 to internalize.
Practical checklist
Write down assumptions, label powers, and run small tests to validate behavior.
Takeaway
Habits prevent many common beginner mistakes and make your reasoning about systems clearer.
