Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Decimal → Binary
Method

Largest-power subtraction

  1. Find largest 2^k ≤ decimal number.
  2. Put 1 at that power, subtract it from the number.
  3. Repeat until remainder is zero, placing 0 where powers are skipped.
Worked example
13 decimal
Largest power ≤ 13 is 8 (2^3) → 1
Remainder 5 → largest power 4 (2^2) → 1
Remainder 1 → 2^1 is too big → 0
2^0 → 1 → 1
Result: 1101 binary
Pitfall

Forgetting to insert zeros for skipped powers changes the meaning. Mark every position explicitly.

Practice prompt
Convert decimal 27 to binary using this method and verify by converting back to decimal.
Why it helps
This builds an intuitive sense of magnitude and helps prevent overflow surprises when choosing storage sizes.
Takeaway
Both directions are worth practicing; they reinforce place-value thinking in base two.
