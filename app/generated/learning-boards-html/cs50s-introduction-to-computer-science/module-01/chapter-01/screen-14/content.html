Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Bit
A single 0 or 1, the smallest piece of information.
Byte
A group of 8 bits, commonly used to store characters or small values.
Binary
Base-2 positional system where each position is a power of two.
Algorithm
A finite sequence of well-defined steps to solve a problem.
CPU
Executes instructions and coordinates computation.
RAM
Volatile memory for active programs and data.
Summary

Keep these core concepts handy: they are the lens through which you will interpret storage, execution, and algorithms throughout computing.
