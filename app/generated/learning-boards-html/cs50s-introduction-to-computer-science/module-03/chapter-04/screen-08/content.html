Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Divide & Conquer
Design checklist
Divide
How to split the input into smaller pieces until they are trivial.
Base
What is the smallest subproblem we can solve directly?
Combine
How do we merge recursive results to form the final answer?
Practice by applying this checklist before writing any code. For example, to sum an array recursively, divide into two subarrays, base case for empty or single-element arrays, then combine by adding sums.
Key Takeaway

State divide, base, and combine before coding. That structure prevents errors and reveals opportunities for memoization.
