Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › When to use recursion
Tree Traversal
Recursion matches the node/children structure, making preorder, inorder, and postorder traversals concise.
Directory walk
Recursively visit nested directories and process files; easy to read and implement.
Divide & Conquer
Algorithms that split inputs and combine results are natural recursion candidates.
Decision checklist
  • Does the problem split into smaller similar problems?
  • Is recursion depth reasonable for the expected input sizes?
  • Do subproblems overlap; would memoization help?
Key Takeaway

Recursion is best when it expresses the problem clearly and depth stays safe; otherwise prefer iteration or memoization.
