Compare vs Swap
Count comparisons and swaps separately; they measure different resource costs.
Best/Average/Worst
Insertion & Bubble can be O(n) best; average and worst are O(n²) for these three algorithms.
Fair Measurement
Always copy the original array before each sort so comparisons are fair and reproducible.
Parting Thought

Simple sorts teach principles you’ll reuse in advanced algorithms. Use measurements and assumptions to guide practical choices.
