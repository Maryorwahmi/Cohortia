Common pitfall
If you forget to copy the original array, the second and third sorts operate on already-sorted data and your comparisons will be invalid. Always reset input for each algorithm.
Key Takeaway

Measure both comparisons and swaps and keep inputs identical when comparing algorithms to get fair, interpretable results.
