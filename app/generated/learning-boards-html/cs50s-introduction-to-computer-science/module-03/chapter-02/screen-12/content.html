Performance considerations
  • Small n: O(n²) sorts are often acceptable and simpler to implement.
  • Large n: prefer O(n log n) sorts; quadratic growth quickly becomes infeasible.
  • Stability: maintain relative order for equal elements when required; selection sort is often not stable.
  • Measurement: count both comparisons and swaps; they reveal different costs.
Common mistake
Running sorts on the same array without copying — this gives invalid comparative results.
Pro tip
Use insertion sort as a subroutine for small partitions in more advanced sorts; it performs well on near-sorted data.
