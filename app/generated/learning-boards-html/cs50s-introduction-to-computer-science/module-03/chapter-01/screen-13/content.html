Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Algorithm
A recipe-like procedure that produces a result from inputs.
Efficiency
Time and space costs that determine whether an algorithm scales.
Big O notation
A classification of growth rates used to compare algorithms abstractly.
Summary takeaway
When you design or review code, identify the algorithm, count the dominant operations, include per-element costs like string length, and report growth with Big O to reason about scaling.
