Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Products activity
Starter templates
-- 1. Product names and prices
SELECT ProductName, Price
FROM Products;

-- 2. All furniture
SELECT *
FROM Products
WHERE Category = 'Furniture';

-- 3. Price greater than 100
SELECT ProductName, Price
FROM Products
WHERE Price > 100;

-- 4. Electronics with low stock
SELECT ProductName, StockQuantity
FROM Products
WHERE Category = 'Electronics' AND StockQuantity < 100;

-- 5. Combined: (Electronics under 100) OR Furniture
SELECT ProductName, Category, Price
FROM Products
WHERE (Category = 'Electronics' AND Price < 100) OR Category = 'Furniture';
Debug checklist
  • Confirm string literals are quoted and numeric literals are not.
  • Repeat the column name for each OR clause.
  • Test queries on a small dataset or LIMIT the results for quick checks.
Example takeaway
A single misplaced OR or missing quote can expand results dramatically. Verify and group conditions before relying on query outputs.
