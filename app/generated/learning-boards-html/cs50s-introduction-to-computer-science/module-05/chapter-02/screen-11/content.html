Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › AND / OR / NOT
Examples
-- AND example
SELECT FirstName, LastName, GPA
FROM Students
WHERE Major = 'Computer Science' AND GPA > 3.5;

-- OR example
SELECT FirstName, LastName, Major
FROM Students
WHERE Major = 'Computer Science' OR Major = 'Mathematics';
Notes and pitfalls
  • Always repeat the column name for each OR condition.
  • NOT negates a condition; use parentheses to scope its effect.
  • Test combined conditions on small samples to confirm results.
Pro tip
Build complex filters incrementally: start with one condition, then add AND/OR and re-run the query.
