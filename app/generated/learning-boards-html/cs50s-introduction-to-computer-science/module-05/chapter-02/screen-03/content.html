Practice and safety goals

Short drills and reminders to keep queries correct and efficient.

Inspect with SELECT *
Use the asterisk for quick debugging but avoid in production where privacy or throughput matters.

Compare correctly
Remember to quote strings and leave numbers unquoted so comparisons behave as expected.

Combine with care
Group complex conditions with parentheses to avoid precedence surprises between AND and OR.

Avoid common pitfalls
Never write an OR clause that omits the column on the right-hand side, as that makes the condition always true.
