Example with parentheses
SELECT FirstName, LastName, Major, GPA
FROM Students
WHERE (Major = 'Computer Science' AND GPA >= 3.8) OR Major = 'Biology';
Parentheses ensure the AND condition applies as a unit, then OR compares that unit to the Biology major condition.
Common error
Without parentheses, the database still applies AND before OR, but the written logic is harder to read and easier to misinterpret.
Key Takeaway

Use parentheses to communicate the grouping of conditions clearly and to ensure correct evaluation order.
