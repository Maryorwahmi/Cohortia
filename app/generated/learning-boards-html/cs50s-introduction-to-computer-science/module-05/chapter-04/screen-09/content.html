Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Denormalized vs Normalized
Denormalized snapshot
Example rows show repeated student info per enrollment:
StudentID | StudentName | StudentEmail | CourseTitle | Grade
1         | Alice Smith | alice@ex     | Intro CS    | A
1         | Alice Smith | alice@ex     | Math101     | B
2         | Bob Johnson | bob@ex       | Intro CS    | A-
Update email requires editing multiple rows.
Normalized snapshot
Separate tables: Students, Courses, Enrollments. Each student email stored once in Students, enrollments reference StudentID and CourseID.
Students: StudentID | StudentName | StudentEmail
Enrollments: EnrollmentID | StudentID | CourseID | Grade
Courses: CourseID | CourseTitle
Update email requires a single Students row change.
