Tables and keys
  • Students: StudentID (PK), FirstName, LastName, MajorID
  • Courses: CourseID (PK), Title, Credits
  • Enrollments: EnrollmentID (PK), StudentID (FK), CourseID (FK), Grade
Enforce referential integrity with foreign-key constraints so that Enrollments cannot reference missing students or courses.
Why linking tables?
They represent many-to-many relationships without duplicating entity attributes. Each linking row models one relation instance, making updates and deletions predictable.
Check: Is the relationship between two entities many-to-many? If yes, create a linking table with FKs to both entities.
