Designing Relational Databases: Normalization and Joins
Structure your data to avoid redundancy, and use joins to reconstruct cross-table views.
In this lesson

Learn the principles of 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, and practice INNER and LEFT JOIN queries using classroom-style tables.

Why it matters

Avoid inconsistent updates, keep a single source of truth for attributes, and make queries reliable and efficient.

Explore Key Topics
Normalization
Split repeating groups and remove transitive dependencies to prevent redundancy.
Joins
Use INNER and LEFT JOIN to combine related rows from normalized tables for reports and views.
Common Errors
Watch for missing ON clauses, wrong join columns, and unintended Cartesian products.
