Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Foreign Keys
Architecture

How tables connect

Foreign keys reference primary keys in other tables to ensure links are valid and meaningful.

Students (StudentID PK)
Enrollments (StudentID FK → Students.StudentID)
Courses (CourseID PK)
Misconception
Foreign keys do not duplicate full records; they store identifiers that point to a single source of truth.
Concrete example
Enrollments
EnrollmentID | StudentID (FK) | CourseID (FK) | Grade
-----------------------------------------------------
201          | 1001           | CS50          | A
202          | 1002           | CS50          | B+
Key Takeaway

Model relationships with foreign keys and, when needed, separate join tables to represent many-to-many links.
