Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Files vs Databases
Comparison

Flat files & spreadsheets

Good for small, single-user tasks. Limitations appear as data grows or multiple programs share data.

  • Redundancy: the same record repeats across files.
  • Updates are manual and error-prone.
  • Queries require custom scripts that are slow and fragile.
  • Concurrency is not handled; two people saving simultaneously can overwrite data.
Analogy
Think of spreadsheets as many photocopied contact lists. If someone moves, every copy must be updated by hand.
Database advantage

Relational databases

Centralized tables, keys, constraints, and indexes reduce duplication and improve reliability and performance.

Central tables enforce a single source of truth.
Keys link related records without repeating data.
Constraints prevent invalid entries and keep integrity strong.
Key Takeaway

Choose a database when you need scaling, correctness, and safe multi-user access; don’t rely on spreadsheets for shared, relational data.
