Definition

Algorithmic Bias

Systematic errors in a computer system that produce unfair outcomes for specific groups. Often a product of biased training data or flawed design choices.

Comparison: biased vs audited model
Biased model
  • Replicates historical discrimination
  • Produces disparate errors
  • Undermines trust
Audited model
  • Measured by fairness metrics
  • Adjusted data or thresholds
  • Documented limitations
Concrete example

A hiring AI trained on past hires ranked applicants. Underrepresented candidates had lower scores because historical hires were not diverse. The correct fix combined balanced data collection, feature review, and human oversight.

Checklist to reduce bias
  • Inspect labels and sources for skew.
  • Measure outcomes by demographic slices.
  • Use mitigation: reweight, resample, or adjust thresholds.
  • Include human-in-the-loop review for critical decisions.
Pro tip

Report both overall accuracy and group-specific error rates. Transparency helps stakeholders trust your model and understand its limits.
