Definition

Explainable AI and accountability

Design systems so stakeholders can understand decisions, assign responsibility, and intervene when things go wrong.

Explainability techniques
  • Feature importance scores to show drivers of predictions.
  • Local explanations for single predictions to aid review.
  • Model cards describing training data, intended use, and limitations.
Concrete scenario

An autonomous vehicle faces an unexpected obstacle. The system logs the sensor data, the decision path, and a human reviewer verifies actions. Clear logs enable accountability and improvement.

Risk

Opaque models in safety-critical domains hide failure modes and make assigning legal or moral responsibility difficult.

Key Takeaway

Build explanations and documentation into deployment. Complexity without interpretability limits accountability.
