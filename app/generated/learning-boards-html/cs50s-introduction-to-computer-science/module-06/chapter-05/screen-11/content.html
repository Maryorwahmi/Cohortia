Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Safeguards Process
Design Checkpoints
  1. Minimize Data
    Collect only necessary signals and prefer on-device processing to reduce breach risk.
  2. Access Control & Logging
    Enforce least privilege and record who accessed what and why for audits.
  3. Fairness Testing
    Evaluate disparate impact and hold out demographic slices for validation.
  4. Transparency & Consent
    Explain decisions and provide granular, revocable consent toggles for users.
  5. Governance
    Require cross-functional review and scheduled audits for high-risk features.
Quick Safeguard
Default to conservative behavior: prefer showing critical notifications and let users tighten suppression rules.
Practical Example
Audit Plan
Run a 30-day simulation where suppressed notifications are logged and randomly delivered to a review panel to detect missed urgencies.
Transparency Example
A short note: "Notifications may be delayed based on inferred focus. See settings to change this and view why a notification was delayed."
Common Mistake
Treating anonymization as permanent: revisit re-identification risks when combining datasets.
