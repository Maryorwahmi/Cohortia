Learning Objectives
A Bloom progression: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Analyze ethical dilemmas

Learn to identify harms in software, data, and AI, and assess who is affected and how. This skill helps you make defensible design choices.

Mnemonic: stakeholder map
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand societal impacts

See how algorithms shape outcomes in hiring, finance, and public life. Understanding impact lets you anticipate indirect harms.

Mnemonic: cause → effect
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply responsible practices

Adopt concrete protections: minimal collection, encryption, access controls, fairness testing, and accessibility checks.

Mnemonic: protect, test, document
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Commit to continuous learning

Ethical challenges evolve. Maintain curiosity, engage with frameworks, and update practices as technology changes.

Mnemonic: review → adapt
