Scenario Walkthrough

Smart Notifications: what changes?

The feature inspects messages and behavior to decide notification timing, learning from user interactions. Economically, anonymized aggregates are sold.

Data Collected
Message metadata, read/tap timings, possibly message content, focus score history.
Risks
Re-identification, surveillance, suppression of critical alerts, mission creep when selling data.
First Safeguard
Process message content locally and only store per-user focus scores. Do not upload raw messages.
Reflection
Which data element is least necessary to the feature, and how would you remove it without losing functionality?
Data Flow & Stakeholders
U
User Device
Local processing of messages and focus heuristics reduces central storage risk.
AI
Model Training
Train on aggregated, vetted data. Require fairness testing and provenance logs.
Questions for Teams
  • What minimal data enables accurate urgency detection?
  • How can users view and revoke their data or opt out of selling?
  • Which edge cases could cause suppression of critical alerts?
Common Mistake
Relying solely on anonymization without a model of re-identification risk.
Practical Tip
Prefer on-device inference, clear opt-in, and monthly privacy audits before commercialization.
