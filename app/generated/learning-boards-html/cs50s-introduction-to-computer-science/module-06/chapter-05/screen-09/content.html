Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Misinformation vs Disinformation
Core Idea

Misinformation and Disinformation

Differentiate by intent. Solutions combine technical detection and human-centered policies.

Misinformation
False info shared without malice; education and labeling reduce harm.
Disinformation
Intentional deception; requires provenance and stronger moderation.
Key Takeaway

Treat intent as a design axis: detection, transparency, and human oversight must work together to protect truth and trust.

Comparison & Examples
Example: Mistaken Health Post
A user shares an unverified health tip. Response: labels, citation prompts, community fact-check.
Example: Coordinated Campaign
Repeated false posts from coordinated accounts. Response: provenance checks, takedown, platform accountability.
Misconception
Automated filters alone are sufficient. Reality: filters must be combined with human review and transparent policy.
