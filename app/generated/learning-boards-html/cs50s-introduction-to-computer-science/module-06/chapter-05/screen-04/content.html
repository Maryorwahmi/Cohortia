Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Data Privacy
Definition

Data Privacy

The right of individuals to control collection, storage, and use of personal information. It requires both technical controls and clear user consent.

Common protections
  • Minimize collection to what is necessary for functionality.
  • Encrypt sensitive data at rest and in transit.
  • Implement strict access controls and audits.
  • Provide user controls for consent, access, and deletion.
Concrete example

A shopping site stores payment tokens separately and never saves CVV codes. They keep purchase logs 90 days then purge them.

Common mistake

Saving sensitive fields unencrypted or keeping full logs forever increases breach impact and legal risk.

Practical checklist
  1. Ask: Do we need this data to deliver the feature?
  2. Classify sensitivity and apply encryption + access rules.
  3. Document retention policy and automate deletion.
  4. Provide transparency and consent flows to users.
Key Takeaway

Treat privacy as a design constraint: fewer fields and stronger protections reduce harm.

Try this

Before adding any analytics event, write the purpose, who will access it, and how long it will be retained. If any answer is unclear, do not collect it.
