Ethical Frameworks in Practice
Choose a Lens
Pick a guiding framework for major trade-offs: consequences, duties, or virtues. Use it consistently when evaluating features.
Reflective Reviews
At milestones, document beneficiaries, potential harms, and assumptions. Record mitigation owners in your issue tracker.
Speak Up Culture
Encourage team members to raise concerns without fear. Document those concerns and include them in acceptance criteria.
Action Planner
Write three measurable ethics goals for the next quarter and attach at least one metric to each.
Personal Development
Skill: Privacy Impact Assessment
Learn to document data flows and risks for new features.
Skill: Fairness Metrics
Practice selecting and interpreting fairness metrics for different contexts.
Skill: Accessibility Testing
Run simple checks and learn assistive tech basics to validate designs.
Closing Prompt
Select one habit you will adopt this week to keep ethics visible in your work. Write it down and set a calendar reminder.
