Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Data Privacy
Control collection, secure stored data, and require informed consent for sensitive processing.
Algorithmic Bias
Bias can emerge from data and design; measure, test, and involve diverse teams to mitigate unfair outcomes.
Explainable AI
Make decisions interpretable so users and auditors can understand system behavior and accept responsibility.
Summary
Protect privacy, design for fairness, and insist on transparency. These practices together build trustworthy systems.
