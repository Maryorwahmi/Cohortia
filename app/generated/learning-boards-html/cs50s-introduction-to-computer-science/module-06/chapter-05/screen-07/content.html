Definitions

Digital Divide & Accessibility

The digital divide is unequal access to internet and devices. Accessibility ensures people with disabilities can use technology. Both affect fairness and opportunity.

Practical tactics
  • Progressive enhancement for unreliable networks.
  • Semantic HTML and ARIA roles for assistive tech.
  • Contrast checks and keyboard navigation testing.
Example

A news site offers a lightweight text-only mode and uses semantic headings so screen readers and low-bandwidth users can access content quickly.

Why it matters

Design choices determine who benefits. Inclusive design widens reach, reduces inequality, and improves resilience of your product.

Checklist

Offer low-bandwidth options, test with assistive tech, and include accessibility in your acceptance criteria.
