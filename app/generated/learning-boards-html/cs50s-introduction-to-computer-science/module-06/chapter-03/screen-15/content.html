Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Quick Reminders
Separate concerns
Keep UI and server responsibilities distinct to protect data and simplify maintenance.
Choose methods
Use GET for retrievable resources and POST for submitting or changing data.
Validate & redirect
Validate request data and redirect after POST to prevent duplicates and improve UX.
Closing thought

These practical habits will scale with your projects and keep server-side code safe and predictable.
