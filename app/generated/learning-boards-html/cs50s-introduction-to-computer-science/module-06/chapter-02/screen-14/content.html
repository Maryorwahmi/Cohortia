Why external files?
They support reuse across pages, version control, and separation from markup, improving team workflows.
Small functions
Encapsulate repeated logic in functions to avoid duplication and make behavior easier to test and extend.
Design question
If a widget grows several hundred lines long, how would you break it into modules and smaller functions? Consider state, rendering, and event binding separately.
