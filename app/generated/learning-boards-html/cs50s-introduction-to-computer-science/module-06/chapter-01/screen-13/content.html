Definition
Accessibility through semantics
Headings form document structure navigable by assistive tech.
Alt provides alternative text for images; use descriptive alt for informative images.
Lists and landmarks (header/footer/nav) give structure to the page.
Try: add alt text to your profile image that clearly describes the image context for someone who cannot see it.
Example alt texts
Good: "Headshot of Alex smiling, wearing a blue sweater."
Bad: "image1.jpg" or missing alt — not descriptive.
Common trap: hiding structural elements because of styling. Keep semantics and use CSS to change visuals, not to remove meaning.
