Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Three Reminders
Initialize to NULL
Avoid uninitialized pointers; they commonly cause crashes.
Think in elements
Pointer arithmetic advances by type size; reason in elements not bytes.
Pair allocate & free
Always plan where to free memory and nullify the pointer after freeing.
Closing reflection

Make these reminders part of your coding ritual. They prevent the most frequent pointer mistakes and keep programs stable.
