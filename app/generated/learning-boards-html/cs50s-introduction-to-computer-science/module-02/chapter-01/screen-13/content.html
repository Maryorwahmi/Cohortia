Your steps
  1. Prompt for number of grades and validate it.
  2. Allocate float *grades = malloc(n * sizeof(float)); and check for NULL.
  3. Loop to read each grade and accumulate a sum.
  4. Compute and print the average.
  5. free(grades); grades = NULL;
Hints & checks
Place free before every return that exits after allocation. Use meaningful error messages and keep the allocation check immediately after malloc.
Key Takeaway

Plan your allocation and deallocation together so the program remains leak-free even on errors.
