# Artificial Intelligence Learning Paths

> **Scope:** Courses and certificates only. Degree programs are excluded.  
> **Source:** Derived from `docs/catalog-artificial-intelligence-research.md` and exported to `docs/artificial-intelligence/catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json`.  
> **Status:** Draft catalog for the AI category.

---

## What’s in this folder

- `catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json` is the source of truth for the AI course/certificate catalog.
- The catalog currently contains **300 items** across **12 subcategories**.
- It mirrors the Computer Science catalog structure, but keeps this category focused on non-degree learning paths.

## Subcategories

1. AI Fundamentals & Principles
2. Machine Learning Engineering
3. Deep Learning & Neural Networks
4. Natural Language Processing & LLMs
5. Computer Vision & Multimodal AI
6. Generative AI & Foundation Models
7. Robotics & Autonomous Systems
8. Reinforcement Learning & Game AI
9. AI Ethics, Safety & Governance
10. AI for Science & Research
11. Edge AI & AI Hardware
12. AI Strategy, Product & Business

## Notes

- Each item in the JSON includes course metadata like `title`, `provider`, `platform`, `level`, `type`, and `skills`.
- Certificate-style items are kept in the catalog, while degrees are intentionally left out.
