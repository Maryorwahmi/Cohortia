## Part 3: How to finalize

1. **Verify uncertain degrees** — especially PhD programs (some are hybrid or require residencies).  
2. **Decide on inclusion rules** — e.g., do we include general engineering degrees (Mechanical, Civil) under CS, or keep them in Physical Science & Engineering?  
3. **Pick top 150–200** from the combined list (existing + additions).  
4. **Convert to JSON schema** once approved.  
5. **Repeat research process** for the other 9 categories.
