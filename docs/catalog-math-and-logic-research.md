## 5. Open Questions

1. **Overlap with Data Science Category:** The Data Science & Analytics (Mathematical Foundations) subcategory overlaps significantly with the existing Data Science category. Should these be duplicated, cross-referenced, or limited to strictly mathematical/theoretical data science courses? Currently includes foundational statistics and mathematical methods courses that serve both categories.

2. **Actuarial Science Scope:** Actuarial science sits at the intersection of mathematics, business, and risk. Should it remain in Math & Logic or be split between Business and Math? The current approach keeps it here due to the heavy mathematical foundation (probability, stochastic processes, financial math).

3. **Programming vs. Mathematics Balance:** Many computational and data science courses require significant programming (Python, R, MATLAB, C++). Should programming prerequisites be explicitly noted, or should these courses be moved to Computer Science? Currently included as they are fundamentally mathematical in nature.

4. **Financial Mathematics Overlap:** Quantitative finance courses overlap with both the Business category (Finance subcategory) and this Math category. Should they be duplicated or should we focus only on the highly mathematical/theoretical finance courses (e.g., stochastic calculus) here while leaving general finance courses in Business?

5. **Level Granularity for Advanced Mathematics:** Many graduate-level pure mathematics courses (topology, algebraic geometry, model theory) are extremely specialized. Should the "Advanced" level be sufficient, or do we need an "Expert/Graduate" level to distinguish these from intermediate courses? Currently all marked as Advanced.

6. **OpenCourseWare and Self-Paced Resources:** MIT OpenCourseWare, Khan Academy, OpenStax, and similar resources are free but lack formal certification/assessment. Should they be included as "Self-Paced" courses without certificates, or should the catalog focus only on credentialed offerings? Currently included because they are high-quality and widely used.

7. **Logic and Philosophy Placement:** Mathematical logic and philosophy of mathematics courses could fit in Arts & Humanities (Philosophy) or here. Should they be duplicated, or should logic remain in Math while philosophy of math stays in Humanities? Currently logic is here, philosophy of math is also here but could be cross-referenced.

8. **Mathematics Education vs. General Education:** Mathematics education courses are specific to teaching math, but general pedagogy courses belong in Education. Should math education stay here, or be consolidated in a future Education category? Currently kept here for math-specific pedagogical content.

9. **Industrial Mathematics Coverage:** Industrial mathematics (manufacturing, supply chain, quality control) overlaps with Engineering and Operations Research. Should we expand this subcategory or keep it focused on mathematical modeling and computation? Currently limited to mathematically rigorous industrial applications.

10. **Topology and Geometry Enrollment:** Topology and geometry are highly specialized and have lower enrollment than applied areas. Should these remain as distinct subcategories or be merged into Pure Mathematics? Currently kept separate for discoverability by specialists but could be consolidated.
