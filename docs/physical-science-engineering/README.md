# Physical Science & Engineering Learning Paths

> **Scope:** Courses and certificates only. Degree programs are excluded.  
> **Source:** Derived from `docs/catalog-physical-science-engineering-research.md` and exported to `docs/physical-science-engineering/catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json`.  
> **Status:** Draft catalog for the Physical Science & Engineering category.

---

## What’s in this folder

- `catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json` is the source of truth for the Physical Science & Engineering course/certificate catalog.
- The catalog currently contains **320 items** across **12 subcategories**.
- It mirrors the other category catalogs already created in Cohortia, but stays focused on science and engineering learning paths.

## Subcategories

1. Physics & Astrophysics
2. Chemistry & Materials Science
3. Mechanical Engineering
4. Civil & Environmental Engineering
5. Electrical & Electronics Engineering
6. Chemical & Petroleum Engineering
7. Aerospace & Aeronautical Engineering
8. Biomedical & Bioengineering
9. Geology, Earth Sciences & Mining
10. Oceanography & Marine Science
11. Environmental Science & Sustainability Engineering
12. Industrial, Systems & General Engineering

## Notes

- Each item in the JSON includes metadata such as `title`, `provider`, `platform`, `level`, `type`, `skills`, and `id`.
- Degree programs from the research draft are intentionally excluded.
- Specialization-style items are normalized into schema-friendly course records.
