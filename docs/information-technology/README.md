# Information Technology Learning Paths

> **Scope:** Courses and certificates only. Degree programs are excluded.  
> **Source:** Derived from `docs/catalog-it-research.md` and exported to `docs/information-technology/catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json`.  
> **Status:** Draft catalog for the Information Technology category.

---

## What’s in this folder

- `catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json` is the source of truth for the IT course/certificate catalog.
- The catalog currently contains **165 items** across **12 subcategories**.
- It mirrors the Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Science catalog structure, but stays focused on IT learning paths.

## Subcategories

1. IT Support & Help Desk
2. Networking & Network Administration
3. Systems Administration & Infrastructure
4. Cybersecurity
5. Cloud Computing
6. IT Management & Governance
7. Database Administration
8. Enterprise Systems & ERP
9. Information Systems & MIS
10. Hardware, IoT & Embedded Systems
11. DevOps & Site Reliability Engineering
12. IT Fundamentals & Digital Literacy

## Notes

- Items include metadata such as `title`, `provider`, `platform`, `level`, `type`, `duration`, `cost`, and `skills`.
- Certification-style items are normalized into schema-friendly `Course`, `Certificate`, `Professional Certificate`, or `Diploma` types.
- The degree lists from the research draft are intentionally excluded.
