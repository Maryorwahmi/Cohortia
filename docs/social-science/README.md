# Social Science Learning Paths

> **Scope:** Courses and certificates only. Degree programs are excluded.  
> **Source:** Derived from `docs/catalog-social-science-research.md` and exported to `docs/social-science/catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json`.  
> **Status:** Draft catalog for the Social Science category.

---

## What’s in this folder

- `catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json` is the source of truth for the Social Science course/certificate catalog.
- The catalog currently contains **325 items** across **12 subcategories**.
- It mirrors the other category catalogs already created in Cohortia, but stays focused on social science learning paths.

## Subcategories

1. Psychology & Mental Health
2. Sociology & Anthropology
3. Economics
4. Political Science & International Relations
5. History & Archival Studies
6. Geography & Urban Studies
7. Social Work & Human Services
8. Criminology & Criminal Justice
9. Public Policy & Administration
10. Development Studies & International Development
11. Gender Studies & Social Justice
12. Interdisciplinary Social Science & Research Methods

## Notes

- Each item in the JSON includes metadata such as `title`, `provider`, `platform`, `level`, `type`, and `skills`.
- Degree programs from the research draft are intentionally excluded.
- Certificate-style items are kept in the catalog, while degrees are intentionally left out.
