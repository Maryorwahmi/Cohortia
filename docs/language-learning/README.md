# Language Learning Paths

> **Scope:** Courses and certificates only. Degree programs are excluded.  
> **Source:** Derived from `docs/catalog-language-learning-research.md` and exported to `docs/language-learning/catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json`.  
> **Status:** Draft catalog for the Language Learning category.

---

## What’s in this folder

- `catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json` is the source of truth for the language-learning course/certificate catalog.
- The catalog currently contains **161 items** across **12 subcategories**.
- It mirrors the Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and IT catalog structure, but stays focused on language learning paths.

## Subcategories

1. TESOL / English Language Teaching
2. Spanish & Portuguese Languages
3. French & Italian Languages
4. German & Germanic Languages
5. Chinese Language & Culture
6. Japanese & Korean Languages
7. Arabic & Middle Eastern Languages
8. Russian, Hindi & Other World Languages
9. Translation & Interpretation
10. Linguistics & Applied Linguistics
11. American Sign Language & Deaf Studies
12. Professional Language Skills & Business Communication

## Notes

- Each item in the JSON includes metadata such as `title`, `provider`, `platform`, `level`, `type`, `skills`, and `id`.
- Degree programs from the research draft are intentionally excluded.
- Proficiency exams and professional credentials are kept in the catalog as non-degree learning paths.
