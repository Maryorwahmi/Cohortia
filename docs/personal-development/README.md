# Personal Development Learning Paths

> **Scope:** Courses and certificates only. Degree programs are excluded.  
> **Source:** Derived from `docs/catalog-personal-development-research.md` and exported to `docs/personal-development/catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json`.  
> **Status:** Draft catalog for the Personal Development category.

---

## What’s in this folder

- `catalog-courses-by-subcategory.json` is the source of truth for the Personal Development course/certificate catalog.
- The catalog currently contains **168 items** across **12 subcategories**.
- It mirrors the other category catalogs already created in Cohortia, but stays focused on personal growth and life-skills learning paths.

## Subcategories

1. Psychology & Counseling
2. Coaching & Mentoring
3. Leadership & Management Development
4. Emotional Intelligence & Well-being
5. Communication & Interpersonal Skills
6. Time Management & Productivity
7. Mindfulness & Meditation
8. Positive Psychology & Happiness
9. Career Development & Job Search Skills
10. Personal Finance & Money Management
11. Stress Management & Resilience
12. Creativity & Innovation

## Notes

- Each item in the JSON includes metadata such as `title`, `provider`, `platform`, `level`, `type`, `skills`, and `id`.
- Degree programs from the research draft are intentionally excluded.
- Specialization-style items are normalized into schema-friendly course records.
