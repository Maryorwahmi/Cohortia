## 5. Open Questions

1. **Hybrid / low-residency doctorates / executive MBAs** – Several DBA programs (e.g., Grand Canyon, University of London) require short residencies. Should these be included as "online" degrees, filtered, or tagged "Hybrid"?
2. **Bootcamp tagging convention** – Some intensive business bootcamps (e.g., General Assembly, Flatiron) exist. Should they be tagged as `Bootcamp` only, or also as `Certificate` / `Diploma`?
3. **Cost normalization format** – Subscription-based platforms (Coursera, LinkedIn Learning) use monthly fees; university degrees use per-credit or total tuition. Should the JSON store a normalized `cost` string or a free-text field?
4. **Overlap with Data Science** – Business Analytics subcategory overlaps heavily with Data Science (Business Intelligence & Visualization). Should courses appear in both categories or be split by primary focus?
5. **Overlap with future IT category** – Project Management and Business Analytics tools (Power BI, Tableau, SQL) may also fit an IT category. How should we handle cross-category courses?
6. **Specialized subcategories** – Real Estate, Healthcare Administration, and Nonprofit Management are included under "Specialized Business." Should they be promoted to their own subcategories or kept consolidated?
7. **Professional certification vs. course distinction** – Should vendor certifications (e.g., SHRM-CP, PMP, CFA) be tagged as `Certification` or `Professional Certificate`?
8. **Regional degrees** – UK programs (University of London, Durham, Warwick) and European programs (IE Business School) are included. Should they be prioritized equally with US programs?
9. **Degree naming consistency** – Some programs use "BS Business Administration" while others use "BBA." Should the JSON normalize these titles or keep the provider's official name?
10. **URL verification** – Most entries lack verified URLs in this draft. Should URLs be added during research finalization or deferred to import time?
