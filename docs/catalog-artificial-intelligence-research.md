## 4. Cross-Category Overlap & Deduplication Notes

- **Data Science**: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, MLOps, and Statistics courses are already covered in the Data Science category. This AI category focuses on AI-specific degrees, AI systems, and emerging AI topics (generative AI, LLMs, AI agents, robotics, RL, AI ethics, edge AI) not duplicated in Data Science.
- **Computer Science**: Programming, algorithms, software engineering, and general CS degrees are in Computer Science. This category focuses on AI-specific and AI-applied degrees and courses.
- **Business**: General business strategy, marketing, and finance courses are in Business. This category includes AI-specific business applications (AI product management, AI consulting, AI in finance/marketing/HR) that are distinct from general business courses.
- **Health**: Clinical medicine, nursing, and public health are in Health. This category includes AI-for-healthcare technical courses (AI for diagnosis, drug discovery, medical imaging) that are AI-focused rather than clinically focused.
- **Physical Science & Engineering**: Traditional engineering (mechanical, civil, electrical) is in Physical Science & Engineering. This category includes robotics, autonomous systems, and AI hardware that are AI-first rather than engineering-first.
- **Social Science**: Psychology, sociology, and policy are in Social Science. This category includes AI ethics, AI governance, AI law, and AI policy that are AI-specific.
- **Arts & Humanities**: Creative writing, art history, and music are in Arts & Humanities. This category includes generative AI for creative industries and AI art generation that are technology-focused.
- **Math and Logic**: Pure mathematics, statistics, and logic are in Math and Logic. This category includes applied math for AI (optimization, linear algebra for deep learning) only when taught as part of an AI curriculum.
- **Language Learning**: Translation and linguistics are in Language Learning. This category includes NLP and computational linguistics that are AI/CS-focused.
- **Personal Development**: Leadership and productivity are in Personal Development. This category includes AI leadership and AI strategy for executives that are AI-specific.
- **IT**: Cloud computing, networking, and cybersecurity basics are in IT. This category includes AI security, AI on cloud, and AI infrastructure that are AI-specific.
- **Environmental Science**: Climate science and sustainability are in Environmental Science. This category includes AI for climate modeling and AI for sustainability that are AI-methodology focused.

## 6. Open Questions (need 10 resolved)

1. **Overlap with Data Science**: How much overlap with Data Science is acceptable? Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, and Computer Vision are already in Data Science. Should this AI category focus exclusively on AI-specific degrees and emerging topics (GenAI, LLMs, robotics, AI ethics, edge AI)?
2. **Overlap with Computer Science**: AI degrees are often CS degrees with AI specialization. Should we include only degrees explicitly named "Artificial Intelligence" or also CS degrees with AI tracks?
3. **Generative AI placement**: Should Generative AI and LLMs be a separate top-level category given their explosive growth, or remain as a subcategory within AI?
4. **Robotics placement**: Robotics overlaps with Physical Science & Engineering (mechanical engineering, electrical engineering). Should robotics degrees be split between AI and Engineering, or consolidated in one category?
5. **AI Ethics placement**: AI ethics courses overlap with Social Science (philosophy, policy, law) and Personal Development (leadership). Should they be cross-listed or kept primarily in AI?
6. **African university representation**: Should we include AI-specific degrees from African universities even if they are general CS degrees with AI specializations, or only explicitly named AI degrees?
7. **Professional vs. academic degrees**: Should we distinguish between professional master's (MPS, MEng) and academic master's (MS, MA) in AI? Many professional programs are coursework-only while academic programs require thesis/research.
8. **Certificate vs. degree**: Many AI "degrees" are actually professional certificates or nanodegrees. Should we be stricter about what counts as a degree vs. a certificate?
9. **Emerging topics**: How to handle rapidly emerging topics (AI agents, multimodal AI, AI for science, neural radiance fields) that may not have established degree programs yet?
10. **Vendor-specific courses**: Should vendor-specific courses (Nvidia DLI, Google Cloud, AWS, Azure) be included when they are not vendor-neutral? They are valuable but may not transfer across platforms.
