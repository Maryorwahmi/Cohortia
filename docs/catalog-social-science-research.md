## 4. Cross-Category Overlap & Deduplication Notes

- **Psychology**: Overlaps with Health (clinical psychology, mental health), Personal Development (counseling, coaching), and Business (organizational psychology, I/O psychology). Keep clinical, counseling, and social psychology here; move coaching and business psychology to respective categories.
- **Economics**: Overlaps with Business (finance, accounting, marketing), Math & Logic (econometrics, game theory), and Environmental Science (environmental economics). Keep general economics, development economics, and political economy here; move finance and accounting to Business.
- **Political Science**: Overlaps with Business (public policy, governance), Law (constitutional law, human rights), and Environmental Science (environmental policy). Keep political theory, comparative politics, IR, and security studies here; move specific policy domains (health, education, environment) to respective categories.
- **Social Work**: Overlaps with Health (medical social work, mental health), Personal Development (counseling, case management), and Business (nonprofit management). Keep clinical social work, child welfare, community development, and policy here; move general counseling to Personal Development.
- **Criminology**: Overlaps with Law (criminal law, procedure), Physical Science (forensic science), and Business (security management, compliance). Keep criminology, criminal justice, corrections, and victimology here; move forensic science techniques to Physical Science.
- **Geography**: Overlaps with Environmental Science (environmental geography, GIS), Physical Science (geology, oceanography), and IT (GIS software, remote sensing). Keep human geography, urban studies, and population geography here; move physical geography and GIS software training to respective categories.
- **Development Studies**: Overlaps with Business (social enterprise, impact investing), Environmental Science (climate & development), and Health (global health). Keep development theory, humanitarian action, and poverty reduction here; move sector-specific development to respective categories.
- **Gender Studies**: Overlaps with Personal Development (women's leadership, empowerment), Health (reproductive health, mental health), and Business (gender in organizations). Keep gender theory, intersectionality, and social justice here; move workplace gender training to Business.
- **History**: Overlaps with Arts & Humanities (art history, literature, philosophy), Personal Development (cultural awareness), and Physical Science (history of science). Keep general history, public history, and archival studies here; move art history and philosophy to Arts & Humanities.
- **Public Policy**: Overlaps with Business (management, finance), Law (regulation, constitutional law), and Health (health policy). Keep policy analysis, public administration, and governance here; move sector-specific policy to respective categories.
- **Research Methods**: Overlaps with Data Science (statistics, programming, machine learning), Math & Logic (probability, statistical inference), and Business (analytics, market research). Keep social science-specific methods, ethnography, and qualitative research here; move general data science and programming to IT/Data Science.
- **Interdisciplinary Social Science**: Overlaps with nearly all categories. Keep broad interdisciplinary programs (e.g., MAPSS, general social science) here; move specialized interdisciplinary programs to most relevant category.

## 6. Open Questions (need 10 resolved)

1. **Clinical vs. academic psychology**: Should we separate clinical/counseling psychology (licensure-track) from academic psychology (research-focused) into different subcategories or keep together?
2. **Social work accreditation**: Should we note CSWE accreditation for social work degrees, and distinguish BSW vs. MSW vs. advanced standing tracks?
3. **Economics vs. Business overlap**: Many economics courses overlap with Business (finance, accounting, marketing). Should we keep broad economics here and move applied business economics to Business?
4. **Law overlap**: Constitutional law, criminal law, and human rights law courses appear in Political Science and Criminology. Should we move law-specific courses to a Law category or keep cross-listed?
5. **African university representation**: Should we prioritize African universities (e.g., University of Lagos, University of Nairobi, University of Cape Town, University of Ibadan, Covenant University, University of Dar es Salaam, Jimma University, University of Fort Hare) that may not have extensive online offerings, or focus on international platforms accessible to African students?
6. **PhD vs. Professional Doctorate**: Should we distinguish between research PhDs (e.g., PhD in Psychology) and professional doctorates (e.g., DSW, DPA, PsyD) in social science fields?
7. **Diploma vs. Certificate**: Should we include sub-bachelor diplomas (e.g., Diploma in Social Work, Diploma in Criminology) common in Commonwealth countries (e.g., Nigeria, UK, Kenya, Tanzania)?
8. **Gender Studies placement**: Should Gender Studies remain in Social Science or be moved to Arts & Humanities? Many universities house gender studies in humanities or interdisciplinary programs.
9. **History placement**: Should History remain in Social Science or be moved to Arts & Humanities? History is often considered a humanities discipline in many universities.
10. **Research methods cross-listing**: Should research methods courses be duplicated across all social science subcategories, or consolidated in the Interdisciplinary subcategory with cross-tags?
