## 5. Open Questions

1. **Clinical degree requirements** – Many health degrees (BSN, DNP, MD-adjacent) require licensure, clinical hours, or in-person residencies. Should these be tagged as "Hybrid" or excluded if they cannot be completed 100% online?
2. **Licensure exam prep** – Certifications like NCLEX-RN, PTCB, CPC, and CCS are tied to licensure. Should they be included as `Certification` or `Professional Certificate`?
3. **Allied health associate degrees** – Some allied health roles (OTA, PTA, radiology tech) require associate-level education. Should associate degrees be included, or should the catalog focus on bachelor's and above?
4. **Overlap with Business** – Healthcare Administration overlaps with Business (MHA, hospital management). Should courses appear in both categories?
5. **Overlap with Data Science / IT** – Health Informatics, medical coding, and health data analytics overlap with Data Science and IT. Should they be duplicated or split by primary focus?
6. **Nutrition and Fitness overlap** – Nutrition and exercise science courses could also fit a future Sports / Fitness category. How should this be handled?
7. **Mental health licensing** – Counseling and psychology courses vary in whether they lead to licensure (LPC, LCSW) vs. general education. Should only licensure-track programs be included?
8. **Alternative medicine accreditation** – Naturopathy, acupuncture, and TCM have varying accreditation standards (NCCAOM, CNME). Should we include only regionally accredited programs?
9. **Cost normalization** – Clinical certifications (AAPC, NASM, ACE) have flat fees; university degrees use per-credit pricing. Should the JSON store a normalized `cost` string or free text?
10. **Medical school prep** – MCAT prep courses and pre-medical prerequisites are included. Should they be tagged as a separate subcategory (Pre-Medical) or kept under Medicine?
