## 5. Open Questions

1. **Hybrid / low-residency doctorates** – Several PhD/DBA programs require short on-campus residencies (e.g., Grand Canyon, Indiana Wesleyan, Capitol Tech). Should these be included as "online" degrees or filtered out?
2. **Bootcamp accreditation** – Bootcamps are not accredited degrees; should they be tagged as `Bootcamp` only or also as `Certificate`?
3. **Cost normalization** – Many programs use subscription models or per-credit pricing. Should the JSON store a normalized `cost` string (e.g., "Subscription $39/mo") or a free-text field?
4. **Overlap with Computer Science** – Some degrees (e.g., MCS-DS, MS in Analytics) sit on the boundary of CS and Data Science. Should they appear in both categories or only Data Science?
5. **Vendor-specific vs. vendor-neutral certifications** – Should cloud/vendor certs be a separate subcategory or kept under their functional topic (e.g., AWS Data Engineer under Data Engineering)?
6. **Generative AI courses** – Exploding area; should GenAI/LLM courses get their own subcategory, or remain under Deep Learning & AI?
7. **Regional degrees** – Should non-English / non-US programs (e.g., HSE University, University of London, Imperial College) be prioritized equally?
