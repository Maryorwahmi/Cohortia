## 5. Open Questions

1. **Hybrid / low-residency MFAs and PhDs** – Creative writing and performing arts degrees often require residencies. Tag as "Hybrid" or filter?
2. **Performance-based degrees** – Music, theater, and dance degrees may require in-person assessments. How to handle?
3. **Overlap with Design / Media** – Graphic design, film production, photography could fit a future Design/Media category. Split or duplicate?
4. **MasterClass inclusion** – High-profile but less structured (Neil Gaiman, Margaret Atwood). Include as `Course` or exclude?
5. **Language learning scope** – Should we include consumer apps (Duolingo, Babbel) or limit to university/certificate programs?
6. **Museum / institution partnerships** – MoMA, British Museum, Smithsonian courses are unique. Special tags?
7. **Digital Humanities overlap with Data Science** – Text analysis, GIS, data viz overlap with DS/IT. Appear in both?
8. **Regional balance** – UK programs dominate master's/doctorate list. Add more US and non-English programs?
9. **Professional vs. academic certificates** – Museum studies, editing, TESOL certificates have professional outcomes. Tag as `Professional Certificate` or `Certificate`?
10. **Cost normalization** – Many humanities courses are audit-free on Coursera/edX. Should the `cost` field explicitly note "Audit free" vs. "Certificate paid"?
