## 5. Open Questions

1. **Overlap with Health Category:** Mental health, stress management, and resilience courses overlap with the Health category. Should they be duplicated or cross-referenced? Currently included for their personal development focus, but some are clinically oriented.

2. **Overlap with Business Category:** Leadership, management, and career development courses overlap significantly with Business. Should these be duplicated or should Personal Development focus only on individual skill-building rather than organizational applications? Currently included for individual skill development.

3. **Coaching vs. Counseling Distinction:** Coaching (non-clinical, future-focused) and counseling (clinical, past-focused) are distinct but often confused. Should they be clearly tagged or separated further? Currently kept in separate subcategories but some providers offer both.

4. **ICF Certification Pathways:** ICF certifications (ACC, PCC, MCC) require specific training hours and mentor coaching. Should the catalog include the pathway requirements (e.g., "60 hours + 100 coaching hours for ACC") or just the course entry? Currently focused on the training programs rather than the full credential pathway.

5. **Self-Help vs. Evidence-Based:** Many personal development courses are based on self-help literature rather than peer-reviewed research. Should we prioritize evidence-based courses (e.g., positive psychology research, MBSR) or include popular self-help approaches (e.g., "The Secret," motivational speaking)? Currently focused on evidence-based and university-backed courses.

6. **App-Based Learning:** Mindfulness and meditation apps (Headspace, Calm, Insight Timer) are major providers. Should they be included as courses, or is the catalog focused on structured learning with clear outcomes? Currently included where they have structured courses, but not general app subscriptions.

7. **Corporate Training Overlap:** Soft skills, leadership, and communication training are often delivered as corporate training. Should we include corporate training providers (e.g., Dale Carnegie, FranklinCovey, Ken Blanchard) or focus on individual learners? Currently included where they have individual enrollment options.

8. **Degree vs. Certificate in Psychology:** Many psychology degrees require licensure for clinical practice. Should we tag degrees as "licensure-track" vs. "non-licensure" or "research-focused"? Currently not tagged but could be important for learners seeking clinical careers.

9. **Life Skills vs. Professional Development:** Personal development spans life skills (budgeting, cooking, home repair) and professional development (leadership, communication). Should the scope be limited to professional-relevant personal development, or include broader life skills? Currently focused on professional-relevant personal development.

10. **Cultural Context:** Many personal development concepts (e.g., mindfulness from Buddhism, ikigai from Japan, hygge from Denmark) have cultural origins. Should we tag cultural origins or note potential cultural appropriation concerns? Currently not tagged but could be relevant for learners seeking authentic cultural practices.
